# Novel Specification

Title: The Worldroot Engineer
Repository slug: novel-worldroot-engineer
Genre: scientific fantasy / ecological progression
Male lead: An agricultural engineer who restores the world's magical root network
Length target: 780 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
An agricultural engineer discovers that magic grows through a hidden root network beneath farms, cities, and monster territory.

## Ending
The root network is restored without turning nature into a second political system.
