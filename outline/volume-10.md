## What this volume refuses to do

**It does not answer whose order it is.** Asked four times across the series, answered never, named once without being asked, and not asked again here and not named. The volume advances that thread the only way a volume may advance it: a roll of paper with no name on it and no body on it, and a man carrying it who has a body, said once.

**It does not say anything about whether the telling is still going on.** Untouched for a seventh volume.

**It does not make the Undercommons a victim.** They have been carrying seed for nine years with no charter and no heading, and they did not agree to that and they are not complaining about it, and the volume says so on the page in more than one mouth. **A chapter that makes them grateful, or angry, or surprised, has replaced nine years of people with a scene about how the reader of this body feels.**

**It does not make the rootwoken a symbol or a message.** One body declines a connection and is right about it, and nobody in the volume speaks for the rootwoken as a body, and the second body that declines is not rootwoken and its reason is different, and **the two are never merged into one grievance.**

**It does not let Tarin Callow be either a villain or a proof.** He is right about the figures and he cut a bridge and a person is dead of it, and the volume does not put the two in one sentence, does not let the roll purchase the harm, and does not catch him and does not excuse him. **A chapter that has him say *I was right about the figures* in the same breath as a death has written a speech and not a scene.**

**It does not put a fight in the volume.** There is no fight anywhere in these forty-nine days. The wall at the cut comes down because of water, and a man is hurt under a load, and a village is lost because of a decision a person had to take and not because anybody fought for it.

**It does not heal the ear and does not apologize for it.** Nobody says he is sorry. Nobody touches her arm. Nobody is thanked. Nobody takes the seed work off her. **What it asks of her is a yard and a count of nine carts and a schedule of nine loads, and what the ear prevents is the second of those and not the other two, and the page says the ear stops none of the three and makes none of the three easier.**

**It does not make him a clean win and does not make him right at no cost.** He walks to both bodies and is told by both that he has no standing to answer. He is the only person in four counties who can hold a pattern at either end of a connection, and the volume spends forty-nine days on what it is like to be needed by people who have not asked him and cannot ask him. **He does not become more able. He becomes harder to get, and that is a cost and not a promotion.**

**It does not fill a form, withdraw an offer, answer a question, or close a drawer.** The register form is on the middle table at one hundred and forty-seven days. The offer is on the low board. The form with four lines has one figure on it and three empty ones. The corridor is neither closed nor opened. The sealing order is valid and opens nothing. **It does not let any office lie.** Every document that comes up that road to this holding in ten volumes has been correct, and the two that were the most dangerous were the most correct, and the roll of paper at the gate this volume is correct about the alteration and says so in the words it was written in. **A chapter in this volume that calls a correct document a lie has taken the only thing this book has spent nine volumes earning off the table.**

**It does not make the division heal.** Wild-root and managed-root bodies divide over whether to reconnect, and one of the two bodies that declined does not come to the signing, and the volume does not put that in a room where anybody is persuaded.

**It does not let the seed be free.** Nine loads is nine loads, the seed in them is the harvest of four counties, and the volume does not make a convoy cheap by calling it a commons.

**It does not end on an inventory.** The last chapter ends on a man ending a count he started and not calling it a total. The thirty-five threads are still open on the last morning and the page does not tally them and does not say that it is not tallying them.

**It does not add a heading where the thread says there is none.** Not on the five blank columns that stay blank, not on the seventh, not on the register form, not on the form with four lines, not on the pulse, not on the day sheet with no date on it, and not on the third column of the sheet of terms.
