# Volume 05 Outline: The Heartroot Rota

Volume number: 5
Chapter range: 197–245
Series layer: World-building layer 3 — professional standards, shared infrastructure, and the slow work of giving a thing away. **The volume is about a thing that can be shared and what a body has to give up to share it, and it is about the difference between a rotation and a share.** It may name the **Rootwright Assembly** and its President as a title on paper, it may name **Thornwild** and its Assemblies, and it may define **False Season** as a maintained pattern. It may not name the **Crown Engine** as a working system, may not name **Chancellor Iona Vey**, may not deliver a history lecture, and may not open a **Deep Archive**. **A pulse repeated on a schedule and not made by the ground is this volume's last beat and it is called what it looks like, which is a pulse, and nothing gets a name out of it.**

## Central pressure

**One heartroot, four bodies of households, about fifteen hundred people, and a professional body that says a heartroot serving more than one body may only be held by a person on the roll.** The node delivers **about 1,500 hundredweight a day** of irrigation water into four villages whose own count of what they need is **about 2,100.** The four villages have been sharing it for eleven years on a set of arrangements nobody wrote down, and the arrangements have a name that the Assembly does not recognise, and the Assembly's third clause is one line: **where a heartroot is shared and no person on the roll holds it, the heartroot is not to be patterned.**

The shape of the pressure is that the correct answer and the workable answer are not the same answer. A rota is four turns and a stop in every line and a rule about what happens when the holder is asleep. A share is a number, and a number is a charter, and a charter is the thing this series has been arguing about since the first volume. **Marek Vale can hold a pattern for four. There are two people in this fen who can, and both are of one holding, and one of them is him, and the Assembly's veto is correct and lands on him personally.**

**Three vocabularies and no villain: a professional standard, a village's arithmetic about fairness, and an engineer's habit of being the one who holds it.**

## Starting state

Marek Vale is **thirty-one, engineer of record, Stage 4 Patternkeeper.** One rootmark **four inches** along the inside of his left forearm, permanent, branching twice, and **no second mark.** Two draws spent in Volume 03, neither recovered. He has held a pattern for a crew of four twice, **and the cost of a hold is entered in a line of his own hand because no form exists for it anywhere in the district.** **He cannot read deep memory. There is no deep archive in this volume and no chapter may imply one.**

The Lantern Reach Work Cooperative has **no charter.** It has a book anybody may read, a refusal clause nobody can tax, a compost line of **eighty pounds a month on a run of twelve months not discharged in twelve**, a leak at three-quarters of an inch with no cause and no due date, a beat not logged with its column open, a well house door nine hundred yards off with a **seventh column at thirty-seven**, and **a use log at fifteen lines.** It has a copy of its ledger in five places and can check one.

**The count of people who can hold a pattern for four is TWO, and it is public, and the fen cannot do the second thing and does not know who can.**

## Escalation sequence

### Movement 1 — Four Bodies And One Node (Chapters 197–206)

A professional standard arrives on the ordinary post with four clauses and names nobody, and the fourth clause says an application is not a vacancy to be filled on the ground. Two of the four villages walk up the fen road with figures. A licensed man confirms the veto and gives the reason the Assembly gives: **eleven years and no form.** A turn sheet is found to be four columns with no headings, so the fairness arithmetic rests on four different numbers. A man who is not the engineer of record draws a rota with a stop in every line and a rule about the holder being asleep. **A seedwright who is out on the route for eleven days in twenty refuses to be the second body, out loud, for a reason about distance.** The first of a month turns the year and the compost line comes in at eighty pounds for the fourth month running. Eleven entries are corrected in a turn sheet and all eleven are accepted. **And on the ninth day at Errow Reach the party finds a fifth body patterning the node at night, out of a schedule, correctly, and has been for eleven weeks, and has never said so.**

### Movement 2 — Fifty-Two Is Not A Household (Chapters 207–216)

The four columns get headings and the headings are worse than the missing headings. Marden's return is withdrawn and rewritten and the withdrawal is refused. A fourth village asks to be counted and a fifth body produces a schedule with a share on it that nobody can find the origin of. **The reading of the twelfth of the shelf well comes down the four-day road and this holding still does not have the figure.** A hold is attempted by somebody who has never held one and the failure is entered by that person and not by the engineer of record. **A pattern is held for a crew of five, which is one more than four, and the cost is entered in a line of its own and the count of people who can hold a pattern for four does not move and a new count starts at one.**

### Movement 3 — The Reading That Was Made (Chapters 217–226)

**The volume's major turn.** A figure is produced in a form that a body of households is asked to act on, and **it is a true figure of a pressure and a false figure of a cause, and it is not a lie, because a man who measured the right thing in the wrong place did not lie about anything.** Marden cuts a branch to save the core. **The loss is real, it is named, and the result proves that Marek's single data set is not a neutral public good**, because the only figure anybody had was his and he gave it away without a second reading and without saying that it was one reading.

### Movement 4 — What A Turn Is (Chapters 227–233)

**Eleven days of a rota running badly, in public, with the failures entered by the people who failed.** Soren and Tova negotiate the terms of a refusal and the terms are a cost. A crew of five cannot be trusted to a schedule written by a body with an interest in the schedule. The Assembly's inspection arrives and is correct and finds eleven things and **ten of them are this volume's own fault and the eleventh is not.**

### Volume climax (Chapters 234–242)

**A flood-season test on the heartroot, run by a rotating crew of five, and the crew keeps it.** **One operator takes a permanent rootmark injury so that the group can discharge the pressure safely.** The injury is entered in the operator's own hand, the count of what can be done by whom moves, and **the fieldbook is opened for the first time in five volumes and it is spent on a person and not on a page.**

### Volume resolution (Chapters 242–245)

A local covenant succeeds and it is a rota with a refusal clause in it and the seat words in it are spent here and nowhere else. **The network's output remains uneven and the volume says the word *uneven* and does not improve on it.** And **a pulse comes on a schedule, twice, from a direction no ground in the volume is, and the two pulses are the same to the second, and the fen enters the two figures and improves neither, and that is the volume's last beat.**

## The volume's three questions

1. Carried from Volume 03 and Volume 04, **asked a fourth time and answered never: whose order is it.** **No chapter of this volume answers it.**
2. Carried and unanswered: **is the telling still going on.**
3. New in this volume and the one that drives it: **a thing that can be shared is a thing somebody has to give up, and nobody in this book has yet said what a village gives up when its turn comes round.** **This is the volume's own question and it is not either of the other two, and the fen is careful about that on the page.**

## Power progression

- **Start:** Stage 4, Patternkeeper. Two holds behind him, neither recoverable, four accounts of the same arm in the book that reconcile none of one another.
- **Stage 4 is the whole of the volume and it deepens by use, not by output.** A rota is a maintenance method. **Holding a pattern for five is attempted once, in Movement 4, and it works and it costs, and the volume enters that it worked.**
- **The bottleneck is the antagonist from inside.** **A man who is the only person who can do a thing is a bottleneck and not a system** is said in the first movement and again in the last, and the sentence does not change because the count changed, and **the count does not change in this volume: it is two at the first of the volume and two at the last, and the sentence is said twice and is not improved on.**
- **The mark may not grow a second mark and may not be lengthened.** The burn is not a mark. The injury in the climax is a **rootmark injury** and it belongs to another person and not to Marek, and it is entered in that person's own hand.
- **No anchor work. No human living anchor. No seedheart. No caretaker link. No deep archive. No memory read.** The fieldbook is closed and lying four hundred miles off until the climax, and the one time it is opened it is opened on a person and spent there.

## Guardrails for this volume

1. **No Crown Engine as a working system, no Chancellor Iona Vey named, no history lecture, no Deep Archive.** The Quiet Break may appear as an age and a number and never as a date and never with a region. The Continuity Office may be named on paper as an issuing office and nowhere else, and **no person in it may be named or appear.**
2. **False Season may be named, and may be defined on the page as a maintained pattern that does not come from the sky, and no chapter may say who maintains it or how it is done.** A pulse repeated on a schedule is a pulse and gets no name.
3. **No month is named in narration or in any document.** Months run thirty days. A day is an ordinal, *of this month*, *of last month*, or a bare ordinal. A named month may not be carried in a distance and a distance may not be carried by a named month. *March* is not a month and the only capitalised *March* may be *the March's tin*.
4. **ASCII and straight marks only, and US spelling.** No curly mark, no em dash, no non-ASCII glyph of any kind.
5. **Marek cannot read deep memory in this volume and no chapter may have him do it.** Everything he learns under the Four Elbows is learned by contact, by counting, by walking it, and by being told by a body that may refuse.
6. **The shelf-well readings are on the sixteenth of every month and no batch may skip one or move one.** The shortfall may not be decomposed and **no chapter may write an entry for that well coming back.**
7. **The standing rule about the engineer of record is used ONCE in this volume and not before Movement 3 and not again.** The count of uses in the last season was two and the count of uses in this season is one, and the two counts are two counts.
8. **No more than three `Entered ... by the clerk` blocks in any one chapter, and NONE in a chapter carrying the climax.** Every speech paragraph opens and closes its own quotation. No paragraph ends with speech open.
9. **No new named member of the cooperative.** The five that travel to Errow Reach are the engineer of record, the man of about fifty, the woman who does the sums, the man of the north row with the cough, and the licensed man. **The man of the north row with the cough may not be given a name and the man of about seventy may not be given a name and the man of about fifty may not be given a name.**
10. **The word *nest* may not appear as a category for the living root community.** *Seat* may appear in the covenant's refusal clause in the resolution and nowhere else.
11. **No romance. Nothing tender on the page. Nobody thanked.**
12. **No new final enemy.** Harlan Vetch and the fifth share are a smallholder and a schedule and not a rung.
13. **The use log stands at FIFTEEN and does not move in this volume**, and a hold, a draw, a gate, a document and a reading get no line. **The day-and-not-the-figure count stands at THREE.** **The count of the arm's accounts stands at four and is not increased by anything said about the arm.** **The count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken goes from seven to eight and no higher.**
