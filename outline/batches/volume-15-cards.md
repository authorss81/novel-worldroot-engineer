# The Card Set For The Fifteenth Volume, *The Root Commons*: Forty-Nine Mornings, One Card Each, In Morning Order

**THE GOVERNING FILE FOR THIS VOLUME IS `outline/volume-15.md` AND IT IS ON DISK AND IT GOVERNS. EVERY FIGURE ON EVERY CARD BELOW WAS GENERATED FROM THE RULES THAT FILE PRINTS AND WAS THEN COMPARED CELL BY CELL AGAINST ITS DAY TABLE AT ITS SECTION TEN, AND THE COMPARISON IS PRINTED IN FULL AT SECTION THIRTEEN BELOW. WHERE A CARD AND THAT FILE DISAGREE, THAT FILE GOVERNS.**

**AND THE ONE DECISION THIS CARD SET OWNS IS WHERE THINGS FALL, BECAUSE THE PLAN DELIBERATELY NAMED NO MORNING. THE MAJOR TURN, THE CLIMAX AND THE RESOLUTION ARE PLACED AT SECTION SEVEN BELOW AND THE THIRTY-THREE ARE THIRTY-FIVE OUT AND THE PRESSURES ARE AT SECTION SEVEN TOO.**

**THIS FILE WRITES NO PROSE. THERE IS NO SCENE IN IT, NO LINE OF DIALOGUE, NO LINE OF NARRATION, NO IMAGE AND NO SENTENCE OF NARRATION. EVERY FIGURE IN IT IS SPELLED IN WORDS AND IT CONTAINS NO DIGIT ANYWHERE, INCLUDING IN EVERY DAY IT NAMES.**

## 1. WHAT ONE CARD IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT, AND EACH OF THESE IS A RULE

**A CARD IS A WORKING SHEET FOR ONE MORNING.** It carries the morning's day, its weekday, its ordinal and its month and its place in the volume; every figure of every series that falls on that morning at the house spelling, with the rule that produces that figure printed beside it so that the writer can check it and is not asked to trust it; the lock state and the floors that the morning must not move; the pressure that the morning is under; what that morning may not do; and an ending that is a consequence and not a hook.

**THE REPETITION IN THIS FILE IS DELIBERATE AND IS NOT PROSE.** The floors and the rules beside the figures stand on all forty-nine cards and are printed on all forty-nine, because a floor that a card does not mention is a floor a writer moves and a rule that a card does not print is a rule the writer has to trust. The duplicate-paragraph sweep this file hands on is scoped to the volume's body prose and not to this file, and a repeated standing block here is the working sheet doing its job and not a defect.

**A CARD MAY NAME A PERSON, A PLACE, AN OBJECT AND AN ACTION.** The people it names are people who are already on the page in this manuscript: the engineer of record, his sister, the seed keeper, the woman who keeps the common key at the second place, the man who read the pair out of his own book and read it for the last time, the woman who carries a slate with two numbers on it that never agree, the bookkeeper, the man who was the Crown Engine's continuity engineer, the political bridge between the cooperative and the wild-root refusal, the clerk who walks a mile and a quarter up a lane in his own boots, the mason, and the small council whose membership this volume names.

**A CARD MAY NOT WRITE THE ACTION AS IT WILL APPEAR ON THE PAGE.** If a card has to describe a page to be usable, what that is, is a page, and prose written twice is worse than prose written once. The volume behind's card set is the form and it is followed here.

**AND A CARD IS NOT ANY OF THE FOLLOWING.** It is not a chapter number and carries none, and no card anywhere prints a chapter range. It is not a batch, it does not plan one, it does not cut one and it does not say anywhere where a cutting falls. It is not a word count and not an expected-length band. It is not a summary, a synopsis or a scene. It does not derive: it takes every figure from a rule the plan prints.

## 2. HOW A CARD IS READ, AND THE FIVE PARTS ARE IN THIS ORDER ON EVERY ONE

1. **THE MORNING.** Its day, its weekday, its ordinal of the day of the month and its month, all produced by running the clock and not by reading a pattern across. It names the morning and it names no chapter, because a card set does not own the chapter numbering.
2. **THE FIGURES.** Every figure that morning carries, at the house spelling, each with its rule beside it.
3. **THE LOCK STATE.** Which locked figure, if any, is spoken whole on that morning. Absent from the card means neither is spoken whole and neither appears in a shortened form.
4. **WHAT THE MORNING IS FOR.** One instruction about the pressure, naming people, places, objects and actions and not writing the scene.
5. **WHAT IT MAY NOT DO.** The prohibitions that bite on that morning specifically.
6. **WHAT IT OWES THE NEXT MORNING.** The one thing the following morning must find changed. This is what stops a run of mornings each of which ends where it began.

Day four hundred and fifty-one is a Tuesday and the first of the fourth month and the months run thirty days each. The weekday is found by counting seven from that Tuesday, the ordinal of the day of the month is what is left after the whole months are taken out of the difference between that day and this one, and the month is four plus the whole months.

**Day eight hundred and ninety-nine is a Tuesday and the twenty-ninth of the eighteenth, and it is the last morning of the volume behind. Day nine hundred is a Wednesday and the thirtieth of the eighteenth, and it is the first morning of this volume, and a volume does not leave a gap.**

**COUNT THE DAYS AND THEN ADD ONE, BECAUSE A MORNING IS A DAY AND NOT A DIFFERENCE, AND A MORNING NUMBER THAT IS OFF BY ONE TAKES EVERY FIGURE BUILT ON IT WITH IT.**

**DAY NINE HUNDRED IS THE THIRTIETH OF THE EIGHTEENTH AND NOT THE FIRST OF THE NINETEENTH. THIS VOLUME HAS TWO MONTH TURNS AND NOT ONE, AND THEY ARE ITS SECOND MORNING AND ITS THIRTY-SECOND MORNING, AND NEITHER IS A FOURTH-LINE MORNING.** A month turn turns a calendar and nothing else. ## 5. THE SEVEN SPELLINGS, PRINTED ONCE HERE, AND THE THREE BARE ROUND HUNDRED SITES

## 7. THE FIVE PRESSURES AND WHERE EACH ONE IS PLACED, WHICH IS THE ONE DECISION THIS FILE OWNS

The five pressures are fixed by the plan and this file may not move, reduce, add or soften any of them. What the plan deliberately left open is the morning, and this is the answer.

1. **THE DEFINITION IS URGENT AND THE EXTERNAL DEADLINE IS NOT NEGOTIABLE.** Mornings one to twelve. The office's schedule fixes the moment at which this coalition can say what it will and will not maintain, and the moment is not negotiable by anybody in this yard. The shape of the definition is fought over by people who will not all be bound by it.
2. **THE COALITION IS PLURAL BY FACT AND NOT BY VIRTUE, AND ITS FIRST TEST OF ITS OWN DEFINITION IS A REFUSAL IT DID NOT ORDER.** Mornings thirteen to thirty, and **the refusal itself is the major turn, on the twenty-eighth morning.** The bridge enters as arithmetic on the thirteenth morning; the small council is named on the seventeenth; its arithmetic is put in front of the yard and is not wrong on the nineteenth; the cost is named before the refusal on the twenty-sixth; **the council refuses on the twenty-eighth.** The refusal is not softened, not explained away as misunderstanding, and nobody in this holding thanks the council for it on any morning of this volume.
3. **MAINTENANCE IS WORK AND NOT A CLAUSE, AND THE LOAD FALLS ON THE CREWS AND NOT ON THE ENGINEER.** Mornings five, twelve, fifteen, twenty-nine, thirty, thirty-five, thirty-six and forty-two. His own contribution everywhere is a schedule and a set of terms and not a command.
4. **THE CLIMAX IS FOUR KINDS OF WORK AT ONCE.** **The climax is the forty-second morning.** The ring of replacement seedhearts goes in on the thirty-fifth, the dry-season draw is applied on the thirty-sixth, the flood load is prepared and one region refuses it on the thirty-seventh, the community names the route it will defend on the fortieth, the four kinds of work are arranged on the forty-first, and all four happen on the forty-second, in four places, by four sets of people, none of them waiting on him. **He is not thanked, not rebuked and not consoled.** The results are published in full on the forty-third with the failures in them and the refusing region's gap in them, and the refusal is not overruled.

THE WINDOW IS NOT CLOSED IN THIS VOLUME AND IT OPENS THE VOLUME THAT FOLLOWS THIS ONE. No card places a scene inside it and no card previews what is in it.**

**AND THE MAJOR TURN IS NOT THE CLIMAX AND THE CLIMAX IS NOT THE RESOLUTION AND NONE OF THE THREE IS THE FIRST MORNING OR THE LAST.** The major turn is the twenty-eighth, the climax is the forty-second, the resolution is the forty-ninth, and the first is the first and the last is the last.

## 11. THE GATE, ITS NORMALISATION IN FULL, THE SELF-COLLISION TEST, AND THE SWEEPS NEITHER GATE RUNS

**RUN THE SELF-COLLISION TEST FIRST, BEFORE EITHER GATE IS RUN AGAINST A SINGLE MORNING. A GATE THAT HAS NOT BEEN CHECKED AGAINST A PARAGRAPH BUILT TO COLLIDE WITH ITSELF IS NOT A GATE AND WILL PUBLISH A NIL ON A VOLUME THAT HAS ONE.** The volume behind's result, to be reproduced before anything is published: **twenty excess sliding eighteen-word windows on one paragraph built to collide with itself, and a ratio of exactly one on the first gate.**

**THE NORMALISATION IN FULL, BECAUSE A GATE THAT DOES NOT NAME ITS MEASURE CANNOT BE REPRODUCED.** Numerals and spelled number words both replaced by one token; the word *and* left alone; every hyphenated compound split on the hyphen before lookup; punctuation dropped; case folded; every chunk a full eighteen words.

**AND IT IS RUN ON BOTH READINGS AND BOTH ARE PUBLISHED EVEN WHEN THEY ARE CLEAN.** The first is non-overlapping whole-paragraph chunks. **The second is every sliding eighteen-word window at every offset inside each paragraph, and a whole-paragraph reading publishes two shapes and eight excess where the sliding reading publishes seventeen and sixty-eight on the same forty-nine mornings, and that has happened nine times in this series. **AND THE SWEEP NEITHER GATE RUNS, WHICH IS CHEAP AND WHICH HAS FOUND EVERY LITERAL DUPLICATE IN THE LAST TWO VOLUMES: A WHOLE-VOLUME EXACT-DUPLICATE PARAGRAPH SWEEP AT ANY PARAGRAPH LENGTH.
