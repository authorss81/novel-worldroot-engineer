## 1. WHAT ONE CARD IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT, AND EACH OF THESE IS A RULE

**A CARD IS A WORKING SHEET FOR ONE MORNING.** It carries the morning's day, its weekday, its ordinal and its month and its place in the volume; every figure of every series that falls on that morning at the house spelling, each with the rule that produces that figure printed beside it so that the writer can check it and is not asked to trust it; the lock state and the floors that the morning must not move; the pressure that the morning is under; what that morning may not do; and an ending that is a consequence and not a hook.

**THE REPETITION IN THIS FILE IS DELIBERATE AND IS NOT PROSE.** The floors and the rules beside the figures stand on all forty-five cards and are printed on all forty-five, because a floor that a card does not mention is a floor a writer moves and a rule that a card does not print is a rule the writer has to trust. The duplicate-paragraph sweep this file hands on is scoped to the volume's body prose and not to this file, and a repeated standing block here is the working sheet doing its job and not a defect. **The sliding reading was run over this file's own cards and what it returned is printed at section fourteen, and it returned the standing block and nothing else that this file did not already know it had put there.**

**A CARD MAY NAME A PERSON, A PLACE, AN OBJECT AND AN ACTION.** The people it names are people who are already on the page in this manuscript: the engineer of record, the seed keeper who works where she can see the mouth, his sister who keeps the moving reserves, the woman who has the boards and gives the figure in force, the mason on the wall who gives the weight in the channel, the woman whose book has two ruled lines and who is one of two hands on a paper, the man in the one room with the drawer shut and the key on its nail, the man who carries the long sheet and speaks for the wild-root refusal on the paths, the woman with a slate who carries two figures that never agree, the clerk who walks a mile and a quarter up a lane in his own boots, the woman of the north branch with eleven households behind her, the mason who works the crossings, the girl from the second place who keeps the rack and the common key, the woman who came up the lane with a pail, the two people at either end of the joint with nothing against it, and the Chancellor, who is named in this volume for the first time and who is not new.

**A CARD MAY NOT WRITE THE ACTION AS IT WILL APPEAR ON THE PAGE.** If a card has to describe a page to be usable, what that is, is a page, and prose written twice is worse than prose written once. The volume behind's card set is the form and it is followed here.

**AND A CARD IS NOT ANY OF THE FOLLOWING.** It is not a chapter number and carries none, and no card anywhere prints a chapter range. It is not a batch, it does not plan one, it does not cut one and it does not say anywhere where a cutting falls. It is not a word count and not an expected-length band. It is not a summary, a synopsis or a scene. **It does not derive: it takes every figure from a rule the plan prints, and it prints the rule beside the figure.**

## 2. HOW A CARD IS READ, AND THE SIX PARTS ARE IN THIS ORDER ON EVERY ONE

1. **THE MORNING.** Its day, its weekday, its ordinal of the day of the month and its month, all produced by running the clock and not by reading a pattern across. It names the morning and it names no chapter, because a card set does not own the chapter numbering. **A month turn, a fourth-line morning, a return morning and a read-aloud morning are named on the card that carries them, and a morning that carries none of the four says nothing about any of them.**
2. **THE FIGURES.** Every figure that morning carries, at the house spelling, each with its rule beside it.
3. **THE LOCK STATE.** Which locked figure, if any, is spoken whole on that morning. **A card that does not name a locked figure means that neither is spoken whole on that morning and that neither appears in a shortened form on it.**
4. **WHAT THE MORNING IS FOR.** One instruction about the pressure, naming people, places, objects and actions and not writing the scene.
5. **WHAT IT MAY NOT DO.** The prohibitions that bite on that morning specifically.
6. **WHAT IT OWES THE NEXT MORNING.** The one thing the following morning must find changed. This is what stops a run of mornings each of which ends where it began.

## 6. THE TWO FIGURES NO PASS MAY REDUCE, THE ALLOWANCE, AND THE CHOICE THIS FILE MAKES

> **"The far end of a thing is held by two people and neither of them is me, and no instrument anywhere in four counties says whether it is on or off."**
>
> **"The comfort is not standing, the father is not alive in the wood, and the day does not come back."**

**A MORNING MAY RESTATE EITHER AND MAY NOT REDUCE EITHER BY A WORD.** The words *anywhere in four counties*, *whether it is on or off*, *far end of a thing*, *the comfort is not standing*, *the father is not alive in the wood* and *the day does not come back* are load-bearing, **and a shortened form of either sentence is a different figure and not a shorter one, and a reuse of either sentence's opening clause as somebody else's construction is also a different figure.**

**THE DEFECT THE VOLUME BEHIND INHERITED IS STILL OPEN AND A SUCCESSOR MUST NOT INHERIT THE RULE AS UNBROKEN.** A load-bearing phrase of the far-end sentence appears once outside its own whole sentence, in a closed morning, where a character reuses the opening clause as a conditional antecedent. **IT IS CLOSED PROSE, IT IS REPORTED, IT IS NOT REPAIRED, AND IT IS THE REASON A WHOLE-VOLUME SWEEP IS NOT OPTIONAL IN A CARD SET THAT CARRIES THIS VOLUME'S FIGURES.** The claim that the sentence was not in the volumes behind at all is false and was withdrawn by name: it stands as a whole paragraph in nine closed mornings.

**THE ALLOWANCE IS ONE CLAUSE AND IT IS THIS, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF IT: each of the two locked sentences may be spoken whole on this volume's first morning and on this volume's last morning and on not more than two mornings in between, and the two may be spoken whole on the same morning only on this volume's first morning and on this volume's last morning and on no other morning in this volume.**

**AND THIS FILE MAKES THE ONE CHOICE THE ALLOWANCE LEAVES OPEN, AND IT DECLARES IT HERE ON THE FACE OF THE FILE. THERE ARE THREE IN-BETWEEN MORNINGS AVAILABLE AND THIS FILE SPENDS TWO OF THEM, ONE FOR EACH FIGURE, AND SPENDS A THIRD ON NEITHER.**

## 7. THE FIVE PRESSURES AND WHERE EACH ONE IS PLACED, WHICH IS THE ONE DECISION THIS FILE OWNS

The five pressures are fixed by the plan at its section two-b and this file may not move, reduce, add or soften any of them. What the plan deliberately left open is the morning, and this is the answer.

1. **THE WINDOW IS OPEN AND IT IS NOT A DEADLINE ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING CAN MOVE.** Mornings one to forty-five, and it never closes inside this volume and no morning names or describes what is inside it.
