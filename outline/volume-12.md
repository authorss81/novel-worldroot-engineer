## 3. WHAT THE VOLUME IS ABOUT, IN THE HOLDING'S OWN IDIOM

**There is a letter on the long table and it has been there since the morning before this one, and nobody has answered it, and nobody has been asked to, and there is no column in four books with a heading on it for a letter that asks a building nine miles off to put its sheet back the way it was.**

**THE VOLUME'S PROBLEM IS NOT WHETHER THE PATTERNS CAME BACK. THEY CAME BACK.
