# Volume 03 Outline: The Memory Orchard

Volume number: 3

Chapter range: 99–147

Series layer: World-building layer 4 — regional history and a competing explanation. The volume leaves the fen. It goes up onto a high plateau of exposed rootwood, old observatories and sealed deep mines, and it puts a *sequence of cuts* in a cut face that a competent engineer can read and a historian cannot. **It may name the Continuity Office as an office that holds, indexes and licenses records, and it may name the Quiet Break as a dated entry in a record. It may not name the Crown Engine as a working system, may not name Chancellor Iona Vey, may not name False Season, and may not deliver a history lecture.** A name on a form is not an explanation. The Crown in this volume is an inventory, a category, and a declaration of unfitness.

## Central pressure

An old orchard at Cauldron Reach in the Glassward high country is putting out a crop of last season's fruit under a sky that is not the sky that grew it. The fruit is real, the dates are real, the trees are alive, and the plateaus' exposed rootwood carries a scar that reads, to anyone who can count healed cuts, as an **ordered sequence of deliberate removals** made by people who are dead. A grower wants the harvest, because it is the best money any of these people have seen in four years. An office wants the records declared unfit for evidentiary use, because a record that can be read is a record that can be cited.

Three pressures, three vocabularies, and no villain: a grower's arithmetic, a records officer's form, and a root engineer's graft.

## Starting state

Marek Vale is thirty, engineer of record, **Stage 2 Grafthand**, with one rootmark four inches along the inside of his left forearm, permanent, branching twice, and two to three active links with a fourth that fails. He can make a small reliable graft. **He cannot draw. He cannot return. He cannot read deep memory safely.** He has no deep memory to read: the fieldbook is a fragment — front board, living root bead, and a handful of pages — and it was not opened in Volume 02 at all.

The Lantern Reach Work Cooperative has **no charter.** It expired at midnight and seven practices are kept by choice. It pays a shilling a month to a district on a declared capacity five times the true one, with the true one published underneath. It has a book anybody may read, a refusal clause nobody can tax, an arrangement nobody can enforce, a toll collected at a gate, sixty pounds ten unpaid in an open file, a leak of five-eighths of an inch at the seventh hour, a pressure beat logged thirteen times with nothing underneath it, and a compost line short three hundred and ninety-nine hundredweight for the fourth month running. **Nia's cart has been past the gate twice in a season and has not stopped.**

## Starting relationships

- **Marek / Tova:** shared records and one book in two countries and neither keeps a private copy. They disagree about who owns a grafted seed and neither has conceded. **A paper she has read, on the shape of a free offer with a signature attached, sits in this volume and may never be given a number or a place.**
- **Marek / Lissa:** unforgiven, unthanked, and running the seed house. She identifies a hand. **She is not a woman who gets to travel lightly, and Volume 03 has to pay for her going.**
- **Marek / Sera Quill:** absent, on a fee, unreachable, and the author of a fourth refusal that is unnumbered, unasked and may not be numbered. **Volume 03 is the volume she breaks.**
- **Marek / Coll Renner:** licensed, available, a copper clip, nine men and not enough work, and the only man in the fen whose license makes a reading admissible.
- **Marek / Gael Senn:** twenty-three or thereabouts by now, the examiner, the person who writes things down in the second wording and puts her name on the decision.
- **Marek / the district:** the water office, a form, a class of use, a rate. Correct, structural, and in this volume writing down that a category of record may be unfit for use.

## Starting power level

Stage 2, Grafthand. Small reliable grafts. Two or three active links, a fourth fails. **No draw, no return, no anchor, no seedheart, no caretaker, no deep archive.** The series contract puts Stage 3 at Chapter 99 and the first severe rootburn inside this volume. **Stage 3 arrives by use and is paid for in the same chapter: darkened veins past the rootmark, fever, numbness, loss of taste, and a downstream well that drops and does not come back.**

## Major locations

- **The seed house, Lantern Reach:** the table in the middle with three books on it and a fourth thing in a shut drawer; the press with the March's tin under the ledger at three and a half degrees; the bed at three and a half degrees; the bay with two boards and a number cut into both that is still going up.
- **The well house door:** the notice, the four sheets of class 4, the schedule with thirty declared and six measured in the same ink, and the refusal clause in the fen's own hand under the seven columns. **It is the fen's only public face and Volume 03 leaves it behind for five weeks.**
- **Fennmere:** the sub-office and the counter, the register, and a printer who has been asked three times in a month for a number on a book that has none.
- **The Glassward road:** nine days up and four down, one change of horses, a plateau at the end of it where the wind comes off the ice and the rootwood stands out of the ground in the open.
- **Cauldron Reach:** eleven acres of old pear and plum on a south-facing shelf, a stone wall, four houses, a well two hundred yards downslope, and a walled yard of pressed rootwood that has been stacked against the north wall for longer than anybody there can date.

## Major factions and pressures

- **Hearth Crown, district water office, records desk:** a form. A schedule of records held. A category of record that may be declared **unfit for evidentiary use** on an inventory, by an officer, in the ordinary course, without a hearing, because an unfit record is not evidence of anything. **No officer in this volume lies, obstructs, or is corrupt.**
- **Continuity Office:** named once or twice, on paper, as the office an index belongs to. **A name at the head of a column is not a history.**
- **Merrid Ollvar, of Cauldron Reach:** about fifty-four, four houses, eleven acres, thirty-one years on the shelf. She has come down off the plateau herself because nobody answered her letters. **She is not a member of the cooperative, she is not a villain, and she is not redeemed.** She wants the harvest and she wants to know why.
- **The buyer:** a house that has offered a price per hundredweight for the fruit for four consecutive seasons and has never been told why the fruit is unusual. **No name is needed and none may be invented as a villain; the offer is a figure on a paper.**
- **Sera Quill:** the volume's antagonist position, inside the protagonist's own history. **She is not a final villain and her confession cannot restore trust.**

## Midpoint reversal (Chapters 119–126)

The sequence in the scarroot is not a scar. It is an **order of operations**, and it is in the order an engineer uses: a branch is taken, the wound is dressed, the wound is allowed to close, the next branch is taken, the wound is dressed. **Eleven of them, in one row, in one season, at intervals a man can count.** The first root engineers did not lose these branches to rot. They took them on purpose, in sequence, with a tool that is not a tool anybody in this volume owns, and the reason is written on nothing that survives.

Then the reversal: **the pruning was not a mistake that went wrong. It was a decision that went right.** The orchards that were pruned hardest are the ones that came back, and the ones that were left alone are the ones that did not. Marek has spent two volumes learning that a readable thing gets indexed and taxed. **He now has to hold the possibility that a readable thing was pruned on purpose by people who were right.**

## Escalation sequence

### Movement 1 — Last Season's Crop (Chapters 99–108)

The arrangement's term ends with the closing of the sowing and the route goes on by a new writing that is shorter than the old one. A grower comes down off the Glassward with a crate of fruit that was gathered last season. The plateaus' exposed rootwood carries a cut that a man with a rule can date. **The first draw, the first rootburn, and a well two hundred yards downslope that drops and does not come back.** The volume's question opens on a form: an inventory of historical rootwork records, and a category that may be declared unfit for use. **The fieldbook is opened for the first time in a year, by Lissa Vale, with a licensed reader in the room, and no use-log line is written for it, because there has never been one and there is not going to be.**

### Movement 2 — The Thing In The Yard (Chapters 109–118)

The pressed rootwood against the north wall of Cauldron Reach is dated, counted and read. A records officer comes up the road with the inventory. Tova recognizes the **shape** of a free offer with a signature attached, and says so, and refuses to say where she has seen that shape. **The deep answer's first hard object:** a graft in living wood whose pattern is a maintenance sequence, made by hand, by somebody who expected to come back.

### Movement 3 — What Sera Took Out (Chapters 119–126)

**The midpoint reversal and the volume's major turn.** Sera Quill comes up the plateau road and does not charge a fee. She admits what she did nineteen years ago: she designed a safety relay that could have isolated a pruning pulse before the **Brinewake Collapse**, she removed the relay's red-line warning, she changed the log to routine recalibration, and she signed a report blaming salt creep and weather. **She gives Marek the relay fragment and nothing else.** The sealed compensation record stays sealed. **And Marek finds a record suggesting his father chose to cut a branch during a real emergency, in his father's own hand, with no reason on the page.**

### Movement 4 — The Unfit Category (Chapters 129–138)

The inventory is served. Copies go out before it can be answered, into four hands and down two roads, because Tova's rule is that a readable thing survives in more places than one. **The orchard's commercial harvest is sacrificed to keep the record alive**, which is the volume's cost stated in fruit and in money. Rootwoken growth comes up through the abandoned rows, and it is not an attack and it is not a symbol.

### Volume climax (Chapters 137–142)

A living archive seed is taken out of the walled yard before the declaration can reach it, and the evidence is published in copied ledgers and local memory. **The truth survives in more places than one office can hold.** Sera's restitution begins. The rootwoken spread is left alone and named as what it is.

### Volume resolution (Chapters 142–147)

The Crown declares the records unsafe, and a migration of farmers begins — not a flight, a movement, with carts and dates and a destination that is not yet decided. The orchard is a working record and not a monument. The fen's own books gain a fourth line about a thing four hundred miles away. **The question the volume leaves standing: if the Quiet Break was engineered, who is still maintaining the machine that caused it?**

## Power progression

- **Start:** Stage 2, Grafthand. One rootmark, two to three links, a fourth that fails. No draw.
- **Stage 3 arrives in Movement 1 by use, not by ceremony.** A draw is water, heat and rootlight moved through a living path and returned, and the cost is paid in the same chapter and is not recovered in the same chapter.
- **The first severe rootburn in the series.** Darkened veins running past the four inches of the rootmark and into the elbow, fever, numbness in three fingers, loss of taste, and a well two hundred yards downslope that four houses drink from. **The downstream debt is on the page with people attached to it and is not refunded in this volume.**
- **The mark may not grow twice and no second rootmark may appear.** Volume 02's mark is a record of what he connected; this volume's burn is a record of what he took.
- **No anchor work.** Nothing in this volume may lean toward a human living anchor; that begins in Volume 06.

## Relationship progression

- **Marek / Tova: the milestone for this range is that they choose not to keep private records.** Not because a rule says so. Because Sera hands Marek a fragment and he has to decide where it lives, and Tova has a thing she has carried for a volume and has never numbered, and the two decisions are the same decision. **Shared information becomes an obligation rather than an advantage.**
- **Marek / Lissa:** she opens the fieldbook. It is in her custody, it is her husband's, and opening it is hers to do and not his to ask for. **The volume must not have him ask for it.**
- **Marek / Sera:** her confession is a set of records decisions and it is not an eyewitness memory. **It buys no trust. It is followed by nothing in this volume except work.**
- **Nothing romantic.** The slow burn runs on shared records, on who is holding what, and on two people who keep finding out what the other one will not do.

## Antagonist position in the ladder

Rung 2 (the Directorate as structure) continues in a new office: the records desk and the inventory. Rung 3 (Halden Pike and the Assembly) is present as a **license** and a professional veto and a man in Renner's ear, and Pike is not on the page in person in this volume's first movement. **Rung 5 — Sera Quill and the inherited First Continuity — is the volume's antagonist position, and it is inside the protagonist's own history.** Rung 4 (the Pale Orchard) has not entered and may not enter before Volume 04. Rung 6 (Iona Vey) may not be named.

## Guardrails for this volume

1. **No Crown Engine as a working system, no Chancellor Iona Vey, no False Season, and no history lecture.** The Quiet Break may appear as a dated entry with a region and a number and nothing else. **The Continuity Office may be named as an office on paper and nowhere else.**
2. **The Fieldbook panel is used once per batch and is never quoted as an instruction in any document**, and no batch's panel may be a person's finding.
3. **No month is named in narration or in any document.** October is five months back by day 151 and is named nowhere.
4. **Marek cannot read deep memory in this volume and no chapter may have him do it.** The sequence in the scarroot is read by **counting healed cuts and dating them with a rule**, which is engineering. No vision, no father's voice, no archive.
5. **The Thornwild settlement is not named, the long line is not used, the word *seat* does not appear, and no bypass is built.**
6. **The pressure pulse stays undiagnosed and the leak is not mended.** Neither is a mystery and neither is a plot.
7. **No romance.** Nothing tender on the page.
8. **A system that reads a seed's history in four seconds is not a soul and not a mind**, and the volume's discovery about grafting holds: a graft keeps the thing put in and loses the thing that was there.
9. **No new named member of the cooperative.** The crew is the same crew. The man of about fifty and the man of about seventy stay held by function and may not be given names without recording it.

## Expected length

Ordinary chapters run **2,600–3,200 words**; the plateau chapters may run longer because the work is one continuous sequence. **The Volume 01 and Volume 02 lessons are binding: trim, do not pad, and do not open five chapters at 3,600.** A batch of ten chapters should land between 27,000 and 30,000 words and the writer should say in the state files what it actually measured.
