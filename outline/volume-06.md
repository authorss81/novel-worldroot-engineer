# Volume 06 Outline: The False Season

Volume number: 6
Chapter range: 246–294
Series layer: World-building layer 3 turning into layer 4 — a local commons that has done everything right and is about to be handed somebody else's shortage. **The volume is about a figure that improves and a cost that arrives somewhere else, and about the difference between a person who is right and a body that has agreed.** It may name **False Season** and define it on the page as a maintained pattern that does not come from the sky. It may name the **Crown Engine** as a working system, the **Continuity Office** as an issuing office, **Chancellor Iona Vey** as the officer whose name is on the paper, and **Auberron** and the **Auberron Basin**. It may open **one** small read of environmental memory out of rootwood, with a recorded consent, a stop signal and a safe disconnect. It may not open the **Deep Archive**, may not name a **caretaker link**, may not bring **Iona Vey** into a room, and may not give the pulse a name beyond the one the fen already gave it.

## Central pressure

**The shortfall closes and it is a bad thing.** One heartroot at the Four Elbows, four bodies of households, and a fifth party who holds a share and has never been in a line with anybody. The node delivered about **1,500 hundredweight a day** for eleven years and the four villages' own count of what they need is about **2,100.** In Chapter 246 the four launders measure **nine hundred, six hundred, five hundred and seventy-five, and twenty-five**, and the total is **two thousand one hundred**, exactly, and the difference between what arrives and what is needed is **nothing.** The fifth line of a covenant four bodies of households signed in the third month says the figure is the same figure it was before the sheet was drawn and the sheet does not change it. **A document that was true when it was signed and is false now is not a lie and it is not harmless.**

**Three hundred and forty hundredweight a day of it is going down a channel that was sawed through at the kerb on the eleventh of the first month and was never repaired**, and nobody knows where that channel goes, and the engineer of record says the wrong thing in a yard on the second day and costs four days because four bodies of households go and look under the node instead of at the far end of a launder. The far end is a stone head and a field gate eleven hundred yards east, and behind the gate are about nine acres of the low ground of Marden worked by about eleven people, and the water is standing against that gate about nine inches deep in the fourth month, and the ground on the far side of it is coming up green.

**The shape of the pressure is that a body of households cannot ask a machine a question, and that the reason is not that the machine is hiding.** The Crown's sheet of the third of the month is correct in every particular: it names a district, it places it in a zone, it states a maintenance operation, it gives the Crown's own two figures, it sets a compensation rate that is real, and it says the maintenance will be reviewed before the next window. **A correct schedule is a schedule that has been carried out, and the water that is gone is gone because somebody did what they said they would do.** The antagonism of this volume is a **doctrine** and a **machine**: *a branch that cannot be predicted cannot be centrally maintained, and a network that cannot be centrally maintained cannot be relied on by the largest number of people.* That doctrine is not Iona Vey's invention and no chapter may make it her opinion, and it has a real record of saving the largest number of people in more than one bad year.

**Marek Vale is thirty-one, engineer of record, Stage 4, and the volume takes him to the edge of Stage 5 and does not put him over in a scene where nobody is hurt.** The device that shows it is a disagreement, not a rescue: **Tova Reed writes four lines of safeguards and Sera Quill writes three, and the two lists differ in one place, and Sera's list is better at what it is for and Tova's is the one with a line in it about a person.**

## Starting state

Marek is **thirty-one**. One rootmark, **four inches**, along the inside of the left forearm, branching twice, and **no second mark.** Two draws spent in Volume 03, neither recovered. **He cannot yet read environmental memory out of rootwood**, and one read is attempted in this volume and it is not clean and it is not the volume's answer. The count of persons in this fen who can hold a pattern for four is **two** and one of them has three inches on the right forearm that do not branch. The count of persons in this fen who have a rootmark is **two**. The count of persons who can hold a pattern for four and walk out of the ground afterwards is **one**, and it is him.

The Lantern Reach Work Cooperative has **no charter.** It has a book anybody may read, a refusal clause nobody can tax, a compost line of **one hundred pounds a month on a run of fifteen months not discharged in fifteen**, a leak at three-quarters of an inch with no cause and no due date, a beat not logged with its column open, a well house door nine hundred yards off with a **seventh column at forty-four** and **six blank columns that have been blank for four years**, and **a use log at fifteen lines.** The covenant of six lines has **four signatures, one objection under it and one refusal to sign**, and its fourth line is Marden's and is written into the document as empty.

**The pulse has two figures in it and no name and is not read a third time.**

## Escalation sequence

### Movement 1 — What A Figure Is For (Chapters 246–255)

The first of the month, the compost line at one hundred pounds and the run at fifteen, the seventh column at forty-four, and **the four launders measure nine hundred, six hundred, five hundred and seventy-five and twenty-five.** The fifth party weighs one hundred and forty hundredweight at the eighteenth ninth-day return and **leaves the second rule blank for the first time in eleven years.** The reader of this body tells four bodies of households in a yard of about nineteen people that the water is coming up out of the ground under the Four Elbows, **and it is not, and the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong goes to four.** A sheet comes up the fen road from an office whose paper is not the Assembly's paper: it places the district in a zone, it gives the Crown's own two figures, and **it names him.** Three places at the same hour with no living path between any two of them. A frost in the fourth month with a clear sky over it. A woman of fifty-eight with a schedule in an earlier hand. An inspector who corroborates a figure nobody asked her to corroborate. A seedwright and an engineer with two lists of safeguards that differ by one line. And, on the eleventh day, **a yard at Errow Reach in about nineteen people where the decision is taken and nobody is asked, and the count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken goes from nine to ten.**

### Movement 2 — The Office That Is Only Correct (Chapters 256–265)

The compensation schedule is real money and four bodies of households are poor. The first memory read is attempted under Tova Reed's four lines and under Sera Quill's three, and **the two lists are used together and the result is not clean.** A second rootwoken movement comes south on a road and is counted by a body that may refuse to be the one that counts. **A Crown inspector arrives with a schedule and is correct and does not lie and does not obstruct and is not the antagonist, and the antagonist is the sentence on the schedule.** The question *what maintenance is on the ground* is put to the same office in the same words as *what maintenance is in a document* and gets two different answers, both correct, both in writing, and **the difference between them is the whole of this volume.**

### Movement 3 — What The Crown Is Pruning (Chapters 266–275)

**The volume's major turn.** A fieldbook is used for the first time in two volumes and it gives a reading that is a local pressure and not a cause, and a set of scars along a maintenance conduit is walked from the top of a valley to the bottom by four people and a Crown officer who is not obstructive. **A branch is found that has been reduced and not broken, and the reduction is dated, and the date is inside a maintenance window, and the window is on a schedule.** The proof that it is the same thing and not four separate things is a **ledger**, and the ledger is public, and **it is incomplete and credible**, and that is the volume's answer and not a victory.

### Movement 4 — The Two Reasons To Stop (Chapters 276–285)

Two bodies refuse for two different reasons and neither refusal is a quarrel. The 1,100-yard channel is either left open or stopped and the second attempt at that decision is taken by other people and **is taken in a room and not in a yard.** Sera's information is tested against a public record and **most of it holds and one item does not, and she says so before anybody asks.**

### Volume climax (Chapters 286–290)

**An engine relay, on the ordinary post, with a seal on it that is a real seal.** Marek, Tova and Soren go in. They recover **a living seed containing the control sequence** — a control sequence, not a mind, not a voice, and a thing that can open a local valve and cannot command a running machine. They come out before the chamber is sealed, and **Soren Rill is the one who makes the timing decision and is right about it**, and nobody thanks him and he is not thanked and did not ask.

### Volume resolution (Chapters 290–294)

**Public proof is incomplete and credible and a Crown office declares root stewardship under military protection, and both of those are true on the same page in the same week.** A memorandum of protection goes out over the name of **Chancellor Iona Vey** and it is correct, and it is a document that puts a uniform between a seed and the ground it is in. **The word *seat* does not appear in this volume at all.** And the network's output stays uneven and the volume says the word *uneven* once and does not improve on it.

## Guardrails for this volume

1. **No Deep Archive and no caretaker link and no seedheart as an instrument anybody carries.** Iona Vey is named; she is not in a room. The **Crown Engine** may be named as a working system and may be described only through what its documents and its pruned branches do. **No history lecture and no date.** The Quiet Break may appear as an age and a number.
2. **False Season may be named and may be defined on the page as a maintained pattern that does not come from the sky, and no chapter may say how it is done inside the machine.** A pulse repeated on a schedule is a pulse and gets the name the fen gave it and no other.
3. **No month is named in narration or in any document.** Months run thirty days. A day is an ordinal, *of this month*, *of last month*, or a bare ordinal. *March* is not a month and the only capitalised *March* may be *the March's tin*.
4. **ASCII and straight marks only, and US spelling.** No curly mark, no em dash, no non-ASCII glyph of any kind.
5. **The shelf-well readings are on the sixteenth of every month and no batch may skip one or move one.** The shortfall may not be decomposed and **no chapter may write an entry for that well coming back.**
6. **The standing rule about the engineer of record is used at most ONCE in this volume, not before Movement 3, and not again.**
7. **No more than three `Entered ...` blocks in any one chapter, and NONE in a chapter carrying a climax.** Every speech paragraph opens and closes its own quotation. No paragraph ends with speech open.
8. **No new named member of the cooperative.** **The word *seat* may not appear anywhere in this volume. *Brinewake* is spent, uncorroborated, and absent.**
9. **No romance. Nothing tender on the page. Nobody thanked. A reluctant or a courteous man saying something sincerely is not a reversal.**
10. **No new final enemy.** The Crown schedule and the doctrine behind it are the pressure; a person is not.

## The prohibition that is specific to this volume

**The engineer's own name.** In forty-nine chapters of the last volume, `Marek` and `Vale` are both zero, in narration and in every document, and the fen's book calls him the reader of this body and the engineer of record and nobody in this fen names him. **That convention is not lifted in narration.** `Marek` and `Vale` may appear **only in speech and on paper**, and the first time either is said out loud in a yard it is entered as a count and the count is one, and the person who said it was not asked to and did not know it was the first time. **A document that names him is a different kind of document from the Assembly's sheet, which named nobody, and the difference between the two documents is the whole of this volume.**
