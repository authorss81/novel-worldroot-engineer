## The five findings, and the state of each

### 1. BLOCKING — the prose was no longer fiction, and the prompt could not be fixed from the chapters

**What the reviewer measured, across the 28,403 words the batch shipped with:** mean sentence
length 49.9 words, median 38; 168 sentences of sixty words or more out of 569; longest sentence
322 words. `the fen entered` 132 times. `about four people` 51. `the count of that in this
holding` 49. `the drawer was not opened` 30. `nobody` 157, `improved` 61, `about` 486. ALL-CAPS
log text between 6 and 23 per cent of a chapter's words. One seven-beat template across all ten
chapters. **The recap apparatus was 591 to 1,293 words of a chapter that ran to about 2,800, and
it restated what the chapter had already said.**

**The reviewer measured the two chapters that should carry the volume and both failed the same
way. `chapter-0333.md` is the batch's one designated dramatic beat — the engineer's own name said
aloud in a yard for the first time in four years — and the moment ran five words, sat
mid-paragraph, was explained away by the narrator in the next sentence, and was then re-narrated
four more times including twice in capitals afterwards, so the reader got the anticlimax first
and the event second. `chapter-0332.md` is the strongest scene in the batch and it had no door,
no chair, no weather and no distance between speakers.**

**The root cause the reviewer named is the prompt, not the writer:**
`workspace/volume-07/batch-0004/PROMPT.md` is 214 lines that mandate the figure set, the day
clock, twenty-four frozen counts, per-chapter card contents and a two-caps-block-plus-`Entered`
format, and that then forbid weather, physical scene, "history lecture," "tender," and "this
batch" in body prose, and its line 168 bans the word *volume* outright. The batch's own self-check
conceded the result in judgement 7 and recorded it as a property of the movement rather than
escalating it.

**The repair, in all ten chapters:**

- The **two ALL-CAPS recap blocks are cut entirely.** With the clerk's block gone by guardrail 9
  in the next batch, this is the whole of the old three-block ending.
- The **`Entered` block is cut from about 300 words to 140-175**, and now carries the entry's
  standing and the day's locked figures and nothing else.
- **The derived figure, the aggregate and the rota are stated once in the prose** instead of three
  times across the recap and the block. The reviewer separately recorded that a chapter about four
  clocks was stating its subject five times.
- **The clause-chains of the form *X, and entered Y, and entered Z* are broken into separate
  sentences and separate paragraphs.**
- **Every chapter now has a body, weather or the absence of it, light, doors, and distance between
  speakers.** New staging includes a barrow wheel down to the axle in a hole in the dyke path, a
  chocked cart wheel split with a knife and not finished, Soren Rill's wet cuff taking longer than
  it needed to because it was soaked, a gate that sticks in wet weather, wind under a seed house
  door moving a document an inch and stopping against its own weight, and nine feet of floor
  between a warden and the wall he stood with his back to.
- **Ch 333's yard is now set before the sentence is spoken instead of described after it.** The
  flat stones, the cart across the mouth of the yard, the light going off the wall, the inside
  door not shut, the nine feet between the two men, the hand on the gate, the kettlestop, the
  man moving to the other side of the cart with no reason to be on it. The one paragraph that
  still explains the moment to the reader is kept, because it is the book's argument, and the four
  re-narrations are gone.
- **No speech was touched.** They were the good part and a repair pass that improves them is a
  different pass.

**AND THE SENTENCE-LEVEL PASS THAT FOLLOWED THE SHAPE-LEVEL ONE, MEASURED, BECAUSE THE SHAPE FIX ALONE MOVED ALMOST NOTHING ON THIS MEASURE AND SAYING IT DID NOT WOULD BE THE SAME ERROR THE SELF-CHECK MADE.** The first pass cut the apparatus and staged the scenes and the mean sentence was still 48.4. **THE MEASUREMENT, BEFORE AND AFTER THE WHOLE REPAIR: mean sentence 49.9 to 41.3, median 38 to 33, sentences 569 to 668, sentences of sixty words or more 168 to 147, and `the fen entered` 132 to 78. WHAT IS LEFT OF THE LONG SENTENCES IS ALMOST ENTIRELY DIALOGUE, WHICH IS WHERE IT BELONGS.**

**WHAT WAS NOT FIXED, AND IT IS THE HONEST LIMIT OF THIS PASS.** `about four people` stands at 61 and `nobody` at 145, because they are this holding's way of saying that nobody has checked, and they are the sound of a place where figures are entered and not verified. **What this does not claim.** The prose is not now Batch 0003's prose with a coat on, and a later
