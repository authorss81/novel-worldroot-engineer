## PART ONE — THE ONE FINDING THAT WOULD HAVE COST TEN CHAPTERS

**The two frames had been cross-contaminated, and both are correct for their own run.** *A month two months back* is the **stop word's** date: Tova Reed has had it since the seventeenth of the fifth month, at `chapter-0288.md` in Volume 06, which is day 497 — `chapter-0288.md:5` prints *The seventeenth of this month was a Saturday* and its aggregate line gives the forty-seventh figure of a series counted at day minus 450, so the day is 497 and the ordinal is the seventeenth of the fifth. **APPLIED IN THREE PLACES**: the clock entry at `:48`, which now carries the derivation and both frames side by side; the Chapter 344 card at `:122`, which now reads *the seventeenth of last month, being the seventeenth of the sixth, and NOT the seventeenth of a month two months back, which is the stop word's date and not this run's*; and the frame note at `:25`, which now says which of the two is which instead of setting a ceiling that licensed the wrong one. `outline/volume-08.md` needed no repair here — its §6 already names the sixth month and its frame table rows were right.

**The generalisation, and it is the same one the Volume 07 close wrote down: two things in this manuscript both begin on a seventeenth, and a frame is a property of a thing and not of a batch.** A batch prompt that says *nothing goes further back than N* is asserting a ceiling where it needed a table.
