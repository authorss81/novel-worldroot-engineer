## PART FOUR -- THE RULE THIS PASS PRODUCED, BECAUSE IT IS THE SECOND TIME THIS REPOSITORY HAS NEEDED ONE

**A FIGURE IS NEVER WITHDRAWN WITHOUT THE TEST THAT PRODUCED IT, AND WHERE A PASS CITES A FIGURE AS A REASON, THE CITING PASS OWNS THE TEST.**

**THE FIRST TIME WAS THE 38.** A figure was quoted forward as the reason not to touch the prose, the citing pass corrected it without naming a test, and the correction withdrew a number that was right under the test the quoting pass had used.

**THE SECOND TIME WAS THE *FOUR AND A HALF* COUNT, AND IT IS THE SAME FAILURE AT A DIFFERENT SIZE.** Thirteen was corrected to twenty-six, correctly, but the correction was made against a list that had been wrong in the same way -- ten sites standing for thirteen occurrences -- and **the two chapters the list had never mentioned are exactly the kind of thing a count is for, and the count was the only thing standing between the close and shipping them.** A list is not a count and a count is not a verification: the thirteen was made by counting a list, and the list was made by reading, and the reading missed two chapters.

**AND THE THIRD TIME, IN THE SAME PASS, IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION, WHICH IS THE TELL.** The *five days* figure was corrected in five files to two different wrong numbers, and neither wrong number was checked against the other's list, although both lists sat in the same files. **A FIGURE THAT APPEARS IN FIVE FILES IN TWO DIFFERENT WRONG FORMS IS NOT A FIGURE WITH A TYPO IN IT. IT IS A FIGURE NOBODY MEASURED.**
