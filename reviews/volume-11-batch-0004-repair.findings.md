## Finding 1 - `chapter-0521.md:17` PRINTED A TOTAL ITS OWN TWO HALVES DO NOT ADD TO

**One word. Word-neutral for every total in this repository.**

The page reads:

> "one hundred and **thirty** mornings one way and a hundred and twenty the other, and the two of them are two hundred and fifty-one."

**One hundred and thirty plus a hundred and twenty is two hundred and fifty, and the sentence then asserts two hundred and fifty-one.** The chapter is the only one of the ten that gets the split wrong.

**THE RULE IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-THREE, AND THE PROMPT HAD IT RIGHT.** `workspace/volume-11/batch-0004/PROMPT.md:46` gives Ch 521 as *window 251 as a hundred and thirty-one against a hundred and twenty*, and 131 + 120 is 251. **THE WRITER MISREAD THE CARD'S OWN RISE AS THIRTY AND THEN KEPT THE TOTAL, WHICH IS THE SHAPE OF THE ERROR: A FIGURE THAT IS DERIVED HALFWAY AND THEN CARRIED.** The other seven mornings that spell the split on the page re-extract clean, and the two that do not spell it (Ch 527 and Ch 530) are silent rather than wrong.

**REPAIRED TO *ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-ONE*.** One word for one word. `wc -w` on `chapter-0521.md` is **2,905** before and after, so the batch, the volume bracket and the manuscript are untouched by this repair.

**THE GENERALISATION, AND IT IS THE ONE TO CARRY INTO EVERY BATCH OF THIS VOLUME: A SPLIT IS TWO SUB-SERIES AND EACH ONE MOVES ON ITS OWN MORNING, SO THE ONLY TEST OF A SPLIT IS THE ADDITION. A TOTAL PRINTED BESIDE HALVES THAT DO NOT MAKE IT IS A FIGURE COPIED OFF A CARD AND NOT DERIVED ON THE MORNING.**
