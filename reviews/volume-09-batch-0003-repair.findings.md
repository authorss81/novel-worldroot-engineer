## Finding 1. A stated invariant that refutes the figure beside it, in three chapters

**This is the review's own first finding and it is the one that mattered, and it is worse than the review made it.** The seventh column of the well house door is read on a fixed pair of mornings. `outline/volume-09.md:354` gives it exactly: **read on days 630 and 631 and on no other morning, at fifty-five and fifty-six, being the sixth consecutive-day pair in four years, and the pair is the thirtieth and the first.**

- `chapter-0421.md:99` — *read on the thirtieth and on nothing else*

**This is not a matter of style, and that is what makes it worth a repair pass. The column reached fifty-six precisely because it was read on the first, being day 631. A rule that forbids the first morning forbids the figure the same sentence prints.** In a manuscript whose engine is exact invariant restatement, a restated invariant that contradicts the figure beside it is the one class of error a reading pass cannot see and a mechanical one catches every time.

**Repaired: the first is restored in all three, and nothing else in the three sentences was changed.** Ch 420's sentence was then rewritten into three, for the reason at Finding 6.

## The rule this pass produced, and it is the thing to carry into Batch 0004

**A restated invariant is a figure, and it is checked against the series it belongs to like any other.** Three chapters in ten dropped half of a two-part rule, and no page disagreed with any other page: each was internally plausible, and each was refuted only by the number standing in the sentence beside it. **The mechanical form of the check is to extract every invariant restatement in a batch, group them by the thing they describe, and require that every member of a group carries the same clause set — because a rule that varies between chapters is either a series or an error, and in this manuscript the answer is almost always the second.** Group them by what they describe, not by where they sit, and the near-duplicate gate does half the work for free, because the variants are near-duplicates of each other by construction.

**And the second half is the clock.** Finding 5 is three figures in the one document the next prompt tells its writer to trust, copied onward into the prompt itself. **A state file that appoints itself authoritative should be re-derived from the outline before the prompt that quotes it is written, and a copied figure should be traced to its page before it is corrected, because the page and the copy are frequently both wrong and only one of them is load-bearing.**
