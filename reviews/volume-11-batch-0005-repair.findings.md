## Finding 1 - `chapter-0538.md:27` REPLACED ONE FALSE STATEMENT WITH ANOTHER, AND THE STATE LAYER CERTIFIED IT

**One paragraph. Word-neutral for every figure of every series. The chapter is 22 words longer.**

Finding 10 of Batch 0005's own sixteen findings found that the page said the read-aloud pair *had gone in on twenty-five mornings of this month*, which is true of no interval in the volume, and repaired it to *has gone in on every odd morning since the run began*. **The first half of that repair is right and the second half was not looked at, and it is false, and `state/continuity.md` records the whole repair as paid and correct.**

The page now read:

> "That pair of figures has gone in on every odd morning since the run began and **there will not be another of them after this morning**, because tomorrow is an even morning and half of the run is half a number on an even morning."

Three faults, and the first clause is not one of them:

1. **The first clause is true.** The numerator is `(day - 509) / 2`, a whole number exactly on an odd morning, so the pair has gone in on every odd morning. Keep it.
2. **The second clause is false, and the sentence is self-contradictory as written.** The pair 121 of 225 is solved by day 751 and by no other day of the manuscript. A sentence that says the pair has gone in every odd morning and then says there will never be another one is denying the rule it has just stated.
3. **The stated reason is not the reason.** Tomorrow being an even morning accounts for one empty space in a book, not for an ending. Day 753 is odd and carries 122 of 227.

The paragraph also carried a second false claim two sentences earlier, which no finding named:

> "that is **the only change of frame in the book since the run began**."

`chapter-0508.md:39` records the previous one: *"the frame of that run is of a month seven months back, and it was of a month six months back yesterday."* The frame moves at a first of a month and at nothing else, so it has moved eight times since the run began, thirty mornings apart on 721 and 751.

**Repaired to:**

> "The frame of that run is of a month eight months back today and was of a month seven months back yesterday, and that frame has moved at a first of a month and at nothing else since the run began, and thirty mornings back is the last morning before this one on which it moved. That pair of figures goes in on an odd morning and on no even one, and half of the run is half a number on an even morning, so there is nothing going in under that heading tomorrow and the day after that will carry a pair again."

Both figures in the paragraph are untouched: 121 of 225, of a month eight months back. **No figure of any series moved, and the sentence prints no figure for the day after tomorrow, so nothing is projected and nothing is asserted about a morning this volume does not contain.**

**WHAT THIS FINDING IS FOR. A state layer that records a repair as paid makes the next phase inherit it as settled. A repair that substitutes one false statement for another false statement and then files it under *paid and correct* is worse than an unrepaired error, because the error is now certified. Finding 10's naming of itself is withdrawn by name rather than edited away.**
