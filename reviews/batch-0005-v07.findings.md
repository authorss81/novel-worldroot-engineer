## 3. FOUR HARD FAULTS, ALL REPAIRED, WITH THE OLD FORMS NAMED

1. **CH 338 CONTRADICTED ITSELF ON DISTANCE.** It had two men walk five days to the node, put them back at the seed house on the evening of the second day, and walk them four miles. **REPAIRED, AND THE REPAIR SETTLES AN INHERITED TENSION.** `outline/volume-07.md` says Errow Reach is five days east of the fen gate by cart road. The page says otherwise: `chapter-0331.md:19` says a place five days east and `:21` says *he got there on the second morning, having gone up on the first and come back down*, being a round trip of a day and a half. **The page governs. WITHDRAWN FROM THE NINE CHAPTERS: every use of *five days* and every use of *four miles* for that road. LIVE: a day's walk east, about five miles of the eleven hundred that a cart can get down.
