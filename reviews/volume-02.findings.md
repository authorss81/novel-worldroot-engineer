## 1. Mechanical audit across all forty-nine chapters

**Duplicate paragraphs and repeated sentences — one exact clone, reviewed and kept.**

`chapter-0073.md` paragraph 38 and `chapter-0082.md` paragraph 17 are the same sentence word for word: *It said three things, the way it says three things, and not in a sentence anybody would want.*

This is not a copy-paste error. The two chapters are a deliberate structural mirror and the sentence is the hinge of it. Chapter 73 is the volume's first Fieldbook panel, at the head of the node, read with a ring against a bead, eleven seconds, on the thirtieth. Chapter 82 is the second panel, on the return, against a willow withy, nine seconds, on the twenty-sixth of the month after. The paragraphs around the clone are near-identical in the same way and were checked together:

- Renner holding the case down the seed-house steps because Marek cannot get the latch with two fingers (73:37 / 82:16);
- the panel opening with a mark on a question and closing with a line nobody wants on a public form.

**The Volume 01 close broke three genuine prose clones. This one is kept, and the reason is that a mirror is only a mirror if the join is the same join.** A reader who has read Chapter 73 and reaches Chapter 82 is meant to feel the second panel as an echo before they understand it, and the sentence is the moment the echo becomes deliberate. **A later writer must not 'fix' it, and must not go through the volume breaking parallels between these two chapters.** Recorded here so that the next close does not spend a finding on it.

## 5. Volume close-up

**Against the outline's central pressure.** A conditional charter expires, the cooperative adopts its own open book as a condition, and a repair fed a twentieth — and the volume's argument is that a readable thing can be indexed, bought, taxed and conditioned. **That argument is delivered by documents, not by a villain, and it is delivered in the right order:** a class of use is named before a rate, a rate before a toll, a toll before a refusal, and a refusal before an arrangement. Nobody in Volume 02 lies.

**Midpoint reversal, Chapters 69–72 — delivered.** The provenance bead works, and works too well. A seed lot wetted against a copper-collared living bead reports where it was grown in four seconds without a ledger, and the memory it carries is the **seedkeeper's**, not the seed's. **A graft carries a memory into a market device, and the volume's central discovery is that a device which makes provenance self-certifying is a device that makes provenance ownable, and that the two are the same mechanism.** Tova's argument that a graft keeps the thing put in and loses the thing that was there is the hinge, and it is a craft fact and not a sentiment.

**Escalation, Movements 1–5 — delivered and correctly ordered.** What the charter is worth → the market → the memory in the seed → the bridge → the tax on a bridge. The bridge is built as crew work on a public schedule with a return path, and the capacity is named in the trade's own words, and the settlement at the dry end has no charter and is not asked to sign anything. **The rate lands on a route that carries a sixth of what it says, and the rate is not the villain.**

**Climax, Chapters 93–96 — delivered.** A toll collected politely at a gate, a far-end refusal used once against eleven carts nobody had asked about, the route smaller by one use, the break not made good, the low quarter's four wells still off, and then an arrangement that is not a charter. **The refusal is the climax and not the argument about the refusal**, and it is used by a person nobody in the fen had met, with a hoe, for no reason, on a fence of thorn and hazel. Renner's eleven pounds is refused by the far end on the **fact** and not on the terms, and the terms of it are written down nowhere on earth.

**Resolution, Chapters 96–98 — delivered.** The exchange survives as a local commons with public ledgers, a copy to Cray, a refusal clause on a public door, an arrangement nobody can enforce, a rate that will be paid on the fen's own document if the district ever says it is a use, sixty pounds ten standing, a graft fed a twentieth, a starving not visible from the top of the field, Nia's cart twice, and a lamp that has to be fed tonight. **Marek's answer to the volume's question is a reputation: a useful engineer and a dangerous independent witness.** That is what the series contract asks for and it is not a triumph.

**Deeper answer — delivered in the right register.** The lineage in the seed is **replaceable, not forged**: ownership is a claim on a thing you made and cannot make again; the method is takable by anybody who watches it four times; nothing in the fen is owned by anybody; **you own the seed and I own the root and those are two properties in two different places and cannot be put in the same hand.** And in four years a printer will decide who checks the book. **Entered, not answered. Neither of them concedes.**

**New question — named plainly on the last day and left standing.** *Who changed the maps, and who was paid.* Tova Reed says it at a door with no history in it, and then she lists **seven things** that this holding knows: a class of use with a number; a name kept off a road at a fence by a woman who cannot read; a cutting sheet with a column the trade does not have; a road carrying a sixth of what it says; a form with a hole in it; a rate of fourpence and a toll of a shilling; a recognized hand in a column of a register. **Not one of those is a map. Not one is a printer. Not one is a reason.** It is the shape of the thing and the thing is nowhere, and the volume closes on *tomorrow the sowing closes, and we do not know.* **The shape must not be mistaken for the thing in Volume 03, by any character, and a body that mistakes the shape for the thing will go and shout at a printer.**

**Final image, line by line — present and load-bearing.** Three books out on the table for the first time and a fourth thing in a shut drawer. A well house door carrying a notice, four sheets with a number at the head, a schedule with a measured figure under a declared one, and a clause in the fen's own hand under seven columns. Eighty-three days cut into two boards and the number still going up. A cart past the gate twice and unstopped. A fence of thorn and hazel with no gate, a post, eleven plots, six crates of a bean whose name is wrong, a woman of thirty-nine, and a road that stops where a person with a hoe said two words. **Two ends with no charter and a book in the middle that both can read and neither has signed.** The lamp on a living bulb that has to be fed tonight. All of it present, none of it softened.

**Guardrails — all holding, itemised in `state/current.md`.** No Crown Engine, no Continuity Office, no False Season, no Iona Vey, no Quiet Break, no history lecture. No month on any document. The word *seat* does not appear. The collie is not interpreted. The leak is not mended. No draw anywhere. The Thornwild settlement is unnamed. The schedule is not produced entire. The Fieldbook panel is never quoted as authority. No romance. **The ending is untouched and no new enemy was introduced.**

**Relationship — the milestone is institutional and not tender.** Marek and Tova share one book and keep no private copy. They disagree about who owns a grafted seed and **the disagreement is not resolved and must not be.** Lissa identifies her husband's hand and it is the loudest thing she has done for him and it is not forgiveness.
