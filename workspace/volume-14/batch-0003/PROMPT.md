- **The fourth permanent loss was paid on day eight hundred and sixty-eight, in a room with the door shut, and its name is on one page of this volume and no other. It may not be recovered, softened, explained into something cheaper, enlarged or shrunk, priced, traded for, or named again by anybody. It is not a fifth loss and no fifth is added. Nobody was paid for it and nobody was told what it was worth, and the only person who put a figure on it is the person who refused to. The morning after it, Marek was very good at his work, and nobody in the yard asked him what it was, and nobody thanked him and nobody put a hand on him, and that is now the standing of this holding about him.**
- **The seed vault was given up on day eight hundred and sixty-four, in her own mouth, and it stays given up. No morning after that one may re-abandon it, re-announce it, have anybody discover it, or have anybody describe her as having given something up. Her seed work stays with her, is not a penance and not a consolation, and her hearing is not healed, softened, excused or thanked for.**
- A man of about fifty gave notice on the key to the drawer behind the near board and asked that six weeks be noticed with him, and the key is on its nail and the drawer is shut and the handover has not happened and the notice is still a word and not a paper.
- The standing question about the standpipe at the far end of the tap house, asked on day eight hundred and thirty-eight and not answered, is still not answered, and no holder of that far end may be named.
- The tap joint was cut the right way round and holds and weeps, and several Tuesdays have happened to it and proved nothing, and the mason said on day eight hundred and sixty-four that if anybody in this yard says it has settled after a week he will take the chalk off that wall himself, and nobody said it had settled.
- A mason has refused a figure on the wall at the north end four times and has said the reason each time, and a fifth refusal was made on day eight hundred and sixty-six on the same ground as before, and the yard did not turn it into a number. **On that same morning he stood in front of a round figure on the near board that was his own arithmetic and the launder's and said he did not know what to do with it.**
- Sera Quill's slate carries the two reckonings and a third line saying the second of the two is carried forward with no rule shown, and she has said that out loud in a yard four times and has named the thirteen as the honest part of the slate.
- Nia Vale is carrying a folded page in her coat that is not one of the three sheets, is not in a book and has never been on the long table, and one reason already given for not saying the two things is that she is the only person who knows which coat it is in.
- The three sheets are on the long table, the first of them headed with the name of the holding it is lying in, all three unentered, unrefused and uncolumned, with about nine inches of bare board either side of each.
- The run is read on the odd mornings and not on the even ones, the number of voices on the last sentence is a property of the yard, and the last morning behind closed on the run not being read with a reason from Tova Reed.
- **Nobody was asked to choose anything on any of the twenty mornings behind, and that is the standing of this holding and it may not break in these ten either.**
- The rootmark is in no public anchor and the word for it is on no page and may not be placed in one and no such operation is rehearsed or described. The place with a name on a map is not named at all and is not used as a symbol, and **Thornwild, which is a place on the network's own column and not that place, may be named and is not a symbol.** The two questions this volume does not touch are not asked, not named, not hinted and not paraphrased, and this batch does not use the word for the second one anywhere.
