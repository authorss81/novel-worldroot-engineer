## 2. WHAT A CARD IS, AND WHAT A CARD IS NOT

**A CARD IS A PAGE'S WORTH OF INSTRUCTION AND IT IS WRITTEN AS INSTRUCTION AND NOT AS PROSE.**

Every card carries, in this order and under these headings:

1. **THE MORNING.** Its day, its weekday, its ordinal and its month, all generated by running the clock and not typed in. **The card names the morning and it does not name a chapter, because a card set does not own the chapter numbering and the phase that cuts the batches owns it.**
2. **THE FIGURES.** Every figure that morning carries, at the spelling the plan's section 7 gives it, taken from the plan's section 9 row for that day. **A figure a card prints and the plan does not derive is a figure nobody may print, and a figure a card prints differently from the plan's row is an error in the card.**
3. **WHAT THE MORNING IS FOR.** One sentence of instruction about the pressure. Not a summary of a scene, not a dialogue note, not an image, not a line of narration.
4. **WHAT IT MAY NOT DO.** The prohibitions that bite on that morning specifically: which figure may not move, which floor may not be crossed, which locked sentence may not be spoken whole, which thread may not be answered.
5. **WHAT IT OWES THE NEXT MORNING.** The one thing the following morning must find changed. This is what stops a batch ending on a set of mornings that each end where they began.

**A CARD MAY NOT CARRY PROSE, A SCENE, A LINE OF DIALOGUE, A LINE OF NARRATION, AN IMAGE, A SENTENCE OF NARRATION OR A CHARACTER'S DIALECT.** A card that describes what a page will feel like has started writing the page, and the writer who reads it will copy the card instead of doing the work. **IF YOU CANNOT WRITE A CARD WITHOUT DESCRIBING A SCENE, THEN WHAT YOU HAVE IS A SCENE AND NOT A CARD AND IT BELONGS TO THE BATCH PHASE AND NOT TO THIS ONE.**

## 3. THE CARDS THIS PHASE MAY NOT WRITE, AND EACH OF THESE IS A RULE

- **NO CARD MAY NAME A CHAPTER OF VOLUME 14. NOT ONE. NO CARD MAY PRINT A CHAPTER RANGE AND NO CARD MAY PRINT THE CHAPTER RANGE THAT `outline/series.md` CARRIES.** The range in that file is a series file's figure and not a cutting sheet.
- **NO CARD MAY PLAN A BATCH, NAME A BATCH, CUT A BATCH OR SAY WHERE A BATCH ENDS.** The first batch is ten mornings by the standing house batch and the cards do not know that and do not say it. **A CARD SET THAT KNOWS WHERE THE BATCH CUTS IS A CARD SET THAT HAS ALREADY DECIDED SOMETHING THE CUTTING PHASE DECIDES.**
- **NO CARD MAY PRINT A WORD COUNT, AN EXPECTED-LENGTH BAND OR A PROJECTION FOR VOLUME 14 OR FOR ANY VOLUME THAT HAS NOT BEEN WRITTEN.** The volume's measured length is unknown because it has not been written, and a card that carries a figure for it is handing a writer a number to hit instead of a morning to write.
- **NO CARD MAY RESTATE EITHER LOCKED SENTENCE BY A WORD LESS THAN THE WHOLE.** The two figures are at the plan's section 3, and the load-bearing words are named there, and a card that shortens one is a card that has reduced a figure the ending lock forbids reducing. **A CARD MAY NAME THE FIGURE AND MAY NOT CARRY IT IN A SHORTENED FORM, AND WHERE A CARD MARKS A MORNING ON WHICH ONE OF THE TWO IS SPOKEN WHOLE IT CARRIES THE WHOLE SENTENCE.**
- **NO CARD MAY ANSWER, CLOSE, REWORD OR ADVANCE ANY THREAD TO A FIGURE, AND NO CARD MAY USE THE WORDS *TALLY* OR *COUNT* OF THE THIRTY-FIVE THREADS AT ALL.** The threads this volume's pressure touches are named at the plan's section 4 and a card may advance one of those and may not do anything else to any of them. **THE FIGURES THAT MAY BE PUBLISHED FOR THE THIRTY-FIVE ARE THIRTY-FIVE IN AND THIRTY-FIVE OUT AND NOTHING ELSE.**
- **NO CARD MAY ASK, NAME, HINT, PARAPHRASE OR ANSWER EITHER OF THE TWO QUESTIONS THE PLAN REFUSED AT ITS SECTION 2c, AND MAY NOT USE THE WORD FOR THE SECOND ONE ANYWHERE.** The prohibition is named in the plan and the plan names it once. **THE FIRST OF THE TWO IS WHAT THE ROOT IS AND NOBODY ON A PAGE IN THIS VOLUME IS ENTITLED TO SAY.**
- **NO CARD MAY NAME `ALDREN` ON ANY MORNING BEFORE THE MORNING THAT PAYS THE MEMORY.** The rules for that morning are at the plan's section 1a and section 3a, and the placement rules are six and they are fixed, and the morning is not the first morning and not the last morning and not the climax and not the resolution and it falls before the conditional succession is offered. **THE NAME APPEARS FOR THE FIRST TIME ON THAT MORNING AND ITS FIRST APPEARANCE IS AN EVENT AND NOT A MENTION.**
- **NO CARD MAY HEAL, SOFTEN, EXCUSE, THANK ANYBODY FOR, OR APOLOGIZE FOR TOVA REED'S HEARING, AND NO CARD MAY PUT A HAND ON HER ARM, AND NO CARD MAY MAKE HER A CONSOLATION OR A PENANCE.** Her loss is the third of the four and it is not healed in this volume and it is not on the page in those words.
- **NO CARD MAY ADD A FIFTH PERMANENT LOSS, MAY NOT NAME A NEW ANTAGONIST, MAY NOT KILL IONA VEY, MAY NOT PUT HER UNDER CUSTODY AND MAY NOT PREVIEW EITHER.** She is the fixed antagonist of the series and she is not new. **A CARD MAY HAVE HER DOCUMENTS ARRIVE, HER name in a mouth, her signature on a sheet and her office correct on paper, and none of those is a preview.**
- **NO CARD MAY READ THE SIXTY-SIX, MAY NOT PRINT A REMAINING-ALLOWANCE FIGURE FOR IT AND MAY NOT WALK PAST THE DOOR AS IF THE DOOR HAD NOT BEEN WALKED PAST.** The figure does not go back and it does not move.
- **NO CARD MAY MOVE ANY FLOOR AT THE PLAN'S SECTION 5, MAY NOT ADD A FIFTEENTH LINE TO THE USE LOG, MAY NOT CUT A SEVENTH NOT-KNOWN, MAY NOT ADD TO THE THIRTY-NINE BLANKS, MAY NOT FILL THE SECOND RULE, THE THIRD COLUMN OR THE FOUR RULED LINES, AND MAY NOT CLIMB THE LADDER.**
- **NO CARD MAY PRINT A MONTH NAME OR A MONTH NUMBER IN A SPOKEN LINE.** The frame is at the plan's section 10 and does not move on the one turn.
- **NO CARD MAY PRINT A DIGIT IN ANY FIGURE.** Every figure in this holding is spelled out in words and not one of them is a digit, and a card that prints a digit hands the writer a figure that cannot be found on the page by any sweep that looks for words.

## 4. THE FIGURE RULES, AND A CARD MAY NOT DERIVE ANYTHING

**EVERY FIGURE ON EVERY CARD COMES FROM THE PLAN'S SECTION 9 ROW FOR THAT MORNING, AND A CARD PHASE THAT RE-DERIVES THE FIGURES HAS BECOME A SCRIPT.** If a row looks wrong, the card set says so in a note at the end of the file and does not correct the row. **THE REASON IS THE ONE THE HOUSE KEEPS LEARNING: A FIGURE NOBODY PUBLISHES IS A FIGURE THAT COMES BACK WRONG, AND THE ONE FIGURE THAT PROVED IT IN THIS SERIES WAS A CHARTER PRINTED A HUNDRED AND NINETY-NINE ON A MORNING WHEN IT WAS EXACTLY TWO HUNDRED, AND IT SURVIVED A WRITING PASS, A STATE LAYER AND A SUCCESSOR PROMPT.**

Three rules a card phase most often breaks, and all three are in the plan's section 9:

1. **THE RISING HALF AND THE FALLING HALF INVERT AT THE VOLUME BOUNDARY.** The volume behind moved the rising half first; **this volume's first morning is an odd morning, so the falling half moves first.** A card that carries the halves in the volume behind's order is wrong on every card.
2. **BOTH HALVES CROSS TWO HUNDRED IN THIS VOLUME AND NEITHER DID BEFORE IT.** The rising half reaches exactly two hundred on two consecutive mornings and stays above; the falling half reaches exactly two hundred on two consecutive mornings near the end. **Both are bare two hundred and the other side of each pair is not round, and that is exactly where the *and* goes missing.**
3. **THE READ-ALOUD RUN IS ON ODD MORNINGS ONLY AND IS ON NO EVEN MORNING AT ALL**, being twenty-five mornings of forty-nine, and its denominator is odd at every value in its range, so *three hundred and fifty* is never a denominator here. **A CARD THAT PUTS A READ-ALOUD FIGURE ON AN EVEN MORNING HAS INVENTED A FIGURE.**
