### Chapter 119 — *A Woman On The Fen Road* — days 191–192, the eleventh and the twelfth, a Monday and a Tuesday

**Goal:** the arrival of the woman, unpaid, and the terms on which a body with no charter puts a stranger up for nothing — and the discovery that a refusal of money is the shape of the offer this fen declined three weeks ago.

**Must happen.** **Sera Quill, about fifty-six, comes into Lantern Reach on foot on the eleventh**, having come off the Glassward road and down the fen road, and she gives her name once, and she is entered by the clerk with the hour, and **she is not a member of anything and is not made one and has not been asked.** She states three things in the order she says them, which is her standing from Ch 107 and is not a courtesy. She says she has come without a fee, and **she says what the fee was for, which is that a paid witness is a witness somebody bought, and that she would rather be the second kind and be argued with.** She asks for a room and **not a hearing**, and she is given a room, and the fen enters that the asking was for a room and that a room is not a hearing and is not a record.

### Chapter 120 — *What She Is Not Saying* — days 193–194, the thirteenth and the fourteenth

### Chapter 125 — *A Decision That Went Right* — days 203–204, the twenty-third and the twenty-fourth

**Goal:** the volume's midpoint reversal, in her words, and the fen's discovery that it cannot check it and has a mechanism that bears on it anyway.

**Must happen.** She says the thing that turns the volume: **the pruning was not a mistake that went wrong. It was a decision that went right.** The ground that was pruned hardest is the ground that came back, and the ground that was left alone is the ground that did not, and she says it in the flat words of somebody reading a table and not as an argument.
