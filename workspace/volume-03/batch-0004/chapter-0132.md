# Chapter 132

## The Category

The sheet came with the served instrument and had been in the satchel with it, and it was not taken out on the second because nobody in this yard knew what to do with a second piece of paper, and it came out on the sixth of this month at about the fourth hour in the seed house with the bay doors shut, because what is on it is a definition and a definition is read in a room and not in a yard.

It is one sheet. It has a heading at the top in the office's hand and a heading is not a formality, and it is four lines long, and it is correct.

> **Records of an experimental character, made in a working district, not confirmed by a second reading at the time of making, are held to be unfit for evidentiary use.** A record so held may be produced by a holder, and is not to be produced by this office, and no officer of this district may be cited as a holder of one.
>
> **The word *experimental* is not defined on this sheet and the office has stated that it does not define it, and that a holder who considers a record experimental may say so, and that a statement so made is itself a classification of the record and is entered against the record.**

Lissa Vale read it out whole. Dunmar Fitch read it back out of the book with the hour on it. The man of about fifty read it back after him and asked for his initial to be put against nothing, which is his standing, and nobody thanked anybody.

And then the man of about fifty stood up and said what it is, and the fen entered his words, and they are the whole of it.

"**So the way to keep a record out of that class is to declare it experimental. And the way to declare a record experimental is to make a statement. And a statement is entered against the record. And the way to make no statement at all is to have it put in by somebody else.**"

"We built a door. The door was built by an office that is right. **And the door is a trapdoor and it was built by an office that is right, and the rightness is not going to be argued with by us in a yard.**" He put his hand on the sheet without touching it. "There is not one word on that paper I would take out if I could put it back, and I have looked at it for two hours with the man of about seventy sitting on the other side of me and he has not said one word about it, and I am going to record that as the only endorsement of it anybody in this holding has ever given anybody."

---

And the reach of it, and the man of about fifty worked it out on a slate in about four minutes and read it out and the fen entered the working and not only the answer, because a body that enters conclusions without its arithmetic is a body that has started doing the thing it has been against all season.

"A reading taken by a licensed man, on a hired trestle, in a stone barn, on ground that is not the holder's. That is one and this body has got it and **it is in this body's own book and it is on the six and it goes to the office on the fourteenth.** That is the first one and I do not have to be clever about it."

"A copy of a page, of a book, in a room, four hundred miles off, that is shut. **That is the second one and I am not going to say a word about whether the book is open or shut, and the fen entered in a month three months back that it was not opened and was not going to be and that line has not moved.**"

"And the third one is an order, copied out by hand, into a book of somebody's own, by somebody who is not an office. **A woman of fifty-four on a shelf has a ledger. It is a bad one and it is thirty-one years old and it is the only book four households have, and if there is a maintenance order written out in that ledger in a hand of her own then that is a record of an experimental character, made in a working district, and not confirmed by a second reading at the time of making, and it is this class, and this class does not need anybody to have done anything at all.**"

"And the next part of it is the part I want read back," he said. "**We do not know whether it exists. We have not asked. We are not going to ask. We decided on the second of this month in a yard not to write to that household about a ledger and the reason was that a warning is a document, and here is the document we would have been warning her about.** I would like the clerk to enter that I worked this out in a room and said it out loud and did not write."

> Entered at the fifth hour of the afternoon of the sixth of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk: **three things in this district fall in a class that an office has defined, and the class is a class and not a finding against anybody.** One, a reading taken by a licensed man on a hired trestle in a stone barn on another district's ground, which is in this body's own book and is one of the six. Two, a copy of a page in a book in a locked room four hundred miles off, and nothing is said about whether that book is open. Three, a maintenance order copied out by hand into the ledger of a household, and **whether it exists is not known and was not asked and is not going to be asked**, and the reason is entered: this body decided on the second of this month not to write to that household about a ledger.

---

And then the count, and it is now four, and the fen entered it, and nobody was pleased with it and that is in the book too.

"**Four," said the man of about fifty. "The part three condition, which an officer of a district declared in a schedule and cannot remove including the officer who signs. The shape the schedule has no part for, which is two pages with nothing named in them. The record a woman of fifty-six will not ask opened and this body cannot produce and cannot cite and cannot attack with. And this.** Four in three weeks. Every one of them correct. Every one of them put up by somebody else in an office and not by us. **And I would like the count entered and I would like the reader to read the count back, and I would like the fen to enter that it is not proud of the count, and that the count is the only thing it is able to do with them.**"

"**Four is a lot of walls in three weeks and not one of them is ours, and I am a man who has voted against this body three times and I have never once been able to point at an office in nine miles and say that is a bad wall, and I would like somebody in four years to know that I noticed it before anybody asked me to notice it.**"

And Dunmar Fitch entered it and the man of about fifty read it back and asked for his initial to be put against nothing.

And then the clerk entered an observation of his own, which is his standing, and nobody asked him for it and he did not explain it and the man of about fifty read it back and did not ask him what it meant, because he has never once asked.

> Entered at the fifth hour of the afternoon of the sixth of this month, by the clerk, on his own motion, unasked: **the door is a trapdoor.** Not explained and not to be explained and no person in this holding has been asked for an explanation of it and the clerk has not offered one.

---

And then Coll Renner said the thing he came into the room with, and he said it standing at the back with his own book under his arm, and he said the first part of it before anybody asked him anything, which is his practice and has been all season.

"I have not been asked to say anything and I am going to say something, and I would like the clerk to enter that I said it and that nobody thanked me for it, because I have noticed that this body has got a habit of thanking people for saying things and I am not one of the people in this yard that is going to be thanked."

"**That sheet does not reach me.** A record of an experimental character is held against the record and not against the holder. That is its own second line. **So a class of record that is experimental does not reach a person's coat.** And the copy in my coat is the only one of the four that no office on this sheet can list, and it is the one I have had on this fen road since the eighteenth of a month three months back."

"So here is the whole of it and I am not going to dress it up. **The one copy that no office can get at is the one a man can lose.** I can lose the coat. I can lose the book. I can be run over on this road in a month, and the clerk has that in a ledger tonight in nine words because I asked him for it in an office and I am asking him for it here as well. **And then this body is holding three copies of that reading and two of them are in its own name and can be listed in a day, and the third is on a plateau in a case in a stone house four hundred miles off, and there is nobody in the world who will be able to say whose hand it went into.**"

"I have thought about that for three weeks. **I have made no decision and I am not going to make one today, and I am saying it in a yard instead of not saying it, and that is the whole of my standing and I would like it read back.**"

And the man of about fifty read it back and asked for his initial to be put against nothing, and Coll Renner wrote the same four sentences into his own book in his own hand, and nobody thanked him and nobody said one word to him for about a minute and a half afterwards, and the fen entered that nobody did and entered the minute and a half.

---

And then the question, and it is the steward's, and it is the same question she has asked every day of this season, and she asked it in a room with the doors shut and about nine people in it.

"Who loses," said Lissa Vale.

And she found one and it took her a while and she did not go quickly.

"**A licensed man. And a child of his.** He is forty-one and he has a boy of nine at Fennmere and the boy has been to this yard four times this season and stood at the end of a bench and was not asked to go, and the boy's name is not in this book and I am not going to put it in."

"**And about nine months of the work of the most careful record in this district.** That is not a feeling. That is a figure and I will defend it. Twenty-two years, one book, a hand a hearing can read, and every entry in it with an hour on it, and the district has had a copy of it since the sixth of a month three months back and that copy was read at a counter and not one line of it was taken out of it. **That is nine months of a man's work and it is the only thing this district has that anybody would call a record of quality, and the thing about it is that the whole of its value is that it can be checked, and a class of record of an experimental character is by definition the record that cannot be used.**"

And she wrote it down in her own hand on the page of the seed house book, at the north end of the bench, in front of about nine people, and she read it back before she initialled it, and she did not recommend anything.

"I am not going to tell him what to do with his coat," she said. "He is twenty-two years in a trade and he is a grown man and I run a seed house. **I am going to write down who loses and I am going to leave the next line blank, and the blank is not me being careful. The blank is that I have not got a thing in this world that would help him and I am not going to invent one at about the fifth hour of a Thursday in front of nine people.**"

> Entered at the sixth hour of the afternoon of the sixth of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a cost, which is that a cost is entered as a cost and not as a fault and not as a virtue: **a licensed man of forty-one with a child of nine, and about nine months of the work of the most careful record in this district.** Written in the steward's own hand and read back by her. **No recommendation was made and none was asked for and the clerk has entered that the line after it was left blank and that the reason given is that the steward has nothing in the world that would help and is not going to invent one.**

And the man of about seventy was not fetched on the sixth and nobody fetched him, and the fen entered that he was not fetched and entered that he was not needed, and entered that a man of seventy-one in this holding is somebody this body brings out of his own house for a thing it cannot do by itself, and that this one was a definition on a sheet and the sheet had been read in a room with about nine people in it and the definition is on the wall now in the same book as everything else and he would have said one thing about it and it would have been the same thing he said in a month three months back.

The reason he would have said that thing is in the book and it has not moved since the twenty-second of a month three months back, and it is that a rule a person has to remember is not a rule, it is a habit with a number in it, and the office that has written this sheet has done the whole of it in four lines and has not needed anybody to remember anything, **and that is the difference between the two and he would have given it in about nine words and the fen would not have been able to improve on a word of it, and the fen entered that it did not fetch him and that this is the first time in a fortnight that this body has had a sheet in its hands that it did not take to that man's house, and that the reason is that the sheet is a door and not a figure and he has never once in this holding been asked about a door.**

And nothing in those two days moved, and the class is a class, and the fen is not proud of the count, and the count is four.
