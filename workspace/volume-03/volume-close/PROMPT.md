## Shape of Volume 03 for handoff, in one paragraph

A fen of sixteen households on one head, with no charter, a leak and a beat, goes four hundred miles up a plateau road with a woman of fifty-four's orchard on a shelf and finds a row of eleven branches taken, dressed, closed and taken again in one week in this season by a method with a single bevel that is on no hire sheet at either end of the road; a stack of sixty-one bales that the woman opened herself yields three living grafts in the waste and a maintenance sequence made by hand by somebody who expected to come back; a records officer arrives with a fourth part of a schedule and a line that says a record a holder does not classify is classified for them, and the fen's own correct answer of four months ago is the mechanism; a woman of fifty-six comes down the fen road without a fee, admits what she did nineteen years back, gives over a piece of a machine that cannot be copied and a statement that cannot be corroborated, and says that a decision went right and that a man who cut eleven branches off one row is not a fool, and the question in this body's book changes from *is the telling still going on* to *whose order is it*, which is worse; a grower sells a season's fruit after being told the truth first and will not come back; four households let a hundred and forty-one trees go on their own ground in daylight rather than be the reason a piece of paper was declared anything, and six days later a green comes up out of the collars along the first row and nobody on the shelf can say what it is; the fen answers a class of record by copying things into four hands it cannot read and then five, and a draw taken in its own low ground costs sixteen households four inches of head that has not come back; a sub-office declares the holder's records unsafe for evidentiary use in four correct lines, one of which says that nothing in it states that anything the holder has said is untrue; a party nine hundred miles off asks one question with standing and gets a form saying such a record is produced only to the person named in it on that person's own request, and nobody makes the request and nobody is named; four carts and a hired waggon come up a fen road the holding did not lay while two hundred and eighty households on that road say out loud that they are staying; the compost line goes to ninety-five pounds a month on a run of eight months not discharged in eight, and the first movement in its favour in eight months is one pound three shillings and fifteen pence for a reason nobody has told this body; the fen's own books gain a fourth line, what this body is looking after and cannot reach; and the volume ends on a question nobody is going to answer, and a man of seventy-one says four words, and the book says that it is not going to find out and it is going to keep asking.
