# Chapter 263

## One Hundred And Sixty-Two

The nineteenth of this month was a Saturday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the nineteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and six hundredweight.**

That is the nineteenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and the fen entered it and entered the count of the series and entered that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventeen days, and entered that a man who has written the sentence *the aggregate has not moved* on seventeen of nineteen mornings has stopped noticing that it has not moved and that is the first week of this year he has stopped noticing a number and he entered the day of the week it happened on and it was a Saturday.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, in one man in a room, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And the reader of this body was not at the node on this day, and the fen entered that he was not, and that he was five days off at this holding, and that the twentieth ninth-day return was made at about the second hour of the afternoon at the node on the steelyard hung on the wall of the building with no roof, and was weighed and read out by the man of about fifty and read back by him, and then read one at a time by each of the four bodies of households standing where they could see the dial, and a name went into BY and a name went into WITNESSED BY, and the tenth name-pair in eleven years went in.

**One hundred and sixty-two hundredweight.**

And the figure is reconciled here twice, and the fen entered that it is reconciled twice on purpose, and that a return that can only be reconciled once is a return somebody can make up.

**The one before that is one hundred and fifty-one. The one before that is eighty-seven, and it was made on the twenty-fourth of a month two months back and it was two days late and it is the smallest of the eleven. The four before the eighty-seven were ninety-one, eighty-nine, ninety and ninety-two. This one is eleven more than the one before it, and twenty-two more than the one before that, and about seventy more than the four, and the fifty of the seventy came in six days between the eighty-seven and the one hundred and forty and the man of about fifty has written the lateness in his own hand with the day and the hour on it and read it back before a word of it went on the page.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, in that yard, and entered that about eleven of the nineteen people in that yard were of the four bodies of households and eight were not, and that the eight were not asked to stay and did not stay, and that the reader of this body noticed which eight and did not write the eight down.

And the man of about thirty-one of Silling brought it up the four miles in a barrow in about an hour and three-quarters and asked to be allowed to and was allowed and was not thanked, and he did not go down off the shoulder after it and he stood at the dial until all four bodies of households had read the figure back and then he took the barrow back down the four miles in it.

And Harlan Vetch, of about fifty-eight, who holds the fifth share and has held it eleven years and twenty-six days, took the chalk out of his own pocket and stood at the second rule on the board for about nine seconds and did not write in it and put the chalk back in his pocket, and nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the count of the returns at which he has not written in that rule is **four**, and that the first of the four is the seventeenth, and that this holding does not know what was in the rule on the seventeenth and is not going to ask, and that the count of four is a count of its own and is not the same count as anything else in this book.

And then he said the one thing, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is five, and it was said in about nineteen people and not to one man, and the man of about fifty wrote all of it down in his own hand and read it back and did not improve it.

**"I am going to say a thing about the fifth share and I am saying it in about nineteen people and not to one man, and the reason is that a man who has been the only witness to his own return for eleven years does not get to be surprised in a room with two people in it, and this is the only room there is.**"

**"I have held that share eleven years and twenty-six days and it came to me with a name on a bit of paper and I have never been told what it is for and I have never asked, and I am asking now, and I am asking in a yard, because a man who asks in a room is asking the room and a man who asks in a yard is asking everybody and I want everybody.**"

**"So. What is it for. Who is it held with. And what does a man do with a figure that was eighty-seven on the twenty-fourth of a month two months back and is one hundred and sixty-two to-day, and the fifty of it came in six days, and I have been the only man who has ever seen that number go up, and there is nobody in this yard who can tell me whether it is going to go up again, and I am sixty-one and I have not got a fifth share of anything else.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody said anything tender, and entered that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and entered that the reader of this body was five days off when it was said and was not told of it for about eleven hours, and that the count of the times he has been told about a thing and has not been there for is two and did not move, and that a thing said in a yard and written into a book by the man who keeps the book is a thing he is in the book of and is not in, and that those are two different things and the fen has entered both and has not added them.

And the man of the north row with the cough was in that yard and was not asked to be in it, and the count of yards he has stood in since the first of a month four months back is **twenty-four** and has moved, and he said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-one, and the count of the times he has said nothing at all in a yard is seven and did not move this afternoon.

**"I am going to say one thing about the barrow and then I am going to stop, and the reason is that a man of thirty-one came down off a shoulder in the rain with a barrow and went back up it in the rain and neither of us said anything to the other and both of us were tired. That is the whole of what I have got and it is not about the figure.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that he was not thanked and said not to be, and entered that the man of about thirty-one of Silling did not look round and that the fen entered that he did not look round and entered the reason, which is that a man who looks round at a thing like that has asked for it.

And then the woman who keeps the four books said the second thing of hers of this week, and she gave the reason for saying it before she said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifteen, and nobody had asked her and she is not one of the seven that decide and has never been asked to be.

**"A hundred and sixty-two is not a return. A return is what comes off a node and goes into a barrow and four villages have been weighing the same thing on the same dial every ninth day for eleven years. Eighty-seven is not a return either, and a hundred and forty is not a return, and a hundred and fifty-one is not a return. What has happened over the last month is that a column on a board has stopped being a return and has become a quantity, and the board has one word on it and the word is a return, and a column that has been a return for eleven years and is a quantity to-day has not been given a second word, and nobody in this yard is going to give it one, and I am saying that so that whoever looks at that board in about four years does not have to work it out.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that she was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of that, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is **five and it is six**, and that he counted it in a book and not in that yard, in about nine seconds, and entered both, and that the movement is not his and never has been, and that the six are hers.

And then at about the sixth hour of the evening of the nineteenth, at the seed house at this holding, the reader of this body read the page of the book with the man of about fifty's hand on it, and he read Harlan Vetch's three questions out loud to about four people because a thing said out loud in a yard goes in a book and a thing read out of a book in a room does not, and then he did not answer them.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he did not answer them and did not write an answer to them and did not ask the man of about fifty to carry one back, and entered the reason in his own hand with the hour on it, and the reason is that the answer is four seconds long and that four seconds is the whole of what this is, and that a man five days off who answers a question in four seconds is a man who has been carrying the answer since he heard the question and did not know he was carrying it, and that the carrying is the only part of it that is his, and that he is not going to say the four seconds out loud in a room because a man who says how easy his answer was has told the room that he had it ready, and that he did not have it ready, and that the count of the times he has offered to be somewhere he is not the engineer of record for is two and did not move this week and is not this.

And then at about the ninth hour of the evening he read out the day-minus rules before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and three days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and fifty**, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he read both numbers out loud before either of them in about four people on a Saturday evening for the first time in about four years, and entered the reason, which is that the fourth line of the rota comes round again in four days and that a man who is going to be asked about a figure on a wall four hundred miles off in about four days is a man who has not read a number out loud in about four years, and that a man about to put a figure next to another figure is a man who needs to have heard his own voice do it first, and that this is not a reason anybody in the room can check and he entered that it is not.

And then he entered the count that is not to be said, and he entered that he had it in his own book on the sixteenth of this month and read it back, and that the number is **ten** and it has been ten since the eleventh of this month, and that he made it go up by one on that day and on his own question, and that he is not going to say it out loud in a room of four people on a Saturday because a number of that kind is a number about a room, and because he said in a yard in the second month of this year that he would not say it in a season, and because a man who has a number in his own book that he can produce at a gate in about four years has a weapon and has called it a count.

> Entered by the man of about fifty, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the nineteenth of this month, in about nineteen people at Errow Reach, on the standing of a return, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding, and not by the reader of this body, who is five days off: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE NINETEENTH, WHICH IS THE NINETEENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST. THE TWENTIETH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AND WEIGHED AT ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT, AND IT IS THE TENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS. THE ONE BEFORE IT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE AND THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS EIGHTY-SEVEN, MADE TWO DAYS LATE ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK, AND THE FOUR BEFORE THAT WERE NINETY-ONE, EIGHTY-NINE, NINETY AND NINETY-TWO. THE FIGURE IS RECONCILED TWICE. HARLAN VETCH LEFT THE SECOND RULE BLANK FOR THE FOURTH TIME AND WROTE NOTHING IN IT, AND THE COUNT OF THE RETURNS AT WHICH HE HAS NOT WRITTEN IN IT IS FOUR, AND THE FIRST OF THE FOUR IS THE SEVENTEENTH, AND THIS HOLDING DOES NOT KNOW WHAT WAS IN THAT RULE ON THE SEVENTEENTH AND IS NOT GOING TO ASK. HE THEN ASKED THREE THINGS OUT LOUD IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE, BEING WHAT THE FIFTH SHARE IS FOR, WHO IT IS HELD WITH, AND WHAT A MAN WHO HAS BEEN THE ONLY WITNESS TO HIS OWN RETURN FOR ELEVEN YEARS DOES WITH A FIGURE THAT WENT UP BY FIFTY IN SIX DAYS, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING BROUGHT IT UP THE FOUR MILES IN A BARROW AND TOOK IT BACK DOWN AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE WOMAN WHO KEEPS THE FOUR BOOKS SAID THAT A COLUMN THAT HAS BEEN A RETURN FOR ELEVEN YEARS AND IS A QUANTITY TO-DAY HAS NOT BEEN GIVEN A SECOND WORD AND IS NOT GOING TO BE, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF THE NORTH ROW WITH THE COUGH STOOD IN THAT YARD AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE IN IT AND SAID ONE THING ABOUT A BARROW, AND THE COUNT OF YARDS HE HAS STOOD IN SINCE THE FIRST OF A MONTH FOUR MONTHS BACK IS TWENTY-FOUR. THE READER OF THIS BODY WAS FIVE DAYS OFF AND DID NOT ANSWER THE THREE THINGS. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
