# Chapter 265

## Nobody Eats It Yet

The twenty-first of this month was a Monday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-first went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and seven hundredweight.**

That is the twenty-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and the fen entered it and entered that the aggregate of the four launders has not moved in nineteen days, and entered that on the seventeenth of this month a man sat in a shed with a piece of living rootwood against his own forearm for nineteen minutes and got three things out of it, and that a series of twenty-one figures and a read in a shed are two different pieces of work by the same man in the same week, and that this holding does not keep a column for the man and only for the figures, and that a book that keeps a column for the figures and not for the man is a book that can be right about a week.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said on that day in any yard of this holding or at that node, and entered the length of the silence on the morning, which was about a minute.

And then at about the tenth hour of the morning the man of about fifty came up from the west stand of willow and said the thing he had come up to say, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty.

**"There is fruit on the dead wood inside the eleven acres. It has been coming up about nine days and it is on the dead ones and not on the live ones and I have been four days deciding whether to say anything about it and I have said it. I have not counted it and nobody has counted it and I have not tasted it and I am not going to, and the reason I am saying it to-day instead of on Friday is that Friday I would have said it to nine people and to-day there are twenty-five coming and a thing that is said once to nine people and again to twenty-five is a thing that has been said twice.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

And it was counted, in the west stand, by two men, over about an hour and ten minutes, on that Monday morning and the Monday morning after, and the two counted it separately and neither of them was told the other's figure before he had given his, and the count is **about two hundred and forty fruiting bodies on the dead wood inside the eleven acres**, and the two books say two hundred and forty and two hundred and forty, and the two men are the man of about fifty and the man of about thirty-one of Silling, and the two figures are the same.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that two men writing the same figure is not a check, and entered that this is the fourth time that has been entered as not a check, and entered the four, which are the first of this month, the sixth of this month, the fifteenth of this month and to-day, and entered that the four are four entries in four books over about twenty days, and that four entries agreeing with one another is one practice and not four checks, and that the practice is never going to be a check, and that a figure two men agree on is a figure this holding can act on and is not a figure this holding has proved.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said the thing, and she said it in about twenty-five people at about the second hour of the afternoon before anybody had asked her anything, and she gave the reason before she said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is five, and it was not tender and it was not a speech.

**"I ate one of them on Saturday morning before any of you knew they were there. I am saying it in about twenty-five people before anybody has asked me because if I say it after somebody asks me then it is a thing I did because I was asked, and I did not do it because I was asked. The reason I ate one is that I am thirty-eight years old and it is the fourth month and there is nothing green on that ground except what came up by itself, and I have three children and one of them is eleven, and I have not had anything green in my hand in about four days and I did not ask anybody and I did not go four hundred miles.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that she was not thanked, and that the reader of this body did not thank her, and entered that nobody in that yard thanked her, and entered that the seedwright of a trust said one sentence at that point and the sentence was that a woman of thirty-eight who has not had anything green in her hand in about four days is not a person anybody in a yard can do a piece of arithmetic about, and that it is the only sentence anybody said for about a minute and a half and that it was not improved on by anybody including her.

And then the resemblance was entered, because it was there and because a man who has a figure of two hundred and forty in his own book from the fifth of this month and a figure of about two hundred and forty on a dead tree two hundred and forty miles off on the same day is not going to get past it by saying nothing.

**THE FRUITING BODIES ON THE DEAD WOOD INSIDE THE ELEVEN ACRES ARE OF THE SAME KIND AS THE ONES COUNTED ON ONE DEAD WILLOW AT THE LONG FUNGAL ON THE THORNWILD EDGE, AND THAT IS ENTERED AS A FACT AND NOT AS A FINDING, AND THE FEN ENTERED THAT NOBODY IN THAT YARD HAS BEEN TO THE LONG FUNGAL AND THAT ONE OF THE PEOPLE IN THAT YARD HAS FOUR PAGES OUT OF A ROUTE BOOK THAT SAYS ABOUT TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY WERE COUNTED ON ONE TREE THERE ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK, AND THAT ELEVEN DAYS OF FRUITING FELL IN ONE MORNING AND IT SHOULD BE ELEVEN WEEKS, AND THAT A BOOK IS NOT A VISIT.**

**THE FRUIT HERE HAS BEEN COMING UP ABOUT NINE DAYS AND IT SHOULD BE ELEVEN WEEKS. THE RESEMBLANCE IS A RESEMBLANCE AND A RESEMBLANCE IS NOT A KIND, AND THIS HOLDING HAS GOT NOBODY IN IT WHO CAN STAND IN A YARD AND SAY WHICH OF THOSE TWO IT IS LOOKING AT, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FIVE OF AN ASSEMBLY, WHO READ FOUR PIECES OF GROUND NINETEEN YEARS AGO, IS NOT IN THIS YARD AND WAS NOT ASKED AND IS NOT GOING TO BE ASKED IN A YARD.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that nobody in that yard said the word *safe* and that nobody in that yard said the word *poison* and that the two of those are the two words this holding has been avoiding for about nine days and that a room which is avoiding two words is a room that has a decision in it and has not taken it.

And then the four bodies of households took the decision, and it was taken in about twenty-five people at about the third hour of the afternoon, and it was taken in the plain voice, and it was short, and nobody thanked anybody.

**NOBODY EATS ONE OF THEM UNTIL SOMEBODY WHO KNOWS HAS SAID SO OUT LOUD IN A YARD.**

And the reader of this body did not improve it and did not add to it, and he said one thing underneath it, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-three, and the thing he said was that he was not reaching for the standing rule and that nobody was to say afterwards that he had.

**"The standing rule about the engineer of record is on the wall of that seed house and it has been used twice in the season before last and once in the season last, and the one was a draw that was not begun, and I have said the word about that rule three times in about nine days and used it not at all, and I am saying it now for the last time and I am not using it, and I want the clerk to enter that I said I was not using it, so that nobody in this yard in about four years can say that I reached for it and did not get it.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the standing rule was not used and that the count of its uses in the last two seasons is two and one and that neither of them was this, and that the three namings are three namings and are not a use and have never been a use, and that a rule that is named three times and not used is not a rule anybody is working and is a sentence a man is holding over himself.

And then the cost of the decision was named out loud, and it was named by the person paying it, and it was the same woman, and it was nine words, and she named it before she began and nobody thanked her and she said that anybody who thanked her could be told to stop.

**"I am the one paying it and I am saying what it is: I have four days of nothing green.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that nobody in about twenty-five people thanked her, and entered that she had said before she began that anybody who thanked her could be told to stop, and entered that the reason is that a person who is thanked for a cost is a person who has been paid and can be asked to do it again, and that she said that in about nine words before the nine words and not after, and that it is the only piece of foresight anybody in this holding has shown about a cost in about four years and it was shown by a woman who is not a member of anything.

And then the twenty-second of this month came round, a Tuesday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-second went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and nine hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the series is now twenty-two figures on twenty-two days and not one of them is the same as any of the other twenty-one, and entered the last of them in his own book in a line of its own, and the line is this.

**TWENTY-TWO FIGURES AND NOBODY IN THIS FEN CAN CALL IT A STEADY ONE AND NOBODY IN THIS FEN CAN CALL IT A PATTERN, AND THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT FAILURES AND A FIGURE THAT IS NEVER THE SAME TWICE IS THE ONLY KIND THAT HAS MANAGED BOTH OF THEM AT ONCE, AND A HELD PATTERN IS A THING A ROOM CAN PLAN AROUND AND A STEADY FIGURE IS A THING A VILLAGE CAN PLAN AROUND, AND THIS HOLDING HAS NEITHER AND IS GOING ON READING IT EVERY MORNING ANYWAY, AND I AM NOT GOING TO IMPROVE THAT AND NEITHER IS ANYBODY IN THIS YARD.**

And then at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-first, in the cart shed at the node, in about two people, the reader of this body read the eleven lines out loud.
He read them to the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden and to nobody else. He had written them at about the seventh hour of the evening of the tenth of this month, in that shed, in about four hours, and he had read them back twice and had not read them to anybody, and the reason on the back of the same sheet was that a man with eleven correct lines in a shed is a man who is going to be the one who says what happens, and that what happens is a thing that has to be said by the person it happens to.

And the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden is the person it happens to, and he read them to her in a shed at about the ninth hour of the evening on the Monday, and she stood and listened to all eleven and did not ask him a question about any of them, and at the end of the eleventh she said one sentence, and the sentence is in this book in her words and it is not improved.

Nobody thanked him and he did not ask to be thanked, and the count of the times he has read those eleven lines out loud is one and it was to one person, and the fen entered that a man who has read eleven correct lines to the one person they are about has not stopped being the man who is going to say what happens and has only changed the room he is going to say it in, and that the room is about twenty-five people and not two, and that he did it on the twenty-first of this month at about the ninth hour of the evening in about nine minutes, and that the cost of it is that he is now a party to what happens to nine acres of another person's ground and that he was not one this morning.

And then the last two things of the day, and the fen entered both and did not improve either, and the first of them is a count and the second of them is not.

**THE COUNT OF THINGS THIS BODY HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR, AND IT IS FOUR, AND IT HAS BEEN FOUR FOR NINE DAYS, AND IT WAS AVAILABLE TO HIM FOUR TIMES IN THE AFTERNOON OF THE TWENTY-FIRST OF THIS MONTH AND HE DID NOT TAKE IT ANY OF THE FOUR TIMES, AND THE FOUR TIMES WERE ALL ABOUT A FRUIT.**

And the second of them is this, and it is a Monday and a Tuesday in about twenty-five people and in a shed, and it is what he wrote in his own book on the twenty-first of this month at about the ninth hour of the evening, and the sentence is eleven days old and he has read it back once a day since and has not taken it back.

And between the two of them, on the evening of the twenty-first and the morning of the twenty-second, the fen entered three things that are not findings and did not improve them.

**THAT THE DECISION OF THE TWENTY-FIRST IS A DECISION ABOUT A FRUIT AND NOT ABOUT A CROP, AND THAT THE CROP BEHIND THE GATE IS STILL THERE AND IS STILL BEING LOOKED AT BY ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE WHO HAVE NOT ASKED ANYBODY FOR ANYTHING, AND THAT THE TWO THINGS ARE ONE WOMAN'S AND THAT A DECISION TAKEN IN ABOUT TWENTY-FIVE PEOPLE ABOUT ONE OF A WOMAN'S TWO THINGS IS A DECISION THAT HALF HER WEEK IS CARRYING ON ITS OWN.**

**THAT THE COUNT OF FIGURES IN THE BOOK THAT KEEPS THE THIRD LAUNDER IS TWENTY-TWO AND THAT NOBODY HAS SAID THE WORD PATTERN ABOUT IT, AND THAT THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY WROTE THE NUMBER DOWN HIMSELF AND DID NOT ASK THE READER OF THIS BODY WHETHER IT WAS ONE.**

**AND THAT ON THE TWENTY-SECOND OF THIS MONTH A FRUIT CAME UP IN THE WRONG MONTH INSIDE ELEVEN ACRES OF STANDING WILLOW AND A CROP CAME UP IN THE WRONG MONTH BEHIND A GATE THAT WAS OPENED BY ITS OWNER ON THE TWELFTH, AND THAT NEITHER OF THE TWO HAS A PERSON WHO KNOWS EITHER, AND THAT THIS HOLDING IS NOT GOING TO BECOME THE PLACE THAT HAS ONE.**

**IN ABOUT FOUR DAYS THERE IS A YARD, AND IN THAT YARD THERE ARE TWO THINGS THAT CAME UP IN THE WRONG MONTH, WHICH ARE A CROP OF ABOUT NINE ACRES BEHIND A GATE AND A FRUIT ON DEAD WOOD, AND BOTH OF THEM BELONG TO ONE WOMAN AND SHE IS THE ONLY PERSON WHO CAN SAY WHAT HAPPENS TO EITHER OF THEM, AND I HAVE SAID FOUR THINGS OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG, AND I DO NOT KNOW WHAT THE FIFTH IS GOING TO BE, AND A MAN WHO HAS ELEVEN LINES IN A SHED AND A FRUIT IN A YARD IN FRONT OF HIM IS A MAN WHO IS GOING TO BE THE ONE WHO SAYS WHAT HAPPENS, AND WHAT HAPPENS IS A THING THAT HAS TO BE SAID BY THE PERSON IT HAPPENS TO. I HAVE GOT THAT SENTENCE IN THIS BOOK ALREADY AND IT IS ELEVEN DAYS OLD AND I DID NOT WRITE IT FOR THIS AND I AM NOT GOING TO WRITE IT AGAIN.**
