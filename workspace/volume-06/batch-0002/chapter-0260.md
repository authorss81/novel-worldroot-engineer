# Chapter 260

## The Fifteenth Figure

The sixteenth of this month was a Wednesday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **fourteen**, and entered that all fourteen of them are the fourth line, and entered that the fourth line has now come round on fourteen mornings since the rota came into force and has been empty on all fourteen of them, and that the seventh line of that sheet says nobody is blamed and the fen entered that nobody is blamed.

And the figure for the sixteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same, and it is **six hundred and three hundredweight**, which is the sixteenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and the fen entered the count and entered that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in fourteen days.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And the man of the north row with the cough was in that yard and was not asked to be in it, and he did not go up the cart road, because it is a Saturday market at Havergate this week and he goes, and the count of yards he has stood in since the first of a month four months back is **twenty-three** and has moved, and he said nothing at all this morning, and that is the seventh time in about five weeks that he has stood in a yard of this holding and said nothing, and the count of that is seven, and the fen entered both counts and entered that he has now written the word *asked* eleven times in five days about a man of about sixty-eight who is not the engineer of record of anything, and that a man who counts how often he has not asked somebody is a man who has begun to want to have asked.

And then the reader of this body went up to the headland above Marden at about the third hour, alone, and stood at the top of it and looked down at the turn that is not being made, and did not go down into it and did not put a rule against it and did not measure anything, and the fen entered that he was about four minutes and that about a hand of green had come up in the field on the Monday and that the ditch along the top of the low ground, the one nobody on that ground had cut, is not the same ditch as the one he has been putting a number on, and that the two ditches are about nine hundred yards apart and that he has not put them in the same column and is not going to.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he was alone on a headland for about four minutes at the third hour of the afternoon of a Wednesday, and entered that a man alone on a headland with a view of a field that is not being worked is a man in the one position in this holding from which he can do nothing useful whatever he decides, and that he has been in that position four times this month and that the count of the times is four, and that it is not a count of anything else and is entered as its own count.

And then, four hundred miles and four days off, at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, in the yard of the first house on a shelf above a road this holding has never laid, the occupier of it read her own well.

She did it with her own nail and her own string with a bit of lead on it, at the level of the water and not the level of the kerb, and the method has not changed in twelve years, and the string goes down the shaft on the nail and comes up wet, and she wrings it out over the bucket and puts the bucket outside the wall and comes back in and does not look at the other three while she does it, and there are three other people in that yard and none of them is of this holding and none of them was sent.

**Five feet.**

And she said it out loud in her own yard before it was written down, which is not her rule and is this fen's rule and she has taken it off this fen without being asked, and she said it in two words and the three of them heard it, and then she wrote it on her own wall in her own hand in the same chalk, under the last one.

Nobody said anything on that shelf and the fen entered that nobody said anything on that shelf, and entered that there are three people on that shelf and that the fen does not know what two of them said afterwards and did not write and ask and is not going to.

And the wall is the only record that well has ever had and it is in nobody's book but hers, and the fen entered the state of it from the copy this holding has been given by her own hand in two houses in the last two years, and the state of it is this.

**THE WALL CARRIES A MARK FOR EVERY ORDINARY READING SINCE THE FLOOD EXCEPT TWO, AND THERE ARE FOURTEEN OF THEM. THE FOURTEENTH IS THE LAST, WRITTEN UNDER THE THIRTEENTH AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THIS DAY, AND THE THIRTEENTH UNDER THE TWELFTH. ONE OF THE TWO IS THE GAP BETWEEN THE NINTH AND THE ELEVENTH AND IS A WHOLE MONTH, AND THE CHALK HAS NEVER TOUCHED IT AND THE PLASTER UNDER IT IS THE SAME COLOR AS THE PLASTER ROUND IT, AND IT IS THE WIDEST GAP ON THAT WALL AND IT IS THE ONLY ONE. THE OTHER IS THE FOURTEENTH READING, WHICH WAS TAKEN OFF HER OWN WALL ON THE AFTERNOON IT WAS TAKEN AND IS NOT ON IT AND IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the place on the wall is under the eaves on the north side of that yard and is about eight feet of coursed stone and has been washed off twice in thirty-one years and rewritten both times, and that a wall that has been washed off twice and rewritten twice is a wall that four households have decided is a record and not a wall, and that four households decided that and this holding had nothing to do with it and was not asked and is not going to write and ask.

And then the packet had a fifth thing in it, as it has had once before in twelve years, being a sheet with a line for the day and a line for the hour and a line for the figure and two more lines under the figure, of which the second is headed NOTHING ELSE, and she wrote in it, in her own hand, and it is this, and the three of them saw her write it and none of them said anything and none of them has ever seen it.

**THE WELL HAS NOT COME BACK. NOBODY IS TO WRITE THAT IT HAS. I HAVE NOT DECOMPOSED THE FOUR FEET AND I AM NOT GOING TO, AND I AM NOT GOING TO ASK ANYBODY WHY I HAVE NOT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that this holding does not know that this line was written until the twentieth of this month at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and entered the reason, which is that a house of four people that has put a nail and a wall in the ordinary course of a Wednesday morning is not a party to anything and does not want to be one, and that this holding has no standing on that wall and has never had and did not write and ask about the last one and is not going to write and ask about this one, and that the count of times this holding has written and asked about that shelf is nil.

And the four lines and the fifth thing went down the four-day road at about the ninth hour of the evening of the sixteenth, in a bag, at the ordinary cost of that road, which is the cost of a house with four people in it and is not anybody else's, and carried by a man who was not hired by this holding and whom this holding does not know, and neither the four lines nor the fifth thing has a name on it and there has not been a name on anything that has come down that road for twelve years and four months.

And at about the seventh hour of the evening of the sixteenth, at the node, four hundred miles and four days off from a wall this holding has never seen, the reader of this body wrote in his own book and read it back and is not going to improve it.

**THE FIFTEENTH ORDINARY READING OF THAT WELL IS BEING TAKEN THIS AFTERNOON AND I AM NOT BEING TOLD ABOUT IT AND AM NOT ASKING. IT WILL COME DOWN THE FOUR-DAY ROAD ON THE FOUR LINES SHE SENDS EVERY MONTH AND IT WILL BE IN THIS HOLDING'S YARD AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE TWENTIETH, WHICH IS FOUR DAYS, AND FOUR DAYS IS THE ROAD AND NOT A CHOICE.**

**WHEN IT IS ENTERED, THE BOOK ON THE TABLE IN THE MIDDLE WILL HOLD FIVE FIGURES OF THAT WELL AND EVERY ONE OF THE FIVE WILL BE FIVE FEET, AND THE SHORTFALL AGAINST THE NINE FEET OF THE SIXTEENTH OF A MONTH TWELVE MONTHS BACK IS FOUR FEET, AND I AM NOT GOING TO DECOMPOSE IT, AND THE HALF INCH THAT IS ON THAT WALL IS NOT CARRIED FORWARD AS A FIGURE ANYWHERE IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND IS NOT TO BE ADDED TO ANYTHING OR TAKEN OFF ANYTHING.**

**THE COUNT OF ENTRIES IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK THAT CARRY A DAY AND NO FIGURE STANDS AT THREE AND IT IS NOT GOING TO MOVE TO-DAY EVEN THOUGH A FIGURE IS COMING, AND THE REASON IS THAT WHAT IS COMING IS A FIGURE AND A FIGURE IS A FIGURE AND NOT A DAY WITHOUT ONE, AND A MAN WHO CHANGES WHAT A COUNT COUNTS IN ORDER TO MAKE AN AFTERNOON TIDY IS A MAN WHO HAS STARTED KEEPING THE COUNT FOR HIMSELF.**

**AND THERE IS NO ENTRY ANYWHERE IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK FOR THAT WELL COMING BACK AND THERE IS NOT GOING TO BE ONE, AND THE ONLY PERSON IN THE WORLD WHO COULD WRITE ONE IS FOUR HUNDRED MILES AND FOUR DAYS OFF AND IS NOT ASKED BY ME AND WOULD NOT ANSWER ME IF SHE WERE, AND I HAVE JUST ASKED HER FOR A FIGURE SHE WAS ALREADY SENDING AND I AM NOT GOING TO ASK HER TWICE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, in one man in a cart shed, and entered that the count of the questions the reader of this body has asked the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden in this holding's book is nil and did not move this week, and that the count of the things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this week, and that neither of those two numbers is the same as either of the others and that a man who has four nils and four-fours in a week and writes them all in the same column is a man who has stopped counting and started collecting.

And then he put in his own book the other two things of that day on that shelf, which are not the well and which the fen has from the copy, and they are here because an afternoon on which a figure does not come in is an afternoon on which the things that did not move are the only things left.

**THE GRASS ALONG THE SOUTH WALL OF THAT YARD WAS CUT ON THE SIXTEENTH, THE SAME AS IT IS CUT EVERY TEN DAYS, BY THE MAN WITH THE SPADE, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT HE DID. THERE IS A PLACE IN THE STACK OF PRESSED ROOTWOOD AGAINST THE NORTH WALL WHERE A THING STOOD FOR THIRTY-ONE YEARS AND DOES NOT STAND NOW, AND THE FOUR OF THEM HAVE NOT AGREED ON A NAME FOR THE PLACE AND HAVE NOT AGREED SINCE A MONTH BEFORE THIS ONE, AND NONE OF THE FOUR WILL SAY WHAT THE THING WAS, AND THIS HOLDING HAS NOT ASKED AND IS NOT GOING TO.**

**AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS WALKED PAST THAT STACK AND NOT LOOKED AT IT IS FOUR, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES HE HAS LOOKED AT IT IS NIL, AND NEITHER OF THOSE TWO NUMBERS IS A FINDING AND BOTH OF THEM ARE HIS OWN AND NOT THIS HOLDING'S.**

And then he did the thing he has done four times this month, which is that he counted the times a count of his own had been available to him and had not been taken, and he wrote the result down at about the ninth hour of the evening, and the result is this, and he entered that he is not going to say it out loud and entered why.

**THERE WERE ELEVEN IN THE ELEVEN DAYS FROM THE SIXTH OF LAST MONTH TO THIS EVENING. FOUR OF THEM WERE ABOUT THE GATE. TWO OF THEM WERE ABOUT THE FIGURE THAT IS COMING DOWN THE ROAD. THREE WERE ABOUT A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WHO KEEPS A SHEET. ONE WAS ABOUT A PROBE, AND ONE WAS ABOUT A NAME, AND I HAVE SAID THE NUMBER OUT LOUD IN A YARD IN THE SECOND MONTH OF THIS YEAR AND I DID NOT SAY IT AGAIN AND I AM NOT GOING TO, AND THE NUMBER I AM WRITING DOWN HERE IS A DIFFERENT NUMBER AND IT IS ABOUT A WEEK AND NOT ABOUT A YEAR, AND A MAN WHO PUTS A WEEK IN A PLACE WHERE A YEAR WAS IS NOT BEING HONEST EVEN TO HIMSELF.**

And then he put the lamp out and the fen entered that he did not sleep, and entered that the count of nights this holding's book records that he has not slept through in four years is twelve, and that the twelfth is the ninth of last month, and that he has not slept a night through since and has entered none of them, and that a man who keeps a count of his own sleeplessness in a book nobody reads is a man counting something he cannot do anything about, and that this is not a not known and does not go on that list, because it is not a thing about the ground.

> Entered by the man of about fifty, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the sixteenth of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a day and of a figure that has not come, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and is five days off: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS FOURTEEN, AND ALL FOURTEEN OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE SIXTEENTH, WHICH IS THE SIXTEENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE AGGREGATE HAS NOT MOVED IN FOURTEEN DAYS. THE MAN OF THE NORTH ROW WITH THE COUGH WAS IN THAT YARD AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE IN IT AND SAID NOTHING, AND THE COUNT OF YARDS HE HAS STOOD IN SINCE THE FIRST OF A MONTH FOUR MONTHS BACK IS TWENTY-THREE. FOUR HUNDRED MILES AND FOUR DAYS OFF, AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, THE OCCUPIER OF THE FIRST HOUSE ON THAT SHELF READ HER OWN WELL WITH HER OWN NAIL AND HER OWN STRING AT THE LEVEL OF THE WATER AND NOT THE LEVEL OF THE KERB, AND SAID IT OUT LOUD IN HER OWN YARD BEFORE IT WAS WRITTEN DOWN, WHICH IS NOT HER RULE, AND THE FIGURE IS FIVE FEET, AND SHE WROTE IT UNDER THE THIRTEENTH ON HER OWN WALL IN HER OWN HAND. THE WALL CARRIES FOURTEEN MARKS, BEING A MARK FOR EVERY ORDINARY READING SINCE THE FLOOD EXCEPT TWO, ONE OF WHICH IS THE TENTH, WHICH IS A WHOLE MONTH, AND THE CHALK HAS NEVER TOUCHED IT AND THE PLASTER UNDER IT IS THE SAME COLOR AS THE PLASTER ROUND IT, AND THE OTHER OF THE TWO IS THE FOURTEENTH READING, WHICH WAS TAKEN OFF THE WALL ON THE AFTERNOON IT WAS TAKEN. IN THE FIFTH THING IN THE PACKET SHE WROTE THAT THE WELL HAS NOT COME BACK AND THAT NOBODY IS TO WRITE THAT IT HAS, AND THAT SHE HAS NOT DECOMPOSED THE FOUR FEET AND IS NOT GOING TO. FOUR LINES AND A FIFTH THING WENT DOWN THE FOUR-DAY ROAD AT ABOUT THE NINTH HOUR OF THE EVENING OF THE SIXTEENTH AND WILL BE IN THIS HOLDING'S YARD AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE TWENTIETH. THIS HOLDING DID NOT WRITE AND ASK AND IS NOT GOING TO. THERE IS NO ENTRY ANYWHERE FOR THAT WELL COMING BACK. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
