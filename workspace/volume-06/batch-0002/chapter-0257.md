# Chapter 257

## The Word That Is Hers

The thirteenth of this month was a Sunday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the thirteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Five hundred and ninety-nine hundredweight.**

That is the thirteenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and the fen entered it and entered the count of the series and entered that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eleven days, and did not say what is changing, and has not said on any of the eleven days this week.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, in one man and a book, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon, in the cart shed at the node, in about eleven people, the reader of this body read out the conditions of a read, standing up, in the two hands they were written in, and the man of about fifty read them back, and the reader of this body did not hold either piece of paper and was not asked to hold either of them.

The seedwright of a trust is out on her route and came back up the fen road on the evening of the fourteenth and he was five days east of her while these were read. Nobody had sent for her. She was not asked whether the conditions might be read out in a room she was not in, and the fen entered that she was not asked, and entered the reason, which is that the four lines are hers and were written to be used and not to be admired, and that a woman who writes four conditions for another person's hands has written them for the day they are used and not for the day somebody reads them out to be kind to her.

> **FOUR LINES, in the hand of a seedwright of a trust, on one piece of paper of about the size of a hand, read out standing up in the cart shed at the node, in about eleven people, on the thirteenth of this month, and read back, entered whole and not summarized:**
>
> **ONE. A WORD. One word of mine stops it. The word is mine and it is not his, and he does not get to choose it and he does not get to hear it said in advance.**
>
> **TWO. THERE IS A SECOND PERSON IN THE ROOM AND THAT PERSON IS NEITHER OF US, AND THEY ARE TOLD WHAT IS HAPPENING BEFORE IT HAPPENS AND NOT AFTER.**
>
> **THREE. HE STOPS WHEN I STOP. HE DOES NOT STOP WHEN HE THINKS I SHOULD, AND NOT WHEN HE THINKS I HAVE NOT STOPPED BECAUSE I AM BEING BRAVE.**
>
> **FOUR. I AM NOT HIS SAFETY DEVICE. IF EITHER OF US IS USING ME AS ONE THEN EITHER OF US ENDS IT AND NEITHER OF US IS ASKED WHY.**
>
> **THREE LINES, in the hand of an engineer, which is Sera Quill's, on one piece of paper of about the same size, read out standing up and read back:**
>
> **ONE. NOT LONGER THAN NINETEEN MINUTES AT A TIME AND NOT FOUR TIMES IN ONE DAY.**
>
> **TWO. THE BEAD GOES ON THE PERSON AND NOT ON THE GROUND, AND THE PERSON IS TOLD WHAT IT WILL COST BEFORE IT GOES ON AND NOT AFTER.**
>
> **THREE. A SECOND STEWARD IN THE ROOM WITH A WATCH AND A STOP, AND THE WATCH IS THE ONLY THING IN THE ROOM THAT DOES NOT LIE ABOUT TIME.**
>
> **AND THE SENTENCE HE SAID OUT LOUD IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE BEFORE THE TWO PIECES OF PAPER WERE READ, GIVING THE REASON BEFORE IT, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS SIXTY-ONE, AND NOBODY IMPROVED IT AFTERWARDS: BOTH LISTS ARE IN FORCE AT ONCE AND WHEN THEY CONTRADICT ONE ANOTHER THE SEEDWRIGHT'S WINS, AND THE REASON IS NOT THAT SHE IS RIGHT ABOUT INSTRUMENTS, AND I AM THE WRONG MAN TO BE THE ONE WHO PUTS A SEEDWRIGHT'S LIST OVER AN ENGINEER'S, AND I SAID THAT IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE ON THE EIGHTH OF THIS MONTH AND I AM SAYING IT AGAIN BECAUSE THE FIRST TIME WAS IN A YARD AND THIS IS IN A ROOM AND A ROOM IS A WEAKER PLACE TO SAY IT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that about four of the eleven held themselves still and that the four were not chosen, and that one of the four was the man of about fifty with a pencil he did not put down.

And then the reader of this body read out the instrument, and the fen entered that he read it out because an instrument that has not been read out in a room is an instrument somebody in the room has to take on somebody else's word, and that is the whole of what the third line of the engineer's list is for.

**THE INSTRUMENT IS A WITNESS BEAD. It is a piece of living rootwood about the size of a thumbnail at the cut end and three inches long, tapering, and it was cut at Errow Reach in about the second month off a scion on this ground, and it has been in a leather fold in the man of about fifty's coat since the day it was cut, and it has never been out of that fold except when it is in a hand. It is the cheapest instrument anybody in this holding has and it is not a gauge and it is not a book and it is not a person.**

**IT READS ONLY WHAT A HAND ON THE NODE ALREADY KNOWS. It has never once answered a question anybody asked it and there is nobody in this fen who has ever asked it one, and I am not going to start on a Sunday by asking a piece of wood something I have not already got, and if I do it will be because I have not got it.**

And then he named what it costs, and the fen entered that he named it in about eleven people and that the cost is a thing he has to know before the thing is done and not after, and that he has not decided what the cost is and that the engineer's second line says the person is told the cost before the bead goes on, and that a man who cannot name the cost is not going to be allowed to put it on anybody, including himself.

And then he said who the person was going to be, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-two, and it was four words and then four sentences.

**"It goes on me.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds.

**"And I am saying that in about eleven people on a Sunday and not in a room, and the reason is the second line of the engineer's list. The bead goes on the person and not on the ground, and the person is told the cost before, and the man of the north row with the cough has three inches on the right forearm that do not branch and they are permanent and they came out of a hold of nine mornings in the third month, and I am not putting a piece of wood on that arm to find out what a thing is.**"

**"And it does not go on the ground either, and the reason is that a bead laid on ground that is not planted has never taken for anybody in this district and the licensed man said so in a yard in the third month and has not changed his mind since, and a man who lays his instrument on a thing that has already been read nine times by four people is a man who wants the nine times to be the answer.**"

**"And the cost is a thing I cannot name, and I am saying that out loud instead of saying the cost is small, and the reason is that on the twenty-first of the third month a bead went against my own arm and I had no use of that hand from the wrist down from the second hour of that afternoon, and that is in this holding's book in my own hand with the hour on it, and I have had nine months with that arm and I do not know what a second thing of any kind on it costs and I am not going to guess a cost in a room and be wrong in a yard about it in about four days.**"

And then he said the thing about the eighth of this month, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-three, and it is the reason the whole of this week is the shape it is.

**"On the eighth of this month I said in about eleven people that I was not using anybody yet and that I was not going to go and make a thing to read so that I could be the man who tried. I am not making a thing. There is a launder that has been cut at a kerb since the eleventh of the first month and has not been healed and is not going to be healed this season, and there are about nine acres behind a shut gate that came up green in the fourth month, and the gate was opened yesterday by the person whose they are, and there is a middle about four feet across and nine feet off the hollow that has come down a foot and one inch in eight mornings. I did not build any of those. I have not gone out and made a thing for an instrument to be put against, and if somebody asks me on Thursday what I read, the answer is going to be a thing that was already on the ground before I got there, and that is the only reason there is going to be a read on Thursday.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that about four of the eleven said nothing in a way that was different from the other seven, and that he did not count them.

And then he came to the part that cost four days, and he gave the reason before he came to it, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-four.

**"The second person in this room has to be neither of us and has to be told before. There is one person inside nine days of this ground who is a professional and is not either of us, and she came up this road on the ninth of last month at my request and at her own cost and she is an inspector of the Rootwright Assembly for four districts, and she is not on the roll for the district this node stands in.**"

**"And on the ninth of last month, in a room, to a room, she said that she checks returns and that anything else is a thing to ask her office for and pay her office for, and that if she is asked for it in a yard she will say no, and she gave the reason, and the reason is that she cannot stand in a yard of about twenty-five people and be an Assembly and a woman at once, and that it has happened to her once and it cost a village a season.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

**"Nobody is going to ask her. I am saying that in about eleven people on a Sunday and not in a room with the door shut, and the reason is that she said no on the ninth of last month in front of about eleven people, and a man who asks a person who has said no in front of eleven people is not asking a question, he is asking whether she will say it again, and that is a worse thing to ask a person than the first one.**"

**"So the count of requests made of her is one, and it was refused, and it has not been asked twice, and a refusal is a refusal and not an absence. An absence is a person who was not there. She was there and she said no and she gave a reason and the reason is in this holding's book in her own words, and the cost of the refusal is four days, and the four days are mine and I am not going to give them back to her and call it a favor.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that he did not ask her for anything this week and that the count of the times he has asked her anything in this holding's book is one and is still one, and that the one was a question about the hour of a return and was answered in nine seconds and was correct.

And then he said the other half of it, and the other half is the reason the four days are going to cost what they cost.

**"Which means I have to go outside this holding to find a person, and I am going to do that on Monday and I am going to say out loud now that I am going to do it, because a man who goes looking for a second person and does not say he is going to is a man who comes back with one and has the room already agreed.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this afternoon, and that this was not a yard, and entered that he had had it available to him at about the second sentence of that and went on, and that a count that does not move on the day a man does not need it to move is the only kind of count this holding has ever been able to keep.

> Entered by the man of about fifty, in his own hand, at about the fifth hour of the afternoon of the thirteenth of this month, in about eleven people, in the cart shed at the node, on the standing of a set of conditions, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and is five days off: **THE CONDITIONS OF A READ WERE READ OUT IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE, FOUR LINES IN THE HAND OF A SEEDWRIGHT OF A TRUST AND THREE LINES IN THE HAND OF AN ENGINEER, AND THEY ARE IN FORCE AT ONCE, AND WHEN THEY CONTRADICT ONE ANOTHER THE SEEDWRIGHT'S WINS, AND THE REASON WAS GIVEN BEFORE THE SENTENCE AND NOBODY IMPROVED EITHER LIST OR THE SENTENCE. THE SEEDWRIGHT WAS OUT ON HER ROUTE AND WAS NOT ASKED WHETHER THE CONDITIONS MIGHT BE READ OUT IN A ROOM SHE WAS NOT IN. THE INSTRUMENT IS A WITNESS BEAD, A PIECE OF LIVING ROOTWOOD ABOUT THE SIZE OF A THUMBNAIL AT THE CUT END AND THREE INCHES LONG, CUT AT ERROW REACH IN ABOUT THE SECOND MONTH AND CARRIED IN A LEATHER FOLD IN A COAT SINCE, AND IT READS ONLY WHAT A HAND ON THE NODE ALREADY KNOWS AND IT HAS NEVER ANSWERED A QUESTION ANYBODY ASKED IT. THE SECOND PERSON HAS TO BE NEITHER OF THE TWO OF THEM AND HAS TO BE TOLD BEFORE. THE ONLY PROFESSIONAL WITHIN NINE DAYS OF THAT GROUND IS AN INSPECTOR OF THE ROOTWRIGHT ASSEMBLY FOR FOUR DISTRICTS, WHO IS NOT ON THE ROLL FOR THE DISTRICT THE NODE STANDS IN, AND WHO SAID ON THE NINTH OF LAST MONTH THAT SHE CHECKS RETURNS AND THAT ANYTHING ELSE IS A THING TO ASK HER OFFICE FOR AND PAY HER OFFICE FOR. NOBODY ASKED HER. THE COUNT OF REQUESTS MADE OF HER IS ONE AND IT WAS REFUSED AND IT HAS NOT BEEN ASKED TWICE, AND A REFUSAL IS A REFUSAL AND NOT AN ABSENCE, AND THE COST OF IT IS FOUR DAYS. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE IS GOING OUTSIDE THIS HOLDING ON MONDAY TO LOOK FOR A SECOND PERSON, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
