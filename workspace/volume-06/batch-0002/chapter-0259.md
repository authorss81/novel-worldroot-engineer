# Chapter 259

## Four Lines Up A Road

The fifteenth of this month was a Tuesday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the fifteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and one hundredweight.**

That is the fifteenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and the fen entered it and entered the count and entered that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in thirteen days and entered that he has now written that sentence in his own book on thirteen of fifteen mornings and that a sentence written thirteen times is a sentence he is going to stop writing in about four days whether the aggregate has moved or not.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And at about the sixth hour of the evening of the fourteenth he wrote four lines on a sheet of his own paper, in his own hand, in the cart shed at the node, in about four people, and the man of about fifty read them back and did not improve them, and the sheet went up the four-day road the next morning and the man was engaged at the change of horses at about the seventh hour of the fifteenth and went up it with it.

> **FOUR LINES, in the hand of the reader of this body, on one sheet of his own paper, written at about the sixth hour of the evening of the fourteenth of this month in about four people at the node, read back by the man of about fifty, and given at the change of horses at about the seventh hour of the fifteenth to a man hired for the four-day road, entered whole:**
>
> **ONE. THE FIFTEENTH ORDINARY READING OF THE WELL AT CAULDRON REACH IS THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE SIXTEENTH OF THIS MONTH AND IT IS YOUR ORDINARY READING AND I AM NOT ASKING YOU TO TAKE IT INSTEAD OF YOUR OWN.**
>
> **TWO. IF IT COMES DOWN WITH A MAN, SEND IT DOWN. IF IT COMES DOWN WITHOUT ONE, SEND IT DOWN ANYWAY.**
>
> **THREE. THE COST OF THE MAN AND OF A CRATE IF THE CRATE IS WANTED IS NOT MINE TO REFUSE AND IS NOT YOURS TO PAY, AND IF I HAVE GOT THE FIGURE WRONG IN THE SECOND LINE YOU ARE TO SEND THE FIGURE AND NOT THE ARGUMENT.**
>
> **FOUR. NO NAME IS ON THIS AND NOBODY IS TO PUT ONE ON IT, AND THAT IS NOT AN INSTRUCTION TO YOU, IT IS A STATEMENT ABOUT ME.**
>
> **AND UNDER THE FOUR, IN THE SAME HAND, ON THE SAME SHEET, WHICH IS THE FIFTH LINE AND IS NOT PART OF THE FOUR, AND WHICH HE READ BACK TWICE: I AM NOT ASKING YOU FOR THE TENTH READING. YOU HAVE REFUSED IT IN YOUR OWN HAND AND I AM NOT GOING TO ASK YOU FOR IT TWICE, AND THE REASON I AM WRITING IT DOWN INSTEAD OF SAYING IT OUT LOUD TO A MAN HIRED FOR A ROAD IS THAT A MAN WHO IS PAID TO CARRY A SHEET IS NOT A PERSON AND HE IS NOT GOING TO BE TOLD WHY HE IS NOT BEING ASKED FOR A THING, AND HE WILL CARRY IT AS A SILENCE, AND A SILENCE HE HAS NOT BEEN TOLD THE REASON FOR IS A THING HE WILL THINK ABOUT ON A HORSE FOR FOUR DAYS.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that the man of about fifty read all five back and that he read the fifth back twice and that the count of the times he has read a line back twice in this holding's book is three and that two of the three are the reader of this body's own and that the fen keeps per-speaker counts and that this is a per-speaker count and is not a standing.

And then the cost of it was entered whole, in about nine people, standing up, in the yard at the node, on the Monday's ordinary reading, and the reader of this body read it out and the man of about fifty wrote it down as it was read and read it back.

**The wage is two shillings and sixpence up and two shillings and sixpence down, which is five shillings. The crate is one shilling and fourpence and the crate is only bought if the thing comes down in it. The two together are six shillings and fourpence. The road is four days up and four days down and the four days are eight days of a man and the man is not this holding's man.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the man of about fifty asked one question about it in about nine words, which was whether the wage is the same as the wage of the twenty-first of the third month, and that it is, and that both of those are two shillings and sixpence up and two shillings and sixpence down, and that the reader of this body said so, and that the question was not about the money.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighteen, and it was about a crate.

**"You have written four lines and a fifth line and not one of them says what to do with a crate if a thing comes down in it and is not a board, and I have been thinking about that on and off since the fourteenth. A crate is a thing a man pays for and a man loses. It is the only line on that sheet anybody in this yard can check, because a crate either comes back with the lid on or it does not, and I would rather be the man who brought it back than the man who says it was not needed.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and entered that the crate was bought and paid for and is a line of its own, and that the man of about fifty is to have the receipt and is to read it into the book when the man comes back, and that the reader of this body said out loud that the receipt is the only paper in this business that anybody in a room can check without going four hundred miles, and that he had not thought of that and the man of about fifty had, and entered that the count of that is five and did not move this evening, and that it does not move because he had thought of it about eleven seconds after the man of about fifty finished talking, and that eleven seconds is not a count of not thinking and is a count of a man being slow, and both of those are entered and neither is improved on.

And the reader of this body gave the reason for the rest of it out loud before he gave the rest of it, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-seven.

**"The hire is a line of its own. It does not go into the compost line, and the compost line for this month is already entered whole and it says PAID and one hundred pounds and fifteen not discharged in fifteen and none of that is my business this morning. It does not go against the two shillings and eightpence of the horse and it is not the same hire and a man who puts a hired man and a hired horse in one column has decided they are the same kind of thing, and one of them comes back and one of them does not.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And the reader of this body was not at the change of horses at about the seventh hour, and the fen entered that he was not, and entered the reason, which is that the man of about fifty took the sheet to the change of horses and hired the man himself and came back, and that a man who watches a man of a road being hired is a man who has not trusted the man of about fifty with a sheet of his own, and that he wrote the sheet and sent it and did not go with it, and that the count of the things he has been told about and has not been there for is two and did not move on the fifteenth of this month either.

And what the man of about fifty said about the sheet when he came back, and what the man of the road said, and what the man of the road was told, was entered in about four hundred words in this holding's book on the fifteenth of this month, and the fen entered the whole of it and entered that the man of the road had been told four things and had not been told a fifth, and that the four things were the wage, the road, the crate, and the line about a figure that came down in a crate, and that the fifth thing was the name on the acknowledgment line of a sheet of the third of this month, and that the man of about fifty had not been asked to say it out loud and had not.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the man hired for the road is not named in this book and was not asked for his name, and entered the reason, which is the reason of the third line of that four-line sheet, which is that no name goes on it and that a name on a sheet of this kind is a way of being counted, and that the man of about fifty did not contradict the sheet on that point and that the count of the times he has not contradicted it is two.

And then he read out the money in the box, because a cost that is not read out is a cost that only one man has, and the fen entered that he read it out in about nine people and that the reason is that a figure about a box in a cart shed is the only kind of figure this holding can check and that a figure about a road is not.

**"The box in this shed held ten pounds and four pence on Monday morning and it holds nine pounds and fourteen shillings this evening, and the six shillings and fourpence is gone out of it and is entered against a road, and the difference is arithmetic anybody in this yard can do in their head, and that is the whole of why I am saying it out loud.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that about four of the nine said nothing in a way that was different from the other five, and that he did not count them, and entered the reason, which is that a man who counts the faces in a yard is a man reading a room.

And the fen entered, before the road, that the line in this holding's own book about the four-day road being used on the fifteenth of this month was written on the evening of the fourteenth at about the seventh hour and was true when it was written, and that it has been half untrue for about a day, because a road of four days up and four days down is not a road a line on a page can be true about after about the seventh hour of the first day of it, and that a book which has to be corrected by its own author inside four days of the correction is a book being used faster than it is being kept, and that both of those are entered and neither of them is anybody's fault.

And then the reader of this body entered one thing about the road that is not money and is in his own book and was read back and is here because a man who hires a man for four days up and four days down is not going to have that man back in this holding before the eighth day of it.

**THE ROAD IS FOUR DAYS UP AND FOUR DAYS DOWN AND THE MAN LEAVES AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE FIFTEENTH AND IS ON THE SHELF AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE NINETEENTH, SO THE FOUR LINES ARRIVE AFTER THE FIFTEENTH READING HAS BEEN TAKEN, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY KNEW THAT ON THE FOURTEENTH AND WROTE THE SHEET ANYWAY, AND THE REASON IS IN THE SECOND LINE AND IS THAT HE ASKED FOR THE FIGURE AND NOT FOR THE DATE, AND A FIGURE THAT IS ASKED FOR ON THE FIFTEENTH AND ARRIVES ON THE NINETEENTH COMES DOWN ANYWAY BECAUSE THE OCCUPIER OF THAT SHELF HAS SENT FOUR LINES DOWN THAT ROAD EVERY MONTH FOR TWELVE YEARS AND FOUR MONTHS AND HAS NEVER BEEN ASKED FOR THEM AND SENT THEM ON THE SIXTEENTH AT ABOUT THE NINTH HOUR OF THE EVENING WHETHER ANYBODY HAD ASKED OR NOT. THE FIFTEENTH READING WILL BE ON THAT WALL ON THE SIXTEENTH AND FOUR LINES WILL GO DOWN THAT ROAD ON THE SIXTEENTH AT ABOUT THE NINTH HOUR OF THE EVENING AND THEY WILL BE IN THIS HOLDING'S YARD AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE TWENTIETH. I HAVE ASKED A WOMAN FOR SOMETHING SHE WAS ALREADY GOING TO DO AND THE PRICE OF THAT IS FOUR DAYS OF A MAN'S WAGE AND A CRATE, AND I AM ENTERING THE PRICE AND NOT THE SENTIMENT.**

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the evening and it was not about the road, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-eight, and what he said was nine words.

**"I have asked a woman four hundred miles off for a figure she was already going to send.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said in that yard, and entered that the count of the things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this evening, and that this was not one of the four, and the reason he knows it was not one of the four is that he said it to nine people and not to nineteen, and the fourth one was said to nineteen in a yard on the second of this month.

> Entered by the man of about fifty, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the fifteenth of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a cost, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and is five days off: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE FIFTEENTH, WHICH IS THE FIFTEENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTEEN DAYS. FOUR LINES WERE WRITTEN IN THE READER OF THIS BODY'S OWN HAND ON THE FOURTEENTH AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR OF THE EVENING, READ BACK, AND WERE GIVEN AT THE CHANGE OF HORSES AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE FIFTEENTH TO A MAN HIRED FOR THE FOUR-DAY ROAD, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY WAS NOT AT THAT CHANGE OF HORSES. THEY ASK FOR ONE THING, BEING THE FIFTEENTH ORDINARY FIGURE OF THE WELL AT CAULDRON REACH, THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE SIXTEENTH OF THIS MONTH, AND THEY NAME NO PERSON. UNDER THE FOUR IS A FIFTH LINE IN THE SAME HAND, WHICH IS NOT PART OF THE FOUR, AND IT SAYS THAT HE IS NOT ASKING FOR THE TENTH READING, THAT SHE HAS REFUSED IT IN HER OWN HAND, AND THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO ASK HER TWICE. THE WAGE IS TWO SHILLINGS AND SIXPENCE UP AND TWO SHILLINGS AND SIXPENCE DOWN, WHICH IS FIVE SHILLINGS, AND THE CRATE IS ONE SHILLING AND FOURPENCE IF THE THING COMES DOWN IN IT, AND THE TWO TOGETHER ARE SIX SHILLINGS AND FOURPENCE, AND THE ROAD IS FOUR DAYS UP AND FOUR DAYS DOWN. THE HIRE IS A LINE OF ITS OWN AND DOES NOT GO INTO THE COMPOST LINE AND DOES NOT GO AGAINST THE TWO SHILLINGS AND EIGHTPENCE OF THE HORSE. THE BOX IN THE CART SHED HELD TEN POUNDS AND FOUR PENCE ON MONDAY MORNING AND HOLDS NINE POUNDS AND FOURTEEN SHILLINGS THIS EVENING. THE COUNT OF THE TIMES THIS HOLDING HAS PUT A THING ON THE ORDINARY POST IN FOUR YEARS IS ONE, BEING A LETTER TO A DIFFERENT OFFICE ON THE SIXTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THE SECOND IS THE LETTER OF THE EIGHTEENTH OF THIS MONTH. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
