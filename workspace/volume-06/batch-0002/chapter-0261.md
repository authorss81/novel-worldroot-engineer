# Chapter 261

## Nineteen Minutes

The seventeenth of this month was a Thursday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the seventeenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and two hundredweight.**

That is the seventeenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and the fen entered it and entered that the count of the series is seventeen and that the aggregate of the four launders has not moved in fifteen days, and entered that he has written that sentence in his own book on fifteen of the seventeen days and did not write it on the two days of the ninth and the tenth of this month because he was in a room with a bag in it.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon the door of the cart shed at the node was shut and there were four people in it, and the fen entered the four, and entered that the man of about fifty wrote the conditions in his own hand before anything began and went out of the shed and stood about nine feet off the door with his back to it until it was over, and came in at the end, and was told by the seedwright in about nine words before he came in, and that the telling was before and not after, and that it is the second line of a seedwright's hand and that it is the reason he was in the room.

They were Tova Reed, who is a seedwright of a trust and who came back up the fen road on the evening of the fourteenth of this month after ten days out in twenty and was not sent for, the man of about thirty-one of Silling, the man of about fifty with a book, and the reader of this body. Nobody else. The count of rooms with the doors shut in this holding's book is one and this is not it, because a shed with a door on a latch is not a room and the fen entered that he is not going to call a shed a room to make this week sound better than it is.

And the conditions were entered as they were met, one at a time, by the man of about fifty in his own hand, in about four people, because a condition that is not written down at the moment it is met is a condition somebody remembers later, and the whole of what happened on the seventeenth of this month is a thing that four people remembered differently afterwards.

**A STOP WORD. It is hers. He does not know it, was not told it, did not ask for it, and there is nobody in that shed but her who knows it, and a stop word that three people know is a word and not a stop. She did not say it.**

**A SECOND PERSON IN THE ROOM WHO IS NEITHER OF THEM, TOLD BEFORE. The man of about thirty-one of Silling. Told on the fourteenth of this month in about four people with the door shut, and told again on the day in about nine words, and the two tellings were the same.**

**THE BEAD GOES ON THE PERSON AND NOT ON THE GROUND. It is a piece of living rootwood about the size of a thumbnail at the cut end and three inches long and it was cut at Errow Reach in about the second month. It goes on his own left forearm, on the flat of it, about four inches along the inside, in the place a mark runs in a person who has been holding, and the skin of it was wet, because a bead will not take hold of a dry thing and two men in his life have found that out the hard way, and he wetted it himself at about the second hour and did not ask anybody to do it.**

**A WATCH. It went on the table face up where three people could see it and the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the number out loud when it was nineteen and said it whether the reader of this body wanted to hear it or not, and it is the only thing in that shed that does not lie about time and it was the only thing in it that could be relied upon and both of those are the same sentence.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the two lists were in force at once and that no contradiction arose, and entered that a pair of lists that do not contradict on the day they are used has not been tested, and that they were written by a woman who has been the person who stops and by a woman who has never been the person who stops and that the two of them were in one room on the seventeenth of this month and neither of them looked at the other's paper, and that the fen entered that he expected a contradiction by the fourth minute and that there was not one and that he does not know which of the two lists was the right one to have brought.

And then he put the bead against his own arm and it took, and the read began, and it was nineteen minutes and not a minute over, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the number at nineteen, and it stopped.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that nobody said thank you and that nobody said anything tender and that about two of the four people in that shed said nothing in a way that was different from the other two, and that he did not count them and entered the reason, which is that a man counting the faces in a room with the door shut is a man who is about to want something out of the room.

And what the read gave is this, and it gave it in about four seconds and then gave nothing for the other fifteen minutes, and the fen entered that the fifteen minutes of nothing were not a failure of the instrument and were a man sitting with a piece of wood against his own arm waiting for his own head to come back with a thing in it.

**IT GAVE A LOCAL PRESSURE WHOSE UNIT IS NOT KNOWN. IT GAVE A DIRECTION. IT GAVE A DURATION.**

**AND THAT IS ALL. THERE IS NO YEAR IN IT. THERE IS NO NAME IN IT. THERE IS NO MOTIVE IN IT. THERE IS NO CAUSE IN IT AND THERE IS NO INSTRUCTION IN IT, AND A CAUSE IN A READ IS THE ONE THING A READ MAY NOT GIVE, BECAUSE A CAUSE IS A THING A PERSON CAN CARRY OUT OF A SHED AND USE ON SOMEBODY, AND THIS IS A SHED WITH FOUR PEOPLE IN IT AND NOBODY IS LEAVING IT WITH A CAUSE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the seedwright of a trust said one word after it stopped, and that the word was *enough*, and that the count of that in this holding's history is one, and that she did not say it to him and she did not improve it afterwards and he did not ask her whether she meant enough for what.

And then he wrote it in his own hand and read it back, and then she came and read what he had written and put four words to the end of it in her own hand, and the four words are hers and the fen has not improved them and is not going to.

> **IN HIS OWN HAND, AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE SEVENTEENTH OF THIS MONTH, IN THE CART SHED AT THE NODE, IN ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE, AND THEN IN HERS, TWENTY MINUTES LATER, IN THE SAME ROOM, WITH THE DOOR STILL SHUT: THE BEAD WENT AGAINST HIS OWN LEFT FOREARM, ON THE FLAT OF IT, ABOUT FOUR INCHES ALONG THE INSIDE, IN THE PLACE A MARK RUNS IN A PERSON WHO HAS BEEN HOLDING, AND THE SKIN OF IT WAS WET, AND IT TOOK. THE READ WAS NINETEEN MINUTES AND NOT A MINUTE OVER, AND THE WATCH WENT ON THE TABLE FACE UP AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SAID THE NUMBER OUT LOUD AT NINETEEN.**
>
> **WHAT IT GAVE. A LOCAL PRESSURE WHOSE UNIT IS NOT KNOWN. A DIRECTION. A DURATION. THE DIRECTION AND THE DURATION HAVE THE SAME SHAPE AS THE TWO FIGURES ON THE SHEET OF THE THIRD OF LAST MONTH, WHICH ARE ELEVEN SECONDS AND SIX HOURS AND ELEVEN SECONDS, AND A FIGURE THAT COMES OFF A SILL AT NINE FEET OFF AND A FIGURE THAT COMES OFF A THUMBNAIL OF ROOTWOOD IN NINETEEN MINUTES WITH THE SAME SHAPE ARE THE SAME THING TWICE.**
>
> **WHAT IT DID NOT GIVE. NO YEAR. NO NAME. NO MOTIVE. NO CAUSE. NO INSTRUCTION. NO PERSON. AND THE COUNT OF READINGS OF THE PULSE AT THE WEST SILL IS TWO AND IS NOT GOING TO BE THREE THIS SEASON, AND THIS IS NOT A THIRD READING OF IT, AND A MAN WHO HAS TWO FIGURES OF HIS OWN AND ONE OF AN OFFICE'S HAS GOT A COMPARISON, AND A MAN WHO PUTS THE COMPARISON ON THE BEAD HAS STOPPED READING AND STARTED WANTING.**
>
> **AND THE COST. FOR ABOUT NINE MINUTES HE DID NOT KNOW THE SECOND WORD OF THE FIFTH LINE OF A COVENANT HE COPIED OUT IN HIS OWN HAND IN THE THIRD MONTH, WHICH IS A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK, AND THE WORD IS TWENTY-TWO, AND IT CAME BACK, AND THE NINE MINUTES ARE ENTERED AND THE FACT OF THE NINE MINUTES IS ENTERED, AND THE FACT THAT SOMEBODY IN THAT ROOM SAW HIS FACE IS ENTERED, AND NOBODY READ HIS FACE OUT LOUD.**
>
> **AND HER FOUR WORDS, IN HER OWN HAND, AT THE END OF IT: I DID NOT STOP IT. HE DID.**

And then, at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, in about nine people in the yard at the node, the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-nine, and he said it once and did not say it twice.

**"A thing I can feel through a sill at nine feet off and a thing I can read in nineteen minutes with a thumbnail of rootwood are the same thing twice, and I have got it twice, and the two of them have the same shape, and that is the best news I have had in about nine weeks and it is the worst news I have had in about nine weeks, and I am not going to write down which of the two it is, because if I write down which one it is then one of them stops being a reading and starts being a conclusion.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this afternoon, and entered the reason, which is that a thing said in nine people in a shed with the door shut and forty minutes afterwards is not said in a yard, and that the count is a count of one kind of wrong and that this was not that kind and that the fen has entered both and has not added them.

And then the reader of this body said the last two things of the day, and they were said to the man of about fifty alone at about the seventh hour of the evening, and the first of them is a thing he is not going to do and the second of them is a thing he is going to do at about the fifth hour of the afternoon.

**"I am not going to the west sill to-day or this season. There is a nine-foot ledge of nothing between that sill and a reading and a man with two figures of his own and one of an office's has got a comparison, and I have said that out loud in about nineteen people once and I am not going to say it twice, and I am saying it now instead, in a room, to one man, because there is going to be a second reading of something in this holding inside a month and I would rather the man who keeps the books heard it from me than find it out.**"

**"And I am going to be five days off at about the eleventh hour of the morning tomorrow with a hired horse, and the horse is two shillings and eightpence and it is a line of its own and it does not go into the compost line, and I am not going to be at this node on Saturday and the twentieth ninth-day return is on Saturday and the man of about fifty is to make it, and he does not need me to make it and has not needed me to make it for eleven years.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, in two men in a shed, and entered that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is five and did not move this week, and entered that the reader of this body had thought of the Saturday absence on the fourteenth and had not said it out loud and had said it to himself in his own book, and that that is not the count and is not on the same column and is not going to be.

And what the nine minutes were is this, and the fen entered it and did not improve it and is not going to.

**HE WAS NOT IN THE ROOM FOR NINE OF THE NINETEEN MINUTES, IN THE ONLY SENSE ANYBODY IN THAT SHED COULD CHECK, WHICH IS THAT HE COULD NOT HAVE ANSWERED A QUESTION ABOUT HIS OWN NAME. THE SEEDWRIGHT OF A TRUST DID NOT USE HER WORD. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SAID THE HOUR OUT LOUD EVERY MINUTE FOR NINETEEN MINUTES, WHICH HE WAS ASKED TO DO AND WHICH NOBODY HAD THOUGHT OF ASKING HIM TO DO, AND HE DID IT OUT LOUD BECAUSE A NUMBER SAID QUIETLY TO ONESELF IS A THING A MAN CAN LOSE AND A NUMBER SAID OUT LOUD TO TWO OTHER PEOPLE IS A THING THREE PEOPLE HAVE.**

**AND THE READER OF THIS BODY ENTERED THAT HE WAS USING A PERSON TO HOLD A WORD AND THAT HE KNEW HE WAS DOING IT AND THAT THE WORD WAS NOT USED, AND THAT A THING THAT WAS NOT USED IS NOT A SAFETY DEVICE, AND THAT HE PUTS THOSE TWO SENTENCES IN THAT ORDER ON PURPOSE AND NOT IN THE OTHER ONE, AND THAT A MAN WHO PUTS THEM IN THE OTHER ORDER IS A MAN WHO IS KEEPING A DEVICE HE HAS NOT USED IN CASE HE NEEDS IT, WHICH IS A THING A PERSON IS FOR.**

And then the last count of the day, and it is small and it is his own and it goes in the book in his own hand, and the fen entered that it goes in the book and not in the standing, and that the standing is read out in about twenty-five people at nine hundred yards off on the first and the thirtieth and that this is not a standing figure and is not going to be one.

**THE COUNT OF TAKES THIS HOLDING HAS PUT A WITNESS BEAD TO ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF THIS MONTH IS ONE, AND THE LINE OF THE ENGINEER'S LIST SAYS NOT FOUR TIMES IN ONE DAY, AND ONE IS NOT FOUR AND IS NOT GOING TO BECOME FOUR THIS AFTERNOON, AND A READ THIS MORNING GAVE A PRESSURE WHOSE UNIT IS NOT KNOWN, A DIRECTION AND A DURATION, AND THREE THINGS ARE NOT A READING TWICE, AND A MAN WHO PUTS THE SAME BEAD ON THE SAME ARM IN THE SAME AFTERNOON TO SEE WHETHER THE THREE THINGS COME BACK THE SAME IS A MAN WHO HAS GOT A COMPARISON AND HAS STARTED WANTING.**
