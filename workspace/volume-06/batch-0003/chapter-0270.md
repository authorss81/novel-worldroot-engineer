# Chapter 270

## The Ledger, Which Is Incomplete

The twenty-seventh of this month was a Sunday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-seventh went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and fifteen hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that it has risen, and that the series has fallen twice and risen three times in five days, and that nobody thanked anybody.

And the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in twenty-five days and the sentence saying so was written for the twenty-fourth morning out of twenty-six this morning, and the fen entered that the reader of this body wrote it and then did not read it back, and that the not reading it back is the eleventh morning and is a change and is not entered as a figure.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, in one man in a shed.

And at about the second hour of the afternoon, before the ledger was opened, three people went down to the third of the eleven places and the man of about fifty put the wire on it, and it is whole and smaller than it was, and the roll is over it, and the face is square. And at about the fifth hour of the afternoon the same three went down to the seventh, and the seventh is whole and smaller than it was, and the roll is over it, and the face is square. And at about the sixth hour of the afternoon they went down to the eleventh, and the eleventh is whole and smaller than it was, and the roll is over it, and the face is square. And the fourth of the four is the branch at the ninth place and was measured on the twenty-fifth.

**THE FOUR OF THE ELEVEN ON THIS LINE ARE THE THIRD, THE SEVENTH, THE NINTH AND THE ELEVENTH. ALL FOUR ARE WHOLE AND SMALLER THAN THEY WERE AND NONE OF THE FOUR IS BROKEN, AND ALL FOUR HAVE A ROLL AND ALL FOUR FACES ARE SQUARE. THE FEN HAS ENTERED THE FOUR AS FOUR AND HAS NOT ENTERED A FIGURE FOR THE SIZE OF ANY OF THE FOUR, AND THE FIGURES ON THE BOARD ARE IN HIS OWN HAND AND ARE NOT IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK, AND THE REASON IS THAT A FIGURE IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS A THING SOMEBODY HAS TO ANSWER FOR, AND HE HAS ANSWERED FOR FOUR OF THEM AND THIS HOLDING HAS ANSWERED FOR NONE OF THEM.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, in three people in a field, and entered that seven of the eleven are not on this line and that this holding cannot get to seven of the eleven and has not asked to and is not going to, and entered the reason, which is that a holding with no charter and no roll has no standing to ask an office to walk a line for it, and that the standing it has is the standing of a body of households that corresponds, which is a body that can be found and is not a body that can be sent.

And the officer of the Office of the Continuity had left the ledger at the node at about the seventh hour of the evening on the Saturday, and the reader of this body did not open it until the second hour of the afternoon of the Sunday, and the fen entered that and entered the reason, which is that a book a man opens at about the seventh hour of the evening on a Saturday is a book he is going to have an opinion about by the second hour of the afternoon on the Sunday, and that eleven lines of this holding's book got corrected by their own author inside four days once already this month, and that the fen is not going to do it twice.

About eleven people were in the yard at the node at about the third hour of the afternoon and the reader of this body read the ledger out whole and the man of about fifty read it back, and it was read in the ordinary hand and in the plain voice and nothing in it was improved and nothing in it was explained.

> **THE LEDGER, in the office's own hand, in print, ninety-one rows, read out whole in about eleven people in the yard at the node on the twenty-seventh of this month at about the third hour of the afternoon, entered whole and not summarized, and anybody may copy it and the office says so at the head of it in one line:**
>
> **THIS IS A PUBLIC RECORD OF THE NETWORK. ANY PERSON MAY COPY IT. THE OFFICE KEEPS IT IN THE ORDINARY COURSE AND DOES NOT SEND IT UNLESS IT IS ASKED FOR. [Head of sheet, first line.]**
>
> **COLUMNS. Row. Object. Line. Date of last work. Condition at last work. [These five have heads, in print, and the heads are in the office's own hand.]**
>
> **AND THEN A SIXTH COLUMN WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT AT ALL, about the width of a finger, ruled in the same ink, and running the same ninety-one rows.**
>
> **AND ABOVE THE SIXTH COLUMN, IN THE MIDDLE OF THE SHEET, ONE LINE IN PRINT, WHICH IS THE ONLY THING THE SHEET SAYS ABOUT IT: THE SIXTH COLUMN IS NOT MAINTAINED AT ROW LEVEL AND IS CARRIED IN THE DISTRICT RETURN ONLY.**
>
> **AND AT THE FOOT OF THE SHEET, IN PRINT, IN THE OFFICE'S OWN HAND: THE FIVE HEADED COLUMNS ARE COMPLETE ON ALL NINETY-ONE ROWS. THE SIXTH IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF THE NINETY-ONE. THE ELEVEN ARE LISTED IN THE DISTRICT RETURN AND THE DISTRICT RETURN IS NOT ENCLOSED.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said, and entered that about four of the eleven said nothing in a way that was different from the other seven, and that he did not count them.

And then the fen entered the arithmetic of it, twice, and the two are the same.

**THE FIFTH HEADED COLUMN, CONDITION AT LAST WORK, CARRIES A FIGURE OR A WORD ON ALL NINETY-ONE ROWS AND THE OFFICE SAYS SO AT THE FOOT AND IT IS TRUE. THE SIXTH COLUMN IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF THE NINETY-ONE. ELEVEN IS NOT A QUARTER AND IS NOT HALF AND IS NOT NOTHING, AND A SHEET THAT SAYS ELEVEN IN PRINT AT THE FOOT IS A SHEET THAT HAS TOLD YOU ITS OWN SIZE, AND THAT IS THE ONLY REASON ANYBODY IN THIS YARD CAN ACT ON IT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said nothing, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the reader of this body then read out the eleven rows by their row numbers only, and did not read what was in them, and entered the reason, which is that the sixth column is blank on all eleven and there is nothing to read, and that a man who reads out a row number and then reads nothing after it is doing the thing the sheet does, which is to show you a place where a thing is not.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-nine, and he said it in about eleven people, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say the thing that is the whole of what has come down that road in about nine days, and I am going to say it once, and the reason is that there are about eleven people in this yard and about half of them can add up."**

**"A ledger that says what it does not have is worth more than a ledger that does not say, and the office has printed its own size at the foot of its own sheet, and I have been in this holding's book for four years being handed numbers by people who want me to use them, and this is the first document that has arrived here with its own gap written on it."**

**"And the other half. Four of the eleven are on this line. We have found four in four days with a wire and a bag and eight pence of steel, and one of the four is the branch at the ninth place, and the record is the only thing on this earth that makes that one and those four and those eleven the same thing."**

**"An instrument could not do it. An instrument only ever tells you about the thing you put it on, and I have put a wire on four pieces of wood and it has not once told me whether those four are four of something or four of nothing, and it never will, and I am not going to stand in about eleven people and pretend that four days of a wire in a valley is a proof of anything. The proof is a record, and the record is ninety-one rows long, and it is incomplete, and it is credible, and those are four separate things and only one of them is a reason for anybody to go and look at the other seven."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and did not move this afternoon, and entered the reason twice, which is that eleven and ninety-one are figures an office handed out and this holding handed out neither of them and is not going to, and that a number a man has not measured and cannot get to is a number he can invent, and that there are two on this holding's list already and the first of them is not named.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-two, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have copied that sheet out and I have copied both halves of it, and the reason I have copied the one that has no head at the head of it as well as the five that have is that in about four years somebody in this holding is going to want to know what was in it, and if I have left an empty space in a copy then the empty space will look like my handwriting."**

**"A ledger with a column and no column is two records in one. The five that have heads go to the office and the office reads them. The one that has not got a head is nobody's, and nobody's is the one that matters, and I am going to keep the ruled lines in my copy so that a person in about four years can see that there was a place there and not that there was nothing there. That is the whole of what I have got and it is about a quarter of an hour with a pen."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is **six and it is seven**, and that he counted it in a book and not in that yard in about nine seconds and entered both, and that the movement is not his and never has been, and that the seven are hers.

And then the last thing of the day is not a figure and is entered in the reader of this body's own hand and is the answer of the movement and is not a victory.

**A LEDGER THAT IS INCOMPLETE AND SAYS SO IS A THING A PERSON CAN BUILD ON. A LEDGER THAT IS INCOMPLETE AND DOES NOT SAY SO IS A THING A PERSON CANNOT DO ANYTHING WITH EXCEPT BE AFRAID OF, AND BOTH OF THOSE ARE THE SAME DOCUMENT AND THE DIFFERENCE IS ONE LINE IN PRINT IN THE MIDDLE OF A SHEET, AND THAT LINE IS THE ONLY THING THAT HAS COME DOWN ANY ROAD IN ABOUT NINE WEEKS THAT I WOULD PUT IN FRONT OF A PERSON WHO HAS ASKED ME A QUESTION I COULD NOT ANSWER. I AM NOT CALLING THIS A WINNING. I AM CALLING IT A DOCUMENT, AND A DOCUMENT IS A THING YOU CAN ARGUE WITH, AND WE HAVE HAD NOTHING TO ARGUE WITH SINCE THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that about eleven people were in that yard and that the man of about thirty-one of Silling was one of them and said nothing at all, and entered that he has said four things out loud in this holding's history and that the fourth was on a bank on Friday and was the best argued of the four, and that a man who has said four good things in a week is a man about to say a fifth and that the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and it is not five and this was not a yard.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-seventh of this month, in about eleven people, at the node, on the standing of a document, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the yard and did not improve one word of it: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH, WHICH IS THE TWENTY-SEVENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND THE SERIES HAS FALLEN TWICE AND RISEN THREE TIMES IN FIVE DAYS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN TWENTY-FIVE DAYS. THE LEDGER OF THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY FOR THE MAINTENANCE OF THE CROWN ENGINE WAS LEFT AT THIS NODE AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING OF THE TWENTY-SIXTH AND WAS NOT OPENED UNTIL THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE TWENTY-SEVENTH, AND WAS READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE IN THE YARD AT THE NODE AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND WAS READ BACK BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY AND WAS NOT IMPROVED. IT IS NINETY-ONE ROWS LONG. IT CARRIES FIVE COLUMNS WITH HEADS AND A SIXTH WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND IT PRINTS AT THE FOOT THAT THE FIVE HEADED COLUMNS ARE COMPLETE ON ALL NINETY-ONE ROWS, THAT THE SIXTH IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF THEM, AND THAT THE ELEVEN ARE LISTED IN A DISTRICT RETURN WHICH IS NOT ENCLOSED. FOUR OF THE ELEVEN ARE ON THIS LINE AND FOUR HAVE BEEN FOUND IN FOUR DAYS WITH A WIRE AND A BAG. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAS COPIED THE SHEET AND HAS KEPT THE RULED LINES UNDER THE ONE THAT HAS NO HEAD, SO THAT A PERSON IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS CAN SEE THAT THERE WAS A PLACE THERE. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE THAT A LEDGER THAT SAYS WHAT IT DOES NOT HAVE IS WORTH MORE THAN ONE THAT DOES NOT SAY, AND THAT THE PROOF THAT ONE THING IS THE SAME AS FOUR OTHER THINGS IS A RECORD AND NOT AN INSTRUMENT, AND THAT A LEDGER THAT IS INCOMPLETE AND SAYS SO IS A DOCUMENT AND NOT A WINNING. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE, AND ELEVEN AND NINETY-ONE ARE FIGURES AN OFFICE HANDED OUT AND THIS HOLDING HANDED OUT NEITHER. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
