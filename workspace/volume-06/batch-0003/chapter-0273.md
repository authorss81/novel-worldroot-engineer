# Chapter 273

## The Standing For The Month

The thirtieth of this month was a Wednesday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the thirtieth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and sixteen hundredweight.**

That is the thirtieth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and the series has fallen four times and risen four times in the eight days since the twenty-third, and the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in twenty-eight days and the sentence saying so has been written on twenty-seven of twenty-nine mornings, the two it was not written on being the ninth and the tenth of this month.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And then at about the seventh hour of the morning the reader of this body read the day-minus rules out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and fourteen days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and sixty-one**, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he has read them out loud on six mornings in about ten days and entered that the two numbers are forty-seven days apart and that the forty-seven has not moved in about four years and that neither of the two numbers is about the ground.

And then at about the eleventh hour of the morning the standing for the month was read out at nine hundred yards off in about twenty-five people, and it was read out by the person who keeps the seed house and read back by the clerk, and it stood in the open and it took about eleven minutes, and the reader of this body was in that yard, and the reason he was in that yard is that he wrote it in his own book on the eleventh of this month in about nineteen people in a yard at Errow Reach, in his own hand, and the sentence is nineteen days old and he has not taken it back.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that about four of the twenty-five said nothing in a way that was different from the other twenty-one, and that he did not count them, and entered that a man who counts the faces in the one yard a year that is read out loud has found a use for his own attention that is not in the standing.

And the standing was this, and it is not summarized, and the person who keeps the seed house read it in the ordinary voice and the clerk wrote it as it was read and read it back and nothing in it was improved.

> **THE STANDING FOR THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH, read out in about twenty-five people at nine hundred yards off at about the eleventh hour of the morning, and read back by the clerk, entered whole and not summarized:**
>
> **THE COMPOST LINE FOR THIS MONTH FELL ON THE FIRST AND IS PAID. IT SAYS ONE HUNDRED POUNDS AND FIFTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN FIFTEEN, AND THE WORD BEFORE THE FIGURE WAS THE SAME WORD AS THE MONTH BEFORE AND THE MONTH BEFORE THAT, AND THE LINE IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND AND IS NOT GOING TO BE USED AS ONE.**
>
> **THE LEAK IS THREE-QUARTERS OF AN INCH. THREE REASONS. NO CAUSE. NO DUE DATE. NOBODY ADDED A FOURTH REASON AND NOBODY WAS ASKED FOR ONE.**
>
> **THE BEAT IS NOT LOGGED. CAUSE NOT ESTABLISHED. THE COLUMN IS OPEN AND WAS NOT OPENED IN FRONT OF ANYBODY.**
>
> **THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. A LEDGER IS NOT A CONTACT. A ROAD IS NOT A CONTACT. A WIRE IS NOT A CONTACT. A KEY IN A SHED IS NOT A CONTACT. A PERSON IS A CONTACT.**
>
> **THE WELL HOUSE DOOR. THIS HOLDING'S BOOK HOLDS FIVE FIGURES OF THAT WELL AND EVERY ONE OF THE FIVE IS FIVE FEET. THE SHORTFALL AGAINST THE NINE FEET OF THE SIXTEENTH OF A MONTH TWELVE MONTHS BACK IS FOUR FEET AND IS NOT DECOMPOSED. THE TENTH READING IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND IS NOT GOING TO BE ASKED FOR. THERE IS NO ENTRY ANYWHERE IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK FOR THAT WELL COMING BACK.**
>
> **THE SEVENTH COLUMN IS READ TO-DAY AND IS FORTY-FIVE. THE OTHER SIX COLUMNS ON THAT DOOR ARE BLANK AND HAVE BEEN FOR FOUR YEARS AND NOBODY ASKED AND NOBODY IS GOING TO ASK IN THIS STANDING.**
>
> **THE BODIES AT THE NODE ARE FIVE. THE NUMBER IS NOT GIVEN IN A YARD.**
>
> **A MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY HAS BEEN FETCHED NINETEEN TIMES AND WAS NOT FETCHED ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH AND THE COUNT HAS NOT MOVED.**
>
> **THE REQUESTS THAT A MAN'S INITIAL BE PUT AGAINST NOTHING STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND THE SECTION-NINE NOTES STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND NEITHER MOVED THIS MONTH.**
>
> **THE COUNT OF ENTRIES IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK THAT CARRY A DAY AND NO FIGURE STANDS AT THREE AND DID NOT MOVE THIS MONTH EVEN THOUGH A FIGURE CAME IN ON THE TWENTIETH, BECAUSE A FIGURE ENTERED IS A FIGURE AND NOT A DAY WITHOUT ONE.**
>
> **AND THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD THAT THE STANDING FOR THE MONTH OF NEXT MONTH IS READ ON THE FIRST AND THE THIRTIETH OF NEXT MONTH AND THAT HE WILL BE IN THAT YARD, AND HE DID NOT SAY WHY AND NOBODY ASKED HIM.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven minutes and then about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that about four of the twenty-five looked at the six places on that door where a heading ought to be, and that the six places have been looked at for four years by about four people and that nobody has ever written a heading on one of them and that the count of that is not taken and is not going to be taken, because a count of people looking at a blank place is a number about looking and not about a column.

And the man of the north row with the cough was in that yard and was not asked to be in it, and the count of yards he has stood in since the first of a month four months back is **twenty-five** and has moved, and he said nothing at all, and that is the eighth time in about six weeks that he has stood in a yard of this holding and said nothing, and the count of that is eight, and the fen entered both counts and entered that they are two counts and have not been added together, and entered that a man who has stood in twenty-five yards since a month four months back and has said nothing in eight of them has not been in twenty-five arguments and has been in twenty-five rooms, and that this holding has not asked him in any of them and is not going to.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and entered that the standing count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is ten and did not move this morning, and that the number was not said in that yard and is not said in a yard, and that the promise about it was made out loud in a yard in the second month of this year and has held for about four months.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-four, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have read that book out twice this month and I am going to say one thing about the door and I am going to stop, and the reason is that there is a line in that standing which is mine and has been mine for four years and I have not said a word about it in a standing since the first year."**

**"Forty-five goes in the seventh column today. The six that are blank are blank and nobody is going to write a heading on one of them, and I have wanted somebody to write a heading on one of them every year for four years, and the reason I have not asked is that a heading is a promise that somebody is going to keep putting a figure in it, and I would have to be that somebody, and I have not got a second thing to put in it and I would rather have six blank places that say nothing than six places with my name over them saying nothing."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is **eight and it is nine**, and that he counted it in a book and not in that yard in about nine seconds, and that the movement is not his and never has been.

And then the reader of this body said the one thing he had come for, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-two, and he said it in about twenty-five people, and nobody improved it.

**"I said in a yard on the eleventh of this month at about the second hour of the afternoon that I would be standing in this yard on the thirtieth, and I am standing in it, and I have had nineteen days to think about what I was going to say when I got here and I have not got one thing, and the reason I am saying that in twenty-five people is that the sentence I wrote on the eleventh had a second sentence in it and the second sentence was about the water on the low ground of Marden being a great deal further along by now."**

**"It is. I have seen it. I stood at that gate on the twenty-sixth of this month in about twenty-five people and nine acres of it is coming off before the middle of next month because the woman who owns it has said so, and nobody in that yard asked her a question, including me, and the count of questions I have asked her is nil."**

**"So I am going to read the standing and I am not going to say the second sentence, and the reason is that the second sentence would be a thing about a woman's ground said out loud in a yard about a well house door, and a man who has been wrong four times out loud in a yard does not get to be right about somebody else's field in a yard either."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this morning, and that this was available to him at about the second sentence of it and he went on, and entered that a man who cannot tell whether a thing is a fifth yet is a man who has a fifth coming and does not know its shape.

And then the reader of this body put the whole of the series into the book, in his own hand, on the thirtieth of a month, because a month is the only interval anybody in this fen has ever agreed on, and he read it back once and is not going to improve it.

**THIRTY FIGURES ON THIRTY MORNINGS, BEING EVERY MORNING FROM THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH TO THE THIRTIETH, AND NOT ONE OF THE THIRTY IS THE SAME AS ANY OF THE OTHER TWENTY-NINE. IN THE EIGHT DAYS SINCE THE TWENTY-THIRD OF THIS MONTH THE FIGURES HAVE FALLEN FOUR TIMES AND RISEN FOUR TIMES, AND NOBODY HAS SAID THE WORD PATTERN ABOUT ANY OF THE THIRTY IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IN FOUR YEARS, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY WROTE THE COUNT DOWN HIMSELF AND DID NOT ASK ME WHETHER IT WAS ONE, AND THAT IS STILL TRUE.**

**A FIGURE THAT IS NEVER THE SAME TWICE IS NOT A STEADY FIGURE AND IS NOT A PATTERN AND THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT FAILURES, AND THE SECOND OF THE TWO IS THE WORSE ONE FOR A FEN AND THE FIRST OF THE TWO IS THE WORSE ONE FOR A PERSON, AND I HAVE BEEN IN THIS HOLDING FOUR YEARS AND I HAVE NOT WORKED OUT WHICH OF THOSE I AM, AND A SERIES THAT IS THIRTY FIGURES LONG IS NOT A BASIS FOR ANYBODY'S OPINION AND I AM NOT GOING TO GIVE ONE AND NOBODY IN THAT YARD ASKED ME FOR ONE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nothing tender was said at nine hundred yards off on the thirtieth of a month and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this morning, and that it has been four for about twenty-four days and that he has not taken it in a yard about a fruit or about a branch or about a figure and that the count of the times it has been available to him and he has not taken it is not a count in this book and is not going to be.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the thirtieth of this month, in about eleven people, at this holding, on the standing of a month, and not by the reader of this body, who was at nine hundred yards off until about the second hour of the afternoon and did not improve one word of it: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE THIRTIETH, WHICH IS THE THIRTIETH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND THE SERIES HAS FALLEN FOUR TIMES AND RISEN FOUR TIMES IN THE EIGHT DAYS SINCE THE TWENTY-THIRD. THE DAY-MINUS RULES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER AND THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-ONE. THE STANDING FOR THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH WAS READ OUT AT NINE HUNDRED YARDS OFF IN ABOUT TWENTY-FIVE PEOPLE AT ABOUT THE ELEVENTH HOUR OF THE MORNING, BY THE PERSON WHO KEEPS THE SEED HOUSE, AND READ BACK BY THE CLERK, AND IT STOOD IN THE OPEN AND TOOK ABOUT ELEVEN MINUTES. THE COMPOST LINE FELL ON THE FIRST AND IS PAID AND DOES NOT FALL AGAIN. THE LEAK IS THREE-QUARTERS OF AN INCH WITH THREE REASONS AND NO FOURTH. THE BEAT IS NOT LOGGED AND ITS COLUMN WAS NOT OPENED IN FRONT OF ANYBODY. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN IS READ AND IS FORTY-FIVE AND THE OTHER SIX ARE BLANK AND HAVE BEEN FOR FOUR YEARS. THE BODIES AT THE NODE ARE FIVE AND THE NUMBER IS NOT GIVEN IN A YARD. A MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY HAS BEEN FETCHED NINETEEN TIMES AND WAS NOT FETCHED ON THE FIRST AND THE COUNT DID NOT MOVE. THE REQUESTS AND THE SECTION-NINE NOTES STAND AT FIFTY-THREE. THE MAN OF THE NORTH ROW WITH THE COUGH STOOD IN THAT YARD AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE IN IT AND SAID NOTHING, AND THE COUNT OF YARDS HE HAS STOOD IN SINCE THE FIRST OF A MONTH FOUR MONTHS BACK IS TWENTY-FIVE, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES HE HAS SAID NOTHING AT ALL IN A YARD IS EIGHT. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE HAS WANTED A HEADING ON ONE OF THE SIX BLANK PLACES EVERY YEAR FOR FOUR YEARS AND HAS NOT ASKED, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE STANDING COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND DID NOT MOVE AND WAS NOT SAID IN THAT YARD. THE THIRTY FIGURES OF THE SERIES WERE ENTERED WHOLE IN THE READER OF THIS BODY'S OWN HAND. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
