# Chapter 268

## Reduced And Not Broken

The twenty-fifth of this month was a Friday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-fifth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirteen hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that it has risen, and that in the two days since the twenty-third the series has fallen once and risen once, and that the fen entered both and did not improve them, and that nobody thanked anybody.

And the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in twenty-three days and the sentence saying so was written for the twenty-second morning out of twenty-four this morning, and the fen entered that the reader of this body wrote it and then sat down and then got up and went out, and that a man who writes a sentence about a number before he has the number is a man reading his own book, and that is a count of his own and is not a count of the ground.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, in one man in a shed.

And then four people went up the cart road past the ford and off it and down to the ninth of the eleven places for the third day, and the fen entered the three days and entered that the branch had been looked at three times by four pairs of eyes and had not been measured until the second of the three, and that a thing looked at three times and measured once is a thing that was being got ready.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that this was said in a cart shed at about the seventh hour of the morning and not in a yard, and that it is his own sentence and is not a count of anything.

And on the bank at the ninth place, the reader of this body put the wire round the stem above the mark and the wire across the face and the man of about fifty read both out loud and the man of about thirty-one of Silling wrote them down on a board he had brought for it, and the two figures are these and they are the first two figures anybody has put on that piece of wood.

**THE GIRTH ABOVE THE MARK IS ELEVEN INCHES AND TWO SIXTEENTHS BY THE WIRE AND ELEVEN AND THREE SIXTEENTHS BY THE RULE, AND THE TWO DISAGREE BY ONE SIXTEENTH AND THE WIRE IS THE FIGURE. THE WIDTH ACROSS THE FACE IS EIGHT INCHES AND NINE SIXTEENTHS. THE DIFFERENCE IS TWO INCHES AND NINE SIXTEENTHS, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED IT AS A DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO FIGURES AND NOT AS A FIGURE OF THE BRANCH, AND HAS NOT CALLED IT A REDUCTION, BECAUSE A REDUCTION IS A FIGURE THAT IS SMALLER THAN THE FIGURE BEFORE IT ON THE SAME INSTRUMENT IN THE SAME PLACE AND THIS IS THE FIRST TIME THE SAME INSTRUMENT HAS BEEN ON THIS PIECE OF WOOD.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that the difference of two inches and nine sixteenths is not a size of anything, and that a man who writes it down and then uses it as a size has done the thing this holding has already done twice, and that the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and the first of them is not named and is going to stay two.

And then the officer of the Office of the Continuity put the folded sheet on the flat of the valve box cover again and opened it at the fourth page, and the fourth page is the line, and the schedule is the office's own, and he read it out in about four people, and the fen entered the whole of it.

> **THE FOURTH PAGE, in the office's own hand, in print, read out in about four people on the bank at the ninth place, entered whole and not summarized: THE LINE IS ENTERED UNDER ITS OWN NUMBER AND NOT UNDER A DISTRICT. THE SEASON'S WORK ON THIS LINE IS PERFORMED IN ONE WINDOW. THE WINDOW OPENS ON THE SIXTH OF A MONTH AND CLOSES ON THE ELEVENTH OF THE SAME MONTH. THE WINDOW IS NOT EXTENDED IN ANY SEASON AND NO WORK ON THIS LINE IS CARRIED OUT OF IT. THE LAST DATE OF WORK ENTERED AGAINST THIS LINE IS THE NINTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK. THE FIGURE OF THE WIDTH OF A SCAR OVER A CUT ON THIS LINE IS NOT HELD.**
>
> **AND AT THE FOOT OF THE FOURTH PAGE, IN PRINT, IN THE OFFICE'S OWN HAND: THE SCHEDULE IS CORRECT AND IS CARRIED OUT. NOBODY FROM THIS OFFICE IS REQUIRED TO BE WITHIN NINE DAYS OF THE DATE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the sixth to the eleventh is six days and that the ninth is inside it, and entered that a reduction in a branch is dated the ninth of a month two months back by nothing whatever except a piece of paper with four lines on it, and that the paper is correct, and that the branch is eight inches and nine sixteenths across a face and healed, and that those two things are on the same page because an office put them there.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the thing that everybody in that field was thinking and that nobody was going to say, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is four.

**"I am going to say the thing that is in all four of our heads and I am going to say it because if I do not say it out loud then somebody is going to say it in a yard in about four days and I would rather it was said on a bank by the man who brought it here, and the reason is that a guess said on a bank stays a guess and a guess said in a yard becomes a thing people repeat."**

**"There were eleven places in four miles where that ground was opened and made good. A man opening ground in a line takes the bank with him, because the bank is in the way and because he has not got time to be careful. That branch is right on the lip of the ninth one. Somebody took two inches off that branch because he was standing where he had to stand and it was in the way. That is not a rumour. That is how ground gets opened."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that it was the fourth thing that man had said out loud in this holding's history and that it was the best argued of the four.

And the reader of this body gave the reason for his answer before he gave the answer, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-six, and he said it in about nine people, because the four who walked had been joined at about the second hour of the afternoon by the woman who keeps the four books and the person who keeps the seed house and three others who had come up for the afternoon and had not been asked to and were not sent for.

**"No, and I am going to give you the reason before I say why, and the reason is that you have just said the most likely thing anybody has said about that branch in three days, and being likely is the whole of the danger."**

**"Here is why it is not a finding. A roll is wood the tree has grown over a cut, and it takes a season to close, and a man with a spade in a hurry does not leave a roll, he leaves a wet face and a ragged edge and he is gone in an hour. I have put a wire on that face and the wood is closed and it is closed square. So the cause you have given me is not impossible, it is only not what is in front of me."**

**"And here is the other half and it is the half that matters. That sheet is dated the ninth of a month two months back and the ninth is inside a window and the window is on a schedule and the schedule is correct. But the date is on a page and the cut is in a piece of wood, and nothing on this bank has put them on the same page except that sheet, and an office put them there, and the office is four hundred miles off in one direction and a good deal more in the other. A date is a thing a nail and a string put on a wall. This holding has not got that nail and that string and I am not going to send for them."**

**"A cause in a branch is not a finding. A cause in a branch is a rumour with a date on it, and a rumour with a date on it is the most expensive kind of thing a holding can carry, because it will be true of something eventually."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said on that bank, and entered that about nine people were in it and that the reader of this body had counted them and had not counted their faces.

And then he said the second thing and it is the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and it is the same reason and the count is seventy-seven, and nobody in about nine people improved it.

**"And the last thing. A branch that is reduced is not a branch that is damaged, and I am right about the difference and I am going to say it once."**

**"Reduced means the wood is whole and smaller than it was. Damaged means something in the structure is not whole. A reduced branch carries what it carried. A damaged branch does not know what it carries. Every use anybody in this holding has for that branch this season depends on which of those two it is, and it is the first, and I know it is the first because I put a wire on it and the wire is the cheapest instrument any of us owns and it cost eight pence."**

**"And I have no figure for the size of that reduction and I am not going to invent one. Two inches and nine sixteenths is a difference between two readings on a morning and it is not a size and it does not go on anything, and if a man in about four years asks me how much was taken off that branch I am going to tell him I do not know and that I knew on the twenty-fifth of this month that I did not know, and both of those are true and the second one is worth more."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the man of about thirty-one of Silling did not argue and that the fen entered that he did not argue and entered the reason, which is that a man does not argue with a better argument, and that the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own argument had been better than most and he knew it and he was not a man who took a thing back.

And then the officer of the Office of the Continuity said the thing that made the next two days, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is three.

**"I am going to say one thing and then I am going to read the rest of this page out and no more of it, and the reason is that you have all four of us standing on a bank asking me a question I have been asked before and I have never answered it and the reason I have never answered it is that the thing that answers it is not a thing I can carry up a hill in a leather case."**

**"What answers it is the ledger. Not this sheet. This sheet is a schedule and it tells you when and it tells you nothing else. The ledger is a record anybody may copy, it is kept in the ordinary course, and it has a column with a heading and a column with nothing at the head of it at all, and the second of those is the one that would tell you, and the second of those is empty on some of the rows and full on others, and nobody in my office has ever written a line at the head of it about that."**

**"You have been walking four miles and reading a wire. The ledger is ninety-one rows long and you have found four places out of it, and the other eighty-seven are behind a counter in a room four hundred miles off, and I am telling you that so that you do not spend a week in a valley with a wire in your hand being certain."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that the reader of this body said nothing at all when it was said, and entered the reason, which is that he had written the word *ledger* in his own book on the seventh of this month at about the ninth hour of the evening, under the sheet of the third of this month, in the column he keeps for things the office has said and this holding has not asked for, and that a man who has written a word down about eighteen days before an office says it out loud has not been surprised and has been waiting, and that those are two different things and the fen has entered both and is not going to decide which one it was.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-fifth of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a branch, and not by the reader of this body, who was on the shoulder until the light went: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH, WHICH IS THE TWENTY-FIFTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN THE TWO DAYS SINCE THE TWENTY-THIRD THE SERIES HAS FALLEN ONCE AND RISEN ONCE. A BRANCH ON THE BANK AT THE NINTH OF ELEVEN PLACES WAS MEASURED TWICE ON ONE MORNING BY THE SAME WIRE, BEING ELEVEN INCHES AND TWO SIXTEENTHS BY THE WIRE AGAINST ELEVEN AND THREE SIXTEENTHS BY THE RULE, AND EIGHT INCHES AND NINE SIXTEENTHS ACROSS THE FACE, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS TWO INCHES AND NINE SIXTEENTHS, AND IT IS ENTERED AS A DIFFERENCE AND NOT AS A SIZE. THE WOOD ON THE FACE IS CLOSED AND THE CLOSING IS SQUARE, WHICH IS A SCAR AND NOT A BREAK. THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY READ OUT THE FOURTH PAGE OF ITS OWN SCHEDULE, WHICH GIVES THE SEASON'S WINDOW ON THAT LINE AS THE SIXTH OF A MONTH TO THE ELEVENTH OF THE SAME MONTH, NOT EXTENDED IN ANY SEASON, AND THE LAST DATE OF WORK ENTERED AGAINST THAT LINE AS THE NINTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK, WHICH IS INSIDE THE WINDOW, AND PRINTS AT THE FOOT THAT THE SCHEDULE IS CORRECT AND IS CARRIED OUT AND THAT NOBODY FROM THAT OFFICE IS REQUIRED TO BE WITHIN NINE DAYS OF THE DATE. IT HOLDS NO FIGURE FOR THE WIDTH OF A SCAR OVER A CUT. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE THAT A BRANCH REDUCED IS NOT A BRANCH DAMAGED AND THAT HE IS RIGHT ABOUT THE DIFFERENCE, AND THAT HE HAS NO FIGURE FOR THE SIZE OF THE REDUCTION AND IS NOT GOING TO INVENT ONE, AND THAT A CAUSE IN A BRANCH IS A RUMOUR WITH A DATE ON IT. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SAID OUT LOUD THAT A MAN OPENING GROUND IN A LINE TAKES THE BANK WITH HIM, AND WAS NOT THANKED, AND WAS NOT AGREED WITH. THE OFFICER OF THAT OFFICE THEN SAID THAT WHAT ANSWERS IT IS NOT A SCHEDULE BUT A LEDGER, THAT THE LEDGER IS PUBLIC AND MAY BE COPIED, THAT IT HAS A COLUMN WITH A HEADING AND A COLUMN WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND THAT IT IS NINETY-ONE ROWS LONG. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
