# Chapter 269

## Her Own Morning

The twenty-sixth of this month was a Saturday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-sixth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twelve hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that it has fallen again, and that the series has fallen twice in four days, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And then at about the ninth hour of the morning the reader of this body was at the north gate of this holding and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came off the shoulder from the east with a barrow and the two of them walked down the eleven hundred yards to the stone head and out to the gate at the far end of the third launder, and neither of them said anything on the way and the fen entered that and entered that the count of the times the reader of this body has asked the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden a question in this holding's book is nil and did not move this morning and has not moved all month.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven hundred yards, and entered that a man who walks eleven hundred yards with a person he has not asked a question in about four days is a man who is behaving correctly and is not being told so by anybody.

About twenty-five people were at that gate at about the second hour of the afternoon and the fen entered the twenty-five and entered that about eleven of them work that ground and about fourteen of them do not, and that the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was there before the rest of them and had been since about the ninth hour, and that the gate stood open and the ground behind it is green to about the height of a hand and the water in the ditch along the top of it is about four inches and level and not moving, and that nobody had asked her anything and that nobody was going to.

The green has not changed in about four days and the fen entered that and entered the reason, which is that a hand's height of green on ground that was not sown is not a crop yet, and that on the third day of this month it had been a finger, and that a thing which has not changed in four days is a thing this holding has no other figure for and is not going to invent one about, and that the reader of this body has stood at that gate eleven times in about a month and has put a rule against nothing and has written no figure against the crop and is not going to, and that a man who has written no figure about a field for a month has not been idle and has not been careful either, and that both of those are on the page and neither is improved.

And then she said it, and she gave the reason before she said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is six, and nobody improved it afterwards.

**"I am going to say what I have decided about my own ground and I am going to say it before anybody asks me, and the reason is that a woman who has been asked first has been put in a room, and I am not in a room, I am standing at my own gate on my own morning."**

**"It comes off before the middle of next month. All of it. The carted part goes to my three animals and the rest of it is left lying where it grew and is not taken by anybody, and I am not putting a row of anything on that ground this season and not the season after if the water is still coming."**

**"I did not sow it. I do not know what it is. I have a field of about nine acres that has been behind a shut gate for about four years and it has grown nothing that I could put a name on in four years, and something has come up on it in the fourth month that I did not ask for, and I am not going to build on a thing I did not put there."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that she was not thanked, and that the reader of this body did not thank her, and that about twenty-five people did not thank her, and entered that nobody asked her one question in about four minutes, and that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and is nil.

And then the fen entered the thing underneath it, because it is the reason the four minutes were quiet and it is a fact and not a finding.

**THE DECISION OF THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH STANDS. IT WAS TAKEN IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE, ALONE, BY THE READER OF THIS BODY, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN WENT FROM NINE TO TEN ON THAT DAY AND IS TEN AND DID NOT MOVE TO-DAY, AND THE NUMBER IS NOT SAID IN A YARD AND WAS NOT SAID AT A GATE IN ABOUT TWENTY-FIVE PEOPLE. THE CUT STAYS OPEN. NOTHING IS TO BE PUT IN THAT GAP THIS SEASON. THE NINE ACRES BEHIND THE GATE CAME OFF BECAUSE THE CUT WAS OPEN AND NOT BECAUSE A ROW OF SOMETHING WAS PUT IN IT, AND THE WATER IS STILL COMING AND NOBODY KNOWS WHEN IT STOPS, AND A DECISION THAT WAS TAKEN IN NINETEEN PEOPLE ON A DAY IN LAST MONTH HAS NOT BEEN REVERSED BY SILENCE AND IS NOT GOING TO BE REVERSED BY A GATE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the reader of this body was the man who took that decision and that he was in about twenty-five people at a gate on a Saturday afternoon while a woman who owns the ground told nine acres of it to be cut off, and that he said nothing, and entered the reason, which is that the first of those two facts is the only reason the second one was hers to take.

And then the seedwright of a trust said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is two, and she had come up from the fen road on the Friday and had not been sent for and had not said why.

**"I am going to say one sentence about the crop and one about the fruit and then I am going to stop, and the reason is that I am a seedwright and there is exactly one question a seedwright has about a crop, and I have not been asked it and I am going to answer it anyway."**

**"The question is who put it there, and nobody in this yard knows, including the four of us who walk a wire up a valley. A crop is a thing a person did. What is on that ground is a thing that happened to a field, and those are not the same thing and a person who treats them as the same thing ends up saving seed off it, and I would not save seed off it and neither would anybody here."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that she was not thanked, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is six and did not move, and that the seedwright of a trust is not a person who keeps a book in this holding and is not on that count and is not going to be.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said the second thing, and it is about the fruit, and she gave the reason before she said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is seven.

**"And the fruit on the dead wood I am not going to eat and I am not going to say one word about whether it is fit to eat, in this yard or in any other, and I am saying that now so that nobody comes to this gate in about four days with a question about it."**

**"That dead wood is inside the eleven acres and I have said in about nineteen people that I am not the one who speaks for the eleven acres and I am saying it again this morning in about twenty-five. I am not the owner of that ground. I am the one who has to eat what is on my own ground and I am not the one a yard asks about a thing that grew on somebody else's. I ate one of them on the Saturday before last and I said so before anybody asked me and I would say it again and I am not going to say anything else about them at all."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that she was not thanked, and that about four of the twenty-five said nothing in a way that was different from the other twenty-one, and that he did not count them and entered the reason, which is that a man counting the faces in a yard is a man reading a room and he has been caught doing that twice this month.

And then the reader of this body said the one thing of the afternoon and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-eight, and he said it in about twenty-five people, and it is on the page with a word on it, which is where the count of the things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is kept.

**"Nobody in this holding knows whether that fruit is fit to eat. I am going to say that out loud in about twenty-five people and I am going to say it in the plain voice, and the reason is that the decision of the twenty-first of this month was that nobody eats one until somebody who knows has said so, and a decision that says *until somebody who knows* is not a decision, it is a way of carrying a thing we cannot do, and it has been carried four days and it is costing somebody."**

**"There is nobody here who knows. Not me, not a seedwright, not the man who counted them into two books. I have a figure of two hundred and forty and a resemblance to something two hundred and forty miles off and a book with four pages in it, and a resemblance is not a kind and a book is not a visit, and the count of things I have said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and it has been four for about twenty days and this afternoon it was available to me again and I have taken it and this is not it."**

**"A thing I have said out loud in a yard and got wrong is a claim about the ground. This is a statement about this holding, and there is a difference, and the difference is that a claim about the ground can be checked by going and looking at the ground and I have looked and this one cannot, because what would settle it is not in this fen."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said at a gate in about twenty-five people on a Saturday afternoon, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this afternoon, and that the fen has entered the distinction between a claim about the ground and a statement about this holding and has not put it in a column of its own and is not going to.

And then the cost of her decision was named, and it was named by her, and it was eleven words, and nobody said it for her and nobody improved it.

**"I am the one in this yard who has had nothing green in her hand in about four days."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that about nine acres of green was going to be cut off a woman's own ground and carted away and that the woman saying it was the only person at that gate who had wanted it, and entered that nobody thanked her and that she had said on the twenty-first of this month that anybody who thanked her could be told to stop and that nobody had thanked her since and had not tried to and that the count of that is two days and is not a count of anything.

And what she decided is not entered as a figure and is entered as a decision, and the entry is a decision and not a number, and this is the only decision in this holding's book in four years that has been entered as a decision.

> **A DECISION, and not a figure, entered in the hand of the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden, on the twenty-sixth of this month at about the second hour of the afternoon, at the gate at the far end of the third launder, in about twenty-five people, and read back by the man of about fifty and not improved: THE GROUND BEHIND THAT GATE COMES OFF BEFORE THE MIDDLE OF NEXT MONTH. THE CARTED PART GOES TO HER THREE ANIMALS. THE REST IS LEFT WHERE IT GREW AND IS TAKEN BY NOBODY. SHE IS PUTTING NO ROW ON THAT GROUND THIS SEASON. NOBODY ASKED HER ANYTHING BEFORE SHE SAID IT AND NOBODY ASKED HER ANYTHING AFTER. SHE ASKED THIS HOLDING FOR NOTHING AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS NIL. SHE IS NOT SAYING ONE WORD ABOUT THE FRUITING BODIES ON THE DEAD WOOD INSIDE THE ELEVEN ACRES IN THIS YARD OR IN ANY OTHER, AND THE REASON IS ON THE PAGE AND IS NOT A FIGURE.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-sixth of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a decision, and not by the reader of this body, who was on the shoulder until the light went: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWELVE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-SIXTH, WHICH IS THE TWENTY-SIXTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND THE SERIES HAS FALLEN TWICE IN FOUR DAYS. ABOUT TWENTY-FIVE PEOPLE WERE AT THE GATE AT THE FAR END OF THE THIRD LAUNDER AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, AND ABOUT ELEVEN OF THEM WORK THAT GROUND, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY ASKED THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN NO QUESTION AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES HE HAS ASKED HER ONE IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS NIL AND DID NOT MOVE. SHE DECIDED, BEFORE ANYBODY ASKED HER AND WITH THE REASON GIVEN FIRST, THAT THE GROUND BEHIND THAT GATE COMES OFF BEFORE THE MIDDLE OF NEXT MONTH, THAT THE CARTED PART GOES TO HER THREE ANIMALS, THAT THE REST IS LEFT WHERE IT GREW AND IS TAKEN BY NOBODY, AND THAT SHE IS PUTTING NO ROW ON THAT GROUND THIS SEASON. THE SEEDWRIGHT OF A TRUST SAID ONE SENTENCE ABOUT THE CROP AND ONE ABOUT THE FRUIT AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN SAID THAT SHE IS NOT GOING TO EAT ONE OF THE FRUITING BODIES AND IS NOT GOING TO SAY ONE WORD ABOUT WHETHER THEY ARE FIT TO EAT, AND THE REASON IS THAT THE DEAD WOOD IS INSIDE THE ELEVEN ACRES AND SHE IS NOT THE ONE WHO SPEAKS FOR THEM. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD IN ABOUT TWENTY-FIVE PEOPLE THAT THERE IS NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING WHO KNOWS, AND ENTERED THAT THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THAT A CLAIM ABOUT THE GROUND AND A STATEMENT ABOUT THIS HOLDING ARE TWO KINDS OF THING AND THAT HE HAS PUT THE DISTINCTION ON THE PAGE. THE COST OF HER DECISION WAS NAMED BY HER IN ELEVEN WORDS AND BY NOBODY ELSE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
