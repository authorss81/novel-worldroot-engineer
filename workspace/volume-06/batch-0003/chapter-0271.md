# Chapter 271

## The Twenty-First Return

The twenty-eighth of this month was a Monday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **seventeen**, and entered that all seventeen of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **forty-six mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and seventeen not**, and that twenty-nine and seventeen are forty-six, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

And the figure for the twenty-eighth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and fourteen hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that it has fallen again, and that the series has fallen three times in six days, and that nobody thanked anybody.

And the barrow came up the four miles in about an hour and three-quarters and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came behind it the whole way and did not push it once, and the fen entered that a hundred and seventy-three hundredweight is a barrow and a half and that the half is the part that decides whether a barrow gets up a shoulder road, and that the reader of this body has watched that barrow go up and come down on eleven ninth-day mornings now and has not once been asked to hold the back of it, and entered the reason, which is that a man who is the engineer of record of a node is not a hand on a barrow and the two have never been the same and are not going to be.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about an hour and three-quarters, and entered that nobody thanked the man of about thirty-one of Silling and that nobody has thanked him on any of the eleven and that the count of that is eleven and is a count of this holding's manners and is not a count of the man.

And the reader of this body was at this holding and was not at the node, and the twenty-first ninth-day return was made at about the second hour of the afternoon at the node on the steelyard hung on the wall of the building with no roof, and was weighed and read out by the man of about fifty and read back by him, and then read one at a time by each of the four bodies of households standing where they could see the dial, and a name went into BY and a name went into WITNESSED BY, and the eleventh name-pair in eleven years went in.

**One hundred and seventy-three hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that about eleven of the nineteen people in that yard were of the four bodies of households and eight were not, and that the eight were not asked to stay and did not stay, and that the reader of this body noticed which eight and did not write the eight down.

And the figure is reconciled here twice and the two are the same, and the fen entered that it is reconciled twice on purpose and that a return that can only be reconciled once is a return somebody can make up.

**THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO, AND ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO PLUS ELEVEN IS ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE. THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE, AND ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE PLUS TWENTY-TWO IS ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE. THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS THE ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY. THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS EIGHTY-SEVEN, MADE TWO DAYS LATE ON THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK, AND IT IS THE SMALLEST OF THE ELEVEN, AND THE FOUR BEFORE THE EIGHTY-SEVEN WERE NINETY-ONE, EIGHTY-NINE, NINETY AND NINETY-TWO, AND THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS, AND THE INTERVAL FROM THE EIGHTY-SEVEN TO THE ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY WAS NINE DAYS AND NOT SEVEN, AND IT WOULD HAVE BEEN SEVEN IF THE DAY HAD BEEN MOVED WITH THE FIGURE, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY DID NOT MOVE IT, AND HE WROTE THE LATENESS IN HIS OWN HAND WITH THE DAY AND THE HOUR ON IT AND READ IT BACK BEFORE A WORD OF IT WENT ON THE PAGE. AND THE ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY, THE ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE, THE ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO AND THE ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE ARE FOUR FIGURES, AND EACH OF THE FOUR IS ELEVEN ABOVE THE ONE BEFORE IT, WHICH IS THREE INTERVALS, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THE THREE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered the third thing about the series, which is the thing he is not going to say out loud.

**THERE ARE NOW FOUR FIGURES IN A ROW OF ELEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON A CYCLE OF NINE DAYS, AND FOUR IN A ROW IS THE MOST PREDICTABLE THING THIS HOLDING HAS EVER HAD, AND THE NEXT FIGURE IF THE PATTERN HOLDS IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY WROTE THAT DOWN IN HIS OWN BOOK AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING OF THIS DAY AND DID NOT SAY IT OUT LOUD IN THE YARD AND ENTERED THE REASON, WHICH IS THAT A MAN WHO SAYS A NUMBER BEFORE IT IS ON A PAGE HAS PUT IT ON A PAGE, AND HE IS NOT A MAN WHO PUTS A NUMBER ON A PAGE BEFORE IT IS ON A PAGE.**

And the man of about thirty-one of Silling brought it up the four miles in a barrow in about an hour and three-quarters and asked to be allowed to and was allowed and was not thanked, and he took the barrow back down the four miles in it, and the fen entered that this is the second time in a month and that this holding does not keep a count of barrows and is not going to start one on a Monday afternoon.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And Harlan Vetch, of about fifty-eight, who holds the fifth share and has held it eleven years and thirty-five days, took the chalk out of his own pocket and stood at the second rule on the board for about nine seconds and did not write in it and put the chalk back in his pocket, and nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the count of the returns at which he has not written in that rule is **five**, and that the blanks are the seventeenth, the eighteenth, the nineteenth, the twentieth and this one, and that the first of the five is the seventeenth, and that this holding does not know what was in that rule on the seventeenth and is not going to ask, and that the count of five is a count of its own and is not the same count as anything else in this book and is not going to be added to any of them.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about nine seconds, and entered that he wrote WET SEASON in that rule in his own hand at the sixteenth return and that nobody asked him to then either, and that the change is not a decision anybody took and is a rule and not a decision, and that a man whose rule has gone quiet five times running has not stopped having a rule and has stopped having anything to write in it, and that those are two different things and the fen has entered both.

And then he said the one thing, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is six, and it was said in about nineteen people and not to one man.

**"I am going to say one thing about my three questions and then I am not going to say them again, and the reason is that I asked them in a yard on the nineteenth of this month and the man they were for was five days off and did not answer them, and I have had nine days with them sitting in me and I am not going to carry them into a yard a second time without an answer under them."**

**"So they are still mine. What it is for, who it is held with, and what a man does with a figure that went up by fifty in six days. I am not taking any of them back and I am not softening one of them. I am going to leave them where they are until somebody in this yard writes an answer to one of them down in a book, and then I will ask the next one, and until there is an answer under them they are not a demand, they are three questions, and I am fifty-eight and I have asked three questions in a yard before and got nothing and I am not going to make it four in the same season."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nothing tender was said, and that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and entered what his not-saying cost, which is that a question left unanswered for a season stops being a question and starts being a habit of the person who asked it, and that the fen entered that as the cost because nobody else was going to and he was not going to be asked.

And then at about the sixth hour of the evening the reader of this body read the three questions out of the page of the book with the man of about fifty's hand on it, in about four people, and did not answer them, and entered the reason in his own hand with the hour on it.

**THE ANSWER IS STILL FOUR SECONDS LONG AND I HAVE HAD NINE DAYS OF IT, AND A MAN WHO HAS NINE DAYS OF AN ANSWER AND WRITES IT DOWN IN A ROOM WITH FOUR PEOPLE IN IT IS A MAN WHO HAS BEEN WAITING SINCE THE NINETEENTH AND DID NOT KNOW IT, AND I AM NOT GOING TO DO THAT IN A ROOM. AND I AM NOT GOING TO CARRY IT UP THE ROAD TO A MAN WHO KEEPS THE BOOKS AND HAVE HIM CARRY IT BACK, BECAUSE THAT IS THE SAME THING WITH ONE MORE MAN IN IT. THE THREE QUESTIONS ARE HIS AND HE HAS SAID SO IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE AND HE IS NOT GOING TO ASK THEM AGAIN UNTIL SOMEBODY WRITES AN ANSWER DOWN, AND THE CHEAPEST WAY FOR THAT TO HAPPEN IS FOR ME TO SAY ONE OF THEM OUT LOUD IN THAT YARD, AND I AM NOT GOING TO DO IT ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF THIS MONTH BECAUSE THE RETURN WENT UP ELEVEN TO-DAY AND A MAN WHO ANSWERS A QUESTION ON A MORNING THE NUMBER WENT UP IS ANSWERING A DIFFERENT QUESTION.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half in a room and about nine seconds in a yard that he was not in, and entered that the count of the times he has offered to be somewhere he is not the engineer of record for is two and did not move this week and is not this, and entered that the count of the times he has been told about a thing and has not been there for is two and did not move this week, and entered that both of those counts are the same and that they are not the same count and have not been added together.

And then at about the seventh hour of the evening he read the day-minus rules out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and twelve days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and fifty-nine**, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that a rule that moves a day a day is a rule that has to be said out loud or it is two numbers in a book and not a rule at all.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he has now read them out loud on five mornings in about nine days and that the reason on each of the five was different and that two of the five were about a figure and one was about a road and two were about a man, and entered that a man who gives a different reason every time for the same habit has not got five reasons and has got one reason he is getting better at saying, and that this is entered and is not a not known and is not going on that list.

And then he said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty, and he said it in about four people and not in a yard, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about the eleventh name-pair in eleven years and I am going to say it in a room and not in a yard, and the reason is that a yard is where things get said that want company, and this one does not want company, it wants a witness, and there are four of them and they have all been through it."**

**"Eleven years and eleven times and a name into BY and a name into WITNESSED BY every ninth day, and there has not been one morning in eleven years when anybody has read a figure off that dial and then said out loud what it means. I have watched four bodies of households take turns saying a number they do not own on a dial they did not make, eleven times, and it is the best thing this holding does, and I am the only man in it who is paid to look at what it means, and I have not got a figure for what it means, and I am not going to invent one on a Monday when the dial has just gone up again."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said in that room, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this evening and that this was not a yard and that the reason it was not a yard is in the second sentence of it.

> Entered by the man of about fifty, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-eighth of this month, in about nineteen people at Errow Reach, on the standing of a return, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding, and not by the reader of this body, who was at this holding and was not at the node: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS SEVENTEEN, AND ALL SEVENTEEN OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FORTY-SIX MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND SEVENTEEN NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH, WHICH IS THE TWENTY-EIGHTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND THE SERIES HAS FALLEN THREE TIMES IN SIX DAYS. THE TWENTY-FIRST NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AND WEIGHED AT ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT, AND IT IS THE ELEVENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS, AND IT IS RECONCILED TWICE, BEING ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE PLUS TWENTY-TWO. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING BROUGHT IT UP THE FOUR MILES IN A BARROW AND TOOK IT BACK DOWN AND WAS NOT THANKED. HARLAN VETCH LEFT THE SECOND RULE BLANK FOR THE FIFTH TIME AND WROTE NOTHING IN IT, AND THE COUNT OF THE RETURNS AT WHICH HE HAS NOT WRITTEN IN IT IS FIVE, AND THE BLANKS ARE THE SEVENTEENTH, THE EIGHTEENTH, THE NINETEENTH, THE TWENTIETH AND THIS ONE, AND THE FIRST OF THE FIVE IS THE SEVENTEENTH, AND THIS HOLDING DOES NOT KNOW WHAT WAS IN THAT RULE ON THE SEVENTEENTH AND IS NOT GOING TO ASK. HE THEN SAID THAT HIS THREE QUESTIONS ARE STILL HIS AND THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO ASK THEM AGAIN UNTIL SOMEBODY WRITES AN ANSWER TO ONE OF THEM DOWN IN A BOOK, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT ANSWER THEM AND ENTERED THE REASON IN HIS OWN HAND. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
