# Chapter 275

## Held For Five

The second of this month was a Friday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **eighteen**, and entered that all eighteen of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota stands at **forty-seven mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and eighteen not**, and that twenty-nine and eighteen are forty-seven, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

And the figure for the second went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and eighteen hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-second figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and the ten figures of the ten days since the twenty-third of last month have fallen five times and risen five times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said in any yard of this holding or at that node on that day.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the reader of this body went up the cart road past the ford and off it and out onto the shoulder above the local lead line, and two other people went with him, and the fen entered the three and entered the reason, which is that the man of about fifty had the book and the man of about thirty-one of Silling had none, and that both were told at about the second hour what was going to be done and how long it would take and that neither was asked whether he wanted to be there.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that neither of them asked what it was going to be done for, and entered the reason, which is that a man told what is going to take how long and not told what for has been told the two things he is owed and one of them is not a reason.

And the two branches are about three hundred yards apart on the same bank, and the reader of this body put his left hand on the flat of the nearer one at about the fourth inch along the inside of his own forearm, where a mark runs in a person who has been holding, and he held it there and did not take anything out of it, and the fen entered that twice on that afternoon, once at the beginning and once at the end, and that it was a hold and not a draw, and that nothing in this fen is going to come up green because of anything done this afternoon.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the man of about fifty kept the book shut and that the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the minutes out loud, and that the count of the minutes is on the page as a count of minutes and is not a figure about the ground.

And it was five and it was a hold, and the man of about fifty said a word at about four minutes and eleven seconds, and the reader of this body went on for about eleven seconds after the word, and the fen entered both facts and did not improve either.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the man of about fifty is not a licensed man and that the four lines on the wall of that seed house say the stop is to be called out loud by a licensed man and entered by whoever holds the book, and that on the second of this month the stop was called by a man who keeps a book and is not licensed, and that the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the minutes out loud and that saying a number out loud is the oldest stop in this fen and is not the fourth line of that rule, and that a rule with four lines about a draw does not reach a hold, and that a man working to the fourth of four lines when the four lines do not apply is a man who has made a habit and not a practice, and that this holding has one of those and it is not going to get another.

And then it stopped, and the reader of this body took his hand off the wood, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the number again, and nobody said anything.

And what the hold was like is not a reading and is not a panel and it is entered here because the man of about fifty wrote it in the book and read it back and it is his and not this holding's.

**I SAT DOWN. I DID NOT FALL DOWN. I SAT WITH MY BACK AGAINST A STONE ABOUT FOUR INCHES HIGH AND I SAT THERE ABOUT NINE MINUTES BEFORE I COULD STAND UP WITHOUT PUTTING A HAND ON SOMETHING, AND I DID NOT PUT A HAND ON ANYBODY, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING WAS ABOUT NINE FEET OFF AND DID NOT OFFER AND WAS NOT ASKED AND DID NOT MOVE, AND I AM ENTERING THAT HE DID NOT OFFER BECAUSE HE WAS TOLD AT THE SECOND HOUR THAT HE WAS THE SECOND PERSON IN THE ROOM AND THE SECOND PERSON IN THE ROOM IS NOT A SIGHT.**

**MY HAND FROM THE WRIST DOWN HAD A USE ALL THE AFTERNOON. I AM ENTERING THAT BECAUSE ON THE TWENTY-FIRST OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK IT DID NOT, AND BECAUSE A MAN WHO LOSES A WORD AND KEEPS HIS HAND SHOULD WRITE DOWN BOTH THINGS ON THE SAME PAGE, SO THAT SOMEBODY IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS CAN SEE THAT THE LOSS WAS NOT THE HAND.**

**AND THE HEADACHE IS NOT THE MARK AND THE MARK IS NOT THE HEADACHE AND THEY CAME ON THE SAME AFTERNOON AND I AM NOT ADDING THEM TOGETHER.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said on a shoulder above a lead line at about the fifth hour of the afternoon, and entered that about two of the three people there said nothing in a way that was different from the third, and that he did not count them and entered the reason, which is that counting the faces in a thing is how a man starts wanting something out of it.

And then the man of about fifty said the one thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-six, and he said it sitting down, because the reader of this body was still sitting down and the man of about thirty-one of Silling had gone about nine feet off and had not been asked back.

**"I said the word four minutes and eleven seconds into it and I did not know I was going to do that, and the reason I am saying so now instead of keeping it is that I have been sitting here for nine minutes watching a man try to stand up and the only two things I have got are the number of minutes and the fact that I said a word, and the number of minutes is a number of minutes and the word is a word I have said about four times in four years."**

**"I am not a licensed man and I said it anyway, and if that was the wrong thing to do then I did the wrong thing and I will do it again at the same second, and the reason is that the alternative is sitting on a stone writing a number in a book while a man is on the ground."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and entered that the reader of this body had not thought of it and entered the reason, which is that he had been thinking about four lines on a wall of a seed house and not about a man with a book, and that those are the same thing seen from two ends and that only one of the two ends had anybody standing at it.

And then at about the seventh hour of the evening the reader of this body went into the seed house and stood in front of the middle table for about four minutes, and the covenant of six lines is on that table, and it has been there for two months, and anybody in this holding may walk up and read it, and about nine people have read it in two months and one of them is him.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about four minutes, and entered that he did not read it, and entered that the person who keeps the seed house was in that room and did not ask him to and did not ask him not to and did not look at him, and entered that a woman who does not look at a man at a table is doing a harder thing than looking, and that he has not asked her about that either.

And then the cost of it, and the fen entered it, and the name of it is the thing itself and is not a figure about it.

**THE SECOND WORD OF THE FIFTH LINE OF THE COVENANT OF SIX LINES THAT FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS SIGNED IN THE THIRD MONTH, WHICH HE COPIED OUT IN HIS OWN HAND IN THAT MONTH AND WHICH HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK TWICE. IT IS NOT THERE. IT WAS THERE AT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON ON THE SECOND OF THIS MONTH AND IT WAS NOT THERE AT THE FIFTH HOUR AND IT IS NOT THERE NOW, AND IT DID NOT COME BACK IN NINE MINUTES THIS TIME AND NOBODY IN THE THREE PEOPLE ON THAT SHOULDER KNEW THAT IT HAD GONE, BECAUSE NOBODY BUT HIM HAS EVER HAD IT AND HE DID NOT SAY.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he did not go and read it, and entered the reason in his own hand with the hour on it, and the reason is that the covenant is on the middle table in the seed house and anybody in this holding may walk up and read it, and that a man who goes and reads a thing he has lost the first time it happens has established that the second time he will read it, and that a man who reads it the second time stops noticing the third time, and a thing a man stops noticing is gone and does not announce itself, and that this is the first time and he is not going to set a precedent with nine minutes in his own head.

And then the reader of this body entered the counts, and the fen entered that he entered all of them himself and that they are the reason he wrote them down, and that the reason is a man who has just lost a word is a man who will start adding things up to prove that he is a person who keeps a book.

**THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR IS TWO. IT IS TWO. THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FIVE IS ONE AND IT IS HIM. THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO HAVE A ROOTMARK IS TWO, AND ONE OF THEM IS A MAN OF ABOUT SIXTY-EIGHT WITH THREE INCHES ON THE RIGHT FOREARM THAT DO NOT BRANCH, AND THAT MAN IS NOT NAMED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND NOTHING SAID ABOUT THAT ARM ON THE SECOND OF THIS MONTH IS A THIRD ACCOUNT OF IT. THE COUNT OF PERSONS WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR AND WALK OUT OF THE GROUND AFTERWARDS IS ONE AND IT IS HIM. NONE OF THOSE FOUR NUMBERS MOVED AND NONE OF THEM WAS GOING TO AND A HOLD FOR FIVE IS NOT A NEW COLUMN AND I AM NOT GOING TO MAKE IT ONE. AND THE MARK ON THIS ARM IS FOUR INCHES AND BRANCHES TWICE AND IS NOT A SECOND MARK AND IS NOT LONGER, AND THE BURN IS NOT A MARK, AND THE MARK BEING HOT IS NOT A MARK, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THIS ARM ARE FOUR AND NOTHING SAID ABOUT IT TO-DAY IS A FIFTH.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said nothing, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he has written the counts and not the size of anything, and entered that what the hold gave him is not a panel and is not a reading and has no unit, and that it is this and that he read it back once.

**THAT TWO BRANCHES THREE HUNDRED YARDS APART HOLD THE SAME SHAPE, AND THAT THE SHAPE IS THE SHAPE THE ELEVEN BLANK ROWS IN A COPY OF A PUBLIC LEDGER ARE IN, AND THAT A MAN'S OWN BODY SAYING SO IS NOT A RECORD AND IS WORTH NOTHING IN FRONT OF ANYBODY, AND THAT A RECORD IS A THING A ROOM CAN CHECK, AND THAT THIS HOLDING HAS ONE WITH ELEVEN BLANK ROWS IN IT AND A COPY IN THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY'S HAND, AND THAT THE TWO AGREE, AND THAT THE AGREEING IS NOT A PROOF AND I AM NOT GOING TO CALL IT ONE, AND THAT IT IS NOT A CAUSE AND IT IS NOT A NAME AND IT IS NOT A DATE, AND THAT THE ELEVENTH FIGURE AND THE DIRECTION AND THE DURATION FROM THE SEVENTEENTH OF LAST MONTH ARE STILL ON A PAGE WITH NOTHING AGAINST THEM, AND THAT THE SIXTH COLUMN IS STILL BLANK, AND THAT A ROLL GROWS AT A RATE THAT NOBODY IN THIS FEN HAS A FIGURE FOR.**

And then the third of this month came round, a Saturday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the third went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same, and the fourth line did not come round at all and the number of fields that have waited is eighteen and did not move.

**Six hundred and twenty hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the series is now thirty-three figures on thirty-three mornings and not one of the thirty-three is the same as any of the other thirty-two, and entered that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in thirty-one days and the sentence saying so has been written on thirty of thirty-two mornings, and entered that the man of about fifty has written the count of the series down four times in five days and has not asked anybody whether it is a pattern and that the reader of this body did not tell him and is not going to.

And then the last four things of the day, and the fen entered all four and improved none, and none of them is a figure and not one of them is a name.

**THAT I SAID A THING WITH THE REASON FIRST IN A YARD ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS EIGHTY-THREE AND IT DID NOT GO TO EIGHTY-FOUR TO-DAY, AND A MAN WHO DOES NOT SAY A THING WITH THE REASON FIRST IS EITHER BEING CAREFUL OR HAS RUN OUT, AND I DO NOT KNOW WHICH ONE IT IS.**

**THAT THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. A HOLD IS NOT A CONTACT. A WIRE IS NOT A CONTACT. A CRATE IS NOT A CONTACT. A ROAD IS NOT A CONTACT. A THING THAT COMES BACK WIDER THAN IT WENT IN IS A CONTACT, AND THAT IS A FIFTH KIND AND IT IS NOT ON THIS LIST.**

**THAT THE COUNT OF THINGS I HAVE SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR, AND IT IS FOUR, AND IT HAS BEEN FOUR FOR ABOUT TWENTY-SEVEN DAYS, AND IT WAS AVAILABLE TO ME TWICE ON THE SECOND OF THIS MONTH AND I DID NOT TAKE IT EITHER TIME.**

**AND THAT THE FOUR LINES WENT UP THE FOUR-DAY ROAD ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH AND THAT THE ROAD PUTS THEM ON THAT SHELF AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING OF THIS DAY, WHICH IS A FIGURE OFF A ROAD AND NOT OFF A WORD AND THAT NOBODY HAS SAID TO ME; AND THAT A CRATE WILL BE IN THE CART SHED AT THIS NODE ON THE SEVENTH OF THIS MONTH IF THE ROAD ALLOWS, AND THAT NEITHER OF THOSE IS AN ANSWER TO ANYTHING, AND THAT I AM NOT GOING TO WRITE DOWN WHICH OF THE TWO THINGS I HAVE GOT IT IS.**
