# Chapter 266

## The Top Of The Valley

The twenty-third of this month was a Wednesday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-third went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and eleven hundredweight.**

That is the twenty-third figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and the fen entered that the series has not fallen since the twentieth of this month, and entered that a series that rises and falls and never repeats is not a series anybody can work with and is a series a man can be made answerable for, and that those are two different things and this holding has one of them and not the other.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN TWENTY-ONE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO HAS NOW BEEN WRITTEN ON TWENTY OF TWENTY-TWO MORNINGS, THE TWO IT WAS NOT WRITTEN ON BEING THE NINTH AND THE TENTH OF THIS MONTH, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY WROTE ON THE FIFTEENTH OF THIS MONTH THAT HE WOULD STOP WRITING IT IN ABOUT FOUR DAYS WHETHER IT MOVED OR NOT, AND THE FOUR DAYS ARE GONE AND HE HAS NOT STOPPED.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, in one man in a shed.

And then at about the seventh hour of the morning four people went out of the north gate of this holding and up the cart road as far as the ford and off it, and the four of them were the reader of this body, the man of about fifty, the man of about thirty-one of Silling, and an officer of the Office of the Continuity who came up the cart road at about the sixth hour with a leather case and a folded sheet under his arm and who is not named in this book and is not named anywhere.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the reason, which is that a person an office sends and a person an office has written down are two different objects, and that the whole of what came down the road on the twentieth of this month was about the second of those two and not the first, and that a name in this book is a thing an office can use, and that the fen does not know what an office would use it for and has entered that not knowing as the reason and is not going to improve it by guessing.

And the five is the clerk, who stayed at the node with a book and did not go, and the fen entered the five and entered that a five is a four and a one, and that the one was a man with a book who is not on the walk and is on the rota, and that the count of people who have gone up the cart road past the ford and off it in four years is nil and nobody has been asked whether it should be.

The line is a lead line and it was laid before any of the four of them were born and it runs from the top of the valley above this holding down and east for about four miles to a valve house at the bottom of the water, and it is under the turf the whole way, and the fen entered that the office's own line is a different line and is four hundred miles off in one direction and a good deal more in the other, and that the last entry on the book of the office's line is a flushing on the nineteenth of a month seven months back, and that the flush went to a standpipe nine miles off and the water in it was put back into the same ground it came out of, and that the two lines have never been joined on any page in this holding's book.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the reason a second time, and it is that a man who walks a different line in an afternoon and writes it down as the line is a man who has brought a name and a date and a mark down a hill and put them on the wrong object, and that the wrong object is worse than no object because it can be checked.

And the officer was not obstructive and the fen entered that he was not and entered why, and it is that he had a folded sheet under his arm and it was in his own hand and he put it on the flat of the valve box at the top of the valley and let all four of them read it before he said anything about it, and that a man who lets four men read a sheet before he uses it is a man who is not managing a walk.

And then he said the thing, and he gave the reason for saying it before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one, and the count is one because no officer of that office has stood in a yard of this holding before and none has said anything out loud in one.

**"I am going to say what this office is and is not, and I am saying it before we go down, and the reason is that you have all four of us on a line of ground you did not choose and if I do not say it now then every one of you will be guessing at it for four miles and a guess is worse than a sentence."**

**"This office answers for a list. That is all it does. It keeps a list of things on the ground and it keeps a date against the work and it does not keep a figure for every row and it has never pretended to. There is no man behind this list. I am a man behind it, and I am a man who changes, and you have a sheet I gave you on the third of this month with a name on a line at the foot of it, and that name is a name of an office and it does not belong to me and I could not take it off if I wanted to."**

**"And there is a thing I have been told to say and I am going to say it and then I am not going to say it again. The work on this line is on a schedule and the schedule is correct and it was carried out. I did not carry it out and there was nobody from this office within nine days of the date on it. I am here because a body of households that corresponds is a body of households that can be found, and you found yourselves by writing to us, and that is the ordinary consequence of a letter and not a favor and I am not going to stand here and pretend otherwise."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body did not thank him, and entered that the sentence about a body of households that corresponds is a body of households that can be found was written in a yard on the eighteenth of this month at about the seventh hour of the evening in the reader of this body's own hand, and that it has come back up the road inside a fortnight in a leather case with a man carrying it, and that the fen entered that a man's own sentence being used against him is not a defeat and is a cost, and that the cost was named out loud by the man who caused it before anybody had walked a mile.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-one, and he said it at the bottom valve box and he said it to the officer and not to the three men he walks with.

**"You have opened both these boxes and I have watched you look at the bolts before you touched anything and I have entered that, and I am going to say one thing about it and then I am going to stop talking to you for the rest of the week, and the reason is that a man who has a sheet under his arm and four men on his ground is a man who will be asked in about four years whether he opened anything, and I am the man who will have to answer it, because there is nobody else here."**

**"So here is the answer while all four of us can hear it. Both boxes were open when we got here. I have not seen them shut. I have no figure for when they were last opened and neither do you, and if it turns out in about four years that one of these was opened on a day with a name on it, then the name is going to be on the box and not on the four of us, and I would rather it was on the box. That is the whole of what I have and it is about eleven seconds of saying it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and entered that the officer wrote in his own book after he had said it and read none of it out and showed it to nobody, and that a man who writes a thing down and does not read it out is making a record and not making a case, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is six and did not move this afternoon, and that this was said to a stranger on a bank and not in a yard, and that it is the same count and the same man.

And the reader of this body gave the reason for his own answer before he gave the answer, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-four, and the answer was four sentences long and he said it once.

**"You have said it and I am going to answer one part of it and not the other, and the reason I am answering one part is that the other part is about four hundred miles of lead and I have not walked it and I am not going to tell this holding what is on it."**

**"You were sent because we wrote to you. That is a fact and I am not going to stand here and be angry about it in front of three men, because a man who is angry about his own letter in front of a stranger has not understood his own letter. What I have understood is that a body with no charter and no roll and no seal can now be found, and that is a thing this holding did on purpose on the eighteenth of this month at fourpence a sheet, and I have entered that it was on purpose and I am not taking it back."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said on the top of a valley by four men and an officer of an office.

And then they went down, and it took the rest of the morning and about an hour of the afternoon, and four miles with two stops, and the fen entered all of it and improved none of it.

**THE WALK. From the top of the valley down to the valve house at the bottom of the water, four miles, four people, on the twenty-third of this month, from about the seventh hour of the morning to about the first hour of the afternoon. THE LINE IS UNDER THE TURF THE WHOLE WAY AND COMES UP INTO TWO VALVE BOXES, AND BOTH BOXES WERE OPEN, AND BOTH WERE CLEAN, AND NEITHER BOX HAD BEEN OPENED SINCE THE TURF ON IT WAS PUT DOWN. THERE ARE ELEVEN PLACES IN FOUR MILES WHERE THE GROUND OVER THE LINE HAS BEEN OPENED AND MADE GOOD AGAIN, AND THE ELEVEN ARE NOT EVENLY SPACED AND THE FEN DID NOT WORK OUT WHY THEY ARE NOT EVENLY SPACED AND DID NOT ASK. AT THE NINTH OF THE ELEVEN, ON THE BANK, THERE IS A BRANCH. THE OTHER TEN PLACES ARE GROUND AND NOT WOOD.**

And the last thing of the day is this and it is in his own hand and it is not a figure and it is the only thing he wrote that evening.

**A SCAR IS A PLACE WHERE LIVING ROOTWOOD HAS BEEN CUT BACK AND HAS HEALED. A BREAK IS A PLACE WHERE IT HAS NOT. A REDUCTION IS A FIGURE THAT IS SMALLER THAN THE FIGURE BEFORE IT ON THE SAME INSTRUMENT IN THE SAME PLACE. I HAVE ONE OF THE FIRST TWO ON THE PAGE AND NOT ONE OF THE THIRD, AND I AM GOING TO FIND OUT TO-MORROW WHETHER THE INSTRUMENT FOR THE THIRD IS A THING A MAN CAN CARRY IN A BAG, AND I AM NOT GOING TO WRITE THE SIZE OF THAT REDUCTION DOWN UNTIL SOMEBODY HAS PUT A FIGURE ON THE SAME PIECE OF WOOD TWICE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the reader of this body did not sleep and entered the reason, which is that a man who has written down eleven places where the ground was opened and made good and has not written down what was done at any of them has written down the shape of a question and a question he has been in the middle of walking down a valley for six hours is a question he is going to want answered by the second hour of the morning.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-third of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a walk, and not by the reader of this body, who was on the shoulder above the line until the light went: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND ELEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-THIRD, WHICH IS THE TWENTY-THIRD FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND THE SERIES HAS NOT FALLEN SINCE THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN TWENTY-ONE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO HAS NOW BEEN WRITTEN ON TWENTY OF TWENTY-TWO MORNINGS, THE TWO IT WAS NOT WRITTEN ON BEING THE NINTH AND THE TENTH OF THIS MONTH, AND THE MAN WHO WROTE ON THE FIFTEENTH OF THIS MONTH THAT HE WOULD STOP IN ABOUT FOUR DAYS HAS NOT STOPPED. FOUR PEOPLE WALKED THE LOCAL LEAD LINE FROM THE TOP OF THE VALLEY DOWN TO THE VALVE HOUSE AT THE BOTTOM OF THE WATER, FOUR MILES, FROM ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE MORNING TO ABOUT THE FIRST HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON. THEY WERE THE READER OF THIS BODY, THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING, AND AN OFFICER OF THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY WHO IS NOT NAMED IN THIS BOOK AND IS NOT NAMED ANYWHERE. THE CLERK STAYED AT THE NODE WITH A BOOK. THE LINE IS UNDER THE TURF AND COMES UP INTO TWO VALVE BOXES AND BOTH WERE OPEN AND BOTH WERE CLEAN. THERE ARE ELEVEN PLACES IN FOUR MILES WHERE THE GROUND OVER THE LINE HAS BEEN OPENED AND MADE GOOD AGAIN, AND THE ELEVEN ARE NOT EVENLY SPACED. AT THE NINTH OF THE ELEVEN THERE IS A BRANCH, AND AT THE OTHER TEN PLACES THERE IS GROUND AND NOT WOOD. THE OFFICER OF THAT OFFICE SAID OUT LOUD, IN ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE, THAT HE WAS A MAN BEHIND A LIST, THAT THE LIST IS NOT A MAN, THAT THE WORK ON THAT LIST IS ON A SCHEDULE AND THE SCHEDULE IS CORRECT AND WAS CARRIED OUT, AND THAT HE HAD NOT CARRIED IT OUT AND THERE HAD BEEN NOBODY FROM THAT OFFICE WITHIN NINE DAYS OF THE DATE ON IT, AND THAT HE WAS HERE BECAUSE A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS THAT CORRESPONDS IS A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS THAT CAN BE FOUND. HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE OFFICE'S OWN LINE IS A DIFFERENT LINE AND IS FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF IN ONE DIRECTION AND A GOOD DEAL MORE IN THE OTHER, AND THE LAST ENTRY ON THE BOOK OF THAT LINE IS A FLUSHING ON THE NINETEENTH OF A MONTH SEVEN MONTHS BACK, AND THIS HOLDING HAS NEVER WALKED IT. THE READER OF THIS BODY ANSWERED ONE PART OF WHAT THE OFFICER SAID AND DID NOT ANSWER THE OTHER PART AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY AND NOTHING TENDER WAS SAID ON THE TOP OF A VALLEY.**
