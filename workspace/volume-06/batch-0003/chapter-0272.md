# Chapter 272

## What A Page Of A Page Is

The twenty-ninth of this month was a Tuesday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-ninth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and seventeen hundredweight.**

That is the twenty-ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and the series has fallen three times and risen four times in the seven days since the twenty-third, and the four days of walking the line are over, and four of the eleven places on this line are the third, the seventh, the ninth and the eleventh, and the seven this holding cannot get to are not going to be got to.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the officer of the Office of the Continuity went off up the cart road at about the eighth hour of the morning on the twenty-seventh with the ledger under his arm, and that the office's copy is the office's and the copy in the man of about fifty's hand is this holding's, and that the two are not the same document and both of them are entered.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon the reader of this body went into the cart shed at the node to fetch the crate, and the crate was there, and the fen entered that it was there before he asked anybody where it was, and that this holding has not known for about four days that there was a crate in that shed.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the reason, which is that a man who sends a man for a thing he has had in his own shed for four days has not sent a man for the thing, he has sent a man to find out whether he was wrong, and that he was wrong, and entered the hour, and entered that the man of about fifty said on the fifteenth of this month that a crate is a thing a man pays for and a man loses, and was not thanked then either.

And then the crate was opened, and it is empty, and it has been empty since the twenty-second of a month two months back.

**THE CRATE IS THE CRATE THE BOOK CAME DOWN IN ON THE TWENTIETH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK AND WENT BACK UP IN ON THE TWENTY-SECOND OF THAT MONTH AND CAME DOWN AGAIN, EMPTY, ON THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH WITH THE LID ON. THE RECEIPT IS TIED TO THE HANDLE IN A LOOP OF STRING AND IS ONE SHILLING AND FOURPENCE, AND THE MAN OF THE ROAD DID NOT CHARGE FOR THE LID AND WROTE NOTHING UNDER THE LID, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY READ THE RECEIPT INTO THIS HOLDING'S BOOK ON THE TWENTIETH. THE OILCLOTH IS IN IT AND FOLDED BACK, AND THERE IS NO LOCK ON IT AND THERE IS A HASP, AND THE HASP IS NOT FASTENED, AND THE KEY IS IN IT, AND THE KEY WAS ON A RING FOR ELEVEN YEARS AND CAME OFF THE RING AND WENT INTO A CRATE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that a key in an empty crate is a thing four people looked at and that none of them said anything about, and entered the reason, which is that the book is not in this holding and the key is, and that a key is not the thing it opens and is not a copy of it and is not a promise about it.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-three, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"You went to send for a thing and it was in your own shed and it has been in your own shed since the twenty-second of a month two months back, and the reason I am going to say one thing about that is that the book went up that road at the seventh hour of a morning on a Monday with a hired man and it did not come down."**

**"Not because anybody took it off the man. Because nobody wrote to anybody to ask for it to come down. I had the receipt for the crate and I had a lid and I had a road and I did not have a line asking for the thing itself, and a crate coming back empty is a crate coming back empty and not a man forgetting, and I have had four days with a key in a shed and I have not said one word about it to anybody until now, and the reason I am saying it now is that you are about to write four lines and go up the same road, and if you go up it without writing in your own book that the book is not here, then in about four years there will be two records of this and one of them will be wrong."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is **seven and it is eight**, and that he counted it in a book and not in that yard in about nine seconds, and that the movement is not his and never has been, and that the eight are hers.

And then the reader of this body wrote the four lines, in his own hand, on his own paper, in the cart shed, in about four people, at about the sixth hour of the evening, and the man of about fifty read them back and did not improve them, and they went up the four-day road at about the seventh hour of the evening in the same leather bag as everything else that has arrived at this holding for four years, given at the change of horses to a man hired for the road, and the road is four days up and four days down.

> **FOUR LINES, in the hand of the reader of this body, on one sheet of his own paper, written at about the sixth hour of the evening of the twenty-ninth of this month in about four people at the node, read back by the man of about fifty, and given at the change of horses at about the seventh hour of the evening to a man hired for the four-day road, entered whole:**
>
> **ONE. I AM ASKING FOR THE BOOK. IT IS IN OILCLOTH IN A CRATE AND THE CRATE AND THE OILCLOTH AND THE KEY ARE IN A CART SHED AT THIS HOLDING AND THE BOOK IS NOT, AND IT WENT UP YOUR ROAD ON THE TWENTY-SECOND OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK AND CAME DOWN EMPTY ON THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH.**
>
> **TWO. IF IT COMES DOWN IN THE CRATE, SEND IT DOWN IN THE CRATE. THE CRATE IS HERE. I AM NOT BUYING A CRATE.**
>
> **THREE. NOBODY IS TO COME UP THE ROAD WITH IT. FOUR DAYS DOWN IS FOUR DAYS AND A MAN WHO CARRIES A BOOK DOWN A ROAD IS A MAN WHO IS LATE BY FOUR DAYS FOR A THING THAT IS NOT A BOOK, AND I AM NOT ASKING ANYBODY TO BE LATE.**
>
> **FOUR. NO NAME IS ON THIS AND NOBODY IS TO PUT ONE ON IT, AND THAT IS A STATEMENT ABOUT ME AND NOT AN INSTRUCTION TO ANYBODY.**
>
> **AND UNDER THE FOUR, IN THE SAME HAND, ON THE SAME SHEET, WHICH IS THE FIFTH LINE AND IS NOT PART OF THE FOUR: I AM NOT ASKING FOR ANY PAGE OF IT TO BE WRITTEN OUT FOR ME. THERE ARE ELEVEN PAGES IN IT AND MOST OF THE SURVEY PAGES ARE GONE AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS EVER KNOWN WHERE THEY WENT, AND THE MOMENT A PERSON ASKS FOR A MISSING PAGE THEY HAVE ASKED A PERSON TO REMEMBER SOMETHING FOR THEM, AND A THING REMEMBERED FOR A PERSON IS NOT A PAGE. SEND ME WHAT IS ON THE BOARD AND I WILL DO WITHOUT THE REST.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the man of about fifty read all five back and read the fifth back twice, and that the count of the times he has read a line back twice in this holding's book is four and that three of the four are his.

And then the cost of it, entered whole, standing up, in about nine people, in the yard, and the reader of this body read it out and the man of about fifty wrote it down as it was read and read it back.

**THE WAGE IS TWO SHILLINGS AND SIXPENCE UP AND TWO SHILLINGS AND SIXPENCE DOWN, WHICH IS FIVE SHILLINGS, AND THERE IS NO CRATE, BECAUSE THERE IS A CRATE. THE ROAD IS FOUR DAYS UP AND FOUR DAYS DOWN. THE HIRE IS A LINE OF ITS OWN AND DOES NOT GO INTO THE COMPOST LINE AND DOES NOT GO AGAINST EIGHT PENCE OF WIRE. THE BOX IN THIS SHED HELD NINE POUNDS AND THIRTEEN SHILLINGS AND EIGHT PENCE ON THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH AND IT HOLDS NINE POUNDS AND EIGHT SHILLINGS, AND THE EIGHT PENCE IS THE WIRE OFF THE TWENTY-FOURTH AND THE FIVE SHILLINGS IS TONIGHT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the use log stands at fifteen lines and a book is not a contact and a road is not a contact and a key in a shed is not a contact, and that the count of things written not known against is six and did not move this evening, and that the fifth of the six is the date of the next window and the sixth is the figure the third launder carried before the eleventh of the first month, and that the unit of none of the six is known to him either and that asking for a book is not going to supply the unit of any of them.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-one, and he said it in about nine people, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say what a page of a page is and I am going to say it once in nine people and not in a yard, and the reason is that I have written the word *ledger* in this book on the seventh of this month and had a man bring me one on the twenty-seventh, and a man who has been given two documents in twenty days is a man who is beginning to be pleased, and a man who is pleased stops asking which of the two he is being handed."**

**"The book is a page somebody wrote down out of an instrument. The thing the office sent me on the twentieth is a page somebody wrote down out of a kind of thing, and it is correct, and it says no branch, and it was not asked which branch. A record in a book four hundred miles off, in a room with a counter, kept by a person whose name is not in this book, is a record and not the thing. Three objects and I have had all three of them inside nine days and I could not tell you the difference between them from memory, and that is the whole of what has gone wrong in this holding's book and it is not anybody's fault and it is mine."**

**"A page of a page is the only one of the three that anybody can copy. And the copy is the one that is wrong, because the copying is where a branch and a kind of thing stop being different things."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nothing tender was said and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this evening and that this was not a yard.

And then the last two things of the day, and both of them are his, and both of them are in his own book, and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE PANEL IS NOT PRINTED. THE BOOK IS FOUR HUNDRED MILES AND FOUR DAYS OFF IN A ROOM WITH A HASP, IT IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING, IT IS NOT IN THIS SHED, AND IT WILL NOT BE IN THIS SHED UNTIL ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING OF THE SEVENTH OF NEXT MONTH IF THE ROAD ALLOWS, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING IS GOING TO SEE IT BEFORE THEN. THE COUNT OF ITS USES IS ONE AND IT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN AND IT IS NOT A SECOND READING OF THE PULSE AT THE SILL AND IT IS NOT THE WITNESS BEAD, AND IT IS NOT A PANEL.**

**AND THE CHEAPEST INSTRUMENT IN THIS HOLDING IS THE BEAD, AND I AM NOT GOING TO USE IT FOR THIS. A BEAD READS WHAT A NODE HAS RECENTLY HAD DONE TO IT. WHAT I WOULD WANT IT FOR IS A BRANCH, AND A BRANCH IS NOT A PAGE, AND AN INSTRUMENT THAT READS A PAGE IS NOT A THING ANYBODY IN THIS FEN OWNS, AND I HAVE ASKED NOBODY FOR ONE, BECAUSE A MAN WHO ASKS FOR AN INSTRUMENT HE HAS NEVER SEEN IS A MAN WHO IS ABOUT TO TRUST SOMEBODY ELSE'S READING, AND THIS WEEK HAS BEEN ABOUT A RECORD.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-ninth of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a hire and of a thing that is not here, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the yard and did not watch it go: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-NINTH, WHICH IS THE TWENTY-NINTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND THE SERIES HAS FALLEN THREE TIMES AND RISEN FOUR TIMES IN THE SEVEN DAYS SINCE THE TWENTY-THIRD. THE FOUR DAYS OF WALKING THE LOCAL LINE ARE OVER AND FOUR OF THE ELEVEN PLACES ON IT HAVE BEEN FOUND, BEING THE THIRD, THE SEVENTH, THE NINTH AND THE ELEVENTH, AND SEVEN OF THE ELEVEN ARE NOT ON THIS LINE. THE CRATE THE BOOK CAME DOWN IN IS IN THE CART SHED AND IT IS EMPTY AND IT HAS BEEN SINCE THE TWENTY-SECOND OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK, AND THE RECEIPT ON IT IS ONE SHILLING AND FOURPENCE, AND THE KEY IS IN IT, AND THE BOOK IS NOT. FOUR LINES WERE WRITTEN IN THE READER OF THIS BODY'S OWN HAND AND READ BACK AND WERE GIVEN AT THE CHANGE OF HORSES AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING TO A MAN HIRED FOR THE FOUR-DAY ROAD, AND THEY ASK FOR THE BOOK AND THEY NAME NO PERSON, AND THERE IS NO CRATE TO BUY BECAUSE THERE IS A CRATE, AND UNDER THE FOUR IS A FIFTH LINE SAYING THAT HE IS NOT ASKING FOR ANY PAGE OF IT TO BE WRITTEN OUT FOR HIM. THE WAGE IS FIVE SHILLINGS. THE BOX HELD NINE POUNDS AND THIRTEEN SHILLINGS AND EIGHT PENCE ON THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH AND HOLDS NINE POUNDS AND EIGHT SHILLINGS. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND A BOOK IS NOT A CONTACT AND A ROAD IS NOT A CONTACT AND A KEY IN A SHED IS NOT A CONTACT. THE PANEL IS NOT PRINTED AND THE BOOK IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
