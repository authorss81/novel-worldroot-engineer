# Chapter 288

## A Condition, In A Room, With Somebody In It Who Is Neither Of Them

The seventeenth of this month was a Saturday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the seventeenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-three hundredweight.**

That is the forty-seventh figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and it is the first fall since the thirteenth of this month, and in the fourteen days since the third of this month the series has fallen four times and risen ten times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in forty-five days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the forty-fourth morning out of forty-six.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about eleven people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the sheet with the real seal on it was on the middle table, and the copy of the ledger was on the middle table under it, and the form with four lines on it was on the middle table under that, and the fen entered the three documents in a stack and entered that about four people looked at the stack for a while and nobody said anything about the order they were in.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and that the man of about fifty did not rearrange the stack, and that a bookkeeper who moves a document to make an order is a bookkeeper who has decided what the order means, and that this holding has three documents on a table that say three different things about one line and the order they are in is the order they arrived in.

And then Tova Reed, who is a seedwright of a trust and who came back up the fen road on the evening of the fourteenth of this month after ten days out in twenty and was not sent for, said the thing that was hers, and she gave the reason before she said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say a word and then I am going to say what it means before anybody uses it, and the reason is that I have been in this holding three days and I have heard a word go round it twice this month with no definition on it, and a word nobody has defined is a word two people are still arguing about in about four years."**

**"The word is anchor. And it does not mean what it is supposed to mean here. An anchor is not the person who goes first and is not the person who is the strongest and is not the person somebody comes to when he cannot hold a thing. An anchor is the person who decides when somebody else stops. That is the whole of it and there is nothing else in it, and the person who decides when somebody else stops is the only one of those three who cannot be asked afterward whether it was right."**

**"I am not going to be that. I have said no to being that once in my life and I said it in about four minutes to nine people and nobody has mentioned it since and I am not going to mention it now. What I am going to do is the other thing and I am going to say it in a room and in front of somebody who is neither of you, and the reason is that a condition said to two people is a thing two people remember, and a condition said anywhere else is a thing one person carries."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the man of about fifty was in that room and was not told the condition, and entered the reason for that, which is that a third person who knows the condition is a person who can be asked whether it was kept, and that a room of eleven that all know the condition is a room that will spend the whole two days watching the two of them instead of the box.

And then she said the condition itself, and the fen entered it whole, and improved none of it.

**THE CONDITION IS THIS AND IT IS ONE SENTENCE AND SHE SAID IT ONCE. IF EITHER OF THEM STOPS, THE OTHER ONE TAKES THE OTHER ONE OUT. NOT THE SEED. THE OTHER ONE. AND SHE IS NOT GOING TO BE THE PERSON WHO DECIDES THAT AT THE TIME, BECAUSE THE PERSON WHO HAS BEEN IN THE GROUND CANNOT BE ASKED, AND SHE HAS SAID THAT BEFORE SHE WAS ASKED AND NOT AFTER. THE STOP WORD IS HERS AND IT IS THE SAME WORD SHE HAS HAD SINCE THE SEVENTEENTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK, AND IT IS STILL A WORD NOBODY ELSE IN THIS HOLDING KNOWS, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT ASK FOR IT THIS AFTERNOON AND DID NOT KNOW IT THIS AFTERNOON AND IS NOT GOING TO KNOW IT BEFORE THE DOOR IS SHUT. IT HAS NOW BEEN SAID TWICE IN THE WHOLE OF THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS TWO.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the man of about fifty was in that room and was told that a condition existed and was not told what it was, and that he wrote the words *a condition was stated* and the hour on it in his own book and nothing under those words, and that the four ruled lines under them are the same four ruled lines he keeps under the sixth column of the office's ledger and under the survey pages that are not in the crate, and that a man who keeps an empty place visible for other people's records keeps one for his own.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-six, and he said it in about eleven people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a rule on the wall of this room with four lines on it that I have never once used and I am going to read it out and then say that I am not going to use it tomorrow, and the reason is that a rule that has been read out in a room the night before a thing happens is a rule somebody expects to be called, and I would rather it was a rule in the book than a promise in a man's mouth about tomorrow."**

**"One. The draw is not begun on ground whose return is not carrying. Two. It is to be stopped at four feet. Three. The stop is to be called out loud by a licensed man and entered by whoever holds the book. Four. The person who calls the stop is not the person doing the work. Four lines and every one of them is about a draw, and there is no fifth line and there never has been one, and on the second of this month I held a pattern for five on a shoulder and it was not a draw and the four lines did not reach it and I said so out loud in that yard and the fen entered that a man working to the fourth of four lines when the four lines do not apply is a man who has made a habit and not a practice."**

**"Tomorrow is not a draw. There is nothing to begin and nothing at four feet and nobody in this fen is going to hold anything. I have read those four lines out in this holding's book more times than I can count and I have not used them once in this month's work, and the count of the times that rule has been named in this month's work and the one before it is now six and the count of the times it has been used is not at all. I am not going to use it tomorrow and I am not going to be the reason it was used, and if a man in about four years wants to know why a rule with four lines about stopping was not used on the morning two people walked out of a door, the answer is that it is four lines about a draw."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and that she was not thanked, and that nobody in about eleven people said the word anchor again, and entered the reason for entering that, which is that the word had been used in this holding twice this month with no definition on it by people who had never met each other, and that a word which is used and not defined and then defined once and not used again is a word that has been cleaned, and that cleaning a word is not the same as having it.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-five, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have kept four books for four years and I am going to say what is going in the one I keep for the office, and the reason is that eleven people have read a paragraph today and four of them read a date and seven of them read a word, and both of those are in a book and only one of them is the same for everybody, and I do not want a page in about four years that has got two different mornings on it."**

**"I am entering the whole of it. Line 41, the twentieth, the window, the seal, the device, the fact that the rider was not stopped. I am entering the word emptied as a word on a sheet and not as a finding. And I am entering that the sheet is one page and there is nothing on the back of it, and that I have turned it over in front of eleven people, because in about four years somebody is going to find the sheet and turn it over and see nothing, and a page with nothing on the back of it looks like a page somebody did not finish."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he had read the sheet and put it down face up, and that a man who puts a document face up has looked at the front of it and decided the front is the whole of it, and that the man of about fifty has been turning sheets over for four years because four years ago somebody did not.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**ELEVEN PEOPLE WERE IN THIS ROOM AND THREE THINGS ARE ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND THE ORDER THEY ARE IN IS THE ORDER THEY ARRIVED IN, AND I HAVE NOT ASKED FOR THE STACK TO BE REARRANGED AND NOBODY HAS ASKED ME TO, AND A ROOM THAT REARRANGES ITS OWN EVIDENCE IS A ROOM THAT HAS DECIDED WHICH DOCUMENT IS THE PARENT.**

**AND A SEEDWRIGHT HAS DEFINED A WORD IN FRONT OF ELEVEN PEOPLE BEFORE ANYBODY USED IT AGAIN, AND HAS STATED ONE CONDITION, AND HAS LEFT ONE MAN IN THE ROOM WHO KNOWS A CONDITION EXISTS AND NOT WHAT IT IS, AND HE WROTE TWO WORDS AND FOUR RULED LINES UNDER THEM, AND NOBODY THANKED HER, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES A WORD HAS BEEN USED IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT DEFINED IS TWO AND NEITHER OF THEM WAS HERS.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the seventeenth of this month, in about eleven people, at this holding, on the standing of a condition, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and did not know the stop word and did not ask: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE SEVENTEENTH, WHICH IS THE FORTY-SEVENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IT IS THE FIRST FALL SINCE THE THIRTEENTH OF THIS MONTH, AND IN FOURTEEN DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN TEN TIMES AND FALLEN FOUR TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FORTY-FIVE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FORTY-FOURTH MORNING OUT OF FORTY-SIX. ELEVEN PEOPLE WERE IN THE SEED HOUSE WITH THE DOOR SHUT AND THREE DOCUMENTS WERE ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IN THE ORDER THEY ARRIVED IN AND NOBODY REARRANGED THEM. TOVA REED, WHO CAME UP THE FEN ROAD ON THE EVENING OF THE FOURTEENTH AND WAS NOT SENT FOR, DEFINED THE WORD ANCHOR OUT LOUD, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AS THE PERSON WHO DECIDES WHEN SOMEBODY ELSE STOPS, AND SAID SHE IS NOT GOING TO BE THAT, AND THEN STATED ONE CONDITION IN FRONT OF THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO WAS NOT TOLD WHAT IT WAS AND ENTERED THE WORDS A CONDITION WAS STATED AND THE HOUR, AND FOUR RULED LINES UNDER THEM, AND NOTHING UNDER THOSE. THE CONDITION IS THAT IF EITHER OF THEM STOPS, THE OTHER ONE TAKES THE OTHER ONE OUT, AND NOT THE SEED, AND SHE IS NOT THE PERSON WHO DECIDES THAT AT THE TIME BECAUSE THE PERSON WHO HAS BEEN IN THE GROUND CANNOT BE ASKED, AND SHE SAID THAT BEFORE SHE WAS ASKED. THE STOP WORD IS HERS AND IS THE SAME WORD SHE HAS HAD SINCE THE SEVENTEENTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK AND NOBODY ELSE IN THIS HOLDING KNOWS IT, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT ASK FOR IT AND DID NOT KNOW IT AND IS NOT GOING TO KNOW IT BEFORE THE DOOR IS SHUT. IT HAS NOW BEEN SAID TWICE IN THE WHOLE OF THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS TWO. THE READER OF THIS BODY READ OUT THE STANDING RULE ABOUT THE ENGINEER OF RECORD IN FULL, FOUR LINES, EVERY ONE OF THEM ABOUT A DRAW, AND SAID THAT TOMORROW IS NOT A DRAW, AND THAT THE COUNT OF THE TIMES THAT RULE HAS BEEN NAMED OUT LOUD TO A ROOM SINCE IT WAS WRITTEN IS NOW SIX AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES IT HAS BEEN USED IS NOT AT ALL, AND THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO USE IT. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID HE WOULD ENTER THE WHOLE OF THE SHEET, BEING LINE 41, THE TWENTIETH, THE WINDOW, THE SEAL, THE DEVICE AND THE FACT THAT THE RIDER WAS NOT STOPPED, AND THE WORD EMPTIED AS A WORD ON A SHEET AND NOT AS A FINDING, AND THAT HE TURNED THE SHEET OVER IN FRONT OF ELEVEN PEOPLE BECAUSE A PAGE WITH NOTHING ON THE BACK OF IT LOOKS LIKE A PAGE SOMEBODY DID NOT FINISH. HE WAS NOT THANKED. NOBODY WAS THANKED. NOBODY IN THAT ROOM SAID THE WORD ANCHOR AGAIN.**
