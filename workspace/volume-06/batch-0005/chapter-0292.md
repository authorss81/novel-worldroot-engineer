# Chapter 292

## The Memorandum

The twenty-second of this month was a Thursday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **twenty-three**, and entered that all twenty-three of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **fifty-two mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty-three not**, and that twenty-nine and twenty-three are fifty-two, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

And the figure for the twenty-second went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and forty hundredweight.**

That is the fifty-second figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the nineteen days since the third of this month the series has risen fourteen times and fallen five times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in fifty days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the forty-ninth morning out of fifty-one.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the fourth hour of the afternoon a second rider came up the fen road on the ordinary post and gave a leather envelope into the hand of the person who keeps the seed house and went on up the road, and nobody stopped him, and the envelope was opened in about eleven minutes in about nine people and the same wax was on it and the same device, and the fen entered that a man who has been handed a second sealed thing in a week by the same road stops noticing the seal, and that stopping to notice a seal is the first thing that has gone wrong in this holding all year and that nobody has noticed it go wrong.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered the whole of the document and improved none of it, and entered that it is dated the twentieth of this month at an office four hundred miles off, and that it came down the same road in three days where a four-day road takes four, and that the fen entered that too and that the road is the road.

**THE MEMORANDUM IS ONE PAGE AND IT IS PRINTED AND IT IS SIGNED AT THE FOOT IN INK BY CHANCELLOR IONA VEY, WHOSE NAME IS ON IT AND WHO IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING AND HAS NOT BEEN IN THIS HOLDING. IT NAMES AN OFFICE AND NOT AN OFFICER. IT NAMES A DISTRICT AND A LINE AND IT NAMES THE LINE. IT SAYS THAT ROOT STEWARDSHIP ON THAT LINE IN THAT DISTRICT IS PLACED UNDER PROTECTION FROM THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH. IT SAYS THAT A PERSON IS APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THE LINE AND IT DOES NOT NAME THE PERSON. IT SAYS THE APPOINTED PERSON IS NOT A STEWARD. IT SAYS PROTECTION IS NOT STEWARDSHIP AND THAT THE TWO ARE NOT THE SAME OFFICE AND DO NOT SIT IN THE SAME ROOM. AND IT SAYS THAT NO SEQUENCE, AND NO PART CONTAINING A SEQUENCE, AND NO CRADLE, ON THAT LINE OR OFF IT, IS TO BE REMOVED, BROKEN, OPENED OR SET AGAINST ANY VALVE BY ANY PERSON WITHOUT A WRITTEN INSTRUMENT OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF IT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody in that room read the last line out loud twice and that about four people read it in their own time out of the corner of their eye, and that the sentence is the only one on that page that is about a thing anybody in the room has touched, and that it was the second sentence from the foot, and that it is the eleventh line on a one-page document and not the first, and entered the reason for entering that, which is that an office which puts the sentence about a part in the eleventh line has put nine lines in front of it on purpose, and that a man who reads a document from the top has read a document about a district and a man who reads a document from the bottom has read a document about his own table.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-seven, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have entered a sheet with a real seal on it on Wednesday of last week and I am going to enter that one too, and I am going to enter it in the same way, and the reason is that somebody in about four years is going to put the two documents side by side and I am not going to be the one who has put them in a different order to make Wednesday look better than Thursday."**

**"So. Line 41, the twentieth, the window, the seal, the device, the rider not stopped, and now root stewardship on that line under protection from the twentieth, appointed person not named, protection not stewardship, and the eleventh line. And the two of those are the same day. The sheet that emptied a cradle is dated the fifteenth and says it for the twentieth. The memorandum is dated the twentieth and says protection from the twentieth. Both are correct. Both are signed by somebody entitled to sign them. And one of them is about a valley and one of them is about eleven lines on a page, and about nine people in this room read both in about nine minutes and did not see it, and I have seen it, and the reason I have seen it is that I keep a column for that schedule and I had already written the fifteenth in it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he had been thinking about a cloth on a table for two days, and that a man thinking about a table is a man who has already decided the table is the middle of the thing, and that a bookkeeper thinking about a column is a man who has not.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eleven, and nobody improved it.

**"I am speaking for nobody but myself and I am going to say one thing and then I am not going to say anything about that paper, and the reason is that there is a sentence in it about a thing nobody is to set against a valve without a piece of paper, and I would like it in the record that the first time anybody in this holding has heard my name near the word valve was on Tuesday and not before, and I have not read that page and I am not going to."**

**"I decided about my nine acres on the twenty-sixth of last month. They come off before the middle of next month. The carted part goes to my three animals and the rest is left where it grew and nobody takes it and I am putting no row on it this season. That is still the decision and this piece of paper has not changed it and was not in the room when I made it, and a document that arrives after a woman has decided something is not a reason to decide it again."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the count of the times she has said a thing in this holding unasked is two and did not move this afternoon, and that the count of the questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and did not move, and that a man who is told nothing this afternoon by a woman in a room has been told the correct thing, and that a thing under a cloth on a table nine feet off her is not a thing anybody can be asked about from three feet.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it, and the fen entered the hundred and did not improve it either.

**"I am going to say the sentence in my own mouth that nobody has said in this room this afternoon, and I am going to say it before anybody else does, and the reason is that a thing under a cloth on a table in the middle of a seed house is now, as of the twentieth of this month, a thing that may not be set against a valve by anybody without a piece of paper from an office four hundred miles off, and that is the whole of what happened this week, and I have been waiting three days for a room to say it and no room has."**

**"It is not a defeat and I am not going to call it one, and here is the difference in one sentence. Three days ago I had a key and a reason not to use it, and the reason was a woman nine hundred yards off who has never been asked a question by me in two months. Today I have a key and a document, and the document does not touch the woman and does not touch her ground and does not name this holding, and the reason is still the woman and not the document, and I would like that in the record in those words because in about four years somebody will say the paper did it and the paper did not do it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said in that room on the twenty-second of this month, and entered that the form with four lines on it was on the middle table under the memorandum and that the line one carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight in the hand of a man who spoke for one body of households and that lines two, three and four are blank and that the line the question is on is entered and not filled, and that a document with a real seal on it came down the fen road and did not put a figure on any of them.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**A MEMORANDUM OF PROTECTION CAME DOWN THE ORDINARY POST IN THE AFTERNOON AND IT IS DATED THE TWENTIETH AT AN OFFICE FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF AND IT IS CORRECT IN EVERY PARTICULAR AND IT NAMES NO PERSON AND IT DID NOT NAME THIS HOLDING, AND THIS HOLDING CANNOT TELL WHETHER IT WAS NOT NAMED BECAUSE IT IS NOT ON THE LIST OR BECAUSE IT IS, AND THAT IS A THING THIS HOLDING HAS NOW GOT AND IS NOT GOING TO ANSWER, AND A THING A HOLDING CANNOT TELL IS A THING IT MUST CARRY.**

**AND THE PROTECTION IS NOT STEWARDSHIP, AND THE APPOINTED PERSON IS NOT A STEWARD, AND THE OFFICE SAYS BOTH OF THOSE IN PRINT, AND NEITHER OF THEM IS A COMFORT, AND BOTH OF THEM ARE TRUE, AND A DOCUMENT THAT IS BOTH COMFORTING AND UNCOMFORTING IS A DOCUMENT AND NOT A POLICY, AND THE ONLY THING ON THAT PAGE THAT IS ABOUT ANYTHING IN THIS ROOM IS THE ELEVENTH LINE, AND IT IS THE ELEVENTH LINE, AND A THING UNDER A CLOTH ON A TABLE IS STILL A THING UNDER A CLOTH ON A TABLE ON SATURDAY MORNING.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-second of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a memorandum, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and said the sentence nobody had said: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS TWENTY-THREE, AND ALL TWENTY-THREE OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-THREE NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-SECOND, WHICH IS THE FIFTY-SECOND FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN NINETEEN DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN FOURTEEN TIMES AND FALLEN FIVE TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FIFTY DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FORTY-NINTH MORNING OUT OF FIFTY-ONE. A SECOND RIDER CAME UP THE FEN ROAD ON THE ORDINARY POST AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND THE ENVELOPE WAS OPENED IN ABOUT ELEVEN MINUTES AND THE RIDER WAS NOT STOPPED. THE MEMORANDUM IS ONE PAGE, DATED THE TWENTIETH AT AN OFFICE FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF, AND SIGNED AT THE FOOT IN INK BY CHANCELLOR IONA VEY, WHO IS NAMED ON IT AND IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING AND HAS NOT BEEN. IT PLACES ROOT STEWARDSHIP ON THAT LINE IN THAT DISTRICT UNDER PROTECTION FROM THE TWENTIETH. IT APPOINTS A PERSON TO BE PRESENT AND DOES NOT NAME THE PERSON. IT SAYS THE APPOINTED PERSON IS NOT A STEWARD, AND THAT PROTECTION IS NOT STEWARDSHIP, AND THAT NO SEQUENCE, AND NO PART CONTAINING A SEQUENCE, AND NO CRADLE, IS TO BE REMOVED, BROKEN, OPENED OR SET AGAINST ANY VALVE BY ANY PERSON WITHOUT A WRITTEN INSTRUMENT OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF IT. THAT IS THE ELEVENTH LINE OF A ONE-PAGE DOCUMENT. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT THE SHEET OF THE FIFTEENTH AND THE MEMORANDUM OF THE TWENTIETH ARE BOTH CORRECT AND BOTH DATED THE SAME DAY, AND THAT ONE IS ABOUT A VALLEY AND ONE IS ABOUT ELEVEN LINES ON A PAGE, AND THAT HE SAW IT BECAUSE HE KEEPS A COLUMN FOR THAT SCHEDULE. HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN SAID, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, THAT SHE DECIDED ABOUT HER NINE ACRES ON THE TWENTY-SIXTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THAT THEY COME OFF BEFORE THE MIDDLE OF NEXT MONTH, AND THAT A DOCUMENT THAT ARRIVES AFTER A WOMAN HAS DECIDED SOMETHING IS NOT A REASON TO DECIDE IT AGAIN. SHE WAS NOT THANKED. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THAT THE ELEVENTH LINE IS THE SENTENCE NOBODY HAD SAID, AND THAT IT IS NOT A DEFEAT, AND THAT THREE DAYS AGO HE HAD A KEY AND A REASON NOT TO USE IT AND THAT THE REASON WAS A WOMAN NINE HUNDRED YARDS OFF WHO HAS NEVER BEEN ASKED A QUESTION BY HIM IN TWO MONTHS, AND THAT TODAY THERE IS ALSO A DOCUMENT, AND THAT THE DOCUMENT DOES NOT TOUCH THE WOMAN AND DOES NOT NAME THIS HOLDING, AND THAT THE REASON IS STILL THE WOMAN AND NOT THE DOCUMENT. NOBODY WAS THANKED. THE FORM ON THE MIDDLE TABLE CARRIES FOUR LINES AND ONE FIGURE AND THREE BLANKS, AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED, AND THE DOCUMENT DID NOT FILL IT. THE COUNT OF THINGS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE THIS AFTERNOON, AND THIS WAS A ROOM.**
