# Chapter 289

## Low Sike, And A Number Said In A Yard

The eighteenth of this month was a Sunday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **twenty-two**, and entered that all twenty-two of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **fifty-one mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty-two not**, and that twenty-nine and twenty-two are fifty-one, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

And the figure for the eighteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-five hundredweight.**

That is the forty-eighth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the fifteen days since the third of this month the series has risen eleven times and fallen four times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in forty-six days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the forty-fifth morning out of forty-seven.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and thirty-two days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and seventy-nine**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be.

And then the three of them went out of the seed house yard at about the eighth hour of the morning, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling walked them to the cart shed and stood at the shed door while the barrow was got out, and the fen entered the three and entered that they were the reader of this body, Tova Reed and Soren Rill, and entered the reason, which is that there is one stop word in this whole business and only one of the three of them knows it, and that a stop word known by three people is a word and not a stop.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing and gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twelve.

**"I have walked that barrow up four miles eleven times and I have not been thanked on any of them and I am not going to be thanked on the twelfth, and I am going to say one thing and it is the only thing I know that is worth saying, and the reason is that three of you are going out nine miles to look at a box and one of me is going to stand in a shed and hold a book, and a man who has been the second person in the room knows what the second person is for. It is not a sight. So do not stand where I would have stood. I do not know what is at Low Sike and I have not said I do, and if any of the three of you comes back and tells me what it was then I am going to say I told you so, and I would rather be a man who said that than a man who said nothing and was right about it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that he took the barrow down the cart road and went back and was not asked to stay, and that the count of the times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is eleven and did not move, and that this was a shed door and the count is for yards.
And then they walked, and the fen entered that they were off the fen road at about the ninth hour and on a cart track that runs north-east over flat ground for about six miles and then up a low rise, and that the rise is the only rise for about four miles and that a building on the top of it can be seen from about two miles off, and that they were at it at about the second hour of the afternoon, which is four hours earlier than anybody in this holding expected, and entered the reason, which is that they had no reason to be late and a great deal of reason to be early, and that a man who arrives two hours early gets asked nothing and a man who arrives late gets asked where he has been.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in that yard, and entered the whole of the yard and did not improve it.
**THE YARD IS GRAVEL, ABOUT THIRTY YARDS BY TWENTY, ENCLOSED ON THREE SIDES BY A BUILDING WITH NO ROOF ABOVE THE FRONT ELEVEN FEET, WHICH IS HOW EVERY BUILDING ON THIS LINE IS BUILT AND WHY EVERY DIAL ON IT IS READ IN DAYLIGHT. THE HASP WAS OFF AND THE DOOR WAS OPEN WHEN THEY ARRIVED, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY PUT HIS HAND ON THE DOOR AND STOPPED AND ASKED THREE PEOPLE TO COME UP THE TRACK BEFORE HE WENT IN, BECAUSE A DOOR THAT IS OPEN ON A MORNING WHEN A CREW IS DUE IS NOT AN INVITATION, IT IS A DOOR. THE COPY SAYS THE WORK IS ON THE TWENTIETH. THE TWENTIETH IS IN TWO DAYS. THE DOOR WAS OPEN ON THE EIGHTEENTH.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody in that yard said the word *empty* or the word *seal* for about nine minutes, and that a man who has brought a sheet four hundred miles down a road has got a word in his mouth, and that his was not one of those two, because he had not been in the building yet.

And then they went in, and the fen entered what the inside of it is, and entered all of it and improved none of it.

**A ROOM ABOUT TWELVE FEET BY NINE WITH A CONDUIT COMING OUT OF THE FAR WALL AT ABOUT THE HEIGHT OF A MAN'S HAND AND GOING DOWN INTO THE FLOOR, AND THE FLOOR IS STONE AND IS NOT DAMP. THE CONDUIT MOUTH IS COVERED WITH AN IRON PLATE AND FOUR HOLES AND A RUST ON IT. A TRAY OF CAST IRON STANDS ON A STAND AT THE HEIGHT OF A TABLE AND THE TRAY IS ABOUT THE SIZE OF A LOAF PAN AND IT IS BOLTED TO THE STAND. IN THE TRAY THERE IS A CRADLE OF THE SAME IRON WITH A HOLLOW IN IT SHAPED FOR ONE THING, AND THE HOLLOW IS NOT EMPTY, AND WHAT IS IN IT IS UNDER ABOUT FOUR INCHES OF WATER, AND THE WATER IS NOT RUST AND IT IS NOT RUNNING AND IT IS NOT STALE, AND NOBODY PUT A HAND IN IT AND NOBODY WAS ASKED TO. ON THE FRONT OF THE CRADLE THERE IS A PLATE ABOUT FOUR INCHES BY TWO, TINNED, WITH ONE CORNER TURNED UP, AND THE PLATE IS BOLTED ON AND NOT SCREWED. ON THE WALL OPPOSITE THE DOOR THERE IS A HOOK WITH NO KEY ON IT.**

And then the reader of this body said one thing out loud in that yard, later, at about the fifth hour of the afternoon, to about four people including Tova Reed and Soren Rill and the woman who keeps the seed house, who had come out in the cart and had not been asked to come in and had not come in, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-seven, and nobody improved it, and the count of things this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is **four** on that afternoon and did not move on that afternoon.

**"There is one relay on this line and it is the one we are standing in, and I am going to say that out loud while it is in my mouth, and the reason is that we have been in that room about two hours and everything in it says one, and a room that says one for two hours is a room that has stopped looking, and I would rather be the man who said the number out loud than the man who found the four other numbers in about four years."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody argued with him and nobody corrected him, and entered the reason, which is that about four people were in that yard and none of them had a copy of the ledger, and that a man who is corrected has to decide whether the man correcting him is right, and that deciding that in front of a building at about the fifth hour of the afternoon is not a thing anybody does.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is two, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about the tray and then I am going to stop talking about the tray, and the reason is that I have been in that room for two hours looking at the hollow in that cradle, and I know what a hollow cut for one thing looks like from four hundred empty crates, and nobody in that yard is going to know that from a look, and I am the only person here who is going to know it."**

**"The hollow is not a mould. A mould is for making a copy. That is a hollow with a shape in it for putting one particular thing back in so that it sits where it sits. And there is four inches of water in it in a building with no roof, and nobody has taken that water out, and a hand with a spanner has unbolted that plate and put it back a great many times and has never once taken the thing out, because the day to take it out is on a sheet that came down the fen road on Wednesday and nobody here had read line 41 in full until Tuesday."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he has been looking at a room for two hours the way a man looks at a room he has to work in, and that she has been looking at it the way a woman looks at a room somebody has been packing.

And then Soren Rill said one thing, from the edge of the yard, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is two, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is a date on a list and the date is the twentieth and I have said that and I am not going to say it again, and I am going to say one other thing and the reason is that the fourth line of a rota came round this morning and there was nobody at the headland above Marden for the twenty-second time and two of the three people standing in this yard did not know that until Tuesday."**

**"So I am going to say the thing nobody is going to want said on a Sunday afternoon. The word on that sheet is emptied and it is a future word, and on the twentieth of this month a crew is coming up this track to take that thing out of that cradle and put a plate over the conduit and shut the door for good. I am not saying they will not come. I am saying they will come, and they will do the work, and what is on that sheet will be done, and none of that depends on us and all of it depends on the day."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody in that yard improved one word of it, and entered the reason, which is that a true thing said out loud in a yard in front of four people cannot be put back into the man who said it, and that a saying in a yard is the one kind in this holding that has no second version.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THREE OF US WALKED NINE MILES AND SAID THE NUMBER ONE OUT LOUD IN THAT YARD AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, AND THE NUMBER IS FOUR, AND I DID NOT READ LINE 41 IN FULL, AND A SHEET WITH A REAL SEAL ON IT IS CORRECT AND CORRECT DOCUMENTS ARE CORRECT ABOUT WHAT THEY PRINT.**

**AND THERE IS FOUR INCHES OF WATER IN THAT CRADLE AND A THING UNDER IT THAT IS CUT FOR EXACTLY THAT HOLLOW, AND ON THE TWENTIETH A CREW IS COMING UP THAT TRACK TO TAKE IT OUT AND SEAL THE BUILDING, AND NOBODY HAS ASKED THEM NOT TO, AND WE ARE NOT GOING TO ASK THEM.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the eighteenth of this month, at Low Sike, on the standing of a room and of a number, and not by the reader of this body, who said the number out loud in a yard and did not know it was wrong: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS TWENTY-TWO, AND ALL TWENTY-TWO OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-ONE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-TWO NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE EIGHTEENTH, WHICH IS THE FORTY-EIGHTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FIFTEEN DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN ELEVEN TIMES AND FALLEN FOUR TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FORTY-SIX DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FORTY-FIFTH MORNING OUT OF FORTY-SEVEN. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND THIRTY-TWO DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-NINE, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE THREE WHO WALKED NINE MILES WERE THE READER OF THIS BODY, TOVA REED AND SOREN RILL. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING WALKED THEM TO THE SHED AND SAID THE SECOND PERSON IN A ROOM IS NOT A SIGHT, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE ROOM AT LOW SIKE IS ABOUT TWELVE FEET BY NINE WITH A CONDUIT IN THE FAR WALL AND AN IRON PLATE OVER ITS MOUTH AND A TRAY ON A STAND AND A CRADLE IN THE TRAY WITH A HOLLOW IN IT CUT FOR ONE THING, AND THE HOLLOW IS NOT EMPTY, AND WHAT IS IN IT IS UNDER ABOUT FOUR INCHES OF WATER, AND THE WATER IS NOT RUST AND IT IS NOT RUNNING, AND NOBODY PUT A HAND IN IT AND NOBODY WAS ASKED TO, AND THE PLATE ON THE FRONT OF THE CRADLE IS ABOUT FOUR INCHES BY TWO AND IS BOLTED ON AND NOT SCREWED, AND THERE IS A HOOK ON THE OPPOSITE WALL WITH NO KEY ON IT. THE HASP WAS OFF AND THE DOOR WAS OPEN WHEN THEY ARRIVED, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY ASKED THREE PEOPLE TO COME UP THE TRACK BEFORE HE WENT IN. TOVA REED SAID, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, THAT A HOLLOW IS NOT A MOULD, AND THAT FOUR INCHES OF WATER IN IT IN A BUILDING WITH NO ROOF MEANS A HAND WITH A SPANNER HAS UNBOLTED THAT PLATE AND PUT IT BACK A GREAT MANY TIMES AND HAS NEVER ONCE TAKEN THE THING OUT, BECAUSE THE DAY TO TAKE IT OUT IS ON A SHEET THAT CAME DOWN THE FEN ROAD ON WEDNESDAY. SHE WAS NOT THANKED. SOREN RILL SAID FROM THE EDGE OF THE YARD, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, THAT THE WORD EMPTIED ON THAT SHEET IS A FUTURE WORD, AND THAT ON THE TWENTIETH A CREW IS COMING TO TAKE THE THING OUT AND SEAL THE BUILDING, AND THAT NONE OF THAT DEPENDS ON US AND ALL OF IT DEPENDS ON THE DAY. HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD IN THAT YARD AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON THAT THERE IS ONE RELAY ON THAT LINE AND THAT IT IS THE ONE THEY WERE STANDING IN. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOAD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR ON THAT AFTERNOON. NOBODY ARGUED WITH HIM AND NOBODY CORRECTED HIM. THE COUNT OF TIMES A PERSON WHO KEEPS A BOOK IN THIS HOLDING HAS SAID A THING IN A YARD THE ENGINEER OF RECORD HAD NOT THOUGHT OF IS ELEVEN AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THIS WAS A SHED DOOR AND THE COUNT IS FOR YARDS.**
