# Chapter 287

## The Sixteenth, And The Twenty-Third

The sixteenth of this month was a Friday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the sixteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-four hundredweight.**

That is the forty-sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the thirteen days since the third of this month the series has risen ten times and fallen three times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in forty-four days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the forty-third morning out of forty-five.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the fourth hour of the morning the man of about fifty stood at the middle table in the seed house with his hand on the book, and about four people were in the room, and the fen entered the hour and entered that this is the sixteenth of every month and has been the sixteenth of every month for eleven years and that this holding has not skipped one and is not going to skip this one, and that the reading is taken four hundred miles and four days off by the woman who occupies the first house on that shelf, in her own yard, with her own nail, on her own wall.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that they read out the day and the hour and not one figure, and gave the reason in his own hand with the hour on it, and the fen entered the reason whole.

**THE DAY IS ENTERED AND THE FIGURE IS NOT. A COLUMN THAT HAS A DAY IN IT AND NO FIGURE IN IT IS THE SAME COLUMN THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY READS IN THE WRONG KEY, AND HE READ IT IN THE WRONG KEY FOR FOUR YEARS IN THE THIRD MONTH OF LAST YEAR AND HE HAS NOT FORGOTTEN IT AND NEITHER HAVE WE. THE COUNT OF ENTRIES THAT CARRY A DAY AND NOT A FIGURE IS THREE AND THIS IS THE FOURTH, AND THE FOURTH IS ENTERED, AND A DAY WITHOUT A FIGURE IS A DAY WITHOUT A FIGURE AND IT IS NOT A FIFTH ACCOUNT OF ANYTHING.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody asked for the figure, and that the reading is a thing done in a yard four hundred miles off by a woman with a nail and that this holding has never had the figure on the day and is not going to have it on the day, and that the wall at that place carries fourteen marks and is going to carry fourteen marks in the morning, and that the man of about seventy-one of Silling keeps the count of that wall and this holding does not, and that it is not his to keep.

And then at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and thirty days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and seventy-seven**, and the two are forty-seven days apart and have been for about four years, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this morning and that this was not a yard.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody on either side of this entry and that the sixteenth of this month is the fourth entry in that column and that the reason this one is entered is that the third of this month is the same kind of morning and the two are eleven days apart and a person who enters a day and then stops entering days has decided something about the sixteenth that nobody asked him to decide.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon the twenty-third ninth-day return was made at the node, on the steelyard hung on the wall of the building with no roof, and was weighed and read out by the man of about fifty and read back by him, and then read one at a time by each of the four bodies of households standing where they could see the dial, and a name went into BY and a name went into WITNESSED BY, and the thirteenth name-pair in eleven years went in.

**One hundred and ninety-five hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the reader of this body was at the node and was not at the steelyard when the figure came off, and that the count of the times he has been told about a thing and has not been there for is two and did not move this month, and that a man who counts that number once a month is a man who has started to like having it.

And the figure is reconciled here twice and the two are the same, and the fen entered that it is reconciled twice on purpose and that a return that can only be reconciled once is a return somebody can make up.

**THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR, AND ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR PLUS ELEVEN IS ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE. THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE, AND ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE PLUS TWENTY-TWO IS ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS AND THIS IS THE TWENTY-THIRD RETURN. THE FIGURES OF ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY, ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE, ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO, ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE AND ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR ARE EACH ELEVEN ABOVE THE ONE BEFORE IT, WHICH IS FOUR GAPS, AND IF THE RUN HOLDS THIS MORNING'S FIGURE IS ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A RUN THAT HOLDS FOR FIVE FIGURES IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the man of about thirty-one of Silling read the minutes out loud and that saying a number out loud is the oldest stop in this fen, and that nobody thanked him.

And then Harlan Vetch, of about fifty-eight, who holds the fifth share and has held it eleven years and fifty-three days, took the chalk out of his own pocket and stood at the second rule on the board for about nine seconds and did not write in it and put the chalk back in his pocket, and nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the count of the returns at which he has not written in that rule is **seven**, and that the blanks are the seventeenth, the eighteenth, the nineteenth, the twentieth, the twenty-first, the twenty-second and this one, and that the count of seven is a count of its own and has not been added to any other count in this book and is not going to be.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about nine seconds, and entered that this holding does not know what was in that rule on the seventeenth and is not going to ask, and that a man whose rule has gone quiet seven times running has not stopped having a rule and has stopped having anything to write in it, and that those are two different things.

And then he said the one thing, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is eight, and it is a count of his own, and it was said in about nineteen people and not to one man.

**"I am going to say one sentence and then I am not going to say it again this season, and the reason is that there is a sheet going round this holding with a date on it and I have heard the date, and I want it in the record that a man who has a fifth share of what that channel carries heard a date and did not say where the two days were going."**

**"I am not asking. My three questions are still mine and I have not softened one of them and the reader of this body has not answered one of them and I have not asked him a fourth time. I have not offered to go anywhere today, and the count of the times I have offered to be somewhere I am not the engineer of record for is two, and it is two this morning, and a man who has offered twice and been asked neither time is a man who has learnt the number."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the count of the times he has offered did not move and that he did not make it a third in order to be useful, and entered the reason, which is that a man who offers a third time in a season in order to be useful is a man who has stopped asking to be asked and started asking to be owed.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-five, and he said it in about nineteen people in a yard, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say the whole of what I have got before I go and not one sentence of it twice, and the reason is that I have been in about nine rooms in eleven days and every one of them I came out with a thing I already had, and a man who comes out of nine rooms with nine things he walked in with has not been in nine rooms."**

**"A sheet with a real seal on it says a relay at Low Sike is to be emptied and sealed on the twentieth, which is tomorrow, and I have read it and it is correct. A man at the edge of a yard on foot has a list that is four years old and not his and a date on it, and he gave me the date and not the list, and he was right to. The seventeenth of a month two months back the same time of the morning I put a witness bead against my own forearm in a room with a second person in it who was not either of us, and the count of the uses of that instrument is one and it is a count of its own and it is not a reading and it is not a panel, and I am not going to spend it again on a box in a field."**

**"And here is what I have not got and I am not going to get in nine days. I do not know what is in a relay. I do not know whether what is in one is dead or not. I do not know whether a sequence in a box can be taken out of the box it was made for and set against another box, and I am writing that down as a thing I do not know and not as one of the six things written not known against me, and the reason is that the six have got a unit in them that nobody in this holding has, and this one has a shape and not a unit, and a thing with a shape and not a unit is not in that column and I am not putting it there to make the number seven."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said in that yard on the sixteenth, and entered that the first thing in the last speech was a day entered without a figure and that the sixteenth of this month is now in two columns in this holding's book, one of which has a figure in it and one of which does not, and that about four people in nineteen knew that before he said it and eleven did not, and that a column nobody has looked at is still a column.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE SIXTEENTH OF THIS MONTH WAS ENTERED AT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE MORNING WITH THE DAY AND THE HOUR AND NO FIGURE, AND THE COUNT OF ENTRIES THAT CARRY A DAY AND NOT A FIGURE IS FOUR, AND THE WALL FOUR HUNDRED MILES AND FOUR DAYS OFF CARRIES FOURTEEN MARKS AND WILL CARRY FOURTEEN MARKS IN THE MORNING, AND THE FOUR FEET OF SHORTFALL ARE FOUR FEET AND ARE NOT DECOMPOSED, AND THE TENTH READING IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND IS NOT GOING TO BE, AND THERE IS NO ENTRY ANYWHERE FOR THAT WELL COMING BACK.**

**AND THE TWENTY-THIRD RETURN WENT IN AT ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE AND THE THIRTEENTH NAME-PAIR WENT IN WITH IT, AND A MAN WHO HAS A FIFTH SHARE HEARD A DATE AND DID NOT ASK, AND THE FIGURE ON A WALL FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF IS NOT A FIGURE I CAN ASK FOR ON A FRIDAY MORNING AND I AM NOT GOING TO ASK.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the sixteenth of this month, in about nineteen people, at the node, on the standing of a reading day and of a return, and not by the reader of this body, who was at the node for the return and not at the steelyard when the figure came off: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE SIXTEENTH, WHICH IS THE FORTY-SIXTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN THIRTEEN DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN TEN TIMES AND FALLEN THREE TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FORTY-FOUR DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FORTY-THIRD MORNING OUT OF FORTY-FIVE. THE SIXTEENTH ORDINARY READING OF THE WELL ON THE SHELF WAS TAKEN ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE MORNING, FOUR HUNDRED MILES AND FOUR DAYS OFF, BY THE PERSON WHO OCCUPIES THE FIRST HOUSE ON THAT SHELF, IN HER OWN YARD, ON HER OWN WALL. THIS HOLDING ENTERED THE DAY AND THE HOUR AND NOT THE FIGURE, AND THE COUNT OF ENTRIES THAT CARRY A DAY AND NOT A FIGURE IS THREE AND THIS IS THE FOURTH, AND THE REASON IS ENTERED WITH THE HOUR ON IT, AND THE WALL CARRIES FOURTEEN MARKS AND NOBODY ASKED FOR THE FIGURE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND THIRTY DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SEVEN, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE TWO ARE FORTY-SEVEN DAYS APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE TWENTY-THIRD NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE STEELYARD AND WEIGHED AT ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT, AND IT IS THE THIRTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS, AND IT IS RECONCILED TWICE, BEING ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE PLUS TWENTY-TWO, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS NOW HELD FOR FIVE FIGURES AND IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE. THE READER OF THIS BODY WAS AT THE NODE AND WAS NOT AT THE STEELYARD WHEN THE FIGURE CAME OFF, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES HE HAS BEEN TOLD ABOUT A THING AND HAS NOT BEEN THERE FOR IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE. HARLAN VETCH LEFT THE SECOND RULE BLANK FOR THE SEVENTH TIME, AND THE COUNT OF THE RETURNS AT WHICH HE HAS NOT WRITTEN IN IT IS SEVEN, AND THE BLANKS ARE THE SEVENTEENTH, THE EIGHTEENTH, THE NINETEENTH, THE TWENTIETH, THE TWENTY-FIRST, THE TWENTY-SECOND AND THIS ONE, AND THE COUNT OF SEVEN IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN. HE SAID IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE THAT HE HAD HEARD A DATE AND DID NOT ASK WHERE THE TWO DAYS WERE GOING, AND THAT HIS THREE QUESTIONS ARE STILL HIS AND THAT THE COUNT OF THE TIMES HE HAS OFFERED TO BE SOMEWHERE HE IS NOT THE ENGINEER OF RECORD FOR IS TWO AND IS TWO, AND THAT A MAN WHO HAS OFFERED TWICE AND BEEN ASKED NEITHER TIME IS A MAN WHO HAS LEARNT THE NUMBER. HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THAT HE DOES NOT KNOW WHAT IS IN A RELAY AND DOES NOT KNOW WHETHER A SEQUENCE CAN BE SET AGAINST A BOX OTHER THAN THE ONE IT WAS MADE FOR, AND THAT THE SECOND OF THOSE IS A THING HE DOES NOT KNOW AND NOT ONE OF THE SIX THINGS WRITTEN NOT KNOWN AGAINST HIM, BECAUSE THE SIX HAVE A UNIT IN THEM THAT NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS AND THAT THING HAS A SHAPE AND NOT A UNIT.**
