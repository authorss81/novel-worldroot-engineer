# Chapter 294

## What Is Left Standing

The twenty-fourth of this month was a Saturday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-fourth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and forty-one hundredweight.**

That is the fifty-fourth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the twenty-one days since the third of this month the series has risen fifteen times and fallen six times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in fifty-two days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the fifty-first morning out of fifty-three.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and thirty-eight days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and eighty-five**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that the count of mornings on which he has read them out loud in this month is four and is a count of his own and has not been added to any other count.

And at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the seed was on the middle table under a cloth with a pail of water and a wet cloth against it, and the memorandum of protection was on the table under that, and the form with four lines on it was on the table under that, and the fen entered four objects in a stack on one table and did not improve the order.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and that nobody looked at the cloth, and that the count of people in this holding who have looked at it is one, and that the one is the man who carried it out of a building on the nineteenth of this month, and that a man not looking at a thing on his own table is either being careful or has stopped, and that the fen has entered both and is not going to say which.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-nine, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and the reason is that about nine people were in a room on the last working morning before the standing was read out, and that this month carried a sheet in a rider's hand and a woman of about thirty-eight deciding about nine acres, and that a month is a thing that gets a word put on it by somebody and that somebody is usually the person who has been keeping the book.

**"I am going to say one word and say why I am saying it out loud instead of putting it in the column, and the reason is that I have written that word in that column four times this month in a hand that is mine, and a word in my own hand written four times about four bodies of households is not a record any more, it is a preference."**

**"The word is uneven. And here is the whole of what it means and I am not going to add to it after today. Eleven hundred yards of water from a stone head to a field gate, and about nine acres behind that gate, and the four hundredweight a day of a fifth share that has come out of it for eleven years and has never been in a line. That is the word. It was that before the fifteenth of this month and it will be that on the first of next month and on the first of the month after that, and a person who has spent a month with a sheet in one hand and a cradle in the other is the last person in this fen who gets to be surprised about it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that nobody in that room improved the word or offered a second one for it, and entered the reason, which is that a month of rooms has taught about nine people that a second word offered for a first word is a correction, and a correction made in a room is a quarrel.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twelve, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing and it is a date and then I am going to go and start on my own ground, and the reason is that a man has just said a true word out loud about my ditch and has not asked me one question in two months, and a woman who has been given that can say her date out loud in the same room without owing anybody for the room."**

**"The twenty-ninth of next month, or thereabouts. The carted part goes to my three animals and the rest of it stays where it grew and nobody takes it and I am putting no row on it. I have said that twice in a room and once in a yard and it is the same sentence three times, and the third time is not me reminding anybody of anything. It is me telling you where a man with a valve is going to be standing when the water comes."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the count of the questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and did not move and is two months old now, and that a date given by a woman about her own ground in a room of nine is a thing four people can work from.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and two, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say what is left standing and then read the standing out, and the reason is that a man has just said a word in this room that I have been circling for a month, and a man who has said the word should not be the last person in the room to be asked a question."**

**"The valley is unchanged. Two feet of gap at the kerb, cut on the eleventh of the first month, not healed, and nothing goes into it this season. The sentence of the eleventh of last month stands and was not reversed by a room this month and has not been reversed by a memorandum. Nine acres come off on the twenty-ninth of next month or thereabouts because a woman decided that on the twenty-sixth of last month, and there is a key on my own table that would put water on them, and I have not used it and am not going to use it this season, and the reason is still a woman and not a document."**

**"And there is a thing under a cloth that is worth one valve, and the valve is at the head of a channel, and a valve at the head of a channel does not tell anybody whose ninety yards in the middle of it is, and who speaks for the eleven acres has had three answers and none of them is an answer and a fourth came into a room ten days ago in the shape of a length and a fifth is a silence about a fruit on dead wood, and there is now a sixth thing in this holding and it is a plate with six lines on it, and a plate is not an answer to anything."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in that room.

And then the twenty-fifth of this month came round, a Sunday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-fifth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and forty-two hundredweight.**

That is the fifty-fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the twenty-two days since the third of this month the series has risen sixteen times and fallen six times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And then the standing was read out, in about nine people, in a room with the door shut, and the fen entered all of it, and improved none of it.

**THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR IS TWO AND THE COUNT WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FIVE IS ONE AND IT IS HIM, AND SIX IS NOT A COLUMN. THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO HAVE A ROOTMARK IS TWO AND NOTHING SAID ABOUT EITHER ARM THIS MONTH IS A THIRD ACCOUNT. THE COUNT OF PERSONS WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR AND WALK OUT OF THE GROUND AFTERWARDS IS ONE AND IT IS HIM. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THIS ARM ARE FOUR AND THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE AND IT IS NOT A SECOND MARK AND IT IS NOT LONGER. THE COUNT OF THINGS THIS BODY HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE, AND IT WENT OFF FOUR ON TO FIVE ON THE TWENTY-THIRD, AND IT WAS FOUND BY ANOTHER MAN IN A ROOM. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND THE FIRST ONE IS NOT NAMED. THE COUNT OF NOT KNOWNS STANDING AGAINST HIM IS SIX AND THE UNIT OF NONE OF THEM HAS BEEN SUPPLIED. THE REQUESTS AGAINST NOTHING AND THE NOTES UNDER SECTION NINE STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND FIFTY-THREE. THE BODIES AT THE NODE ARE FIVE AND THE NUMBER WAS NOT GIVEN IN A YARD. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY HAS BEEN FETCHED NINETEEN TIMES AND WAS NOT FETCHED ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH AND IS NOT FETCHED AGAIN, AND THE NEXT FETCHING DAY IS THE FIRST OF NEXT MONTH. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-SIX AND WAS NOT READ IN THIS MONTH, AND THE SIX OTHERS ARE BLANK AND HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND HAVE HAD NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM FOR FOUR YEARS, AND NOBODY WENT UP TO THAT DOOR AND NOBODY ASKED. THE COMPOST LINE FELL ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH AT ONE HUNDRED POUNDS AND SIXTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN SIXTEEN AND IT IS PAID, AND THE NEXT LINE FALLS ON THE FIRST OF NEXT MONTH AND IS NOT ENTERED. THE LEAK IS THREE-QUARTERS OF AN INCH WITH THREE REASONS AND NO CAUSE AND NO DUE DATE AND NOBODY HAS ADDED A FOURTH. THE BEAT IS NOT LOGGED AND ITS COLUMN IS OPEN AND IT WAS NOT OPENED IN FRONT OF ANYBODY. THE COUNT OF DAY-AND-NOT-THE-FIGURE ENTRIES IS FOUR. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FIFTY-THREE DAYS. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-THREE NOT, AND THE FIELDS THAT HAVE WAITED ARE TWENTY-THREE AND ALL OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE. THE COUNT OF THE SERIES IS FIFTY-FIVE FIGURES ON FIFTY-FIVE MORNINGS AND NOT ONE OF THE FIFTY-FIVE IS THE SAME AS ANY OF THE OTHER FIFTY-FOUR, AND IT HAS RISEN SIXTEEN TIMES AND FALLEN SIX TIMES SINCE THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH, AND NOBODY IN THIS FEN HAS SAID THE WORD STEADY ABOUT ANY OF THEM, AND NOBODY HAS SAID THE WORD PATTERN EITHER, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAS WRITTEN THE COUNT DOWN IN HIS OWN HAND ELEVEN TIMES IN TWENTY-TWO DAYS AND HAS NOT ASKED ANYBODY.**

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the month, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and three, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it, and nobody was thanked.

**"I am going to read the last four lines of this month out and I am not going to say anything after them, and the reason is that a month is a thing that gets closed by somebody and if I close it then it closes on a thing I have been carrying for six weeks, and there is a woman in this room who has a date in it and a man in it who has a book in it and neither of those is mine."**

**"So. The corridor in the pan is open and open is not a road. The vault of forty households is under. The ring of bare ground inside eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has not been walked and has not been measured and is not going to be measured this season. Whose order it is has been asked four times in this holding's book and answered never, and I have not asked it a fifth time this month, and I named it once, on the first of last month, without being asked, and am not going to name it again."**

**"And the eleventh figure and the direction and the duration from the seventeenth of last month are still on a page with nothing against them, and a number off a tinned-iron plate in a building with no roof is not a line to any of those three, and the sixth column of a public record is blank on eleven of ninety-one rows and the return that lists them is not enclosed, and the second word of the fifth line of that covenant may be there and I have not looked to find out."**

**"And a key for one valve is under a cloth on the middle table of a seed house, and the first of next month is six days off, and the well house door is nine hundred yards off and stands at forty-six, and the man of about seventy has been fetched nineteen times and has never once asked which number and neither has anybody else, and I have not asked him, and he has not asked me, and neither of us is going to, and that is two things this holding is not doing, and the list of things it is not doing is longer than the list of things it has done, and the line the question is on is entered and not filled, and I did not write on it, and nobody did.**"

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-fifth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a month, and not by the reader of this body, who read the standing out whole and read four lines after it and did not improve either: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH, WHICH IS THE FIFTY-FIFTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN TWENTY-TWO DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN SIXTEEN TIMES AND FALLEN SIX TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FIFTY-THREE DAYS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SIX DAYS ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX AND DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THE WORD OUT LOUD, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND ENTERED THAT HE HAD WRITTEN IT FOUR TIMES IN HIS OWN HAND THIS MONTH AND THAT A WORD WRITTEN FOUR TIMES IS A PREFERENCE AND NOT A RECORD, AND THAT NOBODY IMPROVED IT AND NOBODY OFFERED A SECOND WORD FOR IT. THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN GAVE A DATE, BEING THE TWENTY-NINTH OF NEXT MONTH OR THEREABOUTS, AND SAID THAT THE COUNT OF THE QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HER IS NIL AND IS TWO MONTHS OLD. THE STANDING WAS READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND IMPROVED IN NO PART, AND EVERY FIGURE IN IT STANDS AS IT STOOD AT THE END OF THE DAY BEFORE. SERA QUILL NAMED THE MAINTAINED PATTERN ON THE TWENTY-THIRD AND REFUSED TO SAY HOW IT IS DONE AND WAS NOT ASKED. SABRA OLLERTON SAID THAT THE MEMORANDUM IS NEITHER A REWARD NOR A PUNISHMENT AND THAT SHE HAS NO CORROBORATION IN HER. THE FOUR LINES AFTER THE STANDING WERE READ AND THE LAST OF THEM IS THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON, AND IT IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED, AND NOBODY WROTE ON IT. NOBODY WAS THANKED. NOTHING TENDER WAS SAID.**
