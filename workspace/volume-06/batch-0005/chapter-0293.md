# Chapter 293

## Incomplete And Credible

The twenty-third of this month was a Friday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-third went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-nine hundredweight.**

That is the fifty-third figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in the twenty days since the third of this month the series has fallen six times and risen fourteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in fifty-one days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the fiftieth morning out of fifty-two.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the copy of the ledger of the Office of the Continuity for the maintenance of the Crown Engine was on the middle table, ninety-one rows long, five columns with heads and a sixth with nothing at the head of it, blank on eleven of the ninety-one, with the ruled lines kept under the one that has no head, and the memorandum of protection was on the table under it, and Sabra Ollerton, of about fifty-two, inspector of the Rootwright Assembly for four districts, was in the room and had come up the fen road on her own cost and had not been sent for and had not asked to be fed.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and that nobody thanked her, and that the count of the times anybody in this holding has asked her to corroborate a figure is nil, and that she said on the eighteenth of last month that nobody has ever asked her, and that the count is nil today and did not move and that a nil is a thing a person can be proud of and is not a thing this holding gets credit for.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-eight, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and the fen entered that he said it first and that the reader of this body had told him to say it first and had been asked for the reason by a woman of about fifty-six in about nine people a fortnight ago and had been given the reason first.

**"I am going to put the number up before anybody puts anything else on the table, and I am going to say that a man said it out loud in a yard on Sunday and it was wrong, and I am going to do it in a room with the door shut because that is where a wrong number belongs, and the reason is that about four people heard the wrong number and none of them corrected it because it was in a yard, and a wrong number that stays in a yard for five days becomes a thing four people remember as the truth."**

**"There are five rows on that line in my copy and he said one. Forty-one A, forty-one B, forty-one C, forty-one D, forty-one E. I have had this book since the twenty-seventh of last month and I did not read down the rows because the sheet of the fifteenth only asked me about one, and I am not going to make a speech about a man with a book. I am going to write the five down and the day and the hour and the yard and the fact that the man corrected it in a room and not in the yard, and that is the whole of the correction and it takes up four lines."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is **five**, and that it went off four on to five on the twenty-third of this month, and that it has been four for about a month and a half and is now five, and that the fen entered that the fifth was found by another man in a room five days after it was said and not by the man who said it, and entered that a count that sits still a long time is a man being careful, and that a count that moves when somebody else finds it is a man being careful in the wrong place, and that the reader of this body said nothing at all when it was read out and did not put the number a fifth thing back on the page, and the reason is that a man who takes a figure back off a page in front of the person he said it to in front of nine people has admitted two things at once and only one of them is new.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and one, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a figure on the table and a memorandum under it and both of them are correct and I am going to say the two things that are true about them in the same breath, and the reason is that the whole of this month has been a man in a room finding out which of two things could both be true, and I would like this afternoon to end with both of them being true and nobody in this room having won anything by which one it was."**

**"The proof this holding has is incomplete. It is a plate with six lines and no date and no district, and a ledger copy with eleven rows blank in a column with nothing at the head of it, and a district return that is not enclosed, and a memorandum that says the appointed person is not a steward. That is what a holding can show and it is not enough to put in front of anybody as a proof of a thing, and a thing that is not enough to put in front of anybody as a proof of a thing is not a proof."**

**"And the proof this holding has is credible. Two documents from one office in one hand agree with each other about a date and about a window, and the memorandum and the sheet are the same day and the same line and the same office. Every one of those is a fact about a document and not about a person, and a set of documents that agree with each other and are incomplete is the ordinary shape of a public record and not a special one, and a public record that is both correct and incomplete is the only kind there is."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered the reason nobody said a word, which is that four people in that room had a sentence ready about a man who has been right about almost everything for a month and who said a number out loud in a yard on Sunday, and that those four people did not say their sentences, and that a room in which four people do not say the sentence they have ready is a room that is being careful and not a room that is being polite.

And then Sera Quill said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is three, and nobody improved it.

**"I have been in this holding thirteen days and I am going to name a word I have not said out loud in four years and then I am going to say what it is and stop, and the reason is that a woman who has been in thirteen days in somebody else's holding has about a fortnight of being listened to and it would be a waste of a fortnight to sit on a word."**

**"The name is False Season. And what it is, in the plain words an instruction would use, is a pattern that is maintained. Not weather. Weather is not a word anybody in this holding would use about the twentieth of last month. What is maintained is a pattern, and the pattern is put where it is put by a schedule, and I am telling you the name and the definition and I am not telling you one single thing about how it is done, and if anybody in this room asks me that then they have stopped listening to a woman and started at a machine, and I will get up and go out."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody asked her, and that she was not thanked, and that the count of the times she has said a thing in a room the engineer of record had not thought of is two and did not move this afternoon, and that a word said once in thirteen days in about nine people with a threat attached to it is a word that four people heard correctly and one of them heard as a threat, and that the fen is not going to say which of the five it was.

And then Sabra Ollerton said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have been in this holding since about the second hour of the afternoon and I have been fed nothing and asked nothing and I am going to say the one thing I came up this road for, and the reason is that a sheet with a seal on it went past me in a rider's hand three weeks ago with a district on it and I did not open it, because I am not on the roll for that district, and I would like nine people in a room to know that before anything else I say in it."**

**"So I have no corroboration in me today and I am not going to manufacture one out of a career. What I can say is a professional thing and not a personal one. That memorandum is not a reward and it is not a punishment. It is the ordinary answer to the ordinary question of what happens to a place where something has gone out of a cradle without anybody's leave, and the ordinary answer is a person standing on the line, and the ordinary answer is a schedule with the twentieth on it, and a person standing on a line is a good deal safer for the person whose ground it is than eleven people with a wire and a length of ditch. That is the whole of what I came for and and it is not a thing anybody here wanted."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he has been treating the eleven lines on that page as a thing that has happened to him and to a table, and that a woman of about fifty-two who has spent twenty years inspecting four districts has just said that the same eleven lines are the ordinary answer to an ordinary question, and that those two facts do not fit together and that the one which is wrong is the one that makes him a person in a story.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE PROOF THIS HOLDING HAS IS INCOMPLETE AND IT IS CREDIBLE, AND BOTH OF THOSE ARE TRUE ON THE SAME PAGE IN THE SAME AFTERNOON IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, AND NEITHER OF THE TWO CAN BE LENT TO THE OTHER TO MAKE IT HEAVIER, AND THE ELEVENTH LINE OF A MEMORANDUM IS ELEVEN LINES FROM THE TOP OF A PAGE AND NOT THE FIRST, AND NO OFFICE LIED OR OBSTRUCTED OR DELAYED OR SOFTENED ANYTHING IN EITHER DOCUMENT, AND A CORRECT PROTECTION IS STILL A PROTECTION, AND A DOCUMENT THAT SAYS SO ITSELF IS BETTER THAN A DOCUMENT THAT DOES NOT.**

**AND A HUNDRED AND ONE THINGS SAID WITH THE REASON FIRST, AND FIVE THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG, AND THE FIFTH OF THOSE WAS FOUND BY ANOTHER MAN IN A ROOM FIVE DAYS AFTER IT WAS SAID AND WAS NOT FOUND BY THE MAN WHO SAID IT, AND I AM NOT GOING TO IMPROVE ON THAT IN A YARD.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-third of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a proof, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and said nothing when the number was read back: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-THIRD, WHICH IS THE FIFTY-THIRD FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN TWENTY DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN FOURTEEN TIMES AND FALLEN SIX TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FIFTY-ONE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FIFTIETH MORNING OUT OF FIFTY-TWO. NINE PEOPLE WERE IN THE SEED HOUSE WITH THE DOOR SHUT. SABRA OLLERTON, OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO, INSPECTOR OF THE ROOTWRIGHT ASSEMBLY FOR FOUR DISTRICTS, CAME UP THE FEN ROAD AT HER OWN COST AND WAS NOT SENT FOR AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE FED, AND SHE IS NOT ON THE ROLL FOR THE DISTRICT THE LINE STANDS IN AND SAID SO BEFORE SHE SAID ANYTHING ELSE, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS ASKED HER TO CORROBORATE A FIGURE IS NIL. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, THAT HE WAS GOING TO PUT THE WRONG NUMBER UP BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE, AND THAT THERE ARE FIVE ROWS ON THAT LINE IN HIS COPY, BEING FORTY-ONE A, FORTY-ONE B, FORTY-ONE C, FORTY-ONE D AND FORTY-ONE E, AND THAT THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID ONE OF THEM OUT LOUD IN A YARD ON THE EIGHTEENTH, AND THAT HE WOULD WRITE THE FIVE DOWN WITH THE DAY, THE HOUR, THE YARD, AND THE FACT THAT THE MAN CORRECTED IT IN A ROOM AND NOT IN A YARD, AND THAT THE CORRECTION TAKES UP FOUR LINES. THE COUNT OF THINGS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG WENT OFF FOUR ON TO FIVE ON THE TWENTY-THIRD AND WAS FOUND BY ANOTHER MAN IN A ROOM FIVE DAYS AFTER IT WAS SAID, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID NOTHING WHEN IT WAS READ OUT AND DID NOT PUT THE NUMBER BACK ON THE PAGE. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THAT THE PROOF THIS HOLDING HAS IS INCOMPLETE AND IS ALSO CREDIBLE, AND THAT THE TWO FACTS ARE TRUE OF DOCUMENTS AND NOT OF PERSONS, AND THAT A PUBLIC RECORD THAT IS BOTH CORRECT AND INCOMPLETE IS THE ONLY KIND THERE IS. SERA QUILL, WHO HAS BEEN IN THIS HOLDING THIRTEEN DAYS, NAMED THE MAINTAINED PATTERN, CALLED IT FALSE SEASON, DEFINED IT AS A PATTERN THAT IS MAINTAINED AND NOT WEATHER, AND SAID SHE WOULD NOT TELL ANYBODY HOW IT IS DONE AND WOULD LEAVE THE ROOM IF ASKED. SHE WAS NOT THANKED AND NOBODY ASKED HER. SABRA OLLERTON SAID THAT THE MEMORANDUM IS NOT A REWARD AND NOT A PUNISHMENT, AND THAT IT IS THE ORDINARY ANSWER TO AN ORDINARY QUESTION, AND THAT A PERSON STANDING ON A LINE IS A GOOD DEAL SAFER FOR THE PERSON WHOSE GROUND IT IS THAN ELEVEN PEOPLE WITH A WIRE AND A LENGTH OF DITCH, AND THAT SHE HAS NO CORROBORATION IN HER TODAY. SHE WAS NOT THANKED. NOBODY WAS THANKED.**
