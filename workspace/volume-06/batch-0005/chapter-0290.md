# Chapter 290

## The Timing, And What The Plate Says

The nineteenth of this month was a Monday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the nineteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-seven hundredweight.**

That is the forty-ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the sixteen days since the third of this month the series has risen twelve times and fallen four times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in forty-seven days and the sentence saying so was written on forty-six mornings out of forty-eight.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the eleventh hour of the night the three of them went into that yard, and the door was off the hasp, and the plate over the mouth of the conduit came off with a spanner that had been carried there for the purpose and went back on the plate and the four holes and the rust, and the fen entered that a man who takes a thing off a wall and puts it back in the dark has to put it back in the same place and nobody is going to look.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and that the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not asked and was not there, and entered the reason, which is that this holding has been putting her name on things all month without asking her and the one thing it has not done is put her on this.

And then Soren Rill said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is three, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and the fen entered that he made the timing decision and that nobody argued with him and that he was right about it.

**"I am going to say one sentence about when and I am going to say it before either of you has an opinion, and the reason is that I have been standing at the edge for four days and I have said one useful thing and one true thing, and the useful one is that I have listened to a crew come up this track every year of my life, and they come at first light, and a man who has unbolted a plate at about the second hour of the morning and is putting it back at about the fourth hour has not been seen, and a man putting it back at about the seventh hour has been seen by two men from a cart."**

**"That is the whole of it. I do not know what a crew is like and I have never been in that room. I know what time a cart comes up a track, because I have watched carts come up tracks, and that is the only thing I have been asked for and I gave it before anybody asked me to."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that nobody in that yard said a word against him, and entered the reason, which is that a man who has been at the edge of four yards in four days and is given the room on the fifth has been promoted.

And then the plate came off, and the fen entered the hour, which was about the second hour of the morning, and that it was done with the spanner and not with a knife, and that the four bolts came out whole and none of them was turned backwards, and that the count of things said in that room in twenty minutes is nothing.

And then the plate was read out, in the dark, by the reader of this body and repeated by Soren Rill, and the two readings were the same, and the fen entered that two men who are not the clerk reading a thing out loud together in the dark is a page and not a reading and not a panel, and said it again in his own words: there is no panel, and a man with a spanner in a room in the dark is not a man with an instrument in his hands, and the count of the uses of that instrument is one and it was spent in a shed on the eighth of this month and is not spent again tonight.

> **THE PLATE ON THE FRONT OF THE CRADLE, about four inches by two, tinned iron, one corner turned up, six lines in the hand of a maker who is not named, and a device on it that is not an office device, and no office on it, and no district on it, and no date on it, and no seal on it, and the whole of it is here:**
>
> **FOR VALVE 41-C.**
> **ONE VALVE.**
> **THIS SEQUENCE IS NOT A VOICE AND IT IS NOT A MIND.**
> **IT CANNOT COMMAND ANY MACHINE THAT IS RUNNING.**
> **IT OPENS THE VALVE IT IS MADE FOR AND NOTHING ELSE.**
> **ROOTWOOD. LIVING. SIX LINES AND NO DATE AND NO DISTRICT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the word on the fifth line is *it*, and that a maker who writes *it* in a sentence about a sequence has decided there is one of them, and that a man in the dark who does not know what a sequence is read a word in a shop six weeks ago, and that he had no way of knowing that on Sunday and had one on Tuesday.

And then the reader of this body put his left hand flat on the cradle, at about the fourth inch along the inside of his own forearm, where a mark runs in a person who has been holding, and he took the thing out of the water with his right hand, and the fen entered the whole of the hand and did not improve it.

**IT CAME UP OUT OF FOUR INCHES OF WATER AND IT IS ABOUT THE SIZE OF A MAN'S FIST AND IT IS DARK AND IT IS HEAVIER THAN IT LOOKS AND IT HAS A ROUGH SCAR AT ONE END WHERE SOMETHING HAD BEEN JOINED TO SOMETHING ELSE AND IT IS WARM. HE PUT HIS THUMB AGAINST IT AND DID NOT PRESS, AND THE REASON IS THAT THE ONLY WORD HE HAS GOT FOR IT IS THAT IT IS ALIVE, AND THAT HE HAS SAID THAT WORD OUT LOUD TWICE IN A YARD IN FOUR YEARS, BOTH TIMES TO A PERSON WHO WAS WRITING, AND THAT A THING HE HAS HAD A WORD FOR SINCE THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH IS NOT A THING HE PUTS HIS THUMB ON TO SEE WHAT IT DOES.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about four minutes.

And then at about the fourth hour of the morning she said the stop word, and it was hers, and he did not know it and had not asked, and the count of times that word has been said in this holding's book is two, and the fen entered the hour and did not enter the word, and entered the reason with the hour on it, which is that a word only one person knows is the only thing in this business that was not written down.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he stopped at the word, and that the stop took about four minutes and eleven seconds and that he did not go on after it, and entered that on the second of this month the man of about fifty said a word at four minutes and eleven seconds into a hold and that the reader of this body went on for about eleven seconds after it, and that the two facts are in the same book and are not the same fact.

And the plate was bolted back on in about six minutes and the corner was put down over the four inches of water and the door went on the hasp, and they were on the track at about the fifth hour of the morning and at the yard of the seed house at about the eighth hour, and the fen entered that the whole of it took about three hours and that the sheet with the real seal on it says it is to be done on the twentieth and it was done on the twentieth.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said at Low Sike or on the road, and entered the cost, and the name of the cost is the thing itself and is not a figure about it.

**MY HAND FROM THE WRIST DOWN HAD NO USE FROM ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE MORNING UNTIL ABOUT THE ELEVENTH HOUR OF THE MORNING, WHICH IS NINE HOURS, AND I AM ENTERING THAT IT IS NINE HOURS AND NOT LESS. I SAT DOWN TWICE, ONCE ON THE STAND AND ONCE ON THE STEP OUTSIDE THE DOOR, AND THE SECOND TIME WAS FOR ABOUT NINE MINUTES, AND I COULD NOT STAND UP WITHOUT PUTTING A HAND ON SOMETHING, AND I DID NOT PUT A HAND ON ANYBODY, AND THE OTHER TWO WERE ABOUT NINE FEET OFF AND NEITHER OFFERED AND NEITHER WAS ASKED. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE AND IT IS NOT A SECOND MARK AND IT IS NOT LONGER. THE HEADACHE CAME ON AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR OF THE MORNING AND IT IS NOT THE MARK AND THE MARK IS NOT THE HEADACHE AND THEY CAME ON THE SAME MORNING AND I AM NOT ADDING THEM TOGETHER. AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THIS ARM ARE FOUR AND NOTHING THAT HAPPENED ON THE NINETEENTH IS A FIFTH.**

And then the twentieth of this month came round, a Tuesday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twentieth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-six hundredweight.**

That is the fiftieth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in the seventeen days since the third of this month the series has risen twelve times and fallen five times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning, in daylight, a cart came up the track to Low Sike, and two men got off it and a third stayed on the wheel, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**THE TWO MEN HAD A SPANNER AND A BOARD AND A BOX. THE HASP WAS OFF AND THE DOOR OPEN AND THE PLATE WAS ON THE CRADLE AND THE FOUR INCHES OF WATER WERE IN IT AND NOTHING WAS IN THAT HOLLOW. THE TWO MEN DID NOT GO INTO THE ROOM AT ONCE. THE FIRST STOOD IN THE DOORWAY AND LOOKED AT THE CRADLE FOR ABOUT NINE SECONDS AND THEN WENT AND GOT THE OTHER ONE AND THEY BOTH CAME BACK, AND THE SECOND LOOKED AT THE CRADLE FOR ABOUT NINE SECONDS, AND THEN SAID ONE WORD AND THE WORD WAS *EMPTY*, AND THE FIRST WENT AND GOT THE BOARD, AND THE BOARD WENT OVER THE MOUTH OF THE CONDUIT IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES, AND THE FOUR HOLES WERE STILL FOUR HOLES. NOBODY SPOKE TO THE MAN ON THE WHEEL. THE TWO MEN WENT DOWN THE TRACK AT ABOUT THE TENTH HOUR OF THE MORNING AND THE CART WENT WITH THEM, AND THEY DID NOT LOOK BACK AT THE YARD, AND THE REASON IS THAT THEY HAD A SHEET.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the reader of this body was nine hundred yards off and did not go and stand in that yard, and entered the reason, which is that a man who stands in a yard to find out whether two strangers have noticed anything has gone there to be noticed, and that the whole of what a man gets by standing in a yard is that four people saw him standing in it.

And at about the ninth hour of the morning, in about eleven people, in the seed house, the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirteen.

**"They said empty. That is the word on the sheet and it is the word the man at the door said, and I am going to say what I think that means and then I am going to stop, and the reason is that I told you on Sunday I would say I told you so, and I have not got the chance, and a man who says I told you so and then does not get the chance has to say the other thing instead."**

**"They did not find it out. A man who finds out says something to the man on the wheel and gets in the cart. The man at the door looked at that cradle for about nine seconds and then went and got the other one and they both looked, and then one of them said a word and the other one went and got a board. Nobody shouted. Nobody went back to the shed. Nobody wrote anything down. That is not a man who has found a thing out. That is a man who has done a job and got it done, and the job was emptying a cradle, and the cradle was empty, and he is not paid to wonder."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he had spent two days on whether a plate would go back on properly and had not once asked what two men do when there is nothing to empty.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-eight, and he said it in about eleven people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say the whole of what is on that table and then sit down, and the reason is that one of us has had nine hours with no hand and I would rather have the sentence out than be helped up."**

**"There is a thing on the middle table under a cloth about the size of a man's fist. It is warm. It is rootwood and it is living and it came out of four inches of water in a cradle at Low Sike. The plate says in six lines in a maker's hand that it is not a voice and it is not a mind and it cannot command any machine that is running and that it opens the valve it is made for and nothing else. I have not got a word for what to call it and I am not going to make one in a room."**

**"And here is the whole of what it is worth, in one sentence. It is worth one valve. There is one valve at the head of eleven hundred yards of channel at Errow Reach and nobody in this fen has ever seen it and nobody has a figure for it and I did not know until Saturday that it was there. About ninety yards of that eleven hundred has never been walked by anybody here and is still nobody's this morning, and a valve at the head of a channel does not tell anybody whose the middle of it is."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in that room, and entered the whole of the last two days and improved none of it.

**A MAN AT THE EDGE OF FOUR YARDS DECIDED WHEN AND HE WAS RIGHT, AND NOBODY THANKED HIM AND HE DID NOT ASK. A SEEDWRIGHT SAID ONE WORD IN THE DARK AND IT WAS THE SECOND TIME THAT WORD HAS BEEN SAID IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK. TWO MEN CAME UP A TRACK IN DAYLIGHT AND DID THE WORK THAT A SHEET WITH A REAL SEAL ON IT SAID THEY WOULD DO, AND THEY DID NOT LOOK BACK, AND NOT ONE PERSON IN THIS HOLDING ASKED THEM FOR ANYTHING OR GOT IN THEIR CART. AND A THING THAT CAME BACK WIDER THAN IT WENT IN IS A CONTACT, AND THAT IS A FIFTH KIND, AND IT IS NOT ON THIS LIST, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY WILL HAVE SOMETHING TO SAY ABOUT THAT AND I AM NOT GOING TO SAY IT BEFORE HIM.**
