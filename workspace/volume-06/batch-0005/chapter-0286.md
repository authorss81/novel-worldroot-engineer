# Chapter 286

## A Sheet With A Real Seal On It

The fifteenth of this month was a Thursday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the fifteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-two hundredweight.**

That is the forty-fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the twelve days since the third of this month the series has risen nine times and fallen three times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in forty-three days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the forty-second morning out of forty-four.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon a rider came up the fen road on the ordinary post and gave a leather envelope into the hand of the person who keeps the seed house, and the rider went on up the road without waiting, and nobody stopped him, and the fen entered that he was not stopped and entered the reason, which is that the ordinary post stops for nobody and does not wait to be made into an event.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, in one man in a yard.

And the wax on the envelope was pressed with a device about the size of a thumbprint, and the person who keeps the seed house carried it to the middle table and put it down and did not open it and went out, and about eleven minutes later nine people were in the seed house and the envelope was open and the reader of this body had it in his hand, and the fen entered the eleven minutes and entered the reason for them, which is that a person who opens a sealed thing in a room is a person who has decided the room is where it gets opened, and this holding has been wrong about that once already this month.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the wax was broken along the fold and not at the head, and that a man who wants a thing opened cleanly breaks it at the head, and that a person who has been handed a document by a rider and has had nine minutes to think about it has broken it where it is easiest and that is not a thing about the office.

**THE SEAL IS REAL AND EVERYBODY IN THAT ROOM KNEW IT IN ABOUT FOUR SECONDS, BECAUSE THE SAME DEVICE IS AT THE HEAD OF THE COPY OF THE LEDGER OF THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY THAT HAS SAT ON THIS TABLE SINCE THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF LAST MONTH, AND A MAN WHO HAS BEEN HANDED A FORGERY BY A CROWN OFFICE IN THE LAST ELEVEN YEARS IS A MAN WHO LOOKS AT THE DEVICE FIRST. THE SHEET IS ONE PAGE AND IT IS PRINTED AND IT IS CORRECT IN EVERY PARTICULAR AND NOBODY IN THAT ROOM FOUND ANYTHING WRONG WITH IT. IT NAMES AN OFFICE FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF. IT NAMES A DATE, THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH. IT NAMES A WORK, AND THE WORK IS LINE 41 OF THE SCHEDULE OF THE MAINTENANCE OF THE CROWN ENGINE, AND THE LINE IS THE LINE THIS HOLDING'S FOURTH PAGE AND THE COPY OF THE LEDGER BOTH CARRY. IT SAYS THE WORK IS TO BE CARRIED OUT ON THE TWENTIETH AND THAT IT IS WITHIN THE WINDOW AND THAT NO WORK ON THIS LINE IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW. AND IT SAYS THE RELAY AT LOW SIKE IS TO BE EMPTIED AND SEALED.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody in that room said one word against the office or the rider or the seal or the schedule, and entered the reason for entering that, which is that an office which writes a true sentence in a true hand about a true date is not doing a thing wrong, and that the only thing anybody in this holding has ever wanted an office to do is be wrong, and that wanting it is the wrong shape and has been the wrong shape for four years.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-four, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have kept a column for that schedule for four years and I am going to say the one word on that sheet that is not a part of the work, and the reason is that eight of us have been reading a paragraph since about eleven minutes ago and eight of us have read the same word and not one of us has said it out loud, and a word nobody says out loud is a word that is going to be in somebody's mouth in about four years without a name on it."**

**"The word is emptied. Not closed. Not sealed. A building with a conduit in it gets shut every year. You shut a thing you intend to come back to. You empty a thing, and then you seal it, and those are two operations and the second one is the one that has never been undone anywhere in this fen in my lifetime."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he had been reading the sheet as a schedule and had got to the word in about the fourth minute and had gone on to the date, and that a man who reads a document for the date has not read the document, and that the man of about fifty reads a document for the thing it is going to do.

And then at about the sixth hour of the afternoon Soren Rill came to the edge of that yard and stopped there and did not come into it, and the fen entered that he was not asked in and was not asked out, and that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked him is three and is three, and that he was not asked a fourth.

And he said the standing form word, which he has said five times in this holding's book and which he has now said a sixth, and he did not say anything else after it, and the fen entered that he did not say anything else.

And then he said one more thing, which was not the form word, and the count of that in this holding's history is one, and he gave the reason before he gave it.

**"I am going to say one thing about Low Sike and I am going to say it from the edge and not from the middle of a room, and the reason is that I have got a list and the list is not mine and the list is four years old and I do not have a copy of it and I have never been asked to keep it and I am not going to be asked, and a man who reads a list in a room is a man who has been given the room for the day."**

**"There are nine sealed places on that list and Low Sike is the eighth and it has a date on it and the date is your twentieth, and that is all I have got, and I am not going to tell you what is inside it because I do not know, and I am telling you the list has a date on it because a man who finds out about a place on the morning it is sealed is a man who has been waiting for the morning, and I would rather you had nine days."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that nobody in about eleven people said one word back to him, and entered the reason for entering that, which is that he had come to the edge and said a thing he could have sent, and that a thing somebody can send is a thing somebody can be asked not to send, and that this holding has not got a procedure for the difference and has not needed one since the fourteenth of last month.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-four, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say what a relay is and then I am going to say what I do not know about one, and the reason is that eight of us have spent an afternoon on a word and I have not yet said one thing that is true, and a man who has been in a room for an hour without saying one true thing has been in it for the light."**

**"A relay is a box on the line. The sequence in it opens one valve. I know that much and I have known it for years and I have never seen one, and everything else a man in this holding knows about it is that the Crown Engine is a working system and that a working system is made of parts that somebody maintains, and I am not going to build a machine in a room with nine people in it out of a paragraph I have read twice."**

**"And here is the thing I have not got, and I am putting it first out loud, which is that I do not know whether what is in a box like that is a component. A component is dead. It is copper and it is iron and it is made to a number and when it fails it fails. There is a shape on the floor of that room that is not one of those and I have not seen it and I am not saying what it is. What I am saying is that nine days is worth more than four, and that the first thing a room does with a date it has been given nine days of is spend it, and we are going to spend it, and I am going to say how."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this afternoon, and that this was a room, and entered the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of, which is eleven, and that it did not move, and that this was a room and the count is for yards.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**A SHEET CAME DOWN THE ORDINARY POST IN THE AFTERNOON AND IT IS CORRECT AND THE SEAL ON IT IS REAL AND NOBODY STOPPED THE RIDER AND NOBODY FOUND ANYTHING WRONG WITH IT, AND THE ONE WORD ON IT THAT IS NOT A PART OF THE WORK IS EMPTIED, AND A MAN WITH A BOOK SAID SO OUT LOUD IN NINE PEOPLE BEFORE THE ENGINEER OF RECORD DID.**

**AND THE NEXT NINE DAYS ARE OURS AND THE PROPER USE OF NINE DAYS IS NOT TO DECIDE ANYTHING. IT IS TO FIND OUT WHAT IS AT LOW SIKE, AND WHO OWNS THE LIST THAT HAD THE DATE ON IT, AND WHETHER THE LINE THIS SHEET NAMES HAS ANYTHING ELSE ON IT, AND NONE OF THOSE THREE IS A QUESTION I AM ALLOWED TO ASK OF ANYBODY IN THAT ROOM AND I AM GOING TO ASK ALL THREE OF THE COPY ON THE MIDDLE TABLE BEFORE THE END OF THE WEEK.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the fifteenth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a sheet, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and said one true thing and one thing he does not know: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE FIFTEENTH, WHICH IS THE FORTY-FIFTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN TWELVE DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN NINE TIMES AND FALLEN THREE TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FORTY-THREE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FORTY-SECOND MORNING OUT OF FORTY-FOUR. A RIDER CAME UP THE FEN ROAD ON THE ORDINARY POST AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND GAVE A LEATHER ENVELOPE INTO THE HAND OF THE PERSON WHO KEEPS THE SEED HOUSE AND WENT ON UP THE ROAD AND WAS NOT STOPPED. THE WAX CARRIES A DEVICE THE SIZE OF A THUMBPRINT AND THE SAME DEVICE IS AT THE HEAD OF THE COPY OF THE LEDGER. THE ENVELOPE WAS NOT OPENED FOR ABOUT ELEVEN MINUTES AND NINE PEOPLE SAW IT OPENED. THE SHEET IS ONE PAGE, IT IS CORRECT IN EVERY PARTICULAR, AND IT NAMES AN OFFICE FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF, THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH, LINE 41 OF THE SCHEDULE OF THE MAINTENANCE OF THE CROWN ENGINE, WITHIN THE WINDOW, AND NO WORK CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW, AND IT SAYS THE RELAY AT LOW SIKE IS TO BE EMPTIED AND SEALED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, THAT THE WORD ON THAT SHEET WHICH IS NOT A PART OF THE WORK IS EMPTIED, AND THAT A BUILDING IS SHUT AND A THING IS EMPTIED, AND THAT THOSE ARE TWO OPERATIONS. SOREN RILL CAME TO THE EDGE OF THE YARD AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND SAID THE STANDING FORM WORD FOR THE SIXTH TIME IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND THEN ONE MORE THING, AND HE SAID THERE ARE NINE SEALED PLACES ON A LIST THAT IS FOUR YEARS OLD AND NOT HIS AND LOW SIKE IS THE EIGHTH AND THE DATE ON IT IS THE TWENTIETH, AND THAT HE DOES NOT KNOW WHAT IS INSIDE IT. HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HIM IS THREE AND IS THREE. NOBODY IN THAT ROOM SAID ONE WORD AGAINST THE OFFICE OR THE RIDER OR THE SEAL OR THE SCHEDULE. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THAT A RELAY IS A BOX ON THE LINE AND THAT THE SEQUENCE IN IT OPENS ONE VALVE AND THAT HE HAS NEVER SEEN ONE, AND THAT HE DOES NOT KNOW WHAT IS IN IT, AND THAT THE NEXT NINE DAYS ARE OURS AND THE PROPER USE OF NINE DAYS IS TO FIND OUT WHAT IS AT LOW SIKE AND WHO OWNS THE LIST AND WHETHER THAT LINE HAS ANYTHING ELSE ON IT. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE THIS AFTERNOON. THE COUNT OF TIMES A PERSON WHO KEEPS A BOOK IN THIS HOLDING HAS SAID A THING IN A YARD THE ENGINEER OF RECORD HAD NOT THOUGHT OF IS ELEVEN AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THIS WAS A ROOM AND THE COUNT IS FOR YARDS.**
