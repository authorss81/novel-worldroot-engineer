# Chapter 291

## What Is Under A Cloth

The twenty-first of this month was a Wednesday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-first went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-eight hundredweight.**

That is the fifty-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the eighteen days since the third of this month the series has risen thirteen times and fallen five times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in forty-nine days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the forty-eighth morning out of fifty.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and thirty-five days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and eighty-two**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on three mornings out of the last six, and that the reason is not a habit, and that a man who has spent a night in the dark needs a morning where two figures are the same shape and forty-seven days apart.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in that house.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about eleven people were in the seed house with the door shut, and there was a thing on the middle table under a cloth, and the fen entered the cloth and entered the reason for the cloth in his own hand with the hour on it, which is that rootwood that is living goes off in about three days if it has nothing wet against it, and that three days is Thursday, and that a thing that goes off on Thursday is a thing four people in this holding will be asked about in about four years, and that four people is the number of bodies of households that can be placed out of four.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that a pail of water and a cloth went under it at about the eighth hour of the morning and that nobody was asked to do it and that the person who keeps the seed house did it, and that she was not thanked, and that a woman who has kept a seed house for four years did not have to be told anything.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fourteen, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a number on a plate in a bag in the cart shed that says 41-C and I am going to say one thing about it before anybody builds on it, and the reason is that on Sunday the reader of this body said out loud in a yard that there was one relay on that line, and it is five, and the man with a book found that out in a room on Friday and was not in that yard, and a man who has been wrong once in a yard this month is not going to be the man somebody holds a number up in a room."**

**"So. Forty-one C is a letter. There is a forty-one A and a forty-one B and a forty-one D and a forty-one E, and a key with a number on it is a key and not a proof, and a word and a figure are one person saying two things, and I said that in a shed on the eighth of this month and I have not stopped saying it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and entered that the letter on the plate and the five rows in the copy agree with each other, and that a thing agreeing with a thing is not a check, and that the count of the times the same figure has been entered by two hands and called a check is five and did not move.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is three, and nobody improved it.

**"It is not a part, and I said that on Sunday about the hollow and I am saying it about this, and the reason it is not a part is that a part has a number a maker gives it and a fault a maker can list, and there is no list on that plate and there is no fault on that plate and the plate says in its own last line that it is living. I have carried four hundred crates and I have never carried one of these, and I am not going to tell this room what it is, because I have got a shape and not a name, and a shape is worth less than a name and a guess is worth nothing."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he has been thinking about what a thing does and not about what a thing is, and that a seedwright is doing something else and that the fen has not got the word for it.

And then Soren Rill said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is four, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I said on Sunday that the crew would come and do the work, and they came and did the work, and I am going to say the one thing I have not said and then I am going to go and stand at the edge of something, and the reason is that nine of us are in a room with a thing on a table that four hundred miles of somebody else's yard will be asking about, and I would rather my sentence was on the page before theirs than after it. I am not going to say they will come for it. I do not know that and I am not going to say a thing I do not know with my name on it. What I am going to say is that there is a form of words an office uses when a thing has been in a cradle and is not in a cradle, and I have heard that form of words, and it is not a question. It is a return. And nobody has to have noticed anything for a return to go out."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he had spent two days on two men in a doorway and had stopped thinking about what an office does with a hole where a thing was, and that a hole is a return and not a mistake.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-six, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"The use log stands at fifteen lines and it is going to stand at fifteen lines on Thursday and on Friday and for as long as I keep it, and I am going to say why once and then I am not going to say it again in a season, and the reason is that I wrote on the second of this month that a thing which comes back wider than it went in is a contact and is a fifth kind and is not on this list, and four people have since repeated that sentence back to me and I would like it struck out of the conversation in those four mouths."**

**"A line in that book has got a day in it and an hour in it and the name of the person who touched a thing. A thing a man carried out of a building has got a day in it and an hour in it and the name of the person who carried it. Those are two different sentences and only one of them is a contact. One of them is a record of the ground. The other one is a record of a man, and a man who starts keeping a record of himself has got two books and about four years later neither of them is any good."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he had been carrying it since Tuesday about the fifth kind and had been treating it as a rule about this holding's log, and that the man of about fifty has been treating it as a rule about himself, and that a rule that is about the man who keeps the book is worth more than a rule that is about the book.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-nine, and he said it in about eleven people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about that table and then I am going to stop, and the reason is that eleven people have been in this room for an hour and nobody has said the thing, and a thing eleven people are all waiting for is a thing that gets said by a man who has no reason to say it."**

**"I am not going to use it. There is a valve at the head of eleven hundred yards at Errow Reach and this is the only key to it that has ever been in this holding, and I have had it on a table under a cloth since Monday morning, and I could open that valve tomorrow morning and I am not going to, and the reason is on the wall of this room in the middle of a covenant that four bodies of households signed in the third month and that says the cut stays open and nothing is to be put in that gap this season."**

**"And opening the valve at the head is not putting something in the gap. It is putting nine inches of water on nine acres that a woman decided about on the twenty-sixth of last month, and the count of the questions I have asked her is nil and it is nil this afternoon. She owns it. I have never stood in it. I would have to go and stand in it before I said one word about a valve that puts water on it, and that is the whole of my reason and I am not going to improve it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in that room, and entered why he did not ask her, which is that a man who has been given that by a person gets it by not asking, and that the count of times he has asked her a question did not move this afternoon and that the count of times she has asked this holding for anything is nil and did not move either.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAS GIVEN THE REASON ONCE AND IS NOT GOING TO GIVE IT AGAIN IN A SEASON, AND THE REASON HE GAVE WAS ABOUT HIMSELF AND NOT ABOUT THE GROUND, AND THAT IS THE FIRST TIME IN FOUR YEARS THAT A RULE IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK HAS BEEN WRITTEN AGAINST THE PERSON WHO KEEPS IT. AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES THE SAME FIGURE HAS BEEN ENTERED BY TWO HANDS AND CALLED A CHECK IS FIVE. AND THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THE REASON FOR THAT WAS GIVEN ON THE EIGHTH AND ON THE THIRTEENTH AND IS NOT GOING TO BE GIVEN A THIRD TIME IN A SEASON, AND A MAN WHO GIVES ONE REASON THREE TIMES HAS STARTED HAVING A HABIT.**

**AND A VALVE AT THE HEAD OF A CHANNEL OPENS OR IT DOES NOT, AND IT HAS NOT BEEN OPENED, AND ABOUT NINETY YARDS OF THE ELEVEN HUNDRED THAT RUNS ALONG THE EDGE OF GROUND NOBODY HAS SPOKEN FOR IS STILL NOBODY'S THIS AFTERNOON AND IS NOBODY'S ON THURSDAY, AND A THING UNDER A CLOTH ON A TABLE WHERE ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING MAY WALK UP AND LOOK AT IT IS NOT A CLAIM ON ANYBODY'S GROUND.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-first of this month, in about eleven people, at this holding, on the standing of a cloth, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and did not open it: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-FIRST, WHICH IS THE FIFTY-FIRST FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN EIGHTEEN DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTEEN TIMES AND FALLEN FIVE TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FORTY-NINE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FORTY-EIGHTH MORNING OUT OF FIFTY. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIVE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-TWO, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. A PESTLE OF ROOTWOOD THAT IS LIVING STANDS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE OF THE SEED HOUSE UNDER A CLOTH WITH A PAIL OF WATER AND A WET CLOTH AGAINST IT, AND ROOTWOOD GOES OFF IN ABOUT THREE DAYS IF IT HAS NOTHING WET AGAINST IT, AND THE PERSON WHO KEEPS THE SEED HOUSE DID IT BEFORE ANYBODY SAID ANYTHING ABOUT IT AND WAS NOT THANKED. NOBODY IN THAT ROOM LOOKED AT IT AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS ONE. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SAID THAT FORTY-ONE C IS A LETTER AND THAT A KEY WITH A NUMBER ON IT IS A KEY AND NOT A PROOF, AND THAT HE SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD ON THE EIGHTEENTH THAT THERE WAS ONE RELAY ON THAT LINE AND IT IS FIVE. HE WAS NOT THANKED. TOVA REED SAID THAT IT IS NOT A PART, AND THAT A PART HAS A NUMBER A MAKER GIVES IT AND A LIST OF FAULTS, AND THAT THERE IS NO LIST AND NO FAULT ON THE PLATE AND THAT THE PLATE SAYS IN ITS OWN LAST LINE THAT IT IS LIVING, AND THAT SHE HAS A SHAPE AND NOT A NAME AND IS NOT GOING TO TELL THE ROOM WHAT IT IS. SHE WAS NOT THANKED. SOREN RILL SAID THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO SAY THEY WILL COME FOR IT, AND THAT THERE IS A FORM OF WORDS AN OFFICE USES WHEN A THING HAS BEEN IN A CRADLE AND IS NOT IN A CRADLE, AND THAT THE FORM OF WORDS IS A RETURN, AND THAT NOBODY HAS TO HAVE NOTICED ANYTHING FOR A RETURN TO GO OUT. HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND WILL STAND AT FIFTEEN LINES, AND GAVE THE REASON ONCE, WHICH IS THAT A LINE IN THAT BOOK HAS A DAY AND AN HOUR AND THE NAME OF THE PERSON WHO TOUCHED A THING, AND THAT A THING A MAN CARRIED OUT OF A BUILDING HAS A DAY AND AN HOUR AND THE NAME OF THE PERSON WHO CARRIED IT, AND THAT ONLY ONE OF THOSE IS A CONTACT. HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO USE IT, AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST, AND THE REASON IS THE SENTENCE OF THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH, WHICH IS THAT THE CUT STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING IS TO BE PUT IN THAT GAP THIS SEASON, AND THAT OPENING THE VALVE AT THE HEAD IS NOT PUTTING SOMETHING IN THE GAP BUT IS PUTTING NINE INCHES OF WATER ON NINE ACRES THAT A WOMAN DECIDED ABOUT ON THE TWENTY-SIXTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THAT THE COUNT OF THE QUESTIONS HE HAS ASKED HER IS NIL AND IS NIL THIS AFTERNOON. NOBODY WAS THANKED. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THIS WAS A ROOM.**
