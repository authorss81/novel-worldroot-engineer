# Chapter 282

## The Second Refusal

The tenth of this month was a Saturday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **twenty**, and entered that all twenty of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **forty-nine mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty not**, and that twenty-nine and twenty are forty-nine, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

And the figure for the tenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-six hundredweight.**

That is the fortieth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in the seven days since the third of this month the series has fallen twice and risen five times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in thirty-eight days.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And then at about the fifth hour of the afternoon eleven people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the form was on the middle table with one line filled and three empty, and the man of about fifty stood up before anybody had said anything, and the fen entered that he stood up and entered the reason he was going to stand up, which is that there were two kinds of empty in that room and about eleven people and one afternoon, and if nobody said which was which the afternoon would be over before anybody knew what had happened to it.

And then the man of about fifty said the difference, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-two, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There are two kinds of empty on that table and I am going to say which is which before either of them happens again, and the reason is that I have kept four books for eleven years and I have made that mistake twice and both times I found out a week afterwards from a person who was not angry and was right."**

**"A blank line is a question that was not answered. A blank space is a question that was not asked. Those are two different objects and I have written them down in the same hand all year and about nine people have read them and nobody has ever once said which was which. Yesterday one of those was a figure being refused. The other one is two bodies of households that were never asked for a figure at all, and if those two things are on the same table and nobody has said which is which, then this holding is about to spend a week arguing about the wrong one."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that the reader of this body has been reading that form for two days as a form and not as two forms, and that a man who has been reading a document in the wrong key gets the right answer to the wrong question, and that is not a rare thing in a holding with one bookkeeper and about eleven readers.

And then a second body of households refused, and the refusal named a person, and the woman who refused gave the reason before she gave the refusal, and the count of that in this holding's history is ten, and nobody improved it.

**"I am speaking for Marden and I am going to say a name, and I am going to say it before anybody asks me why, and the reason is that if I say it after somebody has guessed at it then this room will spend the rest of the afternoon on the guess, and a guess about a man is a worse thing in a room than a refusal about a figure, and I do not want one of those either."**

**"Harlan Vetch holds a fifth share of what that channel carries. He has held it eleven years and forty-seven days. He has been weighed out of a building with no roof twelve times in eleven years with his own name and mine in two columns, and he has never once been in a line, and this room is deciding this afternoon what four bodies of households will stand behind on a length of water he has a share of, and he is not in it."**

**"And I am not saying he has done anything. I have got no charge against him and this holding has not got one either and has not looked for one. I am saying that a room is not the same thing as a yard and that a yard can be a place where a man who is not in it is talked about and a room is a place where a man who is not in it is not talked about, and that is the whole of my reason and I have given it first."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that nobody in about eleven people said one word against her, and entered the reason for entering that, which is that a refusal that names a person is not a refusal about what that person did, and that this holding has no charge against the man she named and did not go and look for one this afternoon, and that a room in which a person is named in order to be put in it is a room doing one thing and a room in which a person is named in order to have something said against him is a room doing another, and that this holding has not got a procedure for telling the two apart and has never needed one until this week.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ten, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about two refusals in two days and I am going to say it before anybody in this room decides what they are, and the reason is that I was the first one yesterday and I have had a night to think about whether what I did was fair and I have decided that it was fair and I want the second one left alone as much as I wanted mine left alone."**

**"Yesterday I refused a figure and today a person was refused and I do not know what either of those is called and I am not going to name them, and I am not going to stand in this room and call one of them a bigger thing than the other. I have got a wire in a bag and eight pence of steel and that is the whole of my standing here."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that the reader of this body has spent five days building a distinction between two kinds of refusal and the man of about thirty-one of Silling has just declined to use it, and that the man is right and the fen is not going to improve on it, and that a distinction a man makes and a man refuses is a distinction that belongs to the man who made it.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden gave a number, and she gave the reason for giving it first, and she said it in a room and not on the form, and nobody improved it.

**"On Friday I said I would not put a figure on that form and I am not going to. I am going to say a number in a room and I am going to say why those are two different things, and the reason is that a number you say in a room is a thing you said and a number you write on another man's form is a thing you signed, and this holding has not asked me to sign anything since the third month and I would like it to go on not asking for a while."**

**"So. Eleven hundred yards. From a stone head to a field gate. I have walked it for about eleven weeks because it is my ditch, and there is one man in this holding who has walked it from end to end, and that is a man going along it and I am a woman who has been going to it for eleven weeks, and I am not saying his is better, and I am not measuring it and I am not calling it mine beyond the walking. About ninety yards of it runs along the edge of ground nobody has spoken for and I am giving you the ninety as part of the eleven hundred and I am not giving you the ninety as anything else, and if anybody in this room wants to make the ninety mean something I would like them to walk it first."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and entered the figure as it was given, and entered that a length is not a claim on the ground and that a length is not a measurement and that nobody is to write the word measured against it, and entered that about ninety yards of the eleven hundred has not been walked by anybody in this holding and that the woman has not walked it either and has said so.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody in that room said whose the ninety yards is, and that the question of who speaks for the eleven acres has three answers in this holding and none of them is an answer, and that a fourth answer has now come into a room in the shape of a length and has not been an answer, and that a fifth silence about a fruit on dead wood is not in this room and was not in it on Friday either.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety, and he said it in about eleven people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"There are two holes in this room this afternoon and I am not going to put my thumb in either of them, and the reason is that a man with a thumb in a hole is a man who has stopped the room and started filling it, and I have been the man who filled a room once and it was in a yard in about four minutes and nineteen people have been carrying the sentence ever since."**

**"One of the holes is a figure that a body of households will not build on. The other is a person that is not in the building. They are not the same hole. A figure can be replaced in about four minutes by anybody who has got one. A person cannot be put in a room in about four minutes, because a person has to be asked, and he is four miles off and he is not been asked, and nobody in this room has got a figure that would stand in for asking him."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said in that room on the tenth of this month, and entered that neither refusal was a quarrel and that neither was a quarrel with him, and the reason is that a man is only quarreled with where he is the thing being quarrelled about, and that the figure was his and the person was not.

And then the last thing of the day is not a figure and it is in his own book and the fen entered it and did not improve it.

**THE FORM HAS FOUR LINES ON IT. ONE OF THEM CARRIES ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT AND WAS WRITTEN BY A MAN WHO SPOKE FOR ONE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS. NOBODY HAS WRITTEN A NUMBER ON THE OTHER THREE AND A NUMBER WAS SAID IN A ROOM AND NOT ON THE FORM, AND A LINE THAT IS BLANK AND A LINE THAT WAS REFUSED ARE TWO DIFFERENT OBJECTS AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID SO OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER HAPPENED TWICE, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. A NAME WAS SAID IN THAT ROOM TODAY AND IT WAS NOT A CHARGE AND IT WAS NOT A DOOR AND IT WAS A MAN WHO HOLDS A FIFTH SHARE AND HAS NEVER STOOD IN A LINE, AND HE IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING AND WAS NOT SENT FOR AND WAS NOT ASKED, AND I AM NOT GOING TO ASK HIM AND NOBODY IN THIS ROOM ASKED ME TO.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the tenth of this month, in about eleven people, at this holding, on the standing of a refusal, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and did not improve one word of it: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS TWENTY, AND ALL TWENTY OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FORTY-NINE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TENTH, WHICH IS THE FORTIETH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN SEVEN DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN FIVE TIMES AND FALLEN TWICE. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTY-EIGHT DAYS. ELEVEN PEOPLE WERE IN THE SEED HOUSE WITH THE DOOR SHUT. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY STOOD UP BEFORE ANYBODY HAD SAID ANYTHING AND SAID, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, THAT A BLANK LINE IS A QUESTION THAT WAS NOT ANSWERED AND A BLANK SPACE IS A QUESTION THAT WAS NOT ASKED, AND THAT THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT OBJECTS THAT HE HAS BEEN WRITING DOWN IN THE SAME HAND ALL YEAR. A SECOND BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS REFUSED AND THE REFUSAL NAMED A PERSON, AND IT WAS SPOKEN BY THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN FOR MARDEN, WHO GAVE THE REASON FIRST, AND SHE NAMED HARLAN VETCH, WHO HOLDS A FIFTH SHARE, HAS HELD IT ELEVEN YEARS AND FORTY-SEVEN DAYS, HAS BEEN WEIGHED TWELVE TIMES IN ELEVEN YEARS WITH HIS OWN NAME AND HERS IN TWO COLUMNS, AND HAS NEVER ONCE BEEN IN A LINE, AND WHO IS NOT IN THAT ROOM. SHE SAID THAT SHE HAS NO CHARGE AGAINST HIM AND THAT A ROOM IS A PLACE WHERE A MAN WHO IS NOT IN IT IS NOT TALKED ABOUT. SHE WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING DECLINED TO CALL EITHER REFUSAL ANYTHING, AND SAID THAT A FIGURE CAN BE REPLACED IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES BY ANYBODY WHO HAS GOT ONE AND THAT A PERSON CANNOT BE PUT IN A ROOM IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES BECAUSE A PERSON HAS TO BE ASKED. SHE THEN GAVE A NUMBER IN A ROOM AND NOT ON THE FORM, BEING ELEVEN HUNDRED YARDS FROM A STONE HEAD TO A FIELD GATE, WHICH SHE HAS WALKED FOR ABOUT ELEVEN WEEKS BECAUSE IT IS HER DITCH, AND WHICH SHE DID NOT MEASURE, AND SHE GAVE ABOUT NINETY YARDS OF IT AS PART OF THE ELEVEN HUNDRED AND AS NOTHING ELSE, AND NOBODY IN THAT ROOM SAID WHOSE THE NINETY YARDS IS. A LENGTH IS NOT A CLAIM AND A LENGTH IS NOT A MEASUREMENT. NEITHER REFUSAL WAS A QUARREL. THE COUNT OF THINGS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
