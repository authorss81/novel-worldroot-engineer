# Chapter 277

## What A Room Is For

The fifth of this month was a Monday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the fifth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-two hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and the series has risen twice since the third of this month and has not fallen in them at all, and has not fallen since the second, and nobody in this holding has said the word steady about any of the thirty-five of them and the man of about fifty wrote the count down himself yesterday and did not ask anybody.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the same nine people were in the seed house again and the door was shut again, and the four sheets were on the middle table in four hands, and the person who keeps the seed house read all four of them out in the ordinary voice and the clerk wrote them as they were read and nothing in them was improved.

**THE FOUR ANSWERS, read out whole in about nine people in the seed house on the fifth of this month at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, entered whole and not summarized. THREE OF THEM ARE THE SAME ELEVEN WORDS, IN THREE HANDS: THE CUT STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING GOES IN IT THIS SEASON. THE FOURTH IS THE SAME ELEVEN WORDS IN A FOURTH HAND, AND UNDER THEM IS ONE LINE, AND THE LINE IS THIS: I HAVE BEEN WALKING THAT DITCH FOR ABOUT ELEVEN WEEKS AND IT IS MY DITCH AND I HAVE NEVER ASKED ANYBODY WHAT IS IN IT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said.

And the four sheets went onto the middle table, and the covenant of six lines is on the middle table, and the four sheets went on top of the covenant, and the fen entered that and entered the reason, which is that a person leaves a sheet on the middle table of a seed house in this holding knowing that about nine people have walked up to that table in four months and read what was on it, and that about nine people have read the covenant in four months, and that the two of those facts are the same fact and this holding has never once had to say so out loud.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the reader of this body did not read the covenant while his own sheet was lying on it, and that the count of times he has walked up to that table and not read it is two, and entered the reason in his own hand with the hour on it, and the reason is that the second word of the fifth line may not be there and he has not looked to find out, and that a man who goes and looks at a thing he may have lost the first time it happens has established that the second time he will look.

And then the fen entered the arithmetic of it, and the arithmetic is the whole of what that room was for.

**NINETEEN PEOPLE WALKED OUT OF A YARD AT ERROW REACH ON THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH CARRYING A SENTENCE. FOUR OF THE NINETEEN WERE THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS AND THE OTHER FIFTEEN WERE NOT, AND THIS HOLDING HAS NEVER ASKED THE FIFTEEN WHAT THEY CARRIED OUT OF THAT YARD AND IS NOT GOING TO ASK THEM THIS WEEK. OF THE FOUR, THREE HAVE NOW WRITTEN DOWN THE SAME ELEVEN WORDS IN THREE HANDS, AND THE FOURTH HAS WRITTEN THE SAME ELEVEN WORDS AND ONE LINE UNDER THEM, AND THE LINE IS NOT A DISAGREEMENT. THE LINE IS A FOOTING. THREE OF THE FOUR CARRIED A SENTENCE OUT OF A YARD AND ONE OF THE FOUR CARRIED A SENTENCE AND A DITCH.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the word ditch is in the fourth hand and not in his own, and the reason is that a word a person has written under a sentence is theirs and not the holding's, and that a holding which copies such a word into its own book has begun to speak for a woman who has not asked anybody to speak for her.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that two of the four hands belong to people this holding cannot place, and that this holding has got two of the four bodies of households on paper in a way it can read and two in a way it cannot, and that the ratio has not changed in twenty-five days.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody had asked the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden whether her line was to be read out, and the reason is that she wrote it in a room with the door shut and put it on the table and left it, and that a thing a person has written and left on a table in a room is not a private thing, and that hearing a person is not asking a person, and that this holding has four years of reading things out in yards that nobody wrote down at all and would rather be careful once than be careful for the rest of its life.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is six, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about three sheets of paper that say the same thing and I am going to say it before anybody tells me I am wrong, and the reason is that I have been in this holding eleven years and I have never once seen three people write down the same words from the same conversation and I have seen a great many people agree with each other and it is not the same thing."**

**"Agreeing is cheap. Four people agreeing about a sentence in a yard in four minutes is cheap and this holding gets it for nothing every week. Writing it down is not cheap. Those three hands cost something and I do not know what and I am not going to guess at it in front of you."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that the reader of this body has been counting agreements for four years and has been counting them as one kind of thing and this man has just put two kinds in the room, and that a man who has been counting one kind for four years and is shown a second kind in a room on a Monday is not a man who has been wrong, he is a man who has been counting the wrong column.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-eight, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Yesterday in that room I said a thing the engineer of record had not thought of and I have been keeping that count for four years and it did not move, and the reason I am saying the count did not move is that the count is a count of things said in a yard, and what I said yesterday was said in a room, and I want that written down somewhere by somebody who is not me."**

**"A column that counts a place is a different column from a column that counts a kind, and I have spent four years being the wrong one. I have been keeping a count of what gets said in the open, on the assumption that what gets said in the open is what this holding does, and a room came along and it does not follow. I am not saying the count is wrong. I am saying it is a count of the yard and I have been reading it as a count of the holding."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is ten and did not move this afternoon, and entered that it did not move because this was a room and not a yard, and entered that the man of about fifty has now said out loud in a room that the column he has kept for four years is about the wrong object, and that a man who has kept a column for four years and has just found out what it is a column of has not wasted four years, he has found out what he has.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that this was available to him in the second sentence of the man's speech and he did not take it, and the reason is that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and is not five, and that this was a room and not a yard, and that a man who takes a fifth in a room has not got a fifth, he has got a habit.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-five, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say what a room in a holding with no charter is, and I am going to say it in a room, and the reason is that I have been in a room in this holding four times in four years and twice of them there was a bag with a hasp on it and a woman who was about to be asked a question, and I have never once been in a room in this holding for no reason at all, and that is a thing about this holding and not about me and I have not noticed it for four years."**

**"So here it is. A room in a holding with no charter is a room in a seed house. It has one table, three books on it, a covenant of six lines with four signatures and one objection and one refusal, and about eleven people who have read that covenant in four months, and a fire that is out by the eighth hour. That is all it is. It is not a council and it is not a chamber and it is not a court, and nobody in it can bind anybody, and if a thing is decided in it the only thing that decides it is that the people who have to live with it said yes in front of each other."**

**"And a yard is the other thing. A yard is where a thing gets said that wants company, and about nineteen people is the size this holding says things in, and nineteen is a good size for being told and a bad size for deciding, and this holding has been deciding in it for four years because it has never had a room it could shut a door on."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in that room, and entered that the three sheets were on the middle table where anybody in this holding may walk up and read them, and that the fourth is there as well in a hand that is not the holding's.

And then the last thing of the day, and it is not a figure, and it is in his own book, and the fen entered it and did not improve it.

**THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A YARD AND A ROOM IS NOT HOW MANY PEOPLE ARE IN IT. IT IS WHETHER THE PEOPLE IN IT HAVE TO CARRY IT OUT OF THE DOOR OR THEY CAN LEAVE IT ON THE TABLE. FOUR SHEETS ON A TABLE IN A SEED HOUSE ARE FOUR THINGS A PERSON MAY WALK UP AND READ, AND NOBODY HAS TO CARRY THEM ANYWHERE, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE OF THE DIFFERENCE, AND IT COST ABOUT TWO HOURS YESTERDAY AND IT IS GOING TO COST FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS SOMETHING EVERY MORNING FOR AS LONG AS THE THING IS UNFINISHED. I AM NOT CALLING THAT A VICTORY EITHER. A VICTORY IS A THING WHERE NOBODY PAYS. FOUR SHEETS ON A TABLE IS A THING WHERE THE PEOPLE WHO OWNED THE ANSWERS HAVE TO COME BACK INTO A ROOM AND BE TOLD THEY WERE WRONG BY SOMEBODY, AND I HAVE ALREADY BEEN THE SOMEBODY ONCE, IN A YARD, IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES, IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE, AND I AM NOT GOING TO BE IT AGAIN IN THAT MANNER, AND WHETHER I AM THE SOMEBODY AGAIN IN A ROOM IS NOT MY DECISION AND IT WILL NOT BE MY DECISION.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the fifth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a room, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and was not asked to write it down: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE FIFTH, WHICH IS THE THIRTY-FIFTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWICE SINCE THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH AND HAS NOT FALLEN IN THOSE TWO DAYS AT ALL. NINETEEN PEOPLE WALKED OUT OF A YARD AT ERROW REACH ON THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH CARRYING A SENTENCE, AND FOUR OF THE NINETEEN WERE THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS AND THIS HOLDING HAS NEVER ASKED THE OTHER FIFTEEN WHAT THEY CARRIED OUT OF THAT YARD. THE FOUR ANSWERS WERE READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE IN THE SEED HOUSE WITH THE DOOR SHUT. THREE OF THEM ARE THE SAME ELEVEN WORDS IN THREE HANDS, BEING THE CUT STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING GOES IN IT THIS SEASON. THE FOURTH IS THE SAME ELEVEN WORDS WITH ONE LINE UNDER THEM IN THE HAND OF THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN, WHICH SAYS THAT SHE HAS BEEN WALKING THAT DITCH FOR ABOUT ELEVEN WEEKS AND THAT IT IS HER DITCH AND THAT SHE HAS NEVER ASKED ANYBODY WHAT IS IN IT. THE LINE IS NOT A DISAGREEMENT AND IT IS A FOOTING. TWO OF THE FOUR HANDS BELONG TO PEOPLE THIS HOLDING CANNOT PLACE. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SAID THAT AGREEING IS CHEAP AND WRITING IS NOT. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT THE COUNT HE KEEPS OF WHAT GETS SAID IN A YARD IS A COUNT OF THE YARD AND NOT A COUNT OF THE HOLDING, AND THAT THE COUNT OF THINGS A PERSON WHO KEEPS A BOOK HAS SAID IN A YARD THE ENGINEER OF RECORD HAD NOT THOUGHT OF IS TEN AND DID NOT MOVE, BECAUSE THIS WAS A ROOM. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID WHAT A ROOM IN A HOLDING WITH NO CHARTER IS, BEING A ROOM IN A SEED HOUSE WITH ONE TABLE AND THREE BOOKS AND A COVENANT OF SIX LINES, AND THAT THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A YARD AND A ROOM IS NOT HOW MANY PEOPLE ARE IN IT BUT WHETHER THE PEOPLE IN IT HAVE TO CARRY IT OUT OF THE DOOR OR CAN LEAVE IT ON THE TABLE. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE AND IT WAS AVAILABLE TO HIM AND HE DID NOT TAKE IT. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
