# Chapter 281

## The First Refusal

The ninth of this month was a Friday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the ninth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-seven hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the six days since the third of this month the series has fallen once and risen five times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in thirty-seven days.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the fourth hour of the afternoon eleven people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the form with four lines on it was on the middle table, and three of the four lines were still empty, and the reader of this body had put a figure on the table on the fourth of this month at about the fifth hour of the afternoon, at the end of his own mouth, as the figure the four lines were to be built on, and that figure was five days old this morning and had been on the table ever since, and the fen entered that he put it there and entered that nobody asked him to put it there.

**THE FIGURE ON THE MIDDLE TABLE SINCE THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH IS SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE FIGURE OFF THE THIRD LAUNDER ON THE FOURTH, AND IT WAS PUT THERE BY THE READER OF THIS BODY AND NOT BY AN OFFICE AND NOT BY ANY OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS, AND IT IS THE ONLY FIGURE IN THAT ROOM THAT NOBODY IN IT HAD BEEN ASKED TO ACCEPT, AND THE FORM ON THE TABLE HAS FOUR LINES ON IT AND THREE OF THEM ARE EMPTY.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that a form with four lines and one figure on the table beside it is a form that has already been given its answer by the man who laid the table, and the reason is that a man who puts a figure down and then asks four other people whether he ought to have put it down has asked four other people to take his figure off the table and put it back, and that this holding has done that once already in a yard.

And the four sheets from Monday were still on the middle table under the form, and the fen entered the difference between the two objects, because the room had both of them on one table and nobody had said they were different.

**THE FOUR SHEETS ARE WHAT FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS UNDERSTOOD A YARD TO SAY ON THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THE FORM IS WHAT FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS WILL PUT THEIR OWN NAME AGAINST. THE FOUR SHEETS ARE ABOUT A GAP OF ABOUT TWO FEET AT THE KERB. THE FORM IS ABOUT ELEVEN HUNDRED YARDS OF CHANNEL FROM A STONE HEAD TO A FIELD GATE. THEY ARE NOT THE SAME QUESTION AND THE ROOM HAS BEEN TREATING THEM AS ONE FOR FIVE DAYS, AND THE MAN WHO PUT THE FORM ON THE TABLE PUT IT THERE BECAUSE THE FOUR SHEETS WERE ON THE TABLE AND NOT BECAUSE HE HAD WORKED OUT THAT THEY WERE THE SAME THING.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that a man who puts a second document on top of four sheets has decided the two are about one thing, and that the fen had not noticed for five days, and that a man who has not noticed for five days has not been thinking and has been arranging.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing about the form, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is nine, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about that form and I am going to say it before the second of the three blank lines gets filled by somebody guessing, and the reason is that I wrote eleven words on Monday about a gap and there are four lines in front of me about a length, and those are not the same question and I have watched this room treat them as one for five days."**

**"So I am not going to put a figure on any of those lines. I have walked the ditch for about eleven weeks and I know how long it is and I have never once measured it and I am not going to be asked for a length by a form that has a length on it. You can have the eleven words. I have given you those and I gave them before anybody asked me for them and I am not going to be asked twice for the same ground."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the count of the questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and did not move this afternoon, and that the count of the times she has asked this holding for anything is nil and did not move either, and that two nils in one afternoon is a woman who has stopped counting, which is her own affair.

And then a body of households refused, and the refusal named a figure, and the man who refused gave the reason before he gave the refusal, and the count of that in this holding's history is nine, and nobody improved it.

**"I am speaking for Silling and I have not been asked why and I am going to say why anyway, and the reason is that there is a figure on that table and everybody in this room has already decided it is a good figure, and it is on there because a man put it there, and I want it off the table before we go any further, and I am not saying it is wrong."**

**"Six hundred and twenty-one hundredweight is the fourth morning of the third launder and it is five days old and it is off a dial four miles off at a node this holding does not steward, and no person in this room has ever had it read out to them, and no name of mine is against it anywhere. I am not refusing it. I am refusing to build four lines on it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body said nothing at all when it was said, and the reason is that the figure was his and the only thing available to him was to take it off the table himself, and that a man who takes his own figure off a table while a body of households is refusing it has decided the refusal was about him, and it was not.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling gave his body its figure, and he did it without being asked twice and he gave the reason for giving it first, and nobody improved it and nobody thanked him.

**"Here is the figure Silling will stand behind and it is one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, and I am going to say why it is that one and not another, and the reason is that it came off a steelyard hung on a wall in front of me on Wednesday with my own name and a second name against it in two columns, and that is the only number in this room that anybody in it has been made to stand next to."**

**"It has been read out to this body of households twelve times in eleven years and not once of those twelve times did anybody ask what it meant, and I am not asking now either. I am saying that a number a body has had read to it and signed for is a number that body can be asked about at a table, and six hundred and twenty-one is not one, and I would rather have the small one that I can answer for than the large one that I cannot."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered the refusal and the figure as they were, and that the figure was written on the first line of the form in the hand of the man of about thirty-one of Silling and read back by the man of about fifty and not improved, and that it is the same figure that came off the dial on the seventh of this month and the two of them being the same number is not a check and the count of that is five and did not move.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-one, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"A form with four lines on it and one line filled and three blank is not a form that has failed and I am going to say that before anybody says it, and the reason is that I have kept four books for four years and three of them are full of lines nobody filled and I have never once read one of those books as a failure. A blank line in a form is the form working."**

**"The other three are not blank because Silling refused. They are blank because four bodies of households have not sat down in this room this week, and I have got a note of which of them was asked and which of them was not, and the answer is that two of the four were never asked, and I have written that in my own book and I have not said it out loud to anybody yet."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that the reader of this body has been counting a room of nine people and had not counted a room of four bodies of households.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-nine, and he said it in about eleven people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"A body of households has refused a figure this afternoon and the figure was mine, and I am going to say the distinction out loud before the second kind of it happens, and the reason is that there are two kinds of refusal and a room that has one of each and has not said which is which has not made a decision, it has made a mess, and this room has about half an hour before somebody arrives who is going to bring the other one."**

**"A refusal that names a figure is a refusal about a figure. It is about that number and nothing else, and it can be answered with a different number or it can be left alone, and the man who refused it has told me the number he will put in its place and he put it in himself and he was not thanked, and that is the whole of what happened. I am not going to be a man who has been wounded by a figure being taken off a table, because I put the figure on the table."**

**"The other kind names a person, and I have not got one of those and I am not going to be surprised by one either, and the count of things this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and it is not five, and that was available to me in the second sentence of this and I did not take it, because this was a room and a man who takes a fifth in a room has got a habit."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in that room, and that about four of the eleven said nothing in a way that was different from the other seven, and that he did not count them, and that he had counted them twice in eleven days in two rooms and is not going to make a column out of it.

And the last thing of the day is not a figure and it is in his own book and the fen entered it and did not improve it.

**THE FORM ON THE MIDDLE TABLE HAS FOUR LINES. THE FIRST OF THEM CARRIES ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT IN THE HAND OF A MAN WHO SPOKE FOR ONE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS AND WAS NOT ASKED WHY. THE SECOND, THIRD AND FOURTH ARE EMPTY, AND TWO OF THE FOUR BODIES WERE NEVER ASKED FOR A FIGURE AT ALL, AND THAT IS A NOTE IN ANOTHER MAN'S BOOK AND IT IS ON THIS PAGE BECAUSE HE READ IT OUT. I AM NOT CALLING A REFUSAL A QUARREL AND I AM NOT CALLING IT A DECISION. A QUARREL IS TWO PEOPLE WHO WANT DIFFERENT THINGS. WHAT HAPPENED AT THAT TABLE THIS AFTERNOON WAS ONE MAN PUTTING A FIGURE DOWN AND A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS PUTTING A DIFFERENT ONE IN ITS PLACE, AND NEITHER OF THEM RAISED THEIR VOICE, AND I PUT THE FIRST ONE THERE MYSELF, AND A FIGURE I PUT ON A TABLE MYSELF IS NOT A FIGURE THE ROOM HAS ACCEPTED, IT IS A FIGURE I HAVE PUT IN FRONT OF SOMEBODY, AND THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THOSE TWO IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH WAS AND IS NOT.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the ninth of this month, in about eleven people, at this holding, on the standing of a form, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and did not improve one word of it: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE NINTH, WHICH IS THE THIRTY-NINTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN SIX DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS FALLEN ONCE AND RISEN FIVE TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTY-SEVEN DAYS. ELEVEN PEOPLE WERE IN THE SEED HOUSE WITH THE DOOR SHUT AND THE FORM WITH FOUR LINES ON IT WAS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND THREE OF THE LINES WERE EMPTY. THE FIGURE ON THAT TABLE SINCE THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH IS SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT AND WAS PUT THERE BY THE READER OF THIS BODY AND BY NOBODY ELSE AND WAS STILL THERE ON THE NINTH. A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS REFUSED AND THE REFUSAL NAMED A FIGURE, AND IT WAS SPOKEN BY THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING FOR SILLING, WHO GAVE THE REASON FIRST, AND HE SAID THAT HE WAS NOT REFUSING THE FIGURE AND WAS REFUSING TO BUILD FOUR LINES ON IT, AND THAT NO PERSON IN THAT ROOM HAD EVER HAD IT READ OUT TO THEM AND NO NAME OF HIS IS AGAINST IT ANYWHERE. HE WAS NOT THANKED. HE THEN GAVE THE FIGURE HIS BODY WILL STAND BEHIND, BEING ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, AND THE REASON HE GAVE IS THAT IT CAME OFF A STEELYARD IN FRONT OF HIM WITH HIS OWN NAME AND A SECOND NAME AGAINST IT IN TWO COLUMNS, AND THAT IT HAS BEEN READ OUT TO THAT BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS TWELVE TIMES IN ELEVEN YEARS. IT WAS WRITTEN ON THE FIRST LINE OF THE FORM IN HIS OWN HAND AND READ BACK BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY AND NOT IMPROVED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT A FORM WITH ONE LINE FILLED AND THREE BLANK IS A FORM WORKING AND NOT A FORM THAT HAS FAILED, AND THAT TWO OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS WERE NEVER ASKED FOR A FIGURE AT ALL, AND THAT HE HAS WRITTEN THAT IN HIS OWN BOOK. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD, BEFORE ANYBODY ELSE SAID IT, THAT A REFUSAL THAT NAMES A FIGURE IS A REFUSAL ABOUT A FIGURE, AND THAT THE OTHER KIND NAMES A PERSON, AND THAT A ROOM WITH ONE OF EACH AND NO WORD ON WHICH IS WHICH HAS MADE A MESS AND NOT A DECISION. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE AND THIS WAS A ROOM. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
