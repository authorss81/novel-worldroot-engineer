# Chapter 279

## The Twenty-Second Return, And The Crate

The seventh of this month was a Wednesday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the seventh went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-three hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-seventh figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in the four days since the third of this month the series has fallen once and risen three times, and the fall is the first one since the second of this month, and the fen entered both and did not improve them.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody.

And the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in thirty-five days and the sentence saying so was written for the thirty-fourth morning out of thirty-six this morning, and the fen entered that the reader of this body wrote it and then went out to the cart shed at the cart shed end of the yard and did not read it back, and the reason is that a man who has written a sentence about a number he has not got yet is a man reading his own book, and that is a count of his own and is not a count of the ground.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, in one man in a yard.

And the barrow came up the four miles in about an hour and three-quarters and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came behind it the whole way and did not push it once, and the fen entered that nobody thanked him, that he has brought that barrow up that road eleven times before this morning and was not thanked on any of them, and that the count of that is a count of this holding's manners and is not a count of the man.

And the reader of this body was at this holding and was not at the node, and the twenty-second ninth-day return was made at about the second hour of the afternoon at the node on the steelyard hung on the wall of the building with no roof, and was weighed and read out by the man of about fifty and read back by him, and then read one at a time by each of the four bodies of households standing where they could see the dial, and a name went into BY and a name went into WITNESSED BY, and the twelfth name-pair in eleven years went in.

**One hundred and eighty-four hundredweight.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that about eleven of the nineteen people in that yard were of the four bodies of households and eight were not, and that the eight were not asked to stay and did not stay, and that the reader of this body noticed which eight and did not write the eight down.

And the figure is reconciled here twice and the two are the same, and the fen entered that it is reconciled twice on purpose and that a return that can only be reconciled once is a return somebody can make up.

**THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE, AND ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE PLUS ELEVEN IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR. THE ONE BEFORE THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO, AND ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO PLUS TWENTY-TWO IS ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY WROTE ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR IN HIS OWN BOOK AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING OF THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF LAST MONTH, WHICH WAS THE DAY OF THE TWENTY-FIRST RETURN, AND HE HAS NOT SAID IT OUT LOUD AND NOBODY HAS ASKED HIM FOR IT, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT. AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A FIGURE WRITTEN DOWN NINE DAYS BEFORE IT CAME OFF A DIAL AND THE FIGURE THAT CAME OFF THE DIAL ARE TWO FIGURES AND NOT ONE, AND THAT THE COUNT OF THE TIMES THE SAME FIGURE HAS BEEN ENTERED BY TWO HANDS AND CALLED A CHECK IS FIVE, BEING THE FIRST, THE SIXTH, THE FIFTEENTH, THE TWENTY-FIRST AND THIS ONE, AND THAT TWO MEN WRITING THE SAME FIGURE IS NOT A CHECK.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered the third thing about the series, which is the thing he is not going to say out loud.

**THERE ARE NOW FOUR FIGURES IN A ROW OF ELEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT ON A CYCLE OF NINE DAYS, BEING ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY, ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE, ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO AND ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE, AND THE FOURTH OF THOSE IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR, AND THE FOUR ARE EACH ELEVEN ABOVE THE ONE BEFORE IT, WHICH IS THREE GAPS, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THE THREE AND NOT FOUR, AND A MAN WHO PUTS FOUR WHERE THERE ARE THREE HAS GIVEN A ROW A SHAPE IT DOES NOT HAVE, AND THE SHAPE OF A ROW IS THE ONLY THING ANYBODY IN THIS FEN WANTS OUT OF IT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the man of about thirty-one of Silling took the barrow back down the four miles in it and was not thanked, and that this holding does not keep a count of barrows and is not going to start one.

And Harlan Vetch, of about fifty-eight, who holds the fifth share and has held it eleven years and forty-four days, took the chalk out of his own pocket and stood at the second rule on the board for about nine seconds and did not write in it and put the chalk back in his pocket, and nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the count of the returns at which he has not written in that rule is **six**, and that the blanks are the seventeenth, the eighteenth, the nineteenth, the twentieth, the twenty-first and this one, and that the count of five was a count of its own and the count of six is a count of its own, and that neither of them has been added to any other count in this book and neither of them is going to be.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about nine seconds, and entered that this holding does not know what was in that rule on the seventeenth and is not going to ask, and that a man whose rule has gone quiet six times running has not stopped having a rule and has stopped having anything to write in it, and that those are two different things.

And then he said the one thing, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is seven and it is a count of his own, and it was said in about nineteen people and not to one man.

**"I am going to say one sentence about my three questions and then I am not going to say them again this season, and the reason is that I asked them in a yard on the nineteenth of a month two months back and nobody has written an answer to one of them down in a book, and a question with nothing under it for two months is a habit and not a demand, and I would rather it were a habit I had chosen than one that happened to me."**

**"They are still mine. What it is for, who it is held with, and what a man does with a figure that went up by fifty in six days. I have not softened one of them and I am not going to. I am going to leave them where they are until somebody in this yard writes an answer under one of them in a book, and until then they are three questions and not a complaint, and I am not going to be the man who turns three questions into a complaint because it is convenient in a room."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and that the reader of this body had not answered the three questions and had entered the reason in his own hand on the twenty-eighth of last month and had not changed it since, and that a man who can produce his own reason for not answering a question in four words has had four words ready for a season and that is not the same as not having them.

And then at about the seventh hour of the evening the cart came down the cart road to the node, and a crate came off it, and the fen entered the hour and entered the arithmetic, and put the arithmetic on the page whole, because the arithmetic is the reason nothing was opened.

**THE FOUR LINES WENT UP THE FOUR-DAY ROAD AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING OF THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH IN A LEATHER BAG, GIVEN AT THE CHANGE OF HORSES, AND THE FOURTH LINE UNDER THEM ASKED FOR THE BOOK AND NAMED NO PERSON. THE ROAD IS FOUR DAYS UP AND FOUR DAYS DOWN. THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH PLUS FOUR DAYS IS THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING, AND THAT IS A FIGURE OFF A ROAD AND NOT OFF A WORD. THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH PLUS FOUR DAYS IS THE SEVENTH OF THIS MONTH AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING, AND IT IS THE SEVENTH OF THIS MONTH, AND A CRATE CAME DOWN IT, AND A CRATE WAS EXPECTED IN THAT SHED ON THE SEVENTH OF THIS MONTH IF THE ROAD ALLOWED, AND THE ROAD ALLOWED.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nothing was opened in a yard, and the reason is that a crate that has come down a four-day road is opened in daylight by the people it was written to, and not at about the seventh hour of the evening by whoever is standing in the shed when it arrives, and that the man of about fifty said that and was not thanked.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the man of about fifty put the crate in the cart shed and put the receipt on the table beside it, and that this holding cannot tell from a receipt whether the crate that came down on the seventh is the crate that went up on the twenty-second of a month two months back, and that the two receipts are side by side in his own book and the figure on each of them is one shilling and fourpence and the crate is named on neither, and that the box in that shed holds nine pounds and eight shillings and did not pay for this one, because the hire down was paid on the twenty-ninth and a crate is not a thing a holding buys twice.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-seven, and he said it in about four people in a room at the node and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about a figure that was written in a book nine days before it came off a dial, and I am going to say it in about four people and not in the yard, and the reason is that the yard was full about twenty minutes ago and what I have to say is about two figures being one figure, and that is a thing a room is for and it is what I said in the seed house on Monday and I am not ashamed of saying it twice."**

**"He wrote one hundred and eighty-four down on the twenty-eighth of last month and he did not say it out loud and I have not asked him for it and I am not going to. He was right. And being right is not the interesting part and I want it entered that I am saying so, because the interesting part is that this is the first time in eleven years that a figure has come off that dial that anybody in this holding had written down beforehand without saying it, and the reason he did it is in his own book and I have not read it and I am not going to."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said at the node, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this evening and that this was not a yard.

And the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE COUNT OF THE PANEL ON THAT INSTRUMENT IS ONE AND IT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN AND IT IS NOT A SECOND READING OF THE PULSE AT THE WEST SILL AND IT IS NOT THE WITNESS BEAD, AND A CRATE THAT HAS COME DOWN A FOUR-DAY ROAD ON THE DAY THE PAGE PROMISED IT IS NOT A READING OF ANYTHING.**

**AND THE ELEVENTH FIGURE AND THE DIRECTION AND THE DURATION FROM THE SEVENTEENTH OF LAST MONTH ARE STILL ON A PAGE WITH NOTHING AGAINST THEM, AND NOBODY HAS PUT THEM AGAINST A DATE, A BRANCH, A MAINTENANCE RECORD OR A CONDUIT, AND A FIGURE OFF A DIAL IN A BUILDING WITH NO ROOF DOES NOT PUT ANY OF THOSE FOUR AGAINST A PAGE.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the seventh of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a return and of a crate, and not by the reader of this body, who was in a room at the node and did not improve one word of it: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE SEVENTH, WHICH IS THE THIRTY-SEVENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FOUR DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS FALLEN ONCE AND RISEN THREE TIMES AND THE FALL IS THE FIRST ONE SINCE THE SECOND OF THIS MONTH. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTY-FIVE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO HAS BEEN WRITTEN ON THIRTY-FOUR MORNINGS OUT OF THIRTY-SIX. THE TWENTY-SECOND NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AND WEIGHED AT ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, AND IT IS THE TWELFTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS, AND IT IS RECONCILED TWICE, BEING ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO PLUS TWENTY-TWO. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING WALKED BEHIND THE BARROW UP THE FOUR MILES AND TOOK IT BACK DOWN AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAD WRITTEN THAT FIGURE IN HIS OWN BOOK ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF LAST MONTH AND DID NOT SAY IT OUT LOUD, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES THE SAME FIGURE HAS BEEN ENTERED BY TWO HANDS AND CALLED A CHECK IS FIVE AND IT IS NOT A CHECK. HARLAN VETCH LEFT THE SECOND RULE BLANK FOR THE SIXTH TIME AND WROTE NOTHING IN IT, AND THE COUNT OF THE RETURNS AT WHICH HE HAS NOT WRITTEN IN IT IS SIX, AND THE BLANKS ARE THE SEVENTEENTH, THE EIGHTEENTH, THE NINETEENTH, THE TWENTIETH, THE TWENTY-FIRST AND THIS ONE, AND THE COUNT OF SIX IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN AND HAS NOT BEEN ADDED TO ANY OTHER COUNT. HE THEN SAID IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE THAT HIS THREE QUESTIONS ARE STILL HIS AND THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO TURN THEM INTO A COMPLAINT BECAUSE IT IS CONVENIENT IN A ROOM, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT ANSWER THEM. AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING A CRATE CAME DOWN THE FOUR-DAY ROAD INTO THE CART SHED AT THE NODE, AND THE FOUR LINES HAD GONE UP IT AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING OF THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THE ROAD IS FOUR DAYS UP AND FOUR DAYS DOWN. NOTHING WAS OPENED IN A YARD. THE RECEIPT IS ONE SHILLING AND FOURPENCE AND THE CRATE IS NAMED ON NEITHER RECEIPT AND THE BOX HOLDS NINE POUNDS AND EIGHT SHILLINGS. THE PANEL IS NOT PRINTED AND THE COUNT OF ITS USES IS ONE AND IT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN. THE DIRECTION AND THE DURATION ARE STILL ON A PAGE WITH NOTHING AGAINST THEM. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
