# Chapter 276

## The Second Time Somebody Else Decides

The fourth of this month was a Sunday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the fourth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-one hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-fourth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and the fen entered that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in thirty-two days and the sentence saying so has now been written on thirty-one of thirty-three mornings, and entered that the reader of this body wrote on the fifteenth of last month that he would stop writing it in about four days whether it moved or not, and that the four days are gone and he has not stopped.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon nine people went into the seed house and the door was shut behind them, and the fen entered that it was shut, and the reason is that a room in a holding with no charter is a room in a seed house, and the seed house is the only room in this holding that anybody can shut a door on, and that a thing said in it on the fourth of this month was not said in a yard and was not heard by nineteen people and is not going to be read out to a yard.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered the nine.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden and the man of about thirty-one of Silling were two of them, and two more of the nine are people this holding has no way of placing, and the fen entered that and entered the reason, which is that this holding has kept four bodies of households in its book for four years as four words and has never once written down which of its own people belongs to which of them, and that a body of households may send a person into a room and this holding cannot tell afterwards who sent whom, and that he did not ask.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the man of about fifty, the person who keeps the seed house, the woman who does the sums and the clerk were the other four, and that the ninth was the reader of this body, and that the man of about fifty read out at about the seventh hour of the morning, in about four people, and before anything else was said in that room, the reason the reader of this body was going to be in it.

**"He is in the room because four bodies of households cannot measure eleven hundred yards and there is one man in this holding who has walked them from end to end, and the reason I am saying that out loud before the room sits down is that a room which is going to argue about a length with nobody in it who has walked the length is a room arguing about a number, and this holding has said numbers out loud in a yard and been wrong four times and it is not going to be wrong a fifth time in a room either."**

**"He is in the room as the man who can walk it and not as the engineer of record, and those are two different objects and I have watched him confuse them for four years and so have you. The man who can walk it is in it. Nobody is deciding anything about what he is called."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he had been told the reason before he was asked for it and had said nothing, and entered the reason he said nothing, which is that a man who argues with the reason he has been put in a room is a man who thinks the room is his, and that the man of about fifty had put it in front of four people four hours before anybody had a chance to want it different.

And then the room did the thing it was in there to do, and the thing is this, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**THE SENTENCE OF THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH IS THE CUT STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING IS TO BE PUT IN THAT GAP THIS SEASON. IT WAS TAKEN IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE IN A YARD AT ERROW REACH, ALONE, BY THE READER OF THIS BODY, IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES, AND NOBODY WAS ASKED. THE ROOM IS NOT REOPENING THAT SENTENCE AND IS NOT ENTITLED TO. THE ROOM IS TAKING THE DECISION THAT SENTENCE WAS TAKEN ABOUT, AND IT IS TAKING IT BY ASKING THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS, ONE AT A TIME, WHAT SENTENCE EACH OF THEM CARRIED OUT OF THAT YARD, AND WRITING THE FOUR ANSWERS DOWN IN FOUR HANDS, AND NOT READING ANY OF THEM BACK TO A YARD.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two hours, and entered that about four of the nine said nothing in a way that was different from the other five, and that he did not count them, and the reason is that he had counted the faces of a room twice this month already and a man who counts the faces in a room is a man deciding which of them he is going to believe.

And when the two people this holding cannot place had answered, the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing about the answer she had given, and she gave the reason before she said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is eight, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about the answer I have just given and I am going to say it now and not at the end, and the reason is that I have been in this room for two hours and four of us have written the same eleven words twice, and if I wait until the end then the thing I am going to say will look like a fifth copy of the eleven words and it is not one."**

**"I wrote down the eleven words. They are what I said in that yard and they are true. And I have written one line under them and I am going to read the line and I am not going to say what it means. The line is that I have been walking that ditch for about eleven weeks and it is my ditch and I have never once asked anybody what is in it, and I am not going to be asked in a room either."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and did not move, and that the line under the eleven words is one line and is not a figure and is not going to be turned into one.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-seven, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have kept a column for that yard for four years and I am going to say what is in it and then I am going to stop, and the reason is that four people have just written down the same eleven words and I have had four sheets in my own hand saying that one man wrote them, and one of those two things is going to have to give and it is not going to be the four people."**

**"Here is what is in my column. I have the day, and the hour, and the number of people, and the length in minutes, and I have not got one single word anybody said, and I have never wanted one until this afternoon. A column with a day in it and no words in it is a record that something happened and it is worth nothing at all to anybody who was not there, and that is the right size for it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he has been reading that column for four years as a record of a decision and it is a record of an event, and that the difference is eleven words, and that a man who reads his own record in the wrong key for four years is not a man who has been idle.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-four, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say what a room is and what a yard is and I am going to say it once and in a room, and the reason is that four of the nine people in this room have been in a yard together on the eleventh of last month and every one of us left it with something we did not have when we went in, and three of those four have just written down the same sentence, and the fourth has written the sentence and one line under it, and I have not got a figure for why and I am not going to have one this afternoon."**

**"A yard is where a thing gets said that wants company. A room is where a thing gets decided that does not want to be repeated. Those are not two places, they are two directions, and this holding has one yard and one seed house and about four years of saying things in the yard, and the count of things this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and it is not five and it did not move this afternoon and it was available to me in the second sentence of this and I went on."**

**"And here is the whole of what I have, and it is not much, and I am giving it last because I was put in this room as the man who can walk a length and not as a man with anything to decide. Eleven hundred yards from a stone head to a field gate. A fall of about a foot in the whole of it. Two feet of gap at the kerb, cut on the eleventh of the first month, not healed, and nothing going into it this season. A wire and a rule and eight pence of steel. About ninety yards of it along the edge of ground nobody has spoken for and I am not going to say whose in a room and I am not going to say it in a yard either."**

**"And the two things I do not have, and I am putting those first in my own book at about the seventh hour of this evening and not first out loud, because a man who leads with what he knows has already decided how the room will hear the rest of it. I have no figure for how fast a roll grows over a cut. And I have nothing whatever about the middle, and I am not going to use a rate I cannot reconcile to get a figure for it this afternoon."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said in that room on the fourth of this month, and entered that about nine people were in it and that four of them had come out of a yard in the same four minutes in the month that ended and had been carrying a sentence for twenty-four days.

And the last thing of the day is not a figure and is entered whole, and it is in the reader of this body's own hand, and the fen entered it and did not improve it.

**A DECISION TAKEN IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE IN A YARD IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES BY ONE MAN PRODUCED A SENTENCE THAT NINETEEN PEOPLE CARRIED OUT OF A YARD AND THAT NOBODY HAS ASKED ANYBODY TO READ BACK. A DECISION TAKEN BY FOUR OTHER PEOPLE IN A ROOM IN ABOUT TWO HOURS PRODUCED FOUR SHEETS IN FOUR HANDS AND A FORM WITH FOUR LINES ON IT AND NOTHING ON THE LINES. I HAVE NOW GOT TWO OBJECTS IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK WHERE THERE WAS ONE, AND NEITHER OF THEM IS THE SAME THING AS THE OTHER, AND THE ONE THAT CARRIES A FIGURE IS THE ONE I TOOK MYSELF IN A YARD, AND THE ONE THAT CARRIES FOUR SHEETS IS THE ONE FOUR PEOPLE TOOK IN A ROOM, AND THE SECOND ONE HAS NO FIGURE IN IT AT ALL AND THAT IS NOT AN ACCIDENT AND IS NOT A DEFECT. I AM NOT CALLING THIS A SECOND DECISION. A DECISION IS A THING THAT IS DONE. WHAT HAPPENED IN THAT ROOM THIS AFTERNOON IS THAT FOUR PEOPLE WERE ASKED WHAT THEY HAD UNDERSTOOD, AND THREE OF THEM ANSWERED THE SAME, AND THE FOURTH ANSWERED THE SAME AND THEN WROTE ONE LINE UNDER IT, AND THE ROOM DID NOT DECIDE ANYTHING, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND DID NOT MOVE, AND I AM NOT SAYING THAT NUMBER IN A YARD AND THIS IS NOT A YARD.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the fourth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a room, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and was not deciding anything: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE FOURTH, WHICH IS THE THIRTY-FOURTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTY-TWO DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO HAS NOW BEEN WRITTEN ON THIRTY-ONE OF THIRTY-THREE MORNINGS. NINE PEOPLE WENT INTO THE SEED HOUSE AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND THE DOOR WAS SHUT. THEY WERE THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN, THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING, TWO PEOPLE THIS HOLDING CANNOT PLACE, THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, THE PERSON WHO KEEPS THE SEED HOUSE, THE WOMAN WHO DOES THE SUMS, THE CLERK, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY READ OUT AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE MORNING AND BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE WAS SAID IN THAT ROOM THAT THE READER OF THIS BODY WAS IN THE ROOM AS THE MAN WHO CAN WALK ELEVEN HUNDRED YARDS AND NOT AS THE ENGINEER OF RECORD. THE SENTENCE OF THE ELEVENTH OF LAST MONTH IS THE CUT STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING IS TO BE PUT IN THAT GAP THIS SEASON, AND IT WAS TAKEN IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE IN A YARD, ALONE, IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES, AND NOBODY WAS ASKED, AND THAT SENTENCE WAS NOT REOPENED IN THE ROOM. THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS WERE ASKED ONE AT A TIME WHAT SENTENCE EACH OF THEM CARRIED OUT OF THAT YARD AND FOUR ANSWERS WERE WRITTEN IN FOUR HANDS. THREE OF THE FOUR ANSWERS ARE THE SAME ELEVEN WORDS. THE FOURTH IS THE SAME ELEVEN WORDS WITH ONE LINE UNDER IT, IN THE HAND OF THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN, WHICH SAYS THAT SHE HAS BEEN WALKING THAT DITCH FOR ABOUT ELEVEN WEEKS AND THAT IT IS HER DITCH AND THAT SHE HAS NEVER ASKED ANYBODY WHAT IS IN IT. THE ROOM DECIDED NOTHING. THE STANDING COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND DID NOT MOVE AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT HIS COLUMN FOR THAT YARD HAS THE DAY AND THE HOUR AND THE NUMBER OF PEOPLE AND THE MINUTES AND NO WORDS, AND THAT HE HAD NOT WANTED A WORD IN IT UNTIL THIS AFTERNOON. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE ELEVEN HUNDRED YARDS, A FALL OF ABOUT A FOOT, A GAP OF TWO FEET, A WIRE AND A RULE AND EIGHT PENCE, AND ABOUT NINETY YARDS ALONG THE EDGE OF GROUND NOBODY HAS SPOKEN FOR, AND SAID THAT HE HAS NO FIGURE FOR HOW FAST A ROLL GROWS AND NOTHING AT ALL ABOUT THE MIDDLE. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
