# Chapter 284

## The One Item That Does Not Hold

The twelfth of this month was a Monday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twelfth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty hundredweight.**

That is the forty-second figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the nine days since the third of this month the series has fallen twice and risen seven times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in forty days.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the same nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the copy of the ledger was on the middle table where it had been the day before, and Sera Quill had her six lines in front of her with the fifth of them ticked in her own hand and the sixth of them not, and she said it before anybody asked her, and the count of times a person from outside this holding has said a thing in a room the engineer of record had not thought of is two, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say the sixth line before anybody asks me for it, and the reason is that if I wait for the room to get to it then the room will get to it by finding a fault, and a fault found in a piece of paper is a fault found in me, and I would rather it came out of my mouth on a Monday than out of a page on Wednesday in about four years."**

**"The sixth line is this. I have seen four branches come off a list in my life for being irregular, and not one of the four was damaged. That is the whole of what I have got and it is the only one of my six that a piece of paper cannot hold, and it does not hold, and I am not going to soften it into something that does."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody in that room asked her anything and that four of the nine had it in their mouths and did not say it, and the reason is that a woman who has said the thing about herself before she was asked is not a woman who is being examined, and that a room which examines her anyway has turned a professional into a document.

And then the working was entered whole, and the fen entered all of it and improved none of it.

**THE SIXTH ITEM WAS PUT AGAINST THE COPY OF THE LEDGER AND THE RECORD DID NOT HOLD IT, AND THE RECORD IS CORRECT. THE FIFTH HEADED COLUMN IS CONDITION AT LAST WORK, WHICH IS THE CONDITION OF A ROW AT THE MOMENT SOMEBODY LAST WORKED IT, AND THE WORD NO CUT IS THE CONDITION OF A STEM ON A MORNING WHEN NOBODY WORKED IT AT ALL, AND THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT MOMENTS AND THE COLUMN HOLDS ONE OF THEM. THE SIXTH COLUMN IS THE COLUMN THAT WAS GOING TO HOLD THE OTHER ONE AND IT IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF NINETY-ONE ROWS AND THE ELEVEN ARE LISTED IN A DISTRICT RETURN WHICH IS NOT ENCLOSED, AND SEVEN OF THE ELEVEN ARE NOT ON THIS LINE AND THIS HOLDING CANNOT GET TO THEM. THE FOUR BRANCHES SHE SAW COME OFF A LIST WERE FOUR BRANCHES IN FOUR OTHER PLACES AND THE LIST WAS FOUR OTHER LISTS. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING CAN PUT HER FOUR ON THIS SHEET AND HER FOUR IS NOT ON THIS SHEET.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered the thing underneath all of it, and entered it whole.

**A DOCUMENT THAT FAILS A TEST HAS SAID SOMETHING ABOUT ITSELF, AND WHAT THIS ONE SAID IS THAT THE COLUMN IT WOULD NEED HAS NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND A COLUMN WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT IS NOBODY'S, AND THE FIVE COLUMNS WITH HEADS GO TO THE OFFICE AND THE OFFICE READS THEM, AND THE SIXTH IS A COLUMN A PERSON STANDS IN A FIELD AND MAKES A JUDGMENT IN, AND THE OFFICE PRINTS AT ITS OWN FOOT THAT THE SIXTH IS NOT MAINTAINED AT ROW LEVEL AND IS CARRIED IN THE DISTRICT RETURN ONLY. SO THE OFFICE TOLD US IN PRINT, IN THE MIDDLE OF THE SHEET, THAT THE ONE COLUMN WHERE A PERSON'S JUDGMENT WOULD GO IS CARRIED SOMEWHERE ELSE, AND THE SOMEWHERE ELSE IS A DOCUMENT WE HAVE NEVER SEEN.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that no office lied and obstructed and delayed and softened anything, and the reason is that an office which maintains a column it does not read, and says so, is not doing a thing wrong, and that the reader of this body is not going to stand in about nine people and find a villain in a sentence that is correct.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-three, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I copied that sheet out on the twenty-seventh of last month and I kept the ruled lines under the one that has no head, and I have been asked twice this week why, and I am going to say it a third time in a room and not in a yard, and the reason is that a document has just said something about itself in about nine people and the only reason anybody could hear it is that somebody kept the empty place visible."**

**"If I had copied that column out as four blank lines with nothing ruling them, then in about four years a person would come to my book and see four blank lines and think the office had nothing. It has got something. It has got eleven. Eleven is a number and I have got it in a book and the office has got it in a return it has not sent, and those are two places to look and not one, and the difference between two places to look and one is about four years of somebody not being able to find anything and thinking there is nothing there."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he has been thinking about what a record says and the man of about fifty has been thinking about what a copy of a record looks like from the other side of four years, and that those are two questions and this holding has only ever had the habit of asking the first one.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eleven, and nobody improved it.

**"I put a wire on four pieces of wood in four days in the last week of last month and I have not put one on anything since, and I am going to say the one thing I know about the word damaged, and the reason is that a woman has just said that not one of four branches was damaged and I am the only person in this room who has held a wire against a cut face and knows what I could not tell."**

**"I could not tell. I had a girth of eleven inches and two sixteenths and a face of eight inches and nine sixteenths and a roll over the face and I wrote down that it was whole and smaller than it was, and I did not write down that it was undamaged, because I have got no figure for how fast a roll grows and without that figure I cannot say a face is closed and I certainly cannot say a branch is sound. I am not disagreeing with her. I am saying that at the ninth of the four places on that line there is a branch and this holding has no word for it yet and the word is not damaged."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he has been writing the word whole and has been pleased with it, and that whole is a word about a piece of wood and sound is a word about a living thing, and that a man pleased with a word for six days because it is weaker than the true word has not been wrong and has been lucky.

And then she wrote one thing out in her own hand and left it on the middle table, and the fen entered the whole of it.

> **THE SIXTH ITEM, written out on the twelfth of this month in about nine people in the seed house by Sera Quill, in her own hand, left on the middle table, and not read out again: FOUR BRANCHES CAME OFF A LIST IN MY LIFE FOR BEING IRREGULAR AND NOT ONE OF THE FOUR WAS DAMAGED. I HAVE SAID THAT TWICE IN TWO YEARS AND BOTH TIMES TO SOMEBODY WHO WAS NOT WRITING IT DOWN. I AM WRITING IT DOWN BECAUSE A PIECE OF PAPER CANNOT HOLD IT, AND BECAUSE A THING A PERSON HAS SAID TWICE AND NEVER WRITTEN IS A THING THAT GOES WHEN THEY DO, AND I AM NOT AWARE OF ANYBODY WHO HAS ANOTHER COPY OF IT BUT ME.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the count of bodies that have asked a question about who holds the pen rather than what a figure is is one and did not move this afternoon, and entered that nobody thanked her, and entered that she was not thanked then and has not been thanked in four months and that a person who is not thanked is a person who can be refused and can be asked again, and that the count of the times she has refused in this holding is not on this page and is not going to be.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-two, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am not going to make anything of her this afternoon and I am going to say that out loud, and the reason is that four people in this room had a sentence ready about a professional who arrives on foot and tells you which of her own six items will not survive your ledger, and I have watched all four of them not say it, and I want it entered that they did not say it and that I did not say it and that nobody in this room is going to be given the chance to hear me say it either."**

**"She is not an example. She is a woman of about fifty-six who has been in five rooms about a list and has not been one of the people in about nineteen years, and she came up a road on foot on the evening of the tenth because of something she said on the seventh, and one of her six items does not hold against a piece of paper, and that is a fact about a column with nothing at the head of it and not about her, and a person who turns that into a lesson has taken a working man's finding and made it into a sermon and the sermon is cheaper than the finding and I am not going to buy it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked and that he did not thank her, and entered the count of the times he has said one thing in a yard and got wrong as four and did not move, and that it was available to him twice in the last speech and he did not take it either time, and the reason is that the only place left to be careful about a person is the mouth.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**SIX ITEMS WENT IN AND SIX CAME BACK AND FIVE OF THEM ARE ON THIS PAGE IN FULL AND THE SIXTH IS ON THIS PAGE IN FULL AND THE SIXTH IS THE ONE THAT DID NOT HOLD, AND SHE SAID IT FIRST, AND A PROFESSIONAL WHO SAYS FIRST WHAT WILL NOT SURVIVE IS DOING THE OPPOSITE OF WHAT SHE HAS DONE IN FIVE ROOMS, AND I AM RECORDING THAT AS A FACT ABOUT HER AND NOT AS A THING ABOUT HER.**

**AND THE SIXTH COLUMN IS STILL BLANK ON ELEVEN OF NINETY-ONE ROWS AND THE DISTRICT RETURN IS NOT ENCLOSED AND SEVEN OF THE ELEVEN ARE NOT ON THIS LINE. A COLUMN WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT IS NOT A GAP IN A DOCUMENT. IT IS A PLACE A PERSON WAS EXPECTED TO STAND IN A FIELD, AND NINE PERSONS HAVE READ THE ELEVEN THIS WEEK, AND NONE OF THEM HAS ASKED HER WHY, AND SHE CAME UP THE ROAD ON FOOT.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twelfth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of an item, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and asked no question: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWELFTH, WHICH IS THE FORTY-SECOND FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN NINE DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN SEVEN TIMES AND FALLEN TWICE. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FORTY DAYS. NINE PEOPLE WERE IN THE SEED HOUSE WITH THE DOOR SHUT AND THE COPY OF THE LEDGER WAS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE. SERA QUILL SAID THE SIXTH ITEM BEFORE ANYBODY ASKED HER FOR IT, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND THE ITEM IS THAT FOUR BRANCHES CAME OFF A LIST IN HER LIFE FOR BEING IRREGULAR AND NOT ONE OF THE FOUR WAS DAMAGED. THE RECORD DID NOT HOLD IT AND THE RECORD IS CORRECT. THE FIFTH HEADED COLUMN IS CONDITION AT LAST WORK AND IS THE CONDITION OF A ROW AT THE MOMENT SOMEBODY LAST WORKED IT, AND THE WORD NO CUT IS THE CONDITION OF A STEM ON A MORNING WHEN NOBODY WORKED IT, AND THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT MOMENTS. THE SIXTH COLUMN IS THE COLUMN THAT WAS GOING TO HOLD THE OTHER ONE AND IT IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF NINETY-ONE ROWS, AND THE ELEVEN ARE LISTED IN A DISTRICT RETURN WHICH IS NOT ENCLOSED, AND SEVEN OF THE ELEVEN ARE NOT ON THIS LINE AND THIS HOLDING CANNOT GET TO THEM. A DOCUMENT THAT FAILS A TEST HAS SAID SOMETHING ABOUT ITSELF, AND WHAT THIS ONE SAID IS THAT THE COLUMN IT WOULD NEED HAS NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND THE OFFICE PRINTS IN THE MIDDLE OF THE SHEET THAT THE SIXTH COLUMN IS CARRIED IN THE DISTRICT RETURN ONLY. NO OFFICE LIED OR OBSTRUCTED OR DELAYED OR SOFTENED ANYTHING. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT THE ONLY REASON ANYBODY COULD HEAR THAT THIS AFTERNOON IS THAT SOMEBODY KEPT THE EMPTY PLACE VISIBLE IN A COPY, AND THAT ELEVEN IS A NUMBER IN TWO PLACES AND NOT ONE. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THAT HE WAS NOT GOING TO MAKE ANYTHING OF HER AND THAT FOUR PEOPLE IN THE ROOM HAD A SENTENCE READY AND DID NOT SAY IT, AND THAT A PERSON WHO TURNS A WORKING FINDING INTO A LESSON IS BUYING SOMETHING CHEAPER THAN THE FINDING. THE COUNT OF THE QUESTIONS HE HAS ASKED HER IS ONE AND IT IS STILL ONE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
