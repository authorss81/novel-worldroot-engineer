# Chapter 280

## A Fragment, Or A Whole Thing

The eighth of this month was a Thursday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the eighth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-five hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-eighth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the five days since the third of this month the series has fallen once and risen four times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in thirty-six days and the sentence saying so was written for the thirty-fifth morning out of thirty-seven this morning.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And then at about the eighth hour of the morning the crate was opened in about nine people in the cart shed at the node, and the oilcloth was folded back and the key was in it and the receipt was on the table beside it, and the thing inside the oilcloth was about the size of two hands together, and the fen entered the size and entered that he had put his own hands on it and had not known what he was going to feel and had not been surprised and had been disappointed, and that a man disappointed by a thing four hundred miles and four days off is a man who had wanted more out of a book than a book is.

**THE THING IN THE OILCLOTH IS A FRAGMENT AND NOT A WHOLE THING. IT IS A FRONT BOARD OF ABOUT THE SIZE OF A FIVE-POUND LOAF WITH A BEAD FIXED IN IT, AND ELEVEN PAGES. THE BEAD IS LIVING ROOTWOOD AND IT IS ALIVE, AND IT IS THE SIZE OF A PEA, AND IT HAS A CUT END AND NO COPPER ON IT AT ALL. IT IS NOT THE WITNESS BEAD AND IT IS NOT THE BEAD ON A COPPER COLLAR AND IT IS NOT PUT AGAINST ANYBODY'S ARM AND IT WAS NOT PUT AGAINST ANYBODY'S ARM THIS MORNING. THE ELEVEN PAGES ARE MOSTLY SURVEY PAGES AND MOST OF THE SURVEY PAGES ARE GONE, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS EVER KNOWN WHERE THEY WENT, AND THE ONE LINE HE WROTE UNDER THE FOUR ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH SAYS THAT HE IS NOT ASKING FOR ANY PAGE OF IT TO BE WRITTEN OUT FOR HIM, AND HE DID NOT ASK AND HE IS NOT GOING TO ASK.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the panel is not printed, and said it again in his own words: there is no panel, and the thing in the crate is a board and a bead and eleven pages, and a man who opens a crate in about nine people in the morning and reads three lines of print in a voice is not a man with an instrument in his hands.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the count of the uses of that instrument is one and it is a count of its own, and that it is not a second reading of the pulse at the west sill and it is not the witness bead, and that two counts in this book are the day of the month and the number of mornings on which he wrote a sentence, and that the count of the uses of that thing is neither of those and is not going to be added to either.

And the eleven pages were looked at for about nine minutes and the fen entered what was on them, which is that they are ruled in the same hand as the board and carry a date at the top of each and nothing else that this holding can read, and that eleven pages of a survey book is not a survey, and that a man who has eleven pages of a book has not got eleven pages of the book.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the reader of this body put his thumb against the outside of the bead and did not touch it, and the reason is that a bead of living rootwood with a cut end is the only living thing in that fragment and that a man who has been told on the seventh of this month that a thing which has not been predicted is not the same as a thing which is damaged has not got a hand that is careful, he has got a rule, and the rule is that he does not put a thumb on the only living thing in a room to see what it does.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody was asked about the bead and that nobody was going to be, and entered that the count of the uses of that instrument is one and it was not used this morning.

And then the front board, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it is nine lines.
**THE FRONT BOARD, in a hand this holding cannot read and does not know, ruled and not ruled, nine lines, entered whole and not summarized. EACH LINE HAS A DATE ON IT AND A PLACE ON IT AND ONE WORD. THERE IS NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THE COLUMN. THE FIRST EIGHT LINES ARE PLACES THIS HOLDING CANNOT GO TO. THE NINTH LINE IS A PLACE THAT IS ENTERED IN THE SAME HAND AS THE OTHER EIGHT AND IS ABOUT NINE MILES FROM THE STONE HEAD, AND THE DATE ON IT IS THE TWELFTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THE WORD ON IT IS NO CUT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in a cart shed, and entered that the man of about thirty-one of Silling read the nine lines out and that the reader of this body read them out again and that the two of them read the same nine lines and the two readings were the same.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eight, and nobody improved it.

**"I have put a wire on four pieces of wood in four days and I know what a cut looks like and I know what no cut looks like, and I am going to say the one thing I know about that word before anybody builds anything on it, and the reason is that a man brought this thing down a four-day road and about nine people are in a shed and one of us is going to be asked in about four years what the ninth line said and I would rather the answer be in the room now."**

**"The word is no cut and I believe it. And I am not going to call it a second witness to anything, and the reason is that a word and a figure are not two witnesses, they are one person saying two things. A hand wrote no cut on a board and a dial gave five hundred and ninety-seven hundredweight on the same morning about a different object nine miles away, and those two things being about the same day is not two people agreeing. It is a coincidence with a date on it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he had been about to write the ninth line down as something held, and that a man stopped by a man with a wire in his bag has been saved a figure, and that the figure he did not write is not in the count of the numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check, which is two, because a figure he did not write is not a figure he handed out.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have kept a book for eleven years and I have never in my life had a book come into this shed with pages missing out of it, and I am going to say what I am going to do about it, and the reason I am saying it in nine people and not to one is that whatever I do to that book is going to be what a person in about four years thinks a bookkeeper does to a damaged book."**

**"I am going to enter the loss as a line of its own, with the number of pages it should have and the number it has, and I am going to leave the ruled lines on my copy under the pages that are not there, the same as I did with the sixth column of the office's ledger on the twenty-seventh of last month, and for the same reason. An empty space in a copy that looks like a bookkeeper's hand is a lie about a book. A ruled line with nothing on it is a thing four people can look at."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is eleven and did not move this morning, and entered the reason once and not twice, which is that this was a shed and the count is for yards, and that he is not going to say that reason a third time in a season because a man who says the same reason three times has stopped having a reason and started having a habit.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-eight, and he said it in about nine people in a cart shed, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a column on that board with nine lines in it and nothing at the head of it, and there is a column in a copy of the office's ledger with ninety-one lines in it and nothing at the head of it, and I am not going to write those two things in one line of my own book, and I am going to say why I am not, and the reason is that a man who joins two things because they look alike has stopped having two things and started having one, and one thing that is half invented is worth less than two things that are both dull."**

**"And here is the whole of what has come down a road to this shed. Four hundredweight of boards came up the cart road in the fourth month and went west two days later, and a crate came down the four-day road last night, and the book is four hundred miles off in a room with a hasp and it has now been in this holding twice and both times it was a piece of it. A front board, a bead, and eleven pages. The most useful thing in it is a column with no heading and the second most useful thing in it is one line that says no cut about a stem nine miles from a stone head on a morning this holding has a figure for, and the two of those together are the first thing anybody has ever put against that board and it held, and holding is not a proof and I am not going to call it one."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this morning and that this was a shed and not a yard, and entered that the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and did not move and that the man of about thirty-one of Silling has just saved him from making it three, and that a man who is saved from a third by a man with a wire in a bag has not got a check and has got a warning.

And then the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**I WROTE THE FIFTH LINE UNDER THE FOUR ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH AND THE FIFTH LINE ASKED FOR NOTHING BUT WHAT WAS ON THE BOARD, AND WHAT CAME DOWN THE ROAD LAST NIGHT IS A BOARD, AND THAT IS THE LINE WORKING, AND I AM NOT GOING TO SAY THE LINE WAS RIGHT, BECAUSE A LINE THAT IS RIGHT BY LUCK IS NOT A RIGHT LINE AND IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS I WILL NOT BE ABLE TO TELL THE DIFFERENCE.**

**AND THE MOST OF THE SURVEY PAGES ARE GONE AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS EVER KNOWN WHERE THEY WENT AND I HAVE ASKED FOR NONE OF THEM, AND A PAGE REMEMBERED FOR A PERSON IS NOT A PAGE, AND THE PERSON WHO WROTE THAT ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH WAS ME AND I HAVE NOT REVISED IT.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the eighth of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a fragment, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the cart shed and did not improve one word of it: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE EIGHTH, WHICH IS THE THIRTY-EIGHTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FIVE DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS FALLEN ONCE AND RISEN FOUR TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTY-SIX DAYS. THE CRATE CAME DOWN THE FOUR-DAY ROAD ON THE SEVENTH OF THIS MONTH AND WAS OPENED IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE IN THE CART SHED AT ABOUT THE EIGHTH HOUR OF THE MORNING OF THE EIGHTH. THE OILCLOTH IS FOLDED BACK AND THE KEY IS IN IT AND THE RECEIPT IS ONE SHILLING AND FOURPENCE. THE THING IN IT IS A FRAGMENT AND NOT A WHOLE THING, BEING A FRONT BOARD, A LIVING BEAD THE SIZE OF A PEA WITH A CUT END AND NO COPPER ON IT, AND ELEVEN PAGES. MOST OF THE SURVEY PAGES ARE GONE AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS EVER KNOWN WHERE THEY WENT AND HE ASKED FOR NONE OF THEM. THE BEAD WAS NOT PUT AGAINST ANYBODY'S ARM. THE PANEL IS NOT PRINTED AND THE COUNT OF THE USES OF THAT INSTRUMENT IS ONE AND IT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN. THE FRONT BOARD CARRIES NINE LINES, EACH WITH A DATE, A PLACE AND ONE WORD, AND THERE IS NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THE COLUMN. THE NINTH LINE IS ABOUT NINE MILES FROM THE STONE HEAD, IS DATED THE TWELFTH OF LAST MONTH, AND SAYS NO CUT. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SAID THAT A WORD AND A FIGURE ARE NOT TWO WITNESSES BUT ONE PERSON SAYING TWO THINGS, AND THAT THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT HE WILL ENTER THE LOSS AS A LINE OF ITS OWN WITH THE NUMBER OF PAGES THE THING SHOULD HAVE AND THE NUMBER IT HAS, AND WILL KEEP THE RULED LINES UNDER THE PAGES THAT ARE NOT THERE FOR THE SAME REASON HE KEPT THEM UNDER THE SIXTH COLUMN OF THE OFFICE'S LEDGER, BECAUSE AN EMPTY SPACE THAT LOOKS LIKE A BOOKKEEPER'S HAND IS A LIE ABOUT A BOOK. THE READER OF THIS BODY DECLINED TO WRITE THE NINTH LINE DOWN AS SOMETHING HELD, AND DECLINED TO PUT THE COLUMN ON THE BOARD AND THE SIXTH COLUMN OF THE LEDGER IN ONE LINE OF HIS OWN BOOK. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE AND THIS WAS A SHED AND NOT A YARD. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
