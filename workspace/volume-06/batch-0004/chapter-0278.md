# Chapter 278

## The Fourth Line Again

The sixth of this month was a Tuesday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **nineteen**, and entered that all nineteen of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **forty-eight mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and nineteen not**, and that twenty-nine and nineteen are forty-eight, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

And the figure for the sixth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-four hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the three days since the third of this month the series has risen three times and has not fallen in them at all, and the fen entered both and did not improve them.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody.

And then at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and twenty days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and sixty-seven**, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that the two are forty-seven days apart and that the forty-seven has not moved in about four years, and entered that he has read them out loud on eight mornings in about three weeks and that a man who gives a different reason every time for one habit has not got eight reasons.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the tin on the shelf above the press is the March's tin and is a thing of this holding's and not a month, and that a person in this holding who uses that word for anything else is not talking about the tin.

And then at about the ninth hour of the morning the rota was read out in the yard at the node in about eleven people, and it was read out in a yard and not in the seed house, and the fen entered the reason before anybody asked for it, which is that a rota is a document and a document in this holding is read in the open, and that a rota read in a room would be a rota about four people and it is a rota about eleven.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven minutes, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nothing tender was said, and entered that about four of the eleven said nothing in a way that was different from the other seven, and that he did not count them.

And the man of the north row with the cough was in that yard and was not asked to be in it, and the count of yards he has stood in since the first of a month four months back is **twenty-six** and has moved, and he said nothing at all, and that is the ninth time in about seven weeks that he has stood in a yard of this holding and said nothing, and the count of that is nine, and the fen entered both counts and entered that they are two counts and have not been added together, and entered that a man who has stood in twenty-six yards since a month four months back and has said nothing in nine of them has not been in twenty-six arguments and has been in twenty-six rooms, and that this holding has not asked him in any of them and is not going to.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and that the count of the times he has offered to be somewhere he is not the engineer of record for is two and did not move this morning and is not this, and that the count of the times he has been told about a thing and has not been there for is two and did not move this morning, and that a man who reads a rota aloud on a Tuesday morning has been told about a thing and has been there, and that the two counts did not move on a morning when one of them could have.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-nine, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"The fourth line has come round nineteen times since this holding began counting it and there has been nobody on it nineteen times, and I am going to say what I think that is, and the reason I am saying it in a yard this morning and not yesterday in the seed house is that this morning is about a rota and a rota is a thing this holding says out loud."**

**"A line that is worked nineteen mornings out of forty-eight is a work line. A line that is empty nineteen mornings out of forty-eight is not a work line. It is a place in a table with Marden written at the head of it, and I have kept the count of it for four years, and the count is a count of mornings and not a count of anybody, and I would like that said in a yard while the rota is being read, because in about four years somebody is going to look at that line and see nineteen and think it means nineteen people owed work."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and entered that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is ten and it is eleven, and that he counted it in a book and not in that yard in about nine seconds, and that the movement is not his and never has been, and that it went up on a sentence about a table and not about a number, and that he is not going to improve that by counting what kind of sentence it was next time.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seven, and he said it in about eleven people in the yard, and nobody improved it.

**"The man of about fifty has said that nineteen is a count of mornings and not a count of anybody, and I agree with him and I am going to add the half of it that he did not say, and the reason is that he has been keeping that column for four years and I have been standing in the field the column is about, and those are two different kinds of knowing."**

**"Nineteen is not nineteen fields. There are more than nineteen fields above Marden and there are fewer than nineteen mornings in a season when a body of households would not work them, and the two numbers are not the same number and they do not stand in for one another. A rota line is a line of work. A field is a field. If anybody in this holding reads nineteen off that table and thinks that nineteen fields are owed somebody by somebody, then a piece of arithmetic has just turned into a debt, and a debt nobody agreed to is the thing this holding has spent four years not doing."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that the reader of this body has been writing nineteen into a book nineteen times without ever once asking what a field is, and that a man who counts a thing nineteen times without asking what it counts is a man counting his own handwriting.

And then at about the eleventh hour of the morning the reader of this body walked up to the headland above Marden by himself, and he was there about twenty minutes, and the fen entered the twenty and entered the reason, which is that a line that has been empty nineteen times is a thing a man goes and stands on, and that standing on a place in a table is not the same as walking a line of ground and he wanted to know which of the two it would feel like.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered what he saw, and entered that it was about eleven fields, and that the ground had not been worked this season and was not going to be, and that there was a ditch at the top of them with about four inches of water in it standing level and not moving, and that it is the ditch the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden has been walking for about eleven weeks, and that he did not walk it, and that he stood about four hundred yards from where it begins and did not go down to it, and the reason is that about ninety yards of it runs along the edge of ground nobody has spoken for, and that a man who stands at the top of a headland to look at a line he is not going to walk has come to the edge of something, and that an edge is a place to stop and not a place to begin.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-six, and he said it in about eleven people in the yard at the node, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say one thing about a line that is empty nineteen times out of forty-eight and I am going to say it in a yard and not in a room, and the reason is that a yard is where a thing gets said that wants company, and this one wants about eleven people, and because the person it is about is not in this yard and is not going to be told I said it."**

**"Nobody is blamed for a field that waited. That is true and I have entered it nineteen times and I am entering it a twentieth time this morning. And it is also true that a line which has been empty nineteen mornings is the only line in this rota anybody in this holding can name, and those two facts have been standing next to each other since the middle of the second month and neither of them is going to move, and a man who puts them next to each other in a yard is going to be asked which of them he means, and he is not going to know."**

**"So I am putting them next to each other and I am not choosing. The count of fields that have waited is nineteen and all nineteen of them are the fourth line and the rota stands at forty-eight in force and twenty-nine taken and nineteen not. The count of things this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and it did not move this morning and it was available to me in the second sentence of this and I went on."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that about four of the eleven looked at the headland while he was saying the second of those two sentences, and the reason is that they cannot see it from there and were looking at the fen and not at the ground.

And then the last thing of the day is not a figure and it is in his own book and the fen entered it and did not improve it.

**THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTY-FOUR DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO HAS NOW BEEN WRITTEN ON THIRTY-THREE OF THIRTY-FIVE MORNINGS. I HAVE WRITTEN THAT SENTENCE THIRTY-THREE TIMES AND THE NUMBER NINETEEN NINETEEN TIMES, AND THOSE ARE THE ONLY TWO RUNNING THINGS IN MY OWN BOOK, AND NEITHER OF THEM IS A FIGURE ABOUT THE GROUND, AND A MAN WITH TWO RUNNING SENTENCES AND NO MEASUREMENT HAS GOT A HABIT AND NOT A PRACTICE. A ROTA IS A DOCUMENT AND A DOCUMENT IN THIS HOLDING IS READ IN THE OPEN, AND THE FOURTH LINE HAS NOW BEEN READ IN THE OPEN NINETEEN TIMES WITHOUT A NAME AGAINST IT, AND A DOCUMENT WITH A PLACE IN IT AND NOBODY'S NAME IN IT IS NOT A ROTA ANY MORE, IT IS A LIST OF THE MORNINGS ON WHICH A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS WAS NOT ASKED, AND BOTH OF THOSE ARE TRUE, AND THE SECOND ONE IS MINE AND NOT THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY'S, AND HE SAID HIS HALF IN A YARD THIS MORNING AND I SAID MINE TO A BOOK AT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THIS EVENING, AND NEITHER OF US SAID THE OTHER'S HALF, AND THAT IS NOT A COLLUSION, IT IS TWO PEOPLE WHO KEEP SEPARATE BOOKS.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the sixth of this month, in about eleven people, at the node, on the standing of a rota, and not by the reader of this body, who was on the headland above Marden until about the eleventh hour and then came down: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS NINETEEN, AND ALL NINETEEN OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND NINETEEN NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE SIXTH, WHICH IS THE THIRTY-SIXTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN THREE DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN THREE TIMES AND HAS NOT FALLEN AT ALL. THE DAY-MINUS RULES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER AND THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND TWENTY DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SEVEN, AND THE TWO ARE FORTY-SEVEN DAYS APART AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE ROTA WAS READ OUT IN THE YARD AT THE NODE AT ABOUT THE NINTH HOUR OF THE MORNING IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE AND TOOK ABOUT ELEVEN MINUTES, BECAUSE A ROTA IS A DOCUMENT AND IS READ IN THE OPEN. THE MAN OF THE NORTH ROW WITH THE COUGH WAS IN THAT YARD AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE IN IT AND SAID NOTHING, AND THE COUNT OF YARDS HE HAS STOOD IN SINCE THE FIRST OF A MONTH FOUR MONTHS BACK IS TWENTY-SIX, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES HE HAS SAID NOTHING AT ALL IN A YARD IS NINE, AND THE TWO COUNTS HAVE NOT BEEN ADDED TOGETHER. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID IN THAT YARD THAT A LINE THAT IS WORKED NINETEEN MORNINGS OUT OF FORTY-EIGHT IS A WORK LINE AND A LINE THAT IS EMPTY NINETEEN MORNINGS OUT OF FORTY-EIGHT IS A PLACE IN A TABLE WITH A NAME AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND THAT HIS COUNT IS A COUNT OF MORNINGS AND NOT A COUNT OF ANYBODY. THE COUNT OF TIMES A PERSON WHO KEEPS A BOOK IN THIS HOLDING HAS SAID A THING IN A YARD THE ENGINEER OF RECORD HAD NOT THOUGHT OF IS ELEVEN AND THE MOVEMENT IS NOT HIS. THE READER OF THIS BODY WALKED UP TO THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN ALONE AT ABOUT THE ELEVENTH HOUR AND STOOD ABOUT TWENTY MINUTES AND SAW ABOUT ELEVEN FIELDS AND FOUR INCHES OF STANDING WATER IN A DITCH AND DID NOT WALK THE DITCH, AND THE REASON IS ON THE PAGE. HE SAID OUT LOUD IN THAT YARD THAT NOBODY IS BLAMED FOR A FIELD THAT WAITED AND THAT A LINE WHICH HAS BEEN EMPTY NINETEEN MORNINGS IS THE ONLY LINE IN THE ROTA ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING CAN NAME, AND THAT HE IS PUTTING THE TWO FACTS NEXT TO EACH OTHER AND IS NOT CHOOSING BETWEEN THEM. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTY-FOUR DAYS. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
