# Chapter 285

## What The Two Refusals Are For

The thirteenth of this month was a Tuesday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the thirteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-nine hundredweight.**

That is the forty-third figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in the ten days since the third of this month the series has fallen three times and risen seven times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And then at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and twenty-seven days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and seventy-four**, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that the two are forty-seven days apart and that this is the ninth morning in about a month on which he has read them out loud and that the reason this morning is that a room opened on Monday over a piece of paper and a room is not a thing a man can get to the bottom of in a week.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and did not move this morning and is not five, and that it has been four for about a month and a half now and that a count that sits still that long is either a man being careful or a man who has run out, and that he entered the same sentence about those two things on the second of this month and is not going to improve it.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon nine people were in the seed house with the door shut for the fifth time in eight days, and the form was on the middle table, and the man of about fifty read the form out in the state it was in and read back the two lines under it that were not lines on the form, and nothing in it was improved and nothing in it was explained.

> **THE FORM, as it stands on the middle table of the seed house on the thirteenth of this month, entered whole, in two hands on the face of it and neither of them the reader of this body's, and the reader of this body's hand is on the back of the same sheet:**
>
> **LINE ONE. SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE FIGURE THE READER OF THIS BODY PUT ON THAT TABLE ON THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH AND THE FIGURE THE FIRST REFUSAL NAMED. IT IS NOT ON THE FORM. IT IS ON THE BACK OF THE SHEET IN HIS OWN HAND WITH THE DAY AND THE HOUR ON IT, AND IT HAS NOT BEEN DELETED, AND A FIGURE THAT IS CROSSED OUT IS STILL IN A ROOM.**
>
> **LINE TWO. ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING, WHO SPOKE FOR ONE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS AND WAS NOT ASKED WHY. IT IS THE ONLY FIGURE WRITTEN ON THIS FORM.**
>
> **LINE THREE. BLANK. THE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS THAT LINE THREE BELONGS TO WAS NEVER ASKED FOR A FIGURE, AND THAT IS IN ANOTHER MAN'S BOOK, AND HE READ IT OUT.**
>
> **LINE FOUR. BLANK. THE SAME IS TRUE OF THE FOURTH LINE AND THE REASON IS THE SAME.**
>
> **AND UNDER THE FOUR LINES, IN TWO HANDS, TWO THINGS THAT ARE NOT LINES ON THE FORM. THE FIGURE THE SECOND BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS SPOKE FOR WAS ELEVEN HUNDRED YARDS, AND IT WAS SAID IN A ROOM AND NOT WRITTEN ON THIS FORM. A NAME WAS ALSO SPOKEN IN THAT ROOM AND IT IS NOT ON THIS FORM EITHER. BOTH WERE SAID BEFORE ANYBODY ASKED AND NEITHER WAS ASKED FOR TWICE.**
>
> **AND UNDER THOSE, ONE LINE, IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHICH HE READ OUT AND WHICH IS THE ONLY LINE ON THIS SHEET HE WROTE HIMSELF: A REFUSAL THAT NAMES A FIGURE IS A REFUSAL ABOUT A FIGURE. A REFUSAL THAT NAMES A PERSON IS A REFUSAL ABOUT A PERSON. A ROOM THAT HAS ONE OF EACH AND NO WORD ON WHICH IS WHICH HAS NOT MADE A DECISION.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two hours, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said, and that this is the fifth room in eight days and that he has not decided anything in any of them, and the reason is that a man who is in rooms too often is a man who is being useful, and that being useful is the most comfortable way of being the person who decides.

And the back of the sheet was turned over in that room and read out, and the fen entered the whole of it, because a figure crossed out and left on a table is still in the room and a room that thinks otherwise is a room that has lost it.

**THE BACK OF THE SHEET, IN THE READER OF THIS BODY'S OWN HAND: SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE FIGURE HE PUT ON THAT TABLE ON THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH AND THE FIGURE THE FIRST REFUSAL NAMED, NOT CROSSED OUT, AND UNDER IT THE NINTH OF THIS MONTH AND THE HOUR AND THE WORDS REFUSED BY A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS, AND UNDER THAT THE WORDS I PUT IT THERE MYSELF AND NOBODY ASKED ME TO, AND HE READ ALL THREE LINES BACK OUT LOUD. THE FIGURE HE WROTE DOWN FOR THE THIRD LAUNDER ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF LAST MONTH IS ALSO ON THAT SHEET, BEING ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVEN, AND THE TWO FIGURES ON THE BACK OF ONE SHEET ARE FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-FOUR APART AND NEITHER OF THEM IS A FIGURE ABOUT THE CHANNEL.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that a man who keeps a refused figure on the back of a sheet intends to be asked about it in about four years, and that the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and did not move, and that six hundred and twenty-one is not the third because it came off this holding's own dial in this holding's own book.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eleven, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a line on that sheet about nine acres coming off before the middle of next month and nobody in this room has said a word to me about it in eight days, and I am going to say one thing and then not again, and the reason is that if I say it twice it starts to sound like a woman reminding a room of something."**

**"I decided that on the twenty-sixth of last month and I have not changed it and nothing in this week has changed it and this room is not the reason it has not changed, because this room has not asked me about it once and a room that does not ask you a question does not get to be the reason you answered one. If this holding ever needs to know anything about my ground it is going to have to come and stand in it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the count of the questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and did not move this week, and that the count of the times she has asked this holding for anything is nil and did not move either, and that a column about a woman not being asked would be a column about this holding and not about her, and she has not asked for one.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-three, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say what the two refusals were for and I am going to say it once, and the reason is that a thing which has been in eight days' worth of rooms and has not been written down anywhere is a thing that about four years from now somebody will guess at, and what they guess at will be worse than what happened."**

**"They were for stopping a decision. That is all and it is a small thing. On the eleventh of last month four minutes in a yard in about nineteen people made a decision and nineteen people carried it and nobody was asked. This week five rooms in eight days have not made one, and the reason they have not made one is on a sheet on a table in two hands, and the two reasons are different kinds of reason, and that is on the sheet as well and it is the only line on the sheet a man wrote for himself."**

**"And it is not a victory and I am going to say why in the plain voice and not in a yard. Nothing about that channel has changed. The gap of about two feet is open and nothing has gone into it and nothing is going to. The water is still coming. Nine acres of the low ground of Marden come off before the middle of the sixth month because the woman who owns it decided that on the twenty-sixth of last month. The man who holds a fifth share is not in this holding and has not been asked and is not going to be asked by me. About ninety yards of eleven hundred has not been walked by anybody here. The sixth column of a public record is blank on eleven of ninety-one rows and the return that lists them is not enclosed."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and that nobody in that room improved one item of that list, and that one of the nine put a hand flat on the table when the ninety yards was named and took it off again, and that the fen did not see whose hand it was and is not going to ask.

And then the fourteenth of this month came round, a Wednesday, and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **twenty-one**, and entered that all twenty-one of them are the fourth line, and that the rota now stands at **fifty mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty-one not**, and that twenty-nine and twenty-one are fifty, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

And the figure for the fourteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and thirty-one hundredweight.**

That is the forty-fourth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the eleven days since the third of this month the series has fallen three times and risen eight times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And then at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and twenty-eight days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and seventy-five**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on ten mornings in about a month and that the reason this morning is that a man who is about to end something should be able to say the day out loud in front of somebody before he ends it.

And then at about the ninth hour of the morning, at the node, in a yard and not in the seed house, in about eleven people, the man of about fifty read out the whole of the rota and the form in one breath because the form has become part of the standing and nobody has moved it off the standing, and the man of the north row with the cough was in that yard and was not asked to be in it, and the count of yards he has stood in since the first of a month four months back is **twenty-seven** and has moved, and he said nothing at all, and that is the tenth time in about eight weeks that he has stood in a yard of this holding and said nothing, and the count of that is ten, and the fen entered both counts and entered that they are two counts and have not been added together.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and that both a yard and a room are on the standing now.

And the standing count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is ten and did not move this month, and it is not said in a yard and was not said in that yard this morning, and the promise about it was made out loud in a yard in the second month of this year and has held, and the fen entered that he has now put that number on a page twice in about eleven days and that twice is the whole of what he is going to do with it in this month.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in forty-two days and the sentence saying so has now been written on forty-one of forty-three mornings, and that the count of the series is forty-four figures on forty-four mornings and not one of the forty-four is the same as any of the other forty-three, and that the man of about fifty has written the count of the series down in his own book four times in eleven days and has not asked anybody whether it is a pattern, and that the reader of this body has not told him and is not going to.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody on either day and nothing tender was said in the seed house on the thirteenth or at the node on the fourteenth, and entered the whole of what the two refusals were for, and improved none of it.

**THE ONLY FIGURES IN THIS ARE THE FIGURES THE TWO BODIES GAVE. ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, OFF A STEELYARD, WITH TWO NAMES AGAINST IT IN TWO COLUMNS, FROM A MAN WHO SPOKE FOR ONE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS. AND ELEVEN HUNDRED YARDS, OFF A DITCH WALKED FOR ABOUT ELEVEN WEEKS BY A WOMAN WHO SAID IT IN A ROOM AND DID NOT WRITE IT ON ANYBODY'S FORM AND DID NOT MEASURE IT AND CALLED IT NOTHING BUT A LENGTH. NEITHER OF THE TWO FIGURES IS A FIGURE ABOUT THE CHANNEL. ONE OF THEM IS A FIGURE ABOUT A SHARE AND ONE OF THEM IS A FIGURE ABOUT A WALK. A ROOM THAT HAS TWO FIGURES IT CAN CHECK IS FURTHER ALONG THAN A ROOM THAT HAS ONE IT CANNOT, AND A ROOM WITH TWO FIGURES THAT ARE BOTH ABOUT SOMETHING ELSE IS NOT FURTHER ALONG, IT IS JUST BETTER INFORMED, AND I AM NOT GOING TO CALL THAT A TURN, AND I AM NOT GOING TO CALL IT NOTHING EITHER, BECAUSE A PERSON WHO PUTS A FIGURE DOWN AND A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS PUTS A DIFFERENT ONE IN ITS PLACE HAS DONE A THING, AND THE THING IS ON A TABLE IN THE SEED HOUSE WHERE ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING MAY WALK UP AND READ IT, AND THE QUESTION ON IT IS NOT ANSWERED, AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED, AND THAT LINE IS THE THIRD THING THAT HAS COME OUT OF A ROOM IN NINE DAYS AND IT IS NOT ONE OF THE TWO REFUSALS, AND I DID NOT WRITE ON IT, AND NOBODY DID.**
