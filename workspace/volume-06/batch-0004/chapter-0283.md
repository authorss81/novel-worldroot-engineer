# Chapter 283

## A Test Against A Public Record

The eleventh of this month was a Sunday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the eleventh went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and twenty-eight hundredweight.**

That is the forty-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in the eight days since the third of this month the series has fallen twice and risen six times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in thirty-nine days and the sentence saying so was written for the thirty-eighth morning out of forty this morning.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute.

And Sera Quill was at the seed house at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and she had come up the fen road on foot on the evening of the tenth at about the sixth hour, and nobody had sent for her, and the fen entered that nobody had sent for her and entered the reason, which is that she had said on the seventh of last month that in about four days somebody was going to give this holding a number and she would rather it had the shape of the number first, and that the four days had come and gone and she had not been sent for and had not gone, and that a person who says a thing is going to happen in four days and then turns up in about five weeks has not broken a word she said.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that about nine people were in that room and the door was shut, and that the count of the times the reader of this body has asked Sera Quill a question in this holding's book is one and it is still one and the one is the one from the seventh of a month five months back and it was answered with a no, and that he did not ask a second time this afternoon and is not going to.

And she put her information on the middle table in six lines in her own hand and she read five of them out and the man of about fifty copied those five into his own book in his own hand and read them back and nothing in them was improved.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the copy of the ledger of the Office of the Continuity for the maintenance of the Crown Engine was on the middle table under her six lines, ninety-one rows long, five columns with heads and a sixth with nothing at the head of it, blank on eleven of the ninety-one, and that it is a copy in the man of about fifty's hand with the ruled lines kept under the one that has no head so that the empty spaces do not look like his handwriting, and that the office's copy is the office's and this one is this holding's and the two are not the same document.

And then the first item was put against the record and the record held it, and the fen entered the whole of the working and improved none of it.

**ITEM ONE OF SIX. SHE WROTE: THE FORM IS NOT NEW. IT WAS PUT AGAINST THE FIFTH HEADED COLUMN, WHICH IS CONDITION AT LAST WORK, AND THAT COLUMN CARRIES A FIGURE OR A WORD ON ALL NINETY-ONE ROWS AND THE OFFICE SAYS SO AT ITS OWN FOOT AND IT IS TRUE. THE COLUMN WAS READ DOWN WHOLE AND IT CONTAINS NINE DIFFERENT WORDS ON NINETY-ONE ROWS. NINE WORDS IS NOT A VOCABULARY THAT GROWS. A COLUMN OF NINETY-ONE ENTRIES IN NINE WORDS OVER ELEVEN YEARS IS A COLUMN SOMEBODY HAS BEEN CHOOSING FROM, AND IT IS NOT A COLUMN SOMEBODY HAS BEEN INVENTING. THE ITEM HOLDS.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that a woman who has seen the form of a thing more than twice and could not say when she first saw it has been shown something better than when, and that better than when is not a date.

And then the second item was put against the record and the record held it.

**ITEM TWO OF SIX. SHE WROTE: THE ZONE IS NOT NEW. IT WAS PUT AGAINST THE FOURTH PAGE, WHICH IS THE LINE, AND THE FOURTH PAGE SAYS THE SEASON'S WORK ON THIS LINE IS PERFORMED IN ONE WINDOW, THAT THE WINDOW OPENS ON THE SIXTH OF A MONTH AND CLOSES ON THE ELEVENTH OF THE SAME MONTH, THAT IT IS NOT EXTENDED IN ANY SEASON AND NO WORK ON THIS LINE IS CARRIED OUT OF IT, AND THAT THE LAST DATE OF WORK ENTERED AGAINST THE LINE IS THE NINTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK. THE SIXTH TO THE ELEVENTH IS SIX DAYS AND THE NINTH IS INSIDE IT, AND THE FOURTH PAGE AND THE LEDGER AGREE WITH EACH OTHER ON THE DATE, AND THE ITEM HOLDS.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that two documents from one office in one hand agreeing is not two witnesses, and that consistency across a schedule and a ledger is worth more to a holding than any single sheet, and is not worth more than the count of two men writing the same figure, which is five and is not a check.

And then the third item was put against the record and the record held it, and the holding of it was not what anybody in that room expected.

**ITEM THREE OF SIX. SHE WROTE: A BRANCH THAT CANNOT BE PREDICTED COMES OFF A LIST, AND THE COMING OFF IS WRITTEN SOMEWHERE. IT WAS PUT AGAINST THE SIXTH COLUMN, WHICH EXISTS, WHICH IS RULED, WHICH RUNS ALL NINETY-ONE ROWS, AND WHICH IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF THEM. A COLUMN THAT IS THERE AND EMPTY IS A RECORD OF A THING THAT HAPPENED AND WAS NOT WRITTEN DOWN, AND THE ELEVEN ARE LISTED IN A DISTRICT RETURN, AND THE DISTRICT RETURN IS NOT ENCLOSED. THE ITEM HOLDS.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is eleven and did not move, and that this was a room and the count is for yards, and that he is not going to say that reason a fourth time in a season.

And then the fourth item was put against the record and the record held it, and the fifth was put against the record and the record held it, and the fen entered that the reader of this body read both of them out himself in the ordinary voice and did not improve them and that the two of them took about nine minutes together.

**ITEM FOUR OF SIX. SHE WROTE: THE INSTRUCTION HAS THREE NUMBERS IN IT AND NONE OF THEM IS ON ANY SHEET THIS HOLDING HAS BEEN GIVEN. IT WAS PUT AGAINST THE SHEET OF THE THIRD OF LAST MONTH, WHICH IS SIX NUMBERED LINES AND TWO AT THE FOOT, AND THERE IS NO FIGURE ON THAT SHEET THAT COULD BE A FRACTION, A COUNT OF READINGS, OR A TIME. THE ITEM HOLDS.**

**ITEM FIVE OF SIX. SHE WROTE: THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN AN UNUSUAL BRANCH AND A SICK ONE IS A DIFFERENCE AN INSTRUCTION MAKES AND NOT A DIFFERENCE THE GROUND MAKES. IT WAS PUT AGAINST THE FIFTH HEADED COLUMN AGAIN, AND THE OFFICE PRINTS THAT THE FIVE HEADED COLUMNS ARE COMPLETE ON ALL NINETY-ONE ROWS, AND THEREFORE SOMEBODY WROTE A WORD OF JUDGMENT NINETY-ONE TIMES, AND THEREFORE THE JUDGMENT IS IN THE RECORD AND IS NOT IN ANY INSTRUCTION, AND A THING IN THE RECORD IS SOMEONE ELSE'S WORD AND A THING IN AN INSTRUCTION IS SOMEBODY WHO IS NOT GOING TO BE IN THE ROOM. THE ITEM HOLDS.**

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-one, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say the thing this room has been doing since about the fourth hour this afternoon and I am going to say it once, and the reason is that five of six items have just been put against a public record and held, and every one of the five was a test against a document and not against a person, and I want it in the record in those words before the sixth one goes in."**

**"A public record is a document. It is correct and it is incomplete and both of those are printed on it. When a document passes a test that is a fact about the document. When a document fails a test that is also a fact about the document and not about whoever wrote it, and a document that fails a test has said something about itself, and the something it says is about the document."**

**"None of the five that held says one word about a cause and I am not going to let the sixth one be the one that does, and if it is, then this afternoon has cost her a page she did not have to spend."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that a professional who is not thanked can be asked again, and that a professional who is thanked has been paid and there is no way to know what for.

And then she said the one thing that was hers, and the count of times a person from outside this holding has said a thing in a room the engineer of record had not thought of is one and the count did not exist before this afternoon, and nobody improved it.

**"I have been in five rooms about a list and I am going to say one thing about this one and then I am going to stop, and the reason is that in the other five the list was on the wall and the people were in front of it, and in this room the list is on a table and I am one of the people, and I have not been one of the people in about nineteen years, and I would like somebody to notice that about me and not to make anything of it."**

**"The five of mine that held held for a reason that is not mine and I want it written down that way. They held because an office did its work properly, and the two documents in front of you are correct, and I did not make them correct and I cannot unmake them, and the only thing I brought up the fen road on foot is six lines and one of them has not been read."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that she was not thanked, and that the reader of this body had not thought of it, and the reason is that he has been putting her information against a record as though she were the instrument under test, and that she has just said in about nine people that she is one of the people and not the instrument, and that a man who has been doing that for two days has been doing it to somebody who came up here on foot.

And then the room came to the sixth line on her sheet, and the reader of this body did not read it out, and she looked at him not reading it, and nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered the reason in his own hand with the hour on it, which is that reading the sixth line out loud would have been asking her a question, and that asking her a question is the one thing he has not done in four years and is not going to do on a Sunday afternoon in a room with nine people in it, and that the count of the questions he has asked her is one and it is still one.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the sixth line on her sheet is about four words long and that he has read it and that he is not going to say what it is, and that reading a thing and asking a woman to say it out loud in front of nine people are two different acts and that he has done the first one and is not going to do the second one.

And the last two things of the day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**FIVE OF SIX HELD AGAINST A PUBLIC RECORD AND THE SIXTH WAS NOT READ OUT BY ME, AND A TEST IS A TEST AGAINST A DOCUMENT, AND A DOCUMENT THAT FAILS ONE WILL SAY SO ABOUT ITSELF AND NOT ABOUT HER, AND SHE KNOWS THAT AND SHE CAME UP A ROAD ON FOOT AND SHE HAS NOT HAD TO USE A SINGLE PIECE OF IT THIS AFTERNOON, AND I AM NOT GOING TO LET HER BE A LESSON IN SOMEBODY ELSE'S BOOK, INCLUDING MINE.**

**AND THE ORDER MATTERS: THE FOURTH PAGE, WHICH IS CORRECT, AND THE NINETY-ONE-ROW LEDGER, WHICH IS CORRECT AND INCOMPLETE AND SAYS SO AT ITS OWN FOOT, CAME DOWN ONE ROAD IN A LEATHER CASE AND WERE READ OUT IN A YARD IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THE SIXTH LINE ON HER SHEET IS ABOUT A BRANCH, AND EVERY DOCUMENT IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK THAT IS ABOUT A BRANCH IS INCOMPLETE IN THE SAME PLACE, AND I AM NOT WRITING THAT IN ONE LINE.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the eleventh of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a test, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and did not read the sixth line: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE ELEVENTH, WHICH IS THE FORTY-FIRST FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN EIGHT DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN SIX TIMES AND FALLEN TWICE. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN THIRTY-NINE DAYS. SERA QUILL CAME UP THE FEN ROAD ON FOOT ON THE EVENING OF THE TENTH AND NOBODY HAD SENT FOR HER. NINE PEOPLE WERE IN THE SEED HOUSE WITH THE DOOR SHUT. SHE PUT HER INFORMATION ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IN SIX LINES IN HER OWN HAND AND READ FIVE OF THEM OUT AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY COPIED THOSE FIVE AND READ THEM BACK. THE COPY OF THE LEDGER OF THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY FOR THE MAINTENANCE OF THE CROWN ENGINE WAS ON THE TABLE UNDER THEM, BEING NINETY-ONE ROWS WITH FIVE HEADED COLUMNS AND A SIXTH WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT, BLANK ON ELEVEN OF THE NINETY-ONE. FIVE OF HER SIX ITEMS WERE PUT AGAINST THAT RECORD AND FIVE HELD. THE FIFTH HEADED COLUMN, CONDITION AT LAST WORK, CARRIES A FIGURE OR A WORD ON ALL NINETY-ONE ROWS AND CONTAINS NINE DIFFERENT WORDS. THE FOURTH PAGE AND THE LEDGER AGREE ON THE LAST DATE OF WORK AGAINST THE LINE, BEING THE NINTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK, WHICH IS INSIDE A WINDOW RUNNING FROM THE SIXTH OF A MONTH TO THE ELEVENTH AND NOT EXTENDED IN ANY SEASON. THE SIXTH COLUMN EXISTS, IS RULED, AND IS BLANK ON ELEVEN ROWS, AND THE ELEVEN ARE LISTED IN A DISTRICT RETURN WHICH IS NOT ENCLOSED. THE SHEET OF THE THIRD OF LAST MONTH CARRIES NO FIGURE THAT COULD BE A FRACTION, A COUNT OF READINGS, OR A TIME. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD THAT A PUBLIC RECORD IS A DOCUMENT, THAT A DOCUMENT IS CORRECT AND INCOMPLETE AND PRINTS BOTH, AND THAT WHEN A DOCUMENT PASSES OR FAILS A TEST THE FACT IS ABOUT THE DOCUMENT AND NOT ABOUT WHOEVER WROTE IT, AND THAT NONE OF THE FIVE THAT HELD SAYS ANYTHING ABOUT A CAUSE. THE ROOM THEN CAME TO THE SIXTH LINE ON HER SHEET AND HE DID NOT READ IT OUT, AND THE REASON IS ON THE PAGE WITH THE HOUR ON IT, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES HE HAS ASKED HER A QUESTION IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS ONE AND IT IS STILL ONE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
