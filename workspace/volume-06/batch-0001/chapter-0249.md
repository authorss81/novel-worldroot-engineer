# Chapter 249

## The Fifth Line Is False

The fourth of this month was a Friday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour, and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited.

The seventh line of that sheet says that the fields waiting is entered by whoever enters anything with the day and the hour on it, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited, and the fen entered that nobody is blamed, and entered the number, which is **eleven**, and entered that all eleven of them are the fourth line, and entered that the fourth line has now come round on eleven mornings since the rota came into force and has been empty on eleven of them.

And then at about the eleventh hour of the morning the covenant was read out again, all six lines, standing up, in the hand it was written in, by the woman who keeps the four books, and read back by the man of about fifty, and the reader of this body did not hold it and was not asked to hold it.

The asking was entered. Renn Ashby, of about twenty-six, of Havergate, one of the six who take the rota in turns, had asked for it at the kerb at about the seventh hour of the morning, and he had given the reason first, and the reason was that the fifth line is the only line of the six with a figure on it and a figure that has stopped being true is still on a document four villages have signed and he wanted to hear it said in a yard.

And he was given a copy of it, and he had asked for one and had been given none the only other time he had asked, and the fen entered that he was given one and entered that the count of times that man has asked this holding for a copy of a document is two and that he has had none and one.

> **SIX. NOBODY IS TO BE THANKED FOR ANY PART OF THIS.**
>
> **THE FOURTH LINE OF THAT SHEET WAS NOT READ OUT AND THE REASON IS ENTERED. It is the refusal clause and it carries the one word in the whole of the five so far that is spent, and the woman who keeps the four books read the other five without a pause and did not look up, and nobody asked her why, and the fen entered that nobody asked her why.**

The second line of that covenant and the fourth line of the rota are the same line and not two lines, and the reader of this body read the number four twice in about eleven minutes and entered both times that they were the same line, because he had spent the morning believing they were two.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that about eleven of the nineteen people in that yard were of the four bodies of households and eight were not, and that the eight were not asked to stay and did not stay, and that the reader of this body noticed which eight and did not write the eight down.

> **THE COVENANT OF THE FOUR ELBOWS, six lines, read out standing up a second time, in about nineteen people, at Errow Reach, at about the eleventh hour of the morning of the fourth of this month, by the woman who keeps the four books, in the hand it was written in, and read back, entered whole and not summarized:**
>
> **ONE. THE NODE IS WORKED, NOT PATTERNED. Four bodies of households take their own turn on their own morning, a turn is two hours at the second hour, one person holds and two or four work, and nobody is on the roll, and the third clause of the Assembly is not met and is not pretended to be met.**
>
> **TWO. THE FOURTH LINE IS MARDEN'S AND IS EMPTY, AND IT IS WRITTEN HERE THAT IT IS EMPTY, AND NOBODY IS TO PUT A NAME IN IT UNTIL MARDEN ASKS FOR ONE, AND MARDEN HAS NOT ASKED.**
>
> **THREE. A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS MAY STOP ANY TURN OF ITS OWN AT ANY TIME, WITHOUT SAYING WHY AND WITHOUT SAYING WHY BEFORE.**
>
> **FIVE. THE FIGURE OF WHAT THIS NODE DELIVERS IS ABOUT FIFTEEN HUNDRED HUNDREDWEIGHT A DAY AND THE FIGURE OF WHAT FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS COUNT THAT THEY NEED IS ABOUT TWO THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS SIX HUNDRED HUNDREDWEIGHT A DAY, AND THAT FIGURE IS THE SAME FIGURE IT WAS BEFORE THIS SHEET WAS DRAWN, AND THIS SHEET DOES NOT CHANGE IT.**
>

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was longer than a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nobody said anything tender.

And the woman who keeps the four books said the second thing of hers of this week, and she gave the reason for saying it before she said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is fourteen, and nobody had asked her and she is not one of the seven that decide and has never been asked to be.

"**A sheet that says a figure cannot change is a sheet that has to be torn up. That is not a hard thing to say and it is the hardest thing anybody in this yard has said this week, and I want it said in about nineteen people and not in a room, and I am not going to say it in a room.**"

"**I have kept the four books for eleven years. I have torn up two of them. I have mended none of them. Neither of the two was torn up because a figure in it was wrong. One was torn up because a body of households had counted a thing two ways for six years and the second way was better and the first way was in four books in four hands, and the only way to get one way was to have none of the other one left. The other was torn up because a household had gone and a line on it had been the only place its name was written down, and keeping it was a way of keeping the line, and keeping the line was a way of keeping a name in a place where nobody could see it, and that is not a book.**"

"**And I want the fifth line torn up, and I am not going to be the one who tears it up, and I am going to say here in about nineteen people that I said that, because in about four years somebody is going to find out whether it was torn up or not, and if it was not torn up I do not want it to be found out that I said nothing.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked her, and entered that the reader of this body did not say he would tear it up and did not say he would not, and entered that the fen has checked the sentence of his own in the cart shed on the third of this month and it is in the book and it stands and it is about a sheet and a name and not about a fifth line, and that the two sentences are about the same day and are not about the same thing, and that a man who has two sentences in a week about two documents is a man who is writing more, and that writing more is what he has been doing since Wednesday and that he entered why.

And then the four bodies of households asked whether they were to answer the sheet that came up the fen road on the third, and the reader of this body gave the answer out loud, in about nineteen people, at about the second hour of the afternoon, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-one, and he was not asked for the answer and he gave it anyway and the fen entered that he gave it unasked and did not improve it afterwards.

"**No. And I am going to say all of it and I am not going to leave the easy half out, because the easy half is the one that gets written down and the hard half is the one I am actually saying.**"

"**The easy half is that the fifth line pays for a flow a body of households counted as its own and is able to show a figure for, and the flow is up, and there is nothing to claim, and one shilling and twopence the hundredweight is the best rate these four villages have ever been offered and none of the four of us can put a hand on a hundredweight of it.**"

"**The hard half is that a claim is not a way of getting money. A claim is a way of being on a list, and being on a list is a way of being counted, and the first number an office writes down about a body of households is the number that office will use about that body of households for the next twenty years, and it will use it about us in a room we will not be in, and there is nothing we can do about that and there is nothing we can do about not being in it either.**"

"**And underneath both of those there is a third thing and I am the only person in this yard who is going to say it, and I would rather somebody else said it. Four bodies of households have not been counted by anybody for eleven years. A claim is a way of being counted. And I am standing in about nineteen people saying no to a way of being counted, and the reason I am saying no is that I do not know what the counting is for, and I have got a sheet in a book in a seed house with a name on the bottom of it and I do not know what that office wants counted, and a man who says no to a counting on the grounds that he does not know the reason for it is not a careful man, he is a man with a preference, and mine is that a body of households that has been left alone for eleven years should be left alone for one more season until somebody can say out loud what it is being left alone for.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And the fen entered that the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is four and it is four this afternoon, and entered that this one was available to him before he had said the third sentence of it and that he went on, and entered that a man who cannot tell whether a thing is a fifth yet is a man who has a fifth coming and does not know its shape, and that the count stands at four and the fen is not going to say it does not move on the twentieth of this month.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing he had said once before this week, in about nine people, at the node, in the third month, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fourteen.

"**I keep the rota. I have kept it since it came into force and I write the mornings in and the mornings not taken in and the fields that waited in, and the eleven on that page are all fourth line, and I am going to say the thing about that page that I have not said in about eleven weeks.**"

"**A rota that writes down a turn a body of households did not take is a rota that is keeping a column against that body of households, and the number in that column goes up every fourth day whether anybody in this yard means anything by it or not, and it is not a number about a field. It is a number about us. The field is not being worked and so the field is not being counted by anybody, including by me, and I have never once gone up that headland to look at a turn that was not being taken, and there are eleven of them.**"

"**And I am not going to stop writing the column. A man who stops writing down the mornings a village does not take has decided that the village is not part of this, and it is part of this, and the column is the only place in this holding's book where Marden's no is written down at all.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he was not thanked and did not ask to be, and entered that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of moved on Thursday last and is five and did not move again this afternoon, and that two men keeping books in this holding have now said one thing each this week and that the reader of this body was the engineer of record in about nine of the eleven people and that a man who is in nine of the eleven people is not an outsider and is not going to be told things the way an outsider is told things, and that the fen has entered all three of those and has entered that he would rather be an outsider in about eleven people this afternoon.

And then Renn Ashby asked a question in about nine words at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and the fen entered the question and the answer and the length of the silence after the answer, which was about a minute.

"**Then has anybody asked Marden what it thinks about the figures?**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the answer, which was nobody, and entered that the man of the north row with the cough had stood in that yard at Errow Reach on Wednesday at about the second hour of the afternoon in about nineteen people and Marden's fields had waited a quarter mile above that yard at that hour, and that Marden is the fourth line of both documents, and that the reader of this body had told four bodies of households on Wednesday where their water was coming from and had not asked the one body of households whose ground it was going on to.

And the fen entered that this is the second thing he has got wrong in three days, and that the count of things he has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is a count of one kind of wrong and does not move, and that he has just done another kind, and that both of the kinds are in this book on the same two pages and that he has not added them together and is not going to, because one of them is a thing a man says and the other one is a thing a man does not say, and a book that has one column for the first kind and none for the second kind is a book that is going to let a man be right about his mouth.

> Entered by the man of about fifty, in his own hand, at about the sixth hour of the afternoon of the fourth of this month, in a yard at Errow Reach, in about nineteen people, on the standing of a document and of an answer, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and was not on the road: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS ELEVEN, AND ALL ELEVEN OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE. THE COVENANT WAS READ OUT A SECOND TIME, ALL SIX LINES, AT THE ASKING OF RENN ASHBY, WHO WAS GIVEN A COPY AND WHO HAD ASKED TWICE AND HAD NONE AND ONE. THE WOMAN WHO KEEPS THE FOUR BOOKS READ IT AND SAID THAT A SHEET THAT SAYS A FIGURE CANNOT CHANGE IS A SHEET THAT HAS TO BE TORN UP, AND THAT SHE HAS TORN UP TWO IN ELEVEN YEARS AND MENDED NEITHER, AND THAT NEITHER WAS TORN UP BECAUSE A FIGURE WAS WRONG, AND THAT SHE IS NOT GOING TO BE THE ONE WHO TEARS UP THE FIFTH LINE AND WANTS IT ENTERED THAT SHE SAID SO IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE AND NOT IN A ROOM. THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS ASKED WHETHER THEY WERE TO ANSWER THE SHEET OF THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH AND THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID NO UNASKED AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST, AND THE REASON WAS THAT A CLAIM IS A WAY OF BEING COUNTED AND THAT FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAVE NOT BEEN COUNTED BY ANYBODY FOR ELEVEN YEARS AND THAT HE DOES NOT KNOW WHAT THE COUNTING WOULD BE FOR. THE COUNT OF THINGS HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FOUR AND IT IS FOUR THIS AFTERNOON. NOBODY ASKED MARDEN. THE FEN ENTERED THAT HE HAS NOW GOT TWO THINGS WRONG IN THREE DAYS AND THAT THE COUNT IS A COUNT OF ONE KIND AND DOES NOT MOVE FOR THE OTHER KIND, AND THAT BOTH KINDS ARE ON THE SAME TWO PAGES AND HAVE NOT BEEN ADDED TOGETHER.**
