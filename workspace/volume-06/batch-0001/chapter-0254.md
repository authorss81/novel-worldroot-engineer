# Chapter 254

## Eleven People And Nine Acres

The tenth of this month was a Thursday and the nineteenth ninth-day return was made at about the second hour of the afternoon at the node, and it was weighed and read out and read back and read one at a time by each of the four bodies of households where they could see the dial, and a name went into BY and a name went into WITNESSED BY, and the ninth name-pair in eleven years went in.

**One hundred and fifty-one hundredweight.**

The one before that was one hundred and forty, and the four before that were ninety-one, eighty-nine, ninety and ninety-two, and this one is eleven more than the one before it and about sixty more than the four.

And Harlan Vetch, of about fifty-eight, took the chalk out of his own pocket and stood at the second rule on the board for about nine seconds and did not write in it and put the chalk back, and nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he said nothing, which is the fourth time, and entered that the count of the returns at which he has not written in that rule is three and that this holding does not know what was in the rule on the seventeenth and is still not going to ask.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden came up onto the shoulder from the east and the reader of this body went out to the kerb with the man of about fifty and the two of them walked the third launder together for the first time in about eleven weeks, and she was not sent for and was not asked and was not one of the seven that decide and has never been asked to be.

She said her two things at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, standing at the kerb, and gave the reason for saying them first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one, and she said them to about nine people and not to him, and they were not a speech.

"**The water has been standing against that gate since about the second of this month and I have walked past it every morning for about nine days and I did not open the gate because it was not mine to open, and the gate itself has not been opened since before I began to count.**"

"**Nine acres, and about eleven of us work it, and it came up on about the fourth and there has been no rain in it in a season, and I know what a crop in the wrong month costs because I have paid it, and I am not going to tell you what it costs in your yard because it is not your figure to pay.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked her, and entered that the reader of this body did not ask her one question in about an hour, and entered that the count of the questions he has asked her in this holding's book is nil, and entered that a man of thirty-one who has been wrong in a yard eight days ago has the strongest reason in the world to ask a woman of about thirty-eight one question and did not ask it, and that the reason he did not is that she had given the reason first and he does not take a reason away from anybody.

And they walked the eleven hundred yards.

It is a cut channel and it is about two feet wide and it is cut to stone and it has been cut since anybody can remember, and it has a drop of about a foot in the whole eleven hundred yards, which is a fall of about one in a thousand, and that is enough to keep it moving and not enough to empty it, and the reader of this body measured the drop twice because it is the one thing about a channel of that length that can be checked with a string and a stick and does not need anybody's permission. The gap at the kerb is about two feet long because of the knee of a man of sixty-eight on the eleventh of the first month, and the standing water in the bottom of it is about nine inches deep all the way down, and it is moving, and the man of about fifty put his hand in it at about three places and said it was the same water at all three, and the reader of this body put his rule against the bottom of it at about three places and wrote the figures down and the figures are four inches and four inches and four and a half inches, and the half inch is the figure of the twenty-first of a month eight months back off a different well and is not the same half inch and is on the page in two books.

And at the end of the eleven hundred yards there is a stone head and a field gate, and the gate is shut, and there is about nine inches of water standing against the inside of it, and the water is not moving, and on the far side of the gate the ground is green.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that there is about nine inches of water behind that gate and that the green on the far side of it is over the whole of the nine acres and about a hand high and that nobody put it there and that nobody willed it and that it is the fourth month and that about eleven people of a village of about three hundred have worked that ground in a normal year and that a body of households is not going to get a normal year out of this one and that this holding has taken it out of them in about nine days by doing nothing at all.

And then the two options were worked out on the ground, with figures on two boards, and both sets of figures are entered and neither is improved on, and the reader of this body did not choose between them on that afternoon and the fen entered that he did not and entered the hour.

**One. Put boards in the cut at the kerb.** Then five hundred and seventy-five hundredweight a day stops at the kerb and goes into the ground under the kerb, and the ground under the kerb is the same ground the middle is standing in, and the middle is about four feet across and nine feet off the hollow and it was a foot and one inch lower at about the second hour of the twenty-second of a month two months back than it was at about the second hour of the fourteenth of that month, and nobody in this holding has ever put a unit on that fall and the fen is not going to put one on it to-day, and the two figures are not in the same unit and the reader of this body knows that and has entered that he knows it. **Five hundred and seventy-five hundredweight a day, stopped, goes onto a thing that has already come down a foot and one inch in eight mornings, and the reader of this body cannot say what that does, and a man who cannot say what a thing does may still say that the thing exists.**

**Two. Leave the cut open.** Then about five hundred and seventy-five hundredweight a day goes onto about nine acres of the low ground of Marden in the fourth month behind a gate that has not been opened, and the ground is green, and about eleven people are going to have to decide in about a fortnight what to do with a crop that arrived without them, and this holding has not asked them and is not going to ask them to-day, and the reason it is not going to is on the board and is in the book and it is that the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said the reason first and gave it to him and he took it and did not improve it.

And then the man of about thirty-five of an Assembly in the Thornwild was at the far end of the eleven hundred yards, at the stone head, and had been there since about the second hour, and nobody had sent for him and nobody had asked him to come, and the fen entered that he had come on his own account and at his own cost and out of a road that is not a cart road.

He said the one thing he can say, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is five.

"**For about ninety yards of this channel it runs along the edge of ground that nobody has spoken for, and I have written to a room about it and the room has told me in writing that it has no standing to make that ground a party to anything and does not propose to acquire one, and I have not taken it further and I am not going to.**"

"**I am not going to tell you whose it is. I do not know whose it is. If I did know I would be the wrong man to say, because a man who knows is a man with a reason, and a man without a reason is the only kind anybody in a yard can act on. You may lay your figure on the ground either side of it and you may not lay it on that ninety yards, and I have not asked you to do either and I am not going to.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he was not thanked, and entered that the reader of this body did not ask the man of about thirty-five one question, and that the count of the questions he has asked him in this holding's book is three and it is three.

And then the reader of this body said the thing about the two numbers and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-eight.

"**There is a count in this holding's book of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check. It is two. It has been two for about four years and the first one of the two is not named in any of these books and is not going to be named in them.**"

"**I am not going to make a third one out of this week. I have got a figure and I have got a need and they agree, and I have had a launder running for nine days with a saw-cut in it that I told four bodies of households was the other side of the ground, and if I put a number on a page in the next two days that I cannot check from this holding, then this holding will have handed out three, and I would rather it handed out two and the second one unfinished.**"

"**And the count of things I have written not known against is five and it is six, and the sixth is a figure nobody ever wrote down, and it is this. That launder carried something for about eleven years before the eleventh of the first month and the figure it carried is in none of the four books, and the books are the record, and the unit of that figure is not known and is not going to be got out of anybody, and I am entering it as not known and I am not going to supply it by inference, because the one thing I can do with a number I do not have is invent it, and I have seen what that looks like in four years and it looks like a page with a figure on it and a week afterwards somebody reading it.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the reader of this body said the first two of those sentences out loud in about nine people and wrote the third one down afterwards at about the seventh hour in the cart shed and read it back twice.

And then at about the sixth hour of the evening the man of about fifty said that he would tell the three other bodies of households himself at the node in the morning, and that Marden's was the fourth line and could not be told by him, and the reader of this body did not thank him, and did not say that he would do the fourth one himself, and did not say that he would not, and entered all three of those.

And the reader of this body was in the cart shed at the node from about the seventh hour of the evening until about the eleventh hour with a lamp and a sheet of his own paper and wrote eleven lines down about what about nine acres of winter wheat does when it comes up green in the fourth month, and the eleven lines are correct, and every one of them is a thing a man of about thirty-eight of Marden already knows without being told by anybody, and he wrote them and read them back twice and he did not read them to anybody.

He wrote the reason under them in his own hand, and it is in the cart shed at the node on the back of the same sheet, and it is this.

> **I HAVE ELEVEN LINES IN THIS SHED THAT WOULD LET A FARMER OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT WORK OUT WHAT HER OWN FIELD IS GOING TO DO AND WHAT IT COSTS HER AND WHAT SHE CANNOT DO ABOUT IT. I HAVE NOT READ THEM TO ANYBODY. I AM GOING TO HAVE THEM IN THE MORNING WHEN I GO BACK INTO A YARD WITH ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE IN IT, AND A MAN WITH ELEVEN CORRECT LINES IN A SHED IS A MAN WHO IS GOING TO BE THE ONE WHO SAYS WHAT HAPPENS, AND WHAT HAPPENS IS A THING THAT HAS TO BE SAID BY THE PERSON IT HAPPENS TO. I HAVE NOT WORKED THAT OUT YET AND THE ELEVEN LINES ARE NO USE TO ME IN THE MORNING AND I AM NOT GOING TO BURN THEM EITHER.**

And then he put the lamp out and went to bed, and the fen entered that he did not sleep, and entered that the count of nights this holding's book records that he has not slept through in four years is twelve, and that the twelfth is the night of the ninth of this month, and that the number is not going in any column and is not going in the standing and is only in the book, and that a man who keeps a count like that in a book nobody reads is a man who is counting something he cannot do anything about, and that the reader of this body has entered that he does not know what it is, and that the count of things written not known against is five and this is not one of the five because it is not a thing about the ground.

> Entered by the man of about fifty, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the afternoon of the tenth of this month, in about nine people, on the standing of a figure and of a series of figures, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and was not on the road: **THE NINETEENTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AND WEIGHED AT ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, AND IT IS THE NINTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS, AND THE ONE BEFORE IT WAS ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY AND THE FOUR BEFORE THAT WERE ABOUT NINETY, AND HARLAN VETCH LEFT THE SECOND RULE BLANK AGAIN AND SAID NOTHING. THE FIGURE OF FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT A DAY ON THE THIRD LAUNDER GOES DOWN A CHANNEL ELEVEN HUNDRED YARDS TO A STONE HEAD AND A FIELD GATE, AND THE GATE IS SHUT, AND THERE IS ABOUT NINE INCHES OF WATER STANDING AGAINST THE INSIDE OF IT, AND THE GROUND ON THE FAR SIDE OF THE GATE IS GREEN. BEHIND THAT GATE ARE ABOUT NINE ACRES OF THE LOW GROUND OF MARDEN WORKED BY ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE, AND IT WAS FOUND BY THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN, WHO IS NOT ONE OF THE SEVEN THAT DECIDE AND WAS NOT ASKED AND WAS NOT SENT FOR. THE READER OF THIS BODY ASKED HER NO QUESTION IN ABOUT AN HOUR. TWO OPTIONS WERE WORKED OUT ON TWO BOARDS AND BOTH ARE ENTERED AND NEITHER IS IMPROVED, AND NO CHOICE WAS MADE IN THEM TO-DAY. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FIVE OF AN ASSEMBLY IN THE THORNWILD WAS AT THE STONE HEAD AND SAID THAT FOR ABOUT NINETY YARDS THE CHANNEL RUNS ALONG THE EDGE OF GROUND NOBODY HAS SPOKEN FOR, AND DID NOT SAY WHOSE. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND IT IS TWO. THE COUNT OF THINGS WRITTEN NOT KNOWN AGAINST IS SIX AND THE SIXTH IS THE FIGURE THAT LAUNDER CARRIED BEFORE THE ELEVENTH OF THE FIRST MONTH, WHICH IS IN NONE OF THE FOUR BOOKS. THE READER OF THIS BODY WROTE ELEVEN LINES IN THE CART SHED ABOUT WHAT NINE ACRES OF WINTER WHEAT DOES WHEN IT COMES UP GREEN IN THE FOURTH MONTH AND DID NOT READ THEM TO ANYBODY. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
