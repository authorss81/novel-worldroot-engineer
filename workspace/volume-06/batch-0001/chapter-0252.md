# Chapter 252

## What She Knows

The seventh of this month was a Monday and Sera Quill was at the seed house at about the second hour of the afternoon, in about eleven people, and she had come up the fen road on foot from the north gate with the same bag in each hand that she had come up with on the Sunday evening, and nobody had sent for her, and the reader of this body had not gone to the road.

She is fifty-eight and she has been an engineer longer than the reader of this body has been alive, and she had been an architect in the office that wrote the sheet, and the fen entered that in a yard of about eleven people on the seventh of this month and did not improve it.

And the reader of this body gave the reason out loud before he asked her for anything, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-five, and the reason was given before the question and the question was not asked.

"**I have not asked you a question in about four years and I have not written to you once in about four years, and a yard is the only place I am going to ask you in, and there are about eleven people in it and about four of them are going to hear whatever you say, and I am telling you that before you say it and not after, and I am not asking you to go outside and I am not asking you to wait.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he had not asked her to go outside and that she had not gone outside, and entered that the four people who are going to hear it are the person who keeps the seed house and the woman who does the sums and the man of about fifty and the clerk, and entered that he counted them and that counting them in a yard is a man reading a room and that he did it anyway because he wanted to know the number.

And Sera Quill looked at the sheet of the third of this month, which was on the table in the middle on top of the three books that live there with the day and the hour on it and the acknowledgment line unsigned, for about nine seconds, and then she said the thing she had come to say, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one.

"**I have seen the form of that before. I am going to say that in about eleven people because I would rather it were in about eleven people than in a room, and I am going to give you the reason for saying it, and the reason is that in about four years somebody is going to ask you how I knew and I would rather the answer was in front of eleven people than in a book I have written for myself.**"

"**The form is not new. The zone is not new. A person who has seen the form of a thing twice can recognize it in about nine seconds, and I have seen the form of it more than twice, and that is the whole of what I have, and I am telling you the whole of what I have before you ask me for any of it so that you cannot say afterwards that I gave you half and kept the rest.**"

"**And I am going to say the second thing now and it is the reason I have not come up this road in four years, and the second thing is that I am dangerous, and I have been dangerous in about four rooms in my life and in none of them was I doing it on purpose.**"

"**If I am right, then the thing that took your water is not an office. Offices can be written to. An office has a door and a clerk and a stamp and it will answer you and the answer will come back correct and correct is the trouble. What I am describing does not have a door. It has a shape and a schedule and a set of instructions that were written by somebody who is not going to be in the room, and you cannot write to that, and a man who spends four years writing to the wrong kind of thing has spent four years and learned nothing except his own handwriting.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that she said the word dangerous herself and did not have it said about her, and entered that nobody in that yard said anything tender and that nobody thanked anybody.

And the reader of this body said the four sentences he had come into that yard with, and he gave the reason for each of them before the sentence, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-six.

"**First. I am not going to ask you how many times you have seen the form of it, and I am telling you in about eleven people that I am not going to, and both of us are going to enter that I did not, and neither of us is going to improve it.**"

"**Second. The reason I am not going to is that the answer is a number, and a number of that kind is a thing I would carry for the rest of my life, and I would carry it in a place where it would be more use to me than to any of the four of you, and I have spent four years finding out that the things I know quickest are the things I have wanted most.**"

"**Third. You have a schedule in an earlier hand in that bag. I have not asked for it and I am not going to ask for it and I did not look at it when you put the bag down, and I am telling you that in about eleven people so that if it goes missing this afternoon the eleven people are a record of me not having wanted it.**"

"**Fourth. I have said four things out loud in a yard and got wrong. If I said a fifth this afternoon it would be five, and the number of that is mine and not yours, and I am not going to trade it to you for the schedule, and that is a poor offer and I am making it deliberately.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that Sera Quill did not put the bag on the table and did not open it, and entered that the count of the times he has asked her anything in this holding's book is one and it is still one and that the one was answered with a no four months ago and that he did not ask twice, and entered that he was not thanked for the four sentences and that she did not thank him for them either and that the two of them said nothing tender to one another in about eleven people and the fen entered both of those.

And then Lissa said the thing, and she said it standing at the wall side with her arms crossed and she said it to the yard and not to either of them, and the count of that in this holding's history is two.

"**I am not going to say she is welcome, because she has not asked to be welcome and she has not asked me for anything. I am going to say she is going to be fed and there is a bed in the cart shed and there is a fire in it by the seventh hour. Those are two different things and I have been keeping them apart for about four years and I can keep them apart for one more week, and I am saying it in a yard because a room is where a person says the other version.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that the reader of this body did not answer it, and entered that he did not answer it in the yard and did not answer it afterward in the cart shed or on the road or at about the seventh hour in his own book, and entered that this is the third time in about four years that his mother has said a thing in a yard and that he has not answered it, and entered that the count is three and that it does not move to-day and that he wrote the three down at about the fifth hour and looked at it and did not add anything to it.

And then the two of them worked, in about eleven people, for about an hour and a half, and the fen entered that it was worked and not talked, and that about four of the eleven people read what they wrote and one of the four was the clerk.

Sera Quill had the sheet of the third of this month flat on the table in front of her with her hand flat on the fourth line, and she put her finger on the word *operation* and kept it there for about a minute and a half and then said the definition, and she gave the reason for giving it before she gave it.

"**A branch is irregular when its flow has moved by more than a stated fraction across a stated number of readings in a stated time, and all three of those are numbers and all three of them are in an instruction and none of them is on the sheet you have got. I have never seen the instruction. I know the shape of it because I have seen four branches come off a list in my life for being irregular and every one of the four of them was a branch that had not been predicted and not one of the four had been damaged.**"

"**That is the whole of what I came up this road to say and it took me four years to work out that it was worth saying, and I am saying it now before I have got it right, because in about four days somebody is going to give you a number and I would rather you had the shape of the number first.**"

And the reader of this body took the book that keeps the third launder's figures and put it flat on the table beside her hand and read the eight lines out loud, and his own hand was not shaking and he entered that it was not shaking and that it had not been shaking since the Wednesday, and that a hand is not a figure and that he is not going to enter it twice.

"**Five hundred and seventy-five, five hundred and eighty, five hundred and eighty-four, five hundred and eighty-eight, five hundred and eighty-nine, five hundred and ninety-four, and nothing, and nothing, on the two days before.**"

"**Six readings in six days and not one of the six repeated, and I have got a man of about seventy-one's ditch that came up on the twenty-eighth of the month before, and an operation on the thirtieth, and a figure that went to five hundred and seventy-five on the first. And I have got a middle that came down a foot and one inch in eight mornings in the third month, and I have never put those four things next to one another in four years, and I have been writing down a figure I did not understand every ninth day for four years, in a book in a yard, and I did not know there was an office and I have been doing its arithmetic without being paid for it.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the reader of this body did not say what he thought the four of them were, and entered that Sera Quill said one sentence about it, and that the sentence is that a rule which has only ever met four branches does not know the difference between an unusual branch and a sick one, and that a man who assumes the difference exists is the man who wrote the instruction, and entered that the two of them did not agree about it and that neither would change and neither was asked to, and entered that the reader of this body thinks she is wrong about the four and thinks she is right about the rule, and that those are two thoughts and that the fen has entered both of them separately and has not added them together.

And at about the seventh hour of the evening the reader of this body was in the cart shed with a lamp and he wrote in his own book, at the top of a clean page, the five letters of his own name and the four letters of his family name, and he has not written either of them in this holding's book in about five years, and the count of the times a name of his has been written on these pages is one, and nobody else has seen the page and he is not going to show it to anybody, and the reason he wrote it is in his own hand under it and it is that a man who has got a sheet with his name on the bottom of it in another office's book ought to be able to say what it says, and that in about four years a room is going to ask him which of two things he is, and that the two things are the engineer of record of a cooperative that has no charter and a man with a family name, and that he has been the second of those four years in about eleven rooms without once being asked to be.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, in one man in a cart shed, and entered that his mother was not in that shed and was not told and was not going to be told, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nobody said anything tender and that a man writing his own name at about the seventh hour of the evening with a lamp on his knee is not a thing anybody is going to be thanked for and is not a thing anybody in this fen will ever be told about.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the seventh of this month, in about eleven people, on the standing of a statement, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the yard and was not asked to write it down: **SERA QUILL CAME UP THE FEN ROAD ON FOOT FROM THE NORTH GATE WITH A BAG IN EACH HAND AND AT HER OWN COST, AND NOBODY HAD SENT FOR HER. SHE LOOKED AT THE SHEET OF THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH FOR ABOUT NINE SECONDS AND SAID THAT SHE HAD SEEN THE FORM OF THAT OPERATION BEFORE, THAT THE FORM IS NOT NEW AND THE ZONE IS NOT NEW, AND THAT A PERSON WHO HAS SEEN THE FORM OF A THING TWICE CAN RECOGNIZE IT IN ABOUT NINE SECONDS. SHE SAID THAT SHE IS DANGEROUS, AND THAT IF SHE IS RIGHT THEN THE THING THAT TOOK THIS HOLDING'S WATER IS NOT AN OFFICE, BECAUSE AN OFFICE HAS A DOOR AND A CLERK AND A STAMP AND WILL ANSWER, AND WHAT SHE IS DESCRIBING HAS A SHAPE AND A SCHEDULE AND INSTRUCTIONS WRITTEN BY SOMEBODY WHO IS NOT GOING TO BE IN THE ROOM. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD, IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, THAT HE WAS NOT GOING TO ASK HER HOW MANY TIMES SHE HAD SEEN IT, THAT SHE HAD A SCHEDULE IN AN EARLIER HAND IN HER BAG AND HE DID NOT ASK FOR IT AND DID NOT LOOK AT IT, AND THAT HE WAS NOT GOING TO TRADE HIS COUNT OF FOUR FOR IT. HIS MOTHER, LISSA, SAID IN THE YARD THAT SHE WAS NOT GOING TO SAY THE WOMAN WAS WELCOME AND WAS GOING TO SAY SHE WAS GOING TO BE FED, AND THAT THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT THINGS, AND HE DID NOT ANSWER HER. THE TWO OF THEM WORKED FOR ABOUT AN HOUR AND A HALF AND DID NOT AGREE ABOUT WHETHER AN IRREGULAR BRANCH IS A DAMAGED BRANCH OR AN UNPREDICTED ONE, AND NEITHER OF THEM WOULD CHANGE AND NEITHER WAS ASKED TO. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
