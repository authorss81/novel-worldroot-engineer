# Chapter 255

## The Word Is Not The Same As Asking

The eleventh of this month was a Friday and the man of about fifty told the three other bodies of households at the node at about the second hour of the morning, in about eleven people, and he told them what he had seen and he did not put a figure on it and he did not put an opinion on it, and the fen entered that he did neither and entered the reason, which is that he had been asked to tell them and not to tell them what to make of it.

And Marden was not there. Marden is the fourth line and the fourth line has been empty on every morning of its own since the rota came into force, and nobody had sent for anybody.

The reader of this body had not slept, and the fen entered that he had not slept, and entered that the count of nights this holding's book records that he has not slept through in four years is twelve, and that the twelfth is the ninth night of this month, and that he did not sleep on the night after the tenth either and has not entered that one and is not going to this week, and that the number is in the book and not in a column and not in the standing.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon a yard at Errow Reach had about nineteen people in it, and the three bodies of households were in it, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in it, and the man of the north row with the cough was in it and had not been asked to be, and the count of yards he has stood in since the first of a month four months back is twenty-one and has moved, and he said nothing at all in that yard and that is the fifth time in about four weeks.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

And the reader of this body told the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden what he was going to decide before he decided it.

He did it first, and he did it out loud, and he gave the reason for doing it first before he did it, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty, and nobody said anything while he did it and the fen entered that nobody said anything and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and that about four of the nineteen said nothing differently from the other fifteen, and that he did not count them.

"**I am going to say what I am going to do, and then I am going to do it, and I am saying it to you first and not to the yard, and the reason is that you are the one it is going to happen to and about eleven people in that yard are not.**"

"**The cut stays open. I am not putting boards in it and I am not closing the kerb and I am not asking the man of about fifty to put anything in it, and that is the whole of the decision and it is mine and I have not got a form for it and there is no line on any of the six lines of that covenant for it and the standing rule about the engineer of record is on the wall of this holding and I am not using it, and I have now said the word about that rule twice in eleven days and used it not at all in eleven days, and I am entering both halves of that sentence and I am not going to improve either.**"

"**And the reason is arithmetic and I have got nothing else. The middle of that node is about four feet across and it is nine feet off the hollow and it was a foot and one inch lower at about the second hour of the twenty-second of a month two months back than it was at about the second hour of the fourteenth of that month, and nobody in this holding has ever put a unit on that fall and I am not going to put one on it this afternoon.**"

"**If that cut is shut this afternoon then about five hundred and seventy-five hundredweight a day stops at the kerb and goes into the ground under the kerb, and the ground under the kerb is the ground the middle is standing in, and there is nowhere lower for it in this holding to go, and I cannot say what that does. I know what I have got. I have got a middle that has already come down a foot and one inch in eight mornings and I have got five hundred and seventy-five hundredweight a day with nowhere to go, and putting boards in a two-foot cut is easy and it takes about a quarter of an hour with two people, and it is the correct thing to do with the things I have in my hand.**"

"**And I am not going to do it, and I am telling you that in about eleven people and not in a room, and I have not slept a night through since the ninth of this month and I am not going to pretend to you this afternoon that the not sleeping is a reason. It is not a reason. It is a thing that is going to be in a book in about four years.**"

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one sentence, and it was not tender, and it was not a speech, and it was not said to a yard, and she did not raise her voice, and the count of that in this holding's history is two.

"**Then it is my gate and it is not yours, and you have told me, and that is not the same thing as asking me, and I know that it is not the same thing as asking me, and I am going to tell you what I am going to do about it, and you are not going to be here when I do it.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the reader of this body heard her, and entered that he did not answer her, and entered that he did not change it, and entered the whole of what that is in his own hand at about the fourth hour of the afternoon with the day and the hour on it, and the whole of it is this.

> **THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN WAS NINE AND IT IS TEN. I HEARD HER. HEARING A PERSON IS NOT ASKING A PERSON. I KNEW THE DIFFERENCE BEFORE SHE SAID IT, AND I HAD IT IN A BOOK IN THIS HOLDING IN MY OWN HAND ON THE EIGHTH OF THIS MONTH AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR IN ABOUT NINE WORDS, AND I READ IT BACK TWICE, AND I DID NOT DO ANYTHING ABOUT IT, AND A MAN WHO HAS WRITTEN A SENTENCE DOWN AND READ IT BACK AND THEN DONE THE OTHER THING IS NOT A MAN WHO MADE A MISTAKE. HE IS A MAN WHO HAD THE MISTAKE AVAILABLE AND TOOK IT ANYWAY, BECAUSE HE WAS RIGHT. I WAS RIGHT ABOUT THE MIDDLE AND ABOUT THE FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE AND ABOUT THERE BEING NOWHERE LOWER, AND BEING RIGHT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT I AM. THE NUMBER IS TEN AND I DID NOT SAY IT OUT LOUD AND I AM NOT GOING TO, BECAUSE I SAID IN A YARD IN THE SECOND MONTH THAT I WOULD NOT, AND I HAVE KEPT THAT ONE.**

And then he decided it, and he said so in about nineteen people at about the third hour of the afternoon, and the fen entered that he said it in the plain voice and did not say it twice and did not improve it, and that the words were short.

"**It stays open. That is all. Nobody is to put anything in that gap this season, and if a man wants to put something in it he is to come and stand in this yard and say so in about nineteen people and not do it in the night.**"

And then the three bodies of households did the thing they had come to do, which was to hear it, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden went down the shoulder at about the fourth hour of the afternoon with nobody, and the fen entered that nobody went with her and entered the reason, which is that she had said he was not going to be there and he was not going to be there, and entered that a man who says he is not going to be there and then is, has told a person something about himself that he has not told her about anything else.

And Soren Rill, who is a man of about thirty-five of an Assembly in the Thornwild, was at the edge of that yard and did not come into it, and he said the standing form word, which he has said four times in this holding's book and which he has now said a fifth, and he did not say anything else, and the fen entered that he did not say anything else and that the reason was given on the tenth of this month and that a reason does not get said twice and improves and gets worse, and that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked him is three and it is three.

And before any of that, at about the eleventh hour of the morning of the eleventh of this month, the reader of this body had walked out to the west sill at the node with a rule in his right hand and about nine feet of open ground to go, and he had stood there about four minutes.

The water on the west sill moved about once in every four or five seconds from the west side, and it makes no sound and shows nothing on the surface, and the only way there is to read it is to put the rule in against the sill and let the water come up the wood and go down again, and that is what every one of his takings of this sill has been, and the count of takings of the sill is not the count of readings of the pulse and the two are two counts and have not been added together.

He did not put the rule against it.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that there was nobody there at all, and entered the length of it, which was about four minutes, in one man on about four feet of ground, and entered that the count of readings of that thing is two and is two and is not going to be three this season, and entered the reason, and the reason is that he said two figures out loud about it in the third month and then read the same two figures out of a Crown sheet on the third of this month, and that a man who has got two figures of his own and one of an office's has got a comparison, and that a man who has got a comparison and goes back to the sill for a third figure is a man who has stopped reading and started wanting.

And the man of about fifty was about nine feet off with the book shut and did not ask him what he had gone out there for, and the fen entered that he did not ask, and entered that that is the second time in about a day that a man of about fifty has not asked a question he could have asked, and that the first time was in a cart shed on the tenth of this month, and that the count of that is two.

And then at about the seventh hour of the evening, which was after the yard at Errow Reach and not before it, the reader of this body was in the cart shed with a lamp and he took out the sheet of the third of this month, which is in the book on the table in the middle on top of the three books that live there, and the clerk brought it to him and read nothing out of it, and he put the sixth line in front of him and read it himself.

**The maintenance will be reviewed before the next window.**

He worked out the next window himself and he did it twice and the two were the same, and the fen entered the working.

**The first of the two figures was at about the fourth hour of the afternoon of the thirtieth of a month two months back and the second was six hours and eleven seconds after it, and the thirtieth is the last day of a month and the first of the next is the first day of a month, and the two figures are six hours and eleven seconds apart inside one day, and there is no interval on that sheet, and no rule, and no column, and the sheet does not say when the next one is and does not say how often there is one.**

**THE READER OF THIS BODY WROTE DOWN THE ONE THING THE SHEET DOES SAY ABOUT THE NEXT ONE, AND THE THING IS THAT IT WILL BE REVIEWED, AND HE WROTE UNDER IT THAT HE CANNOT WORK OUT THE DATE, AND THAT A DOCUMENT THAT DOES NOT SAY WHEN IT WILL HAPPEN AGAIN IS NOT WITHHOLDING A THING, IT IS DESCRIBING A THING THAT A DOCUMENT DOES NOT SCHEDULE, AND THAT THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT THINGS AND HE DOES NOT KNOW WHICH OF THE TWO THIS ONE IS, AND THAT THIS IS THE FIFTH OF THE SIX THINGS HE HAS WRITTEN NOT KNOWN AGAINST IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK, AND THAT THE FIFTH IS THE FIRST ONE HE HAS WRITTEN AGAINST A PIECE OF PAPER THAT CAME DOWN A ROAD, AND THAT THE SIXTH IS THE FIGURE THAT LAUNDER CARRIED BEFORE THE ELEVENTH OF THE FIRST MONTH, WHICH HE WROTE LATER AND WHICH HE IS ENTERING AFTER THE FIFTH AND NOT BEFORE IT.**

And then he wrote two more lines and read them back and they are these and he is not going to improve them.

"**NINETEEN DAYS FROM THIS MORNING IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH. THE FOURTH LINE OF THE ROTA COMES ROUND ON THE TWELFTH, THE SIXTEENTH, THE TWENTIETH, THE TWENTY-FOURTH AND THE TWENTY-EIGHTH, AND IT IS EMPTY, AND ON THE THIRTIETH THE STANDING FOR THE MONTH IS READ OUT IN ABOUT TWENTY-FIVE PEOPLE AT NINE HUNDRED YARDS OFF, AND I WILL BE STANDING IN THAT YARD.**"

"**THE WATER ON THE LOW GROUND OF MARDEN WILL BE A GREAT DEAL FURTHER ALONG THAN IT IS TO-NIGHT AND I AM THE ONLY PERSON IN THIS FEN WHO HAS SAID THAT SENTENCE OUT LOUD, AND I AM GOING TO HAVE TO BE IN A YARD IN ABOUT FOUR DAYS SAYING IT, AND I HAVE SAID FOUR THINGS OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG AND I DO NOT KNOW WHAT THE FIFTH IS GOING TO BE, AND A SCHEDULE THAT SAYS IT WILL BE REVIEWED IS NOT A SCHEDULE THAT SAYS IT WILL STOP.**"

And he put the lamp out and did not sleep, and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that nothing tender was said in any of the eleven days of this week, and entered the count of the words in the two lines he had just written, which is about a hundred and sixty, and entered that a man who has written a hundred and sixty words at about the seventh hour of the evening in a cart shed about a piece of paper is a man who has stopped writing down figures and has started writing down sentences, and that this holding's book has four years of figures in it and about a week of sentences, and that the week came after a Wednesday, and that the fen has entered that too and has not drawn anything from it.
