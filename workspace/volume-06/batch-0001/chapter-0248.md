# Chapter 248

## Eleven Seconds

The third of this month was a Thursday and the ordinary post came up the fen road at about the second hour of the afternoon, and it was one sheet, and it came out of the same leather bag as everything else that has arrived at this holding for four years, and the person who keeps the seed house took it off the top without opening it and looked at the mark embossed on the corner and put it face down on the table and went out into the yard and said one sentence to about eleven people.

"**That is not the Assembly's paper, and I am going to read all of it out loud before anybody says a word about any part of it, and I am going to read the acknowledgment line too, and I am telling you now that there is a name in it and I have not looked at whether the name is yours.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

She read it out in the ordinary voice and the clerk wrote it down as she read it and read it back afterwards and the reader of this body checked the reading back against the sheet and did not improve it and did not take anything off it.

> **From the Office of the Continuity, for the maintenance of the Crown Engine, to the district in which the cooperative at Lantern Reach keeps its works, and to the engineer of record of that cooperative.**
>
> **One.** The district is placed in zone four. The extent of zone four is held by this office and will be furnished to any person who applies for it.
>
> **Two.** A maintenance operation was carried out in that district on the thirtieth of the month last past at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and again at about the tenth hour of the evening of the same day.
>
> **Three.** The figures of the operation are eleven seconds and six hours and eleven seconds. The office holds the same figures and does not propose to alter them.
>
> **Four.** The operation was carried out in the maintained season. The maintained season is a term of this office and not of the district. The office does not state on paper what the operation consists of on the ground.
>
> **Five.** A compensation schedule is offered at one shilling and twopence the hundredweight, for one season, against a flow that a body of households has counted as its own and is able to show a figure for. Applications are received at the office in the district town and are answered in the order in which they are received.
>
> **Six.** The maintenance will be reviewed before the next window.
>
> **At the foot, over the acknowledgment line, in print: CHANCELLOR IONA VEY, OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY. The acknowledgment line beneath it is a line for a person to sign. The person it names is MAREK VALE, ENGINEER OF RECORD, LANTERN REACH WORK COOPERATIVE.**

The yard was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered the minute.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that nobody thanked anybody.

And the reader of this body said the hard part first, and he gave the reason before the hard part, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty, and what he said was not tender and was not a thank.

"**The sheet is correct. I have read it twice and every line of it is a thing that office can do and a thing it is entitled to do, and it does not lie in one word, and it does not obstruct anything, and it has not delayed anything, and if I write to it and say that a thing it did is a wrong thing then I will be writing to it about a wrong thing.**"

"**I want that said first, in about eleven people, on the day it arrives and not later, because the reason I want it said first is that the second thing I am going to say is worse than the first and a yard that has only heard the second thing will hear it as an accusation, and it is not an accusation, and I would rather it were in the room before the second thing and not after.**"

"**The second thing is that the office has our two figures.**"

"**Eleven seconds. Six hours and eleven seconds. Same to the second, which is the phrase the man of about fifty used on a sill at the west kerb on the thirtieth of the month last past at about the fourth hour of the afternoon and again at about the tenth hour of the evening, in daylight and in the dark, with the same stopwatch and the same man on it and the same man calling it, and two men who said the number out loud at the same time and did not ask each other whether they had got it.**"

"**The second one was in the dark. There was a lamp on a stone and a man's hand and a rule and a stopwatch and nine feet of ground and about four people, and there is no office in this holding and no office anywhere that I know of that was on that sill at the tenth hour of the evening of the thirtieth of the month last past, and the sheet in front of us is to the second, and the interval is to the second, and I am not going to stand in this yard and say that an office has a stopwatch on our sill.**"

"**I am not going to say it for two reasons and the second one is the better one. The first is that I have got nothing to stand on. The second is that the document is the evidence and what I feel about the document is not a second document, and a man who puts his feeling in the same line as a piece of paper has stopped being able to tell anybody which is which, and by this time next month somebody in a room is going to ask me which of the two it was and I will not be able to answer, and I would rather be able to answer.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about two minutes, and entered that about four of the eleven said nothing in a way that was different from the other seven, and entered that he did not count them.

And then the woman who keeps the four books read the fifth line out again on her own, without being asked, and worked out one thing out loud, and gave the reason for working it out before she worked it out, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirteen, and nobody had asked her to and she is not one of the seven that decide and has never been asked to be.

"**One shilling and twopence the hundredweight against a flow that a body of households has counted as its own and is able to show a figure for. That is a real rate and I am not going to stand here and say it is not, because the compost line is four shillings on four hundred hundredweight, which is a shilling the hundredweight, and this is a shilling and twopence, and the two rates differ by two pence the hundredweight, and two pence the hundredweight at six hundred hundredweight a day for thirty days in a month is a hundred and fifty pounds a month, and the whole of the rate at the same figure is a thousand and fifty pounds a month, and both of those are figures this holding can check and it is the best rate anybody has ever offered these four villages for anything.**"

"**And none of the four of us can claim a hundredweight of it. The line pays for a flow a body of households counted as its own. The flow is up. It went from about fifteen hundred a day to about twenty-one hundred a day on the first of this month and it is up this afternoon and it was up yesterday. A claim is a claim about a thing that a body of households counted and then did not get, and four bodies of households have not got less. We have got more.**"

"**So I want it read out in about eleven people that the most generous document any office has ever sent this holding is a document none of the four of us can put a hand on, and I want that in the book in the same line as the rate, because in about four years somebody is going to remember the rate and not remember the reason, and a rate in a head by itself is a wrong number.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that nobody thanked her, and entered that the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is four, and that he had not thought of that one either, and that he was not going to count it in that yard and would count it in a book and did, and that the count is five and it moved and the movement is not his.

And the licensed man, Coll Renner, who had come up the fen road with the man of about fifty and with the reader of this body on the third and had been in that yard since about the first hour, said the one thing he had come to say, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ten.

"**I am a licensed man and there is one thing on that sheet I can answer for and I am going to answer it and I am going to leave the other five alone.**"

"**Zone four is not a district and it is not an office. It is a list, and the list is held by that office, and the sheet says the list will be furnished to any person who applies for it, and that is not an obstruction and it is not a trick and it is the correct way for a thing like that to be done, because a man who has the list can look at it and a man who has a copy of a list nobody else has got is not a man who is being given a thing, he is a man who has been given a privilege.**"

"**And the acknowledgment line is not a formality and it is not a courtesy. It is a claim. The moment that line is signed, that office and the man who signed it have the same two figures in the same order in two books, and there is a difference between an office that has told you what it did and an office that has your name against what it did, and I would not sign it in a yard of about eleven people and I would not advise anybody to, and I have not signed it and I was not asked to and I was not asked not to.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the reader of this body did not sign the acknowledgment line and did not refuse it in front of anybody, and that it went into the book on the table in the middle on top of the three books that live there with the day and the hour on it and unsigned, and that the fen has entered that it is unsigned and has not entered a reason, and that a reason would have been a figure and there is no figure.

And the sheet went in whole, and the six lines went in whole, and the two lines at the foot went in whole.

And at about the eleventh hour of the evening the reader of this body read it again in the cart shed with a lamp, and he read the sixth line nine times, and the word in it is **window**, and it is the only word in the whole of the six lines that is not a word a person would use about a river, and a window is a thing with a frame in it, and an office that keeps a season in a frame calls the time it looks at the frame a window, and he entered that.

And then he tried to work out when the next window is, and he could not, and he wrote down that he could not.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, in one man in a cart shed, and entered the reason, and the reason is that the sheet does not say, and a document that does not say when it will happen again is not withholding a thing, it is describing a thing that a document does not schedule, and that those are two different things and that he does not know which of the two this one is, and that he wrote *not known* against it in his own hand with the hour on it, and that the count of things he has written not known against in this holding's book was four at the turn of the year and is four now, and that this is the fifth, and that the fifth is the date of a window, and that the fourth was a boot print in mud in a ditch, and that he is not going to let the fifth be smaller than the fourth because the fifth is on a piece of paper and the fourth was in the ground.

And the reader of this body wrote one line of his own under them, in his own hand, with the hour on it, and it is this and he has read it back twice and it is not going to be improved.

"**A sheet that names a person is a different kind of sheet from one that names nobody, and the difference is not in the office. The Assembly's sheet named no village, no node, no person and no date, and I said in the second month of the year that that was better than what this holding has got. It is not better. It is a different kind of sheet. The Assembly's sheet was asking a body of households to do something. This one is telling a man what an office has already done, and the telling has a line at the bottom of it for the man's name.**"

"**And I am not going to say the difference is the whole of anything. I am writing it down on the third of this month at about the ninth hour of the evening because I was wrong out loud in a yard yesterday and I am writing things down this week that I would not have written down in the month before, and that is not a virtue, it is a man writing down more because he has found one thing he got wrong, and both of those are in this line.**"

> Entered at about the fourth hour of the afternoon of the third of this month, in the seed house yard, by the clerk, on the standing of a document, entered whole and not summarized: **ONE SHEET CAME UP THE FEN ROAD ON THE ORDINARY POST AND IT IS ON THE EMBOSSED PAPER OF THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY, FOR THE MAINTENANCE OF THE CROWN ENGINE, AND IT IS NOT THE ASSEMBLY'S PAPER. IT HAS SIX NUMBERED LINES AND TWO LINES AT THE FOOT. IT PLACES THE DISTRICT IN ZONE FOUR AND SAYS THE EXTENT OF ZONE FOUR IS HELD BY THE OFFICE AND WILL BE FURNISHED ON APPLICATION. IT STATES THAT A MAINTENANCE OPERATION WAS CARRIED OUT ON THE THIRTIETH OF THE MONTH LAST PAST AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND AGAIN AT ABOUT THE TENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING. IT GIVES THE FIGURES AS ELEVEN SECONDS AND SIX HOURS AND ELEVEN SECONDS AND SAYS THE OFFICE HOLDS THE SAME FIGURES AND DOES NOT PROPOSE TO ALTER THEM. IT SAYS THE OPERATION WAS CARRIED OUT IN THE MAINTAINED SEASON, THAT THE MAINTAINED SEASON IS A TERM OF THAT OFFICE AND NOT OF THE DISTRICT, AND THAT THE OFFICE DOES NOT STATE ON PAPER WHAT THE OPERATION CONSISTS OF ON THE GROUND. IT OFFERS COMPENSATION AT ONE SHILLING AND TWOPENCE THE HUNDREDWEIGHT FOR ONE SEASON AGAINST A FLOW A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS COUNTED AS ITS OWN, APPLICATIONS RECEIVED AT THE DISTRICT TOWN AND ANSWERED IN THE ORDER RECEIVED. IT SAYS THE MAINTENANCE WILL BE REVIEWED BEFORE THE NEXT WINDOW. AT THE FOOT, OVER AN ACKNOWLEDGMENT LINE FOR A PERSON TO SIGN, IS THE NAME OF CHANCELLOR IONA VEY, AND THE LINE NAMES MAREK VALE, ENGINEER OF RECORD, LANTERN REACH WORK COOPERATIVE. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD, IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, THAT THE SHEET IS CORRECT AND DOES NOT LIE, AND THAT THE OFFICE HOLDS THE TWO FIGURES THAT WERE TAKEN ON A SILL AT THE FOUR ELBOWS IN DAYLIGHT AND IN THE DARK ON THE THIRTIETH OF THE MONTH LAST PAST, AND THAT HE WAS NOT GOING TO ACCUSE ANYBODY OF HAVING A WATCH ON THE SILL BECAUSE HE HAS NOTHING TO STAND ON AND BECAUSE THE DOCUMENT IS THE EVIDENCE. THE LINE WAS NOT SIGNED. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
