# Chapter 594

## The Twenty-Seventh Of The Fifteenth, And A Dial A Fifth Of A Division Under

Fine dry weather and no wind, and the pack under the near stone dry both ways for the third time this week, and frost in the ruts by six gone by nine and the standpipe full and inside its lip.

The clerk read the launder at nine from the second book, and Soren Rill heard it from the road and kept walking because a figure is not a harvest.

**One thousand and one hundred and forty-three hundredweight.**

Monday took five off Sunday, and the three hundred and fifty-seventh figure of a run that has never held the same figure twice stands this morning, and the fall on an odd morning is the rule and not news.

The halves at the wall were a hundred and sixty-seven one way and a hundred and fifty-seven the other, three hundred and twenty-four in all, and the addition is the only test and the look is not a test.

The morning run was read at seven, of a month nine months back, and Monday is an odd morning for this run and the run goes in on odd mornings, and the heading did not move and will not move before the month turns.

**One hundred and forty-nine mornings of two hundred and eighty-one.**

The man who keeps the gauges came nine miles with a leather case and worked on the dial at the standpipe, and laid his case out in a row and put a washer he did not use back in it, and gave the reason first before he spoke.

**"The needle is a fifth of a division under where it was on Thursday, and I want a stopcock and I have asked for it twice and I am not allowed to ask a third time."**

He closed the case.

**"I have kept that dial nineteen years and I would like it noticed that I did not say the next part before anybody asked me for it, and I am not going to say it now."**

The woman of about thirty-eight asked him how long and he gave her that and refused her the next, and Marek said he is not going to ask that man anything this afternoon, and is not going to say in a room in four counties that years are a qualification.

**"Nineteen years is how long he has kept it, and that is the whole of what was said, and I am not making a qualification out of years."**

He stayed in the doorway.

**"A dial and a stopcock and a washer back in a case, and a man who did not say the next thing, and that is enough for one morning."**

Three hundred and fifty-five days on the four bodies sheet, clause the three hundred and fifty-fourth out of three hundred and fifty-sixth, one under and one over, never added to any launder.

Seven hundred and forty-one near and seven hundred and eighty-eight far, forty-seven apart, drawer shut every hour and at every hour of this month.

There are three sheets on the long table, the first fifty-four days old face up with about nine inches of bare board either side, the second folded from the twenty-eighth, the third correct with name and date and seal off a wall nine miles off, none moved and none answered and none entered.

Fifty-three requests and fifty-three notes and none of the three went into either, and no column was ruled for any of them in any book.

Sabra Ollerton brought a can to the tap and set it down hard and said the standpipe had run full all week and she was not going to call a full pipe a promise, and gave the reason first.

**"A full pipe this week and a dry pipe for a day and a half last month, and I chose the hole in my own book and I am choosing it still."**

She filled the can.

**"One figure and a deliberate space under it is a book, and two figures is a ledger, and my street is not a ledger."**

Tova Reed met her at the tap and said nothing about right or wrong, and later said to Marek that four of nine places have been done differently on purpose and she will not turn that into a body.

**"About nine people found out at the same hour and not one was first, and paid is the word and not discharged, and nobody decided it."**

She went back to the seed house.

**"I am not taking the seed work off anybody and nobody is taking it off me, and nobody put a hand on my arm and nobody said sorry, and that is how it stands."**

Marek said the two standings once with his back to the table and did not soften either by a word.

**"The far end is held by two and neither is me, and no instrument says on or off. The comfort is not standing, the father is not alive in the wood, the day does not come back."**

The clerk derived the ages out loud at the middle table with the books open.

**"Two hundred and fifty-one on the form, a hundred and fifty-seven on the charter, two hundred and forty-five on Silling's second line, all empty and none refused."**

He put the weight on the log.

**"Four bare lines, terms empty, seven sessions, six not knowns, fifteen in the log, eleven journeys, sixty-six on the door, twenty-nine fetchings, paid at twenty-eight."**

No foot in the ring and no figure to it and not added to the low field, offer undated and unmoved, no rung climbed, no figure for either ladder person, drawer shut, no column under any sheet.

Soren Rill cleaned the standpipe mouth with a rag and wrung the rag out on the stones, and said the mouth was clear and the pressure was not his to judge, and that a clear mouth is work a road can see.

**"Clear at the mouth and a fifth under at the needle, and the one I can touch and the one I cannot, and I am leaving both where they are."**

He folded the rag.

**"Nineteen years kept and twice asked, and a third asking not allowed, and I am not the man to ask it for him."**

Tova Reed sieved seed till noon and shook the chaff out downwind, and said the wind was fair for winnowing and foul for talk, and kept her talk for the tap.

The man of about fifty stacked empty cans by the wall in pairs, because pairs stack and singles fall, and said nothing while he stacked, and the stacking was even by noon.

Path dust dry under a leather case, and a needle under by a fifth, and nobody thanked anybody.
