# Chapter 594

## The Twenty-Seventh Of The Fifteenth, And A Dial A Fifth Of A Division Under

Fine dry weather and no wind, and the pack under the near stone dry both ways for the third time this week, and frost in the ruts by six gone by nine and the standpipe full and inside its lip.

The clerk read the launder at nine from the second book, and Soren Rill heard it from the road and kept walking because a figure is not a harvest.

**One thousand and one hundred and forty-three hundredweight.**

Monday took five off Sunday, and the three hundred and fifty-seventh figure of a run that has never held the same figure twice stands this morning, and the fall on an odd morning is the rule and not news.

The halves at the wall were a hundred and sixty-seven one way and a hundred and fifty-seven the other, three hundred and twenty-four in all, and the addition is the only test and the look is not a test.

The run of mornings was read at seven, of a month nine months back, and Monday is an odd morning for this run, so the run goes in, and the heading did not move and will not move before the month turns.

**One hundred and forty-nine mornings of two hundred and eighty-one.**

The man who keeps the gauges came nine miles with a leather case and worked on the dial at the standpipe, and laid his case out in a row and put a washer he did not use back in it, and gave the reason first before he spoke.

**"The needle is a fifth of a division under where it was on Thursday, and I want a stopcock and I have asked for it twice and I am not allowed to ask a third time."**

He closed the case.

**"I have kept that dial nineteen years and I would like it noticed that I did not say the next part before anybody asked me for it, and I am not going to say it now."**

The woman of about thirty-eight asked him how long and he gave her that and refused her the next, and Marek said he is not going to ask that man anything this afternoon, and is not going to say in a room in four counties that years are a qualification.

**"Nineteen years is how long he has kept it, and that is the whole of what was said, and I am not making a qualification out of years."**

He stayed in the doorway.

**"A dial and a stopcock and a washer back in a case, and a man who did not say the next thing, and that is enough for one morning."**

Three hundred and fifty-five days stand on the four bodies sheet, and the clause under that number takes the three hundred and fifty-fourth out of three hundred and fifty-sixth, one under and one over, and neither that figure nor this morning's launder is added to the other in any book kept here.

On the long wall, the near board reads seven hundred and forty-one days and the far board reads seven hundred and eighty-eight, and the two are forty-seven apart as they have been for four years, and the drawer behind the near board was shut at every hour of this morning and at every hour of this month.

There are three sheets on the long table, and the first of them is fifty-four days old and lies face up with about nine inches of bare board on either side of it, and the second is folded from the twenty-eighth, and the third came off a wall nine miles off and carries a name and a date and a seal and is correct in every particular. None of the three was moved or answered or entered, and the requests and the section-nine notes stand at fifty-three and fifty-three and took none of them, and no column has been ruled for any of them in any book in this holding.

Sabra Ollerton brought a can to the tap and set it down hard and said the standpipe had run full all week and she was not going to call a full pipe a promise, and gave the reason first.

**"A full pipe this week and a dry pipe for a day and a half last month, and I chose the hole in my own book and I am choosing it still."**

She filled the can.

**"One figure and a deliberate space under it is a book, and two figures is a ledger, and my street is not a ledger."**

Tova Reed met her at the tap and said nothing about right or wrong, and later said to Marek that four of nine places have been done differently on purpose and she will not turn that into a body.

**"About nine people found out at the same hour and not one was first, and paid is the word and not discharged, and nobody decided it."**

She went back to the seed house.

**"I am not taking the seed work off anybody and nobody is taking it off me, and nobody put a hand on my arm and nobody said sorry, and that is how it stands."**

Marek said neither of the two standings with his back to the table, and the man with the leather case put his case down on the step anyway and waited, and nobody in the yard told him he had missed anything.

**"I have said them every morning since the second of this month and I did not say them today, and the reason is that they are not a report and they are not a weather."**

He turned the form on the middle table a quarter of a way round so it faced the clerk and not the door.

**"Nothing behind me has moved and nothing behind me is going to, and I would rather be asked about the needle than say a true thing for the fifth time in a week."**

The clerk derived the ages out loud at the middle table with the books open and the form out of the rack, and turned the form to the light while he did it.

**"Two hundred and fifty-one days on the form, a hundred and fifty-seven on the charter behind it, two hundred and forty-five on Silling's own second line, all three empty and none of them refused."**

He put the weight back on the use log.

**"Four bare lines and the answer not due, terms ruled and empty, seven sessions and no eighth, six not knowns and no seventh row ruled for them."**

The use log went back under its weight at the ninth hour and is at fifteen lines, the sixteenth never written. The barrow has not moved from the wall and stands at eleven journeys, and eleven is a floor. Sixty-six is on the seventh column of a door nine hundred yards off and does not go back. The man of about seventy is at twenty-nine fetchings and was not fetched. Paid goes in front of twenty-eight on the compost line and is not discharged in twenty-eight.

Nothing was walked into the ring and nothing was measured across it, and it has no figure against it, and the ground that came back on the low field is a different piece of ground and the two are not added. The offer on the low board is undated and it has not moved. Nobody climbed a rung of that ladder and no figure was given for either of the two people standing on it. The drawer is shut and no column is ruled under any of the three sheets.

Soren Rill cleaned the standpipe mouth with a rag and wrung the rag out on the stones, and said the mouth was clear and the pressure was not his to judge, and that a clear mouth is work a road can see, and he left the rag on the third stone where it would be seen and did not carry it off.

**"Clear at the mouth and a fifth under at the needle, and the one I can touch and the one I cannot, and I am leaving both where they are."**

He folded his sleeve back down over his wrist.

**"Nineteen years kept and twice asked, and a third asking not allowed, and I am not the man to ask it for him."**

Tova Reed sieved seed till noon and shook the chaff out downwind, and said the wind was fair for winnowing and foul for talk, and kept her talk for the tap.

The man of about fifty stacked empty cans by the wall in pairs, because pairs stack and singles fall, and said nothing while he stacked, and the stacking was even by noon.

Path dust dry under a leather case, a needle a fifth of a division under, and a washer that went back into the case unused.
