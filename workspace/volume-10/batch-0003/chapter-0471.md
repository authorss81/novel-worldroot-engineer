# Chapter 471

## A Reading Is Not A Maintenance Act

The second line of the rota came round at the second hour on the twenty-fourth of this month and was worked, and it was the outlet end and the two stones at the head of it, and the far stone had not moved since Wednesday and the near one had a quarter of an inch of daylight under it again by the ninth hour.

There is no figure for the run of mornings on this page and the reason is on the page. The pair is read at the seventh hour and this morning it was not, because the door of the one room was shut from the sixth hour and both men who read the boards were inside it, and a board on the wall of a room with the door shut is not a board that has been read.

The two boards stood at **six hundred and eighteen days** and **six hundred and sixty-five**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened and was not going to be opened by anybody on that morning.

The twenty-fourth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and sixty-five hundredweight.**

Wednesday added eight, and that is the two hundred and thirty-fourth figure in a run in which nothing has ever been written down twice, and the rise went on to the rises. A hundred and six rises and ninety-five falls is a hundred and two hundred and one, and the two ends of that have never been set against the figure over them in this building.

The four bodies of households have stood at two hundred and thirty-two days, and the clause written under the figure is the two hundred and thirty-first of the mornings out of two hundred and thirty-third, one under and one over, and the three of those numbers are a count of days and not a count of anything that was carried anywhere.

Twenty-two not discharged in twenty-two is the standing of that line and the word in front of it is paid and a hundred pounds is the amount, and a session entered before it is carried out is not a measurement of any of the three, and none of the three has been added to another in this building in four years.

The clerk of this holding opened the second book at the page with the four things on it at about the sixth hour, in the room, with the door shut, and he read the four out a fourth time in front of about nine people and then he wrote the seventh entry under them.

> THE SEVENTH ENTRY IN THE SECOND BOOK, ENTERED BY THE CLERK OF THIS HOLDING AT THE SIXTH HOUR, IN HIS OWN HAND, BEFORE THE SESSION AND NOT AFTER IT: **THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF THIS MONTH. PURPOSE: TO CARRY A SCHEDULE OF NINE LOADS AND FOUR DAYS AND WHAT IT HAS COST THIS HOLDING'S BOOK BETWEEN TWO PEOPLE, AND TO HAVE PROPOSED TO THEM A READING OF THE STANDING WOOD AS A WAY OF KNOWING WHAT THE GROUND WANTS. DURATION: ABOUT FORTY MINUTES, AND IF IT IS NOT FINISHED IN FORTY MINUTES IT DOES NOT GO ON. STOP SIGNAL: ONE WORD, HERS. THE WORD ITSELF IS NOT ENTERED IN THIS BOOK AND IS NOT TO BE ENTERED IN IT. THE WORD *STOPPED* IS ENTERED BECAUSE IT IS THE PART A PERSON OUTSIDE THIS ROOM IS ENTITLED TO KNOW. WITNESS: THE CLERK OF THIS HOLDING, WHO IS NOT ONE OF THE TWO AT THE ENDS OF IT, WHO IS TO SAY WHAT WAS SAID AND HOW LONG IT WENT ON AND WHETHER THE STOP SIGNAL WAS USED, AND WHO IS NOT TO BE ASKED WHAT THE TWO OF THEM WERE THINKING. IF HER END CLOSES, THE LOAD GOES WHERE IT IS AND IS NOT MOVED BY ANYBODY IN THIS BUILDING AFTERWARDS.** ONE NAME IS AGAINST THE STOP SIGNAL AND THERE IS NO NAME AGAINST THE OTHER THREE.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book went from six to seven on that page and it is seven this evening and it is not six and seven anywhere else in this building, and the six before it were entered in the same way and none of the six has been improved.

Nothing above is a forecast. Nothing above says what the session produced and the clerk of this holding was not asked to write that down before it happened and has not written it down at the foot of it.

The session began at about the sixth hour and it was carried out in the one room with the door shut, and Marek was at one end of the table and the woman who keeps the seed house at the other, and the clerk of this holding stood at the long wall with the book open and his pencil not in his hand.

What crossed between them was a schedule and its cost, and that is the whole of what crossed, and it crossed in about the space of nine minutes and the rest of the forty was a reading that was proposed and the answer to it.

The reader of this body proposed the reading himself, and the reason came before the proposal and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and forty-four, and it is his and of nobody else's, and the woman at the other end of the table did not look at him while he was saying it and the page cannot see what she was doing.

**"I am going to say what a reading of the standing wood would be and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have stood in a piece of ground in this county and felt what it was doing, and a woman who keeps four accounts of her own has asked me in the last fortnight twice whether that is something anybody can do on purpose, and I have not answered her either time."** "A reading would be a way of knowing what the ground wants instead of asking it. And the answer on this table this evening is that a reading is not a maintenance act. A maintenance act is something you do to a piece of ground. A reading is something you take from one, and those two are not the same, and the second of them does not leave the ground in a different state than it was in."

The eight places that did not come out of the standing willow in a month now three months back are not on the page tonight and did not come out of it again, and the order they came out in is the order a wood keeps things in, which is pressure and temperature and season and the order things happened in, and a wood keeps one order and there is no second ground in this holding or in four counties that will give up the same one to check it against.

What came out of that wood on the twenty-ninth of a month now three months back is real and it is a set of real pieces put together in an order by something that has no man in it, and the comfort is not standing and it has not been replaced by anything, and the father of the man who said it is not alive in that wood and was not alive in it then, and the day does not come back.

He said that last part himself and he said it about nine inches above the table, and half of that room heard all of it and half of that room heard the half of it about the order a wood keeps things in, and the two are not added to each other and neither of the two was asked.

The woman who keeps the seed house gave the reason before the thing she said, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-eight, and it is hers and of nobody else's, and there is no figure of the man of about thirty-one of Silling on this page and no figure of the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden on this page.

**"I am going to say the one thing I am not giving him and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been the person every body sends its seed question to and I have never once been asked what the ground wants, and a woman who has not been asked is not going to be told it by a reading."** "He can read it. He can stand in a wood and know what it is doing. I have to have nine carts go out of a yard one at a time and count them, and one of those two is a thing I can be given and the other one is not, and I am not going to sit in a room for forty minutes and be shown the second."

It was over in about thirty-one minutes and nobody used the stop signal, and the clerk of this holding wrote that down at the long wall at the time and not afterward, and the figure of the minutes is his and it is not a figure of the session and the two have not been added in this building in four years.

At about the fourth hour, with the session over and the room still shut and nobody in it having said anything, something came up under the floor of the one room about six inches to the left of where the man of about fifty was standing, and it was over in about a second and a half, and nobody in that room read it.

The man of about fifty did not read it and did not name it and was not asked to, and nobody in that room has said what it was afterwards and the count of times this holding has read it is two and it was not read a third time this morning.

There are two figures in it and no name against either of them and no unit, and a thing that came up under a floor in a room where nine people were sitting still is not a measurement and is not a figure of the ground and is not on the well house door and is not in the second book, and the second book was shut at the ninth hour and the two figures are not in it.

The man of about fifty was at neither end of that session and was not thanked for not knowing what the word was, and he has not been asked what the word is and he has not been told and he has said once that he would rather be in the room at the wrong end of a table.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table the whole of the time and the session was not about either of them, and nobody in that room put them into it, and the two of them are about nine inches apart this evening exactly as they were this morning, and the count of questions the reader of this body has put to the two of them is one and is one.

The two declinings are not settled in this room. They were not settled on the fifteenth, they were not settled on the seventeenth, they were not settled on the twentieth when a clerk ruled a line under both of them and it came out again, and they are not settled on the twenty-fourth, and a room with the door shut is the only instrument this building has and it has been used this evening and it did not open anything.

A session that carries a schedule and its cost and a reading that is refused is a session that has done what it was entered to do, and what it was entered to do is on the page and is not the settling of two things said no in the same week by two bodies who have never spoken to each other.

The two men who read the boards were in the one room from the sixth hour and read their own figures to each other afterwards at about the ninth, on the wall, not at the seventh hour and not out loud in the room, and the run of mornings stands at eighty-eight of a hundred and fifty-nine or it does not, and neither of the two has said which and the page does not supply it.

The arm carried four inches and two branches at the second hour and the same at the ninth, and the four accounts of that arm are four and do not reconcile, and a session is not a mark and a schedule is not a mark and a reading is not a mark and a refusal is not a mark, and this evening did not make a fifth account of anything.

The man who came up the fen road with the list of ten was at the gate at about the sixth hour and went and stood there for the whole of the session without going in, and nobody in this building has asked him why and the count of questions this holding has put to him is nil and is nil, and a man who stands at a gate for half an hour in the cold is a man who has decided not to be in the room.

The man in the bad coat was outside the door at about the fourth hour when the thing came up under the floor and did not come in, and the roll of paper was in his coat and was not wet and nobody has asked him about either of those things.

The four ruled lines under the two words entered on the twentieth of a month now three months back are on their own table in the corner and are still empty and the answer to them is not due, and a session that was entered before it was carried out is not an answer to four lines that anybody wrote down a month now three months back.

The offer is still lying face up on the low board where it has lain since the nineteenth of a month now three months back, undated, unfilled, not withdrawn, with no line for a name on it anywhere. Forty minutes in a room with the door shut is not a reason for taking it off that board, and this holding has never taken one off it for anything in four years.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and twenty-eight days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and a purpose and a duration and a stop signal and a witness are not that form, and the five of those have never been added in this holding in four years.

The day sheet with no date at the head of it is still in a stack of about a foot and a half behind the fourth book, in the order it was used, and nobody has moved it to the top of it.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour and the two sheets are about nine inches apart on it, and the second book is shut with seven entries in it of which the seventh is the one this morning, and the thing that came up under the floor this afternoon is in none of the seven and in none of the four books, and a schedule of nine loads and four days and what it has cost this holding's book is where this holding ends, and the two bodies that said no have still said no and one of them has never been asked anything at all.

> Entered by the clerk of this holding at the first hour, in his own hand, as one unbroken line: **The twenty-fourth. The second line of the rota worked, the outlet end and the two stones, four people from the second hour until the fifth. The pair not read, and the reason is in the room. The same day in and the same day out. Nine hundred and sixty-five hundredweight to the launder, being the two hundred and thirty-fourth. The seventh session was entered in the second book at the sixth hour, before the session, and was carried out with the door shut and was over in about thirty-one minutes and the stop signal was not used. A schedule of nine loads and four days and what it has cost this holding's book crossed the table between the two of them.
