# Chapter 474

## Somebody Who Is Not On Anything

The first line of the rota came round at the second hour on the twenty-seventh of this month and was worked by four people, and it was the pump house and the frame, and the frame went down about six minutes this morning and came up about four, and the black out of the bottom of the seam was about an inch and a half and it went onto the boards outside and stayed there.

The mark in the floor where the base of it stands is four inches deep in the middle and worn round at the corners, and nobody in this building has measured it, and it was there before the floor was laid and the floor was laid before the first of the four books was opened.

It rained from about the fifth hour and it was still raining at the ninth, and the ditch on the middle road came up about three inches in four hours and did not go over the lip at the bend, and the water in the yard was about two inches in the south end of it at ten and the ninth cart was standing in it where it was put yesterday.

Each of the two of them said one figure at the seventh hour and neither of them said the other, and the run of mornings is **eighty-nine of a hundred and sixty-one**. The boards carry **six hundred and twenty-one days** and **six hundred and sixty-eight**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The twenty-seventh went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and sixty-three hundredweight.**

It came in five under Saturday and stands at the two hundred and thirty-seventh figure of a run in which nothing has ever come up twice, and the fall went onto the falls. A hundred and seven against ninety-seven is a hundred and two hundred and four, and nobody in this building has ever added the two ends of that to the figure over them.

The four bodies of households stand at two hundred and thirty-five days and the clause beneath is the two hundred and thirty-fourth of the mornings out of two hundred and thirty-sixth, one under and one over as it is on every row of this sheet, and the three of those numbers are a count of mornings and not a count of hundredweight.

A cart came up the four-mile road at about the seventh hour with four sacks on it and went back down it empty, and it was not one of the nine and it was not on anybody's sheet, and there is no column in any of the four books in this building that a cart that is not one of the nine could go into.

The two men on it were not from this holding and neither of them is from anybody this holding has a name for. The third of the four was a woman walking beside it who did not get off the track and did not speak to anybody at the gate and did not stop.

They came up that road and they went down it again, and the road is a made road with a gate at the top of it and a trough about twenty yards short of the gate, and the count of times a cart has gone up it in eleven years is not a figure anybody in this holding has ever been sent.

Four households hold that piece between the second gate and the top of the four-mile road, and their holding has no name on any paper in this building, and they are not a body of households and they are not one of the four bodies of households and they are not on the maintenance list that went up the fen road on the second of this month and came back on the second with nothing added to it.

They are not in the Undercommons either. Nobody here has told them that they are, and nobody here has told anybody else that they are not, and the roll that came up the fen road on the nineteenth of a month now three months back has no heading at the top of it and no list of members on it and cannot be checked against four households at the second gate by anybody in this county.

**A body that is not on anything uses the road anyway, because a road is a road.**

That is the whole of it and it is said once on this page and it is not improved on, and nobody in this holding has stopped that cart, nobody in this holding has asked the man at the gate to write down who was on it, and nobody in this holding has put a mark against the ninth of the list of ten for four people who were not walking and were not on it at all.

A road is a road, and a gate is a gate, and a trough twenty yards short of a gate is a trough, and the two men on that cart this morning were two men on a cart going to somewhere to do something that is not anybody's business at the gate, and if this holding had stopped them it would have had to say why and there is nowhere in four clauses or in four books where the why would go.

A holding that keeps a list of the bodies that have asked it for things, and it has four years of that, and eleven years of answering about nine hundred letters, and it does not have a list of the people who use a road, and it has never been asked to have one, and nobody in this county has ever sent anybody a form for finding that out.

A list of nine columns and nine duties went up the fen road on the second of this month, and four of those marks are in the sixth column and the other five places in that column have never been filled by anybody, and it came back down the same road on the second with nothing added at the head of it and nothing taken off at the foot of it.

Nobody has written a name in the sixth column and nobody has been asked to and it is not going to be filled by anybody in this holding while it is a list of duties and not a roll of anybody, and a list with four marks in it and five blanks is not a shorter list and is not a longer one and is not a measure of anybody's work.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table all morning with the window open above them against the rain, and the two of them are about nine inches apart, and the first of them has a clause at the head of it and the second of them has not, and neither of them was improved this morning and neither of them was withdrawn.

There are about nine people in this holding who could tell you which of those two sheets is the older, and the count of times either of them has been moved off that table since the first of this month is nil and is nil, and a room that keeps two refusals nine inches apart for a month is a room that has decided something about them and has not said so out loud.

The woman who keeps the seed house came in at about the eighth hour with a sheet under her arm and put it face down on the long table without opening it and went out again, and nobody asked her what it was and nobody asked her to turn it over, and the sheet was still face down at the ninth hour.

She has had four books open since the sixth of this month and one of them shut on Friday, and a woman who puts a sheet face down in a room and leaves it there has decided something about the room, and the man of about fifty looked at it twice and did not ask and has not been asked.

The rain was still going at the ninth hour and the water in the middle road had come up about another inch, and the ninth cart was still in the south end of the yard, and nobody in this holding has moved it and nobody has written down that it is there, and nobody in this holding thanked anybody for leaving it where it was.

The clerk of this holding had the second book open at the two entries about nine inches apart and he was not going to put a third thing in the middle of them, and the count of requests is fifty-three and the section-nine notes are fifty-three and neither of them moved this morning, and a cart that is not one of the nine is not a request.

The clerk of this holding gave the reason first before he said the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is five, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about a road and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been the clerk of this holding for four years and I have opened that second book four years and in four years of opening it there is not one page in it about anybody using a road, and there is not one page in it about anybody not using one either."** "I have a column for what bodies have asked this holding. I have got fifty-three in it. I have got no column at all for the four households at the second gate who went up a made road at seven in the morning in the rain with four sacks and came back down it empty, and I am not going to rule one this morning, and I would like it written down that I have noticed."

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and thirty-one days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and it is not read out a fifth time in this month, and a cart going up a road that nobody logged is not that form, and a form with four ruled lines on its own table in the corner is not that form either, and the three of them have never been added in this building in four years.

Marek was in the one room from about the fifth hour with his coat still on from the fen road and had the third book open in front of him and did not write anything in it, and the man of about fifty gave the reason before the thing and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-two, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say what a column would have to have at the head of it, and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have ruled about four hundred lines in three counties and every single one of them had something at the top of it before there was anything under it, and that is the whole of the trick and it is not a trick anybody in this county is proud of."** "You would have had to write ROAD USERS at the top of it, and then you would have had to decide whether a household is one, and a barrow is one, and a man of about thirty-one of Silling with nine sacks on a barrow he owns is two, and I have watched you three days not write a heading on anything and I am telling you today that you were right and that it is going to be a problem in about two years."

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came through the yard at about the seventh hour with the barrow handle in his hand and a rope over his shoulder and he did not stop, and nobody in this holding has asked him what he thinks about four households going up a road he goes up every morning, and the count of questions this holding has put to him about anything this month is nil and is nil.

The man who came up the fen road with the list of ten was in the lean-to with the slate and did not come out, and the man in the bad coat was at the tap on the north side of the yard, and the two of them have still never spoken and the count of times this building has introduced one of them to the other is nil and is nil.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came in out of the rain at about the eighth hour and put the bar back in the shed without being told to, and the second of the four lines in his own book is a hundred and twenty-five days old this evening and has nothing on it, and he has been asked nothing about the four households and nothing about the four lines and nothing about the bar.

Nobody in that yard thanked anybody. A man with a barrow and a rope goes up a made road every morning of his life and has never once been asked what is at the top of it, and a cart that is not one of the nine went up it yesterday and today and would go up it tomorrow, and neither the barrow nor the cart is in any column in this building and the two of them are not the same journey and have never been compared.

The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour, and the second book was shut with two entries in it, and the register form was still on the middle table with nothing at the head of it, and the paper on the low board at the far end of the room was still face up with nothing filled in and nothing withdrawn and no date and no line for a name on it anywhere.

A sheet face up on a board since the nineteenth of a month now three months back and a sheet face down on a table since the eighth hour this evening are two pieces of paper in opposite positions, and this building has never turned one of them over to see what the other one looks like, and it is not going to on a Sunday.

The four households at the second gate have put a roof on one building this year and the man who did the roof has been paid in sacks and not in money and this holding has not been told any of that and has not asked.

A cart with four sacks on it and a woman walking beside it in the rain at the seventh hour is a fact about four households and not a fact about a road and not a fact about this holding, and the only one of the three of those that is anybody's business at the gate is the third one and nobody has made it a business.

The sheet with the shortage on it is still on the long table an inch and a half above a wet floor and it has one word at the head of it and a figure and a date, and the clerk of this holding did not pick it up on the way to the door and did not put anything under it and did not say anything about it to anybody, and a sheet that has been on a table since before the month turned is a sheet that has never been off a table.

A cart with four sacks on it is the third object in this building this week that is about seed and is not in a column, and the seed house has one schedule, the long table has one sheet, and the road has a cart on it, and this holding has not put the three of them under one another and this page puts no total under any of them.

Nobody thanked anybody. The rain was still going at the ninth hour and the water in the middle road had come up about another inch, and the ninth cart was still standing in the south end of the yard where it was put on Friday, and nobody in this holding has moved it and nobody has written down that it is there.
