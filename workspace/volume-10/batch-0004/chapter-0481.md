# Chapter 481

## Forty-Eight And Thirty-Five

At the second hour of the fourth of this month the fourth line came round and went past unworked, and there was nobody on the headland above Marden to take it, and that is seventy-one mornings of that line gone past.

The count of mornings that rotation has put in front of this holding is now a hundred, of which twenty-nine were worked and seventy-one were not, and the seventy-one have all been this line, and the figure that has not moved in this year or in any year this building can date is the twenty-nine.

The two reckonings of that rotation are four years apart in their origins and neither has ever been corrected against the other, and where they disagree a page prints both. By the reckoning made in this building, today is its forty-eighth morning. By the other, it is the thirty-fifth. The thirteen between the two is four years old and has never once been explained and nobody here has been asked to explain it.

This page carries no figure for the run of mornings, and the reason is on the page: the man who reads the near side of the boards went up the coast road at the sixth hour with the empty carts and was not back in the yard until after the ninth, and the man who reads the far side would not read his own side out by himself, and it is a Sunday and nobody in this holding thought to send anybody else.

The pair stands on the boards at **six hundred and twenty-eight days** and **six hundred and seventy-five**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The fourth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and eighty hundredweight.**

It is the two hundred and forty-fourth figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice, and this morning's went on the rises. The window behind it is a hundred and eleven against a hundred, and a hundred and two hundred and eleven, and nobody in this building has added the two ends of that to the figure over them.

What this morning's sheet carries against four bodies of households is two hundred and forty-two days, and the clause written under that number is the two hundred and forty-first of those mornings out of two hundred and forty-third, which is one under and one over, and a count of mornings is not a count of loads and not a count of days.

The forty-fourth ninth-day return came off the steelyard at about the third hour and it is **four hundred and thirty-seven hundredweight**, and the rise on that board is eleven hundredweight every time, and the pair of names against it is the thirty-fourth pair in eleven years and it is eleven above the pair that went up on the twenty-fifth.

For the twenty-eighth time in eleven years the second rule on that board went into the clerk's book with nothing against it, and a count of blanks is not the count of a man, and no name has ever been entered against that rule in this building and none is going to be.

Before the light was full it was checked twice, once at about the third hour against the sheet that came back inside the same fold, and once at about the fifth hour by the man of about fifty out of the third book, and the two checks came out different and neither half of the figure has ever been added to the other in this building in four years.

> THE SHEET THAT CAME BACK WITH THE FORTY-FOURTH RETURN, IN THE HAND OF THE MAN WHO BROUGHT IT UP, WITH THE FIGURE AT THE HEAD OF IT AND THE DAY BESIDE IT AND NOTHING AT THE FOOT OF IT: **FORTY-FOURTH. FOUR HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT. THIRTY-FOURTH PAIR.** THE SHEET THAT CAME BACK WITH THE ONE BEFORE IT CARRIED NINE DAYS UNDER THE FIGURE AND THIS ONE CARRIES NOTHING UNDER THE FIGURE AT ALL, AND NOBODY AT THE STEELYARD HAS BEEN ASKED WHICH OF THE TWO IS THE USUAL ONE AND NOBODY HERE HAS ASKED. THERE IS NO HEADING AT THE HEAD OF THIS SHEET AND NO NAME AT THE FOOT OF IT AND THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD THERE WAS LEFT BLANK AGAIN AND NOTHING WAS PUT IN IT.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at about the seventh hour with four sacks on it and came down it loaded, and the number of journeys stands at eleven, and eleven is a floor and it is not raised this morning and it is not going to be raised on any morning this month, and a barrow going up a made road is not one of the nine and is not on the coast road and the two of those roads are four miles and about nine apart.

The woman who coordinates the moving reserves came back into this county on the third of this month and was on the coast road yesterday and was in this yard at about the seventh hour this evening with a sheet of paper in her hand, and she is not support staff and has not been asked to be and this holding has not offered her anything.

She is not on any list in this building. She has no name on the second book and no name at the head of anything and the page that goes with the plan does not carry one, and the count of times this holding has written a surname at the head of a piece of paper is one and is one and it is on the acknowledgment at the foot of a schedule and it is not this.

**"I am going to say what I have done and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that nine loads through nine miles of cut one at a time is a plan and not a good plan, and it is mine, and I have not come back to this yard to be told it is the right one."** "It takes longer. I have not written down how much longer and I am not going to, and if anybody in this county asks me for the difference between the two plans they can have the method and not the figure, and the method is a cart, and a second man, and a piece of sacking on a stick, and there is no instrument in four counties that turns that into anything else."

That is a plan. It is not better than the first plan and it is not worse, and the first plan is in the seed house with four days at the head of it, and the two plans are both true and neither of them has been added to the other and nobody in this building has put them in a column.

She said it in the yard and not in the one room, and the door of the one room was open all evening and about nine people were standing about four feet apart in it and the two of them were in the yard, and nobody in this holding went out to the door and stood in the yard with them, and that was decided by nobody and it happened anyway.

A woman who has been out on the fen since the second of this month comes back with a plan and stands in a yard and says it is not a good plan, and neither of those two things is a thing the people of four counties have a column for, and the count of plans that have been brought into this building's yard this year is not a figure anybody has kept.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table in the one room with the door open, and she did not look at them and was not shown them, and nobody in this building has told her that two bodies said no to the same connection in the same week, and the count of questions she has been asked about either of them is nil and is nil.

What she was asked, by the man of about fifty, at about the seventh hour, standing in the yard, was one thing, and it was not about the plan.

**"I am going to ask you one question and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have kept four books in three counties for thirty-one years and I have never once in thirty-one years had anybody hand me a plan they had not written a reason for, and you have not written a reason for this one and I would like to know whether that is a decision or an oversight."** "You do not have to answer it. I am going to write down that I asked it and that is all I am going to write down."

The count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-six and it is his and of nobody else's, and she did not answer it and he did not ask her twice, and the questions column did not move and a question asked in a yard is not a request.

There is a sheet on the low board at the far end of the one room and it has been lying face up since the nineteenth of a month now four months back, when it was refused, with nothing filled in and nothing withdrawn and no date on it and no line anywhere on it for a name. A woman coming into a county with a plan does not make grounds for taking a paper off a board, and four years have gone by in this building without one such ground being found.

The register form lies on the middle table at a hundred and thirty-eight days with nothing at the head of it, and the four ruled lines are on their own table in the corner and are still empty, and the answer to them is not due. None of those three has been filled and none of them has been moved, and a plan that takes a day is not a reason to fill any of them.

Nine hundred yards off, on a door with seven columns ruled on it, the seventh of the seven carries fifty-nine, and that column is read on the first and the thirtieth and on no other morning of the year, and this Sunday is neither of them. Four of the other six on that door have stood with nothing at the top of them for four years.

The arm carried four inches and two branches at the second hour and the same at the ninth, and the four accounts of that arm are four and do not reconcile, and a return of four hundred and thirty-seven hundredweight and a sheet with nothing under the figure and a plan that costs a day are not on it, and this morning did not make a fifth account of anything.

The man of about fifty was at the long wall with the third book shut and not open, and Marek was at the far end of it with his hands behind his back and did not go out into the yard, and neither of them asked the woman who coordinates the moving reserves a question and she did not offer either of them one, and the count of questions anybody in this holding has put to her since the third of this month is nil and is nil.

About four people in this holding would have put a name at the head of that plan this evening and about four would not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man of about fifty was not asked which of them he was in, and a piece of paper with no name on it is a document and it can be held up in a room.

The man who came up the fen road with the list of ten was in the yard at about the eighth hour with the slate and did not come in, and the man in the bad coat was at the tap on the north side, and the two of them have not spoken since Friday and nobody in this building has asked either of them to.

The water in the middle road came up about an inch overnight and went down about one of them by the ninth hour, and the frost will be back in the small hours, and the next time the fourth line comes round is the eighth of this month and there is nobody at the headland above Marden to work it then either.

The man in the cart shed at the mouth of the cut is still in the cart shed. This holding has not been and is not going to, his name is not in this building, and nobody here has a figure of any kind that belongs to him, and nobody has asked him anything and nobody in this holding intends to.

There was rain at about the fourth hour and it had gone off the grass again by the seventh, and the yard had about an inch of standing water in the south end of it at nine for the second time in three days, and the man who came up the fen road with the list of ten put the barrow in the lean-to and got the slate in out of it, and the slate is eight words and a decision about a ford and nothing was done about any of that and nothing was lost.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came in at about the eighth hour with his coat off and his boots full and put the bar back in the shed without being told to, and the second of the four lines in his own book is a hundred and thirty-two days old and has nothing on it, and he was not asked about the plan and was not asked about the bar and was not asked about the four lines and the count of questions this holding has put to him this month is nil and is nil.

The sheet of terms is still carrying its heading on a third column that has nothing in it, and that heading has been on it since the twenty-first of a month now two months back. The five terms have not been improved, have not been unwithdrawn and have not been added to, and a plan brought into a yard by a woman on a Sunday is not a sixth term and is not going to be one.

The clerk of this holding put the day's figure into the second book at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the sheet that came back with it went in behind the figure and not on the same page, and the two of them are two pieces of paper and this building has not put them side by side on purpose.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour and the second book was shut with the day's figure in it and the sheet with nothing under the figure with it, and the barrow is at eleven and the plan is on the coast road and the offer is on the low board and the four ruled lines are empty, and this is a Sunday and nothing in this building was settled this week and two things that were said no in the same week have still been said no.

> Entered by the clerk of this holding at the first hour, in his own hand, as one unbroken line: **The fourth. The fourth line of the rota came round and was not worked, for the seventy-first morning running, and the two counts of that rotation are forty-eight and thirty-five and the thirteen between them is four years old. The pair not read, and the reason is on the page. The same day in and the same day out. Nine hundred and eighty hundredweight to the launder, being the two hundred and forty-fourth. The forty-fourth ninth-day return came off the steelyard at four hundred and thirty-seven hundredweight, the thirty-fourth pair, reconciled twice and the two halves not added, and the second rule on the board was left blank. The barrow went up the four-mile road and the count of journeys is eleven and eleven is a floor. A woman who coordinates the moving reserves came into this yard at the seventh hour with a plan and no name at the head of it and said it was hers and that it was not a good plan and did not say what it costs.** Nothing of this is a forecast and nothing of it is a cause and nothing of it is a total.
