# Chapter 480

## A Day That Is Not Priced

Four people took the first line of the rota at the second hour of the third of this month, which is the pump house and the frame. It went down about five minutes and came up about three, and the black out of the bottom of the seam is about an inch and a half and went onto the boards outside the pump house and stayed there.

The mark where the base of the frame stands is four inches deep in the middle and worn round at the corners, and nobody in this building has measured it this month and nobody is going to, and a man who has never had a mark measured is not thereby given a fifth of anything.

The two of them said their own sides at the seventh hour and stopped there, and the run of mornings is **ninety-two of a hundred and sixty-seven**. The boards carry **six hundred and twenty-seven days** and **six hundred and seventy-four**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The third went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and seventy-two hundredweight.**

Five under Friday, and the two hundred and forty-third figure of a run that has never written a number down twice, and the fall went onto the falls. The window is a hundred and ten against a hundred, a hundred and two hundred and ten, and the two ends of it are not added to the figure above them by anybody in this holding.

Four bodies of households have not moved for two hundred and forty-one days, and the clause printed under that figure is the two hundred and fortieth of those mornings out of two hundred and forty-second, one under and one over. The three of those numbers are a count of mornings and are not a count of loads and are not a count of carts.

The convoy is going to be re-planned.

It is not a decision anybody in this building has taken and it is not a decision the clerk of this holding has been asked to enter, and it is a plan and a plan is a document and the document is not in this building and will not be, and the four counties are not fools and are not saints and nobody has written to any of them this morning.

The plan is that the nine loads go through the nine miles of cut one at a time, and not two abreast, and not in a string, and that a man stands at the narrow place with a bar and a flag and does not let a second cart up onto the shelf until the first one is past it, and that a second man stands at the far mouth of the cut and does the same on that side.

That is the whole of it and it is not better than the first plan and it is not worse, and the first plan said nine loads in four days and this one does not say four days and has not been asked to.

The nine miles of cut is nine miles of a made road with a bank on the south side for most of it and about two hundred yards of it with a bank on the north as well, and at the narrow place both of them come in and the road is about nine feet wide and the shelf above it is about four feet deep and there is nowhere at that place to put a cart that is not the shelf.

There is no flag at that place and there never has been, and the flag in the plan is a piece of sacking on a stick and it is not a signal anybody in this county uses and it is not going to become one, and a man with a piece of sacking on a stick at the top of a cut is a man anybody can see from a mile off and that is the whole of what he is there for.

The first plan did not have the two men in it. The first plan had nine loads and four days and a road, and the road was there and the four days were there and the two men were not, and nobody has said that out loud in this building and the page is not going to say it for them.

**"I am going to say what changed this morning and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been on the coast road twice since Friday and the shelf at the narrow place is about four feet deep with nine sacks stacked four and three and two on it, and there is no second place on that stretch for a cart to stand, and I have never once asked anybody to tell me that and nobody had to."** "One cart at a time. That is the whole of the change and it is not a finding about anything. A man under a wall on Friday was under the part of a wall that had a cart above it, and a man under a wall is a man, and I have written the second of those two things down and not the first."

The count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and forty-nine and it is his and of nobody else's, and the man of about fifty was in the one room at about the fifth hour and has not been asked to write any of it down and was not asked and did not offer, and Marek was at the mouth of the cut for about twenty minutes on Saturday and not on Friday and nobody in this building has asked him which of those two he would rather have done.

The man of about fifty gave the reason first before he said the thing at the long wall, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-five, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about a day and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have entered about nine hundred things in four books in four years and every one of them was a thing I could see the whole of, and a day is the only thing in this county I have never once been able to see the whole of."** "One cart at a time through nine miles costs a day. I know it costs a day because there are nine loads and there is one way through and the way takes what it takes. I am not going to put a figure on it and neither is anybody in this room, and I would like it written down that I have said the word costs and then stopped, and that the stopping is the point and not an oversight."

That is a day, and the day is a day, and this page does not say what it is worth and is not going to and nobody in this building has said what it is worth and nobody has been asked.

The man in the cart shed at the mouth of the cut is still in the cart shed. He is not in this building and he is not in this county's books and his name is not in this holding, and nobody in this holding has been to the shed since Friday and nobody has written to anybody about him, and the count of questions this holding has put to anybody about him is nil and is nil.

A man in a bed at the mouth of a cut is the reason the plan changed and the page does not put the two of them in one sentence with a word under them, and a reason is not a price and a price is not a reason and this building has four years of practice at not putting them together and is not going to stop on a Saturday.

The form with four lines is on the back of the schedule in the seed house and line one still carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, and lines two, three and four are still empty, and no heading has been written at the top of it, and a form is not a column and is not going to be made into one.

The sixth rule of that schedule is headed for the things this holding does not know and carries six rows ruled under it with no unit against any of the six, and the seventh row is not ruled, and a road is not a seventh and a day is not a seventh and a man in a cart shed is not a seventh.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and thirty-seven days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and it is not read out a fifth time in four years, and a plan that costs a day is not that form and the eight days of a man in a shed are not that form and the two of them have never been added in this building in four years.

A door nine hundred yards off has seven columns ruled on it, and the seventh of the seven stands at fifty-nine and is read on the first and on the thirtieth and on nothing else in the year, and the third of a month is not one of those two mornings. Four of the six columns beside it have had nothing at the top of them for four years and have not been given anything since.

The pattern-holder counts are two for four and one for five and there are two rootmarks and one who can hold a pattern for four and walk out of the ground afterwards, and a count of pattern-holders is not a count of ends, and a bar with a flag at the narrow place is not a pattern and is not on either of those two lists.

A man who is the only person in four counties who can hold a pattern at either end of a thing is not the man who stands at the narrow place with a piece of sacking on a stick, and this holding has no instrument that would let anybody put the two of them in the same column, and it is not going to acquire one in a fortnight.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling took the sacks off the top of the ninth cart at about the seventh hour and put them back on in a different order and did not say why and was not asked, and the second of the four lines in his own book is a hundred and thirty-one days old and has nothing on it.

The man who came up the fen road with the list of ten was at the gate at about the eighth hour with the slate and did not come in, and the man in the bad coat was at the tap, and the two of them spoke on Friday for the first time in a fortnight and neither of them has said anything since.

The clerk of this holding had the second book open at a page with a line ruled at the top of it and nothing at the head of the line, and he looked at it for a while and did not write anything at the head of it, and the count of sessions entered in this holding's book is seven and every one of the seven was entered before the thing it was entered for, and there was no eighth entered on Saturday and there is not going to be one, because a session is not a plan and a plan is not a session and the two of them have never been in the same book.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table about nine inches apart with the window shut against the rain, and neither of them was improved and neither of them was withdrawn, and the two bodies have still said no in the same week and neither of them knows that the other one has, and a plan that costs a day has not changed either of those two answers and is not going to.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and thirty-seven days with nothing at the head of it, and the paper on the low board is face up with nothing on it and no date and no line for a name, and a day that a re-plan costs is not a reason to take a piece of paper off a board and this holding has never used one as a reason in four years.

The four ruled lines under the two words entered on the twentieth of a month now four months back are on their own table in the corner and are still empty, and the answer to them is not due, and nobody in this holding has ever written a figure in one of them and nobody is going to write what a day costs in one of them either.

There is a stack of day sheets about a foot and a half deep behind the fourth book and it is in the order it was used, and today's sheet went on the top of it at about the ninth hour, and the sheet with no date at the head of it is still in the middle of that stack, and a day that costs a day is two day sheets and not one and the two have not been compared.

The trough at the top of the four-mile road went over its lip for the fourth time since it was filled and came back down inside its own lip before the light went, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling did not fill it and did not empty it and did not say anything about it to anybody.

The woman who keeps the seed house was in her own building from about the fourth hour with the light on until the ninth and came out once at the fifth, and the schedule of nine loads and four days is in there and there is no second copy of it in this building, and nobody has asked her this morning what she thinks of one cart at a time and she has not offered and she will not be asked.

She is the person every body in four counties sends its seed question to and there is no count of that anywhere in this holding's books and there is not going to be one, and a woman who is asked a question every day of her life and is not asked about a road that is carrying her seed is a woman who has been left out of a room and has not said so.

A plan is not on that arm and a day is not on it and two men with a bar and a piece of sacking on a stick are not on it, and this morning did not make a fifth account of anything and the four of them are four.

The frost was on the yard at about the sixth hour and it was gone by the eighth, and the water in the middle road came up about two inches overnight and went down about one of them by the ninth, and the north end of the ditch is running full, and the fourth line of the rota comes round tomorrow and there is nobody at the headland above Marden to work it.

The clerk of this holding put the day's sheet on the stack behind the fourth book at about the ninth hour and it went on the top, and the second book was shut with the day in it and nothing at the head of the line, and the count of figures this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and is two and the first of the two is not named, and a plan that costs a day is not a third of them.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour, and the second book was shut with the day in it, and the plan is on the coast road and not in this building, and a day is a day, and the ninth cart is on the coast road and not in this yard, and nobody in this holding has said what any of it costs and nobody has been asked.
