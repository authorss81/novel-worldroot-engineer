# Chapter 451

## A Count Of Structures

The fourth of this month was a Friday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the pump house and the frame again, and the frame went down about four minutes and came up about three, and the black in the bottom of the seam was about an inch again and the man of about thirty-one of Silling put it on the boards outside the pump house and did not wash it off.

He came up the eleven minutes and asked the clerk whether it wanted writing down and the clerk said no again, and the man did not ask why, and the count of times the clerk of this holding has said no to that question is two and is two and the clerk has not been asked about either.

The run of mornings is not on this page. The pair is read aloud on alternate mornings only and this is not one of them, and the reason is written here so that nobody has to guess it in four years: both men who read the boards were at the pump house with the frame between the second hour and the fourth, and neither was in this room at the seventh hour, and no third man was asked.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and ninety-eight days** and **six hundred and forty-five**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The fourth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and it is the last morning of this half of the book and there are no half books, the clerk said, and read it back once and did not read it back twice.

**Nine hundred and thirty-five hundredweight.**

Up eight off Thursday, and the two hundred and fourteenth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and the rise went onto the rises, and ninety-six and eighty-five make a hundred and eighty-one, and the figure on the sheet does not reset at anything and is not going to be turned by anybody.

Two hundred and twelve days is what this morning's sheet says about the four bodies of households, written for the two hundred and eleventh of the mornings out of two hundred and thirteenth. The launder is a separate book and the four launders are a separate count and nothing in this building has ever put the two figures in one column.

The list of rough paper and the sheet with the shortage on it were on the long table on the same morning and they had been on it for about four days together and nobody in this building has put them next to each other on purpose and nobody in this building has separated them either.

The shortage has been on a sheet for twelve days. It has one word at the head of it and a figure and a date and no body against it and no cause against it and no unit beside the one there is, and nobody in this holding has added a second word to it and nobody in the four counties has written to ask what a shortage is measured in, and about four people in this holding have wanted to write to one of the counties and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the clerk of this holding was not asked.

The list of ten is a sheet of rough paper with ten lines on it in a man's road hand and eight of the ten are in this building's book and two are not, and the tenth of the ten is a ford and it carries nine feet of water in a bad week and there is no figure against the bad week and there is no year against it either, and the clerk found the year for the ninth on the Wednesday and did not find one for the tenth and about four people in this holding have said that a ford is not dated and about four have said that a ford is dated and this holding has not found out which, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Nobody in this building has added the two sheets together. Nobody has written one figure under both of them and nobody has written one figure under either of them, and about four people in this holding have said that the two of them are not the same kind of object and about four have said that they are the same kind of object because both of them are on a table, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

A count of ten is a count of structures. It is not a count of days, it is not a count of households, it is not a count of loads and it is not a count of people, and this is the last time this building is going to say that sentence this month and it is not going to be said a third time in this holding.

Marek was in the one room at about the sixth hour with the two sheets in front of him and he read both of them a third time, which nobody else in this building has done, and he gave the reason before he said the thing he said, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and twenty-eight, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about those two sheets and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been the only person this holding could name against a ground for eleven years and a shortage is not a ground and a list of ten is not a ground and I have just spent a fortnight finding that out twice."** "I am not going to put a total under either of them and I am not going to be the man who does. If this holding writes one number under a list of ten and a shortage of seed, then in about four years a body in a county can take that number off a page and use it, and it will be wrong, and it will be wrong in the direction of a man who stood here at a table in October and did not have the sense to leave it blank. I have made that mistake with a number before and it took me a year to find out and about four people in this holding know how and about four do not and neither of the two fours has been asked."

Nobody in that room said that was right and nobody in that room said that it was a dodge, and about four people in this holding have said the first and about four have said the second, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man was not asked and has not been asked.

The woman who keeps the seed house was in the room and had been in and out all day and she had said nothing at all about the two sheets, and about four people in this holding have said that the woman who keeps four accounts of her own is the person a room turns to when there is a figure on a table and there was a figure on a table and she did not say anything, and about four have said that there were two things on that table and neither of them was a figure of hers, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked and has not been asked.

She said one thing at about the seventh hour and it was short and it was to nobody.

**"Twelve days. I have not put a second word on that sheet and I am not going to, and I want it written down somewhere that the not putting is mine and not anybody else's, because in about four years somebody is going to find that sheet and think nobody minded."** Nobody in that room minded visibly and about four people in this holding have thought about it since and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked and has not been asked since.

He stood at the back of the one room at about the seventh hour and listened for about twenty minutes and said nothing at all, and the count of questions this holding has put to him in that line is nil and is nil. About four people in this holding have wondered what he came for and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and he has not been asked.

The two declinings were still on the long table as well and there were four things on it by the seventh hour, and about four people in this holding have said that four things on a table is a decision waiting for a room and about four have said that a table is a table, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the table is a table.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and eight days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and a shortage is not that form and a list of ten is not that form and a declining is not that form, and the three of those have never been added in this holding in four years.

Nobody in this building has been told to take the offer off the low board and nobody is going to be. Four things on a long table are not a reason to take a piece of paper off a board, and this holding has never once used one as a reason in four years.

There is a corridor in the pan and there is a sheet in a drawer that claims it and the sheet is correct. A road with ten things on it is not that corridor. Neither is a shortage, and neither is the sentence the man of about fifty said out loud at the sixth hour with his hand on a table. The two have never been added in this building in four years.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-eight and was not read this morning, being read on the thirtieth and the first and on no other morning in the year, and the second of the six blank columns beside it has a heading at the top of it and nothing under it, and the heading is four words and took paint off a brush that had been used for something else, and about four people in this holding have said that a heading is necessary and about four have said that a heading is not sufficient, and neither of the two fours has been asked and that has been said in this building before and is not going to be said again this month.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at about the seventh hour and came down it loaded, and eleven journeys is a floor and was not raised, and the second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and two days old and has nothing on it, and he brought the barrow back and put it away and did not say anything about the two things that were on his barrow and nobody in this holding has asked him what they were.

The Undercommons letter is on the long table and the year has not come back and the ordinary post is the ordinary post, and the request for a certified figure of days is in the second book with a date against it and the answer went out on the twentieth in the clerk's hand and it is one sentence, and neither of those two things is on the drawer this evening and about four people in this holding have said the drawer is for things with no column and both of those have columns and about four have said a column is a place to write and not a place to keep, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The compost figure is twenty-two not discharged in twenty-two and the word PAID is in front of it and the line does not fall again this month. Ten structures is not a measurement of it. A shortage is not a measurement of it that this holding is able to check. And a drawer is not a measurement of anything, least of all of this.

The arm carries four inches and two branches and carried the same at the ninth hour as at the second, and nothing has been added to it. A list is not on it, a shortage is not on it, a declining is not on it, a heading is not on it, and a decision not to write a figure is not on it. Four accounts of that arm stand in four books in this building and do not reconcile, and two sheets on one table and a drawer and a form on another table is not a fifth.

The clerk of this holding did the thing at about the ninth hour and he said the reason out loud, which he has not done in four years, and the count of times this holding has heard him give a reason out loud is one and it is this and it is his.

**"I am putting the sheet of rough paper in the drawer with the sheet that claims the corridor, and I am saying why, because nobody has asked me and because if I do not say it now then in about four years one of the four of us will have to guess, and this building has enough things in it that were guessed at."** "That drawer is where a thing goes when this holding has no column for it and no heading for it and no second person to read it out. That is the whole of what the drawer is. It is not the bin. Everything in it is correct and nothing in it opens anything and I have never taken anything out of it and I am not starting today with a piece of paper off a road."

He put it in the drawer and shut the drawer and the sheet claiming the corridor is face up where it has been and the piece of rough paper is face up beside it and the two of them are in the same drawer and the two of them are not the same kind of object and nobody in this building has ever said so and nobody is going to now.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour and the middle table still had a form on it that had been there a hundred and eight days, and the well house door was nine hundred yards off in the dark with a heading on the second of its seven columns and nothing under it, and the low board at the far end of the room had a piece of paper on it face up with no date and no line for a name, and nobody in this building was told to move any of the three and nobody is.
