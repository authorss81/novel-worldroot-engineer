# Chapter 449

## Two Bodies, Two Instruments

The fourth line came round again on the second, at the second hour, and again there was nobody at the headland above Marden and again it went away empty. Sixty-three mornings now, and the count of them is on the page and the count of the men who have worked them has not moved all year.

The three men who were in the yard on the first line on Tuesday had left the plank in the low corner and about four of them went down at about the eighth hour to look at it and the water had gone and the plank had moved about a foot, and they put it back and put a stone on it and wrote nothing down, and about four people in this holding have said that a stone on a plank is a decision and about four have said that it is a stone on a plank, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The rota stands at ninety-two mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and sixty-three not, and twenty-nine and sixty-three are ninety-two, and the count rises by exactly one on a morning this line comes round and by nothing on any other morning, and twenty-nine taken has not moved this morning or on any morning of this year.

The two reckonings of that rotation disagree and both go down. Today is the fortieth morning of one and the twenty-seventh of the other, which is where they should be on the second of the month and is not where anybody would get by counting days. The thirteen between them has been there four years and has never once been explained.

Nothing goes on this page for the run. The seventh hour came and went and the man who reads one of the two boards had gone east at the first hour with a roll and a sheet and had not returned by the time the lamp was lit, and the second man did not read his side alone and nobody thought to ask a third. The figure is seventy-seven of a hundred and thirty-seven when somebody reads it and it is not read this morning.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and ninety-six days** and **six hundred and forty-three**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The second went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and thirty-two hundredweight.**

Up eight off the first, and the two hundred and twelfth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and it is a rise and the rise went onto the rises, and ninety-five and eighty-four make a hundred and seventy-nine.

Four bodies of households, two hundred and ten days without movement, and two hundred and ninth of the mornings out of two hundred and eleventh written in the clause. Four launders is a different thing with a different figure and the sheet that carries one of them has never carried the other, and the two have not been added in four years.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and six days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and a declining is not that form and a clause is not that form and a body with no charter is not that form, and the three of those have never been added in this holding in four years. The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and nobody in this building was told to take it off that board and nobody is going to, and two sheets on a long table are not a reason to take a piece of paper off a board and this holding has never used one as a reason.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-eight and was not read this morning, being read on the thirtieth and the first and on no other morning in the year, and six of the blank columns beside it are headed nothing and the sixth of them has a heading and nothing under it, and a declining is not a reading and a clause is not a reading and neither of them has gone into the seventh or into the sixth.

The clause was still on the long table where it had been put the evening before, and at about the ninth hour in the morning a man came in who had not been in this building since the twenty-ninth of a month now one month back, and he is a person this holding has met twice and has never asked to name, and he put a second sheet on the table beside the first and did not explain it and went and stood at the long wall.

Nobody in this holding knows whose the second sheet is and nobody asked, and the clerk of this holding did not ask him and has not been asked to, and the count of questions this holding has asked that man is nil and is nil.

> THE DECLINING, AS IT WAS ENTERED UNDER THE PROTOCOLS, IN THE HAND OF THE BODY THAT MADE IT AND IN ITS OWN WORDS, AND IT IS NOT IMPROVED: **THE SECOND CLAUSE OF THE CHARTER OF MANY WELLS GIVES A BODY WITH AN INTEREST IN A GROUND NAMED IN THE SCHEDULE A REAL RIGHT TO DECLINE AN INSPECTION OF IT, AND SAYS THAT A DECLINING ENTERED UNDER THE PROTOCOLS IS A DECLINING AND IS NOT AN APPEAL AND IS NOT A DELAY. WE DECLINE THE INSPECTION NAMED IN THE SECOND LINE OF THE SCHEDULE AGAINST THE GROUND NAMED IN THE FIRST LINE OF IT. THE GROUND IS OURS. THE THING PROPOSED ON IT IS NOT OURS. WE HAVE READ THE SECOND CLAUSE TWICE AND WE ARE USING IT AS IT IS WRITTEN. THIS IS NOT A COMPLAINT. NOTHING IS ASKED OF ANYBODY.**

The woman who keeps the seed house read the thing twice and put it down, and about four people in this holding have said she should have said something and about four have said there is nothing in that sheet to say something to, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked. The man who brought it stood at the long wall for about forty minutes and did not sit and did not read anything else, and about four people here have said that a man who stands at a wall in a room of nine is a man who has come to be in the room, and about four have said he is a man waiting to be asked, and neither of the two fours has been asked and he was not asked and did not ask to be.

The woman who keeps the seed house read it twice and put it down, and about four people in this holding have said that she should have said something and about four have said that there is nothing in that sheet to say something to, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked.

The man who had brought the sheet stood at the long wall for about forty minutes and did not sit and did not read anything else, and about four people in this holding have said that a man who stands at a wall in a room of nine is a man who has come to be in the room, and about four have said that a man who stands at a wall in a room of nine is a man who is waiting to be asked, and neither of the two fours has been asked and he was not asked and did not ask to be and about four people in this holding have said that he ought to have been asked and about four have said that he would have said no, and neither of the two fours has been asked and neither of the two fours has ever been asked anything by anybody in this building in four years.

Nobody in that room read the second line of the schedule against the first line of it because this holding does not hold the schedule, and the clerk of the first council holds it, and about four people in this holding have wanted a copy of two lines out of it since a month now two months back and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the request column does not move and is fifty-three.

What the second clause of the charter gives a body is a way of saying no to somebody who is going to look at something. That is a real thing and it is worth having, and there is a difference between a clause about being looked at and a clause about being left holding, and about four people in this holding have seen that difference since the first sheet went on this table and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and neither of the two fours had seen it before a man from a road wrote it down in his own words.

Marek gave the reason before the thing. The count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and twenty-six, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the thing about that declining and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that it works, and I have been waiting nine days for something around here not to work and this is the first thing and it has been handed to us by somebody who has read it four times."** "I am not going to argue with it. I have got no standing to argue with it. If the ground is theirs and the thing proposed on it is not theirs, then the clause is doing what a clause is for and the man that wrote it never had to be in this room and is not in this room."

The second sheet came up the fen road at about the second hour of the afternoon, on the ordinary post, in an envelope with nothing on the outside of it, and the clerk of this holding read it at about the fourth hour and carried it into the one room and put it on the long table beside the first and said what it was and said whose it was, and this time he said whose it was.

**"This one is not from a body with a charter. This one is from a body with no charter, and it is the second one in nine days, and the man who brought it up the road on Wednesday is the man who wrote the first line of the Undercommons letter, and he came with it himself and he is standing in the yard."**

Nobody went out to the yard for about two minutes. The man of about thirty-one of Silling went first, and then four other people went, and the man of about fifty went last and went slowly, and nobody has ever said why he went last and nobody has been asked.

The man in the yard was sitting on the low wall by the gate with his back to the seed house and his legs out, and he had walked from the fen road and he had not asked anybody for anything and he had not gone to the well, and about four people in this holding have said that he should have gone to the well and about four have said that nobody who has walked a road ought to be asked to walk nine hundred yards for water by people who have just read two sheets off a table, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The woman who keeps the seed house took him a cup of water out at about the fifth hour and did not stay and did not say anything, and nobody thanked her for it and she has not said she expected to be, and about four people in this holding have said that she went out herself because if she had sent one of the nine it would have been an instruction, and about four have said that is a very fine distinction to have made on a Wednesday afternoon, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked and has not been asked.

The second sheet is one page and it is not a declining under any protocol because there is no protocol for it to be a declining under, and it is about the same connection as the first sheet and it is about the same ground and it is about the same week.

The second sheet is one page in the hand of the man who came up the fen road and it is unsigned by any person, and the clerk of this holding read it out in the room in the words it was written in and did not improve them, and the whole of what it says is this. **"We have been given the terms of a connection. The terms say who does the work. The terms do not say how a body stops having the work. The terms say a body may decline an inspection. A body that has declined the inspection is then named as one of the holders of the ground, and a holder of a ground has the work, and a body that has the work and no way of putting it down is not a body, it is a lease. We are not declining. We are saying that the instrument has no door in it and we are not going to walk into a room with no door."**

It is one page and it is not a declining and the clerk entered it as a sheet and not as a declining, and the requests column did not move and is fifty-three, and the second rule on the board is blank and the count of blanks is not the count of a man.

Nobody in that room said the two words together. Nobody in that room put the first sheet and the second sheet in a sentence and said anything out loud, and the word that is in the second sheet is in the second sheet and has not been said out loud by anybody in this building, and about four people in this holding have written it down since and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man of about fifty was not asked and has not been asked.

Nobody thanked anybody. The man from the road went back down the fen road at about the fifth hour and the two sheets were on the long table and one of them was entered under a clause and one of them was entered as a sheet, and the two of them are about the same thing on the same morning and the two of them are not the same kind of object and this building has no column for either of them and has had four years of not having a column for anything.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at about the seventh hour and came down it loaded, and eleven journeys is a floor and was not raised, and the second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred days old and has nothing on it, and he went out to the yard twice this morning before the second hour and nobody in this holding has asked him why and nobody is going to.

PAID stands in front of twenty-two not discharged in twenty-two and it does not fall again inside this month. A declining is not a measurement of it. A sheet is not a measurement of it. A clause is not a measurement of it. Nobody has ever added any of those three to this line in this building and nobody did tonight.

Four inches, two branches, nine at the ninth hour as at the second. A declining does not go on it and a lease does not go on it and a clause does not go on it and a sheet with a body's name on it does not go on it. There are four accounts of that arm in this building and they have never agreed with one another and two sheets of paper put on one table at the fourth hour this afternoon is not a fifth.
