# Chapter 444

## Nine Feet Of Water And No Figure

The third line came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the two culverts on the middle road and the low field gate, and it had rained about two inches in the night for the first time since the ninth of a month now two months back, and the middle road had a sheet on it from the low field gate to about ninety yards up and the sheet had gone over the bank on the left at about the fortieth yard and had taken a bite out of the bank about two feet wide and a foot and a half deep.

Four of them were out on it from the second hour. The bank had to be cut back and the ditch at the foot of it had to be opened, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was on the bank with a spade and two others were in the ditch and Marek was on the road at the low field gate with a line pegged and a string on it, and he was not the man holding the other end of the string and about four people in this holding have noticed that and about four have not.

The other end of the string was held by the man of about fifty, and he held it for about an hour and ten minutes without putting it down, and he is sixty-eight and everybody in that yard knows he is sixty-eight and nobody has ever said so to him, and at about the third hour he asked the man of about thirty-one of Silling for a straight edge and got the back of a spade, and the two of them worked about nine feet of bank that way and did not speak again until it was done.

The two day-minus rules went out loud at about the seventh hour, one man to a figure, and neither figure was named until both had been said. **Seventy-four mornings of a hundred and thirty-one.** The boards: **five hundred and ninety-one days** and **six hundred and thirty-eight**, forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened for them and was not opened for anything else this morning either.

The twenty-seventh went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and the reading did not vary for a fall, because it never has.

**Nine hundred and eighteen hundredweight.**

Down five off Thursday, and the two hundred and seventh figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and it has not been on this board in any month that this holding has kept this board.

The fall went onto the falls. Ninety-two rises and eighty-two falls make a hundred and seventy-four, and that is the whole of the window and the two halves of it have never been added to the figure above them.

Four bodies of households have not moved, and this morning's sheet puts that at two hundred and five days with the two hundred and fourth morning written under it out of two hundred and six. That is not the launder and it is not the window. None of the three has been added to another in four years of this building keeping books.

The clerk of the third county wrote in on Wednesday afternoon about the sheet of rough paper. The letter is two lines long and both of the lines are about the paper and neither of the lines is about the ten, and the second line asks this holding to say whether the man who brought it is a person this county may write to, and the clerk of this holding read it at about the fifth hour and did not answer it the same day and has it in the drawer with the other papers in it, and about four people in this holding have said that the answer is yes and about four have said that the answer is that the county may write to the man directly and does not need this holding in the middle of it, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the letter has not been answered.

The low field gate is the gate that the four bodies of households use on their way to the far common and it is not this holding's gate and it is on a road that is not this holding's road, and the sheet of water on the middle road came up to the inside of the gate post and stopped there, and it did not go through the gate and it did not go past it, and about four people in this holding stood at that gate for about ten minutes in the rain watching it stop and about four went back to work, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

There were about nine people in the one room from about the sixth hour of the evening and the door was shut and it was not the window of the office's work, which does not open until the sixth of a month and which opens on the tenth of this month and shuts on the fifteenth of it.

Nine questions were available in that room at about the seventh hour and eight of them had been available since a month now two months back, and the ninth was taken at about the seventh hour, and the clerk wrote down which one it was and who took it and what the person said he wanted it for, and the clerk's hand is the same hand that put the finding in yesterday's book and the finding and the question are on facing pages and neither of them has a heading over it.

The question was: whether a body that is not a council and has no charter can be asked to answer for the maintenance of a thing that four counties use, and the person who took it was the man who came up the fen road on Wednesday afternoon, and what he said he wanted it for was in his own words and the words were that he had walked a road and could not find anybody to ask and that a person who cannot find anybody to ask has two choices and that he had come here to make the third one, which is to go and be one of the people who answers, and that is not a body either.

**"I am going to say what I think that question is and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that four counties use a road and eight counties in this book have never had it in a column."** He said it standing, and his hand was not on the table. **"I am not saying a man on a road is a council. A council has a roll and a clerk and a way of saying no that leaves a mark. What I am saying is that you have a book with nine columns in it about a ground eleven miles from here, and a man walked forty-one miles this week, and there is nowhere on this road for the two of those to meet. That is what the question is. I would like somebody in this room to write a word at the top of the ninth column and the word can be wrong. I have read a book with nothing at the top of nine columns. I have not read one with the wrong word at the top of the ninth."**

The clerk wrote the question into the second book in the words it was asked and did not improve them, and read them back once and did not read them back twice, and nobody in that room asked whether the man from the road was a member of anything and he said he was not and nobody asked him a second time.

There were about nine minutes after that in which nobody spoke, and the man of about fifty said afterwards that the silence was about the wrong length, and that it should have been about four minutes and not nine, and that he did not know what the other five were for, and he has not worked it out and nobody has asked him.

The man from the road was asked about the tenth line, which he had not been asked about, and he said that the tenth was a ford and that a ford is a place where water goes over and comes back and that in a bad week it carries nine feet and that it has carried nine feet twice in the last eleven years and that both of those weeks are the reason somebody had to write it down at all, and that a ford is not a structure and he had written it because the six things that were structures would not have been walked by anybody who did not know the ford was there, and he said all of that in about thirty seconds and did not say it twice.

Nobody in that room asked him which eleven years and nobody in that room asked him which bad week, and the clerk of this holding wrote **CARRIES NINE FEET IN A BAD WEEK** in the margin of the second book against the tenth line and did not write a figure and did not write a year, and the man of about fifty looked at what he had written and said nothing, and the count of figures this holding cannot check is two and is not three and the row in the list with nothing against it is not an invitation.

The thing that was not said in that room, and was available to be said, and was not said, was about nine days old and it was about the first shortage and what a person plants instead, and it is not written down in this page because it was not said and nobody in that room asked it and nobody in this holding is going to be asked it.

A count of ten is a count of structures. It is not a count of days, it is not a count of households, it is not a count of loads, and it is not a count of people, and about four people in this holding have said that to a room of nine and about four have said that it is obvious, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The man from the road did not stay for the rest of it. He left at about the eighth hour and went out on foot again and the rain had come back a little and he did not have a coat that would do anything about it, and nobody in this holding offered him a coat and nobody in this holding has said since that they should have, and about four people have said that he would have taken it and about four have said that he would not.

Nobody in that room asked him where he slept. Nobody in that room asked him how he ate or what he did between two ends of a forty-one mile walk, and about four people in this holding have said since that those are the three questions a person ought to be asked and about four have said that this holding does not have a column for any of the three and has never had one, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the three questions were not asked.

He is not on the roll of the four bodies of households and he is not on the roll of anything else in this building, because this building has four books and none of the four has a roll in it.

The woman who keeps the seed house was in and out of that room all evening and did not sit down and did not take a part in the question, and about four people in this holding have said that a person who keeps four accounts of her own is the one person in four counties who could have told that room what nine loads of seed costs and about four have said that she was asked and said no, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked and she has not been asked.

She did say one thing at about the eighth hour, on her way out, to nobody in particular.

**"There has been a shortage on a sheet for four days and nobody has said a word about it since, and I have not said a word about it either, and the two of us are not going to be the only ones who notice that."** Nobody in that room answered her and about four people in this holding have written it down and about four have not.

The barrow did not go up the four-mile road this morning and came down it this evening, and eleven journeys is a floor, and the second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one's own book is ninety-five days old and has nothing on it and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in ninety-five days and is not going to this month.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and one days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and a question is not that form and a man who asks one is not a person who has filled one, and the two of those have not been confused in this building in four years.

On the low board at the far end of the room there is a piece of paper face up. It has no date and no line for a name and it has not been filled and it has not been withdrawn. A question about a road is not a reason to take a piece of paper off a board and this holding has never used one as a reason.

Fifty-six is what the seventh column of the well house door carries and it was not read on this morning, because it is read on the thirtieth and the first and on nothing else in the year. The sixth of the six blank columns beside it has a heading and an empty space under it. That is a different column from the seventh and nothing said in this room tonight has put an instrument into either of them.

There is a sheet in a drawer that claims a corridor in the pan and it is correct in every particular it states and it lets nothing out. A question about a road neither opens that route nor shuts it. What the clerk wrote in the second book tonight is a question and not a demand, and this is said once here and is not improved on.

One hundred pounds, paid, and the figure is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one. It weighs nothing. Neither does a count of ten and neither does a question asked on a Friday evening, and this holding has never added one of those three to another and did not tonight.

The arm has four inches on it and two branches off them, unchanged between the second hour and the eighth. None of tonight's business is on it. A question is not on it, a declining is not on it, a session is not on it, a shortage is not on it, a thing said in a room is not on it. The four accounts of that arm stand where they have stood for years and this evening did not make a fifth.

There is a ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres and it is in its eleventh year and no one has ever been in it. There is no figure for it anywhere. The ground beyond it on both sides is still going wrong and going slower, and about four people here think slow is a kind of good and about four think it is a kind of bad that takes its time, and the two fours have not been asked and the ring has not been walked.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour and the second book was shut with the question on the left-hand page and the finding on the right-hand one, and the clerk of this holding did not read either of them back and nobody asked him to.
