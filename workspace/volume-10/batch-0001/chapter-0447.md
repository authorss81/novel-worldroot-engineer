# Chapter 447

## Fifty-Seven

The second line came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the pump house and the frame, and the frame has not been pulled since a month now two months back and the well house is nine hundred yards off and the walk is about eleven minutes at an ordinary pace, and four of them went down at the second hour and four of them came back at about the fourth.

The frame is four feet by four and it is oak and it is the fourth one, and the three before it are stacked against the north wall of the pump house with the iron bands rusted into them, and nobody has ever thrown one out and nobody in this holding has ever said why. It went in about four minutes and came up about three, and it came up with about an inch of black in the bottom of the seam, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling put that on the boards outside the pump house and did not wash it off and nobody in this holding has ever seen him wash anything off a board.

**"That is not the ground and it is not a measurement of it either, and I want somebody to write that down before somebody writes something else down about it."** He said it to nobody in particular and the clerk heard it and put it in the second book in the words it was said, and it is the fourth line in this holding's book this month that is a thing said rather than a thing counted, and the man of about fifty has not commented on the four and has not been asked.

Marek was on the frame with him for the pull and his forearm did not ache, and about four people in this holding have stopped remarking on that and about four have not, and the two fours have not been asked and the man was not asked and is not going to be.

This morning's page carries nothing against the run. The boards go at the seventh hour on alternate mornings and this is an even one, and both men who read them are the two who had the frame in the well house from the second hour to the fourth. Nobody in this building asked a third man to take it and it was not taken and the count stands where it stood on Friday and will be read on Sunday morning at the seventh hour.

The pair of boards stands at **five hundred and ninety-four days** and **six hundred and forty-one**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The thirtieth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and twenty-nine hundredweight.**

Up eight off Sunday, the two hundred and tenth of them. It is the last figure of this month and the series has never once come round to a figure it had already used, and a turn of the month is not a reason to begin again.

The rise went onto the rises. Ninety-four rises and eighty-three falls make a hundred and seventy-seven, and that window is four hundred and eighty-three days wide and is not the boards and is not the launder.

Two hundred and eight days, and the clause under it two hundred and seventh out of two hundred and ninth, for four bodies of households that have not moved. The launder is a different figure and the window is a different figure and the three have been kept apart in this building since before the first of the four books was opened and were kept apart this morning.

The well house door was read at about the fourth hour of the afternoon and it was read by the man of about thirty-one of Silling, and nobody asked him to and he had gone down there for the frame and had come back up with the frame and had gone back down again on his own account about the third hour, and the count of times this holding has asked him anything this month is nil and is nil.

**Fifty-seven.**

That is the seventh column and it has been read on the thirtieth and on the first and on no other morning in the year, and it was read in a month now one month back on the thirtieth and on the first of the next and that was the sixth such pair in four years, and this morning is the first half of the seventh, and the second half is tomorrow and nobody in this holding has said that tomorrow is the eighth of anything and nobody is going to.

It counts readings. It has counted readings for four years and it takes no notice of a man walking up a road, and about four people in this holding have wanted to put something in it in the last three days and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was not asked and did not know and was not told.

He came back up the eleven minutes with the frame and put it down outside the pump house and came in and asked the clerk whether it wanted writing down and the clerk said no, and the man went and washed to the elbow, and about four people in this holding have said that the correct answer was yes and about four have said that the correct answer was no, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The door is a door. It is nine hundred yards off and it is on the outside of a brick building with a slate roof that is older than anybody in this county, and it has seven columns ruled on it in paint and the paint is thick enough in the first four that the ruling is nearly out, and the last three are newer and thinner. Six of the seven have nothing at the top of them and have had nothing at the top of them for four years, and the seventh has a column and it takes a reading every ninth morning and a reading on the thirtieth and the first, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling read the seventh today because he was there with the frame and because nobody told him not to.

He did not touch the other six. About four people in this holding have said that he would have been within his rights to and about four have said that he would not have done it, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man was not asked and has not been asked since, and he does not know that anybody in this holding has an opinion about it.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and four days. It is one sheet with a column on it and nothing at the head of the column, and it arrived on the sixteenth of a month now three months back and it has not been filled and it has not been refused and it has not been moved, and it is the fourth day of a hundred and four and about four people in this holding have said that it ought to go back to whoever sent it and about four have said that sending it back is a decision and this building does not make decisions on paper, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The man of about fifty spoke once about the hundred and four, and the count of reasons given first in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-one, and it is his and nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the thing about that form and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that a hundred days is a length of time and four more days is nothing at all, and anybody who says a form has stood long enough to be sent back has given a reason that is a date and not a reason."** "The form is not going back. It is not being filled either. I have said in this room before that those are two different states and I am not going to improve on it, and I have now said it a second time and I know exactly what that costs me in a room where a man of about fifty is supposed to say something new every nine days."

Nobody in that room said that he had not said something new, and nobody in that room said that he had, and about four people in this holding have written down that he did it on purpose and about four have written down that he did it because he had run out, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man was not asked and has not been asked.

The Undercommons sheet is on the long table and it is not in the register and it is not in the requests and it is in the second book under a date, and the date is the twenty-ninth of this month and it is a day count and not a season and the man who wrote the line about the year has not had an answer and the answer will not come this month because the ordinary post is the ordinary post.

It is in none of the three states. Filling it is not a decision, and refusing it is not a decision, and sending it back is not a decision, and the three of those are the only three states anybody in four counties has ever put that form in. The number of states is three and is not four and nobody has added a fourth and about four people in this holding have said that a fourth would be the obvious one and about four have said that the obvious one is the reason it is not there, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Up at the eighth hour and down loaded, and the count of journeys is still where it has been for years. Ninety-eight days is how long the second of four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's book has now been empty, and no one in this holding is going to be the one to write on it.

The four-mile road is not the road on the sheet of rough paper, and neither of them is the four miles to the gate and trough, and neither of them is the walk to Thornwild, which is four and a half and which is somebody else's road entirely. About four people in this holding have said that this building knows the length of four roads and has never once been asked what any of them is for, and about four have said that it has been asked twice and the answer both times was a number of yards, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the four figures have never been added to one another in four years.

Marek was in the one room at about the sixth hour and he had the Undercommons sheet in his hand and he read it the way the clerk read it, which is twice, and on the second reading he stopped at one line and stood still.

The reason came before the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and twenty-four, and it is his and nobody else's.

**"I am going to say one thing about a line in that letter and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been in this room for nine days saying that a person who uses a road owes something to it, and I have never once asked whether a person who carries seed on a road for nine years has been asked to carry it or has decided to, and this letter says they decided to."** "That is the whole of it. It is one sentence on a piece of paper and it is worth more than the ten lines and the two gaps and the four books. I am not going to build anything on it and I am not going to be the man who goes up there and thanks them, because the second thing I would do is make this holding the body that thanked them, and that is the exact thing a body with no heading on it cannot survive."

Nobody in that room said that was right and nobody in that room said that it was wrong, and about four people in this holding have said that it was the first true thing anybody has said about the road and about four have said that the first true thing said about a thing in four years ought to have a heading on it, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man was not asked and has not been asked.

The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and it has been on that board for about two months and it does not have a heading on it and the low board is a board and not a column, and there is no second instrument of that offer on any table in this holding this season.

The sheet in the drawer that claims a corridor in the pan does not open a route. A form that has stood a hundred and four days on the middle table does not open a route either. They are not the same kind of object and this building has not once confused the two in four years of having both of them in the same room.

The compost line is paid and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one and it measures nothing, and fifty-seven is not a measurement of the compost line and a hundred and four days is not a measurement of anything, and the three of those have never been added in this holding in four years.

Eleven years bare is what the ring inside the eleven acres is, and nobody in this holding has ever stood in it and nobody has ever put a foot in it, and there is no figure for it in this county or in any of the three others. The ground on either side of it is still going wrong and going slower and nobody has been asked about that word this morning or any morning.

There is a sheet on a shelf behind the middle table with no date at the head of it, and it is in the order it was used, and nobody has moved it to the top of the stack and nobody in this holding has been asked to and nobody is going to be.

The arm is four inches and forks twice and that was true at the second hour and at the sixth and it will be true tomorrow. A reading is not on it. A form is not on it. A refusal is not on it. A body with no charter is not on it. The four accounts of that arm do not reconcile and no hour of this Monday added one, and the man who read fifty-seven off a door nine hundred yards off did not know that anybody in this building keeps a count of what does or does not go on a scar.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the middle table at about the first hour and the form stayed on it where it had been all day and the fifty-seven was not entered anywhere except on the door, which is nine hundred yards off, and the door is not a book and the two have not been added in this holding in four years.
