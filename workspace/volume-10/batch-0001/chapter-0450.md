# Chapter 450

## A Lease With A Road On It

The third of this month was a Thursday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the two culverts on the middle road and the fill behind them, and the fill had gone out about nine inches in the rain of the twenty-seventh and the two of them took four people from the second hour until about the fifth and it was finished and it was not the same as it was.

Marek did the fill and Marek did not say anything about the road for about three hours, and about four people in this holding have counted the three hours and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The fill is broken chalk and it packs in about nine inches and goes down in about three. The two culverts on the middle road take the water off the high field and put it into the middle ditch, and the middle ditch has been the same ditch for eleven years. The man of about thirty-one of Silling packed the top nine inches with the butt of a spade and stood on it and it took his weight. About four people in this holding have said that is the only test anybody has ever run on it and about four have said that it is not a test, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man was not asked.

The chalk came out of the same bank the well came out of in a month now eleven months back, and that bank is nine hundred yards off and is not on any of the four lists, and nobody in this holding has ever written the word bank at the head of anything.

Tova Reed was in the room from about the sixth hour and she had been in the seed house since the second hour, and she did not take a part in the first hour of it and about four people in this holding have said that a person who keeps four accounts of her own is entitled to listen first and about four have said that she has been listening first for eleven years and it is not a privilege, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked.

She had a sheet of her own in her coat and did not take it out all evening, and about four people in this holding have noticed that and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked and has not been asked.

She gave the reason before she said the thing she said and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-five, and it is hers and of nobody else's, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in that room this evening and no figure of hers is on this page.

**"I am going to say the one thing about those two sheets and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have four accounts of my own that do not agree at the second figure, and when I read those two sheets I did the only thing I know how to do, which was to ask what each of them would cost in seed, and I can tell you what one of them costs and I cannot tell you what the other one costs and that is the answer to both of them."** "The first one costs nothing, because nothing is going to happen on that ground this season and the people who put it in writing are right and everybody in this room knows they are right. The second one I cannot price, because there is no schedule in this holding and no schedule in any of the four counties and I have asked for one since the fourteenth of a month now one month back and four bodies have sent me four different shapes of nothing. So the second sheet is not a refusal to me. It is a refusal to a document that does not exist, and the man who wrote it knows that and wrote it anyway, and I want that said out loud in a room because it is the kindest reading available and I am not entitled to it."

**Seventy-seven mornings of a hundred and thirty-seven**, read out at the seventh hour by two men who each said one figure and did not say the other. The two boards were **five hundred and ninety-seven days** and **six hundred and forty-four**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer behind them was not opened. Three figures on a Thursday afternoon and a fourth one for the door and none of the four has ever been added to another in this building.

The third went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and twenty-seven hundredweight.**

Down five off the second, and the two hundred and thirteenth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and the fall went onto the falls, and ninety-five and eighty-five make a hundred and eighty.

The four bodies of households are at two hundred and eleven days, the clause under that number reading the two hundred and tenth out of two hundred and twelfth. That clause is a count of mornings and it is not the same count as the figure above it and it has never been added to it. Four bodies of households is not four launders, whatever anybody in four counties has ever said to the contrary.

There were about nine people in the one room from about the sixth hour and the door was shut and it was not the window of the office's work. Both of the sheets were on the long table and the sheet of rough paper with the ten on it was on the table too and the Undercommons letter was on the table, and about four people in this holding have said that four pieces of paper on one table is a meeting and about four have said that a meeting is a thing with people in it and these were the same four people who were in the room, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The man of about fifty had the two sheets side by side and had them the same way up, and he stood at the head of the table for about four minutes and did not touch either of them, and then he said the thing, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-two, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the sentence that this building has been needing since Wednesday and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that a man wrote it on a piece of paper yesterday in his own words and none of the nine of us has said it out loud, and a thing that is true and has never been said out loud is not a fact in this county yet."** "A duty without a way out is not a duty. It is a lease. That is the whole of it and I have had it in my mouth since the fourth hour of yesterday afternoon and I have not said it and I am not saying it because a man from a road said it first in a piece of paper, I am saying it because the second clause of that charter is about being looked at and about four of us have been reading it since a month ago as though it was about being in charge, and it is not, and I read it wrong for a year and I am the one who taught three people here to read it wrong."

Nobody in that room said anything for about a minute and a half and the man of about fifty did not fill it, and about four people in this holding have said that a minute and a half is the right length and about four have said that it is too long and that somebody should have said the next thing, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

He did not say the two words together. He said the sentence about a lease and he did not put the name of the body on the end of it, and about four people in this holding have said that he said it about nobody and about four have said that he said it about the charter, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man was not asked and has not been asked.

Reason first, then the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and twenty-seven and it is his.

**"I am going to say what I think that charter has got wrong and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that the man who read it wrong for a year was me, and I have been in three of the four counties and I have been the person who explains these clauses at a door, and about four people in four counties have learned the second clause from me."** "It is not wrong on the first sheet. The first sheet is right and it is inside the instrument and it is doing exactly what it was written to do and I would not change a word of it. It is wrong on the second, and it is wrong because it says a body may decline an inspection and then names that body as a holder, and a holder of a ground has the work, and I have been the only person in four counties who could do that work and I have never once said out loud that a person who says yes to the first half of a sentence has not said yes to the second half of it, because it never came up before Wednesday."

Nobody in that room asked him which half he had said yes to, and about four people in this holding have wanted to ask and about four have said that nobody in that room was going to enjoy the answer, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man was not asked and has not been asked.

**"I am not going to be the man who fixes it in a room. I have got a clause to write and it is not mine and it is four counties' and the man who lost that argument about the real right of refusal lost it in public and has not been forgiven and is not going to be forgiven by me for saying a sentence in the right order."**

The clerk of this holding had a pencil out by then and did not write any of it down, and the count of things said in a room in this building and not written down is not a count anybody keeps, and about four people in this holding have said that a man of about fifty is not a body and a charter is not a body and neither of them needs a roll.

The third column of the sheet of terms is in the second book and it has had a heading at the top of it since the eleventh of a month now one month back and it is empty from the top of it to the bottom of it. Nobody in this room looked at it, and nobody in this room has looked at it on any of the nine mornings a charter has been in force. The five terms it belongs to are in two hands and are not in that room and are not improved and are not added to, and a sixth term is not added this evening.

There is a thing that four people in this holding want to say and that nobody has said, and it is that the two sheets are the same sentence said by two people who have never met each other, and about four people in this holding have noticed that the two people have never met and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and nobody in this building has the address of either of them and one of them came up the fen road on Wednesday and the other one is at a ground eleven miles from here and neither of them has been in this room at the same time as the other.

The heading went on at about the ninth hour and it went on the second of the six blank columns on the well house door, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was not there and did not do it and has not been asked to and was not asked and does not know about it.

The clerk of this holding walked nine hundred yards in the dark and came back with a pot of paint and a brush that had been used for something else, and he wrote four words at the head of the second column and wiped the brush on his coat, and about four people in this holding have said that a brush that has been used for something else is a heading and about four have said that a brush that has been used for something else is a brush, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The four words are **MORNINGS THE LINE CAME**, and it takes a count of mornings and it is empty from the top of it to the bottom of it, and it is the second of the seven and the seventh still counts readings and the two of them are two different columns and about four people in this holding have said that a heading on the second is not a heading on the seventh and about four have said it is not a heading on the seventh either way, and neither of the two fours has been asked and that was said once in this building this evening and is not going to be said again.

What the seventh column counts besides readings is not answered. Nobody has answered it and nobody in four counties has been asked and the seventh has been read four years and stands at fifty-eight.

Nobody has ever been inside the ring of bare ground in the eleven acres and nobody was inside it on the third of this month. It is in its eleventh year. It has no number against it in this county or in any of the three others. The ground on both sides of it is going wrong and going slower than it was in the ninth, and about four people in this holding have said that slower is better and about four have not said it, and neither of the two fours was asked.

Nothing was put on the arm this morning. It is four inches long and it forks twice and it measured the same at the ninth hour as at the second, and a heading is not on it and a sentence is not on it and a lease is not on it and a body with no charter is not on it. The four accounts of that arm remain four accounts that do not reconcile, and what was said in the one room tonight at the sixth hour does not make a fifth of anything.

One hundred pounds, paid, twenty-two not discharged in twenty-two, and it is not going to fall again before the month is out. A heading does not measure it. A sentence about a lease does not measure it. Neither did a man saying his reason out loud in the dark with a lamp. None of those three has ever been added to this line in four years.

The sheet in the drawer that claims the corridor in the pan is correct and stays where it is. A heading is not a route. A sentence about a lease is not a route. The order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and opens nothing and has not been withdrawn, and nothing said in the one room at the sixth hour this evening reopened it.

Nobody thanked anybody. The pot of paint went back to the pump house where it lives and the brush went back to the wall it came off and the clerk of this holding came in from the gate at about the ninth hour and forty minutes and did not say what the four words were, because the man of about fifty had already read them off the door in the dark with a lamp and had not said them back to himself either.
