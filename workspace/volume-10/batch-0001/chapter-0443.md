# Chapter 443

## A Count Of Ten

The twenty-sixth of this month was a Thursday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. It was the outlet end and the wall of it, and the wall had come away in about four feet in the ninth of a month now one month back and had been put back badly, and the second line was to take the bad work out and put in nothing, and it took three people from the second hour until the fourth and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was one of the three and did the whole of the bottom course with his hands because he said the barrow was not a spade.

No figure stands against the run of mornings on this page and the reason is on the page. The board that carries it was read at the seventh hour on every other morning and this was not one of those mornings, because the man who reads that board went east before the second hour with a roll and a sheet and a bicycle and did not come back before the lamp was lit, and the two men who do the reading did not ask a third man, and nobody has said anything about that in four years either.

The pair of boards stands at **five hundred and ninety days** and **six hundred and thirty-seven**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The twenty-sixth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and twenty-three hundredweight.**

Up eight, and the two hundred and sixth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and the two hundred and seventh is on tomorrow morning and it is in this month.

The rise went onto the rises. Ninety-two and eighty-one make a hundred and seventy-three.

Two hundred and four days is the figure the sheet carries for the four bodies of households, and the clause beneath it is the two hundred and third morning out of two hundred and five. The four bodies of households are not the four launders. Those two aggregates have not been added to one another in this building in four years.

The clerk had the sheet of rough paper on the long table from the second hour and it had the weight on it, and he had the first of the four books open on his left and the second on his right and the third on the knee of the chair and he worked the ten in the order the man who wrote it had numbered them, and he did not do them in a hurry and he did not do them out of order.

Eight of the ten went against something in this building's book. The clerk put a mark in the margin in pencil, a small one, the same mark for all eight, and he did not write a word beside any of them, and the mark is not a heading and it is not a column and it is not a signature and it is a mark in a margin in pencil that any body in four counties could rub out and that nobody has asked to be rubbed out.

The ninth took him a quarter of an hour.

It is not in the first book, and it is not in the second, and it is not in the third, and the clerk went through the fourth for about nine minutes and then went through the second again from the beginning in case he had put it in wrong.

> THE FINDING, ENTERED BY THE CLERK OF THIS HOLDING IN THE SECOND BOOK, IN HIS OWN HAND, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT HE PUT DOWN: **THE NINE OF THE TEN NAMED ON THE SHEET OF ROUGH PAPER IS NOT IN ANY OF THE FOUR BOOKS OF THIS HOLDING. THE EIGHT BEFORE IT ARE, AND THE MARKS BESIDE THOSE EIGHT ARE IN PENCIL AND WERE MADE THIS MORNING BY THE CLERK OF THIS HOLDING AND NOT BY ANYBODY ELSE. THE TENTH WAS NOT LOOKED FOR THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE NINTH WAS NOT FINISHED. NO BODY IS NAMED AGAINST THE NINTH. NO REASON IS ENTERED AGAINST IT. THE NINTH IS A PLACE WHERE A THING WAS AND THIS BUILDING HAS A NOTE THAT A THING WAS THERE AND NO NOTE OF WHAT IT WAS.** THE FINDING IS NOT A HEADING AND IT IS NOT AN ANSWER AND IT IS NOT A REFUSAL.

The tenth of the ten is a ford.

He had not got to it and nobody in the one room asked him about it, and about four people in this holding have wondered since whether a man who writes ten things on a road counts a ford as a thing, and about four have said that it is the only one of the ten that is not a thing anybody built, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the clerk was not asked and the man of about fifty did not ask him.

A count of ten is a count of structures and is not a count of days. That is said once in this page and it is not going to be said again in this room, and a thing said once is not a rule and this building has a bad habit of turning one into the other.

What the fourth book says about the ninth is this. It is a date, in the hand of the man who kept the book in those years, and against the date there are four words, and the four words are **WAS CROSSED IN SPRING**, and there is no figure against the four words and there is nothing under them, and the page is otherwise blank in a book that is not full of blank pages. It is the only page in that book with four words on it and nothing else, and it has been there since before this building had a third book.

Tova Reed came up the fen road on the Tuesday with a cart of her own and she was in the seed house from the second hour of the afternoon until about the sixth, and she came into the one room at about the fourth hour and looked at the ten and at the eight pencil marks and then went and found the clerk.

**"I am going to ask one question and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have four accounts of my own that do not agree at the second figure, and I am not in a position to be rude about somebody else's."** "Who sent it. I do not care what the ten says. I want to know whether there is a person at the other end of it, because a paper off a cart is a thing that happens to people and a paper off a person is a thing that somebody chose to do, and those two have needed different handling since I have been doing this and I have never had a rule for it and I have made one up four times."

The clerk said what he had been told, which was that a man had come up the road on foot with the sheet rolled under his arm and that he had said he had walked it, and that he had not said who for.

**"Then he is a person and he is not a body, and I will treat him as the first one, and I want you to know that I have decided that in about nine seconds and that I do not know whether it is right."** She said it standing, and she did not write anything down, and about four people in this holding have since said that a seedwright who keeps four accounts of her own ought to be the last person in the county to guess and about four have said that she guessed in nine seconds and wrote it down afterwards, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked and she has not been asked.

The man of about fifty came in at about the fourth hour and read the finding standing up, and read it twice, and then went out to the yard and stood there for about ten minutes with his back to the door, and nobody followed him and nobody went to the window.

He gave the reason before the thing, coming back in from the yard, and the count of reasons given first in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty. It is his.

**"I am going to say the thing about the ninth and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that this building has a note in it that says a thing was there and no note of what it was, and that is a different fault from having nothing, and a different fault is the one that takes nine years to notice."** "I am not saying go and find out. I am saying that a man walked the whole of a road this week to bring us ten lines, and eight of them came home to a book and the ninth came home to a gap that has been in this building since before the four of us here were in it, and that is not the road's fault and it is not the man's fault and it is this holding's, and I would rather say that on a Wednesday than find it out in a month from somebody else."

Nobody in that room said anything to that and nobody has said anything about it since, and the man of about fifty has not said it a second time, and about four people in this holding think it was the right thing to say and about four think he should have sent the clerk instead, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The barrow made the four miles at about the seventh hour and came back down loaded. Its journeys stand at eleven and eleven is a floor rather than a total. The second of four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is ninety-four days old and blank, and nobody here has brought it up in ninety-four days.

It went down with nine sacks and came back up with the same barrow and about four of them, and the other five were left at a gate four miles off by a man who is not in this holding, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came up the road and put the barrow against the wall and stood with his hand on it and said nothing at all about the five and was not asked, and about four people in this holding have said that five sacks is a morning and about four have said that five sacks is a morning that somebody else paid for, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man himself was not asked.

Marek read the finding at about the fifth hour and then went and got the first of the four books off the shelf and read the page in it that has a date against it, and he read it twice, and he put the book back in the same place it had been and not a foot to the left or the right of where it had been, and nobody in the one room said that he had looked for something and about four people in this holding have said that he found it and about four have not.

The engineer of record spoke once about the four words, and the count of reasons given first in this holding's history is two hundred and twenty-one, and it is his alone.

**"I am going to say why that page has been blank for nine years and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that the man who wrote those four words wrote a spring and did not write a year, and a spring is a season and not a day, and this building keeps a day against everything it can and has never once written a season against anything."** "That is the whole of what I know about it. I have not been to it, I have not got anybody to go, and I am not going to send a man up four miles with a barrow to look at a place where a thing was crossed in a spring, because a barrow cannot carry that question and neither can I."

Nobody in this holding thanked the clerk for the finding and nobody asked him whether he was going to write anything at the head of it. He has not. There is a line drawn above that finding in the second book with nothing at the top of the line, and there are four lines like it in that book and not one of them has a word on it.

The three men at the wall had it open and empty by the fourth hour and put four feet of the bad work in a heap at the top of the bank and covered it, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came in and washed to the elbow and asked the woman who keeps the seed house for a cloth, and she gave him one, and he wiped down the front of the second book on the long table before he touched it, and the clerk watched him do it and did not say anything about it and has not said anything about it.

The well house door is nine hundred yards off and the seventh column of it stands at fifty-six and is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year, and it is four days off being read, and six of the blank columns beside it are headed nothing and the sixth of them took a heading in a month now one month back and it takes a count of readings and it is empty.

A hundred days is how long the register form has been on the middle table. It is not filled. It is not refused. There is nothing at the head of the column on it, and a hundred is a round number, and nobody in this building has said so and nobody is going to.

A piece of paper lies face up on the low board at the far end of the room. No date on it. No line for a name. Not filled and not withdrawn. A finding is not that instrument. A list of ten is not that instrument. There is no second instrument of that offer on any table in this holding this season.

The drawer still holds the sheet that claims a corridor in the pan and the sheet is still correct in every particular it states. A list of ten is not that corridor. A place where a bridge stood once is not that corridor. Neither of those is a thing this holding has ever been asked to open and neither of them opens anything.

The compost run stands at twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one and the word PAID goes in front of the figure and always has. A count of ten is not the same kind of thing. A nine-year gap in the fourth book is not the same kind of thing. None of the three has been added to another in this building in four years.

Inside the eleven acres there is a ring of ground that has been bare for eleven years. It has not been walked, measured, crossed, priced by walking it or explained on any page in this holding or in the four counties. The ground on both sides of it is still going wrong and going slower. Nobody in this building has ever put a foot in it, including this morning, including the man who went out to the yard for ten minutes with his back to the door.

Four inches, and a fork, and another fork, and the same at the fifth hour as at the second. A finding is not a scar. A gap of nine years is not a scar. A pencil mark in a margin is not a scar and a list of ten is not a scar. There are four accounts of that arm in this building, none of them reconciled, and nothing in this holding added one today, including when the clerk wrote the year in underneath the four words.
