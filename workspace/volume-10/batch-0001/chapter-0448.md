# Chapter 448

## The First Of A Month

The first of this month was a Tuesday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the sluice and the low side of the north end, and the water came across at about the third hour for the first time since the twenty-seventh and went across about four hundred yards of the low side and stood about two inches deep in the low corner by the eighth hour and was gone by the tenth.

The sluice boards are elm and two of them have been replaced and three have not, and the three that have not are the ones that go at the top, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling went at the top with a bar and a wedge and got the middle one out about four minutes and put it back in the same place with a different nail, and nobody asked him why the same place and he did not say.

He was in the low corner at about the sixth hour with a plank across the top of it and the plank is the wrong width and he knows it is the wrong width and has used it about nine times, and about four people in this holding have offered to cut a new one and about four have said nothing, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man has not been asked and has cut nothing.

The month turned overnight and nothing reset. The launder did not start again and the window did not start again and the two boards did not start again, and about four people in this holding looked at all three on the same morning to see whether they had, and about four did not, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Both figures were said out loud at the seventh hour before either was named, and **seventy-six mornings of a hundred and thirty-five** is what they say. The frame of the run is *of a month five months back* today and was *of a month four months back* yesterday, and that change is said once this morning and not a second time and not again. The boards stand at **five hundred and ninety-five days** and **six hundred and forty-two**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened on the first of a month or on any other morning.

The first went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and twenty-four hundredweight.**

Down five off the thirtieth, and the two hundred and eleventh figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and it is the first figure of this month and the figure before it was the last of the last one and the series has no opinion about a turn of a month.

The fall went onto the falls. Ninety-four rises and eighty-four falls make a hundred and seventy-eight, and that window is four hundred and eighty-three days wide and is not the boards and is not the launder and has not been added to either of them in four years.

The morning the month turned, the sheet still carried two hundred and nine days for the four bodies of households, with the two hundred and eighth of those mornings out of two hundred and tenth underneath. A turn of a month resets a column heading and nothing else, and the four bodies of households are not four launders and have never been entered as one.

The man of about seventy was fetched at about the fourth hour of the afternoon by the man of about thirty-one of Silling, in the second barrow, up the four-mile road to the gate and the trough and back. It is the twenty-fifth fetching. The twenty-fourth was on the first of a month now one month back and the interval is thirty mornings and it has been thirty mornings every time since a season turned in the ninth, and **nobody in this holding has ever asked him which number and the count of times this holding has asked him is nil and is nil**, and about four people in this holding have wanted to ask him and about four have not and neither of the two fours has been asked and he was not asked and has not been asked in eleven years.

He was gone about two hours and he came back with the trough full and the bucket empty and he put the bucket down outside the door and went in and drank about a pint of water standing at the wall side of the seed house, and the woman who keeps the seed house was in the room and did not look at him and did not go out, and nobody in this holding has said that she did not look at him on purpose and about four people have said that she did not and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked.

He is seventy-one and he has been fetched twenty-five times and he has never once asked which number, and about four people in this holding have noticed that about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked. He does not know what is in the second book about him because nothing is in the second book about him, and the man of about fifty has said in this room that a man who is fetched twenty-five times without being asked anything is a man this holding has decided to owe something to, and the count of reasons given first did not move on that account because the man of about fifty did not say it out loud in a room and it was written down afterward from something he said to the clerk in the yard and the clerk has entered it as a thing said in a yard and not as a reason.

**PAID. Twenty-two not discharged in twenty-two.**

The word is before the figure and the figure is one hundred pounds and the word has never once disagreed with the figure in four years and it may not. The run has fallen once in this month and does not fall again inside it, and it is not a measurement of the ground and is not going to be used as one, and the figure on the compost line and the figure on the launder are two different figures and have never been added together in this building in four years.

The well house door was read at about the fourth hour by the man of about thirty-one of Silling on his way down and on his way back, and it went to fifty-eight.

That is the second half of the seventh consecutive pair in four years and the page says the seventh once and does not say it again. From fifty-eight it may not go back. It counts readings and it has counted readings for four years and what it counts besides readings is not answered, and about four people in this holding have wanted to put a name against it this month and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was not asked and read it on the way down and read it on the way back and did not know that anybody had an opinion about the column.

There were about nine people in the one room from about the sixth hour and the door was shut, and the shortage was on the sheet, and it had been on the sheet for nine days.

The seed vault of forty households under four hundred yards off was named a third time in that room, and it was named by the man of about thirty-one of Silling, who has never named it before and who said he had heard of it from his own father and that his father's version of it had a number of households in it, and then he stopped.

**"It has not come up and nobody has been down and nobody is going to be, and nobody in this holding has ever seen what is under it."**

That sentence has now been said three times in this holding's history and it has been said three times in this series and it is not improved and it is not going to be improved, and no figure of people was given for it on any of the three mornings and none is given for it this evening, and the number in the name of that thing is of households and not of people and nobody in this building has ever worked out what it is of.

Nobody in that room said the vault would have answered the shortage, and about four people in that room have thought so and about four said so out loud, and neither of the two fours has been asked, and the count of times the man of about thirty-one of Silling has been asked a question by this holding is nil and is nil and he was not asked then either.

The woman who keeps the seed house was in that room the whole evening and did not sit and did not leave, and she counted nothing out loud, and about four people in this holding have said that a person who keeps four accounts of her own is the only person in four counties who could put a number on that shortage and about four have said that she has already put a number on it and it is on the sheet and it has been for nine days, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked.

She said one thing at about the eighth hour and it was to the room and not to anybody in it.

**"There is a figure on a sheet in this room that says what is not in my building. It has been there nine days. Every one of you has read it this month and not one of you has asked me the only question anybody in this county can answer about it, which is what a person plants instead."** Nobody in that room asked it. Nobody in this holding has asked it and nobody is going to be asked it this month, and the six not knowns are six and a seventh is not added for a shortage or for a road or for a month.

Marek put the reason first and then the thing. The count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and twenty-five and it is his alone.

**"I am going to say the thing about the sheet and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that a shortage went on a sheet nine days ago and this month has turned and nobody has planted anything and nobody has decided not to, and those are two different states and only one of them is in this building's book."** "I am not going to say the charter is the answer and I am not going to say it is the wrong size. I am going to say that a charter that took nine days to be signed and a shortage that took nine days to be written down are the same length of time, and that in about four years somebody will check that, and that this building will have written down which of the two it was doing."

Nobody in that room agreed with him and nobody in that room disagreed with him, and about four people in this holding have said that he is right and about four have said that he has put a finger on a thing he cannot move, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man was not asked and has not been asked.

Two trips on the four-mile road this morning and neither of them is a journey anybody counts. The trough came back full. Ninety-nine days have passed since the second of four lines in the man of about thirty-one's book had anything put on it, and nothing was put on it today either.

There is a second thing on the long table this evening and nobody put it there in a room. It is a sheet with a clause on it and the clause is the second clause of the Charter of Many Wells, and the second clause gives a body with an interest in a ground named in the schedule a real right to decline an inspection of it, and says that a declining entered under the protocols is a declining and is not an appeal and is not a delay.

It came up the fen road on the ordinary post at about the fifth hour in an envelope with nothing on the outside of it, and the clerk of this holding read it and did not read it out and carried it to the one room at about the sixth hour and put it on the long table face up and said what it was and did not say whose it was.

There was nobody in the room at that moment. There were about nine in the room at the eighth hour and by then the sheet had been on the table for about two hours and every one of the nine had read it, and nobody knows who read it first, and about four people in this holding have said they were in the room at the sixth hour and were not and about four have said nothing, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the clerk of this holding was not asked and has not been asked.

The Undercommons sheet is still on the long table and the year has not come back and the ordinary post is the ordinary post, and the request for a certified figure of days is in the second book with a date against it and the answer went back on the twentieth in the clerk's hand and it is one sentence.

There is one more thing on that table and it is the sheet of rough paper with the ten on it, and it is the fifth morning it has been on the long table and about four people in this holding have said it should be put away now that it has been copied and about four have said that putting it away is a decision about what a piece of paper is, and neither of the two fours has been asked, and it is on the table.

The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and a month turning is not a date and nobody in this building has written one on it and nobody is going to.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and five days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and a fetching is not that form and a compost line is not that form and a month is not that form, and the three of those have never been added in this holding in four years.

The ring inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and stayed bare this morning. The water that came across the low side went over it and did not stop in it, and nobody in this holding has worked out what that means and nobody has asked. On either side of it the ground is still going wrong, and it is going slower, and this building has never claimed that a slow failure is a small one.

Nobody thanked the man of about thirty-one of Silling for the two journeys and nobody thanked him for the two readings and nobody in this holding has said a word to him about either, and he put the trough full and the bucket down and drank his pint of water and went home at about the sixth hour, and the second of the four lines in his own book is ninety-nine days old and has nothing on it.

The clause on the sheet is still on the long table and nobody in this building has answered it and the clerk of this holding has entered it as it came, with a date and with a clause, and has entered nothing above it, and there are four lines drawn above things in the second book in this building and none of them has a word at the top of it and this is not going to be a fifth.
