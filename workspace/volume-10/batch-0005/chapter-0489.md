# Chapter 489

## Fifty And Thirty-Seven

The fourth line came round at the second hour on the twelfth of this month and went past unworked for the seventy-third morning running, and there is nobody at the headland above Marden to take it, and that line has gone past every fourth morning since the middle of a month now one month back without a name against any of them.

The rota stands at one hundred and two mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and seventy-three not, and twenty-nine and seventy-three are a hundred and two, and the standing rises by exactly one on a morning this line comes round and by nothing on any of the other seven this week.

The twenty-nine has not moved this morning or on any morning of this year, and nobody in this holding is blamed for a field that has waited, because a field that waits is a fact about a field and not about a man.

Of the rotation there are two reckonings kept and they do not agree, and a page that printed one of them would be printing half a fact, so both are here. On the reckoning made in this room the twelfth of this month is its fiftieth morning. On the other it is the thirty-seventh.

Between them is thirteen. It has been thirteen for four years and nobody in this holding has ever worked out what the thirteen counts, and neither of these two numbers can be arrived at by taking twenty-nine away from the other.

There is no figure for the run of mornings on this page and the reason is on the page. The man who reads the near side of the boards was at the well house nine hundred yards off with a chalk and had not come back before the ninth hour, and the man who reads the far side would not read his own half out by himself, and nobody in this holding thought to ask a third man on a Monday.

The pair of boards stands at **six hundred and thirty-six days** and **six hundred and eighty-three**, forty-seven apart at every row, and the drawer was not opened.

The twelfth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and ninety-two hundredweight.**

It came up eight on Sunday with nothing against it, and that is the two hundred and fifty-second figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice since the morning it began, and it is the largest figure that run has carried since this holding began to write it down.

It went onto the rises. A hundred and fifteen of one kind of morning now stands against a hundred and four of the other, which is a hundred and nineteen hundred behind it, and the two ends of that window have not been set against the number over them by anybody in this building in four years.

This morning's sheet puts the four bodies of households at two hundred and fifty days, and what stands under the figure is the two hundred and forty-ninth of those mornings out of two hundred and fifty-first, a day short and a day over.

About the eighth hour the man of about thirty-one of Silling walked the nine hundred yards and read the door.

**Sixty.**

That is the seventh column of a door on the outside of a brick building with a slate roof that is older than anybody in this county, and the column takes a reading and a reading is a reading, and sixty is the figure and the figure is on the door and nowhere else.

The walk is about eleven minutes at an ordinary pace and there and back is about twenty-two, and he was gone about forty minutes, and nobody timed him and nobody has ever asked him to be timed.

The sixth of the six blank columns beside it has a heading and an empty space under it, and a heading on the second of them took paint off a brush that had been used for something else, and neither of those is a heading on the seventh.

Nobody asked him to go. He went because he goes, and the count of times this holding has asked him about the seventh column is nil and is nil, and what that column counts besides readings has not been answered by anybody in four counties.

The door is a door. It is nine hundred yards off and it is on the outside of a brick building with a slate roof, and the seventh of the seven columns has been read for four years by a man who keeps a book of his own with four lines in it and has never once been asked about any of the four.

The second of the four lines in his own book is a hundred and forty days old this morning and has nothing on it, and nobody in this building has asked him about it in a hundred and forty days and nobody is going to.

He came back up the lane about the ninth hour with the chalk in his hand and put it on the shelf by the yard door without saying anything about it, and the count of things this holding has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and it did not move this morning and this was a lane.

There was a room at about the seventh hour with one door in it and the door was shut.

The room is over the seed house and it has been a seed room for about eleven years and it has one door and no window that opens, and nobody in this holding has ever booked it for anything and there is no book in this building that has a line for what happens in it.

There were eight in it and the eight were not a council and not the four bodies of households and not this holding, and about four of them have carried seed on that route for nine years and about four have come up it inside a month, and nobody counted which is which and nobody has asked and nobody is going to.

Nobody in this building booked it and nobody in this building has a column for it, and eight bodies were in it and about four of them have been on that road for nine years and about four have come up it inside a month, and nobody counted which is which and nobody has asked.

The name was said out loud in that room as what a body of people is called and not as a thing anybody in this building belongs to, and none of the four bodies of households is a member of it and this holding is not a member of it, and it has been carrying seed on a road for nine years and has never sent anything up that road that anybody here opened.

It is not a body of households and it is not a council and it has no charter and no heading and no roll and no instrument of any kind, and it now has four clauses read out once in a room with one door, and four clauses are not a charter and this building has not signed anything and is not going to.

The Undercommons is not cheated and is not angry and is not grateful and is not surprised, and about four people here would have said it should be angry and about four would have said it has been carrying seed for nine years without being asked and that is not a thing to be angry about at a table, and nobody in that room said either of those two things out loud.

Soren Rill was in that room and he is not the officer of anything in it and said so before anybody asked him, and he has said it three times before and this is not the fourth because nobody asked.

**"I am going to say the one thing about standing in a room and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been asked eleven times this year whether I am the officer of a thing and I am not, and I would like it said out loud before anything is read out so that nobody in this room can decide afterwards that it was."** "It is not mine and it is not yours and it is not the Assembly's. A thing with no officer is a thing that can be told no by anybody in it, and that is the whole of what I have ever wanted out of a road and I have never once asked anybody for a seal."

The count of that in this holding's history is sixteen and it is his and of nobody else's, and nobody in that room repeated it and nobody in that room improved it.

The instrument is four clauses and it was read out once and it is not improved.

It was read out by a man of about fifty who has never once been the officer of anything either, standing at the end of the table with a sheet of paper he had not written on, and he read it at the speed a person reads when he is not going to read it again, and he did not look up at any point of it.

**"One. The Undercommons carries seed on the road it has carried seed on, and it keeps its own list, and nobody else's list is its list."**

**"Two. A body may revise an agreement it has entered, and the revision is an agreement and not a withdrawal."**

**"Three. A body may leave with its own seed and nobody's permission."**

**"Four. The use of a road makes nobody a member of anything."**

The second clause and the third clause were in the two declinings on the long table upstairs about nine inches apart and neither of them is in the Charter of Many Wells, and the second clause of that charter is about being examined and is not about being the one who ends up holding the work, and a hole in a document four paragraphs long is a hole and nobody has written anybody's name inside it.

The second and third clauses were said out loud in that room by a man who had read them in two sheets written by two people who have never met each other, and neither of them was in the room and neither of them knew the other one had said the same thing in the same week.

One of the two bodies that said no did not come. It was the one with the ground eleven miles off and the rootwoken settlement on it, and it was not asked to and it was not asked to come and it has not been asked anything this week, and nobody in that room described it as having been consulted and nobody has written to it.

Nobody in this building speaks for the rootwoken as a body, and nobody in that room did, and a settlement that is right about a connection is not a symbol and is not a message and did not send one.

The other one was there. It did not speak for the body that did not come and it was not asked to and it sat at the same table with the same four clauses in front of it and it did not sign anything, and a declining is a declining and is not an appeal and is not a delay and is not a thing that gets taken back because a room was warm.

Nobody in that room said that the four clauses were the right four. A list of four things is not a plan and nobody in this holding has ever said it was, and there is no fifth clause and there is no clause struck out and there is nothing at the head of the page it was read from.

The body that was there did not sign and nobody asked it to, and a body that has said no and is in a room with a document in front of it has still said no, and nobody in that room treated the room as an answer.

The woman who coordinates the moving reserves was in that room and is not support staff and has not been asked to be and this holding has not offered her anything, and she did not speak and was not asked to and was not written down anywhere.

The woman who keeps the seed house was in that room because the room is over her own building, and she stood at the door of it the whole evening and did not sit and did not read anything, and about nine people came through that door and went out through the same one.

Of the sheet of terms there is a third column with a heading that has stood over it since the twenty-first of a month now three months back and there is nothing in it, and the five terms in the sheet are in two hands and stand exactly as they were written, none of them improved, none of them withdrawn, none of them added to, and four clauses read out in a room with one door are not a sixth and are not going to become one.

There is a sheet with a clause on it upstairs that came up the fen road a month ago and is correct in every particular and lets nothing out, and a charter is four clauses and this instrument is four clauses and neither of them is a fifth of the other.

Marek was in that room and stood at the back of it and did not speak for about an hour and a quarter, and he was not asked to and did not offer, and the count of times this holding has put a question to him this month is nil and is nil.

He has been the only person this holding could name against a ground for eleven years and he was in a room with one door in it about eight other people and none of them needed him and none of them said so and none of them had to.

The clerk of this holding was not in that room. He was in the one room below it with the second book shut on the table in front of him, and he did not write any of it down and was not asked to and did not offer, and a thing that is not written down in this building has not happened in this building and that is four years of practice and not a rule anybody made this morning.

The man who came up the fen road with the list of ten was not in that room either and nobody in this holding has told him there was one and nobody has been asked to.

Frost was on the yard at about the sixth hour and had gone off it by the eighth. An inch came up in the middle road in the night and about one of them had gone by the ninth. The north end of the ditch is running full and the yard is dry.

The two sheets about the two declinings were still upstairs on the long table about nine inches apart where they have been since the first of a month now one month back, and neither of them was carried into that room and neither of them was left anywhere else, and the two of them are the only paper in this building that has been read out loud in a room and was not improved.

There is a run of reasons given first that has been counted since the seventeenth of a month now six months back, and it is one man's count and nobody else's, and a man who says a thing out loud in a room with the reason before it is not the same thing as a man who writes it down and nobody in this building has ever said that it was.

The window of the office's work has been shut since the eleventh hour of Saturday and the shutter is still on, and no work is ever carried out of one and none was carried out of that one either, and a room with one door over a seed house is not the window of the office's work.

The fourth book has the map in it and it is four years old and nobody in this holding has asked anybody to correct it, and the name on it is on this morning's page once and is not on any other page in this building, and a name on a map is not a signature and a signature is not a witness.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding put the day's figure into the second book at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the paper the four clauses were read from has no name at the head of it and no date at the foot of it and went back into a coat by the door.
