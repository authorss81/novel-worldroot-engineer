# Chapter 487

## What Is Not On The List

The third line was on the boards at the second hour of the tenth of this month and four men had it away by the fifth. It is the two culverts and the fill behind them and the low field gate, and an inch of the northern fill had gone in the rain and went back in and was tamped twice over.

The gate wanted a bar and a stone and took nine minutes, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was not one of the four and had been up the coast road since before the light, and nobody in this building has asked him to come down for a gate he has opened the same way for years.

The run of mornings carries no figure here and the page gives the reason. The man with the near board went up the coast road before it was properly light with the last cart and was not back in the yard until after the ninth, and the man with the far board was in the seed house at about the fourth hour and will not read his own half out alone, and it is not a morning anybody thought of asking a third man for.

The pair of boards stands at **six hundred and thirty-four days** and **six hundred and eighty-one**, forty-seven apart at every row, and the drawer was not opened.

The tenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and eighty-nine hundredweight.**

Friday put eight on it and took nothing off. Two hundred and fiftieth of a run with no figure ever written down twice, and it went to the rises this morning.

The window that sits behind that figure is four hundred and eighty-three days wide and splits into a hundred and fourteen of one sort of morning and a hundred and three of the other, which together are a hundred and seventeen hundred. It is not the boards and it is not the launder and in four years it has not been added to either of them here.

On the sheet this morning the four bodies of households stand at two hundred and forty-eight days, and the clause under the figure is the two hundred and forty-seventh of those mornings out of two hundred and forty-ninth, one under the figure and one over the clause.

The ninth cart went up the coast road at about the first hour and was through the cut at about the sixth and was signed for at the far end at about the seventh, and about the eighth hour the woman who coordinates the moving reserves walked the road back down behind it.

The convoy is done. Nine loads out of a yard one at a time on the twenty-ninth of a month now one month back and nine loads into a yard four counties away, and the page puts no total under the nine and puts no total under the four, and the two have not been added to one another anywhere in this building.

Eight of the nine stood in a field at the head of the coast road for the best part of ten days while the ninth went back down for four sacks in the middle of it and was out again the same evening. How many carts are at the head of that road is not a number this holding has kept and cannot get. The field is not a body of households and the field is not on the maintenance list.

A cart is a cart. A sack is a sack. A day is a day. A yard is a yard, and there are four of those at the far end of a road nine miles from this gate, and none of the four is this holding's yard and none of them is on the maintenance list and this holding has not been asked to keep a count of what is standing in any of them.

The seed in the nine loads is the harvest of four counties in a year this building will not see the end of.

That is said once and is not improved on. There is no figure against it here and there is no weight and no sack and no bushel and no day of food in it, and nobody in four counties has asked this holding for one, and a body that has asked for one has asked a body that cannot answer it.

Four counties gathered it off ground this holding has never walked, in a season that has already been argued about in three rooms that this holding knows nothing about, and the woman who keeps the seed house put her hand flat on the last stack of it on the twenty-eighth and did not say anything and was not asked then either.

Tova Reed was in the seed house from about the second hour with four accounts of her own open and the schedule in front of them and the light on, and she had not gone up the coast road and was not asked to and did not offer.

She gave the reason first at about the seventh hour when the ninth cart came off the tally at the far end and the figure was read back to her, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty, and it is hers and of nobody else's, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in this county today and no figure of hers is on this page.

**"I am going to say the one thing about nine loads and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have four accounts of my own that do not agree at the second figure and that I have made them into one schedule and the schedule is nine loads and it has been nine loads since the eleventh of a month now one month back."** "That is the whole of what I can put a figure on. What is in them is four counties' harvest of a year this building will not see the end of, and I have counted every sack of it and I am not going to write the count under anything, and I would like it written down that the not writing is mine and not anybody else's."

Nobody in that room said that was right and nobody said it was a dodge, and about four people here have said that a seedwright who will not put a number under a year of seed is doing the only careful thing anybody can do with it and about four have said it is a figure somebody will want by the spring, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked.

There is a shortage on a sheet in the seed house with one word at the head of it and a figure and a date, and it is a figure of what is not in a building, and it is not a figure of a harvest and it is not a figure of a ground and it is not a figure of people, and the figure on that sheet and the nine on the schedule are two figures and have never been added together in this building in four years.

The sheet has been on the long table since the thirteenth of a month now one month back and has one word at the head of it and nobody has put a second word beside it, and a figure of what is not in a building is not answered by a figure of what has gone somewhere else in four counties, and nobody in this room put them side by side on purpose and nobody separated them either.

The list of ten is still in the drawer under the counter with the sheet that claims the corridor in the pan, and both of those are face up and both of those are correct, and the ten on the rough paper is a count of structures and it is not a count of loads and it is not a count of days and it is not on a page in any of the four books.

The clerk put it in that drawer on the fourth of this month with the reason given out loud, and it was the first reason that man had given out loud in this building, and the drawer is where a thing goes when this holding has no column for it and no heading for it and no second person to read it out, and it is not the bin.

Eight of the ten are in this holding's book and one is a gap with four words and a year on it and one is a ford with no year and no bad week dated, and the nine that have gone up that road this week are not any of the ten and the ten is not a list of the nine, and neither has been added to the other.

The man of about fifty was in the one room at about the sixth hour with the third book open and nothing written in it, and he did not go out to the gate when the ninth cart came off the tally four miles off and was not told about it until the seventh hour.

He said nothing about the convoy and nobody in this building asked him about the convoy, and about four people here have said that a man who keeps the numbers that come back was owed a figure this morning and about four have said that the ninth cart was not coming back here anyway and that is the answer, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The woman who coordinates the moving reserves came into this yard at about the eighth hour without a sheet of paper and stood at the tap and drank about a pint of water and went out again down the coast road, and she is not support staff and has not been asked to be and this holding has not offered her anything.

Marek was at the mouth of the cut at about the seventh hour when the ninth cart came off the tally four miles off, and he did not go up, and about four miles of coast road finished without him standing at either end of it and nobody has said since that this was arranged or that it was not.

The man in the bad coat was at the lean-to at about the ninth hour with the roll where it has been since the twelfth, and he came out and looked at the empty yard where nine carts had been standing and looked at it for about a minute and went back in and did not say anything to anybody.

The mark in the floor of the pump house is four inches deep in the middle and worn round at the corners and nobody in this building measured it this month and nobody is going to, and a man who is never given a mark measured is not thereby given a fifth of anything.

Four accounts of that arm are kept in four books in this building and none of them reconciles with any of the others, and nine loads are not on it and a year of seed is not on it and a plan is not on it and a completed convoy is not on it, and this morning did not make a fifth account of anything.

The seventh column of the well house door nine hundred yards off stands at fifty-nine and is read on the first and on the thirtieth and on no other morning of the year, and a Saturday is neither of those two mornings, and a completed convoy is neither of those two mornings.

The use log has fifteen lines and got no sixteenth, and a convoy is not a use of anything in this building and the man who keeps that log did not go up the coast road and was not asked to.

Six things this holding does not know are ruled in a column in that schedule with no unit against any one of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and nine loads are not a seventh and a year of seed is not a seventh and a completed convoy is not a seventh.

The four ruled lines under two words entered on the twentieth of a month now four months back are on their own table in the corner and are still empty and the answer to them is not due, and nobody in this building has ever written a figure in one of them and nobody is going to write nine in one of them.

The requests column is fifty-three and it is fifty-three and the section-nine notes are fifty-three and they are a different list, and a request from a body that has a heading on its own paper asking this holding to certify a figure of days for the maintenance of a road has not been answered and was answered once in one sentence on the twentieth of a month now four months back.

The answer said that this holding keeps four books and no column with a heading on it, and nobody in the body that wrote in has said that was insufficient and nobody in this holding has said it was sufficient, and nine loads going through a cut on a coast road is not the answer to a question about days.

There is a name in the second book on a line of its own with a date against it and nothing at the head of the line, and that is the body that has carried seed on that route for nine years, and one line went out to it on the twenty-ninth of a month now two months back asking for the year it started, and the answer has not come and the ordinary post is the ordinary post.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table about nine inches apart and were not touched this morning, and one of them says the ground is ours and the other says a duty without a way out is a lease, and neither of them has been withdrawn and neither of them has been settled and nine carts going up a coast road has not settled either of them.

A body that has said no and a convoy that has gone through are two facts about the same week and they are not a pair, and nobody in that room said the two of them out loud together, and the two bodies have still said no in the same week and neither of them knows that the other one has.

The man who came up the fen road with the list of ten came down off the far end of the cut with the ninth cart behind him at about the seventh hour and stood at the gate of this yard for about nine minutes and did not come in and did not knock and went back up the coast road in the dark.

He has been in this yard for a fortnight and has put no question to anybody in this building, and the two declinings on the long table are about a road and a ground and a work, and nobody in this holding has told him about either of them and nobody has been asked to.

A quarter of an inch of frost was standing in the small hours and it had gone by the seventh hour. An inch came up in the middle road overnight and about one of them had gone by the ninth. The north end of the ditch is running full and the yard is dry.

The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and thirty-eight days old and has nothing on it, and he came down the coast road at about the ninth hour and put the bar back in the shed and washed to the elbow and the woman who keeps the seed house had a cloth out before he asked.

Between the sixth hour and the eighth the one room held about nine people with the door shut. Eight questions were open in that room and every one of them stayed open. The count of questions a room in this holding has available and not taken has stood at eight since the twenty-seventh of a month now two months back, and nobody in there asked to see the list.

The room was a room and not a yard, and the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this morning, and everything that was said between the sixth hour and the eighth was said in a room with the door shut and nobody has written any of it down.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding put the day's figure into the second book at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the ninth cart was four miles off with its sheets off, and this building has no column for nine loads and none for a year of seed and none for the ten things on a sheet of rough paper in a drawer, and nobody in this holding was told to go and make one.
