# Chapter 490

## A Count He Started

The third line came round at the second hour on the thirteenth of this month and was worked, and it was the two culverts on the middle road and the fill behind them and the low field gate, and the fill had gone out about an inch and they put it back and went over it twice, and the gate wanted a bar and a stone and took nine minutes the way it has taken nine minutes since it was hung.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was one of the four and did the bottom of the northern culvert with a spade because he said the barrow was not a spade, and nobody in this building has ever asked him why a barrow is not a spade and nobody is going to.

The chalk in the fill came out of the same bank the well came out of in a month now eleven months back, and that bank is nine hundred yards off and is not on any of the four lists, and nobody in this holding has ever written the word bank at the head of anything.

It had not rained since Friday morning and the yard was dry at the second hour and dry at the ninth, and there is a ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres that is in its eleventh year and that no person in this holding has ever set foot inside and that no person set foot inside this morning, and it has no dimension against it on any page a county could check.

Between them the two men who read the boards said their own sides at the seventh hour and neither of them said the other's, and the run of mornings is **ninety-seven mornings of a hundred and seventy-seven**, and the frame of that run is of a month six months back and has been since the first of this month.

The pair of boards stands at **six hundred and thirty-seven days** and **six hundred and eighty-four**, forty-seven apart at every row they have ever been read on, and the drawer was not opened.

The thirteenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and eighty-seven hundredweight.**

It came in five under Monday and that is the two hundred and fifty-third figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice since the morning it began, and it is the last figure of that run in this year, and a last figure is a figure and is not a total.

It went onto the falls. The window behind it, which is four hundred and eighty-three days wide and is not the boards and not the launder, divides a hundred and fifteen mornings one way and a hundred and five the other, and the two together are a hundred and twenty hundred, and in four years nobody in this building has set either end of it against the figure over them.

Two hundred and fifty-one days without movement is what this morning's sheet carries for the four bodies of households, with the clause beneath it reading the two hundred and fiftieth of those mornings out of two hundred and fifty-second, one short and one over, and mornings are not loads and mornings are not people and mornings are not households.

The forty-fifth ninth-day return came off the steelyard at about the third hour.

**Four hundred and forty-eight hundredweight.**

The rise on that board is eleven hundredweight every time and has been eleven every time for eleven years, and the pair of names against it this morning is the thirty-fifth pair in eleven years and it is eleven above the pair that went up on the second, and the clerk of this holding entered it against the first rule and read it back once and did not read it back twice.

The second rule on that board was left blank for the twenty-ninth time in eleven years this morning, and a count of blanks is not the count of a man, and nobody in this building has ever put a name against that rule and nobody is going to.

It was reconciled twice before the light was full, once at about the third hour against the sheet that came back inside the same fold and once at about the fifth hour by the man of about fifty out of the third book, and the two checks do not agree and the two halves of the figure have never been added to one another in this building in four years.

There are two small cards at the head of that sheet and they are not this building's doing. One of them was written by the man of about thirty-one of Silling and one of them by the man of about fifty, and the two of them put the same two numbers on the same two lines and neither of them copied the other, and the two hands are the two hands and nobody in this building has ever asked either of them why.

The sheet that came back inside the fold has the figure at the head of it and the day beside it and nothing at the foot of it, and the sheet that came back with the one before that carried nine days under the figure and this one carries nothing under the figure at all, and nobody at the steelyard has been asked which of the two is the usual one and nobody here has asked.

There is a form with four lines on the back of the seed house's own schedule and line one carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight and lines two, three and four are still empty, and no heading has been written at the top of it, and a form is not a column and is not going to be made into one.

There is a maintenance list with nine columns and nine duties in it that went up the fen road on the twenty-ninth of a month now one month back, and the sixth column has four marks in it and five empty places, and nobody may put a name in it and nobody has and it is not repaired and not closed and not extended this morning.

The man of about fifty said one thing in that room at about the seventh hour and he gave the reason before it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-eight, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about four clauses and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that a charter is four clauses and the paper that was read out in a room with one door on Monday is four clauses, and I have been in three of the four counties for thirty-one years and nobody has ever said that to me and I have been waiting to see whether anybody would."** "They are not the same four. That is the whole of it and I am not going to say any more about it than that, and I am not going to be the man who puts the two sets side by side and tells a room which two of them to use, because a man who does that is doing what I have done for nine years and I have had enough of it."

Nobody in that room said that to him afterwards and nobody had said it to him before, and about nine people heard the whole of it and nobody has said how many heard the half of it, and the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and it did not move this morning and this was a room.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table about nine inches apart where they have been since the first of a month now one month back, and neither of them has been withdrawn and neither of them has been settled and a room with one door on Monday did not settle either of them, and the two bodies have still said no in the same week and neither of them knows that the other one has.

The one room held about nine people from the sixth hour with the door shut, and eight questions were open in it and all eight stayed open. That count has been eight since the twenty-seventh of a month now two months back and it is eight this evening, and no one in the room asked what they were.

Four ruled lines sit on their own table in the corner under two words entered on the twentieth of a month now four months back, and every one of the four is still empty and the answer to them is not due, and nothing said in that room this evening has supplied it and nobody in that room tried.

The register form has been on the middle table for a hundred and forty-seven days. It is one sheet with a column on it and nothing at the head of the column, and it is not filled and it is not refused and it has not been moved, and a form is a form and a charter is a charter and a body that has said no is a body that has said no and the three of them are not one thing.

The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and it has been lying there since the nineteenth of a month now four months back, and nobody in this building was told to take it off that board and nobody is going to, and four clauses in a room with one door is not a reason to take a piece of paper off a board.

The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and forty-one days old this morning and has nothing on it, and the man who keeps that book was on the third line from the second hour and came in at the ninth hour and was not asked about it.

There is a stack of day sheets about a foot and a half deep behind the fourth book and it is in the order it was used, and today's sheet went on the top of it at about the ninth hour, and the sheet with no date at the head of it is still in the middle of that stack in the place it was on the first morning, and about four hundred sheets have gone over the top of it in four years and nobody in this building has ever looked at one of them.

The day sheet with no date on it has two figures in it and no name against either of them and no unit, and it was not read this morning and nobody asked for it and the count of times a body in this holding has asked about those two figures is nil and is nil.

The comfort is not standing. The father is not alive in the wood. The day does not come back.

That is said once this morning and it is not said again, and it is on the page for the reason it is on the page, which is that a building that keeps a number for everything else in the county keeps that and does not improve on it and has never asked anybody to.

A reading of the standing wood would be proposed and would come out in the order a wood keeps things in, and the eight places that did not come out of it are still not on any page in this building and are still not to be guessed at, and a reading is not a maintenance act and nobody in this county has ever confused the two.

The use log has fifteen lines and got no sixteenth, and the man who keeps it has not been asked to add one and was not asked and has not offered, and a count of uses is not a count of mornings and the two are not added anywhere in this building.

The sheet of terms carries five clauses in two hands, and a third column with a heading that has stood over it since the twenty-first of a month now three months back and has nothing in it. None of the five has been improved, none has been taken back, and a sixth is not being written this morning.

The trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its lip. The man who dug the drain off the back of the lean-to on the eighth of a month now two months back still has not been asked which body fills that trough and has not offered, and if he were asked there is no answer in this building to give him.

The man of about fifty shut the third book at about the eighth hour and put his thumb in it and did not open it again.

He had started a count in it on the morning of the twenty-fifth of a month now one month back, which is the morning a man stood in this yard for four minutes before he knocked on a door with a roll under his arm, and the clerk of this holding said out loud in this room that he had been thinking about those four minutes for two days.

It is one column and it is four ruled lines deep and it has nothing in it but dates, and this morning is the forty-ninth date in it, and forty-nine is the figure he put at the top of the page and it is the whole of what he put there.

**"I am ending a count I started and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been in this chair for a long time and everything else in this room comes back with a sheet on it, and this is the only thing I have ever started that came back with nothing attached to it."** "Forty-nine mornings. It is not a total, it is not a sum, and I am not putting a line under it, and if a body in a county asks me in the spring what the forty-nine was a count of, I am going to tell them it is a count of mornings and that is all it is a count of."

Nobody in that room asked him a second question about it and nobody has asked him since, and the count of figures this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and is two and the first of the two is not named, and a third of them is not a third.

Marek was in the one room at about the ninth hour and said nothing about the forty-ninth date and put nothing in the third book, and nobody asked him what the forty-ninth date was, and the count of questions anybody in this building has put to him this month is nil and is nil.

The man in the cart shed at the mouth of the cut is still in the cart shed. This holding has not been and is not going to be, his name is not in this building, and nobody here has a figure of any kind that belongs to him and nobody has asked him anything at any hour of any day of this month.

A man in a bed is a reason a plan changed and is not a price and a price is not a reason, and this building has four years of practice at not putting the two together and did not put them together this morning at the eighth hour.

Ostrey is about thirty households and the name has been on a map in the fourth book for four years and this building has never been to it, and nobody in this room said the convoy was worth it and nobody said it was not, and neither of those two things has been said in this building at all.

The night was cold enough to leave frost on the ground before light and warm enough to have taken it off again by the eighth hour. Overnight the middle road took an inch of water and had given most of that inch back by nine. The ditch at the north end is running full and the outlet at its head is doing what an outlet does.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding put the day's figure into the second book at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the second book was shut with the day in it and the four hundred and forty-eight hundredweight behind it, and the shutter on the window of the office's work is still on and no work is ever carried out of one.

He opened the third book again at about the tenth hour and said that he was going up the coast road at the second hour in the morning because a man has been in a cart shed at the mouth of it since the second of this month and this building has not been, and he did not say what he was going to do when he got there, and he did not ask anybody to go with him.
