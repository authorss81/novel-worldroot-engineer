# Chapter 488

## The Eleventh

Six men took the first line of the rota at the second hour on the eleventh of this month and they were on the headland embankment above Marden until about the fifth, and the turf came up in sheets about the size of a door and the new toe cut on the seventh of this month took the weight of a boot without complaint and about four feet of the old toe went again anyway by the third hour.

The bank is nine feet at the shoulder and eleven years old at the crown and four at the toe, and the ground on the far side of it above Marden is in a ring and the ring is in its eleventh year, and nobody in this holding has ever set foot inside it and nobody did this morning and it has no dimension against it on any page a county could check.

The embankment work is the only line of the rota that anybody can name a body for and nobody has ever named one, and a turf line on a bank that nobody owns is not a claim on anything and it is not a debt and nobody in this building has ever entered it as one.

There were six on the headland and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was one of them for all three hours. Marek stood in for the first hour and then went down to the pump. Nobody in this building has ever asked either of those two facts a question and nobody has.

The woman who keeps the seed house took a basket up the slope at about the third hour, left it at the top, and went down again without stopping anywhere. Nobody thanked her and she did not climb back up.

It had not rained since Friday morning and the sky on the eleventh was the flat grey that comes after four days of it and does not promise anything, and the light came up about half an hour before it does in the ninth month and nobody in this building keeps a figure of light and has never been asked to.

The run of mornings was read out loud at the seventh hour, one man to a figure, and neither of them said the other's half, and it is **ninety-six mornings of a hundred and seventy-five**.

The pair of boards stands at **six hundred and thirty-five days** and **six hundred and eighty-two**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The eleventh went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and eighty-four hundredweight.**

Down five off Saturday, and the two hundred and fifty-first figure of a run that has never written a number down twice, and the fall went onto the falls. A hundred and fourteen of one kind of morning against a hundred and four of the other is a hundred and eighteen hundred behind it, and nobody in this building has put the two ends of that window against the number over them.

The four bodies of households are written on this morning's sheet as two hundred and forty-nine days without movement, and the clause beneath that number is the two hundred and forty-eighth of those mornings out of two hundred and fiftieth, one short and one over, and a morning is not a household and never has been counted as one.

The window of the office's work shut at about the eleventh hour of the sixth day it had been open.

It opened on the sixth and it shuts on the eleventh and it has been six days long since before this building kept the second book, and no work has ever been carried out of one in four years, and none was carried out of this one, and the shutter went on at about the eleventh hour and was not taken off again.

The requests column is fifty-three and the section-nine notes are fifty-three and they are a different list, and nothing came through that window and nothing goes out of it and this building has never used one for anything.

About the fourth hour a man came down the coast road on foot from the far mouth of the cut.

He had nine miles of coast road and two of cart track behind him and had started at about the second hour at the far end, and he came into the yard about the fourth hour and did not come into the one room and stood at the tap and drank about a pint of water and stood there.

Nobody in this building had sent for him and nobody knew him and he was not asked who he was and he was not asked anything else for about twenty minutes.

**"Ostrey does not come out."**

That is what he said. He said it at the tap with his back to the seed house and he said it once and he did not say how many and he was not asked how many and this page does not say how many.

Ostrey is about thirty households. The name has been on a map in the fourth book for four years and the spur road that goes down to it is on that map too, about a mile and a half past the far mouth of the cut, and nobody in this holding has ever been to it.

What he said after that was four things and he said them in the order he had them. That the bottom of the spur is under about four feet of water. That about nine hundred yards of the spur is gone where it crosses the two side streams. That the people at the top of the spur came up it in the night and are in a barn at the crossing. That he came down the coast road because somebody had to come down it.

He did not say that anybody was dead. Nobody in this holding has asked and nobody is going to, and the number of households is a number of households and not a number of people and this building has never worked out what it is a count of.

He said he had been in the barn. He said there were about forty in it and about nine children, and he said both of those the way a man says a thing he counted once and did not count twice, and neither figure is in this page and neither of them has been put in any of the four books.

He said the water at the bottom of the spur had gone down about a foot since the fourth hour and was still four feet, and he said the nine hundred yards was the worst of it, and he said a cart could get through at the crossing in about four days if somebody put a man in it now.

Nobody in this holding has a cart, a man, or a figure for any of the three, and the requests column is fifty-three and this is not a request, and a thing said at a tap in a yard by a man who has walked nine miles is not a sheet and has not been treated as one.

The man who came up the fen road with the list of ten was at the gate at about the fifth hour and did not come in, and the tenth of the ten is a ford about a mile and a half past the far mouth of the cut, and nobody in this holding has ever asked him whether that ford has water in it and he has not offered.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table about nine inches apart and were not touched all day, and one of them says the ground is ours and the other says a duty without a way out is a lease, and neither of them is about a spur road four miles past a cut and neither of them has been withdrawn.

The clerk of this holding had the second book open at about the eighth hour and there was a page in it with a line ruled at the top of it and nothing at the head of the line, and he looked at the line and did not write anything at the head of it and shut the book again.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book stands at seven, and a man who walks nine miles to say one sentence at a tap is not a session, and a session is not a report and a report is not a figure.

The page does not say by how many. It does not improve on itself and it does not improve on him, and a man who comes down nine miles on foot at the second hour to say one sentence has done everything a page could do and a page is not a man and cannot come down a road.

Nobody in that yard said the convoy was worth it. Nobody in that yard said the convoy was not worth it. The two things were not said in the same breath and were not said in different breaths and neither of them has been said at all.

The one room held about nine people from the sixth hour to the eighth with the door shut. Eight questions were open in that room and none of them was taken, and the oldest of the eight has been open since the twenty-seventh of a month now two months back, and nobody asked anybody in that room what they were.

A man from a village that does not come out is not a question that anybody in that room was going to ask about, and the eight that were available were available before he came and nobody has said so out loud.

The nine loads went up that coast road on the twenty-ninth of a month now one month back and nine loads went through a cut on a coast road on the tenth of this month and were signed for at the far end, and one of the four counties those loads were for is four miles past the crossing and this holding has never had a line of its own against it.

That is a fact about four counties and it is not an argument and it is not a score and nothing in this yard put it in a column, and a fact and an argument are two things and the room had both of them in it for about an hour and the two did not touch.

The woman who coordinates the moving reserves was in the yard at about the sixth hour and had been up the road and had heard him before anybody else had and she had gone back up the coast road at about the fifth hour without a sheet of paper and without saying what she had heard and without being asked.

She has no name on anything in this building and she is not support staff and has not been asked to be and this holding has not offered her anything, and the count of questions this holding has put to her since the third of this month is nil and is nil.

Marek went up the coast road at about the fifth hour and was at the far mouth by the seventh and came back down at about the ninth with wet to the knee and did not go to the spur and was not asked to and did not offer, and he gave the reason first at the gate of the yard at about the ninth hour, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and fifty-two, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about a spur road and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have never been to Ostrey in nine years and I have a map in a book four miles from me with the name on it, and I have never once thought about what the map is for."** "I am not going to go up there tonight and I am not going to go up there tomorrow morning on my own account, and that is not modesty and I would like it written down as not modesty. A county with a name on a map and nobody at it is a county that has to be asked, and a person who has been the only one anybody could send for nine years is exactly the person who must not be the one who goes tonight."

Nobody in that yard said that was right and nobody said it was wrong, and the man of about fifty was at the long wall with the third book shut and had not opened it and was not asked to and did not offer.

The man of about fifty has said in this room that this building keeps the numbers that come back and not the ones that go, and a man coming down a road is a thing that comes back, and the name of a village is a thing that has been on a map since before this holding existed.

The fourth book has the map in it and the map is four years old and was drawn from a sheet sent out by a council that has since been dissolved, and nobody in this holding has asked anybody to correct it and nobody has, and a four-year-old map with a name on it is the only thing this building has ever held about thirty households anywhere.

The man of about seventy was fetched on the first of this month for the twenty-sixth time and is not fetched again inside thirty mornings, and nobody in this holding has asked him which number it was and the count of questions this holding has put to him on any morning of this month is nil and is nil.

Nobody in this building has been told that a village is missing until about the fourth hour of this afternoon, and there is no column in any of the four books for a village that is missing and nobody in this building is going to rule one this evening.

There is a piece of paper face up on the low board at the far end of the one room and it has been lying there since the nineteenth of a month now four months back, when it was refused, with nothing filled in and nothing withdrawn and no date on it and no line anywhere on it for a name.

Nine carts going up a coast road does not make grounds for taking a paper off a board, and one man coming down a spur road does not either, and four years have gone by in this building without one such ground being found and this is not the morning that finds one.

The register form has been on the middle table for a hundred and forty-five days with nothing at the head of it and has been neither filled nor refused, and a village that does not come out four miles past a cut is not a reason to turn it over and this holding has never used one, and the trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip.

Six things this holding does not know are ruled in a column in the seed house's schedule with no unit against any one of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and a village and a spur and nine hundred yards and a barn are not a seventh, and nobody in this holding has added a row.

The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and thirty-nine days old and has nothing on it, and he was on the headland from the second hour to the fifth and came in at about the ninth hour and did not go out again.

The woman who keeps the seed house went out at about the fourth hour, before the man was into the yard, and was gone about eleven minutes and came back and put a cup down at the tap for a man she had not spoken to, and nobody in this building has asked her how long she had been waiting and she has not said.

There was a quarter of an inch of frost in the small hours and none of it by the seventh hour. An inch came up in the middle road in the night and one of them had gone by the ninth. The north end of the ditch is running full.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding put the day's figure into the second book at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the shutter on the window of the office's work went on at the eleventh hour, and a man who had walked nine miles of coast road was sitting on the south wall of the seed house with his coat steaming, and this page does not say how many.
