# Chapter 484

## The Day The Re-Plan Costs

Six men took the first line of the rota at the second hour on the seventh of this month and they were on the headland embankment above Marden until about the fifth, and the turf came up in sheets about the size of a door the way it has come up on that bank since before this building kept the second book.

The bank is nine feet at the shoulder and eleven years old at the crown and four at the toe, and about four feet of the toe went in the winter before last and was put back as a bank and did not hold, and the six of them cut a new toe about a yard and a half out and laid the sheets upside down on the crown.

Of the six on the headland the man of about thirty-one of Silling worked the whole three hours without a word out of him. Marek stood in with them for the first hour and then went off down to the pump. Nobody in this building has ever remarked on which of the two of them left first and nobody has.

The woman who keeps the seed house carried a basket up to the headland at the third hour, set it at the top of the slope, and went straight back down to her own building. She has not been thanked for it, she did not come up a second time, and nobody has asked her why she did it and nobody is going to.

The pump is the cooperative's pump and it is fed off the well house nine hundred yards off, and it took Marek about four minutes to get it going this morning and about eleven minutes to get it going yesterday, and neither of those figures is in any of the four books and nobody has asked him for either of them.

Between them at the seventh hour the two of them read **ninety-four of a hundred and seventy-one**, and the boards carried **six hundred and thirty-one days** and **six hundred and seventy-eight**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer behind them was not opened.

The seventh went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and seventy-eight hundredweight.**

Down five off Tuesday, and the two hundred and forty-seventh of a run that has not repeated itself since the morning it started, and the fall went onto the falls. A hundred and twelve of one kind of morning against a hundred and two of the other is a hundred and fourteen hundred behind the figure, and nobody in this building has put the two ends of that window against the number over them.

The sheet for the four bodies of households says two hundred and forty-five days this morning and the clause under it is the two hundred and forty-fourth of those mornings out of two hundred and forty-sixth, one under and one over, and it has been one under and one over at every site since the first of the four books.

Six carts were through the cut by the fourth hour and the seventh was on the shelf at the narrow place at about the seventh and the eighth was standing on the made road below it in about a foot of rain water that had not been there on Tuesday.

The first six took about two hours each from the mouth to the far mouth and there was a wait of about an hour and a half between some of them and none between others, and the whole of the difference was a man with a bar and a stick deciding when a wheel was past the one place on nine miles of chalk where a wheel has to go past another wheel, and nobody in four counties has asked him how he decides and he has not said.

A cart at a time is the whole of the plan. There is a man at the narrow place with a bar and a piece of sacking on a stick, and there is a man at the far mouth of the cut with a bar and a piece of sacking on a stick, and neither of those two men is the engineer of record and neither of them has ever been asked to hold anything.

What the man on the shelf has instead of a signal is a piece of sacking tied to the top of a stick. Nobody in this county has ever used it as one and nobody is going to start, and its whole use is that a man holding it on a bank nine feet high can be picked out from a mile off by anybody who looks.

The shelf at the narrow place is about four feet deep and it is the only place on that stretch where a cart can stand, and a cart standing on it with a man behind it and a cart coming up underneath it is a thing about four inches of clearance and about a foot of shelf lip, and those two figures are not in any book in this county and were not said out loud by anybody today.

The man at the far mouth of the cut is the man who came up the fen road with the list of ten, and he has been at that end since the fourth hour and he slept about three hours in the back of somebody else's cart and was up before it was light.

The far end of a thing is held by two people and neither of them is the reader of this body. That has been true since the middle of a month now four months back and it is true this afternoon, and no instrument anywhere in four counties says whether it is on or off, and nobody in this building has asked and nobody is going to.

Marek was at the mouth of the cut from about the fifth hour to about the eighth and did not go up it, and he had a bar in his hand at about the sixth hour and put it down at about the seventh, and nobody asked him for it and nobody asked him for anything else.

He stood on the made road and watched a cart go up a face that he could read from about four hundred yards off and that nobody else in four counties can read at all, and he kept both hands in his pockets, and that is the hardest thing he does and he was not asked and has not been asked.

What he could read at four hundred yards was the clay at the top of the face, which was about a foot thick on Thursday and had gone soft overnight and had not been shored at that point because the wedge that comes out first is on the other side of the cart, and there is no chalk in this county that a man can be told to put on a face that is clay on top of it.

He did not say it out loud. There was nobody on that face to say it to, and he has said a thing to himself twice already this month and did not turn either of them into a number, and nobody in this building has asked him about either.

The cart shed at the mouth of the cut was open and Marek went as far as the step and not inside, and the man who is in it was awake and turned his head and nobody in this building has ever written down what either of them said and neither of them has said it twice.

He has been in that shed since the second of this month and this holding has not been and is not going to be, and his name is not in this building, and nobody in this holding has a figure of any kind that belongs to him, and a man in a shed and a foot of clay on a face are two facts about a Wednesday and neither of them is going into a column.

The man of about fifty was in the one room at about the fifth hour with the third book shut and came out to the gate at about the seventh and stood at the gatepost in the rain and watched the carts go up the coast road from about eleven hundred yards off, which is the whole of what a gatepost is for on a morning like this one.

He gave the reason first, at the gatepost, in the rain, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-seven, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to finish the sentence I stopped in front of on Saturday and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I wrote the word costs on the wall of the seed house four days ago and then stopped, and that I have not slept properly since and that a sentence you stop in the middle of is a thing you have asked a room to hold for you."** "One cart at a time through nine miles is a day over the four days at the head of that schedule. I have not worked it out on paper and I am not going to, and I want it written down that I have said the word day and stopped again, and that the stopping is the second time and it is on purpose."

That is a day, and the day is a day, and this page does not say what it is worth and is not going to and nobody in this building has said what it is worth and nobody has been asked.

The seed house's schedule says nine loads and four days and the schedule went out of this county on the eleventh of a month now one month back and came back on the fifteenth with its string cut and retied with a different piece of string, and nobody in this holding has written to anybody about either of those two things.

A count of days on a schedule is a count of days. A count of mornings on a rota is a count of mornings. A count of carts going up a coast road one at a time is a count of carts. The three of them are not one another and have not been added to one another in this building in four years, and a day is the only figure anybody in this county can check with two boards and a memory.

The woman who coordinates the moving reserves came down the coast road at about the eighth hour on the back of the seventh cart's shaft and went into the seed house and was in there about an hour and came out and went back up, and she was not asked what the far end is like and she did not say.

Tova Reed was in the seed house from about the fourth hour with four accounts of her own open on the table and a fifth one, which is the schedule, and she did not go up the coast road and she did not offer to and she was not asked to, and there is nothing at that end for her to count and she has never said that it is the reason and nobody here has asked her whether it is.

The clerk of this holding had the second book open at about the seventh hour and there were three lines ruled at the top of three pages in it with nothing at the head of any of them, and he looked at all three and wrote nothing at the head of any of them, and the count of sessions entered in this holding's book stands at seven and a cart going up a coast road is not an eighth.

Four lines are ruled at the top of things in that book and none of them has a word at the top of it, and a fifth was ruled on Tuesday under a line about a day, and the reason was given out loud and the word is still not there, and this building has four years of practice at leaving a line empty and is not going to stop on a Wednesday.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table about nine inches apart and had been there since the first of a month now one month back, and neither of them was turned over and neither was improved and neither was withdrawn, and one of them says a duty without a way out is not a duty and the other one says the ground is ours, and the two of them are about the same thing on the same road and they are not the same kind of object and this building has never had a column for either.

The ninth cart went up at about the eighth hour and was still on the road at the ninth, and nobody in this building knows where it is and is not going to send anybody to find out, and the count of carts on that road at any hour of any day is a number this holding has never kept.

The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and thirty-five days old this morning and has nothing on it, and the man who keeps that book was on a headland above Marden from the second hour to the fifth and then went back up the coast road, and the count of questions this holding has put to him this month is nil and is nil.

That sheet of terms is in two hands and five of them stand as they were written, and the third column under its heading of the twenty-first of a month now three months back is ruled and has nothing in it. Nobody has touched the five, nobody has taken any of them back, and nobody is adding a sixth to them on the strength of a cart being late.

The fourth book has the seed lines in it going back further than anybody now working remembers, and it has a column for a year and a column for a place and a column headed nothing at the bottom that has about nine hundred days of small marks against it, and the column headed nothing has been headed nothing since before this building was built on this ground.

The face at the narrow place is about nine feet high and is nine feet at one place and about four at another, and nobody in this county has ever been asked for a figure of either and the two of them are the same face and they are not the same thing to a cart.

The man in the bad coat was in the lean-to at about the ninth hour with the roll where it has been since the twelfth, and the roll did not go up the coast road and nobody in this building asked for it and nobody in this building asked about it.

The trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip, and the man who cut the drain off the back of the lean-to on the eighth of a month now two months back is still nobody in particular's man as far as this building is concerned and has never been asked and has never said.

There was no frost on the ground at the second hour and there will be some again before light, and an inch of water came up in the middle road in the night and about one of those inches had gone by the ninth, and the ditch at the north end has not stopped running since Saturday.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at about the seventh hour with four sacks on it and came down it loaded, and eleven journeys is a floor and was not raised today by anybody in this building, and it is a different road from the coast road and the two of them have never been added.

The lamp over the long table was lit at about the sixth hour and was not lit for anything else this evening, and a lamp lit for one reason on a wet Wednesday is a lamp somebody decided, and the count of lamps this holding has lit for a decision is not a figure anybody keeps.

The gate was shut at about the eighth hour and the hinge on the north side has needed a plate since the second of this month, and it was not put on, and nobody in this building has ever asked who was supposed to and a gate is a gate and the plate is somebody's and nobody here knows whose.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour and the second book was shut with the day in it and nothing at the head of the line, and two carts were still on a coast road in the dark with a man at each end of the thing and neither of them in this building, and nobody in this holding has said what the day cost and nobody has asked.
