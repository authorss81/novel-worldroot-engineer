# Chapter 454

## A Motion Citing A Paper On A Board

The steelyard is at the far end of the one room and it has been there for longer than the register form has been on the middle table, and it was weighed at about the tenth hour on the seventh of this month, which is a Monday, and the sheet of the forty-second return went on the pinboard and the man of about fifty read the figure and wrote it and did not read it out loud.

The pinboard is at the far end of the room on the wall the steelyard is under, and it is the only board in the building that is used every ninth day without exception, and the third rule under the two reconciled cards has been blank on every one of those days since the twenty-first return.

**Four hundred and four hundredweight.**

Up eleven off the forty-first, and the forty-second of the ninth-day returns, and the thirty-second pair of names in eleven years on the two small cards at the head of the sheet. The two cards were reconciled twice, once at the reading and once at about the first hour, and the two reconciliations were the same, and the two halves of a reconciliation are two handwritings and are not one figure and have not been added together in four years.

The second rule on the board was left blank. It has now been left blank twenty-six times in eleven years and the count of blanks is not the count of a man, and the man whose name would go in the second rule is not named in this building and has not been named in this building in four years.

The forty-second return is of this month, being the seventh of it, and the thirty-first return was of last month, being the twenty-eighth of that, and no frame changes at a turn of a month in this holding's book and the clerk did not change one.

The first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the north ditch from the pump house out to the field gate, and the frost had lifted about two days of silt out of the top of it and the four of them put it back in and went over it twice.

At the seventh hour two men said two figures and neither of them said the other, and what the run of mornings stands at this morning is seventy-nine of a hundred and forty-one. The two boards stood at **six hundred and one days** and **six hundred and forty-eight**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer behind them was not opened.

The seventh went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and thirty-three hundredweight.**

Sunday took five off it. That is the two hundred and seventeenth figure of a run in which no two have ever matched, and the drop went onto the falls with the other falls. Ninety-seven rises and eighty-seven falls is a hundred and eighty-four, and that addition is the only arithmetic anybody does to that number.

Two hundred and fifteen days is what the sheet says about the four bodies of households, and the clause under that number reads the two hundred and fourteenth of the mornings out of two hundred and sixteenth, one under and one over as it is on every row. It is a different aggregate from the launder and the two have never been added here in four years.

The letter came up the ordinary post on the Saturday and it is one sheet and it has a heading at the top of it in a clerk's hand from one of the four counties, and the clerk of this holding read it at about the fourth hour and carried it into the one room and put it on the long table, and the room was shut at about the sixth hour with about nine in it, and it was the first of the six days of the window of the office's work and no work was carried out of the window in those six days and none of it is carried out of any window in four years.

The motion is four lines long. It was made at a body in a county, in a room of that body's own, and it was carried there, and the count of the room that carried it is not in the letter and is not on this page.

**"THAT THIS BODY NOTES THE OFFER ON THE BOARD OF THE HOLDING AT THE FOUR-MILE GATE, WHICH HAS BEEN REFUSED AND NOT WITHDRAWN, AND WHICH HAS NO DATE AND NO LINE FOR A NAME UPON IT, AND THAT THE SAME BODY IS UNABLE TO CERTIFY A FIGURE TO ANYBODY WHILE AN INSTRUMENT OF THAT KIND IS LYING OPEN AND UNDATED IN A BUILDING FOUR COUNTIES USE; AND THAT THE FIGURE THIS BODY ASKED FOR IS THE SAME FIGURE THAT IS NOT AVAILABLE WHILE THAT INSTRUMENT IS OPEN; AND THAT THE TEN OF THEM ARE NOT TRUSTED IN THE MATTER."**

The refusal of that offer is entered in the second book with a date against it, being the nineteenth of a month now three months back, and it was entered then in the clerk's hand and read back once and not read back twice, and it stands where it stands.

The offer is on the low board at the far end of the one room, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date, no name, no line for a name, and nobody in this building was told to take it off that board and nobody has been and nobody is going to be.

It has been on that board since the nineteenth of a month now three months back and it has been carried into three hundred and something mornings since, and nobody in this building has counted those mornings and nobody is going to, and about four people in this holding have said that the number of days a piece of paper has lain on a board is not a figure about anything, and about four have said that in about four years somebody is going to want one, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

A motion that cites it is a fact about the county and not about this holding. About four people in this holding have said that a piece of paper with nothing filled in on it is a fair thing for a body in a county to be uneasy about, and about four have said that a body which could read a paper with nothing on it would be a body that could read anything, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The body that carried the motion is a body and it is not a body of households and this holding is not a member of it and it has no roll in this building, and about four people in this holding have said that a county body which has never had a figure certified by anybody is not unusually unfortunate, and about four have said that the trouble is not that it has never had one and that the trouble is that it has asked twice in nineteen days, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Nobody in that room said the offer ought to go and nobody in that room said it ought to stay and about four people in this holding have said one and about four have said the other, and neither of the two fours has been asked, and the board is nine feet off the floor at the far end of the room and everybody in it can see the paper on it from the door.

The second request came up with the motion, in the same letter, on the same sheet, under a line at the bottom of it, and it is a request and not a motion and not an offer and it is the second this holding has had about this road.

The first request was the twenty-ninth of a month now one month back and it asked this holding to certify a figure of days for the same road, and the answer went out on the twentieth in the clerk's hand and it is one sentence.

**"This holding holds no figure of days for the four-mile road and will not supply one until a body tells it what a day on that road is for."**

That sentence has been out for seventeen days and nobody has answered it and the ordinary post is the ordinary post, and the first request is in the second book with a date against it and the second one is now in the second book with a date against it beside it, and the two of them are a hundred and eleven days and seventeen days old respectively and neither of them has a figure against it.

The requests column is fifty-three and the section-nine notes are fifty-three, and they are not the same list and they have never been added together. This holding is fifty-three this morning and it was fifty-three yesterday and fifty-three is not a ceiling, and this holding has never once entered a request as though a request were a new request.

The man who keeps the use log entered the second one in the book he keeps, and the log has fifteen lines and it got no sixteenth, and the reason for the fifteen was given once in four years and was not given a second time and is not given again this morning.

He is the man who wrote the first line of the Undercommons letter that came up this road on the twenty-ninth of a month now one month back, and nobody in this holding has asked him whether that is a coincidence and the count of questions this holding has put to him about it is nil and is nil.

The two declinings were still on the long table, one entered under the second clause of the charter and one entered as a sheet, and they had been on that table for six days and five days, and about four people in this holding have said that a body in a county has written four lines about a piece of paper on a board while two sheets about the same week lie unanswered on a table four miles away, and about four have said that the four counties do not know about this table, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The woman who keeps the seed house was in that room for about an hour and said nothing at all in it, and she has four accounts of her own that do not agree at the second figure, and about four people in this holding have said that a person with four accounts would have been the one to be asked what a day on that road is for, and about four have said that she was not asked because nobody in that room had worked out how to ask her, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked and has not been asked.

He gave the reason before the thing, as he has given it two hundred and thirty-one times in this building, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and thirty-one, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say what a request is and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have answered forty-one of those in eleven years and I have never once written the answer on the piece of paper that came, and I did that on purpose in the first year and I have not been able to stop."** "A request goes in a column. The answer goes on a separate sheet and it is dated and it goes out on the ordinary post, and the two of them are not the same object and a person who puts the answer on the request has handed the body that wrote it the right to change it. I have watched that happen twice. One of the two times it was me that did it."

The sheet claiming the corridor in the pan was produced in that room at about the seventh hour and it was produced by the clerk of this holding without being asked, and it is correct in every particular it states and it lets nothing out.

It is a sheet of about nine lines and it claims a corridor in the pan on the strength of a survey of about forty years back, and every figure on it is a figure this holding can check, and the clerk checked two of them out loud and they were right, and about four people in this holding have said that producing it was the correct thing to do and about four have said that it was a mistake, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the clerk was not asked.

It is in the drawer because the drawer is where a thing goes when this holding has no column for it, and the clerk said the reason out loud and the count of times this holding has heard him give a reason out loud is two and it is this, and nobody in that room asked him which drawer the rough paper went into and he said the same drawer without being asked.

He produced it face up, which is how it has lain in the drawer since it was put there, and the man of about fifty did not touch it, and about four people in this holding have said that a man who will not touch a sheet in a room is a man who is letting the sheet speak, and about four have said that it is a man who is afraid of what his own hand will do, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

A corridor in the pan is not a road, and a road is not a corridor, and the sheet is not a route and the route is not a road. It is neither closed nor opened by being read out in a room on the seventh of this month, and the sheet has not been reworded and has not been advanced and has not been summed and has not been grouped with anything.

The order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and opens nothing, and no apology for it exists in this holding and none is going to be written, and the route is not reopened by anything anybody said in that room at about the seventh hour.

The day sheet with no date at the head of it is still in the stack behind the middle table in the order it was used, and nobody moved it to the top of the stack this morning while the room was shut with nine people in it, and about four people in this holding have said that a stack of day sheets is the only thing in the building that gets moved by nobody and about four have said that it gets moved by everybody at about the first hour when they are looking for something else, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and eleven days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and a motion in a county is not that form and a request is not that form and a sheet of nine lines is not that form, and the three of those have never been added in this holding in four years.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling took the barrow up the four-mile road at about the seventh hour and brought it down loaded, and nobody in this holding has raised the number of journeys that barrow has made, and the second of the four lines in his own book is a hundred and five days old and has nothing on it.

The pinboard, the long table, the middle table and the low board are four places in one room where a piece of paper can be in this building, and on the seventh of this month all four of them had something on them, and about four people in this holding have said that a room with four surfaces and no column is a room where things go to be looked at, and about four have said that it is a room where things go to be forgotten, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The ground had gone soft under the north end of the yard with the rain and there is about four inches of it standing in the low corner by the lean-to, and nobody in this holding has cut a drain off that corner in four years and about four people have said the corner has needed one since the spring, and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The compost run is one hundred pounds and paid and it stands at twenty-two not discharged in twenty-two. A motion in a county and a request for a figure of days are not measurements of it, and neither has ever been added to that line in this building in four years.
