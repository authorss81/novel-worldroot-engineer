# Chapter 461

## A Count Of Sacks And A Road That Does Not Open

The fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour on the fourteenth of this month and there was nobody at the headland above Marden to work it, and it went away empty for the sixty-sixth morning running, and about four people in this holding have said that a line which has gone away empty sixty-six times is a line and about four have said that it is a field with a name on it, and neither of the two fours has been asked and nobody is told whose the field is.

Ninety-five mornings of that rotation are now in force and twenty-nine of them have been taken and sixty-six have not, and twenty-nine and sixty-six are ninety-five, and the count goes up by exactly one on a morning the line comes round and by nothing on any other morning, and twenty-nine taken has not moved this morning or on any morning of this year.

This building keeps two reckonings of that rotation and they do not agree, so the page carries both. This morning is the forty-third morning of the first and the thirtieth of the other. The thirteen between them has been there four years and nobody in this holding has ever found out what it is.

There is no figure for the run of mornings on this page and the reason is on the page, and the reason is that the man who reads one of the two boards went to the four-mile road at the top of the hour with the soren of the Undercommons and was still on it at the seventh hour, and the other man did not read his side alone and nobody thought to ask a fourth man to do it on a Monday.

The pair stands on the boards at **six hundred and eight days** and **six hundred and fifty-five**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The fourteenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and fifty hundredweight.**

Sunday is eight on and the figure is the two hundred and twenty-fourth, which is the last of these ten and the twenty-fourth of a run that has never repeated itself. The rise went on the rises. A hundred and one against ninety is a hundred and ninety-one, and those two are kept apart from everything else on the page they are on.

Two hundred and twenty-two days is the figure this morning's sheet carries for the four bodies of households, and the clause beneath the figure is the two hundred and twenty-first of the mornings out of two hundred and twenty-third. That clause is a count of mornings and it is not the launder and it is not the window and it is not the schedule that went out of this county on Friday, and none of the four has been added to another in this building in four years.

Soren Rill came up the fen road on foot at about the second hour of the afternoon with a sheet in a fold of oilskin, and he is the man who has walked the four and a half miles twice before and came back without what he went for both times, and nobody in this holding asked him to walk it a third time and he did it.

> THE COUNT, SENT FROM FOUR AND A HALF MILES OFF ON THE FOURTEENTH OF THIS MONTH, IN A FOLD OF OILSKIN, IN ONE HAND, IN WHOLE FIGURES: **THE SEED AT THORNWILD AGAINST THE SCHEDULE OF NINE LOADS IS ELEVEN THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY SACKS. A SACK IS A SACK AND IS A UNIT THIS HOLDING CANNOT CHECK. NO FIGURE IS GIVEN AGAINST A DAY. NO FIGURE IS GIVEN AGAINST A LOAD. NO FIGURE OF PEOPLE IS GIVEN AGAINST ANYTHING. THE FIGURE ABOVE IS AGAINST A SCHEDULE AND NOT AGAINST A GROUND.** NOTHING ABOVE IS A HEADING AND NOTHING ABOVE IS A TOTAL.

He put it on the long table and said the thing he had walked four and a half miles to say and he said it before he sat down, and the wind was still in the east and the corner of the oilskin had come open and the corner of the sheet was damp and the clerk of this holding did not dry it for him and was not asked to.

He gave the reason before he said it and the count of that in this holding's history is fifteen, and it is his and of nobody else's, and it is the fifteenth and it has not moved on any other page in this holding since the morning this one began.

**"I am going to say who I am not and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have walked this road three times and been asked twice in a month whether I am the officer of a body with no charter, and a man who has been asked that twice is a man somebody has been meaning to use."** **"I am not the Undercommons. I have said so twice and I am saying it a third time because the man from the road and the man in the bad coat are both in this yard and neither of them is in it either, and I have been asked twice in a month whether I am its officer and I am not and nobody has asked me to be."** "Eleven thousand four hundred and sixty sacks. What that is worth in days is not on the sheet and it is not going to be put on it by me. A count of sacks is not a count of loads and a count of loads is not a count of days, and I have walked four and a half miles twice to be told that by a woman in a seed house and I am content to say it in her building as well."

Marek gave the reason before the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and thirty-seven, and it is his and of nobody else's, and the sheet in the oilskin was still damp at the corner where the wind had got into it.

**"I am going to say what a count of sacks against a schedule is and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that a schedule of nine loads came out of this county on Friday and a count of eleven thousand four hundred and sixty sacks came in on Monday, and there is a figure on each of them and neither one of them is a figure of the other."** "I am not going to divide one by the other. I have done that in my head twice since Friday and both times I got a number and both times the number was about a man deciding to do it, and a man deciding to do it is not a sack."

The order sealing the route came out of the drawer at about the seventh hour, and the clerk of this holding took it out without being asked and put it on the long table beside the count, and it is valid and it opens nothing and it has not been withdrawn and there is no apology for it in this holding and there is not going to be one.

A piece of paper which seals a route should not be able to sit in a drawer for four years with nobody knowing it is in there, and a piece of paper which opens nothing is exactly the kind of paper that can sit in a drawer for four years. The clerk was not asked.

The four counties have been written to in eleven years, and a man who walks four and a half miles with a piece of paper is a cheaper post than a county clerk, and a county clerk would have had it sent and would not have had the corner damp. The corner is damp.

A count of sacks is not a route. A road is not a route. A sealed route is not a closed corridor and a closed corridor is not a sealed route. The four of them have never been added in this building in four years and the two sheets on the table tonight are about nine inches apart again and they are not touching.

There is a corridor in the pan and there is a sheet in a drawer that claims it, and the sheet is correct in every particular it states and it lets nothing out, and the corridor is neither closed nor opened in this holding and has not been either on this afternoon, and the route is not reopened by anything that was said in that room.

A man who brought a correct paper and has been in a yard for a week and has not been asked a single question is being handled more carefully than anybody else in the building, or he is not being handled at all and is being left alone.

The count of sacks went back into the oilskin at about the ninth hour and the oilskin went back into a bag, and the bag is the bag it came in, and nobody in this holding has copied a figure off that sheet into any of its four books except the two figures that came off the roll a day before, and those two are about a ground two hundred miles off and are not about sacks.

The man from the fen road was in the yard at about the fifth hour and came in at about the sixth and stood at the long wall for about twenty minutes, and the man of about fifty did not look at him and he did not look at the man of about fifty. Two men in a room who do not look at each other have either agreed not to look at each other, or have agreed nothing at all and are looking at the floor.

The man in the bad coat was in the lean-to at about the eighth hour and the roll of paper was under his arm where it has been since the twelfth, and nobody in this holding has asked him how long he means to be in this county and the count of questions this holding has put to him is nil and is nil, and the letter that went out at the fourth hour is about a sheet and not about him and he does not know that it has gone.

The four counties have been written to in eleven years and this holding has answered about nine hundred letters and has put a total under one document in that time, and the one was a return of seed weight on a steelyard nine years ago and it was not a total anybody in this holding remembers, and nobody in this holding has said a number under a count of sacks and nobody in this holding has said a number under a schedule of nine loads.

Nobody thanked anybody. The letter to the county that holds the first sheet went out on the ordinary post at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, before the count came up the road and not after it, and it is about the sheet and not about the ninth of the ten and not about the man in the bad coat, and there is no second copy of it in this building.

A count of sacks, an order sealing a route and a letter about a sheet are not the register form, which is on the middle table at a hundred and eighteen days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it.

A count of sacks is not a reason to take a piece of paper off the low board. That paper is face up and empty and undated with no line for a name on it, refused on the nineteenth of a month now three months back, and nobody in this building was told to take it off that board and nobody is going to be told.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at about the seventh hour with a man from the fen road walking beside it for the first half mile and then not, and the second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and twelve days old and has nothing on it.

A count of sacks is not on the well house door and a sack is not a reading, and that door is nine hundred yards off with its seventh column at fifty-eight, which is read on the thirtieth and the first and on no other morning in the year. The second of the six columns beside it has four words at the head of it and nothing under them.

The mark is the same four inches with the same two branches off it that it has been for four years, and a count of sacks and a letter gone out and a division nobody performed are not on it, and the four accounts of that arm remain four and this evening did not make a fifth of anything.

The compost run is one hundred pounds and paid and it stands at twenty-two not discharged in twenty-two, and eleven thousand four hundred and sixty sacks is not a measurement of it, and a sack is not a unit this holding can check, and the two figures this holding cannot check are two, and the four of those are not added together anywhere.

Nobody in this holding has written a total under the count and nobody in this holding has written a total under the schedule and nobody has written one across the two of them. A total across them would be a division and a division is a decision, and a body in a county will do the division anyway in about four years. The division has not been done here.

A man who says he is not the officer of a body three times in one afternoon has been asked twice, and a man who has walked four and a half miles three times is not worried about being an officer of anything. The Undercommons was not named out loud in that room this afternoon by anybody and it was not improved and it was not withdrawn.

There is a sheet on the long table with two figures on it and a schedule that went out of this county on Friday with a figure on it, and between the two of those sheets there is a road about four miles long with a ford on it, and the count of carts that have gone over that road this month is not a figure anybody in this holding keeps, and it is not a figure anybody in the four counties keeps, and the barrow has gone up it eleven journeys and eleven is a floor.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour, and the count of sacks went back into the oilskin and into the bag it came in, and the two declinings went back onto the long table from the shelf by the window where the clerk had put them on Sunday, and the route is shut and was shut and the order is in the drawer face up and the roll of paper is in the second book and the list of ten is in the drawer with the sheet that claims the corridor.

Marek walked out to the gate at about the first hour and stood there with his hand on the latch and did not open it. The count of questions this holding's reader has put to the two bodies that declined the same connection in the same week is nil and is nil, and he has decided that it is going to stop being nil. A man who stands at a gate at the first hour is a man deciding to walk somewhere, and a man with his hand on a latch has already decided and is only choosing the morning. He has not decided which of the two bodies he asks first, and the gate was shut behind him at about the second hour.
