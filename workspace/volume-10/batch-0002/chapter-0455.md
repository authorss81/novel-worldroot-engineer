# Chapter 455

## Nine Loads And Four Days

The second line of the rota came round at the second hour on the eighth of this month and was worked, and it was the outlet end and the two stones at the head of it, and one of the two stones had turned about a foot on its short edge in the frost and had to be dug out and set back and packed, and that took three people from the second hour until the fourth.

The stone that turned is the one a cart puts its near wheel against and it has been in that place since before this building had a first book, and about four people in this holding have said that a stone that turns in a frost is doing what a stone does, and about four have said that a stone that turns in a frost is doing what a man who set it did not, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The wind had gone round to the west in the night and the yard was dry for the first time in four days, and the frost was gone out of the top inch by the third hour and the pack under the stone went in wet and has to be looked at again in about a week by somebody, and about four people in this holding know that and about four do not.

There is no figure for the run of mornings on this page and the reason is on the page. The board that carries it was read at the seventh hour on alternate mornings and this was not one of them, because the man who reads that board was in the seed house from the second hour and did not come out until about the eighth, and the second man did not read his side alone and nobody asked a third man to do it.

The pair of boards stands at **six hundred and two days** and **six hundred and forty-nine**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The eighth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and forty-one hundredweight.**

Eight on it since Monday morning, and the two hundred and eighteenth figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice. It went up the up side. Ninety-eight and eighty-seven is a hundred and eighty-five, and no one in this building has ever added those two to the number over them.

Two hundred and sixteen days is the number against the four bodies of households on this morning's sheet, and the clause written under it is the two hundred and fifteenth of those mornings out of two hundred and seventeenth. That clause is a count of mornings and it is not the launder and it is not the window, and none of the three has been added to another in this building in four years.

The nine loads and the four days are a fifth figure and they are on a sheet in the seed house and not on anything in this room, and nobody in this holding has put it in a column with any of the others and nobody is going to, and about four people in this holding have said that a fifth is a fifth, and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Tova Reed had the four accounts of her own on the long table at about the fifth hour and a sheet of paper over the four of them, and she had been in the seed house since the second hour and she had not come out of it once.

The four accounts are four and they do not agree at the second figure. They have not agreed there since a month now three months back, and about four people in this holding have said the second figure is where they always disagree and about four have said one of the four has been wrong there since before this building had a second book, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked.

One of the four has a figure against the ninth of this month in it and one has not, and the one that has is the one she would take the nine from if she were a fool, and about four people in this holding have worked that out and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

She gave the reason before she said the thing she had come in to say, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-six, and it is hers and of nobody else's, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in that room and no figure of hers is on this page.

**"I am going to say what I have done and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that a body asked this holding for a figure of days on that road twice in nineteen days and I am the only person in four counties who can turn what I have got into one answer, and I have turned it into one answer, and it took me two days."** "A count of four accounts is not a count of seed. I want that said in a room. I have had four books open on a table since the sixth of this month and I have got to nine loads and I have got four days on them, and the nine loads are real and the four days are real, and neither of those two things came out of the four accounts, it came out of one of them, and I do not know which one, and I am not going to guess in front of nine people because a guess written down in a book is a figure for about four years."

The schedule went onto the table face down and she did not turn it over for about a minute and a half, and about four people in this holding have said that a woman who has just told nine people that she will not guess does not turn a paper over, and about four have said that she was waiting to see whether anybody would ask, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

> THE FIRST SCHEDULE, AS IT WAS WRITTEN BY THE WOMAN WHO KEEPS THE SEED HOUSE, IN HER OWN HAND, IN THE SEED HOUSE, AND CARRIED IN BY HER AND NOT COPIED: **NINE LOADS. FOUR DAYS. THE NINE LOADS ARE THE SEED ON THE SHEET WITH THE SHORTAGE ON IT AND NO OTHER SEED AND NO MORE THAN THE SEED ON THAT SHEET. THE FOUR DAYS ARE FOUR DAYS OF THE SAME WEEK AND THE FIRST OF THEM IS A DAY NAMED BY THE BODY THAT RECEIVES THEM AND NOT BY THIS HOLDING. THE LOADS GO ON A ROAD. WHICH ROAD IS NOT WRITTEN AT THE HEAD OF THIS SHEET AND IS NOT TO BE WRITTEN AT THE HEAD OF IT BY ANYBODY WHO HAS NOT BEEN ASKED TO WRITE IT. A LOAD IS A LOAD. THIS HOLDING HAS CERTIFIED NO FIGURE, HAS SUPPLIED NO FIGURE AND HAS NO FIGURE TO CERTIFY. THE FIGURE ON THE SHEET WITH THE SHORTAGE ON IT IS NOT A FIGURE OF LOADS AND THE NINE ABOVE ARE NOT A FIGURE OF DAYS.** NOTHING OF THIS IS A HEADING.

There were about nine people in that room at about the seventh hour and the door was shut and it was the second of six days of the window of the office's work and the work was not being carried out of the window and had not been begun inside it.

The man from the fen road was not in that room and he has not been in it since Wednesday, and he was in the yard in the morning with a spade he was not asked for, cutting a drain off the back of the lean-to where the water was standing against the north wall, and he finished it at about the fourth hour and put the spade back and did not say that he had done it.

About four people in this holding have said that a man who is not a member of anything and has no column in this building has cut a drain in this building, and about four have said that he asked for a spade, and neither of the two fours has been asked and he was not asked.

The man of about fifty was in that room and had a pencil out and did not use it, and the two declinings were still on the long table in front of him, one entered under a clause and one entered as a sheet, and about four people in this holding have said that a woman who has just written nine loads and four days sat down in a room with two bodies that have declined the same connection in the same week, and about four have said that she knew, and neither of the two fours has been asked and she was not asked.

About four people in this holding have said that a schedule with nine loads and four days on it is the first thing anybody in four counties has written down about that road in nine years, and about four have said that the reason nobody has written one down is that a schedule is an instrument and an instrument is a thing somebody can be held to, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Nobody in that room asked whose road it was, and about four people in this holding have said that the road is not written at the head of that sheet on purpose, and about four have said that it is not written at the head of that sheet because nobody in that room knows, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Marek read the schedule and turned it back over again and said nothing for about a minute. Then the reason came before the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and thirty-two, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say what I can do with that and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been the only person in four counties who could hold an end of a connection, and I have done it nine times, and there is no instrument anywhere in four counties that says whether any of the nine is on or off, including the one I am standing in."** "I am not going to put my name at the top of that sheet and I am not going to put my name at the bottom of it either. If I put a name on it then the nine loads are nine loads with a name on them, and in about four years a body in a county that has never met me will take that name off a page and use it, and the use will be correct, and the use will be about a thing I cannot refuse and have never been able to refuse, and I have never once been asked whether I can refuse it."

Nobody in that room said anything at all about the last part of that and about four people in this holding have said that the last part of that was the reason for the first part, and about four have said that it was not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and he was not asked and has not been asked.

The schedule went back into the seed house at about the eighth hour in the hands of the woman who wrote it, and it is a real document and it is the first one there has been, and it is not on the long table and it is not on the middle table and it is not on the low board and there is no second copy of it in this building.

It is in a building at the back of a yard and the yard door is shut at the back and the light in there can be seen from the one room through the small window, and that is the only place in four counties where that sheet is on a table, and about four people in this holding have said that a document in one building is a document in four counties, and about four have said that a document in a building with a shut door is a document in one building, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

A schedule, nine loads and four days are not the register form and the register form has been on the middle table for a hundred and twelve days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and the three of those have never been added in this holding in four years.

The sheet with the shortage on it has been on the long table seventeen days and it has one word at the head of it and a figure and a date, and the seed on the schedule is the seed on that sheet, and the two of them have not been added together in this room and the page does not put a total under either of them.

The paper on the low board is face up and empty and undated with no line for a name on it, and it is nine feet off the floor and it has been there since the nineteenth of a month now three months back, and about four people in this holding have said that a schedule of nine loads and a piece of paper with nothing on it are in one room and about four have said they ought not to be, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Eleven journeys is a floor and it was not raised this morning. The barrow went up the four-mile road at about the seventh hour and came down it loaded, and the second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and six days old and has nothing on it.

The man himself was on the ditch side of the middle road from the second hour until the fourth and was in the seed house at about the eighth and did not come into the one room at all, and nobody in this holding has asked him what he thought of nine loads and nobody is going to.

The arm carried four inches and two branches at the ninth hour and the same at the second, and a schedule is not on it and a decision not to guess is not on it and a name not written at the foot of a sheet is not on it, and the four accounts of that arm remain four and this evening did not make a fifth of anything.

The frost was still on the yard when the room filled and the woman who keeps the seed house put her hands on the table twice in about ten minutes without noticing, and about four people in this holding have said that she noticed it twice because the table is oak, and about four have said that it was the cold, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The compost run is one hundred pounds and it is paid and the figure against it is twenty-two not discharged in twenty-two, and nine loads is not a measurement of it and four days is not a measurement of it, and neither has ever been added to that line in this building in four years.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour and the seed house light was still on at the back of the yard, and nobody in this holding went and looked in at it and nobody in this holding has said since why they did not.

The frost came back in the small hours and the yard had it in the morning, and the pack under the stone at the outlet end will be looked at again in about a week by somebody, and the four-mile road is four miles and a barrow is a barrow and a load is a load, and the sheet with the nine on it was face down on a table in a building at the back of a yard for about two hours and is now in a seed house with a woman who keeps four accounts that do not agree, and nobody in this holding has asked her which one she took the nine from and she was not asked and has not been asked.

Nine loads is what is going to be carried and four days is how long it is going to take and the two of those have not been put under one another anywhere in this county, and about four people in this holding have said that a person who keeps four accounts is the last person who ought to be asked to make one, and about four have said that she made one anyway, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the second of those two fours is a fact and not a thing anybody said out loud.
