# Chapter 456

## The Ring Nobody Has Been Inside

The third line of the rota came round at the second hour on the ninth of this month and was worked, and it was the two culverts on the middle road and the fill behind them, and the fill had gone out about six inches in the rain of the twenty-seventh of a month now one month back and the two of them took four people from the second hour until about the fifth.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling packed the top six inches with the butt of a spade and stood on it and it took his weight, and it took his weight on the sixth inch and not on the ninth, and about four people in this holding have said that is a test and about four have said that is a Tuesday, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

There was a hard frost in the small hours and it had gone by the third hour and the water in the middle road had gone down about four inches overnight and gone back up about two of them by the seventh, and it is doing that because the middle ditch is running full from the north end and the north end was cleared on Wednesday.

The water in the middle ditch is the color of the ground it is standing on and it has been that color since the second week of a month now two months back, and about four people in this holding have said that the ditch is doing the only thing it can do with what it is given, and about four have said that the ditch was put there to move clean water and it is not doing that, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the ditch is nine hundred yards of the same ditch in both directions.

Eighty mornings of a hundred and forty-three, read out at the seventh hour by two men who each said one figure and did not say the other. The two boards stood at **six hundred and three days** and **six hundred and fifty**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer behind them was not opened.

The ninth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and thirty-six hundredweight.**

Five off it since Tuesday morning, and the two hundred and nineteenth figure of a run in which no number has ever been written down twice. The fall went onto the falls. Ninety-eight rises against eighty-eight falls is a hundred and eighty-six, and that is the whole of the window this morning.

Nothing has moved in the column for the four bodies of households, and this morning's day against it is two hundred and seventeen, and the clause printed beneath is the two hundred and sixteenth morning out of two hundred and eighteenth. That is a count of mornings and it is not the launder, and this building has never added the two.

The eleven acres are about a mile and a quarter east of the yard and the way in is a gate and a headland and about two hundred yards of rough grass, and Marek walked it at about the sixth hour of the evening with the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden and nobody else.

The gate is set into a hedge that was planted before this holding had a book and it opens on a hinge that has to be lifted, and about four people in this holding have said that a gate you have to lift is a gate nobody comes through, and about four have said that eleven years of nobody coming through is not the same as eleven years of nobody wanting to, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden lifted it and nobody asked her to.

There is no road to the eleven acres. There is a field gate and a headland and about two hundred yards of grass you can get a barrow down in the summer and not in the spring, and about four people in this holding have said that eleven acres you cannot get a barrow to is eleven acres you cannot count in a morning, and about four have said that nobody has ever tried to count them, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Inside the eleven acres there is a ring of ground that has been bare for eleven years. It is bare in its eleventh year and nothing has been put on it and nothing has come up on it and nothing has been put into it, and it has no dimension on any page in this holding or in the four counties.

It was named twice this week as a thing nobody has been inside and it was named twice and no more, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said the first of those two out loud at the gate and he said the second of them at the edge of the rough grass, and neither of them said a third.

**"That is the eleventh year of it,"** she said, at the gate, with her hand on the top bar, and the hand stayed on the bar and did not point at anything. **"I have never been inside it. I am not going to today and I would not go today if you asked me."**

The ground on both sides of the ring is going wrong. It has been going wrong since before this building kept books about it and it is going slower than it was in the ninth, and about four people in this holding have said that slower is a kind of good, and about four have said that slower is a kind of bad that takes its time, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the ring has not been walked.

A ring of bare ground inside a wood of eleven acres that is going wrong on both sides of it is the shape of a thing that went wrong at one place and not at the next, and about four people in this holding have said that a shape like that is the one thing a man of about fifty could read off a page in a second, and about four have said that it is also the one thing nobody can price without going in, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the man of about fifty was not asked and has not been asked.

There is a shelf above the middle road about nine feet up that the wood has run out onto in two places, and the wood on the shelf is the oldest standing wood in this holding and is not on any of the four lists, and about four people in this holding have said that the ring is nine hundred yards from that shelf and about four have said that the distance means nothing because nobody has ever carried a line between the two, and neither of the two fours has been asked and no line has been carried between the two.

He said the reason first and then the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and thirty-three, and it is his and of nobody else's, and the two of them were standing in the rough grass about two hundred yards short of a ring nobody in this county has ever been inside.

**"I am going to say the one thing about that ring and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been the only person in four counties who could stand in a piece of ground and feel what it was doing for eleven years, and I have not stood in that one once, and I have never told anybody why."** "I have not told anybody because I do not know. If I had stood in it in the second year I would know, and I did not stand in it in the second year, and there is no version of this where going now gets me the second year. I have been telling myself for eleven years that I am waiting for the right moment. A man who has said that to himself every year for eleven years is not waiting for a moment. He is standing at a gate."

Nobody said anything at the gate and about four people in this holding have said that two people at a gate is a small enough audience to say a thing in and about four have said it should have been said to a room, and neither of the two fours has been asked and neither of them was asked and has not been asked.

They walked the boundary of the eleven acres and not the ring, and the boundary is about a mile and a half and it took them an hour and ten minutes in the dark with one lamp, and nobody put a foot inside the ring and nobody has at any time in four years.

The boundary is a ditch and a bank and about two feet of wire that has been down since the first year and has been mended about nine times by nine different people, and the wire is not a fence for anything and it is not a fence against anything and it is not in anybody's book, and about four people in this holding have said that a boundary nobody put a name to is the only kind that is still where it was, and about four have said that it is the only kind that a body in a county can come and move, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the wire was not touched in an hour and ten minutes.

She stopped twice. The first time was at the north side, where the ditch has gone out about a foot on the inside and there is a hole in the bank about four feet long that a person could get a hand through, and she looked into it and put her lamp down and did not put her hand in it, and about four people in this holding have said that a woman who puts a lamp down in front of a hole and does not put her hand in it has been doing that for a long time, and about four have said that she put the lamp down so that he could see it better, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The second time was at the east side and she did not stop for a reason that was about the ground. She stopped and turned round and looked back the way they had come and then went on, and he did not ask her why and the count of questions this holding has put to her on that walk is nil and is nil.

They came out at the gate at about the eighth hour with the frost back on the grass about a quarter of an inch, and the lamp belongs to the seed house and came out of it in the hands of a woman of about thirty-eight and went back in without anybody saying thank you or anything at all.

A mark is not a name and a name is not a witness and a ring is not a figure. Nobody in this holding has a dimension for it and nobody in the four counties has a dimension for it and the count of things this holding cannot check is two and is two and no third of them is this.

The six not knowns are six and no unit is supplied for any of the six and a seventh is not added for a ring, and a ring of bare ground is the seventh thing this holding does not know about if anybody in four counties ever writes it down that way, and nobody is going to write it down that way in this holding.

The vault of forty households under four hundred yards off is not recovered and there is no figure of people against it and there has not been one since a month now two months back, and it is not four hundred yards from the ring and nobody has compared the two distances and nobody is going to.

The boundary wire on the north side of the eleven acres is down about four feet of it in two places and has been since before this month, and nobody in this holding has put a staple in it in four years and nobody in the four counties has asked about it, and about four people in this holding have said that wire is the only thing in this county nobody has ever repaired, and about four have said that a wire nobody has repaired is the reason nobody has been inside the ring, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The offer is on the low board at the far end of the room, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, refused on the nineteenth of a month now three months back, and a boundary walked at night is not a reason to take a piece of paper off a board and this holding has never used one as a reason.

The sheet in the drawer that claims a corridor in the pan is correct in every particular it states and it lets nothing out, and a ring of bare ground is not that corridor, and the two of them are about four hundred yards apart on the ground and about four hundred yards apart in every book in four counties, and the two have not been added in this holding in four years.

The schedule of nine loads and four days is in the seed house and is not on any table in this building, and a ring of bare ground is not on that schedule and that schedule is not on a ring, and nobody in this holding has carried a figure from one to the other.

Four sacks came down the four-mile road on the barrow at about the seventh hour and went back up with four, and that is as near as this building gets to counting its own journeys, and the second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and seven days old and has nothing on it.

The well house door is nine hundred yards off and its seventh column stands at fifty-eight, and it is read on the thirtieth and the first and on no other morning in the year, and it was not read this morning. Beside it the second column has four words at the head of it and nothing under them, and the sixth has a heading and a count of readings, and the other four are headed nothing.

The arm carried four inches and two branches at the second hour and the same at the ninth, and a ring of ground is not on it and a mile and a half walked round the outside of one is not on it, and the four accounts of that arm remain four and this evening did not make a fifth of anything.

The frost that came back in the small hours was the third this month and the man of about thirty-one of Silling said it was a month early and nobody asked him how he knew, and the count of questions this holding has put to him about the weather is nil and is nil, and he has been asked nothing about the four lines in his own book in a hundred and seven days.

Nobody thanked anybody. The lamp went off the long table at about the first hour and the two of them came in off the headland about twenty minutes behind that, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden put her boots by the fire and did not say a word about the ring and has not said a word about it since, and the count of questions this holding has put to her about it is nil and is nil.

> Entered by the clerk of this holding at the first hour, in his own hand, as one unbroken line: **The ninth. The hard frost in the small hours gone by the third. The third line of the rota worked, the two culverts on the middle road, the fill packed six inches and it took his weight on the sixth inch and not on the ninth, four people from the second hour until the fifth. The pair read aloud at the seventh hour. The same day in and the same day out. Nine hundred and thirty-six hundredweight to the launder. The eleven acres walked on the boundary and not on the ring, the ring not walked, not measured, not crossed, not priced and not explained, in its eleventh year, with no foot in it. A count of things this holding cannot check is two and is two.** Nothing of this is a forecast and nothing of it is a cause and nothing of it is a total.
