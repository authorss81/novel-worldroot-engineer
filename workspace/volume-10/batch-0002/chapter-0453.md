# Chapter 453

## A Yard At The Node

The fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour on a Sunday and there was nobody at the headland above Marden to work it, and it went away empty for the sixty-fourth morning running.

The rota stands at ninety-three mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and sixty-four not, and twenty-nine and sixty-four are ninety-three. The count rises by exactly one on a morning this line comes round and by nothing on any other morning, and twenty-nine taken has not moved this morning or on any morning of this year.

This building keeps two reckonings of that rotation and they do not agree, so the page carries both. This morning is the forty-first morning of the first and the twenty-eighth of the other. The thirteen between them has been there four years and nobody in this holding has ever found out what it is.

There is no figure for the run of mornings on this page and the reason is on the page. The pair of boards is read at the seventh hour on alternate mornings and this is not one of them, and the reason is that the two men who read them went east with Marek in the morning and were still on the road at the hour, and neither of them read his side alone and nobody in this holding thought to ask a fourth man to do it.

The pair stands on the boards at **six hundred days** and **six hundred and forty-seven**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

Marek went east at about the second hour with the man of about fifty and the two men who read the boards, and it is five miles to the node and a day's walk, and they made it in about seven hours with a stop at the trough.

They went for the fourth line and found there was nobody on it, and then they went on five miles because Marek had been asked for a figure about a road on Wednesday and had not said he would not get one, and about four people in this holding have said that a man who does not say no on a Wednesday has said yes on the Thursday, and about four have said that there was a fourth line to work and it went away empty and that is the whole of what happened, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The four miles east is not a road in the way the four miles north is a road. It is a drove with a gate at each end and about a mile and a half of it under water on the left, and they went up the right-hand side of it because the man of about fifty said the left-hand side takes a man to the knee at the third gate and they would not have got off it before dark.

They passed the trough at about the fourth hour and the man of about fifty put his hand in it and brought it out and looked at it and said nothing, and the trough has not had water in it since the eighth of a month now one month back and there is about two inches of grey in the bottom of it and nobody in this holding has asked which body is meant to fill it.

The window of the office's work opened at the sixth hour and it opens on the sixth of a month and it shuts on the eleventh, and the six of this month was a Sunday, and nobody in four counties has ever moved it and nobody wanted to.

It is six days and no work is ever carried out of it. A thing begun on the first of a window and finished after the sixth day of it does not exist in this holding's practice and has never existed and everybody in four counties knows it and goes on doing it twice a season.

The node is a building with a lean-to on the south side of it and a barrow under the lean-to, and there is about half a hundred yards of ground in front of the building that is flat and hard and drains, and that half hundred yards is the yard, and the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five.

It is five and it does not move this morning, and about four people in this holding have said that the number had better not move and about four have said that a number that cannot move is not a count, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

There were more than five bodies on the node when the four of them came in off the road at about the sixth hour of the evening, and the number on this page is five.

A count of five is a fact about a rota and not a count of a morning. It is the count this holding writes on a door once a month and twice a year, and it has been the same figure for four years, and nobody in four counties has ever asked what it counts, and the bodies standing about a hundred yards off the flat ground were not asked and are not asked now.

About four people in this holding have wanted a figure of the people on the node and about four have said that the figure would be about forty, and about four have said that about forty is a figure about a slope and not about a body, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the number is not given in a yard.

A slope with forty people on it is not a yard. A building with a barrow in it is not a yard. About four people in this holding have wanted those two counted with the flat ground in front of the building, and about four have said that a yard is a place where a thing is said that cannot be unsaid in the next county, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Marek stood in the flat ground and did not go up the slope and about four people here have said that a man who stands in a yard and does not go up a slope has decided something, and about four have said he was waiting to be asked up, and neither of the two fours has been asked and he was not asked.

There is a door on the node building and it is nine feet off the ground and it is shut, and behind it is the counter this holding reads a figure off twice a year and does not touch at any other time, and the number on the door is five and it is the same number it has been for four years.

Marek did not open the door. About four people in this holding have said that a man who has walked five miles does not open a door, and about four have said that there was no reason on that slope to open it, and neither of the two fours has been asked, and the door is shut and the weather is coming.

The man of about fifty went up the slope. He went up it at about the seventh hour of the evening, on a ground that had been wet for four days and had turned to churn about halfway up, and he came back down it about twenty minutes later with his boots to the ankle, and he had not been asked to go and did not say that he had gone.

He said it standing on the flat ground with his back to the door and it was one sentence, and the reason came before the sentence, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-three, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about a road and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been a bookkeeper for thirty-one years in four counties and I have never once in thirty-one years been asked by anybody to write down what a road is for, and I found out about ten minutes ago on that slope that I do not know the answer and neither does the man standing in the yard."**

About four people on that slope heard the whole of that sentence. About four heard the half of it, being the back half, about the man standing in the yard, and about four heard a different half of it and that is a third four and it is not added to either of the other two.

It is the fifteenth time a bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard that the engineer of record had not thought of, and the count of that is fifteen and it is his and of nobody else's, and the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and it did not move this morning.

Those two numbers are about a yard and they are not added to each other. One of them is a count of sentences that were said and one of them is a count of sentences that were wrong, and the difference between those two things is a matter of opinion and about four people in this holding have an opinion and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and the two numbers have not been added in this building in four years.

Marek gave the reason first and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and thirty, and it is his and of nobody else's, and the yard was still wet where the four of them had walked in.

**"I am going to say what I have not thought of and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that he said it in a yard and I heard all of it, and I want it written down that I heard all of it, because in about four years somebody is going to find out that a bookkeeper of about fifty said a thing on a slope that the engineer of record had not thought of, and the two of them are going to be put in a line, and the line is going to be the wrong way round."** "I have not thought of it. He is right and I have not thought of it. A road is not for anything. I have been treating it for nine years as a thing that ground goes along, and that is what it is for, and it took a man with mud to his knee who has never held a spade in his life to say so in one sentence while I was standing in the mud as well."

Nobody on the slope said anything and about four people on it have said since that they would have said something and about four have said that they were glad they had not, and neither of the two fours has been asked and he was not asked.

The two men who read the boards came down the slope last and came into the yard about ten minutes after everybody else had stopped talking, and about four people in this holding have said that the two of them were out of the room for the whole of it and about four have said that they were four miles behind and on a drove under water and that is the same thing, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

One of them asked, at the top of the drove on the way back, whether the five on the node door was a count of people, and the man of about fifty said that it was not, and did not say what it was, and about four people in this holding have said that a man who answers a question with what a thing is not has been in the trade a long time, and about four have said that the two of them were walking on a drove under water and it was not the moment, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The sixth went in at about the ninth hour at the node, in the same book and in the same hand, and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and the reading did not vary for a rise because it never has.

**Nine hundred and thirty-eight hundredweight.**

Saturday put eight on it, and the figure is the two hundred and sixteenth of a run that has never produced the same number twice since the morning it started. It went up the rising side, as the rises do. Ninety-seven against eighty-six is a hundred and eighty-three, and in four years of keeping these books nobody here has added those two halves to the figure over them.

The four bodies of households have now been at the same figure for two hundred and fourteen days, and the clause printed under it reads the two hundred and thirteenth of the mornings out of two hundred and fifteenth. That aggregate is not the launder's aggregate and the two of them have never been added in this building in four years.

The seed house light was still on at about the fourth hour when they came in off the drove and it has been on every night this week, and about four people in this holding have said that a light on late in a seed house is a woman counting, and about four have said that it is a woman waiting for a road to come back, and neither of the two fours has been asked and it was not a question anybody could have asked her.

The paper on the low board has been face up since the nineteenth of a month now three months back and it is nine feet off the floor, and about four people in this holding have said that a thing a person can see from the door is a thing anybody in the county can see, and about four have said that nobody comes through that door who is not already in the county, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

A sentence on a slope is not on the seventh column of the well house door and is not on the second column either, and the second has four words at the head of it and nothing under them, and the seventh is at fifty-eight and is not read except on the thirtieth and the first. That door is nine hundred yards off and it is dark by the time the four of them came off the drove, and nobody in this holding went out to it.

There is a hearth in the one room and the fire had been out since the morning and two of the four of them put it in at about the second hour and the other two sat against the wall, and the boots went in the corner by the long table and stayed there until about the fourth hour, and nobody in this holding has ever written down how many pairs of boots are in that corner in the winter.

The weather came in from the west after the sun went and the sleet started again about the ninth hour, and it was still coming in at about the first hour when the four of them came off the drove, and the two men who read the boards had their shoulders wet through inside their coats and were not asked to say so.

Nobody thanked anybody. The four of them came off the road into this yard at about the first hour with the boots full and put the boots by the fire where the other pair were, and the man of about fifty did not say a word about the sentence on the slope and has not said a word about it since, and nobody in this holding has asked him what he meant by it and is not going to.

The ordinary post had been in the box by the gate while they were gone and it was the ordinary post, four things, none of them from a county and none of them about a road. The Undercommons letter of the twenty-ninth is still in the drawer with the one line this holding sent back on it, and the line has not been answered, and the drawer is the drawer and not the bin.

The man of about seventy was not fetched this morning and the twenty-fourth fetching of that man was the first of a month now one month back and the twenty-fifth is the first of the month after that, and nobody in this holding has worked out why it falls when it falls, and the count of questions this holding has put to him is nil and is nil.

A yard is a place where a thing is said that cannot be unsaid in the next county, and the man of about fifty said a thing in one on a Sunday evening, and the two of those are the only two things about a yard that happened in this holding today, and neither of them is in a column.
