## 9a. THE FIGURE-DELIVERY DEFECT, WHICH IS THE THING THAT WILL FIND YOU

**THE BATCH BEHIND YOU CAME BACK FROM ITS FIRST DRAFT WITH ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX REPEATED SHAPES AND TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SEVEN EXCESS, AND ITS OWN PROMPT HAD NAMED THE CAUSE AS ONE CLOSING PARAGRAPH RECITING THE SAME FLOORS.
