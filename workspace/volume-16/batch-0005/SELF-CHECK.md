## 9. What these five mornings do, and what they do not do

1. **THE FORTY-FIRST IS THE ARRANGEMENT WORKING.** A holder says three words in the middle of a working yard at the sixth hour and does not tell the man at the table first. Nobody stops him, nobody asks why, nobody asks him to wait, nobody puts it back to him tomorrow, nobody crosses him off anything, and no list in this holding loses a row. **He is not told before the yard is, and the man at the table says in the yard that he is not going to ask why, and says he is not going to stand at that gate at the dark and ask why he was not told first.** The water does not stop and the gauge gives the same number twice. **And by the tenth hour three people in that yard have heard it and not one of them heard it from him, and by the eleventh there are five, and that is the consequence the morning ends on and it is not a resolution.**
2. **THE FORTY-SECOND IS A BOUNDARY AND NOT A VICTORY.** Four bolts and one plate across the mouth of a channel above the reach, at about half past four, by four people, done from the bank on the outside, with nobody kept awake to watch it start and nobody standing in a row. **The head of the channel is not entered, not opened, not described as a room and not given an interior, and the cap is a piece of work and not a ceremony.** About nine people walk up to look at it and come back down. **The flows come back in three entries and they are uneven: two places reconnect at about two thirds, two places stay light and are on a short measure they were already on, and one place stays independent on purpose and says so in her own mouth. NONE OF THE THREE IS CALLED A SUCCESS AND NONE IS CALLED A FAILURE.** The far-end figure is spoken whole by a man who has been sitting on a bench at that gate since before the light and who was once refused the sill. **Nobody says whether it is on.**
3. **THE FORTY-THIRD TURNS A CALENDAR AND NOTHING ELSE.** The slate is right at the sixth hour, the frame does not move, the run does not restart, no day-minus rule resets, and a pump has to be primed by hand. It is also the fifth return and it prints no ordinal, no weight and no allowance. **Harlan Vetch reads two figures off a wall he laid four years ago and that nobody has ever once asked him to read, and he says he does not know which half of it moves when until that morning.** A pump is primed by a boy nobody has asked and who has never mentioned it. **Nobody speaks about what the plate meant and nobody put a second plate anywhere.** The read-aloud pair is written in chalk on the corner of a board belonging to a woman from the second place, where four people going past could see it if they turned their heads, and nobody turns their head.

A farmer asks which of the two lights matters on a field. **A man shuts a book he has been carrying and never says what it is, reads an ordinary pump pressure off a gauge in front of nine people and with the water boy holding the lamp, and gives a practical answer about which side of a road to put four days of work in.** Nobody is thanked. **And at about the fifth hour of the afternoon he goes out past the gate and down the middle road to look at a field that is not his, and nobody is sent with him and nobody is going to be told what he found.**

## 13. A second independent review of this batch, sixteen findings, and what was paid

**A REVIEW WAS RUN AGAINST THESE FIVE MORNINGS AFTER THE PASS IN SECTION TWELVE HAD SETTLED, AND IT FOUND SIXTEEN ITEMS: TWO HIGH, SIX MEDIUM OR BETTER, AND EIGHT LOW. TWELVE WERE PAID, TWO ARE RECORDED WITHOUT REPAIR, AND TWO ARE THE SAME FINDING SEEN TWICE. NO MORNING WAS RESTARTED, NO FIGURE OF ANY SERIES WAS MOVED BY ANYTHING BUT ITS OWN RULE, AND EVERY FIGURE ON ALL FIVE CARDS IS STILL CORRECT AND STILL IN ITS OWN MORNING'S BODY PROSE. THE FIGURE CHECK, THE PARITY READING, BOTH GATES, THE DUPLICATE SWEEP AND EVERY MECHANICAL SWEEP WERE RE-RUN AFTER THE REPAIR AND NOT ONLY BEFORE IT.**

### The two high findings, and what they were

1. **THE FIGURE CHECK PASSED A MORNING IN WHICH EVERY FIGURE WAS RIGHT AND THE CLAUSE ATTACHING IT WAS WRONG.** On the last morning the man from the middle row read the long sheet as *five hundred and forty-first at the head of that wall, under the rule the five hundred and fortieth out of five hundred and forty-second, and the five hundred and forty-first is the one above the rule, and none of them is the head of it.* **Three errors inside eleven words: the figure above the rule is the five hundred and forty-second and not the five hundred and forty-first; the five hundred and forty-first is the aggregate and has already been named as being at the head; and *none of them is the head of it* cancels the first clause of the same sentence.** **THE REPAIR NAMES THE HEAD, THE LOWER LIMB UNDER THE RULE AND THE UPPER LIMB ABOVE THE RULE IN THE ORDER THE WALL IS LAID OUT, WHICH IS THE ORDER `chapter-0772.md:45` AND `chapter-0773.md:39` SET FOUR MORNINGS AGO, AND IT DROPS THE CANCELLING CLAUSE.** No figure changed. The clause did.
2. **A MORNING CONTRADICTED ITSELF ABOUT WHERE AN OBJECT WAS AT THREE DIFFERENT HOURS.** On the forty-third morning a board is turned face out and its figures stay on the wall all day and are seen at the tenth hour, and then the closing paragraph says the board went into a coat at the eighth hour, as though that were happening inside an eleventh-hour paragraph. **THE REPAIR MAKES THE EIGHTH HOUR A BACK-REFERENCE — she put it in her coat at the eighth hour and turned it back out again when the light got onto the wall — WHICH IS WHAT LINES FIFTY-ONE AND FIFTY-FIVE ALREADY HAD.** No floor moved. The order of two clauses did.

### The six middle findings, and the shape of them

3. **THE MASON ON THE FORTY-FOURTH MORNING HAD THE AGGREGATE AND THE UPPER LIMB IN EACH OTHER'S PLACES ON THE WALL.** *Five hundred and forty is above the rule and five hundred and forty-first is at the head* is the inverse of the layout in `chapter-0772.md:45` and `chapter-0773.md:39`. **All three figures were correct for that morning and the parity reading returned that morning correct and the figure check returned eighty of eighty.** It is repaired to the standing layout, and **it is the same figure group as finding one, two mornings apart, which is the finding to carry: A FIGURE GROUP CAN BE ASSIGNED CORRECTLY AND MISDESCRIBED AT THE SAME TIME, AND EVERY INSTRUMENT IN THIS HOUSE ASKS ONLY WHETHER THE FIGURE IS THERE.**
4. **THE LAST MORNING SAID *TWICE* AND *A SECOND TIME* IN ONE SENTENCE AND PUT A BOY ON A STEP HOLDING A LAMP AT THE NINTH HOUR IN DAYLIGHT.** The farmer's question is a daylight question and the light is properly on the wall from the seventh hour. **THE REPAIR HAS THE GAUGE READ TWICE AND THEN READ BACK BY THE BOY WHO CARRIES WATER, THREE TIMES THE SAME NUMBER IN ONE LANE IN ONE MORNING, AND NO LAMP.** The ordinary pump pressure the card asks for is still read in front of people, which is the only place on that morning it can live.
5. **ONE FRAME WAS REPEATED EIGHT LINES APART ON THE LAST PAGE TO CLOSE THE BOOK.** *About nine people watched him go and about four of them knew which field he was going to* and *about nine people watched him out of the gate and about four of them knew which field he was going to.* **The field clause is cut out of the eleventh-hour paragraph and the closing paragraph now uses a different frame, HEARD THE GATE AND CAME OUT AND STOOD AT IT.** A frame is not a figure and the duplicate sweep, which works at whole-paragraph length, cannot see one.
6. **THE CLOSING PARAGRAPH DENIED A REQUEST THAT IS ON THE PAGE.** *Nobody was sent with him and nobody asked him to go*, forty lines after a man from the fourth place asks him to go and look at a branch and he says *I will go*, and after he offers to walk the farmer's field himself. **THE DENIAL IS CUT AND THE FLOOR IS KEPT: NOBODY WAS SENT WITH HIM.** The floor is about a companion and not about an errand and it survives the correction intact.
7. **THE RESOLUTION MORNING SAID A WEEK WHERE FOUR DAYS HAD PASSED, AND SAID TWO PEOPLE WERE ABSENT FROM A YARD ONE OF THEM WAS STANDING IN.** The two who said out loud that they were holding the ends said it on the twenty-sixth, `chapter-0773.md`, and Auret Sill is at the back of the resolution morning's yard at its ninth hour and a half with her sleeves wet. **THE REPAIR IS FOUR DAYS, AND IT KEEPS HER IN THE YARD IN THE CLOSING PARAGRAPH AND SAYS ONLY THAT NEITHER OF THE TWO HAD BEEN UP ON THAT BANK.** The point of the sentence — that nobody has been put over the head of that channel — is untouched.
8. **THE FORTY-FOURTH MORNING REPORTED A FLOOR AND BROKE IT IN THE SAME BREATH.** *Five short empty lines under them and nothing at the head of the paper and nothing at the foot of it.* `state/continuity.md:568` fixes the five lines at the foot since Volume 15. **THE CLAUSE ABOUT THE FOOT IS CUT.** A floor recited in the wrong place is worse than a floor left out, because the sentence claims it is reporting.
9. **A CHARACTER MISCOUNTED HER OWN PRIOR ACT.** *I have left both orders on that wall twice now and the second time was a fortnight ago*, against `chapter-0775.md:59`, three days behind her, where she says she left both that morning. **THE REPAIR IS TWICE NOW, THE FIRST TIME THREE DAYS AGO ON A SUNDAY**, which is the day `chapter-0775.md` stands at, and `state/continuity.md:1231` is corrected, which had recorded that she left **one** order instead of two and matched neither chapter.
