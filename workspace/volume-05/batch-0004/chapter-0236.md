# Chapter 236

## What Is Left Of The Number Two

The tenth of this month was a Tuesday, and the examiner of the Rootwright Assembly read the copy of the eleven to the four bodies of households at about the fourth hour of the afternoon in a yard at Errow Reach with about nineteen people in it, and she took forty minutes, and she answered four questions and said she did not know the answer to two of them, in front of nineteen people, and did not go back to either of them.

And then she went, and nobody thanked her and nobody said anything tender and the fen entered both of those, and entered that she had come on the ordinary notice and had not been stayed, delayed, fed or obstructed, and that the four bodies of households had sent four people to stand in a yard for two hours on a Saturday and had not once tried to help.

The reader of this body said one thing in that yard after she had gone, and he said it in four sentences and then stopped, and the fen entered that he said four sentences and that the four is the number he said and not a number he had prepared.

"**The count of persons in this fen who can hold a pattern for four is two. Both are of this holding. One of them is me and one of them is a man of sixty-eight with a cough, and it has not moved in twenty days, and it has not moved in sixty-nine days, and it is going to be two on the last day of this month and it is going to be two at the end of the season, and I am entering that I know that and that I am not going to do anything about it this month.**"

"**A party of five has been working that kerb every fourth morning for twenty days. Five people. One holder and four working. Seven turns in nine mornings were taken, and two were not taken, and both of the two were the fourth line, and a party of five has proved that five people can do a turn every fourth morning for nine mornings and has not proved that there is a third person anywhere in this fen who can hold a pattern for four.**"

"**And that is what a party of five has proved about a number of two, and it is a worse thing than a number of three and a better thing than nothing, and I am not going to improve on it.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that the man of the north row with the cough was in that yard and was not asked to be in it and entered that the count of yards he has stood in since the first of last month is nine and has moved.

And then the reader of this body said the thing about the sentence, and he said it to his own book and not to the yard, and gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-seven, and about nineteen people in that yard did not hear any of it.

"**I have said out loud in a yard, five times in this season, that a man who is the only person who can do a thing is a bottleneck and not a system. It is not going to be said a sixth time in a yard. I am going to say now, once, in about four people, where the five times were, and then I am going to leave it where it is.**"

"**The first was in a yard on the eleventh of a month back, and I was told the number was two in a yard with about eleven people in it and I said it out loud the same afternoon. The second was in this holding, in my own book, on the page where the count is. The third was in a yard at Errow Reach, in about nine people, and a person who keeps four books for four villages had to finish the sentence. The fourth was in a yard at Marden, on a stone the size of a bread oven, in about nineteen people, and a man of about fifty-eight with a stick in his hand told me that it was for nothing and asked for that to be written down. The fifth was in a shed.**"

"**That is a yard, a book, a yard, a yard, and a shed, in that order, and not one of the five was in the same building as the count. And from this morning it is going to be a book, and a note, and not a yard, and the reason is that the sentence has been said five times in about seventy days and every one of the five times it was said by a man standing about nine feet from a person who was not going to be thanked for it, and I have worked out that the nine feet is the whole of what I have been doing.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the man of about fifty said nothing, and entered that the count of times he has been in a yard and said nothing this month is three, and entered that the count of yards the man of the north row with the cough has stood in has not moved and that he did not hear any of what was said because it was not said in a yard.

And then he read out what the examination had cost, in about four lines, and the fen entered that he read it out and entered the reason he read it out, which is that a man who has been given a sheet with ten of his own faults on it and has said nothing about them for three days is a man who is holding it, and a holding is a thing that has to be said in public once.

"**Ten of the eleven were ours and I have had three days with that and here is what it has cost, and it is not the examination's cost, it is the cost of the three days.**"

"**On the seventh of this month the second line of that rota was taken at the second hour and there was a woman of the Assembly in the yard who had been told in writing that the examination does not stop a turn, and she stood about two hundred yards off and watched a body of households do the thing that four villages have been doing without a sheet for eleven years, and she was not in the way of it, and the turn was taken.**"

"**And on the fourth of this month, the same morning as a return of ninety hundredweight that six people read back off a dial, a body of households took a turn at the second hour with a man from an Assembly walking about the ground with a tape and a boy of about sixteen holding the end of it, and neither of those two things touched the other, and about twenty-two people watched, and that is what correct looks like and I had never seen it.**"

"**And the cost of the three days is this: there is no line on that sheet and no form in four districts and no column on that board for the fact that an examination happened, and so in about four years the only record that ten of the four bodies of households have been told ten things about themselves by a person they had not asked for is a page in a book in four hundred yards that a man with a mark on his arm wrote down, and nobody else's.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing, and he said it to the yard, and it was four sentences, and it is the whole of what the tenth of this month came to.

"**The fourth line came round at the second hour of the morning of this day and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields waited, and the seventh line says nobody is blamed for a field that waited, and nobody is.**"

"**That is the third time in eleven days and there have been nine mornings of a rota in force and the fourth line has been empty on every one of them, and the count of persons in this fen who can hold a pattern for four is two, and I am one of the two, and I am not going into the fourth line, and I said that out loud in a yard on the twenty-seventh of a month back and I have not been asked since and I am not going to be asked now.**"

"**So a village of about three hundred people has a line with nothing in it and a man with a mark on his arm has a sentence he said in a yard, and the two of those are the same thing, and neither of them is going to move, and I am going to go home on Friday and I am going to carry a page with ten faults on it and I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to thank anybody.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the fourth line is the only one of the four that this holding has not made better in a month, and that he is not going to make it better on the road, and that a man who improves a rota in a cart is a man who has decided the rota was his.

And then he did one thing on the afternoon of that day that nobody in that yard knew about, and the fen entered it that night and it is four lines long and it is about the eleventh thing and not about the ten.

*I have walked nine feet off that hollow in this month and I did not stop and I went past it four times and I wrote down that there was a ring of bare ground there and I entered that I did not find out what it was doing there. A woman from an Assembly stood in my yard for two hours with a tape and a boy of sixteen and she also did not find out, and she put that on a page that goes to a body in a city, and neither of us has said one word about it since and I am not going to be the one who starts. It is the only one of the eleven that I cannot do anything about, and it is the only one of the eleven that nobody has done anything about, and there is a ring of bare ground in the middle of eleven acres that has been there for eleven years and two professionals have now walked past it and written down that they do not know. A third one of us in about four years and it will be three professionals and a ring. There is no form for it and I am not going to make one.*

And then he said the thing about the where-column, out loud, to four people, and did not improve on the woman of about forty-four who had said it first in her own yard five days and about ninety miles west of here.

"**On Wednesday I asked a woman of about forty-four in her own yard whether she was going to put a where-column on the board in that yard, and she said no, and the reason is that it would need five more, and I have been carrying her sentence for thirteen days and I am not going to say it again in a yard, and I am putting it in this book instead, which is what I said I was going to do.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

> Entered by the reader of this body, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the tenth of this month, in a yard at Errow Reach, on the standing of a count, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and is five days west by the cart road: **THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR IS TWO, both of this holding, one of them the engineer of record, and it has not moved in twenty days and has been two since the first of a month two months back, which is sixty-nine days, and this holding's own book said fifty days on the twentieth of a month back and the book was counting in months and a count kept in months is not a count in days and it has not been converted in a yard. THE COUNT FOR FIVE IS ONE AND IT IS HIM. A PARTY OF FIVE HAS WORKED THAT KERB EVERY FOURTH MORNING FOR TWENTY DAYS, being one holder and four working, and seven turns in nine mornings were taken and two were not, and both of the two are the fourth line, and no party of five has produced a third person in this fen who can hold a pattern for four. THE SENTENCE THAT A MAN WHO IS THE ONLY PERSON WHO CAN DO A THING IS A BOTTLENECK AND NOT A SYSTEM WAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD FIVE TIMES IN THIS SEASON AND IS NOT GOING TO BE SAID A SIXTH TIME IN A YARD. THE FIVE WERE, in order, a yard on the eleventh of a month back, this holding's own book on the page where the count is, a yard at Errow Reach in about nine people, a yard at Marden on a stone in about nineteen people, and a shed, and not one of the five was in the same building as the count. The reader of this body entered in a yard of about four people on the tenth of this month that the nine feet is the whole of what he has been doing, and that the sentence is going into a book and a note from this morning and is not going back into a yard. The Assembly's examination was on the ordinary notice, on the seventh of this month at about the tenth hour, and was not stayed, delayed, fed or obstructed, and found eleven things, and read out the one that has no name on it first and on purpose, being a ring of bare ground about two yards wide and about two hundred yards long, inside eleven acres of standing willow, nine feet off the hollow where the returns go in, bare eleven years, and the inspector stated on her own page that she did not find out what it is doing there and put no name against it. Ten of the eleven are faults of the bodies at that node and every one of the ten was on their own page before she arrived. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. The day-and-not-the-figure count is three and is not reduced. The standing count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken was not said out loud this month and is not going to be. The count of requests that a man's initial be put against nothing is fifty-three. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED AGAIN IN ONE MONTH. THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE MORNING OF THE TENTH OF THIS MONTH AND THERE WAS NOBODY AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN, and the fields waited, and nobody is blamed for a field that waited, and nobody was. Nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything tender and the fen entered both of those.**
