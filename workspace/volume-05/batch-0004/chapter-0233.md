# Chapter 233

## The Thirtieth Of The Second Month

The thirtieth of this month was a Saturday, and the door at nine hundred yards off the seed house was read at about the eleventh hour of the morning in a yard of about twenty-five people, by the person who keeps the seed house, with the reader of this body on her left and the man of about fifty on her right, and the man of the north row with the cough was in the yard and was not asked to be in it.

The clerk read the day-minus figures out loud before either of the numbers was said, which nobody has ever asked him to do on any day he has done it, and the boards stood at **three hundred and fifty-four days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and one days**, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be.

And then the seventh column went to **forty-two**.

And before any of the figures were closed, the standing rule was read out in full, out loud, by the person who keeps the seed house, in front of about twenty-five people, because a rule read only when it is used is a habit and this one is not, and all four of its lines are about a stop, and the third of them does not say in a yard and this body read it as though it did for two years.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

And then the reader of this body said the thing about the water, and he said it to the yard and not to the book, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-five.

"**I am going to say one thing about a number and I am going to say it badly and then somebody else is going to say it better than I am going to say it, and I am telling this yard that now so that nobody thinks I have been waiting for my turn.**"

"**Cutting a launder does not make water less scarce. It makes one village twenty-five hundredweight a day poorer inside a shortage that is exactly the size it was on Monday morning, and the four launders at that node were measured at nine hundred, six hundred, twenty-five and twenty-five and they now measure nine hundred, six hundred, nothing and twenty-five, and the twenty-five is inside somebody's about and I said in a yard on the seventh of this month that I have never been able to make those two agree and am not going to settle it today, and that is still on the sheet.**"

"**And the whole of the shortage did not go up and did not come down. It is six hundred hundredweight a day, and it was six hundred hundredweight a day at the tenth hour of the morning of the Monday, and eleven people of Marden took a saw to a stone on the same afternoon at about the third hour of it, and it is six hundred hundredweight a day at the eleventh hour of this morning, and I have got both of those numbers in this yard this morning and about twenty-five people have heard them.**"

And then the woman who does the sums said it, and she said it in about four sentences and she did not raise her voice, and the fen entered that she is about fifty-one and that she has been stopping him in yards for about a month and that this is the third time and that the first two were a not known and a sentence about a village.

"**Then say the two numbers and stop talking about the cutting. Six hundred hundredweight a day is what that node is short. It was six hundred hundredweight a day on the Monday and it is six hundred hundredweight a day now. The twenty-five is in it and the twenty-five that is in it now is not the same twenty-five as the one that was in it before, and that is the whole of it, and it is a shorter sentence than yours and it is the one that is true.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that hers was the better one and that he was not going to say his again, and entered the reason, which is that a sentence of his that has been improved on in a yard by a woman with four books and nineteen years in them is a sentence he does not get to keep.

Then the standing of the month was read out, and it was read by the person who keeps the seed house and entered as it was read, in order, with the hour on each, and the reader of this body did not add to it and did not take anything off it.

> **THE SEVENTH COLUMN IS FORTY-TWO,** having stood at forty-one since the first of this month, and the door is read on the first and the thirtieth and the rule is unchanged and is not going to be. **IT CARRIES A HEADING AND THE OTHER SIX COLUMNS ON THAT DOOR DO NOT, AND THE SIX HAVE BEEN BLANK SINCE THE DOOR WAS MADE FOUR YEARS AGO, AND THIS HOLDING IS NOT WRITING THEM IN, AND ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE LOOKED AT THE SIX PLACES A HEADING OUGHT TO BE AND NOBODY ASKED.**
>
> **THE ROTA.** The draft rota for the Four Elbows went into force at the second hour of the morning of the twenty-second of this month. **IT HAS BEEN IN FORCE NINE MORNINGS. SEVEN OF THE NINE WERE TAKEN AND TWO OF THE NINE WERE NOT, AND BOTH OF THE TWO ARE THE FOURTH LINE, WHICH IS A LINE WITH NOBODY IN IT.** The second line came round at the second hour of this morning and was taken at the second hour, and that is the seventh of the seven. One of the two that were not was stopped by the body it belongs to under the ninth line, without saying why and without saying why before, on the twenty-fourth of this month, and nobody was to be blamed for a field that waited and nobody was blamed. The other was the fourth line on the twenty-eighth of this month, and there was nobody at the headland, and the fields waited, and it is entered by whoever enters anything with the day and the hour on it. **THE TWO MEN WHO CAME TO THAT KERB ON THE TWENTY-SECOND UNDER THE SIXTH LINE ARE NOT IN THE NINE AND ARE NOT TO BE PUT IN THE NINE**, and the reason is entered, which is that a turn that is not the turn of that morning is not a turn, and that the two of them did not begin because the fifth line of that sheet says a turn does not begin until the holder is on the ground and has said the word, and the sheet does not say what the word is, and both of them were entered by the people who failed, and one of those two is a man of about seventy-one whose name is nobody's. **The second line of that sheet was taken at the second hour on the twenty-second and the third line at the second hour on the twenty-third, and the man of seventy-one said the word on the second morning of those and not on the first.**
>
> **THE SHORTFALL, derived twice out loud and the two agreeing.** The node delivers about one thousand five hundred hundredweight a day and four bodies of households have counted about two thousand one hundred against it, and the difference is six hundred hundredweight a day. Six hundred at fourpence is two thousand four hundred pence, being ten pounds a day, and thirty days of it is eighteen thousand hundredweight at seventy-two thousand pence, being three hundred pounds a month. A pound is two hundred and forty pence. Two thousand four hundred pence over about fifteen hundred people is one and three-fifths of a penny a head a day, and three hundred pounds over about fifteen hundred is forty-eight pence a head a month, and those are the same figure. **THIS IS THE FIGURE THIS MORNING AND IT IS NOT A FIGURE THAT WENT UP AND IT IS NOT A FIGURE THAT CAME DOWN.**
>
> **THE LEAK.** Three-quarters of an inch, twice, two rules, on the twenty-first of a month five months back. Not measured this month, on a third reason, and no fourth reason was invented, and the fen entered that he had three and was not going to make a fourth for the sake of a month. No cause. No due date. No verdict.
>
> **THE BEAT.** Not logged since. Cause not established. Nothing underneath it. Column open. Its day is not restated and is not going to be, and the column was not opened today in front of twenty-five people and the not opening is a decision and not an oversight.
>
> **THE USE LOG AT FIFTEEN LINES,** standing at fifteen through a month in which a rota went into force and a word was not on a sheet and a launder was cut by a village on the eleventh of this month and a figure was read twice by somebody else's hand four hundred miles off. **The reason is entered so that nobody has to ask: a schedule is not a contact, a reading is not a contact, a launder cut by a village is not a contact, a not known is not a contact, a figure read twice by somebody else's hand is not a contact, and a day on which nine mornings of a rota are kept in a man's own book is not a contact.**
>
> **THE PATTERN.** The count of persons in this fen who can hold a pattern for four is **two**, both of this holding, and one of them is the engineer of record. It did not move in twenty days and no hold was made in this month by anybody. The count of persons in this fen who can hold a pattern for five is **one**, and it is him, and it did not move either.
>
> **THE ROOTMARK** is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice, there is no second mark, and the four accounts of the same arm in this holding's book are four and they reconcile none of one another, and nothing said about that arm in this month is a fifth.
>
> **THE WELL ON THE SHELF,** four hundred miles and four days off. **This holding has four figures and still does not have the tenth: the ninth, five feet; the eleventh, five feet; the twelfth, five feet; and the thirteenth, five feet, read on the sixteenth of this month and read out whole in a yard on the twentieth of this month and read back.** Four feet against the nine feet of the sixteenth of a month nine months back, and it is not decomposed, and the half inch of a month seven months back is not carried forward here. **And no entry anywhere for that well coming back.** The next ordinary reading is the fourth hour of the sixteenth of next month and the day is not entered in this yard today because the day belongs to that month and not to this one.
>
> **WHOSE ORDER IT IS** has been asked four times and answered never, and it was not asked a fifth time this month. **The standing count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is not said out loud by the reader of this body again in this season,** and the count of times the question that went into this holding's book on the second of this month has been asked is one, and the count of times it has been available to a room in this holding and not taken is three, and the three counts are three counts and nobody in this book has ever added one of them to another.
>
> **THE COMPOST LINE FOR THIS MONTH IS ENTERED ON THE FIRST OF THE THIRD MONTH AND IS NOT ENTERED TODAY,** and the day is entered and the reason is entered. **THE SIX OTHER STANDING ITEMS ARE NOT RE-COPIED, BECAUSE A STANDING ENTRY IS NOT IMPROVED ON BY BEING WRITTEN TWICE.** The man of about seventy was not fetched this month. The request count stands at fifty-three and has not moved since the first of this month.

Then the reader of this body said the thing about the eleven, and he said it in that yard and not in a book, and the fen entered that he said it in a yard and entered that nobody said anything about it.

"**I have been calling this the eleven days of a rota in my own book since the twenty-first of this month and it is nine mornings, and I am going to say that out loud in about twenty-five people and then I am going to enter why.**"

"**The eleven is a number I chose and not one I counted. I am not going to go and check it, and I am entering the reason, which is that a man who goes and checks the length of his own complaint has stopped complaining and started administering, and this holding has four hundred and eighty pounds of a figure about a person in a bin behind a house five days east and no administrator at all, and I would rather be wrong about a number in a book than right about it in a yard.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

And then a sheet came up the fen road at about the second hour of the afternoon with the ordinary post, one sheet, and the person who keeps the seed house took it off the top of the bag without opening it, looked at the embossed mark on the corner, put it face down on the table, and went out into the yard and said one sentence to about nine people.

"**That is the Assembly's paper, it is on the ordinary notice, it names the seventh of next month, and there is nothing in it that anybody has to answer today.**"

And the reader of this body read all of it out loud before anybody said a word about any part of it, and it is one sheet, and it is four lines and a schedule, and it does not name anybody, and nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything tender and the fen entered both of those.

> Entered by the clerk, at about the eleventh hour of the morning of the thirtieth of this month, at nine hundred yards off the seed house, on the standing of a month, and nothing added and nothing taken off: **the figures were read out whole in a yard of about twenty-five people and were entered as they were read, in order, with the hour on each. The standing rule was read out in full, out loud, before any of the figures were closed, and was not used, and the count of the uses of it this season is one and it went up on a draw that was not begun. THE DAY-MINUS RULES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER and the drawer was not opened. The boards stand at three hundred and fifty-four days. The March's tin stands at four hundred and one days. THE SEVENTH COLUMN IS FORTY-TWO. The other six columns on that door are blank and have been blank for four years and about four people looked at the six places a heading ought to be and nobody asked. THE ROTA HAS BEEN IN FORCE NINE MORNINGS AND SEVEN OF THE NINE WERE TAKEN AND TWO OF THE NINE WERE NOT, and both of the two are the fourth line, which is a line with nobody in it. The shortfall is six hundred hundredweight a day and it is not a figure that went up and it is not a figure that came down. The reader of this body said out loud in that yard that cutting a launder does not make water less scarce, and the woman who does the sums said the same thing in four sentences and shorter, and the reader of this body entered that hers was the better one and that he was not going to say his again. The reader of this body said out loud in that yard that he has been calling this the eleven days of a rota and that it is nine mornings, and that the eleven is a number he chose and did not count, and entered the reason. THE COMPOST LINE IS NOT ENTERED AND THE DAY IS ENTERED and the standing of the day and not the figure is three and is not reduced. THE COUNT OF PERSONS WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE IN TWENTY DAYS, and the count for five is one, and no hold was made in this month by anybody. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND NOTHING ENTERED ON IT. The count of requests that a man's initial be put against nothing is fifty-three. The man of about seventy was not fetched this month. The standing count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken was not said out loud. NO ENTRY ANYWHERE FOR THE WELL ON THE SHELF COMING BACK IS MADE AND NONE IS GOING TO BE, and the wall at Cauldron Reach holds twelve figures and one gap and was not added to and was not taken from. Nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything tender.**
