# Chapter 228

## The Terms Of A Refusal

The twenty-third of this month was a Saturday and the third line of that sheet came round at the second hour of the morning, and the man of it is seventy-one and he is not in this book and his name is nobody's, and he was on the ground at the west end of the kerb with one man and a barrow.

And the reader of this body had the word in his own book by then, written on the page over the day before, and he had not said it in a yard since.

He said it at the kerb at about the second hour of the morning, to the man of seventy-one and to the one man with him, and the man of seventy-one said it back to him once to be sure of it, and the turn was taken, and the fen entered the hour and did not enter why he had waited a day to say it, and then entered the why in his own book that night and it is nine words long and says that a word in one man's book is a word that dies with the book.And a seed-route party went by on the cart road at about the third hour of the morning of the same Saturday, going the other way, eleven of them and two carts and about four hundredweight of boards in the second one, and Tova Reed was in the second cart and got down into the road to get at a wheel that had picked up a stone, and she did not come into the yard and she was not asked into the yard.

The reader of this body went out to the road and she said the first thing and it was about the boards.

"**These are eleven days out and they are late against a road I have not got a figure for, and I am not going to give you the figure in a road, and you would not want it in a road.**"

And the reader of this body said the one question he had, and he gave the reason first, which is that she had said in a yard in front of about eleven people that she was out eleven days in a season of twenty and that this was one of the eleven, and that a rota with a fourth line in it and nobody in the fourth line was the reason she had said no, and that he was not asking her again.

And she said no before he had finished and she said it while crouched at a wheel with her hand in the mud, and the fen entered that she did not soften it and entered that he had not asked twice and entered that the count of asks he has made of her in this book's history is one.

"**No, and the reason is the same one and I have not improved on it. And I will give you the half of it that is work. A body that is four turns and a fourth line with nobody in it has to get through a morning with one person gone, and if the one person who gets through it is a person from a fen then the fen is the rota, and a rota that is one holding is not a rota, and I said that in a yard and I am saying it to a wheel.**"

"**And the boards go west on Thursday and there are four hundredweight of them, and if your Marden is turning a field on the first of next month then a cart is a fortnight gone from that hill. I am telling you that as a person with four hundredweight on a cart and not as a favour.**"

And she got back in the cart and the cart went on west and the reader of this body did not thank her and did not offer to lift anything and entered that he had not, and entered the reason, which is that a man who lifts a box for a woman on a road has made the box a thing he was near.

And the next morning, the twenty-fourth, a Sunday, before anybody had asked him for anything, the reader of this body read the word out in a yard at Errow Reach in front of about eleven people and wrote it on the page in his own book with the day on it, and gave the reason for it out loud before he gave the word.

"**I have had this since Thursday and I gave it to one man on Friday at a gate and kept it off six men on a hill, and a field waited for it, and the field is entered in a yard in about twenty-six-year-old handwriting this holding has never seen before. So here it is, said out loud, in about eleven people, and I am going to say what that costs and the cost is mine and not anybody else's. It costs that a word four villages are going to work on is in my book. If I am in a hole at about the second hour of a morning then the word is gone, and the man who wrote the sheet is five days west and cannot be got, and that is what a schedule with a person in it instead of a letter in it looks like from the inside.**"

"**And I am not going to put it in his book because it is not his book, and I am not going to put it on that sheet because that sheet is not mine and because I have said out loud in a yard that I am not going to touch it. So the word is in one book, in one hand, in one holding, and it is going to be in four hands by tonight and in four mouths by the end of the week, and four hands is not a document.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the count of things this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is three and is not four, and entered the reason, which is that the first two were sums done in a yard and that this one was not arithmetic.

Then about the fourth hour of the afternoon of that Sunday a man came up the cart road on foot with two others and no bags, and the person who keeps the seed house was not in that yard and the man of about fifty came out and wrote down what was said and read it back and nobody improved it.

Soren Rill, of about thirty-five, of an Assembly in the Thornwild, said the standing form first, word for word, and the fen entered that he said it word for word and that he has said it four times in this book's history and that a man who has learned a standing form and uses it exactly is a man who is going to be difficult and that is not a thing anybody in this yard is thanked for.

Then he said what he had come for, and it took him about nine minutes, and he said it standing at the gate with his coat on, and the reader of this body did not ask him one question and did not interrupt him once.

"**I am not here about the rota and I have not come to be on it and I said so in a yard in this fen before and I am saying it now so that nobody in this yard has to wonder whether I have changed my mind. I have not.**"

"**I am here about one question and about the terms on which I will not put it.**"

And then he gave the terms, and he gave them in the order he intended them and he did not soften them and he did not thank anybody and the reader of this body did not thank him and the fen entered that neither of those two things happened and that it is the first time in this fen that a refusal arrived with a schedule attached to it.

"**One. I will not say it in a yard. A yard is about eleven people and about nine of them have come to get something, and the last thing anybody said in a yard in this fen went into four villages in four copies in five days, and I am not putting this one into four copies.**"

"**Two. I will carry one question. Not yours. One of theirs. Yours is a question about your own rota and the fourth line of it has nobody in it, and a question a man puts about his own rota is a question about the man, and the man reads his own question in the answer and learns nothing. Theirs goes from a room their own clerks are in. I will name the day. They cannot move the day.**"

"**Three. I will not see the answer first. It goes to the four villages and not to me. If it comes back to me first then I have a copy of it, and a copy is a thing I would have to keep, and I am not going to keep a copy of anything about that ground.**"

"**Four, and this is the one that is the cost, and it is mine and I am naming it out loud because the other three are cheap. Nineteen miles to that room and two nights, and I am out on other work for a season, and the answer to one question is the answer to one question. It will not be a finding. It will be a thing said by whoever is in the room on the day, and it will be about the question and not about the ground, and there will not be a second occasion, because the only person who could put the same question again is a man in a fen who has said out loud he is not going to ask twice.**"

"**And then the man in the fen will be the only one in this holding who has spent a season of a working man's year on a question that is not his, and he is not the one who will have to live in four villages afterwards. That is the cost. I have named it and I am not going to improve on it and I am not going to be thanked for it, and if anybody in this yard thanks me it will be a mistake and I will correct it.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, and entered that he had not said thank you and was not going to and entered the reason, which is that a man who is thanked for refusing you in a gate has been paid and the payment is that the next refusal costs more.

Then the reader of this body said the one thing he had gone into the fen road to have said, and he gave the reason for asking before he asked it, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty.

"**Then somebody has to go to that room, because the question is theirs and the carrying of it is theirs, and it is not mine and I am not going to carry it. I am not asking you who. I am telling you that somebody is going to have to spend two nights and a day on it and that it is not going to cost one of your four bodies nine pounds ten and it is going to cost one of them a day.**"

And Soren Rill said the second part of it and he said it before he was asked, which the fen entered.

"**That is right and I knew it before I came and I did not name it in the four terms because the four terms are about me and this is not about me. It is the correct use of a refusal and it is going to be expensive for somebody, and I am not going to pretend I did not know that and I am not going to be the one who says who.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

And then the man of the north row with the cough, who had been in that yard since about the ninth hour of the morning with a basket of withy cuttings he had not been asked to bring, said one sentence, and the fen entered that it was the first thing he had said in a yard in this holding's history that was about the arithmetic of a thing he was not part of.

"**Then it is Havergate and it is Marden and it is not Silling and it is not Low Wether, and that is because Havergate has a hundred and forty-four doors and Marden has a count at a gate, and a count at a gate is the only one of the four numbers that a room in Fennmere has ever asked anybody for, and I have been keeping that sheet for eleven years and I am not going to be asked to say whose turn it is by two men from a fen in a yard.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he had thought the same thing on the road and had not said it.

Then at about the eighth hour of the evening of the Sunday, before he went, the man of about thirty-one of Silling came up the cart road with nine other people behind him, and he said his standing sentences and then said the fourth line had been stopped, and the reader of this body did not understand the sentence at first and then did.

"**Marden has stopped its own turn for this morning under the ninth line of that sheet. Without saying why and without saying why before, which is the ninth line, and it is theirs to stop, and nobody is to blame them and I am not going to ask.**"

And the man of about thirty-one said the thing about the seventh line, and he said it in a yard and did not look at the reader of this body while he said it.

"**The fields waited this morning. It goes in the book with the day and the hour on it and nobody is to be named, and the fourth line of that sheet is empty and it was empty this morning and it is empty now, and a body of households has just used the ninth line to say so in a yard, and I have written it down, and I did not ask and I am not going to.**"

And then the fen entered what he was not going to do, and he entered it in his own book that night, and it is the first night he has written nothing in for about four years.

*Two mornings. Friday the turn did not begin because I gave a word to one man at a gate instead of a man of seventy-one on a kerb and six men on a hill. Sunday the fourth line was not taken and the body it belongs to stopped it themselves under the ninth line and told nobody why, and they were right to, and I am not going to write down a reason for it. There is no reason on that page from me. There is a day and an hour and a body of households exercising a line that a man of sixty-eight wrote in my cart shed in about four minutes, and it is the best thing that has happened to that sheet and I was not in the room.*

And before he went, the man of about thirty-one of Silling asked for a sheet, and the man of about fifty gave him one, and the reader of this body did not ask to read it before the man of about thirty-one had read it out himself, and entered that he did not ask.

> Entered by Sennik Vaul, of about thirty-one, of Silling, one of seven that decide and the one who was sent, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-fourth of this month, in a yard at Errow Reach, on the standing of a turn that was stopped, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and is five days west by the cart road, and not by the engineer of record, who has a page of his own and did not ask for this one: **THE FOURTH LINE OF THAT SHEET CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE MORNING OF THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF THIS MONTH AND MARDEN HAS STOPPED THAT TURN UNDER THE NINTH LINE, without saying why and without saying why before, which is what the ninth line is for, and the turn is theirs to stop and nobody is to ask them. THE FIELDS WAITED AND THAT GOES IN WITH THE DAY AND THE HOUR ON IT AND NOBODY IS TO BE NAMED. I asked the seven on Saturday and the seven gave it to me and did not tell me a reason and I did not ask them for one. THE SECOND LINE WAS TAKEN AT THE SECOND HOUR ON THE TWENTY-THIRD AND THE THIRD LINE ON THE SAME MORNING, and the man of seventy-one said the word. A MAN OF THIRTY-FIVE OF AN ASSEMBLY CAME UP THIS ROAD ON THE AFTERNOON OF THIS DAY AND REFUSED FOUR THINGS IN A GATE AND NAMED THE COST OF THE REFUSAL OUT LOUD BEFORE HE AGREED TO IT, and the cost of it is nineteen miles and two nights and a season of a working man's year, and he named it as his, and he is not a body of households and he is not the person this holding sent anywhere. A READER OF THIS BODY WALKED INTO THIS YARD AND SAID THAT SOMEBODY WOULD HAVE TO SPEND A DAY ON IT AND HE DID NOT ASK WHO, and I did not ask him who either. Nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything.**

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything tender and the fen entered both of those.
