## A NOTE ON PROSE

**Sentence order is thing, then the figure, then what the figure means for a person. Documents are block quotes. Spoken sentences are bold inside straight quotation marks. Do not summarize. Do not list. Do not let him apologise and do not let him be forgiven, because there is nothing to forgive: a man measured a thing correctly and did not measure it twice, and a village acted on it, and the loss is real, and the loss is not his fault and is his.**
