# Chapter 216

## The Thirtieth

And first the twenty-ninth, because the fen said out loud in a yard on the twenty-fifth that he would be in a field at the second hour of that morning, and he was, and he did not hold anything and he did not touch the ground.

A man of about sixty-one of Low Wether was on the headland above the ditch with about four people and a barrow each, and the ditch was nine feet south and about a hundred yards long and it was going in, and the water was standing in the cut at the top end and not at the bottom end, and the fen looked at it for a while and said one thing out loud to nobody in particular.

"**That is not a hold. That is a ditch that has not found its level yet, and it will not have found it by dark, and the reason it is standing at the top end is the ground and not the cut. I told a yard on the twenty-fifth that I did not know whether a pattern went a hundred yards from that kerb or six hundred, and I am not going to take the hundred yards back today because the water has done something I can see.**"

The man of about sixty-one said he had not asked him to take it back, and the fen entered that he had not been asked and that he would have refused to give it.

Then he wrote down the day and the hour and what it looked like, in his own hand, on the back of a sheet, and he did not write down a cause, and the man of about sixty-one read it and kept it, and nobody thanked anybody, and it went into this holding's book on the thirtieth with the hour on it and nothing on it about what it means, and the fen entered the reason, which is that a not known is a real thing and is not neutral, and he said that out loud in a yard on the twenty-fifth and he is not going to improve on it in a book.

And the twenty-ninth was a Wednesday and the thirtieth was a Thursday, and the door was read at nine hundred yards off at about the tenth hour of the thirtieth in a full yard of about twenty-five people, by the person who keeps the seed house, with the reader of this body on one side of her and the man of about fifty on the other, and the seventh column went to **forty**, and the rule did not change and is not going to be.

The day-minus figures were read out loud by the clerk before either number, which nobody has asked him for on any day he has done it, and the boards stood at **three hundred and twenty-four days** and the March's tin at **three hundred and seventy-one days**, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be.

And before any of that, in the same yard, the standing rule was read out in full, out loud, by the person who keeps the seed house, in front of about twenty-five people, because a rule read only when it is used is a habit and this one is not, and all four of its lines are about a stop, and the third of them does not say in a yard and this body read it as though it did for two years.

> **THE STANDING FIGURES OF THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH, read out in a yard at nine hundred yards off the seed house at about the eleventh hour of the morning of the thirtieth, in a full yard of about twenty-five people, and entered as they were read, in order, with the hour on each.**
>
> **THE SEVENTH COLUMN IS FORTY,** having stood at thirty-nine since the first of this month, and the door is read on the first and the thirtieth and the rule is unchanged and is not going to be. **IT CARRIES A HEADING AND THE OTHER SIX COLUMNS ON THAT DOOR DO NOT, AND THE SIX HAVE BEEN BLANK SINCE THE DOOR WAS MADE FOUR YEARS AGO, AND THIS HOLDING IS NOT WRITING THEM IN, AND NOBODY IN THAT YARD ASKED.**
>
> **THE COMPOST LINE FOR THIS MONTH IS NOT ENTERED AND THE REASON IS ENTERED.** The line falls on the first of next month, which is a Friday. This holding does not know what that figure is. **The day is entered and the figure is not, and the standing of the day and not the figure is three and is not reduced.** The word before the figure last month was EMPTIER. **WHAT IS ON THE RECORD AND IS NOT YET ON THE LINE: the woman who does the sums has carried fifty hundredweight a month out of that heap by hand for forty-eight months, at four shillings the hundredweight, being two thousand four hundred hundredweight and four hundred and eighty pounds, being six times the line and thirty pounds a household across sixteen. THE LINE HAS THEREFORE BEEN FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY HUNDREDWEIGHT SHORT A MONTH IN FACT FOR FOUR YEARS, being ninety pounds a month and not eighty, and it will be re-entered on the first of next month with the eighty underneath it and the ten beside it, and the reason, and the hour, and the reason is a woman and a barrow and a book nobody has ever seen. IT IS A FIGURE ABOUT A PERSON AND NOT A FIGURE ABOUT A HEAP.**
>
> **THE SHORTFALL, derived twice out loud and the two agreeing:** the node delivers about one thousand five hundred hundredweight a day and four bodies of households have counted about two thousand one hundred against it, and the difference is six hundred hundredweight a day. Six hundred at fourpence is two thousand four hundred pence, being ten pounds a day, and thirty days of it is eighteen thousand hundredweight at seventy-two thousand pence, being three hundred pounds a month. A pound is two hundred and forty pence. Two thousand four hundred pence over about fifteen hundred people is one and three-fifths of a penny a head a day, and three hundred pounds over about fifteen hundred is forty-eight pence a head a month, and those are the same figure. **FIFTY HUNDREDWEIGHT A DAY IS WHAT MARDEN AND SILLING HAVE BEEN LIVING ON AND THEIR LINES HAVE BEEN BLANK FOR ELEVEN YEARS**, being six thousand pence a month, being twelve pence a head a month across about five hundred people. **THIS HOLDING IS NOT PAYING THAT BILL AND IS NOT GOING TO SAY ON THE THENTIETH OF A MONTH THAT IT IS.**
> **THE LEAK.** Three-quarters of an inch, twice, two rules, on the twenty-first of a month four months back. Not measured this month, on a third reason, and no fourth reason was invented, and the fen entered that he had three and was not going to make a fourth for the sake of a month. No cause. No due date. No verdict.
>
> **THE BEAT.** Not logged since. Cause not established. Nothing underneath it. Column open. Its day is not restated and is not going to be, and the column was not opened today in front of twenty-five people and the not opening is a decision and not an oversight.
>
> **THE DAY-MINUS FIGURES** were read out loud by the clerk before either number and the drawer was not opened. The boards, three hundred and twenty-four days. The March's tin, three hundred and seventy-one days, unmoved, on a bed at three and a half degrees.
>
> **THE USE LOG AT FIFTEEN LINES,** the fifteenth a contact on a boarded floor, standing at fifteen through a month in which five people walked five days east, a man held a pattern for five, and a woman carried fifty hundredweight a month out of a bin for four years. **The reason is entered so that nobody has to ask: a schedule is not a contact, a reading is not a contact, a form in a press four miles off is not a contact, a barrow of leaf mould is not a contact, a gate count is not a contact, and a hold is not a contact.**
>
> **THE PATTERN.** The count of persons in this fen who can hold a pattern for four is **two**, both of this holding, and one of them is the engineer of record. It did not move in twenty-nine days. **The reasons are two and they are not the same reason: a man of sixty-eight offered, was asked, held a turn for about two hours, said the word at the two-hour mark, and the count of a person who can hold a pattern for four is a count of persons and not a count of holds; and a seedwright of the trust said no in a yard of about eleven people in daylight for a reason about distance and not about a man, and that refusal has not been improved on by anybody.** A new count begins at one: the count of persons in this fen who can hold a pattern for five is **one**, and it started on the twenty-third of this month at about the second hour of the afternoon, and a new count of one is the easiest thing in this book to be proud of and it is entered in the same line as the count of two on purpose.
>
> **THE ROOTMARK** is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice, there is no second mark, the burn that ran past it into the elbow was not a mark, the mark was hot on the twenty-third of this month and is not a mark, and the headache that went with it is not the mark and is not entered as the mark. **The four accounts of the same arm in this book are four and they reconcile none of one another, and nothing said about this arm this month is a fifth.**
>
> **THE WELL ON THE SHELF,** four hundred miles and four days off. **This holding has three figures and still does not have the fourth: the ninth, the sixteenth of a month three months back, five feet; the eleventh, the sixteenth of last month, five feet; and the twelfth, the sixteenth of this month, five feet, which came down the four-day road at that household's own cost and was read out whole in a yard and read back. THE TENTH IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND WAS NEVER ASKED FOR TWICE,** and a woman four hundred miles off has said in her own hand that she is not going to supply it, and she is right. Three figures the same figure three month running does not tell anybody whether the well has settled or has stopped. Four feet against the nine feet of the sixteenth of a month eight months back, and it is not decomposed. **And no entry anywhere for that well coming back.**
>
> **THE NODE.** Four bodies of households and one fifth share held by one person on the ninth day of every ninth day, eleven years and eight days, never paid, never asked, and the return on the nineteenth was ninety hundredweight weighed on a steelyard and read back by four bodies of households for the first time in eleven years, and on the twenty-eighth a name went into the first rule of the schedule with a witness against it. **THE COUNT OF BODIES AT THE NODE IS FIVE AND HAS NOT MOVED.**
>
> **WHOSE ORDER IT IS** has been asked four times and answered never, and it was not asked a fifth time this month. **The standing count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is nine.** The count of times the question that went into this holding's book on the second of this month has been asked is one, and the count of times it has been available to a room in this holding and not taken is three, and the three counts are three counts and nobody in this book has ever added one of them to another.
>
> **THE SEVEN OTHER STANDING ITEMS ARE NOT RE-COPIED, BECAUSE A STANDING ENTRY IS NOT IMPROVED ON BY BEING WRITTEN TWICE. The corridor, the vault and its two names, the man with a spade, the day letter, the form on the bench and the quarter's number of four hundred and forty-one were all read out in that yard, unchanged, in order, with the hour on each, and not one of them moved this month. ONE OF THE SEVEN IS THE ONLY ONE THAT IS NEW: THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED THIS MONTH.** He was fetched for the seventeenth time on the first of this month and the eighteenth time falls on the first of next month, which is a Friday, and this holding did not fetch him twice in a month and did not write and ask why not.

> Entered at about the eleventh hour of the morning of the thirtieth of this month, at nine hundred yards off the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a month, and nothing added and nothing taken off: **the figures were read out whole in a yard of about twenty-five people and were entered as they were read, in order, with the hour on each. The day-minus rules were read out loud before either number and the drawer was not opened. The seventh column is forty. The compost line for this month is not entered and the reason is entered, and the standing of the day and not the figure is three and is not reduced. The count of requests that a man's initial be put against nothing is fifty-three. The count of the man of about seventy stands at seventeen and he was not fetched this month. Nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything tender.**

Nobody thanked anybody, and the count of requests that a man's initial be put against nothing is fifty-three, and the fen entered that a request is not a count of gratitude and that the two have never been added together in this holding's book.

And then, underneath the month's work, dated the thirtieth of this month, in the hand of the engineer of record and read back before it was initialled, is the fifty-second note under section nine of the charter schedule. There is no charter, and the note says so on its face, and the reader of this body wrote none in the middle of the twenty-six days he was away and entered the reason, which is that a note about a document he did not write is a way of putting his name on it, and that a note written on the last day is a note about the twenty-six days, and that is the only one worth writing.

*Five days east and five days back. A schedule of four sheets with two rules on it and eleven years of figures in the wrong one, brought to a yard at about the tenth hour at the holder's own cost and at the hour he had named eleven days before. A return of ninety hundredweight weighed on a steelyard and read back by four bodies of households, and a name in the first rule with a witness against it, and the first rule had been blank on every line for eleven years. Four columns with headings on them and the third one is a count of wanting, and I offered that village its column back blank and a body of households refused it in its own words, on the ground that a heading that is true about a bad year is still a true heading.*

*A turn held for two hours by a man of sixty-eight who said the word at the two-hour mark, entered in his own hand, and the cost in it is worse than mine and he said so himself. A hold for five across two branches three hundred yards apart, with four people working and a licensed man standing in the middle of it who has told this party for ten days that his bead cannot read a hold and went into one anyway. The count of four did not move. The count of five is one and I am entering it in the same line.*

*Four hundred and eighty pounds out of a bin behind a woman's own house, fifty hundredweight a month for forty-eight months, carried by hand at the hour of the carrying, in a book nobody has ever seen, and I said I in a yard on the first of this month and it was not me. The line is not re-entered today and will be on the first of next month at ninety pounds with the eighty underneath it.*

*A quarter of a mile of ground walked twice and not measured at all, and a body of households given a not known from me in a yard of about eleven people which took four minutes, and a village has decided to put a ditch through it anyway, which is the correct use of a not known and the only use there is.*

*A question of a man of sixty-eight in a yard in daylight, and nobody took it, and the standing count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is nine and the question in that book has now been available and not taken three times, and those are two counts and I have not added them. Six columns on that door have been blank for four years and this holding is not writing them in. The man of about seventy was not fetched this month and the eighteenth time is tomorrow.*

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything tender and the fen entered both of those, and entered that the count of times he has caught himself and not said a thing in this month's history is twenty-one, and that the sentence about a man who is the only person who can do a thing being a bottleneck and not a system has been said in this fen four times and is not coming down.

And then, at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, the man of the north row with the cough, who was at the wall and who had been in a yard three times in this month, being the nineteenth and the twenty-seventh and this, and who had not been asked to be in any of the three, said one sentence out loud in front of about twenty-five people, and the fen entered it and did not answer it and did not improve it.

"**Then somebody write the number down. The two. It is the only figure in this holding's book that has not moved in four weeks and it is the figure that decides whether there is a rota at all, and I am sixty-eight, and I said the word at the two-hour mark on the twenty-second, and I have got about four of them left in me and after that I am not going to be able to stand at that kerb at the second hour of a morning. Write it down. Nobody else is going to write it down. It is not a complaint. It is a count, and a count that is not written down is not a count.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he was not going to answer it, and entered the reason, which is that the answer to it is his own name and that he has had it in his hand since the twenty-second and is not going to write it on the thirtieth of a month in a yard of about twenty-five people.

And the count of bodies at the node is five. And the count of persons in this fen who can hold a pattern for four is two. And both of those went in on the thirtieth of the first month, with the hour on them, and were read back out loud in front of the people they were about, and nobody thanked anybody, and a man of about seventy was not fetched, and a door nine hundred yards off has six columns on it that have been blank for four years and a seventh that says forty, and this holding is not writing the six.
