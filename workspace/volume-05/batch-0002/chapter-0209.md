# Chapter 209

## The Figure That Came Down The Road

The twentieth of this month was a Monday and the ordinary post came up the cart road to Errow Reach at about the ninth hour of the morning, and there was one thing in the bag and the bag came out of the same leather as everything else that has arrived at this holding for four years, and the thing in it was four folded lines and it had come four days up a four-day road from a yard of four households that this holding has never stood in and has never sent anybody to.

About nine people were in the yard. The reader of this body took it out and did not open it and put it on the table flat and stood back, and the man of about fifty put his hand on it and did not open it either, and the fen entered both of those.

Hesta Lyle said the standing before anybody spoke, and it was the fourth time in twenty days that she had said one at that table and the count is entered.

"**I read out what is on a piece of paper in a yard when the yard asks to hear it read, and I read it out in the hand it was written in and not in mine, and I am not going to explain it and I am not going to be asked to explain it.**"

Then she opened it, and she read the four lines out, and she read them as they were, and the man of about fifty wrote them down and read them back before the reader of this body said anything.

> **THE FOUR LINES, read out in a yard at Errow Reach at about the ninth hour of the morning of the twentieth of this month by the woman who keeps the four books, in the hand they were written in, and read back, and entered whole and not summarized:**
>
> **THE WELL AT CAULDRON REACH. SIXTEENTH OF THIS MONTH. FOURTH HOUR. FIVE FEET.**
>
> **WROTE IT BECAUSE A BODY FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF SPENT A MONTH FINDING OUT IT WAS AIMING AT THE WRONG DAY, AND I AM NOT GOING TO BE HANDED A NUMBER FOR A MONTH I DID NOT READ BY A WOMAN IT HAS NEVER MET. THE GAP IS ON THE WALL WHERE THE GAP IS. I HAVE PUT CHALK UNDER IT SO YOU CAN SEE I KNOW IT IS THERE.**
>
> **A MONTH BACK THERE WAS HALF AN INCH. IT IS STILL HERE IN CHALK ON THE STONE BY THE DOOR AND IT IS NOT A READING AND IT IS NOT IN THE COLUMN AND I AM NOT ADDING IT UP.**
>
> **PAID AT THIS HOUSEHOLD'S OWN COST AND NOBODY ELSE'S, AND NOBODY HAS EVER OFFERED.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

Then the man of about fifty read the four lines back, and the reader of this body checked the reading back against the paper, and it was not the same twice, and he said one word.

"**Again.**"

And the man of about fifty read it again and it was right the second time, and the fen entered the difference and said nothing about it at the time and is setting it down now, because the difference was one word and the word was CHALK, and a man who has read out loud in the yards of this holding for four years dropped a word out of a thing he had heard once, and the reader of this body let it go past at the ninth hour of the morning because there were about nine people standing there and because the word was not a figure.

And then the reader of this body said the hour out loud, and the man of about fifty wrote the hour on it, and the hour went on it.

"**The fourth hour of the morning of the sixteenth, and the ninth hour of the morning of the twentieth, and four days. That is the same road and the same four days it has been for eleven years and it is a road and I am not going to say anything about it tonight except that a four-day road is a road and not an opinion.**"

Then he read the figure against what this holding already had, and he did it out loud, and the woman who does the sums had the two earlier figures in her own hand off the wall of the fen's own book before he asked for them.

"**Five feet, on the sixteenth of a month three months back. Five feet, on the sixteenth of last month, which came into this building on the twentieth of last month by this same road in the same bag. And five feet, on the sixteenth of this month, which came into a yard at Errow Reach this morning by the same road in the same bag.**"

"**Three figures, the same figure, three month running, and the ninth and the eleventh and the twelfth are the same figure, and I am entering that in the plainest words I have got and I am not going to improve on it, because the first thing a man does with a figure that has come back the same twice is to say it is holding, and I do not know that, and there is no way on this page to tell the difference between a thing that has settled and a thing that has stopped.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

Then Sennik Vaul of Silling asked the question that four bodies of households have every right to ask and nobody had asked, and the fen did not stop it and gave the reason for answering before he answered, and the count of that is eight in this month's history and the count is entered.

"**I am going to answer that one because I am the only person in this yard who has ever been asked it and I have been waiting eleven days to see whether anybody would.**"

"**You are going to ask why a holding with four pounds and fivepence in a box, a compost line of eighty pounds a month and no charter, has kept a figure on a well four hundred miles off for four years. Here is the reason and it is not a good one. This holding has had four years of a book in which every single time that anybody did not know something, the not-knowing is written down with the day and the hour on it. That is the whole of what this holding is. It is a book of things this holding did not know.**"

"**And a well on a shelf four hundred miles off is the largest single thing this holding has never known. In four years the count of things this holding has not known is a number I could put in a book, and that number is standing outside it going down into the ground four times a year on a day that is always the sixteenth, and if I stop writing it down then the count gets smaller and it gets smaller because I stopped, and that is the one thing in four years I have not been prepared to do.**"

Nothing was said and the fen entered that.

Then Hesta Lyle said one thing about the fourth line, and she said it to the paper and not to the yard, and the fen entered that she was not thanked for it.

"**Nobody has ever offered. I have kept four books for nineteen years and I have been sent for about nine of them and in every single one of those nine somebody carried something up a road and nobody paid them and nobody asked them to and nobody wrote it down. That is not a complaint and it is not a favour and it is not going to be a line in anybody's book. It is the ninth time somebody has done a thing to a body of households for nothing and the fourth of those has been done by a man who has never once been asked whether he wanted to.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he was not going to answer that, and entered the reason, which is that a body of households which is told that its own conduct is admirable has been given a bill.

And then the reader of this body did the rest of it in the ordinary voice without sitting down, and the man of about fifty wrote all of it and read it back.

"**What this holding has. The ninth, five feet. The eleventh, five feet. The twelfth, five feet. What this holding has not got, and has never got, and did not write and ask for, and is not going to: the tenth. The month between the ninth and the eleventh is a month this holding has no figure for and there is no page in this book that says what it was and there is not going to be one, and a woman four hundred miles off has told us in her own hand that she is not going to give it to us, and she is right, and I am not going to write a letter about it.**"

"**Four feet against the nine feet of the sixteenth of a month eight months back. That is the whole of the shortfall and it is not broken down. It is not broken down on her wall and it is not broken down in this yard and it is not going to be broken down in this book, and a figure that is not broken down can still be read and a figure that has been broken down by a man with a stake in it usually cannot.**"

"**The half inch of a month six months back is on her wall in chalk on a stone by a door and she has told us in writing that it is not a reading and is not in the column and that she is not adding it up. It is not carried forward here either. It is not in this book and it is not on the well house door nine hundred yards from where I sleep and it is not going to be, and there are two reasons for that and one of them is that it is not mine to carry and the other one is that I do not know what it is.**"

"**And the standing of the day and not the figure is three. It was three on the seventeenth when I entered the sixteenth and did not write and ask. It is three this morning with the twelfth figure in my hand. It does not come down and it does not go up, and I have stopped trying to make it come down, because a man who counts how many times he has not been told something is a man who is building a case and this holding does not have a case.**"

"**And there is no entry anywhere for that well coming back. There is no entry for the ground under it improving, and none for the water in it being better, and none for the woman who reads it being wrong, and no page of this holding's book is going to make one.**"

> Entered by the engineer of record, in his own hand, at about the eleventh hour of the morning of the twentieth of this month, in a yard at Errow Reach, on the standing of a reading, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and is five days east by the cart road: **four folded lines came up the cart road in the ordinary post at about the ninth hour of the morning of the twentieth, being the fourth hour of the morning of the sixteenth at a yard of four households four hundred miles off, at that household's own cost, on a four-day road, and the hour of the reading and the hour of the arrival and the four days are all entered. The four lines were read out whole in a yard of about nine people in the hand they were written in and read back and are not summarized here. THE FIGURE IS FIVE FEET. It is the same figure as the ninth, of the sixteenth of a month three months back, and as the eleventh, of the sixteenth of last month, and this holding now has three figures and is still without the tenth, and the month between the ninth and the eleventh is on no page in this book and the reader of this body said out loud that he is not going to write a letter about it. The reader of this body entered that three figures the same figure three month running does not tell anybody whether the well has settled or has stopped and that there is no way on this page to tell the difference. The shortfall is four feet against the nine feet of the sixteenth of a month eight months back and is NOT DECOMPOSED. The half inch of a month six months back is on that wall in chalk and is not carried forward as a figure here. The standing of the day and not the figure is three and is not reduced, and there is no entry anywhere for that well coming back. The fourth line of the sheet states that the cost of the road is at that household's own cost and that nobody has ever offered. Nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything.**

And at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one sentence to the reader of this body on the way out of the yard, and the fen entered the hour of it and entered the sentence and did not enter why, and the count of things said to the reader of this body on the way out of a yard in this month's history is four and the count is entered.

"**It is the same figure three times. My father counted our own well in the year he died and got the same number three times and told anybody who asked that it was holding, and the well went in the fourth month. I am not saying it about yours. I am saying that I have been in this yard all morning with a number in my head that I got from my father, and I would like to put it down somewhere, and I do not know whose book it goes in.**"

Nobody answered her and the fen entered that nobody answered her and entered that he was not going to, and entered the reason, which is that the reason is that a body of households that has been handed a number by a man who reads wells for a living will keep it, and that this holding does not hand out numbers it cannot check, and that the count of numbers this holding has handed out without being able to check them is one, and the fen entered that he knows which one it is and did not enter what it is. And he entered the count of things said to the reader of this body on the way out of a yard in this month's history as five, and did not improve on that either.
