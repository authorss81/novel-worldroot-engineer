# Chapter 245

## The Two Figures

The twenty-ninth of this month was a Sunday and the third line came round at the second hour and was taken at the second hour by a man of about seventy-one of Silling who came with one man and a barrow, as he has come for nine years, and who said the word without being told it, and who was not thanked and was not asked for his name, and the fen entered all three of those and entered that the count of times that man has been asked for his name is three and that he has not given it three times.

And the thirtieth of this month was a Monday, and the standing for the month was read out at about the eleventh hour of the morning at nine hundred yards off the seed house, in a yard, in about twenty-five people, by the person who keeps the seed house, and the reader of this body said his own back at the end of it and added nothing and took nothing off.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

> **THE STANDING FOR THE THIRTIETH OF THE THIRD MONTH, read out at about the eleventh hour of the morning, at nine hundred yards off, in about twenty-five people, by the person who keeps the seed house, entered as it was read.**
>
> **THE SEVENTH COLUMN IS FORTY-THREE.** Read on the first and read on the thirtieth, because the door is read on the first and the thirtieth and the rule did not change and is not going to. **THE OTHER SIX COLUMNS ARE BLANK AND HAVE BEEN FOR FOUR YEARS AND NOBODY ASKED.**
>
> **THE DAY-MINUS RULES.** The boards stand at three hundred and eighty-four days and the March's tin at four hundred and thirty-one, and both were read out loud before either number, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be.
>
> **THE COMPOST LINE FOR THIS MONTH IS NOT ENTERED AND THE REASON IS ENTERED.** It falls on the first of next month. It will be one hundred pounds and the word PAID and the run at fifteen not discharged in fifteen. This holding is not going to write a figure on the thirtieth for a line that falls on the first and call it an entry.
>
> **THE LEAK** is three-quarters of an inch, twice measured with two rules, and it was not measured this month on a third reason, and no fourth reason was invented. No cause. No due date. No verdict. **THE BEAT** is not logged, cause not established, column open, and its day is not restated, and the column was not opened in front of twenty-five people this morning and the not opening is a decision.
>
> **THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES.** A crate is not a contact. A bead laid against a man of sixty-eight's arm is not a contact. Four days of a road in each direction is not a contact. A covenant of six lines is not a contact. A day and a night on a stone is not a contact. **A THING HAPPENING IS NOT A CONTACT, AND A PERSON WHO WANTS A SIXTEEN LINE IS A PERSON WHO WANTS SOMEBODY ELSE TO HAVE CARRIED IT.**
>
> **THE STANDING RULE** was not used this month. Four lines, every one of them about a stop, about calling a stop, or about who is standing there when it is called. The count of its uses last season was two and the count this season is one, and the one was a draw that was not begun, and the two counts are two counts.
>
> **THE SHORTFALL IS SIX HUNDRED HUNDREDWEIGHT A DAY** and it did not go up and it did not come down. Six hundred at fourpence over thirty days is three hundred pounds a month, and that is forty-eight pence a head a month across about fifteen hundred people, and that is one and three-fifths of a penny a head a day, and the two agree. **THE FOUR LAUNDERS MEASURE NINE HUNDRED, SIX HUNDRED, NOTHING AND TWENTY-FIVE, AND THE TWENTY-FIVE IS INSIDE SOMEBODY'S ABOUT, AND THE LAUNDER THAT MEASURES NOTHING WAS CUT AT THE KERB ON THE ELEVENTH OF THE FIRST MONTH AND IS NOT HEALED.**
>
> **THE ROTA IS IN FORCE AND IS NOT A TEST AND THE TWO ARE NOT THE SAME THING.** **THIRTY-NINE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN, TEN NOT, AND ALL TEN OF THE TEN ARE THE FOURTH LINE.** The fields waited ten times and the seventh line says nobody is blamed and nobody is. **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND ON THE TWENTY-SECOND OF THIS MONTH AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN WAITED FOR A DAY AND A NIGHT WHILE FIVE PEOPLE WERE STANDING ON A STONE FOUR HUNDRED YARDS BELOW THEM, AND THOSE TWO FACTS ARE IN THE SAME COLUMN FOR THE FIRST TIME AND ARE NOT IN THE SAME UNIT.**
>
> **THE COVENANT HAS FOUR SIGNATURES AND ONE OBJECTION UNDER IT AND ONE REFUSAL TO SIGN.** The fourth line is written into it as empty. The fifth line carries the figure and the figure is the same figure. The old man of about seventy-four with a stick did not sign the fifth line. The man of about thirty-five of an Assembly in the Thornwild did not sign and gave a reason about eleven acres of standing willow. **THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THE WORD UNEVEN OUT LOUD ONCE IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE AND ENTERED THAT IT WAS ONCE AND IS NOT GOING TO BE SAID AGAIN.**
>
> **THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR IS TWO** and it is two on the thirtieth and it is two at the end of the season and it did not move this month, and the man of the north row with the cough asked to be struck off it on the twenty-fourth and the man of about fifty refused and gave a reason, and the reason is in this holding's book and is not a reason about a man of sixty-eight and his hands. **THE COUNT FOR FIVE IS ONE** and it is him and it did not move.
>
> **AND TWO COUNTS BEGIN THIS MONTH, AT TWO AND AT ONE, AND NEITHER IS THE COUNT OF PERSONS WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR, AND THE THREE ARE THREE COUNTS. AT TWO: PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO HAVE A ROOTMARK.** He had one before this month and the other one is me. Four inches branching twice and three inches not branching, and the two injuries are not the same injury, and the two of us have not been added together. **AT ONE: PERSONS WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR AND WALK OUT OF THE GROUND AFTERWARDS.** It was two on the twelfth. It is one, and I am the one, and I am entering this count in the same line as the count of two for the reason I entered that one in the same line in the first month of the year, and the reason is that a man who enters one of them by itself has entered a boast.
>
> **THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE SAME ARM IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK ARE FOUR** and they reconcile none of one another and nothing said about that arm this month is a fifth. **THE MARK ON THE RIGHT ARM OF THE MAN OF THE NORTH ROW WITH THE COUGH IS THREE INCHES, DOES NOT BRANCH, IS PERMANENT, AND WAS ENTERED BY HIM IN HIS OWN HAND ON THE TWENTY-THIRD OF THIS MONTH AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE MORNING, AND IT IS NOT AN ACCOUNT OF MY ARM.**
>
> **THE FIELDBOOK WAS OPENED ONCE THIS MONTH, AT THIS HOLDING, ON A PERSON, AND NOT ON A PAGE,** for about eleven seconds, by a licensed man in about eleven people, with the cost written down and read back three times before the instrument went near anything. **IT WENT BACK UP THE FOUR-DAY ROAD ON THE TWENTY-THIRD AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH HOUR AND IT IS FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF IN A ROOM WITH A HASP, AND THE PERSON WHO PUT THE KEY OFF HER OWN RING IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK AND WAS NOT ASKED AND IS NOT GOING TO BE ASKED TWICE.**
>
> **THE COUNT OF AN ENTRY THAT CARRIES A DAY AND NO FIGURE STANDS AT THREE.** The fourteenth ordinary reading of the well at Cauldron Reach was on the sixteenth of this month at about the fourth hour and the day was entered and the figure was not. The wall carries thirteen marks and this holding's book holds four of the figures and all four of them are five feet, and there is no entry anywhere in this holding's book for that well coming back.
>
> **THE REQUESTS ARE FIFTY-THREE AND THE SECTION-NINE NOTES ARE FIFTY-THREE** and neither moved and no new note was written under section nine this month, and there is no charter and the note says so on its face. **THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED AGAIN THIS MONTH** and the count of the times he has been fetched stands at nineteen. **THE COUNT OF THINGS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS THREE** and it did not go to four this month and it was available to him on the twentieth and he did not take it. **WHOSE ORDER IT IS** has been asked four times in this season and answered never and it was not asked a fifth time. **THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN** was not said out loud this morning and is not going to be. **THE ANSWER OF THE ROOM AT FENNEMERE IS FOUR LINES AND WAS CORRECT IN ALL FOUR AND NOBODY HAS ASKED THAT ROOM A SECOND TIME.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that nobody said anything tender and that the fen entered both of those.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the reader of this body went out to the node with the man of about fifty and the man of the north row with the cough, and there were three of them, and none of them said anything about why they had come, and the fen entered that he had not said and they had not said and that a man does not say to a man of about fifty at about the fourth hour of the afternoon of the last day of a month that he would like to look at a sill for about an hour, and that in about four years somebody is going to write that down as a habit and it is going to be true.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

The water on the west sill has moved about once in every four or five seconds from the west side for as long as anybody at that node has been putting a rule against it, and it makes no sound and shows nothing on the surface, and the only way there is to read it is to put the rule in against the sill and let the water come up the wood and go down again, and that is what all four of the reader of this body's takings have been.

And at about the fourth hour and forty minutes of the afternoon of the thirtieth of this month it moved about once in every second.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

The man of about fifty had the stopwatch and it was on it before the water changed, and the reader of this body was the man who called it, and the two of them had agreed in about nine seconds before the first one which of them was doing what, because a man with a stopwatch and a man calling a change in a thing that is not a sound is two men and not one, and the reason was given out loud and the reason was that the caller had a bad hand and a stopwatch does not.

The water went on at once in every second for **eleven seconds** and then stopped and went back to once in every four or five seconds, and the man of about fifty said the number and the reader of this body wrote it down, and neither of them said anything at all, and the man of the north row with the cough stood about nine feet off with his arms folded and did not say anything either, and the fen entered all three of those.

And at about the tenth hour of the evening of the same day, in the dark, with a lamp on the stone, the water on the west sill moved about once in every second again, and the man of about fifty had the stopwatch and it was on it, and the reader of this body called it, and it went on for **eleven seconds**, and stopped, and went back to once in every four or five seconds.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

"**The two are the same to the second. Not about the same. The same to the second, on a stopwatch, with the same man on it and the same man calling it, in daylight and in the dark, on the same sill, at the same place on the sill, about nine feet off the hollow.**"

"**The interval from the beginning of the first to the beginning of the second is six hours and eleven seconds. Eleven seconds and six hours and eleven seconds, and I am entering those two figures tonight and I am not going to improve either of them and I am not going to read it a third time, and it is half past ten at night and there are three of us and I am going to bed.**"

"**It came in on the north side. The north sill moved first on the first one and the west sill moved with it a half second after, and there is no ground to the north of that node for about sixty miles that anybody in this holding has ever been on, and there is nobody north of that node to ask, because there is nobody north of that node.**"

"**A thing that arrives at the same hour twice is not weather. Weather does not keep time, and I have been in this fen four years and I have watched every kind of water there is come out of a sky, and not one of them has ever come out of a sky at the same hour twice. I have got no name for it and I am not going to give it one. It is a pulse. I am going to write the two figures down and leave them exactly as they are, and there is a thing coming in the next year and it is not mine to fix.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the man of the north row with the cough said nothing at all about the eleven seconds or about the interval, and entered that the count of yards he has stood in since the first of a month two months back is seventeen and has moved, and that he was not asked to be in any of the seventeen.

> Entered by the reader of this body, in his own hand, at about the eleventh hour of the evening of the thirtieth of this month, in about six people, at the west kerb, on the standing of a figure, and not by the clerk, who is at this holding and is nine miles off: **AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR AND FORTY MINUTES OF THE AFTERNOON, AND AGAIN AT ABOUT THE TENTH HOUR OF THE EVENING, THE WATER ON THE WEST SILL AT THE FOUR ELBOWS MOVED ABOUT ONCE IN EVERY SECOND INSTEAD OF ONCE IN EVERY FOUR OR FIVE SECONDS. IT RAN ELEVEN SECONDS, BOTH TIMES. THE STOPWATCH WAS THE SAME STOPWATCH AND THE MAN ON IT WAS THE SAME MAN AND THE MAN WHO CALLED IT WAS THE SAME MAN, IN DAYLIGHT AND IN THE DARK, ON THE SAME SILL, AT THE SAME PLACE ON THE SILL. THE INTERVAL FROM THE FIRST BEGINNING TO THE SECOND BEGINNING IS SIX HOURS AND ELEVEN SECONDS. THE NORTH SILL MOVED FIRST AND THE WEST SILL MOVED A HALF SECOND AFTER IT. THERE IS NO GROUND TO THE NORTH OF THAT NODE FOR ABOUT SIXTY MILES AND NOBODY HAS BEEN ASKED, BECAUSE THERE IS NOBODY NORTH OF THAT NODE. IT WAS NOT READ A THIRD TIME. A THING THAT ARRIVES AT THE SAME HOUR TWICE IS NOT WEATHER. I HAVE ENTERED THE TWO FIGURES AND IMPROVED NEITHER AND GIVEN IT NO NAME.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.
