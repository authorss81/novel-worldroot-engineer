# Chapter 244

## A Rota With A Refusal Clause In It

The twenty-sixth of this month was a Thursday and the fourth line came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden and the fields waited, and the seventh line of that sheet says that the fields waiting is entered by whoever enters anything with the day and the hour on it and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited, and the fen entered that nobody is blamed and entered the number, which is nine, and entered that all nine of them are the fourth line.

And the twenty-seventh of this month was a Friday, and about nineteen people were in a yard at Errow Reach at about the eleventh hour of the morning, and a sheet of paper with six lines on it was read out standing up in the hand it was written in by the woman who keeps the four books for four bodies of households, and read back by the man of about fifty, and the reader of this body did not hold it and was not asked to hold it and entered that he was not asked.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

> **THE COVENANT OF THE FOUR ELBOWS, six lines, read out standing up in a yard at Errow Reach at about the eleventh hour of the morning of the twenty-seventh of this month, in about nineteen people, by the woman who keeps the four books, in the hand it was written in, and read back, entered whole and not summarised:**
>
> **ONE. THE NODE IS WORKED, NOT PATTERNED. Four bodies of households take their own turn on their own morning, a turn is two hours at the second hour, one person holds and two or four work, and nobody is on the roll, and the third clause of the Assembly is not met and is not pretended to be met.**
>
> **TWO. THE FOURTH LINE IS MARDEN'S AND IS EMPTY, AND IT IS WRITTEN HERE THAT IT IS EMPTY, AND NOBODY IS TO PUT A NAME IN IT UNTIL MARDEN ASKS FOR ONE, AND MARDEN HAS NOT ASKED.**
>
> **THREE. A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS MAY STOP ANY TURN OF ITS OWN AT ANY TIME, WITHOUT SAYING WHY AND WITHOUT SAYING WHY BEFORE.**
>
> **FOUR. A SEAT ON THIS ROTA IS NOT AN OFFICE. A SEAT MAY BE REFUSED BY THE PERSON WHO HOLDS IT AT ANY TIME, AND NOBODY OWNS A SEAT ON IT, AND THE HOLDING OF A SEAT IS NOT THE HOLDING OF THE NODE, AND A BODY THAT REFUSES ITS OWN SEAT IS NOT IN ARREARS TO ANYBODY.**
>
> **FIVE. THE FIGURE OF WHAT THIS NODE DELIVERS IS ABOUT FIFTEEN HUNDRED HUNDREDWEIGHT A DAY AND THE FIGURE OF WHAT FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS COUNT THAT THEY NEED IS ABOUT TWO THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS SIX HUNDRED HUNDREDWEIGHT A DAY, AND THAT FIGURE IS THE SAME FIGURE IT WAS BEFORE THIS SHEET WAS DRAWN, AND THIS SHEET DOES NOT CHANGE IT.**
>
> **SIX. NOBODY IS TO BE THANKED FOR ANY PART OF THIS.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody in that yard said anything tender and that the fen entered that too.

And the old man of about seventy-four with a stick did not say anything at all when the fifth line was read, and he waited until the woman who keeps the four books had read the sixth line and had read all six back, and then he said the thing, and it took him about forty seconds, and the reader of this body did not write one word of it down while he was saying it and entered afterwards that he had it exactly and that the man of about fifty wrote it down at the time and read it back.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything.

"**I am not signing that with the fifth line on it and I have wanted to say about the fifth line for about two years and I have never had nineteen people to say it to.**"

"**You are writing a shortage into a covenant. I have been on this bank about twenty minutes and I have wanted to say that arithmetic out loud in a yard for about nine years and I never had nine to say, because nobody counted, and now I have nineteen and I am going to use them and nobody is to stop me.**"

"**Here it is. A hundred and thirty-two months is a hundred and thirty-two tens of days, and thirty days is the month and not thirty-one, and three thousand nine hundred and sixty days at one turn in four is nine hundred and ninety, and there are nine. I have been at that gate and I have been on that field and there are nine mornings in eleven years when the men of that village who could walk to the headland were in the village. Nine in a hundred and thirty-two. That is the honest heading. And if you write six hundred hundredweight a day on a covenant then in about four years somebody is going to count how much of six hundred hundredweight a day that village gave up, and they are going to call it a debt, and a debt is a thing that gets collected, and I have been a man of about seventy-four for long enough to know what gets collected off people who cannot pay.**"

"**A heading that is true about a bad year is still a true heading. Put my name under the fifth line and put the nine there beside it and I will sign. I will not put my name under a covenant that says six hundred hundredweight a day and says nothing about who has to hold it.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about eleven seconds, and entered that the arithmetic is nine hundred and ninety and the count is nine and that the two are not the same figure and were never meant to be and that a man of seventy-four has just said both of them in about nineteen people in about forty seconds, and entered that the reader of this body has been carrying the nine hundred and ninety in a book since the fourth of this month and has not put it in a yard, and that the man who put it in a yard is not him.

And the old man of about seventy-four with a stick signed it, and his name is not on the covenant and his objection is on the covenant under a line of his own, and the woman who keeps the four books read his objection out again in his own words after he had said he wanted it read, and the man of about fifty read it back, and the old man of about seventy-four with a stick did not correct one word of it.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that he was not thanked and did not want to be and that nobody thanked him, and that the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in that yard and is not one of the seven that decide and has never been asked to be and was not asked today.

And the man of about thirty-five of an Assembly in the Thornwild was at the edge of that yard and did not come into it and did not sign, and he said the standing form word for word, which he has said four times in this holding's book, and then he gave the reason, and the reason was new and it was in his own words.

"**I am not signing that because I do not know who I would be signing for. There are eleven acres of standing willow at that node, and a hundred and forty-one trees on the west side of them were there before the first charter anybody in these four villages ever signed, and the room at Fennmere has told us in writing that it has no standing to make that ground a party to anything and does not propose to acquire one.**"

"**That leaves the ground with nobody. It does not leave it with me. A man who signs a covenant about a node that is standing on a piece of ground nobody has spoken for has not been honest and has not been dishonest, he has been somewhere else, and I would rather stand at the edge of it in about nineteen people and say that than be the fifth signature on a sheet that four villages will read in four years as though the ground were in it.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the room did not lie and did not obstruct and did not delay and answered in four lines in about twenty-five days and was correct in all four, and entered that nobody has asked that room a fifth time and that he is not going to, and that the eleven acres are not spoken for and that no office anybody in this holding has written to has made them spoken for.

And then a woman with four hundredweight of boards on a cart behind her came through the gate at about the second hour of the afternoon, and she is a seedwright of a trust and not of this holding and not of a body of households and not of anything at that node, and she was on the road and not in the yard and she stopped at about nine feet off and she said the one thing she said and it was in her own trade and it was not tender and she was not thanked and she was not sent for.

"**I have been out on the route eleven days in twenty and I have watched four bodies of households share that node for eleven years and I am not one of them and I am not on that sheet and I am here because there are four hundredweight of boards going west on Sunday.**"

"**And I am not going to tell you what a turn costs, because I am a seedwright and I read a line of seed in the ground and I cannot read a thing that is not finished growing, and that is not a smaller thing, that is the thing I am for, and I said that once in a yard in about eleven people two months back and I am not going to say it better today. There are four bodies of households in that yard and two of them have not spoken yet this morning, and they are the ones it belongs to.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the boards went west on the Sunday and that nobody saw her off and that nobody thanked her, and entered that the reader of this body did not ask her a second time about anything and did not ask her a first time about anything, and that the count of asks he has made of her in this holding's book is one and it was answered with a no and he did not ask twice.

And the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said the thing that four bodies of households had been in a yard to hear for about eleven weeks, and she gave no reason before it and the fen entered that she gave no reason before it and that this is the first time in this holding's book that a person has said a thing in a yard of about nineteen people with no reason in front of it.

"**You have all been talking, for about a month, about what the node gives a village. Nobody in nineteen people has said out loud what a village gives the node.**"

"**The answer is the morning after its own. That is the whole of it. It is not water and it is not money and it is not a fifth launder, and it is not a thing anybody can put in a line with a number at the end of it, and it is the reason the fourth line has been empty nine mornings in thirty-six and it is not going to get better by writing a name in it.**"

"**If a man puts a name in that fourth line then the man is that village's morning and the village is the man's turn, and in about four years one of those two things is going to stop being true, and nobody is going to write down which one it was, and that is the whole of what a village gives up and I have been giving it for eleven years at a gate on the first and the fifteenth and nobody counted.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute, and entered that nobody thanked her and that she said before she began that anybody who thanked her could be told to stop, and that nobody did.

And then the reader of this body said the one word out loud, once, in about nineteen people, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-six.

"**The four launders measure nine hundred hundredweight a day, six hundred, nothing, and twenty-five. The one that measures nothing was cut at the kerb on the eleventh of the first month by about nineteen people with two saws and it is not healed and it is not going to be healed this season, and I did not cut it and I was in the yard when it was cut. The twenty-five is inside somebody's about and I said on the seventh of last month that I have never been able to make those two numbers agree and I am not going to to-day.**"

"**The word for that is uneven. I am saying it out loud once, here, in about nineteen people, and I am not going to improve on it and I am not going to say it again, and I want it written on this sheet that the word was said and by whom and how many times, which is once.**"

"**And a covenant is not a figure. That sheet has a figure on the fifth line and the figure is the same figure it was before the sheet was drawn, and the sheet does not change it, and nothing anybody has signed this month has made one hundred and forty-one trees on four acres come back or four feet of a well four hundred miles off come up, and I am not going to stand in this yard and improve on the word uneven either, because the word is the whole of what I have got.**"

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the man of the north row with the cough was in that yard and was not asked to be in it, and entered that the count of yards the man of the north row with the cough has stood in since the first of a month two months back is sixteen and has moved, and that he has said seven things in sixteen yards and that he said nothing in this one.

> Entered by the man of about fifty, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the afternoon of the twenty-seventh of this month, in a yard at Errow Reach, in about nineteen people, on the standing of a document, and read back before it was entered whole: **A COVENANT OF SIX LINES WAS READ OUT STANDING UP BY THE WOMAN WHO KEEPS THE FOUR BOOKS AND READ BACK BY ME AND THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT HOLD IT AND WAS NOT ASKED TO HOLD IT. FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAVE SIGNED IT. THE FOURTH LINE IS WRITTEN INTO IT AS EMPTY AND NOBODY IS TO PUT A NAME IN IT UNTIL MARDEN ASKS FOR ONE, AND MARDEN HAS NOT ASKED. THE REFUSAL CLAUSE IS THE FOURTH LINE AND IT IS SPENT AND IT IS THE ONLY USE OF THAT WORD IN A DOCUMENT THAT THIS HOLDING KEEPS, AND IT IS NOT GOING TO APPEAR AGAIN IN A YARD OR IN A BLOCK OR IN A BOOK. THE OLD MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY-FOUR WITH A STICK DID NOT SIGN THE FIFTH LINE AND SIGNED THE REST, AND HIS OBJECTION IS UNDER THE SHEET IN HIS OWN WORDS, AND HE IS NOT ON THE SHEET AND HIS NAME IS NOT ON IT, AND HE ASKED FOR IT TO BE READ OUT AGAIN AFTER HE HAD SAID IT AND THE WOMAN WHO KEEPS THE FOUR BOOKS READ IT OUT AGAIN IN HIS OWN WORDS AND HE DID NOT CORRECT ONE WORD OF IT. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FIVE OF AN ASSEMBLY IN THE THORNWILD DID NOT SIGN AND GAVE A REASON AND THE REASON IS ON A PAGE SOMEWHERE AND IT IS ABOUT ELEVEN ACRES OF STANDING WILLOW AND NOBODY HAS SPOKEN FOR IT AND NO OFFICE HAS BEEN ASKED TO. A SEEDWRIGHT CAME THROUGH THE GATE WITH FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT OF BOARDS AND STOPPED AT ABOUT NINE FEET OFF AND SAID ONE THING AND WENT. THE FIGURE ON THE FIFTH LINE IS SIX HUNDRED HUNDREDWEIGHT A DAY AND IT DID NOT GO UP AND IT DID NOT COME DOWN AND NOBODY IN THAT YARD THANKED ANYBODY, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THE WORD UNEVEN OUT LOUD ONCE IN ABOUT NINETEEN PEOPLE AND ENTERED THAT IT WAS ONCE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered the length of it, which was about a minute and a half, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and nobody said anything tender, and entered that the fen entered both of those.
