## THE THREE THINGS THIS REPOSITORY HAS LEARNED THE HARD WAY, WHICH ARE THE THREE THINGS A CLOSE MUST NOT LEARN AGAIN

1. **A COUNT WRITTEN DOWN WITHOUT BEING CHECKED AGAINST THE THING IT MEASURES.** This has happened in this repository at least six times, it is the subject of Volume 05, and it is the subject of Batch 0005's own ninth-day cycle. The day clock, the month frames, the word counts, the weekday of a day, the interval between two takings, and the number of mornings a thing has been in force are all counts, and **every one of them has been wrong somewhere in these 245 chapters.**
2. **A CERTIFICATE IS NOT A MEASUREMENT.** Batch 0004's continuity file certified zero month-frame mismatches and four stood. **If this close publishes a figure, it publishes the figure and the method that produced it, so that a later writer can re-derive it instead of believing it.**
3. **A STATE FILE THAT DISAGREES WITH ITSELF IS HOW A LATER WRITER GETS HURT.** The Volume 04 close's two own documents disagreed in three places, and its repair-footprint figure was wrong in both of them at once, and nobody noticed for a whole phase. **After this close has written its findings file, read the findings file and the continuity section side by side and check that every number in one is in the other.**
