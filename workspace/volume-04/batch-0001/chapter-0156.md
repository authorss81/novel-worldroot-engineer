# Chapter 156

## The Word He Would Not Let Them Have

The man of about thirty-five came down into the low ground at about the fourth hour of the morning of the twenty-fourth of this month, which was a Thursday, on foot, from the top of the slope, and about four people were on the boards and the engineer of record was one of them and had been down there since about the second hour because his hand was no use and he had nowhere else to be that a hand that does not work is any use in.

He did not knock and he did not stand about and he said what he had come to say, and he gave the reason he had come at that hour before he gave any of it, and about four people heard all of it and the clerk was one of the four and wrote it in the dark by the light of a lamp on the housing.

"**I have been walking the ground since the sixth hour on Tuesday. I did not come down on Tuesday because you were on it and I do not come into a thing while a man is in it. I came down last night at about the tenth hour and I came down again at the first hour and I have come down now because you are on it again and there is nobody in the pit and there is you on a floor, and I would rather say it to a man on a floor than to a yard, because a yard will write it down and a floor will not, and I want the first one and not the second.**"

"**And the reason I am saying it now is that you took four feet out of a held ground on Tuesday and a well nine miles off went off on Wednesday, and one of the four men in that quarter has been telling people since Wednesday that this holding emptied their quarter, and I have been in that quarter twice since and I want to say one thing about the shape of it and then I am going to go up the slope and I am not going to say anything else this week.**"

Then he said the thing about the word, and he said that he had heard it, and he said where he had heard it and who had said it and did not soften any of it.

"**On Wednesday, at the wall side of this ground, the man from the north row with the cough said the word nest. He said it once and he was not talking to me and he was not talking to anybody. I was four feet away and I heard it.**"

"**I am not going to pretend to you that I do not know what he meant, because I do know and because you would not believe a man who did not know. He meant a place where something is being got ready, and there is a word for that and it is longer and harder, and the word is the one you have been not saying in this book for two days, and I am not going to say it for you either, and I want that entered and I want it entered as my refusal and not as a definition.**"

"**And here is the reason, and it is the only reason I am going to give in this yard, and I have given it to two people in nineteen years.** A nest is a thing a thing makes when it is stopping. **That is what a nest is. It is a place a thing builds and then stays in, and it is not a road and it is not a queue and it is not a thing that is on its way anywhere.** A nest has one thing in it and one thing in it has a number."

"**What is under that ground is not that. I have read ground for nineteen years and I have read that ground four times and it is not one thing, and the reason I know it is not one thing is that I have watched it refuse.** I watched it in the spring of a year I am not going to put on a wall, when a crew with a licence and a schedule came down onto a floor and put a probe into it, and the ground closed over the probe and took the schedule with it and the schedule came up in a different place eleven feet on, **and I have never in my life seen a thing do that twice, and I have been waiting two years to see it do it twice and it did not.**"

"So there is no number under that ground. **And a thing with no number under it and a road on it is not a nest, and the word you want is the word for a thing that has to be answered to, and you will not use it because using it means somebody is going to have to answer, and nobody in this district has ever had to answer a thing about a floor.**"

Somebody asked him what the word was. He said no in one word, and then he said the half of it that he would say.

"**I will tell you the half that is checkable and I will not tell you the other half, and you may have the half or you may have nothing. The half that is checkable is that a thing under that ground does not go where the ground goes, and it is doing work, and the work is not water and is not seeds and is not anything anybody in this yard can name, and it is work, and it is the sort of work that stops if nobody does it.**"

"**And the half I will not tell you is what it is.** And I am not going to be asked again by this body and if I am asked again by a person in an office I will say no in one word, and I have said that to four offices in nineteen years and I have never once had to say it twice to the same one."

And then he asked for the thing, and he said what it was in nine words and would not say one word more about it, and about four people heard it and the clerk wrote it down at once because the reader of this body was not in that low ground and had said in a yard nine days ago that he would not know whether to ask the next question and had not asked it.

"**A pail, a measure, and an hour. Not the pail with the boards, this holding's pail, and any measure that will hold an exact amount and not a bucket.**"

"**I am not going to tell you what it is for and I want the clerk to enter that I declined and that I am not going to give a reason, and I want the reason written down as the reason, which is that there is none I am willing to put in a book of a body of sixteen households on one head.**"

And the person who keeps the seed house and the instrument, who is not thanked and is not forgiven and has been in this low ground twice in two days and has not been asked for anything by anybody, asked the only question in that low ground that was hers to ask.

"**Who brings it back.**"

"**Not me.**"

"**Enter it as he said it. Do not improve it and do not put a question after it, and do not let anybody in this book say later that he promised.**"

"**I will bring it back.**"

"And then the man of about thirty-five did the thing that nobody in this yard expected, which is that he said the price out loud before anybody asked him to."

"**One pail. And the losing of a man you have had for four days, who has not been paid and is not going to be paid and is not asking, and who has told you two things in a dark yard with a lamp on it that are worth about four years of work to a man who does this. That is the whole of the price and I am telling you in front of four people so that you can decide whether it is one you want to pay, and not one of those four is going to have an opinion and I would like that entered too.**"

Nobody had an opinion. The clerk entered that nobody in that low ground had an opinion and that the count of the times a thing has been offered in a low ground and not answered in a low ground is now two, **and that the first of the two was in a room on the fifteenth of this month and the second is now and the fen has entered both and has entered that the two people who were in neither of them are the only two in this book who are not owed anything to anybody in it.**

And he went up the slope, and about four people were in that low ground, and the engineer of record said the thing that is on the page and is the first sentence of the next movement of this book.

"**I have been down here since the second hour with a hand that does not work and he came down at the fourth hour and told me the ground is doing work and would not tell me what it is.**"

"**And I want it entered that the first thing he said to me, before anything else, before the word and before the pail, was that the ground closed over a probe once in a year he is not going to name and did not do it twice, and that he has been waiting two years for it to do it twice.**"

---

And at about the eleventh hour of the same morning, in the seed house, with the bay doors shut and about four people in the room, the engineer of record told the seedwright of the trust a thing, and told her in a low voice, and there were four people in the room and one of them was the clerk, and the clerk has entered it.

He told her that the ground under the low quarter was going to move before the end of the next month, and that he had one reading and no second reading, and that he had a hand that had not worked for two days and had worked for four seconds on Tuesday morning, and that **the eleventh of next month** was the word he had for it.

She asked for the number and the reason and he gave her the number.

She said: "**Enter it.**"

And he said: "**I will enter it when I can defend it.**"

And then she said the sentence, and she said it once, in the ordinary voice, to four people, and it is the sentence this book has been building for two volumes and it does not have a number in it and there is nothing on a page that can be got wrong about it, and the reader of this body has never read it back and did not read it back that morning and entered that he was not in the room.

"**A thing that is true and in one room is not a secret yet. The day it is in the same room again is the day it is a secret, and I am telling you the day now so that you cannot have it later, and I am not going to be thanked for it and I have said that before I was thanked.**"

"**And I am not going to write it down. I want that in the book too, from me, in my own hand: the seedwright of the trust was asked for a number by the engineer of record and gave him the one she had, and was asked by the engineer of record to enter it and did not enter it, and is not going to enter it, and the reason she gave is that a thing that one person can carry is not a thing a body can be asked to carry, and that is the fifth time in a year that she has said a sentence of that shape and the sixth time she has refused to have it written as a refusal.**"

"So there is a date in this book and there is no figure against it. **The eleventh of next month, in a room with four people in it, of which one was the clerk.** And the clerk entered it as a date and not as a finding, and read it back to the man of about fifty on the evening of the twenty-fourth, in a room with about nine people in it, and did not say who had said it and the reader of this body did not ask, **which is his standing, and which is the fourth time in a season he has read back a thing he did not ask about, and the count is entered, and the count of times he has read back a figure and not asked where it came from is now two.**"

---

And then on the twenty-fifth, which was a Friday, a body of sixteen households on one head found out what it actually had, which is two pails.

Not three. The examiner counted them in the yard at about the eighth hour of the morning with the young man of twenty and his dog, and she counted them twice and she did not round and there are two, and one of the two is the boards pail that three people carried nine miles on the seventeenth and which is in use, and the other is the pail of the woman with the shilling, which is on her own head and which is not this holding's to give.

And the man of about fifty did the whole of it in a yard of about eleven in about four sentences, which is the shortest thing he has said in a season, and the clerk entered that it was the shortest and entered the count of times a thing has been offered in this holding and not had, which is now three.

"**We have two pails and one of them belongs to a woman and one of them is carrying three people's work, and the man asked for this holding's pail, and there is no such pail, and I am not going to have a yard spend a week on it.**"

"**So there are three things and I will say all three and the third is the one I want entered. One: this holding has no pail and never has and I have never once in eleven years counted them, and I counted them this morning because a stranger asked for one, and that is the correct order to find things out in and I would like it entered as a fault and not as a lesson.**"

"Two: a pail is not the thing that matters. He asked for a pail, a measure and an hour, and the pail is the smallest of the three and the hour is the whole of it. **And four, and this is the third thing and it is the one I want entered: nobody in this yard knows what the hour is for, and every one of us has been standing here four days assuming that the pail is the cost. The pail is not the cost. The hour is the whole of it, and I do not know what this body would be agreeing to for an hour, and I have been in this yard a long time and I have never once agreed to a thing I could not name.**"
