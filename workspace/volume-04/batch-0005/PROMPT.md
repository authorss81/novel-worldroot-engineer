### Defect 1 — a weekday, not a day number

**Ch 178's prose establishes that day 301, the first of the eleventh month, is a Saturday, and Ch 187's prose establishes that day 320, the twentieth of that same month, is a Thursday. **Days 330 and 331 follow from the same pair. Day 330 is twenty-nine days after day 301, which is one day forward from Saturday, and day 331 is thirty days after, which is two days forward. SO DAY 330 IS A SUNDAY AND DAY 331 IS A MONDAY.**

**The state files, `state/current.md`, `state/continuity.md`, `state/open-threads.md` and the Batch 0004 prompt all say that day 330 is a Friday and that day 331 is a Saturday. Both are two days out.
