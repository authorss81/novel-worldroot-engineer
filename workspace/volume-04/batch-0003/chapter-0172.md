# Chapter 172

## What It Eats

The twenty-second of this month was a Thursday, and a woman of a household in the low quarter walked nine miles up the fen road on foot and did not stop at the gate, and came the last four hundred yards down into the low ground, and she has walked the whole of that road to say one thing, and she said it in six sentences, and the fen entered that she said six.

She said them standing on the boards over the shaft with her back to the boards and the lamp on the housing behind her, and the engineer of record was on the boards and the clerk was at the top of the slope and could hear all of it and wrote all of it down afterward in his own hand and read it back and did not improve it.

She had been let in at the gate by the man of about fifty and had not stopped, and about two hundred yards down the fen road she had said that she was not going to the seed house and that she was going to the low ground, and the man of about fifty had not asked her why and had walked the two hundred yards with her and had not said anything on the way, **and the fen entered that he did not ask and entered that the not asking was the correct decision and that this holding has not got a practice for that and has had it twice now.**

The nine people on the slope above heard all six sentences. One of them asked, at the end, whether anybody in that quarter had tried it and had not liked it, and the woman answered that in four words, and the four words were on the page and the fen entered them and entered that nobody asked her a second question.

"**There is a thing in that quarter that was not there a month ago and every one of us can see it and not one of us can name it.**"

"**It is not water and it is not a drain and it is not a building.**"

"**My house has been eating something since about the tenth of this month that we did not plant and we did not buy and we did not find.**"

"**It comes up out of the ground on the low side of the row.**"

"**It is not a root and it is not a seed and it is not a mushroom any of us has a name for, and it is food.**"

"**Four houses on that row have been eating it and one of us has been feeding it to a child.**"

Then she said the thing that made the walk worth nine miles, and she said it in about four sentences more, and this body has not got a fifth thing to add to it and did not try.

"**I am not here to ask this holding for anything. I want that said in my house's name and not in mine, because I am the one who came and I am not the one who decided. I came because there is a thing on that strip and a child in one of those four houses, and I would like a body that is not a quarter to know that in a book, and I would not like one to come.**"

Nobody thanked her and she did not ask to be thanked, and the man of about fifty gave her a cup of something and she took it and drank it standing up, and the fen entered the cup and entered that it was not asked for and entered that she did not ask for it either.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is the whole engine of that afternoon, and he said it standing up with about nine people on the slope above and the woman still on the boards, and he gave the reason first and gave the second reason second, and the second reason is the one this book is about.

"**We are not going to look at it. I want that said out loud in a yard with nine people in it before anybody offers, and I am going to give you the reasons in the order I hold them.**"

"**One. This holding has no standing in that quarter, has no form, is not a party to that row of houses, and has already had one figure in four years that was a person four hundred miles off losing something. That is a wall with nine figures on it. I am not walking nine miles to look at a thing I cannot name so that this book can have a page about it.**"

"**Two, and this is the one that matters, and it is not about standing. A thing that is food is a thing with a food system. A food system is a thing that can run out. This holding cannot say when, and cannot say who would be harmed by it running out, and cannot say whether anybody in that quarter would be harmed at all.**"

"**And I am not going to say what that is and I am not going to say what it means. I have got a book with four lines in it, and one of those lines says what this body is looking after and cannot reach, and a thing it cannot reach and cannot name and cannot put a date at the end of is on that line already and has been on it for a month, and the reason it is on that line and is not written into it is that a line is a standing and a standing is a thing this holding has to keep.**"

And then she gave the check, and the check is the chapter, and it is in a household's own hand and at a household's own cost, and the fen entered that this holding did not pay for it and did not know that it had been paid and did not ask the price, **and entered that this is the fifth time in a season that this body has been beaten home by a household's own expense, and entered that the count is five and is not going to stop being entered.**

> Written on the back of a miller's tally at the fourth house in the low quarter, on the twenty-second of this month, in the hand of the woman who came up the fen road, four questions and four answers, and brought up that road at that household's cost and nobody else's:
>
> **One. When did it start. A. About the tenth of this month. Before that there was nothing in the low side of that row and it was hard ground and it had been hard ground as long as the oldest woman in this house can remember.**
>
> **Two. Where does it come up. B. On the low side of the row, in a strip about two paces wide, and it is the same strip in front of four houses, and it is not in front of the other houses on that row, and nobody has done anything to that strip.**
>
> **Three. What happens to the ground where it comes up. C. The ground there is soft. It was hard. It is not wet and it is not marshy and it does not smell. It is soft the way a seedbed is soft and not the way a drain is soft, and I have put my hands into it and I am not a gardener and I know the difference between a seedbed and a drain.**
>
> **Four. Has anybody in that quarter been told they may not. D. No. Nobody has been told. The man with the cart has not said so and he hauls every day. The holder of that well is eleven miles off and he is not a party to a quarter and he does not come here.**

The fourth answer is the last business of that Thursday, and the fen entered the whole of the four and did not add a fifth question, and entered the reason, which is one sentence: **this holding has no form on which to ask a fifth question of a quarter of four hundred and forty-one people, and a body that writes a fifth question under a household's four answers is a body that has started drafting the household's account for it.**

---

And on the twenty-third, which was a Friday, the man of about thirty-five came down into the low ground on foot at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and about nine people were in that yard above and the woman who had walked nine miles was not in it and had gone home on the Sunday, and he was asked one question and it was about the food and not about the word.

He was asked it by the reader of this body, standing at the top of the slope, and he was given the whole of the four answers before he was asked, and he read them twice, and the clerk entered that he read them twice.

And he gave the half that is checkable first, and it is about nine words long, and then he gave the rest.

"**A thing that eats has a return. A thing with a return can be worked with.**"

"**And that is the whole of the half I will give you and I have given that same nine words to two other bodies in nineteen years and it has been true both times and it was still not enough either time.**"

"**What I will not tell you is what it eats, and I am not going to pretend that this is a different kind of answer from the one you have had twice. This is the fourth time in nineteen years that a person has asked me what is under a ground and what it feeds on, and I have said no four times, and I would like the clerk to enter that it is four and not five, because yesterday a man in a yard asked me a different question and I gave him a different half, and that is not a fifth of anything.**"

And then he said the thing he had not come down to say, and about nine people in the yard above heard all of it and nobody thanked him and the fen entered that he was not thanked and entered that the count of the times a man of about thirty-five has said a thing in this low ground and not been thanked is now five, and entered that the fen does not know what to do with a count of five and is not going to build a column for it.

"**And I am going to say one thing that nobody asked me for and then I am going up the slope. Nobody on this fen has asked me to keep that food off that row, and nobody has asked me to put it on. If somebody asks me to do either one of those things I will want to know who asked, and what they are going to do with the answer, and I have not got a better offer than that this morning and I am not asking anybody for one.**"

Nobody said that to him and about nine people were in that yard and the fen entered that nobody said it and entered that nobody was asked either.

And then he went up the slope, and the engineer of record stayed on the boards for about half an hour afterward and did not put an instrument on them, and a licensed man was not called and did not come, and the fen entered all three of those absences separately and entered the reason for the first of them, which is that there is a thing under those boards and it is not a floor and it is not a shaft and it is not a licensed man's work, and the case stayed in the cart shed where it had been since the ninth.

> Entered at the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-third of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a work: **after a man of about thirty-five went up that slope, the engineer of record stayed on the boards for about half an hour and put no instrument on them. No licensed man was called and none came. The instrument is in the cart shed and has been since the ninth of this month. The fen entered the three absences and entered that this holding has an instrument in a shed and a man who can read ground without it and has not put the two together and is not going to today.**

> Entered at the sixth hour of the afternoon of the twenty-third of this month, in the low ground, by the clerk, on the standing of a check: **four questions and four answers written in a household's own hand on the back of a miller's tally, at that household's own cost, and entered entire. The fourth answer is that nobody in that quarter has been told they may not, and that the man with the cart has not said so, and that the holder of record of the Pans well is eleven miles off and is not a party to a quarter.** The fen entered that it did not pay for the tally and did not know that it had been paid and did not ask the price, and entered that this is the fifth time in a season that this body has been beaten home by a household's own expense. **The fen entered that it did not go and look and gave the reason, which is that it has no standing in that quarter, and that the second reason is that a thing which is food is a thing with a food system, and a food system is a thing that can run out, and this holding cannot say when and cannot say who would be harmed by it running out.**
>
> **And entered on the same line: a man of about thirty-five was asked one question about that food and gave the half that is checkable, being that a thing that eats has a return and a thing with a return can be worked with, and refused the half that is not, being what it eats, and entered that the count of that refusal in nineteen years is four and not five. He was not thanked. He said that nobody on this fen has asked him to keep that food off that row or to put it on, and that if he is asked he will want to know who asked and what they are going to do with the answer.**

And so the twenty-second and the twenty-third of this month went down, and a woman walked nine miles and said six sentences and went home on the Sunday, and four answers came up on the back of a tally at a household's own cost, and a man of about thirty-five gave nine words he has given two other bodies in nineteen years and refused the rest, **and the whole of what this holding has got against the thing under that ground is a strip about two paces wide in front of four houses, a child in one of them, and a line in its own book that says what it is looking after and cannot reach, and that is the first time in this book that a thing under the ground has had a cost in it that this body can describe, and the cost is to a row of houses and not to a floor, and the fen entered that it cannot say when and did not say it cannot.**
