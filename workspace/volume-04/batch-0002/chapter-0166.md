# Chapter 166

## Not A Fifth Line

The twelfth of this month was a Monday, and in the seed house at about the second hour of the afternoon, with the bay doors shut and about eleven people in the room, the standing list of four lines was read out whole and read back whole and the hour went on it, and it was read out in the same words it was last read out in, on the ninth of last month, when a fourth line was added to a list that had had three lines in it for eleven years.

Nobody thanked anybody. The man of about fifty asked for his initial to be put against nothing and it went in, and the count of that request is twenty-five this season, and the fen entered the count and entered that the count is not a complaint.

> Entered at the third hour of the afternoon of the twelfth of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a standing list, read out whole and read back whole, with the hour on it:
>
> **What this body holds.**
> **What this body is owed.**
> **What this body does not know.**
> **What this body is looking after and cannot reach.**
>
> **Four lines. The first three have been in this book since the first year. The fourth was added on the ninth of last month and names no person and no thing in it and no number of things in it, and the clerk entered on the day it was added that it is a line and not a sentence and read it back that way, and it has not been improved on and is not to be.**

Then he asked whether anybody in that room wanted to change a word of it, which is the thing this holding does on the first of a month and has not done since the fourth line went in, and about eleven people were in the room and nobody said a word, and the fen entered that nobody said a word and entered the count of that, which is one, and entered that the count is one and is not going to become two without somebody putting their hand up.

Then he asked the third line out loud, on purpose, and about eleven people were in that room and the clerk was one of the eleven and he gave the reason first, which is that he was about to say something about the fourth one and could not do it in a room that had not been made to hear the third one.

"**What this body does not know. That is a line and it is the oldest line in this book and it is eleven years old and it has never got a number against it, and I have never once put a number against it, and I am going to do that this afternoon and I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to be forgiven for it either, because I have been asked about a form on a bench and I would rather say the count of the not-known while that is still the thing in the room.**"

"**It is four. It was four a month ago. It is not going to be three this month and this holding has not pretended anywhere in this book that it would be.** A flow under a floor four hundred yards from that gate and a fourth cart that went up a road in the eighth month and has not come down and that nobody asked about, and where fourteen days of a quarter went that nobody counted, and a thing a man of about thirty-five has refused to say for nineteen years, which is not ours and is not on our line and which I am putting on our line this afternoon because it has been in this yard and because somebody said it out loud in a room."

"**Four, and it is not one of the five things, because the four are things this body does not know and the five are things it is looking after and cannot reach, and the two lines are different and I am not going to let the two run together this month, and that is the reason the fourth line is not the third line, and the third line is not the fourth line, and if anybody in this room wants to reduce one of them to the other I would like them to say so in a yard and not in a book.**"

Nobody said the next part right. The reader read the four back into a room of about eleven and asked for his initial to be put against nothing, and the count of that request is twenty-six this season, and the fen entered the count and entered that the count is not a complaint.

Then he asked the list a question it has never been asked, and he gave the reason for asking it first, and about eleven people were in that room and the clerk was one of the eleven.

"**What is this body looking after and cannot reach. Now, in a yard, in the ordinary words, and not as a list of about nine.**"

Nobody answered for about a minute and then he answered it himself, and he said he was going to answer it himself first so that nobody in the room had to be the first one, and the fen entered that and entered that it was his practice and not a new thing.

"**One. A shelf four hundred miles off. Four houses, a yard, a wall with eight figures on it, a row of eleven faces and a row nobody has read, and a woman of fifty-four with a nail. That is a person at the end of it and this body has never been in the yard and has had four things come down that road at its own expense.**"

"**Two. A yard four hundred miles off, four hundred miles in the other direction from the first one, and the same wall, and the same nail, and eleven of the eleven faces were read with a rule on the twenty-fifth of a month two months back and two of them were not read and have not been read since and have a stake in the ground at each of them.**"

"**Three. A form on a bench in this room, on a table in this room, with a date and a well and a licensed man and a number on it, and a man of about eleven miles' road who has brought it in the rain and has not been answered.**"

"**Four. A woman in this room who has not been forgiven and has not asked, and who came nine miles on her own money and was refused a bed in one sentence for a reason that was not suspicion, and whose name is on the sixth page of this holding's book from this month forward.**"

"**Five. A name at the foot of a sheet in eleven miles that this body has no address for, and that this body has read out loud in a yard once this month and did not ask the man whether he wanted it read out loud.**"

And then he said the thing the day is for, and it is short, and about eleven people heard all of it.

"**Five things. Four lines. I am not going to write a fifth line, and I am going to say why in one sentence and then I am going to stop.**"

"**A line is a standing. A standing is a thing this holding has to keep. This holding has no charter and four pounds and fivepence and a pail it has lost, and a body that adds a line to a standing list because a thing arrived is a body that has stopped keeping a list and started keeping a diary.**"

> Entered at the fourth hour of the afternoon of the twelfth of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a standing list: **the standing list was read out and read back and the hour went on it, and the reader of this body asked it, for the first time in eleven years, what this body is looking after and cannot reach, in a yard, in the ordinary words.** Five things were named. **One, a shelf four hundred miles off. Two, a yard four hundred miles off. Three, a form on a bench in this room. Four, a woman in this room who has not been forgiven and has not asked. Five, a name at the foot of a sheet in eleven miles that this body has no address for.** Every one of the five has a person or an office at the end of it. **Four lines and five things, and the count of the difference is one.**
>
> **And entered on the same line, in the reader of this body's words, read back: the reader said that he is not going to write a fifth line, and gave the reason, which is that a line is a standing and a standing is a thing this holding has to keep, and that a body which adds a line to a standing list because a thing arrived is a body which has stopped keeping a list and started keeping a diary.**
>
> **And entered on the same line: the count of things this body is looking after and cannot reach is five and the count of lines is four, and the difference of one is entered as a difference and not as a decision, because this body has not decided which of the five it is going to stop looking after and is not going to decide it in a room with about eleven people in it.**

And then the reader of this body asked the seedwright of the trust, in the ordinary way and without any preamble at all, to be the person the engineer of record comes to when he has a figure he cannot defend, and she refused in one sentence and did not soften it and did not number it.

"**I am the person you bring a figure to when it is a figure. I am not the person you bring a thing to when it is not one, and you have known the difference between those two since the fourteenth of last month and so have I, and if you cannot hold the difference then you do not need me, you need a book, and you have one, and it is on the middle of this table and anybody may read it.**"

Nobody thanked her. She was not thanked and she had said before she was thanked that she was not going to be thanked, and the count of that standing in a season is fourteen, and the fen entered that she asked for nothing and did not ask to be thanked.

> Entered at the fifth hour of the afternoon of the twelfth of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a role: **the seedwright of the trust was asked, in the ordinary way and without preamble, to be the person the engineer of record comes to when he has a figure he cannot defend, and refused in one sentence.** The reader of this body was not in that room. Nobody thanked her and she did not ask to be thanked. **The fen entered that nobody thanked her and that she asked for nothing. The fen entered that this is the first time in eleven years that a person in this building has refused a role and not a figure, and that the refusal is the standing and not an exception to the standing, and that the count of that standing in a season is five.**

The engineer of record did not answer her. The fen entered that he did not answer her and entered that nobody else in that room answered her either, and entered the time between the question and the end of the room, which is about nine seconds, and entered that nine seconds is a figure and that this holding has got it because it counts everything and has never once been able to do anything with what it counts.

> Entered on the same line: **the engineer of record did not answer the refusal. Nobody in that room answered it. The fen entered the time between the question and the end of the room and did not improve it, and entered that the number of refusals a person in this building has made in a season is five, and entered that four of the five were about figures and this is the first one that was about a place.**

---

And then at about the seventh hour of the evening of the same day, in a yard of about nine, the reader of this body said the other half of it, and he said it to nine people and not to one, and he said the reason he was saying it out loud and not writing it before he said it.

"**This holding has spent a month learning that a body with no standing can still be asked to put its name to a form. It has spent a month learning that a man with a private record is a party. Neither of those two things was a surprise and both of them have been entered and I have watched them arrive.**"

"**And it has not yet learned the third thing, and the third thing is the one I would like somebody in four years to find this page for, and it is this: a person who is offered the job of being the one you tell is going to be offered it again, and is going to say no again, and the second no is easier and the third one is not.**"

"**I am not going to tell this yard what happened in a room on the twenty-fourth of last month and I am not going to tell it about a page and a form and a party on a return. I am going to say the one sentence and I am going to say it in a yard, because that is where a thing has to go before it is a thing everybody knows, and I have watched that take nine years once and I am not going to watch it take nine years again.**"

Nobody said the first part right. He read the sentence back into a yard of about nine and asked for his initial to be put against nothing, and the count of that request is twenty-seven this season, and the fen entered the count and entered that the count is not a complaint.

> Entered at the eighth hour of the evening of the twelfth of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a fault line: **the reader of this body said, in a yard of about nine, that this holding has spent a month learning that a body with no standing can still be asked to put its name to a form and a month learning that a man with a private record is a party, and has not yet learned the third thing, which is that a person who is offered the job of being the one you tell is going to be offered it again, and is going to say no again, and that the second no is easier and the third one is not.** He gave the reason for saying it out loud in a yard and not in the book first, **and entered that he did not tell the yard what happened in a room on the twenty-fourth of last month and did not tell it about a page and a form and a party on a return, and entered that a thing has to go into a yard before it is a thing everybody knows and that this holding has watched that take nine years once.**
>
> **And entered on the same line: the fourth line of the standing list is not to be reduced to the third, which is what this body does not know, and this body does not know four things today and did not know four a month ago, and the fen has entered the count of the not-known as it stands and has not turned it into a line.**

And so the twelfth of this month put five things on a page against a list of four and refused to make the list longer, and a woman of thirty-one refused a place in this holding's book and was not thanked for it, and a man of about fifty stood in a yard of about nine and said out loud that he does not know yet what happens the third time somebody is asked, **and the whole of what this holding has got against the difference of one is that it entered the difference as a difference and did not decide in a room with about eleven people in it which of the five it is going to stop looking after, and the fen is aware that not deciding is also a decision and has entered that it is aware of that, and has entered that it is not going to decide it this month either.**
