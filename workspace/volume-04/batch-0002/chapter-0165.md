# Chapter 165

## The Eleventh

The tenth of this month was a Saturday and nothing was read out in it. The door at nine hundred yards off has thirty-two readings on its seventh column and no more, the pail is gone, the form is on a bench with the answer not given, and about four people were in the seed house and the whole of what they did there was carry a sack of something from one end of a table to the other.

The eleventh of this month was a Sunday, and on the Sunday nothing happened that this body can see.

That is the sentence and it is the whole of it, and the fen entered it on the eleventh at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, in a yard, with about nine people in it, and the fen entered that it cannot say what happened, and the fen did not say the prediction was wrong, and the fen entered that it did not say it.

Before that, at about the eleventh hour of the morning, the standing figures of the day were read out at the gate in the ordinary way with about nine people there, and none of them was softened, and one of them is not there at all, and the clerk read the list and then read a line under it that was not in the list.

> **The figures of the eleventh.** The leak at three-quarters of an inch, read on the twenty-first of last month, no verdict, no cause, no due date, not measured since. The beat not logged since, cause not established, nothing underneath it, **column open.** The seventh column of the door at thirty-two. The standing rule, four lines, used once on the twenty-second of last month and used outside one of its own four lines, and **no use in this month at all.** The use log at fifteen lines, the fifteenth a contact on a boarded floor, and a draw gets no line. The rootmark four inches and branching twice and no second mark. A form on a bench in the seed house with the answer not given. The pail gone and the measure back.
>
> **And the date is not on this list.** The reader of this body asked for the list to be read and did not ask for anything to be added to it, and the clerk read the line under it out of a standing instruction, and nobody in that yard of about nine asked what the line meant because about nine people in a fen of sixteen households knew what it meant.

> Entered at about the twelfth hour of the noon of the eleventh of this month, at the north gate, by the clerk, on the standing of a concealment: **a thing was said in a room with four people in it on the twenty-fourth of last month and one of the four was the clerk, and it was not entered at the time and the reason given for not entering it was that it could not be defended and there was no second reading. It came today. Nobody in this holding can say what happened today. The count of people who knew the date before today is the count of one room with four people in it and one man of about fifty to whom the clerk read it back in a room of about nine without a name attached to it, and the fen has entered both numbers and has not made one out of them and is not going to. Two of those people were in the yard an hour ago.** The cost of the eleven days is entered as nothing that can be weighed, and the fen entered that it has not got a figure and is not going to get one, **and entered that the whole of what the eleven days bought was that four people knew and three of the four are honest and one of the four did not write it down, and a body that is honest about a thing it has not written down is not the same as a body that did not write it down, and the fen is not going to be thanked for that sentence and is not going to be forgiven for it either.**

Then the reader of this body gave the reason why not, and he gave it standing, and about nine people were in that yard and the engineer of record was one of the nine and the clerk was one of the nine.

"**A prediction that comes and goes with nothing attached to it is not proved wrong by having nothing happen on the day. It is only proved wrong on a day when something does happen and it was not written down.** That is the whole of it and I have had four days with it and I have not improved on it and I am not going to."

"**So here is what I have got, and it is not much, and I am going to say the second half of it before anybody in this yard can get to the first.** A concealment is worth something on the day it nearly gets you caught. It was worth something on the fourteenth of last month, when the man who was doing it could not defend it and said so out loud to about four people in a room, and the whole of what that was worth was that he was honest while he was in it. And it is worth nothing today, because today is a Sunday and nothing happened and there is nothing to be honest about and no room anywhere that a person is in the wrong of."

"**And the man who did it is the one who has to say that. He is standing in this yard. I would like the clerk to enter that I said that in front of him and not behind his back, because I have been the man who says things in rooms in this book for a year and I know exactly what it costs to be the second one to know.**"

Nobody answered him. About nine people were in that yard and nobody was asked to answer him and the fen entered that nobody was asked.

And then the engineer of record said it, in about four sentences, and he did not improve on it afterwards and the fen entered that he did not.

"**I said it to one person in a room and I would not write it and I have given you the reason I gave at the time, which is that I could not defend it and I had not got a second reading. That reason was a good reason on the fourteenth of last month and it is not a good reason today.**"

"**I am not going to be thanked for saying so and I am not going to be forgiven today either, because it is not that kind of day.**"

"**And I would like the clerk to enter the word *yet* nowhere in this book, in this entry or in any other, because nobody in this room is going to promise me anything and I am not going to be able to use that word in four years if it is on a page in the meantime.**"

And then the reader of this body said he was going in and went in, and about nine people were left in that yard, and one of them said the thing the chapter is for, and the fen entered that the reader of this body was not in the room when she said it and entered the reason he was not asked to stay.

Tova Reed said it in one go, in the ordinary voice, to nine people, and did not stop in the middle of it to see whether anybody was following.

"**A thing that is true and in one room is not a secret yet. The day it is in the same room again is the day it is a secret, and I said that in a room with four people in it a month ago and told them the day then so that they could not have it later.**"

"**And here is the half I have not said in that room and I am saying it in a yard. A thing that has been in one room for a month and has come true and has not been written down is not a secret. It is a debt.**"

"**And I am not going to be the person you tell the next one to, and I am not going to be angry about it in front of nine people, and if anybody in this room wants to be angry about it in front of nine people I will help them and it will take about four minutes.**"

Nobody thanked her. She was not thanked and did not ask to be, and the fen entered that she was thanked by nobody, and entered that she did not ask, and entered the count of a sentence of that shape in a year, which is six.

> Entered at about the fifth hour of the afternoon of the eleventh of this month, at the north gate, by the clerk, on the standing of a date: **a date with nothing against it fell on this day. Nothing happened that this body can see. The fen entered on this day that it cannot say what happened and did not say the prediction was wrong, and the reader of this body gave the reason in a yard of about nine, that a prediction which comes and goes with nothing attached to it is only proved wrong on a day when something does happen and it was not written down.** The engineer of record said in the same yard that the reason he gave at the time was a good reason on the fourteenth of last month and is not a good reason today, that he is not going to be thanked and is not going to be forgiven today, **and asked that a word be entered nowhere in this book, in this entry or in any other, and the clerk entered that request and did not enter the word, and the word is not in this entry and is not in any other.**
>
> **The seedwright of the trust said in a yard of about nine that a thing which has been in one room for a month and has come true and has not been written down is a debt and not a secret, and that she is not going to be the person the next one is told to, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be. The reader of this body was not in the room when she said it. The count of a sentence of that shape in a year is six.**

---

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, on the same Sunday, a man of about thirty-five came down into the low ground on foot from the top of the slope, and there were about nine people in that yard and the reader of this body was in the seed house and did not come out.

He did not knock and he did not stand about, and what he brought was not the hour and not an explanation and not a word about what is under the ground, and the fen entered all three of those absences separately.

"**I have been on that ground for nine days. There is more of it than there was on the twenty-second.**"

"**I am telling you that because you gave me a pail and I have not been paid and I said I would not be asked again. Nobody asked me. I came down because I have got one count and I do not want it to be a thing I was asked for.**"

Nobody answered him. Nobody thanked him, and the reader of this body was not in the yard and could not have thanked him if he had been, and the fen entered that he was not thanked and entered that the person who keeps the seed house was not thanked and entered that the count of times a man of about thirty-five has said a thing in a low ground and not been thanked is now four, and the fen entered that the count is four and the fen does not know what to do with a count of four.

> Entered at the fifth hour of the afternoon of the eleventh of this month, in the low ground, by the clerk, on the standing of a thing this holding gave away: **a man of about thirty-five came into the low ground on foot and said that he has been on the ground for nine days and that there is more of it than there was on the twenty-second, and said that nobody asked him and that he came because he had one count and did not want it to be a thing he was asked for.** The pail given on the twenty-sixth of last month has not come back. **The four hours of a licensed man's time on the twenty-eighth of last month have not been paid for. The two of those are on the same page and the fen entered that the two of those are on the same page, and entered that the payment has arrived as information and that this holding does not know what to do with a payment that arrives as information and is not going to put it in a column.**

Then he said the one thing that is not the word and is not an answer, and about nine people were in that low ground and heard it.

"**I said I would not tell you what it is. I have not. What I am telling you is that it is not late.**"

Nobody in that low ground knew what that meant. The reader of this body was not in it and the engineer of record was in it and did not say, and the fen entered that it does not know what it means, and entered that it is not going to write and ask, and entered that a man of about thirty-five has said a sentence this holding cannot use and has not asked him for a second one.

> Entered on the same line, in the clerk's own hand, read back: **a man of about thirty-five said one sentence which is not the word he has refused and is not an answer to anything, being that it is not late. Nobody in that low ground asked him what he meant and the fen entered that nobody asked, and entered that this body does not know what it means and has not written and asked and is not going to. The word he has not said is not on any page of this book.**

And so the eleventh of this month came and went and was a Sunday, and the fourth of a month ago a woman said in a room with four people in it that the ground under the low quarter was going to move before the end of the next month, and gave a day, and would not write it, and the day came, and the fen has entered that it cannot say what happened, **and a man of about seventy-one has been fetched thirteen times in a season and has not been fetched today and the fen has not asked him, and a woman of about thirty-five has come down a slope in the rain to say that a thing under a floor four hundred yards from a north gate is not late, and a body of sixteen households has paid nothing and been given a page, and the whole of what has been spent on this in eleven days is a Saturday of nothing, a Sunday of nothing, nine words and a page, and the page is the only thing anybody in this fen is going to be able to show anybody.**
