# Chapter 163

## A Party That Named Itself

A return came on the ordinary post on the seventh of this month and it is four lines and it is correct, and the third line has four words in it that are the name of a party, and a document is the only place a body can be a party, and this holding has been looking for one of those since the eighteenth of last month.

Lissa Vale read it out whole in the seed house with the bay doors shut, and about eleven people were in the room, and the reader of this body read it back with the hour on it and asked for his initial to be put against nothing, and the count of that request is twenty-three this season.

> Return of a supply made at no price. Entered by the district on the ordinary post, on the seventh of this month, in the office's hand, and the form is in order, and the district has accepted it and entered it and the entry is the office's and not anybody else's:
>
> **One. Water has been supplied into the low quarter at no price between the second and the seventh of this month, out of a source this return does not name.**
>
> **Two. No price has been charged and no pail has been sold and the water was put where it was put and not sold.**
>
> **Three. The supply was made by the Pale Orchard, a party.**
>
> **Four. This return is made in the ordinary course and no part of it is a finding against any person. A party supplying at no price is a party and is entered as one, and a party is not a holder, and a party has the standing of a party and not of a holder and not of an office.**

Nobody in that room said the word *gift* and the fen entered that nobody said it, and entered that the word is not in the return, and entered that this holding has entered it in its own book twice today for the sole purpose of not using it, **because a thing somebody gave you is a thing you can shake hands about and this is four lines on the ordinary post from an office nine miles off and there is nobody in it to shake hands with.**

Then the woman who does the sums did the sum, or rather she did the two halves of it, and the second half is the whole of the chapter.

"**The low quarter had five wells off on the fourth day of this month. That is on this page and it is this holding's own count and it is not an official one and there is no form for it. From the second to the seventh that quarter has had the licensed supply at a shilling a pail, and for every one of those six days it has also had water at no price out of a source nobody has named, from a party that has named itself, and the number of pails in the second supply is not in anybody's book because there was no gate.**"

"**I can add the two. I cannot divide them. And I am going to say the reason slowly, and then I am going to stop, and I would like the clerk to enter the reason and not enter a figure.**"

"**The second one did not come through a gate and nobody counted it. I have got a licensed figure on a hundredweight and a gate count on a pail and they agree, and last month the person who keeps the money entered that a pail is not a hundredweight and that agreeing is not the same as either of them being right, and I said it again in this room on the second and it is still true. Now I have a third thing which is not a number at all, it is a week.**"

"**So a figure that is the sum of two things one of which was never measured is not a figure. It is a shape. And I am not going to enter it and I am not going to be asked to, and if anybody in this room asks me for it I am going to say the sentence again and I am not going to be thanked for saying it.**"

> Entered at the first hour of the afternoon of the seventh of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a return and of a limit: **a party named as a supplying party on a return of a supply made at no price into the low quarter between the second and the seventh of this month, out of a source the return does not name. The form is in order and the district has accepted it and entered it. This holding has no standing over any part of it and can check no word of it.** The number of pails supplied at no price is in no book. **The person who keeps the money stated that the two supplies into that quarter can be added and cannot be divided, and that a figure which is the sum of two things one of which was never measured is not a figure, and entered that she will not enter it and will not be asked to.**

And the third of the three not-askings was entered on the same page, which is a thing the reader of this body did deliberately and about nine people watched him do.

"**I am going to put three lines next to each other and I have been waiting nine days to have a third one, and the three of them are the whole of what this holding has not asked about, and I would like somebody in four years to read them side by side and tell me what sort of body this is.**"

> **One. This body. A price of a hundredweight of compost came down a penny on a sheet with a firm's name at the head of it on the first of last month, and this holding did not cause it and has not written and asked about it, and has entered that it does not know which of three explanations it is.**
>
> **Two. The trust. The seedwright of the trust has carried a figure this body has asked her for twice in eleven years and has entered neither, and has said twice in front of witnesses that a thing one person can carry is not a thing a body can be asked to carry, and the trust has not written and asked either.**
>
> **Three. The haulier. A man with a cart told this body on the twenty-first of last month that the holder's man has a book and has not said anything to him that he could repeat, and that he did not ask and is not going to. That was a month ago and he has not asked and this holding has not asked him whether he has since.**
>
> **Entered: the count of the three is three, they are not to be added to the count of the things this holding has been told no by, which stands at six, and the reader of this body stated that he put them side by side on purpose and that putting them side by side is the only thing this holding can do with all three at once.**

---

And then at about the sixth hour of the evening of the eighth of this month the man of the north row with the cough came back from the low quarter with six sentences from a household's own mouth, and he said he had walked out and back in one day again and had not asked anybody anything, and the fen entered that he had not.

"**Four households in that quarter were saying a thing in a yard on the seventeenth of last month. It was the same sentence in the same words in four mouths, and the engineer of record answered it four times in the same words, and the count of that is four and the count of people in that quarter is four hundred and forty-one and the fen is not going to divide one into the other.**"

"**On the twenty-third of last month one of them said the other thing, that she does not know it is this holding, and four men had said the road was taking it off them and she was not going to be the fifth. On the seventh of this month I stood in that yard and the same four men were standing in it and the sentence was gone.**"

"**And one of the four said why, and she said it in about four words and she said it to me and not to anybody else, and I have got it exactly and I am giving it exactly.**"

> **They gave us water. A person does not say a thing like that with water in the house.**

"**Nobody at that gate asked her whether she still thinks it. I did not ask her. I stood there and I wrote it down and I did not ask her, and I have been turning it over on the nine miles back and I do not know why I did not, and I am not going to pretend I did not know why.**"

"**And the fen is glad. I want that in the book in my own words and I am not going to pretend I am not. Four people in that quarter have stopped saying the thing they were saying and this holding is glad about it, and a body being glad is not a column and there is no form for it, and I do not know what a book does with a thing it is glad about that it cannot check.**"

> Entered at the seventh hour of the evening of the eighth of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a return: **four households in the low quarter that had been saying one sentence in the same words have stopped saying it, one of them gave the reason that they were given water and that a person does not say a thing like that with water in the house, nobody at that gate asked her whether she still thought it, and the fen has entered that nobody asked and entered that this body is glad and entered that being glad is a thing it has no column for.**

And then the man of about seventy was fetched on the afternoon of the seventh, the thirteenth time in a season this body has brought him out to a thing, and he was given the four lines and the two halves of the sum in that order, and he was asked one question and nobody could answer it.

"**Is water at no price better than water at a price.**"

Nobody answered him. The reader of this body did not answer it and did not ask him what it was about and did not explain it to anybody afterwards, and the fen entered that he did not and entered the reason, which is that it is his standing and has never once cost anybody anything.

Then the man of about seventy said four words, and the fen does not know what they were about and has entered that it does not know.

"**Ask the giver's name.**"

> Entered at the fourth hour of the afternoon of the seventh of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk: **a man of seventy-one was brought out for the thirteenth time in a season, was given the four lines of a return and the two halves of a sum, and was asked one question: is water at no price better than water at a price. Nobody in that room could answer it and nobody was asked to.** He then said four words: ask the giver's name. **The fen does not know what they were about and has entered that it does not know. Nobody explained him, he was not asked what it was about, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.**

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is on the page and that he had been carrying since the second, and about eleven people were in that room and the clerk was one of them.

"**I want to say the worst of it and I want to say it in the plainest words I have got. For a month this holding has been telling a yard of about twenty-five that it cannot put a single name on a single form about a quarter of four hundred and forty-one people, and that is a true thing and it is a hard thing to live with and about forty people have accepted it.**"

"**And since the second of this month there is a name on a form nine miles off. It is not ours, we did not put it there, it names a party and not a holder, and we cannot check one word of it and we have no standing over any part of it, and the supply it describes went into the same quarter on the same days as the supply we can do the sum on.**"

"**So a body that cannot name a party on a form is now in a worse position than it was a week ago, because there is a party on a form that this holding cannot name either, and the two of them are going to be read in the same room one day, and the two of them are going to be read in the same room by somebody who has not been born yet.**"

"**And I am not going to say the second half, and I want it understood that I have thought about it and I have decided not to say it, and that is a decision and not an oversight.**"

> Entered at the fifth hour of the afternoon of the seventh of this month, in the seed house, by the clerk, on the standing of a position: **this holding has entered that it cannot name a party on a form and cannot check a party named on a form, and that the two positions are going to be read in the same room one day, and that this holding has no standing over either.** The reader of this body stated that he had considered saying a second thing and had decided not to, **and entered that the decision was made and not overlooked, and did not enter what the second thing was.**

And so a party that put water into a quarter where five wells are off put its name in a document on the ordinary post, and gave the water away, and charged nothing, and has not asked this holding for anything and cannot, and this holding can neither refuse it nor check it nor thank anybody for it, **and four people in that quarter have stopped saying the thing they were saying and are glad about it and cannot say why, and the man of seventy-one has said four words about a giver, and the fen has entered all of it, and the count of the not-askings is three and the three are on a page side by side with a reader's name on it, and the party on a return nine miles off is a fourth of the four and is not on that page and is not going to be put on it, and it is the only one of the four that is still open, and this holding entered on the seventh of this month that it has not asked about it and has not written and asked and is not going to.**
