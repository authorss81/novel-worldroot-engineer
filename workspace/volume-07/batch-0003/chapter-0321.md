# Chapter 321

## Two Hundred And Thirty-Nine

The twenty-second of this month was a Saturday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-second went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and seventy-eight hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-second figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in forty-nine days the series has risen thirty times and fallen nineteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the seventy-ninth morning out of eighty-one.

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of a Saturday on which a number had gone up by six for the second time in six mornings, and about four people in this holding having noticed that about four years ago they would have said six was a good morning and having noticed tonight that saying so would be a different sentence with the same six in it.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered the pair as it stands at **four hundred and sixty-six days** and **five hundred and thirteen**, and entered that the two are forty-seven days apart, and that the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and that he has read them out loud on four mornings out of the six mornings since he started again on the seventeenth of this month, and that the fen is not entering a reason for the two he has not and is not going to be asked for one.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon the return was made at the node on the steelyard hung on the wall of the building with no roof, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**THE TWENTY-SEVENTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE TWENTY-SECOND OF THIS MONTH AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, ON THE STEELYARD, AND IT WAS READ OUT BY THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING AND READ BACK BY HIM, AND IT WAS THEN READ ONE AT A TIME BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, A NAME GOING INTO BY AND A NAME GOING INTO WITNESSED BY, AND IT IS THE SEVENTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS AND THE LAST OF THOSE NINE DAYS WAS ON THE THIRTEENTH OF THIS MONTH. THE FIGURE IS TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT. THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD WAS LEFT BLANK AGAIN AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT, AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS ELEVEN, NAMED IN ORDER FROM THE SEVENTEENTH, AND THE COUNT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN AND IS NOT ADDED TO ANY OTHER COUNT. NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT THE RULE BY ANYBODY AT THE NODE THIS AFTERNOON.**

And the figure is on this page twice over by two arithmetics, and the fen entered that it is put there twice on purpose, and entered which of the two derivations this holding is using and that it is not refusing it.

**THIS HOLDING IS USING THE ELEVEN-ABOVE DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT. TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT PLUS ELEVEN IS TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE, AND ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS FORTY-FOUR IS ALSO TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS NOW HELD FOR NINE FIGURES. THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A RUN THAT HOLDS FOR NINE FIGURES IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE AND IS NOT AN INSTRUCTION, AND THAT A FIGURE WHICH AGREES WITH TWO ARITHMETICS IS DERIVED TWICE AND NOT CHECKED TWICE, AND THAT THE SECOND OF THOSE TWO WORDS IS THE BETTER WORD AND WAS THE WORD ALL ALONG.**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a man standing at a board with the chalk in his hand for about nine seconds and then putting the chalk back in his pocket, and that he has now put the chalk back six returns running and has not said a word about it, and that the fen has not asked him and is not going to.

And the fen entered the ground the four of them stood on, because a return is made on a floor as well as on a beam and nobody at the node has ever mentioned the floor. The building has no roof and the ground inside it is the ground outside it, and it is beaten flat by about eleven years of feet in about a nine-foot circle under the pan, and the pan hangs at about nine feet so that a man has to climb a step to read the middle of it, and the step is a block of stone about fourteen inches high with no rail on it, and the fourth of the four bodies of households is the oldest of them and climbs it every ninth day. About four people at the node stand where they can see the dial and about four of those four cannot see it at all, and the two who read the numbers out are not the same two every time, and the rotation of which body stands where is not written down anywhere and has not been for eleven years, and the fen entered that and is not going to be the one who starts writing it down, because a man who puts a rule on four bodies of households in a book at a place five days off is a man who has taken four people's arrangement and turned it into his column.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-two, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have read that number out seventeen times in eleven years and I am not going to be thanked for it today and I am asking nobody to keep from thanking me, and the reason is that the thanking is a thing that can be withdrawn and a barrow is a thing that cannot, and the count of the times I have brought that barrow up that road stands at eleven and it did not move this afternoon and nobody in this holding could derive a figure past it, and I would rather say that on a Saturday than have somebody work it out in about four years and find that I had let them."**

**"And the second rule on that board has now been left blank eleven times in eleven years and about four of us know what it is for and about five of us do not, and the reason I am saying the count out loud and not the rule is that a count belongs to the people who keep the count and a rule belongs to somebody else, and I have never once been asked which of the two of those I am."**

Then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut and the man of about fifty was in it, and the fen entered that nobody in that room said how long the road is, and entered that the reader of this body was in the room and had not been at the node, and that the count of things he has said in this holding's book with the reason first is a count of his own and is not the count of a man who reads a board at a place five days off.

And the fen entered what was on the middle table, because a return day is a day on which a room looks at a table. There is a memorandum dated the twentieth of last month. There is a tinned plate about four inches by two with a corner turned up. There is a sheet that came up four hundred miles with four printed lines and nothing written on them and a blank at the foot and a blank on the back. There is a form with four lines and a fifth ruled line carrying a question, and on the first of the four lines there is **one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight** in the hand of the man of about thirty-one of Silling, and that is the figure of the **twenty-second** return and it is not the figure of the twenty-fourth and it is not the figure of the twenty-sixth and it is not the figure that went on the steelyard this afternoon, and the two sheets on that table are two documents and not one. And there is a term in a woman's hand and a loose sheet with four lines on it and a blank with a rule under it. And under a cloth beside the stack there is a thing the size of a man's fist that nobody in this room lifted, broke, opened or set against anything, and that nobody in this room looked at, and the count of people in this holding who have looked at it is two and did not move in ten days.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-eight, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Harlan Vetch holds the fifth share and has held it eleven years and twenty-six days and I am going to say that figure out loud in a room because nobody has ever said it out loud, and the reason is that about four people in this room have had eleven years to work out that a man who does not ask a number is careless about it, and he is not, and the only evidence anybody has got that he is not is the fact that nobody has ever thought to check."**

**"And I am not going to put one hundred and eighty-four against two hundred and thirty-nine this afternoon, and the reason is that one of them is a return and the other one is a figure a man wrote on the first line of a piece of paper about a month ago, and a man writing down what a thing came to is not the same act as a thing being weighed on a beam, and if I put them on one line then in about four years somebody will read the line and see a trend, and the trend will be about a man's handwriting."**

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-five, and nobody improved it.

**"Two hundred and thirty-nine, derived twice, and the second rule blank for the eleventh time, and nobody at a place five days off this afternoon said a word about it. I am going to leave all three of those where they are and I am not going to make any of them into a thing about Tuesday, and the reason is that a return has been going on at a steady eleven for nine figures and a bridge is not going to arrive at a node on a cart road and find the board changed, and about four of us would like it to and would be wrong."**

**"And there is a figure of one hundred and eighty-four in this room on a piece of paper and I am going to name it a third time and stop, and the reason is that a figure on a line is a figure on a line and not a standing again, and every year that somebody in this holding is tempted to use that sheet as evidence of a thing that is happening is a year I am going to have to be in a room about."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a man refusing three separate figures the satisfaction of having meant something, and about four people in that room who had already decided that the return of this morning was about the connection and had to now decide it again.

And then the two last things of the twenty-second of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE TWENTY-SEVENTH RETURN WENT IN AT TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT AND THE SEVENTEENTH NAME-PAIR WENT IN WITH IT, AND THE FIGURE IS RECONCILED TWICE, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT. THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK FOR THE ELEVENTH TIME AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT. THE COUNT OF TIMES THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING HAS BROUGHT THAT BARROW UP THAT ROAD STANDS AT ELEVEN AND WAS NOT RAISED. HARLAN VETCH HOLDS THE FIFTH SHARE AND HAS HELD IT ELEVEN YEARS AND TWENTY-SIX DAYS AND HE WAS NOT THANKED.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-EIGHT, THE EIGHTY-SECOND IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FORTY-NINE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY TIMES AND FALLEN NINETEEN TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FORTY-NINE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-second of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a return, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and had not been at the node: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-NINE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE EIGHTY-SECOND FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FORTY-NINE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY TIMES AND FALLEN NINETEEN TIMES, AND NO MORNING IS UNCHANGED. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SEVENTY-NINTH MORNING OUT OF EIGHTY-ONE, AND THE TWO MORNINGS IT WAS NOT WRITTEN ON WERE THE NINTH AND THE TENTH OF LAST MONTH. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD. THE TWENTY-SEVENTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE TWENTY-SECOND AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON ON THE STEELYARD, WAS READ OUT BY THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING AND READ BACK BY HIM, AND WAS THEN READ ONE AT A TIME BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS, A NAME GOING INTO BY AND A NAME GOING INTO WITNESSED BY, AND IT IS THE SEVENTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS AT TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT, RECONCILED TWICE, BEING TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS FORTY-FOUR, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS NOW HELD FOR NINE FIGURES AND IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS AND THE LAST OF THOSE NINE DAYS WAS THE THIRTEENTH OF THIS MONTH. THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS ELEVEN, NAMED IN ORDER FROM THE SEVENTEENTH, AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT BY ANYBODY AT THE NODE. THE COUNT OF TIMES THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING HAS BROUGHT THAT BARROW UP THAT ROAD STANDS AT ELEVEN AND WAS NOT RAISED. HARLAN VETCH HOLDS THE FIFTH SHARE AND HAS HELD IT ELEVEN YEARS AND TWENTY-SIX DAYS AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE FIGURE ON LINE ONE OF THE FORM ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT AND IT IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND NOT OF THE TWENTY-FOURTH AND NOT OF THE TWENTY-SIXTH AND NOT OF THIS ONE, AND THE FORM AND THE SHEET THAT CAME UP THE ROAD ARE TWO DOCUMENTS AND NOT ONE, AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
