# Chapter 317

## A Short One, And It Works

The eighteenth of this month was a Tuesday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the eighteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same. The rota stands at **fifty-eight mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty-nine not**, and that is the same standing it stood at on the fourteenth and it has not moved on any of the four mornings since, and a rota does not go up on a morning a line did not come round, and the fields above Marden did not wait this morning because the fourth line did not come round this morning.

**Six hundred and seventy-four hundredweight.**

That is the seventy-eighth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in forty-five days the series has risen twenty-eight times and fallen seventeen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventy-six days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the seventy-fifth morning out of seventy-seven.

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of a man who had read a number up off the last one by five for the second time running and had started, in about nine people, to think of the two of them as a habit.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and sixty-two days** and **five hundred and nine**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on two mornings running and has entered no reason for either, because a man putting a reason in a book every time he opens his mouth in front of four people would put one there eventually.

And the second line was worked, and then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the door of the seed house was shut and about nine people were in it, and the reader of this body was sitting with his left forearm on the sill under the one window with his hand open and his palm down, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it.

And then it was done, and it worked, and the fen entered all of it.

**A SHORT CONNECTION WAS MADE AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON BETWEEN A LIVING ANCHOR IN THIS ROOM AND A LIVING ANCHOR IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT THAT IS FOUR MILES AND MORE AWAY, AND IT RAN BOTH WAYS FOR ABOUT FOUR MINUTES, AND THE PERSON AT THE FAR END WAS A PERSON HOLDING A PATTERN FOR FOUR, AND PERSONS IN THIS HOLDING WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR STAND AT TWO, AND ONE OF THE TWO IS THE READER OF THIS BODY, AND THE FAR END WAS NOT HIM. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED ANYBODY AT THAT FAR END IS NIL AND IS NIL. NO VALVE WAS OPENED AND NO SEQUENCE AND NO PART CONTAINING A SEQUENCE AND NO CRADLE WAS SET AGAINST ANYTHING, AND THE KEY TO THAT VALVE IS NOT IN THIS HOLDING, AND A THING UNDER A CLOTH ON THE MIDDLE TABLE WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. ABOUT FOUR MINUTES IS A FIGURE ABOUT A BRIDGE AND IS NOT A FIGURE ABOUT WHAT ANY MAN WILL BE ABLE TO DO IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT CLAIMS AND THAT THE FIRST ONE IS THE ONLY ONE THAT CAN BE CHECKED.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man in nine people finding out that the hardest thing he had ever done in a room took four minutes and did not ask anything of the room at all, and that about four of the nine had not spoken and would not be asked to.

And the fen entered what it felt like, because the shape of the thing is not a marvel and the shape of the thing is a man on a sill with his hand open, and what came through in both directions was not a voice and not a picture and not anybody's memory. It was a temperature. The near end was eleven degrees warmer than it had been an hour before, because the room had a fire lit in it and about four people had opened the door in the last ten minutes. The far end was about three degrees colder and it was colder in a way that moved, which is what a person standing near a shut window on a cold afternoon feels like from the other side of the ground. And under the temperature there was a wetness, which was the ground at the far end being wetter than the ground at the near end by about the amount a person can tell by the weight of a boot. And under the wetness there was one other thing, and the fen entered that it was the only one of the three that was not a measurement, and that it was the shape of somebody holding on, and that the reader of this body has never described that to anybody in his life and is not going to start in a room of nine at about the fifth hour of a Tuesday.

And then the fen entered what it cost, which is the half of the afternoon nobody in that room was looking at, and which is the whole of why a bridge is a thing people argue about.

He could not feel his own left hand for about a minute and a half after the far end let go. He has the whole of it, all four inches of it, and it is warm along about two of them, and by the time the others in the room had stopped looking at his forearm it had gone back to what it always is, which is not nothing. The four inches branch twice and are a record of two grafts and a road and about eleven days in a shed, and a mark that is warm is not a mark, and a headache is not a mark, and a bridge is not a mark, and anything that wants to count a bridge as a fifth account of that arm is counting a thing that is not on the arm. He did not sleep well on the night after and has not slept well on the two nights since, and the count of lines in the use log is fifteen and did not move and the reason for the use log was given once and is not going to be given again in a season.

And the half of the cost that is not his, and that this holding cannot put a figure on at all, is that the person at the far end was holding a pattern for four, and that of the two people in this holding who can do that, he is the one who can walk out of the ground afterwards. He was not in that room. He does not know who was in that room. About four people were, and the door of that room was shut, and nobody came out of it while it was running, and when he came out into the fen at about the fifth hour there was nobody at the near end of that path and nobody at the far end of it, and the path is four miles and a half and he walked none of it.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is six, and it is her own and nobody improved it.

**"I have got about forty people at the end of an embankment who are going to be asked in about a week by somebody whether it is safe, and I have been the one who has said the word safe in this holding's rooms and I am not going to say it again today, and the reason I am not is that I have just watched a man hold a thing open for four minutes with a room of nine behind him and nobody at the far end of it in this building, and he did not know either."**

**"And the only thing I am going to ask for, and I am asking it of him and not of this room, is that whoever is at the far end of the next one of these is asked first and can say no, and that a no stops it there. A thing that two people hold can be let go of by one of them. A thing that one person holds cannot be let go of at the far end by anybody, and that is not a small difference and it is the only part of today I want written down."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-four, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is nothing on my table for that, and I am going to say the whole of what my table has, and the reason is that about four of you have been waiting since the sixth of this month for me to produce a piece of paper for it. I have a form with four lines and a fifth ruled line and a figure on the first line that belongs to a return in another month. I have a sheet that came up four hundred miles with four printed lines and a blank at the foot and a blank on the back. Neither of them is about this, both of them are about the same office and the same line, and I have been treating them for a month as though they were about this."**

**"And I am not going to write a new column for it today, and the reason is that a column is for a thing that happens again. I have not got the second one yet, and a column with one line in it is a note, and this room has about nine notes in it already."**

Nobody improved one item of that, and the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in the line of asking that Assembly for a figure is nil and is nil, and the two counts are not added to one another.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-one, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say the two halves of that out loud because about four of you are going to tell one of them in about four years. It worked. And it cost me about a minute and a half of the feeling in my own hand, and I have not slept well since, and the mark on this arm is four inches and branches twice and it is the same four inches it was this morning, and a mark that is warm is not a mark and a bridge is not a mark."**

**"And the second half is that I do not know who held the other end, and I am not going to find out this week and I am not going to write it in a book, and the reason is that a name at the top of a page about a thing that cost somebody something is a thing that gets read out in a room in about four years by a person who was not in this room."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a woman who had asked for one thing and a man who had said he would not put a name on a page, and about nine people who had watched a thing work and had not been asked to be pleased about it.

And then the two last things of the eighteenth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**A SHORT CONNECTION WAS MADE IN A ROOM AND IT WORKED AND IT RAN BOTH WAYS FOR ABOUT FOUR MINUTES, AND THE PERSON AT THE FAR END WAS HOLDING A PATTERN FOR FOUR AND WAS NOT THE READER OF THIS BODY, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS HE HAS ASKED ANYBODY AT THAT FAR END IS NIL AND IS NIL. NO VALVE WAS OPENED. A PERSON IS NEITHER A SEQUENCE NOR A CRADLE, AND ABOUT FOUR MINUTES IS A FIGURE ABOUT A BRIDGE AND NOT A FIGURE ABOUT FOUR YEARS.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FOUR, THE SEVENTY-EIGHTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FORTY-FIVE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-EIGHT TIMES AND FALLEN SEVENTEEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY-SIX DAYS. THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SEVENTY-EIGHTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FORTY-FIVE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-EIGHT TIMES AND FALLEN SEVENTEEN TIMES. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND NINE DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD.
