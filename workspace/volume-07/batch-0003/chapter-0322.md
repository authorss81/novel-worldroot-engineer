# Chapter 322

## A Question That Is Not About Water

The twenty-third of this month was a Sunday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-third went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and seventy-three hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-third figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in fifty days the series has risen thirty times and fallen twenty times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty-one days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the eightieth morning out of eighty-two.

Nothing was said for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what was in it, and it was made of a Sunday on which a number had come down off the last one by five, and about four people in this holding having worked out over the last ten days that about four of those numbers did not matter, and the fen entered that about four of them have not told anybody that either.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and sixty-seven days** and **five hundred and fourteen**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on four mornings out of the seven mornings since he started again on the seventeenth of this month, and that about four people in this holding still do not know that he started again.

And the third line was worked, and the fen entered what it is, because a Sunday third line is a morning about two gates and six feet of wall and it takes about an hour and a quarter with four people on it. The man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it. The two above the Marden headland were not there and the fourth line did not come round and the six feet of wall between the two gates took water and lost it at about the rate it has been taking and losing water since the twenty-sixth of last month, and nobody wrote that rate down anywhere before the second hour this morning and nobody wrote it down this morning either.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and Soren Rill was in it, and he had come four miles and a half on foot and at his own cost, and this is the fourth time in a month, and the fen entered that about four people in this holding have stopped counting, and that the count of the miles is not a count this holding keeps.

And the fen entered that he was wet to about the thigh, and that the road is four miles and a half with about a mile and a bit of it in a cutting that holds water from about the eighth to about the fourth hour on a wet day, and that he had come through that cutting at about the third hour of the afternoon, and that nobody asked him whether he would rather have come in the morning and that the reason nobody asked is that if anybody in this holding starts asking a man from four miles and a half how he prefers to travel then the asking is about the road and it is not about the road.

And then the question came, and it came the other way, and the fen entered the whole of it.

**SOREN RILL ASKED ONE QUESTION AND HE ASKED IT AT THE START, BEFORE HE SATED DOWN, AND THE REASON CAME BEFORE THE QUESTION, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS NINE, AND IT IS HIS OWN, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE QUESTION IS NOT ABOUT WATER. THE QUESTION IS THIS: IF THE PERSON AT THE FAR END LETS GO OF IT, DOES ANYTHING AT THIS END STOP. AND THE SECOND HALF OF IT IS THIS: IF THEY LET GO, WE WILL NOT BE ABLE TO TELL YOU WHO DID IT, AND WE WILL NOT BE ABLE TO TELL ANYBODY ELSE EITHER, AND ABOUT FORTY PEOPLE ARE GOING TO WANT TO KNOW, AND IS THAT ACCEPTABLE TO YOU, AND IF IT IS NOT, WHAT ARE YOU GOING TO DO WITH THE NOT KNOWING.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people in a room in this holding hearing the second half of a question from a man who has to walk four miles and a half back to about forty people who are going to ask it, and neither half of it being about water.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of about four people in this room who had spent ten days working out what a thing was worth and about four who had spent ten days working out what it costs, and that a man from four miles and a half away had walked in and put a third thing on the table that neither of those four had thought of, and that the third thing was the name of a person.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-six, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it, and the fen entered that he did not improve it and that not improving a thing that took a man four miles and a half to bring is a thing that costs something and that the cost was his.

**"I am not going to improve on that question and I am going to say why, and the reason is that it is already the shape of the question and I have got about four phrases in this holding's books that would have made it sound tidier, and every one of those four phrases has been a man deciding on behalf of somebody what a question meant before it got here."**

**"The answer to the first half is yes, and it is yes because a connection is a thing two people are holding and if one of them lets go there is nothing for the other one to be holding, and that is not an answer I have got a figure for and it is the only thing about Tuesday that is not a figure. The second half I am not going to answer in this room, and the reason is that the second half is a question about what happens to a person who does something we cannot record, and the person it is about is not in this room, and about forty people at the other end of that path are going to be told about it before we are, and they are going to be told it by a man who walked in here and handed it over, and I would like about an hour before anybody in this holding says a word about it."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-nine, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have got a book with a page at the back of it that has nothing on it and I have never once written on it, and I have just found out what the page is for, and the reason I am saying that in a room is that a man who keeps a book for four years does not have a page at the back for a figure and has a page at the back for the thing that is going to be asked about later, and I have had that page for four years without knowing it."**

**"And I am going to say the other half of it, which is that the question that was just asked in this room is not written down anywhere, and the reason I am not writing it down is that it is not mine. It is the first thing anybody has said into this room in a month that came from four miles and a half away with a person attached to it, and if I put it in a book then in about four years somebody in this holding is going to read it and find out that a question is a thing that gets collected, and I do not want a question to be a thing that gets collected. I have four books and not one of them has got a question in it and I intend to keep it that way."**

**"And I am not going to write on the blank page this afternoon. I am going to write on it the day somebody at that far end lets go, and not the day we find out that somebody did, because those are two different days and about four years from now a man is going to be able to tell which of them this room was on, and I would like the page to be able to tell him."**

Nobody improved one item of that, and the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is twelve and did not move this afternoon because this was a room, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in the line of asking that Assembly for a figure is nil and is nil and did not move, and he asked nobody one in this room this afternoon and the question came the other way.

And the fen entered the hour, and what about nine people did for about half of it, because nobody in this holding is going to say this and it happened. The man who had brought the question stood at the wall by the door with his wet coat over his arm and did not sit down for about forty minutes. About four people in the room looked at the coat and about four looked at the man. One of the nine put his hand flat on the loose sheet with four lines on it and left it there for about nine seconds and then took it off it, and nobody said anything about that either, and the fen entered both of those and is not going to improve either of them into a scene.

And then the fen entered the one thing about that room that a reader in nine people would want to know and nobody in the room is going to say, which is that about four of the nine had not known until the question was asked that a person at the far end could let go of it whenever they liked and nobody would be able to say so afterwards, and that about four of the nine had not known until the question was asked that this holding does not know who is at the far end, and that these two not-knowings are the same one wearing two coats, and that the reader of this body has had the second of them in a book of his own since the eighteenth of this month and has not entered it in the count of six not knowns, because it is a fact about a person and the six are facts about columns, and a person is not a column and about four years is a long time for that distinction to survive a room.

And then the two last things of the twenty-third of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**AN ASSEMBLY ASKED A QUESTION THAT IS NOT ABOUT WATER, AND THE REASON CAME BEFORE THE QUESTION, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT IMPROVE IT. THE ANSWER TO THE FIRST HALF IS YES AND HAS NO FIGURE. THE SECOND HALF WAS NOT ANSWERED IN THAT ROOM AND THE REASON IS THAT THE PERSON IT IS ABOUT IS NOT IN THAT ROOM. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED ANYBODY IN THE LINE OF ASKING THAT ASSEMBLY FOR A FIGURE IS NIL AND IS NIL AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THE QUESTION CAME THE OTHER WAY.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE, THE EIGHTY-THIRD IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-ONE DAYS. THE NOT KNOWNS STAND AT SIX AND NOBODY ADDED A SEVENTH FOR A QUESTION. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-third of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a question that is not about water, and not by the reader of this body, who did not improve it: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-NINE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY NOT, AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN DID NOT WAIT THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE EIGHTY-THIRD FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-ONE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE EIGHTIETH MORNING OUT OF EIGHTY-TWO. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SEVEN DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD. SOREN RILL, OF AN ASSEMBLY IN THE THORNWILD, CAME FOUR AND A HALF MILES ON FOOT AND AT HIS OWN COST, BEING THE FOURTH TIME IN A MONTH, AND ASKED ONE QUESTION AT THE START OF THE ROOM BEFORE HE SATED DOWN, AND THE REASON CAME BEFORE THE QUESTION, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS NINE AND IT IS HIS OWN. THE QUESTION IS NOT ABOUT WATER. IT IS WHETHER, IF THE PERSON AT THE FAR END LETS GO OF IT, ANYTHING AT THIS END STOPS. AND THE SECOND HALF IS WHETHER, IF THEY LET GO, THE OTHER END WILL BE ABLE TO SAY WHO DID IT, AND IF THEY CANNOT, WHAT THIS HOLDING IS GOING TO DO WITH THE NOT KNOWING. THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT IMPROVE IT AND SAID WHY, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIX. THE ANSWER TO THE FIRST HALF IS YES AND HAS NO FIGURE. THE SECOND HALF WAS NOT ANSWERED IN THAT ROOM. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED ANYBODY IN THE LINE OF ASKING THAT ASSEMBLY FOR A FIGURE IS NIL AND IS NIL AND DID NOT MOVE, AND HE ASKED NOBODY ONE THIS AFTERNOON, AND THE QUESTION CAME THE OTHER WAY. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT HE HAS A PAGE AT THE BACK OF A BOOK THAT HE HAS NEVER WRITTEN ON AND THAT HE HAS JUST FOUND OUT WHAT THE PAGE IS FOR, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND SAID THAT HE WILL WRITE ON IT THE DAY SOMEBODY AT THAT FAR END LETS GO AND NOT THE DAY WE FIND OUT THAT SOMEBODY DID, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE NOT KNOWNS STAND AT SIX AND A SEVENTH WAS NOT ADDED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
