# Chapter 318

## Seven Days Off

The nineteenth of this month was a Wednesday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the nineteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and seventy hundredweight.**

That is the seventy-ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in forty-six days the series has risen twenty-eight times and fallen eighteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventy-seven days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the seventy-sixth morning out of seventy-eight.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a room in which about four people had been waiting all morning for a man to say a thing about four minutes that happened yesterday, and that he had not said it, and that a man not saying a thing about the thing that worked is the only kind of restraint this holding has ever had any practice at.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered the pair as it stands at **four hundred and sixty-three days** and **five hundred and ten**, and entered that the two are forty-seven days apart, and that the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and that he read them out loud on the two mornings before this one and has not read them out loud on this one, and that this is the first morning since he started again that he has not said them out loud, and that the fen is not entering a reason for it and is not going to be asked for one.

And the third line was worked, and the fen entered what it is, because the three lines of a rota are not three jobs and a reader in about nine people will keep asking. The first is the path and the ditch at the low end. The second is the water at the top of the path, which is turned by hand and has been turned by hand for eleven years and is the reason there is a rota at all. The third is the two gates and the six feet of wall between them, and the third line is worked on a Wednesday and it is the shortest of the three, and it is the one where the man of about thirty-one of Silling stands at the second gate and does not go past it, because past the second gate is ground a rota has never covered and a man who stops where a book stops is the cheapest thing in this holding and the only one that costs nothing to keep.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the reader of this body was in the room and had been in it since before the fire was lit, and about four of the nine had come in expecting to be told something and were not going to be told it, and the fen entered all of that and improved none of it.

And the man of about fifty said the first thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-five, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"The standing was read out in this room on the twelfth of this month and it has not been read out since, and that is seven days, and I have been waiting to see whether anybody in this room was going to say so before I did and nobody has, and the reason I am saying it is that a standing nobody has read for seven days is one of two things and this holding cannot tell which."**

**"It is either that nothing in it has moved for seven days, which would be the first time anybody in this holding can remember, or it is that nine people have got seven other things to do, and I have been the one who reads it for four years and I am not going to be the man who decides which of those two it was by not reading it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man doing the only thing in this holding that is free, which is the arithmetic of a room, and about four people finding out in the same minute that they had all noticed the seven days separately and had all decided to leave it to the man with the book.

And the fen entered what a column is, because a thing that worked on the first attempt is a thing nobody in this holding has a column for, and that is the whole of what a column is. A column is not a place to put a figure. A column is a place to put a figure that a person has agreed to be able to compare with the figure that was in it the last time, and a comparison needs two, and a thing that has happened once has not got two, and the pair of things about four minutes that happened on Tuesday has got a first and a second and no third and there is nobody in this holding who has agreed to be compared with anybody about it.

**AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A THING WHICH WORKED ON THE FIRST ATTEMPT IS A THING NOBODY HAS A COLUMN FOR, AND THAT THE COLUMNS IN THIS HOLDING WERE ALL MADE BY PEOPLE WHO EXPECTED THINGS TO TAKE THREE ATTEMPTS, AND THAT ONE OF THE THINGS IN THIS MONTH HAS TAKEN ONE, AND THAT IS NOT A VICTORY AND IS ALSO NOT A COLUMN.**

And then Tova Reed said one thing without giving a reason first, and the fen entered that she gave none, and that she has given one every time she has spoken in this room in a month, and that the count of that in this holding's history did not move because nobody gave a reason this afternoon in her mouth.

**"I am not going to say it is safe and I am not going to say it was stupid. I have walked past the end of that path twice since Tuesday and I have looked at the ground on both sides of it and I have not seen anything that came up that was not already coming up, and that is one afternoon of looking on about four miles of a path by a woman who is not a doctor, and I am telling you what I looked at and not what it means."**

And the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave no reason before it either, and the fen entered that two people in about nine left the reason out in the same hour and about four of the room noticed and about five did not.

**"I was at the second gate this morning at the second hour and the third line is not the gate I would be looking at if something had gone wrong on Tuesday. If something had gone wrong on Tuesday I would be standing at the second gate looking at the path and I would be asking who went past it. Nobody went past it. I have been at that gate three mornings and I have seen four people go past it and every one of them was going to the low ground for a reason that had a name on it, and I am saying that out loud because the man of about fifty has just told us that nobody is writing down what happened yesterday and somebody in this room has to have written down the other half, and the other half is that nothing came up that path at all."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-two, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am not going to improve on the fact that it worked. I have had about two nights of not sleeping and a minute and a half of not having a left hand and I am not going to stand in nine people and turn those two things into a caution, because a caution is a thing you say to somebody about somebody else's ground, and the ground at the far end of that is not mine and the minute and a half was mine and those are not the same size and I have not got a way of writing them on one line."**

**"And I am going to say the thing about the standing that the man of about fifty has not said and I am saying it after him and not instead of him, because he said the half of it first. The seven days are not seven days of nothing. In those seven days a thing was made that nobody in this room can compare, and the man with the book has been carrying a standing that has a figure in it for a fourth line of a rota that has now been empty twenty-nine times, and those are two different kinds of unaccountable, and a room that keeps one of them and not the other is a room that is going to find out in about four years which one it kept."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was two men who had said the same shape of sentence four minutes apart and had not noticed they had done it, and about four people who had heard both.

And then at about the sixth hour of the evening Soren Rill came into the room, and he had come four miles and a half on foot again and at his own cost, and the fen entered that he was the third time in a month and that about four people in this holding had stopped remarking on the distance, which is a thing about four people in this holding and is not a thing about the road, and he stood at the back of the room against the wall and put his wet coat on the back of a chair and did not say anything for about half an hour, and the fen entered that he had not said anything on the ninth of this month for six days and that the two silences are not the same length and the fen is not going to say what either of them is.

And the fen entered what about nine people in a seed house sound like at about the seventh hour of an evening in the third week of a month, because nobody else is going to enter it and it is the only thing that happens in nine people every single day. There is a fire that is not in the room and there is a sound of it in the wall. There is a chair that has to be put back against the wall at the end or it catches the damp, and the man of about fifty puts it back at the end, and has done for four years, and about four people in this holding would notice if he stopped. There is a smell of wet wool off about two coats. And there is a stretch of about four seconds, most evenings, somewhere in the middle of it, in which about nine people all know that the same thing is in the air and none of them is going to be the one to put it down, and the fen entered that this holding has never once had a rule about those four seconds and that a room with no rule about four seconds is a room in which about four people do the work of a rule without being asked, and that this is the whole of what this holding has instead of a charter.

And then the two last things of the nineteenth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE STANDING HAS NOT BEEN READ OUT IN THIS ROOM SINCE THE TWELFTH OF THIS MONTH, AND THAT IS SEVEN DAYS, AND NOBODY IN THIS ROOM CAN TELL WHETHER NOTHING IN IT MOVED OR A ROOM FORGOT A BOOK. A THING THAT WORKED ON THE FIRST ATTEMPT IS A THING NOBODY HAS A COLUMN FOR, AND THE COLUMNS IN THIS HOLDING WERE MADE BY PEOPLE WHO EXPECTED THINGS TO TAKE THREE ATTEMPTS. NOBODY IMPROVED ON THE FACT THAT IT WORKED, INCLUDING THE MAN WHO DID IT.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY, THE SEVENTY-NINTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FORTY-SIX DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-EIGHT TIMES AND FALLEN EIGHTEEN TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FORTY-SIX. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY-SEVEN DAYS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-THREE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the nineteenth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of seven days, and not by the reader of this body, who said he was not going to improve on the fact that it worked: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-EIGHT MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-NINE NOT, AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN DID NOT WAIT THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING, AND A ROTA DOES NOT GO UP ON A MORNING A LINE DID NOT COME ROUND. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SEVENTY-NINTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FORTY-SIX DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-EIGHT TIMES AND FALLEN EIGHTEEN TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY-SEVEN DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SEVENTY-SIXTH MORNING OUT OF SEVENTY-EIGHT. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-THREE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TEN DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD. A THING WORKED YESTERDAY IN ABOUT FOUR MINUTES AND WORKED ON THE FIRST ATTEMPT, AND NOBODY IMPROVED ON THAT, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A THING WHICH WORKED ON THE FIRST ATTEMPT IS A THING NOBODY HAS A COLUMN FOR. THE STANDING WAS LAST READ OUT IN THIS ROOM ON THE TWELFTH OF THIS MONTH, BEING SEVEN DAYS BACK, AND IT HAS NOT BEEN READ OUT SINCE, AND NOBODY CAN TELL WHETHER NOTHING IN IT MOVED OR A ROOM FORGOT A BOOK. TOVA REED WALKED PAST THE END OF THE PATH TWICE SINCE TUESDAY AND SAW NOTHING THAT CAME UP THAT WAS NOT ALREADY COMING UP, AND SAID THAT SHE WAS TELLING THE ROOM WHAT SHE LOOKED AT AND NOT WHAT IT MEANS, AND GAVE NO REASON BEFORE IT, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY DID NOT MOVE. SOREN RILL CAME INTO THE ROOM AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR OF THE EVENING, FOUR AND A HALF MILES ON FOOT AND AT HIS OWN COST, AND SAID NOTHING FOR ABOUT HALF AN HOUR. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, THIS HAVING BEEN A ROOM. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
