# Chapter 319

## The Fourth Line Again

The twentieth of this month was a Thursday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **thirty**, and entered that all thirty of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **fifty-nine mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty not**, and that twenty-nine and thirty are fifty-nine, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

What the silence was made of was two people at a gate on a Thursday who had stopped saying anything to each other at the second hour, and about four fields that have been waiting since the twenty-sixth of last month and are now past the point where anybody in this holding would call them late.

And the figure for the twentieth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour, and it was read back by the man of about fifty out of a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and seventy-six hundredweight.**

That is the eightieth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in forty-seven days the series has risen twenty-nine times and fallen eighteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventy-eight days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the seventy-seventh morning out of seventy-nine.

Nothing was said for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what was in it, and it was made of a Thursday on which a number had gone up by six, and about four people in this holding expecting, on the strength of Tuesday, to be able to read a face across this room and finding that they could not.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered the pair as it stands at **four hundred and sixty-four days** and **five hundred and eleven**, and entered that the two are forty-seven days apart, and that the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and that he has now not read them out loud on two mornings out of the four since he started again on the seventeenth of this month and that the fen is not entering a reason for that and is not going to be asked for one.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the seed house had about nine people in it and the door was shut, and the man of about fifty read the standing out as it stands, and read it out whole, and the fen entered all of it and improved no part of it.

> **THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND THE REASON WAS GIVEN ONCE AND WAS NOT GIVEN AGAIN IN THIS SEASON. PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR STAND AT TWO, THOSE WHO CAN HOLD ONE FOR FIVE AT ONE, AND IT IS THE READER OF THIS BODY, AND SIX IS NOT A COLUMN. PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO HAVE A ROOTMARK STAND AT TWO, AND NOTHING SAID ABOUT EITHER ARM IN THE LAST TEN DAYS IS A THIRD ACCOUNT, AND A BRIDGE IS NOT A MARK AND A MARK THAT IS WARM IS NOT A MARK AND A HEADACHE IS NOT A MARK. ONE PERSON CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR AND WALK OUT OF THE GROUND AFTERWARDS AND IT IS HIM. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR AND RECONCILE NONE OF ONE ANOTHER. THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG STAND AT FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE. NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK STAND AT TWO AND THE FIRST ONE IS NOT NAMED. NOT KNOWNS STAND AT SIX AND NO UNIT WAS SUPPLIED FOR ANY OF THEM AND A SEVENTH WAS NOT ADDED, AND IT WAS NOT ADDED FOR A THING WITH A SHAPE AND NOT A UNIT, WHICH IN TEN DAYS HAS INCLUDED THE THING UNDER A CLOTH AND THE BLIGHT. REQUESTS AGAINST NOTHING AND THE NOTES UNDER SECTION NINE STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND FIFTY-THREE, AND NO NEW NOTE UNDER SECTION NINE WAS WRITTEN. THE BODIES AT THE NODE ARE FIVE AND THE NUMBER WAS NOT GIVEN IN A YARD. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY HAS BEEN FETCHED TWENTY TIMES, THE LAST OF THEM ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH, AND HE HAS NOT BEEN FETCHED SINCE AND HE HAS NEVER ONCE ASKED WHICH NUMBER IT WAS AND NOBODY HAS SAID WHY. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND IT IS TEN AND IT WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ IN ANY OF THE LAST TEN MORNINGS, AND THE OTHER SIX COLUMNS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND HAVE HAD NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM FOR FOUR YEARS, AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE OF THEM. THE COMPOST LINE STANDS PAID AT ONE HUNDRED POUNDS WITH THE RUN AT SEVENTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN SEVENTEEN, ENTERED ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH, AND THE NEXT LINE FALLS ON THE FIRST OF THE SEVENTH, AND THE LINE IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND. A PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON A LINE FROM THE TWENTIETH OF LAST MONTH WAS IN THIS ROOM ON FOUR OF THE SIX DAYS THE WINDOW WAS OPEN AND IS NOT NAMED AND WENT OUT OF THE HOLDING ON THE ELEVENTH OF THIS MONTH WITHOUT BEING SENT FOR, AND THEY ARE NOT A STEWARD AND PROTECTION IS NOT STEWARDSHIP, AND BOTH HALVES OF THAT ARE PRINTED BY THE OFFICE. A BOARD BEARING FOUR LINES IS HUNG ON THE WALL OF THAT ROOM AND THE COUNT OF THE USES OF WHAT IS ON IT STANDS AT TWO IN THE SEASON BEFORE LAST AND ONE IN THE SEASON LAST, AND NOTHING WAS ADDED TO EITHER IN TEN DAYS. THE COUNT OF ENTRIES IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK THAT CARRY A DAY AND NO FIGURE STANDS AT FIVE. IT WAS FOUR AND IT IS FIVE, AND THE SIXTEENTH OF THIS MONTH IS THE ONLY MORNING IN TEN DAYS ON WHICH A FIGURE IN THIS HOLDING WENT TO A COLUMN THAT IS NOT THE ROTA AND NOT THE THIRD LAUNDER, AND THE FIGURE THAT WENT TO THAT COLUMN IS THAT THERE WAS NO FIGURE.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about nine people hearing a book read out loud that is the same book they have heard read out loud before, and about four of them checking nothing, which is the hardest thing a person does in a room when a book is being read, and about four of them waiting to find out whether the man at the end of it would say which one had moved.

And the fen entered what reading a standing out loud sounds like the second time in a month, and the second time is different from the first time, and nobody in the room has said so. The first time it takes about nine minutes and everybody is finding out whether anything has changed. The second time it takes about seven, and the room knows more of the figures by heart than it did a fortnight ago, and the man reading it does not enjoy it and does not dread it and reads it at the same speed and stops at the same four places, and about four people in this holding can tell which four places they are from the sound, and about four of them have never noticed that they can.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-three, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"You read the last line of that standing out last and I am going to say why I asked you to, and the reason is that about four of us have been in a room for ten days where the only thing that moved was a thing that could not be measured, and I wanted it in your mouth and not in mine, because you are the one who is going to have to say it again in about four years and I am not."**

**"And I am going to say the whole of the other half of it as well, and the reason is that the count of days that have gone into that book without a figure on them went from four to five on Sunday, and about four people in this room have been treating that as a small thing about a small well, and it is not that, and the reason it is not that is that a book that keeps one figure in a day for a hundred mornings and then keeps a day without a figure for no reason anybody can defend is a book that has stopped being a book of measurements and has started being a book of dates, and I do not know which of those two is worse and I have had nine days to find out."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-six, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I put that line in the standing myself this afternoon and I put it in because the figure is one, and not because anybody asked me for a figure, and the reason is that if a thing happens once in ten days in this holding then it happens about thirty-six times in a year and in about three years there is a column for it whether anybody has a reason for one or not, and I would rather write the column this week when I know what it is than read it in about four years when I do not."**

**"And I am going to say the other half, which is that a column for it is a column for somebody four hundred miles off sending us a page with nothing on it, and about four years ago a page with nothing on it stopped coming and came back and stopped coming again, and I have not got the date of the middle part of that and I am not going to look it up in a room, because the date of the middle part is the engineer's and not mine, and he has it in a book of his own and he has told nobody in this holding which book."**

And the fen entered the thirty fields themselves, because a figure of thirty has been in this holding's book for five mornings and nobody in this holding has ever been in one of them. They are not one field and they are not a holding's field either. They are about two hundred yards of bank above Marden that four people have cut for eleven years on the four mornings when the fourth line of a rota came round, and what they grow is not entered in any book this holding keeps, and about four people in this holding would not be able to say which of them is the largest without walking out to the gate and looking, and none of them has done that in a month, and the fen entered that and is not going to turn it into a complaint about anybody.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man handing a piece of a thing he has been carrying alone to a room of nine without handing over the whole of it, and about four people in that room understood exactly what had been handed over and about five understood that something had been.

And then the two last things of the twentieth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE STANDING WAS READ OUT IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND IMPROVED IN NO PART. THE ONE THING IN IT THAT HAD MOVED WAS NAMED, AND IT IS THE COUNT OF ENTRIES IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK THAT CARRY A DAY AND NO FIGURE, WHICH STANDS AT FIVE AND WAS FOUR. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ AND THE OTHER SIX HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE OF THEM. THE USES OF WHAT IS ON THE BOARD AT THE WALL STAND AT TWO IN THE SEASON BEFORE LAST AND ONE IN THE SEASON LAST, AND NOTHING WAS ADDED TO EITHER IN TEN DAYS, AND THAT BOARD IS NOT READ OUT AGAIN IN THIS SEASON.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX, THE EIGHTIETH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FORTY-SEVEN DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-NINE TIMES AND FALLEN EIGHTEEN TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FORTY-SEVEN. THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN HAVE NOW WAITED THIRTY TIMES AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-NINE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY NOT. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
