# Chapter 320

## Read Out Whole

The twenty-first of this month was a Friday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-first went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and seventy-two hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in forty-eight days the series has risen twenty-nine times and fallen nineteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventy-nine days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the seventy-eighth morning out of eighty.

The room was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was a Friday, and which was a figure that had come down off the last one by four, and which was the fourth day running on which about four people in this holding had gone to the seed house at the fourth hour expecting to be in a room about something and had been in a room about nothing, and the fen has entered that and is not going to decide whether that is a good habit or a bad one.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and sixty-five days** and **five hundred and twelve**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on four mornings out of the five mornings since he started again on the seventeenth, and that about four people in this holding do not know that he started again.

And the first line was worked, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it, because a room that only ever hears about a working day is a room that has stopped being in the same place as the ground. Four men and a woman and a barrow and about two hundred yards at the low end of the fen path, and the ground is soft about nine months in twelve and the second of the soft ones was on Monday and this is the third, and a ditch that is cut in a soft month comes up again in about four days, and about four people in this holding know that and about four have never once thought about it.

And the fen entered one thing about the third day of the soft ones, because it is a thing that happened and because nobody is going to write it down. The barrow went through the first of the two places where the cut bank comes in to within about four feet of the line of the wheel, and it did not go through the second, because on Wednesday the third line is worked and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the second gate and the man who had the barrow took it up onto the bank and set it down again about nine yards along and carried it the rest of that second place on his legs, and he put it back down on the track without saying anything to anybody, and about four people who were on it saw him do that and about four did not.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the seed house had about nine people in it and the door was shut, and the middle table had the six things on it that it has had for a month, and the first of the two public terms lay face up on the wood with a straight edge along the front of it, and the reader of this body stood at the end of that table and read it, and read it out whole, and the fen entered all of it and improved no part of it.

> **THE FIRST TERM, WRITTEN OUT ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF LAST MONTH IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE IN THE SEED HOUSE BY TOVA REED, IN HER OWN HAND, AND LEFT ON THE MIDDLE TABLE, AND READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE ON THE TWENTY-FIRST OF THIS MONTH BY THE READER OF THIS BODY, AND IMPROVED IN NO PART: NO CONVERSION. A STORE IS COUNTED IN SACKS AND A HOLDING IS COUNTED IN DAYS AND NEITHER NUMBER IS THE OTHER ONE. THE COST OF A THING IS PRICED IN THE UNIT IT IS PAID IN AND NOT IN THE UNIT IT IS WANTED IN. A MAN WHO HAS TO CONVERT BEFORE HE CAN PAY HAS ALREADY DECIDED WHAT THE THING WAS WORTH, AND HE HAS DECIDED IT WITHOUT ASKING EITHER SIDE.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people hearing a thing they had all read being said out loud by the man it is about, and about four of them finding that a sentence you have been looking at for a month is a different object when a person says it in a room.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixteen, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have got nine acres coming off on the twenty-ninth of this month and I have said that date six times in rooms and yards this season and about four of those six times somebody came back with a question about it, and this afternoon is the sixth and nobody has, and I would like the not to keep being a fact about this room."**

**"And I am going to say the half of that term that is mine to say. The cost of Tuesday was priced in about four minutes and about a minute and a half of one man's hand and three nights of that same man not sleeping, and I know all three of those figures because they are in this room, and the cost of it is wanted in something else, and I have been in this holding for eleven years and nobody in it has told me what the something else is in, and I am not going to guess at it and I am not going to be the one who asks."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a woman naming two different prices for one thing in a room of nine and the room not having a way to put the two of them on the same line, and that about four people in that room had known that was coming and about five had not.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-four, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"That term has been on that table for a month and it has been sitting there being correct, and a thing that is correct on a table is not a term, it is a decoration, and the reason I read it out this afternoon is that about four of us were about to price Tuesday in a unit and I have watched the last three days of a room pick one up and turn it over."**

**"The unit it was paid in is four minutes and a minute and a half of a left hand and three nights. The unit it is wanted in has not been said out loud by anybody at the end of that path, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in the line of asking that Assembly for a figure is nil and is nil, and it is nil because I have not asked, and the reason I have not asked is that a man who asks a body of households what a thing is worth in before he has told them what he paid gets an answer to the wrong question, and he will not know it is the wrong one for about four years."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-seven, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I keep four books and I have got a column for what a thing costs and a column for what a thing is worth, and the two columns have been in the same book for four years and they have never been next to each other on a line, and I am going to write today's on a piece of loose paper and put it in the middle of the table and leave it there, and the reason is that a conversion is a thing you do on a page and if nobody can see the page then nobody knows a conversion was done."**

**"I am not going to say what is on it. It is four lines. The first two are Tuesday. The third is a blank with a line under it and I have not got anything to write under it, and the fourth says that a blank is not a zero and is not a refusal and is not a figure anybody has withheld. That is all it says and it is going to sit on that table in about nine people's light for a month and about four of them are going to hate it."**

And the fen entered the sheet itself, because nobody else in that room was going to and it has a shape and it has been left where it can be seen. It is about a quarter of a sheet and it was cut off a larger one with a knife rather than torn, and the top edge of it is straight and the other three are not, and the first two lines are in the man of about fifty's own hand and are short, and the third is a ruled line with nothing on it under it, and the fourth is four lines long and takes up about half the sheet. It is not the form with four lines and it is not the sheet that came up the road, and about four people in this holding have already begun putting the three pieces of paper in the same drawer, which is the whole of what a table is for and is also how a table loses things.

And then the fen entered the first term as an object, and nobody else in that room was going to enter it, and it is the only thing in this holding that a person wrote in order that nine strangers could say it. It is about four inches long on a strip of paper about two inches wide and it is in a hand that goes uphill to the right about the way a hand does when the person writing is not thinking about the pen. It has been on that table for a month and it has been under two other papers, and about four times in a month somebody has moved something off the top of it to look at what was underneath, and the four of them have not said that out loud and the fen has entered the count of four.

And the fen entered what happened after the reading, because about nine people is a small number and the sound of nine people not saying anything is a specific sound and it is not the sound of a room that agrees. It is the sound of about four people looking at the paper on the table and about four looking at the man who read it, and about one looking at the door, and the one at the door was the man who had the second term in his coat and who had put the coat on over it at about the third hour and had not taken it off since, and the fen entered that nobody said anything about the coat in a room of nine.

And then the two last things of the twenty-first of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE FIRST TERM WAS READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE BY THE READER OF THIS BODY AND WAS IMPROVED IN NO PART AND WAS NOT WITHDRAWN, AND THE SECOND TERM IS IN A COAT AND IS NOT WITHDRAWN EITHER, AND THOSE ARE TWO DIFFERENT SENTENCES. THE COST OF TUESDAY WAS ENTERED IN THE UNIT IT WAS PAID IN AND WAS NOT CONVERTED INTO THE UNIT IT IS WANTED IN, AND THE UNIT IT IS WANTED IN HAS NOT BEEN SAID OUT LOUD BY ANYBODY, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED ANYBODY IN THE LINE OF ASKING THAT ASSEMBLY FOR A FIGURE IS NIL AND IS NIL.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-TWO, THE EIGHTY-FIRST IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FORTY-EIGHT DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-NINE TIMES AND FALLEN NINETEEN TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FORTY-EIGHT. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY-NINE DAYS. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-first of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a term read out whole, and not by the reader of this body, who read it and did not improve it: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-NINE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY NOT, AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN DID NOT WAIT THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE EIGHTY-FIRST FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FORTY-EIGHT DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-NINE TIMES AND FALLEN NINETEEN TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY-NINE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SEVENTY-EIGHTH MORNING OUT OF EIGHTY. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIVE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWELVE DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD. THE FIRST OF THE TWO PUBLIC TERMS WAS READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE BY THE READER OF THIS BODY AND WAS IMPROVED IN NO PART AND WAS NOT WITHDRAWN. THE SECOND OF THE TWO IS IN A COAT AND IS NOT WITHDRAWN. THE COST OF THE CONNECTION OF THE EIGHTEENTH WAS ENTERED IN THE UNIT IT WAS PAID IN, BEING ABOUT FOUR MINUTES AND ABOUT A MINUTE AND A HALF OF THE FEELING IN ONE MAN'S OWN HAND AND THREE NIGHTS OF THAT SAME MAN NOT SLEEPING, AND WAS NOT CONVERTED INTO THE UNIT IT IS WANTED IN, AND NOBODY AT THE FAR END HAS SAID WHAT THAT UNIT IS. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED ANYBODY IN THE LINE OF ASKING THAT ASSEMBLY FOR A FIGURE IS NIL AND IS NIL, AND HE DID NOT ASK ONE THIS AFTERNOON. THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN HAS NOW SAID THE DATE THE NINE ACRES COME OFF SIX TIMES AND ABOUT FOUR OF THE SIX HAD A QUESTION ATTACHED TO THEM BY SOMEBODY AND THIS SIXTH DID NOT, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY PUT A LOOSE SHEET ON THE MIDDLE TABLE WITH FOUR LINES ON IT, BEING TUESDAY, A BLANK WITH A LINE UNDER IT, AND A STATEMENT THAT A BLANK IS NOT A ZERO AND IS NOT A REFUSAL AND IS NOT A FIGURE ANYBODY HAS WITHHELD, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. THE FIGURE ON LINE ONE OF THE FORM IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
