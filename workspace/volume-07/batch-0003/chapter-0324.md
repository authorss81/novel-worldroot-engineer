# Chapter 324

## What Is Felt At The Far End

The twenty-fifth of this month was a Tuesday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-fifth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and seventy-five hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in fifty-two days the series has risen thirty-one times and fallen twenty-one times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty-three days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the eighty-second morning out of eighty-four.

The room was quiet for the length of about a minute, and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was the last figure of ten in a book, and the fact that a man who has been putting one of these in a book for eighty-five mornings does not have a way of knowing that today is the last one, and that the only person in this holding who knows it is a person counting, and that a count is not a reason.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and sixty-nine days** and **five hundred and sixteen**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on six mornings out of the nine mornings since he started again on the seventeenth of this month, and that about four people in this holding now know that he started again and have said nothing about it.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon a man came up the fen road on foot from the far end of the eleven hundred and stood in the seed house door with his coat open and did not come in, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it.

**THE LONG FUNGAL IS INSIDE THE INHABITED LINE OF THORNWILD, BEING THE ABOUT TWO HUNDRED YARDS OF SETTLEMENT UNDER THE SOUTH EMBANKMENT WINDBREAK, AND ON THE SIXTEENTH OF THIS MONTH IT WAS INSIDE FOUR HUNDRED YARDS OF THE FIRST DWELLING AND WAS NOT INSIDE THE INHABITED LINE, AND ON THIS MORNING IT IS INSIDE THE INHABITED LINE, AND THAT IS NINE DAYS. THE COUNT OF THE FRUITING BODIES WAS NOT TAKEN AND IS NOT ON THIS PAGE AND WAS NOT TAKEN BY ANYBODY AT THAT END AND BY NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING. NOBODY SAID WHETHER THEY ARE SAFE TO EAT AND NOBODY PUT ONE IN A MOUTH, AND THE COUNT OF PIECES OF THE FUNGUS THAT HAVE COME INTO THIS HOLDING IN SIX MONTHS IS NIL. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING CAN PROVE THAT IT CAME THROUGH THE CONNECTION AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING CAN PROVE THAT IT DID NOT, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A THING NOBODY CAN PROVE EITHER WAY IS NOT A THING THAT IS IN THE COLUMN OF FACTS AND IS ALSO NOT A THING THAT IS IN THE COLUMN OF NOT KNOWNS, AND THAT THE TWO COLUMNS IN THIS HOLDING ARE BOTH FULL.**

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in it, and about four people knew that she had eaten one of them on the twenty-first of a month two months back, and nobody asked her a question, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and is nil and did not move this afternoon.

And then it arrived, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, IN A ROOM, IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, THE READER OF THIS BODY REACHED THE STAGE AT WHICH WHAT IS FELT AT ONE END OF A CONNECTION IS FELT AT THE OTHER, AND THE COST OF IT WAS IMMEDIATE AND IT WAS THIS: WHAT WAS FELT AT THE FAR END ARRIVED IN THE FOUR INCHES ON THE INSIDE OF HIS OWN LEFT FOREARM, AND HE PUT HIS HAND DOWN AND DID NOT PRESS IT. A BRIDGE IS NOT A MARK, A HEADACHE IS NOT A MARK, AND THE MARK BEING HOT IS NOT A MARK, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED ALL THREE OF THOSE AS ONE SENTENCE BECAUSE THEY HAVE BEEN SAID IN THREE ROOMS THIS MONTH AND A ROOM THAT HEARS A THING THREE TIMES IN THREE WEEKS BELIEVES IT TWICE AS HARD. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND BRANCHES TWICE AND IT IS THE SAME FOUR INCHES IT WAS AT THE SECOND HOUR, AND THE COUNT OF ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM IS FOUR AND RECONCILE NONE OF ONE ANOTHER, AND NOTHING THAT HAPPENED IN THAT ROOM IS A FIFTH.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people watching a man put his hand down flat on a table and then not move it, and about four of them not moving either, and about four of them not moving either and for a different reason.

And the fen entered what came, and there is no instrument in this holding that would have shown it, and there was no panel, and the word is in this account only to say that there is none, and the instrument was used once, in a shed, on the eighth of last month, and its count of uses is one and it was not used again. It was not a smell. It was a wet warmth and it was not in his hand, it was in the four inches, and under the wet warmth there was the same three-degree cold that came in on Tuesday with the shut window on it, and under the cold there was something that had a direction in it and the direction was about two hundred yards to the east, and he did not ask anybody what it was and the count of questions he has asked anybody in the line of asking that Assembly for a figure is nil and is nil.

And he put his hand down. And he did not press it. And about four people in that room watched him not press it, and about four of them did not know that there was anything on that arm to press.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of nine people who had all understood that something had arrived and that none of them could see where from, and about four of them had understood where from and were not going to say so in a room, because the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in it and the man who had walked four miles and a half on Sunday was not, and the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-eight, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"That thing has been running for seven days and I cannot close it from this end, and I am going to say the two halves of that out loud because they are different and about four of you are about to put them together. I can feel what is at the far end. I cannot do anything about what is at the far end, and I cannot tell you who is holding the other end of it, and nobody in this holding can, and I have not asked, and I am not going to ask this week."**

**"And the blight is on the other side of a bank from where it was on the sixteenth and it is on the far side of that thing from me, and the two of those are nine days apart and seven days apart, and there is no figure in this holding that joins them, and I am not going to make one. A thing that worked on Tuesday and a thing that came up on the other side of a bank on Tuesday are two things, and about four years from now somebody is going to want them to be one thing, and the want will be in the wanter and not in the ground."**

**"And I am not going to say the second half of that is safe and I am not going to say it is not, because I do not know, and because there is a woman in this room who ate one of them on the twenty-first of a month two months back and nobody has asked her today whether she is well, and I am not going to ask her either, and the reason is that the count of questions I have asked that woman is nil and it is nil, and a man who has kept a count at nil for a month is not the man to break it on the morning something arrives."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a man saying out loud in nine people that he was not going to ask a woman a question, and about nine people who understood that the not asking was the sentence and the sentence was the answer and neither of them was about the blight.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-one, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to write down two things on two different pages this evening and I am not going to write a connection between them, and the reason is that the first of them is a fungal on the far side of a bank and the second of them is a wet warmth in the arm of a man in this room, and about four years ago I had a column that put two such things in one line and I could not keep it, and I have not worked out yet whether the column was wrong or I was."**

**"And I am going to leave the second page blank, and the reason I am leaving it blank is that the count of blanks in this holding's books is a number I keep and that number moved from four to five on Sunday and it is not going to move again this month, and about four of you have been hoping it would."**

And then the two last things of the twenty-fifth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE CONNECTION HAS BEEN RUNNING FOR SEVEN DAYS AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING CAN SAY WHO IS HOLDING THE OTHER END OF IT, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED IN THAT DIRECTION IS NIL AND IS NIL. THE LONG FUNGAL IS INSIDE THE INHABITED LINE AND NOBODY CAN PROVE IT CAME THROUGH THE CONNECTION AND NOBODY SAID WHETHER THE FRUITING BODIES ARE SAFE TO EAT AND NOBODY PUT ONE IN A MOUTH. THE STAGE CAME IN A ROOM AND THE COST CAME WITH IT, AND WHAT WAS FELT AT THE FAR END ARRIVED IN FOUR INCHES ON THE INSIDE OF ONE MAN'S OWN LEFT FOREARM, AND HE PUT HIS HAND DOWN AND DID NOT PRESS IT. THE MINUTES OF A CONNECTION ENTER NEITHER THE COUNT OF YARDS NOR THE COUNT OF SILENCES, WHICH ARE TWENTY-SEVEN AND TEN, AND NEITHER OF THOSE MOVED IN THIS MONTH.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE, THE EIGHTY-FIFTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY-TWO DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-ONE TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-ONE TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FIFTY-TWO. THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN DID NOT WAIT THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-ONE NOT. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
