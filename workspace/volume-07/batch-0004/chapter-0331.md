# Chapter 331

## Refused On The Ground

The second of the seventh month was a Tuesday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **thirty-three**, and entered that all thirty-three of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **sixty-two mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-three not**, and that twenty-nine and thirty-three are sixty-two, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

What the silence was made of was two people at a gate on a Tuesday who have not spoken since Friday, and about four of the thirty-three fields above Marden having been in grass for nine years and about four of them having been in grass for four, and nobody in this holding having ever asked either of those things out loud at a gate.

And the figure for the second went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour, and it was read back by the man of about fifty out of the book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and ninety-four hundredweight.**

That is the ninety-second figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in fifty-nine days the series has risen thirty-five times and fallen twenty-four times, and those two add to fifty-nine, and the fen entered both and improved neither. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in ninety days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the eighty-ninth morning out of ninety-one.

Nothing was said for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what was in it, and it was made of a Tuesday on which a number had come up off the last one by seven, and about four people in this holding who had stopped opening their mouths at the eighth hour about four mornings ago and had not given a reason for it and were not going to be asked for one.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and seventy-six days** and **five hundred and twenty-three**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on ten mornings out of the sixteen since he started again on the seventeenth of last month, and that ten is the first round number in that run and that the fen has not entered a reason for a round number either.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon, at a place five days east of this gate, a man stood at the edge of a piece of ground and refused a shorter road, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING STOOD AT THE EDGE OF THE RING OF BARE GROUND INSIDE THE ELEVEN ACRES OF STANDING WILLOW AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE SECOND DAY OF THE SEVENTH MONTH AND HE STOOD AT THE EDGE OF IT AND NOT IN IT. THE STRAIGHT LINE ACROSS THAT RING, WHICH WOULD MAKE THE ROUTE TO THE THORNWILD EDGE SHORTER BY ABOUT A DAY AND A HALF OF WALKING, WAS PRICED OUT LOUD IN ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE BY HIM. THE RING WAS NOT WALKED AND WAS NOT MEASURED AND WAS NOT CROSSED AND WAS NOT PRICED BY WALKING IT, AND NEITHER LINE ON THE SHEET GOES ACROSS IT, AND HE SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE WAS NOT TAKING THE DAY AND A HALF AND THAT HE WAS NOT GOING TO ARGUE IT EITHER, AND THE FEN ENTERED THE WHOLE OF THAT AND DID NOT IMPROVE IT.**

And the fen entered what the edge of it looks like from a man standing on it, because a refusal made at a place is a refusal made somewhere and the somewhere has a shape. The eleven acres are standing willow with the bark gone grey on the south side and about four of the trees inside them dead at the root and still standing, and the ring is inside that, and from the edge of it at about the second hour of a Tuesday in the seventh month the ground inside the ring is the same colour as the ground under the willow outside it and lies lower, and the fen entered the lower and entered that nobody put a level on it and the fen is not going to, and that the willow at the edge of it grows the way willow grows and about four of them lean out over the ground inside the ring and have done for eleven years, and that nobody in this holding has ever asked a willow why.

And the fen entered the two lines, which are on the back of a sheet about the size of this page and are in pencil, and both of them stop at the inside edge of the ring and start again on the far side of it, and there is no third line, and nobody has drawn one, and the count of lines drawn across that ring by anybody in eleven years is nil.

For about a minute there was nothing said at that place, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about four people standing in a place where a shortcut exists and a man who had come five days to say a number out loud once and then stop talking, and nobody having asked him a second question and the not asking having been the whole of what he came for.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-six, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I priced it and I am going to give the reason for the pricing first, and the reason is that two lines are on the back of a sheet at that place and the man who drew them has never been on the ring and neither have I, and both lines stop where the ring begins and go round it, and the saving between them is about a day and a half of walking, and about four people at that place have known that figure since the second of this month and nobody has acted on it."**

**"And I am standing at the edge of it and not in it, and I am not going to step in, and the reason I am not is that this ring has been bare in its eleventh year and about four of us have known it was bare the whole of that time and none of us has ever once said why it is bare to anybody who was not already standing here. A man who wants a day and a half has to go and get it, and the getting is the crossing, and I am not going to be the man who goes, and I would like that entered with the figure and not after it."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-eight, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have got a column for what a thing costs and I have got nothing at all for what a thing saves, and the reason I am saying that in a room this evening is that a saving is not a cost and about four people in this holding have been treating it as one for a fortnight, and a saving is a figure that only exists if somebody is walking the long way, and the long way is the way four bodies of households have agreed to walk."**

**"So the day and a half is a figure about a man refusing a saving, and not a figure about ground, and it does not go in a column with the third launder and it does not go in a column with the fields above Marden, and I am entering it nowhere, and the reason is that a man in a holding with no charter has about one decision in him in a season and he has had it and he spent it on a rule under a line in this same book a month back."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-four, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a thing in this holding's books that would let me finish this afternoon by pointing at a page, and I am not reaching for it, and I am not going to name it, and the reason is that I have used it twice in four years and both times it was on paper and neither time it was in a room with the man who would have to live in it."**

**"And the second thing is that nobody in this holding has said out loud today that a refusal is a thing a person is entitled to make, and the reason I am saying it is that the last four days have been four days of five terms and a return and four clocks, and every one of those five is a thing that was written down, and a man who has spent four days in a room full of written things is going to start to think that a thing which is not written is a thing which was not refused. He refused it. It is in a book with a day on it and a figure on it, and the figure is a day and a half and the day and a half is his."**

**"And the third thing, and then I will stop. The ring was not walked this morning and it was not walked yesterday and it is not going to be walked this season, and the fen has entered that and is not going to improve it, and I have not got a view about why it is bare and I am not going to acquire one in a room in about four years and put it in a book."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people in a seed house five days and more from the place where the sentence had been said, and about four of them who understood that the refusal had not been reported so much as entered, and about four who understood that the man who had entered it had walked five days to say a number and then stopped talking, and that neither of those two things was going to be improved this evening.

And then the fen entered the rest of that evening, because a refusal at five days off is still a refusal by the time it gets here, and the fen entered the whole of it. The sheet with the two lines on the back of it came up the road with the man who had priced it and it went into the shallow drawer under the middle table on top of the three pieces of paper and the four other pieces of paper, and the man of about fifty did not write the day and a half anywhere, and about four people in the room watched him not write it and about four did not notice. The connection has been running fourteen days and nobody in that room said anything about it and nobody closed it, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody at the far end of it is nil and is nil and did not move this evening.

And then the two last things of the second of the seventh month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN FOR THE THIRTY-THIRD TIME, AND THE NUMBER OF FIELDS THAT HAVE WAITED IS THIRTY-THREE, ALL OF THEM THE FOURTH LINE, AND NOBODY IS BLAMED FOR A FIELD THAT WAITED. THE SHORTCUT ACROSS THE RING OF BARE GROUND WAS PRICED IN DAYS AT THE EDGE OF IT AND REFUSED THERE, AND THE RING WAS NOT WALKED AND NOT MEASURED AND NOT CROSSED AND NOT EXPLAINED, AND THE FIGURE OF THE REFUSAL IS A DAY AND A HALF OF WALKING AND IT IS A FIGURE ABOUT A MAN AND NOT ABOUT GROUND, AND IT IS NOT IN A COLUMN.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND NINETY-FOUR, THE NINETY-SECOND IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FIFTY-NINE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-FIVE TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-FOUR TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FIFTY-NINE. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN NINETY DAYS. THE BOARDS WERE READ OUT LOUD AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the second of the seventh month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a road refused at the edge of it, and not by the reader of this body, who did not reach for a thing he did not name: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS THIRTY-THREE, AND ALL THIRTY-THREE OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-TWO MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-THREE NOT, BEING TWENTY-NINE AND THIRTY-THREE, AND TWENTY-NINE TAKEN DID NOT MOVE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND NINETY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE NINETY-SECOND FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FIFTY-NINE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-FIVE TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-FOUR TIMES, AND NO MORNING IS UNCHANGED. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN NINETY DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE EIGHTY-NINTH MORNING OUT OF NINETY-ONE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING TEN MORNINGS OUT OF THE SIXTEEN SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF LAST MONTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING STOOD AT THE EDGE OF THE RING OF BARE GROUND INSIDE THE ELEVEN ACRES AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, AND NOT IN IT, AND PRICED THE SHORTCUT AT ABOUT A DAY AND A HALF OF WALKING, AND REFUSED IT WHERE HE WAS STANDING, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS TWENTY-SIX. THE RING WAS NOT WALKED AND NOT MEASURED AND NOT CROSSED AND NOT PRICED BY WALKING IT AND NOT EXPLAINED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS SEVENTY-EIGHT AND THE FIGURE OF THE REFUSAL IS NOT IN A COLUMN. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOUR, AND SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE WAS NOT REACHING FOR A THING IN THE BOOKS OF THIS HOLDING AND DID NOT NAME IT. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY AND WAS NOT READ AGAIN. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS EIGHTEEN. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
