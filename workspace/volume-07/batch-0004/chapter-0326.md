# Chapter 326

## Not Improved

The twenty-seventh of this month was a Thursday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the day was read back by the man of about fifty out of the book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and eighty-two hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-seventh figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in fifty-four days the series has risen thirty-two times and fallen twenty-two times, and those two add to fifty-four, and the fen entered both and improved neither. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty-five days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the eighty-fourth morning out of eighty-six. The rota stands at **sixty mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-one not**, and it has not moved since Tuesday and does not move on a morning the fourth line does not come round.

The room was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was a Thursday, and which was a figure that had come down off the last one by two, and about four people in this holding who had spent yesterday being impressed by a nine and who were now, on a Thursday, being handed a two, and about four of them finding that they had no idea what to do with a two.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered the pair as it stands at **four hundred and seventy-one days** and **five hundred and eighteen**, and entered that the two are forty-seven days apart, and that the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and that he has read them out loud on seven mornings out of the eleven since he started again on the seventeenth of this month, and that the fen is not entering a reason for the four he has not and is not going to be asked for one.

And the third line was worked, and the fen entered what it is, because a Thursday third line is a morning of about two gates and six feet of wall and it takes about an hour and a quarter with four people on it. The man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it, as he has not done in eleven years, and the two above the Marden headland were not there, and the six feet of wall between the two gates took water and lost it at about the rate it has been taking and losing it since the twenty-sixth of last month, and nobody wrote that rate down anywhere this morning either.

And then the fen entered one thing about the two gates that no book in this holding is going to improve, which is that a gate is a gate and not a boundary, and that the man who stands at one of them twice a week has never once in eleven years gone up past it, and that on Monday he went up past the other one and stood on a headland for an hour with no tool in his hand, and that the two of those facts have not been put next to one another by anybody, and that this holding has a drawer for paper and no drawer for that.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the middle table had the five terms out of the shallow drawer beside it, and the third line of the rota was still wet on about four people's coats.

And then two of the nine refused one of the five, and the reason came before the refusal, and the fen entered the whole of it.

**TOVA REED AND SOREN RILL REFUSED THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE TERMS, BEING THE ONE IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, AND THE REASON CAME BEFORE THE REFUSAL, AND THE COUNTS OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY ARE EIGHT AND ELEVEN, AND EACH IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON WHO IS SPEAKING AND OF NOBODY ELSE. THE REFUSAL STANDS. NOBODY IMPROVED A WORD OF THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE AND NOBODY IMPROVED A WORD OF THE REFUSAL, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY WAS NOT ASKED TO IMPROVE EITHER AND DID NOT.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people in a room in which two of them had said no to a piece of paper that had been on the table since the previous afternoon, and neither of the two had raised their voice, and the man who had written it had not moved.

And then Tova Reed said it, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eight, and it is her own.

**"A term has to be a thing that somebody on the other end of this can be held to, and that one is a term about a book in this holding and about a man in this holding, and the other end of a connection is not this holding, and I am refusing it because a sentence the other end cannot be held to is not a term between two ends, it is a rule about our own paper with the manners of a term on it."**

And then Soren Rill said it, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eleven, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am refusing the same one and I am giving my reason separately, because about four people in this room are going to hear hers and think that mine is the same as hers, and it is not. Her reason is about the other end. Mine is about what a refusal is for. That fifth term is the only one of the five that can be satisfied by this holding deciding on its own, and a term that this holding can satisfy by itself is a thing that does not need the other end at all, and about forty people at the end of an embankment are not going to be impressed by a clause they had no part in."**

**"And I am not saying the man who wrote it wrote it to keep this holding out of it. I have read it four times since yesterday and it is an honest sentence. I am saying that four of the five on that table are about two ends and one of them is about a book, and that a room where a book gets a vote is a room where the other end stops being one of the two."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-three, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Two of you have refused a sentence of mine in a room of nine and I am not going to argue with it, and the reason I am not going to argue is that a sentence I would have to argue about is a sentence I should not have written down without asking somebody whether it belonged on the list."**

**"It stays on the list. I am not taking it off and I am not going back over one word of it, and I am not going to put a mark against it either, because a mark against a thing on a list is a way of saying it is still on the list, and it is still on the list, and the two of you can be asked about it by anybody at any time, and a man who has been refused twice in two days by two people who walked into this room from different directions has been told the same thing twice and does not need it a third time."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of two people who had said no and had not enjoyed it and a man who had been told no twice and had said out loud that he was not going back over a word of it, and about four people in the room who had understood by the time it was over that nobody in it had lost anything, and about four who had understood that a refusal which nobody argues with is a different object from a refusal which is argued with, and that the second one is what everybody expects and the first one is what just happened.

And then the fen entered the part of the room nobody was looking at, which was the coat. The second of the five terms had been in that coat since the twenty-eighth of last month and had been out of it once, on the previous afternoon, and it went back in, and the man it belongs to did not take it out again and did not say anything about having taken it out. He withdrew the second of the five once, on the third of this month, and he has not unwithdrawn it, and about four people in this holding have been treating those two sentences as one for a month. The fen entered that they are two, and that a term in a coat is still a term, and that the room in which the fifth of the five was refused had not asked to see the second and was not going to.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty, and nobody improved it.

**"They refused one of the five and the refusal stands and the fifth of the five is on the table with the refusal attached to it, and I was not asked to improve either and I am not going to, and the reason is that there is a thing this holding does about four times a year where two people say no to a piece of paper and the piece of paper stays on the table, and about four years from now a person who was not in this room is going to read the two of those entries next to each other and one of the two is going to be in capitals and the other is not."**

**"And I am not going to improve it in either direction, and that includes the part where I think they are right, and the reason for that is that a man who improves a refusal toward the side he agrees with has stopped refusing and started agreeing, and a thing that has stopped being refused is not withdrawn, and the count of things I have said out loud in a yard and got wrong stands at five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room."**

And then the fen entered the rest of that afternoon in the room, because a room where a piece of paper has been refused does not stop, and the fen entered the whole of it. The fifth of the five went back into the shallow drawer at about the fifth hour with the other four, and the man who had written it put it in himself and put it on the top, and about four people saw him do that and about four did not. The coat was over the back of the chair all afternoon and the second of the five stayed in the pocket of it, and the man it belongs to was in the room the whole time and did not take it out. The light came on to the wall at about the fifth hour and lay across the table and not across anybody's hands, and about half the room had gone out into the yard by the sixth hour and the other half had not, and nobody in this holding keeps a count of which half left.

And the fen entered one thing about the room that is a fact and not a scene, which is that a thing which has been running since the eighteenth of this month had now been running nine days, and that nobody in that room said anything about it, and that about four of the nine had felt the inside of a forearm and about four had not and would not, and that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in that direction is nil and is nil and did not move this afternoon, and that the connection was not closed by anybody and that nobody in this holding has said so and that nobody in this holding could.

And then the two last things of the twenty-seventh of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE TERMS WAS REFUSED IN A ROOM BY TWO PEOPLE, THE REASON GIVEN FIRST IN BOTH CASES, AND THE REFUSAL STANDS AND NOBODY IMPROVED A WORD OF THE TERM OR OF THE REFUSAL. IT IS STILL ON THE LIST. THE READER OF THIS BODY WAS NOT ASKED TO IMPROVE IT AND DID NOT. THE COUNTS OF THE TWO WHO REFUSED IT ARE EIGHT AND ELEVEN AND NEITHER IS ADDED TO ANY OTHER. THE CONNECTION HAS BEEN RUNNING NINE DAYS AND HAS NOT BEEN CLOSED AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING CAN SAY WHO IS HOLDING THE OTHER END OF IT.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-TWO, THE EIGHTY-SEVENTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY-FOUR DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-TWO TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FIFTY-FOUR. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-FIVE DAYS. THE BOARDS WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-seventh of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a refusal, and not by the reader of this body, who was not asked to improve it and did not: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-ONE NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE EIGHTY-SEVENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY-FOUR DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-TWO TIMES, AND NO MORNING IS UNCHANGED. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-FIVE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE EIGHTY-FOURTH MORNING OUT OF EIGHTY-SIX. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-ONE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND EIGHTEEN DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD, BEING SEVEN MORNINGS OUT OF THE ELEVEN SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF THIS MONTH. THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE TERMS, BEING THE ONE IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WAS REFUSED BY TOVA REED AND BY SOREN RILL IN A ROOM, AND THE REASON CAME FIRST IN BOTH CASES, AND THE COUNTS OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY ARE EIGHT AND ELEVEN, AND EACH IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON WHO IS SPEAKING AND OF NOBODY ELSE. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON BEFORE WHAT HE SAID AND THE COUNT IS SEVENTY-THREE AND HE SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE WAS NOT GOING BACK OVER A WORD OF IT AND WAS NOT TAKING IT OFF THE LIST. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY, AND HE WAS NOT ASKED TO IMPROVE THE TERM OR THE REFUSAL AND DID NOT. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS SEVENTEEN. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
