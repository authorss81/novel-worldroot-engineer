# Chapter 328

## Nine Acres, Twenty-Ninth

The twenty-ninth of this month was a Saturday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the day was read back out of a different book by the man of about fifty, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and eighty-five hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in fifty-six days the series has risen thirty-three times and fallen twenty-three times, and those two add to fifty-six, and the fen entered both and did not improve them. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty-seven days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the eighty-sixth morning out of eighty-eight. The rota stands at **sixty-one mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-two not**, and the fields above Marden did not wait this morning.

The morning went on the way Saturdays go, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it. The first line is the boundary ditch and the two culverts on the Marden side, and about six people were on it from the second hour until about the fourth, and the near culvert was choked with about nine feet of the same leaf that comes off the willows in the last week of a month and that this holding has cleared out of that culvert every autumn for eleven years without ever once writing down what it weighs.

And the fen entered one thing about that work that nobody in this holding is going to draw a line under, which is that the boundary ditch between the fen and the low ground of Marden is about nine feet wide and that on the Marden side of it, this morning, from about eleven o'clock until the fourth hour, there were about eleven people working ground that this holding has never held and about four people working a ditch that this holding has held every autumn for eleven years, and that the two groups were about nine feet apart and did not speak across. And the man of about thirty-one of Silling carried the near culvert's leaf out as far as that ditch and stopped and put the barrow down, and about four people saw him stop and about four did not, and the fen entered the stopping and is not going to say what it was, because a man putting a barrow down at a boundary nobody has ever told him anything about is a man doing something and not saying what it is, and this holding has never once asked such a man.

And then the fen entered the second thing about that ground, which is that this holding has a rota with four lines on it and that none of the four has ever been a line for the nine acres, and that it never was one, and that about four people in this holding have been waiting eleven weeks for somebody to invent one and that nobody has and that nobody was asked at any gate today, and that a rota line is a thing bodies of households agree on and not a thing a holding invents for itself, and that on the day those nine acres came off there was no line on the board and there was no line wanted.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered the pair as it stands at **four hundred and seventy-three days** and **five hundred and twenty**, and entered that the two are forty-seven days apart, and that the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and that he has read them out loud on eight mornings out of the thirteen since he started again on the seventeenth of this month, and that the fen is not entering a reason for the five he has not.

And then at about the third hour of the afternoon the nine acres came off, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**THE NINE ACRES OF THE LOW GROUND OF MARDEN CAME OFF AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE TWENTY-NINTH OF THIS MONTH, AND THE GATE WAS SHUT FROM THE MARDEN SIDE AT ABOUT HALF PAST THREE BY THE PERSON WHO IS ALLOWED TO SHUT IT, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING ASKED HER TO SHUT IT AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING WAS ON THE GROUND AT THE TIME. THE ARRANGEMENT WAS DATED BY ITS OWN OWNER OUT LOUD SIX TIMES IN ROOMS AND YARDS SINCE THE SECOND OF LAST MONTH AND TODAY WAS THE SEVENTH TIME IT WAS DONE, AND TODAY WAS THE FIRST TIME IT WAS DONE INSTEAD OF NAMED. ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE WERE ON THE GROUND WHEN IT CAME OFF AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE IN THIS HOLDING WHO HAVE ASKED THE OWNER OF THOSE NINE ACRES ANYTHING IS NIL, AND IT IS NIL.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about nine people in a seed house who had all known this morning was the day since the second of last month and about four of whom had not known it was the day until somebody said it out loud in a yard at about the third hour.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventeen, and it is her own, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I said the date six times and about four of those six times somebody came back at me with a question about it, and the last of them was the fifteenth, and I am giving the reason before this one because I have got a gate that shut at half past three today and I am going to be asked about it by somebody in about four years and I would like the reason to be on the page."**

**"The reason is that this holding decided on the eleventh of a month two months back not to put its route across my nine acres, and it did not ask me for a thing, and I want that said clearly, because about four people in this holding have spent a month treating that decision as something that was going to be revised and it is not going to be revised. A bridge existing does not reopen a decision this holding made, and I am not going to be the one who says that again to anybody."**

**"And the rest of it is the sentence this holding is behind and it is fifteen words and it is in three hands and I am not going to improve it and neither is anybody else in this room: THE CUT STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING IS TO BE PUT IN THAT GAP THIS SEASON. The key is in my hand. It has been in my hand since the eighth of this month. It has not been used and it was not used today, and I am not going to say that it will be, and I am not going to be asked about it."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-five, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There are about eleven people who were on that ground an hour ago and about half of them have been on the rota of this holding at some point in eleven years and the count of those who have is not a column and I am not making one, and the reason I am saying that out loud tonight is that a holding that has just had nine acres of somebody else's ground come off is a holding where about four people are going to look for a number to put next to it."**

**"And I am not going to put a number next to it. There is no figure for how much a thing cost the people who worked it, and there is no figure for how much a gate cost the person who shut it at half past three on a Saturday, and if either of those two figures turns up in this holding's books in about four years it will have come from a man with a pencil and not from a weight, and the two are not the same kind of object and the fen has entered that."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-one, and nobody improved it.

**"I am not asking her one thing about those nine acres. I want that said in a room and entered, and the reason is that the count of questions I have asked that woman is nil and it is nil, and it has been nil since the second of this month when she held a key out to somebody and nobody said a word to her about it, and about four people in this holding have been reading that nil as a coldness in me and about four have been reading it as a thing I am proud of, and it is neither of those and I would rather it were written down than read."**

**"And the second half. There is a thing that has been running since the eighteenth of this month and it has been running eleven days, and about four people in this room have been waiting eleven days for a bridge to be a reason to go back and look at a decision made on the eleventh of a month two months back, and I am not going to do it, and the reason is that a decision is not held up by a thing that turns out to work. If it had turned out not to work they would be asking me to look at it, and the asking is the only thing in this holding that would make me look at it, and nobody is going to get that."**

**"Nothing went in that gap this season and nothing is going to, and the cut is open, and a key is a key and is neither a sequence nor a cradle, and about four of you have been looking at that table for a month as though a thing under a cloth on it were waiting to be used, and it is not. It is warm and it is heavier than it looks and about four people in this holding know that, and it is not going anywhere today and it is not going anywhere on a day a bridge works."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a woman who had said fifteen words out loud that she had not written and was not going to improve, and a man who had said out loud that he was not going to be asked, and about nine people who had understood at about the same moment that the thing they were all waiting for from him was a question and that they were not going to get it and that he had told them so in advance and had been right.

And then the fen entered the rest of that afternoon, because the nine acres coming off is a thing that happens to a holding and the fen entered what it did to the room. About four people in that room had worked that ground at some point in eleven years and about four had not, and the fen entered the count of the first four and not a word about what it meant, because a person who worked a field for six years and is not asked about it on the day it comes off has been left alone and that is a thing this holding has been getting right for about eleven years without deciding to. The light went off the wall at about the sixth hour and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was the first of the nine to go out into the yard, and she went out on the same side she always goes out on, and the count of times a person in this holding has gone out of that door and looked back at the table is nil.

And then the two last things of the twenty-ninth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE NINE ACRES OF MARDEN CAME OFF AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND THE GATE WAS SHUT FROM THE MARDEN SIDE BY THE PERSON WHO IS ALLOWED TO SHUT IT. THE DECISION OF THE ELEVENTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK WAS NOT REOPENED BY ANYTHING AND IS NOT TO BE REOPENED BY A THING THAT WORKS. THE KEY IS IN THE HAND OF THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN AND WAS NOT USED AND NOTHING WAS PUT IN THE TWO-FOOT GAP, AND THE CUT STAYS OPEN. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HER IS NIL AND IS NIL.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FIVE, THE EIGHTY-NINTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY-SIX DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-THREE TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FIFTY-SIX. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-SEVEN DAYS. THE BOARDS WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-ninth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of nine acres coming off, and not by the reader of this body, who asked that woman nothing: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-ONE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-TWO NOT, AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN DID NOT WAIT THIS MORNING. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE EIGHTY-NINTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY-SIX DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-THREE TIMES, AND NO MORNING IS UNCHANGED. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-SEVEN DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE EIGHTY-SIXTH MORNING OUT OF EIGHTY-EIGHT. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD, BEING EIGHT MORNINGS OUT OF THE THIRTEEN SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF THIS MONTH. THE NINE ACRES OF MARDEN CAME OFF AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR AND THE GATE WAS SHUT AT ABOUT HALF PAST THREE AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING ASKED HER ABOUT ANYTHING, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HER IS NIL AND IS NIL. THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS SEVENTEEN AND IT IS HER OWN. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS SEVENTY-FIVE. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-ONE. THE FIFTEEN WORDS WERE SAID OUT LOUD BY HER AND WERE NOT IMPROVED BY ANYBODY. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS SEVENTEEN. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
