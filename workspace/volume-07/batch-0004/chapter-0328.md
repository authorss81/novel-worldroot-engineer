# Chapter 328

## Nine Acres, Twenty-Ninth

The first line of the rota came round at the second hour on the twenty-ninth of this month, which was a Saturday, and was worked, and the figure for the day was read back out of a different book by the man of about fifty, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and eighty-five hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In fifty-six days the series has risen thirty-three times and fallen twenty-three times, and those two add to fifty-six. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty-seven days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the eighty-sixth morning out of eighty-eight. The rota stands at **sixty-one mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-two not**, and the fields above Marden did not wait this morning.

The first line is the boundary ditch and the two culverts on the Marden side, and about six people were on it from the second hour until about the fourth, and the near culvert was choked with about nine feet of the same leaf that comes off the willows in the last week of a month and that this holding has cleared out of that culvert every autumn for eleven years without ever once writing down what it weighs.

And the fen entered one thing about that work that nobody in this holding is going to draw a line under, which is that the boundary ditch between the fen and the low ground of Marden is about nine feet wide, and that on the Marden side of it, this morning, from about eleven o'clock until the fourth hour, there were about eleven people working ground that this holding has never held, and about four people working a ditch that this holding has held every autumn for eleven years, and that the two groups were about nine feet apart and did not speak across.

The ditch was four feet down and about two of water in it and the four of this holding stood in that, and the eleven on the other side were on ground that had been under an inch of water that morning and had gone down since. The two sides of a ditch nine feet apart are about eleven feet apart at the faces, and nobody in either group had been across it, and the sound of the work on both sides carried, and that is not speaking.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling carried the near culvert's leaf out as far as that ditch and stopped and put the barrow down. He did not go up the bank. About four people saw him stop and about four did not, and the fen entered the stopping and is not going to say what it was, because a man putting a barrow down at a boundary nobody has ever told him anything about is a man doing something and not saying what it is, and this holding has never once asked such a man.

And then the fen entered the second thing about that ground. This holding has a rota with four lines on it, and none of the four has ever been a line for the nine acres, and it never was one. About four people in this holding have been waiting eleven weeks for somebody to invent one. Nobody has, and nobody was asked at any gate today. A rota line is a thing bodies of households agree on and not a thing a holding invents for itself, and on the day those nine acres came off there was no line on the board and there was no line wanted.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud. The pair stands at **four hundred and seventy-three days** and **five hundred and twenty**, and the two are forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on eight mornings out of the thirteen since he started again, and the fen is not entering a reason for the five he has not.

And then at about the third hour of the afternoon the nine acres came off, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

They had started at the low end and worked up it in a line, and by the third hour there was about a third of a field left standing of nine, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden walked the line herself from the ditch side and did not cross into what was about to stop being hers, and she spoke to about four of the eleven and not to the other seven. At about half past three she shut the gate from the Marden side, which is hers to shut and has been since the twelfth of the fourth month. The gate is a plank gate on a hinge that has been oiled in the past year by somebody who is not in this holding, and it went shut without being lifted, and about eleven people on the Marden side of it heard it go and nobody said anything.

**THE ARRANGEMENT WAS DATED BY ITS OWN OWNER OUT LOUD SIX TIMES IN ROOMS AND YARDS SINCE THE SECOND OF LAST MONTH AND TODAY WAS THE SEVENTH TIME IT WAS DONE, AND TODAY WAS THE FIRST TIME IT WAS DONE INSTEAD OF NAMED. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING ASKED HER TO SHUT IT AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING WAS ON THE GROUND AT THE TIME, AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE IN THIS HOLDING WHO HAVE ASKED THE OWNER OF THOSE NINE ACRES ANYTHING IS NIL, AND IT IS NIL.**

The middle table had the thing under the cloth on one end of it and the cloth had not been moved, and the light going off the wall at about the fourth hour reached the table and stopped about a foot short of the cloth.

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about nine people in a seed house who had all known this morning was the day since the second of last month, and about four of whom had not known it was the day until somebody said it out loud in a yard at about the third hour.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventeen, and it is her own, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I said the date six times and about four of those six times somebody came back at me with a question about it, and the last of them was the fifteenth, and I am giving the reason before this one because I have got a gate that shut at half past three today and I am going to be asked about it by somebody in about four years and I would like the reason to be on the page."**

**"The reason is that this holding decided on the eleventh of a month two months back not to put its route across my nine acres, and it did not ask me for a thing, and I want that said clearly, because about four people in this holding have spent a month treating that decision as something that was going to be revised and it is not going to be revised. A bridge existing does not reopen a decision this holding made, and I am not going to be the one who says that again to anybody."**

**"And the rest of it is the sentence this holding is behind and it is fifteen words and it is in three hands and I am not going to improve it and neither is anybody else in this room: THE CUT STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING IS TO BE PUT IN THAT GAP THIS SEASON. The key is in my hand. It has been in my hand since the eighth of this month. It has not been used and it was not used today, and I am not going to say that it will be, and I am not going to be asked about it."**

She had her hand in her coat pocket when she said that last part, and she did not take the key out, and about four people in that room watched her hand stay where it was and about four watched the table instead.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-five, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There are about eleven people who were on that ground an hour ago and about half of them have been on the rota of this holding at some point in eleven years and the count of those who have is not a column and I am not making one, and the reason I am saying that out loud tonight is that a holding that has just had nine acres of somebody else's ground come off is a holding where about four people are going to look for a number to put next to it."**

**"And I am not going to put a number next to it. There is no figure for how much a thing cost the people who worked it, and there is no figure for how much a gate cost the person who shut it at half past three on a Saturday, and if either of those two figures turns up in this holding's books in about four years it will have come from a man with a pencil and not from a weight, and the two are not the same kind of object and the fen has entered that."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-one, and nobody improved it.

**"I am not asking her one thing about those nine acres. I want that said in a room and entered, and the reason is that the count of questions I have asked that woman is nil and it is nil, and it has been nil since the second of this month when she held a key out to somebody and nobody said a word to her about it, and about four people in this holding have been reading that nil as a coldness in me and about four have been reading it as a thing I am proud of, and it is neither of those and I would rather it were written down than read."**

**"And the second half. There is a thing that has been running since the eighteenth of this month and it has been running eleven days, and about four people in this room have been waiting eleven days for a bridge to be a reason to go back and look at a decision made on the eleventh of a month two months back, and I am not going to do it, and the reason is that a decision is not held up by a thing that turns out to work. If it had turned out not to work they would be asking me to look at it, and the asking is the only thing in this holding that would make me look at it, and nobody is going to get that."**

**"Nothing went in that gap this season and nothing is going to, and the cut is open, and a key is a key and is neither a sequence nor a cradle, and about four of you have been looking at that table for a month as though a thing under a cloth on it were waiting to be used, and it is not. It is warm and it is heavier than it looks and about four people in this holding know that, and it is not going anywhere today and it is not going anywhere on a day a bridge works."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room. Nine people understood at about the same moment that the thing they were all waiting for from him was a question, and that they were not going to get it, and that he had told them so in advance and had been right. The woman who had said fifteen words out loud had not written them down and was not going to improve them.

And then the fen entered the rest of that afternoon, because the nine acres coming off is a thing that happens to a holding and the fen entered what it did to the room. About four people in that room had worked that ground at some point in eleven years and about four had not, and the fen entered the count of the first four and not a word about what it meant, because a person who worked a field for six years and is not asked about it on the day it comes off has been left alone and that is a thing this holding has been getting right for about eleven years without deciding to. The light went off the wall at about the sixth hour and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was the first of the nine to go out into the yard, and she went out on the same side she always goes out on, and the count of times a person in this holding has gone out of that door and looked back at the table is nil.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-ninth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of nine acres coming off, and not by the reader of this body, who asked that woman nothing: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-ONE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-TWO NOT, AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN DID NOT WAIT THIS MORNING. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE EIGHTY-NINTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY-SIX DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-THREE TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-SEVEN DAYS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THREE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY DAYS, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD, BEING EIGHT MORNINGS OUT OF THE THIRTEEN SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN. THE NINE ACRES OF MARDEN CAME OFF AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR AND THE GATE WAS SHUT AT ABOUT HALF PAST THREE FROM THE MARDEN SIDE BY THE PERSON WHO IS ALLOWED TO SHUT IT, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING WAS ON THE GROUND AT THE TIME. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HER IS NIL AND IS NIL. THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS SEVENTEEN AND IT IS HER OWN. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS SEVENTY-FIVE. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-ONE. THE FIFTEEN WORDS WERE SAID OUT LOUD BY HER AND WERE NOT IMPROVED BY ANYBODY, AND THE KEY IS IN HER HAND AND WAS NOT USED, AND NOTHING WAS PUT IN THE TWO-FOOT GAP. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS SEVENTEEN. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
