# Chapter 330

## The Month Turns

The first of the seventh month was a Monday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the day was read back out of the book with the frayed corner by the man of about fifty, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and eighty-seven hundredweight.**

That is the ninety-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in fifty-eight days the series has risen thirty-four times and fallen twenty-four times, and those two add to fifty-eight. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty-nine days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the eighty-eighth morning out of ninety.

And the month turned in the night, and the fen entered the turn, and entered the count that stands beside it, and entered the two of them one under the other and not one on top of the other.

**THE COUNT OF THE SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND IT IS NINETY-ONE THIS MORNING, AND IT WAS NINETIETH YESTERDAY, AND A MONTH TURNED IN THE NIGHT BETWEEN THOSE TWO MORNINGS AND THE COUNT DID NOT RESET. THE NINETY FIGURES THAT CAME BEFORE THIS ONE WERE ALL DIFFERENT FROM EACH OTHER AND THIS ONE IS DIFFERENT FROM ALL OF THEM. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-ONE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-TWO NOT, AND IT DID NOT GO UP THIS MORNING, BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING AND A ROTA DOES NOT GO UP ON A MORNING A LINE DID NOT COME ROUND.**

And at about the second hour the compost line fell, and the fen entered all of it and improved none of it.

**PAID, ONE HUNDRED POUNDS, AND THE RUN IS EIGHTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN EIGHTEEN. THE WORD BEFORE THE FIGURE IS PAID AND IT IS THE SAME WORD THAT WAS THERE ON THE FIRST OF LAST MONTH AND ON THE FIRST OF THE MONTH BEFORE THAT, AND THE FIGURE HAS NOT MOVED AND THE WORD HAS NOT MOVED AND THE RUN HAS MOVED BY ONE, AND THE MOVE BY ONE IS THIRTY DAYS AND NOT ONE DAY, AND THE LINE FALLS ON THE FIRST OF A MONTH AND IT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND.**

The silence was made of a man reading a figure he has read about eighteen times and finding that a run of months is a number about time, and that this holding has not put a figure on time in four years, and that nobody in that room was going to say how many months that was.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered the pair as it stands at **four hundred and seventy-five days** and **five hundred and twenty-two**, and entered that the two are forty-seven days apart, and that the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and that he has read them out loud on nine mornings out of the fifteen since he started again on the seventeenth of last month, and that a month has turned in between those two figures and neither of them has moved by thirty.

And then at about the third hour of the afternoon two of them went up the nine hundred yards and the column was read again, and the fen entered that and improved none of it.

**THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STOOD AT FORTY-NINE AND IS FIFTY, AND IT WAS READ AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE FIRST DAY OF THE SEVENTH MONTH, AND FROM THE THIRTIETH OF LAST MONTH IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FORTY-FIVE AND FROM TODAY IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FORTY-SEVEN. THIS MORNING AND YESTERDAY ARE THE OTHER HALVES OF EACH OTHER, AND THE PAIR IS THE THIRD SUCH PAIR OF CONSECUTIVE READING DAYS IN FOUR YEARS, AND THE SIX OTHER COLUMNS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE OF THEM.**

And then at about the fourth hour the man of about seventy was fetched, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it, and the count of fetchings is **twenty-one**, and it went off twenty on to twenty-one this afternoon, and no person in this holding has said why, and the fen is not going to say why in a book.

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about eleven minutes of a man of about seventy coming up a path at the speed of a man of about seventy, and the person who noticed him at the gate was the person who keeps the seed house, and she was not thanked for it, and he has been fetched twenty-one times and has not once asked which number it was and was not asked today, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked him in twenty-one fetchings is nil and is nil.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon, at a place five days east of this gate, the return was made, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it.

**THE TWENTY-EIGHTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE FIRST OF THE SEVENTH MONTH AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, ON THE STEELYARD HUNG ON THE WALL OF THE BUILDING WITH NO ROOF, AND IT WAS READ OUT BY THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING AND READ BACK BY HIM, AND IT WAS THEN READ ONE AT A TIME BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, A NAME GOING INTO BY AND A NAME GOING INTO WITNESSED BY, AND IT IS THE EIGHTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS AND THE LAST OF THOSE NINE DAYS WAS ON THE TWENTY-SECOND OF LAST MONTH. THE FIGURE IS TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY HUNDREDWEIGHT. THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK AGAIN AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS TWELVE.**

And that figure is on this page twice over by two arithmetics, and the fen entered that it is put there twice on purpose, and entered which of the two derivations this holding is using and that it is not refusing it.

**THIS HOLDING IS USING THE ELEVEN-ABOVE DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT. TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE PLUS ELEVEN IS TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY, AND ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS FIFTY-FIVE IS ALSO TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS NOW HELD FOR TEN FIGURES, AND A RUN IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE AND NOT AN INSTRUCTION, AND A FIGURE THAT AGREES WITH TWO ARITHMETICS IS DERIVED TWICE AND NOT CHECKED TWICE.**

And the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing at that place and not in this one, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-five, and it is his own.

**"The count of the times I have brought that barrow up that road stands at eleven and it did not move this afternoon and nobody in this holding could derive a figure past it, and I am saying that out loud on a day when a number went on to a board and on a day when a month turned, because about four people in this holding are going to hear four figures today and I would like one of them to be mine. Harlan Vetch holds the fifth share and has held it eleven years and thirty-five days, and that is the only other number I have got and it is not a return."**

And then at about the fifth hour of the evening about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the man of about seventy was in it, and about four of the nine had felt the inside of a forearm in the last thirteen days and about four had not.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of four clocks that came round on one Monday and were done by three different people in three different places, and that the reader of this body had announced four of them yesterday afternoon in a yard and had been believed by about four people and doubted by about four, and that about four of the nine in that room had spent the day finding out which of those two groups they were in.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is nine, and it is her own, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Four things on one morning is four things this holding has not had before and I am going to say the one that is worrying me, and the reason is that about four of us have been treating the door as a curiosity, and the door is the only one of the four that anybody in this holding can check twice in two days, and the other three are a gate, a line of accounts and a man of about seventy, and none of the three can be checked twice and none of the three has a figure anybody can put a second name against."**

**"And I am not saying that is a reason to stop. I am saying that a man with four numbers on one page has a page that looks like evidence, and in about four years it is going to be read as a trend by somebody who was not in this room, and the four of them are about a door and a debt and a walk up a path and a return, and about half of this holding does not know that."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-seven, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I wrote four of them in four books this morning before any of them had happened and the reason is that the books are the only part of this holding that never misses a morning, and a book that is empty on a morning when three things are due is a book that is telling a lie by being blank."**

**"And I have written one of the four, which is the compost line, on a page that has got a heading at the top of it, and the other three in a book with no headings, and the reason for that is that three of the four are figures about this holding and one is a figure about a piece of ground four hundred miles off that nobody here has stood on, and the word before that one is PAID, and it has been PAID for eighteen months."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-three, and nobody improved it.

**"A bridge is not a mark, a headache is not a mark, and the mark being hot is not a mark, and I am going to say that in a room this evening because about four of you have been looking at my arm all day and about four have not, and the reason is that a blight is on the far side of a bank inside a line of about two hundred yards of settlement, and a connection has been running thirteen days, and there is no figure in this holding that joins those two things."**

**"The mark is four inches and it branches twice and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and nothing that happened in any room today is a fifth, and the blight does not go in a book and the arm does not go in a book."**

**"And the man of about seventy is sitting in this room and he has been fetched twenty-one times and he has not asked which number and nobody has told him and I am not going to, and the reason I am not is that I have not got a reason, and a reason I made up in a book tonight would read in about four years as a fact about him and be a fact about me."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about nine people in a room on the first morning of a month with four clocks behind them and an old man in a chair who had come up a path for the twenty-first time and had not asked anything, and about four of the nine who had understood by the end of it that nobody in this holding is keeping a reason from him.

And then the two last things of the first of the seventh month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**FOUR CLOCKS FELL ON THIS MORNING AND ON NO OTHER MORNING IN TEN DAYS: THE SEVENTH COLUMN, READ AGAIN AND STANDING AT FIFTY; THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY, FETCHED FOR THE TWENTY-FIRST TIME AND ASKED NOTHING; THE COMPOST LINE, PAID AT ONE HUNDRED POUNDS WITH THE RUN AT EIGHTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN EIGHTEEN; AND THE TWENTY-EIGHTH NINTH-DAY RETURN AT TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY HUNDREDWEIGHT, BEING THE EIGHTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS, WITH THE SECOND RULE LEFT BLANK FOR THE TWELFTH TIME AND THE DERIVATION USED AND NOT REFUSED. THE MONTH TURNED IN THE NIGHT AND THE COUNT OF THE SERIES DID NOT RESET.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVEN, THE NINETY-FIRST IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY-EIGHT DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-FOUR TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-FOUR TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FIFTY-EIGHT. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-NINE DAYS. THE BOARDS WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the first of the seventh month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a month turning with four clocks on it, and not by the reader of this body, who announced four of them at a gate the day before: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-ONE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-TWO NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE NINETY-FIRST FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FIFTY-EIGHT DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-FOUR TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-FOUR TIMES, AND NO MORNING IS UNCHANGED, AND THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND IT DID NOT RESET AT THE TURN OF THE MONTH. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-NINE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE EIGHTY-EIGHTH MORNING OUT OF NINETY. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD, BEING NINE MORNINGS OUT OF THE FIFTEEN SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF LAST MONTH. THE COMPOST LINE FELL AT THE SECOND HOUR AT ONE HUNDRED POUNDS, THE WORD BEFORE THE FIGURE BEING PAID, AND THE RUN IS EIGHTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN EIGHTEEN, AND IT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STOOD AT FORTY-NINE AND IS FIFTY AND WAS READ AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR, AND THIS MORNING AND THE THIRTIETH OF LAST MONTH ARE THE THIRD PAIR OF CONSECUTIVE READING DAYS IN FOUR YEARS, AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE OF THE OTHER SIX. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS FETCHED AT THE FOURTH HOUR AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY-ONE, AND HE ASKED NOBODY WHICH NUMBER IT WAS, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HIM IS NIL, AND NO PERSON IN THIS HOLDING HAS SAID WHY HE IS FETCHED ON THE FIRST OF A MONTH, AND THE FEN IS NOT GOING TO. THE TWENTY-EIGHTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WENT IN AT THE NODE AT TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY HUNDREDWEIGHT, BEING TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS FIFTY-FIVE, AND THE EIGHTEENTH NAME-PAIR WENT IN WITH IT, AND THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK FOR THE TWELFTH TIME. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING GAVE THE REASON FIRST AT THE NODE AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS TWENTY-FIVE, AND THE COUNT OF TIMES HE HAS BROUGHT THAT BARROW UP THAT ROAD STANDS AT ELEVEN AND WAS NOT RAISED. TOVA REED GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS NINE. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS SEVENTY-SEVEN. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THREE. A BRIDGE IS NOT A MARK AND A HEADACHE IS NOT A MARK AND THE MARK BEING HOT IS NOT A MARK, AND THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS STAND AT FOUR. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
