# Chapter 325

## Five Terms And A Third Hand

The twenty-sixth of this month was a Wednesday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the day went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and eighty-four hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in fifty-three days the series has risen thirty-two times and fallen twenty-one times, and those two add to fifty-three, and the fen entered both and improved neither, and entered that nobody thanked anybody, and entered that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty-four days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the eighty-third morning out of eighty-five. The rota stands at **sixty mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-one not**, and the fields above Marden did not wait this morning, because the fourth line did not come round this morning.

The hour went on with nothing said in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of a Wednesday on which a number had come up off the last one by nine, and of about four people in this room who have no way of telling a large rise from a small one and who had begun, somewhere in the second week of this month, to stop trying.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and seventy days** and **five hundred and seventeen**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on seven mornings out of the ten since he started again on the seventeenth of this month, and that about four people in this holding now know that he started again and have not said so.

And the second line was worked, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it, because a room that only hears about a working morning is a room that has stopped standing on the same ground as the people standing on it. The second line is the inside face of the west dyke from the sluice up as far as the black post, which is about three hundred yards, and that face is slumped where the fen path took the water off it in the second of the month, and the work is to take the slumped stuff off the toe and put it back on the shoulder in barrow-loads.

And then the fen entered one thing about that face which nobody in this holding is going to write down, which is that the toe at the second of the three places is lower than it was on the eighteenth and lower than it was on the second of this month, and that nobody measured it and the fen is not going to put a figure on it, and that the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and this afternoon it did not become three.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the middle table had what it has had for a month on it, and three things were written on it this afternoon that had not been written on it before.

> **THE THIRD OF THE FIVE, WRITTEN THIS AFTERNOON ON THE MIDDLE TABLE BY TOVA REED IN HER OWN HAND AND LEFT ON THE TABLE, AND ITS WRITER DID NOT READ IT OUT: WHATEVER IS AT ONE END OF A CONNECTION IS AT THE OTHER END OF IT, AND THE PERSON WHO HOLDS THAT END CAN LET GO OF IT WHEN THEY LIKE, AND WHAT THEY LET GO OF STOPS HERE.**

> **THE FOURTH OF THE FIVE, WRITTEN THIS AFTERNOON BY SOREN RILL IN HIS OWN HAND, THE PEN BEING HIS AND LEFT ON THE TABLE WHEN HE HAD DONE WITH IT, AND THE INK NOT DRY: THE NAME AT THE FAR END OF A CONNECTION IS OURS AND NOT YOURS TO PRINT, AND A HOLDING THAT ASKS FOR IT HAS ASKED FOR A PERSON AND NOT FOR A FIGURE.**

> **THE FIFTH OF THE FIVE, WRITTEN THIS AFTERNOON BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY IN HIS OWN HAND, AND IT IS NOT A TERM THAT ANYBODY ELSE IN THIS HOLDING WOULD HAVE WRITTEN AND HE KNOWS THAT AND DID NOT GO BACK OVER IT: A THING THAT HAS BEEN DONE IS ENTERED IN THE BOOK ON THE MORNING AFTER THE DAY IT WAS DONE AND NOT ON THE DAY SOMEBODY ASKS FOR IT, AND A HOLDING THAT WAITS FOR SOMEBODY TO ASK HAS NOT GOT A COLUMN, IT HAS GOT A COURT.**

The first of the five is the one that has been on that table since the twenty-seventh of last month, in a hand that goes uphill to the right, and its two words are NO CONVERSION, and it was read out whole on the twenty-first of this month and improved in no part.

The second of the five was carried into that room this afternoon in the pocket of a coat, and the man who wrote it has not unwithdrawn it, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it.

> **THE SECOND OF THE FIVE, WRITTEN ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF LAST MONTH BY SOREN RILL IN HIS OWN HAND, WITHDRAWN BY HIM ON THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH, NOT UNWITHDRAWN, AND CARRIED IN THE POCKET OF A COAT THAT WAS OVER THE BACK OF A CHAIR IN THAT ROOM THIS AFTERNOON: WHAT A CONNECTION COSTS AFTER THE FIRST FOUR MINUTES IS PAID BY THE PEOPLE WHO ARE HOLDING IT, AND A PERSON WHO IS NOT HOLDING IT PAYS NOTHING.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people looking at three pieces of paper in three hands and working out, separately and without speaking, that the three of them do not say the same kind of thing, and that the man who had come four miles and a half was the only one of the three writers who had put his pen down face up.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seven, and it is her own, and nobody improved it.

**"I asked for that in a room on the eighteenth of this month and nobody wrote it down, and I am going to give the reason for saying so this afternoon, and the reason is that a thing asked for in a room and not written down is a favour, and a favour is a thing you can be asked for twice and refused once, and I have got about forty people at the end of an embankment who are not going to be able to tell the difference between a favour and a term."**

**"And I have written it in the plainest words I could find this afternoon, which are not good words, and I did not go back over them, because every time I have gone back over a sentence in this holding somebody has lost something in it. If somebody at that far end lets go, this end stops. That is the whole of it and there is nothing under it."**

And then Soren Rill said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ten, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I took that out of my coat this afternoon and put it back in the coat, and the reason is that it is the only one of these five a refusal cannot satisfy, and I have carried it about for a fortnight without putting it on this table and I am not going to put it on this table."**

**"The third can be satisfied by a person saying no and going away, and the fourth by a person never writing a name in a book, and the fifth, whatever else it is, by a man entering a thing on the morning after he did it. That one cannot be satisfied by a refusal at all, because a refusal is not paying, and a term that cannot be satisfied by a refusal is not a term between two ends, it is a demand made by one end of a thing on the other."**

**"And I have not taken it back off the list. It is the second of the five. I wrote it and it is mine, and a man who withdraws a thing in a room and goes on counting it has not withdrawn it."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-two, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I wrote the fifth one and nobody else in this holding would have written it, and I am going to give the reason I wrote it rather than one of the other two, and the reason is that I have kept four books for four years and I have never once been asked in a room of nine what a book in this holding is for, and the answer to that is in the book and not in me."**

**"And I know what it is about. It is about a holding with no charter and about a man keeping figures in four books for nobody, and about four of us having spent ten days saying that a day with no figure on it is not the same object as a day with a figure on it. It is the only one of the five that is about this holding and not about the other end."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a woman who had put a plain sentence on a table because she did not trust herself with a good one, and a man from four miles and a half who had put a term back in his coat and left it on the list, and a bookkeeper who had written about his own books in a room where two of the other four people present were writing about a place neither of them had been to this season.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirty-nine, and nobody improved it.

**"There are five terms on that table and I have not been asked to improve one of them and I am not going to, and I am going to give the reason out loud because in about four years somebody is going to read this entry and wonder whether I thought of one of them first, and the reason is that I have had about four phrases in this holding's books this month that would have made each of these three sound tidier, and every one of those four phrases is a man deciding on somebody else's behalf what a thing meant before it got here."**

**"The three of them are not improved and are not to be improved, and the second of them is in a coat and is on the list, and I can give you no better word for any of the five than the person who wrote it. The count of things I have said out loud in a yard and got wrong stands at five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about four people who had spent ten days waiting for a man to put a mark on a piece of paper and about four who had understood by about the second hour of the afternoon that there was not going to be one, and that the not having one was the thing the five were for.

And then the fen entered what the room did with the next half hour. It put three more pieces of paper into the shallow drawer under the middle table, which is not the drawer the boards are in and is not locked, and that drawer already held the form with four lines and the fifth ruled line, whose first line carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight in the hand of the man of about thirty-one of Silling and which is the figure of the twenty-second return; it held the one-page sheet that came up the road with four printed lines and a blank at the foot, which is a different document from the form; and it held a loose sheet cut off a larger one with a knife, with a blank and a rule under the blank. The fen entered the number of papers in it and not what a drawer is for.

And then the two last things of the twenty-sixth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**FIVE TERMS WERE WRITTEN THIS AFTERNOON IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND ONE OF THE FIVE WAS WRITTEN BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY IN HIS OWN HAND AND IS NOT A TERM ANYBODY ELSE WOULD HAVE WRITTEN, AND THE OTHER FOUR ARE THE WORK OF TOVA REED AND SOREN RILL. THE SECOND OF THE FIVE IS IN A COAT AND IS ON THE LIST AND WAS NOT UNWITHDRAWN. THE READER OF THIS BODY IMPROVED NO PART OF ANY OF THE FIVE AND WAS NOT ASKED TO. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS HE HAS ASKED ANYBODY IN THE LINE OF ASKING THAT ASSEMBLY FOR A FIGURE IS NIL AND IS NIL. NOTHING WENT IN THE TWO-FOOT GAP AND NOTHING WENT AGAINST ANY VALVE AND A THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR, THE EIGHTY-SIXTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FIFTY-THREE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-ONE TIMES, AND THOSE TWO ADD TO FIFTY-THREE. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-FOUR DAYS. THE BOARDS WERE READ OUT LOUD AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-sixth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of five terms, and not by the reader of this body, who improved none of them and was not asked to: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-ONE NOT, AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN DID NOT WAIT THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE EIGHTY-SIXTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FIFTY-THREE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-ONE TIMES, AND NO MORNING IS UNCHANGED. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-FOUR DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE EIGHTY-THIRD MORNING OUT OF EIGHTY-FIVE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER. FIVE TERMS ARE ON THE MIDDLE TABLE. THE THIRD IS IN TOVA REED'S HAND, THE FOURTH IN SOREN RILL'S, AND THE FIFTH IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO GAVE THE REASON BEFORE IT AND WAS NOT THANKED, AND IT IS NOT A TERM ANYBODY ELSE WOULD HAVE WRITTEN. THE COUNTS OF THOSE THREE ARE SEVEN, TEN AND SEVENTY-TWO, AND EACH IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON WHO IS SPEAKING AND OF NOBODY ELSE. THE SECOND IS IN A COAT AND IS ON THE LIST AND WAS NOT UNWITHDRAWN. THE FIRST IS NO CONVERSION AND WAS NOT IMPROVED. THE READER OF THIS BODY IMPROVED NO PART OF ANY OF THE FIVE AND WAS NOT ASKED TO, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS SEVENTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN SEVENTEEN. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
