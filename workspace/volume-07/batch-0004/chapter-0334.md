# Chapter 334

## The Terms, Read Back

The third line of the rota came round at the second hour on the fifth of the seventh month, which was a Friday, and was worked, and the figure for the fifth was read back by the man of about fifty in the book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and ninety-three hundredweight.**

That is the ninety-fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In sixty-two days the series has risen thirty-six times and fallen twenty-six times, and those two add to sixty-two. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in ninety-three days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the ninety-second morning out of ninety-four. The rota stands at **sixty-two mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-three not**.

The morning went as the third line goes. That is four people and an hour and a quarter and about six feet of wall between the two gates, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second one and did not go past it, and the two above the Marden headland were not there, and the wall between the gates took water and lost it as it has been doing since the twenty-sixth of last month, and there is no figure for that on any page in this holding and there was not one this morning.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud. The pair stands at **four hundred and seventy-nine days** and **five hundred and twenty-six**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on eleven mornings out of the nineteen since he started again, and the fen is not entering a reason for the eight he has not.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the five terms came out of the shallow drawer onto the middle table and the copy of the ledger came down off the shelf beside them, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

The terms went down in the order they had been written in, and the third of them was the one everybody could reach without moving, and the second was not on the table at all, and about four people in that room noticed that and about four did not, and the man of about fifty did not say anything about it and nobody asked.

> **THE FIVE TERMS WERE READ BACK IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE ON THE FIFTH DAY OF THE SEVENTH MONTH AND WERE IMPROVED IN NO PART, AND THE ONE OF THE FIVE THAT IS THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY'S WAS NOT IMPROVED EITHER, AND THE REFUSAL OF IT STANDS ON THE PAGE AND WAS NOT TAKEN BACK AND THE TERM WAS NOT WITHDRAWN, AND BOTH OF THOSE ARE TRUE AT THE SAME TIME IN THE SAME ROOM. THE SECOND OF THE FIVE IS IN A COAT AND IS ON THE LIST AND WAS NOT UNWITHDRAWN. THE FIRST IS NO CONVERSION AND WAS READ OUT WHOLE ON THE TWENTY-FIRST OF LAST MONTH AND IMPROVED IN NO PART AND HAS NOT BEEN READ OUT WHOLE SINCE.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people hearing five sentences they had all read and one they had only heard of, and about four of them waiting to find out whether anybody in the room was going to say the word better, and nobody in the room said the word better.

And then the fen entered the copy of the ledger, because it came down off the shelf against the terms and nobody had put it back, and it is a copy of the ledger of the Office of the Continuity for the maintenance of the Crown Engine and it is ninety-one rows long.

> **IT IS NINETY-ONE ROWS LONG WITH FIVE COLUMNS WITH HEADS AND A SIXTH WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND THE RULED LINES UNDER THE ONE THAT HAS NO HEAD HAVE BEEN KEPT. IT IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF THE NINETY-ONE, AND THE ELEVEN WERE COUNTED OFF IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE THIS AFTERNOON, AND SEVEN OF THE ELEVEN ARE NOT ON LINE 41. THE ELEVEN ARE LISTED IN A DISTRICT RETURN THAT IS NOT ENCLOSED, AND IT IS NOT IN THIS ROOM AND NOBODY HERE HAS SEEN IT, AND THE COUNT OF TIMES ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS ASKED FOR IT IS NIL. THE FIVE ROWS ON LINE 41 WERE CORRECTED IN A ROOM ON THE TWENTY-THIRD OF THE FIFTH AND WERE NOT CORRECTED A SECOND TIME THIS AFTERNOON, AND A CORRECTION MADE TWICE IS A CORRECTION A PERSON HAS STARTED ENJOYING.**

The counting took about a quarter of an hour and was done off the page and not out loud, four people on one side of the table and about four on the other, and there was a small noise of a fingernail on paper going along the rows and stopping, and stopping again, and it was the only sound in the room for that quarter of an hour.

And then the fen entered the sixth column, because about four people looked at it and about four did not, and the fen entered both of those and did not supply what it counts.

**THE SIXTH COLUMN STILL HAS NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON IT AND NOBODY WILL. THE FOUR YEARS THAT THE SIX BLANK COLUMNS HAVE HAD NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM ARE NOT A COUNT THIS HOLDING KEEPS AND WERE NOT ENTERED.**

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eleven, and it is her own, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"The third of the five is the one I wrote and it says that if the person at the far end lets go, this end stops, and I have been reading it out for about four minutes and it is the only one of the five that everybody in this room can say from memory, and the reason I am saying that out loud is that a term which can be said from memory by nine people is not the same kind of object as a term which has to be fetched out of a drawer, and about four of us have been reading that shallow drawer as a sign of seriousness."**

**"And I am not saying the shallow drawer is wrong. I am saying that the fourth of the five, which is a man's name that is not ours, can only be honoured by not being written down, and that is the whole of it, and it is the hardest of the five to check and the easiest to break."**

And then Soren Rill said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twelve, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I wrote the fourth of the five and it has not been written on anything, and the reason I am saying that in a room of nine is that about four people have been asking me this week whether I have put it in a book, and the answer is no, and the answer is going to be no in about four years as well, and a term that lives in the mouths of the people it binds is a different kind of term from one that lives in a drawer, and this holding has four people in it who can hold one in their mouths and about nine who cannot."**

**"And the second of the five is still in my coat and I have not unwithdrawn it, and I am not going to, and the reason I am saying that here rather than at a gate is that about four of you have been treating a withdrawn term as a dead one since the third of last month and it is not dead, it is in a coat, and there is a difference and the difference is about four years wide."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-one, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"The fifth of the five is mine and it was refused in a room on the twenty-seventh of last month and it is still on the list and I did not go back over a word of it, and I am going to say the one thing I have not said about it, and the reason is that a man who has been refused and has kept quiet about it for eight days has had eight days to work out whether he was wrong, and he did not work out whether he was wrong, and he worked out that he does not want to be right about it in a room of nine."**

**"So it stays, and the refusal stays, and about four years from now the two of them are going to be read side by side in about four books and one of those books is mine, and I would like the one that is mine to be the one that is boring."**

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she did not give the reason first, and the fen entered that she did not, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventeen and did not move this afternoon and is seventeen, and nobody asked her a question and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and is nil and did not move this afternoon.

**"I am going to read the third of the five out one more time because it is the one about me and I have not read it since the afternoon I wrote it, and I am not going to say why I am reading it and that is on purpose."**

**"Whatever is at one end of a connection is at the other end of it, and the person who holds that end can let go of it when they like, and what they let go of stops here. I wrote those words on this table and there is a copy of them on the back of a day sheet in somebody's pocket and a copy in a pocket book and I have not asked for either. I came in this afternoon to find out whether four people saying the same sentence is a thing I wanted, and it is, and the answer to whoever asks me about it in four years is going to be that a woman read it out once and nobody asked her anything."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-seven, and nobody improved it.

**"A thing has been running since the eighteenth of last month and it has been running seventeen days, and nobody in this holding can say who is holding the other end of it, and the count of questions I have asked anybody in that direction is nil and is nil and did not move this afternoon. It has not been closed. Nobody in this room closed it and nobody in this room can close it, and about four years is going to go past with that fact and the not knowing sitting next to each other and nobody is going to be able to move either of them."**

**"And the sixth column has nothing at the head of it and I am not going to put one there, and the reason is that a heading is a claim about what a column counts, and this holding has eleven blank rows, a district return it has never seen and a sixth column with no name on it, and nobody in this room could write a heading for any of the three that would not be a guess, and a guess in a hand people trust is a figure nobody can check, and the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two, and it did not become three today."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room. Nine people sitting with five sentences and a copy of somebody else's ledger on a table, and a man two evenings ago who had heard his own name said out loud in a yard. The reader of this body did not go near it. About four of the nine noticed that he had not, and about four did not, and neither of those is going to be asked about.

And then the fen entered the end of the afternoon. The terms went back into the shallow drawer and the copy of the ledger went back on the shelf, and the order from Wednesday went in with the rest of the paper and nobody in that room said one word about it, and the count of days it has been in this holding is two and nobody has been asked whether they are going to obey it. The light came off the wall at about the sixth hour and the room emptied the way it empties, and the connection was still running, and the man of about fifty's last act of the day was to put the day sheet on the cart and not to read it out.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the fifth of the seventh month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of five terms read back, and not by the reader of this body, who improved no part of any of them: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-TWO MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-THREE NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND NINETY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE NINETY-FIFTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN SIXTY-TWO DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-SIX TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-SIX TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN NINETY-THREE DAYS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-NINE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SIX DAYS, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD, BEING ELEVEN MORNINGS OUT OF THE NINETEEN SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN. THE FIVE TERMS WERE READ BACK IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND WERE IMPROVED IN NO PART. TOVA REED GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS ELEVEN. SOREN RILL GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS TWELVE. THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN SPOKE WITHOUT THE REASON FIRST AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT SHE DID NOT, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS SEVENTEEN AND DID NOT MOVE, AND NOBODY ASKED HER A QUESTION. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS EIGHTY-ONE. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND THE COUNT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SEVEN. THE COPY OF THE LEDGER IS NINETY-ONE ROWS LONG WITH FIVE COLUMNS WITH HEADS AND A SIXTH WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND IT IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF THE NINETY-ONE, AND SEVEN OF THE ELEVEN ARE NOT ON LINE 41, AND THE DISTRICT RETURN THAT LISTS THEM IS NOT ENCLOSED AND HAS NEVER BEEN SEEN BY ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING. THE FIVE ROWS ON LINE 41 WERE NOT CORRECTED A SECOND TIME. THE ORDER SEALING THE ROUTE IS IN THE SHALLOW DRAWER AND IS TWO DAYS OLD AND NOBODY HAS SAID WHETHER IT IS ONE THAT MAY BE REFUSED. THE CONNECTION HAS BEEN RUNNING SEVENTEEN DAYS AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING CAN SAY WHO IS HOLDING THE OTHER END OF IT. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY AND WAS NOT READ AGAIN. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS EIGHTEEN. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
