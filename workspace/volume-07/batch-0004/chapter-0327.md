# Chapter 327

## The Thirty-Second Time Nothing

The fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour on the twenty-eighth of this month, which was a Friday, and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited. The fen entered the number, which is **thirty-two**, and entered that all thirty-two of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **sixty-one mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-two not**, and that twenty-nine and thirty-two are sixty-one, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

What the silence was made of was two people at a gate on a Friday who have said nothing to each other since Monday, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling standing at the second gate with his back to about nine yards of ground he has not walked behind in eleven years, and nobody on either side of that post having said so.

The figure for the twenty-eighth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and eighty-eight hundredweight.**

That is the eighty-eighth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In fifty-five days the series has risen thirty-three times and fallen twenty-two times, and those two add to fifty-five. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in eighty-six days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the eighty-fifth morning out of eighty-seven.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read. The boards stand at **four hundred and seventy-two days** and **five hundred and nineteen**, and the two are forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on eight mornings out of the twelve since he started again, and about four people in this holding could tell you which eight, and nobody in this holding has ever asked.

And then the fen entered the thirty-two fields themselves, because a number of thirty-two has been in this holding's books for one morning and nobody in this holding has ever been in one of them. There are thirty-two of them and they are not one field and they are not a holding's field either. They are the two hundred yards of bank above Marden and the strip along the top of it and the corner at the north end, and the corner is about half an acre and is the best ground any of the four bodies of households has and has been in grass for nine years. The grass on it stands about shoulder-high in the last week of this month and nobody has cut it, and the reason nobody has cut it is that nobody decided not to.

The wind came over the bank and went through that grass all morning and it made a noise like a thing being combed, and about eleven people could hear it from the fen side of the ditch, and none of them went up.

And then the fen entered the second gate itself, because thirty-two is a figure about a piece of ground and the piece of ground is not the ground the fourth line is for. The fourth line is worked on the mornings when the second gate is not being stood at, and the man who stands at the second gate is the man who would be on that bank, and the reason nobody in this holding has ever put those two facts in one sentence is that it looks like a complaint and it is not one, and a complaint about a rota is a thing that four bodies of households would each have to be named in and none of them has asked for that. The gate is a gate. It is not a boundary and it is not a wall and it does not mean anything this holding has not written down, and the man on one side of it at about the second hour of a Friday in the fourth week of a month has never once been asked what is on the other side and has never once offered to say.

And then the fen entered one thing about the count. The ratio of fields to mornings in this holding was nineteen and nineteen until the fourth line began coming round on mornings this holding does not have anybody free for. A ratio is a figure and a ratio is also a sentence. This holding has never been given a second morning of the fourth kind in a week and has not asked for one. The man who walks up to that headland most weeks is the man who is not on the rota when the line comes round. About four people in this holding know all three of those things, and nobody has said them out loud in the same breath.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was not in it, having been at the second gate from the second hour and going home at about the third, and nobody asked him to stay and nobody in the room said one word about his not being there. The gap where a tenth man would have stood was not a gap anybody looked at, because there was no tenth man and there never had been one, and a person in that room who had come in expecting one would not have known what to be told.

And then the fen entered the thing that the room had actually been about, which was that about four of the nine had begun keeping their own copies of the five terms, and that on Tuesday the man of about fifty found two of them, one written out plain on the back of a day sheet and one in a pocket book with the corners gone soft.

Nobody said anything. What the silence was made of was about four people finding out, at the same time and without speaking, that a thing they had been told was on a table had been copied out by about four pairs of hands and was now in about four places. Nobody had copied it wrong. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong stands at five and did not move this afternoon, and this afternoon was a room and a table and not a yard.

And then the fen entered the two copies as objects, because nobody in that room was going to and a copy is a thing with a shape. One of them is on the back of a day sheet and it is written in a small upright hand that goes slow, and the day sheet it is on is the twenty-sixth of this month and it has a figure on the other side of it that is the figure of that day. The other is in a pocket book with the corners gone soft and it is written on the inside of the back cover where there is nothing else, and it is the fourth of the five that is in that book, and the book has about nine months of other people's day sheets in it. The man of about fifty put the two of them side by side on the middle table at about the fifth hour, and read both of them, and read the third of the five and the fourth of the five out of the shallow drawer after them, and did not read either of them out loud to the room. About four people in the room found the four of them being the same sentence four times in four different hands harder than a difference would have been, and about four found it easier, and the two of those were both on the same side of the table.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she did not give the reason first, and the fen entered that she did not, and entered that the count of that in this holding's history is eight and did not move this afternoon and is eight.

**"Somebody has got the third of the five on the back of a day sheet and somebody has got the fourth of the five in a pocket book and I have found out about it in a room and not in a yard, and I am going to say the one thing I want to say about it, and then I am going to stop. The one thing is that if four people in this holding each have a copy of a thing that was written on a table, then in about four years there are four copies of a thing that a person in this holding wrote on a table, and one of those four is going to have a word changed in it, and the other three are going to say that they have got it right."**

**"I am not asking anybody for their copy. I am telling this room that it knows something about that sentence now and does not know it yet, and that is the whole of what I came in with."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventy-four, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I found them because I went looking for my own, which is the reason I am going to give first, because I did not go looking for anybody else's and if I had gone looking for somebody else's I would not have found these. I keep four books and I have never once in four years wanted to know whether what I wrote down this month is the same as what somebody else wrote down this month."**

**"And now I have looked, and the reason I am saying this in a room rather than at a gate is that the answer is that they are the same, and I have checked them twice, and a thing checked twice in a month by a man with four books is going to be checked twice a month for the rest of his life, and I have got about one decision in me in a season and I am not spending it on a drawer."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave no reason before it, and the fen entered that he gave none and is not going to supply one, and the count of that in this holding's history did not move this afternoon and stands at one hundred and forty.

**"I am not going to say the reason before that one and the fen has entered that I gave none. The thing I want said out loud in a room this afternoon is that four copies of a thing is not a fault. It is what a holding looks like when a holding is not a court and has no way of keeping one copy of anything. If there were one copy and it was on that table then the fifth time somebody wanted to know what the third of the five said they would come and ask, and asking is a thing about nine people have to turn up for, and about four of them are not going to turn up."**

**"So four copies is the price of not having a clerk with a drawer that locks, and I have never once been in a holding where somebody told me the price out loud, and I would rather this holding knows what it costs than finds out. And the man who wrote the fifth of the five is not to be asked to check anybody's copy and has not been asked and is not going to be."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people who had spent two days learning that a thing they had agreed to was already in four pockets, and about four of them who had understood at the same moment that the person in the room who had just said the most against it had been the one who had gone looking for his own copy first, and that nobody was going to say that out loud, and that the fen has entered it and is not going to turn it into a scene.

And then the fen entered the north gate at about the sixth hour, because handoffs are read back there and about four of the nine went out to it and read back what they had carried, standing at the gate in the low light with their voices not raised, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was not among them and went home at about the third hour and did not say that he was going. The light on that gate is on the wrong side until about the fifth hour in this month, and about four people in this holding have known that for years and have never once written down which door a man writes on when the light is wrong, and the fen entered that as a fact about this morning and not as a question and is not going to ask it.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-eighth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a morning on which the fourth line came round and nothing was done about it, and not by the reader of this body, who gave no reason before what he said: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS THIRTY-TWO, AND ALL THIRTY-TWO OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-ONE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-TWO NOT, BEING TWENTY-NINE AND THIRTY-TWO, AND TWENTY-NINE TAKEN DID NOT MOVE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE EIGHTY-EIGHTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FIFTY-FIVE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN THIRTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN TWENTY-TWO TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN EIGHTY-SIX DAYS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-TWO DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND NINETEEN DAYS, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING EIGHT MORNINGS OUT OF THE TWELVE SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN. ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE IN THIS HOLDING HAVE COPIES OF THE FIVE TERMS AND NOBODY WAS ASKED FOR A COPY AND NOBODY GAVE ONE, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY CHECKED TWO OF THEM AGAINST THE SHALLOW DRAWER AND THEY AGREE. TOVA REED SPOKE WITHOUT THE REASON FIRST AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT SHE DID NOT, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY IS EIGHT AND DID NOT MOVE. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON BEFORE WHAT HE SAID AND THE COUNT IS SEVENTY-FOUR. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE NO REASON BEFORE WHAT HE SAID AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THIS HOLDING'S HISTORY DID NOT MOVE AND STANDS AT ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS SEVENTEEN. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
