# Chapter 342

## Rejected As Offered

The fifteenth of the seventh month was a Monday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the boundary ditch and the two culverts took about six people until about the fourth, and the far one was still about half full, and the near one had gone into the cart in two whole barrow-loads for the first time in about a month, and nobody weighed either of them.

**Seven hundred and fifteen hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In seventy-two days the series has risen forty-one times and fallen thirty-one times, and those two add to seventy-two. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and three days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the hundred and second morning out of one hundred and four. The rota stands at sixty-five mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-six not, and it did not move this morning, because the fourth line did not come round.

It came down off Sunday by five, and that is the fourth morning it has come down by five. It has come up by nine five times and gone down by five four times in the eleven mornings of this month, and neither of those is a pattern anybody in this holding has claimed to have found, and the man of about fifty has both of them in a book of his own and no sentence.

The six of them were in the ditch up to the thigh at about the third hour and the far bank of that ditch is the low ground of Marden, and the nine acres that came off on the twenty-ninth of last month are not being worked, and about eleven people on the far bank were not working them and were not doing anything else that anybody in this holding could see, and nobody spoke across nine feet of water at about the ninth hour, and that is not speaking.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud. The pair stands at **four hundred and eighty-nine days** and **five hundred and thirty-six**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on sixteen mornings out of the twenty-nine since he started again on the seventeenth of last month, and the fen is not entering a reason for the thirteen he has not, and about four people in this holding have stopped noticing which mornings they are.

It is the third morning of three that the bridge stands with nobody in charge of it, the other two being the thirteenth and the fourteenth. The field gate was shut when the reader of this body went down to it at about the tenth hour of the morning. The hollow at the foot of the wall was still there and had about a leaf in it, and there was nothing on the wall and nothing in the air. There is no instrument in this holding that says whether eleven hundred yards of channel between that wall and a board against a wall at the stone head are connected this morning.

He did not go into the hollow. He looked at it from about nine feet off, which is the distance the man of about fifty stood at on Friday, and he stood at it for about as long as it takes to look at a hollow in beaten ground and then he went back up the track.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the field gate when he came back up, standing on the other side of the shut gate with his back to the second house, and he had not been sent and had not been asked to be there, and neither of them said anything about it and they stood about nine feet apart for about a minute and then the man of about thirty-one of Silling went down the track towards the two gates.

That is the third morning of three and it is finished at about the tenth hour and there is nobody left in this holding to stand at that wall today, and the sixteenth of this month is tomorrow and this holding's gate opens tomorrow, and tomorrow is the last morning this holding has anything written down for, and nobody in this holding said so out loud at the time and the fen has entered the standing of the gate and improved none of it.

Nobody in this holding has asked anybody at the far end of it whether it is there, and the count of questions asked in that direction is nil and it is nil, and it is not going to be asked this afternoon either, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it.

Soren Rill came in at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, having walked four miles and a half, and he was wet to the middle of the back and he did not take his coat off, and the woman who keeps the seed house put the cloth down by his boots again without saying anything about it.

About nine people were in the room. Four of the five terms had gone across on the ninth of this month in the inside of a coat, and the second of the five had gone with them, and the whole of the five had been read back in a room on the fifth of this month and improved in no part, and they had not been in a drawer since the ninth.

And then Soren Rill said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirteen, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"The Assembly at Thornwild has taken the five of them as offered. As offered, on the ninth, out of a coat, in that order, with the second of them in with the rest of them. It has read them out in a room of about nine people and has not improved one of them and has not withdrawn one of them and has not put a mark against one of them, and it has declined all five."**

**"I am going to say the reason for the declining before I say anything else about it, and the reason is that declining them was the only thing in the room that was available to anybody, and about four people at that end have spent a fortnight waiting for a yes and about four have spent a fortnight waiting to be asked a question, and neither of those came, and a body of about forty people that has been asked nothing for a fortnight does not refuse a thing. It does not answer it."**

Nobody in the room improved a word of it. About four people had wanted the word refused and about four had wanted the word rejected and about four wanted to know which of the five had done it, and none of those three is the word a man who has walked four miles and a half in the rain uses about a body he is not a member of.

And then Soren Rill said a second thing, and it was flat, and nobody improved it.

**"They did not say which one. I am not going to say which one, and nobody in this room is going to make me, and if you had all five of us in here for a week I would not say which one, because a body that has been asked which of five sentences it did not like has been asked a different question from the one you sent, and the answer to the different question is worse for you than the answer you got."**

**"And they did not send anything back. There is nothing in my coat. I have walked four and a half miles to stand in this room and say a word I have been given and there is no paper in this building that came from that end today, and I would like the room to sit with that for about four minutes, because I think about four of you have been waiting since the ninth for a sheet."**

There was nothing in his coat. About four people in that room looked at the coat over the back of the second chair from the end, which is where it goes every time, and about four looked at the middle table, and the coat was about four feet away from both of them and was not empty and was not searched.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventeen, and it is her own, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I wrote the third of the five on this table on the twenty-sixth of last month and it is the only one of the five that nine people in this room can say from memory, and it is the one that went across in a coat inside another coat, and about four of us worked that out together about three weeks ago and were pleased with ourselves about it."**

**"I am going to say the thing I have not said, and the reason I am saying it here and not at a gate is that a gate gets one sentence and this room gets three. A term that can be said from memory by nine people is not stronger than a term that has to be fetched out of a drawer. It is easier to break. Nine people who can say it are nine people who can be asked to say it a different way by somebody with a better coat than mine, and about forty people at the end of an embankment who have nothing in their pockets at all are the ones a term ought to be hardest for."**

**"So I am not going to say the third of the five was the one. I do not know which one it was and I was not in that room and I have not asked and the count of questions I have asked anybody in that direction is nil and it is nil."**

For about a minute there was nothing said in that room, and it was made of about nine people and a wet coat and a word that a man of about thirty-five had been given by about forty people he does not live with, and about four of the nine were doing arithmetic about the ninth of this month and about four were not.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-nine, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Then there is no document about it and I am not going to make one, and I am going to say why in a room of nine and not in a book, and the reason is that I have been the man in this holding who writes down the standing of a thing for four years and the standing of a thing that has no document is a thing that exists because a man stood in a room and said so, and about four years from now that is a thinner kind of standing than a page."**

**"I would rather it was thinner. I have been in rooms where a piece of paper was the only thing keeping a thing alive and every one of those rooms had a day in it when somebody looked at the paper and decided the thing was still happening."**

He wrote three lines in the day book and he read them back out afterwards, and nobody in the room said they were not right, and they say that the five of them went across on the ninth, that they were taken as offered, that they were declined as offered, and that no document came back with the man, and the four of those is the reason there is no fifth line.

The reader of this body said that he was not going to add a note to the fourth of those, and the man of about fifty asked him why not, and the answer was that a man standing in a room in wet clothes at the fourth hour of a Monday is a document, and a holding that cannot read one of those without a page in front of it is a holding that has stopped being able to see anybody who comes to its door.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-three, and nobody improved it.

**"I have not asked Soren Rill a question since the twenty-seventh of last month and the count of questions I have asked anybody in that direction is nil and it is nil and it did not move this afternoon, and I am not going to ask him one now, and the reason I am saying that out loud is that a man has come four miles and a half in the rain to stand in a room and a room that immediately begins to work out which of the five it was is a room that has turned a refusal into a question and a question is a thing a man of thirty-five can be worn down by."**

**"And I want it on the page that the two units on that middle table are still sacks and days. Four days of somebody's seed came off a rack in a room with a stone floor and there is a number of sacks on a board and neither of them has been turned into the other, and the long way round is a day and a half of walking that was priced on the second of this month and refused on the second of this month, and that is a day and a half and not a fraction of four days and not a number of sacks."**

**"And the bridge is on the ground and nobody is in charge of it this afternoon and it was on the ground on Saturday with two men at the ends of it and one of them is a man of about sixty-eight who cannot walk out of the ground afterwards, and none of that has changed because about forty people at the end of an embankment have declined five sentences in a room. It has changed that nobody is carrying anything across it this week, and that is a different thing from it not being there."**

The room emptied about half an hour after that. The coat dried on the back of the chair by the stove and was still there at the eighth hour, and there was nothing in the inside of it but a man's own two hands and about four years of other people's weather, and nobody looked in it again.

The connection has been running twenty-seven days and nobody in that room closed it and nobody in that room can. The thing under the cloth is on the middle table and the count of people who have looked at it is two. The use log stands at fifteen lines, the seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and is not read, the man of about seventy is not fetched, the compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen, and the requests and the notes under section nine stand at fifty-three and fifty-three and no new note was written today, and the count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is ten and it is ten and it is not said in a yard.

Nobody thanked anybody. Five terms went across on the ninth in the inside of a coat and were taken as offered and were declined as offered on this afternoon, in a room, with the reason given first, and nobody improved any part of the terms or of the declining, and there is a hollow in the ground at the foot of a wall at a field gate with a leaf in it, and a bridge is either there or it is not and there is nothing in this holding that says.
