# Chapter 343

## A Corridor, Under Vetoes

The sixteenth of the seventh month was a Tuesday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The inside face of the west dyke from the sluice up as far as the black post was on the rota for the fourth morning running and the four of them had it down to the black post by about the fifth, and the coat of the man at the front was dark at the shoulder to the elbow and stayed that way, and the toe at the second of the three places is lower again and nobody put a rule on it.

**Seven hundred and twenty-four hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In seventy-three days the series has risen forty-two times and fallen thirty-one times, and those two add to seventy-three. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and four days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the hundred and third morning out of one hundred and five. The rota stands at sixty-five mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-six not, and it did not move this morning, because the fourth line did not come round.

The six of them worked it from the sluice end, one at a time, and about the fourth hour the man at the front of the barrow said that the toe at the second of the three places was lower again, and about four of them heard him and about four did not, and nobody put a rule on it, and the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and it did not become three on the last morning of a month.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second. The boards stand at **four hundred and ninety days** and **five hundred and thirty-seven**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on seventeen mornings out of the thirty since he started again on the seventeenth of last month, and that is the whole of the run and nobody keeps it and the fen is not entering a reason for the thirteen he has not.

The three mornings the bridge stood with nobody in charge of it were the thirteenth, the fourteenth and the fifteenth. They finished at about the tenth hour of the fifteenth with a leaf in a hollow at the foot of a wall at a field gate. The man of about fifty said out loud at about the ninth hour of the sixteenth that he was not going to enter the standing of it in any book, and the reason he was not going to was that a standing is a figure and this holding does not have one. He had a word for it and the word was not *off* and it was not *failed* and it was not *waiting*. A man who picks one of those three for a page in the morning after has picked a sentence about a bridge and not about a bridge.

The corridor in the pan is not the bridge and nobody in this holding has ever said it was, and about four people in the seed house have been treating the two of them as one thing for a fortnight because both of them go to a place where somebody else is, and that is the whole of the resemblance and it is not a reason.

The ordinary reading of the well on the shelf falls on the sixteenth of every month, and this is the sixteenth, and the rider came up the fen road at about the fourth hour of the afternoon with a book-board and a slip and the count of miles in his head, and the reading went into this holding's book as a day and nothing else.

It is the second time this holding's book has taken a day and no figure for that well, and the count of entries in this holding's book that carry a day and no figure goes from five to six. There is no ordinal against it and no figure and no guess. Nobody at the far end of it has been asked a question about it in eleven years, and about four years is about how long it takes for a number that was never written down to be spoken of as though somebody had forgotten to write it, and this holding is not going to be the place that starts that.

The wall at that well carries fourteen marks and carries fourteen this afternoon. The four feet of shortfall against the nine feet of day one hundred and thirty-six are not decomposed and are not going to be. And there is no entry anywhere for that well coming back.

The rider had a second thing in his coat, and he took it out when the woman who keeps the seed house put the cloth down by his boots, and it is not the paper of the office named at the head of the memorandum and it is not district paper either.

It is the form of an office whose business is a register. It does not name a district. It does not name a holding. It does not name a person, and it is not a form, and it is not an instrument, and it is a fourth kind of document and it is a different document from the form with four lines and the fifth ruled line that is on the middle table under the middle table's other four objects, and nobody in that room put the two in the same hand.

The reader of this body read it out. He read the whole of it, standing at the window side with the light off the wall behind it, and nobody stopped him at any part of it.

> A REGISTER OF PERSONS WHO MAY SET A SEQUENCE AGAINST A VALVE. NAME OF THE PERSON. THE VALVE. THE STANDING OF THAT PERSON AT THE DATE OF THIS ENTRY. BY WHOM THE ENTRY IS MADE. AND A LINE AT THE FOOT FOR AN OFFICE, WHICH IS ALSO NOT FILLED.

Nobody filled it.

The woman who keeps the seed house put it on the middle table beside the other four objects and did not put it in the drawer, and about four people in that room noticed the drawer and about four noticed the table, and the form went on the table and the drawer stayed shut for the rest of the afternoon, and nobody gave a reason out loud and about four of them could have.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have kept four books for four years and there is no column in any of the four that this piece of paper wants, and I am going to say that first and I am going to say the plain part of it first, and the plain part is that a register of persons is a column, and a column wants a name in it, and about four people in this room have been treating the not having a column as the same thing as the not having a name."**

**"The count of persons in this fen who may set a sequence against a valve is one and it is the man standing at the window, and he is not going to write his name on a piece of paper in a room of nine, and the reason he is not going to is that the standing of him at the date of this entry would be true on the day he wrote it and wrong in about a month, and a register does not have a column for wrong, it has a column for him."**

**"And I am not going to write a reason against it in a book. I am going to leave the paper on the table until the morning, and then I am going to do with it whatever we do on the seventeenth, and the room can watch me do it and that is the whole of the accounting."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-four, and nobody improved it.

**"That office has not lied to us and has not obstructed us and has not delayed anything and has not softened a single thing, and I want the seventeen of this month to have that said out loud in a room once before the month turns, because in about four years somebody is going to write that we were refused by an office, and we were not. We were sent a piece of paper by an office that keeps a list, and the piece of paper asked a question, and the answer to it was that this holding is not going to answer it, and that is not a refusal and it is not an offer and it is not a door."**

**"And I am not going to let this be the first time. There is a thing in this holding that would be a decision about an office and there are about four people in this room who could make it in about a minute, and I am not going near it, and I am not going to name it, and I have used it twice in four years and both times it was on paper and neither time it was in a room with the man who would have to live in it."**

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling asked the question a second time, and the room did not answer it, and nobody in the room had answered it the first time either.

**"I asked it in here on Sunday and I asked it in front of nine people and a piece of paper in a drawer, and I am asking it again on the last morning of the month, and the only thing that has happened since Sunday is that a second office has sent us something and it is a nicer piece of paper than the first one was. Is an order a warden signed a thing that the people at both ends of a bridge may say no to? I am not going to let this be a thing that got asked twice and answered never because a month turned over."**

Nobody answered it. The man of about fifty put his hand flat on the table and left it there. The rider put his hat on. About four people in that room looked at the form on the middle table and about four looked at the door, and the two groups had been in the room for the whole of it and neither of them had an answer and neither of them had said so.

And then the room did the thing that the room had come in to do, and it took about ten minutes, and nobody thanked anybody at any point in it.

The corridor in the pan is open. It has been open for longer than anybody in this holding has been keeping figures. Four bodies of households have been using it and have not been asked about it and have not asked anybody here about it. Nobody in this holding has walked it and the reader of this body said so out loud in a room a month ago and said he was not going to.

The man of about fifty said one thing about it, and he gave the reason first, and it is the same hand and the same ninety and he did not add to it and he did not make it two.

**"We are calling it a migration and trade corridor. That is the whole of the decision and it is a decision and not a state, and I am making it in a room of nine because that is where decisions are made in a holding with no charter, and I am not going to pretend it is a small one."**

**"It is a small one. It is the smallest decision anybody in this room will make this month and it is the only one that is a decision at all. Open is a state and a road is a decision and this holding has had the state for eleven years, and about four years ago a man stood in a yard and said the word corridor and nobody asked him a question, and he was right and he has been right since."**

**"So we name it. A corridor in the pan, a migration and trade corridor, used by four bodies of households, unnamed and undated and with no figure on it, opened on the sixteenth of the seventh month by a holding with no charter over ground it does not hold and a pan it has never measured."**

And then he read out the fifteen words off a slip of paper in the shallow drawer, because that is what the drawer was for and about four people in that room knew it was in there and about four had forgotten.

**"The cut stays open and nothing is to be put in that gap this season."**

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room said the word *veto* out loud and about four said the word *anyway*, and neither of those words is in the fifteen and neither of them is going on a slip of paper, and a corridor that opens under three refusals and one decision is not a corridor that has been answered, and the three refusals stand where they were put and the decision is in a room of nine in a holding with no charter and it is not the same kind of object as either of those.

And the man of about thirty-one of Silling said the plainest thing that was said in the room on that afternoon, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-two, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Silling declines the claim and Silling uses the corridor, and both of those are true at the same time and on the same afternoon, and I am not going to be the man who is asked to pick one of them. If somebody at that office wants to know what a body of households thinks about ground it has not been asked about, they can come and stand at the ditch with me about nine feet apart and not speak across it for a morning, and then they can write a sentence about it."**

Nobody in that room said he was wrong. The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said the decision of the eleventh of a month two months back has not been reopened by any of this, and did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventeen and did not move this afternoon and is seventeen, and nobody asked her a question, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and it is nil.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden had a key in her pocket and did not take it out, and the two-foot gap at the node end of eleven hundred yards has been open since the eleventh of the first month, and the fifteen words on the slip in the drawer are about that gap and about nothing else, and nobody in that room asked her about the key and nobody has asked her about it in a month.

The room emptied at about the seventh hour into an evening that had gone wet again, and the form from the office that keeps a register was still on the middle table when about nine people went out of a seed house door into it, and the door stuck at the latch the way it sticks, and the last of them got it open with a shoulder.

Nobody thanked anybody. The corridor in the pan is open, and the standing of the reader of this body at the date of this entry is on no piece of paper in this building, and the fifteen words on a slip in a drawer are the same fifteen words they were in a month ago. A thing in the pan has been called a migration and trade corridor by about nine people in a room with no charter, over ground nobody has spoken for, and under the refusal of a body of households that has not been withdrawn, and under a decision of a body of households that has not been reopened. No office in the world has answered it and nobody in this holding has asked anybody to answer.
