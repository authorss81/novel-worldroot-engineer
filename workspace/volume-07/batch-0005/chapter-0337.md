# Chapter 337

## Sacks, And Four Days

The second line of the rota came round at the second hour on the eighth of the seventh month, which was a Monday, and was worked. The inside face of the west dyke from the sluice up as far as the black post is about three hundred yards, and the job is to take the slumped stuff off the toe and put it back on the shoulder in barrow-loads, and four people did it from the second hour until about the fifth, and there is no figure anywhere for how much of it there is.

**Seven hundred and eight hundredweight.**

That is the ninety-eighth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In sixty-five days the series has risen thirty-eight times and fallen twenty-seven times, and those two add to sixty-five. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in ninety-six days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the ninety-fifth morning out of ninety-seven. The rota stands at sixty-three mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-four not, and it did not move this morning, because the fourth line did not come round.

It came up off Sunday by nine, which is the second morning of this month it has done that, and four people on a dyke in the rain can tell the difference between a nine and a three and cannot tell you what either of them means.

They went up it from the sluice end, one at a time, the same way they have gone up it since the twenty-sixth of last month. The barrow went through the slop at about the first hundred yards and came out of it heavier than it went in, and the man at the front of it said nothing about it and the two behind him did not either, and about three hundred yards of a dyke is a short job for four people who know it and a long one for four people who are thinking about a number on a board six miles away.

At the second of the three places the toe is lower than it was on the second of this month. Nobody put a rule on it. The count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two, and it did not become three on a Monday morning with the light coming off the wet stone, and the reason the fen does not enter a third is that there is nothing on the page to enter. There is a man saying a place is lower and a man saying it was lower last time, and a man saying that again is not a measurement, it is a habit with a direction in it.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read. The boards stand at **four hundred and eighty-two days** and **five hundred and twenty-nine**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on thirteen mornings out of the twenty-two since he started again on the seventeenth of last month.

Soren Rill came in at about the fourth hour of the afternoon with a slip of paper about the size of a hand, and put it on the middle table and did not take his coat off.

The slip is not from this holding. It is a piece of board off one of the racks in a room built against the south embankment at Thornwild, and the number on it is in a hand about six inches high, and it is a count of sacks and nothing else, and a woman of about sixty and a man of about forty stood on either side of a person who cannot be named here and counted aloud, and the two of them have never once agreed on the word for what they were doing.

The room was about nine people deep at the table end and about four of them could read the number on the board from where they were standing and about four could not.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in it, at the window side with her sleeves down, and she had come in with nobody and had said good evening to nobody and had been let stay. The key is in her hand and it was in her hand this afternoon and it went home in her pocket, and nobody in that room looked at the pocket.

Nobody in that room had asked anybody for that number. The count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in the line of asking that Assembly for a figure is nil and it is nil and did not move this afternoon, and a room of nine people that has been handed a figure it did not ask for is a room that has to decide what to do with it in about a minute and a half.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-four, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have got a column and the column wants a figure and I am going to tell the room what the column is for before I tell it what is going in it, and the reason is that I have kept four books for four years and every one of them is a book about weight, and a book about weight cannot hold a thing that is not weight, and about four of you have known that for a month and have been waiting for me to say it."**

**"I want the weight. I want it the way I want a wind at the back of my neck. I am not going to get it, because the people who keep that room count in sacks and have never once measured a sack against a field, and a number they have not got is not a number anybody is entitled to invent here."**

He turned the board over. The back of it is clean and there is nothing on it at all, which is the thing he was looking for, and he put it back face up.

**"So the column gets a word and not a figure, and the word is sacks, and the column stays as it is."**

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirteen, and it is her own, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"What those sacks cost is about four days of seed, and I am going to say the reason I know that before I say the four, and the reason is that I have walked the route for eleven days out of twenty in a season and I know what a day's eating looks like coming off a rack in a room that counts, and four days is what the woman of about sixty said out loud when the man of about forty said five, and they argued about it for a while and they are still arguing about it and neither of them has sent a correction."**

**"That is four days. It is not a weight. There is no hundredweight in it and there is going to be no hundredweight in it this season, and I am not going to let anybody in this room divide a number of sacks by a number of days and write the answer on anything."**

**"And I want it said out loud that the first of the five of ours has never been applied to anything until this afternoon. NO CONVERSION. It has been on that table since the twenty-seventh of last month and it has been read out once and improved in no part, and it turns out that it was not a term about a bridge. It was a term about a room where a woman of about sixty and a man of about forty have been counting a store in the only unit they have for eleven years, and it was going to be the easiest thing in the world to break the first time anybody was in a hurry."**

Nobody said anything in that room for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people and a table and two units that everybody in the room could name and nobody in the room could join, and about four of them had wanted to join them since Tuesday and about four had not, and the two groups did not look at one another across the table.

For about a minute nothing was said, and what was in it was a board with a number on it lying on a table in a seed house two days after a man in another room said that the only way to build a thing was to go the long way round, and the two of those had not been in the same room and had not been compared and did not need to be.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she did not give the reason first, and nobody asked her for one, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventeen and did not move this afternoon and is seventeen.

**"Nine acres came off on the twenty-ninth of last month and the decision of the eleventh of a month two months back has not been reopened by anything, and I have said that date out loud six times and I am not going to say it a seventh in a room where a board is lying on a table. My ground is not this corridor. I want that said and I want it said by the man of about fifty in his book tomorrow morning, and I am not going to be the one who says it today."**

The man of about fifty said that he would, and gave the reason, which was that a date said by a person in a room is a thing that room remembers and a date said by a man in a book is a thing that anybody can come in and read, and that the second kind is the only kind this holding has ever been able to use.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-eight, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Those sacks do not come up here in one cart, and I am going to give the reason I am saying that in a room before anybody plans a barrow, and the reason is that I have been over that ground eleven times in eleven years with a barrow and the ground between the embankment and the field gate is not a road, it is a floor of roots, and about nine feet of the eleven hundred of it has somebody's dead willow lying across it at the height of a man's knee and it has been there since before I was carrying anything."**

**"They will come along in loads, from that end, to this end, and they will come at the speed a barrow comes over roots, and nobody has asked anybody how many loads and nobody is going to, and the count of loads is not a figure in this holding's book and is not going to be one."**

**"And the four days is not a figure about how long it will take either. It is four days of seed, off a rack, in a room, in somebody else's year. The walking is ours and the four days is theirs and there is no column on this table that takes both of those at once and I would not put them in one if there was."**

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-nine, and nobody improved it.

**"I asked nobody for that number. I have not asked anybody in that line anything in eleven years and the count of it is nil, and I want the room to notice that the first time a figure has come into this holding from that direction it was carried in by a man who was not asked for it, and that if it had been asked for it would have come later, and smaller, and with a reason attached to it."**

**"That is the whole of what I have got to say about it. I am not going to stand here and be pleased about a number arriving. I have been in rooms where a number arrived and everybody stood about being pleased, and about four years later the number was in a book and the room was not, and the number did not know whose hand it had come out of."**

**"And a day of seed is not a day of anything else. If somebody in about four years wants to know what the four days bought, the answer is that they bought the beginning of a thing that is going the long way round, and the day and a half of walking was priced on Tuesday and is not being paid by the same people and is not being paid in the same unit, and I am going to stop there, because I can hear myself about to say it twice."**

The room did the rest of the afternoon without anybody deciding anything else, and what it did was put the board at the back of the middle table with the other five pieces of paper, which is the sixth thing in a shallow drawer under that table, and the drawer is not the drawer the boards are in and is not locked, and about four people in this holding have stopped keeping those six things straight.

Soren Rill did not stay for the end of it. He said that he had a way back in the dark and that four and a half miles is a different thing at the eighth hour than it is at the fourth, and he put his hood up at the door and went out into a night that had gone from rain to a cold drizzle standing in the air, and the door shut behind him and the room was about two degrees better for it.

The line finished at the black post at about the fifth hour and the four of them came down off the shoulder in the dark and one of them had a coat that was wet through to the lining and did not say so and went home.

The woman who keeps the seed house put the cloth back over the thing on the middle table at about the sixth hour, and she did it the way she does it, which is from the near corner and without looking at anything else, and the count of people who have looked at it is two and did not move this week.

The connection has been running twenty days. Nobody in that room said anything about it, nobody closed it, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in that direction is nil and is nil. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and is not read on any morning this week. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-one. The compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen.

The light came off the wall and went across the floor, and the room emptied, and nobody thanked anybody.

The board went into the drawer last, after the four of the five had gone, and the man of about fifty put it in face up, which is not how a paper goes into a drawer in this holding, and nobody said anything about that either.

Four days of seed is the price of the four of the five that is not a weight. The sacks are not the four days and the four days are not the sacks. There is a number on a board from a room with a stone floor and a low roof and a north wall of cold earth, and there is a word in a column in a book kept by a man of about fifty, and neither of them has been turned into the other. That is the first time a term written on a table in this holding has been anything anybody could be held to.
