# Chapter 335

## A Window, And The Long Way Round

The fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour on the sixth of the seventh month, which was a Saturday, and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited. The number is thirty-four, and all thirty-four of them are the fourth line, and the rota stands at sixty-three mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-four not. Nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

Rain off the west, not much of it, the kind that gets a coat wet over about an hour and then stops as though it had been somewhere else. The gate at the seed house end of the path stuck and somebody put a shoulder on it and got it open about four feet and left it that way, and the wind came in along the flagstones and went out through the gap and up under the door, and the door was shut, and the room was warm.

The figure for the sixth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour, and the man of about fifty read it back out of the book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and two hundredweight.**

That is the ninety-sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In sixty-three days the series has risen thirty-seven times and fallen twenty-six times, and those two add to sixty-three. The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in ninety-four days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the ninety-third morning out of ninety-five.

It came up off Friday by nine, which is a large rise for a wet Saturday, and nobody who spent the morning indoors could have told you that from a rise of two.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **four hundred and eighty days** and **five hundred and twenty-seven**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on twelve mornings out of the twenty since he started again on the seventeenth of last month.

The office's window of work opened on this morning, being the sixth, and shuts on the eleventh. The woman who keeps the seed house mentioned it once at about the fourth hour while she was putting the cloth away, and said it to the room and not to anybody in it, and the room took it the way a room takes a thing that changes nothing today.

Whether that window covers the line in question is one of the six not knowns, and the count of six did not move, and no unit was supplied for any of them. The man of about fifty said out loud that the last one of those six could be answered in about a fortnight if anybody ever asked it and that nobody ever has, and he did not give a seventh reason for not asking.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the second hour of the afternoon, and the middle table had the sheet with the two lines on the back of it lying on it, and the man who had drawn them was in the room.

He had come up on Friday and gone back down and he was tired in the way a man is tired on the second day of having said a thing out loud, and he stood on the side of the table that the window side of the room is, with his hat in his hand, and did not sit down. There was a chair against the north wall and about four people could have got it and none of them did, and he did not ask for it.

The light came in low through the two south panes and lay along the table and across the sheet and not across anybody's hands, and the stove ticked, and somebody's coat was still dark at the shoulder from the walk up.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-seven, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"We are going the long way round, and I am going to give the reason for that before I say anything else, and the reason is that both lines on the back of that sheet stop where the ring begins and go round it, and I priced the straight line at about a day and a half of walking on Tuesday and I am not taking the day and a half."**

**"I am not saying the day and a half is wrong. I priced it and the number is the number. I am saying that the only way to get the day and a half is to walk across the inside of that ring, and that ring has been bare in its eleventh year, and I stood at the edge of it on Tuesday and I am not going to be the man who puts a boot in the middle of it."**

**"And nobody is going to say out loud in this room why it is bare, and I am not asking, and if somebody starts on it I will go and stand in the yard. I have not got a view. I have had four years of looking at the edge of it and that is the whole of what I have."**

Nobody improved it. The sheet stayed on the table with both lines stopping where the ring begins and starting again on the far side of it, and the count of lines drawn across that ring by anybody in eleven years is nil, and it did not become one this afternoon.

The ring was not walked this morning and it was not walked on Tuesday and nobody put a level on it and nobody is going to this season.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighty-two, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have got a column for what a thing costs and nothing at all for what a thing saves, and I am entering the day and a half nowhere, and I said in a room a fortnight ago that a saving is a figure that only exists if somebody is walking the long way. We are walking the long way. That is a decision and it is a dear one and it goes in no column, and the reason I am saying that out loud in a room of nine is that in about four years somebody is going to find a book of mine with a gap in it and a gap with nothing in it looks exactly like an oversight."**

**"So I am writing the gap on the page deliberately, and I am writing the date on it, and the date is the sixth of this month, and what the gap says is that the day and a half was available and was not taken. That is all it says. It does not say by whom and it does not say why and if I put a why on it I will be writing a reason for a man who gave his own."**

He wrote it on the day sheet, in the middle of the afternoon, in a hand that went uphill to the right, and put the sheet on the cart's side afterwards, and about four people in that room watched him do it and about four did not.

He had read the sheet out in the room before he wrote on it, which he does on the mornings the fourth line comes round and there is nothing on the ground to stand and look at. Five things, in five places. The line that came round and had nobody on it. The figure for the day, which the two of them say is the same number and it is. The window opening. The thing that was not going to happen today, being sacks out of a store, which had been asked for twice this week and had not been answered. And the ring, which was not a thing on the sheet at all until Tuesday and which he read out as one because it had become one.

He put a stroke beside two of the five and left the other three alone, and he did not say which two, and about four people in that room could work it out and about four could not.

And then there was a stretch of about a minute in which nothing was said, and what was in it was nine people looking at the four objects on the middle table where they have been since before any of this started.

The seed in its rootwood under a cloth. The plate that came off the cradle with one corner turned up. The memorandum of protection with the date at the top of it. The form with four lines and a fifth ruled line, and the first line carrying one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight in the hand of a man who spoke for one body of households, and lines two, three and four blank.

The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody that afternoon. The count of people who have looked at it is two and did not move. It is the size of a man's fist and dark and heavier than it looks, and it is warm, and it has a rough scar at one end, and it is a thing with a shape and not a unit, and the count of not knowns is six and a seventh was not added for it this afternoon.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twelve, and it is her own.

**"We are going to build this without the thing under that cloth, and I am going to say the reason out loud in this room before anybody starts being clever about it. There is no written instrument. Nobody in this holding has asked for one in eleven years and nobody is going to ask for one this month, and a thing that cannot be opened without a piece of paper from an office is a thing we are not opening."**

**"And I want it entered that the not opening is ours. Not the office's. The office has not refused us anything this month. The office has sent a form and the form is correct in every particular and it is a form, and a form is not an instrument, and a man of about fifty said that out loud on the seventh of last month and he was right and it is still right."**

Nobody argued with her. About four people in that room had wanted the thing under the cloth and about four had not and neither of those had been in the room long enough to have said so out loud.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-eight, and nobody improved it.

**"I am the near end. That is not a claim and it is not a boast and I am not going to be thanked for it, and I am saying it now while there are nine people in a room, because in about four years the only record of it is going to be that a man stood up on a wet Saturday and said a thing he could not take back, and a record like that is worth having in a book that anybody can read."**

**"I can hold a pattern for four and I can walk out of the ground afterwards. There are two people in this fen who can hold a pattern for four and one of the two is me, and I am not going to improve that sentence by putting anything in front of it. It is a fact about two men and it is the whole of what I have to put into a thing that joins two points eleven hundred yards apart."**

**"And the far end is not settled and I am not settling it this afternoon. A bridge is a thing with two ends and the two ends are held by people and the people can say no, and I am not going to stand in this room and pick one. That is not mine to pick. I know that. It took me most of a month to know that and the knowing is the only part of the last month I would keep."**

**"And I want the room to have one thing in its head before it empties, and the thing is this. I can do the near end on my own. I cannot do the far end on my own, and I cannot ask anybody to be good enough for it, and I have not got a way of testing a person for it and I am not going to invent one this week. If the far end does not happen, the near end does not happen, and the correct outcome of this afternoon is a bridge that does not run, and I have written that down in my own head and not on a page."**

For about a minute after that there was nothing said in the room, and it was made of nine people and a table with four objects on it and one man standing up who had just told a room that he could not choose the other half of his own work, and about four of them had understood the whole of what he meant and about four had understood that he had said it.

The rain went off about the fourth hour and the light came up the wall behind the stove and lay along the north side where the people were, and somebody opened the back door about a foot to let the wet out and the cold came in with it, and the door stayed that way for the rest of the afternoon.

And then Soren Rill came in, at about the fifth hour, having walked four miles and a half in the wet after a day on the far side of the eleven hundred, and he stood inside the door with the water running off the hem of his coat onto the flags, and the woman who keeps the seed house put a cloth down by his boots without saying anything about it.

Nobody told him what had been decided while he was out. He asked, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling told him, in the middle of the floor, in about nine words, and did not tell him the reason twice.

Soren Rill did not say whether Thornwild had heard about it. He stood by the door and looked at the sheet on the table for a while with his hands at his sides, and then he crossed the room and put two fingers on the blank stretch of the sheet where the ring is, and he left them there for about the length of a breath and took them off again.

**"Then we will walk it,"** he said.

Nobody said anything else about the ring that afternoon, and the sheet went into the shallow drawer under the middle table with the other five pieces of paper, which is not the drawer the boards are in and is not locked.

The room emptied the way it empties. The connection has been running eighteen days and nobody in that room said anything about it and nobody closed it, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in that direction is nil and is nil and did not move this afternoon. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and was not read. The man of about seventy was not fetched. The compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen and it is not a measurement of the ground.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling went down the road at about the sixth hour with the sheet not in his hand, because the sheet was in the drawer, and he did not look back at the house, and about four people watching from the door noticed that and about four were already inside.

Nobody thanked anybody. The route was settled in a room, in one afternoon, on the sixth of the seventh month, in about nine people, and it went the long way round, and the ring inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured and not explained, and there is one thing about that plan that is not on the table with it, which is that a bridge with two ends has to have a person at the far one, and nobody in that room had asked anybody.
