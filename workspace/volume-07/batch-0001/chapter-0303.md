# Chapter 303

## Two Hundred And Seventeen

The fourth of this month was a Tuesday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **twenty-six**, and entered that all twenty-six of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **fifty-five mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty-six not**, and that twenty-nine and twenty-six are fifty-five, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

The quiet in that room was made of the fourth time in nine days that a morning had come round with the same two people on the same road and neither of them saying anything about it.

And the figure for the fourth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and fifty-six hundredweight.**

That is the sixty-fourth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in thirty-one days since the third of this month the series has risen twenty-one times and fallen ten times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in sixty-two days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixty-first morning out of sixty-three.

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a man reading a figure he wrote himself about four hours earlier and finding that it was a round one and that a round one in a series of sixty-four is either a coincidence or a change, and that the fen has entered both of those and is not going to pick one at eight hours in the morning.

And the fen entered the barrow, because a barrow is the reason a figure of this kind arrives at all and nobody in this holding looks at one. It is a two-wheeled barrow with the sides taken off down to about eight inches, and it is on a track that was made for it about eleven years ago and has not been made for anything since, and it goes down about four miles to a gate and a trough at a place where the channel is low and takes what is the fifth share, and comes back up the same four miles with the sides wet to about the eighth inch, and the going is easier down and harder up and the reason it is easier down is that nobody has to hold it sideways at the two places where the bank comes in.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a barrow standing in a gateway with water in it and nobody looking at the water, and that a barrow that has been up and down four miles about twice a month for eleven years has about nine hundred journeys in it and nobody has ever weighed the barrow.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and forty-eight days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and ninety-five**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on four mornings in about a week and that the reason this morning is that a figure was going to be weighed in the afternoon and a man who is about to read a dial likes to have said two other numbers out loud first.

And then at about the second hour of the afternoon the twenty-fifth ninth-day return was made at the node, on the steelyard hung on the wall of the building with no roof, and the fen entered the whole of it, and improved none of it.

**THE TWENTY-FIFTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, ON THE STEELYARD, AND IT WAS READ OUT BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY AND READ BACK BY HIM, AND THEN READ ONE AT A TIME BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, A NAME GOING INTO BY AND A NAME GOING INTO WITNESSED BY, AND IT IS THE FIFTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS AND THE LAST ONE OF THOSE NINE DAYS WAS ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH OF LAST MONTH. THE FIGURE IS TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT. THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD WAS LEFT BLANK AGAIN AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT, AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS NINE, AND THE NINE ARE THE SEVENTEENTH, THE EIGHTEENTH, THE NINETEENTH, THE TWENTIETH, THE TWENTY-FIRST, THE TWENTY-SECOND, THE TWENTY-THIRD, THE TWENTY-FOURTH AND THIS ONE, AND THE COUNT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN. NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT THE RULE BY ANYBODY AT THE NODE THIS AFTERNOON, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT AND IMPROVED IT NOT.**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a man standing at a board with the chalk in his hand for about nine seconds and then putting the chalk back in his pocket, and that he has now put the chalk back four returns running and has not said a word about it, and that the fen has not asked him and is not going to.

And the figure is reconciled twice on the page and the two are the same, and the fen entered that it is reconciled twice on purpose, and entered which of the two derivations this holding is using and that it is not refusing it.

**THIS HOLDING IS USING THE ELEVEN-ABOVE DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT. TWO HUNDRED AND SIX PLUS ELEVEN IS TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN, AND ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS TWENTY-TWO IS ALSO TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS NOW HELD FOR SEVEN FIGURES AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A RUN THAT HOLDS FOR SEVEN FIGURES IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE, AND THAT A RUN IS ALSO NOT AN INSTRUCTION, AND THAT A FIGURE WHICH AGREES WITH TWO ARITHMETICS IS NOT CHECKED TWICE BUT DERIVED TWICE, AND THAT THE SECOND IS THE BETTER WORD AND WAS THE WORD ALL ALONG.**

The room was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was about eleven people at a steelyard not saying the number out loud together, and that four of them had it in their mouths and held it, and that holding a number you have just heard somebody else read out is not agreeing with it.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighteen, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I came up behind that barrow this morning and I did not put a hand on it once and I am going to say the whole of that and then I am not going to say any of it again, and the reason is that about four years ago I told a man in a shed a thing I could not do, and I have spent every working morning since then making sure that I do not have to be told anything twice, and a morning where I put a hand on a barrow would be a morning where somebody is a day late somewhere and it would be my hand on it."**

**"Four miles in about an hour and a quarter. That is my figure and I have had it about eleven years and I have never timed it with anything and I am not going to today. He goes up with it and I come up behind it and neither of us says anything for about an hour and a quarter and that is not a hardship, that is the arrangement, and the arrangement has been in place for eleven years and the only thing that changed about it this month is that I found out I had not been thanked on any of them and had assumed I had, and I want that written down as a fact and not as a complaint."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man correcting a thing nobody had accused him of, and that four years of not being thanked is not a grievance and is not a ledger item and is a fact about this holding's manners and not about the man, and the fen has entered both of those and improved neither.

And then the man of about fifty said the other thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-nine, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to say the count and I am not going to raise it, and the reason is that there is a number on the page that says how many times he has come up that road, and that number is eleven, and eleven is where the last written one stopped, and I have not got a way of getting past eleven that is not a guess, and this holding does not put guesses in columns."**

**"So the column stands at eleven and it is a floor and not a ceiling and about four years from now somebody is going to see that it has not moved for four years and think that a man stopped coming up a road in the fourth year. He did not. He came up it this morning in about an hour and a quarter and I watched him do it from a gate and I did not go out and I am entering that I did not go out, and that is the same count as his and it is not the same count."**

Nothing was said for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what was in it, and it was made of a man doing arithmetic about a man who was not in the room, and that the man he was doing the arithmetic about was at that moment about a mile up a road behind a barrow.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fourteen, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a heading on a sheet in a book from Monday with nothing under it, and the body on the other side of eleven hundred yards has said it is not going to be in a list, and I have not asked it a second question and I am not going to, and the reason is that the sentence I said on Monday was the whole sentence I had and a man who has one sentence and comes back with a second one has not learned anything, he has just got more time."**

**"So the morning has gone: a barrow up four miles, a figure on a steelyard, a name into BY and a name into WITNESSED BY, the fifteenth time in eleven years, and a rule left empty for the ninth time by a man who has stopped explaining it, and a column that stands where it stood because nobody can raise it honestly, and the six hundred and fifty-six figure in a book that has not repeated itself in sixty-four mornings. That is the whole of it and I am not going to make it larger than it is by saying anything kind about it."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of about nine people who had come expecting one thing on a Monday and were being given a different thing on a Tuesday, and that neither of the two things was a thing anybody thanked anybody for.

And then the two last things of a return day are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE TWENTY-FIFTH RETURN WENT IN AT TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT AND THE FIFTEENTH NAME-PAIR WENT IN WITH IT, AND THE FIGURE IS RECONCILED TWICE, BEING TWO HUNDRED AND SIX PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS TWENTY-TWO, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT. THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK FOR THE NINTH TIME AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT. A BARROW WENT UP FOUR MILES IN ABOUT AN HOUR AND A QUARTER AND A MAN WALKED BEHIND IT THE WHOLE WAY AND DID NOT PUT A HAND ON IT ONCE, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED, AND THE COUNT OF THE TIMES HE HAS BROUGHT THAT BARROW UP THAT ROAD STANDS AT ELEVEN AND WAS NOT RAISED THIS AFTERNOON AND CANNOT BE RAISED HONESTLY, AND IT IS A COUNT OF THE MANNERS OF THIS HOLDING AND NOT A COUNT OF THE MAN.**

**AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN HAVE NOW WAITED TWENTY-SIX TIMES, ALL OF THEM THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-FIVE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-SIX NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE AND TWENTY-SIX ARE FIFTY-FIVE, AND NOBODY IS BLAMED FOR A FIELD THAT WAITED, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY TODAY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the fourth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a return, and not by the reader of this body, who watched a barrow go up a road from a gate and did not go out: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS TWENTY-SIX, AND ALL TWENTY-SIX OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-FIVE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-SIX NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-FOURTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN THIRTY-ONE DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-ONE TIMES AND FALLEN TEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SIXTY-TWO DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE SIXTY-FIRST MORNING OUT OF SIXTY-THREE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY-EIGHT DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE TWENTY-FIFTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE STEELYARD AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND IS THE FIFTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS, AND THE FIGURE IS TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT, RECONCILED TWICE, BEING TWO HUNDRED AND SIX PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS TWENTY-TWO, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS HELD FOR SEVEN FIGURES AND IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE. THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS NINE, NAMED IN ORDER FROM THE SEVENTEENTH, AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT BY ANYBODY. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SAID THAT HE CAME UP BEHIND THE BARROW FOR THE WHOLE FOUR MILES AND DID NOT PUT A HAND ON IT ONCE, AND THAT THE TIME IS ABOUT AN HOUR AND A QUARTER, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID THAT THE COUNT OF TIMES THAT BARROW HAS BEEN BROUGHT UP THAT ROAD STANDS AT ELEVEN AND WAS NOT RAISED AND CANNOT BE RAISED HONESTLY, AND THAT IT IS A COUNT OF THE MANNERS OF THIS HOLDING AND NOT A COUNT OF THE MAN, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE HEADING ON A SHEET IN A BOOK FROM MONDAY HAS NOTHING UNDER IT AND NOBODY WROTE A NAME UNDER IT AND THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT ASK A SECOND QUESTION. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
