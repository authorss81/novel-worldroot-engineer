# Chapter 298

## The Line Nobody Wrote On

The twenty-ninth of this month was a Thursday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-ninth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and forty-five hundredweight.**

That is the fifty-ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in twenty-six days since the third of this month the series has risen eighteen times and fallen eight times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in fifty-seven days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the fifty-sixth morning out of fifty-eight.

What the silence was made of was a man at the third launder reading out the number he had written in one book and finding the same number in another book and neither of them being a check.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the form with four lines on it was on the middle table and the copy of the ledger was under it and the memorandum of protection of the twentieth of this month was under that, and the fen entered three documents in a stack in the order they arrived in and entered that nobody rearranged them.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of the sound of a sheet being unfolded along a fold that has been folded and unfolded about nine hundred times.

And then the man of about fifty read the form out as it stands, and read it out whole, and the fen entered all of it, and improved none of it.

> **THE FORM, on the middle table of the seed house on the twenty-ninth of this month, in two hands on the face of it and neither of them the reader of this body's, whose hand is on the back of the same sheet where a figure he handed out and cannot check is: LINE ONE. ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING, WHO SPOKE FOR ONE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS AND WAS NOT ASKED WHY. IT WENT IN ON THE NINTH AND WAS READ BACK AND NOT IMPROVED. IT IS THE ONLY FIGURE WRITTEN ON THIS FORM AND IT IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND NINTH-DAY RETURN AND NOT OF THE TWENTY-FOURTH, AND THE TWENTY-FOURTH IS TWO HUNDRED AND SIX AND IS ON A DIFFERENT SHEET IN A DIFFERENT INK. LINE TWO. BLANK. THE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS THAT LINE TWO BELONGS TO WAS NEVER ASKED FOR A FIGURE, AND THAT IS IN ANOTHER MAN'S BOOK, AND HE READ IT OUT. LINE THREE. BLANK. THE SAME IS TRUE OF THE THIRD LINE AND THE REASON IS THE SAME. LINE FOUR. BLANK, BECAUSE THE BODY THAT SPOKE IN THAT ROOM DECLINED TO WRITE A FIGURE ON IT. THE COUNT OF BLANK LINES HERE IS THREE AND IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN AND HAS NOT BEEN ADDED TO ANY OTHER COUNT. AND UNDER THE FOUR LINES, A FIFTH RULED LINE, AND ON THAT FIFTH LINE THERE IS A QUESTION IN THE CLERK'S HAND, AND THERE IS NO ANSWER UNDER IT, AND THE LINE IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT WRITE ON IT AND NOBODY DID.**

The quiet in that room was made of a sheet of paper being looked at by nine people at once and of nobody wanting to be the first to touch the fifth line.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling read the question on that fifth line twice before he answered it, and then he said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifteen, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am not going to write on that line and I am going to say why before anybody asks me for it, and the reason is that a man who has been asked a question in writing and has put a figure under it has answered it, and this holding has two numbers it cannot check, and I know about both of them, and neither of the two is mine, and I do not want there to be a third."**

**"One of them is on the back of the sheet you are all looking at, in another man's hand, and the other one is a figure that a different man wrote down off a dial and then had to go and find somebody else's dial to see whether he was right, and there is no third one in this holding and I am the man who is going to keep it that way for as long as I am the man who holds a wire."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a man who had said no in a room and four of the nine finding that they had been hoping he would not.

And then the man of about fifty read back the count, and the fen entered the whole of it, and nobody improved it.

**THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND IT IS TWO, AND IT IS TWO THIS AFTERNOON, AND IT IS TWO BECAUSE A MAN WHO SPOKE FOR ONE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS ON THE NINTH OF THIS MONTH DECLINED TO PUT A FIGURE UNDER A QUESTION THAT WAS ENTERED IN THE CLERK'S HAND, AND NOBODY IMPROVED THE REASON AND NOBODY ASKED HIM FOR A BETTER ONE, AND A REASON THAT IS ASKED TWICE IS A REASON THAT IS BEING IMPROVED, AND A REASON THAT IS BEING IMPROVED IS NOT A REASON ANYMORE, IT IS A CASE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man with a pencil putting the pencil down on a table and leaving it there where anybody could pick it up.

And then the memorandum of protection of the twentieth of this month was read out again in that room, and only the eleventh line of it, and the fen entered the line as it stands and did not improve it and did not shorten it and did not say it in a shorter way afterward.

> **NO SEQUENCE, AND NO PART CONTAINING A SEQUENCE, AND NO CRADLE, IS TO BE REMOVED, BROKEN, OPENED OR SET AGAINST ANY VALVE BY ANY PERSON WITHOUT A WRITTEN INSTRUMENT OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF IT.**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a sheet of paper with ten lines above this one and nothing said about any of the ten, and that a page which puts a sentence about a part in the eleventh line has put nine lines in front of it on purpose, and that a man who reads that page from the top has read a page about a district.

And then the fen entered the other half of it, and improved none of it.

**THE ELEVENTH LINE IS THE ONLY LINE ON THAT PAGE THAT IS ABOUT ANYTHING IN THAT ROOM, AND IT GOVERNS A SEQUENCE, AND A PART CONTAINING A SEQUENCE, AND A CRADLE, AND IT GOVERNS NONE OF THE FOURTH OBJECT ON THAT TABLE, WHICH IS A KEY AND IS A KEY, AND A THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS LOOKED AT BY NOBODY THIS AFTERNOON, AND IT WAS NOT LIFTED AND IT WAS NOT OPENED AND IT WAS NOT BROKEN AND IT WAS NOT SET AGAINST ANYTHING, AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE IN THIS HOLDING WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE THIS AFTERNOON, AND THE SECOND OF THE TWO WAS NOT ASKED.**

The room was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was a pail of water being topped up outside the door by the person who keeps the seed house, who was not thanked and who did not come in.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and seven, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"The window of the office's work opens on the sixth, which is seven days off this morning, and shuts on the eleventh, and I am going to say the second half of that out loud in this room because a window is a thing four people in here keep saying one half of. No work is carried out of a window. That is not a rule anybody made. It is the shape of a window, and a thing done outside a window is a thing done with nobody watching the shape of it, and this holding has watched a shape of something go wrong twice in four years and does not need a third."**

**"And I have not asked for a piece of paper from that office and I am not going to ask for one before the sixth, and the reason is that the eleventh line says a written instrument of the office named at the head of it, and an office that has a window has a way of putting a request in it, and a request put into a window comes back with a date on it, and a date on it is a thing a room can spend, and this room has spent nine days twice this month and got a cradle emptied in one of them and a memorandum in the other."**

Nothing was said for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what was in it, and it was made of nine people deciding that the only safe thing to do about a window was to count the days to it, and that counting is not a plan and is the only honest thing available on a Thursday.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-three, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to enter the days to it and I am going to enter the word seventh against a date, and I am going to write under it the two halves together, and I have never in four years written a date with a rule attached to it and I am not going to start because one of the dates is the first of a month and the other is the eleventh of one, and a page that says the sixth is a day that opens nothing."**

**"It opens six days of work and a man in an office four hundred miles off deciding whether to be in a room, and there is a person appointed to be on that line from the twentieth of this month and that person has not been named and is not going to be named by anybody in this room, and I am not going to write a column for a person who has not got a name in it, and that is my whole contribution to the window and it is about nine words long."**

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of a calendar being put down on a table and being agreed with by everybody in the room, and that a room which agrees with a date has agreed with the only thing in the month that nobody in it can argue with.

And then about four people in that room put the seventh day off into their own heads at four separate tables in four separate yards between that hour and dark, and the fen entered that, and entered that none of them said it out loud, and entered that a day off that four people have privately written down is a day off that no clerk in this holding will ever see, and that the standing has a column for work done and not a column for days a man has set aside, and that this holding has never once had the second and that this is the first morning anybody has needed it.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered what the silence was made of, which was four men and two women doing arithmetic on a week they had not been promised.

And then the two last things of that Thursday are his and they are in his own book under his own hand and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED AND A MAN WHO SPOKE FOR ONE BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS ON THE NINTH OF THIS MONTH DECLINED TO FILL IT OUT LOUD WITH THE REASON GIVEN FIRST, AND THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND IS TWO, AND THE FIRST ONE IS STILL NOT NAMED. THE ELEVENTH LINE OF A MEMORANDUM DATED THE TWENTIETH OF LAST MONTH IS THE ONLY THING ON THAT PAGE THAT IS ABOUT ANYTHING IN THAT ROOM, AND IT WAS READ OUT AND WAS NOT IMPROVED ON AND WAS NOT SAID A SECOND WAY AFTERWARD. A THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS LOOKED AT BY NOBODY.**

**AND THE WINDOW OPENS ON THE SIXTH, WHICH IS SEVEN DAYS OFF THIS MORNING, AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW, AND NOBODY IN THAT ROOM SAID THE SECOND HALF OF THAT UNTIL THE ENGINEER OF RECORD SAID IT, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT AND IS NOT GOING TO IMPROVE IT.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-ninth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a form, and not by the reader of this body, who read the eleventh line out and did not improve it: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE FIFTY-NINTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN TWENTY-SIX DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN EIGHTEEN TIMES AND FALLEN EIGHT TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FIFTY-SEVEN DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FIFTY-SIXTH MORNING OUT OF FIFTY-EIGHT. THE FORM WAS READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE. IT CARRIES FOUR LINES, ONE FIGURE AND THREE BLANKS, AND A FIFTH RULED LINE UNDER THEM WITH A QUESTION ON IT IN THE CLERK'S HAND AND NO ANSWER UNDER IT. LINE ONE IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING AND IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND NOT OF THE TWENTY-FOURTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING DECLINED TO WRITE ON THE FIFTH LINE, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND THE REASON WAS NOT IMPROVED AND WAS NOT ASKED FOR A SECOND TIME. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND IS TWO AND THE FIRST ONE IS NOT NAMED. THE ELEVENTH LINE OF THE MEMORANDUM OF THE TWENTIETH OF LAST MONTH WAS READ OUT ALONE AND WAS NOT PARAPHRASED, BEING THAT NO SEQUENCE, AND NO PART CONTAINING A SEQUENCE, AND NO CRADLE, IS TO BE REMOVED, BROKEN, OPENED OR SET AGAINST ANY VALVE BY ANY PERSON WITHOUT A WRITTEN INSTRUMENT OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF IT. IT DOES NOT GOVERN A KEY. A THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS LOOKED AT BY NOBODY AND WAS NOT LIFTED, OPENED, BROKEN OR SET AGAINST ANYTHING, AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENS ON THE SIXTH, BEING SEVEN DAYS OFF THIS MORNING, AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
