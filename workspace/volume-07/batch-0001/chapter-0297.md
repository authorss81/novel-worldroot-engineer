# Chapter 297

## Four Questions And It Is Four

The twenty-eighth of this month was a Wednesday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the twenty-eighth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and forty-seven hundredweight.**

That is the fifty-eighth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in twenty-five days since the third of this month the series has risen eighteen times and fallen seven times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in fifty-six days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the fifty-fifth morning out of fifty-seven.

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about nine minutes of the third launder running without a man standing at it, because there was a man at it and he was in the water up to the knee and the count of that kind of morning is a different count.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and forty-two days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and eighty-nine**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on two mornings in this month and that the reason this morning is that a man was coming to the room at the fourth hour of the afternoon and there is a kind of question you can only ask a man once you have said two numbers out loud in front of him.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and Soren Rill, of about thirty-five, of an Assembly in the Thornwild, was in the room, and not at the edge of a yard, and the fen entered that, and entered that he had come on his own account and at his own cost and that nobody in this holding sent for him and that the reader of this body did not send for him, and that the last mile and a bit of it is not a cart road and has not been a cart road in living memory, and that he came the last of it on foot and said so before anybody asked him where he had been.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of nine people not asking a man who had walked in from a road none of them had been on that day what his journey was, and that the not asking was not politeness, it was a rule nobody had written down.

And the fen entered what the room was like with him in it, because a room is not the same room with a ninth thing in it, and there was no chair for him and nobody fetched one in about four minutes and then a chair was fetched and nobody said why it had taken four minutes, and he sat with his hands on his knees and the mud was off his boots before he sat down and not after, and about four people in that room noticed the boots and about five did not.

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a man working a thing off his boots so as not to put it anywhere.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and five, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to ask you one question and I am going to say the reason for it before I ask it, and the reason is that I have asked you three questions in four months and I have never once written down what I was asking you for, and a man who has asked three questions and cannot say what he wanted has not been asking a man questions, he has been checking whether a man would still be in the room."**

**"So here it is. Three of those three were asked of you and not of the body you come from, and I want them kept apart in the book, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in the line of asking your Assembly for a figure is nil, and it is nil, and it is not added to the number I am about to say."**

The room was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was a man putting a number on one line and refusing to put it on the other and four people in the room noticing that he had done that on purpose.

**"Then here is the question. What does your Assembly call a thing that is done at the near end of a channel by people who are not at the far end of it? Not what it is. What it is called, in the room where it is decided whether to allow it."**

The count of questions the reader of this body has asked him is now four and is four, and it went off three on to four this afternoon, and it went off on a Wednesday afternoon in a room with the door shut and not in a yard, and the fen has entered both of those and improved neither.

And then Soren Rill said one thing, and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is five, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"You asked for the word and you are going to get the word and nothing else, and the reason is that I have been asked four questions in four months and three of them were about what a thing does, and the fourth is the first one anybody has asked me that could be answered in one word, and I am going to answer it and then I am going to say one more thing and the two of them are not the same shape."**

**"The word is *work done*. Not a supply. Not a delivery. Not a favor. In that room a thing done at one end of a channel by people who are not at the other end is called work done, and it is called that because the word carries the fact that people did it, and the moment anybody changes the word the fact goes out of it and what is left is a quantity arriving somewhere."**

Nothing was said for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what was in it, and it was made of a man who has said one word correctly being left alone in a room, and that a man left alone in a room says more, and that this holding has learned that and has not found a way to stop it.

And then he said the other thing, and the fen entered the whole of it, and improved none of it.

**"And now the other shape. I am going to say one thing that is not a word and put it on that table beside the other piece of paper, and I am going to say the reason first, and the reason is that a woman wrote a term yesterday about paying in the unit a thing is paid in, and I have read it twice, and it is correct, and it is not enough, and I am not going to say that to her face in a room and I am not going to say it quietly either."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of about four people deciding not to look at a woman, and that not looking at a woman in a room of nine is a thing the fen has entered before and has never once improved on.

And then he put it on the middle table in his own hand and did not soften one word of it, and the fen entered all of it and improved none of it.

> **THE SECOND TERM, written out on the twenty-eighth of this month in about nine people in the seed house by Soren Rill, in his own hand, and left on the middle table beside the first term, and not read out again: A THING THAT CONNECTS TWO PLACES IS NOT FINISHED UNTIL THE PEOPLE AT THE FAR END HAVE BEEN ASKED AND HAVE ANSWERED. AN ANSWER GIVEN BY SOMEBODY WHO WAS NOT ASKED IS NOT AN ANSWER. A THING THAT CANNOT BE UNDONE BY A REFUSAL IS NOT A CONNECTION AND IS A PIPE, AND A PIPE IS NOT A THING YOU PUT ACROSS A SETTLEMENT WITHOUT ITS PEOPLE HEARING IT FIRST.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man reading two pieces of paper in a language that was not his and finding that one of them could not be satisfied by anybody saying no.

And then the two were read side by side, and the fen entered that they do not agree, and entered what they are.

**THE FIRST TERM SAYS THAT A THING IS PAID FOR IN THE UNIT IT IS PAID IN AND NOT IN THE UNIT IT IS WANTED IN, AND THE SECOND TERM SAYS THAT NOTHING IS FINISHED UNTIL THE FAR END HAS ANSWERED, AND THE TWO OF THEM ARE ABOUT DIFFERENT HALVES OF THE SAME ARGUMENT AND THE SECOND ONE, IF IT IS WRITTEN DOWN, CANNOT BE SATISFIED BY A BODY THAT SAYS NO. THE FIRST TERM CAN BE SATISFIED BY A REFUSAL. THE SECOND TERM CANNOT. THE FEN HAS ENTERED BOTH OF THOSE AND HAS NOT ENTERED WHICH OF THE TWO IS RIGHT, AND THE REASON IS THAT BOTH OF THEM WERE WRITTEN BY SOMEBODY WHO HAS A STAKE AND NEITHER OF THEM HAS ASKED THE OTHER ONE FOR A REASON YET.**

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of a pencil being picked up and put down twice by two different people and neither of them writing.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-two, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have had the first of those two on that table since yesterday afternoon and the second one since about a quarter past the hour, and I have not put a third thing beside them and I am not going to, and the reason is that a third thing on that table would be a third person's answer to two other people and about four people in this room are about to be that third person, and I have watched a page of mine fill up with other people's opinions this month and I would like one table in this holding to stay empty."**

The silence was made of four people thinking better of him than he is and none of them saying it, and that the fen has entered that and is not going to improve it.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the afternoon, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and six, and nobody improved it.

**"I am not going to improve on either of them and I am not going to pick between them this afternoon, and the reason is that I have been in this room since the second hour and the only person in it who has not been asked anything all day is the man of the north row with the cough, and he is not here and nobody went for him, and I am entering that I did not go for him and that I am not going to say that is the right thing to have done."**

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing tender was said in that room on the twenty-eighth of this month, and the fen entered the cost, and the name of the cost is a walk of about four miles and a half each way in one day by a man who is about thirty-five, and that it is not a figure about a bridge and is a figure about a man, and the fen has entered both and improved neither.

**A MAN CAME FOUR MILES AND A HALF ON FOOT IN ONE DAY BECAUSE A QUESTION WAS GOING TO BE ASKED IN A ROOM, AND HE WAS NOT SENT FOR, AND NOBODY PAID HIM AND NOBODY THANKED HIM, AND A ROOM OF NINE PEOPLE HE HAS MET IN A MONTH AND A HALF DID NOT ASK HIM HOW HE CAME, AND THE ONE QUESTION THAT WAS ASKED HIM WAS ANSWERED IN ONE WORD, AND THE WORD WAS WORK DONE, AND THE ARGUMENT HE PUT ON THE TABLE AFTER IT CANNOT BE SATISFIED BY A PERSON WHO SAYS NO.**

**AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HIM IS FOUR AND IS FOUR, AND IT IS NOT ADDED TO THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS HE HAS ASKED ANYBODY IN THE LINE OF A FIGURE FOR THAT STORE, WHICH IS NIL AND IS NIL, AND THE MAN OF THE NORTH ROW WITH THE COUGH IS NOT IN THIS ROOM AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE IN IT AND WAS NOT ASKED TO HOLD ANYTHING THIS AFTERNOON, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED ALL THREE OF THOSE AND IMPROVED NONE OF THEM.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-eighth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a question, and not by the reader of this body, who asked it: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY-SEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE FIFTY-EIGHTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN TWENTY-FIVE DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN EIGHTEEN TIMES AND FALLEN SEVEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FIFTY-SIX DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FIFTY-FIFTH MORNING OUT OF FIFTY-SEVEN. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY-TWO DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-NINE DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. SOREN RILL, OF ABOUT THIRTY-FIVE, OF AN ASSEMBLY IN THE THORNWILD, WAS IN THE ROOM AND NOT AT THE EDGE OF A YARD, HAVING COME ON HIS OWN ACCOUNT AND AT HIS OWN COST, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING SENT FOR HIM, AND THE LAST MILE AND A BIT IS NOT A CART ROAD AND HE CAME THE LAST OF IT ON FOOT AND SAID SO BEFORE ANYBODY ASKED HIM WHERE HE HAD BEEN. HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE READER OF THIS BODY ASKED HIM ONE QUESTION, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS HE HAS ASKED HIM IS FOUR AND IS FOUR AND IT WENT OFF THREE ON TO FOUR THIS AFTERNOON IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT AND NOT IN A YARD, AND IT IS NOT ADDED TO THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS HE HAS ASKED ANYBODY IN THE LINE OF ASKING THAT ASSEMBLY FOR A FIGURE, WHICH IS NIL. THE MAN OF THE NORTH ROW WITH THE COUGH WAS NOT IN THAT ROOM AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE IN IT AND WAS NOT ASKED TO HOLD ANYTHING. A SECOND TERM WAS PUT ON THE MIDDLE TABLE BY SOREN RILL IN HIS OWN HAND AND WAS NOT SOFTENED, AND IT WAS READ BESIDE THE FIRST TERM, AND THE TWO OF THEM DO NOT AGREE, AND NOBODY ENTERED WHICH OF THEM IS RIGHT.**
