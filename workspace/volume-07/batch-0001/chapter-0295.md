# Chapter 295

## Two Hundred And Six

The twenty-sixth of this month was a Monday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **twenty-four**, and entered that all twenty-four of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **fifty-three mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty-four not**, and that twenty-nine and twenty-four are fifty-three, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered what the silence was made of, which was about two people at the north gate not saying anything to each other over a gate that is not theirs.

And the figure for the twenty-sixth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and forty-four hundredweight.**

That is the fifty-sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in twenty-three days since the third of this month the series has risen seventeen times and fallen six times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in fifty-four days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the fifty-third morning out of fifty-five.

The silence was made of the third launder running and of a man counting seventeen and six twice and getting the same two numbers both times.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and forty days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and eighty-seven**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on one morning so far in this month and that the reason this morning is that a man who has been counting a thing every nine days for a month needs two other figures of the same shape to know the month has not moved under him.

The reason for the use log was not given this morning and is not going to be given again in a season, and the use log stands at **fifteen lines** and has stood at fifteen lines for a month and a half, and a man who has given a reason once and then stopped has not forgotten the reason and has decided the reason was not the point.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the copy of the ledger of the Office of the Continuity for the maintenance of the Crown Engine was on the middle table, and the record of the twenty-fourth return came in on the ordinary post of the afternoon before and went on the middle table under it, and the fen entered two documents in a stack and entered that the man of about fifty did not rearrange them and entered the reason, which is that a bookkeeper who puts a return under a ledger is a bookkeeper who has decided the ledger is older.

What the silence was made of was paper being squared off against a table and of nobody asking whose hand the second one was in.

And then the man of about fifty read out the record of the twenty-fourth ninth-day return as it stands, and read it out whole, and the fen entered all of it and improved none of it.

**THE TWENTY-FOURTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH OF THIS MONTH, AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, ON THE STEELYARD HUNG ON THE WALL OF THE BUILDING WITH NO ROOF, AND IT WAS READ OUT BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY AND READ BACK BY HIM, AND THEN READ ONE AT A TIME BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, A NAME GOING INTO BY AND A NAME GOING INTO WITNESSED BY, AND IT IS THE FOURTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS. THE FIGURE IS TWO HUNDRED AND SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS AND THE LAST ONE OF THOSE NINE DAYS WAS ON THE SIXTEENTH OF THIS MONTH AT ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE AND THIS ONE IS NINE DAYS AFTER IT. THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD WAS LEFT BLANK AGAIN AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS EIGHT, AND THE EIGHT ARE THE SEVENTEENTH, THE EIGHTEENTH, THE NINETEENTH, THE TWENTIETH, THE TWENTY-FIRST, THE TWENTY-SECOND, THE TWENTY-THIRD AND THIS ONE, AND THE COUNT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN AND HAS NOT BEEN ADDED TO ANY OTHER COUNT IN THIS BOOK.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of four bodies of households each of which has said a number out loud once every nine days for eleven years and none of which has ever been asked to say it faster.

And the figure is reconciled twice on the page and the two are the same, and the fen entered that it is reconciled twice on purpose, because a return that can be reconciled once is a return somebody can make up.

**ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR PLUS TWENTY-TWO IS TWO HUNDRED AND SIX, AND ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS ELEVEN IS ALSO TWO HUNDRED AND SIX, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS NOW HELD FOR SIX FIGURES AND THAT A RUN THAT HOLDS FOR SIX FIGURES IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE, AND THAT ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR IS THE FIGURE ON LINE ONE OF THE FORM ON THE SAME TABLE AND IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND NOT OF THIS ONE, AND A FIGURE ON A LINE IS A FIGURE ON A LINE AND NOT A STANDING AGAIN.**

The quiet in that room was made of about four people doing arithmetic at the same time and getting the same two answers and neither of them saying so.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and three, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say the whole of what is on that table and then I am going to read the standing out, and the reason is that twenty-four fields have now waited on the same empty morning of a rota, and a man who lets a count like that grow without saying in a room where it came from has stopped keeping the days apart, and the days are the only thing I have got."**

**"The figure is two hundred and six and it is the fourteenth name-pair in eleven years and the second rule is blank for the eighth time. The ledger copy is ninety-one rows long with five columns with heads and a sixth with nothing at the head of it, and it is blank on eleven of the ninety-one, and the eleven are listed in a district return that is not enclosed, and seven of the eleven are not on this line. The five rows on line 41 were written down and corrected in a room five days ago by a man with a book and I am not going to correct them a second time, and a correction made twice is a correction a person has started enjoying."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a man reading a page he has read before and not finding the thing he was looking for and not saying so.

And then the standing was read out, and the fen entered all of it, and improved none of it.

**THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR IS TWO AND THE COUNT WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FIVE IS ONE AND IT IS HIM, AND SIX IS NOT A COLUMN. THE COUNT OF PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO HAVE A ROOTMARK IS TWO AND NOTHING SAID ABOUT EITHER ARM THIS MONTH IS A THIRD ACCOUNT. THE COUNT OF PERSONS WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR AND WALK OUT OF THE GROUND AFTERWARDS IS ONE AND IT IS HIM. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM ON THE INSIDE OF THE LEFT FOREARM OF THE READER OF THIS BODY ARE FOUR AND THEY RECONCILE NONE OF ONE ANOTHER, AND A MARK THAT IS HOT IS NOT A MARK AND A HEADACHE IS NOT A MARK AND A BRIDGE IS NOT A MARK, AND THIS MORNING THE ARM WAS WARM AND WAS NOT LOOKED AT AND THAT IS NOT A FIFTH ACCOUNT. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND THE FIRST ONE IS NOT NAMED. THE COUNT OF NOT KNOWNS STANDING AGAINST HIM IS SIX AND THE UNIT OF NONE OF THEM HAS BEEN SUPPLIED AND A THING WITH A SHAPE AND NOT A UNIT IS NOT IN THAT COLUMN. THE REQUESTS AGAINST NOTHING AND THE NOTES UNDER SECTION NINE STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND FIFTY-THREE. THE BODIES AT THE NODE ARE FIVE AND THE NUMBER WAS NOT GIVEN IN A YARD. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY HAS BEEN FETCHED NINETEEN TIMES AND WAS NOT FETCHED ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH AND IS NOT FETCHED AGAIN, AND THE NEXT FETCHING DAY IS THE FIRST OF NEXT MONTH AND IS FIVE DAYS OFF THIS MORNING. THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STANDS AT FORTY-SIX AND HAS NOT BEEN READ SINCE THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH, AND THE SIX OTHERS ARE BLANK AND HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND HAVE HAD NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM FOR FOUR YEARS, AND NOBODY WENT UP TO THAT DOOR AND NOBODY ASKED. THE COMPOST LINE FELL ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH AT ONE HUNDRED POUNDS AND SIXTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN SIXTEEN AND IT IS PAID, AND THE NEXT LINE FALLS ON THE FIRST OF NEXT MONTH AND IS NOT ENTERED. THE LEAK IS THREE-QUARTERS OF AN INCH WITH THREE REASONS AND NO CAUSE AND NO DUE DATE AND NOBODY HAS ADDED A FOURTH. THE COUNT OF ENTRIES THAT CARRY A DAY AND NOT A FIGURE IS FOUR AND IS FOUR AND THE NEXT ONE FALLS ON THE SIXTEENTH OF NEXT MONTH AND IS NOT THIS MORNING'S BUSINESS. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE FORM ON THE MIDDLE TABLE CARRIES FOUR LINES, ONE FIGURE AND THREE BLANKS, AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED, AND THE DOCUMENT THAT CAME DOWN THE FEN ROAD DID NOT FILL IT AND THE MEMORANDUM OF THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH DID NOT FILL IT, AND NOBODY WROTE ON IT.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a person who keeps a book reading a standing he had read on the twenty-fifth and finding that not one figure in it had moved, and that a man who finds that nothing has moved is either relieved or unemployed and that the fen has entered both and is not going to say which.

And then the two closing things of a first morning are his and they went into his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE TWENTY-FOURTH RETURN WENT IN AT TWO HUNDRED AND SIX AND THE FOURTEENTH NAME-PAIR WENT IN WITH IT, AND IT IS THE FIRST TIME THE FIGURE HAS BEEN ON A PAGE IN THE FORTY-NINE DAYS SINCE A RELAY WAS EMPTIED, AND A FIGURE THAT COMES BACK ONTO A PAGE NINE DAYS LATE IS NOT A CORRECTION AND IS NOT A REVERSAL AND IS A THING THAT WAS DONE ON A MONDAY AFTERNOON BY FIVE PEOPLE WHO COULD SEE THE DIAL.**

**AND THE SEVENTH COLUMN IS FORTY-SIX AND IS NOT READ, AND THE OTHER SIX HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND WILL HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM TOMORROW, AND A DOOR NINE HUNDRED YARDS OFF THAT SIX MEN WALK PAST TWICE A WEEK HAS BEEN CARRYING A COLUMN WITH A HEAD ON IT AND SIX COLUMNS WITH NO HEAD ON THEM FOR FOUR YEARS, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT AND IS NOT GOING TO DO ANYTHING ABOUT IT TODAY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-sixth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a return, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and said one true thing about a day turning: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS TWENTY-FOUR, AND ALL TWENTY-FOUR OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-THREE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-FOUR NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE AND TWENTY-FOUR ARE FIFTY-THREE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE TWENTY-SIXTH, WHICH IS THE FIFTY-SIXTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN TWENTY-THREE DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN SEVENTEEN TIMES AND FALLEN SIX TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FIFTY-FOUR DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE FIFTY-THIRD MORNING OUT OF FIFTY-FIVE, AND THE TWO MORNINGS IT WAS NOT WRITTEN ON WERE THE NINTH AND THE TENTH OF LAST MONTH, AND NOBODY HAS REDUCED THE PAIR BY THOSE TWO. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVEN DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE TWO ARE FORTY-SEVEN DAYS APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE TWENTY-FOURTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH OF THIS MONTH AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, ON THE STEELYARD HUNG ON THE WALL OF THE BUILDING WITH NO ROOF, AND WAS READ OUT BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY AND READ BACK BY HIM, AND WAS THEN READ ONE AT A TIME BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, A NAME GOING INTO BY AND A NAME GOING INTO WITNESSED BY, AND IT IS THE FOURTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS AND THE FIGURE IS TWO HUNDRED AND SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT. IT IS RECONCILED TWICE, BEING ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR PLUS TWENTY-TWO AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS ELEVEN, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS HELD FOR SIX FIGURES AND IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE. THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD WAS LEFT BLANK AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS EIGHT, NAMED IN ORDER FROM THE SEVENTEENTH, AND THE COUNT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN. THE COPY OF THE LEDGER OF THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY FOR THE MAINTENANCE OF THE CROWN ENGINE IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND IS NINETY-ONE ROWS LONG WITH FIVE COLUMNS WITH HEADS AND A SIXTH WITH NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND IT IS BLANK ON ELEVEN OF THE NINETY-ONE, AND THE ELEVEN ARE LISTED IN A DISTRICT RETURN WHICH IS NOT ENCLOSED, AND SEVEN OF THE ELEVEN ARE NOT ON THIS LINE. THE FIVE ROWS ON LINE 41 WERE CORRECTED IN A ROOM FIVE DAYS AGO AND WERE NOT CORRECTED AGAIN THIS MORNING. THE STANDING WAS READ OUT WHOLE AND IMPROVED IN NO PART. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY. NOTHING TENDER WAS SAID IN THAT ROOM.**
