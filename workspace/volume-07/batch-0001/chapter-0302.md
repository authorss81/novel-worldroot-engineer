# Chapter 302

## We Are Not Going In The Schedule

The third of this month was a Monday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the third went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and fifty-one hundredweight.**

That is the sixty-third figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in thirty days since the third of this month the series has risen twenty times and fallen ten times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in sixty-one days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixtieth morning out of sixty-two.

The room was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was the sound of a room that had been told at the second hour that it was going to be asked something at the fourth and had spent two hours not saying so.

And at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the two pieces of paper were on the middle table side by side where they had been put on the twenty-seventh and the twenty-eighth of last month, and the man of about fifty had a sheet of his own in front of him that had a heading on it and nothing under the heading, and Soren Rill was in the room, and the fen entered all of that and improved none of it.

Nothing was said for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what was in it, and it was made of a man with a heading on a sheet and nine people waiting for him to say the heading out loud, and that he did not say it out loud for about a minute and a half and then did, and that the minute and a half has been entered and is not improved either.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that was his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-eight, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to ask a man a question in a room and I am going to say why I want the answer before I ask it, and the reason is that I have kept a column for that schedule for four years and the column has nothing against that line that this holding can put a name to, and I have found out in the last two days that there is a body on the other side of eleven hundred yards and about two hundred yards of settlement, and I have a heading and no name, and a heading with nothing under it is a hole."**

**"So here is the question and it is a question and not a request, and I want the answer in the room. Would your Assembly be willing to be entered in the schedule for that line as a body of households?"**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of nine people finding out at the same moment that they had not all meant the same thing by the four days they had been spending on this, and that about four of them had meant his question and about four had meant a different one and had not said so.

And then Soren Rill said the reason before he said the answer, and he gave it first, and the count of that in this holding's history is six, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"They will not, and I am going to say why first and I am going to say only the one reason, and the reason is that the question is about the schedule and not about the water and not about the channel and not about a bridge, and if it had been about the water I would have to carry a different answer and you would not have liked it either."**

**"A schedule is a list a body is looked up in. A body that is on a list can be found. A body that can be found can be asked again by whoever holds the list, and can be asked twice, and can be asked for a figure it has already said it has not got, and there is nobody standing anywhere between the two ends of that to stop it. We have been in a list before. I do not have to tell this room what a list is for."**

**"And that is the whole of the reason. It is not about the water and it is not about a bridge and it is not about the length of anything, and if anybody in this room wants to improve on it they are going to have to say a better reason about a schedule, and I do not think one of you has got one, and if one of you has got one then it is not about this and I would rather hear it about something else."**

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of about nine people finding out that a refusal with a reason in it is a very different object in a room from a refusal without one, and that this holding has four of the second kind on its own table and has never had one of the first kind from anybody else.

And then the man of about fifty did not get a better reason, and the fen entered all of it, and improved none of it.

**"Then that is the answer and I am not going to ask for a second one and I am not going to ask the Assembly again this season, and the reason I am saying that out loud is that a man who has been refused once and comes back with the same question in a different month has not been refused, he has been answered, and then he has gone home and got used to the answer, and the fen has entered that a heading and no name under it is a hole and a hole is cheaper than a name that is not there."**

The silence was made of a man putting a sheet of paper face down, and that he did not tear it up and did not fold it and did not put it in the fire, and that it is in a book and that the fen has entered where.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twelve, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am not going to argue the point and I am not going to ask a second question, and the reason is that I have been in nine rooms in fifteen days and every one of them I came out with a thing I already had, and a man who comes out of a tenth room with a tenth thing he walked in with has not been in a tenth room."**

**"And the count of questions I have asked him is four and it is four, and it did not move this afternoon, and I am entering that on the same page as the answer, because those two facts sitting next to each other on one afternoon is the whole of what I have learned about asking people things in five months and it cost one refusal to learn it."**

The room did not vote, and nobody in about nine people put a hand up either way, and a room which does not vote on a refusal is a room that has understood that a refusal is not a question and does not need the count of hands, and the count of hands was not taken and the number of hands is not a count this holding keeps.

And then the door of the seed house was opened and shut by somebody going out for air and by somebody coming back in, and about two minutes passed, and in those two minutes about four people in that room looked at the two pieces of paper on the middle table and at the place where the second one had been, and the fen entered that no one of them touched either paper and that the person who went out for air was the man of about fifty and that he came back in and sat down where he had been sitting and that his hands were wet and that the fen has entered the hands and improved neither of them.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered what the silence was made of, which was a room learning the shape of itself with about nine fewer words in it than it had at the second hour.

And then Soren Rill said the other thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seven, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I wrote a second term on Wednesday and I am withdrawing it this afternoon in front of nine people, and the reason is that what I wrote is not a term. A term is a thing a body can refuse and still be inside of. What I wrote needs an answer, and a body that says no is then outside of it, and that is a condition with a tablecloth on it, and I have been writing conditions in this holding for a year and putting them on tables in front of people, and I am not going to do that with a tablecloth on it."**

**"I am not withdrawing it because it is wrong. It is not wrong. I am withdrawing it because it cannot be satisfied by the answer I have just given, and a thing that cannot be satisfied by a refusal is not a term, and I have been calling things terms when they were conditions since about a year ago and I did not know the difference until a man in a room asked me a question and I found out what the difference costs."**

And then he took the second piece of paper off the middle table and put it in his own coat, and the first piece of paper stayed on the table where Tova Reed had put it, and the fen entered both of those movements and improved neither.

What the silence was made of was a woman of about thirty-eight in that room not looking at the second piece of paper going out of it, and entered that she was not asked about it, and entered that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and did not move on the afternoon her own term was left alone on a table, and that a nil that holds on a day like this is a thing this holding earned and is not going to be thanked for.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is seventeen, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"A thing that is refused is not a thing that failed and I am going to say the rest of it, and the reason is that eight of us have been standing in this room for two days waiting for something to go wrong and it has not gone wrong, and a room that has been braced for two days has got to be told what it is standing in."**

**"The paper with four lines on it is on the middle table and nobody wrote on the fifth line and nobody is going to. The second term is in a coat. The heading has no name under it and the name is not going to be invented. The gap of two feet at the kerb is open and nothing is going in it this season. A thing under a cloth is under a cloth. And a body two hundred yards long on the other side of eleven hundred yards has said it is not going to be in a list, and that is not a failure of this holding, that is a thing a body is entitled to say, and I have been in this holding all my life and nobody ever taught me that a body is entitled to say a thing like that to a room."**

Nobody improved one item of that, and the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room and the count is for yards, and he did not say a number out loud this afternoon at all, and a man who has gone fifteen days without saying a number in a yard has not stopped having a number and has stopped putting it where it cannot be taken back.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the afternoon, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirteen, and nobody improved it, and nothing tender was said in that room on any part of that Monday.

**"A thing that is refused is not a thing that failed. I want that on the page with the day on it and I want it read in about four years by somebody who has a page in front of him and no memory of it, because in about four years there is going to be a man in a room who is going to hear that an Assembly said no, and he is going to hear it as a thing that did not happen, and it happened, and it was correct, and nobody was thanked for it and nobody should have been."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the length of that silence was about a minute and a half and that it was the longest one in eight days, and entered what it was made of, which was nine people not applauding, and that is the only noise that nine people can make in a room at about the fifth hour of the afternoon without saying anything, and the fen has entered that and improved it not.

**AND AN ASSEMBLY DECLINED TO BE ENTERED IN A SCHEDULE AT ALL, WITH THE REASON GIVEN FIRST, AND THE REASON WAS ABOUT THE SCHEDULE AND NOT ABOUT THE WATER AND NOT ABOUT THE CHANNEL AND NOT ABOUT A BRIDGE, AND NOBODY IMPROVED IT, AND THE MAN WHO ASKED WAS NOT ANSWERED WITH A BETTER REASON, AND THE ROOM DID NOT VOTE, AND NO DOCUMENT WAS TORN UP AND NO FIGURE WAS FALSIFIED, AND THE HEADING THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAD WRITTEN ON A SHEET OF HIS OWN HAS NO NAME UNDER IT AND THE SHEET IS IN A BOOK AND WAS NOT TORN UP.**

**AND THE SECOND TERM IS IN A COAT AND THE FIRST TERM IS ON THE TABLE, AND THEY DID NOT AGREE WITH ONE ANOTHER ON THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF LAST MONTH AND ONE OF THEM IS GONE, AND THE ONE THAT IS GONE WAS CORRECT, AND A THING THAT IS REFUSED IS NOT A THING THAT FAILED, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FIVE IS FOUR AND IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY TODAY, AND THE NEXT MORNING IS A MORNING ON WHICH A BARROW IS GOING TO COME UP FOUR MILES.**
