# Chapter 300

## Three Clocks On One Morning

The first of this month was a Saturday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the first went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and forty-eight hundredweight.**

That is the sixty-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in twenty-eight days since the third of last month the series has risen nineteen times and fallen nine times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in fifty-nine days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the fifty-eighth morning out of sixty.

And the month turned in the night, and the fen entered the turn and entered the count beside it, and did not enter one figure on top of the other.

**THE COUNT OF THE SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND IT IS SIXTY-ONE THIS MORNING, AND IT WAS FIFTY-FIVE ON THE TWENTY-FIFTH OF LAST MONTH, AND A MONTH TURNED IN THE NIGHT BETWEEN THOSE TWO MORNINGS AND THE COUNT DID NOT RESET, AND A SERIES THAT HAS RUN SIXTY MORNINGS WITHOUT A REPEAT DOES NOT BEGIN AGAIN BECAUSE A CALENDAR DID IT. THE FIFTY-FIVE FIGURES THAT CAME BEFORE THIS ONE WERE ALL DIFFERENT FROM EACH OTHER AND THIS ONE IS DIFFERENT FROM ALL OF THEM, AND THE FIGURE IS SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY-EIGHT, WHICH IS ABOVE EVERY FIGURE THE BOOK HAS EVER HELD IN THIS SERIES, AND THE REASON IT IS ABOVE ALL OF THEM IS NOT THAT THE CHANNEL IS RUNNING BETTER. THE REASON IS THAT A MONTH IS THIRTY DAYS AND A BOOK IS A BOOK.**

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of the particular quiet of a morning where three separate things are due and all three of them are being done by different people at the same time.

And the fen entered the turn itself, because a turn in this holding is not a date. It is a night in which about nine people notice, separately and without speaking, that a thing they have been calling this week is now last week, and that every one of them made the same discovery and none of them said so to another, and that about four of them have a book with a heading on it that now describes a different month than the one they have been writing in, and that the heading is not wrong and the month is not wrong and the two of them simply do not match any more, and that the correct thing to do about that is to go on writing and to be wrong in a small way for about a month.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of nine people not saying the word last month out loud on a morning when all nine of them were thinking it.

And at about the second hour the compost line fell, and the fen entered it whole.

**PAID, ONE HUNDRED POUNDS, AND THE RUN IS SEVENTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN SEVENTEEN. THE WORD BEFORE THE FIGURE IS PAID AND IT IS THE SAME WORD THAT WAS THERE ON THE FIRST OF LAST MONTH, WHEN THE RUN WAS SIXTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN SIXTEEN, AND THE FIGURE HAS NOT MOVED AND THE WORD HAS NOT MOVED AND THE RUN HAS MOVED BY ONE, AND THE MOVE BY ONE IS THIRTY DAYS AND NOT ONE DAY, AND THE LINE FALLS ON THE FIRST OF A MONTH AND IT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND AND IT IS NOT GOING TO BE USED AS ONE. THE WORD PAID HAS STOOD OVER THAT LINE FOR EVERY MONTH OF THAT RUN AND NOT ONE PERSON HAS STOOD THE TWO AGAINST EACH OTHER AND SAID, IN WORDS, HOW MANY MONTHS THAT IS.**

The silence was made of a man reading a figure he had read about seventeen times and finding that a run of months is a number about time and that this holding has not put a figure on time in four years, and that the man of about fifty wrote the word and the run and the day on the same line and did not write the run out in days, and that not writing it out in days is the whole of what this holding knows how to do with time.

What the silence was made of was a ledger being ruled and a line being drawn under a figure that is the same figure as the one above it, and that a man who draws a line under the same figure twice in a row has drawn the line, not the figure.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and forty-five days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and ninety-two**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on three mornings in about a week, and that the reason this morning is that a month has turned in the night and a man who has just lost a month out of a pair of figures wants to be certain the pair did not shrink.

And then at about the third hour of the afternoon the two of them went up the nine hundred yards again, and the seventh column was read, and the fen entered the whole of it, and improved none of it.

**THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STOOD AT FORTY-SEVEN AND IS FORTY-EIGHT, AND IT WAS READ AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE FIRST DAY OF A MONTH, AND FROM YESTERDAY IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FORTY-FIVE AND FROM TODAY IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FORTY-SEVEN. THE FIGURE MOVED BECAUSE SOMEBODY READ A DOOR. THIS MORNING AND THE THIRTIETH OF LAST MONTH ARE TWO READINGS IN TWO DAYS, AND THE PAIR THAT CAME BEFORE THEM, FOUR YEARS AGO, WAS THE THIRTIETH OF THE FOURTH MONTH AND THE FIRST OF THE FIFTH, AND THIS IS THE SECOND SUCH PAIR IN FOUR YEARS, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT AND HAS NOT ENTERED WHETHER IT IS A LARGER FACT THAN THE OTHER ONE.**

The quiet in that room was made of a man writing a two on a door and not writing anything beside it, and that a two on a door is the least interesting figure in this holding's month and is the only one that has been written twice in two days in four years.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon the man of about seventy was fetched, and the fen entered the whole of it, and improved none of it, and the count of fetchings is **twenty** and it is twenty, and it went off nineteen on to twenty this afternoon and no person in this holding has said why and the fen is not going to say why in a book.

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about eleven minutes of a man of about seventy coming up a path at the speed of a man of about seventy, and that he stopped at the gate and waited about four minutes for somebody to notice him, and that the person who noticed him was the person who keeps the seed house, and that she was not thanked for it and did not expect to be.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man being asked nothing and giving nothing, and that he has been fetched twenty times and has not once asked which number it was, and that on this afternoon in particular he was not asked, and that the reader of this body was in the room, and that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked the man of about seventy in twenty fetchings is nil and is nil, and that the fen has entered both of those and is not going to explain the second of them, and that a thing nobody can explain and nobody has explained in twenty fetchings is not a mystery, it is a habit, and this holding has one habit and it is the one it has had since the eleventh of the first month.

And then the man of about fifty said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-six, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Three things came round on one morning and I am going to say the three of them in one breath and then I am going to stop being the man who says things, and the reason is that when three clocks fall together in one morning the thing that gets ruined is not the clocks, it is the person keeping them, and I have kept four books for four years and I am not going to be the reason a page in one of them has three things in it and a shape."**

**"So. The compost line is paid and the run is seventeen. The seventh column is forty-eight and has been read twice in two days for the second time in four years. And the man of about seventy has been fetched twenty times. Three of them, one morning, one Saturday, and I have written all three down in three places and none of the three is in the same book, and that is not tidiness, that is the only way a man can hold three things that are all due at once."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man putting three books down on a table in order and then, after a moment, putting the middle one back where it was, and the fen has entered that and improved neither of the two movements.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and ten, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"The window of the office's work opens on the fifth day from now and shuts on the eleventh, and no work is carried out of a window, and I am saying both halves on the same morning because a month turned overnight and a man who says one half of a thing in one month and the other half in the next has two facts where he had one."**

**"And nothing in this holding has asked that office for a piece of paper and I have not asked and I am not going to ask before it opens, and the reason is the eleventh line of a memorandum dated the twentieth of last month, and the eleventh line is the only line on that page about anything in this room, and it is nine lines from the top and not the first, and a document that puts a sentence about a part in the eleventh line has thought about who is going to read it and where they will be when they do."**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a room that has said the words office and window and eleventh line more in the last four days than in the four months before them, and that the fen has entered that and is not going to say it means anything, because a word used more often is not a word used more meaningfully.

And then the two last things of the first of a month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither, and a first of a month is not a better day for that.

**THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN WAITED ONCE MORE FOR THE FOURTH LINE THIS MONTH AND NOT THIS MORNING, AND THE COUNT OF FIELDS THAT HAVE WAITED IS TWENTY-FIVE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-FOUR MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-FIVE NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE AND TWENTY-FIVE ARE FIFTY-FOUR, AND THE NUMBER DID NOT MOVE THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING, AND A HOLDER WHO ADDS TO A COUNT ON A MORNING THE THING DID NOT HAPPEN ON IS A HOLDER WHO IS STARTING TO PREDICT.**

**AND THREE CLOCKS CAME ROUND ON ONE SATURDAY AND NONE OF THE THREE RESET AND NONE OF THE THREE WAS EXPLAINED AND THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY CAME UP A PATH AND WAS ASKED NOTHING AND THE COUNT OF TIMES HE HAS BEEN FETCHED IS TWENTY, AND THE WINDOW OPENS ON THE SIXTH, WHICH IS FIVE DAYS OFF, AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY ON THE FIRST DAY OF A MONTH THAT ALSO RULED THAT A SERIES WHICH HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF IN SIXTY MORNINGS IS NOT GOING TO START AGAIN BECAUSE THE MONTH CHANGED.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the first of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a first of a month, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room when the man of about seventy was fetched: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-FIRST FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN TWENTY-EIGHT DAYS SINCE THE THIRD OF LAST MONTH THE SERIES HAS RISEN NINETEEN TIMES AND FALLEN NINE TIMES. THE COUNT OF THE SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND IT DID NOT RESET AT THE TURN OF THE MONTH. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN FIFTY-NINE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE FIFTY-EIGHTH MORNING OUT OF SIXTY. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIVE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-TWO DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE COMPOST LINE FELL AT THE SECOND HOUR AT ONE HUNDRED POUNDS, THE WORD BEFORE THE FIGURE BEING PAID, AND THE RUN IS SEVENTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN SEVENTEEN, AND IT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND. THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STOOD AT FORTY-SEVEN AND IS FORTY-EIGHT, AND IT WAS READ AT THE THIRD HOUR, AND FROM YESTERDAY IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FORTY-FIVE AND FROM TODAY IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FORTY-SEVEN, AND THIS MORNING AND THE THIRTIETH OF LAST MONTH ARE THE SECOND PAIR OF CONSECUTIVE READING DAYS IN FOUR YEARS. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS FETCHED AT THE FOURTH HOUR AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY, AND HE ASKED NOBODY WHICH NUMBER IT WAS, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HIM IN TWENTY FETCHINGS IS NIL, AND NO PERSON IN THIS HOLDING HAS SAID WHY A MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS FETCHED ON THE FIRST OF A MONTH, AND THE FEN IS NOT GOING TO. THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN WAITED TWENTY-FIVE TIMES AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-FOUR MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-FIVE NOT, AND THE NUMBER DID NOT MOVE THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENS ON THE SIXTH, BEING FIVE DAYS OFF, AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
