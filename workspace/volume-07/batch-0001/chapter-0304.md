# Chapter 304

## The Sixth Opens And The Eleventh Shuts

The fifth of this month was a Wednesday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the fifth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and fifty-four hundredweight.**

That is the sixty-fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in thirty-two days since the third of this month the series has risen twenty-one times and fallen eleven times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in sixty-three days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixty-second morning out of sixty-four.

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of a man in about four people saying the two halves of a sentence about a window out loud, in order, on the same morning, for the first time in about four years that anybody in this holding has said both halves together.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and forty-nine days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and ninety-six**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on five mornings in about a week and that this is the last morning of them in this week and that a habit is a habit and not a practice and that the difference shows up on the morning it stops.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the middle table had four things on it and a fifth thing beside them, and the fen entered the four and the one and did not put them in one pile, and nobody in that room improved the count.

The silence was made of a table with a pile on one side of it and a cloth on the other, and that about four people in that room had spent a month learning not to call the two sides of that table one thing.

And the fen entered what is under the first cloth, because a room in which four things are being counted has to be able to say what it is counting. There is a pail of water against it and a wet cloth over the top of the cloth, and the person who keeps the seed house put both there this morning before anybody came in, and nobody asked her to, and she was not thanked, and rootwood that is living goes off in about three days if it has nothing wet against it, and it has been on that table for ten days and it is warm.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of nine people not looking at a corner of a table, and that the not looking is not squeamishness and is not politeness and is the fourth discipline this holding has learned this month, after the standing and the column and the rule on a door, and that nobody has written any of the four down anywhere except on this page.

And then the man of about fifty read the four out, and gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"A thing under a cloth with a pail of water against it, which is living rootwood about the size of a man's fist and came out of a cradle at Low Sike on the nineteenth of last month, and the count of people in this holding who have looked at it is two. A tinned plate about four inches by two with a corner turned up and six lines on it in a hand that is not named, and no office on it and no date on it. A memorandum of protection dated the twentieth of last month over a name four hundred miles off, and its eleventh line. And a form with four lines and a fifth ruled line and a question on the fifth and no answer under it."**

**"Four objects. And the fifth thing on that table is under a separate cloth and it is a key, and I am going to say the whole of that out loud because a man with a book in this holding has twice this month had to say it, and I am not going to be a man who says a thing twice in a season. A key is a key. It is not a sequence and it is not a cradle and it does not carry a line of anything, and the eleventh line of that memorandum does not govern it, and I have watched three people in about a month want it to govern it because it would be tidier if it did."**

What the silence was made of was a man finishing an inventory and the room going on being a room instead of a hearing, and that a table with four objects on it and one beside it is not a stack of five and never was, and that anybody who wants it to be a stack of five has found a thing they want to be true.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifteen, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a sheet of paper in front of me and it is a request for a written instrument of the office named at the head of the memorandum. I wrote it out this morning in my own hand and it is four lines long and it is correct and it goes on the ordinary post. I am not sending it, and I am going to say why, and the reason is that a request is the only way a person gets one of those without anybody else's name on it."**

**"That document has two things in it. One of them is that a person appointed to be present on a line is not a steward, and the other one is the eleventh line, which is that a thing may not be set against a valve without a written instrument. Those are about two different people and they are eleven lines apart on the same page. The moment I send a request, both of them are about me. And in about four years somebody is going to read that page and see one man on it, and I would rather there were none, and none is available to me, so I am going to sit here with a sheet of paper and not send it."**

The quiet in that room was made of nine people who had been waiting four days for a man to send something, and that he had said out loud in about four seconds on the Thursday of last month that he was not going to ask before the window, and that nobody in the room had put those two things together until this afternoon, and that the fen has entered that and improved neither half of it.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirteen, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is a key on that table and a piece of paper that has not gone anywhere, and I have not been asked about either, and I am going to say one thing and then I am going to go and start on my own ground, and the reason is that a man has just said out loud in nine people that he is not going to do a thing with it, and a woman who has been given that does not owe the room a question."**

**"The twenty-ninth of this month, or thereabouts. I have said that twice in a room and once in a yard and it is the same sentence three times. My three animals get the carted part and the rest of it stays where it grew and nobody takes it and I am putting no row on it this season, and a piece of paper in a book in a seed house has not changed it, and the man who wrote the paper has said so himself in about four seconds this afternoon, and a woman does not have to check a thing a man has just said in a room."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a woman standing up in a room where nobody had stood up in about a month, and that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and did not move on the afternoon she said a date in a room for the fourth time, and that a nil that holds on four dates is a thing this holding did not do deliberately and is not going to be thanked for.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-one, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to write that it was written out and not sent, and I am going to put the date on it and the day of the week, and I am going to keep the sheet in the book that does not go on the post, and the reason I am keeping it and not burning it is that a man in about four years is going to want to know whether anybody in this holding ever sat down and wrote the request, and the honest answer is that somebody did and somebody chose not to send it, and both of those are facts and the second one is not a failure of the first."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a book being shut on a piece of paper, and that the fen has entered that and is not going to make anything of it, and that nobody thanked anybody.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and sixteen, and nobody improved it.

**"The window opens tomorrow, the sixth, and shuts on the eleventh, and no work is carried out of a window, and no window has ever been extended in a season, and both of those halves are on this page in that order and neither one is mine. Five days. And I want it entered that what this holding wants out of those five days is not a figure and is not an answer and is not a key. It is to be able to say one number to somebody who will write it down somewhere we cannot get to, and to have it go."**

**"And it is not going to, and that is not a defeat and it is not a refusal, because nobody has been asked. There is a sheet of paper in a book in a seed house with a request on it and it is four lines long and it is correct, and the whole of what stands between it and a man four hundred miles off is a road, and a road is not a thing anybody in this holding has ever been able to hurry."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of nine people in a room on a Wednesday in the first week of a month looking at a window that opens in the morning, and that the length of it was about a minute and that about four of them had the days already written down on their own account, and that the fen has entered that and is not going to improve it.

And then the two last things of the fifth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither, and the sixth is tomorrow and there is nothing in either of them about tomorrow.

**THE WINDOW OPENS ON THE SIXTH, WHICH IS TOMORROW, AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW, AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON, AND BOTH HALVES ARE ON THIS PAGE. A REQUEST FOR A WRITTEN INSTRUMENT OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF THAT MEMORANDUM WAS WRITTEN OUT IN ONE HAND ON THIS MORNING IN FOUR LINES AND WAS NOT SENT, AND THE REASON WAS GIVEN FIRST AND WAS NOT IMPROVED ON, AND THE SHEET IS IN A BOOK THAT DOES NOT GO ON THE POST AND WAS NOT TORN UP. NO WRITTEN INSTRUMENT WAS ISSUED, RECEIVED, INVENTED OR WORKED AROUND IN TEN DAYS.**

**AND THE MIDDLE TABLE CARRIES FOUR OBJECTS AND A FIFTH BESIDE THEM, AND A KEY IS A KEY AND IS NEITHER A SEQUENCE NOR A CRADLE, AND THE ELEVENTH LINE GOVERNS THE SEED AND THE PLATE AND NOT THE KEY, AND ANYBODY WHO TREATS THE THREE OF THEM AS ONE THING HAS CONVERTED THE ONE DISTINCTION THIS HOLDING HAS GOT INTO A PIECE OF FURNITURE. THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FOUR AND IT IS THE SIXTY-FIFTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND TEN DAYS END ON IT, AND THE SIXTH DAY OF THE MONTH IS TOMORROW, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS ASKED FOR ANYTHING AND GOT IT, AND NOBODY WAS THANKED.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the fifth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a window, and not by the reader of this body, who wrote out a request in his own hand and did not send it: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-FIFTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN THIRTY-TWO DAYS SINCE THE THIRD THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-ONE TIMES AND FALLEN ELEVEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SIXTY-THREE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE SIXTY-SECOND MORNING OUT OF SIXTY-FOUR. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY-NINE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-SIX DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE MIDDLE TABLE CARRIES FOUR OBJECTS AND A FIFTH BESIDE THEM, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY READ THE FOUR OUT AND SAID THAT THE FIFTH IS A KEY AND IS NEITHER A SEQUENCE NOR A CRADLE AND IS NOT GOVERNED BY THE ELEVENTH LINE, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE READER OF THIS BODY WROTE OUT A REQUEST FOR A WRITTEN INSTRUMENT OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF THE MEMORANDUM, IN FOUR LINES, IN HIS OWN HAND, AND DID NOT SEND IT, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND THE SHEET IS IN A BOOK THAT DOES NOT GO ON THE POST AND WAS NOT TORN UP, AND NO WRITTEN INSTRUMENT WAS ISSUED, RECEIVED, INVENTED, HINTED AT OR WORKED AROUND IN TEN DAYS. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENS ON THE SIXTH, BEING TOMORROW, AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW, AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND THE REASON WAS NOT GIVEN A SECOND TIME IN A SEASON. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO. THE COUNT OF NOT KNOWNS IS SIX AND THE UNIT OF NONE OF THEM HAS BEEN SUPPLIED. THE COUNT OF ENTRIES THAT CARRY A DAY AND NOT A FIGURE IS FOUR. THE REQUESTS AGAINST NOTHING AND THE NOTES UNDER SECTION NINE STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND FIFTY-THREE. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY HAS BEEN FETCHED TWENTY TIMES. THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN HAVE WAITED TWENTY-SIX TIMES. THE HEADING ON A SHEET IN A BOOK FROM THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH HAS NOTHING UNDER IT. THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
