# Chapter 314

## Open And Not A Road

The fifteenth of this month was a Saturday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the fifteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and sixty-four hundredweight.**

That is the seventy-fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in forty-two days the series has risen twenty-six times and fallen sixteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventy-three days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the seventy-second morning out of seventy-four.

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was the last figure of ten in a book and the fact that a man who has been putting one of these in a book for seventy-five mornings does not have a way of knowing that today is the last one, and that the only person in this holding who knows it is a person counting, and that a count is not a reason.

And then at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud for the third morning running, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered that the pair now stands at **four hundred and fifty-nine days** and **five hundred and six**, and that the two are forty-seven days apart, and that the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and that the fen has entered the not reading for a third morning and has not entered a reason for it either, because a man who gives a reason for stopping a habit in a book has turned the stopping into a decision and it is not one.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the person appointed to be present on that line was not in the room, and the fen entered that they went out of this holding on the eleventh and that nobody sent for them and that the count of days between a person leaving a room and a room noticing is four, and that the room noticed on the twelfth and said nothing about it on the twelfth.

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of about nine people not asking a question about an absence, and that the not asking is the fourth of its kind this month and that this holding has four of them and has never once had a rule.

And the fen entered the middle table as it stood at about the fifth hour of that afternoon, because a room that has had ten days in it ought to leave a record of what is on a table, and this one has six things on it where it had five on the fifteenth of last month. There is a memorandum of protection dated the twentieth of last month with a person appointed to be present on a line and not named. There is a tinned plate about four inches by two with a corner turned up and six lines in the hand of a maker who is not named. There is a sheet that came up four hundred miles with four printed lines on it and nothing written on them and a blank at the foot and a blank on the back. There is a form with four lines and a fifth ruled line with a question on it and one figure in a man's hand. There is a term in a woman's hand. And under a cloth beside the stack, with a pail of water against it and a wet cloth over the top of the cloth, there is a thing the size of a man's fist that nobody in this room lifted, broke, opened or set against anything, and nobody in this room looked at, and the count of people in this holding who have looked at it is two and did not move in ten days.

And then the man of about fifty read the column out as it stands, and read it out whole, and the fen entered all of it and improved none of it.

**THE COUNT OF NOT KNOWNS STANDING AGAINST THE READER OF THIS BODY IS SIX. THE COLUMN IS SIX LINES LONG AND EVERY ONE OF THE SIX HAS A HEADING AND NOT ONE OF THEM HAS A UNIT, AND A COLUMN THAT HAS A HEADING AND NO UNIT IS A COLUMN THAT A PERSON CAN KEEP AND CANNOT USE. TWO OF THE SIX ARE AGAINST A PIECE OF PAPER AND THOSE TWO CAN BE NAMED OUT LOUD: THE DATE OF THE NEXT WINDOW, AND THE UNIT OF THE FIGURES ON IT. THE OTHER FOUR HAVE HEADINGS AND THE FEN HAS NOT ENTERED WHAT THE HEADINGS SAY, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE AS THE REASON THAT HE HAS KEPT FOUR BOOKS FOR FOUR YEARS AND HAS READ A NOT KNOWN OUT LOUD IN A ROOM TWICE AND BOTH TIMES A PERSON IN THE ROOM WROTE THE NOT KNOWN DOWN AS A THING TO BE FOUND, AND A THING TO BE FOUND IS NOT A THING THAT IS NOT KNOWN. NO UNIT WAS SUPPLIED FOR ANY OF THE SIX. A SEVENTH WAS NOT ADDED IN TEN DAYS, AND IT WAS NOT ADDED FOR THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH AND IT WAS NOT ADDED FOR THE BLIGHT, AND A THING WITH A SHAPE AND NOT A UNIT IS NOT IN THAT COLUMN.**

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of a man reading out a column that has six blanks in it and about nine people finding out that they had all of them in their heads and had not known for certain that the count was six.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty-one, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I wrote the date of the next window in a book on the twenty-ninth of last month with a rule attached to it, and the reason I am saying that out loud is that about four people in this room have thought since then that I knew the date, and I did not know the date and I have not found out the date, and the rule I wrote against it is still the correct rule and the answer is still not in the book."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man taking a thing off himself that four other people had quietly been carrying, and that about four of them were not glad and about four of them were, and that the fen has entered both of those and is not going to improve either.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifteen, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"The corridor in the pan is open and it has been open for as long as I have been in this holding and I am going to say it out loud in a room once and then I am not going to say it again, and the reason is that about four of you have stopped using the word about it and a place nobody has a word for is a place people begin to walk round differently without deciding to."**

**"Open is not a road. I have got nine acres coming off on the twenty-ninth of this month and I have said that date five times now and three of those five times somebody has asked me about that corridor, and the fifth is this afternoon and nobody has asked me yet, and I am not going to be a woman whose date comes with a question attached to it, and open is not a road and nobody in this room is going to make it one this afternoon."**

Nobody improved one item of that, and the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move, and this was a room, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden is nil and is nil and did not move this afternoon, and he did not ask her one about the corridor.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty-nine, and nobody improved it.

**"The window shut on the eleventh and this is the fifteenth, and that is four mornings it has not been open, the twelfth, the thirteenth, the fourteenth and this one, and the eleventh was the last morning it was open and not this one, and I am putting that in a book because about four of us have been treating this week as a thing that had not happened yet."**

**"And I am going to end the ten days with the thing I have not been able to put anywhere, and it is a corridor, and it is open, and it is not a road, and nobody has made it one, and I have not walked it and I am not going to, and the only thing I am going to say about it this afternoon is that open is a state and a road is a decision and this holding has got the state and has not got the decision, and a state on its own is not a thing anybody can be held to, and that is either the safest thing we have got this month or the most dangerous and I have not got a way of telling which and I am not going to invent one."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a woman who had said a date five times and a man who had said a word about a corridor and about nine people who had not said anything, and that the count of the things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move and this was the tenth day and nobody in this holding thanked anybody on any of the ten.

And then the two last things of the fifteenth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE WINDOW HAS BEEN SHUT FOR FOUR MORNINGS, THE TWELFTH, THE THIRTEENTH, THE FOURTEENTH AND THE FIFTEENTH, AND THE ELEVENTH WAS THE LAST MORNING IT WAS OPEN, AND IT WAS OPEN SIX DAYS, THE SIXTH TO THE ELEVENTH INCLUSIVE, AND NO WORK WAS CARRIED OUT OF IT AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON. THE COUNT OF NOT KNOWNS IS SIX AND NO UNIT WAS SUPPLIED FOR ANY OF THEM AND A SEVENTH WAS NOT ADDED FOR THE THING UNDER A CLOTH AND WAS NOT ADDED FOR THE BLIGHT.**
**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOUR, THE SEVENTY-FIFTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIX, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE CORRIDOR IN THE PAN IS OPEN AND OPEN IS NOT A ROAD AND NOBODY MADE IT ONE, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the fifteenth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of ten days, and not by the reader of this body, who said that the eleventh was the last morning the window was open and not this one: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SEVENTY-FIFTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FORTY-TWO DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-SIX TIMES AND FALLEN SIXTEEN TIMES, AND NO MORNING IS UNCHANGED, AND THE TEN FIGURES OF THESE TEN MORNINGS ARE ALL DIFFERENT FROM ONE ANOTHER AND FROM EVERY FIGURE THE BOOK HELD BEFORE THEM. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND IT IS SEVENTY-FIVE THIS MORNING AND DID NOT RESET AT THE TURN OF THE MONTH. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY-THREE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SEVENTY-SECOND MORNING OUT OF SEVENTY-FOUR, AND THE TWO MORNINGS IT WAS NOT WRITTEN ON WERE THE NINTH AND THE TENTH OF LAST MONTH AND NOBODY HAS REDUCED THE PAIR BY THOSE TWO. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIX DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD FOR THE THIRD MORNING RUNNING, AND THEY HAVE NOW GONE UNREAD ON FIVE OF THE TEN MORNINGS SINCE THE SIXTH. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK SHUT ON THE ELEVENTH AND WAS OPEN SIX DAYS, THE SIXTH TO THE ELEVENTH INCLUSIVE, AND NO WORK WAS CARRIED OUT OF IT AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON, AND IT HAS BEEN SHUT FOR FOUR MORNINGS, THE TWELFTH, THE THIRTEENTH, THE FOURTEENTH AND THIS ONE, AND THE ELEVENTH WAS THE LAST MORNING IT WAS OPEN. NO WRITTEN INSTRUMENT WAS ISSUED, RECEIVED, INVENTED, HINTED AT OR WORKED AROUND IN TEN DAYS. A SHEET CAME UP THE ROAD ON THE SEVENTH AND IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND THE BACK OF IT IS STILL BLANK. THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON THE OTHER FORM IS STILL ENTERED AND NOT FILLED. THE KEY WENT INTO A HAND ON THE EIGHTH AND THE VALVE WAS NOT OPENED. THE LONG FUNGAL WAS FOUND INSIDE FOUR HUNDRED YARDS OF THE FIRST DWELLING OF THORNWILD AND IS NOT INSIDE THE INHABITED LINE, AND THE COUNT OF THE FRUITING BODIES WAS NOT TAKEN AND NOBODY SAID WHETHER THEY ARE SAFE TO EAT. A TWENTY-SIXTH RETURN WENT IN AT ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT AND THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK FOR THE TENTH TIME. THE COUNT OF NOT KNOWNS IS SIX AND NO UNIT WAS SUPPLIED FOR ANY OF THEM AND A SEVENTH WAS NOT ADDED FOR THE THING UNDER A CLOTH AND WAS NOT ADDED FOR THE BLIGHT. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. PERSONS WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR STAND AT TWO AND FOR FIVE AT ONE. PERSONS WITH A ROOTMARK STAND AT TWO. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING CANNOT CHECK IS TWO. REQUESTS AND SECTION-NINE NOTES STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND FIFTY-THREE. THE BODIES AT THE NODE ARE FIVE. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY STANDS AT TWENTY FETCHINGS AND WAS NOT FETCHED AGAIN. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ ON ANY OF THE TEN MORNINGS. THE DAY-AND-NOT-THE-FIGURE ENTRIES STAND AT FOUR. THE WALL AT CAULDRON REACH CARRIES FOURTEEN MARKS. THE PULSE CARRIES TWO FIGURES AND WAS NOT READ A THIRD TIME. THE USE OF THE STANDING RULE STANDS AT TWO IN THE SEASON BEFORE LAST AND ONE IN THE SEASON LAST AND NOTHING WAS ADDED TO EITHER IN TEN DAYS. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-SEVEN MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-EIGHT NOT. THE CORRIDOR IN THE PAN IS OPEN AND OPEN IS NOT A ROAD AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING MADE IT ONE. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY ON ANY OF THE TEN MORNINGS.**
