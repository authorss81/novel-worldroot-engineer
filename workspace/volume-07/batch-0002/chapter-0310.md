# Chapter 310

## The Eleventh Shuts

The eleventh of this month was a Tuesday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the eleventh went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and sixty-one hundredweight.**

That is the seventy-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in thirty-eight days the series has risen twenty-four times and fallen fourteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in sixty-nine days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixty-eighth morning out of seventy.

The room was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was the particular quiet of a morning on which a thing that was going to happen at a fixed hour is going to happen at a fixed hour and everybody in the room has been counting toward it since the sixth.

And then at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered the reason, which was that the second of the two figures is a number about a month and the first of them is a number about a board in a cart shed, and that a man who has been reading two numbers out loud before anything else for nine days is a man who is holding two numbers in front of himself, and that a day on which a window shuts does not need anything held in front of it.

And the fen entered what it is that shuts, because nothing shut. There is no shutter and no door in this holding that has anything to do with it, and the thing that shut at about the seventh hour this morning was a period of six days in the working week of an office four hundred miles off, and the only sign of it in this room was that about nine people stopped saying the word window in the past tense about half an hour later than usual. The schedule on the copy of the ledger of the maintenance of the Crown Engine that is on that table has a column for it, and the column has a date at the top of it and a date at the foot of it, and the eleven rows that are blank in that ledger are blank in that column as well as in the others, and nobody has ever been able to say which of the two blanks a row is blank in.

And the fen entered the six days whole, because a room is entitled to know what it did with six days and this one had not said.

**ON THE SIXTH THE WINDOW OPENED AND A PERSON CAME INTO THIS ROOM OUT OF THE WEATHER WHO HAD NOT BEEN IN IT BEFORE. ON THE SEVENTH A SHEET CAME UP FOUR HUNDRED MILES ON THE ORDINARY POST AND IT WAS CORRECT IN EVERY PARTICULAR AND IT IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT, AND A MAN ADMITTED IN A ROOM OF NINE PEOPLE THAT HE HAD PUT A REQUEST ON THE POST ON THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH WITHOUT TELLING ANYBODY. ON THE EIGHTH THE FOURTH LINE OF THE ROTA CAME ROUND AND WAS EMPTY FOR THE TWENTY-SEVENTH TIME, AND A KEY WENT OUT OF A HAND ON THE MIDDLE TABLE INTO THE HAND OF A WOMAN WHO WAS ASKED NOTHING, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY ASKED HER IS NIL. ON THE NINTH A WOMAN WHO KEEPS A ROUTE AND THE READER OF THIS BODY STOOD ABOUT A HUNDRED YARDS OF OPEN GROUND BETWEEN A SETTLEMENT AND A DEAD WOOD AND CAME BACK WITH NOTHING. ON THE TENTH A BARROW WENT UP FOUR MILES AND A MAN WALKED BEHIND IT AND DID NOT TOUCH IT AND WAS NOT THANKED. AND ON THE ELEVENTH IT SHUT. THAT IS THE SIX DAYS AND THE FEN HAS IMPROVED NO PART OF IT, AND THE COUNT OF FIGURES THAT CAME OUT OF ANY OFFICE IN THOSE SIX DAYS IS NIL.**

The silence in that room was made of nine people who had spent six days doing nothing that could be entered and about four of them finding out at about the fifth hour of the afternoon that the nothing had cost somebody four hundred miles of road and about four days each way, and that the somebody had not complained once, and that this holding is not going to thank him for it either.

And then the man of about fifty read out what came back down the road, and read it out whole, and the fen entered all of it and improved no part of it.

**THE SHEET CAME UP THE ROAD ON THE SEVENTH OF THIS MONTH AND IT IS ONE PAGE AND IT IS CORRECT IN EVERY PARTICULAR, AND THE FOUR LINES UNDER ITS HEADING ARE PRINTED AND NONE OF THEM IS WRITTEN ON, AND THE SPACE AT THE FOOT FOR THE NAME OF A PERSON IS BLANK AND THE LINE UNDER IT IS BLANK, AND THE BACK OF THE PAGE IS BLANK AND HAS BEEN BLANK FOR FOUR DAYS. THE SHEET IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE FACE UP. THE FORM WITH FOUR LINES AND A FIFTH RULED LINE WITH A QUESTION ON IT IS ON THE SAME TABLE AND ITS LINE ONE CARRIES ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING AND THAT IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND NINTH-DAY RETURN AND NOT OF ANY OTHER, AND ITS FIFTH LINE IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED AND NOBODY WROTE ON IT. THE FIRST OF THE TWO PUBLIC TERMS IS ON THE SAME TABLE IN TOVA REED'S HAND AND WAS WRITTEN ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF LAST MONTH. THE SECOND OF THEM IS IN SOREN RILL'S COAT AND HE PUT IT THERE HIMSELF ON THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH. NEITHER OF THE TWO PIECES OF PAPER WAS TOUCHED TODAY BY ANYBODY.**

What the silence was made of was about four people in a room looking at three pieces of paper on one table and a fourth piece of paper in a coat on a man standing by the door, and the fen has entered that all four of them were read out in about nine people on one afternoon and that not one of them was improved on by anybody who was not the man who wrote it.

And the fen entered the order of the table, because a table is a thing and this one has had five things on it and a sixth this week and the order of the six has not been improved by anybody in five months. The memorandum is at the bottom because it came first. The form with four lines is on the memorandum because it came second. The tinned plate came off a cradle on the nineteenth of last month and went on top of the form. The living seed came out of the same cradle and went under a cloth beside the stack rather than on it, with a pail of water against it and a wet cloth over the top of the cloth, and nobody has moved it. The key went out of this holding on Saturday and the middle of the table is about four inches narrower on the right than it was on Friday, and the sheet that came up the road on the seventh is face up in the space the key was in, and the fen has entered that and is not going to say what it means.

And then the person appointed to be present on that line said one thing, and the reason was given first, and no figure of any kind went into any column, and they were not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"The window shut this morning and I am going out of here this afternoon, and I am going to say the reason for that out loud because about four of you have worked out by now that nobody sent me and nobody will recall me, and the reason is that the line I was appointed to be present on has a person on it and that person is in this room on the days the person is in this room, and on the days the person is not in this room the line is watched by the people who live on it, and that is what it has been doing all six days, and it is not nothing."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about nine people being told by a person they could not name that a line is watched, and about four of them wanting to ask by what, and the count of questions the reader of this body asked that person over six days is nil and is nil.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty-four, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"Six days, and the first of the six was the sixth, and I am going to say that count out loud in a room because about four of us have been saying five this week, and the reason is that a day a thing is shut is not a day the thing was not open, and the difference between five and six is a day on which somebody four hundred miles off had a window and a person on this line was present in it."**

**"And I want to say the harder half of it, and I am going to say it in this room rather than leave it in a book, and the harder half is that I have wanted one thing out of those six days and I did not ask for it, and the thing I wanted was a piece of paper about four hundred miles off, and I did not ask for it, and there is no version of this week in which I was right to hold out on that, and I want the not asking to be in the record as a decision about the window and not as a matter of a man's character. A matter of a man's character is a thing four years from now that people are kind about. A decision about the shape of six days is a thing four years from now that people can argue with."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man correcting an arithmetic that four other people in a room had also got wrong and had not said, and that the fen has entered the four and is not going to name them because naming them would be a second thing done to them.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-seven, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to write the six days in my book as six days and not as a week, and I am going to write under them the word none, and I am going to rule four lines under the word none, and the reason is that the first of this month I wrote the word none under a column that wanted a figure and left four lines, and a man in this room told me on that afternoon that a man who leaves the lines in finds them in about four years, and I have thought about that sentence every day since and it is still the correct one."**

And then the two last things of the eleventh of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK SHUT THIS MORNING, BEING THE SIXTH OF SIX DAYS, THE SIXTH, THE SEVENTH, THE EIGHTH, THE NINTH, THE TENTH AND THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK WAS CARRIED OUT OF IT IN ANY OF THEM, AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON, AND A PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THAT LINE WAS IN THE ROOM ON THE MORNING IT SHUT AND IS NOT NAMED. THE SHEET THAT CAME DOWN THE ROAD IS ON A TABLE AND THE BACK OF IT IS STILL BLANK, AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS STILL ENTERED AND NOT FILLED, AND NOBODY WROTE ON IT.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-ONE, THE SEVENTY-FIRST IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE KEY IS IN A HAND AND NOT IN A LOCK, AND THE VALVE WAS NOT OPENED, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the eleventh of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a window shutting, and not by the reader of this body, who said six out loud in a room where about four people had said five: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SEVENTY-FIRST FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN THIRTY-EIGHT DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-FOUR TIMES AND FALLEN FOURTEEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SIXTY-NINE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SIXTY-EIGHTH MORNING OUT OF SEVENTY. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FIVE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWO DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENED ON THE SIXTH AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR AND SHUT THIS MORNING, BEING SIX DAYS, THE SIXTH, THE SEVENTH, THE EIGHTH, THE NINTH, THE TENTH AND THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK WAS CARRIED OUT OF IT IN ANY OF THE SIX, AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON, AND BOTH HALVES ARE ON THIS PAGE. THE SHEET THAT CAME UP THE ROAD ON THE SEVENTH IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE FACE UP AND ITS FOUR PRINTED LINES ARE UNWRITTEN AND ITS FOOT IS BLANK AND THE BACK OF IT IS BLANK AND HAS BEEN FOR FOUR DAYS. THE FORM WITH FOUR LINES AND A FIFTH RULED LINE IS ON THE SAME TABLE, LINE ONE BEING ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING AND BEING THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND NOT OF ANY OTHER, AND THE FIFTH LINE BEING ENTERED AND NOT FILLED. THE FIRST OF THE TWO PUBLIC TERMS IS ON THAT TABLE IN TOVA REED'S HAND AND THE SECOND IS IN SOREN RILL'S COAT AND NEITHER WAS TOUCHED. A PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THAT LINE FROM THE TWENTIETH OF LAST MONTH WAS IN THE ROOM AND IS NOT NAMED AND SAID OUT LOUD THAT THE LINE IS WATCHED BY THE PEOPLE WHO LIVE ON IT, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY ASKED THAT PERSON OVER SIX DAYS IS NIL. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND THE REASON WAS NOT GIVEN A SECOND TIME IN A SEASON. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-SIX MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-SEVEN NOT. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE WINDOW SHUT THIS MORNING AND IS NOT OPEN AGAIN THIS SEASON. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
