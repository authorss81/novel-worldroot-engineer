# Chapter 306

## A Form Came Down The Road

The seventh of this month was a Friday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the seventh went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour, and the man of about fifty read it back out of a different book in the same words, and the two are one number written twice.

**Six hundred and fifty-seven hundredweight.**

That is the sixty-seventh figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in thirty-four days the series has risen twenty-two times and fallen twelve times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in sixty-five days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixty-fourth morning out of sixty-six.

The hour went on without a word in it, and the fen entered the length of it and what was in it, and it was made of a figure that had come down off a number and a man who reads figures off numbers all morning waiting to find out whether it was going back up before the end of the week.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and fifty-one days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and ninety-eight**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on two mornings in this month, and that the reason this morning is that a form came up the road on the ordinary post at about the fifth hour and a man who has been handed a page from an office four hundred miles off wants to have said two numbers that are about his own holding before he looks at it.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and there was a person in the room who had been in it on the day before and had come in out of the weather again, and the middle table had four things on it and a fifth thing beside them and a sixth thing face up on the wood, and the fen entered all three arrangements and entered that nobody moved anything.

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of about nine people looking at a sheet of paper face up on a table and about four of them not looking at it.

And then the man of about fifty read the form out as it stands, and read it out whole, and the fen entered all of it and improved none of it.

**THE SHEET CAME UP THE ROAD ON THE ORDINARY POST OF THIS MORNING AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR. IT IS ONE PAGE. THE DEVICE AT THE HEAD OF IT IS THE DEVICE OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF THE MEMORANDUM, WHICH IS THE ONLY THING ON IT THAT ANYBODY IN THIS ROOM CAN MATCH AGAINST SOMETHING ELSE THEY HAVE SEEN. THE HEADING NAMES A LINE AND NAMES A DISTRICT AND NAMES NO HOLDING AND NAMES NO PERSON, AND A LINE IS NOT A HOLDING AND A DISTRICT IS NOT A PERSON. UNDER THE HEADING ARE FOUR LINES, AND ALL FOUR OF THEM ARE PRINTED, AND NONE OF THEM IS WRITTEN ON, AND THEY ARE THE FOUR THINGS AN OFFICE HAS TO HAVE IN FRONT OF IT BEFORE IT WILL PUT ITS NAME AT THE HEAD OF A THING, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY READ ALL FOUR OUT AND READ THEM AGAIN AND SAID THEY WERE THE RIGHT FOUR, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY READ THEM A THIRD TIME AND SAID NOTHING ABOUT THEM. AT THE FOOT OF THE PAGE THERE IS A SPACE FOR THE NAME OF A PERSON AND A LINE UNDER IT, AND NOBODY WROTE IN EITHER, AND THE BACK OF THE PAGE IS BLANK.**

The quiet in that room was made of a man who had turned a piece of paper over and found nothing on the back of it and had not known what to do with that.

And the fen entered what the page is, because a page is a thing and it had been in about nine hands in about four hours before it got here. It is a good sheet and it is the same weight as the memorandum and it came off the same press, and the four printed lines under its heading are set in the same face as the four lines of the other form that has been on that table for a month, and the foot of it is ruled in the same way, and the difference between the two sheets is that one has had a man's handwriting on its first line for a month and this one has had nothing on any line at all.

And the fen entered that about four people in that room looked at the ruled foot of the new sheet and then at the fifth line of the old form, and that the two pieces of paper were about eleven inches apart on the same table, and that neither moved.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-three, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I sent it. I put four lines of paper on the ordinary post on the third of this morning and I did not tell this room, and I am going to say the reason before anybody works out that it is the same four lines the engineer of record wrote out on Wednesday and did not send, and the reason is that he wrote his in his own hand and mine was in mine, and a thing in your own hand that is not sent is a thing you have thought about, and a thing in your own hand that is sent is a thing you have done, and I have known the difference since about half past four hours ago and I would rather have done it."**

**"The engineer of record wrote a request on Wednesday and sat with it and told a room of nine people he was not going to send it, and the reason he gave was that a request is the only way a person gets one of those without anybody else's name on it. That is a true reason. It is also the reason a man gives when he wants the not sending to be a decision. I have kept four books for four years and I know the difference between a decision and a thing I have not got round to, and I am not going to sit here and be the second of those in a room."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man having been right out loud about another man in a room of nine, and that nobody defended the man he was talking about and nobody asked him for a better reason, and that the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong did not move because this was a room and he had not said it in a yard and had not said it in the first person either.

And then the appointed person said the second thing of the six days, and they gave the reason first, and no figure of any kind went into any column, and they were not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have been in this room twice and neither time has anybody asked me what I am for, and I am going to say the whole of what I have been asked, and the reason is that it is a short list and I would like somebody in this room to have it. What I have been asked is whether the work at the end of the line is being kept up. That is all of it, twice. What I have not been asked is whether anything under a cloth on that table is going to be opened, and I have been in the room longer than the asking has been going on."**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of four people in a room finding out at the same moment that somebody in the room had been keeping a list of the questions and about four others finding out that the list was shorter than they had assumed.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and nineteen, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"A form is not an instrument. I am going to say that in this room and I am going to say it once, and the reason is that about four of us have been treating that page all afternoon as though it were a thing that had gone wrong on a road, and it has not gone wrong on a road. It went up the road. It came back down the road. It is one page and it is correct in every particular and every particular on it is a particular about what an office has to have before it will do a thing, and none of the four of those is about this holding."**

**"And I am not going to hold the man of about fifty against it. He has told the truth about me in a room of nine people this afternoon and he did it with the reason first and he did not ask me whether I wanted it done, and that is three things that are rare and I have got no better sentence for it than that one."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man refusing to be the injured party in a room, and that a person who has spent a month refusing to be handed a figure is not in a different place when somebody hands one to somebody else, and that the fen has entered both of those and is not going to improve either.

And then the reader of this body said the other thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I want to say what those four lines are, because about four of us have been reading them as a condition and they are not a condition, and the reason is that a condition is written by the person who has to satisfy it. Those four are written by the office that has to be satisfied, and they are the four things that office has to have in front of it, and a thing an office requires before it will act is not a thing it is doing to anybody. It is the shape of the work. Every office has one and none of them prints that they have one."**

**"So there is nothing in those four lines for this holding to refuse, and that is not a kindness, and I want it said that it is not a kindness, because a thing with nothing in it to refuse is the most dangerous kind of page to have lying face up on a table for a month, and about nine people have now spent an afternoon not filling one in, and not filling one in is the whole of what a person can do with it and it is going to look like cooperation in about four years."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about nine people doing arithmetic about four years, and that a room which is not refusing anything is in the same position as a room which is, and that the difference between them is entirely in what gets written down.

And then the two last things of the seventh of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**A FORM CAME DOWN THE ROAD AND IT IS CORRECT IN EVERY PARTICULAR AND IT IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT, AND A REQUEST IS NOT A THING OBTAINED, AND NO WRITTEN INSTRUMENT WAS ISSUED OR RECEIVED OR INVENTED OR HINTED AT OR WORKED AROUND TODAY. A MAN PUT A REQUEST ON THE ORDINARY POST ON THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH AND DID NOT TELL THE ROOM AND SAID SO OUT LOUD WITH THE REASON FIRST, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SEVEN, THE SIXTY-SEVENTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SIXTY-FIVE DAYS, AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON THE OTHER FORM IS STILL ENTERED AND NOT FILLED, AND THE NEW PAGE IS ON THE SAME TABLE AND WAS NOT WRITTEN ON, AND THE BACK OF IT IS BLANK, AND THE WINDOW IS OPEN FOR A SECOND DAY AND NOTHING HAS BEEN CARRIED OUT OF IT YET.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the seventh of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a form that came down the road, and not by the reader of this body, who said a form is not an instrument once and did not say it again: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-SEVENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN THIRTY-FOUR DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN TWELVE TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SIXTY-FIVE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SIXTY-FOURTH MORNING OUT OF SIXTY-SIX. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-EIGHT DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. A SHEET CAME UP THE ROAD ON THE ORDINARY POST THIS MORNING AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR. IT IS ONE PAGE, THE DEVICE AT THE HEAD OF IT IS THE DEVICE OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF THE MEMORANDUM, THE HEADING NAMES A LINE AND A DISTRICT AND NO HOLDING AND NO PERSON, AND FOUR LINES UNDER IT ARE PRINTED AND NONE OF THEM IS WRITTEN ON, AND AT THE FOOT THERE IS A SPACE FOR THE NAME OF A PERSON AND A LINE UNDER IT AND NOBODY WROTE IN EITHER, AND THE BACK OF THE PAGE IS BLANK. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY ENTERED THAT HE SENT A REQUEST ON THE ORDINARY POST ON THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH WITHOUT TELLING THE ROOM, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. A FORM IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT AND A REQUEST IS NOT A THING OBTAINED, AND NO WRITTEN INSTRUMENT WAS ISSUED, RECEIVED, INVENTED, HINTED AT OR WORKED AROUND IN TEN DAYS. THE PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THAT LINE WAS IN THE ROOM AND IS NOT NAMED, AND SAID THAT THE ONLY QUESTION THEY HAVE BEEN ASKED TWICE IS WHETHER THE WORK AT THE END OF THE LINE IS BEING KEPT UP. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED THAT PERSON IS NIL. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, THIS HAVING BEEN A ROOM. THE FIGURE ON LINE ONE OF THE FORM ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-FIVE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-SIX NOT. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE WINDOW IS OPEN FOR THE SECOND OF SIX DAYS. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
