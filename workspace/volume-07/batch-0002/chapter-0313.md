# Chapter 313

## He Is Not Reaching For It

The fourteenth of this month was a Friday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the fourteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and sixty-eight hundredweight.**

That is the seventy-fourth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in forty-one days the series has risen twenty-six times and fallen fifteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventy-two days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the seventy-first morning out of seventy-three.

The room was quiet for about a minute and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was a Friday, and that about four people in this holding notice Fridays and that the noticing is not about the day of the week and is about the fact that nothing in a week was ever going to be done about anything and everybody knows that by about the fourth hour of a Friday.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud, and the fen entered that they were not, and entered that they had not been read on three mornings out of the eight that have run since the sixth, and that this is the ninth of them and the second morning running, and that he did not read them and did not give a reason, and the fen has entered that the habit stopped on the morning before and has not entered what stopped it, and that a habit is a habit and is not a practice, and the difference between the two shows up on the morning after it stops, and that about four people in this holding did not notice that anything about the seventh hour of this morning was different.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the reader of this body had a sheet in his hand and was holding it flat on his knee and had been holding it flat on his knee for about four minutes before anybody said anything, and the fen entered all of that and improved none of it.

And then the reader of this body read the standing rule about the engineer of record out, in full, in about nine people, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved no part of it.

**THE RULE IS FOUR LINES. EVERY ONE OF THE FOUR IS ABOUT A DRAW, AND A DRAW IS A RAPID TRANSFER OF EXISTING WATER, HEAT OR ROOTLIGHT THROUGH A LIVING PATH, AND A DRAW MAY SAVE A CROP AND CREATES A DOWNSTREAM DEBT UNLESS A RETURN IS ARRANGED. THE RULE IS ABOUT THE MAN IT IS ADDRESSED TO AND NOT ABOUT THE GROUND. IT HAS BEEN USED TWICE IN THE SEASON BEFORE LAST AND ONCE IN THE SEASON LAST, AND THE ONE WAS A DRAW THAT WAS NOT BEGUN, AND NOTHING WAS ADDED TO EITHER COUNT IN TEN DAYS AND THE COUNT STANDS AT TWO AND ONE. THE READER OF THIS BODY READ THE FOUR LINES OUT AND DID NOT USE THEM, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A NAMING IS NOT A USE AND THAT A RULE WHICH IS NAMED AND NOT USED IS NOT A RULE ANYBODY IS WORKING AND IS A SENTENCE A MAN IS HOLDING OVER HIMSELF IN FRONT OF OTHER PEOPLE.**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of four lines being read out loud in a room of nine people and the room not being changed by them, and that about four of the nine had heard the four lines before and about five had not, and that the four lines have been on the wall of that seed house for longer than anybody in the room has been keeping a book.

And the fen entered what is on that wall, because a room reads a thing off a wall about twice a year and about four people in this holding have never looked at what else is on it. The four lines are about nine inches from the floor and about fourteen inches long on a piece of board the size of a chopping board, and the board is nailed at two corners and the other two corners have never held a nail, and above it there is a hook with nothing on it, and to the left of it there is a nail with a nail in it, and nobody in this holding has said in about four years what either of those is for. The lines were written by a hand that is not any hand in this room and there is no date on the board and no name on it, and a man who has kept four books for four years has copied the four lines out twice and has never once put in either book what the hook is for.

And then the reader of this body said the thing under it, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty-seven, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I have not got a reason anybody would recognise and I am going to say the nearest thing I have, and the reason is that a draw is a thing you do to water and the only thing anybody in this room has wanted done to water in eleven days is a thing done to about four hundred yards of dead willow, and a rule about moving water fast through a living path is not a rule about that and I have watched about four people this week get near to using it and I have watched myself get near to using it and the nearness is what I am putting on the page."**

**"And I want it entered that I said I was not reaching for it, so that nobody in about four years can come into a room and say that I reached for it and did not get it. I have read the four lines out today. I have not used them. I am not going to use them in the next four days either, and the reason for that second half is the same reason and not a new one, and a new reason would be a decision and this is not one."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a man who had just said out loud that he had come close to something, and about four people in a room deciding what to do with a piece of information about a man they have to work beside, and the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is twelve and did not move this afternoon because this was a room.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is sixty, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have a sheet with a heading on it and four ruled lines under the heading, and the heading is the fourth line of the rota, and I have had it four months, and I am going to say that I am not going to fill it this season, and the reason is that the fields it is for are not this holding's fields and I have never once been asked for a figure for them by anybody who is entitled to one, and a line under a heading I wrote myself is a line I would have to be wrong about in a book that goes on a post."**

And the fen entered what the sheet is, and the shape of it is the same shape as two others, and that is the whole of the trouble with it. It is the size of a page and it is ruled into five, and the four are under a heading in his own hand, and the fifth is under the four and has nothing at the head of it and four ruled lines under that, and the two sheets made that shape in this room in a month are the one with the fourth line of the rota on it and the one with the fourth line of the rota's twenty-eight waiting fields named in a second column that he added and did not tell anybody he had added, and about four people in this holding know about the second column and about five do not, and neither of them has ever asked him for it.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty-eight, and nobody improved it.

**"The window was open for six days and it is shut and I am going to say the six out loud because about four people in this room have been saying five, and a person who says five has dropped the day it shut, which is a day it was open on. The sixth, the seventh, the eighth, the ninth, the tenth, the eleventh. On the sixth a person came into this room who had not been in it. On the seventh a sheet came up four hundred miles. On the eighth a key went into a hand and nobody asked a question. On the ninth two people stood about a hundred yards of ground between a settlement and a wood and came back with nothing. On the tenth a barrow came up four miles. And on the eleventh it shut and it shut correctly."**

**"And none of that is going to be worth a line in anybody else's book, and I am not going to pretend it is, and the only thing I have got is that six days of an office's window went past in this holding without a single question being put into it, and about four years from now a man is going to be able to read that and decide whether that was discipline or whether we simply ran out of things to ask for, and I do not know which, and I would rather he had the six than my opinion."**

**"And the last thing I have got, and it is a small one and it is the only one of these I am sure of. Nothing came down that road in six days except a page with nothing written on it, and nobody in this holding filled in a line on it, and the holding has two bodies of households that have said out loud this month that they would rather be asked for nothing than be on a list, and those two facts have not been put next to each other in any book yet and I am putting them next to each other here, and I do not know what the two of them make when they are side by side."**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a room of nine people hearing a man say that he could not tell whether something had been hard or merely empty, and about four of them finding that the not knowing is easier to be in a room with than either of the two answers.

And the fen entered the hour the man had been holding the sheet flat on his knee for, which was about four, and entered that he had not unfolded it and had not looked at it, and that a man holding a piece of paper flat on his knee for four minutes in a room is a man who is holding a thing he has already decided the answer to, and that the reader of this body had written that answer out on Wednesday and had not sent it, and that there were two pieces of paper in that room on Wednesday and there is one on Friday, and that the one that is not there is in a book that does not go on the post, and that the fen has entered where and is not going to say what it means.

And then the two last things of the fourteenth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE STANDING RULE ABOUT THE ENGINEER OF RECORD WAS READ OUT IN FULL IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, FOUR LINES, EVERY ONE OF THEM ABOUT A DRAW, AND WAS NOT USED, AND ITS USES STAND AT TWO IN THE SEASON BEFORE LAST AND ONE IN THE SEASON LAST AND NOTHING WAS ADDED TO EITHER IN TEN DAYS, AND A NAMING IS NOT A USE. THE DAY-MINUS FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING, BEING THE SECOND MORNING RUNNING THAT THEY HAVE NOT BEEN, AND THEY HAVE NOW GONE UNREAD ON FOUR OF THE NINE MORNINGS SINCE THE SIXTH, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT, THE SEVENTY-FOURTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE WINDOW HAS BEEN SHUT FOR THREE DAYS AND WAS OPEN FOR SIX, AND THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-EIGHT DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIVE, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the fourteenth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a rule that was not used, and not by the reader of this body, who read it out in full and said he was not reaching for it: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SEVENTY-FOURTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN FORTY-ONE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-SIX TIMES AND FALLEN FIFTEEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY-TWO DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SEVENTY-FIRST MORNING OUT OF SEVENTY-THREE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-EIGHT DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIVE DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING, BEING THE SECOND MORNING RUNNING THAT THEY HAVE NOT BEEN, AND THEY HAVE NOW GONE UNREAD ON FOUR OF THE NINE MORNINGS SINCE THE SIXTH. THE STANDING RULE ABOUT THE ENGINEER OF RECORD WAS READ OUT IN FULL IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, BEING FOUR LINES, EVERY ONE OF THEM ABOUT A DRAW, AND WAS NOT USED, AND ITS USES STAND AT TWO IN THE SEASON BEFORE LAST AND ONE IN THE SEASON LAST AND NOTHING WAS ADDED TO EITHER IN TEN DAYS, AND A NAMING IS NOT A USE. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY DECLINED OUT LOUD TO FILL A SHEET WITH A HEADING ON IT AND FOUR RULED LINES UNDER IT, THE HEADING BEING THE FOURTH LINE OF THE ROTA, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE COUNT OF TIMES A PERSON WHO KEEPS A BOOK IN THIS HOLDING HAS SAID A THING IN A YARD THE ENGINEER OF RECORD HAD NOT THOUGHT OF IS TWELVE AND DID NOT MOVE, THIS HAVING BEEN A ROOM. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-SEVEN MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-EIGHT NOT. THE WINDOW WAS OPEN FOR SIX DAYS, THE SIXTH TO THE ELEVENTH INCLUSIVE, AND NO WORK WAS CARRIED OUT OF IT, AND IT HAS BEEN SHUT FOR THREE DAYS. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
