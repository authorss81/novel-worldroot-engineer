# Chapter 307

## The Key Goes Into A Hand

The eighth of this month was a Saturday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **twenty-seven**, and entered that all twenty-seven of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **fifty-six mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty-seven not**, and that twenty-nine and twenty-seven are fifty-six, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

What the silence was made of was two people at a gate on a Saturday who have said to each other on three Saturdays in a row that there is nobody to send and on the fourth of them neither of them said it.

And the figure for the eighth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour, and it was read back by the man of about fifty out of a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and sixty-two hundredweight.**

That is the sixty-eighth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in thirty-five days the series has risen twenty-three times and fallen twelve times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in sixty-six days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixty-fifth morning out of sixty-seven.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of twenty-seven fields standing on ground that a rota has been keeping empty since the twenty-sixth of last month, and that no figure anywhere in this holding says whose they are.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and fifty-two days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and ninety-nine**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on three mornings in this month and that the reason this morning is that there was going to be a hand put out this afternoon and a man who is about to put a thing into somebody else's hand reads two numbers out loud first.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the middle table had four things on it and a fifth thing beside them, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in the room and had been in it about four minutes, and the fen entered that she had come of her own account and that nobody had sent for her and that the reader of this body had not sent for her and had not spoken to her that day.

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a man in about nine people working out how to do a thing he had been thinking about for about nine days without once making it into a question.

And then it was done, and the fen entered all of it and improved none of it.

**THE CLOTH THAT WAS OVER THE FIFTH THING ON THAT MIDDLE TABLE WAS TAKEN OFF BY THE READER OF THIS BODY IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, AND IT WAS NOT THE CLOTH THAT WAS OVER THE FIRST THING, AND THE TWO CLOTHS ARE THE SAME CLOTH AND THE MAN WHO TOOK ONE OFF KNEW WHICH HE WAS REACHING FOR. UNDER IT IS A KEY. IT IS A KEY FOR ONE VALVE AND IT IS THE ONLY KEY TO THAT VALVE THAT HAS EVER BEEN IN THIS HOLDING, AND IT IS NOT A SEQUENCE AND IT IS NOT A CRADLE AND IT CARRIES NO LINE OF ANYTHING, AND THE ELEVENTH LINE OF THE MEMORANDUM OF THE TWENTIETH OF LAST MONTH DOES NOT GOVERN IT. THE KEY WENT INTO THE HAND OF THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN, AND IT WENT INTO AN OPEN HAND AND NOT ONTO A TABLE, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HER IS NIL AND IT IS NIL AND IT DID NOT MOVE THIS AFTERNOON, AND HE ASKED HER NONE BEFORE HE PUT IT DOWN AND NONE AFTER, AND ABOUT NINE PEOPLE WERE IN THE ROOM FOR BOTH.**

The quiet in that room was made of nine people not thanking a woman for taking a thing, and not one of the nine of them had any procedure for that, and not one of them needed one, and the fen has entered all three of those and improved none of them.

And the fen entered what the middle table looked like for about eleven seconds, because nobody else in that room was going to enter it and it is the only thing that has happened on this ground so far that had a shape. The cloth came off in one movement and the key came up in a hand and it is not a large thing, and it is heavier at the head than at the bow, and it is about four inches of iron with a flat about two inches by one and a quarter across the flat and a stub of a shank on it where the wooden grip was taken off about eleven years ago. It came out of a bag with a plate in it once. Nobody entered that. It went into her hand in one movement and her hand closed and she put it in the pocket of the coat she has her tools in and did not look at it again, and the count of people in that room who saw the whole of it is nine and the count of people who were asked to see it is none.

And then the person appointed to be present on that line said the other thing, and the reason came before the thing, and not one figure went into any column, and they were not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have been in this room twice and I have looked at that table twice and I am going to say the one thing I have been keeping, and the reason is that you have all just watched a thing leave a table and not one of you has asked me whether it was allowed to, and I have been waiting since the second hour to see whether anybody was going to, and the answer is that nobody was, and I would have said no if somebody had."**

**"I am not going to say the document does or does not allow it, because I have read the document four times and the document is about a thing under the other cloth, and I am not a person who extends a document past the end of its own words. What I am going to say is that I saw it and I have entered it in my own head against a day and an hour, and that is all that has happened, and that is what being appointed to be present turns out to be, and it is a worse thing than being asked to keep a channel up."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about four people discovering that a person who cannot say who appointed them can still be a person who saw, and that a thing which is witnessed and not permitted and not refused is a third shape and this holding has no column for it.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fourteen, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Nobody asked me anything and I am going to keep it that way, and the reason is that I own nine acres that come off on the twenty-ninth of this month and I have said that date three times in rooms and yards this season and every one of those three times somebody came back with a question about it, and this afternoon is the fourth and nobody did, and I would like the not to be a fact about the room and not a favor done to me."**

**"And I am going to say the half of it that is about my own ground and not about that key, because about four of you have been looking at my hand for about a minute. I have not said I am going to open anything. I have not said I am not. I have taken a key that somebody else could not use and put it in my own pocket and I am going to go and start on the carted part of that ground, and if anybody in this room wants to know what happens to a key after that they can come and stand at the gate on the twenty-ninth and watch me not answer them."**

Nobody improved one item of that, and the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move, and this was a room and not a yard, and the count of times she has said a thing in a room that nobody asked her for is four and was three.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-four, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to write the word handed on the sheet with the heading on it, and I am going to put the day on it and the day of the week, and I am going to leave the four ruled lines under it, and the reason is that a man with a book who writes one word and leaves the lines is a man with a book, and a man with a book who writes one word and rules them off has decided he knows what comes next, and I do not know what comes next, and I have got a column that has wanted a figure in it for four years and I have never once been able to say what the figure is."**

And then the reader of this body said the other thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty-one, and nobody improved it.

**"The valve at the head of that channel was not opened this afternoon and the key is in a hand and not in a lock, and I am going to say both halves of that out loud on one afternoon because about four people in this room are going to tell a different version of it in about four years. Nothing was done. That is the whole of what was done. A key that has been lying under a cloth for nine days went into the hand of the person who owns the ground on the other side of the gate, and I did not ask her a question about it and the count of questions I have asked her is nil and it was nil before this afternoon."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people sitting with a thing having been done, and that the thing had no figure in it and no consequence anybody could write down, and that this is what the eleventh line is for, and that the eleventh line does not mention keys.

And then at about the seventh hour of the evening the reader of this body walked out to the field gate and stood on the near side of it, and she was on the other side with her sleeves rolled and the key was in a pocket and not in her hand, and he did not go in, and the fen entered that he did not go in and entered the reason he gave, which was that the gate was hers and he was not going to be on her ground on the evening a key changed hands, and that the count of questions he asked her on the evening a key changed hands is nil and is nil.

And then the two last things of the eighth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE KEY WENT INTO A HAND AND NOT INTO A LOCK, AND THE VALVE WAS NOT OPENED, AND A KEY IS NEITHER A SEQUENCE NOR A CRADLE AND IS NOT GOVERNED BY THE ELEVENTH LINE, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN IS NIL AND DID NOT MOVE THIS AFTERNOON, AND NOBODY THANKED HER. THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN HAVE NOW WAITED TWENTY-SEVEN TIMES AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-SIX MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-SEVEN NOT.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO, THE SIXTY-EIGHTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND A THING UNDER A CLOTH ON THE SAME TABLE WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY THIS AFTERNOON, AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE IN THIS HOLDING WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THE BACK OF THE PAGE THAT CAME DOWN THE ROAD IS STILL BLANK, AND THE WINDOW IS OPEN FOR THE THIRD OF SIX DAYS.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the eighth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a key, and not by the reader of this body, who stood on the near side of a gate in the evening and did not go in: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS TWENTY-SEVEN, AND ALL TWENTY-SEVEN OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-SIX MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-SEVEN NOT, BEING TWENTY-NINE AND TWENTY-SEVEN. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-EIGHTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN THIRTY-FIVE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN TWELVE TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SIXTY-SIX DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SIXTY-FIFTH MORNING OUT OF SIXTY-SEVEN. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-TWO DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-NINE DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE CLOTH WAS TAKEN OFF THE FIFTH THING ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND IT WAS NOT THE CLOTH OVER THE FIRST THING. A KEY FOR ONE VALVE WENT INTO THE HAND OF THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN, AND IT IS THE ONLY KEY TO THAT VALVE THAT HAS EVER BEEN IN THIS HOLDING, AND IT IS NOT A SEQUENCE AND NOT A CRADLE AND THE ELEVENTH LINE DOES NOT GOVERN IT. THE VALVE WAS NOT OPENED. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HER IS NIL AND DID NOT MOVE, AND HE ASKED HER NONE BEFORE HE PUT IT DOWN AND NONE AFTER. SHE GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY ENTERED THE WORD HANDED ON A SHEET WITH A HEADING ON IT AND LEFT FOUR RULED LINES UNDER IT. THE PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THAT LINE WAS IN THE ROOM AND IS NOT NAMED AND WAS NOT ASKED ANYTHING. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, THIS HAVING BEEN A ROOM. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE WELL ON THE SHELF WAS NOT READ. THE FIGURE ON LINE ONE OF THE FORM ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED. THE WINDOW IS OPEN FOR THE THIRD OF SIX DAYS. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
