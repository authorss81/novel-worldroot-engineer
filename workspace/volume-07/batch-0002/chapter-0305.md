# Chapter 305

## The First Of Six Days

The sixth of this month was a Thursday and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the sixth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and fifty-nine hundredweight.**

That is the sixty-sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in thirty-three days the series has risen twenty-two times and fallen eleven times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in sixty-four days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixty-third morning out of sixty-five.

The room was quiet for the length of about a minute, and the fen entered what the quiet was made of, which was a man writing a figure with a hand that had started the day braced for a smaller one.

**THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND IT IS SIXTY-SIX THIS MORNING, AND THE FIFTY-FIVE THAT STANDS BESIDE IT IN THE SAME LINE OF THE SAME PAGE IS THE NUMBER OF MORNINGS THE ROTA HAS BEEN IN FORCE, AND THE TWO FIGURES ARE IN ONE SENTENCE ONLY BECAUSE A MAN PUT THEM THERE. SIXTY-SIX COUNTS A MEASUREMENT AND FIFTY-FIVE COUNTS A WEEK, AND A WEEK IS NOT A MEASUREMENT, AND NEITHER FIGURE HAS EVER ONCE BEEN THE SAME NUMBER AS THE OTHER. A HOLDING THAT TAKES THE FIFTY-FIVE OFF THIS LINE HAS TAKEN A COLUMN OUT OF A DIFFERENT COLUMN, AND THE ERRORS THAT COME OF IT ARE NOT SMALL ONES, THEY ARE THE KIND THAT LOOK CHECKED.**

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and fifty days** and the March's tin at **four hundred and ninety-seven**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on one morning in this month, and that the reason this morning is that a day four people here have been counting toward for a month arrived at about the sixth hour, and a man counting toward a day wants two other numbers out loud before it gets here.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the middle table had four things on it and a fifth thing beside them, and there was a person in the room who had not been in that room before this month and who had not come in out of the weather.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of about nine people not looking at the door behind a person who was standing against it, and that the not looking was not rudeness, and that the reason for it had been given out loud in a room in another part of the world and had been printed in a document four hundred miles off, and that neither of those two things could be improved on by anybody in this holding.

And the man of about fifty said the thing that was his, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-two, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is a window and it opened at about the sixth hour this morning and it shuts on the eleventh, and I am going to say the two halves of that out loud in this room in order, on one morning, and the reason is that about four people in this holding have been saying one half of it for eleven days and a half of a thing said on its own is a rule nobody has checked, and I have got a book and a rule nobody has checked is a figure I cannot check, and that is where I have got two of them already."**

**"So. No work is carried out of a window. And no window has ever been extended in a season. That is the whole of what I know about windows and I have got it from two places and neither place is this room. The first is a thing four years old about people putting work outside a shape. The second is that a window has an end on it and an end that moves is not an end."**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a man who had said a rule out loud and then not been asked where he had got it, because four people in that room had each got it from a different one of the two places he had named and had not heard the other one.

And then the person who is on that line and is not named said one thing, and the reason came before the thing, and nothing went into any column that has a head at the top of it, and they were not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I was appointed to be present. I am not able to tell you who appointed me, and I am not going to try, and the reason is that the paper names an office and it does not name a person and it does not name this place either, and a document that leaves a name off has left it off for a reason I am not in the room for. I have been in this room four days and I have not been asked what I am for and I would have given you the same answer either way."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of about nine people finding out in about four seconds that they had a person in a room who could not tell them what the person was for, and that the count of questions the reader of this body has asked in that room this afternoon is nil, and that he had worked out at about the fifth hour of the morning that he was not going to ask one.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and seventeen, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"I am not going to ask you anything, and I am going to say the reason before the afternoon is over instead of saying it now, because a man who says a reason for not asking a question at the same moment as not asking it is doing something with the room and I would rather not do something to the room this afternoon."**

**"What I am going to say is that the eleventh line of that memorandum governs the seed and the plate and does not govern the key, and I have said that in this room on two mornings and I am going to say it a third time because this is the first morning there has been a person in here who was sent by anybody at all, and about four of you have been looking at that table this afternoon the way a person looks at a table when they have been told a thing about it and are waiting to see if it is acted on. It is not going to be acted on. The eleventh line says: NO SEQUENCE, AND NO PART CONTAINING A SEQUENCE, AND NO CRADLE, IS TO BE REMOVED, BROKEN, OPENED OR SET AGAINST ANY VALVE BY ANY PERSON WITHOUT A WRITTEN INSTRUMENT OF THE OFFICE NAMED AT THE HEAD OF IT. A key is not a sequence and a key is not a cradle, and the key is under a cloth on that table about four feet from where the seed is under a cloth, and the two of them are not one thing."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a man reading a line of a document out loud in a room where a person he had not asked anything of was standing, and that he had said it out loud rather than writing it down because a document read in a room is heard by a person and a document written on a table is not.

And then the man of about fifty read the day out as far as the day had got, and the fen entered all of it, and improved none of it.

**THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-SIXTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN THIRTY-THREE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN ELEVEN TIMES. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENED AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW, AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON, AND BOTH HALVES WERE SAID OUT LOUD IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE ON ONE MORNING FOR THE FIRST TIME IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS. A PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THAT LINE FROM THE TWENTIETH OF LAST MONTH WAS IN THE ROOM AND IS NOT NAMED, AND SAID OUT LOUD THAT THEY CANNOT SAY WHO APPOINTED THEM. THEY ARE NOT A STEWARD AND PROTECTION IS NOT STEWARDSHIP AND THE OFFICE PRINTS BOTH HALVES. THE ELEVENTH LINE GOVERNS THE SEED AND THE PLATE AND NOT THE KEY, AND A THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY, AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE IN THIS HOLDING WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE.**

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and eighteen, and nobody improved it.

**"This is the first of six days. I want it said that I have not got one thing I want to happen inside those six days, and the reason is that the only thing I have wanted out of that office in five months is one piece of paper with a name on the top of it, and I have not asked for it and I am not going to ask for it inside a window, because a man who asks inside a window is a man who has put the asking in the shape, and then the shape is the thing that decides."**

**"So there is a person in this room from an office and there is nothing in this room for that person to be present at, and both of those are correct, and I would rather have six days of that than one day of something done outside the six."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the length of that silence was about a minute and that it was made of a window with six days on it and nothing in it and about nine people who had not expected to be comfortable about that and were not, and that the fen has entered the discomfort and is not going to improve it into either a virtue or a waste.

And then the two last things of the sixth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE WINDOW OPENED ON THE SIXTH AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH AND THAT IS SIX DAYS, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW, AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON, AND A PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THAT LINE IS IN THE ROOM AND IS NOT NAMED AND IS NOT A STEWARD, AND THE OFFICE PRINTS PROTECTION IS NOT STEWARDSHIP ON THE SAME PAGE, AND NEITHER HALF OF THAT IS A COMFORT AND BOTH HALVES ARE TRUE. NO WRITTEN INSTRUMENT WAS ASKED FOR INSIDE THE WINDOW AND NONE IS GOING TO BE.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-SIXTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND DID NOT RESET AT THE TURN OF THE MONTH, AND THE FIFTY-FIVE IN THE COLUMN BESIDE IT IS THE ROTA AND IS A DIFFERENT FIGURE IN A DIFFERENT COLUMN, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY, AND THE FIFTH DAY OF THIS MONTH WAS THE LAST DAY BEFORE THE WINDOW AND THE SIXTH IS THE FIRST DAY OF IT.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the sixth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a window, and not by the reader of this body, who read the eleventh line out loud in a room and did not ask the person appointed to be present anything: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-SIXTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN THIRTY-THREE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN ELEVEN TIMES. THE COUNT OF THE SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND THE FIFTY-FIVE IN THE SAME LINE OF THE SAME PAGE IS THE ROTA'S MORNINGS IN FORCE AND IS A DIFFERENT FIGURE IN A DIFFERENT COLUMN. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SIXTY-FOUR DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SIXTY-THIRD MORNING OUT OF SIXTY-FIVE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-SEVEN DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENED THIS MORNING AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, BEING SIX DAYS, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF A WINDOW AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON, AND BOTH HALVES WERE SAID OUT LOUD IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE ON ONE MORNING FOR THE FIRST TIME IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS. A PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THAT LINE FROM THE TWENTIETH OF LAST MONTH WAS IN THE ROOM IN THE AFTERNOON AND IS NOT NAMED AND SAID OUT LOUD THAT THEY CANNOT SAY WHO APPOINTED THEM, THE REASON BEING THAT THE PAPER NAMES AN OFFICE AND NOT A PERSON AND NOT THIS PLACE. THE APPOINTED PERSON IS NOT A STEWARD AND PROTECTION IS NOT STEWARDSHIP AND BOTH ARE PRINTED BY THE OFFICE. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY ASKED IN THAT ROOM THIS AFTERNOON IS NIL. THE ELEVENTH LINE OF THE MEMORANDUM GOVERNS THE SEED AND THE PLATE AND NOT THE KEY AND WAS READ OUT ALOUD AND NOT IMPROVED ON AND NOT SAID A SECOND WAY AFTERWARD. A THING UNDER A CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE IN THIS HOLDING WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO AND DID NOT MOVE. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND THE REASON WAS NOT GIVEN A SECOND TIME IN A SEASON. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND THE FIRST ONE IS NOT NAMED. THE FIGURE ON LINE ONE OF THE FORM ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IS THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND IS NOT THE FIGURE OF ANY OTHER, AND THE LINE THE QUESTION IS ON IS ENTERED AND NOT FILLED AND NOBODY WROTE ON IT. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-FIVE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-SIX NOT, AND THE COUNT OF FIELDS THAT HAVE WAITED IS TWENTY-SIX AND ALL OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE AND NOBODY IS BLAMED FOR A FIELD THAT WAITED. THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ AND THE OTHER SIX HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE TWO TERMS ARE ON ONE TABLE AND ONE IS IN A COAT AND NEITHER WAS TOUCHED. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
