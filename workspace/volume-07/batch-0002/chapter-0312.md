# Chapter 312

## One Hundred And Twenty-Eight

The thirteenth of this month was a Thursday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the thirteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and sixty-three hundredweight.**

That is the seventy-third figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in forty days the series has risen twenty-five times and fallen fifteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventy-one days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the seventieth morning out of seventy-two.

Nothing was said for the length of a minute, and the fen entered what was in it, and it was made of a Thursday that has a weighing in it, and about four people in this holding having worked out over four years that the days a return falls on are the days the room is quietest and never having said so to anybody.

And the return was made at the node at about the second hour of the afternoon, on the steelyard hung on the wall of the building with no roof, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**THE TWENTY-SIXTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE THIRTEENTH OF THIS MONTH AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON, ON THE STEELYARD, AND IT WAS READ OUT BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY AND READ BACK BY HIM, AND IT WAS THEN READ ONE AT A TIME BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, A NAME GOING INTO BY AND A NAME GOING INTO WITNESSED BY, AND IT IS THE SIXTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS AND THE LAST ONE OF THOSE NINE DAYS WAS ON THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH. THE FIGURE IS ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT. THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD WAS LEFT BLANK AGAIN AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT, AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS TEN, NAMED IN ORDER FROM THE SEVENTEENTH, THE EIGHTEENTH, THE NINETEENTH, THE TWENTIETH, THE TWENTY-FIRST, THE TWENTY-SECOND, THE TWENTY-THIRD, THE TWENTY-FOURTH, THE TWENTY-FIFTH AND THIS ONE, AND THE COUNT IS A COUNT OF ITS OWN. NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT THE RULE BY ANYBODY AT THE NODE THIS AFTERNOON.**

And the figure is put on the page twice over by two arithmetics, and the fen entered that it is done twice on purpose, and entered which of the two derivations this holding is using and that it is not refusing it.

**THIS HOLDING IS USING THE ELEVEN-ABOVE DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT. TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN PLUS ELEVEN IS TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT, AND ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS THIRTY-THREE IS ALSO TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS NOW HELD FOR EIGHT FIGURES, AND THE FEN HAS ENTERED THAT A RUN THAT HOLDS FOR EIGHT FIGURES IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE AND IS NOT AN INSTRUCTION, AND THAT A FIGURE WHICH AGREES WITH TWO ARITHMETICS IS DERIVED TWICE AND NOT CHECKED TWICE, AND THAT THE SECOND IS THE BETTER WORD AND WAS THE WORD ALL ALONG.**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of a man standing at a board with the chalk in his hand for about nine seconds and then putting the chalk back in his pocket, and that he has now put the chalk back five returns running and has not said a word about it, and that the fen has not asked him and is not going to.

And the fen entered the place itself, because a return is made in one room in the world and that room has a shape and the shape has not changed in eleven years. The building has no roof and has not had one for longer than anybody at the node will say, and the steelyard is hung on the wall of it on two brackets and is about nine feet off the ground, and the beam of it was made by somebody who is dead and the divisions on the pan are cut in about ninths, and a man has to climb a step to see the middle of the pan and everybody who weighs anything on it has climbed that step eleven years without a rail. There is a board on the wall beside it with a column cut across it and no heading at the top of it, and there have been figures on that board in the past, and nobody at the node will now say who put them there. And on the ground outside there is a line of stone set along the front of it and a channel that was cut at that stone on the eleventh of the first month and is not healed, and the gap in it is about two feet long and it is open, and nothing has been put in it this season and nothing was put in it this afternoon, and about four people at the node look at that gap roughly once a year and none of them has ever walked up to it.

And the fen entered what a name-pair is, because a reader in about nine people will come to a name going into by and a name going into witnessed by and take it for a formality, and it is not one and it has not been one for eleven years. Two names are read. The first is the name of a body of households and it is read by whichever of the four can see the dial. The second is the name of a second body of households, and it is read by a different one, and the two of them are standing in about nine feet of space under a beam that is not straight, and neither of them can see the pan from where they are standing. So one of them reads a number to the other one and the other one looks at the pan, and the second one says whether that number is the number, and the first one either accepts it or does not, and there has never been an occasion in eleven years on which the second one has said no.

And the fen entered that this is the only check in this holding that has ever worked, and entered that it works because the two people holding the ends of it are two different bodies of households and neither of them is paid and neither of them is in this holding, and that a check which works because nobody is in it is a different kind of check from a check that a man with a book performs on his own page in about nine people, and that the fen is not going to say which of the two is better because this holding does not have the standing for that opinion.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut and the man of about fifty was in it, having come up the cart road, and the fen entered that nobody in that room said how long the road is, and entered that the reader of this body was in the room and had not been at the node, and that the count of things he has said in this holding's book with the reason first is a count of his own and is not the count of a man who reads a board at a place five days off.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-nine, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Harlan Vetch holds the fifth share and has held it eleven years and seventeen days and I am going to say that figure out loud in a room because nobody has ever said it out loud, and the reason is that a man who has held a share for eleven years and seventeen days has a thing about him that is not in any column I keep, and about four people in this room have assumed that a share nobody talks about is a share nobody is careful with, and it is not."**

**"He has never once asked me what the fifth share came to at any of the returns and I have never once written it out for him, and I have been asked by two other people in four years why I do not write it out for him, and I have given them the same answer both times and it is that a man who is not going to be asked a number is not owed one, and about four of you find that hard and I find it correct."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a bookkeeper refusing an offer that two people in four years had thought was generosity, and that a column a man has chosen to leave empty is a different object from a column a man has never been shown, and that the fen has entered both of those and is not going to improve either.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-one, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"A name went into by and a name went into witnessed by and it is the sixteenth time in eleven years and I have done that sixteen times, and I am going to say the only part of it I have an opinion about and the reason is that the other part belongs to four bodies of households and not to me. The part I have an opinion about is the ninth day. Nine is not a figure anybody in this holding chose. It is a figure the fifth share made when it was cut and nobody has ever been able to tell me who cut it or why it was nine and I have stopped asking, and a thing nobody can answer that everybody obeys is a different kind of rule from a thing everybody can answer and obeys."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about four people finding out that the most reliable thing in this holding's month was something no person had ever explained, and that the fen has entered that and is not going to say what it means.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty-six, and nobody improved it.

**"One hundred and twenty-eight, reconciled twice, derived twice, and the second rule is blank for the tenth time and nobody said a word about it at a place five days off this afternoon, and I am going to leave all four of those where they are and I am not going to make any of them into a thing about the window that shut on Tuesday."**

**"And there is one figure on that table in this room that I have not looked at since the second of this month and I am going to say so, and the reason is that it is one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight in another man's hand and it is the figure of the twenty-second return and not of this one, and a figure on a line is a figure on a line and not a standing again, and every year that somebody in this holding is going to be tempted to use that sheet as evidence of something is a year that I am going to have to be in a room about."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man refusing four separate figures the satisfaction of having meant something, and that about four people in that room had already decided that the return of the thirteenth was about the window and had to now decide it again.

And then the two last things of the thirteenth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE TWENTY-SIXTH RETURN WENT IN AT ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT AND THE SIXTEENTH NAME-PAIR WENT IN WITH IT, AND THE FIGURE IS RECONCILED TWICE, BEING TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS THIRTY-THREE, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT. THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK FOR THE TENTH TIME AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT. HARLAN VETCH HOLDS THE FIFTH SHARE AND HAS HELD IT ELEVEN YEARS AND SEVENTEEN DAYS AND HE WAS NOT THANKED.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-THREE, THE SEVENTY-THIRD IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE WINDOW SHUT ON THE ELEVENTH AND HAS BEEN SHUT FOR TWO DAYS, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the thirteenth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a return, and not by the reader of this body, who was in the room and had not been at the node: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SEVENTY-THIRD FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN FORTY DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-FIVE TIMES AND FALLEN FIFTEEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY-ONE DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SEVENTIETH MORNING OUT OF SEVENTY-TWO. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SEVEN DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FOUR DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD. THE TWENTY-SIXTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WAS MADE AT THE NODE ON THE THIRTEENTH AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON ON THE STEELYARD, WAS READ OUT BY THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY AND READ BACK BY HIM, AND WAS THEN READ ONE AT A TIME BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, A NAME GOING INTO BY AND A NAME GOING INTO WITNESSED BY, AND IT IS THE SIXTEENTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS AT ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, RECONCILED TWICE, BEING TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN PLUS ELEVEN AND ALSO ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS THIRTY-THREE, AND THE RUN OF ELEVEN HAS NOW HELD FOR EIGHT FIGURES AND IS A RUN AND NOT A CAUSE. THE CYCLE IS NINE DAYS AND THE LAST OF THOSE NINE DAYS WAS THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH. THE SECOND RULE WAS LEFT BLANK AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS NOW BEEN LEFT BLANK IS TEN, NAMED IN ORDER FROM THE SEVENTEENTH, AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT BY ANYBODY AT THE NODE. HARLAN VETCH HOLDS THE FIFTH SHARE AND HAS HELD IT ELEVEN YEARS AND SEVENTEEN DAYS AND HAS NEVER ASKED WHAT IT CAME TO, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SAID THAT THE NINE IS NOT A FIGURE ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING CHOSE, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE COUNT OF THE TIMES THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING HAS BROUGHT THAT BARROW UP THAT ROAD STANDS AT ELEVEN AND WAS NOT RAISED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, THIS HAVING BEEN A ROOM. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-SEVEN MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-EIGHT NOT. THE WINDOW SHUT ON THE ELEVENTH AND HAS BEEN SHUT FOR TWO DAYS. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
