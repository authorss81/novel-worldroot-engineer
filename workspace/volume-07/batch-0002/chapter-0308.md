# Chapter 308

## Four Hundred Yards Inside The Line

The ninth of this month was a Sunday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the figure for the ninth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same number and the same weight.

**Six hundred and sixty hundredweight.**

That is the sixty-ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen, and in thirty-six days the series has risen twenty-three times and fallen thirteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in sixty-seven days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixty-sixth morning out of sixty-eight.

What the silence was made of was a mile and a bit of a footpath going round the end of a channel that nobody on it could be seen from, and about four people walking it at the speed of a person who is going to look at something rather than get somewhere.

And the fen entered what the day was, because a Sunday inside a window is a different day from a Thursday inside one and about four people in this holding have stopped noticing the difference and the fen has not. Nobody carried anything. Nobody took a basket. There is a rule about this holding and a person can go out of a gate with his hands empty or with what he is carrying in them, and on a working morning the second of those is a bucket or a tool, and on a Sunday going to a wood it is a bucket or a tool as well, and about four people at the second hour on the ninth of this month stood at the gate and worked out which of those two a piece of dead willow in a hand is.

And at about the fourth hour of the morning the reader of this body went out with Tova Reed, and the two of them went round and not along, and the fen entered that the route did not go through the ninety yards in the middle of the eleven hundred, and that nobody has spoken for that ground and that he did not ask to go through it and did not go, and that a woman who keeps a route for eleven days out of twenty knows where the ground is that has not been spoken for and did not have to be asked.

For about a minute there was nothing said on the path, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was two people walking a boundary and both of them knowing which side of it they were on.

And they were on the Thornwild edge at about the sixth hour of the afternoon and they were back at about the seventh hour of the evening, and the fen entered what they saw and entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**THE LONG FUNGAL CARRIES ON ABOUT NINE ACRES OF DEAD WILLOW ON THAT EDGE AND THE FRUITING BODIES ON IT ARE UP WHERE THEY SHOULD NOT BE FOR THE TIME OF YEAR, AND THE PLACE THEY WERE FOUND IS INSIDE FOUR HUNDRED YARDS OF THE FIRST DWELLING OF THORNWILD AND IS NOT INSIDE THE INHABITED LINE, WHICH IS ABOUT TWO HUNDRED YARDS OF SETTLEMENT UNDER THE SOUTH EMBANKMENT WINDBREAK. THE LAST OF THE ROOFS AND THE NEAREST OF THE DEAD WOOD ARE SEPARATED BY ABOUT A HUNDRED YARDS OF OPEN GROUND AND A BANK. THE COUNT OF THE FRUITING BODIES WAS NOT TAKEN AND IS NOT ON THIS PAGE, AND THE COUNT WAS NOT TAKEN BY ANYBODY ON THAT GROUND AND BY ANYBODY IN THIS HOLDING. NOBODY SAID WHETHER THEY ARE SAFE TO EAT AND NOBODY PUT ONE IN A MOUTH, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY DID NOT BRING A PIECE OF ROOTWOOD BACK AND DID NOT BRING TWIG NEITHER, AND THE COUNT OF PIECES OF THE FUNGUS THAT HAVE COME INTO THIS HOLDING IN FIVE MONTHS IS NIL.**

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of two people standing in about a hundred yards of open ground between a settlement and a wood, and neither of them going the last hundred yards.

And then about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and Soren Rill was in it, and the fen entered that he was in it and had not said a word in that room since the third of this month and that six days is a floor on how long a man can be in a room in this holding and not speak, and that the fen has not said whether he was silent out of anything.

And then Soren Rill said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is eight, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have been in this room six days and I have not said anything, and I am going to say the half of it that is about a line and not about a wood, and the reason is that about four of you are about to say that it is outside our line and be glad, and I have come four miles and a half to say that outside your line is not out of anybody's, and that the Assembly does not draw a line round a settlement and call the ground past it nobody's. We live on the other side of that bank and we have known about that wood since I was a boy and nobody told us."**

**"What I am not going to say is anything about the fruiting bodies, and there is a reason for that too, and the reason is that I have about forty people at the end of that embankment who will ask me inside a week whether the ground they are living on is safe, and I do not know the answer, and I have had two chances in two days to be in a room with people who know more about it than I do and I have used both of them by standing up."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man who had come four miles and a half to hand a question to a room and had said out loud in the same breath that he did not have the answer to it, and that about four people in that room understood that this was the first time they had heard a person from that line say the word we and mean a set of people who are not in the room.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is five, and it is her own and nobody improved it.

**"Nobody has brought a piece of it back in five months and I want that said in a room and not in a book, and the reason is that I am the one who goes out there, and if there is a piece of that wood in this holding in about four years then it got here on a cart and a cart does not ask anybody. A person carrying a piece in a hand asks everybody on the path what it is, and the path runs past four gates."**

**"And I am not going to say what is on that wood is good or not good to eat, because I do not know, and because about four of you have been looking at the door since I sat down, and a woman who says no to a question in a room gets asked it in a yard next week, and I have watched a man in this holding do exactly that to a man on that line and the man on that line has not stopped walking."**

Nobody improved one item of that, and the count of things the reader of this body has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this evening, and this was a room, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody in the line of asking that Assembly for a figure is nil and is nil, and he asked a man from that line a question on the third of this month and a different one on the twenty-eighth of last month and neither of them was about a figure, and the two counts are not added to one another.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-five, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There are two figures on that page of mine now and they are about a hundred yards apart and they are not the same kind of thing, and I am going to say that out loud in a room instead of in a book, and the reason is that a column with two figures in it and one head over the pair is a column that a person in about four years is going to add, and I have added them three times this afternoon and got a hundred yards every time, and a hundred yards is the distance between two roofs and it is not the distance between a roof and a wood."**

**"So I am going to write them in two lines and I am going to write the word of under each of them, and under the four hundred I am going to write FROM THE FIRST DWELLING and under the two hundred I am going to write OF THE SETTLEMENT, and that is a rule about my own book and not a fact about anybody's ground, and I have got about eleven months before anybody in this holding has a reason to complain about it."**

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty-two, and nobody improved it.

**"Inside four hundred yards and outside about two hundred. I am going to leave it at those two figures and I am not going to convert them into one another, and the reason is that the first one is a distance from a house and the second one is a length of people, and about four years ago I had a day when I turned one of those into the other and a woman on a route had to tell me I was wrong, and she was right to and I have not forgotten it and I am not going to do it again on ground with a wood on it."**

**"And there is a second thing I am not going to say and it is the harder of the two, and it is that the window has been open for four days now and it shuts on the eleventh, and there is a piece of ground about a hundred yards outside a settlement where something is coming up that nobody in about nine people can put a word on, and I have had four days in which an office four hundred miles off had an open window, and I have not used one hour of it, and I am not going to be able to explain in about four years why that was right."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was a man sitting down about a yard and a half from a woman who had not asked him anything and had been about four seconds from asking him something, and that the fen has entered both of those and is not going to improve either of them.

And the fen entered what the ground was like under their boots for the last hundred yards, because nobody entered it in the room and a man who has stood on a bank in the afternoon and then come back and talked for an hour has it still under him. It is not thin. It has not been thin in eleven years in the way the ground between the two ends of that channel has been, and the difference is not one of care, and the fen is not going to say what it is, because nobody in this room is going to find out this season and a person who guesses at a floor in a book writes a fact about a floor.

And then the two last things of the ninth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE LONG FUNGAL WAS FOUND INSIDE FOUR HUNDRED YARDS OF THE FIRST DWELLING OF THORNWILD AND IS NOT INSIDE THE INHABITED LINE OF ABOUT TWO HUNDRED YARDS, AND THE COUNT OF THE FRUITING BODIES WAS NOT TAKEN AND IS NOT NARRATED, AND NOBODY SAID WHETHER THEY ARE SAFE TO EAT AND NOBODY PUT ONE IN A MOUTH. NO PIECE OF THE FUNGUS HAS COME INTO THIS HOLDING IN FIVE MONTHS AND NONE CAME BACK TODAY.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY, THE SIXTY-NINTH IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN DID NOT WAIT THIS MORNING BECAUSE THE FOURTH LINE DID NOT COME ROUND THIS MORNING, AND THE WINDOW IS OPEN FOR THE FOURTH OF SIX DAYS, AND A PERSON APPOINTED TO BE PRESENT ON THAT LINE WAS NOT IN THE ROOM THIS EVENING AND WAS NOT ASKED TO BE.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the ninth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a wood, and not by the reader of this body, who went out to it and came back with nothing: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SIXTY-NINTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN THIRTY-SIX DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTEEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SIXTY-SEVEN DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SIXTY-SIXTH MORNING OUT OF SIXTY-EIGHT. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-THREE DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING FOR THE FIRST TIME IN FOUR MORNINGS. THE READER OF THIS BODY AND TOVA REED WENT ROUND AND NOT ALONG AND DID NOT GO THROUGH THE NINETY YARDS IN THE MIDDLE OF THE ELEVEN HUNDRED, AND NOBODY HAS SPOKEN FOR THAT GROUND. THE LONG FUNGAL WAS FOUND INSIDE FOUR HUNDRED YARDS OF THE FIRST DWELLING OF THORNWILD AND IS NOT INSIDE THE INHABITED LINE OF ABOUT TWO HUNDRED YARDS, AND THE COUNT OF THE FRUITING BODIES WAS NOT TAKEN AND IS NOT NARRATED, AND NOBODY SAID WHETHER THEY ARE SAFE TO EAT AND NOBODY PUT ONE IN A MOUTH, AND NO PIECE OF THE FUNGUS CAME INTO THIS HOLDING AND NONE CAME BACK. SOREN RILL, OF AN ASSEMBLY IN THE THORNWILD, WAS IN THE ROOM AND HAD SAID NOTHING IN IT SINCE THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH, AND SAID THAT OUTSIDE A LINE IS NOT OUT OF ANYBODY'S, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND WAS NOT THANKED. TOVA REED SAID THAT NOBODY CARRIES A PIECE OF THAT WOOD IN A HAND AND THAT A CART DOES NOT ASK ANYBODY, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, AND WAS NOT THANKED. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED ANYBODY IN THE LINE OF ASKING THAT ASSEMBLY FOR A FIGURE IS NIL AND IS NIL. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, THIS HAVING BEEN A ROOM. THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-SIX MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-SEVEN NOT, AND THE COUNT OF FIELDS THAT HAVE WAITED IS TWENTY-SEVEN. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT OF FETCHINGS IS TWENTY. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE WINDOW IS OPEN FOR THE FOURTH OF SIX DAYS. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
