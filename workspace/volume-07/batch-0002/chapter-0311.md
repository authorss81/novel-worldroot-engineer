# Chapter 311

## The Standing Read Out And Not Improved

The twelfth of this month was a Wednesday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited, and the fen entered the number, which is **twenty-eight**, and entered that all twenty-eight of them are the fourth line, and entered that the rota now stands at **fifty-seven mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and twenty-eight not**, and that twenty-nine and twenty-eight are fifty-seven, and that nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

About a minute went by and nothing was said in it, and the fen entered the whole of it, and it was made of about four people working out at the same time that the count had gone up by one again and that the number it had gone up by was the same number it had gone up by for three mornings, and that this holding has no other kind of count.

And the figure for the twelfth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour, and it was read back by the man of about fifty out of a different book, and the two are the same.

**Six hundred and sixty-six hundredweight.**

That is the seventy-second figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen, and in thirty-nine days the series has risen twenty-five times and fallen fourteen times, and the fen entered both and did not improve them, and entered that nobody thanked anybody and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in seventy days and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the sixty-ninth morning out of seventy-one.

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a number that had gone up by one on four mornings out of four and had no opinion about it, and that about four people in this holding would be willing to bet money on the fifth.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, and the boards stand at **four hundred and fifty-six days** and the March's tin at **five hundred and three**, and the two are forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not to be, and the fen entered that he has read them out loud on five mornings in this month and that the fen has not entered whether that is five or four or something else, because the count of mornings a man says two numbers is not a count this holding keeps.

And then at about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the man of about fifty read the standing out as it stands, and read it out whole, and the fen entered all of it and improved no part of it.

> **THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES AND THE REASON WAS GIVEN ONCE AND WAS NOT GIVEN AGAIN IN THIS SEASON. PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR STAND AT TWO, THOSE WHO CAN HOLD ONE FOR FIVE AT ONE, AND IT IS THE READER OF THIS BODY, AND SIX IS NOT A COLUMN. PERSONS IN THIS FEN WHO HAVE A ROOTMARK STAND AT TWO, AND NOTHING THAT WAS SAID ABOUT EITHER ARM IN TEN DAYS IS A THIRD ACCOUNT, AND A MARK THAT IS HOT IS NOT A MARK AND A HEADACHE IS NOT A MARK AND A BRIDGE IS NOT A MARK. ONE PERSON CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR AND WALK OUT OF THE GROUND AFTERWARDS AND IT IS HIM. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR AND RECONCILE NONE OF ONE ANOTHER. THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG STAND AT FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE IN TEN DAYS. NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK STAND AT TWO AND THE FIRST ONE IS NOT NAMED. NOT KNOWNS STAND AT SIX AND NO UNIT WAS SUPPLIED FOR ANY OF THEM AND A SEVENTH WAS NOT ADDED FOR A THING WITH A SHAPE AND NOT A UNIT, WHICH IN TEN DAYS INCLUDED THE THING UNDER A CLOTH. REQUESTS AGAINST NOTHING AND THE NOTES UNDER SECTION NINE STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND FIFTY-THREE, AND NO NEW NOTE UNDER SECTION NINE WAS WRITTEN IN TEN DAYS. THE BODIES AT THE NODE ARE FIVE AND THE NUMBER WAS NOT GIVEN IN A YARD. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY HAS BEEN FETCHED TWENTY TIMES, THE LAST OF THEM ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH, AND HE HAS NOT BEEN FETCHED AGAIN AND HE HAS NEVER ONCE ASKED WHICH NUMBER IT WAS AND NOBODY HAS SAID WHY. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND IT IS TEN AND IT WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ ON ANY OF THE TEN MORNINGS, AND THE OTHER SIX COLUMNS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND HAVE HAD NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM FOR FOUR YEARS, AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE OF THEM. THE COMPOST LINE STANDS PAID AT ONE HUNDRED POUNDS WITH THE RUN AT SEVENTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN SEVENTEEN, ENTERED ON THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH, AND IT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND. THE DAY-AND-NOT-THE-FIGURE ENTRIES IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK STAND AT FOUR AND THE NEXT ONE IS NOT IN THESE TEN DAYS. THE WALL AT CAULDRON REACH CARRIES FOURTEEN MARKS AND DOES NOT CARRY FIFTEEN. THE PULSE CARRIES TWO FIGURES AND NO NAME AND WAS NOT READ A THIRD TIME. THE USE OF THE STANDING RULE ABOUT THE ENGINEER OF RECORD STANDS AT TWO IN THE SEASON BEFORE LAST AND ONE IN THE SEASON LAST, AND NOTHING WAS ADDED TO EITHER IN TEN DAYS.**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was about nine people hearing a book read out loud that is the same book they have heard read out loud before, and about four of them checking nothing, which is the hardest thing a person does in a room when a book is being read.

And the fen entered what reading a standing out loud sounds like, because it is not a thing anybody in this holding does often. A standing is not a list of work. It is a set of figures that a room has agreed to notice, and the agreeing is the whole of what it is for, and it takes about nine minutes to read and about four seconds for nine people to realise that nothing in it has moved and that the nine minutes were not for finding that out. The man who reads it does not enjoy it and does not dread it. He reads it at the same speed every time and stops at the same four places, and about four people in this holding can tell which four places they are from the sound, and about four of them have never noticed that they can.

And then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Twenty-eight fields above Marden have now waited for the same empty morning of a rota and I am going to say the reason I have never complained about it out loud, and the reason is that I have been in a room and heard a man say that a thing nobody can check is not the same as a thing nobody has checked, and a field that waits for a fourth line is a field that nobody has checked, and I would rather have that than a person on that headland who was put there to make the column look as though somebody went."**

**"And I am not going to be thanked for saying that, and the reason is that saying it out loud is the only thing I can do about it and it is not a lot, and a man in this holding who had been thanked for it would be a man I would have to watch."**

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-eight, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Not one figure in that has moved that was not supposed to move, and I am going to say the list of what did move out loud in a room instead of in a book, and the reason is that I have kept these books for four years and about a third of what I have found in them when I copy them over is a figure that moved because somebody decided it should, and this morning I have gone through the seven days twice, the sixth to this one, and the list is short enough to say out loud in about nine people."**

**"The rota has gone from fifty-five mornings in force to fifty-seven, and the fields that have waited have gone from twenty-six to twenty-eight, and twenty-nine taken has not moved on any of the seven. The count of the third launder's series has gone from sixty-six to seventy-two and the seven figures in it are all different from one another and all of them are above anything the book has ever held. The sentence about the four bodies of households has gone from sixty-four days to seventy. A key went out of this holding on Saturday and it went into a hand and not into a lock. A return is due tomorrow and the second rule on that board is going to be left blank again and that will be the tenth. And about five people in this room have said a thing out loud with the reason before it, and each of them has a number of his own and none of the five numbers is the same as any of the other four and none of the five is added to another."**

Nobody said anything and the fen entered that nobody said anything, and entered that the silence was made of a man reading out a short list in a room where nine people had expected a longer one, and that the count of things a person who keeps a book in this holding has said in a yard that the engineer of record had not thought of is twelve and did not move this afternoon because this was a room.

And then the reader of this body said the last thing of the day, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and twenty-five, and he said it in about nine people in a room, and nobody improved it.

**"There is one figure in that standing I am not going to let go by tonight, and it is the one about the standing rule, and the reason is that about four people in this room have been waiting ten days for me to use it, and the use of a standing rule is four lines about a draw and it would take about nine seconds, and I am not going to do it, and I am going to say the whole of that out loud this afternoon rather than let it sit in me until somebody asks me about it in about four years."**

For about a minute there was nothing in the room, and the fen entered what the silence was made of, which was nine people being told a thing they had already worked out, and the fen has entered that and is not going to improve it, and that the reader of this body had worked it out on the eighth of this month and had said so in about nine people and about five of them had not heard him.

And then the two last things of the twelfth of this month are his and they are in his own book and the fen entered both and improved neither.

**THE STANDING WAS READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND WAS IMPROVED IN NO PART, AND NOT ONE FIGURE IN IT HAD MOVED THAT WAS NOT SUPPOSED TO MOVE, AND THE LIST OF WHAT DID MOVE IS ON THIS PAGE AND IT IS SHORT. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ ON ANY OF THE SEVEN MORNINGS, AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE OF THE SIX OTHERS, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM IN THIS HOLDING AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD.**

**AND THE FIGURE FOR THIS MORNING IS SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX, THE SEVENTY-SECOND IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ITSELF SINCE THE FIRST, AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN HAVE NOW WAITED TWENTY-EIGHT TIMES, ALL OF THEM THE FOURTH LINE, AND NOBODY IS BLAMED FOR A FIELD THAT WAITED, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twelfth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a standing, and not by the reader of this body, who said out loud in a room that he is not going to use a standing rule and gave the reason first: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND NOBODY WAS AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, AND THE NUMBER IS TWENTY-EIGHT, AND ALL TWENTY-EIGHT OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT FIFTY-SEVEN MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND TWENTY-EIGHT NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE TAKEN DID NOT MOVE ON ANY OF THE SEVEN MORNINGS. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE SEVENTY-SECOND FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN THIRTY-NINE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN TWENTY-FIVE TIMES AND FALLEN FOURTEEN TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN SEVENTY DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE SIXTY-NINTH MORNING OUT OF SEVENTY-ONE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SIX DAYS, DAY MINUS SIXTY-SIX, AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND THREE DAYS, DAY MINUS NINETEEN, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE STANDING WAS READ OUT WHOLE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND IMPROVED IN NO PART. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. PERSONS WHO CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR STAND AT TWO AND FOR FIVE AT ONE AND IT IS THE READER OF THIS BODY. PERSONS WITH A ROOTMARK STAND AT TWO. ONE PERSON CAN HOLD A PATTERN FOR FOUR AND WALK OUT OF THE GROUND AFTERWARDS. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR AND RECONCILE NONE OF ONE ANOTHER. THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG STAND AT FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE. NUMBERS THIS HOLDING CANNOT CHECK STAND AT TWO. NOT KNOWNS STAND AT SIX AND NO UNIT WAS SUPPLIED FOR ANY. REQUESTS AND SECTION-NINE NOTES STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND FIFTY-THREE AND NO NEW NOTE WAS WRITTEN. THE BODIES AT THE NODE ARE FIVE. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY STANDS AT TWENTY FETCHINGS AND WAS NOT FETCHED AGAIN. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS AVAILABLE TO A ROOM AND NOT TAKEN IS TEN AND WAS NOT SAID IN A YARD. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FORTY-EIGHT AND WAS NOT READ. THE COMPOST RUN IS SEVENTEEN. THE DAY-AND-NOT-THE-FIGURE ENTRIES STAND AT FOUR. THE WALL AT CAULDRON REACH CARRIES FOURTEEN MARKS. THE PULSE CARRIES TWO FIGURES AND WAS NOT READ A THIRD TIME. THE USE OF THE STANDING RULE STANDS AT TWO IN THE SEASON BEFORE LAST AND ONE IN THE SEASON LAST AND NOTHING WAS ADDED TO EITHER IN TEN DAYS, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO USE IT AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING THANKED ANYBODY.**
