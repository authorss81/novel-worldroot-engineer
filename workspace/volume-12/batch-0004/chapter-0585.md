# Chapter 585

## Four People On A Bank And A Hundred And Twenty-Six Mornings In Force, And Three Sheets On A Table

The fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and went up the headland in the dark for the eleventh time this month, and the wind had gone back round to the north and there was ice on the stones along the top of the bank before four people had found half of them.

Two hundred yards of boundary stone were marked with chalk and set by hand, the ditch was cut along the eleven acres, the loose stone was barrowed off the top into the heap by the gate, and the pack under the near stone came out frozen and went back wet by seven.

No field was taken, and the standing is a hundred and twenty-six mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and ninety-seven not, and twenty-nine and ninety-seven is a hundred and twenty-six.

Both reckonings of the rotation went on the board this morning and neither one of them came out where the other did. It is the seventy-fourth morning of the cycle one way and the sixty-first the other, the thirteen in that gap has been there four years, and nobody in this building has ever asked what it is for.

**"The count in force is one higher than it was on Friday and the line came round and went up a bank in the dark and there is still nothing at all in the taking column, and twenty-nine has not moved on any morning of this year and nobody in this holding is to blame for a field that has been waiting four years."**

The clerk said that to the board at about the seventh hour and then said one more sentence that he has not said on any of the other mornings of the rotation, and he said it to the board and not to the yard.

**"This is the last time this cycle comes round inside this half of a month, and I am not going to mark the board in any way, and I am not going to write a figure in the margin of the page where the blanks are counted, and the next time that page is opened it will be because a load came up a road and not because I wanted to close a month."**

He went back to the second book and read the launder figure back once at the ninth hour.

**One thousand and one hundred and thirty-six hundredweight.**

Saturday put eight onto Friday, and the three hundred and forty-eighth figure of a run in which nothing has ever been entered twice is what stands against the launder this morning.

The two halves at the long wall were a hundred and sixty-three mornings one way and a hundred and fifty-two the other, and the two of them came to three hundred and fifteen, and neither of the two men said the total out loud this morning and it is on the board in chalk from about six o'clock.

There is no figure against the run of mornings in the second book this morning, and the reason is on this page and not in the book. Half of that run is half a number on a Saturday, and the heading of a month nine months back is standing over the empty space, and it has not moved since the first of this month and it is not going to move before the month turns again.

There are three sheets on the long table and they have been on it for a day, a month and about two days, and there is about nine inches of bare board between each of them and the next, and the clerk of this holding walked the length of that table at about the eighth hour and looked at all three of them and ruled nothing under any of them.

**"Three sheets in ten days and not one column, and that is not an oversight and it is not a decision either, because a decision would have a reason attached to it and I have not got a reason for any of the three of them."**

He put his hand flat on the bare board and not on any of the papers.

**"A request is a thing somebody can be held to and this is not a request. A decision is a thing somebody can be asked why and none of these three can be asked why from this table by anybody in this holding. What they are is three pieces of paper about a district, and this holding has three pieces of paper about a district and nothing else to do with them."**

Nia Vale came in at about the seventh hour from above the low field, came down to the long table, read all three sheets standing up without touching any of them, and then said one thing and went out again.

**"There is a line on the third of those about the districts beyond the maintenance zone, and I have been in nine places this month and I know what is on the far side of that line and what is not on it, and I am not going to say any of that in a yard at eight in the morning."**

She squared the third sheet up with the edge of the table and left it there.

**"I am going to say the one thing I have got. There are nine places above one road in this county, and four of them have been done differently from the other five this month, on purpose."**

She looked at the gate and not at the table.

**"The doing of it differently is the only thing any of us has got that a schedule cannot take off us, and I am not going to be the woman who says that out loud to a body four counties off in a room."**

She went out through the first gate and the man of about fifty was at the gate and did not say anything to her about it.

Marek had the bank and all three sheets through the gap in the open door of the one room from the fifth hour, and came out into the yard at about the tenth hour and stood in the ice with his sleeve down over his forearm.

**"Three sheets are on a table in this yard and nobody in this holding has been asked to choose anything this month, and I want the second of those two facts to be the harder one in four years rather than the easier one."**

He put the reason in front of the thing and left his own arithmetic where he kept it.

**"There is a person at the head of the third of them and she is not in this county and she is not going to be in this yard, and every single thing I have done for four months has been aimed at getting somebody to put a figure next to her arithmetic so that the arithmetic would be seen to be wrong in a room with witnesses in it."**

He turned the sleeve back over his forearm and stood there a while.

**"That is not going to happen in this holding and it is not going to happen in this county in a month."**

Four people came down off the bank behind him and went out through the first gate without lighting a lamp.

**"The ground on that low field is going to keep doing what it is doing nine different ways while somebody four counties off works out a number that fits all of it, and there is no instrument in four counties that can tell her she is wrong before she has to stand in front of the people she is going to abandon."**

Three hundred and forty-six days is what the sheet in this yard carries against the four bodies of households, and its clause is one under and one over, taking three hundred and forty-fifth of those mornings out of three hundred and forty-seventh.

Four people on a bank in the dark and a hundred and twenty-six mornings in force. On the long wall the near board reads seven hundred and thirty-two days and the far board reads seven hundred and seventy-nine days, forty-seven between them.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden looked at both of them and then at the three sheets on the long table and said one word about none of the four things she had just looked at, and the drawer behind the near board stood shut at every hour.

The first sheet on the long table is forty-six days old and has not been answered and has not been refused and has not been copied, and the second came on the twenty-eighth and has not been opened, and the third came on Thursday afternoon and is correct in every particular and has not been called a lie by anybody in this holding.

There is a form on the middle table two hundred and forty-two days old with a column at the head of it carrying no heading and nothing in the column. A charter behind it is a hundred and forty-eight days through a door nine miles off. The second of the four ruled lines in Silling's own book is two hundred and thirty-six days old.

Four hundred yards of boundary stone went up a bank in the dark this morning and not one of those three pieces of paper moved a figure.

Twenty-eight is on the compost line with paid in front of it, and it is not discharged in twenty-eight. The rule in the board in the one room stands empty, thirty-nine blanks are counted on that board, and nobody in this holding has written anything in the margin of the page where they are counted.

Six rows are ruled in this building for what this holding does not know about and no unit has ever gone against any of them, and three sheets of paper about a district are not a seventh, and the seventh row is not ruled.

This holding's book shows seven sessions entered and nothing this morning made an eighth. Fifty-three requests, fifty-three section-nine notes, and not one of the three sheets on the long table went into either of them.

Fifteen lines are in the use log and the sixteenth is not one of them. The barrow is against a wall where a man put it a fortnight ago, its journeys stand at eleven, and eleven is a floor.

Nobody has walked the nine hundred yards to that door this month and the seventh of its seven columns is at sixty-six, where it stays. The old man of about seventy is at twenty-nine fetchings and this morning was not one, and the count of questions this holding has put to him is nil at every one of its sites, and it is going to be nil at the next one as well.

Chalk on two hundred yards of boundary stone along the top of a bank in the dark, and three sheets on a long table with nine inches of board between each of them, and nobody thanked anybody.
