## 10. WHAT THE BATCH BEHIND THIS ONE LEFT ON DISK, IN ONE PARAGRAPH, SO THAT IT IS NOT REPEATED

**The third launder crossed one thousand and one hundred on its second morning and no one in that yard treated it as an event. A woman put a book in her pocket and then left her own column deliberately short and said out loud that the empty space was not an oversight. A stone came out of its bed at the outlet end and two people put it back without arranging it. A gate pin moved half an inch in the winter and the gate will not shut, and two people held it for half an hour without speaking. A trough at the head of the four-mile road is down half a foot and nobody owns it. The seventh column of a door nine hundred yards off was read at sixty-five and then at sixty-six, both on the rule, and nobody asked what it counts besides readings. A man of about seventy was fetched for the twenty-ninth time on the morning the month turned and said *the gate is open* and was not asked which number he was. A man with a barrow asked a clerk for a spanner out loud in a yard and was given one and neither of them said the word. The use log stands at fifteen lines and the sixteenth was not written. The barrow is at eleven journeys and eleven is a floor. The letter is twenty-nine days old on a table with about nine inches of bare board on either side of it and no column anywhere in four books for it.** **NONE OF THAT MAY BE SAID AGAIN IN THESE NINETEEN MORNINGS AS THOUGH IT WERE NEW, AND NONE OF IT IS A FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT MAY BE REPEATED AS A FIGURE, BECAUSE EVERY FIGURE OF EVERY SERIES IS RE-DERIVED AT SECTION 11 AND NOT CARRIED.**
