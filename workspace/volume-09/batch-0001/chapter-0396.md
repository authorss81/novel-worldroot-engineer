# Chapter 396

## A Motion That Carries On A True Sentence

The ninth of this month was a Saturday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The two places where the low ground lets water off, between the sluice and the north gate, took four people until about the fourth hour, and the second of the two was empty and the chain stayed on the grate.

The man at the front of it said that a thing which has been empty four mornings running is a thing the four of them will go and look at in about four years, and the three behind him did not answer him. He has said a version of that sentence on and off for two months and the three behind him have never taken it up, and it is not a complaint about the three of them and he has never once said it was.

At about the seventh hour the two day-minus rules were read out loud before either of the numbers, one man taking the first and the other taking the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both of them. They stand at **five hundred and forty-three days** and **five hundred and ninety**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened. Fifty mornings out of eighty-three since the seventeenth of a month three months back, and it is a habit and not a measurement, and the second count of it has been kept since a morning in a month three months back and the frame on that one moves when the month turns and the change is said once.

The ninth was entered at about the eighth hour and came back out of the book with the frayed corner, in a voice that makes no distinction between a rise and a fall, and the two figures were the same.

**Eight hundred and twenty-three hundredweight.**

Down five off Friday, and it is the hundred and fifty-ninth figure of a series which has never once repeated itself since the first morning of it. The window the rises and the falls are counted in stands at a hundred and twenty-six, and the fall went onto the falls, which puts it at sixty-eight rises and fifty-eight falls, and the two add.

A hundred and fifty-seven days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the hundred and fifty-sixth of those mornings out of a hundred and fifty-eight. Four bodies of households. Not the launders. Nobody added the two today and nobody has added them in four years and the day sheet shows it.

A rider came up the fen road at about the eleventh hour with a folded sheet and a wet saddle and did not dismount at the gate, and he had come nine miles and he said so before anybody asked him, and nobody did ask him, and he said it because a man who has come nine miles in the wet and is not asked has to say it himself or go home having said nothing.

The sheet was from a body calling itself the first regional council of three counties, and it had met at a house with a barn behind it, and eleven people had been in the room and four of them were of the four bodies of households and none of those four was a member of the cooperative.

> THE FIRST MOTION OF THE FIRST REGIONAL COUNCIL OF THIS DISTRICT, CARRIED ON THE NINTH OF THIS MONTH BY A COUNT OF HANDS TAKEN ONCE AND NOT READ BACK. THAT THE district IS DISCONNECTED FROM THE SCHEDULE OF THE MAINTENANCE OF THE CROWN ENGINE AT THE NEXT TAKING OF ANY BRANCH AT ANY PLACE WITHIN IT, AND THAT NO OFFICE, PERSON OR BODY IS ASKED TO PERFORM ANY ACT IN CONNECTION WITH THE DISCONNECTION, THE COUNCIL HAVING NO POWER TO ASK ONE. THAT THE COUNCIL HAS READ THE SHEET OF THREE PARTS DATED THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH AND HAS NOT FOUND A FALSE STATEMENT IN IT, AND RECORDS THAT THE SAID SHEET STATES THAT THE ENGINE MEANS TO TAKE OFF THE BRANCH AT NINETEEN NAMED PLACES, EIGHT OF THEM WITH A DAY AND AN HOUR, AND THAT THE SAID SHEET ALSO STATES THAT A MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE DOES NOT TAKE A BRANCH OFF ANYTHING. THAT THE COUNCIL HAS ASKED IN WRITING FOR THE COST OF HOLDING A NODE BETWEEN THE NINTH OF THIS MONTH AND THE SAID TAKING, AND HAS BEEN TOLD THAT NO SUCH FIGURE IS KEPT BY THE BODY IT ASKED, AND RECORDS THAT IT WAS TOLD SO IN A REPLY THAT NAMED NO PERSON. THAT THE COUNCIL IS AWARE THAT A NODE HAS A HEARTHROOT AND THAT HOLDING ONE IS NOT THE SAME AS HOLDING A BRANCH, AND THE COUNCIL RESOLVES TO PROCEED ON THE ADVICE IT HAS BEEN GIVEN AND TO ASK AGAIN.

Nobody in this building has asked anybody to perform any act. Nobody in this building sent anybody to that room and nobody in this building was told it was going to be held.

There were nine people in the one room at this holding from about the second hour of the afternoon with the door shut. The sheet went on the long table at the wall side and stayed where the rider put it, and the man of about fifty did not put it beside the other three papers, and nobody asked him to.

Marek read it twice and then gave the reason before he said what he thought of it, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and seventy-seven, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the sentence that is at the top of that sheet out loud in this room, and I am going to give the reason before I say it, and the reason is that if I do not say it somebody else will say it this evening and I would rather this building heard it from me with the reason attached."

"The engine means to take off the branch at nineteen named places. That sentence is true. It is in the office's own book and it came out of a wood in the order a wood keeps things in, and nobody in this room has ever said that office was wrong about it and nobody in that room was right to say it was wrong, because it is not wrong."

"And the second sentence on that sheet is also true. A maintenance schedule does not take a branch off anything. Both of those are in the same hand and they are on the same page, and about four people in this room have been carrying one of them for four years and about four have been carrying the other, and the reason it carried this morning is not that anybody lied."

Tova Reed had not been in the room when the rider came and had come in at about the third hour, and she said one thing and gave the reason before she said it, and the count of that in the holding's history is twenty-seven.

"I am going to say the thing I am not going to say, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that a room this afternoon is going to spend its whole time on whether that office lied, and it did not, and if we spend it there we will have nothing left for the sentence that actually did the work."

"The sheet says a node has a hearthroot and that holding one is not the same as holding a branch. That sentence is in that office's book and it is true and it is the sentence a council can be frightened by, because it is the only one of the three on that page that anybody has to do something about, and I can cost it in seed, and I am not going to cost it in this room this afternoon because the room has not asked me for a figure and a figure I give unasked is a figure somebody will quote back at me in a fortnight."

Nobody argued with her about the figure. The woman who keeps the seed house said there was bread, and the man of about seventy-four with a stick stood in the door for the whole of it and was not asked in and was not asked out, and about half of the afternoon was spent by two men not saying anything to each other across a table.

Tova Reed did not leave and did not stay in the middle of the room, and she worked the room the way she has always worked a room with one side of her hearing gone. She did not turn her head when the man of about fifty spoke on her right at about the fourth hour, and she got the whole of what he said out of the shape of his mouth at ten feet, and about four people noticed and about four did not, and none of the four who noticed said anything about it afterward and none of the four is going to.

She said the reason for that, and gave the reason first, and the count of that in the holding's history is twenty-eight, and it is hers and of nobody else's, and nobody thanked her for it and nobody was going to.

"I am going to say something about the way I am sitting in this room and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that four people have been careful with me all afternoon and about four of them have been careful by standing on my left, and both of those are the same kindness and only one of them is any use to anybody."

"Standing on my left is the correct way to be kind to a person with one ear and it is useless in a room where a vote is going to be taken, and I am not going to spend the rest of a season being spoken to correctly on the side I cannot hear. I have been doing this for about a month and I am good at it and I am not going to explain how, and a person who asks me how has decided that the thing I lost is the interesting part of me, and it is not."

Nobody asked her how. About four people in that room had wanted to and about four had not wanted to and had said nothing, and the man of about fifty said afterwards that this holding has never once been told how she does it and is not going to be told this week either.

Tova Reed was the person every one of those four bodies of households sends its question to, and she has been for about eleven years, and it is not because she is anybody's and it never has been. She keeps four accounts of her own and none of them is in this holding's book, and the four of them are the only figures in four counties that say what a season costs in seed, and about four people in this room have known that since before anybody in it had heard of a council.

The man of about fifty asked one question and it was whether anybody had answered the council's request for a cost, and Marek said no, and the man of about fifty said that the sheet says he was told in a reply that named no person, and Marek said that was correct and that this holding has a habit of replying to a body of households without signing, and that the reason is four years old and it is a bad reason and it is the one there is.

The clerk of the council had written the word district four times in four paragraphs and had not written the name of the county once, and the man of about fifty noticed that at about the fifth hour and said it out loud, and Marek told him to leave it out of the book, and he left it out and told two people in the yard afterward.

"There is a reason a body writes *the district* instead of a name," Marek said, "and the reason is that a name is somebody's and *the district* is nobody's, and a clerk who has to be answerable for a page in a fortnight will find that out on his own. I am not going to be the man who teaches it to him first, because the first man to point it out is the man who has made it a thing, and then it is a habit in four counties inside a year."

Tova Reed said that the reason was also that four bodies of households have been called *the district* in a book in this building for four years without anybody noticing, and nobody thanked her for that either.

The rider was fed before he went and did not know he was being fed, and he ate standing up in about four minutes and said that he had a house with a barn behind it to get back to, and the woman who keeps the seed house said that was not a reason and gave him a bag, and he took it, and the count of things said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong did not move, because a door with a man eating at it is not a yard and a bag of bread is not a speech.

Nobody thanked anybody. A rider who came nine miles in the wet and said so before anybody asked him was not thanked and did not expect to be, and a sheet from a body of eleven people was read twice in a room of nine and neither reading was improved.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the four-mile road at about the ninth hour with the barrow and turned it at the top of the yard and went home, and nobody in this holding told him anything about the sheet and nobody was going to. The second of the four lines in his own book is forty-seven days old and has no answer on it, and the barrow stands at eleven journeys and eleven is a floor.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular, and the order sealing the route is in a drawer beside it and is valid and opens nothing, and the route is not reopened by anything in this holding's possession this afternoon. The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page.

The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of that room, face up, and it is not filled. It was refused on the nineteenth of last month and it has not been withdrawn, and it has no date on it and no name on it and no line on it for a name, and nobody in this building has been told to take it off that board. The register form is on the middle table where it has stood fifty-three days, not filled and not refused, and the sheet that came up the road at the eleventh hour was not put beside either of them and nobody asked anybody to.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-four and is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning, and the man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-three, and the compost line is paid at twenty not discharged in twenty. The use log stands at fifteen lines and a motion carried nine miles off is not a use, and the requests stand at fifty-three and the section-nine notes stand at fifty-three, and a request that names no person is not a fifty-fourth of anything.

What this holding cannot check is two figures and what it does not know is six things, and a council asking what a node costs to hold is not a seventh of either and is not a third of the other. The mark on the arm is four inches and branches twice, and a motion is not a mark, a nine-mile ride is not a mark, and a reason given before a thing is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled.

There are ten questions available to a room in this holding and not taken, and it is ten, and it is not said in a yard, and a room of nine with the door shut on a Saturday afternoon is not a yard and is not a place where a count of that kind is kept.
