# Chapter 399

## Three Hundred And Thirty-Eight

The twelfth of this month was a Tuesday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The two gates and the about six feet of wall between them took four people an hour and a quarter and the four of them were still on that six feet of wall at the seventh hour, and the toe at the second of the three places on that line is up a finger's width off what it was on the third of last month.

The second line ran past the seventh hour and Marek was one of the four on it and had both hands full, and the two day-minus rules were not read out loud this morning and this page carries no figure for the run, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. The pair stands at **five hundred and forty-six days** and **five hundred and ninety-three**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The twelfth did not go in until after the eighth hour, the four men still being on the wall, and the clerk read it back out of the other book and the two figures were the same.

**Eight hundred and thirty-six hundredweight.**

Nine above Monday, being the hundred and sixty-second figure of a series that has never put the same figure down twice since the first morning of it. The window the rises and the falls are counted in stands at a hundred and twenty-nine, and the rise went onto the rises, which puts it at seventy rises and fifty-nine falls, and seventy and fifty-nine are a hundred and twenty-nine.

The four bodies of households have gone a hundred and sixty days without a move in their aggregate, and the sentence that says so went onto the sheet this morning for the hundred and fifty-ninth of those mornings out of a hundred and sixty-one. The four launders are a different aggregate and the two have not been added in four years, and nobody put them on one line this afternoon.

The return came off the steelyard at about the second hour of the afternoon, in the building at the node that has no roof and has not had one for as long as the oldest man in this holding can remember. The four bodies of households that weigh it are at Havergate and Low Wether and Marden and Silling, and not one of them is a member of the cooperative and none of them was made one this afternoon.

**Three hundred and thirty-eight hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-sixth of them. It is the twenty-sixth pair of names to go against a ninth-day return in eleven years, and it stands eleven hundredweight above the thirty-fifth, which was three hundred and twenty-seven on the fourth of last month. He took three hundred and thirty-eight off the dial, said the figure out loud, and then said it again out of the second book, and the two were the same.

It was reconciled twice, out of the two figures the man of about fifty had put on that day sheet at the fifth hour of the morning, and the two ways it comes are two halves and not the whole.

**Two hundred and eighty-eight plus fifty. And two hundred and forty-one plus ninety-seven.**

The two halves have not been added to each other this afternoon and are not going to be added to each other by anybody. The word using is on the page of this holding's book and it is on the page this morning. This holding is using that derivation and is not refusing it, and the reason that is said out loud rather than left is that a man who refuses a derivation is a man who thinks the number is in doubt, and the number is on a dial that has been on that wall longer than this holding has had a book, and it said what it said this afternoon with nobody standing near it.

The board the names go on is a board and not a page of a book, and it is the same board it was on eleven years ago, and the two rules on it are painted in the same hand and the second of them has never once been filled, and nobody in this building has ever said aloud what would go in it if it were.

The second rule on that board was left empty again this afternoon, and it has been left empty twenty times in eleven years. Nothing went into it and nothing was said about it by any of the four or by anybody standing near the dial. It stood at nineteen on the fourth of last month, which was eight mornings back, and a count of blanks is not the count of a man and has never been the count of a man.

A name went into BY, and then one into WITNESSED BY, and then a name into BY again and a name into WITNESSED BY again, and the order of the four of them was the order they came up to the dial. Nobody has ever been able to say that any of them hurried, and about four people in this holding have wondered in four years whether a man who has been told that his figures are correct hurries, and about four have not wondered about it.

There were five bodies at the node this afternoon and five is the number on the page, and the number is not given in a yard and this was not a yard, and a count of five is a fact about a rota and not a count of an afternoon.

The man of about fifty said why he had written two on the day sheet at the fifth hour and had told nobody, and the reason came before the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirteen.

**"I am going to say why I wrote two ways of coming at that number on this morning's sheet and said nothing about either of them until this afternoon, and I am going to give the reason before I say it, and the reason is that a figure which can only be arrived at one way is a figure somebody chose the way of, and I would rather the two ways were on the sheet than that anybody in about four years had to trust the one.**"

**"And I am not going to tell this room which of the two I believe, and there is no believing to do. That dial was on that wall before this holding had a book and it said what it said this afternoon with nobody standing near it. The two of these are arithmetic and the arithmetic is not the finding, and a man who stands in front of a dial and makes a sum sound like a reason is a man who has started selling."**

Nobody said a word about it, and the four of them came down the road and about half of them were talking about the weather, and the man of about fifty carried the day sheet in his coat pocket and did not take it out again until he was in the one room at this building.

About nine people were in the one room at this holding from about the seventh hour of the evening with the door shut, and the second council had been formed at the same hour at a house with a barn behind it, in a second county, nine miles off, and the sheet had come up the road behind a rider who had not been asked to bring it and had not been paid for the trouble of it.

Marek read it and put it on the long table at the wall side and said one thing about it, and the reason came first, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and eighty-one.

"I am going to say one thing about that sheet and give the reason first, and the reason is that there are now two bodies in four counties with a sheet between them and neither of them has asked this holding a question, and I would like the room to notice that that is a different kind of week from last week and not a better one."

"They have not asked us anything. Last Saturday they asked us what a node costs to hold and we had to say we had never costed one, and that was an honest afternoon. This week they have not asked, and what they have done instead is decide among themselves, and a thing decided among yourselves is not a worse thing than a thing decided by an office, and I am not going to stand in this room and say it is."

The man of about fifty said that the sheet asked for one thing, which was a copy of the list, and Marek said that the clerk from the third county had been told on Monday morning, at this door, by both of them, that there is no list, and the man of about fifty said that a county which is thirty miles off did not hear that, and Marek said it did not.

Nobody argued about it after that. About four people in that room had already worked out that the answer to a body which has not asked is a body which will ask, and about four had worked out that the reason it will ask is that the third county has now told two counties that there is no list, and that is how a thing becomes a question without anybody deciding it should be one.

The man of about fifty said the thing that the room had been coming to since the second hour of the afternoon, and he said it without a reason first, which he almost never does, and nobody supplied one.

"Nine days," he said. "That page went out of this building on the fourth of a month now one month back, and it has been in four counties for nine days, and in that time three bodies have formed or refused to form and two councils have met and one gate has been spoken about, and not one of those five things was in it. It was a page about a schedule. What happened was somebody read the part of it that says what a thing will not do."

He was not thanked for that and nobody disagreed with him, and Marek said that it was the correctest thing anybody had said in that room in four years, and the man of about fifty said that he had not meant it to be anything, and Marek said that he had not supposed he had, and the two of them have been in this building long enough for that to be a real sentence.

The four of them came down the road from the node and about half of them were talking about the weather, and the man of about fifty carried the day sheet with the two figures on it in his coat pocket and did not take it out again until he was in the one room at this building, and about four people in this holding have wondered in four years whether he ever says which of the two he believes and about four have not wondered about it, and he has never been asked and is not going to be.

The offer sheet has not moved off the low board at the far end of that room, face up, unfilled, refused on the nineteenth of last month and not withdrawn since, with no date on it and no name on it and no line on it for a name. The register form is on the middle table where it has stood fifty-six days, not filled and not refused, and nobody wrote a heading on it, and the sheet with the two halves on it went into the day-sheet stack in the order it was used.

Nobody thanked anybody, and the rider who brought the second council's sheet up the road behind the ordinary post was not paid for the trouble of it and was not thanked for it, and the sheet went on the long table at the wall side where the other two were and was not put beside either of them.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling had been in the room at the node and not in the room at this building, and the second of the four lines in his own book is fifty days old and has no answer on it, and the barrow stands at eleven journeys and eleven is a floor.

A bridge was run once for about nine minutes five weeks ago and no person in this holding has an instrument that says whether it is on or off, and the man of about fifty wrote nothing about it this evening. The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured.

The seventh column of the well house door is read on the thirtieth of a month and on the first of the next and nowhere else, and it stands at fifty-four and is not read this morning. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count stays at twenty-three. The compost line is paid at twenty not discharged in twenty.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and a return is not a use, a dial is not a use, and a rule painted on a board and never filled is not a use. The requests stand at fifty-three and the section-nine notes stand at fifty-three, and the two have never been added.

What this holding cannot check is two figures and what it does not know is six things, and a return off a dial is a figure this holding can check and is not one of the two. The mark on the arm is four inches and branches twice, and a return is not a mark, a name is not a mark, and a blank rule is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twelfth, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE TWO GATES AND SIX FEET OF WALL, FOUR PEOPLE TO PAST THE SEVENTH HOUR, AND THE FIGURE WAS NOT ENTERED UNTIL AFTER THE EIGHTH. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED EIGHT HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SECOND FIGURE, AND IT HAS RISEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE, BEING SEVENTY RISES AND FIFTY-NINE FALLS, AND THE TWO ADD. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-ONE, AND IT IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS WERE NOT READ ALOUD THIS MORNING AND NO FIGURE FOR THE RUN GOES ON THE PAGE, THE SECOND LINE HAVING RUN PAST THE SEVENTH HOUR. THE PAIR STANDS AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIX DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND NINETY-THREE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE THIRTY-SIXTH NINTH-DAY RETURN CAME OFF THE STEELYARD AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AT THREE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE TWENTY-SIXTH PAIR OF NAMES IN ELEVEN YEARS, ELEVEN ABOVE THE THIRTY-FIFTH, RECONCILED TWICE OUT OF TWO FIGURES ON A DAY SHEET, BEING TWO HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-EIGHT PLUS FIFTY AND TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY-ONE PLUS NINETY-SEVEN, AND THE TWO HALVES WERE NOT ADDED. THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD WAS LEFT BLANK FOR THE TWENTIETH TIME IN ELEVEN YEARS, AND A COUNT OF BLANKS IS NOT THE COUNT OF A MAN. THE HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT, AND THAT IS ENTERED AS WHAT WAS SAID. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT TWO BODIES NOW HAVE A SHEET BETWEEN THEM AND NEITHER HAS ASKED THIS HOLDING A QUESTION. THE MAN WHO KEEPS THE BOOK GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID HE WAS NOT GOING TO TELL THE ROOM WHICH OF THE TWO HE BELIEVED, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THE HOLDING'S HISTORY IS ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
