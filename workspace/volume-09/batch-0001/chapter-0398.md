# Chapter 398

## Six Days, And What Came Out Of One

The eleventh of this month was a Monday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The channel along the inside face of the west dyke has been finished on both days of it for a fortnight and the six feet at the top end of it is still soft, and four of the six put their heels in it on the way past and the prints closed over by themselves inside a count of three, and they went and did the boundary ditch instead.

The window of the office's work shut this morning. It opened on the sixth, as it opens on the sixth of every month, and it is six days and not five and not seven, and it shuts on the eleventh whether anybody has used it or not, and it is not extended in a season, and no window has ever been extended in a season in the four years this holding has kept a book, and no work was ever carried out of one.

Nothing was put in it. Nothing was taken out of it. A thing that is neither put into a window nor taken out of one is the whole of this holding's business with a window, and it is not a complaint and it is not a figure.

At about the seventh hour the two day-minus rules were read out loud before either of the numbers, one man taking the first and the other taking the second. They stand at **five hundred and forty-five days** and **five hundred and ninety-two**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened. Fifty-one mornings out of eighty-five since the seventeenth of a month three months back, and it is a habit and not a measurement.

The eleventh was entered on the Monday at about the eighth hour and read back out of the other book, and the two were the same.

**Eight hundred and twenty-seven hundredweight.**

Five under Sunday. The hundred and sixty-first figure of a series in which no figure has ever appeared twice, and the man of about fifty keeps the whole of it in a book with nothing else in it. The window the rises and the falls are counted in stands at a hundred and twenty-eight, and the fall went onto the falls, which puts it at sixty-nine rises and fifty-nine falls, and the two add.

A hundred and fifty-nine days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the hundred and fifty-eighth of those mornings out of a hundred and sixty. It is about four bodies of households and the four launders are a different figure, and the two aggregates have never shared a line of a page in four years and did not share one this morning.

The third county's clerk came up the fen road at about the tenth hour on his own feet and stood at the door for about a quarter of an hour before he knocked, and he had a sheet of his own in his hand and it was folded in three and the creases were not worn soft, because he had not carried it in a coat, he had written it that morning.

He said his county was not forming a council. He said it in about four sentences and he said it the way a man says a thing he has had to say in three rooms already, and then he asked for a list.

"What we want," he said, "is a list of the work. Not your figures. The work. What has to be done to a node between now and the next taking, in the order it has to be done, and who is answerable for each of those things. We are not asking you for a number. We are asking whether there is a piece of paper in the world with that on it, and if there is, we would like a copy of it."

The man of about fifty looked at Marek, and Marek gave the reason before he answered, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and seventy-nine, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to answer that clerk in full and I am going to give the reason before I answer it, and the reason is that he has come ten miles on his feet to ask a question and he is going to get the whole of the answer or none of it, and a half answer to a man who has walked is a thing I have seen this building do once and it cost somebody a winter."

"There is no list. Not in these four books, not in a drawer in this building, not in an office in a town, and not in a book in an office in a town. There is not one because in four years nobody has ever written a heading at the top of a column to say what that column is for, and a column with no heading is a column nobody can ask a question about, and this morning is the first morning anybody outside this building has asked one."

He gave the reason and then he said it again, because the man of about fifty had asked for the whole of it, which is what the man of about fifty does, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and twelve, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the same thing a second time for the man who keeps the book, and the reason is that he has to write this down tonight and I would rather he wrote down what I said than what he thinks I meant. It is not a refusal. It is that the answer is a fact about this holding and not about your county, and if I sent you a list it would be a list of things this building has not done in four years, which is a different thing from a list of things a node needs."

The clerk wrote on the back of his own sheet while Marek was talking, which nobody had expected, and he read back what he had written before he folded it and asked whether that was right, and Marek said it was right and that he was not going to improve it.

"And I want to say one more thing before I go, and I am not asking you to agree with it." The clerk did not unfold the sheet again. "I have been three days asking four counties a question and I have had two answers that were kind and one answer that was exact, and I would like to say that the exact one is the one I will use, and I would like to say why, and the reason is that a kind answer takes a man four days to decide whether it is a yes."

Nobody in that room agreed with him and nobody disagreed with him, and the man of about fifty said afterwards that a clerk who has said a thing like that out loud in a room is a man who has put himself in a place he cannot get out of, and that about four people in this holding have done that and about four have not.

The clerk said thank you and said it to both of them and not to the room, and that is the only thanks anybody in this building has been given in four years, and the man of about fifty wrote it down as being said and not as being owed.

After the door had shut he said one thing, and he gave the reason for saying it before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and eighty.

"I am going to say the thing I have been not saying since Monday, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that this holding has told the truth to a man who walked ten miles and has now got a page with four lines on it, and there are about four people in this room who have understood all week what those four lines will do in a county."

"A county does not read an answer. A county reads a shape. Four lines about a list that does not exist reads as a man who does not do the work, and I am not going to stand in this room and say the four lines were wrong, because they were true and they were the whole of what we have, and a holding that would rather be understood than correct is a holding that has decided it is an office and not a holding."

The man of about fifty said that the sentence was worth more than the clerk's, and Marek said it was not, and the man of about fifty said he had not said it was, and about four people in that room heard the exchange and about four were already out of the door.

The clerk had folded his sheet into three again before he got to the door, which meant he had read his own answer on the way down the road and thought it was worth carrying home in that shape, and Marek said afterward that this was the only reaction to the whole morning that he had been hoping for and that it was not one he would have admitted to hoping for.

The man of about fifty said one thing about the four lines and gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and thirteen, and nobody thanked him for it.

"They are true and they are going to be read as a no," he said, "and the reason I am putting both of those on the same line of the book tonight is that in about four years somebody is going to find this morning in here and only want to know which of the two it was, and if they are on different lines he will be able to have it both ways and he will have it wrong."

Nobody in that room improved it, and the man of about fifty entered both of them on one line and did not number them, because a man who numbers a contradiction has decided which half of it he means.

The clerk left at about the eleventh hour and walked back, and the man of about fifty stood in the door until he was out of sight, and Marek came up beside him and neither of them said anything, and about four people in that room watched the two of them stand there and about four went out of the room, and both of those have been true every time two men in this building have watched a road together in four years.

The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of that room, face up, and it is not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month and it has not been withdrawn, and it has no date on it and no name on it and no line on it for a name. The register form is on the middle table where it has stood fifty-five days, not filled and not refused, and a list a clerk came ten miles for is not a heading and is not going to become one on the back of a form.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and a request for a list is a request and not a use, and the requests stand at fifty-three and the section-nine notes stand at fifty-three, and a thing asked for at a door by a man who came on his feet is entered as what was said and not as a fifty-fourth of anything. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-four and is not read this morning, and the compost line is paid at twenty not discharged in twenty.

Nobody thanked anybody else, and the one thank you in this building in four years was said at a door to two men and not to a room, and it went into the second book as being said and not as being owed.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the four-mile road at about the ninth hour and turned the barrow and went home and was not asked anything. The barrow stands at eleven journeys and eleven is a floor, and a barrow's journeys and a man's feet are two figures and are not added.

A bridge was run once for about nine minutes five weeks ago and no person in this holding has an instrument that says whether it is on or off, and the man of about fifty did not say it this morning. The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular, and the ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page.

Four inches and two branches on the inside of the left forearm, and the same four inches at the second hour of this morning as at any hour of it. A list is not a mark, a window is not a mark, and a man who walked ten miles to a door is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled and nothing said in that room this afternoon is a fifth of anything.
