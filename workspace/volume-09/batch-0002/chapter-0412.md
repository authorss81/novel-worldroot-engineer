# Chapter 412

## One Of The Ten

The twenty-fifth of this month was a Monday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The channel along the inside face of the west dyke, the lower nine feet, took four people from the second hour to the sixth hour, and the chalk and grit over the hole where the drain's own root was has held through a fortnight of rain and about four people have walked over it and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has said anything about it.

Marek was one of the four and had the bar and then the shovel, and both hands were full from the second hour to the sixth. At about the seventh hour the two day-minus rules were read out loud before either of the numbers, one man taking the first board and another taking the second. They stand at **five hundred and fifty-nine days** and **six hundred and six**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

Fifty-eight mornings out of ninety-nine, counted from the seventeenth of a month three months back.

The twenty-fifth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and that has been the practice of this holding for four years and is not going to change inside a month.

**Eight hundred and fifty-five hundredweight.**

Down five off Sunday, and the hundred and seventy-fifth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The fall went onto the falls and the window stands at a hundred and forty-two, being seventy-six rises and sixty-six falls, and that is the last morning of this month and the number does not turn with the month.

The sentence this morning says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and seventy-three days, and it was written for the hundred and seventy-second of those mornings out of a hundred and seventy-four. Four bodies of households and not the launders, and the two have not been added in this building in four years and were not added this morning.

He came down the four-mile road at the ninth hour with the barrow, turned it at the top of the yard and went home, and the road was going hard in about nine inches of its length.

The second of the four lines in the book he carries is sixty-three days old this morning and has no answer on it and no place for one, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in sixty-three days. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that four-mile road, and eleven is a floor, and it did not rise this morning.

About nine people were in the one room at this holding from about the second hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and the man of about seventy-four with a stick was in the door for the whole of it as he is on most Mondays, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in the room and had come in from the yard without her coat on.

There are ten questions available to a room in this holding and not taken, and it is ten this afternoon as it was on the first of the month, and it is not said in a yard, and this is a room with the door shut and about nine people in it.

Nobody in this building has ever written down what the ten are.

The man of about fifty has said twice in four years that they are not in any of the four books, and that a list of ten would be a list, and a list is what this holding has a problem with. He has said that the ten are ten things a person in a room can see about half a minute before anybody else in the room sees them, and can say the sentence out loud before the sentence is finished being thought.

He said on Monday that he had nine left and could not tell the room which nine, and about four people in that room believed him and about four thought he could and had decided not to, and neither of those two fours is going to be asked.

Nobody in this holding has ever asked him to write them down and nobody is going to.

The woman who keeps the seed house said one at about the fourth hour and did not wait to be asked for it, and the man of about fifty put his pen down, which he does, and about four people in that room had thought of it in the last fortnight and about four had not.

"What I want to know," she said, "is whether this holding is going to say out loud, once, in a room, what it has done in this month and what it has not done. Not to a council. To four bodies of households, in a room, on a day named in advance, with the day said out loud before anybody comes." "I have read the four letters that have come up this road and there is not one of them about a thing anybody in this building did. There is one about what a man is called, one about what a woman is called, one about a column, and one about a gate, and four counties are about to decide something on the strength of those four letters, and a body of households is going to sit in a room somewhere and be told that a building which has not answered a question has answered it."

Nobody said she was right and nobody said she was wrong, and about four people in that room had wanted somebody to ask that for a fortnight and about four had wanted somebody to stop asking, and neither of those two fours said a word and neither of them is going to.

The count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken went from ten to nine this afternoon, and it is a count of questions and not a count of anybody, and a count of blanks on a board is not the count of a man and a count of questions not taken is not the count of a person who has not thought of one.

The woman who keeps the seed house said what she said she wanted it for, and she said it without a reason in front of it, which she is allowed to do about four times a year, and the count of that in the holding's history did not move because it does not move on a page where the reason is not given.

"I want it because in about four years somebody is going to come through that door and ask what this holding did when four counties voted, and there is going to be nobody here who was here, and there is going to be four books, and the four books are going to have four answers in them in a row that a man wrote on a Friday in a room with the door shut, and none of the four is a list of a month."

He gave the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and ninety-three, and it is his and of nobody else's, and the woman who keeps the seed house did not look up.

"I am going to say the one thing I have been not saying since Thursday and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that she has asked the whole of it out loud in a room and about four of us have been carrying it for a fortnight, and a thing that four people in a room are carrying and not saying is a thing that gets said by somebody else first, in a yard, in a month, with his name in it." "She has not asked me to do it and she is not going to ask me to do it, and I am not going to do it this season. It is hers. She said she wanted it and she said what for, and nobody in this room is going to improve either, and I am not going to stand in here and take a thing off a woman who has not offered it and has not been thanked for asking."

Nobody thanked her.

The man of about fifty wrote the question into the second book on a page of its own, and wrote the reason she gave for wanting it underneath it, and did not number the page, and did not write a heading at the top of it. About four people in that room had wanted him to write a heading at the top of it and about four had not, and he is not going to.

He read both of them back out loud afterward, which is the practice of this holding, and then he read the reason again on its own without the question above it, and said that a page with a question at the top of it and a reason underneath it is two sentences and that two sentences are not a list, and about four people in that room had needed to hear the second one said out loud and about four had not.

The count of that in the holding's history, for the man who keeps the book, is one hundred and twenty, and he gave the reason first, and nobody thanked him for it either.

"There are nine of them left now and there were ten, and I am not going to write down what the other nine are and I am not going to improve the one that has been taken, and the reason is that a question taken is a question somebody has agreed to do, and the agreement is the whole of the thing, and it is not in a column and it is not on a board and it is not a line under a name." "If I put it in a book as a finding, then in about four years somebody will find that this holding answered a county in a month, and that will be a true sentence, and it will not have a person in it, and about four of us will know who and about four will not, and neither of those two fours is going to be asked."

The offer is on the low board at the far end of that room, face up, and it is not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month, and it has not been withdrawn, and it has no date and no name and no line for a name, and nobody in this building has been told to take it off that board and nobody is going to be, and there is no second instrument of that offer on any table in this holding this season.

The register form is on the middle table at sixty-nine days, not filled and not refused, with nothing at the head of it, and nobody wrote a heading on it and nobody is going to.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road. The sheet that claims it has been in a drawer face up since a month now three months back and is correct in every particular, and nobody in this holding can put it down.

The order sealing the route is in the same drawer, and it is valid, and it was read out in full on Wednesday in a doorway at the node, and it opens nothing, and nothing this holding has reopens a route, and the corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road.

The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-three, and the next of them is on the first of a month, which is six mornings off this one, and nobody has ever asked him which number and no page in this building gives a reason for it. The compost line is paid at twenty not discharged in twenty and does not fall this month.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-four and is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year, and it was not read this morning, and nobody has written a heading at the head of any of the six columns beside it and nobody is going to this season.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page, and it was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced and not explained, and nothing goes into the two-foot gap at the node this season and nothing has gone into it since a month now six months back.

Four inches on that arm and the same two branches, and the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning. A question is not a mark, an agreement is not a mark, a refusal to take a thing off a woman is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled and nothing said in that room this afternoon is a fifth of anything.
