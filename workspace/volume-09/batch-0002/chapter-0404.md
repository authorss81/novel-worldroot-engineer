# Chapter 404

## The Fourth Word Is A Column

The seventeenth of this month was a Sunday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked.

The channel along the inside face of the west dyke, the lower nine feet of it, took four people from the second hour to the sixth, and the toe at the bottom of that nine feet is up a finger's width off what it was on the third of last month and has been for a month.

The man at the front of it put a bar in under the toe and levered, and the toe came up in a slab about the length of a forearm, and under the slab there was a root as thick as two fingers that is not a willow's and does not come from a willow. It is the old root of the drain itself, laid when the drain was laid, and it has been in the clay under the toe since before this holding had a book.

They cut it out with a saw and put it on the bank and covered the hole with chalk and grit.

The four of them were on the nine feet again by the fourth hour and finished it by the sixth, and Marek was one of the four and had the bar before the saw and the saw after it.

The root went into the seed house fire that evening because there is nowhere else for a thing like it to go.

The woman who keeps the seed house asked the man at the front of the work whether anybody had ever told the office it was there, and he said that nobody had ever told anybody and that it had been under that toe since before the dyke had a name in this building.

That is the whole of what the four of them said about it, and it is entered here because it was said, and it does not go into any of the four books, because a drain's own root is not a defect and is not a figure and is nobody's work.

At about the seventh hour, in the yard, with the door shut behind them, the two day-minus rules were read out loud before either of the numbers, one man taking the first board and another man taking the second so that neither of them is trusted with both.

Fifty-four mornings out of ninety-one, counted from the seventeenth of a month three months back. The frame on that count moves when the month turns and the change is said once, and it was said in the yard, and nobody improved it.

The number was said first and the man of about fifty said the second one, and the two figures in this building are the number of mornings a man has said a thing with a reason in front of it and the number of mornings since he started again, and those are two different counts and are not added to each other by anybody in four years.

The seventeenth was entered at about the eighth hour and read back out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two of them were the same, and nobody in the yard said anything about that, because there has never been anything to say about it.

**Eight hundred and thirty-nine hundredweight.**

Down five off Saturday, and the hundred and sixty-seventh figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the first morning of it.

The window the rises and the falls are counted in stands at a hundred and thirty-four, the fall having gone onto the falls, and it stands at seventy-two rises and sixty-two falls, and the two of them add the way they have added on every morning of this year without anybody making them add by hand.

The sentence about the aggregate of the four bodies of households was written this morning for the hundred and sixty-fourth of those mornings out of a hundred and sixty-six, and what it says is that the four have not moved in a hundred and sixty-five days. Four bodies of households. The launders are a different aggregate and the two have never shared a line of a page in this building.

At about the ninth hour the man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the four-mile road, turned the barrow at the top of the yard and went home without stopping, and he was not asked anything about the two letters that came up that road this week.

Fifty-five mornings is how old that second line is. It is blank, and it has been blank for fifty-five mornings, and asking about it is not a thing anybody in this building has done in four years.

He was not asked anything about the letter either. He had been in the room at the node for the first hour of it on Saturday and had not been in this room on Sunday, and there was nothing in the letter that touched him.

About four people in this building have wondered in four years whether he reads the letters that come up that road and about four have not wondered about it, and neither of the two fours has ever said so to him.

Marek had put the saw back in the tool roll himself and had not been thanked for that either, and the roll went under the bench in the corner where it has gone every evening for four years.

About nine people were in the one room from about the fourth hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and the letter had come up the fen road on Saturday night with the ordinary post, and it was from the clerk of the second county and it was two paragraphs long and the second one was the reason for the first.

> THE SECOND COUNTY ASKS, IN ONE SENTENCE, WHAT THE SEEDWRIGHT NAMED AS THE KEEPER OF ITS OWN FOUR ACCOUNTS HAS DONE. THE COUNTY RECORDS THAT IT PUT THE QUESTION TO HER DIRECTLY, IN A ROOM AT THE SEED HOUSE, ON THE FRIDAY, IN FRONT OF FOUR PERSONS, AND THAT SHE ANSWERED IT AT LENGTH, AND THAT HER ANSWER WAS NOT A LIST OF WORK AND WAS NOT REFUSED BY HER. THE COUNTY FURTHER RECORDS THAT IT HAS NO BODY TO SEND THE SAME QUESTION TO, THE SEED HOUSE NOT BEING A BODY, AND THAT IT THEREFORE ASKS THE COOPERATIVE WHICH KEEPS A BOOK. THE COUNTY DOES NOT ASK THAT THE SHEET OF THE THIRTEENTH BE SENT AGAIN AND HAS NOT COPIED THE NAME FROM IT.

Tova Reed had been in the room about ten minutes when it was read out and she had not been asked to be there and had not gone out.

The man of about fifty said that the second paragraph was the whole of it, that the first paragraph was a formality, and that the county had done a thing on Friday that four counties had not done, which was to ask the question to a person before asking it to a building. He put the letter down as he said it and nobody in that room said the word formality out loud after him.

She gave the reason before she said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is thirty, and it is hers and of nobody else's, and she was not thanked for it.

"I gave that woman a room on Friday with four people in it and I gave her the whole of an answer, and I am going to say why I did that and I am going to give the reason for saying it first. The reason is that if I had sent her away to fetch a book, she would have come back with a book, and a book is what she asked me for, and what she asked me for is the one thing I have never been able to give anybody." "What I gave her instead was the truth, which is that the four accounts are mine and were my mother's before mine and are not a list of work, and that a season of disconnection costs a number of sacks and not a number of duties, and that the two do not convert into one another. She wrote all of that down. She is a clerk and she is not a fool and she will have written it down exactly." "And she has come here and asked it of a building. That is not the same question. A building can be asked for a thing it has. I am not a building and I have four accounts and no duty on any of them, and if I had wanted a duty on them I would have written the heading at the top in about four years and I would not have had to ask anybody for anything."

Nobody argued with her about it. About four people in that room had understood the difference between a number of sacks and a number of duties before she said it and about four had not, and neither of those two fours said anything, and the two are not the same four as the two fours in any other room in this building.

He gave the reason first, the way he gives it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and eighty-seven, and it belongs to him and to nobody else, and nobody in that room wrote it down either.

"I am going to say the part that is actually useful this afternoon and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that two counties have now asked this building a question in a man's name and in a woman's name, and a third county's clerk is expected here on Monday morning, and I would like this room to have worked out by Monday which word he is going to use." "The first county asked what a man has done and the second asked what a woman has done, and those are the same question in two different words out of two different rooms, and neither of those rooms has a column to put an answer in."

The man of about fifty put his pen down, which he does when he is going to say something he has thought about, and gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fourteen.

"Then here is what I have worked out and I am going to give the reason for it first. The reason is that I have four books in front of me and about nine people in this room and none of us has ever written a heading at the top of one of our own columns, and I am not going to be the man who discovers that on Monday from a letter." "Three of those four counties are going to use a name. A man, a woman, and I would lay money on a third name, and every one of those three is going to be unanswerable in the word it is using, because a name is a person and a person is not a place to keep work in." "The fourth one is going to use a column. That is the only one of the four that can be answered, and it is going to be answerable on Monday morning by a person with four books open in front of him, and I have thought about that for about an hour and I still cannot tell whether that is good news or the worst thing that has happened this month."

The woman who keeps the seed house said that the worst thing that had happened this month was a letter with one paragraph in it and that the clerk who wrote it had been kind about it, and the man of about fifty said that kindness in a letter is the part that costs the most to be wrong about, and that the county was not wrong.

Nobody thanked anybody. The bread from Saturday was finished by the fourth hour and there was more of it on the table by the seventh, and the man of about fifty cut it and did not hand it round and everybody took some, and that is the whole of what happened in that room about food.

Marek was the only person in that room who had read all three of the letters, and he had not been in this building for any of the four of them, and that is a fact about where a person has to stand and not about a man keeping count.

The offer is still on the low board at the far end, face up, and it carries no date and no name and no line for a name, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month and nobody in this building has been told to take it off there.

A second county has now written to this building asking what a person has done, and the answer to that is in this room and is not going on the paper.

A bridge was run once for about nine minutes five weeks ago and no person in this holding has an instrument that says whether it is on or off, and the man of about fifty did not say so this afternoon and wrote nothing about it.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up, and every particular of it is correct, and there is nothing in this building that can put it down and nothing in this building that can answer the question a county asked about it on Saturday.

Nobody went near the eleven acres this afternoon. The ring of bare ground inside them has been bare eleven years and was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page, and the work in that wood gets done outside it because of the light off the west end and for no other reason.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and a letter is not a use and a name is not a use, and the reason for the fifteen is not given in this holding in a season.

The requests stand at fifty-three and the section-nine notes stand at fifty-three, and the two have never been added to one another, and two letters from two clerks in two days are two letters and not two fifty-fours, and a letter that puts a question in a man's name is a letter and not a fifty-fourth of anything.

The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-three, and the compost line is paid at twenty not discharged in twenty, and the word before that figure has never once disagreed with it.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-four and is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year, and it was not read this morning and nobody in this building went nine hundred yards to read it.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the seventeenth, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE LOWER NINE FEET OF THE CHANNEL INSIDE THE WEST DYKE, FOUR PEOPLE TO THE SIXTH HOUR, AND A ROOT THAT IS NOT A WILLOW'S WAS CUT OUT OF THE TOE. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD AT THE SEVENTH HOUR AND BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, ONE MAN TO EACH, BEING FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND NINETY-EIGHT, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. IT IS THE FIFTY-FOURTH MORNING OUT OF NINETY-ONE COUNTED FROM THE SEVENTEENTH OF A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK, AND THE FRAME ON THAT COUNT MOVES WHEN THE MONTH TURNS. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED EIGHT HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SEVENTH FIGURE, AND IT HAS FALLEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FOUR, BEING SEVENTY-TWO RISES AND SIXTY-TWO FALLS, AND THE TWO ADD. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIVE DAYS, WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOURTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX. A LETTER OF TWO PARAGRAPHS CAME FROM THE CLERK OF THE SECOND COUNTY ASKING WHAT THE SEEDWRIGHT HAS DONE, AND IT RECORDS THAT THE QUESTION WAS PUT TO HER DIRECTLY ON THE FRIDAY IN A ROOM AT THE SEED HOUSE AND WAS ANSWERED, AND THAT THE ANSWER WAS NOT A LIST OF WORK AND WAS NOT REFUSED BY HER. A WOMAN IN THIS ROOM GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT A NUMBER OF SACKS AND A NUMBER OF DUTIES DO NOT CONVERT INTO ONE ANOTHER, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IN THE HOLDING'S HISTORY IS THIRTY. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT TWO COUNTIES HAVE NOW ASKED THE SAME QUESTION IN A MAN'S NAME AND IN A WOMAN'S NAME, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVEN. THE MAN WHO KEEPS THE BOOK GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT THREE OF THE FOUR COUNTIES WILL USE A NAME AND THE FOURTH WILL USE A COLUMN, AND THAT THE COLUMN IS THE ONLY ONE OF THE FOUR THAT CAN BE ANSWERED, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
