# Chapter 410

## Four Miles And A Half

The twenty-third of this month was a Saturday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The boundary ditch and the two culverts took six people until the fourth hour, and the near one has been clear since the ninth and the far one was about a fifth full and going down by itself, and the six of them put one barrow-load out of it and stopped there.

The two day-minus rules were read out loud at about the seventh hour before either of the numbers, one man taking the first and another taking the second. They stand at **five hundred and fifty-seven days** and **six hundred and four**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened. Fifty-seven mornings out of ninety-seven, and the figure is the denominator of a run that began on the seventeenth of a month three months back and is not the other pair of figures in this building and the two are never added.

The twenty-third went into the third launder's book at about the eighth hour and was read back out of the other one, and the reading did not vary for a fall, because it never has.

**Eight hundred and fifty-one hundredweight.**

Down five off Friday, and the hundred and seventy-third figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the first morning of it.

Seventy-five rises and sixty-five falls make a hundred and forty, and the fall went on to the falls, and that window is four hundred and eighty-three days wide and is not the pair of boards that was read out loud at the seventh hour and is not the launder.

A hundred and seventy-one days is what the sentence on this morning's sheet says about the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and it was written for the hundred and seventieth of those mornings out of a hundred and seventy-two. Four bodies of households and not the launders, and the two have not been added in this building in four years and were not added this morning.

Soren Rill came down the four and a half miles into the seed house yard at about the eleventh hour of the morning, wet to the middle of the back, and he had the last mile and a bit of it on foot and said so before anybody asked him where he had been.

The four and a half miles is a walk, not a cart road, and it has not been a cart road in living memory. It is the walk to Thornwild, and this is the twenty-seventh time it has been made and the first time it has been made by anybody else, and every one of the twenty-seven before this morning was made by the man standing in the yard.

Nobody thanked him for it. He did not ask.

The woman who keeps the seed house put a cloth down by his boots the way she does, without saying anything about it, and it stayed on the flags by the door after he had gone and nobody picked it up.

He was in the building for about two hours and neither of the four books was open while he was in it, and about four people in this holding have wondered in four years whether he knows what is in them and about four have not, and he has never been asked and is not going to be. He has never once asked this building for a figure, and the count of questions this holding has asked him in that line is nil and is nil.

There were about nine people in the one room at this holding from about the second hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and he had a folded sheet inside the front of his coat and he took it out and put it on the long table at the wall side and did not put it beside any of the other papers.

> OF THE ASSEMBLY AT THORNWILD, CARRIED AT A MEETING OF THE ASSEMBLY ON THE EIGHTEENTH OF THIS MONTH BY A COUNT OF HANDS TAKEN ONCE. FIRST, THAT THE ASSEMBLY HAS READ THE SHEET OF THREE PARTS DATED THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH AND HAS FOUND NO FALSE STATEMENT IN IT, AND RECORDS THE SENTENCE IN IT THAT THE ENGINE MEANS TO TAKE OFF THE BRANCH AT NINETEEN NAMED PLACES. SECOND, THAT THE ASSEMBLY HAS BEEN HARMED ONCE BY A CONNECTION AND HAS ASKED FOR AN ANSWER AND HAS RECEIVED AN INSTRUMENT, AND THAT A SETTLEMENT ASKED ONCE HAS A RIGHT TO ASK TWICE. THIRD, THAT THE ASSEMBLY RESOLVES TO ASK THE HOLDING WHICH KEEPS THE NODE FOR THE HEARTHROOT AT THAT NODE, AND TO HOLD IT, AND TO KEEP IT AFTER THE NEXT TAKING, AND THE ASSEMBLY WILL SEND FOUR PERSONS ON THE FOURTH DAY OF NEXT MONTH TO BE TOLD WHAT IS TO BE DONE AND TO DO IT. FOURTH, THAT THE ASSEMBLY HAS HAD ITS OWN STORE OF SEED COUNTED BY A PERSON WHO IS NOT OF THORNWILD AND THAT THE COUNT IS SEVEN HUNDRED AND FOUR SACKS, AND THAT THE ASSEMBLY OFFERS EVERY ONE OF THEM AGAINST THE REQUEST. THE ASSEMBLY ASKS NOBODY TO DO ANYTHING BY THIS SHEET.

Nobody in that room improved a word of it, and the clerk of this holding read the second clause out loud twice.

About four people in the room had a reason for that clause and about four had the same reason as the first one, and neither of the two fours is going to be asked which they were. The second clause says that a settlement harmed once by a connection has a right to ask twice, and the long fungal was found inside four hundred yards of the first dwelling of Thornwild and not inside the inhabited line of about two hundred yards, and nobody in this room can prove where it came through and nobody in this room is going to say it came through the connection.

Nobody in this room has ever put one of the fruiting bodies in a mouth and nobody in this room is going to say whether they are safe to eat, and that has not changed since a month now three months back, and the woman who keeps the seed house said so out loud at about the fourth hour, and nobody in that room had asked her either way.

Tova Reed said one thing about it and gave the reason before she said it, and the count of that in the holding's history is thirty-five, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the part that is about my work and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that a woman in this room has been asked nothing for a week and has had a road cut under her feet, and the two things are now in the same letter, and nobody in this room is going to say the letter is about both." "Seven hundred and four sacks is a number I can work with. It is a number of sacks and it is not a number of days, and a number of sacks does not convert into a number of days without four things that nobody in this building has, and I am not going to stand in this room and pretend I can do it in my head, and I am certainly not going to do it on the floor." "Offered against the request. That is the fourth clause and it is the only clause in that sheet with a figure in it, and it was put in last, and I would like this room to notice that a body which was refused an answer in a month now one month back has come back with the one thing it can actually give."

Then the count came back, and it came back because a body of households that keeps four books of its own had sent a person up the road to do it, and that person was not from Thornwild and had never been to Thornwild.

She had come down the four and a half miles on the seventeenth with a hired man and a tally stick and had come back down it on the nineteenth alone, and she said the walking was the easy part and that the two days of counting were the part that took it out of her, and she said that in the yard and not in the room, and about four people heard her say it and about four did not.

Hesta Lyle, of about fifty-one, keeps the four books for the four bodies of households at the Four Elbows and is the only person who does, and she is not of this holding and is not a member of anything. She had been at the store at Thornwild for two days and had counted it herself with a tally on the end of a stick, and she would not give the tally to anybody and said the tally was a method and not a figure.

**Seven hundred and four sacks,** she said. **"Counted on the fourteenth, the fifteenth and the sixteenth, and again on the morning I left, and the four counts are the same figure, and I have written down what is in them and not what they are worth. What they are worth in days is not a thing I was asked and is not a thing I am able to do standing in a room with a door shut."**

Nobody thanked her and nobody asked her again, and the six things this holding does not know are six, and nobody in that room added a seventh for sacks, and a count of sacks is not a count of days and the two are not added to one another anywhere in this building.

Soren Rill said one thing and he gave the reason before he said it, and the count of that in the holding's history is fifteen, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the reason I carried it down on my own two legs and not on a cart, and I am going to give the reason for saying it first, and the reason is that the last mile of that walk is not a cart road and a cart would have got into the ground at the ford, and I would have got there on Thursday instead of Saturday and Thursday was the day the Assembly voted." "And I am not going to be thanked for it, and I did not come for that. I have been asked nothing in this room and I am not going to be asked, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked me is thirteen and is thirteen, and it has not moved this afternoon and it is not going to move this week."

Nobody in that room asked him anything, and about four people in it had wanted to and about four had wanted to and did not, and the two fours are not the same fours as the two fours that carry the four lines in this manuscript, and nobody is going to be told which they were.

He gave the reason before the thing, in that room, with the door shut, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and ninety-one, and it is his and of nobody else's, and Hesta Lyle was at the wall and heard all of it.

"I am going to say the one thing in that sheet that I can answer this afternoon and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that four reasons in that sheet are four reasons I have given out loud in this room in four years, and a body that was handed one of our own pages four counties ago and read the part of it that says what a thing will not do is not a body we get to be clever about." "The reasons are the reasons. A settlement harmed once by a connection is entitled to ask whether there should be a second one. We have said that in this room. A body that has been handed a document and got an instrument instead of an answer is not a body anybody is going to leave alone. We have said that. And I am not going to stand here and say the reasons are weaker than the reasons I would give in that chair, because they are the reasons, and the reason I am standing here at all is that four counties have started using sentences out of this building as if this building had said them to them."

Nobody thanked anybody for that, and nobody in that room disagreed with it, and about four people in it had heard him say a version of it on the eighth and about four had not, and Hesta Lyle said afterwards that he was the first person in four years who had said a thing out loud in a room and let it stay in the room, and she said it to the man of about fifty and not to Marek.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the four-mile road on foot at about the ninth hour, without the barrow, and went past the gate and the trough and turned at the trough and came back, and nobody asked him why.

Nobody asked him about the second of the four lines in his own book either, which is sixty-one days old this morning and has nothing on it and no place on it, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in sixty-one days. A barrow's journeys and a man's feet are two figures and are not added to each other.

A barrow's journeys and a man's feet are two figures and are not added to each other. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road and eleven is a floor and did not rise, and the man walked four miles and did not take it and was not asked about either.

The trough had about four inches in it and had had them since the second of this month, and he stood at it for about the length of a pipe before he turned, and the woman who keeps the seed house was in the yard and saw him do it and said nothing to him then or afterward, and nobody in this holding has asked him about that either.

The offer is on the low board at the far end of that room, face up, and it is not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month, and it has not been withdrawn, and a second council has given it as one of its reasons for not relying on this holding's account of anything, and an Assembly four and a half miles off has offered seven hundred and four sacks against a request.

Neither of those two things is on the low board and neither of them is going to be, and the woman who keeps the seed house looked at the board for about a second and a half when the fourth clause was read and then looked away, and about four people in that room saw her do it and about four did not.

The register form is on the middle table where it has stood sixty-seven days and nobody wrote a heading at it and nobody is going to, and nothing on that table was moved this afternoon except the day sheet, which went on the long table at the wall side and stayed there until the seventh hour.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road.

The sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular, and the order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and opens nothing, and this holding has nothing that reopens a route and has not had since a month now five months back.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page, and nobody has walked it or measured it or crossed it or priced it or explained it, and the work in that wood is done outside it because of the light off the west end and for no other reason.
