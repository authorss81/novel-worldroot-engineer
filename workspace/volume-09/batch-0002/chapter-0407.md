# Chapter 407

## A Yard Is Not A Building

The twentieth of this month was a Wednesday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The byre drain behind the seed house and about twelve feet of it took four people until the fourth hour, and there was a wasp's nest in the top of it that came down whole and was burned at the end of the wall, and none of that is entered in any of the four books.

The near culvert was clear and has been since the ninth. The far one was about a third full and going down by itself, and the four of them put one barrow-load out of it and stopped there, because a thing that is going down by itself is a thing a holding does not go into twice in a week.

Marek was one of the four and had both hands full from the second to the fifth hour, and the two day-minus rules were not read out loud this morning and this page carries no figure for the run, and the reason is on the page and not left off it.

The pair stands at **five hundred and fifty-four days** and **six hundred and one**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened. A man does not stand at a table and say two figures with a barrow-load in front of him, and this holding has never in four years asked him to.

The figure for the twentieth went in at about the eighth hour and was read back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and the two men who read them do not know what either of them is for.

**Eight hundred and fifty-two hundredweight.**

Up nine off Tuesday, and the hundred and seventieth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice in seventy mornings.

The rises and the falls stand at seventy-four and sixty-three this morning, the rise having gone onto the rises, and the window they are counted in is a hundred and thirty-seven, and it is a window and not a count of anything anybody in four counties has done, and the man of about fifty said so on the cart road on the way down without being asked.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in a hundred and sixty-eight days, and the sentence for it was written this morning for the hundred and sixty-seventh of those mornings out of a hundred and sixty-nine. Four bodies of households, and the four launders are a different aggregate, and the two have never been added in this building.

He came down the four-mile road about the ninth hour with the barrow, turned it at the top of the yard and went home, and the frost had gone off the low ground by then.

Nobody asked him about the second of the four lines in the book he carries, and it is fifty-eight days old, and it has nothing on it and no place on it. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road, and eleven is a floor.

There is a building at the node that has no roof and has not had one for as long as the oldest man in this holding can remember. It is a building with a barrow in it and four men standing about, and the wall of the node is the wall in it, and the number that is on the page for bodies at the node is a fact about a rota and it does not go into a yard and is not given in one this afternoon.

Then about forty people came up the cart road from the west onto the slope above the low ground, and they stopped there, in the wind off the west end, in about two hours of the afternoon, and nobody made them come and nobody made them go.

That is a yard. The building with the barrow and the four men in it is not a yard.

The count of things said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong is five, and it is five this afternoon, and it did not move. About four of the people on that slope could have told you what the five were and did not, and the not telling is not modesty either.

The two yard counts are not added to one another on this slope and are not added to one another in the building either, and they are not added to one another anywhere.

The man who keeps the book said so out loud twice this afternoon, once on the slope and once standing in the doorway of the roofless place with his back to the dial, and the second time was for the four men in coats, who had come up that road to be told a thing by somebody and were going home without it.

The man of about fifty came up the cart road at about the second hour of the afternoon with the day sheet in his coat, and he had not been asked to come and had not been sent for, and about four people in four counties know that he keeps four books and about four of those four do not know that he is the one who does it.

He has come up that road before on a ninth day and this was not one, and the difference is that on a ninth day there is a dial and a board and four names to go in, and on an ordinary Wednesday there is a slope and about forty people and nothing at all for him to do here.

He was not asked anything on the slope for about an hour, and he stood at the top of it with his hands behind his back the way he stands, and he said one thing, and nobody had asked him, and he did not know he was going to say it, and it is the fourteenth of those in this holding's history, and the count of that in this holding's history is fourteen, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I have been in this building eleven years and I have watched every one of us talk about the taking,"** he said. **"The taking is the hour the work shows up. We have been talking about the taking for a fortnight as though it were the work, and it is not the work, and the work is the nine days between now and then, and nobody in this yard has been told what the nine days are for because nobody in four counties has ever written it down."**

About four people on that slope heard the whole sentence. About four heard half of it, with his name in the half.

A sheet went into the second book on the thirteenth with his employer's name at the head of it and a day and an hour against it, and about four people who had read that sheet had been carrying the name about all week without knowing that they were carrying it, and one of the halves of that sentence on that slope had a name in it and the other half did not, and neither of the two is the whole of it.

Nobody said he was right and nobody said he was wrong.

The woman who keeps the seed house was on the slope. She had come up with the seed for the three households above the low ground and had been putting it into sacks on a board she brought up herself, and she said afterwards that it was the first time in four years she had heard a thing said in this holding that she had not already worked out. She said it to Marek and not to the slope, and she was not thanked for it and did not look to be.

About four people on that slope had worked out the same thing in the same fortnight and had not said it, and about four had not worked it out at all, and the four that had are not going to be told which they were.

Then the clerk of this holding read out the order, and he read it out because four of the county's men were on that slope with their coats on and had asked for it, and he read it standing in the doorway of the roofless place with the wind off the west end going past him.

> THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY, BY THE PRESIDENT, ONE SHEET, FOUR CLAUSES, AN EMBOSSED SEAL AT THE FOOT OF THE FIRST CLAUSE, THE SEAL IN RED AND NOT SMUDGED AND THE SAME SEAL AS ON EVERY OTHER SHEET THAT OFFICE HAS EVER SENT TO THIS HOLDING. FIRST, THAT THE ROUTE NAMED IN THE SCHEDULE AT LINE THIRTY-ONE IS SEALED FROM THE DATE OF THIS SHEET UNTIL SUCH TIME AS A SCHEDULE IN REVISION SHALL BE ISSUED, AND THAT SEALED MEANS SEALED, AND THAT THE SEAL IS NOT A DIRECTION TO ANY PERSON AND ASKS NOBODY TO DO ANYTHING. SECOND, THAT NO OFFICE, PERSON OR BODY IS REQUIRED BY THE SEAL TO ENTER, KEEP, ANSWER OR MAINTAIN ANYTHING. THIRD, THAT THE SEAL TAKES EFFECT ON THE DATE ABOVE AND NOT BEFORE, AND THAT ANY WORK DONE AT A PLACE SEALED BEFORE THAT DATE IS WORK DONE WITHOUT INSTRUCTION AND IS ENTERED AS SUCH. FOURTH, THAT THIS SHEET IS ISSUED UNDER THE DELEGATED AUTHORITY OF THE PRESIDENT AND MAY BE READ BY ANY PERSON WHO HAS ASKED FOR IT. SIGNED, H. PIKE, PRESIDENT.

He read it out in full and then he said two things about it, and he said them in the doorway and not on the slope, and he gave the reason first for the first of them, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and seventeen.

"I am going to say what that sheet is and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that four men in coats have just heard a president of an office read a document out loud in a doorway, and if I do not say what it is before they get back down that road, somebody else will say what it is, and what they are about to say it is will be in a county book by Friday." "It is valid. Nothing in it has been broken and nothing in it has been disobeyed, and it seals a route, and the route is the route it has always sealed, and the route has not been reopened this afternoon and is not going to be reopened by anything in this holding's possession this season." "And it opens nothing. That is the second thing and I am not giving a reason first for it because it is not a thing I have a reason for, it is a fact about a piece of paper. A sheet that asks nobody to do anything does not put anybody in a room. It is a correct document and a correct document is a door with the hinges described, and I have said that sentence once this week in this building and I am not going to say it again on a slope."

Nobody thanked anybody. The four men in coats wrote nothing down, or if they wrote something down it was in a book of their own that is not in this building, and about four people on that slope have watched one of them not write anything down and about four have not been watching.

The count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard that the engineer of record had not thought of went from thirteen to fourteen this afternoon, and it is a count of one man on one slope, and it is not added to the count of things said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong, and the two counts are not added to each other on any afternoon of the year.

Marek stood about ten feet off the man of about fifty for the whole of the hour after the man spoke and said nothing, which is not what he usually does, and the reason he gave afterwards, to nobody in particular, at the cart road on the way down, was a fact about a page and not about a person.

"He is right and I had not got there and that is the whole of what happened this afternoon." He said it once and did not improve it. "In four years I have been standing at that dial at the second hour of a ninth day and counting what came off it, and he has been sitting in a room counting the days between, and he is the one who has been counting the right thing."

The offer is on the low board at this building and it is not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month, and it has not been withdrawn. Nothing was said about it on that slope and nobody asked about it and it is not going to be said there, and a sheet with an embossed seal on it that opens nothing is not a reason to take an instrument off a board four miles away.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road. The sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular, and the order that has just been read out on a slope is in the same drawer and is not on the same piece of paper.

The two are not the same thing and are not added, and a slope with about forty people on it is not a room, and a thing said on a slope goes up a cart road at night and into four counties by Friday and nobody in this holding will ever be able to check what it became.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page, and the light off the west end is the only reason anybody in four counties knows where it is.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twentieth, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE BYRE DRAIN BEHIND THE SEED HOUSE AND TWELVE FEET OF IT, FOUR PEOPLE TO THE FOURTH HOUR, AND A WASP'S NEST CAME DOWN WHOLE AND WAS BURNED. THE BOARDS WERE NOT READ ALOUD AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR THE RUN. THE PAIR STANDS AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FOUR DAYS AND SIX HUNDRED AND ONE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED EIGHT HUNDRED AND FIFTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND SEVENTIETH FIGURE, AND IT HAS RISEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SEVEN, BEING SEVENTY-FOUR RISES AND SIXTY-THREE FALLS, AND THE TWO ADD. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT DAYS, WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SEVENTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-NINE. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING CAME DOWN THE FOUR-MILE ROAD AT THE NINTH HOUR AND WAS NOT ASKED ANYTHING, AND THE SECOND OF THE FOUR LINES IN HIS OWN BOOK IS FIFTY-EIGHT DAYS OLD. THE BARROW STANDS AT ELEVEN JOURNEYS AND ELEVEN IS A FLOOR. THERE WAS A YARD ON THE SLOPE ABOVE THE LOW GROUND AT THE NODE THIS AFTERNOON, WITH ABOUT FORTY PEOPLE ON IT, AND A BUILDING WITH A BARROW AND FOUR MEN IN IT IS NOT A YARD, AND THAT IS SAID TWICE THIS AFTERNOON. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD IN THIS HOLDING AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THE COUNT OF TIMES A PERSON WHO KEEPS A BOOK IN THIS HOLDING HAS SAID A THING IN A YARD THE ENGINEER OF RECORD HAD NOT THOUGHT OF IS NOW FOURTEEN, AND THE TWO ARE NOT ADDED TO EACH OTHER. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SAID ON THE SLOPE, UNASKED, THAT THE TAKING IS THE HOUR THE WORK SHOWS UP AND THAT THE WORK IS THE NINE DAYS BETWEEN, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS FOURTEEN AND IS HIS AND OF NOBODY ELSE'S. ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE HEARD THE WHOLE SENTENCE AND ABOUT FOUR THE HALF OF IT WITH HIS NAME IN IT. THE ORDER SEALING THE ROUTE WAS READ OUT IN FULL IN THE DOORWAY OF THE ROOFLESS PLACE AND IS VALID AND OPENS NOTHING.**
