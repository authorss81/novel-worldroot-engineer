# Chapter 409

## Three Hundred And Forty-Nine

The twenty-second of this month was a Friday, and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and went away empty again, there being nobody at the headland above Marden to work it. The fields on that headland have waited for the fifty-third morning.

The rota stands at eighty-two mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and fifty-three not, and twenty-nine and fifty-three are eighty-two.

Nobody in this holding is blamed for a field that waited, and a field that waited is a fact about a rota, and twenty-nine taken did not move this morning and has not moved on any morning of this year.

This holding keeps two counts of that rotation and they disagree, which is why a page this morning carries both. Thirty mornings of the run that began on the twenty-sixth of a month four months back. Seventeen mornings of the other run, out of the eighteenth of a month two months back, and the frame on that one turns with the month and the change is said once and not twice.

The six who would have gone to the headland were on the boundary ditch, and Marek was one of them, and both hands were full from the second hour to the sixth, and the sleet that came at the fifth hour went through everything anybody at this holding was wearing.

This page carries no figure for the run, because the two day-minus rules were not read out loud this morning, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. A man who has been in a ditch since the second hour is not a man this holding has ever asked to say two figures at the eighth, and nobody in this building has ever treated that as a rule.

The pair stands at **five hundred and fifty-six days** and **six hundred and three**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened. A man who has been in a ditch since the second hour is not a man this holding asks to say two figures at the eighth, and it is a fact about the morning and not a fact about the rule.

The twenty-second was entered at the eighth hour and read back out of the other one with the frayed corner, and the two of them were the same, and that is the whole of the eighth hour on a morning when nothing else has gone wrong.

**Eight hundred and fifty-six hundredweight.**

Up nine off Thursday, and the hundred and seventy-second figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the first morning of it.

A hundred and thirty-nine is the window this morning, the rise having gone onto the rises, which leaves seventy-five rises and sixty-four falls, and the man of about fifty wrote both halves on the day sheet in one line because a man who numbers a contradiction has decided which half of it he means.

The four bodies of households have gone a hundred and seventy days without a move in their aggregate, and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the hundred and sixty-ninth of those mornings out of a hundred and seventy-one.

Four bodies of households and not the launders, and the two aggregates have never shared a line of a page in this building, and about four people in four counties have read that sentence and about four have not read it at all.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the node at the second hour of the afternoon and not in the room at this building, and the second of the four lines in the book he carries is sixty days old this morning and has no answer on it, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in sixty days.

He was one of the four who weighed the return and he is the only one of the four who has ever been in this room, and he has been in it about four times in four years, and about four people in this holding have noticed that he comes on ninth days and about four have not, and neither of those two fours has ever said so to him.

The barrow stands at eleven journeys up the four-mile road and did not rise, and eleven is a floor, and a barrow's journeys and a man's feet are two figures and are not added to each other on any morning of the year.

The return came off the steelyard at about the second hour of the afternoon, in the building at the node that has no roof.

It was sleeting by then and not much of it, the kind that does not lie on anything, and the four of them had it in under the lee of the wall in about nine minutes and stood there with their coats open while the man of about fifty did the dial. The four bodies of households that weigh it are at Havergate and Low Wether and Marden and Silling, and not one of them is a member of the cooperative and none of them was made one this afternoon, and nobody asked them to be and nobody in this building has ever asked them to be.

**Three hundred and forty-nine hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-seventh of them. It is the twenty-seventh pair of names to go against a ninth-day return in eleven years, and it stands eleven hundredweight above the thirty-sixth, which was three hundred and thirty-eight on the thirteenth of this month and is the figure the last return in this holding's book carries.

The man of about fifty took it off the dial, said the figure out loud, and then said it again out of the second book, and the two were the same.

It came out of the dial twice checked, against the two figures the man of about fifty had put on that day sheet at the fifth hour of the morning, and the two of those are halves of one thing and not the thing.

**Two hundred and ninety-six plus fifty-three. And two hundred and forty-one plus a hundred and eight.**

The two halves were not added to each other this afternoon and are not going to be added by anybody, and this holding is using that derivation and is not refusing it.

The reason is said out loud rather than left, because a man who refuses a derivation is a man who thinks the number is in doubt, and the number is on a dial that has been on that wall longer than this holding has had a book, and it said what it said this afternoon with nobody standing near it.

The names go on a board and not on a page of a book, and it is the same board it was on eleven years ago with the two rules painted on it in the same hand, and the second of the two has never once been filled, and nobody in this building has ever said aloud what would go into it if it were, including today, including with four people standing at the dial.

The second rule on that board was left empty again this afternoon. It has now stood empty twenty-one times in eleven years and nothing has ever gone into it and nobody standing near that dial has ever said a word about it, and it stood at twenty on the thirteenth of this month, which was nine mornings back, and a count of blanks is not the count of a man and has never been the count of a man in this building.

The four of them went in one at a time in the order they came up to the dial, a name into BY and then a name into WITNESSED BY and then a name into BY again and a name into WITNESSED BY again, and nobody has ever been able to say that any of them hurried, and about four people in this holding have wondered in four years whether a man who has been told that his figures are correct hurries, and about four have not wondered about it.

There were five bodies at the node this afternoon and five is the number on the page, and this was not a yard and the number is not given in a yard and is not going to be given in one.

The man of about fifty said why he had written two on the day sheet at the fifth hour and had told nobody, and he said it on Thursday and he said it again on Friday in almost the same words, and nobody improved either reading.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was there and gave him a look at the second one that about four people standing near her saw and about four did not, and he gave it back, and neither of the two of them said anything, and the second reading went into the second book as it had been said.

**"A figure which can only be arrived at one way is a figure somebody chose the way of,"** he said, **"and I would rather the two ways were on the sheet than that anybody in about four years had to trust the one. And I am not going to tell this room which of the two I take, and there is no taking to do. That dial was on that wall before this holding had a book and it said what it said this afternoon with nobody standing near it."**

The reason came first, as it has come first in that room for four years, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and ninety, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the one thing I have not said on any of these nine days, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that a second council has taken a node and a fourth county has refused to be a body, and on a morning like this one it would be very easy for a man in this room to say that the work has stopped." "It has not stopped. The four of them came up that road with a load and weighed it and put four names on a board that has been on that wall for eleven years, and not one of them is a member of this holding and not one of them was asked to be, and they did it anyway, and that is what maintenance is, and it happened this afternoon while two councils were doing something else entirely." "And I am not going to put that in a column, because there is no column for it and I have been wrong once this week about four lines and I am not going to be wrong twice in the same fortnight about a shape."

Nobody in that room thanked anybody, and nobody was thanked for the two hundreds and forty sacks, and the loaf from Friday was still on the table at the seventh hour.

Marek walked the cart road up to the node at about the first hour of the afternoon and back down at the fourth, which is about five miles each way, and the reason he gave for going up in that weather was the return, and the reason he gave for coming back in it was the same reason, and about four people in this building have noticed over four years that he walks to the node on ninth days and about four have not.

The four of them came down the road from the node and about half of them were talking about the weather, and the man of about fifty carried the day sheet in his coat pocket and did not take it out again until he was in the one room at this building, where about nine people were waiting with the door shut.

About nine people were in that room from about the seventh hour of the evening with the door shut, and the day sheet went on the long table at the wall side and nowhere else, and nobody in the room asked to see it and one man asked to see it and was given it and gave it back inside a minute.

There was a second thing at the node this afternoon that is not entered, which is that a man from the second council was on that slope at about the fourth hour with a coat he did not need on, and nobody spoke to him and he spoke to nobody, and about four bodies on that slope saw him and about four did not, and this is a fact about a slope and not a count of anybody.

The offer is on the low board, face up, and unfilled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month and has not been withdrawn since, and it carries no date and no name and no line for a name.

A second council has given an open offer as one of its reasons for not relying on this holding's account of anything, and the instrument has not been moved and has not been copied onto a second sheet and the refusal stands in the second book as it was given.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular and cannot be put down by anybody in this holding.

The order sealing the route is in the same drawer, valid, and opens nothing, and it was read out in full on Wednesday in a doorway at the node, and the route is not reopened by anything in this holding's possession.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-four and nobody in this building read it this morning, because it is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year, and it was the seventh morning of a run of mornings on which it is not read, and the count of those is not kept anywhere.

The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count stays at twenty-three, and the compost line is paid at twenty not discharged in twenty, and the word before that figure has never once disagreed with it, and the ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page and was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced and not explained.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page, and no person in this holding has put a foot inside it in four years and nobody is going to this season.
