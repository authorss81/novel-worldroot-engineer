# Chapter 405

## The Seizure

The fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour on the eighteenth, a Monday, and went away empty, there being nobody at the headland above Marden to work it, and the fields on that headland have waited for the fifty-second morning. The count rises by exactly one on a morning this line comes round and by nothing on any other morning.

The rota stands at eighty-one mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and fifty-two not, and twenty-nine and fifty-two are eighty-one.

Twenty-nine taken did not move this morning and has not moved on any morning of this year, and nobody in this holding is blamed for a field that waited, because a field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man and a man is not a field.

Two ways of counting that rota are kept in this building and the two of them do not agree, so both go on this morning's page. This is the twenty-ninth morning of the run out of the twenty-sixth of a month four months back, and the sixteenth of the run anchored on the eighteenth of a month two months back, and the frame on that second one moves when the month turns.

The six who would have been on the headland were on the boundary ditch instead, and Marek was one of the six, and both hands were full from the second hour to the fifth, and the frost on the low ground had gone by the fourth hour.

This page carries no figure for the run, because the two day-minus rules were not read out loud this morning, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. A man with a spade in his hand at the fifth hour is not a man this holding has ever asked to say two figures at the eighth.

On the two boards it is **five hundred and fifty-two days** and **five hundred and ninety-nine**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer is shut.

The eighteenth went in at the eighth hour and came back out of the other one with the frayed corner, and in four years nobody in this building has ever read back a figure that disagreed with the one that went in.

**Eight hundred and forty-eight hundredweight.**

Up nine off Sunday, and the hundred and sixty-eighth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice in sixty-eight mornings.

The window the rises and the falls are counted in stands came to a hundred and thirty-five this morning, the rise going onto the rises and leaving seventy-three rises and sixty-two falls, and seventy-three and sixty-two are a hundred and thirty-five.

A hundred and sixty-six days is how long the aggregate of the four bodies of households has stood, and the sentence for it this morning was the hundred and sixty-fifth of those mornings out of a hundred and sixty-seven. Four bodies of households and not water, and the four launders are a different aggregate, and the two have not been added in this building in four years.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the four-mile road at about the ninth hour with the barrow, turned it, and went home, and the second of the four lines in his own book is fifty-six days old and nobody here has asked him.

The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road and eleven is a floor, and a barrow's journeys and a man's feet are two figures.

A rider came up the fen road at about the tenth hour with a folded sheet under his coat and a wet saddle. He had come nine miles and said so before anybody asked him, and nobody did ask him.

The sheet was from the second council and it was four clauses long and it had been carried on Saturday night. The rider had been asked to bring it and had not been paid for it and had not been thanked for it.

> OF THE SECOND REGIONAL COUNCIL, CARRIED ON THE FIFTEENTH OF THIS MONTH BY A COUNT OF HANDS TAKEN ONCE AND NOT READ BACK. FIRST, THAT THE COUNCIL HAS RESOLVED TO TAKE INTO ITS OWN KEEPING AND ITS OWN MAINTENANCE THE NODE AT THE JUNCTION OF THE THREE DISTRICTS NAMED IN THE SCHEDULE, AND THE STONE AT IT, THE GROUND BEING GROUND THE HOUSEHOLDS OF THIS COUNCIL KEEP, AND THE COUNCIL HAVING NO POWER TO ASK ANY OFFICE OR PERSON TO PERFORM ANY ACT IN CONNECTION THEREWITH. SECOND, THAT THE COUNCIL HAS SET OUT A ROUTE BY WHICH SEED MAY PASS BETWEEN THE HOUSES AT THE WEST END AND THE HOUSES AT THE EAST END, BEING BY THE FIELD GATE, ALONG THE TOP DYK, PAST THE STONE, AND OUT BY THE LOW GATE, AND THE COUNCIL NOT HAVING NAMED IT ANYWHERE IN THIS SHEET. THIRD, THAT THE HOUSEHOLDS TO BE SERVED BY THAT ROUTE HAVE NOT BEEN ASKED, WHICH THE COUNCIL RECORDS AS A FACT AND NOT AS AN OBJECTION. FOURTH, THAT AN ENGINEER OF RECORD WHO HOLDS AN OFFER FROM THE OFFICE STANDING OPEN AND UNFILLED IS AN ENGINEER OF RECORD WHOM SOMEBODY MIGHT HAVE BOUGHT, AND THE COUNCIL GIVES THAT AS ONE OF ITS REASONS FOR NOT RELYING ON THE ACCOUNT OF THE HOLDING WHICH KEEPS THE GROUND.

It went on the long table at the wall side and nobody put it beside any of the other papers. The man of about fifty read it three times, and after the second time he said the word *maintenance* out loud to himself and nobody picked it up for about a minute.

Then he did pick it up, and he picked it up in his own mouth, which is where the whole of this morning is.

"Take into its own keeping and its own maintenance," he said. "That is the whole of the first clause and it is the only clause in four counties that anybody has ever printed the right word on. I have been in this building eleven years and I have never once seen the word *maintenance* on a piece of paper that was not a schedule from an office." "I am going to say how that clause came to be written, and I am going to give the reason for saying it first, and the reason is that the woman who keeps the seed house and the man of about fifty are the only two people in four counties who have ever used that word to a person, and the second clause of that sheet proves the room where they got it." "Taking a node is maintenance work. It is what this holding has done on a Tuesday for four years. You go out to it, you stand at it, you read what it gives you, you answer what it gives you, and you put the answer back before you leave. That is the job." "A council that has resolved to take a node has resolved to do a Tuesday, and it has had to do it correctly because the alternatives were worse. They did not know that was what they were doing, because nobody has ever printed the word on anything, and a body cannot do a thing it has not been shown the name of." "The first council asked us in writing for the work in order and we said it was not kept. We were right. Then we said nothing else, and about four of us knew at that moment that saying nothing else was also an answer."

The reason came before the thing, in that room, the way it has come before a thing in that room for four years, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and eighty-eight, and it is his.

"I am going to say the part of it that is not in this building's favor, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that in about a month a clerk is going to read that fourth clause out in a room and somebody is going to agree with him." "The seizure is legal. The route is real, the ground is theirs, the stone is theirs, and they have every right to stand at all three. Nobody in this room is going to say one word to the contrary, and I would like the man who keeps the book to enter that as what was said today rather than as what was not said."

Marek put both hands flat on the long table the way he does, and the sheet with his name at the head of it was on the other side of the room.

"And the offer is a real instrument and it is correct about what a post is. A man who has been offered a post inside an office is a man somebody might have bought. That is a true statement about a post and not a thing anybody has said about me, and I will not be able to argue with it in about a month and I cannot argue with it today."

What Tova Reed said was the only thing said in that room about the second clause, and she gave the reason before she said it, and the count of that in the holding's history is thirty-one, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say what that second clause has already cost and I am going to give the reason for saying it first, and the reason is that I found it out on Saturday morning in the seed house and not in this room, and that I have been carrying it for two days past two counties, and that is my work and not his and not anybody's in this room." "There were to have been two carts of seed at that gate on the day after tomorrow. They came off the road at the first one and stopped, because a clerk in a council nine miles off has told a carter that nothing crosses ground that council has taken. The carter believed him and went home." "And both of those councils are right, and that is the part I have not been able to put down since Saturday. The road is cut and the cut is correct. Nobody lied about the road and nobody has to be sorry. Two councils have found out about a road on the same morning from two different clerks, and the woman who found out is me."

Nobody thanked her. The man of about fifty wrote the whole of it down and read it back and did not improve it, and about four people in that room had understood it before she said it and about four understood it when she finished, and neither of those two fours has ever been asked which one it was.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in the room on Monday. She was at the north gate with a bar and a chain and the woman who keeps the seed house, on a thing that is not entered anywhere, and the man of about seventy-four with a stick stood in the door of this room for an hour of it.

Then the clerk of this holding opened the second drawer from the left and the sheet that claims the corridor came out of it.

The second paragraph of it is a table of six places and the third gives a measurement at each of the six, and every figure in that table can be checked against a page in one of the four books inside a quarter of an hour, and none of them is wrong. It does not say the corridor is open and it does not say it is shut. It says what the corridor is, in the only words anybody has ever had for it, and nothing has ever come out of it that this holding has been able to use.

A county asked in writing on Saturday whether that corridor is open. The answer this holding can give is where the answer is not. It is not in any of the four books and not on that sheet and not in this room, and the whole of what Marek has to say about it is short.

"I can show a council that the thing is there. I cannot show a council that it is open, and I cannot show a council that it is shut, and the second of those is the one they want and I have never had it in four years." He put his hand flat on the paper without touching the writing. "What I have got is a page that is correct, and a correct page is not a door. It is a door with the hinges described."

Nobody in that room improved it and nobody argued that the sheet was a lie, because it is not one. About four people in that room have wanted for four years to find one thing wrong with it, and about four have stopped wanting.

The offer sheet was on the low board at the far end of that room through all of it, face up, and it was not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month, and it has not been withdrawn.

The refusal was entered in the second book at about the sixth hour of the afternoon in the words it was given in and was not improved, and the offer was not moved off the board and not turned over and not copied onto a second sheet.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and naming it this morning did not open it. The route in the second clause of that council's sheet is a different thing from the corridor and is not called that, and the two of them are on two pieces of paper in this room and are not the same thing and are not added.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page.

Nobody asked the man of about thirty-one of Silling anything this afternoon, and the barrow stands at eleven journeys and eleven is a floor, and a barrow's journeys and a man's feet are two figures and are not added to each other on any morning of the year.

Four counties have asked this holding a question in a fortnight and not one office has lied and not one document has been wrong, and about four people in that room have wanted for four years to find one thing wrong with that sheet and about four have stopped wanting, and the four that stopped are not going to start again this month.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the eighteenth, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WENT AWAY EMPTY, THERE BEING NOBODY AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN, AND THOSE FIELDS HAVE WAITED FOR THE FIFTY-SECOND MORNING. THE ROTA STANDS AT EIGHTY-ONE MORNINGS IN FORCE WITH TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND FIFTY-TWO NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE TAKEN DID NOT MOVE. BOTH COUNTS OF THE ROTATION ARE ENTERED AND DO NOT AGREE, BEING THE TWENTY-NINTH MORNING OF A RUN OUT OF THE TWENTY-SIXTH OF A MONTH FOUR MONTHS BACK AND THE SIXTEENTH SINCE THE EIGHTEENTH OF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED EIGHT HUNDRED AND FORTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHTH FIGURE, AND HAS RISEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIVE, BEING SEVENTY-THREE RISES AND SIXTY-TWO FALLS, AND THE TWO ADD. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX DAYS, WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIFTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SEVEN. THE PAIR STANDS AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-TWO DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND NINETY-NINE, FORTY-SEVEN APART. A SHEET OF FOUR CLAUSES CAME UP THE FEN ROAD FROM THE SECOND COUNCIL, CARRIED ON THE FIFTEENTH BY A COUNT OF HANDS TAKEN ONCE AND NOT READ BACK. IT TAKES A NODE AND THE STONE AT IT INTO ITS OWN KEEPING AND ITS OWN MAINTENANCE. IT SETS OUT A ROUTE FOR SEED IN GATES AND DISTANCES AND DOES NOT NAME IT. IT RECORDS THAT THE HOUSEHOLDS TO BE SERVED HAVE NOT BEEN ASKED. AND IT GIVES AN ENGINEER HOLDING AN OPEN OFFER AS ONE REASON FOR NOT RELYING ON THIS HOLDING'S ACCOUNT OF ANYTHING. THE MAN WHO KEEPS THE BOOK GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT TAKING A NODE IS MAINTENANCE WORK, AND THAT A COUNCIL WHICH HAS RESOLVED TO TAKE ONE HAS RESOLVED TO DO A TUESDAY WITHOUT KNOWING THAT WAS WHAT IT WAS DOING, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THE SEIZURE IS LEGAL AND THE OFFER IS CORRECT ABOUT WHAT A POST IS, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-EIGHT. THE SEEDWRIGHT GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT TWO CARTS OF SEED TURNED OFF THE ROAD ON SATURDAY BECAUSE A CLERK TOLD A CARTER THAT NOTHING CROSSES TAKEN GROUND, AND THAT BOTH COUNCILS ARE RIGHT, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS THIRTY-ONE. THE SHEET THAT CLAIMS THE CORRIDOR WAS PUT ON THE TABLE AND SAYS NEITHER OPEN NOR SHUT.**
