# Chapter 408

## The Two Halves Are Not Added

The twenty-first of this month was a Thursday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The long drain on the west side of the third field has not been on the rota since a month now two months back, and four people went down it this morning and found a swallow's nest at the second bend and a boot at the third, and neither of them went into any book.

They took the nest out on a stick and set it on the bank, and the bird came back to it inside a quarter of an hour and stood on the edge of it for about a minute before going in.

The boot was a left one and had been in about nine inches of water long enough to have lost its heel, and the man at the front of it said that a boot tells you a season and not a year, and the three behind him did not take it up. It was still in the bank on Friday morning and nobody came back for it and nobody has come back for it since.

Marek was one of the four and had the ditching tool from the second hour to the fifth, and the boot at the third bend was still in the bank when he came off it.

At about the seventh hour the two day-minus rules were read out loud before either of the numbers, one man taking the first board and another taking the second. They stand at **five hundred and fifty-five days** and **six hundred and two**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

Fifty-six mornings out of ninety-five, counted from the seventeenth of a month three months back.

The twenty-first went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other one, and nobody said anything when it did, because there is nothing anybody in this building has ever said when two books agree.

**Eight hundred and forty-seven hundredweight.**

Down five off Wednesday, and the hundred and seventy-first figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the first morning of it.

A hundred and thirty-eight is the window, being seventy-four rises and sixty-four falls, the fall having gone onto the falls, and about four people in this holding have added those two in their own heads every morning for four years and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has ever said which.

The sentence for this morning went onto the sheet for the hundred and sixty-eighth of those mornings out of a hundred and seventy, and it says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and sixty-nine days. Four bodies of households and not the launders, and the two aggregates have not shared a line of a page in this building in four years and did not share one this morning.

He came down the four-mile road with the barrow at the ninth hour, turned it at the top of the yard, and went home without a word to anybody.

Fifty-nine days is the age of the second of the four lines in his own book this morning and it is blank, and the count of people who have asked him about it is nil and has been nil for four years.

The frost had gone off the low ground by the time he came, which it had not on Saturday, and the four-mile road was going hard in about nine inches of its length and soft in the other three miles, and he came down it at his own speed and did not hurry it and was not asked to hurry it. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road, and eleven is a floor and did not rise this morning.

About nine people were in the one room from about the second hour of the afternoon with the door shut. The reason for the meeting was on the long table at the wall side and it was a sheet of the seed house's own, four lines long, with a date on it and a day of the week on it and nothing else.

> LOT OF THE TWELFTH, HELD AT THE SEED HOUSE, TWO CARTS TO CROSS AT THE LOW GATE ON THE TWENTIETH. TWO CARTS DID NOT CROSS. NO REASON IS ASKED FOR AND NONE IS GIVEN HERE. THE LOT REMAINS WHERE IT IS AND THE ROAD IS NOT USED AGAIN UNTIL A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS SAYS IN WRITING THAT IT MAY BE. THE SEED HOUSE IS NOT A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS AND IS NOT ONE OF THE FOUR, AND SIGNS NOTHING.

The fourth line of that sheet has been on the wall of a seed house for eleven years and nobody outside this building has read it, and about four people in this room have known it was there for four years and about four have not.

Tova Reed brought it up herself at about the first hour of the afternoon and did not sit down while she was in the room, and she gave the reason before she said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is thirty-four, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say what has happened to a road and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that four counties have spent a fortnight finding out what this holding does not know, and the only person in this room who has found anything out this week is me, and I found it out in a room with four doors." "Two carts of seed were to have crossed at the low gate on Wednesday. They turned off on Saturday morning and went home. A clerk in a council nine miles off has told the carter that nothing crosses ground that council has taken, and the carter believed him and went home, and both of those things were correct on the morning they happened." "What that means for me is that I have two hundred and forty sacks of nothing to do with between the first of next month and the second, because that is when the next lot was coming, and I cannot put a second lot on that road, and I am not going to. That is the whole of what has happened and I have not put a number on it and nobody in this room has asked me for one."

Marek gave the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and eighty-nine, and it is his and of nobody else's, and the boot in the ditch at the third bend is still in the bank and nobody has come back for it.

"I am going to say the one thing I can say about that and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that in about a month this holding is going to be asked by two counties at once whether it can move seed, and the honest answer is no, and the reason the answer is no is that this building has never moved a sack of anything in its life." "It has moved water and it has moved silt and it has moved a form four miles in a drawer. What it has not done is carried a thing for a body of households along a road that a council had not thought about, and a road that a council has not thought about is not a road this holding has, it is a road that happens to be underfoot."

Then the man of about fifty explained a derivation, and he had been asked for it on Saturday and had said not yet, and on Thursday he said it, and he gave the reason before he explained it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and eighteen, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to explain how I get two figures for a ninth-day return, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that there is going to be a return on Friday and I would rather this room heard it from me than worked it out on the wall."

The dial at the node is a day's walk east of this building and it is not in this room, and about four people in that room had it in their heads anyway.

"The first way is what the four bodies of households say they sent. Havergate's own book, Low Wether's own book, and two books that are Marden's and Silling's in the sense that they are the books this holding has kept for Marden and Silling, which is a different thing, and about four people in four counties do not know it is a different thing." "The second way is the dial. Two hundred and eleven years the dial has not been wrong about what came off a cart in one load, and I do not mean that as a figure. I mean that a load goes on, a load comes off, and the number on the wall between those two is the number." "Those are two halves and they are not added. If you add them you have a figure of four hundred and something that describes nothing on this holding and that is the only way anybody in four years has been able to get a wrong number out of that wall, and it happened once, in a month four years back, and I merged the two myself and wrote down that I had merged them."

He stopped there and the room waited, and about four people in it had been waiting all week for him to say which of the two he takes his figure from, and about four had known before they sat down that he was not going to say.

"I am not going to say which of the two I believe, and I said on Saturday that I would not and I am not. There is no believing to do. The dial was on that wall before this holding had a book and it said what it said on Friday with nobody standing near it, and both of the two of these are arithmetic, and the arithmetic is not the finding." "Write them both down. That is the whole of the derivation and it has taken me eleven years and two people asking me the wrong question to be able to say it in a room, and if either of you thinks I have improved it, I have not, and I would like it read back exactly as I have said it."

Nobody said a word about it. About four people in that room had put the two halves together in their own heads inside a minute of the man finishing, and about four had not, and the four that had were looking at the boot in the ditch through the window and not at him.

The woman who keeps the seed house asked him one question then, and she asked it in the plain way she asks everything, which is as a fact about a sack and not as a position about a derivation.

"Whose books are the two you called this holding's?" "Marden's and Silling's are the books this holding has kept for Marden and Silling since before I came, and they were kept by a man who is dead, and Marden and Silling have not sent anybody up that road in four years to look at them. They send a load every ninth day and they do not send a person." "Then the first way of getting that number is a number two of your four bodies have never seen. Has anybody said that out loud to a carter?" "Nobody has said it out loud to a carter and nobody has said it out loud to a county either, and I am not going to be the man who teaches it to a clerk in nine miles first, because the first man to point it out is the man who has made it a thing, and then it is a habit in four counties inside a year."

Nobody argued with him about that, and about four people in that room had known that was the answer before he gave it and about four had not, and neither of those two fours has ever been asked which one it was. The man of about fifty put his thumb on the page he had written and said that the entry would go in as a derivation and not as a finding, and that the difference is that a derivation can be checked by a person who does not believe it.

He read his own account back into the second book word for word, including the two sentences about the two books, and then he sat down, which he does not do.

The man of about seventy-four with a stick stood in the door for the whole of it and said one thing at about the fifth hour, and what he said was that a man who explains how he gets a number ought to be asked to say the number out loud in a room, and the man of about fifty said the number is on the dial and the dial is nine hundred yards off a different door, and neither of them improved it.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in the room on Thursday. She was on the west dyke with the woman who keeps the seed house, and there is nothing about that afternoon in this book and nobody has asked for it.

Nobody thanked anybody. Tova Reed was not thanked for two hundred and forty sacks of nothing to do with and she did not expect to be, and the man of about fifty was not thanked for a derivation he has been carrying for eleven years.

About four people in that room had known for four years that he was carrying it and about four had not, and the four that knew are not going to say so, and nobody in this building has ever asked him to write it down and nobody is going to this season. The woman who keeps the seed house came in at about the sixth hour with bread and put it on the long table at the wall side, and about four people in that room had already been waiting for it since the fourth hour and about four had not.

The offer is on the low board at the far end of that room, face up, and it is not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month and has not been withdrawn, and a second council has given it as one of its reasons for not relying on this holding's account of anything, and the instrument has not moved and has not been copied onto a second sheet.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular and cannot be put down by anybody in this holding, and the route set out in a council's resolution on Saturday is not that corridor and does not have that name.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and a derivation is not a use and a boot in a ditch is not a use, and the reason for the fifteen is not given in this holding in a season. The requests stand at fifty-three and the section-nine notes stand at fifty-three, and a sheet of four lines brought up from a seed house by hand is a request and not a fifty-fourth of anything.

What this holding cannot check is two figures and what it does not know is six things, and a road that a council has cut, two hundred and forty sacks, a cart that turned off it, and a clerk who was right are none of them a third of the one and none of them a seventh of the other.

The room emptied at about the seventh hour and the door was shut for the last hour of it. Marek did not stay to the end of the reading back, and went down to the drain at the third bend at about the sixth hour and stood over the boot for a while and then went up again, and about four people in this building have noticed in four years that he goes and looks at a thing after a room has told him something he did not know, and about four have not noticed.

The arm is four inches and it branches twice, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning.

A derivation is not a mark, a sack is not a mark, a boot is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled and nothing said in that room this afternoon is a fifth of anything.
