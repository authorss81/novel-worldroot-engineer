# Chapter 440

## A Shortage

The twenty-third of this month was a Monday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The compost from the north end to the sluice took six people from the second hour to the seventh hour and it is the second time in this season that the whole length of it has been done in five hours, and the six of them were on the high side of it all morning and the water was on the low side and it did not come across.

Marek was one of the six and had the bar for four of those five hours and his forearm did not ache and about four people in this holding have stopped remarking on that and about four have not.

The two boards were read aloud at the seventh hour before either figure was named, one man to each, and the run of mornings is **seventy-two of a hundred and twenty-seven**, and the pair stands at **five hundred and eighty-seven days** and **six hundred and thirty-four**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The twenty-third went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and the hour was the hour it has been for a fortnight and the man of about fifty wrote the day against it before he wrote the hour.

**Nine hundred and eleven hundredweight.**

Down five off Sunday, and the two hundred and third figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and the two hundred and fourth is on the morning of the twenty-fourth and is the last of them in this month.

The fall went onto the falls. Ninety and eighty make a hundred and seventy, and that is the whole of the window and the two halves of it have never been added to the figure above them.

The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in two hundred and one days and was written for the two hundredth of those mornings out of two hundred and two.

The woman who keeps the seed house counted the seed house on Friday evening and counted it again on Saturday morning and did not tell anybody either time, and about four people in this holding have known since Friday that she was counting it and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has asked her.

Tova Reed came up the fen road on the Saturday with a cart of her own and was in the seed house from the second hour of the afternoon until about the sixth, and she did not go into the one room and she did not go into the yard and nobody in this holding has said that she was avoiding a room.

The figure came up to the one room at about the seventh hour on the Saturday evening and it came in her own hand and it was one line and it had a figure in it and a date, and the man of about fifty wrote it into the sheet of this holding in his own hand and read it back and did not add a word to it and did not take one out.

> THE SHEET OF THIS HOLDING, THE ENTRY FOR THE TWENTY-THIRD OF THIS MONTH, IN THE HAND OF THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, AND THE ENTRY IS A FIGURE AND A DATE AND A HEADING OF ONE WORD AND NOTHING ELSE: **A SHORTAGE. OF SEED. AGAINST A SEASON OF DISCONNECTION ACROSS FOUR COUNTIES, THE FIGURE BEING ONE THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE FIGURE GIVEN AND PUBLISHED BY TOVA REED ON THE TWELFTH OF THIS MONTH OUT OF FOUR ACCOUNTS OF HER OWN THAT DO NOT AGREE AT THE SECOND FIGURE, AND IS NOT A FIGURE OF A HARVEST, AND IS NOT A FIGURE OF A GROUND, AND IS NOT A FIGURE OF PEOPLE. THE DATE IS THE TWENTY-THIRD OF THIS MONTH. NO BODY IS NAMED AGAINST IT. NO CAUSE IS ENTERED AGAINST IT. NO UNIT IS GIVEN BESIDE THE ONE THERE IS.**

That is the first shortage this holding has had since it began keeping books, and the sheet says so in the words it was given in and does not say what it is like, and this page does not say what it is like either.

The number of the things that are not in the buildings is a number of a building and a room and a sack, and the woman who keeps the seed house counted in sacks because sacks are what she has, and the count of the sacks is not on the sheet and is not going to be, because a sack is a thing four counties can check and a shortage is not a thing four counties can check this morning and the two of them are not going to be written in the same hand.

There is still no word from anybody about what a person plants instead in a shortage of seed, nobody in this holding has been asked, and nobody is going to be, and about four people in four counties have asked each other that question in the last nine days and about four have not, and neither of the two fours has been told anything by this building.

The fourth county wrote in about the figure on the Saturday and its letter is one line long and the line says that the figure is noted and that the county will send its own against a fourth, and it did not say which fourth, and the clerk of this holding read it twice and did not supply one, and the clerk of the fourth county has not been thanked and has not asked to be and the letter is in the drawer with the other papers in it.

The word that four counties have used for it in the last nine days is not on any page in this holding and it is not going to be, and about four people in four counties have said that the figure on that sheet is a figure of a season and not a figure of a week and about four have said that a season has started and a week has not, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

Tova Reed gave the reason before she said the thing and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-four, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say what that figure is and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I published the same number on the twelfth in a room with the door shut and there are now about four people in four counties who have written it down as a cost."** "It is the same number. It is not a second number and it is not a worse number and it is not the number it will be in a month. It is one thousand one hundred and sixty hundredweight of seed, and this morning it is a quantity that is not in a building in this county, and I am the only person in four counties who can say how much is not in the buildings, and I have said it, and nobody is going to put a second figure beside it this month."

Nobody thanked her. Nobody in that room said she was the only one who could have given it, nobody put a hand on her arm, nobody took the seed work off her, and nobody in that room said he was sorry about anything, and nobody has said he was sorry about anything in eleven years.

The fourth part of the protocols is three days old this morning and it is the part with the heading the clerk rewrote on Friday night with the charter's own words in it, and it says who holds a ground and on what paper, and the first thing it has been tested by is a shortage, and about four people in this holding have said that three days is too short to know anything and about four have said that three days is the only length anybody has ever had.

Nobody in this holding can say what the fourth part of the protocols would do with a shortage, and the clerk of this holding said so on Saturday and said it in a sentence and has not said it since, and the reason he has not said it since is that anybody who says it twice in a week is asking a second question.

The clerk of this holding had one other thing in that letter and read it out at the seventh hour, and it was that the protocols go to four counties and the shortage is on a sheet in one of them, and that a document can be read by a body and a shortage cannot, and that in about a fortnight somebody in a county is going to join those two things in a sentence and the sentence will be wrong in a way this building cannot correct with a letter.

The barrow came up the four-mile road at about the ninth hour this morning and went down it loaded, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling brought it, and the second of the four lines in his own book is ninety-one days old and has nothing on it, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in ninety-one days and nobody is going to this month.

It went down loaded with nine sacks and came back up with the same barrow and about four of the nine sacks and the other five were left at a gate four miles off and the five of them were left by a man who is not in this holding and whose name is not on any page in this building, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came back up with the barrow and said nothing about the five sacks and did not write the five sacks anywhere and was not asked.

Marek was in the yard at the top of the road at the ninth hour and watched it come up and did not go down the road to meet it and did not offer to, and about four people in this holding have said that he should have and about four have said that a man who goes down a four-mile road in the ninth hour of the morning after a day like that is a man who is not going to be any use to anybody on Tuesday.

It is eleven journeys up that road and it has been eleven since before anybody in this holding started counting and eleven is a floor and it was not raised this morning, and about four people in four counties have asked in the last nine days how much can be carried up it in a week and about four have not asked, and this holding has not worked the figure out and is not going to this month and there is no column for it.

He said it in the one room at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-first and this page gives the whole of what he said, and it is the same sentence as the one said in this room in a month that is now three months back and the page says that and does not improve on it.

**"It has not come up and nobody has been down and nobody is going to be, and nobody in this holding has ever seen what is under it."**

The sentence was said once before and it is said here the second time and it is not improved and it is not going to be improved, and no figure of people was given for it on either morning and none is given for it now, and there is a number in the name of that thing and the number is of households and not of people and nobody in this building has ever worked out what it is of.

Nobody in that room said the vault would have answered it, and about four people in that room have thought so and about four have said so out loud, and neither of the two fours has been asked, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling said it in about nine words and did not improve it afterward and asked the man of about fifty whether the sentence was going in the book and the man of about fifty said that it was going in the book exactly as it had been said, and that exchange is the whole of what either of them said to the other.

Marek gave the reason before he said the thing and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and seventeen, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about that sheet and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that this holding has spent nine days writing nine columns and has spent this morning writing one word, and the one word is worse than the nine columns because it is a fact about what is not in a building."** "A list of nine duties can be checked by a body in a county this afternoon. A shortage cannot be checked until somebody plants something and does not get it up, and that is not a season, and I am not going to say what it is. The charter is three days old and the shortest of these is a document, and a document is not a seed, and nobody in this room is going to be asked to pretend otherwise this month."

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody in this holding has said that the charter will hold and nobody has said that it will not, and a charter three days old and a shortage in the same room on the same evening is the whole of what there is on the table and neither of the two is going to be improved on.

The two rates for that node are not reconciled and the list carries both with a mark between them and this holding uses neither and no total is stated, and a shortage is not a total and the two of those are not added to each other.

The four ruled lines under the condition of the man of the north row with the cough were empty when the answer fell due on this morning under the twelfth clause of the protocols, and the clerk entered the answer in the words of the clause, which is that it is an answer that does not exist and is not a delay and is not a refusal, and he read that entry back once and did not read it back twice, and nobody in this building has asked that man anything about it and he was not in the room.

The register form is on the middle table at ninety-seven days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it. The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and a shortage is not that instrument and there is no second instrument of that offer on any table in this holding this season.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-six and was not read this morning, and it is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year, and the sixth of the six blank columns beside it has a heading at the top of it and nothing under it, and one word on a sheet is neither of those two columns and has not been entered in either of them.

The compost line is paid and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one and it measures nothing, and a shortage of seed is not a measurement of the compost line and the two of those have never been added in this holding and are not going to be this month.

A sheet in a drawer claims a corridor in the pan and is correct in every particular it states, and the order sealing the route is in that drawer and is valid and opens nothing, and a shortage does not open a route and does not close one.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page and was not walked, measured, crossed, priced or explained this morning, and the ground on both sides of it is going wrong and going slower and nobody in this holding has called slower better, and a shortage on a sheet nine hundred yards off has not been put against that ground in any column in this building.

Eleven journeys is a floor, and the second of the four lines in that man's own book is ninety-one days old with nothing on it, and a shortage is not a mark, a charter is not a mark and a name under a word is not a mark. There are four accounts of that arm and they do not reconcile with one another, and the only thing the man of about fifty wrote on any sheet this evening was a figure and a date and one word, and the word is not one of the four.
