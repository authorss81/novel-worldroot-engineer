# Chapter 435

## A Chamber Of Nine

The eighteenth of this month was a Wednesday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The west side of the boundary ditch took six people from the second hour to the seventh hour and took a foot and a half out of it in two days, and Marek was not one of the six, and that is the second morning in four years on which he has not been one of them.

The two men who take the boards were in the one room from the fourth hour with the sheet from Friday and the roll of the protocols and did not come out of it, and the pair is read out at the seventh hour on the four mornings of a fortnight it is read on and this is not one of them.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and eighty-two days** and **six hundred and twenty-nine**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened and this page carries no figure for the run of mornings.

The eighteenth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and eight hundredweight.**

Up nine off Tuesday, and the hundred and ninety-eighth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The rise went onto the rises. Eighty-eight and seventy-seven make a hundred and sixty-five.

The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and ninety-six days and was written for the hundred and ninety-fifth of those mornings out of a hundred and ninety-seven.

He went east on the bicycle at the first hour and got to that node about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and the road is a cart road and not the four miles, and the two of them are not the same road, and nobody in this holding has added his hours to anybody else's in four years.

The last mile of that road is under water about two inches deep at the fourth hour and has been since the water came through the yard on the morning before, and he walked the bicycle the last mile rather than ride it, and the two of the wheels that have been loose since a month now three months back were still loose at the gate and he did not stop to do anything about them because there is no column in this holding that says what a loose wheel is.

The light at that node goes early because of the wood on the west of it, and the chamber had one lamp in it and the lamp was on a bench and not on the table, and about four of the nine had their faces to the lamp and about four did not, and the two men whose voices the count turned on were one of each.

The multi-region assembly began its sitting on this morning and it is to sit three days, and it is a body and its people are not named, and there are nine in the chamber and about four outside it and the nine are nine places at a table and not nine men.

The chamber is a room with one door and four walls and a stove in the corner that was lit at some point before anybody came in and not by anybody in the chamber, and the nine sat with the door shut and it was shut for about four hours and it was opened twice and both of those times were for a person who came back in.

Tova Reed was in the chamber. She was asked at about the fifth hour of the afternoon to count the votes of the four regional councils and to publish the count, and nobody suggested that anybody else should do it, and nobody in that room said one word about her left ear, and nobody in that room is going to.

The count was asked of her and not of the clerk because the four councils had each sent a number and the four numbers were four different numbers, and a number sent from a council is a number about the room it was sent from, and the disagreement between the four of them is not a disagreement anybody can settle by adding.

She said the reason for taking it before she said that she would take it, and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-two, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say why I can be useful in that room and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that there is a figure only I can give and it is what a season of disconnection costs in seed, and every one of these four bodies has asked me for it and I have not given it to any of them and I am not going to today."** "That is why they will give a number to me and not to a clerk. It is not because I am careful. It is because I am the one who has something they want, and a person who has something you want counts your hands more honestly than a person who has nothing, and if that is not true then this morning was a waste of a room."

The clause that was argued for the longest part of that afternoon is the one about what happens when a body says no.

It had been drafted in a shape that would have made a declining an appeal. The shape was that where a body declines an inspection, the declining shall be entered and referred, and referred went to a place that is not in that room.

The argument about it took most of the second hour and about half of the third, and it was not an argument about whether a body may say no. Everybody in that room had accepted inside a week that a body may say no, and one of the nine said so out loud in the first ten minutes as a thing that had been settled before anybody arrived, and it was settled and it did not help them at all, because the clause on the table was not a clause about whether.

It was a clause about what a no is once it has been said.

The argument on one side of it was that a declining that is referred is not a declining at all, it is a request that somebody else decide, and that a request that somebody else decide is a thing a body ought to have to make in writing with a reason, and that the making of it in writing is the price of having said no.

The argument on the other side was that a body which has to write to a place it does not sit in to say it will not be inspected has not been asked, and that the reason nobody in that room had noticed the shape of the clause until this afternoon was that everybody in it had been on the receiving end of an instrument rather than on the end of a body, and that an instrument reads exactly the same from both sides until somebody is on the wrong one.

About four people in that chamber thought that the second of those was a speech and about four thought it was a fact, and neither of those two fours has been asked, and the count of the nine did not move while it was being said.

The clause was put. Two of the nine sat on the side it came from and it went to a division of the nine and the division was close and the number of it depended on one sentence that Marek did not hear from where he was standing in the doorway, and he heard a voice on the far side and then he heard a voice on the near side and he could not tell which of the two had said the sentence the count turned on.

She heard the same two voices and she could not put a sound on either of them, and the whole of the count of that morning turned on who had said it.

She did not ask either man to speak again. She asked the man on the near side to read the clause out, and while he was reading it she watched him and watched the other one, and about nine people in a room of nine are the only witnesses there are going to be.

The one on the far side moved his mouth twice while the other was reading, and the other moved his mouth not at all while he was reading, and she said so, in the room, with the two of them looking at her, and the count she gave was the count her own mouth gave her and not the count her ear would have given her.

**"I am going to give the count and I am going to give the way I got it first, and the reason is that the count is going into a thing four counties can check and a count without a way is a number anybody can argue with instead of a number anybody can look at."** "Seven and two on that clause, and I got it by mouths and not by ears, and I have said out loud in this room which of the two of you read and which of the two of you did not, and one of you can tell me I have got it wrong and I would rather you did that here than in about four years."

Nobody in that room told her she had got it wrong. The clause survived the afternoon by one, and it is in the draft protocols, and the number seven and the number two are on a page that four bodies of people now hold.

The count of a division of nine is not a count of reasons and the nine in that chamber were not asked to give one, and the page does not say that the seven on the side it came from were right and the two were wrong, and nobody in that room thought they were, and the two who went the other way said out loud that they would be asked about it in about a fortnight and they have not been asked yet.

A published count is a different thing from a count, and she said the difference out loud before the sheet was carried out of the room, and she gave the reason for saying it first and the count of that in this holding's history is forty-three, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say what this number is before it leaves the room, and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that a number with a way attached to it survives a person and a number without one does not."** "Seven and two is what nine bodies did with their hands this afternoon. It is not what they thought. Four of these nine have told me in the last nine days that they think the clause is wrong and three of those four put a hand up for it, and I am not going to take that off the page and I am not going to add it to the page either, because the moment a hand and a thought go in the same column a body in a county can check one of them and not the other, and the one they can check is the one that will get looked at."

Nobody in that room argued with that either. The sheet went out at the eighth hour of the evening with the number on it and the way on it, and the four counties have it by the ordinary post on Saturday, and the number seven and two is in a county book and the sentence that goes with it is in the same county book, and the sentence is hers and was not improved after she said it.

Marek said one thing about it from the doorway afterwards and gave the reason for it before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and twelve, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say what I saw and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been standing in a doorway for four hours and there is a man in this holding who put a heading on a column five mornings ago because a body in a county could not see what was in it, and a man in a doorway is the same kind of problem in a worse place."** "I could not tell which of those two men said the sentence the count turned on, and the woman who could not tell either worked it out by watching their mouths in about a minute and a half, and I have been doing this for eleven years and I have never once worked a room out that way, and I am not going to be the person in this holding who suggests she is exempted from a count because of an ear when the count is a thing that gets published."

Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody in that chamber put a hand on her arm and nobody in that chamber said he was sorry about anything, and nobody has said he was sorry about anything in eleven years, and the seed work is still hers and nobody in that room suggested otherwise and nobody is going to.

The four people of the Assembly held their strip on the north side of that node from the fifth hour of the afternoon until the light went, about nine feet outside the ring of bare ground and about four yards wide, and they stopped about six yards short of the edge of it and nobody told them to and nobody has said why.

The ground on both sides of that ring is going wrong and going slower than it was in the ninth, and the ring is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page and was not walked, measured, crossed, priced or explained this afternoon by anybody in that chamber or outside it.

The count of the four bodies of households on that node is five and there were more than five bodies on it this afternoon and the number on this page is five because a count of five is a fact about a rota and not a count of a morning, and it is not given in a yard and there was no yard on that node this afternoon.

He came back on the bicycle at the eighth hour and put it against the wall by the door and the barrow came up the four-mile road at the ninth hour and was turned at the top of the yard, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling brought that one and not this one, and eleven journeys is a floor, and the second of the four lines in his own book is eighty-six days old this morning and has nothing on it and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in eighty-six days.

The register form is on the middle table at ninety-two days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it. The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and a count of seven and two is not that instrument.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-six and was not read this morning, and it is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year, and the sixth of the six blank columns beside it has a heading at the top of it and nothing under it, and the two of those things were done on the eleventh and nothing today improved either of them.

The compost line is paid and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one and it measures nothing, and a count of hands is not a measurement of a ground and the two of those figures have never been added to each other in this holding and are not going to be this month.

A sheet in a drawer claims a corridor in the pan and is correct in every particular it states, and the order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and it opens nothing, and a clause about what happens when a body says no is not a clause about a route and does not open one.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice, and a division of nine is not a mark, a clause is not a mark, a count is not a mark and a mouth moving in a room of nine is not a mark, and there are four accounts of that arm in this building and they do not reconcile with one another and nothing said in that chamber this afternoon is a fifth of anything.
