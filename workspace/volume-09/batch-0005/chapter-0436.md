# Chapter 436

## Three Hundred And Eighty-Two

The nineteenth of this month was a Thursday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The low drain from the north end to the sluice took six people from the second hour to the sixth hour, and it is the third time in this season that the whole length of it has been done in four hours and a half, and nobody in this holding has entered the three of them in the same column and nobody is going to.

Marek was one of the six and had the shovel for two of those hours and the bar for two, and the two figures he was on are in the book and he could not have told anybody which of the four hours was which.

There was a wind off the north for the whole of that morning and the six of them were digging into the north wall of that drain, which is the wrong way round, and one of the six said so out loud and the other five heard him and the man at the head of it said that the wind would drop by the fourth hour and it did not drop by the fourth hour.

The two boards were read aloud at the seventh hour before either figure was named, one man to each, and the run of mornings is **seventy of a hundred and twenty-three**, and the pair stands at **five hundred and eighty-three days** and **six hundred and thirty**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The nineteenth went in at about the eighth hour, a fortnight of mornings now on the same hour, and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and three hundredweight.**

Down five off Wednesday, and the hundred and ninety-ninth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and eleven of the last twenty have been above the one before them and nine have not and no morning has been the same as the morning before it in four years.

The fall went onto the falls. Eighty-eight and seventy-eight make a hundred and sixty-six.

The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and ninety-seven days and was written for the hundred and ninety-sixth of those mornings out of a hundred and ninety-eight.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling took the sheet of nine columns down the cart road on the bicycle at about the second hour of the afternoon and the second of the four lines in his own book is eighty-seven days old this morning and has nothing on it, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in eighty-seven days and nobody is going to this month.

The four bodies of households on that node had each sent a number in before this morning and the four numbers had come in on the ordinary post on the fifteenth and the sixteenth, and three of the four were the same and one of the four was two different numbers, and the one that was two was not the fourth county's, and nobody in this holding has worked out which of the four it was and nobody has asked.

The three counties that have councils and the county that has refused to form one all sent one, and the difference between what four bodies sent and what nine people did with their hands yesterday afternoon is a difference of about a week and a day, and it is on a page and it is correct, and this holding is not going to reconcile it because it was not in the room.

The return came off the steelyard at that node at about the fourth hour of the afternoon.

**Three hundred and eighty-two hundredweight.**

It is the fortieth of them in eleven years and it is the thirtieth pair of names, and it is eleven above the thirty-ninth, and the rise is eleven and has been eleven since a month that is now four months back and it is not twelve and has never been twelve.

It was reconciled twice and the two of those are not added to each other. It came off the steelyard as three hundred and one and eighty-one, and it was also three hundred and fifty-nine and one hundred and twenty-three, and the two of those are two ways of standing at the same place and not two loads, and a man who adds them has got a figure of about seven hundred and sixty-four hundredweight out of a return that weighed three hundred and eighty-two and has never weighed anything else.

The four went up to the dial one at a time in the order they came, and the first and the third under the upper rule and the second and the fourth under the rule beneath it, and a fifth held a lamp and did not put it down, and there are five on that dial and the fifth is not one of the four and has not been entered.

The second rule on the board at that node was left empty for the twenty-fourth time in eleven years.

Nobody has ever said out loud what would go in that space and nobody in this holding has been asked and nobody is going to ask. A count of blanks is not the count of a man, and it has never been, and the twenty-four is a count of returns and not a count of anything else, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling stood in front of that board for about a minute with the sheet of nine columns in his hand and did not write in it and went and put the barrow away.

He had carried that sheet down there to have it looked at by a body that had not looked at it, and it was looked at, and about four people at that node said that the sixth column being empty was the first thing they had understood about a list and about four said it was an excuse, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling heard both of the two fours and carried the sheet back up the four miles on the bicycle without saying one word to anybody about either of them.

He turned it round at the top of the yard, and the sixth column of that sheet is empty from the top of it to the bottom of it and there are four small crosses in the margin of it and there is nothing in that column and there is not going to be anything in that column this month, and the sheet that goes to a county is a copy of that sheet and the copy carries the crosses.

The second day of that sitting began at about the second hour of the afternoon and a motion was moved in the first half hour of it, and the motion was to table the whole question.

Nobody in that chamber opposed the motion being moved. About four of the nine had wanted it moved for a fortnight and about four had wanted it moved and not to be the one who moved it, and the two of those fours are not added to each other and neither has been asked.

The arguments for it were that three days is three days and that a body which has sat through three days has spent three days, and that the question in front of them is not a question that can be finished in three days and that finishing a thing that cannot be finished is worse than not starting it inside a room of nine.

The arguments against it were that the question had been in front of four counties since a month that is now three months back and had been given an instrument three times, and that tabling a question is not the same as deferring it, and that a thing which is tabled has no clerk watching it and a thing which is deferred has a day on it.

The vote was taken by hands and the count was made by the clerk of that assembly in his own book and he did not read it back into the room.

That is the whole of what happened. There is no figure for it on this page, because there is no figure for it in that room, and the clerk did not say it out loud and nobody asked him for it and nobody in this holding has asked him for it, and a page that puts a number on that vote is a page that has invented a clock.

A count of hands is not a count of reasons, and this page says that once and does not improve on it, and the nine in that chamber did not give reasons and were not asked for any, and a man who has been in a room of nine in the last four years knows that the two are not the same thing and knows also that he could not put the difference into a column if somebody gave him one.

After the vote the motion went on the floor of the chamber in the order the rules of that chamber put it, and the chamber went back to the clause, and about eleven minutes later somebody read out the seven and two from the afternoon before and about four of the nine wrote it down and about four did not, and the two fours have never been asked and there is no column in this holding that either of them would go in.

The clerk of that assembly has a book with the count in it and the count is the count of the ninth of that month and the number is in that book and it is in a book nine miles off, and about four people in four counties have been told what it is by somebody who heard it from somebody else and about four have not, and neither of those two fours has been told anything by this holding.

Marek was in the room for the whole of it and said nothing at all, and the reason he gave afterwards, to one person and not to the room, was a fact about a page and not about a person, and about four people in that room have said since that they expected him to speak at a vote and about four have said that they are glad he did not.

A sheet came into that chamber at about the seventh hour of the evening from the office that has sent this holding every document it has ever had to be frightened of, and it is one sheet and it is correct in every particular it states, and it was read out in full by the clerk of the assembly and nobody in that room called it a lie, because there is nothing in it that is not so.

> FROM THE OFFICE OF THE CONTINUITY, ONE SHEET, THE DEVICE AT THE HEAD OF IT, AND NO SEAL: **THAT THE MAINTENANCE OF A GROUND WHICH IS NO LONGER CONNECTED TO A PATTERN IS WORK, AND THAT THE WORK IS WORK WHETHER OR NOT A BODY HAS ASKED FOR IT, AND THAT IT IS CARRIED ON THE SCHEDULE OF THE ENGINE AND NOT ON THE SCHEDULE OF ANY COUNTY, AND THAT WHERE A GROUND HAS BEEN OUT OF CONNECTION FOR A SEASON ITS MAINTENANCE IS GREATER THAN IT WAS AND NOT LESS, AND THAT NO FIGURE IS GIVEN HERE FOR WHAT THAT MAINTENANCE COSTS, AND THAT NO FIGURE WILL BE GIVEN HERE FOR IT, AND THAT ANY BODY SEEKING SUCH A FIGURE IS ADVISED TO APPLY TO THE OFFICE IN WRITING ON THE FORM FOR FIGURES, WHICH IS THE FORM THIS OFFICE HAS USED SINCE THE FIRST OF THE SCHEDULE, AND THAT THE FORM IS CORRECT.**

Nobody in that room said it was a lie. Nobody in that room said it was a softening of anything, and about four of the nine said afterwards that it was the most honest sheet that office had ever sent anybody and about four said that it was true and that it was the true thing that had cost them the last three days, and neither of those two fours has been asked which of them was right and the page does not know.

Marek said one thing about the sheet and gave the reason for it before he said it, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and thirteen, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say what that sheet has done and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that the three counties who have been voting against this all afternoon have been voting against it on a figure, and the figure has just been told to them in writing that it will not be supplied."** "It has not argued with them. It has told them the price is real and that nobody is going to print it, and that is the whole of a Crown's office, and it is correct, and if I were standing in that office I would issue it in the same words. It is the reason the charter will go through tomorrow and I would rather it went through on that than on a number anybody in this holding made up on Wednesday."

The sitting ended at about the ninth hour and the chamber door was open for about a minute and then it was shut, and the three days were not three days, they were two days and an hour into a third, and there was no instrument dissolving anything in that room tonight and there was one coming, and about four people in that room knew that and about four did not.

He came back on the bicycle at about the tenth hour and the barrow was already at the top of the yard and the man of about thirty-one of Silling had gone home about an hour before him, and eleven journeys is a floor and it did not go up this morning and it came down this evening with a sheet of nine columns on the back of it and nothing in the sixth column.

He came in through the gate at about the tenth hour into a yard that had about two inches of standing water in it from the water that came through on the sixteenth and had not gone, and the lamp was still on the table in the one room, and the roll of the protocols was still on the long table at the wall side with its string on it, and about four people in this holding were still up at that hour and about four had gone to bed at the seventh, and neither of the two fours was asked which of them had been right to do what they did with the evening.

The register form is on the middle table at ninety-three days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it. The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and a sheet with no seal on it is not that instrument and nobody in this building has said that it is.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-six and was not read this morning, and it is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year, and the sixth of the six blank columns beside it has a heading at the top of it and nothing under it, and the twenty-four blanks on a board at a node is neither of those two columns and has never been either of them.

The compost line is paid and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one and it measures nothing, and a count of blanks is not a measurement of a ground and the two of those have never been added.

A sheet in a drawer claims a corridor in the pan and is correct in every particular it states, and the order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and opens nothing, and a form for figures that is correct is not an instrument and a form in a drawer is not a route.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page and was not walked, measured, crossed, priced or explained this morning, and the ground on both sides of it is going wrong and going slower and nobody in this holding has called slower better.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice, and a return is not a mark, a motion is not a mark, a division of nine is not a mark, a sheet with no seal on it is not a mark and a blank on a board is not a mark, and there are four accounts of that arm in this building and they do not reconcile and nothing said in that chamber this evening is a fifth of anything.
