# Chapter 423

## The Second Window, And A Request That Is Not An Offer

The sixth of this month was a Friday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The east dyke between the second sluice and the field gate took four people from the second hour to the sixth, and the turf on the top length had been lifted in three places by the frost and set down again by the same frost in a different shape, and the man at the front said a thing about the second shape that the three behind him did not write down.

He said it twice, and the second time he turned round, and the third man behind him said the thing was that a bank of a dyke that has been moved will move again in the same direction, and the man at the front said that was not what he had said, and neither of them said what he had said.

Marek was one of the four and had the bar from the fourth hour to the sixth, and his left forearm did not ache, and about four people in this holding have noticed that this month and about four have not. The water in the ditch at the field gate end was up about a finger on Thursday and about the same this morning, and nobody has written either of those two figures down and nobody is going to, because the ditch is not a column and a finger is not a unit anybody in this building has agreed to.

This page carries no figure for the run of mornings read out loud, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. The two boards are read at the seventh hour and the man who takes the second of them went down to the outfall at the sixth hour with the sill stones and has not come back up, and this holding has never in four years asked a man to stand at a table and say two figures out loud after two hours in a wet ditch.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and seventy days** and **six hundred and seventeen**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer stayed shut.

The sixth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other one, and the two of them were the same, and the man of about fifty read it out with the frayed corner between two fingers the way he has read every morning of the four years.

**Eight hundred and eighty-four hundredweight.**

Up nine off Thursday, and the hundred and eighty-sixth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and it did not reset on the sixth of a month and it is not going to reset on anything.

The rise went onto the rises. Eighty-two and seventy-one make a hundred and fifty-three, and that window is four hundred and eighty-three days wide and has nothing to do with the two boards and is not the launder.

Four bodies of households have gone a hundred and eighty-four days without a move in their aggregate, and the morning sentence is the hundred and eighty-third of those mornings out of a hundred and eighty-five. Those are four households and not four launders, and nobody in this building has ever added one to the other.

The window of the office's work opened at the sixth hour this morning and it is open for six days, the sixth to the eleventh, and the office that keeps it puts it on the same day of every month by its own housekeeping and nobody in four counties arranged that and nobody can move it.

Nothing has ever been carried out of one. There have been four of them in four years that anybody in this building has written about, and the pattern holds: a request goes in on the first day, a second thing comes in on the third, and on the sixth the window shuts and the two are on a long table at the wall side and neither of them has been in a column in this building's book, and this month the column for a thing that goes to an office has not been written and is not going to be written on the strength of a window opening.

Hesta Lyle keeps the four books for the four bodies of households and is not a member of anything and was in the room for that part of the morning, and she has never in eleven years put a figure into a book she was not the keeper of, and she said one thing about the window that nobody had said in four years of windows.

**"I have been the one who sends the thing out, and I have been the one who does not send it, and I have never once been the one who decided whether the six days were a good idea,"** she said. **"And I want it said that the six days are the office's own housekeeping and not a favor to us, and that a person who is given six days of somebody else's calendar will spend the six days of their own working out whether they have been given something."**

The clerk of this holding wrote that down in the words it was given in, and it went on the long table at the wall side with the other two, and it did not go up the fen road with anything.

A letter came up the fen road on Wednesday and the clerk of this holding read it out in that room with the door shut at about the third hour of the afternoon. It was four paragraphs and it was in a clerk's hand and it carried a seal and the seal is a council's seal and not a man's.

> FROM THE SECOND REGIONAL COUNCIL, DATED THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH, SENT BY THE ORDINARY POST AND BY A RIDER, READ ALOUD IN THE ONE ROOM AT THE THIRD HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE SIXTH: THE COUNCIL RESOLVES THAT THE COOPERATIVE HOLDING BE INVITED TO NOMINATE AN ENGINEER OF RECORD FOR THE NODE HELD BY THE COUNCIL SINCE THE TWENTY-SECOND OF THIS MONTH, AND THAT THE NOMINATION BE LAID BEFORE THE COUNCIL AT ITS NEXT SITTING, AND THE COUNCIL RECORDS THAT IT HAS ASKED FOR A NOMINATION AND NOT FOR A POST AND THAT A PERSON WHO ACCEPTS A NOMINATION ACCEPTS IT FOR AS LONG AS THE NODE IS HELD AND NOT FOR ANY LONGER, AND THE COUNCIL RECORDS THAT IT HAS ASKED THE HOLDING TWICE IN FOUR WEEKS FOR SOMETHING ELSE AND HAS BEEN ANSWERED BOTH TIMES IN FOUR WEEKS, AND THAT THIS THIRD LETTER IS THE SAME KIND OF LETTER AS THE FIRST TWO AND IS THE THIRD, AND THAT THE COUNCIL HAS NO COMPLAINT TO MAKE ABOUT ANYTHING ELSE IN THE FOUR.

It was written in four parts and not one of the five sentences in it was wrong, and about four people in that room read the fourth part twice before they read anything else in it.

A nomination is not an offer. That is the whole of the difference and it took the room about eleven minutes to get to it and the getting to it was not a matter of anybody being slow.

A post is a piece of paper. It has a date on it, it has a place at the bottom of it for a name, and a person who signs one has given something and gets something, and the thing on the low board at the far end of that room is a piece of paper. A motion is not a piece of paper. It is four people in a room in a town nine miles off putting a sentence in a book because a sentence in a book is what they have, and the sentence asks this holding to name somebody for a duty that already belongs to a body of people and that nobody asked the reader of this body to take.

Harlan Vetch came in at about the fifth hour and was in the room for the end of it, and he read the letter standing up and gave no reason for what he said and it is not entered and the count of that did not move.

**"I have held an end of a thing since Sunday and nobody sent me anything and nobody asked me, and I want it written down that I noticed the difference in that letter before I got to the third part,"** he said. **"A body of people have asked a question and got no answer, and it reads like a favor, and it is not a favor, and I have been on the other side of the road for four years and I know what a favor written in a book sounds like."**

Nobody in that room improved it and nobody thanked him for saying it.

Marek gave the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is two hundred and four, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say that this holding is not nominating anybody, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that a nomination is a request and a request goes in the column this building has for requests, and there are fifty-three of those and fifty-three section-nine notes beside them, and the two are never added to each other and neither of them becomes fifty-four because a letter came up a road on Wednesday."** "I am not refusing the council, because refusing it would be a sentence about their motion and the motion is correct and it asks for a thing. What I am doing is entering it where a request goes, and the reason is that a body of people four days ago asked this holding to put what the work is into one column, and a column is a thing a clerk can read, and that letter is the only one of the fifty-three that asked for a column."

**"And I am going to say the second thing and I am not giving a reason for it, and the count of that in this holding's history did not move this morning."** "There is a piece of paper on the low board at the far end of this room that a man put on that board in a month that is now two months back, and it has no date on it and no line for a name, and this morning a letter came up a road three counties off asking this building to name a person for a duty, and I have watched about four people in this room go across to that board and look at it in the space of an hour, and not one of them has touched it, and not one of them is wrong to look at it, and a thing that draws the eye of everybody in a room twice a week is a thing that is being used by the room whether anybody in the room means it or not."

Nobody in that room said the second thing was unfair and nobody said it was fair. It is in the second book in his own hand with the hour against it and no reason, and the count of reasons given first did not move.

The clerk of this holding asked one question and it was the only question anybody asked in about an hour, and it was whether a request of that shape ought to go up the fen road with an answer in it.

It does not go up with an answer. It goes up as a request, in the words it was made in, with the date on it and the seal, and a line at the bottom that says this holding is not nominating a person this month and does not say why, because the why is in the second book and the second book does not go up a road.

A request is a thing somebody wants. It is not a thing anybody has agreed to do, and the difference between those two is the whole of a morning like this one, and this building has kept four books for four years without a column anywhere in any of them that says what anybody has agreed to do, and there is no version of this morning in which a piece of paper is made out of a want.

The count of requests is fifty-three at the eighth hour of the evening and it is fifty-three, and the section-nine notes are fifty-three, and the motion is in the requests column and not on any table in this building.

The offer is on the low board at the far end of the room, face up. It is not filled, it was refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back, it has not been withdrawn, it has not been moved, it carries no date and no name and no line waiting for a name, and nobody in this building has been told to take it off that board and nobody is going to be. A motion in a council's book three counties off is not that piece of paper and is not going to become it, and no rung of anything was climbed this morning and nobody climbed one.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling brought the barrow up the four-mile road at the ninth hour and turned it at the top of the yard and went home. Eleven journeys is where it stands and eleven is a floor. The second of the four lines in the book he carries is seventy-four days old this morning and has nothing on it, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in seventy-four days and nobody is going to this month.

The register form came on the sixteenth of a month now three months back and has stood on the middle table eighty days. Not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and nobody has written a word there.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-six and was not read this morning. It is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year. The six columns beside it are headed nothing and nothing this morning put a word at the top of one of them.

A sheet in a drawer claims a corridor in the pan and is correct in every particular it states. The order sealing the route is in the same drawer, valid, and it opens nothing, and nothing in this building reopened a route this morning.

The man of about seventy-four with his stick was in the door of the one room for about two hours of that afternoon and did not sit down and did not say a word about the letter, and he has been in that door on and off for eleven years, and nobody in this holding has ever asked him what he comes for and nobody is going to this month.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and got no sixteenth line this morning. A motion is not a use, a nomination is not a use, and a request is not a use, and the reason for the fifteen was told twice on the first of this month because a season turned and it is not going to be told a third time in this season by anybody.

Inside eleven acres there is a ring of bare ground, in its eleventh year, with no dimension on any page in four counties. It was not walked, not measured, not crossed, not priced and not explained. Four stakes stand outside it on the north side with waxed covers and nothing written on them, and the four people who put them there are about six yards short of the edge of that ring and have not been told why.

The compost line is paid, one hundred pounds, and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one, and the word in front of the figure has never once been wrong about it, and it is not a measurement of the ground.

The man of about seventy was fetched on the first of this month and is not fetched today, so twenty-four is the count, and the twenty-fifth is the first of the next, and no page in this holding has ever given a reason he is brought on the first of a month and nobody has ever asked him which number he is on.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice. A motion is not a mark, a nomination is not a mark, a request is not a mark, a column is not a mark, and nothing said in that room this afternoon is a fifth account of anything.
