# Chapter 430

## The List Is Not A Plan

The thirteenth of this month was a Friday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The two gates and the sill at the outfall took five people from the second hour to the sixth hour, and the sill has been two inches under the silt since a month that is now two months back and there is no figure for how far it was before that, and nobody in this holding has guessed at one.

Marek was one of the five and was in the water to the knee from the fourth hour, and the cold in it is a different cold from the cold in the dyke and both of them are the same month.

The boards were read aloud at the seventh hour, before either figure was named, and both of the men who take them were in the building. **Five hundred and seventy-seven days** and **six hundred and twenty-four**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer stayed shut.

The man who takes the second board was in the water at the outfall until about the sixth hour and came up wet to the waist and read his figure standing up with the water running off the end of the book, and about four people in this holding have decided that is how it should be done and about four have decided that it should not, and neither of those two fours has been asked and neither of them is going to.

Sixty-seven mornings out of a hundred and seventeen, and the man who takes the first board said the first figure out of the second book rather than off the first board this morning because he had got it wrong twice in eleven years and has not been wrong since, and nobody in this holding has thanked him for that either.

The thirteenth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Eight hundred and ninety-one hundredweight.**

Down five off Thursday, and the hundred and ninety-third figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The fall went onto the falls, so the window stands at eighty-five rises and seventy-five falls and the two add to a hundred and sixty. The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and ninety-one days and was written for the hundred and ninetieth of those mornings out of a hundred and ninety-two.

The list went down to the node on a bicycle with the man of about thirty-one of Silling at about the ninth hour of the morning, and it came back at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and it came back with a figure written on the back of it in a hand that is not the clerk's.

He said three things about the bicycle and the first of them was that the road out to that node is a day's walk and there is no cart road and a basket is what a sheet goes in, and the second of them was that nobody at that node asked him what the sheet was, and the third of them was this.

**"There is a dial in that building with a mark on the face of it and a man stood in front of it for about a minute and a half and then wrote a number on the back of your paper and gave it back to me and did not say the number out loud, and I have carried about nine hundred words up and down this road in four years and I have never once had a piece of paper handed to me with a number on the back of it."** "I did not ask him to write it. I want that written down, because in about four years somebody is going to ask me what I did at that building on the thirteenth of this month and I am not going to be able to remember whether I asked."

He gave no reason for any of it in front of it and the count of reasons given first in the holding's history did not move, and the clerk of this holding wrote all three sentences down in the words they were given in and read them back and did not improve one of them.

The dial at that node has a scale on the face of it with a mark against about a third of the way round, and the figure this holding has been entering against that node for four years is the figure on the mark, and the figure that dial gave the man of about thirty-one of Silling at about the third hour of the afternoon, standing in front of it with the list in his hand, was about a tenth different from it, and the two are not reconciled, and nobody in this building reconciled them this afternoon, and the list carries both of them in the fifth column of the row for the dial with a mark between them and no explanation of the mark.

Marek gave the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is two hundred and eight, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the sentence about that difference and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that a difference of about a tenth between two figures for the same thing is either one of them wrong or one of them measuring a different thing, and this holding does not know which and is not going to guess at it in a fortnight because a council is going to be able to read this sheet."** "This holding uses neither of those figures for anything. Not this month and not this season. And I am not going to stand in that room at the third hour of the afternoon and say a total, because a total is what a person says when they have put two figures together, and the two figures in that fifth column are not two figures for the same thing until somebody has found out what the mark between them means, and nobody has."

The man of about fifty asked what went in the fifth column then, and the answer was both figures and a mark, and the clerk wrote that down and read it back, and the row for the dial is the only row on that sheet with two figures in one cell.

The seventh column of that sheet is headed FIGURES WE CANNOT CHECK and it has two rows in it this afternoon, and neither of the two has a name against it anywhere in this building or on any page in four counties.

The first of the two has nothing against it at all. Not a date, not a place, not a hand, not a reason and not a name, and the second has a hand and a month against it and neither of them has anything else. The first of the two is not on this page and is not going to be on this page and is not named in this room.

The clerk of this holding put his thumb on the empty row and asked whether a row like that should have a line ruled under it, and the answer he gave himself, out loud, with nobody else in the room being asked, was that it should not, and the reason was that a line under it would be a place to write something later, and this holding has four years of things written later on the strength of a place to write them.

Nobody in that room disagreed with him and nobody thanked him for saying it in his own mouth, and the row has no line under it this afternoon and did not have one this morning.

A row with nothing in it is not an invitation. It is a shape with an empty place in it, and about four years of this holding has had one of those at the top of a column for four years and called it a column, and this afternoon the difference was said out loud in a room and the saying of it did not fill the row.

The eighth column is headed THINGS WE DO NOT KNOW and it has six rows in it, and there is no unit against any of the six, and the seventh row is not ruled.

A list of six things you do not know is not a plan. It is a list. The seventh row is not ruled because a seventh thing this holding does not know has not arrived this month, and a row is not ruled in anticipation, and about four years of this holding has ruled rows in anticipation of things and then had to carry them.

The ninth column of that sheet is the back of a form with four lines on it, and there is a form with four lines on it in this building and it has been on the long table at the wall side for about four years, and line one carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, which is the figure of the twenty-second return, and lines two, three and four are empty, and nobody has filled them and nobody is going to.

A form is not a column. A form is a thing a person fills in and a column is a thing a person reads, and the back of that form is where the ninth column is because there was nowhere else on the sheet, and it is not going to be made into a form and it is not going to be made into anything else, and a heading has not been written at the top of it and about four people in this room wanted to write one and about four did not and neither of those two fours has been asked.

Harlan Vetch was in that room when the list came back and he had been in it since the fourth hour and he said the thing that made the morning into an argument about a sheet and not about a number.

**"I have held an end of a bridge since the first of this month with one other person I did not ask and who did not say he would, and about four of us in this holding are the only people in four counties who can do what happens at that end, and there is no instrument in this building that says whether the thing is on or off, and the count of questions asked at that far end is nil before and nil after."** "I have just watched five people spend an afternoon finding out what a sheet can and cannot do, and what it can do is tell a clerk in a county that the same nine things are in the same places. What it cannot do is tell me to be at the other end on Thursday. And it will not, and I am not asking it to, and I want the clerk to write down that I did not ask it to."

The clerk of this holding wrote that down in the words it was given in and read it back and did not improve it, and nobody in that room asked him a question about it.

A list is a list and not a plan, and the difference between the two of them is that a plan says what a person is going to do and a list says what a person has done, and a list with nine columns on it that four counties can check is the first thing anybody in this holding has made in four years that a person outside it can check, and it cannot tell anybody what to do on Thursday, and nobody in this room has asked it to.

Nobody in that room wanted it to and nobody said so.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at the ninth hour and came down it empty and eleven journeys is a floor and the man of about thirty-one of Silling brought it and the second of the four lines in his own book is eighty-one days old this morning and has nothing on it and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in eighty-one days.

The offer is face up on the low board and is not filled and is not withdrawn and carries no date and no line for a name, and a list of nine columns is not that instrument and is not going to be put beside it on any table in this building.

The register form is on the middle table at eighty-seven days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and a form with four lines is a different form from a register form and the two of them are not the same size and are not on the same table and are not added to each other.

The seventh column of the well house door is at fifty-six and was not read this morning. It is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year. The sixth of the six blank columns beside it has a heading at the top of it that takes a count of readings and there is no figure under it this morning, and the other five are headed nothing.

The compost line is paid at twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one and it measures nothing, and the ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year with no dimension on any page and was not walked, measured, crossed, priced or explained this morning, and the ground on both sides of it is going wrong more slowly and nobody in this holding has called that better.

A sheet in a drawer claims a corridor in the pan and is correct in every particular it states, and the order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and it opens nothing, and a list is not a corridor and a mark between two figures in a cell is not an order.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice and it was four inches in the water at the outfall at the fourth hour and it is four inches now, and a form is not a mark, a column is not a mark, a list is not a mark, a plan is not a mark, and a figure written on the back of a sheet by a man standing in front of a dial is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm are in the second book and they do not reconcile and this afternoon added nothing to any of them.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the thirteenth, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE TWO GATES AND THE SILL AT THE OUTFALL, FIVE PEOPLE FROM THE SECOND HOUR TO THE SIXTH HOUR. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD AT THE SEVENTH HOUR AND THE RUN IS SIXTY-SEVEN OF A HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN, AND THE PAIR STANDS AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SEVEN DAYS AND SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FOUR, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE THIRD LAUNDER IS EIGHT HUNDRED AND NINETY-ONE AND IS THE HUNDRED AND NINETY-THIRD FIGURE OF THE SERIES. THE WINDOW STANDS AT EIGHTY-FIVE RISES AND SEVENTY-FIVE FALLS AND THE TWO ADD. THE LIST OF NINE COLUMNS WENT DOWN TO THE NODE ON A BICYCLE AND CAME BACK AT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON WITH A FIGURE ON THE BACK OF IT IN A HAND THAT IS NOT THE CLERK'S. THE FIGURE THE DIAL GAVE IS ABOUT A TENTH DIFFERENT FROM THE FIGURE THIS HOLDING HAS BEEN ENTERING AGAINST THAT NODE FOR FOUR YEARS, AND THE TWO ARE NOT RECONCILED, AND THE LIST CARRIES BOTH IN THE FIFTH COLUMN OF THE ROW FOR THE DIAL WITH A MARK BETWEEN THEM AND NO EXPLANATION OF THE MARK, AND THIS HOLDING USES NEITHER FIGURE FOR ANYTHING AND STATES NO TOTAL. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS TWO HUNDRED AND EIGHT. THE SEVENTH COLUMN HAS TWO ROWS IN IT AND THE FIRST OF THE TWO HAS NOTHING AGAINST IT AND IS NOT NAMED AND IS NOT ON THIS PAGE, AND A ROW WITH NOTHING IN IT IS NOT AN INVITATION. THE EIGHTH COLUMN HAS SIX ROWS IN IT WITH NO UNIT AGAINST ANY OF THE SIX AND THE SEVENTH ROW IS NOT RULED, AND A LIST OF SIX THINGS YOU DO NOT KNOW IS NOT A PLAN. THE NINTH COLUMN IS THE BACK OF A FORM WITH FOUR LINES, WHICH CARRIES ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT ON THE FIRST LINE AND THREE EMPTY ONES, AND A FORM IS NOT A COLUMN AND IS NOT GOING TO BE MADE INTO ONE, AND NO HEADING HAS BEEN WRITTEN AT THE TOP OF IT. THE COUNT OF REQUESTS IS FIFTY-THREE AND THE SECTION-NINE NOTES ARE FIFTY-THREE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
