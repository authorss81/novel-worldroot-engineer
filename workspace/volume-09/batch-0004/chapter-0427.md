# Chapter 427

## Three Hundred And Seventy-One

The tenth of this month was a Tuesday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The west side of the boundary ditch from the corner post to about halfway took six people from the second hour to the seventh, and the water in it had come up about two fingers on the foot side since Friday and had not gone down in the night, and the man at the front of it said the word silt twice and neither time did the five behind him write it down.

Marek was one of the six and had the bar for the first three hours and the shovel for the last two, and the two of them were handed over about the fifth hour in the middle of a job without anybody saying so, and the man who took the bar from him said nothing about the trade either and then said, about an hour later, that the ditch on this side is a different ditch from the one on the other side and always has been, and that nobody had ever asked him why.

Nobody asked him why. He has not said it since.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling waited at the corner post from about the sixth hour of the morning with the lamp and a folded sheet under his arm and did not go up the road with the six, and he said that he had been asked to take a lamp to a building with no roof in it and not to be told anything about what it was for, and that was the whole of what he had been asked, and he is not a member of this holding and has not been asked to be.

This page carries no figure for the run of mornings, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. The man who takes the second of the two boards went down to the node at about the second hour of the afternoon to take the return off that dial, and the boards are read at the seventh hour, and that dial is about five miles off and there is no cart that does it and back in the time.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and seventy-four days** and **six hundred and twenty-one**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not going to be.

The tenth went in at about the ninth hour, an hour later than usual, and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Eight hundred and ninety-two hundredweight.**

Up nine off Monday, and the hundred and ninetieth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and it has not reset at a month turn and it is not going to reset at anything.

The rise went onto the rises. Eighty-four and seventy-three make a hundred and fifty-seven, and that window is a count of mornings and not a count of work, and the two halves of it have never been anything else and have never been added to the figure above.

The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and eighty-eight days, and it was written for the hundred and eighty-seventh of those mornings out of a hundred and eighty-nine.

The return came off the steelyard at the node at about the second hour of the afternoon, in about nine minutes of rain that did not lie on anything, and there are four people in this holding who have now stood in that building with no roof in it and watched that dial read and the fourth of them is the man of about thirty-one of Silling, who was sent with a lamp and a sheet and no message, and who is not a member of anything and has been twice.

> THE BOARD AT THE NODE, PAINTED ELEVEN YEARS AGO IN ONE HAND AND NOT REPAINTED SINCE. THE UPPER RULE IS PAINTED BY AND THE RULE UNDER IT IS PAINTED WITNESSED BY, AND THE FOUR NAMES OF THIS AFTERNOON WENT IN ONE AT A TIME IN THE ORDER THEY CAME UP TO THE DIAL, TWO INTO EACH RULE. THE SECOND RULE HAS A LINE PAINTED UNDER IT AND A SPACE BESIDE THAT LINE, AND THE SPACE HAS BEEN LEFT EMPTY EVERY TIME THE FIRST RULE HAS BEEN FILLED SINCE THE BOARD WAS PAINTED. NOBODY STANDING NEAR THAT DIAL HAS EVER PUT ANYTHING IN THAT SPACE, INCLUDING THIS AFTERNOON, AND NOBODY HAS EVER SAID OUT LOUD WHAT WOULD GO IN IT IF SOMETHING DID.

**Three hundred and seventy-one hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-ninth of them and the twenty-ninth pair of names in eleven years, and it stands eleven hundredweight above the thirty-eighth, which was three hundred and sixty on the first of this month and is the figure the last return in this holding's book carries.

The man of about fifty took it off the dial, said the figure out loud, and then said it again out of the second book, and the two were the same, and it came off the dial twice checked against the two figures he had put on that day sheet at the ninth hour of the morning.

**Two hundred and ninety-six and seventy-five. And two hundred and fifty-four and a hundred and seventeen.**

This holding uses that derivation and does not refuse it. It has used it since the second return in eleven years and nobody has ever asked it to do anything else, and the two halves of it were not added this afternoon and are not going to be added by anybody, because a figure that has been added to itself on a page stops being a figure anybody can check and becomes a figure somebody can argue with.

There are four names in the two rules on that board and every one of the four came up to that dial this afternoon in an order that was not decided in advance, and the two who went into the second rule were the second and the fourth, and about four years of this holding has treated the order those four stand in as a fact about the four of them rather than a fact about the afternoon.

The second rule on that board was left empty again. It has now stood empty twenty-three times in eleven years, and a count of blanks is not the count of a man, and it stood at twenty-two on the first of this month, which was nine mornings back.

Marek got back from the node at about the fifth hour of the afternoon and came into the one room with rain still on the shoulders of his coat and said the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is two hundred and five, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say one thing about a blank line and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that in about four years a person in a room is going to ask what the second rule is for, and the honest answer is that four people in this building do not know, and the dishonest answer is the one that would go in a column."** "The line under it was painted there before the four of us were born, or before the four of us were working, and the space beside it has been empty twenty-three times and nobody has ever written down what would go in it, and I am not going to guess at it this afternoon. Not because it is anybody's fault. Because a guess put in a book in a room with the door shut is the exact thing this holding has been refusing for four years and I have signed my name to about four of them."

Nobody in that room improved it and nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty wrote it into the second book as it was given and read it back, and the second rule on the board at the node is still empty and nobody has said a word about it to the four people standing under the awning there.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came back into the yard at about the seventh hour with the lamp still lit and put it on the step and said three things, and the first of them was about the weather and the second of them was about the road and the third of them was this.

**"There is a board with two lines on it and a space beside the second one and there is wax on the ground in four places about a yard apart, and the four people there did not look at me once while I was holding the lamp, and I want it written down that they were not rude and that I would have felt better if they had been."** "I have stood in a room with a man from a county and he asked me a question about a barrow and I did not have an answer, and I have thought about that for about four days, and this afternoon I stood in a building with no roof in it and nobody asked me anything at all and I have thought about that for about two hours. Put one of the two of them in a column and I do not care which."

He gave no reason for any of it in front of it and the count of reasons given first in the holding's history did not move on account of that either, and the clerk of this holding wrote all three sentences down in the words they were given in and read them back and did not improve them.

The barrow came up the four-mile road at the ninth hour and went down it empty, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling had brought it up at the seventh and had been at the node since about the second hour of the afternoon with a lamp, and one man brought a barrow four miles and another man stood under an awning five miles east on the same morning, and those are two things and they are not added to each other. Eleven journeys is where it stands and eleven is a floor. The second of the four lines in his own book is seventy-eight days old this morning and has nothing on it and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in seventy-eight days.

The window of the office's work is on its fifth day and it shuts on the eleventh and nothing has been put in it. A return off a dial five miles east is not a thing that goes to an office and the six days do not make it one.

There have been four of those windows in four years and the number of things that came out of one of them and went into a column of this building's book is nil, and the sheet with nine columns on it has been on the long table at the wall side since Sunday afternoon and is not on the long table at the wall side for a window.

The offer is face up on the low board at the far end of the room and it is not filled and was refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back and it has not been withdrawn and there is no date on it and no line for a name, and a blank line painted under a rule on a board at a node is not that instrument and nobody in this building has ever said that it is.

The register form is on the middle table at eighty-four days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and the sheet with nine columns on it is not on the middle table and is not going on it, because a register form is a form and a list is a list and this holding is not going to find out this month what a form is when it has nine columns on it.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-six and was not read this morning and is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year. The six columns beside it are headed nothing and stayed that way through this morning, and a return off a dial is not a reason to head one of them.

The compost line is paid and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one and the word before the figure has never once been wrong about it, and it is not a measurement of the ground and nobody used it as one today.

The man of about seventy is at twenty-four fetchings and is not fetched today and the twenty-fifth falls on the first of the next, and no page in this holding has ever given a reason for that and nobody has ever asked him which number it is.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page in four counties, and it was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced and not explained this morning, and a stake stands outside it with a waxed cover and nothing written on it, and the four people who put those stakes there are working a strip about four yards wide on the north side and are about six yards short of the edge of that ring and have not been told why.

The lamp that was carried out to that building at the sixth hour this morning came back with about a foot of the wick black on it and has not been trimmed, and it is on the step of this building, and about four people have looked at it this week and about four have not.

A sheet in a drawer claims a corridor in the pan and is correct in every particular it states, and the order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and opens nothing, and no room with the door shut and a figure in it is a corridor.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice and was four inches at the second hour and is four inches at the ninth hour, and he was in a wet coat and nobody in that room looked at it. A return is not a mark, a blank rule is not a mark, a name under a rule is not a mark, a lamp carried four miles is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand in the second book where they have stood for four years, in four hands, and nobody has ever put the two ends of any two of them together.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the tenth, in about four people, at this holding: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE WEST SIDE OF THE BOUNDARY DITCH FROM THE CORNER POST, SIX PEOPLE FROM THE SECOND HOUR TO THE SEVENTH. THE BOARDS WERE NOT READ ALOUD AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR THE RUN, THE MAN WHO TAKES THE SECOND BOARD HAVING GONE TO THE NODE AT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON TO TAKE THE RETURN OFF THAT DIAL, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE THIRD LAUNDER IS EIGHT HUNDRED AND NINETY-TWO AND IS THE HUNDRED AND NINETIETH FIGURE OF THE SERIES. THE WINDOW STANDS AT EIGHTY-FOUR RISES AND SEVENTY-THREE FALLS AND THE TWO ADD. THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAVE NOT MOVED IN A HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-EIGHT DAYS, WRITTEN FOR THE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVENTH OF THOSE MORNINGS OUT OF A HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-NINE. THE THIRTY-NINTH NINTH-DAY RETURN CAME OFF THE STEELYARD AT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AT THREE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE TWENTY-NINTH PAIR OF NAMES IN ELEVEN YEARS, ELEVEN ABOVE THE THIRTY-EIGHTH, RECONCILED TWICE OUT OF TWO HUNDRED AND NINETY-SIX AND SEVENTY-FIVE, AND TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FOUR AND A HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN, THE TWO HALVES NOT ADDED, AND THIS HOLDING USES THAT DERIVATION AND DOES NOT REFUSE IT. THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD AT THE NODE WAS LEFT EMPTY FOR THE TWENTY-THIRD TIME IN ELEVEN YEARS, AND A COUNT OF BLANKS IS NOT THE COUNT OF A MAN. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT HE WAS NOT GOING TO GUESS AT WHAT WOULD GO IN THAT SPACE, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS TWO HUNDRED AND FIVE. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING WENT TO THE NODE WITH A LAMP AND A SHEET AND NO MESSAGE AND IS THE FOURTH PERSON IN THIS HOLDING TO STAND IN THAT BUILDING. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
