# Chapter 431

## What A County's Engineer Asked For

The fourteenth of this month was a Saturday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The length of the boundary ditch from the corner post to the sluice took six people from the second hour to the seventh hour, and the water in it has been up two fingers on the foot side for four days now and has not gone down once in that time, and nobody in this holding has written two fingers in a column and nobody is going to.

Marek was one of the six and had the shovel for the last three hours and his left forearm did not ache and about four people in this holding have stopped remarking on that and about four have not.

This page carries no figure for the run of mornings, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. The two men who take the boards were both in the one room with the sheet from the fifth hour and the room is where the fifth, sixth, seventh and eighth of the nine places get read out, and nobody in this building has asked two men to leave that room in order to be tidy about a pair of figures at the seventh hour.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and seventy-eight days** and **six hundred and twenty-five**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened and is not going to be.

They came off the ditch at about the seventh hour into a hail that had not been in the air at the second hour and lasted about eleven minutes, and the six of them stood in the yard under the eaves of the long building for the whole of it and nobody went home early, and the man at the front of the ditch said the word silt a third time and this time one of the three behind him wrote it down on the back of a hand as well as the other one had on Thursday.

The fourteenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and about four people in this holding have wondered this week whether the second book is checked against the first by anybody other than the man who writes in both, and about four have not wondered, and the answer to that question is in the second book and has been since a month that is now two months back.

**Nine hundred hundredweight.**

Up nine off Friday, and the hundred and ninety-fourth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and it is a round figure and there has never been a round one in it while anybody in this building has been keeping it, and nobody in this room said anything about that and nobody is going to.

The rise went onto the rises. Eighty-six and seventy-five make a hundred and sixty-one, and the two halves have never been added to the figure above them and are not going to be.

The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and ninety-two days and was written for the hundred and ninety-first of those mornings out of a hundred and ninety-three.

The man came up the fen road with a second man at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and the first of them gave his name and the name of the council and the word engineer in front of it, and the second of them carried a case about two feet long and did not open it and put it down against the wall by the door and picked it up again twice in about an hour.

Marek was in that room and had come in wet from the sluice and did not change, and the engineer looked at him for about four seconds and then looked away, and about four people in the room thought he had decided something and about four thought he had not noticed anything at all, and neither of the two fours has been asked and neither of them is going to be.

He had a sheet. It was written in four parts and it was in an engineer's hand and not a clerk's, and the request was in the second part and the other three were about why he was asking.

The clerk of this holding read it out in the one room with the door shut at about the fourth hour of the afternoon.

He read the request out himself in the second part and it went like this, in his own mouth, in an engineer's hand and not a clerk's.

**"I am asking this holding for the figures of anything it holds from the valley of the middle, and I am asking for the figures and not for what they mean, and I am going to tell you why in three more sentences and then I will stop talking."** "I have been told by a person in my county that something came twice in one week out of a place where there is no ground. I have also been told that the person who said it will not write it down. I believe both of those, and I have believed both of those for about four days, and I am not here to argue a person out of the second one. A thing that is both true and unwriteable is a thing I cannot put in an engineer's report, and I have a report to put it in on the first of next month."

He stopped talking there, because he had said he would, and the clerk of this holding read the fourth part of that sheet out twice, because the fourth part is the one that says he is not asking this holding to explain anything, and about four people in that room have said since that they have never in their lives had a letter ask them for less and arrive with more in it, and about four have said the same thing about a different paragraph and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The man of about fifty said the no first.

**"No."**

Then he gave the reason for it, and it came after the thing and not before it, and the count of reasons given first in the holding's history did not move this afternoon on account of it, and the man of about fifty did not correct himself afterwards and was not asked to.

**"The reason is a fact about a page and not about a person. There are two figures in the second book on a page of their own, and that page has no heading at the top of it and has not had one since it was written, and there is no unit against either of them, and there are two of us in this building who have seen that page and no third person has."** "You have been told that the person who said it will not write it down. That is correct and I am not going to say a word against him for it. What I am telling you is that a figure which two men in a building have seen and which has no unit and no heading and no third pair of eyes is not a figure an engineer's report can stand on, and if you copy it and your report goes wrong in about four years, the two men in this building will be the reason, and neither of us will be able to say to you which of the two figures you should have crossed out."

He read the request out again at about the sixth hour and said that the answer goes back in the words of the request, and that the two figures in the second book are not in the answer, and that the refusal goes back the same way a refusal went back last month, in the words it was given in, on the back of the engineer's own sheet, and nobody signed either.

The engineer read the refusal twice, standing up, in the same way the clerk of the fourth county had read a letter on the fen road five mornings ago, and then he asked one question and it was the only question anybody asked in about an hour.

He asked whether this holding would give him the two figures with a heading written over them, and the answer was that a heading written this afternoon would be a heading on a page that has had nothing at the top of it for four years and that a heading does not make a figure checkable, and that the difference between the two of those things was said out loud in this building on the eleventh of this month and is not going to be said again this month.

He said one more thing before he picked the case up, and he said it to the man of about fifty and not to the room, and it was short.

**"I have been an engineer for about nineteen years and I have had a figure put in a report by a man who could not check it in about nine of those, and every one of the nine was somebody who liked me, and I am telling you that I noticed the difference between the two of those about four years ago and I have said so to nobody until this afternoon."**

Nobody in that room said thank you for that and the man of about fifty said nothing at all in reply to it, and the count of reasons given first in the holding's history did not move, and about four people in that room have wondered since whether nineteen years is a figure that ought to go in a column and about four have wondered why nobody has ever put a man's years in a column anywhere in four counties.

The case against the wall was picked up a third time and the two of them went out at about the seventh hour, and nobody in this building sent anything with them, and the two figures in the second book are in the second book and were read by two people this morning and by the same two people this afternoon and by nobody else.

Harlan Vetch was in that room and did not say anything while the engineer was in it, and at about the seventh hour, after the door had shut, he said one thing and gave no reason for it in front of it and the count of reasons given first in the holding's history did not move on that account either.

**"I asked you on Sunday whether anybody was going to ask the four people at that node whether that was their work, and nobody asked, and this afternoon an engineer came five miles to ask you for two numbers that have been on a page for four years with nobody's name on them, and he is the first person from outside this building who has come for a thing that is on the page and not for a thing that is on a board."** "I am not saying he should have got them. I am saying he is the first one, and I have been in four counties for four years and I have never known it to happen, and about four people in that room did not know it either and about four did."

Nobody in that room said thank you and nobody in that room said the reader of this body had been right about anything, and the man of about fifty put the second book back on the shelf with the page in it turned the same way it has been turned every morning of the four years.

The barrow came up the four-mile road at the ninth hour and was turned at the top of the yard, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling brought it and the second of the four lines in his own book is eighty-two days old this morning and has nothing on it, and nobody in this building has asked him about it in eighty-two days and nobody is going to this month.

The offer is on the low board at the far end of the room, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back, and an engineer's case against a wall is not that instrument and nobody in this room has suggested that it is.

The register form is on the middle table at eighty-eight days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and the sixth of the six blank columns on the well house door has a heading at the top of it and no figure under it and the other five have nothing at the top of them, and the seventh stands at fifty-six and is not read this morning and is read on the thirtieth and on the first and on nothing else in the year.

The compost line is paid at twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one and it measures nothing, and the ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year with no dimension on any page and was not walked, measured, crossed, priced or explained today by anybody in this building, and the ground on both sides of it is still going wrong and going slower and nobody here has called slower better.

A sheet in a drawer claims a corridor in the pan and is correct in every particular it states, and the order sealing the route is in that drawer and is valid and opens nothing, and an engineer who comes five miles for two figures has not reopened a route.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice, and a refusal is not a mark, a figure is not a mark, a heading is not a mark, and a page with no unit on it is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm are four and the two figures in the second book are two and none of the six of them has been added to any other this afternoon by anybody in this room.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the fourteenth, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE BOUNDARY DITCH FROM THE CORNER POST TO THE SLUICE, SIX PEOPLE FROM THE SECOND HOUR TO THE SEVENTH HOUR. THE BOARDS WERE NOT READ ALOUD AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR THE RUN, BOTH MEN BEING IN THE ONE ROOM WITH THE SHEET FROM THE FIFTH HOUR, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE THIRD LAUNDER IS NINE HUNDRED AND IS THE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FOURTH FIGURE OF THE SERIES, AND NOBODY HAS SAID ANYTHING ABOUT IT. THE WINDOW STANDS AT EIGHTY-SIX RISES AND SEVENTY-FIVE FALLS AND THE TWO ADD. THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAVE NOT MOVED IN A HUNDRED AND NINETY-TWO DAYS, WRITTEN FOR THE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIRST OF THOSE MORNINGS OUT OF A HUNDRED AND NINETY-THREE. THE ENGINEER OF THE FIRST REGIONAL COUNCIL ASKED FOR THE FIGURES OF ANY READING THIS HOLDING HOLDS FROM THE VALLEY OF THE MIDDLE, AND ASKED FOR THE FIGURES AND NOT FOR WHAT THEY MEAN, AND CAME BY HAND WITH A CASE HE DID NOT OPEN. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY REFUSED AND GAVE THE REASON AFTER THE THING, AND THE REASON WAS A FACT ABOUT A PAGE AND NOT ABOUT A PERSON, AND THE COUNT OF REASONS GIVEN FIRST IN THE HOLDING'S HISTORY DID NOT MOVE ON THAT ACCOUNT AND HE WAS NOT ASKED TO CORRECT HIMSELF. THE TWO FIGURES ARE IN THE SECOND BOOK ON A PAGE OF THEIR OWN WITH NO HEADING AND NO UNIT AGAINST EITHER, AND TWO PEOPLE HAVE SEEN THAT PAGE AND NO THIRD PERSON HAS. THE REFUSAL WENT BACK IN THE WORDS OF THE REQUEST ON THE BACK OF THE ENGINEER'S OWN SHEET AND NOBODY SIGNED EITHER. THE ENGINEER ASKED WHETHER THE FIGURES WOULD BE GIVEN WITH A HEADING WRITTEN OVER THEM AND WAS TOLD THAT A HEADING DOES NOT MAKE A FIGURE CHECKABLE, AND THAT THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THOSE TWO THINGS WAS SAID ONCE IN THIS BUILDING ON THE ELEVENTH OF THIS MONTH AND IS NOT GOING TO BE SAID AGAIN THIS MONTH. THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS ASKED AT THE FAR END OF THE BRIDGE IS NIL BEFORE THIS MORNING AND NIL AFTER IT. THE COUNT OF REQUESTS IS FIFTY-THREE AND THE SECTION-NINE NOTES ARE FIFTY-THREE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
