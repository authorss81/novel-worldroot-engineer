# Chapter 424

## A Rate Nobody Can Certify

The seventh of this month was a Saturday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The head ditch of the low field and the foot of the west dyke took four people from the second hour until the light went, and the light went at about the fifth hour, and the four of them finished the last eleven yards by the light of the lamp one of them carried and did not put down twice.

Marek was one of the four. He carried the lamp for the last hour and his forearm did not ache, and about four people in this holding have noticed that this month and about four have not.

They came off the dyke at about the sixth hour in a rain that had not arrived at the second hour and made up for it in twenty minutes, and the toe at the foot of the west dyke is up two fingers off what it was on the third of a month that is now a month back, and the man at the front of it said a toe that has come up two fingers in a month is a toe that has started, and the three behind him did not write that down either.

The two boards were read aloud at the seventh hour, and reading them came before either figure was named, which is the order. One man took the first and another took the second, so that neither of them is trusted with both. **Five hundred and seventy-one days** and **six hundred and eighteen**, forty-seven between them, and the drawer stayed shut.

Sixty-four mornings out of a hundred and eleven, and both of the men who take those boards were in the building and had been all day, and about four people in this holding think the run is the best figure in the four books and about four think it is the only one that has never once been anybody's fault, and neither of the two fours has been asked.

The seventh went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Eight hundred and seventy-nine hundredweight.**

Down five off Friday, and the hundred and eighty-seventh figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and there have been a hundred and eighty-seven mornings of it.

The fall went onto the falls, so the window stands at eighty-two rises and seventy-two falls, and eighty-two and seventy-two are a hundred and fifty-four.

The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and eighty-five days, and it was written for the hundred and eighty-fourth of those mornings out of a hundred and eighty-six.

The letter came up the fen road on Friday afternoon and was opened in that room at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and it came in inside the six days the office keeps its window open, which is the first thing anybody noticed about it and the only thing anybody said about it for about ten minutes.

> FROM THE CLERK OF THE FIRST REGIONAL COUNCIL, DATED THE FIFTH OF THIS MONTH, SENT BY THE ORDINARY POST AND RECEIVED INSIDE THE SIX DAYS OF THE OFFICE'S OWN WINDOW, READ ALOUD IN THE ONE ROOM AT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE SEVENTH: THE CLERK ASKS THAT THE COOPERATIVE HOLDING CERTIFY THE FIGURE ON THE ATTACHED SHEET AS THE FIGURE OF THE RATE PER HUNDREDWEIGHT OF MAINTENANCE FOR A NODE OF THE KIND DESCRIBED, TO THE EFFECT THAT THE FIGURE MAY BE ENTERED IN A RATE AND CHARGED BY THE COUNCIL TO THE HOUSEHOLDS WITHIN ITS AREA, AND THE CLERK RECORDS THAT THE HOLDING IS NOT A MEMBER OF THE COUNCIL AND THAT THE COUNCIL IS NOT A MEMBER OF THE HOLDING, AND THAT THE FIGURE ON THE ATTACHED SHEET HAS BEEN IN A BOOK IN THIS TOWN FOR NINE YEARS AND HAS BEEN USED IN A RATE TWICE, AND THAT THE SECOND TIME IT WAS USED A COLLECTION WAS MADE AND NOBODY OBJECTED, AND THAT NOBODY OBJECTING IS NOT THE SAME AS THE FIGURE BEING RIGHT.

Nobody in that room said the last sentence was unfair. The clerk of the first council is not a fool and is not a saint and has put the two things in one paragraph on purpose, and the reason he put them in one paragraph is that he did not have a second column and has said so in the fourth sentence.

The attached sheet is one page and it is a good page. It gives the rate as a fraction of a hundredweight, and it gives the year the figure was last looked at, and it gives the name of the office the figure came out of, and it has a line at the bottom that says that any person who has kept a node of that kind may send in a figure and that the figure will be entered in a second column. That line is the reason the clerk sent it and it is the first time in four years that anybody has sent this holding a piece of paper with a place to put a figure on it.

Nobody put a figure on it.

The fraction on that sheet is not a figure this holding has ever entered anywhere, and it is not going to be entered this morning, because a rate nobody has certified is not a figure and putting it in a book would make it one on paper without making it one in fact, and about four years of this holding has made that move twice and been sorry for it once.

The clerk of this holding read it out twice and then read out the words certify and certification and nobody in the room said anything for about two minutes.

Then he said what a certification is, in his own mouth, and he had not written it down beforehand and about four people in that room said afterwards that they had not known that was what the word meant.

**"A certification is a sentence with a name under it and it says one thing only, and the thing it says is that this person has looked at this figure and will stand behind it in front of anybody who comes after. That is the whole of it and it is a real thing and I am not standing here saying it is a soft one."** "What it does is move the figure out of the book it was written in and into a rate that somebody else will charge. From the day the name goes under it, the figure stops being this holding's figure and becomes a number in a rate in a town nine miles off, and the households on the list of the council pay against it, and if it is wrong then the person whose name is under it is the person who was wrong, and not the council, and not the town, and not the book it came out of."

**"And I am going to say the second half of it and I am not being asked to. A person in this building whose name is under a figure becomes answerable for that figure, and there is no column in this holding that says what anybody's name is answerable for, and there has not been one for four years, and I am the clerk of this holding and I have looked for one this morning and there is not one."**

The man of about fifty was in that room with the frayed corner of the second book under his thumb, and he said two things and gave no reason for either of them in front of them, and the count of reasons given first in the holding's history did not move on account of either one.

He said that a collection had been made in that town on the strength of the figure and that about nine people had paid it and that nobody had asked what would happen to the ground if it stopped. He said that a thing which has been collected on is a thing which has been believed, whether or not anybody who believed it ever looked at it, and that the second time the figure was used was not the second time it was right.

Then he said the thing he had actually come in to say.

**"The figure is not the problem and I want that on the page before the seventh hour, because in about four years somebody is going to come in here with a piece of paper and say the figure was wrong, and on that day the only thing anybody in this building will be able to point at is a rate and a collection and a man who read a dial in four minutes."** "The problem is that a rate is a thing a body of people can charge and a dial is a thing a body of people have to stand next to, and the second one costs a body of people somebody standing next to it every week, and that is not a thing anybody is going to put in a rate."

Marek was in that room and said one thing, and he gave no reason for it in front of it, and the count of reasons given first in the holding's history did not move this morning.

**"I could read that dial in about four minutes and I have done it four hundred times and the figure would be a figure and it would be as good as the one in their book for as long as the dial says what it says. What it would not be is checked. A figure a man reads off a dial in four minutes has not been checked by anybody, and a man who signs it has not checked it either, and the two of those are the same man, and that is the whole of what a certification is when there is nobody else to check it against."**

The request was entered at about the sixth hour in the words it was made in. It was not answered, and the answer is not going up the fen road this week, and the clerk wrote that on the back of the council's own sheet in the same hand the request was in and not in his own, and nobody signed either.

The count of requests in this holding is fifty-three and is fifty-three, and the section-nine notes are fifty-three, and the two are not added to each other. A request that is entered is a request that was asked, and a request that was asked is not a fifty-fourth of anything, and about four years of this holding has been getting that wrong in a different direction, which is by treating every letter that came up a road as a thing that had to be carried.

It came in inside the six days. The column for a thing that goes to an office has not been written in this month, and it is not going to be written this month, and the six days are the office's own housekeeping and a letter arriving inside them is a letter that arrived on a day and not a letter that has somewhere to go.

There have been four of those windows in four years that anybody in this holding has written about, and the number of things that came out of one of them and went into a column of this building's book is nil, and nobody in this room offered to be the first.

The barrow came up the four-mile road at the ninth hour and went down it empty. The man of about thirty-one of Silling brought it and the second of the four lines in his own book is seventy-five days old this morning and has nothing on it, and this building has not asked him about it in seventy-five days. Eleven journeys is a floor.

On the low board at the far end of the room an offer nobody has filled lies face up, refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back, not withdrawn, no date, no name and no line for a name. A request from a council three counties off is not that instrument and has not been put on that board and is not going to be.

The register form is on the middle table at eighty-one days, not filled, not refused, and nothing has been written at the head of it in eighty-one days and nothing is going to be.

The seventh column of the well house door is at fifty-six and was not read this morning, and it is read on the thirtieth and on the first and on nothing else in the year. The six columns beside it stand headed nothing and still are.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the sheet that claims it lies in a drawer face up and is true in everything it says about itself, and the order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and opens nothing, and no route was reopened by a letter about a rate.

The compost line is paid and its run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one, and it has never once disagreed with the word in front of it, and it measures nothing at all.

The man of about seventy has not been fetched since the first of this month and twenty-four is the count, and the twenty-fifth falls on the first of the next, and nobody in this building has ever asked him which number he is on and no page here gives a reason.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is in its eleventh year and carries no dimension on any page. It was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced and not explained this morning, and the ground on both sides of it is going wrong more slowly than it was and nobody in this holding has called slower better.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice and was four inches at the second hour and is four inches at the ninth. A certification is not a mark, a refusal is not a mark, a rate is not a mark, and a thing read off a dial by a man in four minutes is not a mark, and there are four accounts of that arm and they do not agree with one another.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the seventh, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE HEAD DITCH OF THE LOW FIELD AND THE FOOT OF THE WEST DYKE, FOUR PEOPLE FROM THE SECOND HOUR UNTIL THE LIGHT WENT. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD AT THE SEVENTH HOUR AND THE RUN IS SIXTY-FOUR OF A HUNDRED AND ELEVEN, AND THE PAIR STANDS AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-ONE DAYS AND SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTEEN, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. THE THIRD LAUNDER IS EIGHT HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-NINE AND IS THE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVENTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT COME OUT AT THE SAME FIGURE TWICE. THE WINDOW STANDS AT EIGHTY-TWO RISES AND SEVENTY-TWO FALLS AND THE TWO ADD. THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAVE NOT MOVED IN A HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FIVE DAYS, WRITTEN FOR THE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOURTH OF THOSE MORNINGS OUT OF A HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SIX. THE CLERK OF THE FIRST REGIONAL COUNCIL ASKED THIS HOLDING TO CERTIFY A FIGURE FOR A RATE, AND THE REQUEST WAS ENTERED IN THE WORDS IT WAS MADE IN, AND IT WAS NOT ANSWERED, AND THE COUNT OF REQUESTS IS FIFTY-THREE AND THE SECTION-NINE NOTES ARE FIFTY-THREE, AND A REQUEST THAT IS ENTERED IS NOT A FIFTY-FOURTH OF ANYTHING. THE CLERK OF THIS HOLDING SAID IN HIS OWN MOUTH THAT A CERTIFICATION IS A SENTENCE WITH A NAME UNDER IT AND THAT THE NAME BECOMES ANSWERABLE FOR THE FIGURE, AND THAT THERE IS NO COLUMN IN THIS HOLDING THAT SAYS WHAT ANYBODY'S NAME IS ANSWERABLE FOR. NOBODY IN THAT ROOM GAVE A REASON BEFORE A THING THIS MORNING AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR THAT. THE FIGURE ON THE ATTACHED SHEET HAS BEEN IN A BOOK IN THAT TOWN FOR NINE YEARS AND NOBODY OBJECTING TO A COLLECTION IS NOT THE SAME AS THE FIGURE BEING RIGHT, AND NOBODY IN THIS ROOM SAID THE LAST SENTENCE OF THAT DOCUMENT WAS UNFAIR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
