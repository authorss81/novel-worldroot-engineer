# Chapter 418

## Four Things On One Morning

The first of this month was a Sunday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The top dyke between the gate and the old sluice took six people from the second hour to the sixth hour, and the frost was harder than it has been in this month and the top half of the dyke was like iron and the bottom half was mud to the ankle, and the five who could use a bar used one and the sixth carried.

The boards were read at the seventh hour, before either figure was named, which is the order. **Five hundred and sixty-five days** and **six hundred and twelve**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer stayed shut.

Both men who take those boards were in the building this morning, which is not the case on most Sundays, because the second of them spent the whole morning in the one room over the fetching.

Sixty-one mornings out of a hundred and five, counted from the seventeenth of a month four months back. The frame on those boards came with the month last night, the change is said here once, and it is not going to be said again.

The first went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Eight hundred and sixty-seven hundredweight.**

Down five off Saturday, and the hundred and eighty-first figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began, and it did not reset at midnight and it is not going to reset at anything.

The fall went onto the falls. Seventy-nine and sixty-nine make a hundred and forty-eight, and that window did not shift its footing at the turn of the month either.

Four bodies of households have gone a hundred and seventy-nine days without a move in their aggregate. The morning sentence is the hundred and seventy-eighth of those mornings out of a hundred and eighty, and the number did not change with the month.

The man of about seventy was fetched at about the fourth hour of the morning, from a house about a mile off, and it is the twenty-fourth time he has been fetched and the interval is thirty mornings, and the last of the twenty-three was on the first of a month thirty mornings back.

Nobody in this building has ever asked him which number it is and nobody is going to, and no page in this holding gives a reason he is fetched on the first of a month, and there is a column in the second book with a number in it and that column has no heading at the top of it and has not had one for eleven years.

He was in the one room from about the fifth hour to about the ninth with a blanket over his knees and a cup of tea he did not finish, and he was not asked a single question by anybody in that room, and the count of questions this holding has asked him is nil and is nil and did not move this morning.

He looked at the board on the wall for about a minute. Nobody has ever told him what the two rules on it are for and nobody has ever asked, and about four people in this building have wondered whether he knows and about four have not wondered, and the man of about seventy-four with his stick was in the door the whole of the three hours and did not say a word to him.

At about the seventh hour the clerk of this holding walked the nine hundred yards to the well house and read the seventh column of that door again.

It stands at fifty-six.

Yesterday it was fifty-five and the day before that it was fifty-four, and those two mornings together are the sixth time in four years that this column has been read on two days running, and about four years of a holding that keeps four books has not managed that six times, and the page is going to say the sixth of it and is not going to say it again.

The compost line fell this morning. The word before the figure is PAID, and the figure is one hundred pounds, and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one, and the word has never once disagreed with the figure and did not disagree this morning.

It is not a measurement of the ground. Nobody in this building is going to use it as one and nobody has used it as one in five years, and the fact that it fell on the first of a month and that the fetching fell on the first of a month is a fact about a calendar and is not a thing anybody in four counties arranged.

The return came off the steelyard at about the second hour of the afternoon, in the building at the node that has no roof, in about nine minutes of sleet that did not lie on anything.

> THE BOARD AT THE NODE, PAINTED ELEVEN YEARS AGO IN ONE HAND AND NOT REPAINTED SINCE. THE UPPER RULE IS PAINTED BY AND THE RULE UNDER IT IS PAINTED WITNESSED BY, AND THE FOUR NAMES OF THIS AFTERNOON WENT IN ONE AT A TIME IN THE ORDER THEY CAME UP TO THE DIAL, TWO INTO EACH RULE. THE SECOND RULE HAS A LINE PAINTED UNDER IT AND A SPACE BESIDE THAT LINE, AND THE SPACE HAS BEEN LEFT EMPTY EVERY TIME THE FIRST RULE HAS BEEN FILLED SINCE THE BOARD WAS PAINTED. NOBODY STANDING NEAR THAT DIAL HAS EVER PUT ANYTHING IN THAT SPACE, INCLUDING THIS AFTERNOON, AND NOBODY HAS EVER SAID OUT LOUD WHAT WOULD GO IN IT IF SOMETHING DID.

**Three hundred and sixty hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-eighth of them and the twenty-eighth pair of names in eleven years, and it stands eleven hundredweight above the thirty-seventh, which was three hundred and forty-nine on the twenty-second of this month and is the figure the last return in this holding's book carries.

The man of about fifty took it off the dial, said the figure out loud, and then said it again out of the second book, and the two were the same, and it came out of the dial twice checked against the two figures he had put on that day sheet at the fifth hour of the morning.

**Three hundred and six plus fifty-four. And two hundred and fifty-one plus a hundred and nine.**

This holding uses that derivation and does not refuse it. The two halves of it were not added this afternoon and will not be added by anybody.

The second rule on that board was left empty again. It has now stood empty twenty-two times in eleven years, and a count of blanks is not the count of a man, and it stood at twenty-one on the twenty-second of this month, which was nine mornings back.

Then the man who keeps the use log said it again, in that room, with the door shut, and he said it because a season turned last night, and nobody in that room had asked him to.

He gave the reason before the thing, as he has in that room for four years, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and twenty-four, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"The use log stands at fifteen lines and it is going to stand at fifteen lines on Monday and on Tuesday and for as long as I keep it, and I said that once, a long time ago, in the fifth month of a year that is now a month five months back, and I am saying it again today because a season turned last night and that is the only reason in the world I am saying it." "A line in that book has got a day in it and an hour in it and the name of the person who touched a thing. A thing a man carried out of a building has got a day in it and an hour in it and the name of the person who carried it. Those are two different sentences and only one of them is a contact. One of them is a record of the ground. The other one is a record of a man, and a man who starts keeping a record of himself has got two books and about four years later neither of them is any good."

The count of lines is fifteen. The count of times the reason has been told is two. The two are not added to each other and neither of them is going to be added to the other by anybody in this building.

Nobody thanked him for saying it a second time and nobody asked him for it a third and he was not thanked the first time either, and about four people in that room had heard it once before and about four had not, and the two fours have never been separated.

At about the eighth hour Tova Reed spoke in that room, and the reason came before the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is thirty-eight, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say one thing about the four clocks that have gone on one morning, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that four bodies of households are going to read about today in a book and none of the four of them is a figure of people, and a person who counts clocks instead of people is going to get this month wrong in a way that will take about four years to unpick."** "It does not get harder because of my left ear. It gets harder because a vote is coming, and I have said that once and I am not going to say it twice, and anybody waiting nine days for a reason to take the seed work off me has not got one."

The ninth hour of the evening, and the reason ahead of the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and ninety-nine, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the one thing that is mine this morning and I am going to give the reason for saying it first, and the reason is that I taught a woman from another county how to read a dial on Saturday afternoon for four minutes and she thanked me for it, and a man in this building has been thanked by somebody in four years." "Four things went this morning. Two of them are about this holding and two of them are about a holding of people who are not this holding, and all four of them fell on the first of a month because that is the shape of a calendar and not because anybody arranged it, and about four years of this building has been hoping that a shape of a calendar would turn into somebody's decision." "It has not, and it is not going to, and the four of them are on the second book this evening with the day against them and nobody's reason against them, and that is what a month looks like in this holding, and I have stopped being surprised by it and I have not started being used to it."

Nobody thanked anybody, and the day sheet went on the long table at the wall side at about the ninth hour, and Marek did not sign it and has not signed one since the thirteenth of this month, and the clerk has not asked him to and is not going to.

There is an offer face up on the low board. It is not filled, it was refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back, it has not been withdrawn, it has not been moved, and no instruction to take it off that board has ever reached this building.

A register form came on the sixteenth of a month now three months back and has stood on the middle table seventy-five days. It is not filled and it is not refused and there is nothing at the head of it, and nothing is going to be written there.

A drawer at the wall side holds a sheet describing a corridor in the pan. It is face up, and correct in every particular it states, and it does not say the corridor is open and it does not say it is shut. The order sealing the route is in the same drawer and remains valid, and it opens nothing.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at the ninth hour and came down it empty. Eleven journeys is where it stands and eleven is a floor. The man of about thirty-one of Silling brought it and the second of four lines in his own book is sixty-nine days old and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in sixty-nine days.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page. Nobody has walked it, measured it, crossed it, priced it or explained it. Four stakes went into the open ground outside it yesterday, and nobody in this holding was asked whether that was a good idea and nobody in this holding is going to be asked now.

The use log is at fifteen lines and got no sixteenth line this morning. A fetching is not a use, a column of figures is not a use, a day is not a use, and a reason that has been told twice is not told again. Fifty-three requests and fifty-three notes under section nine.

Four inches, two branches, and the same mark as at the second hour. A fetching is not a mark, a blank rule is not a mark, a thing said twice is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm are four and are unreconciled.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the first, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE TOP DYKE BETWEEN THE GATE AND THE OLD SLUICE, SIX PEOPLE TO THE SIXTH HOUR. FOUR THINGS FELL ON THIS MORNING AND NOT ONE OF THEM WAS ARRANGED. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS FETCHED AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR, BEING THE TWENTY-FOURTH TIME, AT THIRTY MORNINGS' INTERVAL, HE WAS IN THE ONE ROOM FROM THE FIFTH HOUR TO THE NINTH, HE WAS NOT ASKED A SINGLE QUESTION BY ANYBODY, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THIS HOLDING HAS ASKED HIM IS NIL AND IS NIL. THE COMPOST LINE FELL, THE WORD BEFORE THE FIGURE BEING PAID, THE FIGURE BEING ONE HUNDRED POUNDS, AND THE RUN TWENTY-ONE NOT DISCHARGED IN TWENTY-ONE, AND IT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND. THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR WAS READ AT THE SEVENTH HOUR AND STANDS AT FIFTY-SIX AFTER FIFTY-FIVE YESTERDAY, AND THOSE TWO MORNINGS ARE THE SIXTH TIME IN FOUR YEARS IT HAS BEEN READ ON TWO DAYS RUNNING. THE THIRTY-EIGHTH NINTH-DAY RETURN CAME OFF THE STEELYARD AT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AT THREE HUNDRED AND SIXTY HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE TWENTY-EIGHTH PAIR OF NAMES IN ELEVEN YEARS, ELEVEN ABOVE THE THIRTY-SEVENTH, RECONCILED TWICE OUT OF THREE HUNDRED AND SIX PLUS FIFTY-FOUR AND TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE PLUS A HUNDRED AND NINE, THE TWO HALVES NOT ADDED, AND THE SECOND RULE ON THE BOARD WAS LEFT BLANK FOR THE TWENTY-SECOND TIME IN ELEVEN YEARS. THE MAN WHO KEEPS THE USE LOG GAVE THE REASON FOR THE FIFTEEN A SECOND TIME, FOUR YEARS AND A DAY AFTER THE FIRST, BECAUSE A SEASON TURNED LAST NIGHT, AND THE COUNT OF TELLINGS IS TWO AND THE COUNT OF LINES IS FIFTEEN AND THE TWO ARE NOT ADDED. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT FOUR THINGS FELL BECAUSE OF THE SHAPE OF A CALENDAR AND NOT BECAUSE ANYBODY ARRANGED IT, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-NINE. THE SEEDWRIGHT GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID IT DOES NOT GET HARDER BECAUSE OF HER LEFT EAR, AND THAT ANYBODY WAITING NINE DAYS FOR A REASON TO TAKE THE SEED WORK OFF HER HAS NOT GOT ONE, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS THIRTY-EIGHT. THE REQUESTS STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND THE SECTION-NINE NOTES AT FIFTY-THREE. THE MONTH TURNED LAST NIGHT AND NOTHING RESET.**
