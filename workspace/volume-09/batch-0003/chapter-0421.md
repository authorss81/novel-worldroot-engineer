# Chapter 421

## The Other End Is Not His

The fourth of this month was a Wednesday. At the second hour the fourth line came round again and stood there empty, the way it has stood empty every fourth morning since the sixth of last month, and the fields at the headland above Marden have waited for the fifty-sixth morning.

The rota stands at eighty-five mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and fifty-six not, and twenty-nine and fifty-six are eighty-five.

The count goes up by one when this line comes round and by no amount at all when it does not. Twenty-nine taken has not shifted this morning, or on any morning of this year, and a field that waited is not a man's fault.

Two ways of counting that rotation are kept in this building and the two of them do not agree, so both of them go on this morning's page. This is the thirty-third morning of the run out of the twenty-sixth of a month five months back, and the twentieth morning of the run anchored on the eighteenth of a month three months back, and that second frame came with the month a few mornings ago and is not said again.

The six who would have gone to that headland were on the boundary ditch, and Marek was not one of the six this morning for the first time in four years, and nobody in this holding has written down where he was instead and nobody is going to.

He was at his end from about the second hour until about the ninth, and he was at it alone, and what he did there for seven hours is not entered anywhere in this holding and is not going to be entered anywhere, and about four people in four counties have wanted to ask him about it and about four have not, and neither of those two fours is going to.

The end he was at is about four miles from his end, which is a day's walk, and the man of about seventy-one of Silling who now shares it walked over on Tuesday morning and got there before Marek did, and nobody sent him and nobody asked him and he has not said he would come again.

What he can be said to have done is this. He wrote two names on a page and read them out to the two people, and he wrote down the hour each of them took it, and he wrote down which of them was there first.

This page carries no figure for the run, because the two boards were not read out loud this morning, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. A man who has spent the morning at a different end of a thing is not a man this holding has ever asked to say two figures out loud at the eighth.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and sixty-eight days** and **six hundred and fifteen**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The fourth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Eight hundred and eighty hundredweight.**

Up nine off Tuesday, and the hundred and eighty-fourth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The rise went onto the rises. Eighty-one and seventy make a hundred and fifty-one.

The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and eighty-two days, and it was written for the hundred and eighty-first of those mornings out of a hundred and eighty-three.

The far end of the bridge is held by two people and neither of them is the reader of this body.

Harlan Vetch has held it since Sunday morning and the man of about seventy-one of Silling has held it since Tuesday morning, and they took it between them without a vote and without a document and without anybody in this building asking whether they would, and they are both of them about as qualified as anybody in four counties, which is to say not at all.

What was given up this morning was not a thing and not a right and not a share. What was given up was exclusive access, which is the whole of the difference, and it was given up in about eleven seconds in a room with the door shut and nobody thanked him for it and nobody is going to.

Harlan Vetch was in the yard at about the ninth hour on the Sunday and said one thing before he went, and he said it without a reason in front of it and the count of that did not move, because it does not move on a page where the reason is not given.

**"I have carried a barrow up a road for four years because somebody had to,"** he said. **"That is the whole of what I know how to do with an end of anything and I would like it written down that I said it in that order and not the other one."**

About four people in that yard had wanted to tell him he was doing the right thing and about four had wanted to tell him he did not know what he was taking, and neither of the two fours said a word and neither of them is going to.

The man of about seventy-one of Silling took it on Tuesday morning at about the seventh hour, alone, having said on Monday that he would, and he had been asked nothing about it and had volunteered it, and the count of questions asked at that far end is nil before and nil after.

> ENTERED IN THE SECOND BOOK OF THIS HOLDING AT ABOUT THE ELEVENTH HOUR OF THE MORNING OF THE FOURTH, IN THE CLERK'S OWN HAND, AND NOT UNDER A HEADING, BECAUSE THERE IS NO HEADING AT THE TOP OF THIS COLUMN AND NOBODY WROTE ONE. THAT FROM THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH THE FAR END OF THE BRIDGE IS HELD BY TWO PERSONS, NAMED IN THE COLUMN ITSELF AND NOT ON ANY OTHER PAGE, AND THAT THE READER OF THIS BODY IS NOT ONE OF THEM. THAT THE STANDING OF THAT BRIDGE IS IN NO BOOK IN THIS BUILDING AND THAT NO PERSON IN THIS HOLDING HAS AN INSTRUMENT THAT SAYS WHETHER IT IS ON OR OFF. THAT NOBODY HAS ASKED EITHER OF THEM WHETHER THEY WOULD, AND THAT NEITHER OF THEM HAS SAID THAT THEY WOULD HAVE SAID YES, AND THAT NEITHER OF THEM WAS ASKED. THAT THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS ASKED AT THAT FAR END IS NIL BEFORE THE FOURTH OF THIS MONTH AND NIL AFTER IT.

Nobody in that room improved a word of it and the man of about fifty read it back twice and did not put a heading at the top of it and was asked to by one person and did not.

A count of pattern-holders is not a count of ends. Two people can hold four, one person can hold five, and there are two of them in this holding with a rootmark between them, and none of those four figures is a figure about a bridge and the four of them are not added to the two names in that column.

Marek read that line over twice after the clerk had read it back and did not say anything about it, and about four people in that room had expected him to and about four had not.

The count of questions the reader of this body has asked the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden is one, and it was nil an hour before, and nobody in this holding has asked her a question about a number in four years, and the question was asked at about the second hour of the afternoon at the gate.

He asked it with the reason first, and the count of that in the holding's history is two hundred and two, and it is his and of nobody else's. She gave hers first, and the count of that in the holding's history is nineteen, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to ask you one question and I am going to give the reason for it first, and the reason is that a council is going to vote in a fortnight on a schedule and a connection and a holding, and there is one number you say out loud in rooms that nobody in four counties can put a unit to, and it is the only number in this holding with that property, and I would rather ask you than go and guess at it in a room with a door shut."** "What is it a number of?"

**"It is a number I say when somebody in a room needs a number,"** she said, **"and I have been saying it for about eleven years, and I have never once known what it is a number of, and I have never had a unit for it, and I am not going to invent one at about two in the afternoon because a man has finally asked me after four years."** "It is not a figure of anything anybody could check. If it were, one of the four books in this building would have it in it. You have got two figures in this holding that you cannot check and you have got six things you do not know, and I am not a seventh of either, and I am not a unit, and you have not got a unit out of me this afternoon and you were not going to."

There was no figure in what she had said and no denial of one, and the six not knowns are six, and about four people in this holding have known for four years that she does not know and about four have not known, and neither of those two fours has ever asked her.

She said one more thing to him at the gate before she went, and she gave no reason for it and it is not entered and the count did not move.

**"You have been eleven years getting somebody to ask me that and you have done it in about four seconds, and I would like you to understand that it has cost me nothing and that is not the same as it not being a thing."**

She was at the north gate with the bar and the chain at about the fourth hour of the afternoon and she did not open the cut at the kerb and did not go near the valve and the key went back into her pocket about four feet from where the valve is, and the two-foot gap is still open and nothing has gone into it this season.

A letter came up the fen road on the Friday before with a request in it, and the clerk of this holding read it out in that room, and the request was that this holding enter, as a further account against the mark on the engineer's arm, a statement of what the work had cost him.

It came from a council and it was in a clerk's hand and it was four paragraphs long and it ended with a thank-you in advance, and the reason it is on this page at all is that it is the first request anybody in four counties has made of this holding that was asking for a person and not a figure.

About four people in that room thought the request was a kindness and about four thought it was a way of getting at the only thing in this holding that a body like theirs could not get any other way, and neither of the two fours has been asked which it was, and the second of the two fours is the one that was right.

He refused it, in about nine people, with the reason first, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and two, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to refuse a request from a county and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that there are four accounts of that arm in this building and they do not agree with one another, and a fifth would not be an account, it would be a decision about which of the four was right, and it is not this building's to make and it is not a county's to ask for."** "A refusal is not a fifth account. The mark is four inches and it branches twice and it was four inches at the second hour this morning and it is four inches at the ninth hour, and a warm mark is not a mark, and a session is not a mark, and a name is not a mark, and a gate is not a mark, and a vault is not a mark, and a seizure is not a mark, and an outbreak is not a mark, and a thing said in a room is not a mark."

Nobody in that room argued with it and nobody improved it, and the man of about fifty wrote the refusal into the second book as it was given and read it back, and the request stands in its own column and the column is a request column and not an account of anything.

The clerk put the council's letter in the long table at the wall side with the other two and it is still there, and nobody in this building is going to write back and nobody is going to be asked to.

At about the fifth hour the man of about fifty put the reason in front of the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and twenty-five, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the one thing about that column I have been asked for twice this week and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that in about four years somebody is going to find a page in this book with two names in it and no heading above it, and they are going to ask what those two names are doing in a column, and nobody in this building will be able to tell them." "The answer is that they are holding an end. That is the whole of it. There is no figure for how many ends there are and there is no figure for what an end costs and there is no figure for whether one is better than two, and the reason I am not going to invent a heading for it is that a heading would be the only thing on the page and a page with a heading and nothing under it is the ninth not-known and this holding has six."

Nobody thanked anybody. An offer is still face up on the low board, unfilled and not withdrawn, refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back and not moved since.

It is on the middle table at seventy-eight days, neither filled nor refused, with nothing at the head of it, and the day sheet was the only thing that went on that table this morning.

There is a sheet in a drawer, face up, claiming a corridor in the pan. It is correct in every particular. The order sealing the route is in the same drawer and is valid and opens nothing. The route is not reopened by anything here.

He was fetched on the first of this month and not today, so twenty-four is the count. The compost line is paid, and its run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one, and it has never been a measurement of the ground.

The seventh column of the well house door is at fifty-six and is not read this morning, and is read on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next and on no other morning in the year.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year, carries no dimension on any page, and was not walked, measured, crossed, priced or explained. The ground on both sides of it is still going wrong and is going slower, and nobody in this holding has said that slower is better.

An end of a bridge is not a use and the log is at fifteen lines. The reason for the fifteen was told twice on the first of this month. Fifty-three requests and fifty-three notes, and a refusal entered as a refusal is one of those fifty-three.

Four inches and two branches, and nothing said about that arm this morning is a fifth of anything.
