# Chapter 419

## What The Ground Does

The second of this month was a Monday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The west side of the boundary ditch took five people from the second hour to the sixth hour and they got about two hundred yards of it done in four hours, which is the worst rate anybody in this holding can remember for that side, and the reason was in the ground and not in the men.

The water in the ditch at the two hundred yard mark was coming up. Not standing, not spilling, coming up through the silt at about the pace a man can walk, in a band about a foot wide, on the upslope side of a ditch that has carried water down that line every day of the four years anybody in this holding has been reading it.

This page carries no figure for the run, because the two boards were not read out loud this morning, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. There is a thing going wrong in two hundred yards of ditch and there is no version of a morning on which the two men who take those boards stand at a table and say a pair of figures in the middle of it.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and sixty-six days** and **six hundred and thirteen**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The second went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Eight hundred and seventy-six hundredweight.**

Up nine off Sunday, and the hundred and eighty-second figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The rise went onto the rises. Eighty and sixty-nine make a hundred and forty-nine.

A hundred and eighty days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, entered this morning for the hundred and seventy-ninth of those mornings out of a hundred and eighty-one.

The ground at the node began to go wrong in the night after the taking and yesterday was the first of the two mornings of it, and nobody in this holding was at the node yesterday and nobody in this holding has a figure for yesterday and is not going to invent one.

Marek had been out at the node before the light and had been out again at about the tenth hour and had come back down the road with about four hundred yards of the north side walked over and nothing done about it, because there was nothing in the four years of a Tuesday that he knew to do about a thing that behaves like that.

A rider came up the fen road at about the second hour of the afternoon with a sheet from the Assembly at Thornwild, and it is the first document that body has sent to this holding in four years, and it went on the long table at the wall side and it is correct in every particular and it lets nothing out.

Nobody read that sheet out. The clerk of this holding read it standing, and the man of about fifty read it after him, and about four people in that room have wanted to hear it read out and about four have not, and it is going to be read out on Thursday because a thing that arrives from four and a half miles off does not sit in a drawer for a fortnight.

> OF THE ASSEMBLY AT THORNWILD, ENTERED IN THEIR OWN BOOK ON THE THIRTIETH OF LAST MONTH, AND SENT TO THE HOLDING WHICH KEEPS THE NODE ON THE SECOND OF THIS MONTH, AND COPIED TO THE FIRST AND SECOND COUNCILS. THE ASSEMBLY RECORDS THAT IT HAS TAKEN INTO ITS OWN KEEPING AND ITS OWN MAINTENANCE THE HEARTHROOT AT THE NODE, AND THAT FOUR OF ITS PEOPLE WERE AT THAT NODE FROM THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON UNTIL THE FIFTH ON THE THIRTIETH, AND THAT FOUR STAKES WERE PUT IN THE OPEN GROUND ON THE NORTH SIDE, EACH WITH A WAXED COVER AND NO WRITING. THE ASSEMBLY RECORDS THAT IT HAS NO POWER TO COMPEL ANY PERSON TO ANSWER THE READING AT THAT NODE, AND THAT ITS OWN REASON FOR THE FIRST CLAUSE IS THAT A SETTLEMENT HARMED ONCE BY A CONNECTION IS ENTITLED TO BE ASKED, AND THAT IT HAS BEEN ASKED, AND THAT IT WAS GIVEN AN INSTRUMENT AND NOT AN ANSWER. THE ASSEMBLY RECORDS THAT THE FIRST FOUR DAYS OF THAT HELD WERE FOUR DAYS OF TRAVEL AND FOUR DAYS OF STANDING AT A PLACE AND WERE NOT A REFUSAL OF ANYTHING. THE ASSEMBLY ASKS NOBODY TO DO ANYTHING BY THIS SHEET.

It is a consequence of damaged and contradictory patterns. It is not an army. It is not a command. It is nobody's fault, and the page has to say those four things in that order because in about four years this holding is going to be asked which of them was true and all four of them are, and the order they are said in is the order they were found in.

What is happening is that two answers are being given to the same ground. A heartroot is a pattern and a pattern is not a valve and a valve is not a lock, and a body of four people has been at that node since Saturday putting one answer into it, and this holding has been putting a different answer into the same ground every Tuesday for four years, and neither answer is wrong and both of them are being given at once.

The water is not doing what water does. It is going two ways from a place where there is one way out of it, and it is letting off at two low points about a quarter of a mile apart, and the low ground between those two points is where anybody who has to cross it on foot at about the wrong hour is going to have a bad morning.

A willow at about four hundred yards off the node lost a year's growth on its north side between Saturday evening and this morning, all of it, in one night, on one side, and the man of about fifty stood in front of it for a long time and put his hand flat on the bark of the wrong side and took it away again.

A second willow, about ninety yards off and about forty years old, went the other way and put a season of soft wood on its south side in the same night, and the two of them are about ninety yards apart and they are not in the same field and nobody has put them in the same field.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is the one place on that node that nobody in this holding can read, and nobody in this holding has ever read it, and it is bare in its eleventh year and it is bare this morning exactly as it has been for eleven years.

A man put a stake outside it at about the eleventh hour and did not go in. The stake is in the open ground about nine feet out from the edge, in the same line of open ground where four other stakes went in on Saturday, and the two lines of stakes can be seen from each other and the ring is between them and nothing has gone into it and the outbreak did not put a dimension on it.

It has not been walked and it has not been measured and it has not been crossed and it has not been priced by walking it and it has not been explained, and the fact that the ground on both sides of it is going wrong this morning is the first time in eleven years that anything anybody can name has happened on either side of it, and that is not a reason to walk into it and nobody in this holding has walked into it.

There were more than five bodies on that node this morning, and five is the number on the page, and a count of five is a fact about a rota and not a count of a morning, and the number is not given in a yard and this was not a yard.

Then somebody on the slope asked the man of about fifty for the figures in the pulse.

He did not read it out and he did not say what it is called and he is not going to, and it carries two figures and no name and it has been read on a page three times in this holding's history and it is not read a fourth.

He gave the reason after the thing instead of before it, which is the one time in four years either of the two of them has done that in a room with the door shut, and the reason is a fact about a page and not about a person.

"There are two figures in it and there is no name against them, and the last time either of us put those two figures into a book somebody in a room four years later treated the page as though the name had been left off by accident and went looking for a man to put on it." "That is what I have. It is a fact about a page. It is not a fact about you and it is not going to be asked of you this morning, and I have never once in four years asked you what it is either, and that has been an arrangement and not an accident, and about four years of the two of us have not broken it."

Nobody in that room improved that and nobody thanked him for the shape of it, and the count of that in the holding's history did not move this morning because he gave the reason after the thing and it does not move on a page where the reason is not given first.

The person who had asked was a man from one of the four counties and not from a council, and he took the answer and did not argue with it, and he has not written to this holding about it since and is not going to, and about four people in that room had expected him to argue and about four had not.

With the door shut, at about the seventh hour, Marek put the reason in front of the sentence, and the count of that in the holding's history is two hundred, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the sentence this morning is going to be remembered for, and I am going to give the reason for saying it first, and the reason is that I have been at that node four years on a Tuesday and I have never once seen the ground do anything I could not account for, and this morning I cannot account for it, and a man who cannot account for a thing says so while there is still time for somebody to write it down wrong." "A heartroot held by a body that is not holding a pattern puts damaged and contradictory patterns into the ground around it. That is what has happened here. It is not an army, it is not a command, and it is nobody's fault, and if this holding says any of those three things about it in a room in about four years, then this holding has been wrong since the twenty-sixth of last month and not since this morning, and I would rather have it written down now with my name on the page while I still know that."

Nobody in that room said he was wrong and nobody said he was right, and about four people in it had wanted him to say the first two of those four things and about four had wanted him to say the third, and neither of the two fours is going to be asked.

The low board still holds an offer. Face up, unfilled, refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back, not withdrawn, not moved, and with no second paper of it on any table in this holding this season.

Seventy-six days is the standing of the register form on the middle table. Not filled, not refused, nothing written at the head of it.

A corridor in the pan has a name and is open and is not a road. The sheet claiming it lies face up in a drawer and is right in every particular. The order sealing the route is in that drawer with it, valid, opening nothing. Neither has moved this month and neither is going to.

The man of about seventy was fetched yesterday, so his count stands at twenty-four, and he is not fetched again today. The compost line is paid, and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one, and the word before that figure has never once been wrong about it. It is not a measurement of the ground, and this is the morning on which anybody who wanted to use it as one would have used it.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-six. It was read yesterday and it is not read this morning, and it is read on the first of a month and on the thirtieth and on nothing else in the year. The six columns beside it are headed nothing.

A stake is not a use and ground going wrong is not a use, so the log is still at fifteen lines. The reason for the fifteen was told twice on the first of this month and is not going to be told again. Fifty-three requests, fifty-three notes, and a thing nobody can name is not a fifty-fourth.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice. Four accounts of it are kept and they do not reconcile. A figure not read a third time is not a mark, a hand flat on the wrong side of a tree is not a mark, and nothing that happened at that node this morning is a fifth of anything.
