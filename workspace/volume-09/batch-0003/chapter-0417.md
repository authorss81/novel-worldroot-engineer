# Chapter 417

## The Assembly Takes The Node

A Saturday, and the thirtieth of this month with it. The fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour as it does every fourth morning, and it went away empty, because there is nobody at the headland above Marden to work it. Those fields have now waited fifty-five mornings.

The rota stands at eighty-four mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and fifty-five not, and twenty-nine and fifty-five are eighty-four.

The figure rises by exactly one on the mornings this line comes round and by nothing on the other mornings. Twenty-nine taken has not moved this morning and has not moved on any morning of this year, and a field that waited is nobody's fault.

This building keeps two counts of the same rotation and they do not agree, so the page carries both. The thirty-second morning of the run out of the twenty-sixth of a month four months back. The nineteenth morning of the run anchored on the eighteenth of a month two months back.

The frost was back in the shade at the second hour and the day came out about the fifth, hard and clear, the sort of morning that makes a slope look like a shape somebody drew.

This page carries no figure for the run, because the two boards were not read out loud this morning, and the reason is on the page rather than left off it. Four of the six who would have gone to the headland went up the road to the node instead, because four people from an Assembly four and a half miles off were expected at that node before the second hour, and the two men who take the boards are not two of the four.

The pair stands on the boards at **five hundred and sixty-four days** and **six hundred and eleven**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened.

The thirtieth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Eight hundred and seventy-two hundredweight.**

Up nine off Friday, and the hundred and eightieth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The rise went onto the rises. Seventy-nine and sixty-eight make a hundred and forty-seven.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in a hundred and seventy-eight days, and the line written for it this morning is the hundred and seventy-seventh of those mornings out of a hundred and seventy-nine.

At about the seventh hour the clerk of this holding walked the nine hundred yards to the well house with a lamp that was not needed in that light and came back at about the eighth, and he read the seventh column of that door.

It stands at fifty-five.

Six columns on that door stand headed nothing and have stood headed nothing for four years, and nobody has written a word at the top of any of them, and the seventh is the one that is read, and it is read on the first and the thirtieth and on no other morning, and it is read again tomorrow and not on any morning after that until the turn of the next month.

It stood at fifty-four for four months and it is fifty-five now, and from this morning it does not go back.

The node is a day's walk east and about five miles, and five of the people from this holding went up the road at about the second hour of the afternoon and were at it by the fourth.

The Assembly's four people had been on the road four days counting the day they arrived, and they came in off the drove at about the third hour of the afternoon, and they were not carrying anything except a lantern, a bundle of oilcloth and a sheet of their own with a waxed cover on it.

They were not late and they were not early and there is no page anywhere in four counties that says a time they were due, and that is the first thing about this that nobody in this building has been able to get used to: a body that has come four and a half miles to take a thing off a place arrived at the hour they said they would and not an hour either side of it.

About nine people from four counties were on the slope above the node, and they had come from three directions and none of them were from this holding, and they had been there since about the third hour and had not come down.

They were not there for a fight and they were not there to watch a fight, and they had brought nothing with them but boots, and they stood about forty feet up the slope in a line that got longer as the afternoon went on, and the ones who came late stood behind the ones who came early.

Two of them were from the county that refused to form a council and had asked for a maintenance list instead, and that clerk was among them and had a sheet under his arm, and nobody in this building has ever asked that county for anything and that clerk is the only person on that slope who has ever wanted a page of paper from this holding rather than a figure.

The building at the node with a barrow standing in it and four men around the barrow is not a yard. The slope above it with about nine people standing on it is not a yard either. The count of things said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong is five and it is five, and it did not move this morning and nobody on that slope has ever been in a yard and nobody in that building has either.

What happened at that node at about the fourth hour of the afternoon took about eleven minutes and there was no fight in it and there has never been a fight in this holding's history and there was not going to be one on a hard clear Saturday with a woman of about seventy-one of Silling sitting on the wall with her stick across her knees.

The four people of the Assembly went to the node and read the dial, and they read it the way anybody reads it, which is badly, and then one of them asked the man of about fifty to read it and stood about four feet off and watched his hands while he did it, and he read it and gave the figure and did not explain where it came from.

Then they put four stakes in the open ground on the north side of the node, in a line, driven about a hand's depth, with a waxed cover over the top of each one and nothing written on any of them, and the woman at the middle of that group read out from their own sheet what the four stakes were for.

One of the four was about thirty and one was about fifty and one was the woman and the fourth was a man who had walked up from the low gate and had not said anything at all for an hour, and about four people on that slope had counted them and about four had not.

**"That is the reading of this ground,"** she said, **"and from today the reading of this ground is ours, and the answering of it is ours, and the four of us will come here on the days this sheet says and not on any other days, and the ground on the far side of that line is held by us and not by anybody else. We are not taking anything off this place. We are taking the duty. There is no other word for it that we are willing to use in front of you."**

Nobody in this building improved that sentence and nobody in this building has ever printed a better one.

The reason came before the thing from the man of about fifty, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and twenty-three, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the whole of what I have got, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that I have been in this building eleven years and I have watched four bodies of households do four things this month and not one of them has been a lie, and I have run out of sentences that are against anybody." "They have a right. That was said on Thursday at Thornwild by a woman who is not a fool and who had a worse answer than an instrument four years ago. They have not been asked what they are. They have been told what the holding of a thing is, which is a thing I have said out loud in that room, and they have taken it at the word, and nobody in this building is going to say today that they are thieves for taking a thing at the word." "And I have not got one thing to say that is against that, and I have been asked for one by two people in this room in the last ten minutes, and I am not able to supply it, and that is a fact about a morning and not about a man."

At about the fifth hour Marek spoke, and the reason came first, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and ninety-eight, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I asked them on Thursday to give it back and they said no with four reasons that are mine, and I told them in that room that they were right, and I meant it, and I have not stopped meaning it between then and this afternoon." "I am going to say the part of it that is mine and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that I walked four and a half miles to lose an argument and then came home and put my name at the head of a page about it, and that page is in the second book, and a page with my name on it is the only instrument I have." "What they have just done is correct and it is not a right and it is not a wrong. It is a thing done by people who were not asked and who have taken the duty in the only words anybody gave them, and those words were mine, and I have been saying them out loud in rooms for four years and I have never once been asked what happens to a ground when a body holds a thing that was never a valve." "And I am not going to be able to say on the next page what happens, because nobody in this building has a way of knowing that yet, and the six not knowns are six, and I am not going to make a seventh out of an afternoon."

Nobody in that room said anything for about a minute, and about four people in it had wanted to say something and about four had not, and about four people on that slope have been watching two buildings' worth of people say nothing at each other and about four of those went home before the end.

A man from the second council was on that slope and had a coat he did not need on, and he did not come down and he did not speak to anybody, and about four people near him saw him and about four did not, and that is a fact about a slope and not a count of anybody.

The clerk of the county that refused to be a body came down at about the fifth hour to look at one of the four stakes and did not touch it, and read the waxed cover and found nothing on it, and went back up without saying a word to anybody about it.

The four stakes stood in the open ground with waxed covers on them and nothing written on them, and the ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is about nine feet inside the north line of the node and the stakes are not in it, and the stakes are not in it because nobody put a foot in it and nobody is going to.

Nobody said anything out loud in a yard this morning, at this holding or at the node, because there was no yard at either, and the count of things said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong is five, and it is five.

At about the sixth hour the woman at the middle of the Assembly's four came back across to where Marek was standing and asked him one question, which was what the dial reads when a body is not standing at it.

He told her. It took about four minutes and he told her the whole of what a person needs to know to read it and to answer it, and he did it because the four of them are going to be at that node on Tuesday and there is nobody else within a day's walk who can read it and who is not one of them.

He did not tell her what it costs. She did not ask, and it is not on any page in this building, and the six not knowns are six and this morning is not a seventh of them.

She thanked him for it, in front of the other three and in front of about four people on the slope, and she did not shake his hand and did not touch his arm and did not use the word ally, and he was thanked by a person he lost an argument to on Thursday and he has not yet worked out what that is going to be in about four years.

He came down the road at about the ninth hour and the frost had come back into the ruts and his boots went through it twice.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the node and had been all afternoon and came down with the last of them, and nobody asked him anything, and the second of the four lines in the book he carries is sixty-eight days old and has nothing on it, and the barrow stands at eleven journeys and eleven is a floor.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden came down with the second group and had the bar and the chain over her shoulder and had not opened the two-foot gap at the kerb and had looked at the four stakes for about as long as it takes to count to four, and nobody asked her what she thought of them and she has not said.

The offer is on the low board, face up, and not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of last month, and it has not been withdrawn, and one of the four clauses of the Assembly's own motion is the reason a body of people four and a half miles off has just put four stakes in the ground of a node this holding works on a Tuesday.

The register form is on the middle table at seventy-four days, not filled and not refused, with nothing at the head of it.

In the drawer at the wall side, under a sheet that describes a corridor in the pan and is right in every particular it states, there is an order sealing a route. The order is valid and it opens nothing. The corridor is named and open and it is not a road, and neither of the two has moved in four years, and neither of them was moved by anything that happened at a node this afternoon.

The man of about seventy has not been sent for and stands at twenty-three fetchings, and the twenty-fourth is tomorrow morning, on the first of a month, one morning off. The compost line is paid and its run is twenty not discharged in twenty, and it does not fall this month.

Fifteen lines in the use log, and four stakes driven into the ground of a node do not go in it, and the reason the log stands at fifteen is not given again in a season. Fifty-three requests, fifty-three notes under section nine, and a stake is not a fifty-fourth of anything.

The mark on that arm is four inches long and branches twice, and the four accounts of it in this building are four and do not reconcile with one another. A stake is not a mark. A duty is not a mark. Being thanked is not a mark. Nothing that happened on that slope this afternoon is a fifth of anything.
