# Chapter 420

## No Command Over Consent

The third of this month was a Tuesday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The inside face of the west dyke above the old sluice took six people from the second hour to the sixth hour, and the ground there had begun to set hard at the top and had not at the bottom, and the two do not meet anywhere a bar can be put in.

Marek worked it from the second hour to the sixth and was at the node from the seventh hour of the morning until about the third hour of the afternoon and the ground was still doing what it began doing on Saturday night, and it was slower on the second day than it was on the first, and nobody in this holding knows whether slower is better.

**Five hundred and sixty-seven days** and **six hundred and fourteen**, forty-seven apart, and both were said out loud at the seventh hour with the numbers spoken only after the boards had been read, and the drawer was not opened.

Sixty-two mornings out of a hundred and seven.

The third went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Eight hundred and seventy-one hundredweight.**

Down five off Monday, and the hundred and eighty-third figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The fall went onto the falls. Eighty and seventy make a hundred and fifty.

Without a move, the four bodies of households have now gone a hundred and eighty-one days in their aggregate, and that was written this morning for the hundred and eightieth of those mornings out of a hundred and eighty-two.

The Assembly's four people were at the node by about the ninth hour and they were working the open ground on the north side, where the four stakes stand, in a strip about four yards wide, and they were doing it the way anybody does a job they have not been trained for and have decided to do properly, which is badly and without stopping.

They had brought a bar and a spade and a mattock and a line, and the line was their own and not anybody's, and they stretched it twice and both times it came out four inches out, and neither of them said anything about the four inches to anybody on that slope.

The strip is eleven yards long. They had done about four of it by the second hour of the afternoon and had opened the turf along the top edge with a mattock brought from Thornwild, and the turf came up in squares and they were stacking them the way a man stacks turf who has watched it done and not been shown.

The man of about fifty watched them do it for about ten minutes and did not go over and did not say anything, and about four people in four counties have since asked him why he did not go over, and the answer he has given all four of them is that a man who has asked two people to move off a strip of ground and been refused twice does not get a third try for free.

Marek held the pattern at that node from about the tenth hour of the morning.

There is no word in this holding's four books for what that is and there is not going to be one, and it is not a valve and it is not a key and it is not a switch, and the man who did it has done it about four hundred times and it takes about as long as reading a dial and costs about the same as reading a dial and gives him a headache by the second hour.

The four of the Assembly worked to within about six yards of the edge of the ring of bare ground and stopped there, and the reason they stopped there is that it is bare and bare ground is not ground anybody wants to stand in, and they had not been told that there is a thing on this node that cannot be read at all and they are going to find it out on their own in about a week, and nobody in this holding is going to be in a position to stop them finding it out.

He asked one of them to move. The woman at the middle of the four looked at him for about a minute and then went back to the strip without moving, and she did not say no and she did not say why, and about four people who were not there have since said that she ought to have said why.

He asked the second one. He asked her in a different way and with the reason given first and the second one heard the reason, and the second one did not move either, and she said one sentence about a woman at a table on Thursday and about a right, and that sentence is in the second book of this holding in four lines that a man wrote down from memory and read back.

Then he said it out loud, once, in about nine people, at the node, on a Tuesday, with the ground going wrong on both sides of him.

**"No command over consent. That is the whole of what I have got and I have had it since the fifteenth of this month and I did not know that was its name until about an hour ago. I have no power over those four people, I have no instrument that says one word to them, and there is no column anywhere in four counties that anybody has ever written a heading at the top of that would give me one, and I have found that out on my own feet and not on anybody's paper."**

Not one person in that room said whether the sentence was the right one. About four of them had been waiting three days for somebody to say it and about four had hoped nobody ever would, and neither of the two fours is going to be asked which it was.

At about the fourth hour of the afternoon a woman came up the road from the west end on foot and stood at the gate and said there was somebody in the low ground between the two low points and asked for a cart and a hand.

Nobody in this building asked her who it was. The man of about fifty took the cart down himself and two of the holding's men went with him and they were gone about an hour and a half, and they came back up the road at about the sixth hour with the cart and with the man of about thirty-one of Silling's barrow borrowed off somebody, and nobody in that room asked them what they had found and they did not volunteer it.

The page is not going to name the person and is not going to give the hurt a number, and about four people in this holding are going to find out who it was inside a week and are not going to be told on this page, and the two of those facts are both of them true.

The page is also not going to name a person who did it, because no person did it. The page is not going to name a person who could have stopped it, and it is going to say why: there were about nine people in four counties who could have been in that low ground at the sixth hour this morning instead of where they were, and one of the nine is the reader of this body, and he was in a room with a door shut at the fourth hour asking a body of people to move off a strip of ground four yards wide, and a thing nobody in this holding has ever been able to do is put a duty and a person in the same sentence and have both of them still mean what they said on Saturday.

Nobody in that room said that this was anybody's fault and nobody said it was nobody's fault, and the four things about the ground were said on Monday afternoon in the order they were found in and this page is not going to say them again.

And a person in a low place on a Monday afternoon is a fact about a slope and a morning. It is not a reason anybody stops arguing, and nobody in that room stopped arguing, and the argument was about four yards of ground on the north side and it went on after the cart came back up the road.

He spoke at about the seventh hour and the reason came first, and the count of that in the holding's history is two hundred and one, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the one thing I have not said to anybody about this and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that four days ago I told a woman in a room at Thornwild that being right is not a thing a man gets for free, and I have had four days to work out what that sentence is for, and this is it." "I have been right all week. I have been right about the pattern and I have been right about the two answers going into the same ground and I have been right about the cost of it, and none of that has been worth anything to anybody in a low place at the sixth hour this afternoon." "The thing I have not got is the other half of the sentence, and the other half is that being right is not a thing a man can use, either, and a man in four counties who has spent a week being right about a heartroot is a man who has spent a week not being anywhere near the four yards of ground that would have helped."

Tova Reed was in the one room at this holding at about the eighth hour and she had come down the road behind the cart, and she gave the reason before she said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is thirty-nine, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing I am going to say tonight and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that about four people in this room have been waiting for me to say something about what happened in that low ground, and I have not got anything, and a woman who says a thing because four people are waiting for it is a woman who is counting a room and not doing her work."** "It does not get harder because of my left ear. It got harder this afternoon and it will get harder on Friday, and anybody waiting nine days for a reason to take the seed work off me has not got one and is not going to get one, and I am going to be asked what a season of disconnection costs in seed in about a week and I am going to have to say a number, and that number is not a thing the ear touches and it is the only thing anybody in four counties is actually waiting for out of me."

Nobody in that room improved it and nobody thanked her. It went into the second book on a page of its own, written down, read back, and left without a heading at the top.

Face up on the low board is an offer that nobody has filled and nobody has withdrawn, refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back, and there is no second paper of it on any table in this holding this season.

The register form stands at seventy-seven days on the middle table. Nobody filled it, nobody refused it, and there is nothing at the head of it.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the sheet that claims it is face up in a drawer and right in every particular, and the order sealing the route is in that drawer and is valid, and it opens nothing, and nothing this holding owns puts a road back.

The fetching of the man of about seventy happened on the first of this month and not today, and twenty-four is where that count stands. The compost line is paid and its run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one, and it is not a measurement of anything.

The seventh column on the well house door is at fifty-six, and nothing on that door was read this morning. It goes on the thirtieth of this month and on the first of the next. There is no other morning in the year.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is in its eleventh year with no dimension on any page, and it was not walked, measured, crossed, priced or explained this morning. Four people from an Assembly are working four yards off its edge and do not know what it is.

A cart going down a road is not a use, so the log stands where it stands at fifteen lines, and the reason for the fifteen was told twice on the first of this month. Fifty-three requests and fifty-three notes, and a person in a low place is neither a fifty-fourth of anything nor a seventh of the six not knowns.

Four inches of mark, two branches, unchanged since the second hour. Asking is not a mark, a cart is not a mark, a hurt is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm are four and do not reconcile, and nothing said in that room tonight is a fifth of anything.

THE RING OF BARE GROUND WAS NOT ENTERED BY ANYBODY.
