# Chapter 416

## What A Decision Does Not Open

The twenty-ninth of this month was a Friday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The east shoulder of the dyke, the three hundred yards above the sluice that nobody has re-cut since a month now two months back, took five people from the second hour to the sixth hour, and the clay was in a condition that a bar went into about four inches and stopped, and that is the condition a dyke wants in a wet month.

The bar on that shoulder has a habit of coming off its handle in the first hour, and two men in this holding have wrapped the heads of theirs with tarred twine, and about four people have noticed the twine and about four have not.

Marek had the bar for the whole of it and both hands were full and his left forearm ached at about the fifth hour in the way it does when the ground is right, which is a thing about the work and not about the arm.

He had been out at the node before the light and had read the dial standing in it, and the reading was down a little on Monday and down a little more on Wednesday, and the third of those is not a figure anybody in this building has ever entered and he wrote it on the back of his own hand with a stick and rubbed it out at the gate.

That is how the node has been read for four years. There is no column for it anywhere in this building and there is no heading on the page it is written on, because it is written on the back of a hand.

The four people of the Assembly were on the third of their four days on the road, and they were somewhere north of the fen and about nine miles off, and the road they were on is a road this holding has never had anything to do with.

The boards were read at the seventh hour, and they were read before either figure was named, which is the order they are read in. **Five hundred and sixty-three days** and **six hundred and ten**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer stayed as it is.

Sixty mornings out of a hundred and three.

The twenty-ninth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and the frayed corner has been the frayed corner since the spring.

**Eight hundred and sixty-three hundredweight.**

Down five off Thursday, and the hundred and seventy-ninth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The fall went onto the falls. Seventy-eight and sixty-eight make a hundred and forty-six.

A hundred and seventy-seven days is the standing of the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence for it on this morning is the hundred and seventy-sixth of those mornings out of a hundred and seventy-eight.

A rider came up the fen road at about the eleventh hour with a sheet from the second council, and he had come nine miles and said so before anybody was asked, and he was asked nothing and was given bread by the woman who keeps the seed house, which she does and has never entered anywhere.

> FROM THE SECOND REGIONAL COUNCIL, DATED THE TWENTY-EIGHTH OF THIS MONTH, SENT BY THE ORDINARY POST AND BY THE SAME RIDER WHO BROUGHT THE FIRST SHEET OF THIS COUNCIL ON THE FIFTEENTH. FIRST, THAT THE COUNCIL REPEATS ITS RESOLUTION OF THE FIFTEENTH AND WITHDRAWS NOTHING FROM IT. SECOND, THAT THE COUNCIL HAS HAD THE BOUNDARY OF THE NODE NAMED IN THAT RESOLUTION TRACED BY ITS OWN SURVEYOR AND BY ONE SURVEYOR IT HIRED AGAINST ITSELF, AND THAT THE TWO TRACES ARE THE SAME WITHIN THE ERROR OF TWO INSTRUMENTS, AND THAT THE GROUND OF THAT NODE IS SHARED, ON THE LINE GIVEN IN THE SECOND PLACE OF THE SCHEDULE, WITH A COUNTY THAT WAS NOT ASKED BEFORE THE RESOLUTION WAS CARRIED AND HAS NOT BEEN ASKED SINCE. THIRD, THAT THE COUNCIL RECORDS THIS AS A FACT AND NOT AS AN OBJECTION, AND THAT IT CARRIES THE RESOLUTION ANYWAY, AND THAT IT WILL NOT SEND THE TRACE TO THAT COUNTY UNTIL THE NODE IS HELD. FOURTH, THAT THE COUNCIL ASKS NOBODY TO DO ANYTHING BY THIS SHEET.

The clerk of this holding read all four clauses twice, and the man of about fifty read the third one a third time on his own and then put the sheet down flat on the long table and did not say anything about it for about a minute.

What the man of about fifty said had the reason in front of it, as it has in that room for four years, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and twenty-two, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the part of that which is about this holding and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that a council has just told us in writing that it is going to keep a border back from a county for as long as it can hold the thing, and it has told us that in the fourth clause of a sheet, in a sheet that nobody in that council has to answer for." "Every word of that is correct. The two traces are the same. The ground is shared. That county has not been asked and has not been asked since. None of it is a lie and none of it is an obstruction and the second council has not delayed a document or softened one, and the whole of what it has done is write down a true thing and then carry a resolution anyway." "And I am not going to stand in this room and tell anybody that this holding has a column for the fourth clause, because it has not, and a fact that a body is ashamed of in about four years is a fact that somebody has to have written down somewhere and this building has four books and no heading on any of them."

Nobody thanked him, and the sheet went on the long table at the wall side beside the first council's letter of the day before, and the two of them were not put one under the other.

The man of about fifty took the second sheet out of his own book afterwards and copied the fourth clause onto the back of the day sheet, word for word, and did not copy the second one, because the second one has a figure in it that this holding could check and there is no column here to put a checked figure.

About four people in this room have wanted him to copy all four clauses, and about four have wanted him to copy none, and neither of the two fours said so and neither of them is going to.

He read the fourth clause out loud again to about four of them, on the ground that a clause that is going to be true for a long time is worth hearing twice, and nobody improved it.

Marek spoke at about the second hour of the afternoon and the reason came before the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and ninety-seven, and it is his and of nobody else's.

"I am going to say the one thing about that fourth clause that I have not said, and I am going to give the reason for saying it first, and the reason is that I walked four and a half miles yesterday and lost an argument in about nine people with a door shut, and I have not slept, and a man who has not slept is a man who reaches for the sentence that is nearest." "The nearest sentence is that a council is not allowed to do that. It is not allowed and it is not going to be stopped, and there is nothing in this building, in this county, or in four counties that will stop it before Saturday, and the only thing that is going to happen on Saturday is that four people who are not from here are going to stand inside a ring of bare ground." "So the honest sentence is not that they are wrong. The honest sentence is that the third clause is a fact and the fourth clause is a promise to keep a thing shut, and I am the only man in four counties who could have named that on Tuesday and I was in a room with a door shut and I asked a different question."

He was agreed with by nobody in that room and disagreed with by nobody in it, and about four people in it had wanted to be asked whether the second council ought to be told to send the trace, and about four had wanted to be asked nothing at all, and neither of those two fours has ever been separated from the other.

The two sheets from two different councils have been lying on that table for two days now, and neither of them has been answered and neither of them is going to be, and about four people in this building have decided that a table with two unanswered letters on it is worse than a drawer with one in it, and about four have not.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in the yard at the third hour of the afternoon with a bar and a chain, and the two-foot gap at the kerb was open where it has been open since a month now five months back, and nothing has gone into it this season and nothing is going to.

The valve at the low end of that cut is not opened. The key is in her hand and has been in her hand since a month now five months back and is not going into anything this season.

Nobody in this holding has asked her for it. About four people in this building have noticed the chain and about four have not, and the two fours have never said which of them was which.

She said one sentence about the key, to nobody in particular, at the third hour, and it was that the key has not been asked for and that she had noticed that as well.

The decision itself is of a month now five months back. It has not been reopened by anything that has happened this month, nor by the four clauses of that sheet, nor by anything a man said at Thornwild yesterday.

No part of this building is going to reopen it. The ground that came off is not coming back on this season and there is nobody in four counties who is going to be asked to put it back.

The clerk of this holding said that out loud so that it would be on a page, and then said that saying it out loud is not the same as it being decided, and did not say which of the two he thought it was.

Nobody asked the man of about thirty-one of Silling anything this morning and nobody is going to. The second of the four lines in his own book is sixty-seven days old.

He stood at the trough for the length of a pipe, the way he has stood at that trough on most of the mornings of this year, and the woman who keeps the seed house was in the yard and saw him do it and has not said a word about it to anybody in this building, and nobody here has asked her what he does there.

The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road. Eleven is a floor and it did not rise, and a barrow's journeys and a man's feet are two figures and are not added to each other on any morning of the year.

The offer has not moved. It is on the low board, face up, unfilled, refused on the nineteenth of last month, not withdrawn, and there is no second paper of it on any table in this building this season.

A man has looked at that board three mornings running without turning it over. Nobody counted the mornings and nobody is going to.

The register form is on the middle table at seventy-three days, not filled and not refused, with nothing at the head of it and nobody going to write anything at the head of it.

The man of about seventy was not sent for. His count of fetchings is twenty-three and the twenty-fourth is two mornings off, on the first of a month, and nothing on any page here says why a month should matter to that, and nobody has ever asked him.

The compost line is paid, one hundred pounds, and the run is twenty not discharged in twenty. It does not fall this month.

The seventh column of the well house door is at fifty-four and is read on the thirtieth and on the first of next and on no other morning. It was not read today, and the six columns beside it are headed nothing and stay that way.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres has been bare for eleven years. Nobody has walked it, measured it, crossed it, priced it or explained it, and no page gives a figure for it. A body of people who were not asked is not a reason to walk round it, and a border two surveyors have traced is not a border this holding has agreed to.

The use log is at fifteen lines. A second trace of a border gets no line in it, and the reason the log is at fifteen was given once and is not given again in a season. There are fifty-three requests and fifty-three notes under section nine, and a correct sheet that is going to be carried anyway is one of those fifty-three and not a fifty-fourth.

Four inches of mark on that arm, branching twice, and the same at the second hour as at the ninth. There are four accounts of it in this building and they do not reconcile.

A border is not a mark, a key is not a mark, a decision left shut is not a mark, a trace two surveyors agree on is not a mark, and a sentence about a council in a room is not a mark. Nothing said this afternoon is a fifth of anything.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-ninth, in about nine people, at this holding: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE EAST SHOULDER OF THE DYKE ABOVE THE SLUICE, FIVE PEOPLE TO THE SIXTH HOUR. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD AT THE SEVENTH HOUR AND BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED. A SHEET CAME UP THE FEN ROAD FROM THE SECOND REGIONAL COUNCIL, DATED THE TWENTY-EIGHTH, REPEATING THE RESOLUTION OF THE FIFTEENTH AND WITHDRAWING NOTHING FROM IT, AND RECORDING THAT THE GROUND OF THAT NODE IS SHARED ON A LINE IN THE SCHEDULE WITH A COUNTY THAT WAS NOT ASKED BEFORE THE RESOLUTION WAS CARRIED AND HAS NOT BEEN ASKED SINCE, AND THAT THE COUNCIL WILL NOT SEND THE TRACE TO THAT COUNTY UNTIL THE NODE IS HELD. EVERY WORD OF IT IS CORRECT AND NOTHING IN IT IS A LIE, AN OBSTRUCTION, A DELAY OR A SOFTENING. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT THE THIRD CLAUSE IS A FACT AND THE FOURTH IS A PROMISE TO KEEP A THING SHUT, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THE HONEST SENTENCE IS THAT THE COUNCIL IS NOT GOING TO BE STOPPED BEFORE SATURDAY BY ANYBODY, AND THAT HE COULD HAVE NAMED THAT ON TUESDAY AND WAS IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT AND ASKED A DIFFERENT QUESTION, AND THE COUNT OF THAT IS ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-SEVEN. THE CUT AT THE KERB STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING HAS GONE INTO THE TWO-FOOT GAP THIS SEASON AND NOTHING IS GOING TO, THE VALVE IS NOT OPENED, THE KEY IS IN HER HAND AND IS NOT USED, AND THE DECISION OF THE ELEVENTH OF A MONTH FIVE MONTHS BACK IS NOT REOPENED BY ANYTHING IN THIS BUILDING. THE TWO SHEETS ARE ON THE LONG TABLE AT THE WALL SIDE AND HAVE NOT BEEN PUT ONE UNDER THE OTHER. THE REQUESTS STAND AT FIFTY-THREE AND THE SECTION-NINE NOTES AT FIFTY-THREE.**
