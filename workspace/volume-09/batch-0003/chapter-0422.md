# Chapter 422

## A Reading Is Not A Maintenance Act

The fifth of this month was a Thursday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The whole length of the low drain from the north end to the sluice took five people from the second hour to the sixth hour, and it was done in four hours and a half, which is the quickest anybody has done that length in this holding's history, and nobody has worked out why.

Marek had the bar and then the shovel and both hands were full from the second hour to the sixth, and his left forearm did not ache at all, and about four people in this holding have noticed that and about four have not.

He had been out at the node before the light and had read the dial and had not written the reading down anywhere, and the reason he did not write it down is that a figure that has not moved is not a figure this building has ever entered a second time.

The ground on both sides of the ring has gone back to about what it was on Monday. That is not the same as right. The water in the two low places is still going two ways and it is going more slowly, and the second willow that put a season of soft wood on its south side has put about four days more of it, and nobody in four counties has ever had a second willow to watch.

They were read aloud at the seventh hour, and reading them came before the figures. **Five hundred and sixty-nine days** and **six hundred and sixteen**, forty-seven between them, and the drawer stayed shut.

Sixty-three mornings out of a hundred and nine.

The fifth went in at about the eighth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same, and it is the hundred and eighty-fifth figure and there have been a hundred and eighty-five mornings of it and not one morning has brought out the same figure twice.

**Eight hundred and seventy-five hundredweight.**

Down five off Wednesday, and the hundred and eighty-fifth figure of a series that has not come out at the same figure twice since the morning it began.

The fall went onto the falls. Eighty-one and seventy-one make a hundred and fifty-two, and that is the last morning of this movement and the window does not turn with it and is not going to be turned by anybody.

The sentence on this morning's sheet says the four bodies of households have not moved in a hundred and eighty-three days, and it was written for the hundred and eighty-second of those mornings out of a hundred and eighty-four, and it is the last sentence of that kind this building will write in this month.

The letter had come up the fen road on the Tuesday and the clerk of this holding read it out in that room with the door shut at about the second hour of the afternoon, and it was four paragraphs long and it was in a clerk's hand and it ended with a thank-you in advance.

The request was that this holding read the dial at the node once a week, and enter the reading, and send the reading to the council that had asked for it, and that the reading be entered as a way of knowing what the ground wants.

It was not a foolish request. The clerk who wrote it had a rate in a book in a town nine miles off, and the rate is for a node worked on a Tuesday by a body that has been doing it for four years, and every one of those three things is true and none of them is a thing this holding can put on a piece of paper in a letter.

There is a reading on a page in this building already. It came out of a standing willow five miles east of here on the twenty-ninth of a month now two months back, and it is nineteen lines long and it says what and it says when, and it has no name at the head of it and it is the only thing in four counties that anybody has ever been able to hand to a body and mean it by.

The council that sent the letter this week has that sheet. Every one of the three regional councils has it and the fourth county has it, and it went out on the ordinary post to four bodies of households in the first week of this month, and not one of the four has written to anybody about it since it arrived.

Marek gave the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is two hundred and three, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say no to a reading once a week and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that a reading is not a maintenance act, and this building has spent four years being unable to say that in a sentence anybody could carry out of a room, and I can say it this afternoon because I have been at that node four hundred times and I know what a reading is for."** "A reading tells you what a thing is doing. It does not answer it. You can read that dial every morning of the year and the ground will do exactly what it does tomorrow, and the only thing you will have at the end of the year is a column of numbers that four counties can all check and not one of them can act on." "There is a sheet in a drawer five miles east of here with nineteen places on it and a day and an hour against eight of them, and that sheet did not stop anything, and it was not supposed to. It is a record of what a wood keeps and a wood keeps one order and there is no second ground in four counties that will give up the same order, and that is the whole of what is known."

Then he said the part that is his own, and the room did not improve it.

"What came out of that standing willow on the twenty-ninth came out in the order a wood keeps things in. Nineteen lines came out of it. Eleven of them came out again on the twenty-third and eight of them did not, and the order has no gap in it where the eight would stand, and those eight places are not on this page and are not going to be guessed at by me or by anybody in this room, and I have been asked four times this month by four different people to guess at them and the answer is the same four times." "Eleven of nineteen is a figure and it is not to be guessed at either. It is not a rate and it is not a fraction and it does not say that eight out of nineteen of anything anywhere is a figure anybody in four counties can work from, and about four years of this holding has been the practice of putting a fraction next to a thing because a fraction is a thing you can hold."

The room did not find for him and did not find against him, and about four people in it had wanted to vote on it and about four had wanted to be asked a question first, and neither of the two fours is going to be asked.

The clerk asked one question and it was a clerk's question and it was the only one anybody asked in about half an hour, and it was whether a refusal ought to go back to the council that asked or whether the letter ought to go in the drawer with the other two.

Marek said it goes back, and he said the reason first, and the reason is that a request that has been answered is a request and a request that has been put in a drawer is a thing somebody has to remember is in a drawer, and this holding has enough of those and about four years of a clerk's memory is the wrong instrument.

The clerk wrote the answer out in the words it was given in, on the back of the council's own letter, and it went out on the Saturday with the other post, and nobody signed it and nobody read it out before it went.

The man of about fifty gave the reason before the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is one hundred and twenty-six, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about that request that is not about the reading, and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that the request was made in good faith by a clerk who has never been wrong about a figure in four counties, and that is going to matter in about four years when somebody looks at this letter and asks why the answer was no."** "The answer is going to be on this page with the reason under it, and the reason is that there is no column in this holding for a reading, and there has not been one for four years, and a thing with nowhere to go gets put on a long table and then gets put in a drawer, and a council asking for a weekly figure is a council asking for a fifth book, and this building has four and has had four since before anybody in four counties formed a council."

Nobody thanked him, and the request was entered as a request and the count of them is fifty-three and the section-nine notes are fifty-three and the two are not added and the letter went on the long table at the wall side and is still there.

Tova Reed was in that room and she had been in it since the first hour, and she gave the reason before she said the thing, and the count of that in the holding's history is forty, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing I have left to say this month and I am going to give the reason first, and the reason is that a reading once a week would be the first number anybody in four counties has ever given me that I could put a figure next to, and I have wanted one of those for about four years."** "It is also the reason not to take it. The minute somebody gives me a weekly number I will build a season of seed on it, and four counties will build a season on it, and it will be a number about a dial and not about seed, and in about four years there will be a figure on a page and a plan in a county book and not one of them will be about what a seedwright knows." "It does not get harder because of my left ear. It has not got harder because of my left ear once this month and it is not going to, and anybody waiting nine days for a reason to take the seed work off me has not got one."

Nobody improved it, nobody thanked her, and nobody put a hand on her arm. The whole of it went into the second book on a page of its own and was read back, and no heading was written at the top.

The reading came up again while they were in that room, and nobody asked for it, and it was Marek who said it.

He said that the thing that came out of that wood on the twenty-ninth of a month now two months back is a season and a quality of light in his own hand and nothing else, and that the comfort in it is not standing and is not going to be standing this morning or any other morning, and that the father is not alive in the wood and has not been for some time, and that the day the severe bleed took does not come back, and that nothing anybody in this room is about to do this month brings it back.

He gave the reason for saying it first, and the count of that is still two hundred and three, because he had already given the reason for the sentence he came in with and the two sentences were one speech.

**"I am going to say that out loud in a room once and I am going to give the reason for saying it first, and the reason is that in about four years somebody in this building is going to be asked to read that page out to a county, and I would rather they read it with a line under it about a thing that is not standing than have them read it as though it were a figure that was still good."**

Nobody in that room said anything tender and nobody said anything about it at all, and about four people in it had heard him say that day and about four had not, and neither of the two fours has ever been asked which of them was which.

The barrow came up the four-mile road at the ninth hour, was turned at the top of the yard and went home. The man of about thirty-one of Silling brought it, and the second of the four lines in his own book is seventy-three days old and unanswered, and nobody here has asked him about it in seventy-three days. Eleven journeys is a floor. He walked four miles without the barrow and nobody asked him why, and a barrow's journeys and a man's feet are two figures that are not added to each other, and about four years of this holding has pretended that is a small thing.

On the low board, face up, an offer nobody filled, nobody dated and nobody withdrew, refused on the nineteenth of a month now two months back. Nobody in this building has been told to take it off that board, and there is no second paper of it on any table here this season.

The register form is on the middle table at seventy-nine days. Not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of it, and nobody has written a heading on it and nobody is going to.

A sheet in a drawer, face up, claims a corridor in the pan and is correct in every particular. The order sealing the route is in that drawer with it, valid, and it opens nothing, and nothing here reopens a route. What a page is and is not was said once this month, in a room, and is not said again.

The man of about seventy was fetched on the first of this month, which is where the count of twenty-four comes from, and he is not fetched today. The compost line is paid, and the run is twenty-one not discharged in twenty-one, and it has never once disagreed with the word in front of it, and it measures nothing.

The seventh column of the well house door is at fifty-six and is not read this morning. It is read on the thirtieth of this month and on no other morning of the year. The six columns beside it are headed nothing and are going to stay that way this season.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and has no dimension on any page. It was not walked, not measured, not crossed, not priced and not explained this morning. A stake stands outside it. Nothing has gone into it, and the ground on both sides has gone back to about what it was on the second, slowly, and nobody in this holding has said that is better and nobody is going to say it is.

The mark on that arm is four inches and branches twice. A reading is not a mark, a refusal is not a mark, a season in a wood is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled, and nothing said in that room this afternoon is a fifth of anything.

The ground is going to go again, and everybody in this building knows the shape of the next two weeks better than any of them knew it a fortnight ago, and on the twenty-fifth of next month the fourth line of the rota will come round and go away empty for the fifty-ninth morning, and nobody is going to write that down this morning.
