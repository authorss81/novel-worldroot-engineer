# Chapter 500

## About Nine Miles

The third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the two culverts on the middle road and the fill behind them and the low field gate, and the fill had gone out about an inch in the night and the two of them put it back and went over it twice, and the gate wanted a bar and a stone and took its nine minutes.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling did the bottom of the northern culvert with a spade and the man of the north row with the cough did the top of the gate alone, and neither of them was asked to hold an end of anything and neither of them was asked that.

The run of mornings was read out loud at the seventh hour, one man to a figure, and neither of them said the other's half, and the two of them said **one hundred and two of one hundred and eighty-seven**, and the frame of that run is of a month six months back.

The boards stand at **six hundred and forty-seven days** and **six hundred and ninety-four**, and the forty-seven is the same forty-seven, and the drawer behind them was not opened at any hour today and nobody in this building has asked what is in it in four years.

The twenty-third went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**One thousand and two hundredweight.**

It came in five under Thursday and the fall went onto the falls, and that is the two hundred and sixty-third figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice since the morning it began. The window standing behind it divides a hundred and twenty mornings one way and a hundred and ten the other, which is two hundred and thirty, and in four years nobody in this room has set either end of that window against the figure printed over it.

The four bodies of households are on this morning's sheet at two hundred and sixty-one days, and the clause beneath it is the two hundred and sixtieth of those mornings out of two hundred and sixty-second, a day short of the figure and a day over the clause.

A clerk in one of the four counties wrote in this week and the letter came up the fen road with the post on Wednesday morning, and the question in it is who holds the pen on a machine four hundred miles of schedule away.

> THE ANSWER AS IT WENT UP THE FEN ROAD THIS MORNING, IN THE CLERK'S OWN HAND, ON ONE SHEET, WITH A HEADING AT THE HEAD OF IT BEING THE NAME OF A COUNTY AND NO NAME UNDER IT: **THE QUESTION PUT TO THIS HOLDING IS WHO HOLDS THE PEN ON A MACHINE FOUR HUNDRED MILES OF SCHEDULE AWAY. THIS HOLDING KEEPS FOUR BOOKS AND THE FIRST OF THE FOUR HAS A COLUMN WITH A HEADING AT THE HEAD OF IT AND THE COLUMN IS THE NAMES OF THE MEN AND WOMEN WHO CARRY THE LOADS IN AND OUT OF THIS HOLDING, AND THE PEN IS HELD BY SUCH A PERSON ON SUCH A MORNING AND BY NOBODY ELSE ON ANY OTHER MORNING, AND THE PERSON IS SOMETIMES TWO PERSONS. THIS HOLDING HAS NEVER BEEN FOUR HUNDRED MILES OF SCHEDULE AWAY. THIS HOLDING HAS NO COLUMN WITH A HEADING ON IT AGAINST ANY SUCH MACHINE AND HAS NEVER RULED ONE. A PEN IS A PEN AND A SCHEDULE IS A SCHEDULE AND THE TWO ARE NOT A COLUMN.** NOBODY IS NAMED IN IT AND NO DATE IS PUT AGAINST IT AND NOBODY HAS ASKED THIS HOLDING TO PUT ONE THERE.

The request went in the requests column and the count is fifty-three, and it was fifty-three in the evening, and the section-nine notes are fifty-three, and the two of them are a different list and have never been added to one another.

A request that is entered is a request and is not an offer and is not a rung, and no rung of anything has been climbed in this holding in this month and no offer of any kind has been made from this building to anybody who reads these books, and the paper on the low board is not a new one and is not described as a new one by anybody in this room.

The count of bodies that have asked who holds the pen is one, and it is one, and it is a count of a question and not a count of anybody, and nobody in this building has answered it in a room and the answer that went up the road this morning is on paper and is not a name.

The man who keeps the use log entered the request against the log at about the tenth hour and the log has fifteen lines and got no sixteenth. The reason for the fifteen was given twice in four years and it is not given again this morning, and a request is not a use and a letter is not a use and a county four hundred miles of schedule away is not a use.

The man who keeps that log has not been asked what the fifteen are and has not offered and was not asked, and a count of uses is not a count of mornings and a request is not a use.

The notice that called the meeting went on the long table on the seventeenth of this month and it was written by a body of households with a heading at the head of it, and it asked for people to go and look at the thing the sheet from the fen road came from, and it named a morning and it named the hour.

Marek is not the person who called that meeting. He read the notice on the long table on the seventeenth along with everybody else in this building and he was not asked to go and he did not call it and he did not suggest a morning and he has not claimed any part of calling it, and a body that calls a meeting is a body that calls a meeting and the engineer of record is not the officer of it.

The party goes out of this gate at about the ninth hour. Two clerks of bodies of households, four of the Undercommons, Soren Rill, Nia Vale, a seed schedule carried by a woman who does not go inside, a man with a slate, and the engineer of record.

Soren Rill is not the officer of the Undercommons and has said so three times before without being asked and he did not say it a fourth time this morning because nobody asked him. Nia Vale coordinates the moving reserves and is not support staff and has not been offered a chair and did not ask for one.

About four of the eight in the Undercommons have carried seed on that road for nine years and about four have come up it inside a month, and nobody counted which is which and nobody asked and nobody is going to. The count of who came this morning is not given in a yard, because this is not a yard, and a figure of a morning is not a count of anybody and this page does not make it one.

The Undercommons is not a body of households and is not a council and has no charter and no heading and no roll and no instrument of any kind, and it has been carrying seed on a road for nine years and it is not a member of the four bodies of households and this holding is not a member of anything, and none of those four facts moved this morning and none of them was on anybody's list.

The woman carrying the seed schedule is the woman who keeps the seed house and she carries it and she does not go inside, and nobody has asked her why and she has not offered, and a schedule is a schedule and a door that is not ours is a door that is not ours.

She put the schedule in a satchel of oilskin and the satchel has been on the long table since the seventeenth with a weight on it, and the weight came off it at about the eighth hour and the schedule went into the satchel and the satchel went over her shoulder, and nobody in this building said one word about a sheet of paper going nine miles to a building that has never been asked a question by anybody in this holding.

The man with a slate is a man with a slate and nothing else this holding knows about him, and a figure of draw is not a figure of households and neither of them is a figure of anybody, and he is going to write down what he can stand in front of and not what he cannot.

A slate is not a column and a slate is not the second book, and what goes on it at the far end of nine miles this morning is not going into any of the four books in this building whatever is on it, and nobody has told him that and nobody is going to.

The distance is about nine miles and the coast road is about nine miles, and the two of them are two distances and they are not added, and the barrow does not go and the number of journeys that barrow has made is eleven and eleven is a floor and it is not raised because a party is walking.

A morning's walk and a cart is what the notice said and there is no cart in this holding, and a notice written by a body is a notice written by a body and the words in it are the words of the body that wrote it and not the words of the engineer of record.

The far end of a thing is held by two people and neither of them is the reader of this body, and no instrument anywhere in four counties says whether it is on or off, and it has not been said to be on by anybody in this building and it is not said to be on this morning, and a party of people going to stand outside a door is not an instrument and is not a way of finding out.

The corridor in the pan is named and is not established as on, and a culvert under a machine house nine miles off is not that corridor, and a door on the other side of that wall is not that corridor either, and the three of them are three things and none of them is in this building.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table about nine inches apart where they have been since the first of a month now one month back, and neither of them was touched this morning, and a party going nine miles to look at a thing is not a ground for settling either of them and nobody in this room suggested one.

One of the two bodies that said no is not in this building and was not asked to be and nobody in this room has described it as having been consulted, and it has not been written to this month, and a body that has said no is a body that has said no whether or not anybody went nine miles to look at a wall this morning.

A charter with four clauses and a subscription in a second hand are two documents and two correct documents and the two hands do not know about each other, and three of the four bodies of households that signed the charter sixty-three days ago are still signed to a machine, and that is said in this yard and it is not improved on and it is not being improved on this morning by anybody who is walking out of the gate.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and fifty-seven days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of the column, and it is not going to be read out a fifth time in this building. The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and nobody in this building has been told to take it off that board.

Six things this holding does not know are ruled in a column in the seed house's schedule with no unit against any of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and a machine nine miles off is not a seventh and a party walking to look at one is not a seventh.

The schedule is in the satchel with the six ruled rows still in it and no unit against any of them, and it goes nine miles this morning with a weight taken off it, and the seventh row is not ruled and is not going to be ruled by anybody in this holding before it gets there or after it gets back.

The four ruled lines under two words in the corner are still empty and the answer to them is not due, and nobody in this room tried to fill one of them this morning and nobody walking out of the gate tried either.

Behind the fourth book the day sheets are a foot and a half deep in the order they were used and this morning's went on the top of them at the ninth hour with the party written in the ordinary place, and the sheet with no date at the head of it is still in the middle of that stack in the place it was on the first morning.

There was no rain in the night and no frost, and the yard was dry at the second hour and dry at the ninth, and the north end of the ditch is running full and the outlet at its head is doing what an outlet does, and the trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip.

The clerk of this holding stood in the gate with the second book shut under his arm while the party went out and did not write down the number of them and did not write down the hour they went and did not ask anybody where they were going, because the notice is on the long table and the notice says where they are going and a thing that is written down in a body does not get written down again in this building.

The man of about fifty was at the long wall with the third book shut and did not open it and was not asked to and did not offer, and he did not go, and nobody in this yard asked him why he was not going and nobody has ever asked him that about anything.

Marek gave the reason before he said the thing at the gate, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and fifty-six, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about walking nine miles and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been the only person this holding could send for nine years and I have got a morning of daylight and a party on the road and a gate I have never been through."** "I am not the man who called this and I am not going to stand in front of that door and ask anybody to open it. If they open it, they open it, and if they do not, we have walked nine miles and we have looked at a wall, and both of those are things a person can do on a Friday."

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding wrote the twenty-third in at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the shutter on the window of the office's work is still on and no work is ever carried out of one, and the party is out of the gate on the nine miles and the engineer of record is walking with it and is not the man who called the meeting.
