# Chapter 491

## The Eleventh Consecutive

A sheet came up the fen road in a hand that is not this building's and it was put on the long table under a weight at about the seventh hour, and nobody turned it over, and the weight that went onto it is the same four pound weight that has been sitting on the roll of terms since the twenty-first of a month now three months back.

It came in a fold of two with the fold at the top and a wet mark along the bottom edge where it had been against somebody's coat, and there is no envelope and no seal and no wax and no letter with it, and the man who brought it in said the road and said nothing else and went out again and was not asked where on the road he had picked it up.

The second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. It was the outlet end and the two stones at the head of it, and the near stone had a quarter of an inch of daylight under it on the first of this month and has had none since the fourth, and the far stone has not moved since the twenty-eighth of a month now two months back.

The pack under the near stone has gone back in wet and will want doing before the frost is into it, and a week is the ordinary answer and it has not been written down anywhere in this building, and nobody here has ever asked the man who put that pack in what a week is to him.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was on the outlet end with a bar and a wedge from the second hour until about the fourth and said one thing about the pack, and what he said was that the pack is doing the job it was put in to do and the water has not asked it a question a pack answers, and nobody asked him for more than that.

The frame in the pump house is oak and has been since before this building kept a book of anything, and it goes down four minutes and comes up three on an ordinary morning, and there is an inch of black in the seam by the fourth hour, and that is a pump with an inch of air in it and not a pump that is broken.

Frost was on the yard at the second hour and gone off it by the seventh. The middle road took an inch of water in the night and had given most of that inch back by the ninth hour. The ditch at the north end is running full and the outlet at its head is doing what an outlet does and nobody has ever put a figure against it.

The fourteenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and ninety-five hundredweight.**

It came up eight on Tuesday with nothing against it, and that is the two hundred and fifty-fourth figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice since the morning it began. The count of that run is day minus four hundred and fifty, and it did not reset when the month turned and nobody in this building has ever asked what a count of days is a count of.

It went onto the rises. The window standing behind it is day minus four hundred and eighty-three days wide, and this morning it divides a hundred and sixteen mornings one way and a hundred and five the other, which is two hundred and twenty-one, and in four years nobody in this room has set either end of that window against the figure printed over it.

This morning's sheet puts the four bodies of households at two hundred and fifty-two days without movement, and the clause under the figure reads the two hundred and fifty-first of those mornings out of two hundred and fifty-third, one short and one over. Mornings are not loads and mornings are not households and mornings are not people, and the four of them are bodies and not a building.

The two boards stand at **six hundred and thirty-eight days** and **six hundred and eighty-five**, forty-seven apart at every row they have ever been read on, and the drawer behind them was not opened and nobody put a hand on it.

There is no figure for the run of mornings on this page and the reason is on the page. One of the two men who read the boards went off at the second hour to a thing nine miles off on the other side of this holding, on a cart that is not this holding's and that is standing at the mouth of the road this morning with nobody sitting on it, and the other one would not read his own half out by himself, and it is a Wednesday and nobody in this building thought of asking a third man. The number is not in the second book either, and the second book is the only place in this county where it would go.

**It is a schedule.** That is the whole of what anybody in this room could say about it before the seventh hour of the next day. It has a date at the foot of it and nothing at the head of it, and the date is the twenty-second of this month.

The date is inside this month. Nobody said that out loud in the yard when the sheet was put on the table and nobody said it after, and the man of about fifty read it standing up and put his thumb under the line and left it there while he read the rest of it.

The sheet is four ruled columns of figures and nothing else. There is a place on it and there is a figure against the place and there is a figure in a second column, and neither column has a heading at the head of it, and there is no body named anywhere on it and no person named anywhere on it, and there is nothing at the foot of it but the day. It is correct about what it prunes and correct about how many people eat on the result, and nobody in this room has found anything wrong with it, and nobody in this room was asked to.

Marek came in at about the seventh hour and stood at the long side of the table and read it twice, and the second time he read it from the bottom up, and he gave the reason before he said the thing. The count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and fifty-three, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about that sheet and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been able to feel the far end of a thing for nine years and I have never once had paper in my hand that told me when it was going to happen."** "It is the eleventh of these that has come down that road. I have not seen the other ten and I am not going to be asked to account for them, and I have never been inside the place it comes from, and I am not going to stand in this room and tell anybody what is in there."

Nobody in that room said that was right and nobody said it was wrong, and nobody asked him to go and look, and he was not asked and did not offer, and the count of questions this holding has put to him this month is nil and is nil.

The man of about fifty had it twice by the time Marek had read it once, and he gave the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and forty-nine, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about a piece of paper and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have kept four books in this room for thirty-one years and not one of the four has ever wanted to be told when."** "That sheet wants to be told when. It has a day at the foot of it and no reason at the foot of it and no name at the head of it, and I am not going to stand here and tell anybody that a piece of paper has to be more than that to be worth putting a weight on, because I have watched four counties go without a piece of paper and I am not going to be the man who decides that that is a virtue."

Nobody in that room agreed with him and nobody in that room disagreed with him, and the man of about fifty did not ask either and the man of about fifty was not asked, and the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and it did not move while he was saying it.

A schedule is a record of a decision and is not a record of a reason. That is said once in this building this morning and it is not going to be said again in this building, and the four books can carry it in any order anybody likes and nobody is going to rule a column for it.

What is on the sheet is what it was made to be on it. A branch whose flow will not hold a season with the flow beside it is taken off, and the season after that is one that can be told to a great many people in the winter, and a person who is told a season in the winter eats what the season says. Nobody in this room has said that is wrong and nobody in this room has said it is right, because a reading and a cut are two facts and they are not a pair.

And four bodies of households signed a charter in a room nine miles off fifty-four days ago that gave them a real right to decline an inspection, and three of those four are still signed to a machine for stored light. The signature is on paper and the paper is correct and the two hands are on two different documents and neither one knows the other one exists. That is the price of what the charter gave them, and it is said in this yard this morning for the first time, and it is not going to be improved on.

A body with two needs and one set of hands is a fact about a body. It is not a fact about a machine nine miles off and it is not a fact about a person in a room with gauges in it, and the four bodies of households are not villains this morning and they are not fools, and nobody in this yard is going to ask one of them to choose between the two things it has signed for.

The requests column is fifty-three and the section-nine notes are fifty-three, and they are a different list, and neither of them moved this morning and the two of them are never added. A sheet that arrives by hand is not a request and nobody in this building entered it as one and nobody was going to.

The ninth-day returns are every ninth morning without exception, and the board in the corner carries the days on it, and this morning is the fourteenth and the next of them is the twenty-second and has not been weighed, and there is nobody in this building who has asked why the rise on that board is eleven hundredweight every time and has been eleven every time for eleven years.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling read the sheet on his own at about the eighth hour, standing at the other end of the table, and did not touch it, and the second of the four lines in his own book is a hundred and forty-two days old this morning and has nothing on it. Nobody in this building has asked him about that line in a hundred and forty-two days and nobody is going to.

The fen road is not the coast road and it is not the four-mile road and it is not the lane at the second gate, and the thing on the sheet came up one of them, and the road it came up is about the same length as the road to the machine, and the two distances are two distances and they are not added and nobody in this yard added them.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table about nine inches apart where they have been since the first of a month now one month back, and neither of them was touched this morning and neither of them was moved, and the sheet from the fen road went down at the near end of the same table about two feet from the two of them and did not touch either.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and forty-eight days, one sheet with a column on it and nothing at the head of the column. It is not filled and it is not refused and it has not been moved, and a form is a form and a schedule is a schedule and the two have never been added in this building.

The window of the office's work is shut with the shutter on it, and the shutter does not come off until the sixth of a month, and the sixth of the next one is outside this one. No work is ever carried out of that window and none was carried out of the last one either, and a sheet that comes up a road by hand is not work carried out of a window.

The offer is on the low board at the far end, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date on it and no line anywhere on it for a name, and it has been lying there since the nineteenth of a month now four months back, and nobody in this building has been told to take it off that board and nobody is going to be told.

Four ruled lines sit on their own table in the corner under two words entered on the twentieth of a month now four months back, and every one of the four is still empty and the answer to them is not due, and nothing said in this building today has supplied it and nobody tried.

The woman who keeps the seed house came in at about the eighth hour, put a cup down at the tap for a man who was not spoken to, and went out again without looking at the long table. She has four counties' councils' worth of a sowing date in her head this month and no sheet, and nobody in this yard asked her for it and she did not offer it.

The count of things this holding has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and it did not move this morning, and this morning was a yard, and a man who has been able to feel a far end for nine years put two hands on a table and read a piece of paper and did not say a single thing out loud in a yard about what it meant.

Six things this holding does not know are ruled in a column in the seed house's schedule with no unit against any one of them, and the seventh row is not ruled. A machine nine miles off is not a seventh and a date at the foot of a sheet is not a seventh and nobody in this holding has ruled a row this morning.

Behind the fourth book there is a stack of day sheets about a foot and a half deep in the order it was used, and today's went on the top of it at about the ninth hour, and the sheet with no date at the head of it is still in the middle of that stack in the place it was on the first morning, and some hundreds of sheets have gone over the top of it in four years and nobody in this building has ever looked at one of them.

The clerk of this holding wrote the day in at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the sheet from the fen road went into the long drawer and the weight went back onto the roll of terms.

Nobody thanked anybody. There will be frost again before light and there was none of it left on the yard by the ninth hour, and an inch came up in the middle road in the night and about one of it had gone by the ninth.

The sheet is still on the long table under the weight where it was put, and the hand it came in is not any hand in this building. The date at the foot of it is the twenty-second of this month.
