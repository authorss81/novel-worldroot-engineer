# Chapter 498

## Named A Fourth Time

Four people took the first line of the rota at the second hour, and it was the pump house and the frame, and the frame went down four minutes this morning and came up three, and there was an inch of black in the seam by the fourth hour and the rag came away with it and went on the stone wall to dry.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling shut the wheel at the fourth hour and stood with his hand on it and said nothing, and the pump is a pump and the shut is a shut and the two have been in this building since before the second book was started.

The run of mornings was read out loud at the seventh hour, one man to a figure, and neither of them said the other's half, and the two of them said **one hundred and one of one hundred and eighty-five**, and the frame of that run is of a month six months back.

The boards stand at **six hundred and forty-five days** and **six hundred and ninety-two**, and the forty-seven is the same forty-seven, and the drawer behind them was not opened and the man who reads the far side put his thumb on it and took his thumb away.

The twenty-first went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and ninety-nine hundredweight.**

It came in five under Tuesday and the fall went onto the falls, and that is the two hundred and sixty-first figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice since the morning it began. The window standing behind it divides a hundred and nineteen mornings one way and a hundred and nine the other, which is two hundred and twenty-eight, and in four years nobody in this room has set either end of that window against the figure printed over it.

The four bodies of households are on this morning's sheet at two hundred and fifty-nine days, and the clause beneath it is the two hundred and fifty-eighth of those mornings out of two hundred and sixtieth, a day short of the figure and a day over the clause.

The seed vault of forty households under four hundred yards off was named a fourth time this morning as the answer to a shortage, and it is not recovered, and nobody has been down, and nobody is going to be. The sentence is said four times in this series and it is not improved, and there is no figure of people against it, and this page does not add the fourth naming to any count.

It was named at a table in this room on the eighth of a month now one month back by a man who said he had heard of it from his own father's version of it, and it was named on a long table in a building in another county, and it was named in a yard at Thornwild, and it was named in this room this morning, and four namings in a series is four namings and not a finding and not a count of anybody.

The nine loads went up that coast road on the twenty-ninth of a month now one month back and nine carts went through a cut on it, and they are in a yard four counties away this morning with the tally on a slate, and the slate is not in this building and the figure on the slate is not in any of the four books and nobody in this room has been to that yard and nobody has asked to go.

A man who came up the fen road with a list of ten was at the gate at about the fourth hour and did not come in. As many people in this holding could give you that list as could not, and he was not asked which half of the room he had in mind and he did not offer and he said the road and went on up it.

A list of ten is a route and a route is not a body, and the tally on a slate in a yard four counties away is a tally of loads and not a count of households, and neither of those two things is a count of anybody and neither of them was added to the four namings of a vault nobody has been down.

The four bodies of households are still signed to a machine nine miles off and still signed to a charter with four clauses in it, and a body with two needs and one set of hands is a fact about a body, and nobody in this yard improved on that this morning and nobody asked anybody to stop drawing and nobody in this yard stopped drawing.

The man who came down nine miles on foot to the tap is still at the seed house. He came into this yard about the fourth hour on the eleventh of this month with wet to the knee and drank about a pint of water standing at the tap and said four things and then stopped, and he has been at the south wall of that building since, and nobody in this holding has asked his name and his name is not in this building and is not going to be put in it.

He has not been asked whether the spur road is under and he has not offered and nobody has offered him anything, and a man who has been in a yard four counties away this morning has not been asked about it either.

Ostrey is about thirty households and the name has been on a map in the fourth book for four years, and this holding has never been to it and nobody in this yard went this morning, and the spur road that goes down to it is on that map about a mile and a half past the far mouth of the cut and nobody in this holding has asked anybody whether that spur has water on it.

A name on a map is not a signature and a signature is not a witness, and a village of about thirty households on a sheet in a drawer in this building is not a body of customers and is not a body of households, and nobody in this room has counted it into anything this morning and nobody is going to.

Marek came in at about the seventh hour and read the shortage sheet off the long table and gave the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and fifty-five, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about a shortage and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been in this room for nine years and I have watched a vault under four hundred yards off get named every time a seed house runs short, and naming it has never once put a sack on a cart."** "I am not going to say it a fifth time in a way that sounds better than the four times it has already been said, and I am not going to be the man who says the naming was wrong either, because it was not wrong. It is just not a plan, and the four of them that are on this page are four namings and there is nobody in four counties who has gone down and there is nobody in four counties who can say what is in it."

Nobody in that room said that was right and nobody said it was wrong, and nobody asked him to go down and look, and he was not asked and did not offer, and the count of questions this holding has put to him this month is nil and is nil.

A vault is not a plan and a shortage is not a question and a document is not a question, and the three of those were said in a room in this building on the seventeenth of this month and they are not said again this morning, and the seed house's own column carries the six and does not carry the seventh.

The woman who keeps the seed house was in the seed house the whole of that and did not come out, and the sheet with the shortage on it is her sheet and it has a column headed for the things this holding does not know and the column carries six rows and no unit against any of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and she put the sheet down and left it and did not say a word.

The corridor in the pan is named and is not established as on, and a culvert under a machine house nine miles off is not that corridor, and a vault under four hundred yards off is not that corridor either, and the three of them are three things and are not added to one another anywhere in this building.

The mark on his arm is four inches long and branches twice and is the same four inches it was on the fourteenth and on the seventeenth, and there is no second mark on it, and the four accounts of that arm in this building are four and this morning is not a fifth and the four do not reconcile with one another.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and fifty-five days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of the column, and it is not going to be read out a fifth time in this building. The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and a vault that is under is not a ground for taking anything off that board.

Six things this holding does not know are ruled in a column in the seed house's schedule with no unit against any of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and a vault under four hundred yards off is not a seventh, and a slate in a yard four counties away is not a seventh, and nobody in this holding has ruled a row this morning and nobody is going to.

A list of six things you do not know is not a plan and that was said on the page of that schedule on the sixteenth of this month and it is not said again here, and the four namings of a vault this morning are not a seventh thing and are not a seventh row and are not a unit for any of the six.

The far end of a thing is held by two people and neither of them is the reader of this body, and no instrument anywhere in four counties says whether it is on or off, and it has not been said to be on by anybody in this holding and it is not said to be on this morning, and a vault that has not been opened is not that far end and is not on either of the two lists.

The use log has fifteen lines and got no sixteenth, and the man who keeps it has not been asked to add one and was not asked and has not offered, and a man on a south wall is not a use and a shortage is not a use and neither of them went into it this morning.

The window of the office's work is shut with the shutter on it and the shutter does not come off until the sixth of a month, and the sixth of this month went past with it on, and no work is ever carried out of that window and nine carts going up a coast road is not work carried out of a window.

The man of about fifty is in this building this morning and he came back up the coast road on Friday and has not said what he found at the mouth of the cut, and he was not asked this morning and did not offer, and the third book was on the long table shut with his thumb in it and he did not open it at any hour.

He said one thing at the long wall at about the eighth hour and he gave the reason before it and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-one, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about two places and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been to neither and I have had thirty-one years to go to both and did not."** "A man in a cart shed at the mouth of a cut and a vault under four hundred yards off are two things this building has not been to, and I am not going to be the man who decides which of them this building is going to go to. I am going to be the man who writes down that it has not been to either, and there are two of them on this page and the two are not a total."

Nobody in that room said that was a decision and nobody said it was not a decision, and a man who says a thing out loud and does not act on it has still said it, and he did not act on it and was not asked to and did not offer.

The seed house's own schedule carries a form on the back of it with four ruled lines on it, and one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight is on the first of them and nothing whatever is on the other three, and no heading has been written at the top of it in four years. A form is not a column and nobody in this holding is going to make it into one over a shortage sheet.

Behind the fourth book the day sheets are a foot and a half deep in the order they were used and this morning's went on the top of them at the ninth hour, and the sheet with no date at the head of it is still in the middle of that stack in the place it was on the first morning, and it carries two figures and no name against either of them and it was not read this morning.

There was no rain in the night and there was no frost and the yard was dry at the second hour and dry at the ninth, and the middle road had taken nothing and given nothing back, and the north end of the ditch is running full and the outlet at its head is doing what an outlet does. The trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip, and the barrow went up that road and came down loaded, and the number of journeys that barrow has made is eleven and eleven is a floor and it is not raised this morning because a man is standing against a wall.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in this building this morning and nothing in this room was said to her and nothing was said about her, and the count of questions this holding has put to her stands where it stood and is not moved by a vault, a slate, a route or a man on a wall.

The four ruled lines under two words in the corner are still empty and the answer to them is not due, and a shortage is not an answer to them and a vault is not an answer to them and nobody in this room tried to make either of them one.

There was a quarter of an inch of frost in the small hours and none of it left on the yard by the seventh hour, and the light came up about half an hour before it does in a month now nine months back, and nobody in this holding keeps a figure of light and has never been asked to.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding wrote the twenty-first in at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the second book was shut with the day in it and the south wall of the seed house still has a man against it and nobody has asked him his name.
