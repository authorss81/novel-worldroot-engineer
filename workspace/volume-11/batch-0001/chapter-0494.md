# Chapter 494

## A Defect Is Not A Question

Four people were on the first line of the rota at the second hour and it was the pump house and the frame, and the frame was down about five minutes this morning and up about three, and the black out of the seam came away on a rag and the rag went on the stone wall to dry, which is what the rag has been for since the ninth month of last year.

The water came up the standpipe at the fourth hour and the man of about thirty-one of Silling shut the wheel and stood with his hand on it and said that the wheel is the wheel and the shut is the shut, and nobody asked him a question about it and he did not say anything else about it.

Between the two men who read the boards, at the seventh hour, one man to a figure, and neither of them said the other's half, the run of mornings is **ninety-nine mornings of a hundred and eighty-one**, and the frame of that run is of a month six months back.

The boards stand at **six hundred and forty-one days** and **six hundred and eighty-eight**, and the forty-seven between them is the same forty-seven it has been at every row, and the drawer behind them was not opened this morning and nobody put a finger in it.

The seventeenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and ninety-three hundredweight.**

It came in five under Friday and the fall went onto the falls, and that is the two hundred and fifty-seventh figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice since the morning it began. The window behind it stands at a hundred and seventeen mornings one way and a hundred and seven the other, which is two hundred and twenty-four, and the two ends of it have never been set against the figure standing over it.

The four bodies of households are written on this morning's sheet at two hundred and fifty-five days, and the clause beneath it is the two hundred and fifty-fourth of those mornings out of two hundred and fifty-sixth, one short of the figure and one over the clause, and a morning is not a household and a household is not a machine and a machine is not a person.

Marek went out of the gate at the second hour with nothing in his hands and went the nine miles on foot, on the road that is not the coast road, and he came back into this yard at about the ninth hour with his coat white at the shoulders and did not say where he had been until somebody in the seed house doorway asked him.

He had been looking at it. He has never been inside it, and nobody in this holding has ever been inside it, and nobody in this building has a key to it and nobody has asked anybody for one, and what he could say about the outside of it after nine hours on his feet is on this page and it is not a column and it is not going to be made into one.

What is on the outside of it is a wall of concrete about the height of the seed house and about four times its length, and a rail on posts along the front of it with about nine hundred yards of it, and three wheels standing behind that rail, and a door in the middle of the wall that is shut and has a man beside it. There is a smaller door at the near end with a desk in it and a woman at the desk, and she looked up once when he came along the rail and then went back to what she was doing.

There is a schedule on the wall inside the near end of it, and a man reads it off the wall and copies it, and the man who reads it has been doing that for a long time and did not stop when he went past. There is no name over the door and no name on the sheet and no date cut into the concrete anywhere along the front of it, and he looked for both of those for about an hour and found neither.

He could not get in and he did not try, and he did not stand at the door and ask to be let in, and nobody at that building spoke to him and nobody at that building knew who he was, and a man who can feel the far end of a thing and did not feel this one says so in this room at the ninth hour and says it once.

**"I put a hand on the rail at about the sixth hour and there was a flow in it I could name and I could not have told you what it was doing for anybody."** "That is the whole of nine hours and I am not going to make it into more than that, and I did not ask to go in and I did not stand at that door, and a man who has been able to feel the far end of a thing since he was nine has got to be able to stand next to a door all day and not knock on it."

Nobody in that room said that was discipline and nobody in that room said it was anything else, and the man of about fifty was at the long wall with the third book shut and had not opened it and was not asked to and did not offer.

He had not opened it because of what was in the margin of the second book, and the clerk of this holding was still standing at that table with the pencil in his hand, and the two of them had not said one word to each other about the thirty-five and neither of them was going to.

The maintenance list that went up the fen road on the twenty-ninth of a month now one month back has nine columns and nine duties in it, and the sixth column has four marks in it and five empty places, and nobody may put a name in it and nobody has and it is not repaired and not closed and not extended this morning.

A list of duties is not a roll of anybody, and a mark in a column is not a name, and four marks and five blanks in a column on a sheet that went up a road a month ago is not a shorter list and is not a longer one and is not a measure of anybody's work.

Tova Reed was at the middle table from the second hour with four counties' sowing dates in front of her and a sheet of her own under them, and she is not support staff and has not been asked to be, and the four counties' councils have given her nothing in writing and have given her three dates out of four.

Nobody in this room apologized to her for anything and nobody in this room said a word about the ear. It is one ear and it is permanent, and it was lost in a month now four months back, and it was lost to memory work and it is not going to be got back by anybody in this building, and nobody in this yard has said the word sorry to her this morning and nobody is going to.

What is asked of her this month is the seed front in four counties, a sowing date, and a schedule that not one of those four councils will put a name to. The ear stops none of the three and makes none of the three easier, and that is said once in this building and it is not going to be said again, and she said so herself before anybody else said anything about it.

She gave the reason before she said the thing, at the middle table, with the door open, and the count of that in this holding's history is fifty-one, and it is hers and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about the twenty-second and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have four councils' dates in front of me and three of them are signed and the fourth is not, and a man came back into this yard at the ninth hour having stood outside a place for nine hours and did not tell me one figure."** "I am not going to hold the seed front for that. The seed front is four counties and a sowing date and a schedule, and the twenty-second is a day in a room nine miles off and it does not put a spade in anybody's hand, and I have two hands and they are both full and both of them are seed."

Nobody in that room said that was right and nobody said it was wrong, and Marek did not answer her and she did not answer him, and nobody put a hand on her arm and nobody thanked her and nobody took the seed work off her, and the two of them were in the same room for about eleven minutes and most of that was about paper.

The count of questions this holding has put to her this month is nil and is nil.

The clerk of this holding stood up at about the seventh hour with the second book open and said a thing out loud that a person who has to write down a thing has to say out loud, and then he said the thing itself, and then the count.

**"I am going to say the one thing about the number and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have written in this book every morning for four years and there is a number in it that I have never been able to make come out, and if I do not say it out loud somebody else will make it come out in ten years and put it in a column."** "There are thirty-five things this holding does not know and cannot account for. That is the count and it is thirty-five and it has been thirty-five at every count anybody has taken of it. And a defect is not a question. A document is not a question. A corridor being named is not an answer. A heading is not an answer. A mark is not a name. A machine is not a villain."

He wrote thirty-five in the margin of the second book and wrote the six of the schedule under it, and he did not add the two, and he did not rule a seventh line, and the thirty-five are thirty-five and this page does not tally them against anything and is not going to.

There is a room upstairs over the seed house with one door in it and no window that opens, and it was booked by nobody in this building and there is no book in this building that has a line for what happens in it, and nothing happened in it this morning and the door was shut at the fourth hour and open at the seventh.

Tova Reed went up to that room at the fourth hour with her four sheets and came down at about the fifth with nothing added to them and nothing taken off, and nobody in this building asked her what she had gone up there for and she did not say, and a room with one door over a seed house is not the window of the office's work and nothing was carried out of it.

There is a form with four lines on the back of the seed house's own schedule, and line one carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight and lines two, three and four are still empty and no heading has been written at the top of it, and a form is not a column and is not going to be made into one by anybody in this holding this morning.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and fifty-one days, one sheet with a column on it and nothing at the head of the column, and it is not filled and it is not refused and it is not going to be read out a fifth time in this building. The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and a man walking nine miles is not a ground for taking it off that board.

The two sheets about the two declinings were upstairs on the long table about nine inches apart where they have been since the first of a month now one month back, and neither of them was touched this morning, and one of the two bodies that said no is not in this building and was not asked to be and nobody in this room has described it as having been consulted.

It has not been written to. Nobody in this building has written to it this month and nobody is going to, and a body that has said no and has not come to a room is still a body that has said no, and the room was warm this morning and the room being warm is not a reason to have come.

Neither of the two sheets has been settled and neither has been withdrawn and neither has been carried into that room and neither has been left anywhere else, and a declining is a declining and is not an appeal and is not a delay and is not a thing that gets taken back because a room with one door in it was warm on a Saturday.

The woman who coordinates the moving reserves was in that room from about the sixth hour and did not speak and was not asked to and was not written down anywhere, and she is not support staff and has not been offered a chair, and she had come up the four-mile road on foot and went back up the coast road at about the seventh hour and said one thing at the gate with a figure of four hundred thousand people in it and did not say it twice.

A figure of four hundred thousand is a figure of people and it is not a figure of this holding and it is not a figure of anybody in this yard, and nobody in this building has a column with a heading on it for it and nobody ruled one, and the man of about fifty heard it and wrote nothing and did not open the third book.

There was no rain and there was no frost, and the yard was dry at the second hour and dry at the ninth, and the middle road had nothing in it and gave nothing back, and the north end of the ditch is running full and the outlet at its head is doing what an outlet does.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding put the day's figure into the second book at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the second book was shut with the day in it and the schedule from the fen road is still in the long drawer under two books.

> Entered by the clerk of this holding at the first hour, in his own hand, as one unbroken line: **The seventeenth. The first line of the rota worked, the pump house and the frame, the frame down about five minutes and up about three, four people from the second hour. The pair read aloud at the seventh hour. The same day in and the same day out. Nine hundred and ninety-three hundredweight to the launder, being the two hundred and fifty-seventh. A man went the nine miles on foot at the second hour and came back at the ninth and did not go inside and nobody asked him to. A woman held the seed front in four counties and gave the reason before she said the thing, and nothing in this building has been said to her about the ear. A count of thirty-five was said out loud in a room and written in a margin and not added to the six on a schedule.** Nothing of this is a forecast and nothing of it is a cause and nothing of it is a total.
