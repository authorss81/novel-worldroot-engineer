# Chapter 496

## A Difference And Not A Person

The third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the two culverts on the middle road and the fill behind them and the low field gate, and the fill had gone out about an inch in the night and the two of them put it back and went over it twice.

The gate wanted a bar and a stone and took the nine minutes it has taken every time it has been hung, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling did the bottom of the northern culvert with a spade because he said the barrow is not a spade, and nobody in this building has ever asked him why a barrow is not a spade and nobody is going to.

The run of mornings was read out loud at the seventh hour, one man to a figure, and neither of them said the other's half, and the two of them said **one hundred of one hundred and eighty-three**, and the frame of that run is of a month six months back.

The boards stand at **six hundred and forty-three days** and **six hundred and ninety**, and the forty-seven is the same forty-seven it has been at every row these four years, and the drawer behind them was not opened.

The nineteenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and ninety-six hundredweight.**

It came in five under Sunday and the fall went onto the falls, and that is the two hundred and sixtieth figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice. The window standing behind it divides a hundred and eighteen mornings one way and a hundred and eight the other, which is two hundred and twenty-six, and in four years nobody in this room has set either end of that window against the figure printed over it.

The four bodies of households are on this morning's sheet at two hundred and fifty-seven days, and the clause under the figure is the two hundred and fifty-sixth of those mornings out of two hundred and fifty-eighth, a day short of the figure and a day over the clause.

A body of households with a heading at the head of its own paper wrote into this holding this morning and the heading is a body and not a man, and the question on the sheet is one question and it is in nine words, and the question is who speaks for the eleven acres.

> THE SHEET AS IT CAME UP THE FEN ROAD THIS MORNING, IN THE HAND OF A CLERK WHO IS NOT NAMED AT THE FOOT OF IT, WITH A HEADING AT THE HEAD OF IT BEING THE NAME OF A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS AND THE NAME OF THE HOLDING IT IS ADDRESSED TO BOTH BEING IN THE HEADING AND NEITHER BEING A PERSON: **THE ELEVEN ACRES STAND IN THE SCHEDULE OF THIS HOLDING AND IN NO OTHER SCHEDULE THAT WE HAVE SEEN. THE FOURTH BOOK HAS A MAP IN IT FOUR YEARS OLD AND THE ELEVEN ACRES ARE ON THAT MAP AND ON NO LIST THAT GOES WITH IT. WE ASK ONE QUESTION. WHO SPEAKS FOR THE ELEVEN ACRES. WE ARE NOT ASKING FOR A DISPUTE TO BE SETTLED AND WE ARE NOT ASKING FOR ANYBODY TO BE ASKED ANYTHING. A NAME IS WHAT WE HAVE ASKED FOR AND NOTHING ELSE.** NO DATE IS PUT AGAINST THE ANSWER IN THE MARGIN AND NOBODY HAS WRITTEN ONE AND NOBODY IS TO WRITE ONE.

The clerk of this holding read it twice and went and got the fourth book and stood with it open on the middle table for about a quarter of an hour, and the map in it is four years old and it was drawn from a sheet sent out by a council that has since been dissolved, and nobody in this building has ever asked anybody to correct it and nobody has.

What this holding can give a body with a heading on its own paper is a difference and not a person. It can say that the four bodies of households are four and that the Undercommons is not one of the four and this holding is not a member of anything, and it can say that seven answers have been given to that question in four years and none of them is an answer and there is no eighth.

Nobody was named in the reply that went up the fen road at about the tenth hour. It went up in the clerk's own hand and it gave the difference and it did not give a person, and it did not give a reason either, because a letter is not a room and a reason given to one man in a room is not a thing that goes on paper with a heading at the head of it.

A body of households is not a body of customers and the two are not one thing. Four bodies of households signed a charter in a room nine miles off on the twelfth and three of the four are still signed to a machine for stored light, and neither signature is wrong and neither signature knows about the other one, and a holding that gave those two a single column would be inventing a body that is not there.

There is a man who came up the fen road with a list of ten and the list is a route. It is a list of ten places between here and a ford about a mile and a half past the far mouth of the cut, and four of the ten are named as a place by the name the local people use and six are named as what the thing is, and the numbering is his and not anybody else's.

A list of ten is a route and a route is not a body. He was at the gate at about the fifth hour on Saturday and did not come in, and nobody in this holding has told him that a body with a heading on its paper asked who speaks for eleven acres, and nobody has been asked about it and he has not offered.

The tenth of the ten is a ford about a mile and a half past the far mouth of the cut, and nobody in this holding has ever asked that man whether the ford has water in it, and the ninth of the ten is not in any of the four books and was not looked for this morning because the eighth was not finished.

A route is a route and a list is a list and a body of households is a body of households, and the three of them have never been in the same column in any of the four books and they are not going to be in one this morning, and nobody in this building has ever been asked to put them there.

Marek read the sheet on the middle table at about the eighth hour and gave the reason before he said the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is two hundred and fifty-four, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about eleven acres and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been the only person this holding could name against a ground for eleven years and I have been wrong about it at least four times and nobody ever found out because nobody ever asked me."** "I am not the answer to that letter and I am not going to write my name on a reply to it. There is a ring of bare ground inside those eleven acres that has been bare eleven years and I have never set foot in it, and if I put my name at the head of a column about those acres the first thing anybody would say is that the man who cannot get into the ground is the man who speaks for it."

Nobody in that room said that was right and nobody said it was wrong, and nobody asked him to go and stand at the edge of it, and the ring was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced by walking it and not explained this morning, and it has no dimension against it on any page a county could check.

The decision of the eleventh of the fourth is not reopened by anything in this building and the nine acres that came off in a month now seven months back came off and stayed off, and the cut at the kerb stays open and nothing goes into the two-foot gap this season and the valve is not opened and the key is not used.

None of that is on the reply that went up the fen road this morning, and none of it was asked for by the body that wrote, and a reply that carried any of it would have been a reply about something else and it was not one.

There is a growth that came up in the north row in a month now one month back that nobody in this holding has been able to put a word on, and a man with a barrow has no word for it either, and it is not in the ring and it is not priced and it did not go into the reply that went up the road this morning.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and fifty-three days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of the column, and it is not going to be read out a fifth time. The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and a question about eleven acres is not a ground for taking anything off that board.

Six things this holding does not know are ruled in a column in the seed house's schedule with no unit against any of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and eleven acres is not a seventh and a question nobody can answer is not a seventh, and nobody in this holding has ruled a row this morning.

The window of the office's work is shut with the shutter on it and the shutter does not come off until the sixth of a month, and the sixth of this month went past with it on, and no work is ever carried out of that window and a reply written at a table in a room with the door open is not work carried out of a window.

The requests column is fifty-three and the section-nine notes are fifty-three and they are a different list, and the sheet that came this morning is not a request and was not entered in the requests column, and nobody in this building has entered it and nobody is going to.

There is a form with four lines on the back of the seed house's own schedule and line one carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight and the other three are empty and no heading has been written at the top of it, and a form is not a column and is not going to be made into one this morning by anybody in this holding.

The far end of a thing is held by two people and neither of them is the reader of this body, and no instrument anywhere in four counties says whether it is on or off, and it has not been said to be on by anybody in this holding, and a letter asking who speaks for a ground is not an instrument and is not a column.

There was no rain in the night and the yard was dry at the second hour and dry at the ninth, and the middle road had taken nothing and given nothing back, and the north end of the ditch is running full and the outlet at its head is doing what an outlet does and the trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip and the man who dug the drain off the back of the lean-to has still not been asked which body fills it.

The chalk in the fill came out of the same bank the well came out of, and that bank is nine hundred yards off and it is not on any of the four lists, and nobody in this holding has ever written the word bank at the head of anything, and the seventh column of the door on that bank is read on the first of a month and on the thirtieth of a month and on no other morning, and this is a Monday and the walk of nine hundred yards was not made.

The man of about fifty was in the one room from about the sixth hour with the third book open in front of him and did not write anything in it, and he did not give the reason first this morning because nobody asked him anything, and a count of a man is not a column and is not ruled this morning and did not move.

The woman who keeps the seed house came in at about the seventh hour with a sheet of her own under her arm and put it face up on the long table and did not open it and went out again, and the sheet is still face up on the long table at the ninth hour and nobody in this building has asked her what is on it.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in this building this morning and nothing in this room was said to her and nothing was said about her, and the count of questions this holding has put to her stands where it stood and is not moved by a letter with a heading on it.

The two sheets about the two declinings are still upstairs on the long table about nine inches apart and neither has been touched this morning and neither has been settled, and a body that has said no is a body that has said no whether or not anybody in this building can name the acres it is saying it about.

The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and forty-seven days old this morning and has nothing on it, and he did the bottom of the northern culvert with a spade from the second hour until the fifth and came in at the ninth hour and was not asked about it and did not offer.

Behind the fourth book the day sheets are a foot and a half deep in the order they were used, and this morning's went on the top at the ninth hour with the eleven acres written in the ordinary place, and the sheet with no date at the head of it is still in the middle of that stack in the place it was on the first morning.

The comfort is not standing. The father is not alive in the wood. The day does not come back, and it is said once in this building this morning and it is not said again, and a letter that asks who speaks for eleven acres is not a reason to say it twice.

The man in the cart shed at the mouth of the cut is still in the cart shed, and this holding has not been and is not going to be, and his name is not in this building and is not going to be put in it by anybody because a body with a heading on its paper asked a question this morning.

The use log has fifteen lines and got no sixteenth this morning, and a question with a heading on it is not a use, and the man who keeps that log was not asked to add a line and did not offer and was not asked.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding wrote the nineteenth in at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the fourth book was shut with the map in it and the four-year-old map did not get a name written on it this morning and nobody is going to write one.
