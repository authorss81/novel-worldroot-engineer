# Chapter 492

## Fifty-Three And Fifty-Three

The low field gate wanted a bar and a stone and took the nine minutes it has taken every time it has been hung, and the fill behind the northern culvert had gone out about an inch in the night and the two of them put it back and went over it twice, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked until about the fifth.

Water came through the northern culvert in a thread about the width of a finger and came through the southern one in nothing at all, and the southern one is the one that has been dry since the first of this month and is not a thing anybody in this holding has put a figure against, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling put his hand in it and said the word dry and nobody asked him what he meant by it.

The fill is the same chalk the well came out of and the chalk is nine hundred yards off at a bank that is not on any of the four lists, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling dug the new toe on the seventh of this month and it has taken the weight of a boot without complaint and four feet of the old toe went anyway by the third hour, and that is what a toe does and nobody has written a figure against it.

Marek was on the fill from the second hour until about the fifth and then went to the pump and stayed there, and he did not go to the long table and did not look at the sheet under the weight, and nobody in this building has ever asked him why a man who can feel a far end would rather pack chalk than read a piece of paper.

The man of about fifty is not in this building this morning. He went up the coast road at the second hour yesterday because a man has been in a cart shed at the mouth of that road since the second of this month, and he said before he went that he was not going to say what he was going to do when he got there, and nobody asked him to and he did not offer.

The woman who keeps the seed house came through at about the fourth hour with two sheets under her arm and put them face down on the long table without opening them and went out again, and nobody asked her what they were and nobody asked her to turn them over, and they were still face down at the ninth hour with the fen road sheet under its weight two feet away.

Between them the two men who read the boards said their own sides at the seventh hour and neither of them said the other's half, and the run of mornings is **ninety-eight mornings of a hundred and seventy-nine**, and the frame of that run is of a month six months back and has been since the first of this month.

The boards themselves stand at **six hundred and thirty-nine days** and **six hundred and eighty-six**, forty-seven between them at every row, and the drawer behind them stayed shut.

The fifteenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**Nine hundred and ninety hundredweight.**

It came in five under Wednesday, and the fall went onto the falls. That is the two hundred and fifty-fifth figure of a run that has never written the same number down twice, and the window standing behind it divides a hundred and sixteen mornings one way and a hundred and six the other, which is two hundred and twenty-two, and the two ends of it have not been set against the figure over them by anybody in this building in four years.

The four bodies of households are written on this morning's sheet at two hundred and fifty-three days, and the clause under the figure is the two hundred and fifty-second of those mornings out of two hundred and fifty-fourth, a day short of the figure and a day over the clause.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at about the sixth hour with four sacks on it and came down loaded, and the number of journeys that barrow has made is eleven, and eleven is a floor and not a total and it is not raised this morning because a morning happened. The sacks that came down were of nothing this holding weighs, and the four that went up were of chalk, and neither of those is a figure and neither of them is in the second book.

Eleven is what the barrow's journeys are. The feet of a man who walks four miles are a different figure and the two have never been added in this building, and the nine miles to the machine that put the sheet on the long table yesterday is not one of the four roads this holding keeps figures for, and it is not one of the four and it was not added to any of them this morning.

The letter came up the fen road with the post at about the eighth hour and it is in a hand that is not any hand in this building and it has a heading at the head of it, being the name of a body, and the body is a body of households and not a person, and the heading is a heading and not a signature.

> THE LETTER AS IT CAME, UNFOLDED ON THE MIDDLE TABLE, IN THE HAND OF A CLERK WHO IS NOT NAMED AT THE FOOT OF IT, WITH A HEADING AT THE HEAD OF IT BEING THE NAME OF A BODY OF HOUSEHOLDS: **THIS HOLDING ASKS THE HOLDING THAT KEEPS THE FOUR BOOKS TO CERTIFY THE FIGURE OF DRAW SET AGAINST OUR SUBSCRIPTION IN THE SCHEDULE OF THE MACHINE NINE MILES OFF ON THE OTHER SIDE OF YOUR HOLDING. THE FIGURE WE HOLD IS THE FIGURE IN THE SCHEDULE AND THE SCHEDULE HAS A DAY AT THE FOOT OF IT AND NO NAME AT THE HEAD OF IT. A FIGURE WITH A DAY ON IT AND NO NAME ON IT IS A FIGURE THAT NOBODY CAN CHECK, AND A FIGURE THAT NOBODY CAN CHECK CANNOT BE CERTIFIED BY A HOLDING THAT KEEPS FOUR BOOKS. WE ASK FOR A CERTIFICATE AND NOT FOR AN OPINION AND WE ASK FOR IT IN WRITING. OUR DRAW HAS NOT BEEN ALTERED SINCE WE SIGNED. WE HAVE NOT STOPPED DRAWING.** NOTHING IS ASKED FOR IN THE FOOT OF IT AND NO DATE IS PUT AGAINST ANYTHING IN IT AND THE HEADING AT THE HEAD OF IT IS A BODY AND NOT A MAN.

The clerk of this holding read it twice and gave the reason out loud before he answered it, in the one room, with the door open, and the count of that in this holding's history is six, and it is his and of nobody else's.

**"I am going to say the one thing about a letter and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have been the clerk of this holding for four years and I have had eleven letters with a heading at the head of them and I have answered nine of them the same way."** "This holding keeps four books. There is no column in the four books with a heading on it, and I am not going to rule one this morning for a body that has not asked this holding to make a column, and a certificate is a document and this holding does not make documents."

He wrote the answer out in full and it said what it always says, that this holding keeps four books and no column with a heading on it, and he read it back once and did not read it back twice, and it went up the fen road with the post at about the tenth hour.

The request went into the requests column and the count is fifty-three, and it was fifty-three in the evening, and the section-nine notes are fifty-three, and the two of them are a different list and the two of them have never been added to one another in this building and are not going to be.

A body of households that has drawn on a machine for eleven years is not a customer of this holding and is not a member of it and is not a body of this holding, and a request entered in a column in the second book does not make it one, and nobody in this room has written anything under the request that would say that it did.

The letter from the fen road about a date at the foot of a sheet is still on the long table under the weight where it was put yesterday, and nobody turned it over this morning, and the answer that went up the road this evening says nothing about it and does not refer to it.

A date at the foot of a sheet nine miles off and a date in the head of a letter in a clerk's own county are two dates and they are not the same date, and this building has one of them in a drawer and one of them in a column, and no page in this room has put the two together and nobody is going to.

The woman who coordinates the moving reserves came into this yard at about the sixth hour with a list of what four counties draw out of that machine in a bad week and did not leave the list, and she is not support staff and has not been asked to be and this holding has not offered her anything, and the count of questions this holding has put to her since the third of this month is nil and is nil.

She read the letter on the middle table standing up and said one thing about it, and what she said was that a body which draws on a machine for eleven years knows what it draws and does not need a figure certified, and then she said that was not a reason to refuse and she was going up the coast road at the ninth hour to find out whether the man in the cart shed was still in it.

Nobody in this room asked her which of the two things she had said was the one she meant, and the man of about fifty is four miles off with a cart at that hour and was not asked and did not offer.

The four ruled lines under two words in the corner are still empty and the answer to them is not due. The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and forty-nine days and it is not filled and it is not refused, and it has not been read out this morning and it is not going to be read out a fifth time in this building.

The offer is on the low board, face up, not filled, not withdrawn, no date and no line for a name, and a letter from a body of households nine hundred yards off is not a ground for taking it off that board and nobody in this yard has suggested one.

There are about four people in this holding who would say that a body which has signed a charter and signed a subscription has signed the same document twice, and about four who would say it has signed two, and neither of the two fours has been asked and neither of the two fours is going to be.

The trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip, and a body of households that asks a holding to certify a figure does not thereby fill a trough, and the man who dug the drain off the back of the lean-to has still not been asked which body fills that trough and has not offered.

Nobody in this yard said that the machine was wrong and nobody in this yard said that the machine was right, and nobody fought anybody, and a body with two needs and one set of hands is a fact about a body and a fact about a body is not a fight.

The count of things this holding has said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and it did not move this morning, and the four people at the tap at about the eighth hour heard the whole of what the clerk said in the room and nobody has said how many heard the half of it.

The clerk of this holding shut the second book at about the ninth hour and put his thumb in the page and did not open it again, and the third book was on the table with the roll of terms on it and nothing came off it, and the third column of the sheet of terms is ruled and empty with a heading that has stood over it since the twenty-first of a month now three months back and a sixth is not being written.

The window of the office's work is shut with the shutter on it and the shutter comes off on the sixth of a month, and the sixth of the next one is outside this one. No work is ever carried out of that window, and a letter answered at a table in a room with the door open is not work carried out of a window and is not going to be treated as one.

The compost line is paid at twenty-four and not discharged in twenty-four, with the word in front of the figure, and the figure did not move this morning and is not going to be moved by a letter from a body of households, and nobody in this holding has ever worked out what a count of undischarged runs is a count of.

There is a thing under a cloth on the shelf in the one room that was put there before this holding kept the second book, and it has not been opened and it has not been removed and it has not been broken and it has not been set against anything, and the count of people who have looked at it is two, and it is not the instrument of anything that happened this morning.

There was a quarter of an inch of frost in the small hours and none of it on the yard by the seventh hour. An inch came up in the middle road in the night and one of them had gone by the ninth. The north end of the ditch is still full and has been since Saturday.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding put the day's figure into the second book at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the second book was shut with the day in it, and the shutter on the window of the office's work is still on and no work is ever carried out of one.

> Entered by the clerk of this holding at the first hour, in his own hand, as one unbroken line: **The fifteenth. The third line of the rota worked, the two culverts on the middle road and the low field gate, the gate lifted with a bar and a stone, four people from the second hour until the fifth. The pair read aloud at the seventh hour. The same day in and the same day out. Nine hundred and ninety hundredweight to the launder, being the two hundred and fifty-fifth. A letter with a heading at the head of it came up the fen road asking this holding to certify a figure of draw for a machine nine miles off, and the reason was given out loud before the answer, and the answer was written out in full. The request is in the requests column and the column is fifty-three and the section-nine notes are fifty-three and the two are a different list. The barrow went up the four-mile road and came down loaded and the number of journeys is eleven and eleven is a floor.** Nothing of this is a forecast and nothing of it is a cause and nothing of it is a total.
