# Chapter 510

## A Reason Somebody Else Gave

The first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the water: the north end of the ditch, the trough at the head of the four-mile road and the standpipe at the yard. The ditch at the north end had taken about two inches of silt in the night and the man of about thirty-one of Silling went through it with a bar and the bar came up black and he put it back and did not say anything about the black.

The two of them read the boards to each other at the seventh hour and neither read the other's half, and the run of mornings is **one hundred and seven mornings of one hundred and ninety-seven**, of a month seven months back.

The third went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**One thousand and seventeen hundredweight.**

Five under Monday, and the fall went onto the falls, and that is the two hundred and seventy-third figure of a run that has never once had the same number put into it twice. The window behind it came to a hundred and twenty-five mornings against a hundred and fifteen, which is two hundred and forty, and a man walked nine miles out and back this morning and did not once look at either end of it.

The morning sheet carries the four bodies of households at two hundred and seventy-one days and the clause under it at the two hundred and seventieth of those mornings out of two hundred and seventy-second, and one of the four passed a motion about a piece of paper in another county on Saturday evening and neither of those two numbers has any opinion about it.

**Six hundred and fifty-seven days** and **seven hundred and four** on the long wall at the ninth hour, the gap the same gap it has always been, and the drawer was not opened.

A body of households with a heading at the head of its own paper passed a motion on Saturday evening at a meeting of its own, and the clerk of that body wrote it out and sent it up the fen road with the post on Sunday, and the clerk of this holding read it at the eighth hour and read it again at the ninth.

The motion says that while an open offer stands on a board in this holding, undated and unfilled and not withdrawn, that body will not sign a subscription to the machine nine miles off, and it gives that as its reason, and the reason is in the motion in the motion's own words.

The refusal is entered in the second book in the ordinary place and nobody in this building asked that body for an explanation and nobody offered one. Three of the four bodies of households are signed to a machine and one of them has just written down that it is not signing again, and a body with two needs and one set of hands is a fact about a body and this building is not going to improve on it.

**"They have cited a piece of paper that has been lying face up on a board in this building for four months as a reason for a decision they are taking in a room nine miles off."** The man of about fifty said it to the second book and gave the reason for it first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-five, and it is the second bookkeeper of this holding to say a reason out loud in a month, and it is his. **"I want to be careful here, because four years of watching bodies of households turn pieces of paper into reasons has taught me one thing and it is that they are usually right about the reason and wrong about the paper."** "They are right that an offer sitting undated on a board is a thing that ought to be answered by somebody. They have answered it by not subscribing. That is not the same answer and it is not my answer and I am not going to stand here and tell them they have done it wrong, because a body of households is not a body of customers and the two are not one thing."

Nobody in that room asked him which of the two he would have done and he did not offer and was not asked, and nobody thanked him and nobody agreed with him.

The offer is face up on the low board at the far end of the long table with nothing written on it and no date and no line anywhere on it for a name, and it has lain there since a month now four months back, and nobody in this building has ever been told to take it off that board and nobody is going to be told, and a motion passed in another county is not a reason for taking it off.

It is not this holding's offer and it is not a new one and nobody in this room described it as a new one. This holding makes no offer of any kind this month, to anybody, from the office or from anywhere else in this building, and the paper on the low board is not it and has never been it.

Marek went out of the gate at about the sixth hour on foot and with no coat and came back in at the fen road side at about the fourth hour in the afternoon, and the road out there is about nine miles and the coast road is about nine miles and nobody added them.

The great door of that building was standing open when he got to it, and it was open on the third of an ordinary Monday afternoon, and the three wheels were behind the rail and a man was on one of them and not one man on two of them, and a woman was at the desk in the smaller door and the smaller door was open too.

There is no name over the great door and there is no date cut in the concrete and he looked for both of them again on Monday and neither of them is there, and there is a rail and about nine hundred yards of it and a wall, and about four hundred thousand people eat out of what the three wheels do in an ordinary winter, and none of that is a name on a door.

Iona Vey was standing inside the great door with her hand off the rail and she did not step out of it and she did not step back from it, and nobody in that road put a name to her and nobody in that road had expected to see her there.

She said two things and she said both of them, and both of them are true. She said that the schedule on the wall behind her is the eleventh of those that has come up a road and that every one of them has been kept. She said that a door is open when the work in front of it is finished and not when somebody nine miles off decides it would be convenient, and she said it without heat and without a figure and without asking him for anything.

Marek did not go in. He stood in the road in front of it for about an hour and a half and he did not put a foot on the step, and the door stayed open the whole time and neither of them closed it and nobody came out and asked him to and nobody sent anybody out to stop him.

What runs that place has taken root branches off for thirty-one years and has never once asked the people living on them, and nobody in this holding said a word about that this morning, and nobody will, and the two facts on this page are going to sit exactly where they are for as long as this volume lasts.

She is also right about the schedule, and being right about a schedule for thirty-one years is not a thing anybody in this yard is going to argue with this morning. A document on a wall that a person has kept honestly for eleven schedules and a woman who has been on the other end of every one of them are two separate pieces of the morning, and the man of about fifty looked at the great door for a while and then went back inside without saying which of the two he had come out here to think about.

**"I am going to say the one thing about an open door, and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that I have wanted to walk through that door for four days and I have been telling myself I would know what I was doing when I got there."** He said it at the gate of this yard at about the fourth hour in the afternoon with his hands in his pockets and the road still on him. **"I stood in the road. It was open and I did not go in, and I want that written down in the plainest words anybody in this holding can stand behind, because inside a week a good half of this county is going to say I stood in front of an open door and turned away from it, and that is not what happened."**

**"I did not go in because this holding has no charter that says anything about a door nine miles off, and the body that has a charter and has a key has not asked us, and because I do not know what is behind the door and neither does anybody in this building, and a man who does not know what is behind a door and goes through it is not doing the work, whatever anybody calls it."**

Nobody in that yard said that was right and nobody said it was wrong, and nobody in this yard used the word coward and nobody has used it anywhere this month.

The man of about fifty was at the gate when he came in and asked him one question, which was whether anybody had come out of the door, and Marek said no, and the man of about fifty said that he had not expected anybody to and shut the third book he had not opened.

Tova Reed was at the middle table with the four counties' sheets when he came in and she did not get up and he did not sit down, and she said one thing to him about the fourth council and the sowing date and did not say it twice, and he said one thing back about the ditch at the north end having taken two inches of silt, and the two of them were in the same room for about eleven minutes and most of that was about paper.

Nobody in that room said a word about her ear and nobody is going to. What is asked of her this month is the seed front in four counties and a sowing date and a schedule that not one of those four councils will put a name to, and nobody in this building has offered to take any of it off her and nobody has thanked her for carrying it.

She put the four counties' sheets into a stack and squared the edges of them against the table, which she does at the end of every morning, and then she said one more thing to the room, which was that the fourth council wrote on the twenty-eighth and the letter said nothing at all, and that a letter which says nothing at all is not worse than a letter that says no.

A door standing open nine miles off is not the far end of a thing, and the far end is held by two people who are not him and no instrument in four counties says otherwise.

He came in off nine miles with his sleeve down and the four accounts of what is under it are four and this morning did not make a fifth.

The sheet from the fen road is in the long drawer under two books with a day at the foot of it, and that day was eleven mornings ago, and nobody in this building has said a word about it this month and nobody is going to and nobody in this yard called it anything this morning.

It is a day like any other day and it is gone the way days go, and the machine had a date in front of it and a reason, and the reason was given by somebody else and it came from nine miles off and it was not this holding's to accept, and somebody else's reason is not a date.

The man who keeps the gauges has kept them for nineteen years and the woman at the desk in the outer office is not the man who reads the schedule off the wall and the clerk of the machine house is a fourth, and none of the four has a name on any page in this building, and none of the four was asked by anybody in this holding to be anything this month.

The three leaves from the fen road are still on the long table under one weight where they have been since Sunday, and nobody has put a figure of this morning against any of them, and a stack of three correct sheets is a stack and not a column.

A motion in another county is not a seventh and a door standing open is not a seventh.

The window of the office's work is shut with the shutter on it and the shutter does not come off until the sixth of a month, and a door nine miles off that stands open is not that window and is not a substitute for it, and no work is ever carried out of that window and this holding is not going to work through a door that somebody else happens to have left open.

Nobody has entered an eighth session and the answer under the two words in the corner is still not due.

Paid at twenty-five and it did not move, and the log got no sixteenth line, and the second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and sixty-one days old and has nothing on it.

The sheet that claims the corridor is in the drawer under the two books and it is correct in every particular, and the order sealing the route is in the next drawer and it is valid and it opens nothing, and an open door nine miles off is not a withdrawal of either of them and no apology is going to be written for either of them by anybody in this holding.

Two can hold a pattern for four and one can hold it for five, and there are two rootmarks, and none of those three counts moved today. A door standing open is not a count of any of them and does not turn into one by being open.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling put the bar back on the wall at the sixth hour and said one thing about a door, which was that a door that stands open is a door that has been worked, and nobody asked him how he knew that and he did not say and nobody is going to.

Fifty-three and fifty-three, and a motion passed nine miles off and a refusal entered here went into neither of them.

The schedule did not go nine miles this morning because the man who went out with it in the oilskin had a bar in his hand and nothing else, and a man carrying a bar does not carry a satchel nine miles and get there with both.

A man walked nine miles and the barrow walked four, and the two figures have never been added in this building.

The north wall was done by the old man with the stick and by nobody else, and a man of about seventy went unfetched this morning and has been fetched twenty-seven times and will be fetched again on the first of a month.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and sixty-seven days, not filled, not refused, nothing at the head of the column, and it is not going to be read out a fifth time in this building.

A motion from nine miles off went into the ordinary place on this morning's day sheet, and the undated one in the middle of the stack stayed where it is.

A dry morning from the second hour to the ninth, the middle road neither taking nor giving, the north end of the ditch running full, and the trough at the head of the four-mile road full by the seventh hour and inside its own lip.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding wrote the third in at about the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and a door nine miles off is standing open this evening on a day of its own choosing, and the worst day on anybody's calendar was a reason somebody else gave, and this holding never took it as a date.
