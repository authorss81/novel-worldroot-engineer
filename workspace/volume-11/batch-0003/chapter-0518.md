# Chapter 518

## Six Days Up, And Nothing Carried Out Of One

The first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the water: the north end of the ditch, the trough at the head of the four-mile road and the standpipe in the yard. The standpipe had a skin on it a finger's width after the rain of the day before and the man of about thirty-one of Silling broke it with the back of a spade and let the run go and shut it and said nothing about it.

The eleventh came in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**One thousand and twenty-nine hundredweight.**

It came in five under Monday, so the fall went onto the falls, and that is the two hundred and eighty-first figure of a run in which no number has ever been put into it twice. The window behind it came to a hundred and twenty-nine mornings against a hundred and nineteen, which is two hundred and forty-eight, and neither end of that window has ever been set against the number printed over the other in this building.

This morning's sheet carries the four bodies of households at two hundred and seventy-nine days, and the clause beneath it takes the two hundred and seventy-eighth of those mornings out of two hundred and eightieth, and neither of those three is a person and neither of them is a household.

At the seventh hour the two men who read the boards read the pair to each other and neither read the other's half. The run of mornings is **one hundred and eleven mornings of two hundred and five**, and the frame of it is of a month seven months back.

The near board is at **six hundred and sixty-five days** and the far one at **seven hundred and twelve**, forty-seven apart as ever, and the drawer behind the near one was not opened.

The shutter on the window of the office's work went back on at about the eleventh hour.

It had been up six days. It came off on the sixth of this month at about the sixth hour and it went on on the eleventh at about the eleventh hour, and nobody in this building took it off and nobody in this building put it back on, and nobody in this building has ever asked who does either of those things. The paint beside it is old enough that nobody can remember who last did it and nobody has asked.

No work was ever carried out of it. That has been the case for eleven years and it has been the case for these six days, and nothing went out of that window on the sixth and nothing went out of it on the seventh or the eighth or the ninth or the tenth, and nobody in this building ever learned what was carried out of it, and this page is going to say that once and is not going to say it a second time in a month.

**"It has been up since Thursday and I have not been able to stop looking at it."** The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said that to the second book at about the seventh hour and gave the reason before she said it, and the count of questions this holding has put to her this month is nil and is nil. **"The reason is that I have been going nine miles four days running and I have got nothing out of it that I can put in front of a council, and there is a shutter on a wall in this yard that has been up the whole time, and I have caught myself twice this week wanting to say the two things out loud in the same breath."** She put her thumb on the page and did not write anything on it and nobody asked her to.

Nobody in that room said they understood that and nobody said they did not. A body with two needs and one set of hands is a fact about a body and this building said that once in an earlier week and is not going to say it again in the same breath as a wall.

The man of about fifty had the third book open on the long table from the fifth hour and did not write in it, and he said one thing at about the eighth hour that nobody had asked him for and that nobody in this room had thought of, and he printed no figure for it and this morning consumed no number of his.

**"Six days is not a long time."** He said it to the open book and not to the window. **"I have been treating that shutter like a hole somebody has dug and this week I have been in a building nine miles off six days running, and I did not once think about the shutter, and that is not a thing I am proud of and it is not a thing I am going to write down either."** Nobody in that room disagreed with him and nobody agreed with him and he shut the book on it.

Marek was in the one room with the door open and nothing in front of him for about an hour in the middle of the morning and he did not go out to the ring and he did not go out of the gate, and the count of questions this holding has put to him this month is nil and is nil. He did not say anything about the shutter being back on and nobody in that room expected him to.

Five of them sat down at the long table at about the second hour in the afternoon because they had gone out four days running and nobody had put anything in a book about any of it, and each of them said one thing about what they had actually done, and none of the five answers was the answer they went out for.

Nia Vale said that she had stood at the tank end for an hour and a half on one of the days and had watched a man walk a line of gauges twice with a cloth and a card and had come home able to say that he did it twice an hour and had come home unable to say one thing about what any of it was for. She said that she had a list in her own book and had not taken it out in that building and was not going to, and that a list of neighbors is a list of neighbors and not a column.

Soren Rill said that he had stood at a bar laid across a wheel that was not turning for the better part of an hour on one afternoon and had worked out that it was not turned because it was not wanted that morning and that the bar across it was how you said so. He said that he had carried seed up a road for nine years and had never once been shown how a machine said a thing it did not need to say, and that this was a smaller thing than the reason he went out and he had not expected it to be the thing that stayed with him.

**"I am not the officer of anything and nobody has made me one this week and I would like it written down that I said that at a table on a Tuesday and not on a road."** He said it to the table and did not say it again for the rest of the morning, and the clerk of this holding did not write it down and nobody asked him to.

The man of about fifty said that he had looked at a drawing of three wheels on the back of a sheet for a quarter of an hour and had copied it badly and had not asked anybody to help him, and that the reason he had not asked was that he wanted to find out what he could not do by himself. He said that he had been unable to do the spokes. He said that and nothing else and nobody in that room laughed at him and nobody offered to do them for him.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said that she had got a level and not a figure and had written it down as a level, and that she had spent six days in front of a wall that had a column headed for the things somebody did not know with six rows ruled in it and no seventh, and that she had come home and looked at her own six and had not ruled a seventh either, and that this was the first decision she has made this year that she did not have to explain to anybody.

Marek said the least of the five and it was that he had stood at a casting and put his hand on it, and that the casting was colder than the room and that nobody had told him it would be and that he had expected to be told things all week and had not been, and that he was not sure yet whether that was a kindness or simply how a building is.

Nobody thanked anybody for any of the five answers and nobody said anybody else had said theirs well. A morning with five answers in it is not a meeting and it was not entered as one and there is no entry for it in any of the four books and there is not going to be one.

Sera Quill came down the fen road at about the seventh hour and went into the seed house for about half an hour and came out and went down to the second bus without coming into the one room, and she has a list of eighty-one families in four hands in a county book four counties off and this holding has no copy of it and did not ask for one.

**"I have been in eleven counties' record rooms and I have never once been asked what a building is for."** She said it at the gate and not to anybody in it. **"I was asked what a record is for, which is a different question and one I can answer, and I have answered it about eighty times, and the answer I give does not have any room in it."** Nobody in that yard asked her what her answer was and she did not offer it and she got on the bus.

The man with the slate came back into this building at about the ninth hour on Tuesday and has not been in it since, and the slate is still leaning against the shelf in the one room, and nobody in this holding has asked him where he has been and nobody is going to.

The old man with a stick worked the north wall from the third hour to the sixth and said one number out loud to nobody and it was the right one, and nobody in this yard asked him how he knew it and nobody in this holding is going to.

The man of the north row with the cough did the top of the low field gate alone in the afternoon with a bar, and the four ruled lines under his condition are bare and the answer is not due, and nobody in this holding has asked him to hold an end of anything and he is not going to be asked.

The woman who keeps the seed house was in the seed house door for most of the afternoon with the schedule and the six ruled rows and did not come out, and the oilskin satchel was on the long table under the four pound weight, and a sheet that went nine miles in that satchel and came back in it is the same sheet and is not a column and the seventh row is not ruled.

Three of the four bodies of households that signed a charter in a room nine miles off seventy-nine days ago are still signed to a machine for stored light and one of them passed a motion at its own meeting and sent it up the fen road, and a body with two needs and one set of hands is a fact about a body and none of the four bodies was asked to give either of the two up and none of them did.

The corridor in the pan is named in a drawer in this building and is not established as on, and the sheet that claims it is correct in every particular and it lets nothing out. The order sealing the route is in the next drawer and it is valid and it opens nothing, and no apology is going to be written for it by anybody in this holding and none was written this week.

The man who keeps the use log came in from the shed at about the seventh hour with the log under his arm, looked at the shutter, and wrote nothing down, because a shutter coming off a wall on the sixth and going back on the eleventh is not a use and the fifteen lines in that book have never once held one.

The shutter is on. The window behind it is a window in a wall of this holding. There is nothing behind it that anybody in this county can put a figure to, and there was nothing behind it for the six days it was up either, and a man went nine miles four times in six days and got a drawing and a rule that says two and about nine hours and got nothing else, and this holding is not going to pretend that a shutter and a building are the same kind of thing.

The sessions entered in this holding's book stand at seven and nobody entered one this month and the count is seven. There is work this month that this holding has not entered and is not going to enter, and a thing that is not entered is not in the book and the answer to the four ruled lines under the two words in the corner of that room is still not due and every one of the four is bare.

The compost line is paid at twenty-five and did not move. The register form is a hundred and seventy-five days old on the middle table with nothing at the head of its column, and it is not filled and it is not refused and it is not going to be read out a fifth time. The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and sixty-nine days old and has nothing on it.

The use log has fifteen lines and got no sixteenth for six days of a shutter being up. The barrow went up the four-mile road at the sixth hour with three sacks and came down loaded, and its journeys are eleven and eleven is a floor, and the four miles are not the nine and the nine are not the coast road.

The two sheets about the two declinings were still nine inches apart where they have been for a month, and the shutter coming back on did not move either of them. The third column of the sheet of terms is ruled and empty and its heading has not been improved.

Six things this holding does not know are ruled with no unit against any of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and a shutter is not a seventh and a drawing on the back of a sheet is not a seventh and neither of them is a column.

The offer is face up on the low board, undated, unfilled, not withdrawn, no line anywhere on it for a name, and the shutter being back on is not a reason to take anything off that board and nobody in this room touched it.

There was no rain in the night and the ground had begun to set again by the seventh hour, and the middle road neither took nor gave. The north end of the ditch is running full, and the trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding wrote the eleventh in at the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice, and the shutter is on the window of the office's work, and it was up six days, and nothing was carried out of it, and nobody in this building ever learned what was carried out of it.
