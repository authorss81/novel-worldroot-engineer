# Chapter 529

## Four Days After It Was Worked, And Nobody There

Four days after it was worked, the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and there was nobody at the headland above Marden to take it, and that is the first morning running since. The count resets because the line was worked and not because anybody did anything, and that is the whole of the arithmetic of it.

The standing is a hundred and twelve mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and eighty-three not, and twenty-nine and eighty-three come to a hundred and twelve, and the count rises by exactly one on a morning this line comes round and by nothing on the other three.

Twenty-nine taken did not move this morning. It did not move on the morning the line was worked and it has not moved on any morning this year, and nobody in this holding is blamed for a field that waited and nobody in this holding is thanked for the one that did not.

Kept in this room the twenty-second of this month is the sixtieth morning of the rotation, and kept the other way the same morning is the forty-seventh, and both of those are on this page because a page that carried one of them would be carrying half a fact. Thirteen has stood in that gap for four years and nobody in this building has ever worked out what the thirteen counts and it does not arrive by taking twenty-nine off either figure.

The twenty-second went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**One thousand and fifty-two hundredweight.**

Saturday came up eight on Friday and nothing was set against it, and the launder takes the two hundred and ninety-second figure of a run in which no number has ever been put down twice. The window behind it came out at a hundred and thirty-five mornings one way and a hundred and twenty-four the other, and the two of them are two hundred and fifty-nine, and the two men who read the boards read them at the seventh hour and the man who reads the far side put his thumb on the drawer behind the near one without looking down and nobody in that room has ever seen him do it and asked him why.

Two hundred and ninety days against the four bodies of households, the sheet says, and one under and one over in the clause beneath it, which takes the two hundred and eighty-ninth of those mornings out of two hundred and ninety-first. A field worked four days ago is not a household and did not move any of those three figures.

Near board of the long wall, six hundred and seventy-six days; far board, seven hundred and twenty-three; the drawer behind the near one not opened at any hour of this morning. Forty-seven between them, as always.

Second book, ninth hour, and nothing written in the place the run of mornings goes, for the reason this page gives itself: half of the run is half a number on an even morning and not a whole one. The frame of that run is of a month seven months back.

The headland bank is a different color where it was worked on Tuesday and it has not gone back, and a man with a bar can get up most of it now and not all of it, and there is no figure on any page in this building for how much of it a man with a bar can get up.

At about half past six Harlan Vetch went up the four-mile road on foot to the bottom of the headland bank and stood there for about twenty minutes without going up it, and nobody followed him up the road and nobody asked him why he had gone.

**"I got up it on Tuesday with a barrow I could hardly lift and there were two other men there who knew the ground."** He said it to the bank and not to anybody. **"There is a place on it about forty feet up where the ground stops being bank and starts being wall, and I found it because somebody who worked here every day for eleven years was on it and did not say anything."** He said it to the bank and not to anybody. **"That is not a figure of anything. That is a thing three people knew and one of them has never once been asked about it in four years because nobody thought to ask him."**

He stood at the bottom of it with his hands in his pockets and looked up at the place he had just described.

**"I have thought about asking him about it every day since Tuesday and I have not done it, and I am not going to do it this week."** Nobody at the bottom of that bank asked him and he came back down and went to the north wall.

The far end of a thing is held by two people, this body is not one of them, and no instrument anywhere in four counties says whether it is on or off, and nobody said it today and nobody has ever said it.

About nine hundred yards is how much rail there is in that building. A wheel behind that rail is not that thing, and neither is a hand-lever under one of three wheels, and neither is a list of districts in a book, and the three have never been added together in this building.

Marek was in the one room with the door open for about two hours in the middle of the morning with nothing in front of him, and he did not go out to the ring and he did not go out of the gate, and the count of questions this holding has put to him this month is nil and is nil. He has said nothing about a wheel this week except that it was not him, and nobody in that room expected him to say anything else.

**"I have found out something about myself and I am going to say it because I have been not saying it for four days and it has got worse."** He said it to the shut door and he gave the reason before he said the thing and printed no figure for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his. **"When somebody at the tap told me which wheel, I felt something about it, and what I felt was that somebody else had already decided."** He said that to the shut door and did not open it.

**"About nine days ago I would have felt that as relief. Four days ago I would have felt it as the ordinary shape of the thing. This morning I felt it as a thing that had happened to me while I was in a room with a hand on a spoke, and it was not the same feeling twice, and I do not know which of the two is the one I am."** Nobody in that room told him which one was the right one and nobody thanked him and nobody asked him a second question.

The man of about fifty said one thing at the long table at about the fifth hour, and he gave the reason for it before he said it and printed no figure for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his twice over, and the two blank mornings in a week are both his and neither of them moved him.

**"There is a thing that was worked on Tuesday and it is not a thing that has been worked."** He said it to the shut third book. **"The count of this line is counting mornings when somebody went. It does not count the bank and it does not count whether the bank is still there and it does not count whether anybody could get up it. In four years nobody has walked back up that bank and looked at what they did, including the man who did it, and I am going to go up there and look at it, and that is all I am going to do."** Nobody at that table asked him which day he meant and he did not say and he was not asked.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said one thing at the tap at about the eighth hour and she gave the reason for it first. **"A man has told me that a number went up by one on Tuesday and that a field got worked, and I have been waiting four years for a figure and a fact to arrive in the same week and I have decided, this morning, at a tap, that I do not want them."** She filled a bucket and shut it off. **"The number is what everybody in this county is going to be arguing about in six weeks and the field is a bank a man can get up with a barrow, and when those two are in the same sentence the bank stops being a bank and becomes evidence."** She set the bucket on the step and stood with her hand on the handle of it. **"I have watched that happen four times in four years and I have been the one holding the paper on three of them."** Nobody at the tap argued with her and nobody asked her what she wanted instead and she carried the bucket to the seed house.

Nia Vale was out with the moving reserves and the districts that draw on the stored light are in her own book and the number of them was not said out loud in this yard and was not written in the second book. Tova Reed is on the seed front in four counties and nobody has taken any of it off her and nobody thanked her and nobody put a hand on her arm.

**"I have been out on the road since before the light and I want to say one thing about the order of it."** She said it at the tap at about the eleventh hour with the road still on her boots. **"I went past four districts this morning before I got here, and in three of them I asked the same question I always ask, which is whether anybody has heard anything, and in all three of them somebody had heard something, and in all three of them it was about a wheel."** She put the bucket down on the flags and did not pick it up again straight away.

Nobody at the tap asked her which three and she did not say and she was not asked. **"I am not angry about it. I am saying it because the second somebody in this county decides to tell a district something, the thing I have been protecting for nine years stops being protected by the fact that nobody thought of it."** She picked the bucket up again and did not go out of the yard with it. **"That is a thing that happened this morning and I do not think anybody meant anything by it and I am not going to pretend I did not notice it."**

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came in off the middle road at about the ninth hour and put the bar back on the wall and stood at the tap until the water came through, and the second of the four lines in his own book is a hundred and eighty days old and nobody has asked him about it in a hundred and eighty days.

From the third hour to the sixth the old man with a stick had the north wall with the wind behind him, and he came in and said one number out loud to nobody, and it was the right one, and nobody in this yard asked him how he knew it and nobody is going to.

At about the ninth hour Sera Quill came down the fen road, stopped at the gate, and did not come in. She stood there for about ten minutes looking up the four-mile road, and then she got on the second bus again and went back up it, and nobody in this yard asked her why she had come and nobody has asked her since.

**"I have four counties of people who have heard a thing and none of them are my business."** She said it at the gate and not to anybody in the yard. **"I put a name to a relay in a room with more than one door in it about four months ago and eighty-one families went into a county book, and I have spent every day since being asked what the sum is worth."** She did not come in. **"This morning I walked up that road because I heard there was a field worked and I wanted to have said something about a field."**

Somebody at the far end of the yard shut a gate and neither of them looked up.

**"I got to the gate and I stood there for ten minutes and I found out that I had nothing to say about a field, because I do not know what a field is in this county and I have never once in forty years been the person anybody came to when a field got worked."** Nobody at the gate said that was a shame and nobody said it was a good thing and she went back up the road on the bus.

A headland nobody went up is not a use, so the log has fifteen lines this morning and no sixteenth. The barrow went up the four-mile road at the sixth hour with three sacks and came down loaded, and its journeys are eleven and eleven is a floor, and eleven was eleven before anybody in this county heard of a wheel.

Twenty-five, and paid, and the figure has not moved since the last morning. A hundred and eighty-six days is the age of the register form on the middle table, where there is nothing at the head of its column and nothing in it, and nobody has filled it and nobody has refused it. The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and eighty days old, and has nothing on it, and he has not been asked.

There is a corridor in the pan named in a drawer in this holding and it is not established as on, and the sheet claiming it is correct in every particular and lets nothing out. A culvert under a machine house nine miles off is not that corridor, and the two have never been added here, and the order sealing the route is in the drawer under the two books, valid, and opening nothing.

The two sheets about the two declinings were about nine inches apart on the long table and have been for a month and a day, and a wheel that somebody chose in another room did not move either of them. The third column of the sheet of terms on the long wall is ruled and empty, its heading is not improved, and a sixth term is not going to be written this month.

Face up on the low board, undated, unnamed, and with nothing written on it at all, is the paper that has lain there since a month now four months back. Nobody in this building has ever been told to take it off that board and nobody is going to be told, and a headland with nobody at the top of it this morning is not a reason for taking anything off it.

Four inches, branching twice, and not a second mark on it: this holding has four accounts of that arm and they reconcile none of one another.

A blank morning consumes no number and nobody entered a session this morning, so the count of them in this holding's book is still seven, and the four ruled lines under the two words are bare and the answer is not due.

Fifty-three requests and fifty-three section-nine notes, and six not knowns with no unit against any of them and no seventh row ruled, and a bank is not a seventh and a wheel is not a seventh and a place forty feet up a headland where the ground stops being bank is not a seventh.

A bar, the low field gate, the middle of the day. He came in with his coat wet at the shoulders, the four ruled lines under his condition are bare, the answer is not due, and nobody in this holding has asked him to hold an end of anything this month.

There was no rain and the middle road took nothing and gave nothing back. The north end of the ditch is running full, and the trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip, and it was full by the sixth hour yesterday and nobody has written down what the difference is between those two mornings.

Nobody was thanked this morning, and nobody was thanked on Tuesday either. The clerk of this holding wrote the twenty-second in at the ninth hour and read it back once. The standing is a hundred and twelve in force with twenty-nine taken and eighty-three not, and twenty-nine taken did not move, because working a line and taking it are two different things.
