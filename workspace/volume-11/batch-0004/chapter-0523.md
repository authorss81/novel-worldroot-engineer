# Chapter 523

## A Reading Is Not A Maintenance Act

The second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the outlet end and the two stones at the head of it, and the pack under the near stone came out and went back dry for the third morning running after the wet, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling put his thumb in the seam and showed there was still air in it and said nothing at all.

The sixteenth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**One thousand and forty-three hundredweight.**

The launder carries the two hundred and eighty-sixth figure of a run in which no number has ever been written down twice, and it is eight on Saturday with nothing set against it. The window behind it stands at a hundred and thirty-two mornings one way and a hundred and twenty-one the other, and the two of them are two hundred and fifty-three, and nobody in this room has ever set one end of that window against the number printed over the other.

The four bodies of households are at two hundred and eighty-four days on this morning's sheet, and the clause under that takes the two hundred and eighty-third of those mornings out of two hundred and eighty-fifth. One under and one over. A morning is not a household and a morning is not a man, and a man who is not in a room at nine miles off this morning did not move any of the three.

Near board of the long wall, six hundred and seventy days, and the far one seven hundred and seventeen, and the gap between them the forty-seven it has always been. Nobody put a hand on the drawer behind the near one at any hour of this morning.

Both men who read the boards were at the outlet end with a bar and a stone, and the second book carries nothing this morning where the run of mornings goes, and the reason is on this page: half of the run is half a number on an even morning and not a whole one. The frame of that run is of a month seven months back.

At about the fourth hour this holding read the well on the shelf.

It is four hundred miles and four days off and it is entered as a day and not as a figure, and there is no ordinal against it and no guess against it and no figure of any kind supplied with it, and the far end of it is nine hundred yards behind a wall in a building this holding has never been inside.

That is the thirteenth time this holding has read it, and the count of day-and-no-figure entries against that well is twelve before this morning and thirteen after it, and the number moved on this page and on no other page, and no question has been put to anybody at that far end in eleven years about what the reading is or what it is a reading of.

The clerk of this holding wrote it into the fourth book as a day and as nothing else, in the ordinary place, and he did not write a figure against it and he did not write a word against it.

The well house door is nine hundred yards from this building and there are seven columns cut into the stone of it with chalk in four of them and nothing at the head of any of the three that are blank, and the seventh of those columns is not read this week and the rule for reading it is the first of a month and the thirtieth and this morning is the sixteenth. Nobody walked those nine hundred yards to read it and nobody wrote a figure against it anywhere, and the next mornings it is read are days 750 and 751, which are the thirtieth and the first, and this building did not choose those either.

**"I have been thinking about that well all week and I want to say what about it before somebody else says it for me."** The man of about fifty shut the third book on the long table before he spoke, gave the reason first, and printed no figure for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his. **"Something came back in the ground about a month back and it came back in a place, and everybody in this county now wants to know what it wants."** He put his thumb on the shut book and said the rest of it in one breath: **"The way you find out what a thing wants is you read it. I have been in a room in a building nine miles off all week and there is a wall in it with a column headed for the things that building does not know, and six rows ruled in it, and no unit against any of the six."**

Nothing in the room answered him and the room went on with what it was doing.

**"I want to say that reading something and doing something about it are two different kinds of work, and that a reading is not a maintenance act. If a person reads a thing and then goes and does the ordinary morning's work it does not mean the reading and the work were the same thing."** He said it to the third book and not to the wall. Nobody in that room said he had been slow and nobody said he was right.

**"I wanted that sentence all week and could not get it, and I have got one now."**

Nia Vale came in at about the seventh hour with her own book shut and did not open it, and she said one thing about the reading that nobody had asked her for.

**"There were four of them out on the reserves yesterday and I did not go with them and I have been told since that all four of them asked the same question and that I was not there."** She said it to the table and not to anybody. **"The question was what a person is supposed to do when something comes up that was not put there."** She said it to the second book and did not open her own. **"I am going to tell you the answer I would have given and then I am going to say that I have not given it yet, because I have been three days thinking about it and I have not found one that is true, and I would rather say that in a room than say a thing on a Wednesday that I have not stood behind by the end of the week."** Nobody in that room asked her what the answer was going to be and nobody said she was being slow.

At about the seventh hour Sera Quill came down the fen road and went straight into the one room, and stood at the long wall with her hand not touching it. She read the shutter line from where she was standing and did not go up to the window and did not touch anything.

**"There is a thing I could say about a reading and I am not going to say it."** She said it to the wall and not to the room. **"I have been the person who says the thing that makes a room feel better for forty years and it turns out I have never once been in a room where a reading was not a document and a document was not a promise."** She said that to the wall and not to the shutter line above her. **"There is a well nine hundred yards from a wall in this county that gets read on a fixed day and nobody has ever been asked what it is a reading of in eleven years, and I have just stood here and worked out that I would have asked."** Nobody in that room asked her what she would have asked and she went down to the second bus at the ninth hour and this holding has no copy of her county book and did not ask for one.

From about the fifth hour Marek was at the long side of the table with nothing in front of him, and he did not go out to the ring and he did not go out of the gate, and the count of questions this holding has put to him this month is nil and is nil.

Harlan Vetch was at the gate at about the eighth hour and he came in and stood at the end of the long table and did not sit down, and nobody asked him to sit down, and after about a minute he said one thing and then he did not say anything else for the rest of the morning.

**"You have all been talking about a thing that came up in the ground and not one of you has said where."** He said it to the table and not to anybody. **"I have held an end of a thing for four years and I know that where a thing comes up is the whole of what it is, and I have not asked, and I am not asking, and I have noticed that four of you have asked each other all week and not one of you has asked me."** Nobody in that room said he was right and nobody said he was wrong and nobody asked him.

Three councils have given Tova Reed a sowing date in writing and the fourth wrote on the twenty-eighth and the letter said nothing at all. She is not in this building and she is on the seed front in four counties, and what a released pattern does to a sowing date is the question she is living inside this month, and nobody in here has offered to take any of it off her and nobody thanked her and nobody put a hand on her arm.

The eight places that did not come out of the standing willow are still not on any page in this holding and are not going to be guessed at this week, and a reading is not a guess and a count of sacks is not a count of patterns and a count of patterns is not a count of days.

Two people hold the far end of a thing and this body is not one of them, and there is no instrument anywhere in four counties that says whether it is on or off, and nobody in this holding has ever said it is on. About nine hundred yards of rail is what is in that building and a rail is not that thing, and neither is a wheel behind it, and neither is a lever under one of three, and nobody in this yard has put the three of them together this morning.

Through the middle of the day he was out on the low field gate with a bar, and he came in with his coat wet at the shoulders. The four ruled lines under his condition are bare, the answer is not due, and nobody has asked him to hold an end of anything.

The woman who keeps the seed house stood in the seed house door from the second hour with the schedule under her arm and the six ruled rows on it and did not come out, and the oilskin satchel was on the long table under the four pound weight, and a sheet that goes nine miles and comes back in a satchel is the same sheet and not a column. She said one thing to the man of about fifty on his way past and she said it to the satchel and not to him. **"You have a column on your wall with six rows in it and no unit against any of them, and you have been telling people all week that a reading is not a maintenance act."**

The man of about fifty put his hand on the edge of the table and did not turn round.

**"Somebody is going to read your six rows and put your reading in the seventh and there is no seventh ruled, and that is not me being difficult, that is a row not being there."** He said that was a fair thing to have said and did not say anything else, and she went back inside and shut the door to a hand's width.

Soren Rill came in at about the ninth hour off the four-mile road and said one thing at the gate that nobody asked him for, and he gave no reason for it in front of it because he was tired and the count of reasons given first in this holding's history did not move on account of it. **"I have been in this building four days this month and every morning I have come back and been asked by somebody with a piece of paper what I think should happen, and this morning nobody has asked me that and I noticed the difference about halfway up the road and I have been thinking about it since."** Nobody at that gate asked him what the difference was and he said he did not know and went in.

The two sheets about the two declinings were on the long table about nine inches apart, where they have been for a month, and a reading of a well on a shelf did not move either of them or put them closer together. The third column of the sheet of terms on the long wall is ruled and empty and its heading has not been improved, and a sixth term is not going to be written this month by anybody.

The drawer behind the near board stayed shut. The order sealing the route is in the drawer under the two books and it is valid and being valid it opens nothing whatever, and no apology is going to be written for it by anybody in this holding and none has been written this month.

There is nothing written on the paper on the low board, and no date on it, and no line anywhere on it for a name, and it is lying face up. Nobody in this holding has ever been told to take it off and nobody is going to be told, and a reading on the sixteenth of a month is not a reason for taking anything off that board.

Four inches, and it branches twice, and there is no second mark on that arm anywhere. What this holding knows about it is four accounts and the four do not reconcile with one another in any particular. He kept his sleeve down from the gate to the gate, nobody asked him once what is under it, and he did not offer it, and a morning on which a well was read did not make a fifth.

Twenty-five is what the compost line stands at, and paid is the word in front of it, and it has not moved. A hundred and eighty days is how long the register form has been on the middle table, and nothing has been written at the head of its column and nobody has filled it and nobody has refused it and it will not be read out a fifth time. The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and seventy-four days old with nothing on it.

Seven is what this holding's book says it has, and nobody put an eighth in this morning, because the rehearsal that is coming is not a session and will not be entered as one, and a thing that is not entered did not happen whatever anybody says about it afterwards. In the corner of the one room the four ruled lines under the two words are bare and the answer they are waiting for is not due. Fifty-three requests and fifty-three section-nine notes, and the six not knowns are six with no unit against any of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and a reading is not a seventh and a day is not a figure.

A reading is not a seventh, a day is not a figure, and a well on a shelf nine hundred yards off is not a seventh of anything. The six things this holding does not know are six, with no unit against any of them, and the seventh row is not ruled.

The wind was hard off the west all morning and the hedges on the four-mile road had stopped dripping by the eighth hour, and there was no rain on it at all. The middle road neither took nor gave, the north end of the ditch is running full off the first line, and the trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip.

The clerk of this holding wrote the sixteenth in at the ninth hour, read it back once and did not read it back twice, and nobody in this yard thanked anybody for any part of the morning. The well on the shelf went into the fourth book as a day and as nothing else. A reading is not a maintenance act, this holding said that once this morning, and is not going to say it a second time.
