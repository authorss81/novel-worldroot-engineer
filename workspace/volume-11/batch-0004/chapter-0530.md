# Chapter 530

## Paid At Twenty-Six, And A Place Instead Of A Sum

The first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the water: the north end of the ditch, the trough at the head of the four-mile road, and the standpipe in the yard. The ditch was two inches below its own lip at the second hour, which is the lowest it has been this month, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling walked it the whole length with a bar and said nothing about the two inches.

The compost line fell this morning.

Paid at twenty-six, and not discharged in twenty-six, and the word goes in in front of the figure where it has gone in for eleven years. It has been paid at twenty-five for four months and it fell on the twenty-six mornings' interval carried forward from a morning in the middle of a month now five months back, and no page in this holding anticipated it and no book in this building carries a column for it.

The twenty-third went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**One thousand and forty-seven hundredweight.**

Sunday gave five back off Saturday and the fall went onto the falls, and what stands on the launder is the two hundred and ninety-third figure of a run in which no number has ever been written down twice. The window behind it came out at a hundred and thirty-five mornings against a hundred and twenty-five, and those two are two hundred and sixty, and the man of about fifty read both halves and did not say either of them. The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden wrote both of them in her own book and put her thumb on the page when she had done it.

Two hundred and ninety-one days is the last figure this run of mornings puts against the four bodies of households, and the clause beneath it takes the two hundred and ninetieth of those mornings out of two hundred and ninety-second, one under and one over at every site. The number on the compost line did not move any of those three and was never going to.

**Six hundred and seventy-seven days** on the near board of the long wall and **seven hundred and twenty-four** on the far one, forty-seven apart as they have been for four years, and the drawer behind the near one stood shut at every hour of this morning and nobody put a thumb on it.

The second book took **one hundred and seventeen mornings of two hundred and seventeen**, of a month seven months back, at the ninth hour, and both men who read the boards said it to each other at the seventh hour and one of them said the other man's half back to him for the first time in a month, and nobody in that room noticed.

At about the eighth hour Sera Quill came down the fen road, went into the seed house, came out again with the county book under her arm, and stood in the doorway.

**"There is a second payment."** She said it to the door frame and not to anybody. **"Not a sum. A place. Four households in a county four counties off have been given a place in a distribution that did not have a name on it in a month now two months back, and the place has a road to it and a household in it and a field behind the household, and nobody has to be in a room to know whether it is theirs."** She shifted the book under her arm and did not go in.

**"I did that."** She said it to the door frame. **"I asked for it, in a room, in the ordinary way, and the sum went out first and this came second."** She said that to the county book. **"I do not know which of the two is worth more, and I have spent four months being asked what a sum is worth by people who wanted a figure they could put on a shelf."** Nobody in that doorway said the second payment was bigger and nobody said it was smaller.

What she said next is not in the same sentence as the first of those two things and is not going to be this month, and this holding is not going to put a payment and an authority into one line again.

**"I have no office. I have had no office since about the fourth of this month and I did not lose it in a way that anybody had to be told about, and the people who signed the sheet I read out in that room are not going to come and find me about anything because I am not the person they signed it to."** She said it to the same door frame. **"I am not saying that as a complaint and I am not saying it as a thing that has been done to me."** She turned the book over under her arm and did not look at the door frame while she said the rest of it. **"I am saying it because a person in four counties has asked me this week whether the second payment means I am back in charge of something, and I want the answer to be the same in a room as it is in a county book."**

The yard was quiet and somebody at the far end of it shut a gate.

**"The answer is that a place with a road to it is not an office, and I have not got one and I am not going to be given one back by anybody."** Nobody in that doorway thanked her and nobody in this yard has thanked her for anything this month.

**"A list of eighty-one families is not a list of people who trust me."** She said that at the tap on her way out and not to anybody. **"It is a list of people who were written down, and a person who has been written down has a great deal to lose if the writing turns out to be wrong, and I know that because I have written people down for forty years."** She said that to the tap and then said the rest of it to the road. **"A record that keeps a person's name on it is a weight and not a comfort, and I have spent four months learning that the hard way from the other side of the table."** She got on the second bus at the ninth hour and this holding has no copy of the county book and did not ask for one.

He came in wet to the elbow about the seventh hour and put one thing on the long table, reason first, no figure printed for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his.

**"The compost line went last night and nobody in this building told me it was going to and I want to say that I would not have believed anybody who had."** He said it to the shut third book. **"It is on an interval and the interval is not a plan, and a number that moves on a day that nobody chose is a different sort of thing from a number that moves because a room decided something."**

He turned the page of the third book and looked at what was under it and shut it again.

**"I have written both kinds down in this book for four years and this morning the first kind happened."** He said that to the shut book. **"I have gone and looked at it before the ninth hour and I have not been told it was coming and nobody is going to be."** Nobody at that table said that was a good way to find out and nobody thanked him.

**"I am going to say the thing about it that I have been not saying since Tuesday and then I am going to stop."** He said that at about the eleventh hour to the shut book. **"On Tuesday two men went up a bank and a number went up by one and I have spent four days being proud of the bank and suspicious of the number, and this morning a number went up by one and nothing at all happened, and I do not know which of those is the thing to be proud of. A figure that moves because a morning came round is not anybody's work."** He put both hands flat on the closed book and left them there for a second before he said the other half of it. **"A bank with a barrow on it is somebody's work."** He said that to the shut book too. **"I have been in this building four years writing the first kind down and I have got about nine hours a week for the second kind, and the rota says that is how it is meant to be, and I am not going to say that is right this morning."** Nobody at that table said it was wrong and nobody said it was right and nobody thanked him and he went out to the ditch and worked it for about an hour.

He had the barrow out by the sixth hour and it never went on the four-mile road at all this morning; it stood at the gate with its handles up, and nobody in this holding has asked him why and nobody is going to.

**"I was asked yesterday whether I would be the man for a wheel and I said I did not know."** He said it at the gate and gave the reason for it before he said it. **"I said I did not know because it is not the same question as whether I would hold a frame for nine minutes on a Monday."** He turned the barrow about and set the handles up against the gate again. **"I have been in this yard eight days and I have watched about four people answer the second question when they were asked the first one, and I am not going to be the fourth."** Nobody in that yard asked him which of the two he had answered and he did not say and he was not asked.

Marek was at the long side of the middle table from about the fifth hour with nothing in front of him and he did not go out to the ring and he did not go out of the gate, and the count of questions this holding has put to him this month is nil and is nil. He has not been asked how he slept this week and nobody is going to.

**"There is no date."** He said that at about the ninth hour to the table and he gave the reason for it before he said it, and he printed no figure for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his. **"There is a rule that says two, a wheel, a lever, about nine hours, three of them, and one of them has been chosen in a room I was not in, and there is a man who was not chosen to hold the other end of it, and I have about nine minutes in my head that will not come out, and that is the whole of what this holding has as of this morning."** He put his hand flat on the table and left it there.

**"There is no morning on any of it."** He said that to the second book and not to the window. **"A compost line fell last night on an interval nobody chose and it is the only number in this building that moved this morning for a reason that was not a room full of people deciding something, and it moved because twenty-six mornings went by, and I would like to be able to say that I have noticed that more than I have noticed the other thing."** Nobody in that room said he was right and nobody thanked him.

By the ninth hour he was inside again, coat wet at the shoulders, bar back where it lives. The four ruled lines under his condition are bare, the answer is not due, and nobody in this holding has asked him to hold an end of anything this month.

The compost line is paid at twenty-six and not discharged in twenty-six, the word in front of the figure, on the twenty-six mornings' interval carried forward from a morning in a month now five months back. The register form is a hundred and eighty-seven days old on the middle table and is not filled and is not refused and is not going to be read out a fifth time. The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and eighty-one days old and has nothing on it.

Seven is what the book in this holding says and nobody entered an eighth this morning, and the nine minutes at a hand-lever nine miles off is not a session and was not entered as one and is not going to be.

The answer they are waiting for is not due, and the four ruled lines under the two words in the corner of the one room are bare. Fifty-three requests and fifty-three section-nine notes. Six things this holding does not know are ruled with no unit against any of them and the seventh row is not ruled, and a rehearsal is not a seventh and a reading is not a seventh and a wheel is not a seventh.

Fifteen lines are in the use log and no sixteenth went in this morning. The barrow went up the four-mile road at the sixth hour with three sacks and came down loaded, and its journeys are eleven and eleven is a floor.

Two people hold the far end of a thing and neither of them is him, and there is no instrument anywhere in four counties that says whether it is on or off, and nobody in this holding has ever said it is on. A wheel behind about nine hundred yards of rail is not that thing, and a hand-lever under one of three wheels is not that thing, and nobody in this yard has put any of them together this morning.

Three hours to the sixth had the north wall, and when he came in he stood under the eaves with his coat still on and said one number out loud to nobody. It was the right one, and nobody in this yard asked him how he knew it and nobody is going to.

Two days of rain had left the ground soft by the ninth hour and the middle road had taken a little and given back a little before the light. The north end of the ditch is running full, and the trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip.

Nobody thanked anybody. The clerk of this holding wrote the twenty-third in at the ninth hour, read it back once, and did not read it back twice, and the compost line is paid at twenty-six and not discharged in twenty-six, and a figure moved this morning because a word went in front of it and a morning went by, and this holding has a wheel, a lever, a rule that says two, about nine hours, three of them, and no date and no plan and nobody to ask.

> Entered by the clerk of this holding at the first hour, in his own hand, as one unbroken line: **The twenty-third. The first line of the rota worked, the north end of the ditch and the trough and the standpipe. The compost line fell to twenty-six not discharged, paid, on the twenty-six mornings' interval. The pair read at one hundred and seventeen of two hundred and seventeen, of a month seven months back. The same day in and the same day out. One thousand and forty-seven hundredweight to the launder, being the two hundred and ninety-third. No session entered and no figure printed for anything that was not entered. No date against any of it.** Nothing of this is a forecast and nothing of it is a cause and nothing of it is a total.
