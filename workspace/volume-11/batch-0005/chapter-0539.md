# Chapter 539

## A Letter On The Long Table, And Five People In At The Second Hour

The letter came up the fen road with the second bus and it was handed in at the gate at about the eighth hour of the morning by a man from a village whose name nobody in this building had to write down twice, because it is on the sheet he came in with and it is the same sheet a letter about a district of that council came on four days ago.

It is from a council of a county four counties off. It is about the schedule, and what it asks for in writing is that the schedule be restored, and it says in three lines why and then asks for a reply and gives no date for the reply and asks for none.

The clerk of this holding read it standing up and then carried it to the long table and put it down on the long table with the two sheets about the two declinings, face up, and he did not rule a line for it and he did not put it in the requests column and he did not put it in the section-nine notes, and both of those are fifty-three and stay fifty-three.

**"There is no column in these four books with a heading on it for a letter that asks a building nine miles off to put its sheet back the way it was."** He said that to the letter and not to anybody. **"I am not going to rule one because a letter came in, and I am not going to put it in the requests column either, because a request that has been entered can be asked about in four counties and this one cannot be answered from this table by anybody in this holding."**

He put his hand flat on it and left it there for a second.

**"That is not a refusal and I am not going to let anybody in that county call it one. There is a piece of paper on this table and nobody has answered it and nobody has been asked to and I have written down the hour it came in and nothing else about it."**

Nobody at that table asked him what the answer would be and nobody in this building has been sent one and this page is not going to say what it ought to be.

Six hundred and eighty-six days on the near board of the long wall and seven hundred and thirty-three on the far one, forty-seven apart, and the drawer behind the near one stood shut at every hour. The man who reads the far side said both halves of the window out loud this morning and the other man said the first man's half back to him.

The thirty-second went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**One thousand and sixty-seven hundredweight.**

Tuesday came up eight on Monday, and what stands against the launder is the three hundred and second figure of a run in which nothing has never been entered twice. The window came out at a hundred and forty mornings one way against a hundred and twenty-nine the other, and those two are two hundred and sixty-nine.

Three hundred days is what the sheet gives against the four bodies of households this morning, and the clause beneath takes the two hundred and ninety-ninth of those mornings out of three hundred and first. One under and one over. A letter asking for a sheet to be put back does not change a figure of a body of households, and the four bodies of households have not been asked to give up one of their two signatures and neither of them has given one up.

The second book carries nothing this morning where the run of mornings goes, and the clerk left the space empty and this page carries the reason and no figure. Half of the run is half a number on this morning and not a whole one.

Five people came into this yard at about the second hour from a building about nine miles off, on foot, in the dark, and they came in through the first gate and not the second one, and there were lamps on two of them and nobody in that yard had arranged the lamps.

Harlan Vetch was first through the gate and the man of about fifty was behind him and Soren Rill and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden came in together, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was last with a folded sheet under his arm. Nobody thanked them and nobody asked them anything at the gate, and the clerk of this holding looked up from the second book once and went back to it.

The second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the work was the outlet end and the two stones at the head of it and the pack under the near stone, which came out wet and went back dry by the seventh hour. The trough at the head of the four-mile road was full by the seventh hour and stayed inside its own lip.

**"There is a letter on your long table and it is the first one in four years that anybody has sent me asking for something that would undo a thing."** The man of about fifty said it at about the ninth hour to the shut third book and he gave the reason for it before he gave the thing, and he printed no figure for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his. **"I have had four letters in a fortnight about a figure of draw and I have entered all four. This one is not a figure of draw and it is not going in either of those columns, and I want it on the page that I know the difference and that I am not the man who decides which of the two a letter is."**

He turned the book over and did not open it again.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden went to the long table and looked at the letter for about a minute and then did not touch it.

**"I have been asked in four counties this month whether I would put a heading on a piece of paper, and I have said no five times."** She said it to the letter and not to the man of about fifty. **"That one is on the floor with a heading on its own paper and it is a proper letter from a body that has a heading, and the answer to it is not mine to write and it is not yours either, and it is not going to be written this morning by anybody in this yard."** Nobody in that yard said that was right and nobody said it was wrong.

Tova Reed came in from the seed house at about the ninth hour and put the four counties' sheets down on the long table on the other side of the letter from the two sheets about the declinings, and the letter was not moved and the four sheets were not moved, and about nine inches separated them and it has been about nine inches between two other sheets for a month.

**"I have a council of about nineteen households that wrote on the twenty-eighth and said nothing and I have carried that letter folded since then, and this morning another council in a county four counties off has asked for the opposite of what I have spent this month not asking them for."** She said that to the table and gave the reason before the thing. **"I am not going to open either letter in a room in this county this week, and I am not going to say anything about that to either of them, because the moment I do that it becomes a position and I have got four counties to be in in six weeks and I have three dates in my hand."**

She squared her four sheets against the edge of the table.

**"Nobody has asked any household in four counties to stop drawing on that building and I have not told one to, and that has not changed this week and it is not going to change this month."** Nobody at that table said she was wrong and nobody thanked her and nobody put a hand on her arm.

Nia Vale came in at about the sixth hour and put her own book on the table and left it shut.

**"There are places above that road doing what they are doing and there is a letter on your table asking for a sheet to go back how it was, and I have got no advice about how those two facts sit next to each other."** She said that to her own book and did not open it. **"I am not going to tell a district what to think about either of them this week and I am not going to give anybody a number, and I am going back out to the boundary in an hour because that is what I do on a Monday."**

Sera Quill came down the fen road at about the seventh hour and read the letter standing up and then went into the seed house and did not come out of it again.

**"A letter that asks for a thing to be put back and gives no date for an answer is the most useful kind of letter anybody has sent me in four months."** She said it to the door frame. **"It has a date on it and a heading and the hand of a clerk who has had it read out to him, and in about two years there is going to be an argument about whether anybody answered it, and there is going to be a column somewhere that says no, and that column is going to be the most honest thing in four counties."** Nobody in that doorway asked her to write anything and she did not write anything.

Marek was in the yard from about the fifth hour and did not go out of the gate at any hour of this morning, and about four people in this county have noticed that and about four have not and neither of them has ever said so to anybody.

**"There is a thing I am not going to do with this morning and I want it written down that I did not do it."** He said that to the middle road and he gave the reason before he gave the thing, and he printed no figure for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his. **"The comfort is not standing. The father is not alive in the wood. The day does not come back. That is three lines and they were true last week and they are true this week, and there is a letter on that table that asks for a sheet to go back to what it was, and the two of those are on the same table this morning and neither of them cancels the other."**

Nobody in that yard asked him about the three lines and nobody thanked him.

**"She is nine miles off and she is the person who answers for what she did, and nobody in this holding has been asked to say a word about her this week and I have not said one."** He put his sleeve back down over his forearm. **"The far end of a thing is held by two people and neither of them is me and there is no instrument anywhere in four counties that says whether it is on or off, and none of the four sentences I have just said has anything to do with that, and I am not going to put them together this morning."**

The low field above the middle road was still doing what it has done since Friday, and the water in it had gone down about a hand's width by the seventh hour and come back by the ninth, and nobody put a name on it this morning either.

Behind the fourth book there is a foot and a half of day sheets with no date at the head of any of them. They are in the order it was used. Nobody has moved one of them to the top and nobody has ever asked about them in this holding.

Paid goes in front of that figure and the figure is twenty-six, not discharged in twenty-six, and it does not fall again this month.

The register form is on the middle table at a hundred and ninety-six days with nothing at the head of its column. It is not filled. It is not refused. Nobody has read it out a fifth time and nobody is going to. The charter was signed a hundred and two days ago in a room nine miles off and three of the four bodies of households that signed it are still signed to a machine for stored light, and neither of those two documents has an opinion about the other one. The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and ninety days old and has nothing on it and nobody has offered him a pen for it.

Seven is what this holding's book says it has and it is still seven this morning, and the rehearsal and the wheel and the shut door are not sessions and none of them was entered as one. Fifty-three requests and fifty-three section-nine notes. Six rows are ruled for the things this holding does not know, with no unit against any of them and no seventh row.

A man with a barrow took three sacks up the four-mile road at the sixth hour and brought them down again, and that is where its journeys stand, at eleven, and eleven is a floor. There are fifteen lines in the use log and there is no sixteenth. About nine hundred yards of rail is in a building nine miles off and it is a distance and not a fact about anybody.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres was not walked this month and it was not measured and it was not crossed and it was not priced and nobody in this holding has ever been inside it, and the low field above the middle road is not that ring and the two have not been added to one another on any page in this building.

The four ruled lines under the two words in the corner of the one room are bare, and the answer they are for is not due, and nothing that anybody has said in this month has put a word on any of them. The third column of the sheet of terms is ruled and empty and its heading has not been improved and no sixth term has been written. Nobody climbed a rung and this building has made no offer to anybody at any hour of this month.

On the low board, where it has lain since a month now four months back, is a sheet with nothing written on it, no date on it and no line anywhere on it that a name could go into. It is not filled and it is not withdrawn and it has not been dated and it has not been moved, and nobody in this holding has ever been told to take it off that board.

Neither took nor gave anything back: the middle road. No frost in the ground either, and the water in the ditch running full, and the trough at the head of the four-mile road full by seven and inside its own lip.

The five of them who had come nine miles were still standing in this yard at the ninth hour with their lamps out, and the register form was on the middle table in front of all of them at a hundred and ninety-six days with nothing at the head of its column and nothing in it. Nobody thanked anybody, and the clerk of this holding wrote the thirty-second in at the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice.

> THE LETTER AS IT STANDS ON THE LONG TABLE IN THIS HOLDING, ENTERED BY THE CLERK OF THIS HOLDING IN HIS OWN HAND AT THE NINTH HOUR WITH THE HOUR IT CAME IN AND NOTHING ELSE: **FROM A COUNCIL OF A COUNTY FOUR COUNTIES OFF, ON ITS OWN HEADED PAPER, OF ITS OWN DATE, ASKING IN WRITING THAT THE SCHEDULE BE RESTORED.** THIS SHEET IS NOT THE SHEET THAT CLAIMS THE CORRIDOR IN THE PAN AND IT IS NOT THE PAPER LYING FACE UP ON THE LOW BOARD, AND IT IS NOT IN THE REQUESTS COLUMN AND IT IS NOT IN THE SECTION-NINE NOTES, AND NO COLUMN HAS BEEN RULED FOR IT IN ANY OF THE FOUR BOOKS IN THIS HOLDING. IT HAS BEEN ENTERED AND IT HAS NOT BEEN ANSWERED. THIS IS A FACT OF WHAT IS ON THE TABLE THIS MORNING AND IT IS NOT A FORECAST AND IT IS NOT A CAUSE AND IT IS NOT A TOTAL.
