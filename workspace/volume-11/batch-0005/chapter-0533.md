# Chapter 533

## Three Places, And Not The Fourth, And Nobody Can Say Which

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden saw the first one at about the third hour from the middle road on her way to the seed house, and she stood in the middle of the road with the bucket in her hand for a while and then went and got the man of about fifty out of the one room.

**"Come and look at it and bring the book and do not write anything down while you are walking."** She said it at the door and she did not explain it. **"I want you to see it before you have a pen in your hand, because I have watched people in this county write a thing down first and see it second this month, and every one of them was wrong about what they had seen."**

He came out with the book shut under his arm and they walked four hundred yards up the middle road to a low place on the north side of it, and he stood at the edge of it and did not go in.

**"That ground has not done that since I was a boy and I do not know the word for what it is doing."** He said it to the grass and not to her. **"I want to say two things about it and then I am going to stop talking about it. The first is that I have got four accounts in four books of what came up in this county a month back and I could not get one of them to agree with another, and this is a fifth thing and it is on different ground and it is not the same as any of the four."**

He shifted the book to his other arm.

**"The second is that a pattern which comes back is not a harvest and I am going to keep saying that until somebody in this county gives me a reason to stop."**

Nobody argued with him about it and nobody asked him for a reason.

The fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour and went past unworked, the second time in eight mornings, and there was nobody at the headland above Marden at any hour of it. The standing is a hundred and thirteen mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and eighty-four not, and twenty-nine and eighty-four come to a hundred and thirteen.

The twenty-nine taken is a figure that has not moved on any morning this year. It did not move on the eighteenth when the line was worked, it did not move on the twenty-second when the line went past unworked, and nobody in this holding is blamed for a field that waited four years and nobody was thanked on the eighteenth for a field that did not.

On the count kept in this room the twenty-sixth of this month is the sixty-first morning of the rotation. On the other count the same morning is the forty-eighth. A page carrying one of those two would be carrying half a fact, and there has been thirteen in that gap for four years and nobody in this building has ever found out what the thirteen counts.

The twenty-sixth went in at about the ninth hour and came back out of the other book, and the two of them were the same.

**One thousand and fifty-eight hundredweight.**

Wednesday came up eight on Tuesday with nothing set against it, and the number standing against the launder this morning is the two hundred and ninety-sixth of a run in which nothing has ever been entered twice. The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden had the window off the wall herself and read both halves of it out loud to nobody, a hundred and thirty-seven mornings one way and a hundred and twenty-six the other, which come to two hundred and sixty-three.

Two hundred and ninety-four days is what the sheet gives against the four bodies of households, and the clause beneath it takes the two hundred and ninety-third of those mornings out of two hundred and ninety-fifth. One under and one over. Three places in this county doing a thing nobody has a name for did not move a figure of a body of households this morning.

Near board, six hundred and eighty days; far board, seven hundred and twenty-seven; forty-seven between them as always. The clerk of this holding shut the drawer behind the near board at every hour of this morning and nobody put a thumb on it.

The clerk left the place in the second book empty this morning the way he leaves it every second morning, and the reason is on this page. Half of the run is half a number on an even morning and not a whole one, so there is no figure of it to enter, and the frame it has carried since the first of this month is of a month seven months back.

There were three places by the fourth hour and by the seventh hour there were still three.

One was the low place on the north side of the middle road above the trough. One was at the head of the four-mile road where the ditch takes the water off the high field, and a man with a barrow who goes up there every morning of the week had to move his barrow to get past it. The third was on the far side of the low field gate and it was the one everybody walked to.

There was no fourth one and there was no figure anywhere in this county of how many places there ought to be. Four places in this county are low enough to hold water from the north side and nobody in this building has ever walked the whole of that and nobody is going to this month.

Soren Rill was the second person to see the one at the head of the four-mile road and he had it by about the sixth hour with a bar and a stone and nothing else.

**"I have been at the head of that road four days running for the ditch and it has been the same ditch all four mornings."** He said it at the trough. **"This morning the ground at the second bend has come up about a hand's width and there is water standing in it that was not standing in it on Tuesday, and I have got no word for it that a man of my age in this county would use."**

He looked at the water for a while without touching it.

**"I am not going to say how many there will be and I am not going to say where the fourth one is. I have been asked four times in nine years where the next one will be and I have been wrong four times, and I would rather be asked a fifth time and be wrong than tell a room something this morning that turns out to be a place in a book."**

Nia Vale was on the road at about the eighth hour with the moving reserves and came in with the list shut and put it on the long table face down.

**"Three people in three districts told me before the light and I did not ask any of them how many and none of them offered it."** She put her hand flat on the closed book. **"The fourth council's district has not said anything to me and that is not because they do not know, because I would have heard it by now, and I am not going to go and find out why."**

She turned the book over without opening it.

**"What came back in those three places is not a harvest. It is a thing in three places. Nobody in four counties is going to eat off three places and anybody who says that this morning is going to be very good in about six weeks and very quiet by the middle of the next season."** Nobody in that room asked her what it was worth and she did not say and she was not asked.

Tova Reed came in from the seed house at about the ninth hour with the fourth sheet under her arm and stood at the middle table for a while without sitting down.

**"I have had two councils ask me about it by the middle of the day and one of them asked me whether it changes the sowing date."** She said it to the table and she gave the reason for it before she said the thing. **"It does not change the sowing date and I have told that council so in writing and I am not going to be talked out of it by a third thing coming up. A pattern that came back is not a sowing date and a sowing date is not a pattern that came back, and the two of them are about different lengths of time and they are kept in different books."**

She put the fourth sheet down on top of the three that carried a date and squared all four.

**"The fourth council wrote on the twenty-eighth and the letter said nothing and it is still folded. Three of these four have a date in my hand on them. I am not going to rule a line for the fourth and I am not going to mention a wheel to any of the three before the sowing."** Nobody at that table thanked her and nobody said she was wrong and nobody put a hand on her arm.

The man of about fifty wrote one page in the third book between the fourth hour and the ninth hour and he wrote down three places and the times he had seen them and he wrote down that he had been shown one of them before he had the book open.

**"I have written what I saw and I have not written what it means and I have not written how many there should be."** He said that to the shut book and he gave the reason for it first and printed no figure for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his. **"There is a reading of a standing willow four books and a counting of pattern-holders in two books, and there are two men who can hold a pattern for four and one who can hold it for five, and those figures have not moved this morning and did not move on Sunday and are not going to move because three places came up."**

He turned the book over and did not open it again.

**"A pattern is not a pattern-holder. Two men on a wheel yesterday were on neither of those two lists and neither list has got a line for yesterday on it, and I am not going to rule one."**

Marek was in the one room from the fifth hour with the door open and nothing in front of him, and he went out of the gate for about an hour and came back in and did not say anything for about twenty minutes.

**"Nine minutes is still in my head."** He said that to the table and he gave the reason for it before he gave the thing, and he printed no figure for it, so that this morning consumed no number of his. **"I have been awake most of the night and both ends of it are still where they were and neither of them has let go, and the two men turned that wheel for nine hours yesterday and that has not done anything about it, and I did not expect it to and I am not going to stand here and be disappointed about it in front of four people."**

He put his sleeve back down over his forearm.

**"There is a thing at the far end of that and two people hold it and neither of them is me, and there is no instrument anywhere in four counties that says whether it is on or off, and nothing that came up in three places this morning has told me anything about it. I felt a frame yesterday. I did not feel that."** Nobody in that room asked him what that was and he did not offer it.

Sera Quill came down the fen road at about the seventh hour and went straight to the low field gate and stood about thirty feet from the third place for about ten minutes without going near it, and then she came back in and went into the seed house.

**"I want the time of it and I want the place written down and I want nothing else written down."** She said it to the door frame. **"Three places in one morning and no fourth one by the seventh hour is not a count and it is not the start of a count, and in four years somebody with a county book will ask me what this was and the answer is going to be that a woman told me there were three and I did not see the fourth and I did not ask."**

She turned round in the doorway and looked back down the middle road.

**"There is a field about four counties off where a thing came back a month back and eight places in this county that did not come out of it are still not written down anywhere and are not going to be this week. I am not asking for them and I am not going to guess at them in a book."**

Nobody in that doorway asked her to and she got on the second bus at the ninth hour.

He had the north wall from the third hour until six. When he came in he said a number out loud to nobody, and it was the right one, and nobody in this yard has ever asked him how he knew it.

The man of the north row with the cough came in off the low field gate at about the seventh hour and went as far as the corner of the one room and stood there and looked down the middle road at the third place for about four minutes without saying anything to anybody. He did not go through the gate and he did not touch anything. The four ruled lines under his condition are bare and the answer is not due, and nobody in this holding has asked him to hold an end of anything and nobody is going to ask him this month.

The headland above Marden was a different color where it had been worked on the eighteenth and it has not gone back, and there was nobody at the top of it this morning either, and the second bend of the four-mile road held about a hand's width of standing water at about the sixth hour that was not there on Tuesday and had gone down by an inch before the light this morning.

Not one figure on that line has moved this month and it is twenty-six. A hundred and ninety days is the age of the register form, and nothing has been written at the head of its column, and it is neither filled nor refused nor going to be read out a fifth time. The second of the four lines in the man of about thirty-one of Silling's own book is a hundred and eighty-four days old and has nothing on it. The charter was signed ninety-six days ago in a room nine miles off.

Seven is what the book says it has, and nobody entered an eighth, and nothing that came up in three places is a session. Fifty-three requests and fifty-three section-nine notes. Six rows are ruled for the things this holding does not know, with no unit against any of them and no seventh row; a place where the ground came up is not a seventh.

About nine hundred yards of rail is in a building nine miles off and it is not that thing. The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is not one of the three places and has not been added to any of them; it was not walked this month, it is not measured, it is not crossed and it is not priced, and there is no dimension against it on any page in this building.

The barrow went up the four-mile road at the sixth hour and had to be moved out of the way at the second bend. Eleven is where its journeys stand, and eleven is a floor. Fifteen lines are in the use log and three places coming up did not make a sixteenth.

The clerk of this holding wrote the twenty-sixth in at the ninth hour and read it back once and did not read it back twice. Three places came up and a fourth did not, and nobody in this yard can say which of those two is a number, because there is only one of them on the page. Nobody thanked anybody.
