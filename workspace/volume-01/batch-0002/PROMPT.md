## Movement 2 — Somebody Else's Hand

Days 12–20. The work of this movement, in the order `outline/volume-01.md` sets for it:

Sera examines the well head, offers the dangerous job, and names its cost: certify the node as maintained **after a real documented repair attempt**, with her own liability on the line. Marek assembles a crew who are not rootwrights — Ilsa Kern, Gael Senn, two tenant farmers — and trains them in safe ordinary practice: log, witness, date, return. They find that the cut branch is not neglected work but **scheduled Crown maintenance carrying a serial from a table the cooperative has never been given**. Kellan offers Crown-funded monitored repair in exchange for exclusive access; the Undercommons sends a courier warning that the same serial appears on branches in three other provinces. **The first real pump seal is built by hand here**, because the compound pad over the wound is failing and the pad's only maintenance was a top-up from a crew that has now left the fen. **The day-18 inspection happens here and the cooperative loses it on a technicality**, which converts the charter from a date into a siege. Nia leaves at the end of this movement, on day 20, with a seed consignment and a hired cart, and the volume does not get her back.

The batch's promise to the reader: a repair built by ordinary people, correctly recorded, that is still not enough. Everything they do is real and none of it is admissible, or it is admissible and the wrong side of the line.

## Hard rules for this batch

- **Still Stage 0 at the end of the batch.** No skin rootmark, no anchor work, no bridge above local conduit, no seedheart, no caretaker link. Marek has two contacts behind him from Batch 0001 and cannot repeat either. Nothing in this batch should give him a third. Sera says so; make her say it in a way that costs him something.
- **The graft is crew work, not Marek's.** `outline/volume-01.md` locks this: the graft in Chapters 21–25 is performed by his crew under Sera's license, and Marek's first independent graft — and his first skin rootmark — is Volume 02. Movement 2 is therefore the *training* half of the work and the *seal*, not the graft.
- **The compound pad is a pad.** Laid by Kellan's crew four seasons ago as a surface hold, topped up annually, never replaced, logged as a proper seal. Day 3 was the last top-up. There is no compound left in the cooperative; it went up the fen road on a cart at eleven o'clock on the night of the tenth. What they build in Chapters 14–17 is the **first real seal**, and it is built by people who have never done it.
- **Nothing romantic.** Tova and Marek are in a written disagreement and a shared plan. Nia's departure is not a subplot about anybody's feelings; it is a list of crates and a hired cart.
- **No Crown Engine, no False Season, no Iona Vey, no Quiet Break, no history lecture.** A serial number, a stamp, a table, a schedule, and a clause. Sera may say she has seen the shape of that table before. She does not say where.
- **The pressure pulse stays undiagnosed.** About one beat in four seconds, arriving from below the sump. Nobody names it, nobody explains it, nobody writes it into a conclusion. It is a clock, not a diagnosis.
- **Every chapter ends on a decision, cost, discovery, or new obligation.** Never cut away mid-action to manufacture suspense.
- **The cards are a plan, not a script.** The day-by-day shape above is mine, not the outline's. If a chapter needs a different shape to be a complete scene, change the shape and keep the card's change, cost, and ending type. Do not pad to reach ten and do not split a complete scene to make one.

## What this batch must deliver

- **Sera's dangerous job**, with her personal liability named out loud, and Marek accepting it with her name ahead of his on the page.
- **A crew** of people who are not rootwrights, trained in the ordinary discipline: log it, witness it, date it, return something. At least one chapter must show a **refusal to sign** something, and the refusal must be correct.
- **The first real seal**, built by hand, and it must be described as work. Fiber, compound, stakes, hours, cold hands, somebody's shoulder. It is not magic and it is not a spell and nobody sees anything.
- **The Crown serial's meaning**, reached by a cooperative that is not supposed to be allowed to ask: a scheduled branch is not drawn on a local map, and a schedule is not neglect. This is the batch's central discovery and it is a bureaucratic one.
- **Refused Crown money.** Kellan or the district offers monitored repair or money. It is refused in front of somebody, and refusing it costs something specific. Do not stage it as another Ch 10 vote.
- **The Undercommons courier**: one courier, one warning, never a rescue, and the warning is that the same serial appears on branches in three other provinces.
- **The day-18 inspection, lost on a technicality.** Not by defiance and not by villainy. The cooperative loses on a rule about who was allowed to be in the pit, or about what counts as a record, or about the date an application was lodged. Whatever the technicality is, it must be something a reasonable reader accepts, it must be the cooperative's own doing in some part, and it must convert the charter from a date into a siege.
- **Nia's departure on day 20**, with the list she asked for in Ch 10, in columns, in the cooperative's own book, with a witness against each line. The volume does not get her back.
