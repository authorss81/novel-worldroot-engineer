## Movement 3 — The Repair That Bites

Days 21 onward. The work of this movement, in the order `outline/volume-01.md` sets for it:

The trained crew's small living graft works. Part of the outer field greens, the pump runs clean, and the cooperative believes the volume is settled. Then the return is measured, and the return is the problem. A graft needs a return; the return available to Lantern Reach is compost; the breeding flock is gone; the bought substitute is a hundredweight a fortnight instead of four hundred a month. So the graft feeds itself by drawing balance out of the branch below — the branch that feeds Fennmere's low quarter. Within a week that quarter's wells go sour, the Directorate bills the cooperative for damage it did not cause, and an adult pathhound settles on the dead flow and holds its route rather than herding through it, so the cooperative can no longer reach the branch to argue about it. Tova refuses a draw that would bring the outer field back at the cost of the seed vault's return, and Marek lets the refusal stand and loses the field he spent three seasons saving. **Marek argues for the bypass and is refused** — no contractor, no license, no money. Sera states the public-proof rule: an unlicensed reading is a rumor with a witness, and the only cure is a second licensed reader and dated records.

The batch's promise to the reader: **it worked, and it was wrong, and the person who said so was right.**

## Hard rules for this batch

- **The graft is crew work, not Marek's.** `outline/volume-01.md` locks this: the graft in Chapters 21–25 is performed by **his crew under Sera's license**. Marek's first independent graft — and his first skin rootmark — is Volume 02. He may plan it, argue for it, hold the boards, and be the one who says the numbers, and he may not make it.
- **The graft is a real small graft and it is licensed.** It needs an anchor, a path, and a return, and all three are named on the page. The anchor is a living thing they choose. The path is a made connection. **The return is the thing that is missing**, and that is the whole of the reversal.
- **The reversal's mechanism is the compost, and it is the compost.** The flock was driven off alive on the tenth and not one animal came back. Ilsa Kern said the link out loud in the flooded pit. Tova's arithmetic is 4 s/cwt and 400 cwt/month and £80 against a treasury of £5 11. Do not spend this connection anywhere else and do not substitute a new mechanism.
- **The adult pathhound is the second animal, and it is not the weir animal.** It **holds a dead flow** rather than herding through a live one, it is adult, and it is a different animal with a different reason. Lissa Vale named the word in Chapter 9 and put its rules on the page: it herds, it does not hunt; it cannot be called off or tied up; killing the thing it stands on does not get rid of it; the only thing that moves it is a change in where the water goes; and it is not commanded. **Do not restage the weir night and do not have the first animal return.**
- **The cooperative has no charter.** It has no right to draw from the head, no right to work on it, and no right to enter anything about it in a district book. The pump runs because nobody has sealed it. Any report from anybody reopens the emergency clause, and **the file does not travel with Inspector Bray.** This is the background condition of the whole movement. It is not a crisis to be resolved in these ten chapters; it is a siege that gets more expensive.
- **The open book is still not a filing.** The claim in the log was moved and lost 9–22 in Chapter 4. Dunmar filed a *cause* on the seventeenth and never a corrected figure. **Do not let a clerk, a hearing officer, or Sera quietly treat the cooperative's books as an accepted correction.** The district still holds Kellan's number in its own filing.
- **The seal is standing and wet at one corner.** It has been proven at 1,100 turns, not at 720. There is a dark thumb's width of seepage at six o'clock, measured and dated, **not a leak**. A finger's width is a leak, and a leak under sacking at full draw goes into the housing and not into the pit. **Do not resolve this before Chapter 41** and do not let it be quietly mended. Gael Senn is the monthly examiner and her entry is worded *nothing found* and not *all well* — use that form.
- **The pressure pulse stays undiagnosed.** About one beat in four seconds, arriving from below the sump, now logged four times and always as *cause not established*. Nobody names it, nobody explains it, nobody writes it into a conclusion. It is a clock, not a diagnosis.
- **The twenty-seventh is a live clock.** A Crown crew with a die is due on a branch the cooperative cannot stop and was never told about. This is Movement 3's first real problem and it should not be resolved by anybody talking anybody out of it.
- **Nothing romantic.** Tova and Marek are in a written disagreement, a shared annex, and a condition she named out loud in a yard. The hard test of this movement is professional: she refuses something and he lets it stand.
- **No Crown Engine, no False Season, no Iona Vey, no Quiet Break, no history lecture.** A serial number, a stamp, a table, a schedule, a form, and a clause. Sera has admitted to a district clerk that she has worked with district schedule forms, and has refused three times by the same two names. If this batch makes her give ground, let it come out of the schedule and never out of the relay. **She has not been asked about Brinewake. Do not ask her here.**
- **Every chapter ends on a decision, cost, discovery, or new obligation.** Never cut away mid-action to manufacture suspense.

Take this movement's decisions at a pit, a bench, a road, a bank, a low-quarter doorstep, and a sub-office counter. A vote is not required and one would be a repetition.
- **Bray is not outsmartable and he is not the villain in the low quarter.** He is a sincere man with a real clause, and the clause works on the people it is written for. He is moved by a document, and in this movement the document that moves him is the cooperative's own honest record, which is why it hurts that it is honest.
- **Renn Tallow's refusal is never softened.** He may appear and he may be helpful. He does not apologize and nobody makes him.
- **Tova is not wrong because she is close to him.** Her refusal has to be arithmetically correct about a specific downstream cost, and the outer rows have to be real ground that three seasons of Marek's work are actually lost.
- **The end of the batch is a reversal, not a cliffhanger.** It is a repair that succeeded, a field that is gone, a bill that exists, and a proof rule that cannot be satisfied this month.
