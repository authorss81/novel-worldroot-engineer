## The batch in one paragraph

Days 1–11 in a fen cooperative whose well charter expires on the eighteenth. Marek, an ordinary irrigation surveyor, proves Kellan's maintenance log is false, is told so by an auditor before he can prove it himself, spoils part of a seed line to get the proof, reads his dead father's fieldbook and gets an impossible maintenance pattern, spends that reading to move an inspection from the tenth to the eighteenth, and loses the breeding flock to a pathhound that night. The batch ends on day 11 with seven days left, no contractor, and an instrument that is no longer his.

## Hard rules for this batch

- **Stage 0 the whole way.** No skin rootmark, no anchor work, no bridge above local conduit, no seedheart, no caretaker link. Marek ends the batch at Stage 0 with one contact behind him. Stage 1 is not sustained until the end of Volume 01.
- **Chapter 3 performs no rootwork at all.** Hand rig, mechanical pump, coupling. What he feels is exhaustion and his hands on wet living rootwood. Make that visible or the reader will assume a power he does not have.
- **Chapter 9 is tools, not a spell.** Crowbar and graft shear prying open a healed seam. The pathhound is redirected by changing the flow and is never commanded. Gael is unhurt.
- **Memory bleed**, not "sensory bleed" — see `state/continuity.md`.
- **Nothing romantic.** Tova and Marek end as professionals with a written disagreement and a shared plan.
- **No Crown Engine, no False Season, no Iona Vey, no Quiet Break, no history lecture.** A serial number, a stamp, a table, and a schedule are the whole of the Crown here. The pattern in the fieldbook is a work order, and nobody in this batch names what that means.
- **Every chapter ends on a decision, cost, discovery, or new obligation.** Never cut away mid-action to manufacture suspense.
- **The cards are a plan, not a script.** If a scene needs a different shape to be complete, change the shape and keep the card's change, cost, and ending type. Do not pad to reach a count and do not split a complete scene.

## Watch items from the outline review

- **POV is Marek in all ten chapters.** Chapter 4 was previously mislabelled "Marek, with Lissa" and is now `POV: Marek`. Lissa is in the room and gets the scene's hardest lines; she is not a second viewpoint.
- **Day 2 is Tova's, not a chapter.** Her two-day look at the records runs days 2–3 and closes on the afternoon of day 3, so Chapter 3 lands inside its final hours. Do not move Chapter 3 later.
- **Chapter 10 votes four things and one refusal.** The bypass is *not* a fifth vote; it is an unfunded plan that enters the book when Dunmar rules on whether a plan is a filing. Keep the count exact and do not stage the bypass as a vote.
- **The compound pad is a pad.** Laid by Kellan's crew four seasons ago as a surface hold, topped up annually, never replaced, logged as a proper seal. Day 3 is the last top-up.
- **Chapters 4 and 10 are both seed-house meetings.** The outline already differentiates them — Ch 4 is a courier in person and a spoiled line to survive, Ch 10 is a letter refused before a clerk and a plan to sign. Keep them that way: Ch 4 is reactive and accusatory, Ch 10 is procedural and slow. Do not let Ch 10 replay Ch 4's confrontation.
