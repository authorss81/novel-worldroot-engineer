## The shape of Volume 01, in one paragraph, for the handoff

A cooperative with no charter, four pounds five in a box, a bed at three and a half degrees, a wound under a piece of sacking that a woman of twenty-three goes down a ladder to look at on the first of every month, and a licensed engineer who has put the hole in the node in her own handwriting, spends one season discovering that the most competent thing anybody in the yard can do is write down what it cannot do. **The bypass nobody could fund is still unbuilt when the water comes and the body breaches its own embankment by hand and floods one man's three acres on purpose to save a seed house, and the man says the six words in daylight and nobody votes.** What is dug in the dark that night is the same hole made bigger and a hundred and forty yards of old channel rodded out, and **there is no new run anywhere in it.** The proof is not a confession. **It is four records on a bench in the mud that cannot all be true — the cooperative's own log, a cheap reed gauge with a brass mark whose reed went under, a miller's chalk figure and the oldest book in the yard — and a reader's finding that what is in the wood is not a memory of a season that has not happened but a work order written in advance by somebody who never comes.** The hearing gives them a charter for a season, adopts their own open book as a condition of it, and reserves the right to reopen the file on any new report. **The volume's promise is paid in food, in a new obligation, and in three acres of a man's own ground that will not crop for two more seasons and that nobody is going to be repaid for.**
