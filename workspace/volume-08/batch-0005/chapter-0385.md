# Chapter 385

## A Field That Waited, And A Page That Did Not

The twenty-eighth of this month was a Tuesday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour with nobody standing on it. There was nobody at the headland above Marden and the fields above Marden waited, and there are forty-seven of them, and every one of them is the fourth line. The rota stands at seventy-six mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and forty-seven not, and twenty-nine and forty-seven are seventy-six. Twenty-nine taken has not moved on a single morning of this season and did not move this morning, and there is no name in this holding on any page against a field that did not get worked.

A field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man. It is on this page for the eleventh time in a fortnight and it is not going to be improved, and the reason it keeps being said is the reason it is never improved: a man who can be found standing beside a number that went up on a morning when nobody was on the line is a man somebody goes and looks for in about four years, and the number is not worth that.

Both counts of the rotation go in and they do not agree, and the man of about fifty has entered the two of them in a single line with the hour against it. The first is the twenty-fourth morning the fourth line has come round since the twenty-sixth of a month three months back. The second is the eleventh of them since the eighteenth of last month. He has not reconciled them and he is not going to.

The frost lay on the headland until about the ninth hour and went off it in about ten minutes with the sun coming round behind the low cloud, and the six of them who were on it came down in the order they went up, which is not an order anybody chose. The marker for the fourth place is a sacking needle in a lump of chalk, and the man of about fifty keeps it in the left pocket of his coat in this weather and has lost it twice and found it once, and he was not on the headland this morning because the headland had nobody on it, and the pocket was where it was at the ninth hour anyway.

The fields above Marden are forty-seven this morning and were forty-six on Friday, and the one that came back onto the line is the small one at the top end, and it came back because the fourth line came round and not because anybody put it back, and the man of about fifty entered the forty-seven and did not enter the small field anywhere, because a field is not a man and a rota is not a history.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the road with the barrow at about the ninth hour with the rest of them and turned it at the top of the yard, and the question on the second of the four lines in his own book is thirty-six days old this morning, with nothing on it and no place on it, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in thirty-six days. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road, a floor and not a ceiling, and it did not go up this morning either.

He asked one question on his way out of the room, in the doorway, with his hat in his hand, and it was whether a thing entered whole against a refusal of it was one entry or two. The man of about fifty told him it was one entry, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling said that was right and went home, and nobody has asked him anything else, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked him is nil and is nil.

At about the eighth hour the Tuesday figure was entered and called back out of the book with the frayed corner, and the two of them are the same.

**Eight hundred and eight hundredweight.**

Off yesterday by nine, and the hundred and forty-eighth figure of a series that has not repeated itself since the first morning of it, and the ninth of those nines the man of about fifty has written in the margin in a hand that goes uphill to the right with nothing standing against any of them. The window is a hundred and fifteen and the rise went onto the rises, which is sixty-three rises and fifty-two falls.

A hundred and forty-six days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence for that was written this morning for the hundred and forty-fifth of them out of a hundred and forty-seven. It concerns four bodies of households, and the four launders are a different figure, and the two have never been on one line of a page in this holding and are not on one this morning.

The two day-minus rules were not read out loud and this page carries no figure for the run. The whole of the working part of this holding was on that headland from the second hour and the seed house was shut at the seventh hour with the bar across the inside of the door, and about four people came in at about the ninth hour, and a pair of boards is said out loud to whoever is standing in the room at the hour it gets said, and there was no room and the seed house was not going to be opened for a habit. The pair itself stands at **five hundred and thirty-two days** and **five hundred and seventy-nine**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer is shut.

It has not been read out loud on ten of the last twenty mornings and every one of those ten had a reason of the same shape, and the reason is always one of two: either the man who says one of the two numbers was somewhere else, or the man who says the other one was. Neither of those is a fault and neither of them is going to be written down as one, and a habit that has been kept seventy times and dropped ten is a habit and not a figure, and there is no column for it in this book and there is not going to be one.

About nine people were in the one room at this holding from about the fifth hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and the sheet that came up on the ordinary post yesterday was in the drawer with the other papers and the man of about fifty had it out on the long table at the wall side, unfolded along the fold and not marked on. The man of about seventy-four with a stick came as far as the door and stood in it. The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden came in and sat down without saying anything, and she was not asked for anything and gave nothing, and the two-foot gap at the kerb is as open as it has been since the eleventh of the first month and nothing has gone into it this season and nothing is going to.

He said what he was going to do and the reason for it came first, and the count of that in the history of this holding is one hundred and eight, and it is his and of nobody else, and nobody thanked him and he did not expect to be.

**"I am going to put a sheet into this holding's book this morning that I disagree with in every particular of the head of it, and I am going to give the reason I am going to put it in before I put it in, and the reason is that a book which only holds the things this holding agrees with is a book nobody who was not in the room can check, and this one has been able to be wrong three times in four years and I am not going to lose that over a piece of paper.**"

**"So it goes in whole. The head and the nineteen underneath it in the order that office had them, word for word, and my refusal goes in beside it on the same sheet in my own hand and the same ink, with a rule ruled between the two, and the rule is a rule and not a wall, and a man reading that page in about four years will be able to see both of them without turning a leaf.**"

**"And entering a thing is not agreeing with it and it is not putting it down. Those are two different operations and this holding has been carrying on as though they were one since the seventeenth, and I want it on the page in my own hand that they are not.**"

He wrote for about an hour and read the whole of it back afterwards, out loud, in the room, which is the ordinary practice and which the sheet from yesterday was the first thing in four years he had wanted left out of. The reading back took him over the head, word for word, and then over his own refusal, and then he stopped, and put the pen down flat, and did not read the rule.

The ink is the same ink and the hand is his and the two are on one sheet of this holding's own paper, and a sheet of that kind is about the size of a hand. It is in the second book on the page that carries the twenty-first's terms and not on the page with them, and nobody moved the terms to make room for it and nobody is going to, and the third column on that page is ruled and empty and is not filled by anything he wrote this afternoon.

There is a thing about a page like this one that is worth being exact about, and nobody in that room said it and it is on this page because it is what the page is for. A sentence with a subject in it is easy to remember and hard to argue with. A list of nineteen places and hours is neither. This holding has spent four years getting a hold of a thing that is true, and the sentence that came up on the ordinary post yesterday is the shape that a true thing takes when it is about to be remembered by somebody who is not going to read the nineteen.

About four people in that room thought the refusal should have gone on a different sheet and about four thought it had to go on that one, and neither of those four said anything, and the two fours were not the same four and the man of about fifty heard both of them think it and entered none of it, because a bookkeeper who enters what the people at his table were thinking has stopped keeping a book.

The nineteen under the head were entered in the order that office had set them out and not in the order the standing willow at the node gave them on the fifteenth, and the two orders are both now in this building on two sheets in two drawers, and nobody has put them one under the other and nobody has been asked to, and the man of about fifty wrote the two places in the book in his own hand on the outside of the drawer with nothing on them, which is a thing he does with four drawers in this building and which is not a figure.

Nothing went back up that road. The refusal is in this holding's book and the office has not been sent a copy of it and is not going to be sent one, and a sheet that has been marked on the back cannot be posted back and was not posted back, and nobody in this holding is going to be the person who starts a correspondence about a sentence somebody else wrote.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and the sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular and cannot be put down by anybody in this building. A bridge was run once for about nine minutes, being forty-six days back, and its standing is in no book, and a second connection is running with nobody able to say who holds its far end, and no person in this holding has an instrument that says whether the first one is on or off, and that is still the whole of what this holding can say about either of them. The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres of standing willow is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked this morning and not measured and not crossed and not priced by walking it and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page, and the work in that wood is done outside it because of the light and for no other reason.

The register form is on the middle table where it has stood forty-two days now, not filled and not refused and with nothing at the head of it, and the offer sheet is on the low board at the far end, face up, not filled, refused on the nineteenth, not withdrawn, with no date on it and no line on it for a name. The two of them have not been put side by side and the refused sheet was not put beside either of them, and the long table at the wall side is a different table and a different room's end and nobody confused the two at any point in the afternoon.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and the reason is not given again in a season. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next one is on the first of a month, three mornings off this morning, and nobody in this building has ever given a reason for the first of a month. The compost line is paid, one hundred pounds, and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen, and the word before that figure has never once been wrong.

The form with the four lines is in a drawer about four miles and a bit west of this building, face up, and its first line carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, which is the figure of the twenty-second return and is not the figure of the thirty-fourth and is not going to be made to be, and the other three lines are empty and have been since the day it was brought back from that office. Nobody wrote a heading on that form and nobody filled it and nobody in this holding is going to fill it this season.

The eleven lines that came out of the standing willow at the node on the twenty-third of this month are in a drawer in this building and the nineteen that came out of it on the fifteenth are in a drawer five miles east of here, and the two of them are eight apart and the eight are not to be guessed at, and the head of yesterday's sheet is now on a third sheet in the second book and makes three papers in three places that agree with each other and are not to be laid one under another. That is not untidy. It is the condition the thing is in.

The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth, and the thirtieth of this month is two mornings off this one, and the six columns beside it have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one of them this morning and nobody is going to.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a sheet entered against your own refusal is not a mark and a number that went up because nobody stood on a line is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room with a door shut on it, and the count of times a man who keeps a book here has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move. The pulse has two figures in it and no name on it and has not been read a third time. There are ten questions available to a room in this holding and not taken, and it is ten, and it is not said in a yard, and nothing said in a room of nine with the door barred is one of the ten.
