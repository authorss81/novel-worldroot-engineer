# Chapter 392

## A Line That Is Not An Answer

The fifth of this month was a Tuesday and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and it was the last morning of the forty-nine, the run that starts on the sixteenth of last month and comes to an end at this one. The two places where the low ground lets water off, between the sluice and the north gate, took four people until about the fourth hour, and the second of the two was empty and the chain stayed on the grate, and the man at the front of it said that a thing which has been empty four mornings running is a thing the four of them will go and look at in about four years, and the three behind him did not answer him.

The two men who come to the seed work come on a Tuesday and they came this morning, and they are not to be asked to come on any other day and nobody asked them to. The woman who keeps the seed house has four accounts of her own and none of them is in this holding's book, and all four of them were on the table in front of her every day of the week, and the seed work went on this morning the way it has gone on every morning of this year, and nobody in this building has stopped it and nobody in this building is going to stop it.

The man with the bar at the sluice has been the man with the bar for eleven years and is not going to be asked to give the bar up, and the low ground between the two places is about nine feet wide and is the only piece of ground in this holding that holds water in a dry month, and no man in this building has ever been able to say why in a sentence that would survive being written down, and nobody has written one.

Nobody in this holding asked the man of about thirty-one of Silling anything this morning, and he came down the four-mile road at about the ninth hour with the barrow and turned it at the top of the yard and went home. The second of the four lines in his own book is forty-three days old, and a page in this holding that is forty-three days old is a page nobody has put a hand on, and there is no answer on it and no place for one, and nobody is going to put either of those there. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road and did not rise this morning.

At about the eighth hour the fifth was written into the book that keeps the third launder, and the clerk called it back out of the frayed one into the room, and the two numbers were the same, as they have been on every morning of this year and on every morning of the four years before it.

**Eight hundred and fifteen hundredweight.**

It has fallen off Monday by five, and it is the hundred and fifty-fifth figure of a series that has not come out at one figure twice since the first morning of it. The window the rises and the falls are counted in is a hundred and twenty-two, and the fall went onto the falls, so that sixty-six rises and fifty-six falls are in it, and the two of those add up and have added up on every morning of this year without anybody making them add up.

A hundred and fifty-three days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the hundred and fifty-second of them out of a hundred and fifty-four. It is a sentence about four bodies of households and the four launders are a different figure and the two have never been added in four years and are not going to be added this morning by the last morning anybody had to add them.

At about the seventh hour the two day-minus rules were read out loud for the last time this year, both numbers said before either of them, one man taking the first and one taking the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both of them. They stand at **five hundred and thirty-nine days** and **five hundred and eighty-six**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened. Forty-eight mornings out of seventy-nine since the seventeenth of a month three months back, and it is a habit and it is not a measurement, and there is no column for it in this book and there never has been.

About nine people were in the one room at this holding from about the fifth hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and nothing was on the table in that room that had not been on a table in it in the last three weeks, and that was on the page this afternoon as a fact about the table and not as a finding.

The rota stands at seventy-seven mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and forty-eight not, and twenty-nine and forty-eight are seventy-seven. Twenty-nine taken did not move on any morning of this year and did not move this morning, and the forty-eight that are not are all of them the fourth line, and a field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man, and that sentence has been on the page thirteen times in a fortnight and is on it for the last time this year and is not going to be improved on.

Here is what was in that room, in the state it was in at the fifth hour of the afternoon, and it is the whole of what a holding of this kind has after four years of it. A register form on the middle table, forty-nine days, not filled. A sheet of terms in two hands with a third column ruled and empty. An offer on the low board, face up, refused and not withdrawn and not filled. A sheet with three parts and a gap in it, gone out of this building unsigned on Monday morning, and a copy of it gone to four bodies of households. An assessment from an office, correct in every particular, in the second book. A schedule of nineteen places in a drawer here, and the same nineteen in a drawer five miles east in a different order, and an order in a third drawer that came out of a wood and has no other copy in the world. Eleven lines in a drawer and eight gaps beside them, and the eight are not to be guessed at. A block of rootwood under a cloth at the end of the long table, warm, with a control sequence for one valve on it, and two people who have looked at it.

That is the inventory and it is not a resolution, and there is no morning on which it becomes one, and nobody in that room said that it did.

The man of about fifty did one thing before he shut the second book, and it is on the page because he entered it, and a thing that is entered in this holding's book is a thing this holding has said out loud in a room with the door shut. He ruled a line on the second page of the session book, under the entry for the twelfth of last month, and he wrote against it that the standing of the bridge is not in this book, and he wrote nothing under the line and nothing above it and no second line against it, and he did not read it back.

Nobody in that room said a word about it. About four people had been waiting four years for somebody to write down something about that bridge and about four had not, and the four that had not are the ones who are not going to be disappointed by a line in a book that says where a thing is not, and neither of the two fours said so, and the man of about fifty heard both of them and entered none of it.

He shut the book after that and did not open it again this evening, and the four books of this holding all stand on one shelf at the wall side in the order they were started in, and he has never reordered them and is not going to. There is a stack of day sheets on the table by the door, one for every morning for four years, in the order they were used, and the pile is about a foot and a half high now and it gets about a quarter of an inch taller in a fortnight, and nobody in this building has ever measured it and nobody is going to.

The four years of them are not a history. They are about four things happening on a morning, and a figure being the same figure in two books, and a pair being said out loud, and about four people being in a room with the door shut, and the four of those are not the same on any two mornings and the two of them that are the same are the figure and the pair, and that is the whole of what a person can be told by reading four years of a holding's days.

The corridor in the pan is named and open, and it has not been closed this season and it has not been established as on, and naming it is not answering it, and the sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular and cannot be put down by anybody in this building. The seed vault of forty households went under four hundred yards off about two months back and has not come up, and nobody has been down and nobody is going to be.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres of standing willow is bare in its eleventh year. It was not walked this morning and not measured and not crossed and not priced by walking it and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page, and the work in that wood is done outside it because of the light off the west end and for no other reason. Six answers have been given in four years to the question of who speaks for those eleven acres, and none of the six is an answer, and nobody in this holding has a seventh.

The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of that room, face up, and it is not filled. It was refused on the nineteenth of last month and it has not been withdrawn and it has not been taken up and it does not lapse on the day it was made. There is no name on it and there is no line on it for a name, and nobody in this building has been told to take it off that board and nobody is going to be, and there is not going to be a second instrument of an offer on any table in this holding this season.

The register form is on the middle table where it has stood forty-nine days, not filled, not refused, and nobody has written a heading on it and nobody is going to. The form with the four lines is in a drawer about four miles and a bit west of here, face up, and its first line carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, which is the figure of the twenty-second return, and the three lines under it are empty and have been since the day it came back, and there is no way from this building to put a second figure on that form.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was in that room for the last hour of it and said nothing about any of the three instruments, and the cut at the kerb stays open, and the two-foot gap at the kerb is as open as it has been since the eleventh of the first month of this year, and nothing has gone into it this season and nothing is going to, and the key is in her hand and was not used this morning and was not asked about, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and is nil.

The man of the north row with the cough was not in that room this morning and was not sent for and nobody has been to him, and his two counts are not printed in this book and have not been printed in it for four years, and what he will be able to do in about four years is not a question anybody in this building has put to anybody.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and the reason for that was given once and he said in the same breath that he is not going to give it again in a season. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is five, and every one of the five was entered before the thing it was entered for, and the four ruled lines under the two words on the twentieth of last month are still empty and were not filled this morning. The pulse has two figures in it and no name and has not been read a third time and has not been named.

There are ten questions available to a room in this holding and not taken, and it is ten, and it is not said in a yard. There are two people who can hold a pattern for four and one who can hold one for five and two of them carry a rootmark, and one of the two is the man whose four inches along the inside of the left forearm branch twice, and none of that moved this morning and none of it is a thing a room of nine can vote on, and nobody in this building has asked him this year what it costs and nobody is going to.

There are two numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check, and there have been two for two years, and the first of them is still not named. There are six things this holding does not know, and no unit has ever been supplied for any of them, and none of the six is the standing of that bridge or the author of a sentence at the head of a sheet that came up on the ordinary post or the reason a man of about seventy is fetched on the first of a month or the year in the eighth of a page of somebody's, and the count of not knowns did not move this morning and a thing with a shape and not a unit is not in that column and never has been.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of a morning four years ago. The four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled and are going to be unreconciled when this holding closes its books, and a loss in one ear is not a mark and a refusal is not a mark and a page nobody can use is not a mark.

The last of the forty-nine mornings ended with nobody thanking anybody, which is what the nine mornings of this last week came to. The count of things said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong is five and it did not move this morning, and this was a room with a door shut on it, and there has not been a yard in this holding since Monday the thirteenth of last month. The count of times a man who keeps a book here has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move, and there is no yard in this holding for either of them to move in.

The man of about fifty closed the second book at about the ninth hour and put the pen in his coat and did not say what any of it was for, and the clerk read the figure for the fifth back out of the other book and the two were the same, and the day sheet went into the stack in the order it was used, which is to say last, and the drawer with the rest of the boards in it is shut and has not been opened in four years. The figure for the sixth of this month will go into the same book in the same hand and it will not be this one, and he put the book back on the shelf with the other three.
