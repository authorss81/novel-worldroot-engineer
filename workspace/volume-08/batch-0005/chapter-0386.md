# Chapter 386

## One Ear

The twenty-ninth of this month was a Wednesday and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The boundary ditch and the two culverts took about six people until about the fourth hour, and the near culvert has been clear since the ninth and nobody has looked down it since the ninth, and the far one was about a third full and the six of them put the rakes down at the top of it and did not go in, because the far one has been going down by itself since the twenty-third and there is no need to be in a thing twice in a week.

Nobody in this holding went up that four-mile road with the barrow except the four bodies of households, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling who brings it went at about the ninth hour and turned it at the top of the yard. The second of the four lines in the book in his coat has a question on it and that page is thirty-seven days old this morning, and there is nothing on it and nowhere for anything to go, and he has not been asked about it in thirty-seven days and nobody in this holding is going to ask him. It is eleven journeys up that road and it did not go up this morning.

The figure for the twenty-ninth was in the book that keeps the third launder before the clerk had ruled the margin, and the man of about fifty read it back out of the other one and the two are the same.

**Eight hundred and three hundredweight.**

Down five off yesterday. The hundred and forty-ninth figure of a series that has not come out at one figure twice since the first of it, and the fall went onto the falls, so the window the rises and the falls are counted in stands at a hundred and sixteen with sixty-three rises and fifty-three falls in it.

A hundred and forty-seven days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the line saying so was written this morning for the hundred and forty-sixth of them out of a hundred and forty-eight. It is a line about four bodies of households and not about the launders, and the two aggregates have been kept apart in this holding for four years and are going to be kept apart after the man of about fifty is gone.

At about the seventh hour the two day-minus rules were said out loud, one man to the first and another to the second and both before either number, to about four people and with nobody trusted with two. They stand at **five hundred and thirty-three days** and **five hundred and eighty**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer was not opened. Forty-five mornings out of seventy-three since the seventeenth of a month two months back.

At about the fifth hour of the morning, in a room with the door shut, the count of sessions entered in this holding's book went from four to five, and it went before any of it was done, and the entry carries the day, which is the twenty-ninth, and the hour, which is about the fifth, and the four of the four, being the reader of this body and Tova Reed and the man of about fifty and the man of about thirty-one of Silling, and the fourth of those sat with his hat on his knee and was not asked anything.

**THE PURPOSE: TO GO FURTHER INTO THE STANDING WILLOW AT THE NODE THAN THE FIFTEENTH OF THIS MONTH AND NOT FURTHER THAN THE TWENTY-THIRD, AND TO TAKE OUT OF IT THE ORDER IN WHICH THE WOOD KEEPS WHAT THE ENGINE MEANS TO TAKE OFF, AND NOTHING ELSE, AND TO BRING BACK NO PART OF ANYTHING OUT OF A PERSON. THE DURATION: THE FOURTH HOUR UNTIL THE FIFTH, AND IF THE FIFTH COMES FIRST IT COMES FIRST AND THE READING STOPS. THE STOP SIGNAL: THERE IS ONE, IT IS HERS, IT IS NOT ENTERED HERE AND IS NOT GOING TO BE, AND THE REASON IS THE REASON THIS BOOK IS CARRIED BETWEEN ROOMS AND NOT THROUGH THEM. THE INDEPENDENT WITNESS: THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO IS AT NEITHER END, WHO HAS BEEN TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND HAS NOT BEEN TOLD WHAT IT IS, AND WHO IS NOT TO BE TOLD, AND NOBODY IN THAT ROOM ASKED HIM FOR IT.**

Under the four of the four he wrote two words and the hour, being ORDER SOUGHT, and ruled the four lines under them, and nothing went under those four lines.

The settlement of the twentieth of this month governs everything that was done after it and it was not rewritten this morning and it was not improved on and it was not settled a fifth time, and anybody in that room who wanted to reopen it was going to have to say the word about a clause, and nobody said it. What he is holding, he holds alone; the man of about fifty holds no end and is a witness and not a holder; nobody is to be asked afterward whether the morning was right, by anybody, in any yard, in any season; and if what comes out of it comes out wrong it comes out wrong and is entered as it comes out.

The man of about fifty said one thing about the four ruled lines and he did not give the reason first and nobody in that room asked him for it, because he is the one who writes them and he has never owed anybody a reason for a pencil mark.

"They are empty because the number of lines on that sheet is four and was four on the twentieth, and a rule is a rule and the number of lines on it is not a quantity. Whatever came on the last two mornings went on a page of its own, and whatever comes on this one will go on a page of its own, and the four lines under those two words will be the shape they were ruled to be, and I am not going to rule a fifth line for a morning that is bigger than the morning I ruled them for."

About nine people went up the five miles of cart road at about the second hour of the afternoon. The channel along the inside of the line of the stems was finished on both days of it a fortnight ago, and the six of them who came for the leaf put the rake down at the far end and did not start a third day, and nobody at the node asked them to and nobody at the node said anything about it.

The reading was done in the standing willow at the node and it was done outside the ring of bare ground. Nobody went near that ring and nobody put a foot in it, and it is bare in its eleventh year and there is no dimension for it on any page of this book, and the hour it was done in is the fourth hour because of the light off the west end of that wood and for no other reason anybody has ever been able to put in a sentence.

The man of about fifty stood on the bank with the second book open on a board and the pen in his hand and his eyes on the wood. He has been told that a condition exists and he has not been told what it is and he was not told this morning and he was not asked, and he is not going to be thanked for not knowing and has never asked to be thanked for it.

What came back was the order. That is the whole of what came back and it is not nothing and it is not the nineteen again. It came in the order the wood keeps them in, and the order the wood keeps things in is the order the things happen in, and it is the only order a wood has, and it is not the order the sheet five miles east of here is in and it is not the order the head of the sheet that came up on the ordinary post gave them in on the twenty-seventh, and the man of about fifty entered the order and not the nineteen and did not enter one as an opinion on the other.

Nineteen places, what against each and when against each, and the eight that carry a day and an hour as well as a season are the same eight and carry the same ones. The order it came back in puts the whole of the small ground at the north end of the pan after the wide branches below the sluice, and it puts the two places on the Havergate side in the middle of a season and not at the end of one, and about four people in this holding looked at those two lines for about a minute each and about four of them had not known that a schedule could be read for an order at all. It says what and it says when. It has nothing else on it and no name anywhere on it and no office and no subject, and the sentence that was refused on the twenty-seventh is the one thing it is not.

And the eight that did not come out of the wood on the twenty-third of this month are not in the order and did not come this time either, and the order does not have a gap in it where they would be, and that is what came back and nobody has made anything of it, and nobody in this building is going to guess at eight places.

Then she came out of that wood at about ten minutes to the fifth hour and the man of about fifty was on the bank and the reader of this body was at the near end and the light had gone off the west end of the trees about a quarter of an hour before, and she came out of it and she was standing about four feet off the reader of this body and he said her name and she turned her head to find him, and that is on this page because a man who does not write down the moment when he found that out is a man who is going to get the account of it wrong later and be believed.

She said it herself, in her own words, in about nine people on the bank, and the reason came before the thing, and the count of that in the history of this holding is twenty-five, and it is hers and of nobody else's, and nobody thanked her.

**"I am going to say one thing about myself out loud in front of these people and I am going to give the reason I am going to say it before I say it, and the reason is that this holding has four books and one clerk and about nine people in a room, and a thing about me that gets written down by somebody else is a thing I will be asked about in about four years whether it is true.**"

**"So this is what it is. I can hear. That is not the whole of it and I am not going to let anybody in this room decide that it is. What I cannot do is put a sound on a side. There are three of us talking on this bank and if I stop looking I cannot tell which of you is on my left, and that is not going to come back, and nobody in this holding is going to lose a morning to finding out whether it would.**"

**"And it is not a wound and it is not on his arm and it is not a fifth account of anything and it does not go on the wall at the well and it does not go in the use log. I am the person who decides when he stops and I am going to go on deciding that, and it does not get harder because of my left ear, and anybody in this room who has been waiting nine days for a reason to take the seed work off me has not got one and is not going to get one this season.**"

Nobody said anything for about a minute, and the silence was made of about nine people not saying a thing they had all three answers to, and then the woman who keeps the seed house said that there was tea in the seed house and that it would go cold, and that was the whole of what anybody said about it. Nobody said he was sorry. Nobody touched her arm. Nobody put a hand on a shoulder and nobody said that it would be all right, and the man of about fifty went on with the order and wrote down the order and did not write down the ear, and about four people in that room wanted to write down the ear and about four did not, and the four that wanted it were wrong to want it and none of the two fours said so.

The reader of this body did not go after her and did not ask her a second question about it and did not say anything at all about it, and the count of questions he has asked her since about the twelfth of this month is nil and is nil, and the key is in the hand of the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden and was not used and was not asked about. The two-foot gap at the kerb is as open as it has been since the eleventh of the first month and nothing has gone into it this season.

They came down the five miles in about an hour and a half and the order was written out on the bank before anybody left the wood, on a sheet of this holding's own paper, in the reader of this body's own hand, with him sitting on the stump at the near end and the wind taking the corner of it every few minutes, and the man of about fifty checked it against the session book twice and found the same order both times and wrote no correction against either check. The four of them came down the road in the order they went up and nobody asked anybody anything on the way, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling read the order twice with his hat on his knee and was asked nothing about it, and about four people in this building have worked out that he is not going to be asked and about four have worked out that he is not going to be told either, and he knows about both of those fours and has said nothing to either of them.

The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of the seed house, face up, and it is not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of this month and it has not been withdrawn and it has no date on it. The register form is on the middle table where it has stood forty-three days and is not filled and was not refused and nobody wrote a heading on it, and the sheet of terms from the twenty-first is on the same table with its third column ruled and empty, and the three of them have been on that table inside a fortnight and have not been put side by side and are not going to be.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a loss in one ear is not a mark and a loss of hearing is not a mark and an order read out of a wood is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled. The six things this holding does not know are still six and no seventh was added this afternoon for an ear, and a thing with a shape and not a unit is not in that column and never has been.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and the reason is not given again in a season. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is five, and all five of them were entered before the thing they were entered for, and the four ruled lines under the two words this morning are empty. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next of them is on the first of a month, which is two mornings off this one and is not tomorrow.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a bank and a room and not a yard, and the last yard in this holding was on Monday the thirteenth of this month and there has not been one since.
