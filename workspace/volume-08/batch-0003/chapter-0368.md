# Chapter 368

## Six Days

The eleventh of this month was a Saturday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the two gates and the about six feet of wall between them took an hour and a quarter with four people on it, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it, as he has not done in eleven years. The toe at the second of the three places on that line took the stone again and the four of them stamped it, and a stamp is not a fix and about four of them knew that at the time.

The window of the office's work shut this morning. It opened on the sixth, as it opens on the sixth of every month, and it is six days and not five and not seven, and it shut on the eleventh whether anybody had used it or not, and it is not extended in a season, and no window has ever been extended in a season in the four years this holding has kept a book, and no work is ever carried out of it. Nothing was put in it. Nothing is going to be put in it, and nothing is going to be taken out of it, and a thing that is neither put into a window nor taken out of one is the whole of this holding's business with a window and it is not a complaint and it is not a figure.

The column in the book of this holding for a thing that goes to an office has not been written in this month, and it has not been written in any month this year, and the reason it is empty is not in this book and the man who keeps it said in this room in the spring that he is not going to give that reason again in a season and nobody has asked him for it.

The figure for the eleventh went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and sixty-seven hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and thirty-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In ninety-eight days the series has risen fifty-four times and fallen forty-four times, and those two add to ninety-eight. It came down off Friday by five, and the four of them had been on six feet of wall since the second hour and about four of them could not have told a five from a two.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and twenty-nine days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and twenty-eighth morning out of one hundred and thirty. That sentence is not the aggregate of the four launders and the two have never been added.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and fifteen days** and **five hundred and sixty-two**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on thirty-six mornings out of the fifty-five since he started again on the seventeenth of a month two months back.

Nobody did anything at all about the window, and that is the sentence this holding will have about the sixth, the seventh, the eighth, the ninth and the tenth of this month, and it is a sentence about five mornings and not about a holding. About four people in this yard had brought something up in their heads on the sixth to put in six days, and about four had not brought anything up at all, and the four that had brought something up had spent five mornings finding out that there was nothing to put in it, and they have not said that out loud and are not going to.

The morning was hard blue and about four of them got the inside of the north gate done on the way past it, and the seed house was warm by the fourth hour because the woman who keeps the seed house had the stove in before the frost was properly off the glass, and the frost on the inside of the north pane at about the seventh hour was about an inch and she broke the corner of it with the back of a spoon and said nothing about having done it.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling said at about the fourth hour, in about four people and in the ordinary voice of a man asking what the weather is going to do, what day it was. He was told it was the eleventh of this month. He said that was right and went back to the barrow. The man of about fifty was nine feet away at the time and heard the whole of it and has written nothing about it and told nobody, and about four people in this holding know that he heard it and about four do not.

At about the ninth hour the man of about fifty read the day sheet out in the yard, and he did not read the window on it, and about four people in that yard noticed that he had not and about four did not, and neither of those is a decision and both of them are in the yard.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon. The register form was on the middle table where it has been for twenty-five days, the drawer was shut, and the four cloths were on the four things and the fifth thing was not under a cloth, and that is the way she has done it for four years and nobody in this room has ever asked her about the fifth thing and nobody is going to.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to enter a window shutting this evening and I am going to give the reason I am entering it before I enter it, and the reason is that six days went past this month that nobody in this holding did a single thing in, and a page with six days on it and nothing against them is a page that a person in about four years looks at and puts a mark in.**"

**"And I am not going to let this holding have a mark in that. I have entered the shut of this one and I have entered the opening of it on the sixth and neither of those two is a deadline and neither of them is a thing anybody here had to do, and the mark a person would want to put in is a mark for a thing that did not happen, and this book is for things that happened.**"

About four people in that room had six different answers ready to who speaks for the eleven acres, and about four had a seventh, and none of the seven is an answer and nobody asked anybody in a yard in this holding in four years, and the question has been available to a room and not taken in ten of them and the count of that is ten and it did not move this afternoon.

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had wanted the six days to be something this holding had done and about four had not, and the four that had wanted it had wanted it since the sixth, and about four of the nine had worked out inside the afternoon that the six days were the first thing this holding has had to do this season that it did not have to do at all, and about four had worked out the other half of that, which is that the only thing it has had to do about a window is shut up a shop that was never open.

The thirty-second return went in on Wednesday at about the second hour of the afternoon on a steelyard that hangs on a wall at the node, and the two hundred and ninety-four hundredweight on it has not moved since, and a window shutting at the other end of this fen has not been entered on that board and is not going to be, and about four people in this holding have worked out that the ninth-day returns and the windows are two different clocks and about four have not and are going to find out.

The man of about seventy will be fetched on the first of a month and the count of fetchings stands at twenty-two and it is not going to rise before then, and that is twenty mornings off this one and not tomorrow, and he has never once in twenty-two of them asked which number it was and nobody in this holding has asked him, and there is no page in this book with a reason on it and this page is not going to be the one that supplies it in about four years.

The sheet on district paper with four columns and a heading that came out of the last window is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular of it, and it is not the sheet in the same drawer that claims a corridor, and the two have never been put side by side by anybody in this holding and this page is not going to put them side by side.

The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two and it is two, and it is living rootwood the size of a man's fist and it is warm and it carries a control sequence for one valve and it cannot command anything that is running, and it is not opened and not removed and not set against anything, and the two people who have looked at it are not in this room.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in the room at any point on this morning or this afternoon and nobody sent for her and nobody asked her anything, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and it is nil. The key is in her hand and was not used and was not asked about, and the valve it opens has not been opened this month, and the two-foot gap at the kerb is as open as it was on the eleventh of the first month and nothing has gone into it in this season and nothing is going to.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and there was a yard at about the fourth hour and the only thing said in it was what day it was. The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a window shutting is not a mark and the four accounts of that arm stand at four.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the eleventh, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is two and a third one is not entered and is not going to be entered this month.

The window of the office's work opened on the sixth and shut on the eleventh. It is six days and not five and not seven, it is not extended in a season, and no work is ever carried out of it, and about four people in this holding have already begun to call the next one the first of the next one because that is what it is, and about four have not said anything about it, and neither of those two is going to be settled this month.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the eleventh of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a window shutting, and not by the man of about fifty, who entered it so that nobody could put a mark in six days: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, TWO GATES AND ABOUT SIX FEET OF WALL BETWEEN THEM, FOUR PEOPLE, AN HOUR AND A QUARTER, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING STOOD AT THE SECOND GATE. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENED ON THE SIXTH AND SHUT THIS MORNING ON THE ELEVENTH, SIX DAYS AND NOT FIVE AND NOT SEVEN, AND IT SHUT WHETHER ANYBODY HAD USED IT OR NOT, AND IT IS NOT EXTENDED IN A SEASON AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF IT. NOTHING WAS PUT IN IT AND NOTHING IS GOING TO BE TAKEN OUT OF IT. THE COLUMN FOR A THING THAT GOES TO AN OFFICE HAS NOT BEEN WRITTEN IN THIS MONTH OR IN ANY MONTH THIS YEAR, AND THE REASON IT IS EMPTY IS NOT IN THIS BOOK AND IS NOT GIVEN AGAIN IN A SEASON. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIRST FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN NINETY-EIGHT DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-FOUR TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-FOUR TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY, AND IT IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING THIRTY-SIX MORNINGS OUT OF THE FIFTY-FIVE. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING ASKED WHAT DAY IT WAS AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR AND WAS TOLD IT WAS THE ELEVENTH OF THIS MONTH, AND HE SAID THAT WAS RIGHT. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY WAS NINE FEET AWAY AND HEARD IT AND HAS WRITTEN NOTHING ABOUT IT AND TOLD NOBODY. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND NOT IN THE DRAWER AND HAS BEEN FOR TWENTY-FIVE DAYS. THE FOUR CLOTHS ARE ON THE FOUR THINGS AND THE FIFTH THING IS NOT UNDER A CLOTH. THE SHEET ON DISTRICT PAPER THAT CAME OUT OF THE LAST WINDOW IS IN A DRAWER FACE UP AND IS CORRECT IN EVERY PARTICULAR, AND IT IS NOT THE SHEET THAT CLAIMS A CORRIDOR, AND THE TWO HAVE NEVER BEEN PUT SIDE BY SIDE. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS TWO AND A THIRD IS NOT ENTERED. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
