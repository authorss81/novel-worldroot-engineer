# Chapter 364

## Two Hundred And Ninety-Four

The seventh of this month was a Tuesday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the two gates and the about six feet of wall between them took an hour and a quarter with four people on it, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it, as he has not done in eleven years.

The toe at the second of the three places on that line is up a finger's width off what it was on the third of this month, and the four of them put a spade in it and a hand's depth of stone and stood about it afterward the way men stand about a thing they have just made worse, and about four people in this holding could not have told you which of the three is the toe.

The window of the office's work is four days off this morning and not tomorrow. It is six days and not five and not seven, it shuts on the eleventh whether anybody has used it or not, and it is not extended in a season and no window has ever been extended in a season in the four years this holding has kept a book, and no work is ever carried out of it.

The figure for the seventh went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and fifty-nine hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and twenty-seventh figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In ninety-four days the series has risen fifty-two times and fallen forty-two times, and those two add to ninety-four. It came down off Monday by five, and the four of them had been on six feet of wall since the second hour and about four of them could not have told a five from a two.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and twenty-five days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and twenty-fourth morning out of one hundred and twenty-six. That sentence is not the aggregate of the four launders and the two have never been added.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and eleven days** and **five hundred and fifty-eight**, forty-seven days apart, and the forty-seven is a consequence of the two and not a third rule. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on thirty-four mornings out of the fifty-one since he started again on the seventeenth of a month two months back.

Two hours later he went down the five miles of cart road with the two books in a satchel and a day sheet in his coat, and the frost was gone off the road by the time he got to the top of it and the ruts had about half an inch of water standing in them.

The light was already off the west end of the standing wood when he got there, and the wood stood in its own shadow with the sun on the bank above the channel, and the eleven acres have done that for longer than this holding has had a book. The ring of bare ground inside them is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked this afternoon and was not walked on Friday, and nobody at that place put a level on it, and the two lines on the back of a sheet in an inside pocket still stop at its inside edge and go round it.

The barrow came in about a quarter of an hour behind him with four of them in it, and the load went on the sling and the sling went up and the beam came down slow. The four bodies of households that weigh it are at Havergate and Low Wether and Marden and Silling, and not one of them is a member of the cooperative and none of them was made one this afternoon and none of them has asked to be.

The return went in on the steelyard on the wall of the building with no roof, and the dial came down off the figure that was on it nine days ago and the beam had come down slow, the way it comes down on a day when the wind is off the north.

**Two hundred and ninety-four hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-second return and the twenty-second name-pair in eleven years, and it is eleven hundredweight above the thirty-first, which was two hundred and eighty-three on the twenty-eighth of last month. The dial came down off two hundred and eighty-three and the reader of this body read it out loud and then read it back out of the second book, and the two are the same.

A name went into BY. Then a name went into WITNESSED BY. Then a name went into BY, and a name went into WITNESSED BY, and so on, one at a time, by each of the four bodies of households standing where they could see the dial, and the four of them did it in the order they arrived and not in any order anybody chose.

And then it was reconciled twice, which is what has happened on this board at every one of them since the first, out of two figures the man of about fifty had written on a day sheet at about the fifth hour of the morning and had told nobody he had written them, and which the reader of this body carried down the five miles in the inside pocket of a coat.

**Two hundred and fifty-one plus forty-three. And two hundred and nine plus eighty-five.** They are the two halves and they are not the whole and they have not been added together and they are not going to be added together this afternoon, and the word using is on the page of this holding's book: this holding is using that derivation and is not refusing it. A man who refuses a derivation is a man who thinks the number is in doubt, and the number is not in doubt, the number is on a dial that has been on that wall longer than this holding has had a book.

The second rule stayed blank. Nothing was written in it and nothing was said about it, and it is the sixteenth return in eleven years at which it has been left blank, and the count of returns at which it has been left blank is sixteen, and it was fifteen on the twenty-eighth of last month, and a count of blanks is not the count of a man and has never been the count of a man.

The form with the four lines and the fifth ruled line is in a drawer face up about four miles and a bit west of that wall, and its first line carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight in the hand of a man who spoke for one body of households, and that is the figure of the twenty-second return and not of this one and not of the thirty-first, and nobody wrote a heading on it and nobody filled the other three lines and the fifth ruled line is still under them and empty.

The number of times the man of about thirty-one of Silling has brought a barrow up the four miles to that place is eleven, and it was eleven the last time anybody said it out loud in this holding and it is eleven now, and the count is a floor and not a ceiling and it did not rise this afternoon because a room got quiet, and nobody asked him where he was going to be this week and he was not at that place this afternoon.

And then about six people were in the one room at that place that has a door on it, at about the sixth hour, and the door was shut, and the light came in low through one small pane and lay along the floor and not up the wall, and the two books were on the sill and the second one was shut.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-nine, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to write the number of blanks on the page this evening and I am going to give the reason I am writing that number before I say anything about it, and the reason is that a board with a rule on it that nobody fills is going to be read in about four years by somebody as a rule that cannot be filled, and it is not that, and the only way anybody is ever going to know it is not that is if a person writes down that he had the chance and did not take it.**"

**"That is the whole of it. I have had the pen in my hand sixteen times in eleven years with that rule in front of me and I have had the figures to fill it with every one of the sixteen times, and I did not fill it, and there is no reason for that in this holding's book and I am not going to supply one tonight, and a page that has the count on it is a page that says the sixteen were chances and not refusals.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people standing at that wall had wanted the rule filled since the nineteenth of last month and about four had not, and the four that wanted it had wanted it since they had seen the second way the two hundred and eighty-three came, and the four that had not had understood that the two ways are two halves of one thing and not two opinions about it.

The woman who keeps the seed house was not in that room and had not been at that place this afternoon, and the seed house is a day's walk and a half off in the other direction, and there is nobody in this holding who keeps a second set of books, and that is not a complaint and not a figure, it is the reason a man can put two books in a satchel and walk five miles and leave a cloth lying on a table, and neither of those two is a thing anybody here has standing to correct.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the seventh, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next fetching is the first of a month, and that is twenty-four mornings off this one. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen and it does not fall again until the first.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice and it has no second mark, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a dial is not a mark and a blank is not a mark and the count of blanks is not a fifth of anything. The four accounts of that arm in the books in this holding stand at four and reconcile none of one another.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and the two things that were said at that wall were said in the open with four bodies of households standing there and not one of them got anything wrong. The count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move, and there was no yard at that place this afternoon and there has never been one at that place.

A man went out of a wood on Friday afternoon and into a room on Monday with a season in him and put it on no page. A dial on a wall of a building with no roof said two hundred and ninety-four hundredweight for the thirty-second time in eleven years and eleven more than the last, and four people who are not of this holding wrote their names in two lines one at a time, and one rule on a board was left blank for the sixteenth time, and the count of blanks is a count of blanks, and the month is on its seventh and does not turn for twenty-three more mornings.

THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK IS FOUR DAYS OFF AND NOT TOMORROW, SIX DAYS AND NOT FIVE AND NOT SEVEN, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF IT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVENTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN NINETY-FOUR DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-TWO TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHER COLUMNS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS ONE AND NOTHING WAS DONE THAT NEEDED ONE. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
