# Chapter 370

## No Living Branch

The thirteenth of this month was a Monday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the boundary ditch and the two culverts took about six people until about the fourth hour. The near culvert was clear for the thirteenth morning running and the far one was about half full, and the six of them put four barrow-loads out of the far one and put them on the far shoulder where they have been put every morning this month.

The figure for the thirteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and seventy-one hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and thirty-third figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In one hundred days the series has risen fifty-five times and fallen forty-five times, and those two add to one hundred. It came down off Sunday by five, and the six of them had spent the morning taking leaf out of a culvert and about four of them had no way of telling a five from a two.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and thirty-one days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and thirtieth morning out of one hundred and thirty-two. That sentence is not the aggregate of the four launders and the two have never been added.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and seventeen days** and **five hundred and sixty-four**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on thirty-seven mornings out of the fifty-seven since he started again on the seventeenth of a month two months back.

The morning was cold and the far culvert had a skin of ice on it at about the second hour that four of the six broke with the back of a spade, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the road about the ninth hour, turned the barrow at the top of the yard and went home, and the four lines in his own book have a question on the second of them and the page on it is twenty-one days old and it has no answer on it and no place for an answer, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in twenty-one days and nobody is going to.

The line about the branch is not this holding's paper. It came on a sheet with four columns and a heading at the top, and the sheet is in a drawer face up, and every particular of it is correct, and the office that sent it claimed a corridor under it, and a body of households declined the corridor and did not decline the bridge, and none of those two facts has anything to do with a branch.

At about the fourth hour of the afternoon there were about four people in the one room at this holding with the door shut, and not the nine, and the reason it was four is in the entry for this evening and is not in this paragraph.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and one, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is a thing I worked out on Saturday afternoon and did not write in this book, and I am going to give the reason I did not write it before this room hears it, and the reason is that a thing which takes away a thing a man wanted is not a thing a man is fit to write down while he still wants it.**"

**"I have kept four books for four years and I have entered things that made this holding look good and things that it made look bad, and the difference between the two has never once been how I felt about them at the time. It has been whether I would still enter it in a month. On Saturday I would not have. This morning I would, and that is the whole of the reason it goes in this evening and not on Saturday, and I am not going to be thanked for the two days.**"

Nobody improved it. The woman who keeps the seed house put her hand flat on the table the way she puts it flat on the sacks and said nothing, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling sat where he sat and did not open the book of his own, and about four people in that room had worked out inside a minute that the man who had brought the thing into the building on Thursday was going to be the one who took it out of it, and about four had not worked that out and were not going to be told.

Then the reader of this body said it, and he said it in the plainest words he had, and nobody improved the words, and this page is not going to improve them either.

**"The shaft in this holding's own low ground, four hundred yards below the north gate, is supposed to have had no living branch in it. That is not my opinion and it is not this room's. It is a line on somebody else's paper and the line is correct in every particular of it, and this holding has had four years to put something against that line and has not got anything.**"

**"And here is the whole of it, and it is one sentence and it has nothing to do with what anybody in this room feels. A wood does not invent anything. A wood keeps what the ground has touched. If a man is in a place with no living branch in it, then nothing that man does in that place can get into any ground anywhere, because there is no road out of it for anything to travel on, and a wall where a branch would be is not a thing a wood can get through.**"

**"So those four things are not a man in that place four hundred yards below this north gate. And they are not a man in a wood either, because the man of this holding's own family went into the ground eight years ago four hundred yards below this north gate and he did not come out of it, and this holding has had nothing from him since and is not going to get anything, and a man who is not alive cannot be alive four miles and a half of cart road away in a wood at the node either.**"

**"And that leaves one thing and I am not going to dress it up, because dressing it up is what this holding would do. Every piece of what came out of that wood on the ninth is a true piece of a man who lived. The hands are his hands. The cuff is torn the way it was torn. The four things in that order is the order he did that work in. What is in that wood is real, and it is a set of real pieces put together in an order by something that has no man in it, and it is not a message and it is not anybody, and it is not a comfort, whatever anybody in this room has been calling it since Friday.**"

Nobody said anything for a moment, and what was in the moment was four people and a table and a man who had said a thing out loud and had not said anything else, and about four of the four had understood that the man in the wood had been alive for about a minute on Thursday and had not been alive since about the fourth hour of the afternoon of that day, and the fourth had understood the same thing and had understood where the minute had been spent.

Then the man of about fifty said the second half of it, and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and one and did not move and is one hundred and one.

**"The line decided that and not the man. I want the room to have heard it said that way, because about four people in this holding are going to be told in about four years that somebody in a wood in a season like this one refused his own father, and it is not going to be said as a character. It is going to be said as a piece of paper about a branch that is not there, and the piece of paper is older than the man who read it and the man who read it is not the reason.**"

About four people in that room had already worked out that the man who had put the thing down on Friday and picked it up on Saturday was not a man being hard with himself, and about four had worked out that it made no difference either way, and neither of those two said so, and one of them was the woman who keeps the seed house, who said nothing at all and put her hand flat on the table again and left it there until he had finished.

The room emptied about half an hour afterwards and it did not empty all at once, and the woman who keeps the seed house went out first and did not take the cloths off anything, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling went second and did not say where he was going, and the man of about fifty sat with the second book shut in front of him after the other two had gone, and nobody waited on him.

There is going to be another day in that wood. The work has not stopped and this page is not going to pretend that a thing being disqualified is the end of a season's work, and the next time anybody goes into that standing willow, that session will be entered in a room with the door shut before any of it is done, and the man of about fifty will write the four ruled lines and leave them empty again, and the stop signal is hers and its words are not in this book.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year, and it was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced by walking it and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page, and nothing that was said in that room about a wall where a branch would be is a fact about the ring.

The comfort is not standing and it has not been replaced by anything. There is a fact in this holding now and the fact is that a wood keeps pressure and temperature and season and the order things happened in, and a wall where a branch would be is a wall, and the second of those two is a fact about ground and not about a man, and this page is not going to be improved on and is not going to be made into a better sentence about anybody.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room with the door shut and four people in it, and the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move, and there was no yard this afternoon.

The man of about seventy is twenty mornings off his next fetching and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and he has never once in twenty-two of them asked which number it was, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked him is nil and it is nil, and no page of this holding's book is going to supply a reason for a man in about four years.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the thirteenth, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is two and a third one is not entered and a fourth is not going to be entered, because a thing that is argued out loud in a room is not a thing done in a wood.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the thirteenth, with four people in it, at this holding, on the standing of a disqualification, and not by the man of about fifty, who put it off until he had said it out loud and watched it for two days: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE BOUNDARY DITCH AND THE TWO CULVERTS, ABOUT SIX PEOPLE TO THE FOURTH HOUR, AND THE NEAR CULVERT WAS CLEAR FOR THE THIRTEENTH MORNING RUNNING AND FOUR BARROW-LOADS CAME OUT OF THE FAR ONE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-THIRD FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN ONE HUNDRED DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-FIVE TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-FIVE TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-ONE DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTIETH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-TWO, AND IT IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOUR, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING THIRTY-SEVEN MORNINGS OUT OF THE FIFTY-SEVEN. THERE WERE FOUR PEOPLE IN THE ONE ROOM AT THIS HOLDING AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR WITH THE DOOR SHUT, AND NOT THE NINE. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SPOKE WITH THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT A THING WHICH TAKES AWAY A THING A MAN WANTED IS NOT ONE A MAN IS FIT TO WRITE DOWN WHILE HE STILL WANTS IT, AND THAT IT IS BEING ENTERED THIS EVENING AFTER HE HAS SAID IT OUT LOUD AND WATCHED IT. THE READER OF THIS BODY THEN SAID THAT THE SHAFT IN THIS HOLDING'S OWN LOW GROUND, FOUR HUNDRED YARDS BELOW THE NORTH GATE, IS SUPPOSED TO HAVE HAD NO LIVING BRANCH IN IT, THAT THE LINE SAYING SO IS ON SOMEBODY ELSE'S PAPER AND IS CORRECT IN EVERY PARTICULAR, THAT A WOOD KEEPS WHAT THE GROUND HAS TOUCHED AND DOES NOT INVENT, AND THAT A MAN IN A PLACE WITH NO LIVING BRANCH IN IT CANNOT GET ANYTHING INTO ANY GROUND. HE SAID THAT THE MAN OF THIS HOLDING'S OWN FAMILY WENT INTO THE GROUND EIGHT YEARS AGO FOUR HUNDRED YARDS BELOW THIS NORTH GATE AND DID NOT COME OUT OF IT, AND THAT WHAT CAME OUT OF THE STANDING WILLOW ON THE NINTH IS A SET OF TRUE PIECES OF A MAN WHO LIVED PUT IN AN ORDER BY SOMETHING THAT HAS NO MAN IN IT, AND IS NOT A MESSAGE AND NOT A COMFORT. NOBODY SOFTENED IT AND NOBODY IMPROVED IT. NOBODY WALKED TO IT AND NOBODY WENT NEAR IT. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS TWO AND A THIRD IS NOT ENTERED. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
