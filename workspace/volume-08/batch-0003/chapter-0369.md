# Chapter 369

## Four Days Old

Nothing was on the fourth line on Sunday, the twelfth of this month, when it came round at the second hour, and the fields above Marden waited, and nobody was at the headland above Marden. Forty-three of them, every one of them the fourth line. The rota stands at seventy-two mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and forty-three not, and the two of those go to seventy-two. Twenty-nine taken did not move, and has not moved on any morning of this season, and nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

A field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man. It is on the page again this morning, for the seventh time in a fortnight, and nobody improved it when it was said. The reason it goes in every time the fourth line comes round is not suspicion of anybody. It is that a count which rises by one on a morning when nobody did anything will be asked about in about four years, and the answer has to be in the book and not in the mouth of whoever is holding the book.

That is the twentieth morning the fourth line has come round since the twenty-sixth of a month three months back. It is also the seventh of them since the eighteenth of last month, and the man of about fifty writes both, in that order, on the same line, and he has never given a reason for the second one either.

It went in at about the eighth hour, in the book that keeps the third launder, and the man of about fifty read it back out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and seventy-six hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and thirty-second figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In ninety-nine days the series has risen fifty-five times and fallen forty-four times, and those two add to ninety-nine. It came up off Saturday by nine, and about four people in this holding spent Saturday in a house at the north end of a village four miles off on account of a woman of about forty-four who is on the cart road and about four of them came back without saying what it was about.

A hundred and thirty days, then, without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence went in this morning for the hundred and twenty-ninth of them out of a hundred and thirty-one. The aggregate of the four bodies of households is what it is and the aggregate of the launders is what it is, and no one in this holding has ever put one under the other.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud. The pair stands at **five hundred and sixteen days** and **five hundred and sixty-three**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. The run does not move and this page carries no figure for it, and the man of about fifty has read that pair out loud on four mornings of the last six and has not on two, and neither of those two numbers is a measurement of anything.

The wind was off the north-east and stayed off it and about four people in this holding looked up at the headland from the door of the seed house and none of them went up, and there was a man standing on the road at the bottom of that slope at about the second hour of the afternoon with his hands behind his back looking up at it, and he was there for about the length of a pipe and he did not go up, and nobody in this holding has ever asked him what he was looking at, and the count of times a person has come up that road and stood under that headland and not gone up is not a figure anybody in this holding keeps.

There is no window in this morning. The window of the office's work shut yesterday on the eleventh, six days and not five and not seven, and the next one opens on the sixth of the next month and shuts on the eleventh of that one, and nothing is ever carried out of either, and a holding that has been busy about six days that were never on its table is now going to be busy about the fact that there are no six days at all until the sixth.

About nine people in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon, the register form on the middle table where it has been for twenty-six days and the drawer shut. The light came in low off the south wall, lay along the table, and lay across the five things on it.

The copy of the ledger came down off the shelf about the fifth hour and went back on it about half an hour later, and in between it was on the end of the table and nobody turned it round, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the end of the table with his hat in his hand for the whole of it and did not open the book of his own, and the four lines in it are still four lines with a question on the second of them and no answer and no place for an answer, and the page on it is twenty days old this afternoon, and nobody in that room has asked him about it in twenty days.

The reader of this body said one thing in that room and the reason for it was said first, and that is the hundred and sixty-fifth of them. It is a count of his own keeping and nobody else keeps it, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is a thing in this building that has been in it four days and nobody in this room has said what it is, and I am going to say the reason I am going to say it before I say it, and the reason is that this holding's book is a book of what happened, and what happened includes a man choosing a thing he wanted over a thing he could check, and a book with only the choices in it that came out right is a book about a person and not about a man.**"

**"So here it is and I am not going to have it improved on. On Friday night I had two things in front of me and one of them was the four things in the right order with nothing in between, and the other one was four lines about what a wood keeps. And I took the first one and put the second one down, and I knew at the time that I was doing it, and I did it again yesterday at about the fourth hour of the afternoon with nobody in the building, and yesterday at about the eighth hour I nearly did it a third time and did not.**"

**"That is the whole of what happened. Nobody knew about it. Nobody in this room is going to be told that I am a better man for having said it, because I am not, and a man who is praised for telling the truth about himself in a room is a man who has bought something, and this holding does not sell.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had understood inside a minute that he had thrown something away on Friday and picked it up on Saturday, and about four had understood that the thing he had thrown away was the only piece of the work anybody could check, and neither of those four said anything, and the room went on doing what it had been doing.

The thing that came out of that wood on the ninth is four days old this afternoon. It came out on the ninth and this is the twelfth, and those are four mornings, being the ninth, the tenth, the eleventh and the twelfth, and four is a count of mornings and it is not a count of anything else and it is not a season and it is not an age and nobody in this holding is going to put a unit on it.

What came out of it is in nine people in a room and it is not in this book, and there is no column for it in any of the four books, and the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and this is not one of the two and it is not going to become a third, and a thing that has been in a building for four days without a column is not yet a fact and is not going to be one on Thursday.

Nobody improved that either, and about four people in that room had gone to the window while he was saying it and about four had not, and the four that had gone had not gone for the light.

What came out of that wood is in nine people in a room and it is not in this book, and that is on purpose and not by accident, and the man of about fifty said so out loud in this holding on the third of this month in a room with the door shut, and about four people in that room have thought since the ninth that a thing which is only in nine people is going to be nine different things by the spring, and about four have thought that it is going to be one thing that nine people saw, and neither of those two is in this book either, and the count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is ten and it did not move this afternoon and is not said in a yard.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a decision is not a mark and four days is not a mark and the four accounts of that arm stand at four.

The man of about fifty wrote nothing on Sunday. The copy of the ledger went back on the shelf untuned and the second book went into the satchel and the satchel went on a hook by the door, and the four ruled lines under a session entry are four ruled lines under a session entry and not a column with a man's name at the head of it, and the count of persons with a rootmark in this holding is two and neither of the two is in this room and neither of the two was on the bank on Friday.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in the room at any point on this morning or this afternoon and nobody sent for her and nobody asked her anything, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and it is nil.

At about the ninth hour the man of about fifty read the day sheet out in the yard and there was no window on it to read, and the five things in the five places were the line that came round, the figure for the day, the form on the table and the drawer shut, the man of about seventy not fetched, and the distance to the first, and he put a stroke beside one of the five and left the other four alone and did not say which one, and about four people in that yard could work it out and about four could not.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and there was a yard at about the ninth hour with one stroke put beside one of five things, and nothing wrong was said in that yard, and the count of that does not move. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the twelfth, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is two and a third one is not entered.

THE NUMBER IS FORTY-THREE AND ALL OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE. THE ROTA STANDS AT SEVENTY-TWO MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND FORTY-THREE NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE TAKEN DID NOT MOVE, AND A FIELD THAT WAITED IS A FACT ABOUT A ROTA AND NOT A FACT ABOUT A MAN. THAT IS THE TWENTIETH MORNING OF THE FOURTH LINE SINCE THE TWENTY-SIXTH OF A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK AND THE SEVENTH SINCE THE EIGHTEENTH OF LAST MONTH. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SECOND FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN NINETY-NINE DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-FIVE TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-FOUR TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-ONE, AND IT IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTEEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-THREE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING, AND THE RUN DID NOT MOVE. THERE IS NO WINDOW IN THIS MORNING. THE WINDOW SHUT YESTERDAY ON THE ELEVENTH, SIX DAYS AND NOT FIVE AND NOT SEVEN, AND THE NEXT OPENS ON THE SIXTH OF THE NEXT MONTH, AND NOTHING IS EVER CARRIED OUT OF EITHER. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND HAS BEEN FOR TWENTY-SIX DAYS. THE READER OF THIS BODY SPOKE WITH THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT ON FRIDAY NIGHT HE TOOK ONE OF TWO THINGS IN FRONT OF HIM AND PUT THE OTHER DOWN AND KNEW AT THE TIME THAT HE WAS DOING IT, AND DID IT AGAIN YESTERDAY AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR WITH NOBODY IN THE BUILDING, AND NEARLY DID IT A THIRD TIME AT ABOUT THE EIGHTH HOUR AND DID NOT. NOBODY KNEW ABOUT IT. WHAT CAME OUT OF THE STANDING WILLOW ON THE NINTH IS FOUR DAYS OLD THIS AFTERNOON, THOSE BEING THE NINTH, THE TENTH, THE ELEVENTH AND THE TWELFTH, AND FOUR IS A COUNT OF MORNINGS AND NOT OF ANYTHING ELSE, AND IT IS IN NINE PEOPLE IN A ROOM AND NOT IN THIS BOOK, AND NO COLUMN HAS BEEN RULED FOR IT. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND THIS IS NOT ONE OF THE TWO. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS TWO. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
