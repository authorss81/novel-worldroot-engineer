# Chapter 366

## Further In

The ninth of this month was a Thursday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the boundary ditch and the two culverts took about six people until about the fourth hour, and the far culvert was about two-thirds full of the same leaf and the six of them put five barrow-loads out of it. The near culvert was clear for the ninth morning running, and the man of about fifty has a page in a book of his own on which he has written that number, and he has never told anybody the page is there.

The window of the office's work is two days off this morning and not tomorrow. It is six days and not five and not seven, it shuts on the eleventh whether anybody has used it or not, and no work is ever carried out of it, and about four people in this holding have been treating the six days as a deadline since the sixth and about four have not, and a window is not a deadline.

About the eighth hour the ninth went into the book that keeps the third launder, and the man of about fifty had it read back out of the other book with the frayed corner before either of them was put away, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and sixty-three hundredweight.**

Down five off Wednesday, and the six of them had been standing in a ditch since the second hour. It is the hundred and twenty-ninth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first of it, and it has fallen, and of the ninety-six days of it fifty-three were rises and forty-three were falls.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households is where it was a hundred and twenty-seven days ago, and the sentence for it is on the page this morning for the hundred and twenty-sixth of them out of a hundred and twenty-eight. It is a sentence about four bodies of households. The four launders are counted somewhere else entirely and have never been added to it and are not going to be.

The two day-minus rules were said out loud at about the seventh hour of the morning and before either number, one man taking the first board and the other taking the second, so that neither of them is trusted with both. They stand at **five hundred and thirteen days** and **five hundred and sixty**. The drawer is not opened. That is thirty-five mornings out of fifty-three counted from the seventeenth of a month two months back.

About nine people came up the five miles of cart road at about the second hour of the afternoon, and the light was already off the west end of the standing wood when they got there, which is the whole of the reason there is an hour in that wood and not a morning, and the ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced by walking it and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page and not one of the nine put a foot inside it.

The channel along the inside of the line of the stems was on its second day of the two days of leaf, and the six of them had it a third of the way along and the rakes going, and the water in it was about a foot deep at the near end and warm enough that nobody had put a hand in it. The light lay along the bed of the channel and stopped at the line of the stems and did not go into them, and the reason a man can only work in that wood for an hour on a day is not that the wood is dangerous and is that by the fifth hour there is no light in it.

The session was in the standing willow outside the ring and not inside it.

**THE HOUR, AS ENTERED YESTERDAY IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT BEFORE ANY OF IT WAS DONE: FROM THE FOURTH HOUR UNTIL THE FIFTH, AND WHICHEVER CAME FIRST, AND NOT A MINUTE PAST THE FIFTH.**

The man of about fifty stood on the bank above the channel at about nine feet off the near end of the wood with the second book open on a board and the pen in his hand, and he was the independent witness and the entry said he was, and he did not come into the wood and he was not asked to, and the whole hour of it was in his sight from where he stood. He kept his eyes on the wood and not on the two people in it, and about four of the nine on the bank noticed that he was doing that on purpose and about four thought he was watching the light.

Tova Reed was at the other end of the hour and she had come up the road on the morning of the day and had not been sent for and had not asked to be fetched, and about four people in that wood had worked out that she had come a day and a half of cart road for an hour and about four had not, and nobody in that wood asked her anything.

He had the near end of the channel and a rake in it and he stayed on the near end. There is a question on a page in a book the size of his hand. It has been seventeen days, it has no answer on it, and it has no place for an answer. Nobody asked him about it this afternoon. The book was not in that wood and nobody has said where it was.

One thing was said there by the man of about thirty-one of Silling, and he put the reason for saying it first, which is the practice in this holding and is not a practice he learned here. Thirty-six is the count of it in this holding's history. It is his own count, nobody has thanked him for it, and he was not asking to be.

**"I am going to say the reason I am not going to ask anybody in that wood what came back, and the reason is that I have got a question on a page in a book of my own and I wrote it in a fortnight when I did not know what I was asking, and a man who opens a page he wrote himself and finds out he does not know what he is asking does not get it back by asking somebody else in a wood.**"

**"So I am not going to ask today and I am not going to ask on Friday and I am not going to say the reason again, and if the day ever comes when I do not know the reason, that will be the day somebody in this holding has a right to write down that I could not give one.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that wood had wanted to ask him and about four had not, and the four that wanted to had been wanting to since the twenty-seventh of last month, and the four that had not had worked out that a page with a question on it and no place for an answer is a page a man is allowed to keep.

What came back was not a season and it was not an hour. It was one step further in than the outside of a stem, and what was in it was a man.

There was a place with walls close enough on both sides that the light came off one and not the other, and a lamp on a chain throwing one moving edge of light, and a wheel on a stand, and hands on the wheel that were older than his own with a torn cuff at the wrist, and the hands went through an arc and stopped, and the hands went through an arc and stopped, and the four of those things happened in the same order and then the four of them happened again, and again, and nothing came between one of them and the next.

It did not come in sentences. There was no sentence in it and no name in it and no lamp burning in it, because a wood keeps pressure and temperature and season and the order things happened in and it does not keep the two ends of an arc at once. He could not have said afterwards how long it had been there. He knows what was in it and he cannot put the two halves together, and that is the whole of what a wood does and it is not a small thing.

The cost of it was a headache that came back about the fifth hour and did not go, and about a quarter of an hour at the end of the hour that is not on any line in this holding's book and that nobody has entered and that this page is not going to enter, and the man of about fifty put his pen down at about the fifth as the entry said he would and he wrote nothing else.

Then, on the line under the hour, and in the same hand, he wrote one thing and left it with no figure against it: A HEADACHE FROM THE FIFTH HOUR, NOT GONE AT THE SIXTH. That is on the page and it is a line and not a column, and the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and it is not going to become three because a man had a headache in a wood, and about four people in that wood had wanted a figure on that line more than anything else that afternoon and about four had not.

They came down the five miles of cart road at about the sixth hour in the dark with the wind off the north behind them, and about four of the nine noticed on the road that he was walking as though the ground were doing something and about four had worked out inside a mile that what was wrong with it was not his legs.

And then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in any of it, and nobody sent for her and nobody asked her anything, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and it is nil. The key is in a pocket on the other side of that pocket and it was not used and nobody looked at the pocket, and the valve it opens has not been opened this month, and the two-foot gap at the kerb is as open as it was on the eleventh of the first month and nothing has gone into it in this season and nothing is going to.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a wood and a cart road and not a yard. The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a headache that does not go is not a mark and a man in a wood is not a mark and a face is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and nothing that came out of that wood is a fifth of them.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is two, and the second was entered yesterday before the thing and not after it, and it has now been carried out as entered. The use log stands at fifteen lines and the reason was not given. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the ninth, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the eighth hour of the evening. The register form was on the middle table where it has been for twenty-three days and the drawer was shut, and the light came in low off the south wall and lay along the table and across the five things on it and not across anybody's hands, and about four people in that room had been waiting since the sixth hour to be told something and had not been told anything and were not going to be.

Nothing was cut and nothing was brought down out of that wood this afternoon, and the eleven acres are where they were on the twenty-seventh of last month, and the copy of the ledger was not on any table at that place.

THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK IS TWO DAYS OFF AND NOT TOMORROW, SIX DAYS AND NOT FIVE AND NOT SEVEN. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SIXTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN NINETY-SIX DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-THREE TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVEN DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SIXTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT, AND NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING THIRTY-FIVE MORNINGS OUT OF THE FIFTY-THREE. THE SESSION WAS ENTERED YESTERDAY WITH THE DOOR SHUT AND WAS CARRIED OUT FROM THE FOURTH HOUR TO THE FIFTH, AS ENTERED. IT WAS IN THE STANDING WILLOW OUTSIDE THE RING OF BARE GROUND, AND THE RING IS BARE IN ITS ELEVENTH YEAR AND WAS NOT WALKED AND NOT EXPLAINED. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY STOOD ON THE BANK AT NINE FEET OFF THE NEAR END WITH A BOOK AND KEPT HIS EYES ON THE WOOD. THE STOP SIGNAL WAS NOT SAID OUT LOUD. IT IS ENTERED ON THE LINE WITH THE REST OF THE DAY AND NOT A COLUMN. THE COST WAS A HEADACHE THAT CAME BACK AND DID NOT GO, ON A LINE WITH NO FIGURE, AND A QUARTER OF AN HOUR AT THE END OF THE HOUR THAT IS NOT ON A LINE. THE OTHER END OF THE HOUR WAS HELD BY A PERSON WHO CAME UP THE ROAD ON THE MORNING OF THE DAY AND WAS NOT SENT FOR. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING SPOKE WITH THE REASON FIRST AND SAID HE WAS NOT GOING TO ASK, AND HIS PAGE IS SEVENTEEN DAYS OLD WITH NO ANSWER. NOTHING WAS CUT AND NOTHING WAS BROUGHT DOWN OUT OF THAT WOOD. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
