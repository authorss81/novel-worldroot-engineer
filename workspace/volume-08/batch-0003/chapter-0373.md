# Chapter 373

## Four Things On One Morning

On Thursday, the sixteenth of this month, the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour, and the fields above Marden waited, and nobody was at the headland above them. Forty-four of them, and every one of them the fourth line. The rota stands at seventy-three mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and forty-four not, and twenty-nine and forty-four are seventy-three. Twenty-nine taken did not move, and has not moved on any morning of this season, and nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

A field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man. It is on the page again this morning, for the eighth time in a fortnight, and the sentence has not been improved in a fortnight and is not going to be improved. A man who raises a number by one on a morning when nothing was done is a man somebody will question in about four years, and this holding intends to have the answer written down already.

That is the twenty-first morning the fourth line has come round since it started coming round on the twenty-sixth of a month three months back, and it is the eighth of them since the eighteenth of last month. This morning is the sixteenth of this month and the seventeenth of it is tomorrow, and the two of those are a day apart and not four days apart, and about four people in this holding have had the wrong one in their heads since Monday and about four have not, and neither of those four is going to say so out loud.

The frost was on the low ground again at about the second hour and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the road with the barrow at about the ninth, turned it at the top of the yard and went home, and the four lines in his own book have a question on the second of them and the page on it is twenty-four days old and it has no answer on it and no place for an answer, and nobody has asked him about it in twenty-four days, and the barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road and it is a floor and not a ceiling and it did not rise this morning.

At about the eighth hour the man of about fifty had the other book with the frayed corner open and read back out of it what had gone into the book that keeps the third launder for the sixteenth, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and eighty-four hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and thirty-sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. The series has stood at this for one hundred and three days now, rising on fifty-seven of them and falling on forty-six. It came up off Wednesday by nine, and the man of about fifty wrote the nine in the margin in a hand that went uphill to the right and did not put a row against it, and there are seven of those nines in a book of his own with no note against any of them and he has never been asked and has not offered.

A hundred and thirty-four days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence saying so written this morning for the hundred and thirty-third of them out of a hundred and thirty-five. That is not the aggregate of the launders. The two have never been added and they are not added this morning, and the distance between them is a fact about this holding and not a gap in a record.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud. The pair stands at **five hundred and twenty days** and **five hundred and sixty-seven**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. The run does not move and this page carries no figure for it, and it is the fifth morning out of the last six on which the man of about fifty has not read that pair, and about four people in this holding have said nothing about that and about four have said something to each other about it, and the count of questions a body can put to a habit and not get an answer to is not a number anybody keeps.

At about the fourth hour of the afternoon the well on that shelf was read, and it is four hundred miles and four days off, and it went into the entry for this holding as a day and not as a figure.

That is the whole of it. There is no figure against it in this book and there is not going to be one and nobody has supplied a unit for it in eleven years. The count of entries in this holding's book that carry a day and no figure was six on Tuesday and is seven now, and that is the only thing about that well this holding's book has ever been able to hold, and the count moved by one because a person read a well on a Thursday, and a person reading a well is not the same thing as a person being able to say what came out of it, and about four people in this holding know that and about four do not.

A reading of that well is a person going to a wall about four hundred miles off, finding the mark that a wet string and a nail have left on a stone, and wringing the string out over a bucket and putting the bucket outside the wall and not looking at the other three houses while she does it, and that is what the fourth hour of this afternoon was, and a person who does that once a month for eleven years has not done it once a month for this holding and did not come here to do it.

Nobody was asked anything at that far end and nobody has been asked anything there in eleven years, and the count of questions asked in that direction is nil and it is nil. The wall at that place carries fourteen marks and gave none of them up this afternoon and may not carry a fifteenth, and the four feet of shortfall are not decomposed, and there is no entry anywhere in this holding for that well coming back.

About the second hour of the afternoon the barrow came in at that place with four of them in it, and the load went on the sling and the sling went up and the beam came down slow, and the four bodies of households that weigh it are at Havergate and Low Wether and Marden and Silling, and not one of them is a member of the cooperative and none of them was made one this afternoon.

The return went in on the steelyard on the wall of that building with no roof, and the instrument has hung on that wall for longer than this holding has had a book and is the same instrument it was on the twenty-eighth of last month.

**Three hundred and five hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-third return, and the twenty-third name-pair in eleven years, and it is eleven hundredweight above the thirty-second, which was two hundred and ninety-four on the seventh of this month. Off two hundred and ninety-four came the dial, and the reader of this body read it out loud, and then read it back out of the second book, and the two are the same.

A name went into BY, and then a name went into WITNESSED BY, and then a name went into BY again, and a name went into WITNESSED BY again, and so on, one at a time, by each of the four bodies of households standing where the dial was in sight, and the four of them did it in the order they arrived and not in any order anybody chose for them.

And then it was reconciled twice, out of two figures the man of about fifty had written on a day sheet at about the fifth hour of the morning and had not told anybody he had written them.

**Two hundred and fifty-nine plus forty-six. And two hundred and fourteen plus ninety-one.** Two halves, and not the whole, and they have not been added together and are not going to be added together this afternoon. The word using is on the page of this holding's book: this holding is using that derivation and is not refusing it. A man who refuses a derivation is a man who thinks the number is in doubt, and the number is on a dial that has been on that wall longer than this holding has had a book.

The second rule was left blank again this afternoon, and this is the seventeenth time in eleven years it has been. Nothing went into it and nothing was said about it. The count of returns at which it has been left blank is seventeen, and it stood at sixteen on the seventh of this month, which is nine mornings back. A count of blanks is not the count of a man and has never been the count of a man.

The form with the four lines is in a drawer face up about four miles and a bit west of that wall, and its first line carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, and that is the figure of the twenty-second return and not of this one and not of the thirty-second, and nobody wrote a heading on it and nobody filled the other three lines.

And then about six people were in the one room at that place that has a door on it, at about the sixth hour, and the door was shut.

One thing, said by the man of about fifty, and the reason for it before the thing. That is a hundred and two in the history of this holding. Nobody else keeps the number. He was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There are four things on today's sheet and I am going to give the reason I am going to enter all four in one line before I say what they are, and the reason is that this holding has been wrong about this before and a person in about four years is going to read a page with four things on it and see a pattern, and a page with four things on it is a page and not a pattern, and I said that out loud in this room on the first of a month and I am saying it again because it has happened again.**"

**"The fourth line came round and there was nobody on it and the number went up by one and that is a rota. A well four days off was read and nothing came back that anybody can put in this book and that is a reading. A return went in at eleven more than the last and that is a dial. And the month did not turn and is not going to turn for another fifteen mornings and that is a calendar.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had wanted a fourth thing on the list and about four had not, and the four that wanted it had wanted it since the third of this month, when a session was entered and a reading came back and a headache went on a line, and the four that had not had worked out that those three had been put on one page already by two different people and had survived a fortnight without anybody turning them into anything.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room with a door on it and not a yard, and there has been no yard in this holding since Sunday and there is not going to be one this evening. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth, and the next of those is the thirtieth of this month, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next fetching is on the first of a month, which is fifteen mornings off this one and not tomorrow. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen and it does not fall again until the first.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a dial is not a mark and a day with no figure against it is not a mark and a month that does not turn is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the sixteenth, in about six people, at the node, on the standing of four things on one morning, and not by the man of about fifty: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND THERE WAS NOBODY AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, THE NUMBER BEING FORTY-FOUR, AND THE ROTA STANDS AT SEVENTY-THREE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND FORTY-FOUR NOT, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN UNMOVED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIXTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN ONE HUNDRED AND THREE DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-SEVEN TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-SIX TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FOUR DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-THIRD MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIVE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SEVEN, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD, AND THE RUN DID NOT MOVE. AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR THE WELL ON THAT SHELF WAS READ, FOUR HUNDRED MILES AND FOUR DAYS OFF, AND IT WENT INTO THE ENTRY AS A DAY AND NOT AS A FIGURE, AND NOBODY HAS SUPPLIED A UNIT FOR IT IN ELEVEN YEARS. THE COUNT OF ENTRIES IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK THAT CARRY A DAY AND NO FIGURE WAS SIX ON TUESDAY AND IS SEVEN NOW. NOBODY WAS ASKED ANYTHING AT THAT FAR END IN ELEVEN YEARS AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS ASKED THERE IS NIL. THE WALL THERE CARRIES FOURTEEN MARKS AND GAVE NONE UP, AND THERE IS NO ENTRY IN THIS HOLDING FOR THAT WELL COMING BACK. THE THIRTY-THIRD NINTH-DAY RETURN WENT IN ON A STEELYARD AND READ THREE HUNDRED AND FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT OUT LOUD AND READ IT BACK. IT IS THE TWENTY-THIRD NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS AND ELEVEN ABOVE THE THIRTY-SECOND, AND THE TWO WAYS IT COMES ARE TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE PLUS FORTY-SIX AND TWO HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN PLUS NINETY-ONE, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT, AND THE TWO HALVES ARE NOT ADDED. A NAME WENT INTO BY AND A NAME WENT INTO WITNESSED BY, ONE AT A TIME, BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS. THE SECOND RULE STAYED BLANK AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS BEEN LEFT BLANK IS SEVENTEEN, IT HAVING BEEN SIXTEEN ON THE SEVENTH, AND A COUNT OF BLANKS IS NOT THE COUNT OF A MAN. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SPOKE WITH THE REASON FIRST AND ENTERED ALL FOUR ON ONE LINE, BEING A ROTA, A READING, A DIAL AND A CALENDAR, SO THAT A PAGE WITH FOUR THINGS ON IT IS A PAGE AND NOT A PATTERN. THE MONTH DID NOT TURN AND WILL NOT FOR ANOTHER FIFTEEN MORNINGS. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE NEXT OF THOSE IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
