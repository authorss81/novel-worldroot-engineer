# Chapter 367

## What The Wood Keeps

The tenth of this month was a Friday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the inside face of the west dyke from the sluice up as far as the black post took four people from the second hour until about the fifth and a half. The slop at about the first hundred yards had gone hard enough in the two days of dry to take a barrow wheel without it going through, and the four of them got the line done in about half an hour less than the month has been taking, and nobody wrote down that either, and the man at the front of it said that a hard slop is a gift and that a gift is not a reason, and the three behind him agreed with one half of that.

The window of the office's work is one morning off this morning. It is six days and not five and not seven, and it shuts on the eleventh whether anybody has used it or not, and it is not extended in a season and no window has ever been extended in a season in the four years this holding has kept a book, and no work is ever carried out of it, and about four people in this holding have been treating the six days as a deadline and about four have not, and a deadline is a thing somebody has to do inside and there is nobody in this holding who has to do anything inside those six days.

The figure for the tenth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and seventy-two hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and thirtieth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In ninety-seven days the series has risen fifty-four times and fallen forty-three times, and those two add to ninety-seven. It came up off Thursday by nine, and the four of them had been on the same hundred yards of wall since the second hour and about four of them could not have told a nine from a five.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and twenty-eight days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and twenty-seventh morning out of one hundred and twenty-nine. That sentence is not the aggregate of the four launders and the two have never been added.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud. The pair stands at **five hundred and fourteen days** and **five hundred and sixty-one**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. The run does not move and this page carries no figure for it, and it has been done on three mornings now out of the last five, and a man who reads a pair out loud on some mornings and not on others is a man with a habit and a habit is not a measurement.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon. The register form was on the middle table where it has been for twenty-four days, the drawer was shut, and the woman who keeps the seed house had gone out to the cart shed about the second hour and had come back in with cold hands and had put them on the seed sacks for a while before she did anything else, and about four people in that room had noticed and about four had not.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and sixty-four, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I said a thing out loud in a wood on Thursday afternoon and about four of the nine of you heard it and about four did not, and I am going to say it again in a room with the door shut, and I am giving the reason I am going to say it again before I say it, and the reason is that a line in a book with a man in it on it and no account of what kind of man is a thing that becomes a story by the third winter, and I have watched this holding do that to a sentence once and I am not going to let it do it to me.**"

**"A wood keeps four things. Pressure, temperature, season, and the order things happened in. That is the whole of what it keeps and it is not a short list, and there is a thing in that list that a person hearing it for the first time will take for a fifth thing, and I have been that person twice this week and I am saying so in front of you before either of you has a chance to notice me being it again.**"

**"The order things happened in is not a time. There is no now in a wood. What came back on Thursday was four things in one order and then the four again with nothing in between, and that is not a man doing a job across a stretch of anything. That is a set of pieces in a fixed order, and a man in a room saying the word man about a set of pieces in a fixed order is doing a thing I have not got a column for, and I have not got a column for it because there is no such column and I am not ruling one.**"

Nobody said that was right. About four people in that room had understood in about four seconds that he had just called a comfort a set of pieces in a fixed order, and about four had understood that he had come in with a thing and put it down on the table in front of nine people and had told them what kind of thing it was, and neither of those four had the same idea about what he had done and about four of the nine had not worked out that there were two ideas in the room.

About four people in that room had worked out inside a minute that the man of this holding's own family was in it, and about four had not, and nobody asked the man who had brought it in whether the face in it was anybody, and nobody in that room said the word comfort, and the page says it because the page is not the room.

It was the largest thing that has come into this building in four years and it is on no page of this holding's book, and there is no column in any of the four books for it and nobody is going to rule one, and the man of about fifty has not written it down and was not asked to and did not offer, and the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and this is not one of the two and is not going to become a third.

The copy of the ledger came down off the shelf about the fifth hour and went back on it about half an hour later, and in between it was on the end of the table and nobody turned it round. It is ninety-two rows long with five columns with a head on them and a sixth with nothing at the head of it, and the five rows on line forty-one were corrected once in the second month of this season by a man with a knife and not by a clerk, and nobody corrected them a second time this afternoon, and about four people in that room had looked at the sixth column and about four had not and the column has had nothing at the head of it for four years.

The drawer is four feet from the middle table along the same board and it is shut, and in it are the form with four lines with one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight on the first of them, and a sheet of district paper with four columns and a heading that claims a corridor and is correct in every particular of it, and an order signed on the third of last month by a warden which is valid and which opened nothing, and a memorandum dated the twentieth of a month three months back with eleven lines on it, and six other documents. Any two of them set side by side are a different document from the one it is set beside, and nobody in this holding has ever put two of them together, and the eleventh line of that memorandum is not paraphrased and is not improved on and is not going to be quoted a second time in this building.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the end of the table for the whole of it and did not open the book of his own, and the four lines in it are still four lines with a question on the second of them and no answer and no place for an answer, and the page on it is eighteen days old this afternoon, and nobody in that room has asked him about it in eighteen days and nobody is going to.

The man of about fifty said one thing and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-nine and did not move this afternoon and is ninety-nine.

**"I keep four books and I have entered a summer, and I have entered an hour, and I have entered a headache and a quarter of an hour, and I am not going to enter a man. If a person brings me a man I can put in a column I will do it, and I have not got one, and I would rather be told on a Tuesday that I have not got one than find it out in about four years from a page somebody else wrote.**"

Nobody improved that either.

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a man who had brought a thing into a room and told nine people what kind of thing it was, and about four of them had heard the telling and about four had heard the bringing, and the two of those were not the same four.

Nobody in that room said whether the bridge is on, and nobody in that room can say, and this holding has no instrument of any kind that says, and the count of questions asked in that direction is nil and it is nil, and a second thing has been running for about forty days of which nobody can say who holds the far end, and the count of questions asked in that direction is nil and it is nil, and it is not the bridge and this page is not going to call it anything else.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the tenth, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is two and the second one is entered and was carried out as entered, and a third one is not entered and is not going to be entered because a man has come back from a wood with something in him.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a headache is not a mark and a thing in a wood is not a mark and a man in a wood is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and the yard at about the ninth hour yesterday was the last time five things were read out of one and nothing has been said in a yard since. The form from the office that keeps a register was not filled and not refused and not improved and is on the middle table and not in the drawer and has been for twenty-four days, and being on a table for twenty-four days is not a decision and the page is not going to make it one.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the tenth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of what a wood keeps, and not by the man of about fifty, who was asked nothing and entered nothing: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE INSIDE FACE OF THE WEST DYKE FROM THE SLUICE UP AS FAR AS THE BLACK POST, FOUR PEOPLE UNTIL ABOUT THE FIFTH AND A HALF, AND THE SLOP AT ABOUT THE FIRST HUNDRED YARDS HAD GONE HARD ENOUGH TO TAKE A BARROW WHEEL. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK IS ONE MORNING OFF THIS MORNING, SIX DAYS AND NOT FIVE AND NOT SEVEN, AND IT SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH WHETHER ANYBODY HAS USED IT OR NOT, AND NO WORK IS CARRIED OUT OF IT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTIETH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN NINETY-SEVEN DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-FOUR TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-THREE TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVENTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE, AND IT IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-ONE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING, AND THE RUN DID NOT MOVE AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR IT. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND NOT IN THE DRAWER AND HAS BEEN FOR TWENTY-FOUR DAYS, AND IT IS NOT FILLED AND NOT REFUSED AND NOT IMPROVED. THE READER OF THIS BODY SPOKE WITH THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT A WOOD KEEPS PRESSURE, TEMPERATURE, SEASON, AND THE ORDER THINGS HAPPENED IN, AND THAT THE ORDER THINGS HAPPENED IN IS NOT A TIME AND THAT THERE IS NO NOW IN A WOOD, AND THAT WHAT CAME BACK ON THE NINTH WAS FOUR THINGS IN ONE ORDER AND THEN THE FOUR AGAIN WITH NOTHING IN BETWEEN, AND THAT IT IS ENTERED ON THE LINE WITH THE REST OF THE DAY AND IS NOT A COLUMN AND THAT NO COLUMN IS GOING TO BE RULED. THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING HAS HANDED OUT THAT IT CANNOT CHECK IS TWO AND THIS IS NOT ONE OF THE TWO AND IS NOT GOING TO BECOME A THIRD. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY SPOKE WITHOUT THE REASON FIRST AND SAID THAT HE KEEPS FOUR BOOKS AND IS NOT GOING TO ENTER A MAN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS TWO AND THE SECOND ONE IS ENTERED AND WAS CARRIED OUT AS ENTERED, AND A THIRD IS NOT ENTERED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
