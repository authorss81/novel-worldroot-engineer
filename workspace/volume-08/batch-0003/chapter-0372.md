# Chapter 372

## What And When

On Wednesday, the fifteenth of this month, the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The two gates and the about six feet of wall between them took four people an hour and a quarter. The man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it, as he has not done in eleven years.

The toe at the second of the three places on that line is up about a finger's width again and the four of them put a spade in it and a hand's depth of stone, and the man at the front of it said that a toe which comes up in a fortnight is a toe nobody is going to be told about twice, and the three behind him did not answer him.

At about the fifth hour the man of about seventy-four with a stick came as far as the north gate and turned round there and went back down the lane, and nobody in this holding has ever asked him where he is going.

At about the eighth hour the fifteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder, and the man of about fifty read it out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and seventy-five hundredweight.**

It has fallen, and it is the hundred and thirty-fifth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first. A hundred and two days of it: fifty-six rises, forty-six falls. Down five off Tuesday, and the four of them had been on the same six feet of wall since the second hour, and about four of them could not have told a five from a two.

A hundred and thirty-three days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, the sentence for it written this morning as the hundred and thirty-second of them out of a hundred and thirty-four. It is not the aggregate of the four launders, and the four launders have never been added to it, and there is no page in this holding on which that addition would look like anything but an error.

The two day-minus rules were said out loud at about the seventh hour of the morning and before any figure of the day, one man to a board, with about four people in the room and nobody trusted with both. **Five hundred and nineteen days**, and **five hundred and sixty-six**, forty-seven apart. The drawer with the rest of them is shut and is to stay shut. Thirty-eight mornings out of fifty-nine since the seventeenth of a month two months back.

At about the seventh hour of that same morning, in a room with the door shut and about four people in it, the count of sessions entered in this holding's book went from two to three, and it stopped being two before any of it was done, and the entry carries the day, which is the fifteenth, and the hour, which is about the fifth, and the four of them.

**THE PURPOSE: TO READ AGAIN IN THE STANDING WILLOW AT THE NODE, FURTHER IN THAN THE NINTH OF THIS MONTH, AND TO TAKE OUT OF IT WHAT IT KEEPS ABOUT WHAT THE ENGINE MEANS TO TAKE OFF AND WHEN IT MEANS TO DO IT, AND TO BRING BACK NOTHING ELSE AND TO BRING BACK NOTHING OUT OF IT. THE DURATION: FROM THE FOURTH HOUR UNTIL THE FIFTH, AND WHICHEVER OF THE TWO COMES FIRST, AND NOT A MINUTE PAST THE FIFTH. THE STOP SIGNAL: THERE IS ONE, IT IS HERS, IT IS NOT ENTERED HERE, AND IT IS NOT GOING TO BE, AND THE REASON IS THE SAME REASON AS BEFORE AND IT IS THE REASON THIS BOOK IS CARRIED BETWEEN ROOMS. THE INDEPENDENT WITNESS: THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO IS NOT AT EITHER END, WHO HAS BEEN TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND HAS NOT BEEN TOLD WHAT IT IS, AND WHO IS NOT GOING TO BE TOLD.**

Under the four of them he wrote two words and the hour, being A CONDITION WAS STATED, and he left the four ruled lines under them empty, and nobody in that room asked him about them and he was not thanked for not being told and did not expect to be.

The four people in that room at the seventh hour were the man of about fifty, the reader of this body, Tova Reed and the man of about thirty-one of Silling, and the last of those sat with his hat on his knee and was not asked anything, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked him about a page in a book of the size of a hand is nil and it is nil, and the page on it is twenty-three days old.

About nine people went up the five miles of cart road at about the second hour of the afternoon. The channel along the inside of the line of the stems was finished, both days of it, and the six of them who came for the leaf put rakes down at about the third hour and did not pick them up again, and the light was already off the west end of the wood when the nine of them got there, which is why the hour is an hour.

The session was in the standing willow outside the ring of bare ground and not inside it, and the ring is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced by walking it and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page. The man of about fifty stood on the bank with the second book open on a board and the pen in his hand and kept his eyes on the wood.

She was at the other end of the hour again. She came up the road on the morning of the day, unsent for, and had not asked to be fetched. About four people in that wood had worked out that this was the second time in a season she had made that road, and about four had not.

What came back was not a season and it was not an hour and it was not a man. It was a list.

It came in the order the things happened in, which is the only order a wood has, and it came without a word on it, and it was a line for each place with an hour against each line, and there are nineteen lines on it, and every one of the nineteen is a place where the Crown Engine means to take something off a branch, and every one of the nineteen has a season on it, and eight of the nineteen have a day and an hour as well.

It says what and it says when. That is the whole of what it says.

And then about nine people were in the seed house from about the seventh hour of the evening, and the light was off the south wall by the time it started, and the reader of this body wrote it out in his own hand on a sheet of this holding's own paper, and Tova Reed read it over his shoulder and did not touch the pen, and the man of about fifty copied the hour off the session book and put the two against each other and read the two of them back.

One thing was said then by the reader of this body, and the reason for saying it was said first, and the count of that in the history of this holding is a hundred and sixty-seven. It is his count. He was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to say the one thing that is not on that sheet and I am going to give the reason I am going to say it before I say it, and the reason is that a page which says what and when and stops there reads in about four years as a man who did not go on, and I would rather it read as a thing that is not on the page than as a man who stopped.**"

**"It does not say whose order it is. Not one of the nineteen lines has a name on it and not one of them has an office on it, and there is nothing in the wood at the node to find out with and there is not going to be anything found out with in about four years. And I want that said once, in a room, and then said never again in this building: that is a limit of the object. It is not a refusal of the question, and this holding has asked the question four times across four years and has not been answered once, and it is not going to be answered by me refusing to ask it a fifth time.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had wanted the fifth question asked in that room on that evening and about four had not, and the four that had wanted it had wanted it since the sixth of last month, and about four of the nine had noticed that he had said the words *asked the question four times* out loud, which nobody in this holding had done in a room before, and about four had not noticed.

Nobody asked it a fifth time. The sheet has nineteen lines on it and a head at the top that says what a wood keeps and not what the sheet is, and it is this holding's own paper and not office paper, and it is in a drawer and not in the middle table, and about four people in this holding will read it in about four years and about four will not, and it is the same shape as the sheet on district paper that came out of the last window in every particular except the four columns and the heading at the top, and neither of them has been put beside the other.

The writing out took about an hour and a half and the man of about fifty checked it against the session book twice, and the second time he found a place that had been read as two places and the reader of this body went back into it on Friday and it is one place, and the nineteen has stayed nineteen and nobody has entered the correction as a figure.

The count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is ten and it did not move this evening and is not said in a yard. The man of the north row with the cough has not been asked anything in this month and his two counts are not printed in this book, and his refusal in an earlier month is not a fifth account of anybody's arm. The number of times a bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move and there was no yard. Nobody thanked anybody.

The pulse has two figures in it and no name and is not read a third time and is not named. The use log stands at fifteen lines and a sheet of this holding's own paper is not a use and gets no line, and the reason the log stands at fifteen lines is not in this book and is not going to be given again in a season.

Four inches along the inside of the left forearm, branching twice, and the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning. A list is not a mark. A session is not a mark. The four accounts of that arm stand at four.

The register form is on the middle table where it has been for twenty-nine days, and it is of last month from the first of this month, and the paper from the office that keeps a register is a real document and is correct and is not an answer to a question nobody in this holding has put to it.

Fifty-two is what the seventh column of the well house door stands at, and it is not read. The door is read on the first and on the thirtieth, and this morning was the fifteenth. The other six columns have nothing at the head of them. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, with the next of them sixteen mornings off this one. The compost line is paid and the run stands at nineteen not discharged in nineteen.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the fifteenth, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a list of nineteen lines, and not by the reader of this body, who wrote it out in his own hand and said once why no name is on it: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, TWO GATES AND SIX FEET OF WALL, FOUR PEOPLE, AN HOUR AND A QUARTER, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING STOOD AT THE SECOND GATE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIFTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWO DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-SIX TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-SIX TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-THREE DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SECOND MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FOUR, AND NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND NINETEEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING THIRTY-EIGHT OF THE FIFTY-NINE. AT THE SEVENTH HOUR OF THE MORNING, IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT, THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK WENT FROM TWO TO THREE BEFORE ANY OF IT WAS DONE, AND THE ENTRY CARRIES THE DAY, THE HOUR, A PURPOSE, A DURATION, A STOP SIGNAL WHICH IS HERS AND WHOSE WORDS ARE NOT ENTERED, AND AN INDEPENDENT WITNESS WHO HAS BEEN TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND NOT WHAT IT IS, AND UNDER THE FOUR HE WROTE TWO WORDS AND THE HOUR, BEING A CONDITION WAS STATED, AND LEFT THE FOUR RULED LINES EMPTY. IT WAS IN THE STANDING WILLOW OUTSIDE THE RING OF BARE GROUND, AND THE RING IS BARE IN ITS ELEVENTH YEAR AND WAS NOT WALKED AND NOT EXPLAINED. WHAT CAME BACK WAS A LIST AND NOT A SEASON AND NOT AN HOUR AND NOT A MAN, BEING A LINE FOR EACH PLACE WITH AN HOUR AGAINST IT, AND THERE ARE NINETEEN LINES ON IT, AND EVERY ONE OF THEM IS A PLACE WHERE THE ENGINE MEANS TO TAKE SOMETHING OFF A BRANCH AND EVERY ONE HAS A SEASON, AND EIGHT OF THE NINETEEN HAVE A DAY AND AN HOUR AS WELL. IT SAYS WHAT AND IT SAYS WHEN AND IT DOES NOT SAY WHOSE ORDER IT IS, AND NOT ONE LINE HAS A NAME ON IT OR AN OFFICE ON IT, AND THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID THAT IS A LIMIT OF THE OBJECT AND NOT A REFUSAL OF THE QUESTION, AND SAID IT ONCE. IT IS IN HIS OWN HAND ON THIS HOLDING'S OWN PAPER AND IS IN A DRAWER. NOBODY ASKED IT A FIFTH TIME. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
