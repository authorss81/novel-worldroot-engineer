# Chapter 365

## Forty-Two Fields

The eighth of this month was a Wednesday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour with nobody on it. Nobody was at the headland above Marden and the fields above Marden waited. Forty-two of them are the fourth line and all forty-two of them are the fourth line, and the rota stands at seventy-one mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and forty-two not, and those two go to seventy-one. Twenty-nine taken did not move, and has not moved on any morning of this season, and nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

A field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man. It is on the page for the sixth time in a fortnight. It is not going to be improved, and the reason it goes in every time the fourth line comes round is not that anybody in this holding has begun to doubt it. It is that a number which goes up by one on a morning when nobody did anything is a number somebody will ask about in about four years, and this holding would rather have the answer in the book than in the mouth of whoever is holding the book.

That is the nineteenth morning the fourth line has come round since it started coming round on the twenty-sixth of a month three months back, and it is the sixth of them since the eighteenth of last month, and both of those are true and neither of them is the other one, and the man of about fifty enters both of them and does not give a second reason for entering either.

At about the eighth hour the number for the eighth went into the book that keeps the third launder. Out of the other book with the frayed corner it came back, in the mouth of the man of about fifty, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and sixty-eight hundredweight.**

It has risen, and it is the hundred and twenty-eighth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first. Ninety-five days of it, fifty-three rises and forty-two falls. It came up off Tuesday by nine, and about four people in this holding spent Tuesday being handed a five, and about four of them had a nine this morning, and about four of those did not care.

One hundred and twenty-six mornings now without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, this morning making the hundred and twenty-fifth sentence written out of a hundred and twenty-seven. That sentence is a sentence about four bodies of households and not about water, and the four launders are a different figure and have never been added to it.

The two day-minus rules were not read out loud at about the seventh hour of the morning. They stand at **five hundred and twelve days** and **five hundred and fifty-nine**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. The run does not move and this page carries no figure for it, and the man of about fifty has said that a man who reads a pair out loud on most of the mornings has made it a habit, and that a habit is not a measurement and is not going to become one.

The morning was the sort of morning that makes a person stand at a gate and look up a slope. The grass on the top of the headland had gone the color of a wet rope and had not been cut since the year before last, and the wall along the near side of the track has forty-two stones in it above the gate and about four of those have moved in eleven years. There was a man standing on the road at the bottom of that slope at about the third hour of the afternoon with his hands behind his back, looking up at it, and he was there for about the length of a pipe and he did not go up, and he was not of this holding and had not been sent for and nobody in this holding has ever asked him what he was looking at.

The window of the office's work is three days off this morning and not tomorrow. It is six days and not five and not seven, it shuts on the eleventh whether anybody has used it or not, and no work is ever carried out of it, and there is a column in the book of this holding for a thing that goes to an office and it has not been written in this month and is not going to be written on the strength of a window opening.

At about the ninth hour the man of about fifty read the day sheet out in the yard, from the step and not from the middle of the yard, and he did not raise his voice. Five things, in five places: the line that came round and had nobody on it, the figure for the day, which two people say is the same number and it is, the form on the table and the drawer shut, the man of about seventy not fetched and how many days it is to the first, and the window and how many days it is to the sixth. He put a stroke beside two of the five and left the other three alone and did not say which two. About four people in that yard could work it out and about four could not.

There was about a foot of rain in the low ground between the sluice and the north gate by the fourth hour, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the road with the barrow and turned it at the top of the yard and went home again, and nobody in that yard asked him whether he was going up to the node this week and nobody has asked him that in a fortnight, and the count of journeys is eleven and the count is a floor and not a ceiling.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon. The register form was on the middle table where it has been for twenty-two days, the drawer was shut, and the woman who keeps the seed house had been in and out all afternoon on account of a sack that had come up on the cart from the crossing and was not a seed sack. She put her hand on it once, at the stitching and not at the mouth, said nothing at all about it, and had it carried out to the cart shed and set down against the north wall, and about four people in that room had watched her do that and about four had not, and the sack is on the north wall of the cart shed and has not been opened in this holding and is not going to be.

In that room the reader of this body said one thing, and the reason for it came before the thing, which is the whole of the practice. A hundred and sixty-three is the count of that in this holding's history. He keeps it and nobody else does, he was not thanked, and he did not ask to be.

**"I am going to ask this room to let a second thing be written down tomorrow, and I am giving the reason I am asking before I say what the thing is, and the reason is that I have been in that standing wood twice and both times I came back with something that four books in this holding have no column for, and the second time it took a day out of me that I did not know at the time it had taken.**"

**"And a thing that is entered after it has happened is a memory. It belongs to the two people who were in it. There are nine people in this room who will not be in it and will not be able to check one word of it and will be told it happened, and I am not willing to be the reason this holding's book carries a thing that nobody can check in it.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had wanted the second session entered and about four had not, and the four that wanted it had wanted it since Friday, and the four that had not had understood that the first one was entered by a man with a pen before a room with the door shut and had not understood that anybody had to ask for it at all.

At about the sixth hour the man of about fifty got the second book out and squared it against the edge of the middle table and uncapped the pen, and about nine people watched him do it and about nine of them had watched him do it once before and knew the order of it.

And then the four of them were said out loud in that room, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it.

**THE PURPOSE: TO GO ONE STEP FURTHER IN THAN THE OUTSIDE OF A STEM, WHICH IS FURTHER IN THAN A PERSON STOOD ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF LAST MONTH AND FURTHER IN AGAIN THAN THE WORK OF THE THIRD OF THIS MONTH, AND TO FIND OUT WHETHER THERE IS A THIRD PLACE IN THERE OR WHETHER THERE ISN'T, AND THE FINDING OUT IS THE PURPOSE AND NOT WHAT WOULD BE FOUND. THE DURATION: FROM THE FOURTH HOUR UNTIL THE FIFTH, AND WHICHEVER OF THE TWO COMES FIRST, AND NOT A MINUTE PAST THE FIFTH. THE STOP SIGNAL: THERE IS ONE, IT IS HERS, IT IS NOT ENTERED HERE, AND IT IS NOT GOING TO BE ENTERED HERE, AND THE REASON IS THE SAME REASON AS LAST TIME AND IT IS THE REASON THIS BOOK IS CARRIED BETWEEN ROOMS. THE INDEPENDENT WITNESS: THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO IS NOT AT EITHER END, WHO KEEPS A RECORD OF WHAT HAPPENED, AND WHO HAS BEEN TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND HAS NOT BEEN TOLD WHAT IT IS, AND WHO IS NOT GOING TO BE TOLD.**

He wrote it, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it. The day, which is the ninth. The hour, which is about the fifth. The four of them, in the words she had used and not in his own. And the line, THE STOP SIGNAL IS HERS AND ITS WORDS ARE NOT ENTERED, and there he left it.

Those are the four ruled lines he keeps under the sixth column of the office's ledger and under the survey pages that are not in the crate, and nobody in that room asked him this evening why a man who keeps an empty place visible for other people's records keeps one for his own. Under the two words and the hour, being A CONDITION WAS STATED, he left them empty.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book was one at about the fifth hour of the afternoon and is two at about the seventh, and it stopped being one before the thing happened and not after it, and a session that is not entered did not happen, and this evening is the entering of the second one and the second one is tomorrow.

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a book open on a table with four ruled lines under two words and nothing under those, and about four of them had understood that the empty place is a thing a man keeps for a person who has not been asked, and about four had understood that nobody in the room knew who that person was.

The stop word was not said in that room and is not in that book and the man of about fifty did not ask about it and the count of his questions about the condition is nil and it is nil. Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and there was a yard at about the ninth hour on Tuesday and nothing has been said in one since.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and an hour in a wood is not a mark and a request is not a mark and the four accounts of that arm stand at four. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the eighth, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the eighth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of the second session, and not by the man of about fifty, who wrote it whole and left four lines empty: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND THERE WAS NOBODY AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED. THE NUMBER IS FORTY-TWO AND ALL FORTY-TWO OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE. THE ROTA STANDS AT SEVENTY-ONE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND FORTY-TWO NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE TAKEN DID NOT MOVE, AND A FIELD THAT WAITED IS A FACT ABOUT A ROTA AND NOT A FACT ABOUT A MAN. THAT IS THE NINETEENTH MORNING OF THE FOURTH LINE SINCE THE TWENTY-SIXTH OF A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK AND THE SIXTH SINCE THE EIGHTEENTH OF LAST MONTH. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN NINETY-FIVE DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-TWO TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SIX DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIFTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVEN, AND IT IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWELVE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING, AND THE RUN DID NOT MOVE AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR IT. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK IS THREE DAYS OFF AND NOT TOMORROW, SIX DAYS AND NOT FIVE AND NOT SEVEN, AND THE COLUMN FOR A THING THAT GOES TO AN OFFICE IS NOT WRITTEN IN THIS MONTH. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK WAS ONE AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND IS TWO AT ABOUT THE SEVENTH. THE ENTRY CARRIES A PURPOSE, BEING TO GO ONE STEP FURTHER IN THAN THE OUTSIDE OF A STEM; A DURATION, BEING FROM THE FOURTH HOUR UNTIL THE FIFTH AND WHICHEVER COMES FIRST; A STOP SIGNAL, WHICH IS HERS AND WHOSE WORDS ARE NOT ENTERED HERE, THE REASON BEING THAT THIS BOOK IS CARRIED BETWEEN ROOMS; AND AN INDEPENDENT WITNESS, BEING THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO IS NOT AT EITHER END AND WHO HAS BEEN TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND HAS NOT BEEN TOLD WHAT IT IS. UNDER THE FOUR HE WROTE TWO WORDS AND THE HOUR, BEING A CONDITION WAS STATED, AND LEFT THE FOUR RULED LINES UNDER THEM EMPTY. THE STOP WORD WAS NOT SAID IN THAT ROOM AND IS NOT IN THIS BOOK, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS ASKED ABOUT THE CONDITION IS NIL. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
