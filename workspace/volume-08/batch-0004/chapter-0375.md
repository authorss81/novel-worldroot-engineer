# Chapter 375

## What She Did Not Say

The eighteenth was a Saturday and the second line came round at the second hour and was worked. The two gates and the about six feet of wall between them took four people an hour and a quarter. The toe at the second of the three places on that line is up a finger's width off what it was on the third of this month, and the four of them put a spade in it and a hand's depth of stone and stood about it afterwards the way men stand about a thing they have just made worse. The man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it, as he has not done in eleven years.

The eighteenth was entered at the eighth hour on the page with the ruled margin, and read afterwards out of the frayed book into a room with about four of them in it, and the two numbers were the same to the hundredweight.

**Seven hundred and eighty-eight hundredweight.**

It has risen, and it is the hundred and thirty-eighth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first. It came up off Friday by nine, and the four of them had been on the same ditch since the second hour and about four of them could not have told a nine from a five.

The window the rises and the falls are counted in stands at a hundred and five, and the split in it is fifty-eight rises and forty-seven falls, and the rise this morning went onto the rises and that is the only check anybody has ever needed on it.

A hundred and thirty-six days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence saying so was written this morning for the hundred and thirty-fifth of them out of a hundred and thirty-seven. It is not the aggregate of the launders and the two are never added, and there is no page in this holding on which that addition would look like anything except an error.

The two day-minus rules were not read out loud at about the seventh hour and no figure for the run is on this page. The second line had run an hour and a quarter late on the wall and was still running at the seventh hour, and the man of about fifty was on the second gate with a barrow and there was nobody standing in a room to read a pair out loud to, and a pair read to nobody is a habit and not a measurement. The pair itself stands at **five hundred and twenty-two days** and **five hundred and sixty-nine**, forty-seven apart, and the drawer is shut.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and the register form was on the middle table where it has been for thirty-two days, and the offer sheet was on the low board at the far end of the room where it was put yesterday, face up, and neither of the two of them has moved in a day.

The woman from the Continuity Office came up the fen road at her own cost and was not sent for, and nobody in this holding had asked whether she was coming, and nobody had sent her.

She did not ask for the two papers to be put side by side and she did not ask to see the register again. She asked for the list of nineteen lines, and it was brought out of the drawer and read to her in a room, and she read it herself after that, standing, and she put her thumb on the eight that have a day and an hour as well as a season and did not say anything about those eight for about a minute.

Then she said the thing her office holds, and she said it correctly, and this page is not going to say she is wrong about it.

**"A record only one man can produce is not a record. It is a story with a witness in it, and the witness is in it, and a witness who is also the man who carried it is the least reliable kind of witness there is, and that is not a thing I say about you. You have been in the ground. A man who has been in the ground is a man whose account of the ground cannot be checked by anybody else in the world.**"

**"Our register carries a day against every figure and an hour and the name of the person who read it, and a person who reads for us does not keep the figure. The figure is in the register. His own copy is a copy. I am not asking you to give up a single thing you have found. I am asking you to stop being the only place any of it exists.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had come in ready to tell her that a thing read out of a wood is a different kind of thing from a figure in a register, and about four of them sat down again without saying it, and about four of them had worked out in the time it took her to say the second of those two things that she was not going to answer the first of them and had not been asked twice. The clerk put the sheet back in its own order afterwards, which is the order it came in, and nobody asked him to.

Then the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden said the thing that was hers, and she gave the reason before the thing, and the count of that in this holding's history is eighteen, and it is her own count and nobody else keeps it, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have been in this room four times since the sixth of this month and I have not once said out loud that the two of you were right about the cut at the kerb, and I am not going to say it this afternoon either, and the reason I am going to say that I am not going to say it is that a woman from an office has now been in this room two mornings running and about four of us have been sitting here waiting to see whether one of us would.**"

**"The cut stays open. Nothing goes into it this season and nothing is going into it while I hold what I hold. Nobody has asked me for it and I am not going to be asked, and I would like it written down that the not being asked is not a favor anybody has done me. It is the ordinary shape of the thing and it is how it was on the twelfth of a month four months back and it is how it is going to be.**"

Nobody argued with her and nobody thanked her, and the woman from the office looked at her for a while and then wrote one line in a book of her own and did not read the line out, and nobody in that room asked to see the book, and that was the whole of what happened about the cut.

And then the morning comes to the part that is in its title, and it is put on the page in one paragraph because it is the whole of what she did not say, and none of it was a lie and none of it was a softening and none of it was an obstruction.

She did not say that this holding was right to refuse the post. She did not say it and she was not asked and nobody in that room thought she had wanted to say it, and a person who goes about softening documents is a person who has gone about softening documents, and no office in this book has ever done that and this one did not begin today.

She did not say what would become of the eight lines that have a day and an hour on them if the office took the reading over. She did not say whether her office has any view about the schedule the list comes out of, or whether it agrees with the list, or whether it would prune differently from what the wood says. She did not say whose order the schedule is, and the reader of this body has asked that question four times across four years and has not been answered once, and she was not asked it today and did not offer it, and a woman who volunteers an answer to a question nobody put to her is telling you what she has been asked about and not what she knows.

And she did not say one word about a piece of rootwood under a cloth, then or yesterday, and nobody in this holding has asked her a question about it and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and is nil.

The register form is still on the middle table and is not filled and was not refused and nobody wrote a heading on it, and the man of about fifty read it out whole again in that room at about the sixth hour for the third time since it came, and being read out is not being answered and has not been mistaken here for being answered. The offer sheet is still on the low board and is not filled and was not refused, and yesterday's day has gone without anybody filling either, and a form of that kind does not lapse on the day it is made, and she said so yesterday in a room with the door shut and nobody wrote it down in this holding's book and it is not going to be written down now.

The man of about fifty said the thing that was his, and the reason came first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and four, and he was not thanked and did not expect to be.

**"She has now read the only copy of the nineteen lines in this building, and I am going to write that on the page tonight, and I am going to give the reason I am going to write it before I write it, and the reason is that a page which says a document was refused to be seen in a room is a page that will be read in about four years as a document nobody ever asked for, and it was asked for twice and one of the two times was this afternoon.**"

**"And I am going to write down the count of the times that sheet has been out of that low board, which is two, and I am going to write down that nobody filled it and nobody refused it and a day was asked for and a day was given. I am not going to write down what is on it. It is her paper. I have read it and I have not got anything out of it that is mine to put in this book, and a bookkeeper who puts what is on a paper into his book has stopped being a bookkeeper.**"

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon and this was a room with a door on it. The count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next is on the first of a month and is thirteen mornings off this one.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is three and did not move, and a woman reading a list of nineteen lines in a seed house is not a session and nothing was read in any wood today. The use log stands at fifteen lines and a thing a person from an office reads is not a use and gets no line. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the next of the two mornings it is read on is the thirtieth of this month. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The mark is four inches and it branches twice, and a doctrine is not a mark and a day is not a mark and a thing not said is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the eighteenth, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a list read out and four things not said: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, TWO GATES AND SIX FEET OF WALL, FOUR PEOPLE, AN HOUR AND A QUARTER. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND FIVE, FIFTY-EIGHT RISES AND FORTY-SEVEN FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIX DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIFTH OF A HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SEVEN, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-NINE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER IS SHUT, AND THEY WERE NOT READ ALOUD BECAUSE THERE WAS NOBODY TO READ A PAIR TO, AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR THE RUN. A WOMAN OF THE CONTINUITY OFFICE CAME UP AT HER OWN COST AND WAS IN THIS BUILDING ON HER OWN ACCOUNT AND NOT SENT FOR. SHE WAS ASKED FOR THE LIST OF NINETEEN LINES AND READ IT HERSELF STANDING, AND PUT HER THUMB ON THE EIGHT THAT HAVE A DAY AND AN HOUR, AND SAID NOTHING ABOUT THEM FOR ABOUT A MINUTE. SHE SAID THAT A RECORD ONLY ONE MAN CAN PRODUCE IS NOT A RECORD BUT A STORY WITH A WITNESS IN IT, AND ASKED HIM TO STOP BEING THE ONLY PLACE ANY OF IT EXISTS. NOBODY IMPROVED IT. THE WOMAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT OF MARDEN GAVE THE REASON FIRST, EIGHTEEN, AND SAID SHE HAD NOT ONCE SAID OUT LOUD THAT THE HOLDING WAS RIGHT ABOUT THE CUT AT THE KERB, AND THAT THE CUT STAYS OPEN AND NOTHING GOES INTO IT THIS SEASON, AND THAT NOBODY HAS ASKED HER FOR WHAT SHE HOLDS AND THAT IS NOT A FAVOR AND IS HOW THE THING HAS BEEN SINCE THE TWELFTH OF A MONTH FOUR MONTHS BACK. SHE WAS NOT THANKED. SHE DID NOT SAY THE HOLDING WAS RIGHT TO REFUSE THE POST, AND DID NOT SAY WHAT WOULD BECOME OF THE EIGHT LINES THAT HAVE A DAY AND AN HOUR IF THE OFFICE TOOK THE READING OVER, AND DID NOT SAY WHETHER HER OFFICE HAS ANY VIEW ABOUT THE SCHEDULE, AND DID NOT SAY WHOSE ORDER ANYTHING IS. NONE OF THAT WAS A LIE AND NONE OF IT A SOFTENING, AND NOBODY HAS ASKED HER ABOUT A PIECE OF ROOTWOOD UNDER A CLOTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST, ONE HUNDRED AND FOUR, AND SAID HE WOULD ENTER THAT THE LIST HAD BEEN READ TWICE OUT OF THIS BUILDING, AND THAT THE OFFER SHEET HAD BEEN OUT OF THE LOW BOARD TWICE. THE OFFER SHEET IS ON THE LOW BOARD AND IS NOT FILLED AND IS NOT REFUSED. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND IS NOT FILLED AND WAS NOT REFUSED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE NEXT OF THE TWO IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
