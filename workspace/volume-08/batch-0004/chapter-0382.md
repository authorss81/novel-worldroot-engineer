# Chapter 382

## A Term, A Reason, A Count

The twenty-fifth of this month was a Saturday and the first line came round at the second hour and was worked. The boundary ditch and the two culverts took about six people until about the fourth hour and the near culvert was clear for the twenty-fifth morning running, and the far one was about a third full and the six of them put two barrow-loads out of it and stopped, because the far one has been going down by itself since the twenty-third and there was no need to be in it twice in one week.

The number for the twenty-fifth went in at the eighth hour and was read out of the frayed book by a man who does not read figures out of that book for anybody else, and the two were the same.

**Seven hundred and ninety-five hundredweight.**

It has fallen off Friday by five and it is the hundred and forty-fifth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first. The window is a hundred and twelve and the fall went onto the falls, which is sixty-one rises and fifty-one falls, and the two have not been that shape since the middle of the seventh month.

A hundred and forty-three days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, the sentence for that written this morning for the hundred and forty-second of them out of a hundred and forty-four. It concerns four bodies of households and the four launders are a different figure and the two are not added in this holding and have not been since a visitor was shown a page.

The two day-minus rules were said out loud at about the seventh hour, before either of them, one man to each, to about four people and with nobody trusted with both. They stand at **five hundred and twenty-nine days** and **five hundred and seventy-six**, forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened. Forty-three mornings out of sixty-nine since the seventeenth of a month two months back.

Nobody in this holding went up that road with the barrow this afternoon. The four of them in it are four bodies of households, and the four are at Havergate and Low Wether and Marden and Silling, and not one of them belongs to the cooperative and none of them was made one this afternoon.

The load went onto the sling at the node and the sling went up and the beam came down slow, and the return went in on the steelyard on the wall of the building that has no roof, and the instrument has hung on that wall for longer than this holding has had a book.

**Three hundred and sixteen hundredweight.**

The thirty-fourth of them, and the twenty-fourth pair of names to go against it in eleven years, and it stands eleven hundredweight over the thirty-third, which was three hundred and five on the sixteenth of this month. He took three hundred and five off the dial, said the figure out loud, and then said it again out of the second book, and the two were the same.

Twice reconciled, both times out of a pair of numbers the man of about fifty had put on a day sheet at about the fifth hour of the morning and had said nothing to anybody about.

**Two hundred and sixty-eight plus forty-eight. And two hundred and twenty plus ninety-six.** The two halves are not the whole and they have not been added to each other and are not going to be added this afternoon. The word using is on the page of this holding's book and this holding is using that derivation and is not refusing it, and the reason that is said out loud rather than left is that a man who refuses a derivation is a man who thinks the number is in doubt, and the number is on a dial that has been on that wall longer than this holding has had a book, and about four people in this holding would have preferred the other way and about four would not.

The four of them wrote in one at a time, a name into BY and then a name into WITNESSED BY, and then a name into BY again and a name into WITNESSED BY again, and they did it in the order they came up to the dial and nobody in this holding chose that order for them and nobody has ever been able to say that any of the four hurried.

The second rule on that board was left empty again this afternoon, and it has been left empty eighteen times in eleven years. Nothing went into it. Nothing was said about it by any of the four or by anybody standing near the dial. It stood at seventeen on the sixteenth of this month, which is nine mornings back. A count of blanks is not the count of a man and never has been.

Then about nine people were in the one room at this holding from about the seventh hour of the evening with the door shut, and the thing that was said in that room is a thing this holding has a form for, and the form is a term, a reason and a count, and nobody thanked anybody for any part of it.

The reader of this body said it first, and the reason came before the term, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and seventy-one, and it is his count and not hers and neither of the two of them adds to the other.

**"I am going to say a word and I am going to give the reason before I say the word, and the reason is that two people have agreed five things in one hand and one hand in a fortnight and have not said one word about what they are to each other, and a thing that is not said has a way of turning into something else by about four years, and I would rather it turned into a word in a room than into a story in somebody's mouth.**"

**"The term is partners. Said once, in this room, with the door shut, on the twenty-fifth of this month, and said in a room and not in a yard, because a word said in a yard is a thing somebody was talking about and a word said in a room in about nine people is a thing a person can be held to. And I am not going to improve on it and nobody is going to thank me for it and I am not going to say it again this season unless somebody asks me whether I said it, and then I am going to say yes.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had been waiting a fortnight to hear one of the two of them say a plain word for a plain thing and about four had not, and the four that had been waiting were sorry about it afterwards, and the four that had not were not sorry at all, and neither of the two fours said anything whatever, which was the correct thing for both of them to do, and which is also the correct thing for a yard, and this was a room.

Then Tova Reed said hers, and the reason came before the term, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-three, and it is her count and it is hers alone, and she was not thanked.

**"I am going to say the same word and I am going to give the reason before I say it, and the reason is that he has said it in a room and a thing one person has said in a room and the other person has not said is a rule, and a rule with one person holding it is not a rule, it is an instruction.**"

**"The term is partners. And here is the half of it that is mine and not his, and he is not going to say it for me and I am not going to let him say it for me. I keep the seed work. I am going to keep the seed work after this and through the end of it and I am not going to stand in a wood five miles east of here because a man in a room said a word. He does not decide what I am for. Not this season and not in about four years. I decide what I am for and I have been deciding it for a long time and I am not going to hand it to anybody as part of a word said in a room.**"

**"And the other half of the word is that we keep separate books. His four books and my accounts and the count of that in the history of this holding is mine and I have never once added it to his and I am not going to start. A reason each for everything. A reason before the thing. I have not thanked him and he has not thanked me and nobody in this room is going to thank either of us this evening.**"

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this evening, and this was a room with a door on it, and the word that was said in it has not been said in a yard and is not going to be.

And then the reader of this body said the thing that is still owed from this evening, and he said in one sentence that he was not going to say the rest of it tonight.

**"I am not going to say the rest of it tonight and I am going to give the reason I am not going to say it before I say that I am not going to, and the reason is that a thing said on the night it is written is a thing that gets said again in a fortnight, and the second time it is said it is a habit and not a thing, and it will be said in this room tomorrow with the door shut and it will be said once and nobody is going to thank anybody for it.**"

What was on the page that night, without a sentence on it yet, is all of that. She has the seed house and the accounts and the sacks and the two men who come on a Tuesday, and he has the ditch and the culverts and the four books and the wood five miles east of here. Neither of them was asked to give any of that up and neither of them offered it, and nobody in that room asked either of them, and nobody in this holding is going to ask either of them, and the woman who keeps the seed house said that the two men come on a Tuesday and are not to be asked to come on anything else, which was the only thing anybody said about the arrangement on either of the two evenings.

Nobody improved that either. The woman who keeps the seed house said afterwards that there was bread in the seed house and that the loaf from last week was still good, which is the only thing anybody said in that room for about a minute, and she said it because somebody had to say something and she is the one who can say it without it meaning anything.

The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of the room, face up, and it is not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of this month and it has not been withdrawn and it is not going to be taken off that board by anybody. The register form is on the middle table where it has been for thirty-nine days and is not filled and was not refused, and the sheet of the twenty-first is on the same table and the three of them have been on that table inside a fortnight and have not been put side by side.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the thirtieth of this month is five mornings off. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next of them is on the first of a month and is six mornings off this one, and he has never once asked which number it was. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-fifth, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a term, a reason and a count: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE BOUNDARY DITCH AND THE TWO CULVERTS, ABOUT SIX PEOPLE TO THE FOURTH HOUR, AND TWO BARROW-LOADS CAME OUT OF THE FAR ONE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND HAS FALLEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND TWELVE, SIXTY-ONE RISES AND FIFTY-ONE FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THREE DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SECOND OF A HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOUR, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, ONE MAN TO EACH, BEING FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND IT IS THE FORTY-THIRD OF SIXTY-NINE. THE THIRTY-FOURTH NINTH-DAY RETURN WENT IN ON A STEELYARD AND READ THREE HUNDRED AND SIXTEEN HUNDREDWEIGHT OUT LOUD AND READ IT BACK. IT IS THE TWENTY-FOURTH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS AND ELEVEN ABOVE THE THIRTY-THIRD, AND THE TWO WAYS IT COMES ARE TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT PLUS FORTY-EIGHT AND TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY PLUS NINETY-SIX, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT, AND THE TWO HALVES ARE NOT ADDED. A NAME WENT INTO BY AND A NAME WENT INTO WITNESSED BY, ONE AT A TIME, BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS. THE SECOND RULE STAYED BLANK AND THE COUNT OF RETURNS AT WHICH IT HAS BEEN LEFT BLANK IS EIGHTEEN, IT HAVING BEEN SEVENTEEN ON THE SIXTEENTH, AND A COUNT OF BLANKS IS NOT THE COUNT OF A MAN. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID, GIVING THE REASON FIRST, ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-ONE, THAT THE TERM IS PARTNERS, SAID IN A ROOM AND NOT IN A YARD, AND NOT TO BE IMPROVED ON. TOVA REED SAID THE SAME WORD AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST, TWENTY-THREE, AND SAID THAT A THING ONE PERSON HAS SAID IN A ROOM AND THE OTHER PERSON HAS NOT IS A RULE AND A RULE WITH ONE PERSON HOLDING IT IS AN INSTRUCTION, AND THAT SHE KEEPS THE SEED WORK AND IS NOT GOING TO STAND IN A WOOD FIVE MILES EAST BECAUSE A MAN IN A ROOM SAID A WORD, AND THAT HE DOES NOT DECIDE WHAT SHE IS FOR, AND THAT THEY KEEP SEPARATE BOOKS AND SEPARATE COUNTS AND A REASON EACH FOR EVERYTHING. THE READER OF THIS BODY SAID HE IS NOT GOING TO SAY THE REST OF IT TONIGHT, GAVE THE REASON FIRST, AND SAID IT WILL BE SAID IN THAT ROOM ON THE TWENTY-SIXTH WITH THE DOOR SHUT AND WILL BE SAID ONCE. NEITHER OF THEM WAS ASKED TO GIVE UP ANY WORK AND NEITHER OFFERED. NOBODY IMPROVED IT AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY. THE OFFER SHEET IS ON THE LOW BOARD, NOT FILLED, NOT WITHDRAWN. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE, THIRTY-NINE DAYS, NOT FILLED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED, THE COUNT TWENTY-TWO, AND THE NEXT IS THE FIRST OF A MONTH, SIX MORNINGS OFF. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN.**
