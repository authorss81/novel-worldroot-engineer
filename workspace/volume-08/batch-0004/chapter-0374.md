# Chapter 374

## A Real Instrument

The seventeenth of this month was a Friday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked. The near culvert and nine feet of the ditch above it took six people until the fourth hour. The far one was still about half full and nobody made a second barrow-load out of it, and that has been on and off since the third.

The frost was on the low ground at the second hour and four of the six broke the skin of it with their spades, and the reader of this body was one of those four and could not have told a five from a two. The man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the road with the barrow at about the ninth hour, turned it at the top of the yard and went home, and the question on the second of the four lines in his own book is twenty-five days old with nowhere for an answer, and nobody here has asked him about it in twenty-five days. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road.

At about the eighth hour the figure for the seventeenth went into the book that keeps the third launder, and out of the other book with the frayed corner it came back in the mouth of the man of about fifty, and the two are the same, which is the whole of what the reading back is for.

**Seven hundred and seventy-nine hundredweight.**

It has fallen, and it is the hundred and thirty-seventh figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first, and it came down off Thursday by five, on four people who had been in a ditch since the second hour and could not have told a five from a two.

A hundred and thirty-five days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, the sentence for that written this morning for the hundred and thirty-fourth of them out of a hundred and thirty-six. It concerns four bodies of households and not water, and the launders are a different figure.

Two figures stand at a hundred and four this morning and they are the same figure and are not the same count, and this is said once and not again in this building. One is the number of mornings in the series. The other is the window the rises and the falls are counted in, and that window is the count of mornings back from a day four hundred and eighty-three off, and it did not re-anchor at the first of this month. The split inside it is fifty-seven rises against forty-seven falls.

About the seventh hour the two day-minus rules were said out loud and before either number, one man taking the first and one taking the second so that nobody is trusted with both. They stand at **five hundred and twenty-one days** and **five hundred and sixty-eight**, forty-seven apart, and that forty-seven is a consequence of the two and not a third rule. Thirty-nine mornings out of sixty-one, counted from the seventeenth of a month two months back.

The window of the office's work is nineteen days off this morning and not tomorrow. It is six days when it comes, and no work is ever carried out of one, and the column in this holding's book for a thing that goes to an office has not been written in this month.

About nine people were in the seed house from the second hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and the register form was on the middle table where it has been for thirty-one days, and it is a real document and it is correct and it is not an answer to anything nobody in that room has put.

"Iona Vey," she said, before anybody asked her. "Of the Continuity Office. The office that sent the form on that table. I came up the road at my own cost and nobody sent for me, and I am not here about the two papers."

The reader of this body did not put the form forward. He put his hand flat on the near side of it, the way the woman who keeps the seed house puts her hand flat on a sack, and said the two documents had not been put side by side in four years and were not going to be this afternoon, and gave the reason, and the reason was that a holding which lays two papers together to make them look like an answer has begun to think it knows what both mean.

She did not argue it. She set a folded sheet on the low board at the far end of the room, face up, four feet off the middle table.

> A PROVISIONAL TECHNICAL POST OF CO-CARETAKER IN THE CONTINUITY OFFICE, ON THE SCHEDULE OF THE MAINTENANCE OF THE CROWN ENGINE. PROVISIONAL, AND PROVISIONAL FOR AS LONG AS THE READINGS ARE SOUGHT. THE HOLDER IS NOT ASKED TO HOLD AN END OF ANYTHING. THE HOLDER IS ASKED TO GIVE UP THE MAKING OF HIS OWN FIGURES AND TO WORK FROM THE FIGURES ENTERED IN THE REGISTER OF THE OFFICE, WHICH ARE ENTERED BY THE OFFICE. THE HOLDER MAY READ THE FIGURES OF THE OFFICE AT ANY TIME AND MAY KEEP HIS OWN COPY OF THEM, AND HIS OWN COPY IS NOT A FIGURE. THE HOLDER IS NOT ASKED TO STOP WHAT HE IS ASKED TO STOP. THE POST CARRIES NO DATE AT WHICH IT ENDS, AND IT IS NOT TO BE GIVEN ONE, AND NOBODY IS TO BE ASKED AFTERWARD WHETHER HOLDING IT WAS RIGHT. THERE IS NO LINE ON THIS SHEET FOR A PERSON TO WRITE THAT DOWN ON, AND NONE IS TO BE ADDED. THE FORMS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE ARE NOT TO BE FILLED ON THE STRENGTH OF THIS. THIS SHEET IS NOT AN ANSWER TO ANY QUESTION AND NOBODY HAS ASKED IT ONE.

Nobody read it out. It got read twice, once by her and once by the man of about fifty, and both times it was the same fourteen lines, and about four people who could read had their eyes on the ninth, which is the one about there being no line to write on, and about four had not got there yet.

It is correct in every particular and it lies about nothing at all, and the whole of the danger in it is in the correctness. Every office that has come up that road with paper to this holding has lied about nothing, and the two documents upstairs are correct and cannot be put down, and nobody here is going to put them down.

She said four things and each was true and none was a lie and nobody improved any of them.

"I have not come to tell you that you are wrong about anything. I have read the book on that table and have not found one figure in it wrong. I have had the men who keep our register look at your figures against ours, which is a thing I am allowed to do and not a thing you are."

"The post is provisional, and I want the word understood this afternoon, and the reason I am saying it is that people read provisional as temporary and I do not mean temporary. I mean it continues until somebody decides it does not, and the person who can decide that is me, and I am telling you with the door shut that I am not going to."

"Nobody in this office has asked me about a piece of rootwood under a cloth and I am not going to ask about it. Put a question in writing and it will be answered in writing, and the answer will be true."

"And this sheet does not lapse on the day it is made. There is no date on it and I am not going to put one there, and the reason I am telling you is that a sheet with a date on it is a sheet you can put down, and I have not brought you one."

It was then that the reader of this body put his own sentence into the room, and the reason for it came out ahead of the sentence, and the count of that in the history of this holding is one hundred and sixty-eight, and it is a count of his own and of nobody else, and nobody thanked him and he did not want to be.

**"I am going to say one thing about the woman who sat at that low board, and I am going to give the reason before I say it, and the reason is that about four people in this room have waited four years for somebody from an office to come up that road and say something false, and a week like this one will be read in about four years as the week this holding stopped looking.**"

**"Nobody from an office has ever lied to this holding about a document. Not the sheet that claims the corridor. Not the sealing order. Not the register on that table. Every one of them was correct in every particular, and two of them cannot be put down, and neither was stopped by a lie. And she had the book read by the men who keep their register and found no figure in it wrong, and it is the first thing anybody outside this building has said to me about the figures in it that was not a question.**"

Nobody argued him out of it. About four people in that room had wanted her to have been wrong, and the four that had not were the four who had gone looking for a bad figure in their own book that week and had not found one either.

The man of about fifty spoke after him, with the reason in front of the thing, and the count of that in the history of this holding is one hundred and three, and nobody thanked him and he did not expect to be, and what he had come to say was about what he was going to put into a book that evening.

**"I am going to enter a thing this evening that I did not write, and I am going to give the reason before I say what I am going to do about it, and the reason is that a book which only holds what the people inside it wrote is a book that cannot be wrong and cannot be right, and this one has been able to be wrong three times in four years, and I would not trade that for a good shelf in an office with a lamp on it.**"

Then the reader of this body said one more thing, and it was not a figure and it went on the page as an act, and nobody argued with a man about a day.

**"I am not filling it and I am not refusing it this afternoon, and I am asking for a day to read it in a room with the door shut, and I am going to read it twice. A form of that kind wants a day and not an hour. If a person says yes or no to a thing of that shape on the afternoon it is put on a board, then whatever he said is about the afternoon and not about the fourteen lines, and about four people in this room can tell you which of those they would rather read about in about four years."**

She said the day was hers to give, and that she would be here on the eighteenth at about the same hour if the room wanted her, and nobody did, and she said it anyway.

The sheet is on the low board at the far end of the room, face up, and it is in neither drawer and it is not on the middle table, and nobody decided those two things this afternoon. They are the result of a hand put flat on a table four feet off.

The form with the four lines is in a drawer about four miles and a bit west of here, face up, its first line carrying one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, the figure of the twenty-second return and not of the thirty-third, and the other three lines are empty and are going to stay empty. The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road. A bridge was run once for about nine minutes a month and five days back and its standing is in no book, and nobody here has an instrument that says whether it is on or off. The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured and not explained.

The thing under a cloth at the end of the long table is living rootwood, warm, carrying a control sequence for one valve, and the count of people who have looked at it is two and did not move, and nobody asked the visitor about it and she did not offer it.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room with a door on it, and there has not been a yard in this holding since Sunday. The mark on the arm is four inches and branches twice, and a form is not a mark and an offer is not a mark and a decision is not a mark.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the seventeenth, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a real instrument: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE NEAR CULVERT AND NINE FEET OF DITCH, SIX PEOPLE TO THE FOURTH HOUR. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SEVENTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN. A HUNDRED AND FOUR MORNINGS STAND IN IT, WHICH IS ALSO THE WINDOW THE RISES AND THE FALLS ARE COUNTED IN, AND THE SPLIT IS FIFTY-SEVEN RISES AND FORTY-SEVEN FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIVE DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FOURTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIX, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD AND BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, ONE MAN TO EACH, BEING FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND IT IS THE THIRTY-NINTH MORNING OUT OF SIXTY-ONE. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK IS NINETEEN DAYS OFF AND IS NOT TOMORROW. A WOMAN CAME UP THE FEN ROAD UNSENT FOR AND WAS IN THIS BUILDING ONCE. THE FORM ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IS HERS AND THE OFFER SHEET IS A DIFFERENT INSTRUMENT AND IS ALSO HERS, AND THE TWO WERE NOT PUT SIDE BY SIDE, AND THE OFFER IS ON THE LOW BOARD FOUR FEET OFF, FACE UP, IN NO DRAWER. IT IS FOURTEEN LINES AND EVERY PARTICULAR OF IT IS CORRECT AND IT LIES ABOUT NOTHING, AND THE POST CARRIES NO DATE AT WHICH IT ENDS AND NOBODY IS TO BE ASKED AFTERWARD WHETHER HOLDING IT WAS RIGHT, AND THERE IS NO LINE ON THE SHEET FOR A PERSON TO WRITE THAT DOWN ON. SHE SAID SHE HAD THE BOOK READ BY THE MEN WHO KEEP THEIR REGISTER AND HAD NOT FOUND ONE FIGURE IN IT WRONG, AND THAT IS ENTERED AS WHAT SHE SAID AND NOT AS A FINDING. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST, ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT, AND SAID NO OFFICE HAS EVER LIED TO THIS HOLDING ABOUT A DOCUMENT, AND THAT A WEEK LIKE THIS ONE WILL BE READ IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS AS THE WEEK THIS HOLDING STOPPED LOOKING. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST, ONE HUNDRED AND THREE, AND SAID HE WOULD ENTER A THING HE DID NOT WRITE. THE OFFER WAS NOT FILLED AND WAS NOT REFUSED AND A DAY WAS ASKED FOR, AND THE SHEET DOES NOT LAPSE ON THE DAY IT IS MADE. NOBODY WAS ASKED ABOUT THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE NEXT OF THOSE IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS THREE. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
