# Chapter 383

## And Nobody Thanked

The twenty-sixth of this month was a Sunday and the second line came round at the second hour and was worked. The two gates and the about six feet of wall between them took four people an hour and a quarter. The toe at the second of the three places is up a finger's width off what it was on the third of this month and has been for three weeks and is not going down, and the man at the front of it said that a toe which has been up three weeks is a toe somebody is going to have to come back and look at in about four years, and the three behind him did not answer him.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the road with the barrow at about the ninth hour, turned it at the top of the yard and went home, and the question on the second of the four lines in his own book is thirty-four days old this morning and has nothing on it and no place for an answer, and nobody in this holding has asked him about it in thirty-four days. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road, which is a floor and not a ceiling, and it did not rise this morning.

At about the eighth hour on the last morning of this week the figure was entered, and it was read back out of the second book with the frayed corner, and the two of them are the same, and the man of about fifty did not comment on either fact.

**Eight hundred and four hundredweight.**

It has risen off Saturday by nine and it is the hundred and forty-sixth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first of it. The window is a hundred and thirteen and the rise went onto the rises, which is sixty-two rises and fifty-one falls.

A hundred and forty-four days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, the sentence for that written this morning for the hundred and forty-third of them out of a hundred and forty-five. It concerns four bodies of households. The launders are not part of it and are not added to it, and the two aggregates have been kept apart in this holding for four years and are going to be kept apart after the man of about fifty is gone.

The two day-minus rules were not said out loud this morning and no figure for the run goes on this page. The four on the wall did not come off that six feet of wall until about a quarter past the seventh hour and by then the man of about fifty had shut the second book and put it under his arm and gone in, and a pair is not read out loud to a room that has already gone to its supper, and a habit that has been kept for forty-three mornings does not become a measurement because it has been kept forty-four times.

The pair stands at **five hundred and thirty days** and **five hundred and seventy-seven**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer is shut and has not been opened in four years and is not going to be opened before the month turns.

About nine people were in the one room at this holding from about the seventh hour of the evening with the door shut, and the last of them to arrive was the man of about seventy-four with a stick, who came as far as the door and stood there for the whole of it, and nobody asked him to come further in and nobody asked him to go.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was in that room and had his hat on his knee, and the question on the second of the four lines in his own book is thirty-four days old and has no answer on it and no place for an answer, and nobody asked him about it and nobody in this holding is going to, and he did not raise it himself, and about four people in that room had worked out that he was not going to and about four had not worked it out, and the four that had not were sorry afterwards and said nothing about being sorry.

The sentence was hers and the reason came ahead of it, and the count of that in the history of this holding is twenty-four, and it is a count of hers and of nobody else, and she was not thanked for it and did not expect to be, and she said it standing and did not sit down for it.

**"Yesterday two people said a word in this room and one of the two of them said a second sentence that the other one has not yet answered, and I am going to say the answer now and I am going to give the reason I am going to say it before I say it, and the reason is that a word said on the night it is written is a word that gets said again in a fortnight, and the second time it is said it is a habit and not a thing.**"

**"So here is the sentence, and it is said once. I am not going to stop running the seed work. He does not decide what I am for. We keep separate books and separate work and separate counts and a reason each for everything we do, and I am not going to be one thing in his life and he is not going to be one thing in mine, and nobody is going to be thanked for that this evening or any other evening.**"

Nobody improved it. Nobody said it again, and nobody in that room wrote it down except the one man who keeps a book, and the clerk was not asked to enter it in the middle table page and did not. The reader of this body wrote it down in his own hand on this holding's own paper and put the sheet in the drawer with the others and did not read it back out, which is the one time in four years he has entered a thing without reading it back, and the man of about fifty wrote that down as a fact about the entry and not as a fault.

The man of about fifty did not say a word about it. He has been told that a condition exists and has not been told what it is, and he was not told this evening and did not ask, and he was not thanked for not knowing and has never asked to be. He closed the second book at about the ninth hour and put the pen in his coat and said that the thirtieth of this month is four mornings off and that the first of the next month is the fifth, and that neither of those two mornings resets anything this holding keeps, and that the count of the third launder does not go back to the first, and that the window the rises and the falls are counted in does not re-anchor, and that the two boards do not start again, and that the aggregate of the four bodies of households does not start again, and that the man of about seventy is fetched on the first of a month and not before and has never once asked which number it was.

The man of about seventy-four with a stick stood in the doorway for the whole of it and did not come into the room and did not go away from it, and at about the eighth hour he said one thing, which was that the light on the south wall had gone by, and about four people in that room heard him and about four did not and the four that did not had not been listening, which is not a fault in either of the two.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this evening, and this was a room with a door on it and there has not been a yard in this holding since Monday the thirteenth of this month. The count of times a bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move, and the count of questions available to a room and not taken is ten and it did not move, and it is not said in a yard, and the sentence that was said in this room at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-sixth is not going to be said in a yard in this holding this season.

The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of the room. It is face up. It is not filled. It was refused on the nineteenth of this month and it has not been withdrawn and it is not going to be taken off that board by anybody in this building, and it has no date on it and it did not lapse on the day it was made and it is not going to lapse, and there is nobody's name on it and there is no line on it for a name.

The register form is on the middle table where it has stood for forty days and it is not filled and it was not refused and nobody wrote a heading on it. The form with the four lines is in a drawer about four miles and a bit west of here and is not filled and its first line carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, which is the figure of the twenty-second return, and that is not the figure of the thirty-fourth and is not going to be made to be.

The window of the office's work is ten days off this morning and it is not tomorrow and it is not this month, and it is six days when it comes and no work is ever carried out of one.

The eleven lines that came out of the wood on the twenty-third are in a drawer in this building, face up, and the eight that did not come are not on the page and are not to be guessed at, and the nineteen are in a drawer about five miles east of here and are not to be corrected from the eleven, and nobody in this holding has asked the reader of this body to go up that road again and nobody is going to, and he has not offered, and about four people in this building have worked out that he is not going to offer either, and about four have not, and he knows about both of those fours and has said nothing to either of them and is not going to.

There is a drawer in this building with eleven lines in it and eight gaps in it and the two together are not nineteen and are not going to be made into nineteen, and there is a drawer about five miles east of here with nineteen lines in it, and nobody has said the one is right and nobody has said the other one is wrong, and the man of about fifty has entered both on two separate pages and has not put a mark on either and has not put one between them.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and the reason is not given again in a season. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the door is read on the first and on the thirtieth, and the thirtieth of this month is four mornings off this one and the first of the next month is the fifth, and the column will be read on both of them and will be a different figure on each and neither of those two figures is one anybody in this holding can check. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two and the next of them is the first of a month, five mornings off this one, and no page of this book is going to say why a man is fetched on the first of a month and has never once asked which number it is. The compost line is paid, one hundred pounds, and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen, and the word before the figure has never once disagreed with the figure and is not going to.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a sentence said once in a room is not a mark and four mornings to the end of a month is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled and are going to be unreconciled when this holding closes its books.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-sixth, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a sentence said once, and not by the clerk, who entered it without reading it back: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, TWO GATES AND SIX FEET OF WALL, FOUR PEOPLE, AN HOUR AND A QUARTER, AND THE TOE UP A FINGER'S WIDTH FOR THREE WEEKS. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED EIGHT HUNDRED AND FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIXTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND HAS RISEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN, SIXTY-TWO RISES AND FIFTY-ONE FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOUR DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THIRD OF A HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIVE, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND THIRTY DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SEVEN, FORTY-SEVEN APART, THE DRAWER SHUT AND NOT OPENED IN FOUR YEARS, AND WERE NOT SAID ALOUD, AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR THE RUN. TOVA REED SAID THE SENTENCE ONCE AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST, TWENTY-FOUR, AND THE REASON WAS THAT A WORD SAID ON THE NIGHT IT IS WRITTEN IS A WORD THAT GETS SAID AGAIN IN A FORTNIGHT. THE SENTENCE IS THAT SHE IS NOT GOING TO STOP RUNNING THE SEED WORK, THAT HE DOES NOT DECIDE WHAT SHE IS FOR, AND THAT THEY KEEP SEPARATE BOOKS AND SEPARATE COUNTS AND A REASON EACH FOR EVERYTHING. NOBODY IMPROVED IT AND NOBODY SAID IT AGAIN. THE READER OF THIS BODY WROTE IT IN HIS OWN HAND AND PUT IT IN THE DRAWER AND DID NOT READ IT BACK, WHICH IS THE FIRST TIME IN FOUR YEARS, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY ENTERED THAT AS A FACT ABOUT THE ENTRY AND NOT AS A FAULT. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAS BEEN TOLD A CONDITION EXISTS AND NOT WHAT IT IS, AND WAS NOT TOLD THIS EVENING AND DID NOT ASK. THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH IS FOUR MORNINGS OFF AND THE FIRST OF THE NEXT MONTH IS THE FIFTH, AND NEITHER RESETS THE COUNT OF THE THIRD LAUNDER, NOR THE WINDOW, NOR THE TWO BOARDS, NOR THE AGGREGATE. THE OFFER SHEET IS ON THE LOW BOARD, FACE UP, NOT FILLED, REFUSED ON THE NINETEENTH, NOT WITHDRAWN, WITH NO DATE ON IT AND NO NAME ON IT AND NO LINE FOR A NAME. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE, FORTY DAYS, NOT FILLED, NOT REFUSED. THE ELEVEN LINES ARE IN A DRAWER IN THIS BUILDING AND THE EIGHT THAT DID NOT COME ARE NOT TO BE GUESSED AT, AND THE NINETEEN ARE NOT TO BE CORRECTED FROM THE ELEVEN. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK IS TEN DAYS OFF AND IS NOT TOMORROW AND IS NOT THIS MONTH. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE NEXT OF THE TWO IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED, THE COUNT TWENTY-TWO, AND THE NEXT IS THE FIRST OF A MONTH, FIVE MORNINGS OFF. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
