# Chapter 378

## Two Hands

The twenty-first of this month was a Tuesday and the first line came round at the second hour and was worked, and about six people had the boundary ditch and both culverts until the fourth hour. They got four barrow-loads out of the far one and stacked them on the far shoulder in the same place they have gone in every morning this month, and the near one has been clear since the ninth and nobody has looked down it since the ninth.

At the eighth hour on the Tuesday the number went into the launder book and came out of the frayed one in the same breath, and nobody in this holding has ever known those two come out otherwise.

**Seven hundred and eighty-seven hundredweight.**

It has fallen off Monday by five and it is the hundred and forty-first figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first. The window is a hundred and eight and the fall went onto the falls, which puts it at fifty-nine rises and forty-nine falls, and those two are a hundred and eight.

A hundred and thirty-nine days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, the sentence for that written this morning for the hundred and thirty-eighth of them out of a hundred and forty. It concerns four bodies of households and the four launders are a different figure and the two are never added in this holding, and the man of about fifty has never in four years put the two on the same line of a page and has been asked about it once by a visitor and answered it by showing the page.

The two day-minus rules were said out loud at about the seventh hour, one man to each, before either number and to about four people who were not trusted with both. They stand at **five hundred and twenty-five days** and **five hundred and seventy-two**, forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened. Forty-one mornings out of sixty-five since the seventeenth of a month two months back, and nobody was asked for the word and nobody gave one.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fifth hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and there was one sheet of paper on the middle table and it was not the register form and it was not the offer sheet, both of which are still where they are, and the one that was on the low board is still on the low board and the one on the middle table has been on the middle table for thirty-five days.

The head of the sheet said what it was, in the man of about fifty's hand, and there were five lines under it and a third column ruled off at the right-hand side, and there is nothing at the head of the third column and nothing in it.

The head said this and it is on the page because a page that does not say what it is not for gets read as being for something else in about four years.

**THE TERMS OF THE READER OF THIS BODY AND OF TOVA REED FOR CARRYING ONE LIMITED PATTERN BETWEEN THEM, WRITTEN ON THE TWENTY-FIRST OF THIS MONTH, IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT, AND NOT THE FIVE TERMS THAT WERE DECLINED IN THE SIXTH MONTH, WHICH ARE ON A DIFFERENT PAGE IN A DIFFERENT HELDING AND ARE NOT IN THIS ROOM AND HAVE NOT BEEN TOUCHED.**

The man of about fifty put the pen down on the table, turned it so that the nib was pointing away from him, and said the thing that was his, and the reason came before it, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and six, and nobody thanked him and he did not expect to be.

**"I am not going to write in the third column and I am going to give the reason before I say that I am not going to, and the reason is that three hands on one sheet is three books, and I have kept four for four years and three of them have been wrong at some point and the fourth one is not right yet either.**"

**"And the second reason, which is a better one. A page with two hands on it is a page about what two people have agreed to do. The moment a third hand goes on it, it stops being that and becomes a page about what three people have agreed to do, and it does not matter that I would only be entering it and not deciding it, because in about four years the third column is the one anybody reads first and nobody will be able to tell from the page which of us was deciding.**"

Nobody argued it. The woman who keeps the seed house said that it was the first sensible thing anybody had said in that room in a month, which is not a thing anybody thanked her for, and the clerk made a third column at the man of about fifty's request and ruled it and wrote nothing at the head of it and put nothing in it, and it is on the page and it is empty and the rule against it is not going to be broken in about four years by anybody.

Then the two of them wrote. The reader of this body wrote the first and the fourth and Tova Reed wrote the second, the third and the fifth, and they wrote them in two sittings with the room quiet in between, and she gave the reason before the second one, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty-one, and it is her own count and it is hers alone.

**"I am going to write one line on this sheet about a word and I am going to give the reason I am going to write it before I write it, and the reason is that a word two people have been careful with for a season is a word somebody will be careless with in about four years, and the only way anybody in this room can stop that is to write down which of the meanings of it is meant and refuse to write the meaning itself.**"

**"So this is the line, and it is the whole of it, and the meaning is not on this sheet and is not going to be on this sheet. The word is used in one sense only. That is the sense it was given in a room with the door shut in the sixth month, in about eleven people, by the person who used it, and I said it once then and I am not going to say it again in this building. Anybody in this room who wants to know which sense is being used will have to ask her, and I am telling you now that the answer is the same on Thursday as it was in the sixth month and it is not going to change because a sheet of paper got written on.**"

And here is the whole of what the two hands put down, and it is entered exactly as it was written and neither hand improved the other one at any point, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling read all five lines twice with his hat on his knee and was asked nothing.

**ONE, IN HIS HAND. EACH END OF THIS IS A PERSON AND NOT A PART OF ANOTHER PERSON. NEITHER OF THEM HOLDS THE OTHER'S PART. WHAT CROSSES IS THE PATTERN AND WHAT THE PATTERN COSTS, AND NOTHING ELSE CROSSES, AND NOTHING ELSE IS TO BE LET ACROSS ON ANY MORNING INCLUDING THURSDAY. TWO, IN HERS. THE WORD IS USED HERE IN ONE SENSE ONLY, AND THAT SENSE IS THE ONE IT WAS GIVEN IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT IN THE SIXTH MONTH, AND IT IS NOT WRITTEN OUT HERE. THREE, IN HERS. EITHER END MAY CLOSE WITHOUT THE OTHER END'S LEAVE, WITHOUT WARNING, AND WITHOUT A CONVERSATION, AND THE OTHER END IS NOT TO BE ASKED WHY AFTERWARD AND IS NOT TO BE TOLD IN ADVANCE. FOUR, IN HIS. WHAT HAPPENS TO THE LOAD WHEN AN END CLOSES IS WRITTEN DOWN BEFORE THE MORNING AND NOT DURING IT, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY ENTERS IT AND RULES FOUR LINES UNDER IT AND LEAVES THEM EMPTY, AND IT WAS ENTERED ON THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH. FIVE, IN HERS. A COUNT EITHER OF THEM KEEPS IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON KEEPING IT AND OF NOBODY ELSE, AND NEITHER OF THEM WILL ADD ONE TO THE OTHER'S.**

The third of those five lines is the one that was hardest to put down and the reader of this body has been asked about it by nobody and is not going to talk about it. What is on the page about it is that it is hers, that he wrote it because she wrote it, and that on Thursday morning she is going to be able to close her end of a thing he is standing at the other end of without saying a word to him first and without any warning at all, and that he has agreed to that in advance, in his own hand, on a sheet of paper, in a room with the door shut. There is no part of this holding that is going to come and find him about it on Thursday. He worked that out on the seventh of this month, on his own, and did not tell anybody, and the man of about fifty found it out this afternoon by reading the line and said nothing about having found it out.

Nobody filled the third column and nobody wrote a heading on it, and the six blank columns on the door of the well house a quarter of a mile off stand with nothing at the head of them and have done for four years and nobody has written a heading on one of them today and nobody is going to.

The two hands were on the sheet for an hour and a half and at no point did either of them stop to ask the other one whether a line was right, and the reader of this body has thought about that since and cannot work out whether it was trust or an agreement not to stop, and the page is not going to decide it.

There is no column on the paper that says whose hand is whose and nobody is going to put one there. They are on the middle table, and they are not in a drawer, and the register form is on the middle table too, and the three of them have been on that table inside a fortnight and have not been put side by side and are not going to be.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling went out at about the eighth hour and did not say where he was going and nobody asked, and the question on the second of the four lines in his own book is twenty-nine days old and has no answer on it and no place for one and nobody has asked him about it in twenty-nine days, and the barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road.

Nobody thanked anybody, and that is the whole of the thanking this afternoon. Five things have been said out loud in a yard in this holding and got wrong, and it is still five, and this was a room with a door shut on it and there has been no yard in this holding since Sunday the thirteenth of this month. A bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of thirteen times and it is still thirteen. Ten questions were available to a room and not taken and it is still ten, and it is not said in a yard.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is four and did not move, and a sheet of terms written in a seed house is not a session and nothing was read in any wood today. The use log stands at fifteen lines and a sheet this holding wrote itself gets no line. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the next of the two mornings it is read on is the thirtieth of this month and that is nine mornings off this one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next is on the first of a month and is ten mornings off, and he has never once asked which number it was. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice, and a term is not a mark and two hands on one sheet is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-first, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of five terms in two hands and a third column empty: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE BOUNDARY DITCH AND THE TWO CULVERTS, ABOUT SIX PEOPLE TO THE FOURTH HOUR, AND THE FAR CULVERT GAVE FOUR BARROW-LOADS. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIRST FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND HAS FALLEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND EIGHT, FIFTY-NINE RISES AND FORTY-NINE FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHTH OF A HUNDRED AND FORTY, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, ONE MAN TO EACH, BEING FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIVE DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-TWO, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND IT IS THE FORTY-FIRST OF SIXTY-FIVE. THERE WERE ABOUT NINE PEOPLE IN THE SEED HOUSE FROM THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON WITH THE DOOR SHUT, AND ONE SHEET WAS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE WITH FIVE TERMS ON IT AND A THIRD COLUMN RULED AND EMPTY. THE TERMS ARE NOT THE FIVE TERMS DECLINED IN THE SIXTH MONTH, WHICH ARE ON A DIFFERENT PAGE IN A DIFFERENT HELDING. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY DECLINED TO WRITE IN THE THIRD COLUMN AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST, ONE HUNDRED AND SIX, AND SAID THREE HANDS ON ONE SHEET IS THREE BOOKS. THE COLUMN WAS RULED AND LEFT EMPTY AND NO HEADING WAS WRITTEN ON IT. THE READER OF THIS BODY WROTE THE FIRST TERM AND THE FOURTH. TOVA REED WROTE THE SECOND, THE THIRD AND THE FIFTH, AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST, TWENTY-ONE, AND SAID THE WORD IS USED IN ONE SENSE ONLY AND THE MEANING IS NOT ON THE SHEET. THE FIVE TERMS ARE THAT EACH END IS A PERSON AND NOT A PART OF ANOTHER PERSON AND WHAT CROSSES IS THE PATTERN AND WHAT IT COSTS, THAT THE WORD IS USED IN ONE SENSE ONLY, THAT EITHER END MAY CLOSE WITHOUT THE OTHER END'S LEAVE OR WARNING OR A CONVERSATION AND THE OTHER END IS NOT TO BE ASKED WHY AFTERWARD AND NOT TO BE TOLD IN ADVANCE, THAT WHAT HAPPENS TO THE LOAD IS WRITTEN DOWN BEFORE THE MORNING AND WAS ENTERED ON THE TWENTIETH, AND THAT A COUNT EITHER KEEPS IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON KEEPING IT. NEITHER HAND IMPROVED THE OTHER. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING READ ALL FIVE TWICE AND WAS ASKED NOTHING, AND HIS PAGE IS TWENTY-NINE DAYS OLD. NOBODY FILLED THE THIRD COLUMN AND THE SIX BLANK COLUMNS ON THE WELL HOUSE DOOR HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE OFFER SHEET IS ON THE LOW BOARD AND IS NOT FILLED AND WAS NOT WITHDRAWN. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE, THIRTY-FIVE DAYS, AND IS NOT FILLED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE NEXT OF THE TWO IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS FOUR. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
