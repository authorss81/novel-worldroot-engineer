# Chapter 380

## Without Asking

The twenty-third of this month was a Thursday and the third line came round at the second hour and was worked. The two places where the low ground lets water off between the sluice and the north gate had gone down a foot since Sunday, and the four of them cleared the first one and could not get the second one down without a pump that this holding does not own, and they put stones in it and left it, and nobody has blamed anybody for that and nobody is going to.

By the eighth hour on the Thursday the number was in the launder book, and the man of about fifty read it back out of the second book before anybody had asked him to, and they were the same.

**Seven hundred and ninety-one hundredweight.**

It has fallen off Wednesday by five and it is the hundred and forty-third figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first. The window is a hundred and ten and the fall went onto the falls, which is sixty rises and fifty falls, and that is the first time in this season the two have been exactly sixty and fifty and it is not a thing anybody has written a rule about.

For a hundred and forty-one days nothing has moved in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the line that says so was written this morning for the hundred and fortieth of them out of a hundred and forty-two. Four bodies of households, on that line. Not the launders, and never the launders on the same line, in four years.

The two day-minus rules were said out loud at about the seventh hour and before either of them, one man to each. They stand at **five hundred and twenty-seven days** and **five hundred and seventy-four**, forty-seven days apart, and the drawer was not opened. Forty-two mornings out of sixty-seven since the seventeenth of a month two months back.

About nine people went up the five miles of cart road at about the third hour of the afternoon. The channel along the inside of the line of the stems was finished on both days of it a week ago and the six of them who came for the leaf took the rake to the far end and put it down again and did not start a third day, because a third day of that channel is not this holding's to spend and nobody at the node asked them to.

The session was in the standing willow outside the ring of bare ground and not inside it. The ring is bare in its eleventh year and it was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced by walking it and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page, and the light off the west end of the wood is the reason the fourth hour is the hour and not the fifth.

The man of about fifty stood on the bank with the second book open on a board and the pen in his hand and his eyes on the wood. He has been told that a condition exists and he has not been told what it is. Nobody told him this morning and nobody asked him, and nobody is going to ask him, and he is not going to be thanked for not being told and has not once expected to be.

She was at the far end. He was at the near end. The arrangement of the twenty-first says what crosses between them and it is the pattern and what the pattern costs, and it says that either end may close without the other end's leave, without warning and without a conversation, and it says the other end is not to be asked why afterward.

It came at about ten minutes to the fifth hour and it did not come as a door and it did not come as a voice. It came the way a thing goes out of a hand that has been holding it, which is that the weight of it goes from somewhere to nowhere in about the length of a breath, and then there was one end of it and it was his, and it was heavier than one end of it is.

He did not ask. That is the whole of what happened in that wood at about ten minutes to the fifth hour and it is on the page because a page that leaves the not asking out is a page that lets a reader in later put it in.

He did not ask her what the word was. He did not ask her why, and he did not go after her, and he did not stand in the wood and look at the place she had been standing in, and the man of about fifty on the bank watched him not do all three of those and wrote nothing about any of them.

What happened to the load was what was settled at the fifth hour of the morning of the twentieth of this month, in advance, in a room with the door shut, with four ruled lines ruled under it and left empty. He held his end alone from about ten minutes to the fifth hour until the fifth hour, and it came off him at the rate a thing comes off a person holding it alone, and nobody in that wood made it sound like anything else and nobody said a word to him about it at any point, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not at the node and had not been asked to be and was not missed.

The fifth hour came and it was not a minute past the fifth hour. He came out of the wood and did not sit down on the bank and the man of about fifty did not offer him anything, and after a while the man of about fifty closed the second book and put the pen in his coat and said one thing, and the reason for it was given before it, and the door of the room was not opened for any of it.

**"I have entered a settlement this morning on a sheet of this holding's own paper and I want to say one thing about what I have done with the four lines under it, and I am going to give the reason before I say it, and the reason is that I ruled them on the twentieth for this morning and I am not going to pretend that I did not know which of those two things was more likely, and I did not know, and I wrote it down when I did not know, which is the only time it is worth writing.**"

**"Eleven lines came back. Four ruled lines cannot hold eleven lines, and a man who widens four ruled lines to eleven has stopped keeping a shape and started keeping a note. So the eleven are on their own page in this book, in my hand, and the four lines under the two words are empty, and the eight that did not come back are not on this page at all, and nobody is going to guess at the eight, and nobody is going to correct the nineteen from the eleven.**"

The count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and seven, and nobody thanked him and he did not expect to be, and about four of the nine people standing at the edge of that wood had wanted the four lines filled and about four had not and the four that had not knew why and are not going to say.

The reader of this body wrote the eleven out on the bank before anybody left the wood, on a sheet of this holding's own paper, in his own hand, sitting on the stump at the near end with the sheet on his knees and the wind taking the corner of it every few minutes, and the man of about fifty checked the eleven against the session book twice and found the same eleven both times and wrote no correction, and the count of what came out of that wood this afternoon is eleven and not nineteen and is not going to be rounded to nineteen by anybody.

They came back down the five miles in about an hour and a half and he walked at the front of the nine of them and did not turn round once, and nobody asked him anything on the road and nobody has asked him anything on the road since.

He sat on the stump for about a quarter of an hour after the other eight had gone to the road and he did not go after them either, and about four people in that wood had worked out in under a minute that he was not going to, and about four had worked out that he was not going to and were not going to be told either, and the two fours are the same four and the woman who keeps the seed house is one of them and put her hand flat on her own knee and left it there.

Nothing in this holding is going to say anything to him about it this evening. That was decided by nobody and arrived at by about nine people separately, and the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move, and this is not a yard and there has not been one since Monday the thirteenth of this month, and a thing that is not said in a yard cannot be got wrong in a yard, and there is a man of about thirty-one of Silling whose own page is thirty-one days old today and who has not been asked about that one either.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the eighth hour of the evening. The eleven lines are on their own page in the second book and the nineteen are in a drawer and the two of them are not to be compared this month by anybody, and a man who puts the two side by side and finds the difference is a man who has begun to correct a record he was not in when the record was made.

The offer sheet is on the low board and is face up and is not filled and it was refused on the nineteenth of this month and it has not been withdrawn and it is not going to be. The register form is on the middle table where it has stood for thirty-seven days and is not filled and was not refused. The form with the four lines is in a drawer about four miles and a bit west of here, face up, and its first line carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight, which is the figure of the twenty-second return, and nobody wrote a heading on it and nobody filled the other three.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of times a bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is four and did not move today, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden is nil and is nil, and he did not ask her anything this evening and was not going to.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and eleven lines out of a wood is not a use and gets no line. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the door is read on the first and on the thirtieth, and the thirtieth of this month is seven mornings off this one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next of them is on the first of a month and is eight mornings off, and he has never once in twenty-two of them asked which number it was. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a channel closing is not a mark and eleven lines are not a mark and a man not asking a question is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the twenty-third, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a channel closed without a conversation: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, TWO PLACES WHERE THE LOW GROUND LETS WATER OFF, FOUR PEOPLE, THE FIRST CLEARED AND THE SECOND STONED AND LEFT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND NINETY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THIRD FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND HAS FALLEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND TEN, SIXTY RISES AND FIFTY FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-ONE DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND FORTIETH OF A HUNDRED AND FORTY-TWO, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, ONE MAN TO EACH, BEING FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVEN DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FOUR, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND IT IS THE FORTY-SECOND OF SIXTY-SEVEN. THE SESSION WAS IN THE STANDING WILLOW OUTSIDE THE RING OF BARE GROUND AND NOT INSIDE IT, AND THE RING IS BARE IN ITS ELEVENTH YEAR AND WAS NOT WALKED. THE ENTRY WAS MADE AT THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE MORNING OF THE TWENTIETH, IN ADVANCE, IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT, WITH FOUR RULED LINES RULED UNDER IT AND LEFT EMPTY. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY STOOD ON THE BANK WITH THE SECOND BOOK OPEN AND THE PEN IN HIS HAND, AND HAS BEEN TOLD A CONDITION EXISTS AND NOT WHAT IT IS, AND NOBODY TOLD HIM AND NOBODY ASKED HIM. HER END CLOSED AT ABOUT TEN MINUTES TO THE FIFTH HOUR, WITHOUT HIS LEAVE AND WITHOUT WARNING AND WITHOUT A CONVERSATION, AND IT CAME THE WAY A THING GOES OUT OF A HAND, IN ABOUT THE LENGTH OF A BREATH, AND WHAT WAS LEFT WAS ONE END OF IT AND IT WAS HEAVIER THAN ONE END OF IT IS. HE DID NOT ASK HER WHAT THE WORD WAS AND HE DID NOT ASK HER WHY AND HE DID NOT GO AFTER HER AND HE DID NOT LOOK AT THE PLACE SHE HAD STOOD IN. WHAT HAPPENED TO THE LOAD WAS THE SETTLEMENT OF THE TWENTIETH. HE HELD HIS END ALONE AND IT CAME OFF HIM AT THE RATE A THING COMES OFF A PERSON HOLDING IT ALONE, THE FIFTH HOUR CAME AND IT WAS NOT A MINUTE PAST IT. ELEVEN LINES CAME BACK, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY PUT THEM ON THEIR OWN PAGE IN HIS OWN HAND, AND THE FOUR RULED LINES UNDER THE TWO WORDS ARE EMPTY, AND THE EIGHT THAT DID NOT COME BACK ARE NOT ON THAT PAGE AND ARE NOT TO BE GUESSED AT, HE GAVE THE REASON FIRST, ONE HUNDRED AND SEVEN, AND SAID FOUR RULED LINES CANNOT HOLD ELEVEN LINES. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS FOUR AND DID NOT MOVE. THE OFFER SHEET IS ON THE LOW BOARD, NOT FILLED, NOT WITHDRAWN. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE, THIRTY-SEVEN DAYS, NOT FILLED. THE FORM WITH THE FOUR LINES IS NOT FILLED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE NEXT OF THE TWO IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED, THE COUNT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
