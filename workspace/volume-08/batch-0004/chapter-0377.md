# Chapter 377

## Four Lines And Nothing Under Them

The twentieth of this month was a Monday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour with nobody on it. Nobody was at the headland above Marden and the fields above Marden waited, and there are forty-five of them and every one of them is the fourth line. The standing of the rota is seventy-four mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and forty-five not, and twenty-nine and forty-five are seventy-four. Twenty-nine taken did not move this morning, and it has not moved on any morning of this season, and no man is named anywhere in this holding in connection with a field that did not get worked.

A field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man, and it is on the page for the ninth time in a fortnight and it is not going to be improved, because the day it is improved on is the day somebody in about four years finds a number that went up on a morning when nobody was on the line and has to go looking for who did it.

The two counts of the rotation do not agree and both of them go in. This is the twenty-second morning the fourth line has come round since it began coming round on the twenty-sixth of a month three months back, and it is the ninth of them since the eighteenth of last month, and the second of those is the one the man of about fifty has been keeping since the middle of the sixth month and the first of them is the one the office has been keeping since before this holding had a book, and neither of the two offices has ever asked the other one which they are using.

The frost was on the headland until about the ninth hour and did no harm there. The man of about thirty-one of Silling came down the road with the barrow at about the ninth hour, turned it at the top of the yard and went home, and the question on the second of the four lines in his own book is twenty-eight days old this morning, with nothing on it and no place for anything, and nobody has asked him about it in twenty-eight days. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that road and did not rise this morning.

They weighed the Monday figure at the eighth hour, and the man of about fifty had it in the frayed book before the clerk had ruled the margin, and the two of them agreed, and that agreement is the test.

**Seven hundred and ninety-two hundredweight.**

It has risen, and it is the hundred and fortieth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first, and it came up off Sunday by nine. The window the rises and the falls are counted in stands at a hundred and seven and the rise went onto the rises, which makes fifty-nine rises and forty-eight falls.

A hundred and thirty-eight days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, and the sentence for that was written this morning for the hundred and thirty-seventh of them out of a hundred and thirty-nine, and it concerns four bodies of households and not the launders, and the two are never added in this holding and have not been added today.

The two day-minus rules were not said out loud at about the seventh hour and this page carries no figure for the run. The man of about fifty had the settlement to enter and he entered it in a room with the door shut at the fifth hour of the morning and a pair read aloud at the seventh hour is a pair read on the back of something else, and the drawer is shut and the pair itself stands at **five hundred and twenty-four days** and **five hundred and seventy-one**, forty-seven days apart.

About four people were in the one room at this holding at about the fifth hour of the morning with the door shut, and the man of about fifty had the second book open on the board and the pen out, and the count of sessions entered in this holding's book went from three to four before any of it was done, and it is not to go to four and a half when it is done.

He entered the four of the four, and then he entered the two words and the hour, and then he ruled the four lines under them, and nothing went under those four lines and nothing is going to.

**THE PURPOSE: A FURTHER READING IN THE STANDING WILLOW AT THE NODE, FURTHER IN THAN THE FIFTEENTH OF THIS MONTH, AND WHAT IS TO COME OUT OF IT IS THE LIST OF PLACES AND HOURS THE ENGINE MEANS TO ACT ON, AND NOTHING ELSE IS TO COME OUT OF THAT WOOD ON THAT MORNING. THE DURATION: THE FOURTH HOUR UNTIL THE FIFTH, AND NOT A MINUTE PAST IT, AND IF THE FIFTH HOUR ARRIVES FIRST THEN IT ARRIVES FIRST AND THE READING STOPS. THE STOP SIGNAL: THERE IS ONE, IT IS HERS, IT IS NOT ENTERED HERE AND IS NOT GOING TO BE, AND THE REASON IS THE REASON THIS BOOK IS CARRIED BETWEEN ROOMS AND NOT THROUGH THEM. THE INDEPENDENT WITNESS: THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO IS AT NEITHER END, WHO KNOWS THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND DOES NOT KNOW WHAT IT IS, AND WHO IS NOT TO BE TOLD, AND NOBODY IN THAT ROOM ASKED HIM FOR IT AND HE WAS NOT THANKED FOR NOT HAVING IT.**

Under the four of the four he wrote two words and the hour, being LOAD SETTLED, and ruled four lines under them and left them empty, and the reader of this body read out the settlement and the man of about fifty wrote it in his own hand and read it back, and the two are the same.

**THE SETTLEMENT. IF HER END CLOSES, HER SIDE IS NOT HELD BY ANYBODY ELSE AND NOT BY HIM. WHAT HE IS HOLDING, HE HOLDS ALONE, AND IT COMES OFF HIM AT THE RATE IT COMES OFF A PERSON HOLDING IT ALONE, AND NOBODY IN THIS ROOM IS TO MAKE THAT SOUND LIKE A THREAT AND IT IS NOT ONE. THE LIST COMES BACK WHOLE OR IT DOES NOT COME BACK, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING IS TO WRITE ANY PART OF IT OUT OF HIS OWN HEAD ONTO A SHEET, AND NOBODY IS TO BE ASKED FOR A MEMORY OF IT. IF IT COMES BACK WRONG IT COMES BACK WRONG AND IS ENTERED AS IT COMES BACK. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HOLDS NO END AND IS A WITNESS AND NOT A HOLDER. NOBODY IS TO BE ASKED AFTERWARD WHETHER THE MORNING WAS RIGHT, BY ANYBODY, IN ANY YARD, IN ANY SEASON. THE FOUR RULED LINES UNDER THIS ARE FOR WHATEVER COMES OUT OF IT, AND IF NOTHING COMES OUT OF IT THEY STAY EMPTY, AND THEY ARE NOT TO BE FILLED WITH A REASON.**

The reader of this body read the settlement out loud, all six of its clauses, and he read it slowly and he did not stop in the middle of a clause to look at anybody, and while he was reading it the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden put her thumb on the edge of the board and left it there, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling read the second clause twice with his lips and did not say it out loud, and the man of about fifty wrote all of it down and put his pen down flat when it was finished and did not rule the four lines until the reader had said the last word of the sixth clause.

Tova Reed was in that room and she did not give the reason before the thing, and the count that moves in this holding when she gives it is hers alone and nobody is going to hear it this morning. What she said was one sentence and it was said after the settlement had been read back.

"Heard. And I am not going to say one word about that page until Thursday, and anybody in this room who wants to ask me what happens to me if it goes wrong can ask me on Thursday and not before, and if they ask me on Thursday they can ask me after and not during."

He gave the reason first, as the room requires, and the count of that in the history of this holding is one hundred and five, and he was not thanked and does not expect to be, and what he had to give a reason for was entering a thing before it had happened.

**"I have put a settlement on the page this morning about something that has not happened yet, and I am going to give the reason I have done that before I say why I have done it, and the reason is that a thing agreed in a room at the fourth hour on the day it goes wrong is a thing that was agreed in a room at the fifth hour on the day it did not go wrong, and everybody in this building knows that and about four of them have stopped saying so.**"

**"So it goes in first. And the four lines under it stay empty tonight, and they stay empty tomorrow, and if the morning goes the way it may go, what goes under them is what came out of it and not what it felt like. A bookkeeper does not enter what a thing felt like. If he does, he has stopped keeping the book and started keeping a diary, and a diary is a thing that agrees with the man who wrote it.**"

Nobody improved that either. About four people in that room thought the four ruled lines were a waste of a good sheet and about four thought a man who keeps an empty place visible for a thing that has not happened is the only kind of bookkeeper there is, and neither of the two fours said so, and the man of about fifty heard both of them think it and wrote nothing at all against either.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling, who had said nothing at all in that room for the whole of the hour, asked one question on his way out, and it was whether the four ruled lines were ruled on the sheet or on a second sheet, and the man of about fifty told him they were on the same sheet, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling said that was right and went out, and nobody has asked him anything else.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the seventh hour of the evening. The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of the room and it is face up and it is not filled, and it was refused on Sunday and it has not been withdrawn, and the two of those are not the same and the man of about fifty entered both of them separately with a day against each. The register form is on the middle table where it has been for thirty-four days and it is not filled and was not refused, and nobody wrote a heading on it.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this morning and there was no yard this morning, and this was a room with a door on it. The count of times a bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the door is read on the first and on the thirtieth, and the thirtieth of this month is ten mornings off. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next of them is on the first of a month and is eleven mornings off this one, and nobody in this holding has ever asked him which number it is. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen, and it does not fall again until the first.

The count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is ten, and it did not move this morning, and it is not said in a yard, and the settlement that was entered at the fifth hour was a question taken.

The mark is four inches and it branches twice, and a settlement is not a mark and an empty place under a ruling is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twentieth, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of four lines ruled and nothing under them: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND THERE WAS NOBODY AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED, THE NUMBER BEING FORTY-FIVE, ALL FORTY-FIVE OF THEM THE FOURTH LINE. THE ROTA STANDS AT SEVENTY-FOUR MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND FORTY-FIVE NOT, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN UNMOVED. IT IS THE TWENTY-SECOND MORNING THE FOURTH LINE HAS COME ROUND SINCE THE TWENTY-SIXTH OF A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK AND THE NINTH SINCE THE EIGHTEENTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THE TWO COUNTS DO NOT AGREE AND BOTH GO IN. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND NINETY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND FORTIETH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND HAS RISEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND SEVEN, FIFTY-NINE RISES AND FORTY-EIGHT FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHT DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SEVENTH OF A HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FOUR DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-ONE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER IS SHUT AND AND THEY WERE NOT SAID ALOUD BECAUSE THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAD A SETTLEMENT TO ENTER, AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR THE RUN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK WENT FROM THREE TO FOUR AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE MORNING, BEFORE ANY OF IT WAS DONE, AND THE ENTRY CARRIES THE DAY, THE HOUR, A PURPOSE, A DURATION, A STOP SIGNAL WHICH IS HERS AND WHOSE WORDS ARE NOT ENTERED, AND AN INDEPENDENT WITNESS WHO HAS BEEN TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND NOT WHAT IT IS. UNDER THE FOUR HE WROTE TWO WORDS AND THE HOUR, BEING LOAD SETTLED, AND RULED FOUR LINES UNDER THEM EMPTY. THE SETTLEMENT IS THAT IF HER END CLOSES HER SIDE IS NOT HELD BY ANYBODY ELSE AND NOT BY HIM, THAT WHAT HE HOLDS HE HOLDS ALONE AND IT COMES OFF HIM AT THE RATE IT COMES OFF A PERSON HOLDING IT ALONE, THAT THE LIST COMES BACK WHOLE OR DOES NOT COME BACK AND NOBODY IS TO WRITE IT OUT OF HIS OWN HEAD ONTO A SHEET, THAT IF IT COMES BACK WRONG IT COMES BACK WRONG, THAT THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HOLDS NO END AND IS A WITNESS, AND THAT NOBODY IS TO BE ASKED AFTERWARD WHETHER THE MORNING WAS RIGHT. TOVA REED SAID SHE IS NOT GOING TO SAY ONE WORD ABOUT THE PAGE UNTIL THURSDAY. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY GAVE THE REASON FIRST, ONE HUNDRED AND FIVE, AND SAID A THING AGREED THE DAY IT GOES WRONG WAS AGREED THE DAY IT DID NOT, AND THAT A BOOKKEEPER DOES NOT ENTER WHAT A THING FELT LIKE. THE OFFER SHEET IS ON THE LOW BOARD, FACE UP, REFUSED ON THE NINETEENTH AND NOT WITHDRAWN, AND IS NOT FILLED. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE, THIRTY-FOUR DAYS, AND IS NOT FILLED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
