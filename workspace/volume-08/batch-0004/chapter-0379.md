# Chapter 379

## Separate Channels

The twenty-second of this month was a Wednesday and the second line came round at the second hour and was worked. The two gates and the about six feet of wall between them took four people an hour and a quarter, and the toe at the second of the three places is up a finger's width off what it was on the third of this month, and the four of them put a spade in it and a hand's depth of stone. The man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it.

The Wednesday figure was taken at the eighth hour and set against the frayed book, and a hundred and forty-two mornings of that series have not once come out otherwise and this one did not either.

**Seven hundred and ninety-six hundredweight.**

It has risen off Tuesday by nine and it is the hundred and forty-second figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first. The window is a hundred and nine and the rise went onto the rises, which is sixty rises and forty-nine falls, and the two of them have not been in that shape since the first of the sixth month.

A hundred and forty days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, the sentence for it written this morning for the hundred and thirty-ninth of them out of a hundred and forty-one. It concerns four bodies of households and the launders are not part of it and are not added to it and have not been added to it in four years, and a visitor in an earlier month asked the man of about fifty about that and he showed her the two pages rather than answering her.

The two day-minus rules were not said out loud this morning and no figure for the run goes on this page. The four on the wall did not finish before the seventh hour and the man of about fifty was one of the four and had both hands full, and a pair is not read out loud by a man with a hod of stone in each hand. The pair stands at **five hundred and twenty-six days** and **five hundred and seventy-three**, forty-seven days apart, and the drawer is shut and stays shut.

The rehearsal was in the low ground of this holding and not in any wood, and the count of sessions entered in this holding's book is four and did not move, and a session is a thing entered before it happens in a room with the door shut, and nothing was read out of any ground today and nothing was read into any.

About the second hour of the afternoon the two ends were about four hundred yards apart, along the ditch that runs from the sluice to the north gate, with the hedge on the left of it and a stand of alder on the right. She took the end by the north gate and he took the end by the sluice, and both of them walked, because the arrangement of the twenty-first says that what crosses is a pattern and its cost and that nothing else is to be let across, and a person standing still while another person reads is a person who has been asked to hold still, and nobody asked anybody to hold still.

The man of about fifty stood in the middle by the second culvert with the second book shut in his hand, because he is a witness to this holding and not to that arrangement, and he wrote nothing down, and he has entered the fact of standing there in the entry for the evening and not a word of what he felt, which is that a man standing between two ends of a thing with a shut book is the least comfortable place in this holding and the only one that is his.

The reader of this body said the reason out loud before the other end was walked to, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and seventy, and nobody thanked him and nobody asked to be thanked.

**"We are going to try it once, walking, and I am going to give the reason we are trying it before we try it, and the reason is that a thing agreed in a room on Tuesday and used for the first time on Thursday is a thing nobody in this holding has any idea what it will do, and this holding does not go into a wood with a thing it has not stood in a ditch with.**"

**"And the second reason is the one that matters and I am giving it second because it is the harder one. If it works this afternoon, then on Thursday I am going to know something about your end before you have said it, and I am telling the man with the book there now so that he can hear me say it. That is what it is going to be like and there is no way round it, and I would rather it was said in a ditch at the second hour of the afternoon than discovered in a wood at the fourth hour with about nine people watching.**"

What crossed was the pattern, and what crossed with it was the wear, and the two of them were the same shape and neither of them was carrying the other's end of it.

About the second hour and twenty minutes he felt it arrive and turned round, and she had not stopped and had not turned round and had not been near anything, and the thing that had arrived at the far end of it was that she had been carrying something since before the second hour and had not put it down, and it came into him at the back of the left side of his head as a cold place about the size of a coin and it stayed for about a minute and a half and then went.

He went on walking after he had turned round. That is on the page because a man who stops in a ditch to work out what has just happened to him is a man who has begun to hold the far end of a thing in his own head, and the whole of the arrangement of the twenty-first is that he does not. He walked the remaining three hundred yards at the same pace he had walked the first three hundred and she did the same at her end, and neither of them crossed the ditch and neither of them came back toward the other, and about four people who were not there would have got that wrong.

Neither of them knows what she was thinking. That is not a thing anybody established this afternoon and it is not a thing anybody is going to establish, and the two of them both said afterwards that they would rather not know, and the reader of this body put that on the page in his own words and Tova Reed said it was the correct entry and not an improvement.

And here is where the two of the sides of it are not the same, and this page is going to say which side each of them is on, because a thing that is not symmetrical and is written down as though it were is a lie with a table under it.

His side of the cost is in his body and it is small and it is measurable, and it is a cold place at the back of the left side of his head that comes about a minute before she says anything and goes about a minute after she has dealt with it, and a headache after that is a headache and not a mark and not an account and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and the mark is four inches and branches twice and is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning.

Her side of the cost is not in her body and it is not small, and it is this. She is the person who decides when he stops, and the reason she said the word in a room in the sixth month and gave the reason first is the only reason anybody has for believing her, and a person carrying the far end of a thing cannot put it down at the end of an hour, and cannot stop reading her own ground while she is carrying his, and cannot go home until he has gone home. She has not told him what the second half of that costs and he has not asked her, and the reason he has not asked her is not a courtesy. It is that a person who is told the price of a thing stops looking at the price, and he would rather be looking at it.

About four people in this holding know both halves of that and about four know one, and the two fours are not the same four, and nobody in this holding is going to be told which is which, and the count of questions available to a room and not taken is ten and it did not move this afternoon.

He stayed at the sluice end of that ditch for about half an hour after the others had gone up to the gate, on his own, and the alder stand was making a noise on the right of him the whole time and he did not hear it. He thought about asking her the thing he has wanted to ask since the sixth month, and he did not ask it, and he is not going to ask it on Thursday either, and the reason he is not going to is not a courtesy and has never been a courtesy. It is that the word is hers and it was said in a room with the door shut in about eleven people and the count of times it has been said in this holding's book is two, and a third time would be a third time.

There is no clause on the sheet of the twenty-first that stops him from asking her. She left it out on purpose and he worked out that she had left it out on purpose on the twenty-first, before she wrote the other four, and neither of them has said so out loud and this page is not going to make them say it.

The two of them walked back up the ditch together and did not talk about it for about a hundred yards, and then the woman who keeps the seed house said that there was tea on the step and that it would go cold, and that is the whole of what either of them said about it in front of anybody that afternoon, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was not there and nobody has asked him about a page in a book of the size of a hand that is thirty days old today and has no answer on it.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a field and not a yard. The count of times a bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move, and there was no yard.

The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of the seed house and it is face up and it is not filled and it was refused on the nineteenth and it has not been withdrawn and it is not going to be. The register form is on the middle table where it has been for thirty-six days and is not filled and was not refused, and the three sheets that have been on that table inside a fortnight are still not side by side.

There are fifteen lines in the use log and a sheet this holding wrote itself does not get one. Nothing is entered against the column on the well house door, which stands at fifty-two and is read on the first and on the thirtieth and on no other morning, and the thirtieth of this month is eight mornings off. A man of about seventy has been fetched twenty-two times and not once asked which number it was, and the twenty-third of those is on the first of a month, which is nine mornings off this one. The compost run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen and the word paid is on the line before the figure, and it falls again on the first.
