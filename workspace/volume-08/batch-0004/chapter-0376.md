# Chapter 376

## A Post With No Exit

The nineteenth was a Sunday and the third line came round at the second hour and was worked. The two places where the low ground between the sluice and the north gate lets water off took four people until about the fourth hour, and there was a foot of standing water in the second of the two and about four of the four were in it to the knee before the third hour. The man of about thirty-one of Silling did not come down the road with the barrow this morning and has not come down it on a Sunday in eleven years.

It was the eighth hour before the Sunday figure was down, and it was called back out of the second book in a voice that does not change for a rise or for a fall, and the two of them were the same.

**Seven hundred and eighty-three hundredweight.**

It has fallen, off Saturday by five, and it is the hundred and thirty-ninth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first of it. The window the rises and the falls are counted in stands at a hundred and six with fifty-eight rises and forty-seven falls still in it, and this morning's fall went onto the falls, which makes the two fifty-eight and forty-eight, and fifty-eight and forty-eight are a hundred and six.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in a hundred and thirty-seven days, and the sentence for that was written this morning for the hundred and thirty-sixth of them out of a hundred and thirty-eight. It concerns four bodies of households. The launders are not in it and have never been put in it.

The two day-minus rules were said out loud at about the seventh hour and before either of the numbers, one man taking the first and one taking the second. They stand at **five hundred and twenty-three days** and **five hundred and seventy**, forty-seven days apart. Forty mornings out of sixty-three, counted from the seventeenth of a month two months back, and this is a habit and not a measurement, and the drawer was not opened.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon with the door shut. The register form was on the middle table where it has stood for thirty-three days, and the offer sheet was on the low board at the far end, face up, and the woman from the Continuity Office came up the road at her own cost and was not sent for and was not asked to come and came.

That is three mornings, and it is one more than any office has managed in this building in four years, and nobody arranged them, and she paid her own way each time and asked for nothing to do with her hands.

She asked for it in the first minute, before anybody sat down, and she asked for it plainly and without a reason first, because the reason-first habit in this holding belongs to the people in it and she has not got it.

"You asked for a day and you have had a day. I am not going to pretend that you have had more than one, and I am not going to give you a second one before you have answered the first, and that is not me being difficult. That is me doing the only thing I know how to do with a form."

The reason came out ahead of the sentence that time and not the other way round, and the count of that in the history of this holding is one hundred and sixty-nine, and it belongs to him alone, and he was not thanked for it and did not expect to be. Nobody in that room had asked him to refuse anything.

**"I am going to refuse the post and I am going to give the reason I am going to refuse it before I refuse it, and the reason is that this holding has been wrong about something four times in four years and every one of those four times it was out of a document somebody else wrote, and I am not going to be wrong a fifth time about a document that is correct.**"

**"The first clause is mine and it is one sentence. A post with no exit is a place a person cannot be asked afterward whether it was right. Your sheet says it in the ninth line and it says there is no line on it for a person to write that down on, and you put those two sentences next to each other on purpose and I do not think you were told to.**"

**"The second clause is not mine and I am going to say whose it is before I say it, because I have been in this room four years and I am not going to take a sentence off a person who is not in it. It is Tova Reed's. She said it once in a room with the door shut in the sixth month, in about eleven people, and she said it before she used the word, and nobody improved it then and nobody is going to improve it now.**"

**"A person who cannot be asked afterward whether it was right is the third thing an anchor is not. I am not going to explain that. It has been explained once in this building by the person it belongs to and she is not here today and did not lend it to me for this, and a page that explains a thing twice is a page that has found somebody who cannot be asked.**"

Nobody improved either clause. The man of about thirty-one of Silling began to say something at the fourth minute, with his hand off the table, and stopped, and put the hand back on the table, and about four people in that room knew what the second half of the sentence had been and about four did not and the four that knew are not going to be told.

Nobody argued him out of it either. Iona Vey did not argue him out of it. She wrote for about a minute and a half, and then she read back what she had written, in a room, out loud, and the reading back is a thing this holding has been doing to figures for four years and there is no way to do it to a refusal other than to read it.

"What I have written is that the post is refused, and that the reason given is that the post has no exit, and that a second reason was given in a sentence belonging to a third person who is not present, and that the first person has said he is not going to explain it and I have not asked him to. That is all of it. I have not written down that he thinks the office is wrong, because he has not said that, and I am not going to put a sentence in a record that a man did not say to me."

Then she said the thing about the sheet, and she gave the reason before it.

**"I am going to tell this room what has happened to the instrument and I am going to give the reason before I tell you, and the reason is that a withdrawal is an answer, and it is not a morning on which any of us is going to get one.**"

**"The sheet is not withdrawn. It is not torn up, it is not returned, it is not in a drawer, and it stays where it is, on that board, face up, and it does not lapse on the day it was made and it did not lapse yesterday and it is not going to lapse this morning. You have refused it. That is a different thing from the offer being taken back, and in about four years somebody is going to read a page that has those two things on it and I would rather the page said it properly the first time.**"

She then asked one question, and it was the first and the only question she asked in three mornings in this building, and nobody else in that room was asked anything at all this afternoon.

"You have two things in that offer and you have refused the post. I am going to ask whether you are refusing the figures as well, because a man who wants a post and does not want the figures is a man I can send a different paper to, and a man who wants neither is a man I can send nothing to, and I would rather know which one I am looking at before I leave this room."

The reader of this body said the answer in one sentence and it was not softened and he did not explain it.

"I am refusing both halves of it and I am giving a reason for one, and the reason I am not giving a second reason is that a man who gives two reasons has a choice, and I have not got one."

She wrote that down too and read it back and the two readings were the same, and the count of sessions entered in this holding's book is three and did not move, because a refusal in a room with the door shut is a thing that was argued out loud and not a thing done in a wood.

About half an hour afterwards the room emptied and it did not empty all at once. The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden went out first and put her hand flat on the door frame going through it, the way she puts her hand flat on a sack. The man of about thirty-one of Silling went second and said nothing at all about the question on the second of the four lines in his own book, which is twenty-seven days old and has no place for an answer on it, and nobody asked him about it and nobody is going to. The barrow stands at eleven journeys up that four-mile road, a floor and not a ceiling, and it did not go up this morning because it is a Sunday.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a refusal is not a mark and a sentence belonging to somebody else is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled and nothing said in this room this afternoon is a fifth of anything.

The offer sheet is on the low board and it is not filled and it has not been withdrawn and it is not going to be carried back up the road this evening. The register form is on the middle table and it is not filled and was not refused and nobody wrote a heading on it. The thing under a cloth at the end of the long table is living rootwood and warm and the count of people who have looked at it is two and did not move this afternoon, and nobody asked the visitor about it, and she did not offer.

Fifteen lines in the use log, and the reason is not given again in a season. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and nobody has read it, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth, and the thirtieth of this month is eleven mornings off. Twenty-two fetchings of the man of about seventy, and the next one is on the first of a month, twelve mornings off, and he has never once asked which number it was. The compost line is paid, one hundred pounds, nineteen not discharged in nineteen, and it does not fall again until the first.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room with a door on it and there has not been a yard in this holding since Sunday the thirteenth of this month. The count of times a bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move. The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and a second connection is running with nobody able to say who holds its far end, and no person in this holding has an instrument that says whether the bridge that was run once is on or off, and that is still the whole of what this holding can say about either of them.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the nineteenth, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a post refused and an offer not withdrawn: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, TWO PLACES WHERE THE LOW GROUND LETS WATER OFF, FOUR PEOPLE TO THE FOURTH HOUR, WITH A FOOT OF STANDING WATER IN THE SECOND. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND HAS FALLEN. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND SIX, FIFTY-EIGHT RISES AND FORTY-EIGHT FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SEVEN DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIXTH OF A HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHT, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS WERE READ ALOUD AND BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, ONE MAN TO EACH, BEING FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND IT IS THE FORTIETH OF SIXTY-THREE. A WOMAN OF THE CONTINUITY OFFICE WAS IN THIS BUILDING FOR A THIRD MORNING AT HER OWN COST, AND IT IS ONE MORE THAN ANY OFFICE HAS MANAGED HERE IN FOUR YEARS, AND NOBODY ARRANGED THE THREE. SHE ASKED FOR THE ANSWER BEFORE ANYBODY SAT DOWN AND GAVE NO REASON FIRST, AND SAID SHE WAS NOT GOING TO GIVE A SECOND DAY BEFORE THE FIRST WAS ANSWERED. THE READER OF THIS BODY GAVE THE REASON FIRST, ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-NINE, AND REFUSED THE POST ON TWO CLAUSES, THE FIRST BEING THAT A POST WITH NO EXIT IS A PLACE A PERSON CANNOT BE ASKED AFTERWARD WHETHER IT WAS RIGHT, AND THE SECOND BEING THAT A PERSON WHO CANNOT BE ASKED AFTERWARD WHETHER IT WAS RIGHT IS THE THIRD THING AN ANCHOR IS NOT, WHICH HE SAID BELONGS TO TOVA REED AND WAS SAID ONCE IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT IN THE SIXTH MONTH IN ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE, AND HE DID NOT EXPLAIN IT AND SHE WAS NOT HERE AND DID NOT LEND IT. NOBODY IMPROVED EITHER CLAUSE AND NOBODY ARGUED HIM OUT OF EITHER, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING BEGAN TO SAY SOMETHING AT THE FOURTH MINUTE AND STOPPED. SHE ASKED ONE QUESTION IN THREE MORNINGS AND WAS TOLD HE IS REFUSING BOTH HALVES OF THE OFFER AND GIVING A REASON FOR ONE. SHE WROTE THE REFUSAL DOWN AND READ IT BACK ALOUD, AND DID NOT WRITE THAT HE THINKS THE OFFICE IS WRONG, BECAUSE HE DID NOT SAY THAT. THE SHEET IS NOT WITHDRAWN AND NOT TORN UP AND NOT RETURNED AND IS NOT IN A DRAWER, AND IT STAYS ON THE LOW BOARD FACE UP, AND A REFUSAL IS NOT A WITHDRAWAL. THE OFFER IS REFUSED AND NOT FILLED. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND IS NOT FILLED AND WAS NOT REFUSED. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS THREE. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE, AND NOTHING SAID IN THAT ROOM IS A FIFTH ACCOUNT. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE NEXT OF THOSE IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
