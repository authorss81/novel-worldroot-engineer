# Chapter 381

## The Third Thing An Anchor Is Not

The twenty-fourth of this month was a Friday and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour with nobody on it. Nobody was at the headland above Marden and the fields above Marden waited, and there are forty-six of them and every one of them is the fourth line. The rota stands at seventy-five mornings in force with twenty-nine taken and forty-six not, and twenty-nine and forty-six are seventy-five. Twenty-nine taken has not moved on any morning of this season, and there is no name in this holding attached to a field that did not get worked and there is not going to be one.

A field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man, and it has gone on the page ten times in a fortnight and it is not going to be improved, and the reason is that the improvement is what somebody in about four years will be reading instead of the number.

The two counts of the rotation do not agree and both go in. This is the twenty-third morning the fourth line has come round since it began coming round on the twenty-sixth of a month three months back, and it is the tenth of them since the eighteenth of last month, and the man of about fifty has entered both of them in the same line with the hour against it and has not reconciled them and is not going to.

The figure for the Friday was entered at the eighth hour and read back at the eighth hour out of the book with the frayed corner, and the two of them are the same, and that is the hundred and forty-fourth morning that has been true.

**Eight hundred hundredweight.**

It has risen off Thursday by nine and it is the hundred and forty-fourth figure of a series that has not come to the same figure twice since the first of it, and it is a round figure, and that series has not had one in four years, and nobody in this holding has said a word about that and about four of them noticed. The window is a hundred and eleven and the rise went onto the rises, which is sixty-one rises and fifty falls.

A hundred and forty-two days without a move in the aggregate of the four bodies of households, the sentence for it written this morning for the hundred and forty-first of them out of a hundred and forty-three. It concerns four bodies of households and not the launders and the two are not added and have not been added since a visitor in an earlier month asked what the addition would mean and was shown a page.

The frost lasted until about the eleventh hour and the six of them who had been on the headland came down in the order they went up, which is not an order anybody chose, and the marker for the fourth place is a sacking needle in a lump of chalk that the man of about fifty keeps in the left pocket of his coat in this weather and has kept in that pocket for four years and has lost twice and found it once.

The two day-minus rules were not said out loud this morning and this page carries no figure for the run. The pair stands at **five hundred and twenty-eight days** and **five hundred and seventy-five**, forty-seven days apart, and the drawer is shut. It was not read out loud because the fourth line came round on a morning when about nine people were on the headland in the dark and the man of about fifty was the one carrying the marker for the fourth place and came in at about the tenth hour with frost in his eyebrows, and a pair is not read out loud to a man who has been out in the dark since the second hour.

About eleven people were in the one room at this holding from about the fifth hour of the afternoon with the door shut, and the count of eleven is the count of the room in the sixth month and nobody chose it.

It was Tova Reed who said the third of them, reason first, and the count of that in the history of this holding is twenty-two, and it is hers and of nobody else, and nobody thanked her and she did not expect to be, and the man of about fifty was in that room and did not hear the other two because she did not say the other two.

**"I am going to say one of the three out loud in this room, and I am going to give the reason I am going to say it before I say it, and the reason is that a man used one of my sentences in this building on Sunday and said whose it was before he used it, which is more than most people do, and I would rather he had had it from my mouth than out of the six weeks he has had it.**"

**"So the reason is this. I took that word apart once, in a room with the door shut, in the sixth month, in about eleven people, and I said what it meant before anybody used it again. It is now being used in this building five days a week by people who were not in that room, and the only defense a thing that was said once in a room has is being said again in front of the people who are about to use it.**"

**"The third thing an anchor is not is a person who cannot afterward be asked whether it was right. That is the whole of it and I am not going to say one word more about it than that."**

The room was the man of about fifty, the reader of this body, Tova Reed, the man of about thirty-one of Silling, the woman who keeps the seed house, the clerk, the man of about seventy-four with a stick, who came as far as the door and did not go past it, and about four others whose names are in this holding's book and are not on this page.

Nobody improved it and nobody elaborated it and nobody asked her a second question about it, and about four people in that room had the sentence in their mouths and nobody said it and the man of about thirty-one of Silling took his out of the room with him and did not say it anywhere, and the count of times that sentence has been said out loud in this holding is three, and one of those three is this afternoon.

Then she said the rest of it, and it was not a reason and it was not the word.

"I am not going to say the other two. They are on the same page as that sentence, in the same room, in the sixth month, and a word that has been taken apart once in a room does not get better by being taken apart twice in front of more people, and if it needs saying twice in front of more people then it was not said properly the first time, and I said it properly the first time. The count of times that word has been used in this holding with no meaning on it is two, and neither of them was mine, and it has not gone up this month and it is not going to go up before the end of the season."

Nobody thanked her. The man of about fifty was in that room and he has been told that a condition exists and he has not been told what it is, and nobody told him this afternoon and nobody asked him, and he was not thanked for not knowing it and has never asked to be.

About four people in that room had wanted to ask her what the word is and about four had not, and the four that wanted to had wanted to since the sixth of this month, and the four that had not had worked out inside a minute that the whole of the third term on the sheet of the twenty-first is the answer and that asking would be spending it. Neither of those fours said a word, and the reader of this body, who wrote the fourth term in his own hand on the twenty-first, sat where he sat and did not say what the four of them were thinking and did not know all of it.

The sheet of the twenty-first is on the middle table where the register form has been for thirty-eight days, and the two of them are not side by side, and the third column on the terms is still empty and has not been filled and nobody is going to fill it.

The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road, and naming it is not answering it, and the sheet that claims it is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular and cannot be put down by anybody in this holding. The vault of forty households went under four hundred yards off on a day about two months back and has not come up and nobody has been down and nobody is going to be. The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not priced by walking it and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page, and the fact that the work in that wood is done outside it is a fact about the light and not a reason to go in. A bridge was run once for about nine minutes, being forty-two days back, and its standing is in no book, and a second connection is running with nobody able to say who holds its far end, and no person in this holding has an instrument that says whether the first one is on or off, and that is still the whole of what this holding can say about either of them.

The offer sheet is on the low board at the far end of the room and it is face up and it is not filled, and it was refused on the nineteenth of this month and it has not been withdrawn and it is not going to be, and there is nobody in this building who has been told to take it off that board and there is nobody going to be.

Nobody thanked anybody, and there was no point in the evening on which anybody in that room could have. Five things said out loud in a yard in this holding have been got wrong and it is still five, and this was a room with a door shut on it, and there has not been a yard here since Sunday the thirteenth of this month. A bookkeeper has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of thirteen times and it is still thirteen. Ten questions were available to a room and not taken and it is still ten, and it is not said in a yard, and a sentence said in a room of eleven is not one of the ten.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is four and did not move, and nothing was read in any wood today. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the door is read on the first and on the thirtieth, and the thirtieth of this month is six mornings off this one, and the six columns beside it have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one today.

The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two, and the next of them is on the first of a month and is seven mornings off, and he has never once in twenty-two of them asked which number it was, and no page of this holding's book is going to supply a reason for a man in about four years. The compost line is paid, one hundred pounds, and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen, and it is not a measurement of the ground and is not going to be used as one.

The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a sentence said out loud in a room is not a mark and being right about a word is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm stand at four and are unreconciled.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-fourth, in about eleven people, at this holding, on the standing of the third thing: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR WITH NOBODY ON IT AND THE FIELDS ABOVE MARDEN WAITED, THE NUMBER BEING FORTY-SIX, ALL OF THEM THE FOURTH LINE. THE ROTA STANDS AT SEVENTY-FIVE MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND FORTY-SIX NOT, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN UNMOVED. IT IS THE TWENTY-THIRD MORNING THE FOURTH LINE HAS COME ROUND SINCE THE TWENTY-SIXTH OF A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK AND THE TENTH SINCE THE EIGHTEENTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THE TWO COUNTS DO NOT AGREE AND BOTH WENT IN. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED EIGHT HUNDRED HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND HAS RISEN, AND IT IS THE FIRST ROUND FIGURE THAT SERIES HAS REACHED IN FOUR YEARS. THE WINDOW IS A HUNDRED AND ELEVEN, SIXTY-ONE RISES AND FIFTY FALLS. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-TWO DAYS, THE SENTENCE WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIRST OF A HUNDRED AND FORTY-THREE, AND IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-EIGHT DAYS AND FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, THE DRAWER SHUT, AND WERE NOT SAID ALOUD, AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR THE RUN. THERE WERE ABOUT ELEVEN PEOPLE IN THE ONE ROOM AT THIS HOLDING FROM THE FIFTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON WITH THE DOOR SHUT, AND THE COUNT OF ELEVEN IS THE COUNT OF THE ROOM IN THE SIXTH MONTH AND NOBODY CHOSE IT. TOVA REED SAID THE THIRD THING OUT LOUD AND GAVE THE REASON FIRST, TWENTY-TWO, AND THE REASON WAS THAT A MAN HAD USED ONE OF HER SENTENCES IN THIS BUILDING ON THE NINETEENTH, SAID WHOSE IT WAS BEFORE HE USED IT, AND THAT THE ONLY DEFENSE A THING SAID ONCE IN A ROOM HAS IS BEING SAID AGAIN IN FRONT OF THE PEOPLE WHO ARE ABOUT TO USE IT. THE THIRD THING AN ANCHOR IS NOT IS A PERSON WHO CANNOT AFTERWARD BE ASKED WHETHER IT WAS RIGHT, AND SHE SAID NOTHING FURTHER ABOUT IT. NOBODY IMPROVED IT AND NOBODY ELABORATED IT AND NOBODY ASKED HER A SECOND QUESTION, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING TOOK HIS OUT OF THE ROOM WITH HIM AND SAID IT NOWHERE. THE COUNT OF TIMES THAT SENTENCE HAS BEEN SAID OUT LOUD IN THIS HOLDING IS THREE. SHE DID NOT SAY THE OTHER TWO, AND THE COUNT OF TIMES THE WORD HAS BEEN USED IN THIS HOLDING WITH NO MEANING ON IT IS TWO, AND NEITHER OF THEM WAS HERS. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY WAS IN THE ROOM AND HAS BEEN TOLD A CONDITION EXISTS AND NOT WHAT IT IS. THE THIRD COLUMN ON THE SHEET OF THE TWENTY-FIRST IS EMPTY. THE OFFER SHEET IS ON THE LOW BOARD, NOT FILLED, NOT WITHDRAWN. THE REGISTER FORM IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE, THIRTY-EIGHT DAYS, NOT FILLED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE NEXT OF THE TWO IS THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED, THE COUNT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
