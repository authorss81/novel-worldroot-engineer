# Chapter 346

## Two Hundred And Seventy-Two

The nineteenth of this month was a Friday, and the reader of this body was at the node, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, four miles and a bit behind him and out of sight, and it is the boundary ditch and the two culverts and it took about six people until about the fourth hour, and nobody at that place has any business knowing how it went and this page does not know either.

The man of about fifty had the two books out on a board in the open air by about the eighth hour of the morning because it is the only place they have been read out loud at that is not the seed house, and the second reading at that place was on the tenth of this month and this is the third.

The figure for the nineteenth went into the book that keeps the third launder and was read back out of the other one, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and twenty-three hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and ninth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In seventy-six days the series has risen forty-three times and fallen thirty-three times, and those two add to seventy-six. A fall is a fall and the page does not apologise for the number and does not remark on where it sits against anything else, because a number that gets a sentence around it in a book gets a sentence around it in a head.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and seven days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the hundred and sixth morning out of one hundred and eight.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **four hundred and ninety-three days** and **five hundred and forty**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and was not opened on the road either. He has read them out loud on twenty mornings out of the thirty-three since he started again on the seventeenth of last month.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the wall with his coat off and his sleeves up before the light was properly on the ditch, and he had it written out on the knee of his leg in a bit of pencil on the back of a day sheet, and he had not written the second rule.

About nine people stood at the wall over the morning in ones and twos and none of them stood there the whole time, and the four of them who came up the cart road and stayed came up the cart road and are not of this holding and are not members of it and were not asked to be and none of them was made one this morning. They are a body of households at Havergate and a body of households at Low Wether and a body of households at Marden and a body of households at Silling, and each of the four wrote a name, and the names went one at a time into two lines and the two lines are the whole of the form.

The return went in at about the second hour of the afternoon on a steelyard that hangs on the wall of a building with no roof and has hung on that wall for longer than this holding has had a book.

**Two hundred and seventy-two hundredweight.**

That is the thirtieth return and the twentieth name-pair in eleven years, and it is eleven hundredweight above the twenty-ninth, which was two hundred and sixty-one on the tenth of this month. The dial came down off two hundred and fifty-eight and the man of about fifty wrote it into the second book and the man of about thirty-one of Silling wrote it into the first one, and the two are the same.

And then the man of about fifty read out of the day sheet what he had written on it at about the fifth hour of the morning, before anybody had asked him for it and before the second book was open, and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety and did not move this afternoon and is ninety.

**"It came in two ways and both of them land on it. Two hundred and thirty-nine on the second of this month, plus thirty-three. And one hundred and ninety-five on the twenty-second of last month, plus seventy-seven.**"

**"This holding is using that derivation and is not refusing it, and I want the word using on the page. A man who refuses a derivation is a man who thinks the number is in doubt, and the number is not in doubt, the number is on a dial that has been on that wall longer than this holding has had a book. What is in doubt is the two halves and the two halves are what I write about, and they are two and not one figure and they have not been added together.**"

Nobody improved it. The man of about thirty-one of Silling did not look up. He put the pencil behind his ear and then took it out again and then put it behind his ear.

The second rule stayed blank. Nothing was written in it and nothing was said about it, and it is the fourteenth return in eleven years at which it has been left blank, and the count of returns at which it has been left blank is fourteen and that is a count of blanks and is not the count of a man and has never been the count of a man.

Harlan Vetch holds the fifth share and has held it eleven years and fifty-four days as of yesterday, and he was not at that place and did not need to be, and the number of times the man standing at that wall has brought a barrow up the road to it is eleven, and it was eleven the last time anybody said it out loud in this holding and it is eleven now, and the count is a floor and not a ceiling and it was not raised this afternoon and it is not going to be raised because a room got quiet.

The form on the middle table at the seed house, a day's walk west of where that dial hangs, still carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight on its first line, and that is the figure of the twenty-second return and not of this one and not of the twenty-fourth, the twenty-sixth, the twenty-seventh, the twenty-eighth, the twenty-ninth or the thirtieth, and nobody wrote a heading on it and it is a different document from the paper on the table beside it.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres was about four hundred yards from the door of that building and it was not walked this morning and it was not walked on the tenth of this month, and nobody at that place put a level on it, and the two lines on the back of a sheet in an inside pocket both still stop at its inside edge and go round it.

The light went off the ditch at about the fifth hour and the four bodies of households went down the cart road in twos and threes, and the woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not among them and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and it is nil.

And then, at about the sixth hour, in the one room at that place that has a door on it, where the handles are and where nobody has sat this season, about six people were, and the door was shut.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety and did not move this evening either.

**"I have said the same sentence in two rooms in two days and I am going to say it a third time in a third room this evening, and the reason I am not giving a reason first is that a sentence a man has already given a reason for does not need one twice, and I am not going to invent a third reason for it and call it the first.**"

**"Nobody in this holding has an instrument that says the bridge is on. That is three mornings and three rooms and a voice that is not always mine. I have said it in the seed house, I have heard it said in the cart shed, and I am saying it in a room at the end of a cart road with a door shut, and about four years from now three people in this holding are going to be able to say that a thing was said three times and that is going to sound like a fact and it is not one.**"

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-five and did not move this evening and is one hundred and fifty-five.

**"And I want the other half said out loud in this room as well, because I have not heard anybody say it in three days and it is the half that the first half gets used for. A corridor being named is not a corridor being answered.**"

**"We named it on the sixteenth of this month. Nine people, a table, about ten minutes, and no line under the word. That is a name. A name is not a road and a name is not a claim and a name is not the bridge and it is not the thing that has been running for a month with nobody at the far end of it. About four years from now somebody is going to read the word in a book of ours and be sure a thing was established, and the answer is that nine people wrote a word on a table in a holding with no charter and could not write one line under it, and the not writing the line is the only part of it that is on a page.**"

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was six people at a stone wall with a door shut and about four of them who had heard a sentence for the second time in three days and about four who were hearing it for the first time and understood that the second and the fourth of those were the same fact about a sentence and not about a bridge.

The room emptied. The man of about fifty closed the door behind the last of them and put the two books under his arm and did not write a line about any of it in either of them, and the count of questions available to a room in this holding and not taken is ten and it is not said in a yard. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move on any of the three of these mornings, and all three of these were rooms and a room is not a yard.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and is not read. The man of about seventy is not fetched. The compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen. The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is nil, and nothing has been done that needed one.

Nobody thanked anybody. A dial on a wall of a building with no roof said two hundred and seventy-two hundredweight for the thirtieth time in eleven years and four people who are not of this holding wrote their names in two lines, and one rule on a form was left blank for the fourteenth time, and a sentence was said for the third time in three days in a third room and nobody improved it, and the barrow count is eleven and is a floor.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the nineteenth of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of the thirtieth return, and not by the man of about fifty, who said a thing for the third time in three days without giving a reason first: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED FOUR MILES AND A BIT WEST OF THIS ACCOUNT AND THE ROTATION OF THE ROTA WAS NOT ALTERED BY ANYBODY BEING STANDING IN A DIFFERENT PLACE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE HUNDRED AND NINTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN SEVENTY-SIX DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN FORTY-THREE TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTY-THREE TIMES. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND SEVEN DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE HUNDRED AND SIXTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHT. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-THREE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY DAYS, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING TWENTY MORNINGS OUT OF THE THIRTY-THREE SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN. THE THIRTIETH NINTH-DAY RETURN WENT IN ON A STEELYARD HUNG ON THE WALL OF A BUILDING WITH NO ROOF AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND READ TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT OUT LOUD AND READ IT BACK. IT IS THE TWENTIETH NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS AND IT IS ELEVEN ABOVE THE TWENTY-NINTH, AND THE TWO WAYS IT COMES ARE TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE PLUS THIRTY-THREE AND ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE PLUS SEVENTY-SEVEN, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT, AND THE TWO HALVES HAVE NOT BEEN ADDED TOGETHER. A NAME WENT INTO BY AND A NAME WENT INTO WITNESSED BY, ONE AT A TIME, BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, AND NONE OF THE FOUR IS A MEMBER OF THE COOPERATIVE AND NONE WAS MADE ONE. THE SECOND RULE IS BLANK AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT, AND THAT IS THE FOURTEENTH RETURN IN ELEVEN YEARS AT WHICH IT HAS BEEN LEFT BLANK, AND A COUNT OF BLANKS IS NOT THE COUNT OF A MAN. HARLAN VETCH HAS HELD THE FIFTH SHARE ELEVEN YEARS AND FIFTY-FOUR DAYS AND IS NOT AT THAT PLACE. THE COUNT OF TIMES THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING HAS BROUGHT THAT BARROW UP THAT ROAD IS ELEVEN AND IS A FLOOR AND NOT A CEILING AND WAS NOT RAISED. LINE ONE OF THE FORM WITH FOUR LINES STILL CARRIES ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, BEING THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND NOT OF THIS ONE, AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON IT. THE RING INSIDE THE ELEVEN ACRES WAS NOT WALKED AND NOT MEASURED AND NOBODY PUT A LEVEL ON IT. A SENTENCE WAS SAID FOR THE THIRD TIME IN THREE DAYS IN A THIRD ROOM, THAT NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS AN INSTRUMENT THAT SAYS THE BRIDGE IS ON, AND IT WAS SAID AGAIN THAT A CORRIDOR BEING NAMED IS NOT A CORRIDOR BEING ANSWERED. ALL THREE MORNINGS WERE ROOMS AND THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE. THE COUNTS OF THE TWO WHO SPOKE ARE NINETY AND ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FIVE AND NEITHER MOVED, AND EACH IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON WHO IS SPEAKING AND OF NOBODY ELSE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
