# Chapter 353

## Still On The Table

The twenty-sixth of this month was a Friday, and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour, and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited. The number is thirty-nine, and all thirty-nine of them are the fourth line, and the rota stands at sixty-eight mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and thirty-nine not, and twenty-nine and thirty-nine are sixty-eight. Twenty-nine taken did not move, and it has not moved on any morning in this season, and nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

The man of about fifty read the day sheet out in the yard at about the ninth hour, which he does on the mornings the fourth line comes round and there is nothing on the ground to stand and look at. Five things, in five places. The line that came round and had nobody on it. The figure for the day. The form on the table and the drawer shut. The man of about seventy not fetched, and how many days it is to the first. And the window, and how many days it is to the sixth. He put a stroke beside two of the five and left the other three alone and did not say which two, and about four people in that yard could work it out and about four could not.

The figure for the twenty-sixth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and forty-four hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and sixteenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In eighty-three days the series has risen forty-seven times and fallen thirty-six times, and those two add to eighty-three. It came up off Thursday by nine, and that is the third morning in a row it has come up by nine, and the man of about fifty wrote the third one in the margin and did not go over it and did not write anything under it.

The reader of this body went down to the field gate at about the tenth hour on a morning when the fourth line had come round and gone away again, and the gate was shut and the gap at the far end of it was open, and the ground against the foot of the wall was beaten flat and had been beaten flat for longer than anybody has been keeping figures. He stood about nine feet off it on the side of the track, which is the distance the man of about fifty stood at on the twelfth, and he stood there for about as long as a man stands at a thing he is not going to put his hand on, and he came back up the track without putting a hand on it and without going into the hollow.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and fourteen days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the hundred and thirteenth morning out of one hundred and fifteen. That sentence is not the aggregate of the four launders and the two have never been added and the man of about fifty reads the first of them out loud every morning and does not read the second one out loud with it and about four people in this holding have stopped hearing the difference and about four have not.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred days** and **five hundred and forty-seven**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on twenty-six mornings out of the thirty-nine since he started again on the seventeenth of last month.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and the light was off the south wall and on the middle table and on the five things on it, and the five things were in the order they arrived in and had been in that order for four days and nobody had put a hand on one of them.

The form from the office that keeps a register was on the table. It came up the fen road on the sixteenth of this month in a leather envelope. It was read out whole on that afternoon. It was read out whole a second time on the seventeenth, with about nine people in this room and the door shut, and it is the twenty-sixth and it is still on the table and it is still not in the drawer and it is still not filled, and no name has been written on it and no office has been written at the foot of it.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-seven, and nobody improved it.

**"That paper has been read out in this room twice and it has been answered neither time, and I am going to say that out loud on the last morning this holding has anything written down for this month, and the reason is that about four years from now somebody is going to open a book and find a piece of paper that was read out twice, and the two mornings are in the book, and that is going to look like a decision.**"

**"It was not a decision. It is not a decision now. It has been on that table for ten days and about four of you have looked at it every morning and about four have looked at the drawer every morning and neither of those two things is a decision and I am not able to turn either of them into one and I am not going to try.**"

**"And being read out twice is not being answered. And being read out twice is not being refused either, and I want the second half of that said in this room as well, because about four people in here have been treating the not filling it as though it were a yes and the not answering it as though it were a no, and it is neither, and there is no third thing to put in the gap and anybody who puts one there is filling it.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had wanted it put in the drawer and about four had wanted it burned, and neither of those two is a thing anybody in this holding has the standing to do to a document that is on a table and not in a drawer, and about four people in that room had known that before this afternoon and about four had not.

The reader of this body did not say the rest of it and about four people in that room had been waiting since the seventeenth of this month for him to say the rest of it, and the rest of it is an offer, and nobody in this holding has been made one, and the paper from an office that keeps a register is the plant of the office and not the offer itself, and the difference between a plant and an offer is about four years long and this holding has not got to that morning yet.

The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and is not read, and the two next mornings it is read on are the thirtieth of this month and the first of the eighth, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the next fetching is the first of the eighth, and no page of this holding's book is going to say why. The compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen and it does not fall again until the first. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is nil.

The bridge is not established as on. The corridor in the pan is open and is not a road and is not a claim and is not the bridge. There is no instrument in this holding that says whether a hollow in beaten ground at the foot of a wall at the field gate is connected this morning to a board against a wall at the stone head, and the count of questions asked in that direction is nil and it is nil, and this page is not going to put a yes or a no in it.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was in the room from about the fourth hour until about the sixth and did not open the book of his own, and the four lines in it are still four lines with a question on the second of them and no answer and no place for an answer, and a question that has been on a page for four days in a room where nobody has asked him about it is doing something to the room that about four people in that room could feel and about four could not.

The reader of this body said one more thing before the room emptied, and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-seven and did not move again this afternoon.

**"There is a man half a mile from here who said no on Tuesday with a reason about his own arm, and there is a man at the end of this table with four lines in a book and no answer on them, and I am not going to put those two in one sentence tonight, and I am not going to ask either of them anything this month.**"

**"The first of the eighth is four mornings off. In those four mornings a column on a door nine hundred yards off gets read twice and a man of about seventy gets fetched for the twenty-second time and has never once asked which number, and a line of compost falls, and a window of six days opens on the sixth, and the next two mornings after that are the first and the second of a window that shuts on the eleventh whether anybody has used it or not.**"

**"And none of that is this holding's to decide today. Today is a piece of paper on a table that has been read out twice.**"

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a table with a piece of paper on it that had been read out twice and filled never, and about four of them had worked out over ten days that a piece of paper on a table is not an offer and is not a refusal and is not a decision, and about four had worked out something else.

Nobody thanked anybody. A piece of paper that arrived on the sixteenth of this month is still on the middle table on the twenty-sixth and has been read out whole twice and is not filled and is not refused and has not been improved, and the morning ends on it and not on a figure, and the count of figures in this holding's book is a different thing from the count of documents on a table and the two have never been added.

The first of the eighth month is four mornings off this one. In those four mornings the seventh column of that door is read twice, a man of about seventy is fetched for the twenty-second time, a compost line falls, and a window of six days opens on the sixth of the eighth. None of those four things is in the ten days that this book has just finished, and every one of them is in the days after them, and nobody in this holding has been told which of the four matters.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-sixth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of a form, and not by the reader of this body, who read it out twice and did not fill it: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND THERE WAS NOBODY AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED. THE NUMBER IS THIRTY-NINE AND ALL THIRTY-NINE OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-EIGHT MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-NINE NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE AND THIRTY-NINE ARE SIXTY-EIGHT, AND TWENTY-NINE TAKEN DID NOT MOVE ON ANY MORNING IN THIS SEASON. NOBODY IS BLAMED FOR A FIELD THAT WAITED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE HUNDRED AND SIXTEENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN EIGHTY-THREE DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN FORTY-SEVEN TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTY-SIX TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE HUNDRED AND THIRTEENTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN, AND THAT SENTENCE IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SEVEN DAYS, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING TWENTY-SIX MORNINGS OUT OF THE THIRTY-NINE SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN ON THE SEVENTEENTH OF LAST MONTH. THE FORM FROM THE OFFICE THAT KEEPS A REGISTER ARRIVED ON THE SIXTEENTH OF THIS MONTH AND WAS READ OUT WHOLE ON THAT AFTERNOON AND WAS READ OUT WHOLE A SECOND TIME ON THE SEVENTEENTH IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE WITH THE DOOR SHUT, AND IT IS ON THE MIDDLE TABLE AND NOT IN THE DRAWER, AND IT IS NOT FILLED AND NOT REFUSED AND NOT IMPROVED, AND A DOCUMENT READ OUT TWICE IS NOT A DOCUMENT THAT HAS BEEN ANSWERED, AND IT IS ALSO NOT A DOCUMENT THAT HAS BEEN REFUSED, AND NEITHER OF THOSE IS A THIRD THING. FIVE THINGS ARE ON THE MIDDLE TABLE IN THE ORDER THEY ARRIVED IN AND NOBODY MOVED ONE. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY AND IS NOT READ AND IS READ NEXT ON THE THIRTIETH OF THIS MONTH AND AGAIN ON THE FIRST OF THE EIGHTH, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE NEXT FETCHING IS THE TWENTY-SECOND, ON THE FIRST OF THE EIGHTH. THE COMPOST LINE DOES NOT FALL AGAIN UNTIL THE FIRST. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS NIL. THE BRIDGE IS NOT ESTABLISHED AS ON AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS ASKED IN THAT DIRECTION IS NIL AND IT IS NIL. NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS BEEN MADE AN OFFER AND NOBODY WAS OFFERED ANYTHING IN THESE TEN MORNINGS. THE COUNTS ARE ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SEVEN FOR THE READER OF THIS BODY AND NINETY-TWO FOR THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, AND EACH IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON WHO IS SPEAKING AND OF NOBODY ELSE, AND NEITHER WAS ADDED TO THE OTHER. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
