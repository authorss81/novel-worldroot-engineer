# Chapter 347

## The Word And The Definition Behind It

The twentieth of this month was a Saturday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the inside face of the west dyke from the sluice up as far as the black post took four people from the second hour until about the fifth and a half. The toe at the second of the three places is lower again and it was lower on the eighteenth and it was lower on the second of this month, and nobody put a rule on it, and the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and it did not become three in the rain.

The figure for the twentieth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and thirty-two hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and tenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In seventy-seven days the series has risen forty-four times and fallen thirty-three times, and those two add to seventy-seven. It came up off Friday by nine, and the four of them had been barrowing slumped stuff off the toe and putting it back on the shoulder since the second hour and the wheels had gone through the slop twice.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and eight days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the hundred and seventh morning out of one hundred and nine.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **four hundred and ninety-four days** and **five hundred and forty-one**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on twenty-one mornings out of the thirty-four since he started again on the seventeenth of last month.

At about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut. The light came in low through the two south panes and lay along the middle table and across four objects and not across anybody's hands, and the stove ticked, and one coat was still dark at the shoulder to the elbow from the wall side of the dyke.

The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody. The count of people who have looked at it is two and did not move.

The word had gone round the holding twice in ten weeks before the seventeenth of a month two months back, in two rooms, by two people who had never met, and on that afternoon a woman took it apart out loud in about eleven people with the door shut and gave the reason first, and said it does not mean what it is supposed to mean here, and left it there.

The woman who keeps the seed house had been in that room. She was not in this one and she was in that one, and the reason she was in that one is that she keeps the room those eleven people were standing in.

And then she said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-one, and it is her own, and nobody improved it.

**"I am going to say a word that was said once in this room in the middle of last month and I am going to say what it meant then, and I am going to say the reason I am saying it out loud on a Saturday afternoon in about nine people is that it was said at the back of this room on Tuesday by a man about who we were talking, and I would like it stopped before it is said in the yard this evening.**"

**"The word is anchor. And I am not defining it, I am repeating it, and the difference is that a definition is somebody deciding now what a word means and a repetition is somebody saying what it meant on the seventeenth of a month two months back to about nine people who were all there. She said it does not mean what it is supposed to mean here, and she said it is not the man who goes first, and she said it is not the strongest man in the room, and she said it is not the man people come to when they cannot hold a thing, and then she said the one it is, and about four of us in that room have been living on the last of those for a fortnight and have not been able to put it down.**"

Nobody said it a second time. The count of times the word has been used in this holding with no definition behind it is two and neither of the two was hers, and it stands at two, and it stands at two this afternoon because the only use since the seventeenth of a month two months back was made at the back of this room on Tuesday in front of a person who was in the room and corrected it inside the hour, and a word that is corrected in the room it was used in is not a word that has gone anywhere with nothing behind it.

**"And the man at the front of that dyke this morning is the strongest person in about four rooms in this holding and I have watched him carry a wall end all day and get up a set of steps with a barrow that two men could not have got up, and that is not what the word means, and I am not going to be the one who lets it mean that in this holding for the next four years.**"

**"And the other one is the one I want said out loud, because it is the one that is going to hurt somebody. It is not the man people come to when they cannot hold a thing. It is not him and it has never been him and he has told a room of nine that he can do the near end of a thing on his own and cannot do the far end, and the reason the word is not him is that the man people come to when they cannot hold a thing is a man who gets asked afterwards whether it was right, and he can say yes or no, and the other one cannot be asked at all.**"

Nobody in that room said she was wrong. The man of about thirty-one of Silling came in about a quarter of an hour after that with his sleeves still wet and stood at the end of the table and did not say anything, and about four people in that room looked at him and about four looked at the window.

**"I am not saying the word is not going to be used badly. I am saying that when it is, somebody in the room is going to know what it should have meant, and that is the only thing any of us can do about a word, and I have been the woman who keeps this room for eleven years and I have watched about nine people not know a thing and go on using it, and one of them was me.**"

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a woman who keeps a room standing at a table having said a thing she had heard said once, and about four of them had understood that a repetition is not a definition and about four had not.

Then the man of about thirty-one of Silling said one thing, and he did not give the reason first either, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-three and did not move this afternoon.

**"She is right and I said it wrong and I am not going to argue with a woman who has been in the room I was not in. I have been carrying a wall end since the second hour and I am the strongest man in about four rooms in this holding and neither of those is what a word means.**"

**"And I am going to say the other part, which is that I have been saying *come and hold this* at the second gate three mornings a week since the second of this month and I did not think I was saying a word. It was a direction and it went to a man who does not have to be the one that goes first and I would like somebody in this room to have heard me say it, because in about four years one of us is going to be asked whether I was told and I would like there to be about nine people in a room who heard it.**"

**"He is not going to do it because I asked him to. I want that in as well. I am going to keep saying come and hold this in a yard and I am going to be surprised every time somebody comes, and that is the arrangement and I have made my own arrangements for eleven years.**"

Nobody improved any of that, and the woman who keeps the seed house put the cloth back over the thing on the middle table at about the sixth hour and did it the way she does it, from the near corner, without looking at anything else.

The man of about fifty said one thing after that, and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety and did not move this afternoon and is ninety.

**"I am not going to be the man who says the word and I am not going to be the man who says what it means, and the reason I am saying that is that I was in that room on the seventeenth of a month two months back and there was a condition stated in front of me and I was told that a condition existed and not what it is, and I wrote the words and the hour and four ruled lines under them and nothing under those.**"

**"I have not asked. I am not asking this afternoon. I have had about nine weeks to ask and I have not asked once and there is no reason I am going to give for that in a room of nine, and I want it noticed that I have said the word *condition* and not the thing itself, and that there is a difference between the two and the difference is the entire content of my having been in that room.**"

Nobody improved that. The man of about fifty put the pen down face up beside the second book and did not write the word down, and the count of questions he has asked anybody about that is nil, and he did not ask this afternoon, and the page does not put the word anywhere near him and is not going to.

The reader of this body said nothing about the mark that afternoon. It is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and it branches twice and it has no second mark, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a mark that is warm is not a mark and a headache is not a mark and a bridge is not a mark, and there are four accounts of that arm in the books in this holding and they reconcile none of one another and nothing said in this room this afternoon is a fifth of them.

A room that has just watched a person decide that somebody else stops is the only place that word is used properly, and there is a thing that has been standing in that room since the twelfth of this month and about four people in that room know what it is and are not going to say so, and this page is not going to point at it either.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and is not read. The man of about seventy is not fetched. The compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is nil, and nothing has been done that needed one, and the first time anything is entered in that book it will be entered before the thing and not after it.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and nothing said in it was wrong, and a woman who keeps a room said a word out loud with about nine people standing in front of her and did not define it.

THE ROTA DOES NOT MOVE, THE FOURTH LINE HAVING COME ROUND YESTERDAY AND NOT COMING ROUND AGAIN FOR THREE MORE MORNINGS, AND IT STANDS AT SIXTY-SIX MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-SEVEN NOT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND THIRTY-TWO HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE HUNDRED AND TENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN SEVENTY-SEVEN DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN FORTY-FOUR TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTY-THREE TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHT DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE HUNDRED AND SEVENTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND NINE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-FOUR DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY-ONE DAYS, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING TWENTY-ONE MORNINGS OUT OF THE THIRTY-FOUR SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN. THE WORD ANCHOR WAS USED AT THE BACK OF THIS ROOM ON TUESDAY TO MEAN THE STRONGEST MAN IN THE ROOM AND IT WAS CORRECTED IN THE ROOM IT WAS USED IN BY A PERSON WHO WAS IN THE ROOM, AND IT WAS USED AGAIN THIS AFTERNOON BY THE SAME PERSON AND IT WAS NOT DEFINED A SECOND TIME AND WHAT IT MEANT WAS REPEATED AND NOT DECIDED. THE COUNT OF TIMES THE WORD HAS BEEN USED IN THIS HOLDING WITH NO DEFINITION BEHIND IT IS TWO AND NEITHER OF THEM WAS HERS, AND IT IS TWO THIS AFTERNOON, AND A WORD CORRECTED IN THE ROOM IT WAS USED IN IS NOT A WORD THAT HAS GONE ANYWHERE WITH NOTHING BEHIND IT. THE WORD IS NOT THE MAN WHO GOES FIRST AND IS NOT THE STRONGEST MAN IN THE ROOM AND IS NOT THE MAN PEOPLE COME TO WHEN THEY CANNOT HOLD A THING. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY WAS TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTED AND NOT WHAT IT IS AND HAS ASKED NIL QUESTIONS ABOUT IT AND DID NOT ASK THIS AFTERNOON, AND THE WORD IS NOT PUT NEAR HIM AND IS NOT GOING TO BE. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND BRANCHES TWICE AND IS THE SAME FOUR INCHES IT WAS THIS MORNING, AND A MARK THAT IS WARM IS NOT A MARK, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THAT ARM STAND AT FOUR. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY AND IS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS EIGHTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN EIGHTEEN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS NIL, AND NOTHING HAS BEEN DONE THAT NEEDED ONE, AND THE FIRST ENTRY WILL BE MADE BEFORE THE THING AND NOT AFTER IT. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, THIS HAVING BEEN A ROOM. THE COUNTS ARE THIRTY-ONE, THIRTY-THREE AND NINETY, AND THE SECOND AND THE THIRD DID NOT MOVE, AND EACH IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON WHO IS SPEAKING AND OF NOBODY ELSE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
