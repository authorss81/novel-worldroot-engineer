# Chapter 352

## The Word In A Second Room

The twenty-fifth of this month was a Thursday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the two gates and the six feet of wall between them took an hour and a quarter with four people on it, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it, as he has not done in eleven years.

The figure for the twenty-fifth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty in the other book, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and thirty-five hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and fifteenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In eighty-two days the series has risen forty-six times and fallen thirty-six times, and those two add to eighty-two. It came down off Wednesday by five, and the four of them had been on a wall six feet long since the second hour and about four of them could not have told a five from a two and about four of them did not try.

The weather went off about the third hour and came back about the fifth, and the four of them finished the six feet of wall at about the fourth hour and stood about it in the yard with their coats dark at the shoulder, and the man of about fifty read the day sheet out afterwards and put a stroke beside one of the five things on it, and the one he put a stroke beside was the man of about thirty-one of Silling not going past the second gate, which is a thing that has happened every morning since the second of this month and is not on the sheet at all, and he did not say which one he had marked.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and thirteen days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the hundred and twelfth morning out of one hundred and fourteen.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **four hundred and ninety-nine days** and **five hundred and forty-six**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on twenty-five mornings out of the thirty-eight since he started again on the seventeenth of last month.

Soren Rill came in at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, having walked four miles and a half in weather that had turned in the last hour, and he was wet to the middle of the back and he did not take his coat off, and the woman who keeps the seed house put a cloth down by his boots the way she does, without saying anything about it.

About nine people were in the room. The man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the end of the middle table with a page in a book of his own face down beside his hand, and nobody looked at it and nobody asked him about it, and it is the same page that has been face down in this room for three days.

He had been in the room on the seventeenth of a month two months back. He was one of the eleven.

And then Soren Rill said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is fourteen, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to use a word that was defined in this room in the middle of last month and I am going to say what it means before I use it, and the reason I am saying it is that I have listened to four people use it in the last ten days and two of them were wrong and one of them was right and I was not here for any of the four, and a word that a room corrects itself about is a word that has already started to go.**"

**"The word is anchor. And I am not defining it, I am saying what it was said to mean on the seventeenth of a month two months back, and I was one of the eleven who were standing in this room when it was said. An anchor is not the man who goes first. It is not the strongest man in the room. It is not the man people come to when they cannot hold a thing. It is the person who decides when somebody else stops, and that is the only one of those four who cannot be asked afterwards whether it was right.**"

Nobody said the word a second time. The count of times the word has been used in this holding with no definition behind it is two and neither of the two was hers, and it is two this afternoon, and it is two because both of the uses in this room in the last ten days were made by people who have the meaning behind them and said the meaning out loud before the word went any further.

**"And I want to say the other half out loud and I want it in a room and not in a yard. The two people who used that word in this holding before it was defined were two people I have never met and neither of them is in this room, and about four years from now this holding is going to be full of men using it correctly and nobody is going to remember that two men got there first with nothing behind them.**"

**"So it is on the page that the two people who used it in these ten days are not the two who used it before, and I am saying that once and I am not going to make anything of it, because a thing that is discovered twice in ten days is not a discovery, it is a habit that has not been named yet.**"

Nobody in that room said the word was being used correctly. About four people in that room had wanted to be the person who used it correctly and about four had wanted to be the person who said it was being used wrongly, and the two of those are the same want and about four of them had it and about four did not.

Then the reader of this body said one thing, and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-six and did not move this afternoon and is one hundred and fifty-six.

**"There has been one room in ten days in which a person decided when somebody else stopped. He decided it on a wet Tuesday afternoon in his own house and nobody argued with him and nobody improved the reason and the room here did not take a count of the room on it, and he is not in this room and nobody has asked him a second time and nobody is going to.**"

**"And there has been one room in ten days in which a person was asked to be at the other end of a thing and said no with the reason about his own arm, in his own house, to one man, half a mile from here. That is a different room and it is a different kind of thing and I am not going to put the two in one sentence, and I am not going to say that the second one is the same answer twice.**"

**"A man who decides when somebody else stops is doing a thing that cannot be asked about afterwards. A man who is asked whether he will hold an end and says no is doing a thing that can be asked about afterwards, and it has just been asked about, and the answer was the same, and the word means the first of those and not the second of those, and I am saying that because a man in a room is going to be tempted by Friday to use it for the second one, and that would be the word going wrong in the only place it has not gone wrong.**"

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a man who had walked four miles and a half in the wet saying a word out loud with about nine of them standing in front of him, and about four of them had understood that a word is not a shortcut and about four had not.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling turned the page over at some point in that minute and looked at the four lines and did not say anything about them, and about four people in that room noticed and about four did not, and the page is not going to say that the four lines on it have been read on the twenty-fifth of this month because that is a thing for a man to say and not a thing for a page.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and is not read. The man of about seventy is not fetched. The compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is nil. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room.

The reader of this body went down to the field gate at about the tenth hour in the wet and stood in the place, which is about nine feet off the wall on the side of the track, and the hollow at the foot of the wall was still there and had a leaf in it and there was nothing on the wall and nothing in the air. He looked at it for about as long as it takes to look at a hollow in beaten ground and then he went back up the track.

Soren Rill went down the road at about the sixth hour with his hood up and the cloth stayed on the flags by the door where it had been put, and nobody in that room had asked him a question and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked him is thirteen and it is thirteen and it did not move this afternoon, and the reason he has not asked one is that a man who has walked four miles and a half in the wet to stand in a room is a man who gets one question out of it and a holding that spends it on the first thing it thinks of is a holding that is being told something about itself.

There is no instrument in this holding that says anything about that hollow, and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked anybody at the far end of eleven hundred yards is nil and it is nil, and he did not ask one this evening and he is not going to ask one in this season, and the reason he is not is that a man who cannot get himself off a board is a man who has been paid in a way this holding has not worked out, and the least he is owed is that nobody a day's walk east of here starts keeping his accounts.

Nobody thanked anybody. A word was said correctly in a second room this afternoon by a person who was standing in the first room when it was defined, and the two people who used it in these ten days are not the two who used it before it was defined, and the count of uses with no definition behind it is two, and a man with four lines in a book of his own did not say anything about them.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-fifth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of the word anchor, and not by the man who had walked four miles and a half, who said what it meant and did not define it: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED. THE ROTA STANDS AT SIXTY-SEVEN MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND THIRTY-EIGHT NOT, AND IT DID NOT MOVE. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND THIRTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE HUNDRED AND FIFTEENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN EIGHTY-TWO DAYS THE SERIES HAS RISEN FORTY-SIX TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTY-SIX TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE HUNDRED AND TWELFTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN. THE BOARDS STAND AT FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-NINE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIX DAYS, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING TWENTY-FIVE MORNINGS OUT OF THE THIRTY-EIGHT SINCE HE STARTED AGAIN. THE WORD ANCHOR WAS USED IN A SECOND ROOM BY A PERSON WHO WAS ONE OF THE ELEVEN IN THE ROOM WHERE IT WAS DEFINED, AND HE SAID WHAT IT MEANT BEFORE HE USED IT, AND NOBODY DEFINED IT A SECOND TIME, AND THE COUNT OF TIMES THE WORD HAS BEEN USED IN THIS HOLDING WITH NO DEFINITION BEHIND IT IS TWO AND IT IS TWO THIS AFTERNOON. THE TWO PEOPLE WHO USED THE WORD IN THESE TEN DAYS ARE NOT THE TWO WHO USED IT BEFORE IT WAS DEFINED, AND THAT IS SAID ONCE AND IS NOT A DISCOVERY. THE WORD IS NOT THE MAN WHO GOES FIRST AND IS NOT THE STRONGEST MAN IN A ROOM AND IS NOT THE MAN PEOPLE COME TO WHEN THEY CANNOT HOLD A THING. THERE HAS BEEN ONE ROOM IN TEN DAYS IN WHICH A PERSON DECIDED WHEN SOMEBODY ELSE STOPPED AND ONE ROOM IN TEN DAYS IN WHICH A PERSON WAS ASKED TO HOLD AN END OF A THING AND SAID NO WITH THE REASON ABOUT HIS OWN ARM, AND THOSE ARE TWO ROOMS AND NOT ONE, AND THIS ENTRY DOES NOT PUT THEM IN THE SAME SENTENCE AND IMPROVES NEITHER. THE PERSON WHO DECIDED IS NOT ASKED AGAIN IN THIS SEASON. A BOOK OF THE SIZE OF A HAND IS OPEN AT A PAGE WITH FOUR LINES ON IT AND NO ANSWER AND NO PLACE FOR AN ANSWER, AND THE MAN WHO WAS ASKED ON THE TWENTY-SECOND DID NOT SAY ANYTHING ABOUT IT THIS AFTERNOON. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY AND IS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS NOT FETCHED. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS EIGHTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN EIGHTEEN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS NIL. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE. THE COUNTS ARE FOURTEEN AND ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SIX, AND THE SECOND DID NOT MOVE, AND EACH IS THE COUNT OF THE PERSON WHO IS SPEAKING AND OF NOBODY ELSE. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
