# Chapter 363

## The Window Opens

The sixth of this month was a Monday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the inside face of the west dyke from the sluice up as far as the black post took four people from the second hour until about the fifth and a half. The slop at about the first hundred yards had gone hard enough in the frost to take a barrow wheel without it going through, and the four of them got the line done in about half an hour less than the month has been taking and nobody wrote down that either.

The frost had been gone off the ground by about the fifth hour and the shoulder of the dyke had gone soft in about a quarter of an hour, which is the whole of the trouble with a frost and a shoulder and a barrow and has been the whole of the trouble for four years, and the man at the front of it said so out loud to about four people and one of them wrote it down and the other three did not, and the count of times a person has said a thing out loud in a yard that the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move this morning and is not going to move because a man knows what wet ground does.

The figure for the sixth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and sixty-four hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and twenty-sixth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In ninety-three days the series has risen fifty-two times and fallen forty-one times, and those two add to ninety-three. It came up off Sunday by nine, and this page does not say anything else about that figure and does not say where it sits against anything, because a number that gets a sentence put round it in a book gets a sentence put round it in a head.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and twenty-four days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and twenty-third morning out of one hundred and twenty-five.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and ten days** and **five hundred and fifty-seven**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on thirty-three mornings out of the forty-nine since he started again on the seventeenth of a month two months back.

The window of the office's work opened this morning on the sixth, as it opens on the sixth of every month, and it is six days, and it shuts on the eleventh, and it is not extended in a season and no window has ever been extended in a season in the four years this holding has kept a book, and no work is ever carried out of it.

That is the whole of what a window is in this holding, and it is the same in every month of the four years, and the sheet on district paper that came out of the last one is in a drawer face up and correct in every particular and is not the sheet in this one and is not connected to the form on the middle table and the two have never been put side by side by anybody.

The last one was six days in the second week of last month and it shut on a Monday, and the only thing that came out of it was a sheet on district paper with four columns on it and a heading at the top, and that sheet is in a drawer face up and is correct in every particular of it, and the sheet in the same drawer that claims a corridor is a different document with four columns on it and a heading at the top, and either of them set beside the other is a different document from the one it is set beside, and nobody in this holding has ever put the two together and this page is not going to.

The form from the office that keeps a register of persons who may set a sequence against a valve came up the fen road on the sixteenth of last month, which was after that window had shut, and it did not come out of a window, and nobody in this holding has ever said where it did come from, and the count of things that arrived in a leather envelope in four years is two.

Whether any window covers the line in question is one of the six not knowns, and the count of those is six, and nobody has supplied a unit for one of the six in four years, and this morning did not change the count of the six and did not add a seventh, and the sixth is a not known and not a seventh.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon. The register form was on the middle table where it has been for a fortnight and the drawer was shut, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the end of the table with his hat in his hand and had been there since the fourth hour, and the four lines in his own book have a question on the second of them and no answer and no place for an answer, and the page on it is fourteen days old, and nobody in that room has asked him about it in fourteen days and nobody is going to today.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and sixty-two, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is a thing in this holding that has opened this morning on a day it has opened on every month for four years, and it is six days, and nothing has ever come out of it, and I am giving the reason I am standing up about a thing that nothing has ever come out of, and the reason is that about four people in this room have begun treating the six days as a deadline and about four have not, and both of those are wrong and I want it said in this room while the door is shut.**"

**"It is not a deadline. A deadline is a thing somebody has to do inside. There is nobody in this holding who has to do anything inside those six days and there has not been anybody in four years, and a man who puts six days of somebody else's office on a table in a room with nine people in it has started a clock that has never been started, and when it shuts on the eleventh there will be nothing on this table that is not on it now.**"

**"And I am not going to say that anything should be put in it. I have got four books and there is a column in this one for a thing that goes to an office, and I have not written in that column this month, and I am not going to write in it on the strength of a window opening, and a man who fills a column because a window opened has filled a column and not done a piece of work.**"

Nobody said that was wrong. About four people in that room had wanted the six days to be a deadline and about four had not, and about four of the nine had worked out in the last fortnight that the reason they wanted it to be a deadline is that a deadline is the only kind of thing this holding has ever been able to say no to, and about four had not worked that out and were still thinking about the sixth column of the office's ledger.

The woman who keeps the seed house came in from the yard at about the sixth hour, looked at the four cloths on the table, put her hand flat on the near corner of the one over the seed, said nothing at all, and went out again, and about four people in that room had been waiting an hour to see whether she would lift any of them and about four had not known that there was anything to wait about.

The man of about fifty put the two books down at about the seventh hour and squared them against the edge of the middle table and did not put the register form on them, and did not put the form in the drawer, and the drawer was shut before the window opened this morning and it is shut now, and about four people in that room had looked at the drawer twice this afternoon and about four had looked at the table twice and neither of those is a decision and neither of them is going to become one this evening.

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a window that had opened on the sixth of a month and a table with five things on it that had not moved, and about four of them had understood that a thing which can be carried out of a window is a thing that has a use and about four had not.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is one and the second one is not entered, and the second one is not going to be entered in a hurry, and a session that is not entered did not happen. The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a window that opens is not a mark and the four accounts of the arm stand at four.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room. The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two. The copy of the ledger was not on the table this afternoon and was not turned round. The corridor in the pan is named and open and is not a road and is not a claim, and nobody in this holding has an instrument that says whether a hollow in beaten ground at the foot of a wall at the field gate is connected this morning to a board against a wall at a stone head, and the count of questions asked in that direction is nil and it is nil, and this page is not going to put a yes or a no in it.

The window opened this morning and it is six days and it shuts on the eleventh and it is not extended and no work is ever carried out of it. That is the whole of the sixth and there is nothing else due on it, and about four people in this holding looked at the calendar this morning the way they look at a calendar, and the calendar is the one thing in this room that works whether anybody is caring about it or not.

Nothing is going into the six days. Nothing is going to be taken out of them either, and a thing that is neither put in nor taken out of a window is the only kind of business this holding has with a window, and that is not a complaint and it is not a figure, and it is the sixth of a month in a season in which a column on a door went to fifty-one and then to fifty-two and a man of about seventy came up a path and a line of compost fell and a first session was entered in a book in a room with the door shut, and a second one is not entered, and about four people in this holding would rather there were more things on that table this evening than five, and about four would not, and neither of those two is going to be settled this week.

THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SIXTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN NINETY-THREE DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-TWO TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-ONE TIMES, AND THIS PAGE SAYS NOTHING ELSE ABOUT THAT FIGURE. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FOUR DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THIRD MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIVE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SEVEN, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING THIRTY-THREE MORNINGS OUT OF THE FORTY-NINE. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENED THIS MORNING ON THE SIXTH, AS IT OPENS ON THE SIXTH OF EVERY MONTH, AND IT IS SIX DAYS AND IT SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, AND IT IS NOT EXTENDED IN A SEASON AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON IN THE FOUR YEARS THIS HOLDING HAS KEPT A BOOK, AND NO WORK IS EVER CARRIED OUT OF IT. THE COLUMN FOR A THING THAT GOES TO AN OFFICE HAS NOT BEEN WRITTEN IN THIS MONTH AND IS NOT GOING TO BE WRITTEN ON THE STRENGTH OF A WINDOW OPENING. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS ONE AND THE SECOND ONE IS NOT ENTERED AND IS NOT GOING TO BE ENTERED IN A HURRY. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR AND A WINDOW THAT OPENS IS NOT A MARK. THE CORRIDOR IN THE PAN IS NAMED AND OPEN AND IS NOT A ROAD AND IS NOT A CLAIM, AND NOBODY IN THIS HOLDING HAS AN INSTRUMENT THAT SAYS, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS ASKED IN THAT DIRECTION IS NIL AND IT IS NIL. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
