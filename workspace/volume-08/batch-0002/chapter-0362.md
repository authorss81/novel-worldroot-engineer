# Chapter 362

## No Benefit Of The Doubt

The fifth of this month was a Sunday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the boundary ditch and the two culverts took about six people until about the fourth hour. The near culvert was clear for the fourth morning running and the far one was about half full, and the six of them put four barrow-loads out of the far one and nobody weighed them, and nobody in this holding has ever written down which of the two culverts fills faster.

The figure for the fifth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and fifty-five hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and twenty-fifth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In ninety-two days the series has risen fifty-one times and fallen forty-one times, and those two add to ninety-two. It came down off Saturday by five, and about four people in this holding spent Saturday being handed a nine and about four of them had a five this morning and about four of those did not care.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and twenty-three days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and twenty-second morning out of one hundred and twenty-four.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and nine days** and **five hundred and fifty-six**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on thirty-two mornings out of the forty-nine since he started again on the seventeenth of a month two months back.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon. The register form was on the middle table, the drawer was shut, and the light came in low off the south wall and lay along the table and across the five things on it.

The frost had got into the flagstones again in the night, and the woman who keeps the seed house had put a board down flat across the two stones inside the door on the third of this month and had not mentioned it, and about four people in this holding had walked over that board since and one of the other four had asked who had put it there.

The register form was where it has been for nineteen days and the drawer was where it has been for four years, and the two of them are about four feet apart on the same table, and about four people in that room had worked out that the distance between the two is the whole of a decision nobody in this holding has made, and the rest were looking at the frost.

Tova Reed had come up the road on the morning of the second of this month and had not been sent for and had not asked to be fetched, and she is in the room from the fourth hour, and she had not been in it on any of the mornings between, and about four people in that room had worked out why and about four had not.

She is a seedwright of a trust and she is up this road eleven days out of twenty in a season, and she came on the morning of the second and went down the road on the fourth and came back up it this morning on the cart from the crossing, and she brought about nine pounds of paper packets in a satchel and left them in the cart shed and did not open them in the room, and nobody asked her why.

She stood at the window end with her back to the light for about an hour and said perhaps six sentences, none of them about the seed, and about four of the nine in that room were watching the cloth on the middle table to see whether she looked at it and about four were not, and she did not look at it, and the count of people who have looked at what is under it is two and it is two and neither of the two was in that room this afternoon.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is twenty, and it is her own, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is a word going round this holding this week in front of a headache, and the word is about, and I am going to say the reason I am saying something about a word before I say anything about the headache. The reason is that I keep seed. A seed is either in the ground or it is not in the ground and I can put my hand in a bag and tell you which, and there is no third thing and there is no about a seed. And I have spent eleven years in a trade where the worst thing that can happen to a person is a figure that has been rounded, because a rounded seed is a seed that will be counted in April by somebody who was not there in January.**"

**"And I have listened to about four people in this room and about four in that yard this week say about four in front of a thing that happened to a man in a wood, and about four of those were talking about bodies and rooms and how long a thing took, and about four were talking about a figure, and both of those sound exactly the same and one is how we speak and the other is how we stop being able to check each other.**"

**"A headache is not a figure and it is not going to get one. I am not asking anybody to put a number on it and I would be ashamed of the room if somebody did. What I am asking is that a man who has been in the ground once and come out with something he cannot put in a book does not get about four, because the about four is for the number of people in a room, and if it is put on him it will be in the book in four years in a column of about fours and a person will be able to enter it twice and it will not be a figure at all and it will look like one.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had said the word in the last seven days and the other half had not, and nobody who had said it had counted themselves, and the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and some of the ones who had said it had said it in a yard and some of those had said it about a room.

Then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he did not give the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-eight and did not move this afternoon and is ninety-eight.

**"She is right and I have got four books and every one of them has got a column with the word about at the top of it and the word about is on no figure of mine that anybody can enter twice. I have been putting it there for four years. The count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two, and one of the two has got a unit and one of the two has not, and neither of the two has got about at the head of its column, and I am not going to put one there this week because a woman from a seed bank has told me what happens in April.**"

Nobody said that was a thing anybody else could check either.

Then the reader of this body said one thing, and he did not give the reason first either, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and sixty-one and did not move this afternoon and is one hundred and sixty-one.

**"I have had the headache once now, and it was small, and it was gone inside two hours, and I am not going to describe it again in this room because she has just said the word that would be used on it and I am not going to hand it to anybody. It is a headache. It is on a line in a book with an hour against it and there is no figure on the line and there is not going to be one.**"

**"And I am not going to argue with her about the seed. A man who reads a wood for a season and reads a wood for an hour and got a headache about a quarter of an hour's walk from a cart road is not in a position to tell a seedwright what may be rounded. She is in a position and I am not, and about four people in this room have worked out which of us is which in about four seconds and about four of them are still working on it.**"

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a seedwright who keeps a trade in which a rounded thing is a lost thing, and a man with a headache and a bookkeeper with four columns with the word about at the head of them, and half of them had understood that the three of them were arguing about the same word and half had not.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was at the end of the middle table for the whole of it and did not open the book of his own, and the four lines in it are still four lines with a question on the second of them and no answer and no place for an answer, and the page on it is thirteen days old this afternoon, and nobody in that room has asked him about it in thirteen days.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is one and the second one is not entered. The stop word was not said in this room and is not in that book and the man of about fifty did not ask about it and the count of his questions about the condition is nil and it is nil. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two. The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a headache is not a mark and a word is not a mark and the four accounts of the arm stand at four.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and about four people in that room had spent seven days saying a word and some of them had stopped since about the fifth hour and some had not and the rest of the nine had not heard either of them say it at all.

The window of the office's work opens in the morning. It is six days and it shuts on the eleventh whether anybody has used it or not, and it is not extended in a season and no window has ever been extended in a season, and nothing that was said in this room this afternoon is going into it, and nothing that has been in this holding's book for a fortnight is going into it either, and about four people in that room have already decided that they will not put anything in it and about four have not decided anything and will be told what the six days are when the six days start.

The frost is still on the boards inside the door and it will be gone by the ninth hour tomorrow. A board on flagstones is not a column and it is not a holding and it is not a figure, and the woman who keeps the seed house put it down because the stones were cold, and nobody in that room wrote it down and nobody is going to.

THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FIVE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIFTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN NINETY-TWO DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-ONE TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY-ONE TIMES. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS ONE AND THE SECOND ONE IS NOT ENTERED, AND THE STOP SIGNAL WAS NOT SAID IN THAT ROOM AND IS NOT IN THAT BOOK, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAS ASKED ABOUT THE CONDITION IS NIL. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR AND A HEADACHE IS NOT A MARK AND A WORD IS NOT A MARK.
