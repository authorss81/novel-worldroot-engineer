# Chapter 361

## Forty-One Fields

The fourth of this month was a Saturday, and the fourth line of the rota came round at the second hour, and there was nobody at the headland above Marden, and the fields above Marden waited. The number is forty-one, and all forty-one of them are the fourth line, and the rota stands at seventy mornings in force, twenty-nine taken and forty-one not, and twenty-nine and forty-one are seventy. Twenty-nine taken did not move, and nobody is blamed for a field that waited.

A field that waited is a fact about a rota and not a fact about a man. That sentence is on the page of this holding's book for the fifth time in a fortnight and it is not going to be improved, and the reason it is said every time the fourth line comes round is not that anybody has begun to doubt it. It is said because a man who keeps a number going up by one on a morning when nobody has done anything is a man who will be asked about it in about four years, and the answer to that question has to be somewhere in the book and not in his mouth.

That is the eighteenth morning the fourth line has come round since it started coming round on the twenty-sixth of a month three months back, and it is the fifth of them since the eighteenth of last month, and both of those are true and neither of them is the other one, and the man of about fifty enters both of them for the reason he gave in this room on the thirtieth of last month and does not give it a second time.

The figure for the fourth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and sixty hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and twenty-fourth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In ninety-one days the series has risen fifty-one times and fallen forty times, and those two add to ninety-one. It came up off Friday by nine, and the man of about fifty wrote the nine in the margin in a hand that went uphill to the right and did not put a row against it, and there are five of those nines in a book of his own with no note against any of the five and he has never been asked about them and he has not offered.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and twenty-two days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and twenty-first morning out of one hundred and twenty-three.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud. The pair stands at **five hundred and eight days** and **five hundred and fifty-five**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. The run does not move and this page carries no figure for it, and it has been done on two mornings now out of the last four, and the man of about fifty has said that a man who reads a pair out loud on most of the mornings has made it a habit and that a habit is not a measurement and is not going to become one.

The morning was hard blue and cold after the rain and the ground had gone hard enough in the yard that the barrow wheels left a mark on it that was still there at the ninth hour, and about four people in this holding stood in that yard at about the ninth hour on a Saturday and looked at the mark.

The frost is the fourth this month and it is the second that has got into the seed house, and there was about an inch of it on the inside of the north pane at about the seventh hour and the woman who keeps the seed house broke it at the corner with the back of a spoon and did not say anything about having done it. The man of about thirty-one of Silling said that a spoon against a pane in a holding is a sign of a good winter, and about four people in that yard had agreed with him and about four had not, and this page is not going to find a way of counting a man wrong about a winter on the strength of a rule about a yard.

At about the ninth hour the man of about fifty read the day sheet out in the yard, and he did it from the step rather than from the middle of the yard, and he did not raise his voice to do it, and about four people came in closer and about four did not. Five things, in five places. The line that came round and had nobody on it. The figure for the day, which two people say is the same number and it is. The form on the table and the drawer shut. The man of about seventy not fetched, and how many days it is to the first. And the window, and how many days it is to the sixth. He put a stroke beside two of the five and left the other three alone and did not say which two, and about four people in that yard could work it out and about four could not.

The window opens on the sixth of this month and shuts on the eleventh, and it is two days off this morning and not tomorrow, and it is six days and not five and not seven, and no work is ever carried out of it, and it is not extended in a season, and no window has ever been extended in a season in the four years this holding has kept a book, and whether any window covers the line in question is one of the six not knowns and the count of those is six and it did not move on a Saturday in the yard.

About nine people were in the seed house from about the fourth hour of the afternoon. The register form was on the middle table where it has been for eighteen days, the drawer was shut, the five things on the table and the four in the drawer were in the order they arrived in, and the copy of the ledger came down off the shelf about the fifth hour and went back on it about half an hour later, and in between it was on the end of the table and nobody turned it round. It is ninety-one rows long with five columns with a head on them and a sixth with nothing at the head of it, and the five rows on line forty-one were corrected once in the second month of this season by a man with a knife and not by a clerk, and nobody corrected them a second time this afternoon.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-eight, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I am going to say the reason I have got a fifth share in my mouth this afternoon when I have not put a figure on it, and the reason is that this holding has one count in it that a month has never touched and one word that a month renames every thirty days, and about four people in this room have worked out in the last ten days that those are not the same kind of thing and about four have not.**"

**"The fifth share is a count of days. It is not a count of months and it has never been re-based by one and it is not going to be, and a man who takes a day count and divides it by thirty to make it fit a calendar has thrown away the only figure in this holding that a turn of a month cannot touch. I am not going to divide it and I am not going to print it this afternoon because printing it would be entering a figure to make a point, and I do not do that.**"

**"And the other one is a word. A thing of last month is of last month from the first and it stopped being of this month in a night, and nobody decided that and nobody can undecide it, and about four years from now somebody is going to find a piece of paper on a table with a date at the top of it and a heading that does not match and will assume one of the two is a mistake. Neither of them is a mistake. One of them is arithmetic and the other one is a calendar and this holding has both.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people in that room had wanted a figure on the fifth share and about four had not, and the two of those had been in the room for the whole of it and neither of them said which side they were on.

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a table with a copy of a ledger on the end of it that nobody had turned round, and about four of them had understood that a man who keeps four books has just told them which of his own numbers he trusts and about four had not.

About four people in that room had noticed that the copy of the ledger had been on the table for half an hour and about four had not, and the woman who keeps the seed house had not turned it round either, and the count of times a person who keeps a book in this holding has said a thing in a yard the engineer of record had not thought of is thirteen and did not move this afternoon and is not going to move because a book came off a shelf and went back on it.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was in the room from the fourth hour until the sixth and did not open the book of his own, and the four lines in it are still four lines with a question on the second of them and no answer and no place for an answer, and the page on it is twelve days old this afternoon, and nobody in that room has asked him about it in twelve days and nobody is going to.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is one and the second one is not entered and a second one that is not entered has not happened. The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and the four accounts of the arm stand at four and a count of fields that waited is not a fifth of them.

The man of about fifty squared the two books against the edge of the table when he had finished and did not put the form on them, and the form from the office that keeps a register was still on the middle table where it has been for eighteen days, not filled, not refused, read out twice, and being read out twice is not being answered and is not being refused either.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and there was a yard at about the ninth hour with five things read out of it and a stroke beside two of them, and nothing wrong was said in that yard, and a man who has said the same sentence out loud in a yard six days running in a fortnight has not said anything wrong in it and this page is not going to find a way of counting one.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the fourth of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of the fourth line, and not by the man of about fifty, who said that a day count and a calendar are not the same thing: **THE FOURTH LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND THERE WAS NOBODY AT THE HEADLAND ABOVE MARDEN AND THE FIELDS WAITED. THE NUMBER IS FORTY-ONE AND ALL FORTY-ONE OF THEM ARE THE FOURTH LINE. THE ROTA STANDS AT SEVENTY MORNINGS IN FORCE, TWENTY-NINE TAKEN AND FORTY-ONE NOT, AND TWENTY-NINE AND FORTY-ONE ARE SEVENTY, AND TWENTY-NINE TAKEN DID NOT MOVE. NOBODY IS BLAMED FOR A FIELD THAT WAITED, AND A FIELD THAT WAITED IS A FACT ABOUT A ROTA AND NOT A FACT ABOUT A MAN. THAT IS THE EIGHTEENTH MORNING OF THE FOURTH LINE SINCE THE TWENTY-SIXTH OF A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK AND THE FIFTH SINCE THE EIGHTEENTH OF LAST MONTH, AND BOTH ARE ENTERED. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND SIXTY HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FOURTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN NINETY-ONE DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY-ONE TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIRST MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND EIGHT DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FIVE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING, AND THE RUN DID NOT MOVE AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR IT. A DAY SHEET WAS READ OUT IN A YARD, FIVE THINGS IN FIVE PLACES, AND A STROKE WAS PUT BESIDE TWO OF THE FIVE AND NOBODY WAS TOLD WHICH TWO. THE COPY OF THE LEDGER CAME DOWN OFF THE SHELF AND WENT BACK ON IT UNTURNED. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENS IN TWO DAYS ON THE SIXTH AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, SIX DAYS, AND NO WORK IS EVER CARRIED OUT OF IT AND NO WINDOW HAS EVER BEEN EXTENDED IN A SEASON. THE FIFTH SHARE IS A COUNT OF DAYS AND WAS NOT RE-BASED BY A MONTH, AND NO FIGURE FOR IT WAS ENTERED THIS AFTERNOON. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING DID NOT OPEN HIS BOOK, AND NOBODY HAS ASKED HIM IN TWELVE DAYS AND NO FIGURE WAS ENTERED AGAINST HIM. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS ONE AND A SECOND ONE THAT IS NOT ENTERED HAS NOT HAPPENED. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR AND A COUNT OF FIELDS THAT WAITED IS NOT A FIFTH OF THEM. NOTHING WRONG WAS SAID IN A YARD THIS MORNING. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
