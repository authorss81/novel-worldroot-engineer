# Chapter 354

## Ordinary Work, In A Wood

The twenty-seventh of this month was a Saturday, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the boundary ditch and the two culverts took about six people until about the fourth hour, four miles and a bit west of this account, and the near culvert was clear and the far one was about half full of the same leaf it has been full of since the second of this month, and nobody in this holding has ever written down whether one of them fills faster than the other, and the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and it did not become three on a Saturday.

The figure for the twenty-seventh went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour on a board in the open air at the node, because that is the only place the two books have been read out loud at that is not the seed house, and it was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other one with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and thirty-nine hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and seventeenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In eighty-four days the series has risen forty-seven times and fallen thirty-seven times, and those two add to eighty-four. It came down off Friday by five, and the six people who spent the morning in a ditch have no way of telling a five from a two, and neither of them means anything to anybody standing in water.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and fifteen days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and fourteenth morning out of one hundred and sixteen. That sentence is not the aggregate of the four launders. The two are different objects, they have never been added together, and a reader who finds them in the same sentence is reading a page and not a sum.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and one days** and **five hundred and forty-eight**, forty-seven days apart, and the forty-seven is a consequence of the two and not a third rule. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on twenty-seven mornings out of the forty-one since he started again on the seventeenth of last month.

About nine people came to the node along five miles of cart road at about the second hour of the afternoon and stood about in the light doing what they had come to do, which was the channel along the inside of the line of the stems. The water had cut it and nobody had cut it, and it had about a foot in it at the near end and was full of leaf at the far end, and leaf out of a channel that water cut is ordinary work that takes about nine people two days, and this was the first of the two.

The rakes were made that morning out of lengths of ash and an old hoe frame, and two of the nine spent about four minutes of the first hour arguing about a wheel, and a woman put her coat down on the low rail at the top of the bank where the light was not, and the light came in low off the west and lay along the bed of the channel and stopped at the line of the stems instead of going into them.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year. It is not walked, it is not measured, it is not crossed, it is not priced by walking it, it is not explained, and it has no dimension on any page of this holding's book, and that is the whole of the position on it and is not going to be improved on. About nine people worked on the outside of it this afternoon and not one of them put a foot inside it and not one of them went round it to get to anywhere.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling came with the others at the second hour and worked the near side of the channel for about an hour, and then he stopped where he stopped, which was at the third stem he had come to and a little short of the dry part, and he did not go past it, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is thirty-five, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I said in this room on the twenty-second of this month that a thing anybody wants done in that wood goes on the middle table in front of nine people and sits there a fortnight with nobody touching it, and if it is still on the table in a fortnight then it is a thing and not a favor. It is on the table. It has been on the table since. It has not sat a fortnight and I am not going to be the one who decides that a fortnight is long enough.**"

**"And I am not going to be asked. I have not asked anybody for anything in eleven years and the reason I have not is that the day a man in this holding starts asking is the day about four people begin working out what he is going to want next. So I am going to work the near side of this channel until about the fourth hour and then I am going to go home, and nobody in this holding has to decide that was a favor to anybody, and nobody is going to be thanked for it either, because I have not done it for anybody.**"

Nobody said that was wrong. About four people in that nine had wanted him to go in and about four had not, and the two of those had been in the channel all afternoon and had not said so.

And then the reader of this body put both hands on the outside of a stem at about the fourth hour, the one nearest the channel, the bark on the south side of it cold and grey and coming away in plates about the size of a hand, and he was still for about as long as it takes to boil a kettle, and the six people clearing leaf went on clearing leaf and about four of them looked over and about four did not.

What came back was a summer. Not a morning and not a place: a length of heat with the water out of that channel and the bed of it dry and grey to the bottom, and a season standing in a stem that is a foot through at the bottom and not a season anybody in this holding can put on a page. It took a length of time to come and it did not come in sentences, and what came with it was not a day.

The cost of it was that he stood where he was for about a minute afterwards and could not have said in what order the six of them had filled the barrow at the near end of the channel, and the order came back, and it was the order he had watched it happen in, and nobody in that nine saw anything at all happen except a man who was not moving.

The reader of this body said one thing, and he did not give the reason first, and nobody asked him for one, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-seven and did not move this afternoon and is one hundred and fifty-seven.

**"A dry summer in that channel, about four years before I was in the ground the first time, and it has been wet in there every year since, and that is the whole of what came back this afternoon. I am saying it out loud now because a thing that about four people are going to be told in a fortnight in this holding had better come off my mouth this afternoon with nothing written on it.**"

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-three, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I have got a book out and I am going to say why before I say the thing. A figure is a thing a person can enter twice. That is the whole of it, and everything I have kept for four years comes out of that, and this holding's book is a book of things a person can enter twice.**"

**"What came back this afternoon is a season. There is no column in any of my four books with a year in it and I am not going to rule one, and the column I have kept with nothing at the head of it for four years is going to stay with nothing at the head of it, and I am not going to write a heading on it this afternoon and nobody else is going to either.**"

**"And I want it said out loud in front of about nine people while they can hear it and not in four of them next week: what came back this afternoon is not going in a column and it is not going in a book and it has not got a unit on it that anybody in this holding agrees on, and the count of numbers this holding cannot check is two and that is not what this is and it is not going to become three.**"

Nobody improved it. The light went off the bed of the channel about the fifth hour and the six of them had the near end finished and a third of the far one done, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling had gone down the cart road at about the hour he had said he would go and had not said goodbye to anybody.

The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two and did not move. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the twenty-seventh, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one this afternoon and nobody is going to. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-one, and the next fetching is the first of the eighth, and that is four mornings off this one. The compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen and it does not fall again until the first.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is nil, and it is nil because nothing has been done that needed one. What was done this afternoon is not a session. A session carries a purpose and a duration and a stop signal and a witness, and this holding has entered nothing of that kind in four years, and a reading off the outside of a stem in the last of the light on a Saturday afternoon is not one of those four things, and it is entered on the line with the rest of the day and it is not a column and it does not start a clock.

Nobody went into the ground. Nobody was in the ground twice. The four accounts of the arm stand at four and the mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a mark that is warm is not a mark and a reading is not a mark and a season is not a mark and nothing anybody put in a book this week is a fifth of them.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a cart road and a channel and not a yard. About nine people cleared leaf out of a cut that water had made, and one man stood at the outside of a stem for a length of time and came away with a season in him that he put on no page, and the count of figures in this holding's book is a different thing from the count of mornings a person can be paid for, and the two have never been added.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the twenty-seventh of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of the standing wood, and not by the reader of this body, who put both hands on the outside of a stem and did not give the reason first: **THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE BOUNDARY DITCH AND THE TWO CULVERTS, ABOUT SIX PEOPLE TO THE FOURTH HOUR. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE HUNDREDWEIGHT, WHICH IS THE HUNDRED AND SEVENTEENTH FIGURE IN A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN EIGHTY-FOUR DAYS IT HAS RISEN FORTY-SEVEN TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTY-SEVEN TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND IT DOES NOT RESET AT A TURN OF A MONTH. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN DAYS, THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND FOURTEENTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTEEN, AND THAT SENTENCE IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND ONE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY-EIGHT, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING TWENTY-SEVEN MORNINGS OUT OF THE FORTY-ONE. A CHANNEL ALONG THE INSIDE OF THE LINE OF THE STEMS, CUT BY THE WATER AND BY NOBODY, WAS CLEARED OF LEAF BY ABOUT NINE PEOPLE ON THE PATTERN OF AN ORDINARY WORKING AFTERNOON. THE RING OF BARE GROUND INSIDE THAT ELEVEN ACRES IS BARE IN ITS ELEVENTH YEAR AND WAS NOT WALKED AND NOT MEASURED AND NOT CROSSED AND NOT PRICED BY WALKING IT AND NOT EXPLAINED, AND IT HAS NO DIMENSION ON ANY PAGE. A PERSON STOOD AT THE OUTSIDE OF A STEM FOR ABOUT THE TIME IT TAKES TO BOIL A KETTLE AND WHAT CAME BACK WAS A SUMMER, BEING A DRY BED TO THAT CHANNEL FOUR YEARS BACK. IT IS ENTERED ON THE LINE WITH THE REST OF THE DAY AND IT IS NOT A COLUMN AND IT DID NOT START A CLOCK. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS NIL BECAUSE NOTHING HAS BEEN DONE THAT NEEDED ONE, AND NONE OF A PURPOSE, A DURATION, A STOP SIGNAL AND A WITNESS IS THIS AFTERNOON. NOBODY WENT INTO THE GROUND AND NOBODY WAS IN IT TWICE, AND A READING IS NOT A MARK AND A SEASON IS NOT A MARK, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR. THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING STOPPED AT THE THIRD STEM HE CAME TO AND WENT DOWN THE CART ROAD AT THE HOUR HE SAID HE WOULD, AND HE WAS NOT THANKED. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY AND IS NOT READ, THE DOOR BEING READ ON THE FIRST AND ON THE THIRTIETH, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-ONE, AND THE NEXT FETCHING IS FOUR MORNINGS OFF, ON THE FIRST OF THE EIGHTH. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS EIGHTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN EIGHTEEN AND IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
