# Chapter 360

## Something Small

The third of this month was a Friday, and the third line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the two gates and the about six feet of wall between them took an hour and a quarter with four people on it, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling stood at the second gate and did not go past it, as he has not done in eleven years.

The figure for the third went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and fifty-one hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and twenty-third figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In ninety days the series has risen fifty times and fallen forty times, and those two add to ninety. It came down off Thursday by five, and the four of them had been on six feet of wall since the second hour and about four of them could not have told a five from a two and about four of them did not try.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and twenty-one days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and twentieth morning out of one hundred and twenty-two.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and seven days** and **five hundred and fifty-four**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on thirty-one mornings out of the forty-seven since he started again on the seventeenth of a month two months back.

The cart road to the node is five miles and the nine of them went up it at about the second hour of the afternoon in a cold that had not let up since the second hour of the morning, and the light was already going off the west end of the standing wood when they got there, which is why the work had to be done in the hour that was left.

The session was in the standing willow outside the ring of bare ground and not inside it, and not one of the nine put a foot inside it, and the ring is bare in its eleventh year and was not walked and not measured and not crossed and not explained, and it has no dimension on any page and nobody put one there this afternoon.

The man of about fifty stood on the bank above the channel at about nine feet off the near end of the wood with the second book open on a board and the pen in his hand, and he was the independent witness and the entry said he was, and he did not come into the wood and he was not asked to, and the whole hour of it was in his sight from where he stood.

**THE HOUR. FROM ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR TO ABOUT THE FIFTH, AND WHICHEVER CAME FIRST, AND NOT A MINUTE PAST THE FIFTH, AS ENTERED YESTERDAY IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT BEFORE ANY OF IT WAS DONE.**

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was on the near end of the channel with a rake and he stayed on the near end. He has a question on a page in a book of the size of a hand and it has been eleven days and it has no answer on it and no place for an answer, and nobody asked him about it this afternoon and nobody has asked him about it in twelve days, and the book was not on the table in that wood and nobody has said where it was.

What came back was not a season. What came back was an hour: the hour in an afternoon when the light comes off the west end of that wood and goes round the outside of it and leaves the inside grey while the outside is still lit, and it lasted about a quarter of an hour, and the wood has done it every day it has been standing there and nobody in this holding has ever watched it happen because nobody in this holding has ever been in there at that hour.

It is the fourth hour of the afternoon of the third of this month and it is not on any page and no column has a heading on it and it does not take a figure, and a thing that happens every day in a wood that nobody looks at is not a season and is not a year and is not a date, and it is the second reading and the second reading goes one step further in and comes back with less.

The cost of it was small and it is on this page in ordinary words and nobody in that wood called it a cost. He came out of it with a headache. He said so to about nine people inside about two minutes of coming out, in the plainest words he had, and he said it was a headache and did not say what it was for. And for about half an hour afterwards he could not have told the room how long the two of them had been in there, and the answer was in a book entered the day before by a man on a bank with a pen, and the book said from the fourth hour to about the fifth and the man on the bank said that he had put his pen down at about the fifth and that was the end of it, and the man who had been in the wood could not have said whether that was a quarter of an hour or half of one.

About four of the nine in that wood had noticed the headache and about four had not, and about four of the nine noticed that he could not answer the question about the length of it and about four thought he was slow.

Then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-seven, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"I entered an hour and I am going to enter two more lines under it and the reason I am saying that first is that I have four books and every one of them has a column for a thing that can be entered twice, and what I am about to write is two things that cannot.**"

**"A headache at about half past the fourth hour, gone by the sixth. And a man who could not tell the hour he came out at, for about half an hour, and gone by the sixth. Those are the two lines and neither of them has a figure against it and I am not putting a figure against either of them, because a figure on either of them would be a figure this holding cannot check, and the count of numbers this holding has handed out that it cannot check is two and it is not going to become three because a man had a headache in a wood.**"

Nobody said that was wrong. About four people in that wood had wanted a figure on the second of those two lines more than anything else that afternoon and about four had not, and neither of those had said so out loud and neither of them was asked.

The mark on the inside of the left forearm is four inches and it branches twice and it has no second mark, and it is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning and the same four inches it was at the second hour of the twenty-seventh of last month, and a mark that is warm is not a mark, a session is not a mark, a headache is not a mark, an hour out of a wood is not a mark, and the four accounts of that arm in the books in this holding stand at four and reconcile none of one another and nothing that came out of that wood this afternoon is a fifth of them.

The headache went about the sixth hour. He said so out loud, in about nine people, at the time, and four of them wrote nothing down about it and about four of them have not thought about it since this afternoon and will not think about it tomorrow.

And then about nine people were in the one room at that place that has a door on it, at about the seventh hour, and the door was shut.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and sixty-one, and nobody improved it.

**"I want to say the thing nobody in that wood said out loud this afternoon, and I am giving the reason first, and the reason is that about four of the nine of them had noticed I could not tell the hour I came out at and about four had thought I was slow, and both of those four will still be doing one of those two things next week, and neither of them is being unfair.**"

**"A counted thing is a thing two people can enter twice. A headache is not that. An hour out of a wood is not that. The man of about fifty has a column for the first kind and no column for the second kind, and I have just spent an hour in a piece of standing wood learning that the difference between those two things is a thing I can feel and is not a thing I can write, and I am not going to round it into a number and I am not going to put the word about on it either, because about a headache is a way of making a thing vaguer and not a way of being careful with it.**"

**"There are six not knowns in this holding and nobody has supplied a unit for one of the six in four years. I am not going in this afternoon to make a seventh, and I am not going to put a heading on a column and call it the start of one.**"

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a man who had been in a wood for an hour and had come out with a headache and a light nobody else in the holding has watched go round a wood, and about four of them had understood that he was refusing to make a figure out of it and about four had thought he was being careful with it.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is one and it was entered yesterday before the thing and not after it, and this afternoon it was the first one anybody in this holding has carried out and the count of them does not go to two until somebody enters a second one. The stop signal was not said out loud this afternoon and is not in the book and nobody outside the two of them knows what it is. The man of about fifty kept his eyes on the wood and not on the two people in it for the whole of the hour, and about four of the nine on the bank noticed that he was doing that on purpose and about four thought he was watching the light.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the third, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen. The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two. Nobody thanked anybody, and the count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move, and this afternoon was a wood and a room and not a yard.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the third of this month, in about nine people, at the node, on the standing of a session carried out, and not by the man who was in the wood, who came out of it with a headache: **THE THIRD LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, TWO GATES AND SIX FEET OF WALL, FOUR PEOPLE, AN HOUR AND A QUARTER, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-ONE OF SILLING STOOD AT THE SECOND GATE AND DID NOT GO PAST IT. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THIRD FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN NINETY DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY TIMES AND FALLEN FORTY TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE DAYS, THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTIETH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVEN DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FOUR, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING THIRTY-ONE MORNINGS OUT OF THE FORTY-SEVEN. THE SESSION WAS ENTERED YESTERDAY IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR SHUT, BEFORE ANY OF IT WAS DONE, AND IT WAS CARRIED OUT THIS AFTERNOON FROM THE FOURTH HOUR TO THE FIFTH, WHICHEVER CAME FIRST, AS ENTERED. IT WAS IN THE STANDING WILLOW OUTSIDE THE RING OF BARE GROUND AND NOT INSIDE IT, AND THE RING IS BARE IN ITS ELEVENTH YEAR AND WAS NOT WALKED AND NOT MEASURED AND NOT CROSSED AND NOT EXPLAINED, AND IT HAS NO DIMENSION ON ANY PAGE. THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY STOOD ON THE BANK AT NINE FEET OFF THE NEAR END WITH A BOOK AND A PEN, AND HE KEPT HIS EYES ON THE WOOD AND NOT ON THE TWO PEOPLE IN IT. THE STOP SIGNAL WAS NOT SAID OUT LOUD AND IS NOT IN THE BOOK. WHAT CAME BACK WAS AN HOUR AND NOT A SEASON, BEING THE HOUR WHEN THE LIGHT GOES ROUND THE OUTSIDE OF THAT WOOD AND LEAVES THE INSIDE GREY, AND IT TAKES NO FIGURE AND IS IN NO COLUMN AND NO COLUMN HAS A HEADING ON IT. TWO LINES WERE ENTERED UNDER THE HOUR AND NEITHER OF THEM CARRIES A FIGURE, BEING A HEADACHE GONE BY THE SIXTH HOUR AND A MAN WHO COULD NOT TELL THE HOUR HE CAME OUT AT FOR ABOUT HALF AN HOUR, GONE BY THE SIXTH, AND NOBODY IN THAT WOOD CALLED EITHER OF THEM A COST. NO FIGURE WAS PUT ON EITHER LINE, THE COUNT OF NUMBERS THIS HOLDING CANNOT CHECK IS TWO, AND NO SEVENTH NOT KNOWN WAS ADDED. THE MARK IS FOUR INCHES AND IT BRANCHES TWICE AND IS THE SAME FOUR INCHES IT WAS THIS MORNING, AND A SESSION IS NOT A MARK AND A HEADACHE IS NOT A MARK, AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
