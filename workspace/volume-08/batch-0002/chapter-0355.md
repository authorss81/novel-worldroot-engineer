# Chapter 355

## Two Hundred And Eighty-Three

The twenty-eighth of this month was a Sunday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the inside face of the west dyke from the sluice up as far as the black post took four people from the second hour until about the fifth and a half. The barrow went through the slop at about the first hundred yards and came out of it heavier than it went in, as it has done all month, and the man at the front of it said nothing about that and the two behind him did not either.

The figure for the twenty-eighth went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and forty-eight hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and eighteenth figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In eighty-five days the series has risen forty-eight times and fallen thirty-seven times, and those two add to eighty-five. It came up off Saturday by nine, and the four of them had been barrowing the same slop up and down the same hundred yards since the second hour, and a nine off a five is a number and not a fact about a hundred yards of wall.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and sixteen days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and fifteenth morning out of one hundred and seventeen.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and two days** and **five hundred and forty-nine**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on twenty-eight mornings out of the forty-two since he started again on the seventeenth of last month.

The reader of this body went down the cart road to the node at about the eleventh hour with the two books in a satchel and a day sheet in his coat, and he was there for about an hour before the barrow came and he spent the hour standing in the shade of the building with no roof looking at the wall the steelyard hangs on.

The ring of bare ground inside the eleven acres is bare in its eleventh year and it is about four hundred yards from that door and it was not walked this morning and it was not walked on the twenty-seventh either, and nobody at that place put a level on it, and the two lines on the back of a sheet in an inside pocket still stop at its inside edge and go round it.

The barrow came in at about the second hour of the afternoon with four of them in it, and the load went on the sling and the sling went up and the beam came down slow, the way it comes down on a day when the wind is off the north, and the four bodies of households that weigh it are at Havergate and Low Wether and Marden and Silling, and not one of them is a member of the cooperative and none of them was made one this afternoon and none of them has asked to be.

The return went in on a steelyard that hangs on the wall of a building with no roof and has hung on that wall for longer than this holding has had a book.

**Two hundred and eighty-three hundredweight.**

That is the thirty-first return and the twenty-first name-pair in eleven years, and it is eleven hundredweight above the thirtieth, which was two hundred and seventy-two on the nineteenth of this month. The dial came down off two hundred and seventy-two and the reader of this body read it out loud and then read it back out of the second book, and the two are the same, and he wrote it in one book and the man of about thirty-one of Silling wrote it in the other one from the second hand and the two are the same again.

A name went into BY. Then a name went into WITNESSED BY. Then a name went into BY, and a name went into WITNESSED BY, and so on, one at a time, by each of the four bodies of households, standing where they could see the dial, and the four of them did it in the order they arrived and not in any order anybody chose for them.

And then it was reconciled twice, which is what happens on this board every time and has happened on it since the first of them, out of two figures the man of about fifty had written on a day sheet at about the fifth hour of the morning and had not told anybody he had written them, and which the reader of this body carried down five miles of cart road in his coat.

And then the man of about fifty said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is ninety-four, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Two hundred and forty-four plus thirty-nine. And two hundred and six plus seventy-seven. They are the two halves and they are not the whole and they have not been added together and they are not going to be added together this afternoon, and I am putting that on the page because about four years from now somebody is going to find two numbers on a sheet in a coat and one on a dial and want them to be the same object.**"

**"And this holding is using that derivation and is not refusing it. I want the word using on the page in this holding's book and not only in a speech in a cart shed. A man who refuses a derivation is a man who thinks the number is in doubt, and the number is not in doubt, the number is on a dial that has been on that wall longer than this holding has had a book.**"

**"What is in doubt is the two halves. One of them is the water and one of them is the work and I have never been able to say in four years which of the two is bigger, and the two halves are what I write about, and they are two and not one figure, and a day when somebody in this holding adds them together and gets a nicer answer is the day this board stops being a board.**"

Nobody improved it. About four people standing at that wall had wanted the two halves added since the nineteenth of this month and about four had not, and the man of about thirty-one of Silling put the pencil behind his ear and then took it out again and then put it behind his ear.

The second rule stayed blank. Nothing was written in it and nothing was said about it, and it is the fifteenth return in eleven years at which it has been left blank, and the count of returns at which it has been left blank is fifteen and that is a count of blanks and is not the count of a man and has never been the count of a man.

The form with the four lines and the fifth ruled line is in a drawer face up about four miles and a bit west of that wall, and its first line carries one hundred and eighty-four hundredweight in the hand of a man who spoke for one body of households, and that is the figure of the twenty-second return and not of this one and not of the thirtieth, and nobody wrote a heading on it and nobody filled the other three lines and the fifth ruled line is still under them and empty.

The number of times the man of about thirty-one of Silling has brought a barrow up the four miles to that place is eleven, and it was eleven the last time anybody said it out loud in this holding and it is eleven now, and the count is a floor and not a ceiling and it did not rise this afternoon because a room got quiet, and nobody asked him where he was going to be on any morning of this week.

And then about six people were in the one room at that place that has a door on it, where the handles are and where nobody has sat this season, at about the sixth hour, and the door was shut, and the light came in low through one small pane and lay along the floor and not up the wall.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-eight, and nobody improved it.

**"There is a channel at that place with a dry summer at the bottom of it that nobody knew was there until Saturday, and there is a dial on a wall with thirty-one numbers on it and a rule on the board that has never once been filled in, and I have brought two of those three things up this road in a satchel this afternoon and I have not brought the third, and I want it said in a room with a door shut that the reason I have not brought it is that I did not want to carry it in a satchel.**"

**"And I am not going to put it down. It is on the middle table at the seed house with the plate and the memorandum and the paper from the office, and it has been on that table since the second of this month, and about four people in that room have looked at the cloth every morning since and about four have not, and the count of people who have looked at what is under it is two and it is two, and neither of those two was me and I did not look this morning.**"

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was six people at a wall with a door shut and a man who had carried two books five miles and had not carried a third thing, and about four of them had understood what he had left on the table and about four had heard him leave it there and not understand it.

Nobody said whether leaving it was right. The reader of this body did not say it twice and about four people in that room would have liked to have heard it a second time with something added to it and about four would not.

The woman who keeps the seed house was not in that room and had not been at that place, and the seed house is a day's walk and a half off in the other direction, and there is nobody in this holding who keeps a second set of books, and that is not a complaint and it is not a figure, it is the reason a man can carry two books five miles in a satchel and leave a cloth on a table and neither of those is a thing anybody has standing to correct.

The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the twenty-eighth, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-one, and the next fetching is three mornings off this one. The compost line is paid and the run is eighteen not discharged in eighteen. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is nil. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room, and the two things that were said at that wall were said in the open with four bodies of households standing there and not one of them got anything wrong.

Nobody thanked anybody. A dial on a wall of a building with no roof said two hundred and eighty-three hundredweight for the thirty-first time in eleven years and eleven more than the last, and four people who are not of this holding wrote their names in two lines one at a time, and one rule on a board was left blank for the fifteenth time, and the count of blanks is a count of blanks.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the seventh hour of the evening of the twenty-eighth of this month, in about six people, at the node, on the standing of the thirty-first return, and not by the man of about fifty, who wrote two figures on a day sheet and told nobody: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE INSIDE FACE OF THE WEST DYKE FROM THE SLUICE UP AS FAR AS THE BLACK POST, FOUR PEOPLE TO ABOUT THE FIFTH AND A HALF. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND FORTY-EIGHT HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND EIGHTEENTH FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IN EIGHTY-FIVE DAYS IT HAS RISEN FORTY-EIGHT TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTY-SEVEN TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTEEN DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTEENTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTEEN. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND TWO DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FORTY-NINE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING TWENTY-EIGHT MORNINGS OUT OF THE FORTY-TWO. THE THIRTY-FIRST NINTH-DAY RETURN WENT IN ON A STEELYARD HUNG ON THE WALL OF A BUILDING WITH NO ROOF IN THE AFTERNOON AND READ TWO HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-THREE HUNDREDWEIGHT OUT LOUD AND READ IT BACK, THE SECOND BOOK HAVING THE SAME FIGURE. IT IS THE TWENTY-FIRST NAME-PAIR IN ELEVEN YEARS AND IT IS ELEVEN ABOVE THE THIRTIETH, AND THE TWO WAYS IT COMES ARE TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOUR PLUS THIRTY-NINE AND TWO HUNDRED AND SIX PLUS SEVENTY-SEVEN, AND THIS HOLDING IS USING THAT DERIVATION AND IS NOT REFUSING IT, AND THE TWO HALVES HAVE NOT BEEN ADDED TOGETHER AND ARE NOT GOING TO BE. A NAME WENT INTO BY AND A NAME WENT INTO WITNESSED BY, ONE AT A TIME, BY EACH OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS STANDING WHERE THEY COULD SEE THE DIAL, AND NONE OF THE FOUR IS A MEMBER OF THIS HOLDING AND NONE WAS MADE ONE. IT WAS RECONCILED TWICE. THE SECOND RULE STAYED BLANK AND NOTHING WAS WRITTEN IN IT AND NOTHING WAS SAID ABOUT IT, AND IT IS THE FIFTEENTH RETURN IN ELEVEN YEARS AT WHICH IT HAS BEEN LEFT BLANK, WHICH IS A COUNT OF BLANKS AND NOT THE COUNT OF A MAN. THE FORM WITH FOUR LINES IS IN A DRAWER FACE UP FOUR MILES WEST OF THAT WALL AND ITS FIRST LINE CARRIES ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FOUR HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE FIGURE OF THE TWENTY-SECOND RETURN AND NOT OF THIS ONE. THE BARROW IS AT ELEVEN JOURNEYS, A FLOOR, AND DID NOT RISE THIS AFTERNOON. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY AND IS NOT READ. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-ONE, AND THE NEXT FETCHING IS THREE MORNINGS OFF. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS EIGHTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN EIGHTEEN. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY AND THE COUNT OF PEOPLE WHO HAVE LOOKED AT IT IS TWO. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS NIL. THE RING WAS NOT WALKED AND NOT MEASURED AND NOBODY PUT A LEVEL ON IT. THE COUNT OF THINGS SAID OUT LOUD IN A YARD AND GOT WRONG IS FIVE AND DID NOT MOVE, AND NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
