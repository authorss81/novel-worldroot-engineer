# Chapter 359

## Entered Before

The second of this month was a Thursday, and the second line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the inside face of the west dyke from the sluice up as far as the black post took four people from the second hour until about the fifth and a half. The ground had taken two days of rain into the same place about two hundred yards along where a barrow wheel found a hole in the slop on the eighteenth, and the man at the front went round it and said nothing about going round it, and the other three went round it because he did.

There is a rule on the wall of the seed house with four lines on it, and it did not go near this morning's work, and the man of about fifty did not reach for it, and he was not asked about it and did not offer, and the count of the times that rule has been named in the work of a season is a number he keeps and has never been asked for and has never offered it.

The figure for the second went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and fifty-six hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and twenty-second figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has risen. In eighty-nine days the series has risen fifty times and fallen thirty-nine times, and those two add to eighty-nine. It came up off Wednesday by nine, and it is the fourth morning in a row that has come up by nine, and the man of about fifty has three of them in a margin in a book of his own with no note against any of the three and he was not asked about the fourth and did not offer it.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and twenty days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and nineteenth morning out of one hundred and twenty-one.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were not read out loud. The pair stands at **five hundred and six days** and **five hundred and fifty-three**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. The run does not move and this page carries no figure for it.

At about the fourth hour of the afternoon about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut. The register form was on the middle table where it has been for a fortnight, the drawer was shut, the light came in low off the south wall and lay along the table and across the five things on it and not across anybody's hands, and the woman who keeps the seed house went out and shut the door behind her and did not come back into the room for about an hour.

She took the fire with her, which she has never done, and the room got cold about a quarter of an hour in and nobody opened the door to ask her about it. About four people in that room noticed the cold and about four did not, and the four that did not were the four that had come in last.

The man of about thirty-one of Silling was in it with the book of the reader of this body shut on the table in front of him and his hand flat on the cover of it, and he did not open it, and nobody asked him whether he was going to, and about four people in that room had worked out in the fortnight that the hand on the cover is a thing a man does and not a thing a man is saying.

And then Tova Reed said one thing, and she gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is nineteen, and it is her own, and she was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"There is a thing this holding is going to do at that wood and I am one of the two ends of it, and I am going to say the reason I want it written down tonight and not on Saturday morning before any of it goes wrong. The reason is that a thing that is written down afterwards is a memory, and a memory belongs to the two people who were in it, and there are nine people in this room who are not going to be in it, and they will not be able to check a word of it and they will be told it happened.**"

**"And a book that carries a thing down because two people said so, and no third person could have said otherwise, is a worse book than a book with a line in it and nothing under the line, and I have watched what this holding does with the empty places in its own ledger and I would rather there were one of mine in it.**"

Nobody improved it. The man of about fifty got the second book out and the pen and put the book on the middle table square against the edge, and he did not write anything while she was talking, which is not what he usually does.

And then she said the four of them, and the fen entered the whole of it and improved none of it.

**THE PURPOSE: TO READ ONE STEP DEEPER IN THAT STANDING WILLOW AT THE NODE THAN A PERSON CAN READ FROM THE OUTSIDE OF A STEM, WHICH IS WHAT A PERSON STOOD ON THE TWENTY-SEVENTH OF LAST MONTH AND WHICH CAME BACK AS A DRY SUMMER IN A CHANNEL IN ABOUT A MINUTE. THE DURATION: FROM THE FOURTH HOUR UNTIL THE FIFTH, AND WHICHEVER OF THE TWO COMES FIRST, AND NOT A MINUTE PAST THE FIFTH. THE STOP SIGNAL: THERE IS ONE, IT IS HERS, IT HAS BEEN HERS SINCE THE SEVENTEENTH OF A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK, AND ITS WORDS ARE NOT ENTERED HERE AND ARE NOT GOING TO BE ENTERED HERE, AND THE REASON IS THAT THIS BOOK IS CARRIED BETWEEN ROOMS. A STOP SIGNAL THAT IS IN A BOOK IS NOT A STOP SIGNAL, IT IS A WORD SOMEONE ELSE CAN FIND OUT AND THEN WAIT FOR. THE INDEPENDENT WITNESS: THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO IS NOT AT EITHER END, WHO IS THE ONLY PERSON IN THIS HOLDING WHO KEEPS A RECORD OF WHAT HAPPENED, AND WHO HAS BEEN TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND HAS NOT BEEN TOLD WHAT IT IS, AND WHO IS NOT GOING TO BE TOLD.**

And then the man of about fifty wrote it, and the fen entered the whole of that and improved none of it. He wrote the day, which is the second of this month. He wrote the hour, which is about the fifth. He wrote the four of them, in the words she had used and not in his own. He wrote the line, THE STOP SIGNAL IS HERS AND ITS WORDS ARE NOT ENTERED, and he left it at that.

Under those he wrote two words and the hour, being A CONDITION WAS STATED, and he left the four ruled lines under them empty, and they are the same four ruled lines he keeps under the sixth column of the office's ledger and under the survey pages that are not in the crate, and nobody in that room has ever asked him why a man who keeps an empty place visible for other people's records keeps one for his own, and nobody is going to ask him tonight.

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a book open on a table with four ruled lines under two words and nothing under those, and about four of them had understood that a man who cannot be told what the stop is is the only kind of witness a thing like this can have, and about four had understood that he had been chosen for it and not volunteered for it.

The woman who keeps the seed house came back in at about the sixth hour and put the cloth down again over the seed and the plate and the memorandum and the form, four cloths over four things and not one over the fifth, the way she does it, from the near corner, without looking at anything else, and neither she nor anybody else in that room has said a word about the fifth in four years.

The room did not empty when the book was shut. It sat in the cold for about a quarter of an hour with nine people in it, and about four of them looked at the four ruled lines from where they were sitting and about four looked at the window, and nobody moved to the middle table to look at them properly and nobody was invited to and nobody would have come if they had been. The count of questions he has asked about the condition is nil and it is nil, and he did not ask this afternoon, and the stop word was not said in this room, and it is not written in this book, and a page that put either of those two things near a man who has not asked is a page that has spent a thing this holding does not have.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and sixty, and nobody improved it.

**"She has said why it is entered before and I am going to say the other half of it, and the reason is that the last clause of what is in that book is about a witness, and about four people in this room have read that clause and thought it was a courtesy, and it is not a courtesy. A man who does not know the stop cannot be the reason the stop was not called.**"

**"That is the whole of what it is for. If he knew it he would be watching for it on a man and not on the work, and a witness who is watching for a word is not a witness. He has been in this holding eleven days and he has not asked me one question about any of it and I am not going to put a reason on that in a book either, and I would like the room to have heard that said without anything added to it.**"

Nobody said that was right. About four people in that room had wanted to be told the stop word and about four had not, and the four that wanted it had wanted it for about nine minutes and had worked out in about nine minutes that it was the wrong thing to want.

The count of sessions entered in this holding's book was nil at about the fourth hour of the afternoon and is one at about the fifth, and it stopped being nil before the thing happened and not after it, and a session that is not entered did not happen, and a scene of a thing that is not entered is a scene this holding's book cannot vouch for and about four years from now nobody will be able to tell it from a thing two people made up in a room. The session itself is tomorrow and is entered, and this afternoon is the entering of it and not the doing of it.

Nobody went into the ground. Nobody was in the ground. The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two and did not move. The use log stands at fifteen lines. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read, the door being read on the first and on the thirtieth and this is the second, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them and nobody wrote a heading on one. The man of about seventy is not fetched and the count of fetchings stays at twenty-two. The compost line is paid and the run is nineteen not discharged in nineteen and it does not fall again until the first of a month two months back from now.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this afternoon, and this was a room with the door shut, and a session that is entered before it happens is not said out loud in a yard at about the ninth hour on the morning of it by anybody. The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and a session is not a mark and four accounts of that arm stand at four.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the eighth hour of the evening of the second of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of the first session, and not by the man of about fifty, who wrote it whole and left the four lines under the two words empty: **THE SECOND LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE INSIDE FACE OF THE WEST DYKE FROM THE SLUICE UP AS FAR AS THE BLACK POST, FOUR PEOPLE UNTIL ABOUT THE FIFTH AND A HALF. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SIX HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SECOND FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS RISEN, AND IN EIGHTY-NINE DAYS IT HAS RISEN FIFTY TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTY-NINE TIMES. THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY DAYS AND THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WAS WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND NINETEENTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND SIX DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-THREE, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE NOT READ OUT LOUD THIS MORNING, AND THE RUN DID NOT MOVE AND THIS PAGE CARRIES NO FIGURE FOR IT. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK WAS NIL AT ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON AND IS ONE AT ABOUT THE FIFTH, AND IT STOPPED BEING NIL BEFORE THE THING HAPPENED AND NOT AFTER IT. THE ENTRY CARRIES A PURPOSE, BEING TO READ ONE STEP DEEPER IN THE STANDING WILLOW AT THE NODE THAN A PERSON CAN READ FROM THE OUTSIDE OF A STEM; A DURATION, BEING FROM THE FOURTH HOUR UNTIL THE FIFTH AND WHICHEVER COMES FIRST; A STOP SIGNAL, WHICH EXISTS, WHICH IS HERS, AND WHOSE WORDS ARE NOT ENTERED HERE AND ARE NOT GOING TO BE, THE REASON BEING THAT THIS BOOK IS CARRIED BETWEEN ROOMS; AND AN INDEPENDENT WITNESS, BEING THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY, WHO IS NOT AT EITHER END AND WHO HAS BEEN TOLD THAT A CONDITION EXISTS AND HAS NOT BEEN TOLD WHAT IT IS AND IS NOT GOING TO BE. UNDER THE FOUR HE WROTE TWO WORDS AND THE HOUR, BEING A CONDITION WAS STATED, AND LEFT THE FOUR RULED LINES UNDER THEM EMPTY, AND THOSE ARE THE SAME FOUR RULED LINES HE KEEPS UNDER THE SIXTH COLUMN OF THE OFFICE'S LEDGER AND UNDER THE SURVEY PAGES THAT ARE NOT IN THE CRATE. THE SESSION ITSELF IS TOMORROW. THIS AFTERNOON IS THE ENTERING OF IT AND NOT THE DOING OF IT, AND NOBODY WENT INTO THE GROUND. THE STOP WORD WAS NOT SAID IN THAT ROOM AND IS NOT WRITTEN IN THAT BOOK, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY HAS ASKED ABOUT THE CONDITION IS NIL. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE SEVENTH COLUMN STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS NOT READ, AND THE SIX OTHERS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY IS NOT FETCHED AND THE COUNT STAYS AT TWENTY-TWO. THE COMPOST LINE IS PAID AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR AND A SESSION IS NOT A MARK. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
