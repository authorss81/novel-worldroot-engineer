# Chapter 358

## Three Clocks On One Morning

The first of the eighth month was a Wednesday, and the month turned in the night, and the first line of the rota came round at the second hour and was worked, and the boundary ditch and the two culverts took about six people until about the fourth hour. The near culvert was clear. The far one was about half full of the same leaf and the six of them took four barrow-loads out of it and put them on the far shoulder where they have been put every morning this month.

Nothing reset in the night. The count of that series is day minus four hundred and fifty and it was a hundred and twenty on the thirtieth of this month and it is a hundred and twenty-one this morning. The rises and the falls do not re-base and the window stood at eighty-seven yesterday and stands at eighty-eight this morning. The boards and the March's tin did not reset and the aggregate of the four bodies of households did not reset, and a calendar is the one thing in this holding that works whether anybody is caring about it or not.

The frames did move, and that is a different thing. A thing of the seventh month is of last month from this morning and was of this month yesterday, and the order that came on the third of last month and the paper that came on the sixteenth of last month are both of last month this morning and not before it. The memorandum on the middle table is dated the twentieth of a month three months back. The nine acres came off on the twenty-ninth of a month two months back. The seed in its rootwood and the plate came off a cradle on the nineteenth of a month three months back. None of them moved and all of them have been renamed by a night.

The figure for the first went into the book that keeps the third launder at about the eighth hour and was read back by the man of about fifty out of the other book with the frayed corner, and the two are the same.

**Seven hundred and forty-seven hundredweight.**

That is the hundred and twenty-first figure in a series that has not repeated one figure since the first, and it has fallen. In eighty-eight days the series has risen forty-nine times and fallen thirty-nine times, and those two add to eighty-eight. It came down off Tuesday by five, and the six of them had spent the morning taking leaf out of a culvert and about four of them had no way of telling a five from a two.

The aggregate of the four bodies of households has not moved in one hundred and nineteen days, the sentence saying so having been written this morning for the one hundred and eighteenth morning out of one hundred and twenty. That sentence is not the aggregate of the four launders and the two have never been added.

And at about the seventh hour of the morning the day-minus rules were read out loud before either number, in about four people, the way they are read, one saying the first and the other saying the second so that neither of them has to be trusted with both. The boards stand at **five hundred and five days** and **five hundred and fifty-two**, forty-seven days apart. The drawer was not opened and is not to be. He has read them out loud on thirty mornings out of the forty-five since he started again on the seventeenth of a month two months back.

At about the third hour of the afternoon two of them went up the nine hundred yards and the column was read again, and nobody made a day of it beyond the reading.

> **THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO AND IS FIFTY-TWO, READ AT ABOUT THE THIRD HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON OF THE FIRST OF THIS MONTH. IT CAME UP OFF FIFTY ON THE THIRTIETH OF LAST MONTH AND IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FIFTY, AND FROM YESTERDAY IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FIFTY-ONE. YESTERDAY AND TODAY ARE THE OTHER HALVES OF EACH OTHER, AND THE PAIR IS THE FOURTH SUCH PAIR OF CONSECUTIVE READING DAYS IN FOUR YEARS. THE SIX OTHER COLUMNS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE OF THEM, AND NONE IS GOING TO BE.**

Two people were at that door and there had not been two people at that door on the morning before, and neither of them mentioned it, and about four miles of path between that building and a seed house has a way of making a remark feel like an event.

At about the fourth hour the man of about seventy was fetched, and the count of fetchings went off twenty-one on to twenty-two, and no person in this holding has said why and this holding is not going to say why in a book.

He came up the path at about the speed of a man of about seventy, which is the speed he has come at twenty-two times, and he stopped at the gate and put his hands flat on the two gate posts, one on each, the way he does, and the person who noticed him was the person who keeps the seed house and she was not thanked for it and did not expect to be. He had been in the room for about an hour when he asked whether the window opens on the sixth, and he was told that it opens on the sixth and shuts on the eleventh, and he said that he had known that, and that was the whole of what he said in that hour.

He is about seventy and walks with his left hand on the rail of the path for the first half of it and lets go of it at the gate, and he has done that on twenty-two mornings and about four people in this holding have watched him let go of it and about four have stopped watching. He sits in the chair by the stove with his hat on his knee and his coat on, and the woman who keeps the seed house puts the cloth down over the seed and the plate and the memorandum and the form without being asked and without saying anything, and puts four cloths over four things and not one over the fifth, and neither of them has ever said a word about the fifth and it has been four years.

Nobody asked him which number it was. He did not ask. The count of questions the reader of this body has asked him in twenty-two fetchings is nil and it is nil, and there is no page in this holding's book with a reason on it, and this page is not going to be the one that supplies it in about four years.

And at about the second hour of the afternoon, in the same building, the compost line fell, and it is entered whole.

**PAID, ONE HUNDRED POUNDS, AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN. THE WORD BEFORE THE FIGURE IS PAID AND IT IS THE SAME WORD THAT WAS THERE ON THE FIRST OF LAST MONTH, WHEN THE RUN WAS EIGHTEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN EIGHTEEN, AND THE FIGURE HAS NOT MOVED AND THE WORD HAS NOT MOVED AND THE RUN HAS MOVED BY ONE, AND THE MOVE BY ONE IS THIRTY DAYS AND NOT ONE DAY, AND THE LINE FALLS ON THE FIRST OF A MONTH AND IT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND AND IT IS NOT GOING TO BE USED AS ONE. THE WORD HAS STOOD OVER THAT LINE FOR TWELVE MONTHS AND NOT FOR THE WHOLE OF THAT RUN, AND NOT ONE PERSON HAS STOOD THE TWO AGAINST EACH OTHER AND SAID, IN WORDS, HOW MANY MONTHS THAT IS.**

Three things on one morning, and each of them done by a different person in a different place, and about four of the nine people in this holding have known all three were coming since the thirtieth and about four have not, and the four that knew have not said so out loud and are not going to.

The woman of about thirty-eight of Marden was not in the room at any point on this morning or this evening and nobody sent for her and nobody asked her anything and the count of questions the reader of this body has asked her is nil and it is nil. The key is in her hand and was not used and was not asked about, and the valve it opens has not been opened this month and the two-foot gap at the kerb is as open as it was on the eleventh of the first month and nothing has gone into it in this season and nothing is going to.

And then at about the fifth hour of the evening about nine people were in the seed house with the door shut, and the man of about seventy was in it, sitting in the chair by the stove with his hat on his knee, and the register form was on the middle table where it has been since the sixteenth of last month, and the drawer was shut.

And then the reader of this body said one thing, and he gave the reason first, and the count of that in this holding's history is one hundred and fifty-nine, and it is his own, and he was not thanked and did not ask to be.

**"Three things on one morning and I am going to say the reason I am not going to make anything of them, and the reason is that I have been in this room on the first of a month six times and I have watched this holding put three things on one page and then get excited about it, and about four years from now that page is going to be read as a pattern by somebody who was not in this room.**"

**"A column on a door goes up because a man reads it. A line of compost goes up by one because a month is thirty days. A man of about seventy comes up a path because he has always come up that path on the first of a month and nobody has ever asked him why and I have not asked him why in twenty-two of them and I am not going to start this morning. That is three different things that happen to be on the same page, and the page is a page, and a page with three things on it is not a pattern and I am not going to let this holding turn it into one.**"

Nobody said that was wrong. About four people in that room had wanted it made into a pattern and about four had not, and the man of about seventy sat in the chair by the stove with his hat on his knee throughout and did not appear to be listening and was not asked to be.

For about a minute there was nothing in that room, and what was in it was nine people and a man of about seventy and a man who has been fetched twenty-two times saying out loud that he is not going to work out why, and about four of the nine had understood that the not working out is the work and about four had heard a man decline a question he had been asked twenty-two times.

The use log stands at fifteen lines and the reason was not given. The seventh column of the well house door stands at fifty-two and is not read again today, and the door is read on the first and on the thirtieth and the next of those is the thirtieth of this month, and the six other columns have nothing at the head of them. The count of sessions entered in this holding's book is nil, and none of the three things that fell this morning is one of the things that needs one, and a page with three clocks on it is not a session. The mark is four inches along the inside of the left forearm and branches twice and is the same four inches it was at the second hour of this morning, and the four accounts of the arm stand at four and a month turning is not a fifth of them.

Nobody thanked anybody. The count of things said out loud in a yard and got wrong is five and did not move this morning or this evening, and nothing was said in a yard today. The thing under the cloth was not looked at by anybody and the count of people who have looked at it is two. The window of the office's work opens on the sixth of this month and shuts on the eleventh, and it is five days off this morning and not tomorrow.

A thing of the seventh is of last month from this morning, and the man of about seventy came up a path that has not changed its name in eleven years, and the compost line has said the same word over the same line for twelve months running, and about four people in this holding have decided that three things that happen every month are not three things that happen and are not going to say so this evening.

> Entered by the clerk, in his own hand, at about the ninth hour of the evening of the first of this month, in about nine people, at this holding, on the standing of three clocks, and not by the man of about seventy, who has never once asked which number it was: **THE MONTH TURNED IN THE NIGHT AND THE FIRST LINE CAME ROUND AT THE SECOND HOUR AND WAS WORKED, THE BOUNDARY DITCH AND THE TWO CULVERTS, ABOUT SIX PEOPLE UNTIL ABOUT THE FOURTH HOUR. NOTHING RESET. THE THIRD LAUNDER MEASURED SEVEN HUNDRED AND FORTY-SEVEN HUNDREDWEIGHT, THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIRST FIGURE OF A SERIES THAT HAS NOT REPEATED ONE FIGURE SINCE THE FIRST, AND IT HAS FALLEN, AND IN EIGHTY-EIGHT DAYS IT HAS RISEN FORTY-NINE TIMES AND FALLEN THIRTY-NINE TIMES, AND THE COUNT OF THAT SERIES IS DAY MINUS FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY AND DID NOT RESET IN THE NIGHT. THE RISES AND THE FALLS DID NOT RE-BASE. THE BOARDS AND THE MARCH'S TIN DID NOT RESET. THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR BODIES OF HOUSEHOLDS DID NOT RESET AND HAS NOT MOVED IN ONE HUNDRED AND NINETEEN DAYS, THE SENTENCE SAYING SO WRITTEN THIS MORNING FOR THE ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTEENTH MORNING OUT OF ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY, AND THAT SENTENCE IS NOT THE AGGREGATE OF THE FOUR LAUNDERS. THE BOARDS STAND AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIVE DAYS AND THE MARCH'S TIN AT FIVE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-TWO, FORTY-SEVEN APART, AND THE DRAWER WAS NOT OPENED, AND THE TWO FIGURES WERE READ OUT LOUD BEFORE EITHER NUMBER, BEING THIRTY MORNINGS OUT OF THE FORTY-FIVE. THE SEVENTH COLUMN OF THE WELL HOUSE DOOR WAS READ IN THE AFTERNOON AND STANDS AT FIFTY-TWO, AND IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FIFTY AND FROM YESTERDAY IT MAY NOT GO BACK TO FIFTY-ONE, AND THE PAIR IS THE FOURTH SUCH PAIR IN FOUR YEARS, AND THE SIX OTHER COLUMNS HAVE NOTHING AT THE HEAD OF THEM AND NOBODY WROTE A HEADING ON ONE. THE MAN OF ABOUT SEVENTY WAS FETCHED AND THE COUNT WENT OFF TWENTY-ONE ON TO TWENTY-TWO, AND NO PERSON IN THIS HOLDING HAS SAID WHY, AND NOBODY ASKED HIM WHICH NUMBER IT WAS, AND THE COUNT OF QUESTIONS THE READER OF THIS BODY HAS ASKED HIM IN TWENTY-TWO FETCHINGS IS NIL. THE COMPOST LINE FELL AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR OF THE AFTERNOON. PAID, ONE HUNDRED POUNDS, AND THE RUN IS NINETEEN NOT DISCHARGED IN NINETEEN, AND THE WORD BEFORE THE FIGURE HAS NEVER DISAGREED WITH THE FIGURE AND MAY NOT, AND THE LINE IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE GROUND. A THING OF LAST MONTH IS OF LAST MONTH FROM THIS MORNING AND WAS OF THIS MONTH YESTERDAY, AND THE ORDER AND THE PAPER FROM AN OFFICE ARE BOTH OF LAST MONTH, AND THE MEMORANDUM ON THE TABLE IS DATED A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK, AND THE NINE ACRES CAME OFF A MONTH TWO MONTHS BACK, AND THE SEED IN ITS ROOTWOOD AND THE PLATE CAME OFF A CRADLE A MONTH THREE MONTHS BACK, AND NONE MOVED IN THE NIGHT AND ALL FOUR HAVE BEEN RENAMED BY IT. THE USE LOG STANDS AT FIFTEEN LINES. THE COUNT OF SESSIONS ENTERED IN THIS HOLDING'S BOOK IS NIL, AND A PAGE WITH THREE CLOCKS ON IT IS NOT A SESSION. THE THING UNDER THE CLOTH WAS NOT LOOKED AT BY ANYBODY. THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE ARM STAND AT FOUR. THE WINDOW OF THE OFFICE'S WORK OPENS IN FIVE DAYS ON THE SIXTH AND SHUTS ON THE ELEVENTH, SIX DAYS. NOBODY THANKED ANYBODY.**
