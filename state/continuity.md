## 1. The premise, and a serious standing doubt about it

**The specified novel is *The Worldroot Engineer*.** Marek Vale, twenty-nine, an agricultural
engineer and irrigation surveyor, finds that the world's magic grows through a living
subterranean network. He wants the charter, the well, the next sowing, and a sister who has a
life beyond the fen. He is good at finding leaks and bad at asking for help, and his flaw is
control through expertise: he reaches the technically correct answer and then believes
correctness entitles him to decide alone. **The premise, the world, the power system and the
ending lock are in `bible/`, `outline/series.md` and `outline/ending.md`, and those are the
contract.**

### The standing doubt, stated once and left for a human decision

**The manuscript on disk is not that novel.** Across 677 chapter files `Rootway` appears in zero
and `Worldroot` in zero. What has been written for fourteen volumes is a ledger about a holding
that keeps a wall, a gate, a launder, a book and thirty-five unresolved questions, with a
recurring cast, a day clock and a set of numeric series. It is internally consistent and
competently written, and it is not the book the spec, the bible and the series file specify.
**This pass did not change the planned plot and did not attempt to.** The drift is first in the
decision queue at `state/open-threads.md`, and **a later pass, or a human, owns it.** Two
things follow. **The bible is still authoritative and still describes a story that has not been
written; do not treat the manuscript as evidence that the premise is retired.** And **Iona Vey
is the fixed antagonist of the series: not new, not killed, not in custody at the end. No new
final enemy may be introduced**, and a writer who does so is obliged to flag it.

## 2. The world as the bible states it, which is the world the book must be in

**`bible/`, `outline/series.md` and `outline/ending.md` are the contract, and this section is a
reminder of them and not a substitute for them.** Every settlement in Veyra is tied to the
Rootway, a living tissue whose activity is the source of magic, in three layers with no clean
boundary: topsoil exchange, a living trunkway that must stay alive and matched to local water
pressure, and a deep archive of old environmental patterns that is damaged and open to
deliberate editing. **Four constraints bind.** Rootlight is metabolic energy and is not
unlimited mana. Memory is environmental pattern and is not prophecy. **A place may have
rootlight and still lack water, seed or stable soil, and a place may have rich soil and be cut
off from the trunkway.** Magic cannot replace labour, fertiliser, water management, seed
selection, medicine or negotiation.

**The one sentence of the history a morning needs: the White Mercy is a schedule, not a season.**

## 4. The volumes

Full detail is in `outline/volume-NN.md` and the position of the series in
`outline/series.md`. **Volume 14, *The White Mercy*, is current**: the first pruning phase
takes branches outside the basin, the climax is the regional network reaching the capital and
leaving one secondary city unwatered, and the resolution is an offer across a table, a refusal,
and a record of the refusal by Nia Vale. Volume 15 is *The Root Commons* and Volume 16 is *The
Returning Root*. `outline/ending.md` is the lock on both.

**Volume 14's own lock:** forty-nine mornings, days 851 to 899, so the closing batch is nine
mornings and not ten. The Aldren memory is paid inside this volume, before the offer, on a
morning that is neither its first nor its last, and not as its climax. The volume does not
explain what the root is, and nobody in it is entitled to say.

**At day 890, forty mornings in.** Four places want a pipe off one head. The second place has
now said out loud that it will not be first and that it should not be asked again until the ford
will take a loaded barrow. The third place's water comes round the long way. This holding is
the first thing in four years to ask that head for anything, and **its cistern at the north
side is being filled by hand because no pipe has been agreed**, which is nine or eleven trips a
day by a woman who carries water past that gate every morning. A woman from the second place
stood in this yard and gave that place's own cost in its own arithmetic and nobody improved on
it. A woman offered her room to be the place left and was refused by a man inside ten seconds
for a reason about being believed. On the last morning of this batch the arithmetic came out
with one place in it. Nobody has been asked to choose anything on any of the forty mornings.

## 5. Two questions this series may never answer

They are named once, here, and not again, and no morning may ask, name, hint at, paraphrase
or answer either.

1. **What the root is.** The answer is that nobody on the page knows. The volume's business is
   branches, load, water, a network and an offer. It does not explain the wood, the maintenance
   zone or the sixty-six, and it does not let Marek become the person who finally says what is
   under the floor.
2. **The second one.** Untouched for ten volumes counting the one before this. It is not named
   in this file and is not to be named in any state file.
