## 1. The premise, and a serious standing doubt about it

**The specified novel is *The Worldroot Engineer*.** Marek Vale, twenty-nine, an agricultural
engineer and irrigation surveyor, finds that the world's magic grows through a living
subterranean network. He wants the charter, the well, the next sowing, and a sister who has a
life beyond the fen. He is good at finding leaks and bad at asking for help, and his flaw is
control through expertise: he reaches the technically correct answer and then believes
correctness entitles him to decide alone. **The premise, the world, the power system and the
ending lock are in `bible/`, `outline/series.md` and `outline/ending.md`, and those are the
contract.**

### The standing doubt, stated once and left for a human decision

**The manuscript on disk is not that novel.** Across 667 chapter files `Rootway` appears in
zero and `Worldroot` in zero. What has been written for fourteen volumes is a ledger about a
holding that keeps a wall, a gate, a launder, a book and thirty-five unresolved questions,
with a recurring cast, a day clock and a set of numeric series. It is internally consistent
and competently written, and it is not the book the spec, the bible and the series file
specify. **This pass did not change the planned plot and did not attempt to.** The drift is
first in the decision queue at `state/open-threads.md`, and **a later pass, or a human, owns
it.** Two things follow. **The bible is still authoritative and still describes a story that
has not been written; do not treat the manuscript as evidence that the premise is retired.**
And **Iona Vey is the fixed antagonist of the series: not new, not killed, not in custody at
the end. No new final enemy may be introduced**, and a writer who does so is obliged to flag it
rather than quietly add it.

## 2. The world as the bible states it, which is the world the book must be in

**`bible/`, `outline/series.md` and `outline/ending.md` are the contract, and this section is a
reminder of them and not a substitute for them.** Every settlement in Veyra is tied to the
Rootway, a living tissue whose activity is the source of magic, in three layers with no clean
boundary: topsoil exchange, a living trunkway that must stay alive and matched to local water
pressure, and a deep archive of old environmental patterns that is damaged and open to
deliberate editing. **Four constraints bind.** Rootlight is metabolic energy and is not
unlimited mana. Memory is environmental pattern and is not prophecy. **A place may have
rootlight and still lack water, seed or stable soil, and a place may have rich soil and be cut
off from the trunkway.** Magic cannot replace labour, fertiliser, water management, seed
selection, medicine or negotiation.

**The history and the four fixed points are in the bible and the series file, and the one
sentence of it a morning needs is this: the White Mercy is a schedule, not a season.**

## 4. The volumes

Full detail is in `outline/volume-NN.md` and the position of the series in `outline/series.md`.
**Volume 14, *The White Mercy*, is current**: Iona begins the first pruning phase, Tova
abandons a seed vault, the climax is the regional network reaching the capital and leaving one
secondary city unwatered, and the resolution is an offer across a table, a refusal, and a record
of the refusal by Nia Vale. Volume 15 is *The Root Commons* and Volume 16 is *The Returning
Root*, in which Marek defeats Iona by dividing his own access and breaking the Engine's single
caretaker. `outline/ending.md` is the lock on both.

**Volume 14's own lock:** forty-nine mornings, days 851 to 899, and the closing morning is day
899, which is why its last batch is nine mornings and not ten. The Aldren memory is paid inside
this volume, before the offer, on a morning that is neither its first nor its last, and not as
its climax. The volume does not explain what the root is, and nobody in it is entitled to say.

**At day 880, thirty mornings in.** The compost line has gone over once, to paid at thirty-one,
and stands there. Four places want a pipe off one head; the second cannot be reached this
month, the third has shut its head sluice at the fifth hour, and this holding has become the
first thing in four years to ask that head for anything, for a cistern at the north side of the
room where the seed work is done. The seed work is off a shelf and onto a floor and one row of
its bench is bare. Nobody has been asked to choose anything on any of the thirty mornings.

## 5. Two questions this series may never answer

They are named once, here, and not again, and no morning may ask, name, hint at, paraphrase
or answer either.

1. **What the root is.** The answer is that nobody on the page knows. The volume's business is
   branches, load, water, a network and an offer. It does not explain the wood, it does not
   explain the maintenance zone, it does not explain the sixty-six, and it does not let Marek
   become the person who finally says what is under the floor.
2. **The second one.** Untouched for ten volumes counting the one now closed. It is not named
   in this file and is not to be named in any state file.

## 7. The figures a page or a script overrode, 2026-10-02, Batches 0003 and 0004

Each was printed wrongly in a prompt or a card, and a page or a derivation says otherwise. **A
successor that finds one of these in a card has inherited an error and not a rule.**

1. **The compost line from day 873 is paid at thirty-one and does not go back, and it is seven mornings before day 880 and not two.** A prompt printed it as paid at thirty on the nine mornings that are not day 873. `chapter-0660.md` pays the going over, on a Thursday, and `chapter-0667.md` says it has stood at thirty-one since Thursday. **AND THE DIRECTION IS NOT A FALL.** The plan and the day-873 card call this a fall and the only figure that moves downward, and the page has it the other way round: thirty-one paid is one more than thirty paid, and not one load has ever been let out of that line. **Only the direction was ever wrong, the figure is not in doubt, and the page governs.** This is the third placement a card has lost to a page, and the rule is the same as items 3 and 6.
2. **The read-aloud numerator is `one hundred and [WORD]`,** not `a hundred and`. The plan's day table prints it in the window half's form on every odd morning and that cell is withdrawn for the volume. **The same cell is wrong in the plan's rows for days 881 to 890.** Run forward over those ten mornings: one hundred and thirty fields, one hundred and twenty-five agree as strings, and the five that do not are those five cells and nothing else.
3. **The seed vault was not given up on day 879.** It was given up on day 864 in `chapter-0651.md`, in her own mouth. Nothing on 879 or 880 re-abandons it, re-announces it, has anybody discover it, or describes her as having given something up.
4. **The bare three hundred in the register form is on day 856, not day 886.** On day 886 the form is three hundred and thirty. **A state file is a prompt input, and a wrong day in one is a figure invented downstream.**
5. **Two gate measurements published by the writing pass did not reproduce and are withdrawn.** Both counts are wrong; the fixed implementation reads four defects involving mornings of this batch, not a nil. **A duplicate gate never run against a paragraph that must collide with itself will publish a nil on a batch that has one.**
6. **The fourth permanent loss is not paid on day 885.** Its card places it there; it was paid on day 868 in `chapter-0655.md` and the page governs, so days 885 and 886 are ordinary working mornings. **This is the second placement a card has lost to a page, and the rule is the same both times: the plan assigns no morning to a pressure, so a card's placement stands until a page contradicts it.**
7. **The in-between allowance for each locked figure is three and not two.** The plan's headline prints *not more than two mornings in between* and its first reason corrects itself to three. Cards thirty-four and thirty-seven carry the withdrawn figure. **Measured on the pages: both figures were spoken whole once in this volume, on its first morning, `chapter-0638.md`, and the in-between allowance was untouched across the thirty mornings written.** Batch 0004 spends one of three for each, on days 884 and 887.
8. **The far board on day 890 is eight hundred and seventy-one.** A table typed by hand carried eight hundred and sixty-one, which is day 880's figure, and three rows carried a reckonings cell the header did not have. **Both were caught by regenerating the table from the rules, which is the argument for doing that.**
