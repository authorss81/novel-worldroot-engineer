# Continuity State

## Bootstrap canon status

The world, power rules, protagonist, relationships, terminology, antagonist ladder, and ending constraints are established in `bible/` and `outline/`. No chapter prose has been written yet. Do not treat the volume and batch scaffolds as canon; they remain pending for their dedicated phases.

## Opening state

Marek Vale is twenty-nine, an ordinary agricultural engineer and irrigation surveyor at the Lantern Reach Work Cooperative in Tarrow Fen. The third consecutive White Harvest has failed. The cooperative's charter expires at the end of the season, the well water tastes of iron, and crops grow sideways. Crown inspectors can seal the well and seize seed stock if the node cannot be shown worth keeping.

Marek is Stage 0. His father's fieldbook and a first impossible pressure reading are the only signs of rootwork. He wants a funded repair, a working well, and a future for Nia; he does not want to become a magical ruler or a hero.

## Rules locked for later prose

- Rootlight is metabolic energy in living tissue; it cannot create soil, water, seed, food, or obedience.
- Every rootwork operation needs a living anchor, a compatible path, and a return.
- Human living anchors are possible only through recorded consent, a stop signal, and a safe disconnect. Shared anchors keep separate channels and never fuse minds.
- Rootwork is a licensed profession; a living bind is the forbidden arrangement, not the craft itself.
- False Season changes local root, soil, heat, and migration conditions; it cannot conjure sky weather.
- Bridge capacity is named by load: local conduit, seed-route bridge, regional bridge. A practitioner does not own a bridge's consequences.
- Public proof requires an independent external read and at least two corroborating records. A surrendered memory alone is not enough.
- A caretaker handoff is staged. Replacement seedhearts must be planted, load-tested, and carrying flow before the old link is isolated.
- The recovered living control seed can open local valves but cannot command a running Engine without a caretaker link and compatible return path.
- The first White Mercy phase is a limited core test in Volume 13; the final pruning window opens the decisive activation in Volume 16.

## Historical locks

- The Quiet Break was an engineered Crown Engine failure concealed as a natural collapse.
- The **Brinewake Collapse** was a Salt March regional failure nineteen years before the present: 4,200 people died in the first winter and 9,000 were displaced. Sera Quill removed the safety relay's red-line warning, falsified its log, and signed a report blaming weather and salt creep.
- Aldren Vale died eight years ago while trying to interrupt the current pruning cycle. He did not survive as a hidden ally.
- The Rootway is adaptive ecological memory, not a deity, single mind, or simple machine.

## Relationship and cost locks

- Marek and Tova's romance is the only approved romance. Their first cooperation is professional and contentious; later intimacy requires informed consent and does not give either person authority over the other.
- Marek's final sacrifice transfers the living resonance of his rootmark into one of the tested public seedhearts, not his mind, and costs him continuous Deep Archive access.
- The earlier Volume 13 public-evidence sacrifice costs Marek one irreplaceable Aldren memory, not all archive access.
- Tova's Volume 08 anchor work leaves partial hearing loss. Lissa's forgiveness remains partial and practical. Halden's reconciliation requires a specific public concession. Tarin is captured, testifies under guard, and is not redeemed.

## Ending lock

Iona's White Mercy is stopped through a braided, staged handoff to tested local seedhearts, not one overwhelming rootlight draw. Iona survives under public custody. The Engine becomes a bounded reservoir and archive, the Root Commons has local charters and vetoes, and some regions remain damaged or deliberately independent.
