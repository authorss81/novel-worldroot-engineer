## 1. The premise, and a serious standing doubt about it

**The specified novel is *The Worldroot Engineer*.** Marek Vale, twenty-nine, an agricultural engineer
and irrigation surveyor, finds that the world's magic grows through a living subterranean network.
He wants the charter, the well, the next sowing, and a sister who has a life beyond the fen. He is
good at finding leaks and bad at asking for help, and his flaw is control through expertise: he
reaches the technically correct answer and then believes correctness entitles him to decide alone.
**The premise, the world, the power system and the ending lock are in `bible/`, `outline/series.md`
and `outline/ending.md`, and those are the contract.**

### The standing doubt, stated once and left for a human decision

**The manuscript on disk is not that novel.** Across 686 chapter files `Rootway` appears in zero
and `Worldroot` in zero. What has been written for fourteen volumes is a ledger about a holding
that keeps a wall, a gate, a launder, a book and thirty-five unresolved questions. It is
internally consistent and competently written, and it is not the book the spec, the bible and the
series file specify.
**This pass did not change the planned plot and did not attempt to.** The drift is first in the
decision queue at `state/open-threads.md`, **where it was escalated to the operator on 2026-10-02 by
the review-fix pass over Batch 0005, with the note that it should be settled before Volume 15's plan
is written and that no writing phase may settle it**, and **a later pass, or a human, owns it.** Two
things follow. **The bible is still authoritative and still describes a story that has not been
written; do not treat the manuscript as evidence that the premise is retired.** And **Iona Vey
is the fixed antagonist of the series: not new, not killed, not in custody at the end. No new
final enemy may be introduced**, and a writer who does so is obliged to flag it.

## 2. The world as the bible states it, which is the world the book must be in

**`bible/`, `outline/series.md` and `outline/ending.md` are the contract and this section is a
reminder of them, not a substitute.** Every settlement in Veyra is tied to the Rootway, a living
tissue whose activity is the source of magic, in three layers with no clean boundary: topsoil
exchange, a living trunkway that must stay alive and matched to local water pressure, and a deep
archive of old environmental patterns that is damaged and open to deliberate editing. **Four
constraints bind.** Rootlight is metabolic energy and is not unlimited mana. Memory is
environmental pattern and is not prophecy. **A place may have rootlight and still lack water, seed
or stable soil, and a place may have rich soil and be cut off from the trunkway.** Magic cannot
replace labour, fertiliser, water management, seed selection, medicine or negotiation.

**The one sentence of the history a morning needs: the White Mercy is a schedule, not a season.**
