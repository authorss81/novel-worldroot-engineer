## 2. Volume 14, mornings one to twenty, one line each

**Batch 0001, days 851 to 860, files 638 to 647.** 18,492 words.

- **851**, Wednesday, the eleventh. A schedule is being carried out, and nobody is
 standing behind any of it.
- **852**, Thursday, the twelfth. A paper comes by hand with a covering letter on it.
- **853**, Friday, the thirteenth. A hundred days exactly, on a figure that is bare.
- **854**, Saturday, the fourteenth. The count comes round and comes back empty.
- **855**, Sunday, the fifteenth. Three doors out of four.
- **856**, Monday, the sixteenth. A return comes at the hour nobody set.
- **857**, Tuesday, the seventeenth. The second paper that nobody in the yard can call
 wrong.
- **858**, Wednesday, the eighteenth. Two pieces of work in one room.
- **859**, Thursday, the nineteenth. A place asks for a figure.
- **860**, Friday, the twentieth. A place that would not be first.

**Batch 0002, days 861 to 870, files 648 to 657.** 19,330 words.

- **861**, Saturday, the twenty-first. The clerk copies the office's third column onto
 his own paper, walks a mile and a quarter up a lane in his own boots to the nearest of
 the four households, and comes back at half past one to say that nobody signed, that he
 did not ask anybody to sign, that the house asked him what the two signatures were for,
 and that he told them he did not know. The clerk's third column in his coat is why he
 is still in the yard at dusk.
- **862**, Sunday, the twenty-second. The run is not read and the woman of about
 thirty-eight of Marden says why. The rotation rises by one with no field taken and
 nobody blamed. Sera Quill turns her slate face down and then back up, carries Silling's
 book to the middle table, and says what a round figure in a book does to a line with
 nothing written on it. The mason comes down the wall and says it is the first figure
 he has ever seen in this holding with no work behind it, that he does not mind it, and
 that nobody is to mistake that for agreement.
- **863**, Monday, the twenty-third. Tova Reed works out loud at the bench that seed
 reaches a person by way of a place and not by way of a person; that a household with
 nowhere to put a tray is not refused but has nowhere to sit down; that a branch takes
 the last hour of light off a room. She says she is not finished and the yard does not
 press her, because she said so in front of everybody.
- **864**, Tuesday, the twenty-fourth. **Tova Reed gives up the seed vault**, in her own
 mouth, with the reason that the branches going are the ones outside a zone that ended
 four miles off and did not end politely; that water goes up a pipe that does not care
 whose land it came out of, and seed only reaches somebody standing where it can reach.
 **The card set placed this at day 879. The page governs and it may not be paid again.**
- **865**, Wednesday, the twenty-fifth. A return comes up the fen road and nobody in the
 building has to answer for it. Hesta Lyle says that after forty mornings of watching
 such a thing go past and waiting for somebody to answer for it, this is the first time
 she has watched one go past and thought that nobody is going to be asked anything about
 it at all, and that there is no villain in it anywhere.
- **866**, Thursday, the twenty-sixth. Eight hundred stands on the board the mason reads
 from where he stands, bare, against a far board that is not round.
- **867**, Friday, the twenty-seventh. Hesta Lyle shows the yard a sum that cannot come
 out: four households, two signatures each, a deadline in five working days, and a
 second house nine miles from the fourth over a soft road with a ford that will not take
 a loaded barrow. She says there is no man in this, that nobody is standing behind any
 of those four places, that nobody wants the second one left, and that this is what a
 refusal by a village would have sounded like and this is not one. Soren Rill says he was
 angry at the right sum and the wrong reason, and that the right reason is a road.
- **868**, Saturday, the twenty-eighth. **A name is said once in the one room.** Marek
 refuses to be touched; Nia Vale starts his name and does not finish it; the door is
 left open; she works at the seed house with her back to the yard and he finds that he
 is slower to begin a thing by about a second and a half. **The fourth permanent loss is
 paid and the name is spoken once on this page and on no other page of the batch.**
- **869**, Sunday, the twenty-ninth. A man is very good at his work on the Sunday. He
 reads the three ages before anybody is up, finds the weep at the tap joint and puts a
 saucer under it and writes nothing down, goes down to the ford and gives Hesta Lyle a
 fact and not an opinion, asks the man of about fifty about the six weeks and advises him
 about nothing. **Nine people do not ask him what it was that he gave away**, and that
 was not kindness; it was that they worked out in about four hours on Saturday evening
 that there was nothing they could do.
- **870**, Monday, the thirtieth. A network carrying more than it was built to carry. The
 week adds up to a road and a ford and a pencil and one place that is still not reached.

## 3. What Batch 0002 owes the batch behind

No morning of it repaired a morning behind it. It repeated none of the nine reasons those
mornings gave for not saying the two things, and none of the five reasons the run was not
read. It took nine fresh reasons from nine people, and five fresh reasons the run was not
read from five people, none of them among the five who gave reasons behind it.

It left behind: the standings in `state/current.md` section 2; the seed vault given up and
unrepeatable; the Aldren memory paid and unrecoverable; the signature on the office's
fourth line unclaimed and the column for it uncut; and the clerk's own paper, folded into
his coat, with a doorstep's question on it.

**What the next batch owes this one, all of it confirmed against the pages:**

- It may not repair any of these twenty mornings. They are closed to every pass after the
 one that wrote them.
- It may not pay the seed vault again, re-announce it, or have anybody describe her as
 having given something up.
- It may not recover, soften or explain the Aldren memory, and it may not add a fifth
 permanent loss.
- It may not print a counter for the second rule other than the thirty-ninth time, a bare
 two hundred for the charter, or three hundred and fifty in the read-aloud denominator.
- It may not spell the read-aloud numerator in the window half's form.
- It may not read the run on an even morning, may not offer the door nine hundred yards
 off to anybody in a yard, and may not print an allowance figure for the sixty-six.
- It may not take a field, and it may not ask anybody in this yard to choose anything.
