# Open Threads

## Immediate volume promises

- Can the Lantern Reach well be repaired before the charter inspection, and can Kellan's falsified gauge be exposed without destroying the field the crew came to save?
- Why does Aldren's fieldbook carry a pattern the sky has not produced, and why does the damaged branch answer a fieldbook no ordinary gauge can verify?
- Why is a rootwoken pathhound following the failed branch, and can the cooperative distinguish a territorial community from a monster nest?
- Can Tova's Salt March seed trust and Lantern Reach agree on a temporary relay when both sides need the other's records?

## Historical and political threads

- The Quiet Break's engineering origin and the Crown's concealment must be revealed in stages.
- False Season must be shown as a maintained control pattern rather than natural weather.
- Sera's Brinewake confession, the falsified relay log, and the missing compensation records need evidence, restitution, and a public cost.
- The First Continuity covenant's prohibition on permanent binding must be recovered and compared with later charter law.
- Iona's three succession offers must remain distinct: technical co-caretaker, conditional succession, and final permanent link.

## Relationship and consequence threads

- Marek and Tova must learn to disagree in public without making each other foolish; consent must be a recorded part of anchor work and maintenance.
- Lissa's refusal to forgive Marek for taking the capital contract needs a later, non-absolution beat.
- Halden's professional veto needs a specific reconciliation after the Charter of Many Wells.
- Tarin's critique of central control must remain separate from his responsibility for deaths; his post-climax custody is planned.
- Nia's route out of Tarrow Fen must become freely chosen work rather than a debt repaid to the family.

## Final-volume dependencies

- Regional replacement seedhearts must be planted and load-tested before the caretaker handoff can begin.
- The living control seed recovered in Volume 06 can open local valves but cannot command the running Engine without a caretaker link; its limitation must stay visible as the coalition builds the replacement.
- The final network must use named capacities—local conduits, seed-route bridges, and regional bridges—without making one bridge the central authority.
- Some regions will remain damaged or refuse reconnection; do not erase that cost to make the ending look complete.
